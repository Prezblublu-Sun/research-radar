Motivation· To compare functional outcomes and complications between tripolar total hip arthroplasty and bipolar hemiarthroplasty for displaced femoral neck fractures in elderly patients.
Method· A randomized controlled trial with 19 patients per group, using modified Hardinge approach and one-year follow-up.
Result· Tripolar group showed better Harris Hip Score but longer operative time and more blood loss than bipolar group.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with radiographs and Harris Hip Score at 1, 3, 6, and 12 months.
