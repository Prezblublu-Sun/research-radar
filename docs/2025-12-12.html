HUMIGARD™ REDUCES PARTICLE COUNTS WITHIN THE WOUND: A CADAVER MODEL OF TOTAL HIP ARTHROPLASTY

Molly I. Abraham, C. J. T. Spence, Peter R. Riordan, Rocco P. Pitto Orthopaedic Proceedings 2025-12-12 10.1302/1358-992x.2025.16.034
单位: Fisher & Paykel Healthcare, Auckland, New Zealand
通讯: Molly I. Abraham @ Fisher & Paykel Healthcare, Auckland, New Zealand
相关性: 该论文关注全髋关节置换术中减少颗粒污染的加湿装置，与髋关节假体的应力遮挡或微动分析无关。
边界: This paper quantifies airborne particle counts during surgery, not femoral stem stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics.
动机· 评估手术加湿装置减少全髋置换术中颗粒污染的效果
方法· 在人体标本上模拟手术，用光学粒子计数器测量切口处颗粒数

English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the ability of the HumiGard surgical humidification device to reduce airborne particle counts during dynamic simulation of total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A simulated total hip arthroplasty was performed on a human cadaver, and airborne particles were continuously measured at the wound using an Optical Particle Sizer.
Result· HumiGard significantly reduced median particle counts by 61% compared to standard care, with immediate deflection of exogenous particles.
Validation· Validation via a dynamic cadaveric model of total hip arthroplasty under laminar downflow conditions.

Patient reported outcomes during the first month following anterior and posterior total hip arthroplasty and hip resurfacing

Laura Elisa Streck, Bernhard Springer, Lyubomir Haralambiev, Kimi Spilo, Ulrich Bechler et al. BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders 2025-12-12 10.1186/s12891-025-09337-w
单位: Adult Reconstruction and Joint Replacement Service, Hospital for Special Surgery, 535 East 70th Street, New York, NY, 10021, USA
通讯: Laura Elisa Streck @ Adult Reconstruction and Joint Replacement Service, Hospital for Special Surgery, 535 East 70th Street, New York, NY, 10
相关性: 该论文专注于不同手术入路（DAA vs PA）及髋关节置换术后的患者报告结局，不涉及任何生物力学建模、应力遮挡、微动或植入物设计。
边界: This paper does not quantify hip implant stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics; it is a clinical outcome study without any finite element analysis or implant-related biomechanics.
动机· 比较不同手术入路和髋关节置换术后的早期患者报告结局
方法· 回顾性分析207例髋关节，使用App报告疼痛、用药、功能等
结果· 三组在疼痛、阿片用量、并发症上无差异，20天内阿片停药率99%
验证· 多组比较，调整年龄和BMI后进行统计检验
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare early patient-reported outcomes including pain, opioid use, and function among direct anterior THA, posterior THA, and hip resurfacing.
Method· A retrospective study of 207 hips in 199 patients who used a mobile app to report outcomes daily for 30 days post-surgery.
Result· No significant differences were observed between groups in pain, opioid dosage, or complications; opioid cessation reached 99% by 20 days.
Validation· Clinical comparison with statistical adjustment for age and BMI, using validated patient-reported instruments (HOOS-JR).
