Enhanced bone formation in locally-optimised, low-stiffness additive manufactured titanium implants: An in silico and in vivo tibial advancement study.

Medium AM Biomedical Materials pubmed Direct Save for project animal Exp. validation
Josephine M Shum, Benjamin C Gadomski, Seamus J Tredinnick, Wilson Fok, Justin Fernandez et al. Acta biomaterialia 2023-01-15 10.1016/j.actbio.2022.04.006
单位: Christchurch Regenerative Medicine and Tissue Engineering (CReaTE) Group, Department of Orthopaedic Surgery & Musculoskeletal Medicine, Centre for Bioengineering & Nanomedicine, University of Otago, C
通讯: Tim B F Woodfield @ Christchurch Regenerative Medicine and Tissue Engineering (CReaTE) Group, Department of Orthopaedic Surgery & Musculoske
相关性: 该论文直接研究了低刚度增材制造钛植入物的骨整合性能，与本方向的植入物力学匹配和骨整合相关。
边界: Lacks explicit mechanical testing (static or fatigue) and surface roughness/as-built morphology characterization.
动机· 探讨局部优化的低刚度增材制造钛植入物能否改善骨整合。
方法· 通过有限元模型和绵羊体内实验，比较不同刚度设计的骨整合表现。
结果· 优化低刚度设计促进了更均匀的骨形成和更高的骨-植入物接触率。
验证· 在绵羊体内进行12周植入实验，通过组织形态计量学评估骨整合。
英文摘要与术语

Method· A finite element model of the sheep stifle was developed to design homogeneous and locally-optimized TTA implants with varying stiffness (0.8, 0.6, 0.3/0.1 GPa), which were then fabricated via LPBF and implanted in an in vivo ovine pilot study for 12 weeks.
Result· The optimized low-stiffness design (Ti3) exhibited uniform bone ingrowth and greater bone-implant contact compared to homogeneous designs, with higher bone formation scores.
Validation· In vivo ovine pilot study with static histomorphometry and mineral apposition rate analysis.

Result· The SLM specimens exhibited higher crack growth rates than casted ones but were only slightly higher than forged ones; however, when evaluated by effective stress intensity factor range, all three processes showed similar rates.
Validation· Experimental fatigue crack propagation testing with SEM fractography and comparison to SS400 steel.
