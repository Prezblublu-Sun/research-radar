Influence of Hip Prosthesis Size and Its Coating Area on Bone Remodeling

High Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Direct Read now clinical Exp. validation
Bernd‐Arno Behrens, Anas Bouguecha, Matthias Lerch, Henning Windhagen, Amer Almohallami IEEE Transactions on NanoBioscience 2017-09-11 10.1109/tnb.2017.2750724
单位: Institute of Forming Technology and Machines, Leibniz Universität Hannover, Garbsen, Germany
通讯: Bernd‐Arno Behrens @ Institute of Forming Technology and Machines, Leibniz Universität Hannover, Garbsen, Germany
相关性: 该论文直接研究髋假体尺寸和涂层面积对股骨近端骨重塑的影响，与用户关注的方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not report micromotion values or contact mechanics at the bone-implant interface.
动机· 探究髋假体尺寸和涂层面积对骨重塑的影响，以减少全髋置换后的骨丢失。
方法· 建立骨重塑数值模型，结合有限元分析，模拟不同假体尺寸和涂层面积下的骨密度变化。
结果· 假体越小骨重塑越少；涂层面积增大可减少外侧骨重塑但增加内侧骨重塑。
验证· 通过双能X射线吸收法（DEXA）临床数据验证模型。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate how hip prosthesis size and coating area affect periprosthetic bone remodeling, aiming to reduce bone resorption after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A numerical bone remodeling model was developed and coupled with finite element analysis to simulate bone density changes for different implant sizes and coating configurations.
Result· Smaller implants reduced bone remodeling by 14.4%; extending the coating area decreased lateral remodeling but increased medial remodeling.
Validation· The model was validated against dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DEXA) data from clinical studies.
