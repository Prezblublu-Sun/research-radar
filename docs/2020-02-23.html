通讯: Zemin Wang @ Wuhan National Laboratory for Optoelectronics, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan, 430074, PR China

Result· Higher laser power coarsened columnar grains but did not significantly affect strength or ductility.
Validation· Validation via tensile testing and microstructural characterization (SEM, EBSD).

单位: Department of Diagnosis and Surgery School of Dentistry, Sao Paulo State University Araraquara Brazil
通讯: Guilherme dos Santos Trento @ Department of Diagnosis and Surgery School of Dentistry, Sao Paulo State University Araraquara Brazil
相关性: 该论文研究了钛种植体表面处理对骨形成的影响，但未涉及LPBF制造、应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), no stress shielding or stiffness matching analysis, and no surface roughness or as-built morphology characterization.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the histological and microtomographic response of peri-implant bone tissue around titanium implants with different surface treatments placed in bone defects with or without bone substitute materials.

Validation· Histological, histomorphometric, and micro-computed tomography evaluation at 15, 30, and 60 days post-implantation.
