Failure mechanisms of additively manufactured porous biomaterials: Effects of porosity and type of unit cell.

J Kadkhodapour, H Montazerian, A Ch Darabi, A P Anaraki, S M Ahmadi et al. Journal of the mechanical behavior of biomedical materials 2015-10-01 10.1016/j.jmbbm.2015.06.012
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Shahid Rajaee Teacher Training University, Tehran, Iran; Institute for Materials Testing, Materials Science and Strength of Materials (IMWF), University of Stuttg
通讯: J Kadkhodapour @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Shahid Rajaee Teacher Training University, Tehran, Iran; Institute for Materials T
相关性: 该论文研究了选择性激光熔融制备的多孔钛合金支架的失效机制，直接关联应力遮挡和刚度匹配问题，但未涉及疲劳和骨整合。
边界: This paper focuses on static compression and does not cover fatigue loading, surface roughness, or osseointegration characterization.
动机· 研究增材制造多孔钛合金支架的变形和失效机制，以避免应力遮挡。
方法· 采用Johnson-Cook塑性损伤模型的有限元模拟，与立方和钻石晶格结构支架的压缩实验对比。
结果· 立方晶格（拉伸主导）逐层失效，钻石晶格（弯曲主导）沿45°剪切带失效。
验证· 计算应力-应变曲线与实验误差小于18%，变形模式与标度律分析一致。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the deformation and failure mechanisms of additively manufactured porous Ti6Al4V scaffolds with different unit cell types and porosities, aiming to achieve mechanical compatibility with bone and avoid stress shielding.
Method· Finite element models incorporating Johnson-Cook plasticity and damage were developed and validated against experimental compression tests on cubic and diamond lattice structures fabricated by selective laser melting.
Result· Stretch-dominated cubic lattices exhibited layer-by-layer collapse, while bending-dominated diamond lattices failed via 45° shear bands, with computational stress-strain curves matching experimental data within 18% error.
Validation· The computational results were compared with previously published experimental compression tests on the same porous structures, and the deformation modes were confirmed by scaling law analysis.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To address poor primary stability and osseointegration of dental implants in cases of severe posterior maxillary atrophy with thin sinus floor.
Method· Autogenous tooth bone graft blocks were used to wrap the implant during sinus membrane elevation.
Result· Four-year clinical follow-up demonstrated the applicability and improved outcomes of this technique.
Validation· Clinical case series with follow-up.
