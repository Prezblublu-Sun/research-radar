Effect of the shot peening on surface properties of Ti-6Al-4V alloy produced by means of DMLS technology

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Skim only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Remigiusz Żebrowski, Mariusz Walczak, Tomasz Klepka, Kamil Pasierbiewicz Eksploatacja i Niezawodnosc - Maintenance and Reliability 2018-12-19 10.17531/ein.2019.1.6
相关性: 该论文研究了DMLS Ti-6Al-4V合金喷丸处理对表面性能和耐腐蚀性的影响，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper does not address porous structure design, stress shielding, stiffness matching, fatigue testing, or osseointegration, which are core to the AM biomedical materials direction.
动机· 研究喷丸处理对DMLS Ti-6Al-4V合金表面性能的影响，以改善植入物长期功能。
方法· 在不同压力下使用三种介质对DMLS试样进行喷丸处理，并进行显微硬度、表面形貌和电化学测试。
结果· 喷丸压力增加导致耐腐蚀性下降，ZrO2陶瓷球介质效果最佳。
验证· 通过显微硬度、表面粗糙度和动电位极化曲线验证表面改性效果。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of shot peening on the surface layer and corrosion resistance of DMLS-produced Ti-6Al-4V alloy for improved implant performance.
Method· Specimens were shot peened under three pressures using CrNi steel shot, crushed nut shells, and ZrO2 ceramic balls, followed by microhardness, surface topography, and potentiodynamic tests.
Result· Increased working pressure deteriorated corrosion resistance, with ZrO2 ceramic balls providing the best corrosion performance.
Validation· Validation was performed through microhardness measurements, surface roughness evaluation, and potentiodynamic polarization tests.
