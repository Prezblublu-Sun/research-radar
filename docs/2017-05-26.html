边界: This paper applies UQ to reservoir saturation mapping, not to FEA surrogate modeling for implant or biomechanics design; no contact mechanics, no structural optimization, no biomedical context.

相关性: 该论文针对油藏模拟中的不确定性量化，与生物力学植入物设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on reservoir engineering, not biomedical implant design or FEA surrogate modeling; no contact mechanics, no implant validation, no biomechanics context.
动机· 高效采样非线性多模态后验分布以量化不确定性。
方法· 将随机最大似然法嵌入多目标遗传算法，各方程作为独立目标。
结果· 与MH算法和LM-RML相比，获得可接受样本集且易于并行化。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To efficiently sample nonlinear multimodal posterior distributions for uncertainty quantification in reservoir forecasting.
Method· A multi-objectivised randomised maximum likelihood method is proposed, where each equation is treated as a separate objective within a genetic algorithm.
Result· The proposed method yields an acceptable set of samples and benefits from straightforward parallelization, reducing total processing time.
Validation· Validation is performed on the IC-Fault model by comparing against Metropolis-Hastings and RML with Levenberg-Marquardt minimizer.
