Crossword puzzle: a tool for enhancing medical students' learning in microbiology and immunology

Exclude FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Ignore none
B S Mohan, Vinod Nambiar, Shivaraj Gowda, Rajeev Arvindakshan International Journal of Research in Medical Sciences 2018-02-22 10.18203/2320-6012.ijrms20180591
单位: Department of Microbiology and Immunology, Oman Medical College, Sohar, Oman
通讯: B S Mohan @ Department of Microbiology and Immunology, Oman Medical College, Sohar, Oman
相关性: 该论文与用户的研究方向（FEA与代理模型）完全无关，属于医学教育领域。
边界: This paper is about medical education using crossword puzzles, with no FEA, surrogate modeling, uncertainty quantification, or biomechanics content.
动机· 评估填字游戏在微生物学和免疫学教学中的效果。
方法· 创建填字游戏并通过问卷调查学生感知。
结果· 超过85%的学生认为填字游戏增强了学习效果。
验证· 通过Likert量表问卷收集学生反馈。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the effectiveness of crossword puzzles as an active learning tool in microbiology and immunology courses.
Method· Crossword puzzles were created using free online resources and administered during lectures; student perceptions were collected via an 8-item Likert-scale questionnaire.
Result· Over 85% of students reported that crossword puzzles enhanced their learning and helped identify important topics.
Validation· Validation was based on student self-reported perceptions through a structured questionnaire.

单位: Süleyman Demirel University, Faculty of Medicine, Department of Orthopaedics and Traumatology, Süleyman Demirel Caddesi, Isparta, 32260, Turkey. Electronic address: emrahkovalak@yahoo.com
通讯: Emrah Kovalak @ Süleyman Demirel University, Faculty of Medicine, Department of Orthopaedics and Traumatology, Süleyman Demirel Caddesi,
相关性: 该论文评估全髋关节置换术后臀肌脂肪萎缩与功能结果的关系，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡或生物力学建模。
边界: This paper does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics; it is a clinical outcome study without FEA or implant design.
动机· 评估全髋置换后臀肌脂肪萎缩对功能的影响
方法· 回顾性分析22例患者MRI和Harris髋关节评分

Motivation· To evaluate how fatty atrophy of hip abductors affects functional outcome after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Retrospective analysis of MRI and Harris Hip Score in 22 patients with unilateral THA via posterolateral approach.
Result· Fatty atrophy was more pronounced in the operated hip; contralateral gluteus minimus atrophy and age were strongly correlated with lower HHS.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with MRI-based Goutallier grading.
