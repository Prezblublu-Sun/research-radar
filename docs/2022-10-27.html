动机· 探究3D打印骨支架中丝径和间距对骨传导性的影响。

Result· When all filaments aligned with bone ingrowth direction, filament size and distance (0.40-1.25 mm) had no significant effect; with 50% alignment, osteoconductivity decreased significantly with larger filament dimension and distance.
Validation· In vivo rabbit calvarial defect model.

Method· A prospective cross-sectional study without control group, collecting ICIQ-SF, WOMAC scores, and hip range of motion before and 3 months after THA via direct lateral approach.

Degree of Pelvic Rotation in the Coronal Plane on Postoperative Radiographs Obtained after Total Hip Arthroplasty

单位: Department of Long-Term Care and Management, Wu Feng University, Chiayi County 621303, Taiwan
通讯: Chen‐Kun Liaw @ Department of Orthopedics, School of Medicine, College of Medicine, Taipei Medical University, Taipei City 110, Taiwan
相关性: 该论文仅关注术后X光片上的骨盆旋转角度测量，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡、微动或接触力学建模。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain, micromotion, or implant-bone interface mechanics; it is purely a radiographic measurement study without any biomechanical modeling or implant design context.
动机· 量化全髋置换术后X光片上骨盆旋转的角度。
方法· 测量骶尾关节与耻骨联合的水平位移和距离，计算旋转角。
结果· 平均骨盆旋转角度为2.50°，不可忽略。
验证· 通过观察者间和观察者内误差的配对t检验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify the degree of pelvic rotation on postoperative radiographs after total hip arthroplasty, which is often neglected in cup anteversion measurements.
Method· Pelvic rotation angle was calculated as arcsin(h/ssd) using horizontal displacement (h) of the sacrococcygeal junction relative to the pubic symphysis and the distance (ssd) between them.
Result· The mean pelvic rotation was 2.50°, with a standard deviation of h of 7.84 mm, indicating non-negligible rotation.
Validation· Interobserver and intraobserver errors were assessed using paired t-tests, all p-values > 0.05.

边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain, micromotion, or contact mechanics; it is purely a statistical imputation study on patient-reported outcomes, with no biomechanical modeling or implant design.
动机· 处理全髋关节置换术后FJS-12问卷的缺失数据
方法· 使用预测均值匹配进行多重插补
结果· 插补后FJS-12评分可靠性高，完整回答者评分显著高于部分回答者

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To address missing responses in the Forgotten Joint Score-12 after total hip arthroplasty, which can bias analyses.
Method· Predictive mean matching with 65 imputation models was applied to recover missing items in partial respondents.
Result· Imputed FJS-12 scores showed excellent reliability, and complete respondents had significantly higher scores than those with 4-11 missing items.
Validation· Validation was performed by matching partial respondents with complete respondents and comparing imputed scores to true scores.
