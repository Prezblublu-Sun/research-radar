English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of different heat treatments on the microstructural evolution and mechanical properties of a novel Al-Si-Cu-Ni-Fe-Re alloy fabricated by selective laser melting.

Validation· Mechanical testing (bending) combined with scanning electron microscopy and energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy for microstructural characterization.
