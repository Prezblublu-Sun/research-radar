Motivation· To evaluate transfusion risk factors in anterolateral supine total hip arthroplasty without autologous blood donation and closed suction drainage.
Method· Retrospective analysis of 316 patients with logistic regression and ROC analysis on preoperative hemoglobin and other variables.

Validation· Clinical data from 316 patients with statistical validation.

Motivation· To evaluate the safety and efficacy of immediate weight-bearing protocols after extended trochanteric osteotomy in revision total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A multicenter retrospective review of 53 patients undergoing ETO during RTHA with immediate weight-bearing protocol, analyzing union rates, complications, and functional scores.
