Development of collagenous filaments with tuneable mechanical properties using a 3D bioprinter and molecular crowding

Alejandro Rossello Martinez, Zixie Liang, Zihan Zhou, Xiangyu Gong, Ryan Nguyen et al. Biofabrication 2026-05-11 10.1088/1758-5090/ae6371
单位: Department of Biomedical Engineering, Yale University, New Haven, CT, United States of America
通讯: Alejandro Rossello Martinez @ Department of Biomedical Engineering, Yale University, New Haven, CT, United States of America
相关性: 该论文涉及3D生物打印和胶原纤维的力学性能调控，但未使用AI/ML技术，与AI生物打印方向不直接相关。
边界: This paper does not incorporate any AI/ML method into the bioprinting workflow, nor does it involve autonomous decision-making or experimental validation of an AI model.
动机· 开发具有可调力学性能的胶原纤维用于承重组织修复。
方法· 结合商业3D生物打印机和分子拥挤原理，调控拉丝速度和喷嘴直径。
结果· 获得抗拉强度0.64 GPa、模量8.9 GPa的纤维，超过天然肌腱。
验证· 通过SEM、偏振光显微镜和力学测试验证结构-性能关系。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To engineer collagen filaments with tunable mechanical properties for load-bearing tissue regeneration.
Method· A commercial 3D bioprinter combined with molecular crowding in a hypertonic polyethylene glycol bath, varying pulling speed and nozzle diameter.
Result· Filaments achieved an ultimate tensile strength of 0.64 GPa and Young's modulus of 8.9 GPa, surpassing native tendon.
Validation· Structural and mechanical characterization via SEM, polarized light microscopy, and tensile testing.

3D Printed Silk Fibroin-Gelatin Construct Targeting BMP Signaling for Articular Cartilage Regeneration Within a Full-Thickness Cartilage Defect in a Surgically Induced Osteoarthritic Rat Knee Joint.

Sayeda Fauzia Iqbal, Nilotpal Majumder, Bhupendra Kumar, Chandrashish Roy, Saahiba Thaleshwari et al. Advanced healthcare materials 2026-05-11 10.1002/adhm.71252
相关性: 该论文涉及生物打印和软骨再生，但未使用AI技术，与AI生物打印方向不直接相关。
边界: No AI/ML integration in any bioprinting workflow stage; purely biomaterial and signaling pathway study.
动机· 开发一种能同时调控BMP和Wnt信号通路的生物墨水，用于骨关节炎软骨再生。
方法· 将BMP抑制剂LDN-193189共价结合到丝素蛋白-明胶生物墨水中，3D打印构建支架。
结果· 在大鼠骨关节炎模型中成功再生了透明软骨，无肥大或纤维软骨形成。
验证· 大鼠膝关节全层软骨缺损模型，组织学和免疫组化验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a biomaterial ink that simultaneously inhibits aberrant BMP signaling and promotes Wnt/β-catenin signaling for cartilage regeneration in osteoarthritic joints.
Method· LDN-193189, a BMP inhibitor, was covalently conjugated to silk fibroin-gelatin bioink and 3D-printed into scaffolds.
Result· The functionalized scaffold regenerated hyaline cartilage in a rat OA model, evidenced by Safranin O staining and collagen expression without hypertrophy.
Validation· In vivo validation using a critical-sized cartilage defect in a surgically induced OA rat knee joint.

Flash Assembloids: A Rapid Biofabrication of a Platform for Modeling Early Glioblastoma Invasion at the Glioblastoma-Brain Organoid Interfaces.

边界: This paper does not incorporate AI/ML into any bioprinting workflow stage, lacks experimental validation of AI models, and does not advance toward autonomous decision-making.

方法· 利用氧化海藻酸微凝胶封装GBM细胞与脑类器官共培养构建组装体。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a reproducible bioengineered platform that recapitulates early glioblastoma invasion at the tumor-brain interface.
Method· GBM cells encapsulated in self-degradable oxidized alginate microgels were integrated with dorsal forebrain organoids to form assembloids.

Validation· Validation included live-cell imaging, transcriptomic profiling, and in vivo implantation confirming aggressive infiltration.
