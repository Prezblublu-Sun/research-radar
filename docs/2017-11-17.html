相关性: 该论文将机器学习用于量子反应动力学中的逆散射问题，与生物力学有限元代理建模无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on quantum reaction dynamics and potential energy surface construction, not on surrogate modeling for FEA, implant biomechanics, or structural optimization.
动机· 减少量子反应动力学中势能面构建所需的从头算能量点数。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce the number of ab initio energy calculations required for constructing accurate potential energy surfaces in quantum reactive scattering.
Method· A Bayesian optimization algorithm iteratively selects new ab initio points to improve the potential energy surface without analytical fitting.
Result· Accurate global potential energy surfaces were obtained using only 30 ab initio energies for the H+H2 reaction and 290 for the OH+H2 reaction.
Validation· The surfaces were validated by comparing reaction probabilities from rigorous quantum dynamics calculations over a wide energy range.
