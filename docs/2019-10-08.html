单位: School of Aircraft Engineering Nanchang Hangkong University 330063 Nanchang China
通讯: Xiao‐An Hu @ School of Aircraft Engineering Nanchang Hangkong University 330063 Nanchang China
相关性: 该论文研究Inconel 625合金的SLM工艺和高温力学性能，缺乏生物医学植入物所需的孔隙结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能分析。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue, surface roughness, or osseointegration for biomedical implants.
动机· 研究SLM制备的Inconel 625合金在不同温度下的力学行为。
方法· 在不同温度下进行拉伸试验，并用OM、SEM和EDS分析微观结构。
结果· SLM Inconel 625在低温时塑性好，但高温下因非均匀组织和晶界碳化物而脆化。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To experimentally investigate the mechanical behavior of Inconel 625 alloy fabricated by selective laser melting across a wide temperature range up to 1000°C.
Method· Tensile tests were conducted at seven temperatures from 20°C to 1000°C, and microstructural analysis was performed using optical microscopy, scanning electron microscopy, and energy dispersive spectroscopy.
Result· The SLM-processed Inconel 625 exhibits excellent low-temperature plasticity but significant embrittlement at elevated temperatures due to non-homogeneous microstructure and grain boundary carbide formation.
Validation· Validation was achieved through experimental tensile testing and microstructural characterization.
