英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare functional outcomes and survival between internal fixation and hip replacement for undisplaced femoral neck fractures in elderly patients.
Method· A registry-based study using the Swedish National Hip Fracture Registry, analyzing 1-year survival, reoperation rates, and functional outcomes in patients aged ≥70 years.
Result· Patients undergoing total hip arthroplasty had lower adjusted 1-year mortality, but baseline health differences confound comparisons between surgical methods.
Validation· Clinical registry data with multivariable adjustment for confounders.
