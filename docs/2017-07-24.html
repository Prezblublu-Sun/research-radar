Motivation· To improve acetabular component orientation during THA by correcting pelvic obliquity in the lateral position.
Method· A gradienter and plumb were used to correct pelvic obliquity intraoperatively under fluoroscopy.
Result· The bias of abduction angle was significantly reduced in the correction group compared to controls.
Validation· Randomized clinical trial with 50 patients.
