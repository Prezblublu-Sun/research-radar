Parameter uncertainty quantification using surrogate models applied to a spatial model of yeast mating polarization

单位: Department of Mathematics, Ohio State University, Columbus, Ohio, United States of America
通讯: Ching‐Shan Chou @ Department of Mathematics, Ohio State University, Columbus, Ohio, United States of America
相关性: 该论文使用多项式代理模型进行参数不确定性量化，方法可迁移到生物力学植入物设计中的代理建模与UQ。
边界: This paper applies surrogate modeling to systems biology models (ODE/PDE of yeast mating), not to FEA-based implant mechanics or biomechanical design optimization.
动机· 解决复杂生物模型中参数不确定性量化计算成本高的问题。
方法· 用多项式代理模型替代昂贵仿真，结合MCMC进行贝叶斯参数推断。
结果· 代理模型显著提升效率，推断出与实验一致的参数分布。
验证· 通过ODE和PDE模型与已有单点估计结果对比验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To enable computationally efficient uncertainty quantification and parameter estimation for complex biological models where direct simulation is prohibitive.
Method· Polynomial surrogate models are fitted from a small number of simulations and then used for global sensitivity analysis and Bayesian MCMC inference.
Result· Surrogate-based inference achieved convergence for 15 parameters in a spatial PDE model, revealing parameter distributions consistent with experimental observations.
Validation· Results were validated against published single-point estimates for an ODE model and by consistency with experimental data from another yeast species.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To survey expert opinions on the incidence and prevention of trunnionosis in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A 25-question web-based survey was sent to 345 fellowship-trained adult reconstruction orthopedic surgeons.
Result· Surgeons identified head-neck material composite as the most important factor and commonly use ceramic heads with metal stems.
Validation· No validation; the study is based solely on survey responses.
