Simulations of 3D bioprinting: predicting bioprintability of nanofibrillar inks

Johan Göhl, Kajsa Markstedt, Andreas Mark, Karl Håkansson, Paul Gatenholm et al. Biofabrication 2018-05-29 10.1088/1758-5090/aac872
单位: Fraunhofer-Chalmers Centre, Chalmers Science Park, Gothenburg, Sweden
通讯: Johan Göhl @ Fraunhofer-Chalmers Centre, Chalmers Science Park, Gothenburg, Sweden  |  Kajsa Markstedt @ Department of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering, Chalmers University of Technology, Kemigården 4, Gothenburg, Sweden  |  Fredrik Edelvik @ Fraunhofer-Chalmers Centre, Chalmers Science Park, Gothenburg, Sweden
相关性: 未使用AI方法，仅为CFD模拟，与AI驱动生物打印的研究方向不直接相关。
边界: No AI/ML method is used; only CFD simulation without integration of AI into bioprinting workflow.
动机· 开发生物墨水时，打印性评估耗时且需减少实验。
方法· 利用计算流体动力学模拟，基于流变学模型预测打印过程。
结果· 模拟能有效预测打印参数对打印精度和粘弹性应力的影响。
验证· 通过振荡频率测量数据验证流变模型。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Experimental evaluation of bioink printability is time-consuming, and simulations can reduce the need for costly cell-laden experiments.
Method· A computational fluid dynamics simulation was employed, using a full stress rheology model derived from oscillation frequency measurements to capture viscoelastic behavior.
Result· The simulation successfully predicted the influence of printing parameters on printing fidelity and viscoelastic stresses in the bioink.
Validation· Validation was performed indirectly using rheological measurement data to define material properties for the simulation.

Validation· In vitro bacterial adhesion assays using S. sanguinis and osteogenic differentiation assays (alkaline phosphatase, mineralization, gene expression).
