Getting PEEK to Stick to Bone: The Development of Porous PEEK for Interbody Fusion Devices

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
F. Brennan Torstrick, David L. Safranski, J. Kenneth Burkus, James L. Chappuis, Christopher S.D. Lee et al. Techniques in Orthopaedics 2017-08-23 10.1097/bto.0000000000000242
单位: Petit Institute of Bioengineering and Biosciences, Georgia Institute of Technology
通讯: F. Brennan Torstrick @ Petit Institute of Bioengineering and Biosciences, Georgia Institute of Technology  |  Robert E. Guldberg @ Petit Institute of Bioengineering and Biosciences, Georgia Institute of Technology
相关性: 该论文涉及多孔PEEK植入物的骨整合，但未涉及LPBF制造或疲劳测试，与AM生物医学材料方向的相关性有限。
边界: This paper does not involve LPBF fabrication, fatigue testing, or stress shielding analysis; it focuses on porous PEEK for spinal fusion with limited mechanical characterization.
动机· 开发多孔PEEK植入物以增强骨整合和脊柱融合效果。
方法· 通过挤出工艺制造多孔PEEK结构，模拟松质骨力学性能。
结果· 多孔PEEK提高了细胞附着、增殖和矿化，动物实验中骨整合和固定强度优于光滑PEEK。
验证· 生物力学测试、体外细胞实验、动物实验和临床病例研究。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a porous PEEK interbody fusion cage that enhances osseointegration and improves spinal fusion outcomes compared to smooth PEEK cages.
Method· A porous PEEK structure was extruded directly from solid PEEK to mimic trabecular bone properties, and its biomechanical and biological performance was evaluated.
Result· Porous PEEK demonstrated greater expulsion resistance, cell attachment, proliferation, mineralization, and bone ingrowth, leading to twice the fixation strength of smooth PEEK in animal studies.
Validation· Validation included biomechanical testing, in vitro cell assays, preclinical animal studies, and two clinical case studies.

Motivation· To evaluate the cytotoxicity of a titanium alloy coated with hydroxyapatite via a novel atmospheric plasma jet deposition method.

Result· Single-side coated samples were non-cytotoxic, while double-side coated samples showed slight-to-moderate cytotoxicity, which was reduced by pre-conditioning in culture medium.
Validation· In vitro cytotoxicity assays following ISO 10993-5 standards.
