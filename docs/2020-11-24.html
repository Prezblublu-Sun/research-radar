Håvard Dale, Pål Høvding, Sindre M Tveit, Julie B Graff, Olav Lutro et al. Acta Orthopaedica 2020-11-24 10.1080/17453674.2020.1851533
单位: Department of Clinical Medicine, University of Bergen, Bergen;
通讯: Håvard Dale @ Department of Clinical Medicine, University of Bergen, Bergen;
相关性: 该论文是关于全髋关节置换术后感染风险的临床队列研究，未涉及生物力学建模或植入物设计。
边界: No biomechanical modeling, no stress/strain analysis, no implant design, no FEA or contact mechanics.
动机· 评估2005-2019年间挪威全髋关节置换术后深部感染导致的翻修风险变化趋势。
方法· 利用挪威关节置换登记处108,854例初次全髋关节置换数据，采用Cox回归分析不同时间段翻修风险。
结果· 翻修风险在研究期间增加，但2010年后趋于稳定，早期翻修风险增加明显。
验证· 基于大型国家登记处数据的临床流行病学分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To assess trends in the risk of revision due to deep infection after primary total hip arthroplasty from 2005 to 2019 using a national registry.
Method· Cox regression analysis was performed on 108,854 primary THAs from the Norwegian Arthroplasty Register, stratified into 5-year periods.
Result· The risk of revision due to infection increased over the study period but leveled off after 2010, mainly driven by early revisions.
Validation· Clinical registry-based study with multivariable adjustment and confidence intervals.
