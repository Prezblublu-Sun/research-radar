单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, University of Virginia, Charlottesville, Virginia

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the trend in postoperative inpatient ultrasound screening for DVT after THA over the past decade and its impact on patient outcomes.
Method· A retrospective analysis of a national inpatient database was performed to assess ultrasound utilization and DVT incidence.
Result· Ultrasound use decreased dramatically without an increase in DVT-related complications or mortality.
Validation· Retrospective statistical analysis of a large-scale national database.
