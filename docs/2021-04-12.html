English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the long-term clinical outcome of implant-induced injury on adjacent teeth and determine necessity of immediate extraction.

Validation· Clinical validation through long-term follow-up (mean 122.7 months) and statistical analysis of complications and implant stability.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate whether systemic omeprazole treatment affects osseointegration of titanium dental implants.
Method· Titanium implants were placed in rat tibiae, and animals received omeprazole at two dosages; after four weeks, reverse-torque testing and serum biochemical assays were performed.

Validation· In vivo rat model with biomechanical reverse-torque testing and biochemical analysis of bone-related serum parameters.
