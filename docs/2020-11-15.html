Mansureh‐Sadat Nabiyan, Faramarz Khoshnoudian, Babak Moaveni, Hamed Ebrahimian Structural Control and Health Monitoring 2020-11-15 10.1002/stc.2647
单位: Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering Amirkabir University of Technology Tehran Iran
通讯: Faramarz Khoshnoudian @ Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering Amirkabir University of Technology Tehran Iran
相关性: 该论文提出了基于贝叶斯有限元模型更新的数字孪生方法，可迁移至生物力学植入物设计中的虚拟传感与不确定性量化。
边界: This paper focuses on offshore wind turbines, not biomedical implants or biomechanics, and does not address contact mechanics, stress shielding, or implant-specific constraints.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop mechanics-based digital twins for offshore wind turbines using sparse measurement data for virtual sensing and fatigue life prediction.
Method· A time-domain sequential Bayesian finite element model updating approach is proposed to jointly estimate model parameters and unknown input forces.
Result· Both Bayesian and modal-based methods accurately predict strain response, with the Bayesian approach yielding slightly better results and enabling input load identification and uncertainty quantification.
Validation· Validated using measured strain data from a 2-MW offshore wind turbine at the Blyth wind farm.

Motivation· To determine whether hip movement precautions reduce prosthesis dislocation rates after total hip arthroplasty via a posterior approach.
Method· A systematic review and meta-analysis following PRISMA guidelines, searching CINAHL, MEDLINE, and Embase up to September 2020.
Result· No significant difference in dislocation rates was found between patients with movement precautions and those with minimal or no restrictions (RR=0.98, 95%CI 0.58-1.67).
Validation· Meta-analysis of 7 studies with 9599 total hip arthroplasties, assessed using the Downs and Black checklist.
