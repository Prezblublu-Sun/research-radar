单位: Kaiser Permanente, Surgical Outcomes and Analysis, 8954 Rio San Diego Drive, Suite 406, San Diego, CA, 92108, USA. Liz.w.paxton@kp.org
通讯: Elizabeth W. Paxton @ Kaiser Permanente, Surgical Outcomes and Analysis, 8954 Rio San Diego Drive, Suite 406, San Diego, CA, 92108, USA. Liz.w
相关性: 该论文仅关注全髋关节置换术后再入院的临床风险因素，未涉及任何生物力学建模或植入物设计。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain, micromotion, or any biomechanical metrics; it is a clinical outcomes study with no FEA or implant mechanics.
动机· 识别全髋关节置换术后30天内再入院的风险因素。
方法· 基于Kaiser Permanente关节置换登记处的12,030例患者数据，采用逻辑回归分析。
结果· 再入院率为3.6%，主要原因为感染和脱位；肥胖、并发症等为可改变风险因素。
验证· 使用电子健康记录和索赔数据验证再入院事件。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To identify modifiable risk factors for 30-day hospital readmission after primary total hip arthroplasty in a large integrated healthcare system.
Method· A logistic regression model was applied to 12,030 primary THA patients from the Kaiser Permanente Total Joint Replacement Registry to analyze patient, clinical, and system-related risk factors.
Result· The 30-day readmission rate was 3.6%, with infection and dislocation as leading causes; modifiable factors included obesity, comorbidities, and discharge destination.
Validation· Readmissions were identified using electronic health records and claims data, capturing events both within and outside the system.
