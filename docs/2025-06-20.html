Mesh-Informed Neural Operator : A Transformer Generative Approach

Yaozhong Shi, Zachary E. Ross, Domniki Asimaki, Kamyar Azizzadenesheli arXiv preprint 2025-06-20 link
相关性: 该论文提出的网格信息神经算子（MINO）能够处理非规则网格，对生物力学代理模型有潜在应用价值，但未涉及约束优化或不确定性量化。
边界: This paper introduces a generative neural operator architecture but lacks application to FEA surrogate modeling, constraint handling, or uncertainty quantification, and does not validate on implant/biomechanics problems.
动机· 克服傅里叶神经算子对规则网格的依赖，扩展函数空间生成模型的应用范围。
方法· 使用图神经算子和交叉注意力机制构建网格无关的生成神经算子。
结果· 提出MINO框架，支持非规则域上的生成、逆问题和回归任务。
验证· 未提供实验验证，仅理论架构设计。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Overcome the reliance of functional generative models on regular grids by introducing a mesh-informed neural operator that is domain- and discretization-agnostic.
Method· Integrate graph neural operators with cross-attention mechanisms to build a backbone that extends generative modeling to irregular domains.
Result· Propose MINO, a unified framework enabling generative, inverse, and regression tasks on arbitrary meshes with standardized evaluation metrics.
Validation· No experimental validation is provided; the paper focuses on architectural innovation and theoretical perspective.

相关性: 该论文研究了SLM打印的Inconel 718点阵结构的力学性能，但未涉及生物医学应用或应力遮挡问题。

Result· Significant improvements in yield strength and energy absorption were achieved for high-performance applications.
Validation· Mechanical validation was performed via compression testing on as-built and heat-treated specimens.

Ke Li, Chenyu Zhang, Yuxin Ding, Xianbiao Hu, Ruwen Qin arXiv preprint 2025-06-20 link
相关性: 该论文研究自动驾驶场景分类，与有限元代理建模和植入物设计完全无关。
边界: This paper focuses on multi-label scene classification for autonomous vehicles, with no connection to FEA, surrogate modeling, constraints, uncertainty quantification, or biomechanical design.
动机· 克服多标签场景分类中数据标注困难和标签扩展问题
方法· 提出知识获取与积累结合一致性主动学习的深度学习方法

English summary & terms
Motivation· To address the challenges of acquiring balanced multi-label datasets and re-annotation when new attributes emerge in driving scene classification.
Method· Proposes a deep learning method integrating Knowledge Acquisition and Accumulation (KAA) with Consistency-based Active Learning (CAL) to leverage heterogeneous single-label datasets.
Result· Achieves 56.1% improvement over ImageNet-pretrained baseline on DSI dataset and outperforms state-of-the-art methods with 85% less data.
Validation· Ablation studies on DSI and comparative evaluations on BDD100K and HSD datasets.

Critical Appraisal of Fairness Metrics in Clinical Predictive AI

João Matos, Ben Van Calster, Leo Anthony Celi, Paula Dhiman, Judy Wawira Gichoya et al. arXiv preprint 2025-06-20 link
相关性: 该文献讨论临床人工智能的公平性度量，与有限元代理模型方向无关。
边界: This paper is a scoping review on fairness metrics in clinical AI, with no FEA, surrogate modeling, constraint handling, or uncertainty quantification, and no transferability to implant/biomechanics design.
动机· 临床预测AI存在偏见风险，但公平性定义不清。
方法· 对2014-2024年文献进行系统综述，提取62个公平性度量。
结果· 发现度量标准碎片化，缺乏临床验证和不确定性量化。
验证· 综述性研究，未进行实际实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Predictive AI in clinical practice risks bias, yet the definition of fairness remains ambiguous.
Method· A scoping review of 820 records from five databases (2014-2024) identified 62 fairness metrics from 41 included studies.
Result· The landscape of fairness metrics is fragmented, with limited clinical validation and overreliance on threshold-dependent measures.
Validation· No experimental validation; the study is a literature review without empirical testing.
