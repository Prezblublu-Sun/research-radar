通讯: Zhilong Fang @ University of British Columbia
相关性: 该论文涉及不确定性量化方法，但应用于地球物理波场反演，与生物力学有限元代理建模无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on wavefield reconstruction inversion in geophysics, with no application to biomedical FEA, implant design, or biomechanics constraints.
动机· 为波场重建反演提供高效的不确定性量化方法
方法· 提出基于贝叶斯推断的新后验分布，利用对角Gauss-Newton Hessian近似高斯分布

Motivation· To enable computationally tractable uncertainty quantification for wavefield reconstruction inversion (WRI).

Result· The method makes UQ for WRI computationally feasible and provides reasonable uncertainty estimates in numerical experiments.
Validation· Numerical benchmark examples demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed UQ approach.

单位: Hip Surgery Unit, Institute of Orthopaedics "Carlos E. Ottolenghi", Italian Hospital of Buenos Aires, Potosí 4247, C1199ACK, Buenos Aires, Argentina. martin.buttaro@hospitalitaliano.org.ar
通讯: Martín Buttaro @ Hip Surgery Unit, Institute of Orthopaedics "Carlos E. Ottolenghi", Italian Hospital of Buenos Aires, Potosí 4247, C1199
相关性: 该论文专注于诊断髋关节假体周围感染的生物标志物，与股骨柄应力遮挡、微动或有限元分析无关。
边界: This paper does not quantify femoral stress shielding, micromotion, or any biomechanical modeling; it is purely a clinical diagnostic study.
动机· 比较术中滑液C反应蛋白与冰冻切片诊断髋关节假体周围感染的价值
方法· 前瞻性纳入76例髋关节翻修患者，比较SCRP与冰冻切片的诊断效能

Motivation· To compare the diagnostic accuracy of intraoperative synovial C-reactive protein with frozen section for detecting periprosthetic joint infection in revision total hip arthroplasty.
