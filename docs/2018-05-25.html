A Surrogate Modelling Approach Based on Nonlinear Dimension Reduction for Uncertainty Quantification in Groundwater Flow Models

C. H. Gadd, W. Xing, Mohaddeseh Mousavi Nezhad, A.A. Shah Transport in Porous Media 2018-05-25 10.1007/s11242-018-1065-7
单位: School of Engineering, University of Warwick, Coventry, CV47AL UK
通讯: C. H. Gadd @ School of Engineering, University of Warwick, Coventry, CV47AL UK
相关性: 该论文使用非线性降维和代理模型进行地下水流动模型的不确定性量化，与生物力学植入物设计中的有限元代理建模方向不直接相关。
边界: This paper focuses on groundwater flow models, not on implant biomechanics, stress shielding, or any biomedical FEA application; the method is not validated on solid mechanics or implant-related problems.
动机· 高效量化地下水流动模型中的不确定性。
方法· 结合KL展开、局部切空间排列和GP贝叶斯推断构建代理模型。
结果· 在2D达西流和3D Richards方程上验证了代理模型的准确性。
验证· 通过两个数值模拟案例验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To efficiently quantify uncertainty in groundwater flow models with stochastic input fields.
Method· A surrogate model is built using Karhunen-Loève expansion, manifold learning (local tangent space alignment), and Gaussian process Bayesian inference with Hamiltonian Monte Carlo.
Result· The surrogate accurately predicts pressure head fields and enables forward uncertainty quantification in 2D and 3D test cases.
Validation· Validation is performed on two numerical simulation cases: a 2D Darcy flow with contaminant transport and a 3D Richards equation model.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the efficacy and safety of combining tranexamic acid with diluted epinephrine for blood loss reduction in primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A meta-analysis of five randomized controlled trials comparing combined therapy to tranexamic acid alone was conducted using RevMan 5.3.
Result· Combined therapy significantly reduced total blood loss, hidden blood loss, and transfusion requirements without increasing deep vein thrombosis risk.
Validation· Statistical analysis of pooled data from five RCTs with 496 patients.

Participation in Regular Physical Activity After Total Knee or Hip Arthroplasty for Osteoarthritis: Prevalence, Associated Factors, and Type

Justine Naylor, Natasha C Pocovi, Joseph Descallar, Kathryn Mills Arthritis Care & Research 2018-05-25 10.1002/acr.23604
单位: Ingham Institute Applied Medical Research Sydney New South Wales Australia
通讯: Justine Naylor @ Ingham Institute Applied Medical Research Sydney New South Wales Australia
相关性: 该论文研究全髋关节置换术后患者体力活动参与率，未涉及任何生物力学建模或植入物力学分析。
边界: This paper is a clinical cohort study on physical activity participation after hip arthroplasty, with no biomechanical modeling, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no FEA, and no implant design analysis.
动机· 调查全髋/膝关节置换术后患者规律体力活动参与率及影响因素。
方法· 多中心前瞻性队列研究，3年随访，通过访谈和问卷收集数据。
结果· 术后参与率从45.2%升至63.5%，但约三分之一患者未每周活动。
验证· 多变量逻辑回归分析关联因素。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To describe rates of regular physical activity participation before and 3 years after hip or knee arthroplasty and identify associated factors.
Method· A multicenter prospective cohort study with 3-year follow-up, using interviews and questionnaires to assess physical activity participation.
Result· Participation increased from 45.2% to 63.5% post-surgery, but about one-third of recipients did not engage in weekly physical activity.
Validation· Multivariable logistic regression modeling to determine factors associated with 3-year participation.
