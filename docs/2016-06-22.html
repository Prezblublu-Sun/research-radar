POD/DEIM Reduced-Order Modeling of Time-Fractional Partial Differential Equations with Applications in Parameter Identification

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark
Hongfei Fu, Hong Wang, Zhu Wang arXiv preprint 2016-06-22 10.1007/s10915-017-0433-8
相关性: 该论文提出的POD/DEIM降阶模型可用于加速有限元模拟，但未涉及生物力学或植入物设计的具体应用。
边界: No implant mechanics, biomechanics context, or contact analysis; the method is demonstrated only on fractional PDEs, not on FEA-based structural or biomedical problems.
动机· 针对时间分数阶偏微分方程的高效模拟和参数识别问题。
方法· 采用POD/DEIM降阶模型作为替代，结合Levenberg-Marquardt方法求解逆问题。
结果· 降阶模型在保持精度同时大幅降低计算成本，参数识别准确。
验证· 通过数值算例验证降阶模型和逆问题算法。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To efficiently simulate time-fractional PDEs and solve the associated parameter identification inverse problems that require repeated solver evaluations.
Method· A reduced-order model based on proper orthogonal decomposition and discrete empirical interpolation method is constructed as a surrogate for the full-order model, and coupled with Levenberg-Marquardt regularization for parameter estimation.
Result· The ROM matches the accuracy of the full-order model in long-term simulations while drastically reducing computational cost, and accurately estimates the fractional derivative order even under noisy observations.
Validation· Numerical examples on linear and nonlinear time-fractional PDEs demonstrate the ROM's speedup and the inverse algorithm's robustness.
