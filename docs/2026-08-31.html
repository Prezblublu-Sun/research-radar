相关性: 该论文展示了数字孪生与自主导航框架，虽面向血管微机器人，但其将2D成像映射到3D拓扑并实现自主决策的方法可迁移至生物打印中的实时监控与闭环控制。
边界: This paper does not address bioprinting processes, bioink properties, or any printing workflow stage; it focuses on endovascular microrobot navigation.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· The lack of depth information in single-plane fluoroscopy impedes the clinical translation of autonomous navigation for untethered microrobots.
Method· VISTA constructs a 3D digital twin of the vascular anatomy, discretizes the centerline into milestones, and maps the 2D robot position to the nearest projected milestone to generate magnetic actuation commands.
Result· In phantom and in vivo rat experiments, VISTA reduced navigation time by up to 62%, corrective commands by up to 98%, and radiation exposure by up to 57% compared to conventional human-in-the-loop control.
Validation· Validation was performed using anatomically distinct vascular phantoms under flow and in the inferior vena cava of a live rat.
