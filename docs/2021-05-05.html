Registration-based model reduction of parameterized two-dimensional conservation laws

Andrea Ferrero, Tommaso Taddei, Lei Zhang arXiv preprint 2021-05-05 10.1016/j.jcp.2022.111068
相关性: 该论文提出的基于配准的模型降阶方法可潜在用于生物力学有限元代理建模，但未涉及植入物设计或不确定性量化。
边界: This paper does not address constraint handling, uncertainty quantification, or biomechanical/implant design applications, focusing instead on inviscid supersonic flow problems.
动机· 加速含移动间断的参数化守恒律仿真
方法· 基于配准的非线性映射与超缩减最小二乘Petrov-Galerkin降阶模型

English summary & terms
Motivation· To accelerate the simulation of parameterized conservation laws with moving discontinuities while maintaining reliability.
Method· A registration-based mapping combined with a hyper-reduced least-squares Petrov-Galerkin reduced-order model and multi-fidelity offline costs.
Result· The method effectively captures parameter-dependent discontinuities for 2D inviscid supersonic flow past a bump.
Validation· Numerical benchmark on a parameterized supersonic flow problem demonstrating accuracy and efficiency.

单位: Department of Materials Science and Engineering, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, Hsinchu, 30013, Taiwan
通讯: Jayant Jain @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology, New Delhi, 110016, India. Jayant.Jain@m  |  Soo Yeol Lee @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Chungnam National University, Daejeon, 34134, Republic of Korea. sylee2

Result· BDFe-Mo alloy achieved an ultimate tensile strength of 1200 MPa and 13.5% elongation, attributed to ferrite grain refinement and precipitate strengthening.
Validation· Mechanical testing (tensile tests) was performed to validate the strength-ductility combination.

Motivation· To enhance surface quality and fabrication efficiency in selective laser melting.
Method· Implemented an oscillating laser beam scan strategy and compared it to conventional linear scanning.
Result· Oscillation reduced surface profile variation by 50%, increased area rate by 50%, and decreased laser energy input by 28%.
Validation· Comparative experimental analysis of manufactured specimens' surface roughness and density.
