通讯: Ken Gall @ Department of Mechanical Engineering and Materials Science Duke University Durham North Carolina USA  |  Jennifer L. West @ Department of Biomedical Engineering Duke University Durham North Carolina USA

边界: This paper uses extrusion-based 3D printing of polycarbonate urethane, not LPBF of metal; no stress shielding, fatigue testing, or osseointegration evaluation.

相关性: 该论文研究量子集合与L-代数，与AM生物医学材料方向完全无关。

Motivation· To characterize symmetric quantum sets and their representation via orthomodular spaces and Hilbert spaces.

Result· Symmetric quantum sets decompose into classical irreducible components; large components correspond to Hilbert spaces over R, C, or H.
Validation· Theoretical proofs only, no experimental validation.
