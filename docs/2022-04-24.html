Beam Shaping in Laser Powder Bed Fusion: Péclet Number and Dynamic Simulation

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only simulation_case
Sergey N. Grigoriev, A.V. Gusarov, Alexander Metel, Tatiana Tarasova, М. A. Volosova et al. Metals 2022-04-24 10.3390/met12050722
单位: Department of High-Efficiency Processing Technologies, Moscow State University of Technology STANKIN, 127055 Moscow, Russia
通讯: Anna A. Okunkova @ Department of High-Efficiency Processing Technologies, Moscow State University of Technology STANKIN, 127055 Moscow, Rus
相关性: 该论文研究激光粉末床熔融中的光束整形与Péclet数模拟，未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或生物医学植入物力学性能。
边界: No porous structure design, no stress shielding analysis, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), no surface roughness or osseointegration evaluation.
动机· 比较不同激光光束形状对LPBF过程热效应的影响
方法· 通过Péclet数计算和动态模拟分析三种光束分布的温度场
结果· 高斯光束在宽温度范围内最有效，平顶光束仅在窄温度范围有效
验证· 仅基于数值模拟，无实验验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the thermal effects of different laser beam profiles (Gaussian, flat-top, donut) in laser powder bed fusion.
Method· Dynamic simulation and Péclet number analysis were used to evaluate temperature fields and material losses for each beam profile.
Result· The Gaussian mode (TEM00) is most effective over a wide temperature range, while the flat-top profile is only effective in a narrow range.
Validation· Validation is limited to numerical simulation; no experimental measurements were performed.

单位: Department of Mining Engineering and Management, South Dakota School of Mines and Technology, Rapid City, SD 57701, USA
通讯: Richard Amoako @ Department of Mining Engineering and Management, South Dakota School of Mines and Technology, Rapid City, SD 57701, USA
相关性: 该论文使用机器学习的回归方法预测岩石破碎尺寸，与生物力学有限元代理建模方向无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address finite element analysis, surrogate modeling for expensive simulations, uncertainty quantification, or biomedical implant design.
动机· 传统岩石破碎模型精度不足，需更准确的预测方法。
方法· 使用ANN和SVR机器学习模型预测平均岩石碎片尺寸，并用贝叶斯优化调整ANN结构。
结果· 两种ML模型均优于传统Kuznetsov模型，预测精度更高。
验证· 与经典经验模型对比，基于实际爆破数据验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Empirical rock fragmentation models suffer from inaccuracy due to their inability to capture nonlinear relationships, motivating the use of machine learning for improved prediction.
Method· Multilayered artificial neural network and support vector regression models were developed using geometric, explosive, and rock parameters to predict mean fragment size, with Bayesian optimization for ANN architecture tuning.
Result· Both ML models outperformed the conventional Kuznetsov model in predicting mean fragment size.
Validation· Model performance was validated by comparing predictions against actual blast data and the Kuznetsov model.

单位: Amity University
通讯: Tanuj Joshi @ Amity University
相关性: 该论文分析了多材料髋关节假体的力学行为，但未涉及应力遮挡、微动或骨-植入物界面的定量评估。
边界: This paper does not quantify stress shielding in Gruen zones, does not report micromotion with contact/friction modeling, and does not compare intact vs implanted femur.

Method· A 3D standard Charnley femoral stem model was developed and analyzed under 2.3 kN axial load for twelve material combinations using finite element analysis.
Result· CoCrMo with zirconia and UHMWPE showed minimal deformation but highest stress, while Ti-6Al-7Nb was preferred due to better modulus matching.
Validation· Mechanical parameters were validated against published literature.
