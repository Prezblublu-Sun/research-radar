AL-ELM: One uncertainty-based active learning algorithm using extreme learning machine

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Hualong Yu, Changyin Sun, Wankou Yang, Xibei Yang, Xin Zuo Neurocomputing 2015-04-25 10.1016/j.neucom.2015.04.019
单位: Key Lab of Measurement and Control of Complex Systems of Engineering, Ministry of Education, Southeast University, Nanjing Jiangsu 210096, China
通讯: Changyin Sun @ Key Lab of Measurement and Control of Complex Systems of Engineering, Ministry of Education, Southeast University, Nanji
相关性: 该论文提出了一种基于不确定性的主动学习算法，可提高代理模型的采样效率，对生物力学设计中的昂贵仿真优化具有参考价值。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, constraint handling, or biomechanical implant design; it focuses on general regression active learning with ELM.
动机· 提高主动学习采样效率，减少标注成本。
方法· 利用极限学习机的预测方差作为不确定性度量，迭代选择高不确定性样本。
结果· 在多个基准数据集上，AL-ELM比随机采样和其他主动学习方法更高效。
验证· 在UCI等标准回归数据集上进行数值实验对比。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To improve sampling efficiency in active learning by leveraging model uncertainty, reducing the number of labeled samples needed.
Method· Proposes AL-ELM, which uses the prediction variance of extreme learning machines as an uncertainty measure to select informative samples iteratively.
Result· AL-ELM achieves higher accuracy with fewer training samples compared to random sampling and other active learning methods on multiple benchmark datasets.
Validation· Validated on standard regression datasets from UCI repository through numerical experiments.

动机· 量化静电驱动MEMS开关动态响应中的不确定性。
方法· 采用多项式混沌展开和蒙特卡洛模拟进行不确定性传播分析。

Keynote: Non-random vibration analysis based on non-stochastic process model

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only numerical_benchmark UQ
Chao Jiang, Bingyu Ni The 6th International Conference on Computational Methods (ICCM2015) 2015-04-25 link
单位: Hunan University
通讯: Chao Jiang @ Hunan University
相关性: 该论文提出了一种非随机过程模型用于时变不确定性量化，但未涉及有限元代理建模或生物力学设计。
边界: This paper focuses on non-stochastic vibration analysis for general structural dynamics, with no surrogate modeling, no FEA acceleration, no implant/biomechanics context, and no uncertainty quantification beyond interval bounds.
动机· 解决随机振动建模所需数据量大、难以获取的问题
方法· 提出非随机过程模型，仅需上下界信息，通过自相关函数转换独立过程
结果· 给出单自由度和多自由度系统在激励下的动态响应上下界
验证· 通过数值算例验证，包括阻尼/无阻尼、平稳/非平稳激励
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To address the challenge of requiring large amounts of data for stochastic process modeling in time-varying uncertainty quantification.
Method· A non-stochastic process model is proposed that only requires upper and lower bounds, with a self-correlation function to transform dependent interval variables into independent ones.
Result· Upper and lower bounds of dynamic responses are obtained for both SDOF and MDOF systems under various excitations.
Validation· Numerical examples including damped/undamped systems and stationary/non-stationary excitations are used for validation.
