边界: This paper does not embed AI/ML into any bioprinting workflow stage; it uses statistical Design of Experiments, not AI-driven methods.
动机· 开发用于生物打印的稳健生物墨水。
方法· 采用实验设计方法优化藻酸盐、纳米纤维素和纤维蛋白原的配方。
结果· 藻酸盐浓度增加提高机械完整性，其他成分无显著影响。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a robust bioink for scalable bioprinting by systematically optimizing formulation parameters.
Method· A Design of Experiments approach was used to vary alginate, nanocellulose, and fibrinogen concentrations, and their effects on extrudability, strength, and stiffness were measured via dynamic mechanical analysis.
Result· Alginate concentration significantly increased mechanical integrity, while fibrinogen and nanofibril concentrations had no statistically significant effect; the optimized ink supported fibroblast viability and printability.
Validation· The bioink was validated through dynamic mechanical analysis and fibroblast cell viability assays.

Applying Layer-by-Layer Photo-Crosslinking in Green Bioprinting: Shape Fidelity and Cell Viability of Printed Hydrogel Constructs Containing Algae Cells

Ketan Thakare, Laura Jerpseth, Zhijian Pei, Hongmin Qin Journal of Manufacturing Science and Engineering 2022-03-15 10.1115/1.4054086
单位: Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering, Texas A&M University, College Station, TX 77843
通讯: Ketan Thakare @ Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering, Texas A&M University, College Station, TX 77843
相关性: 该论文不涉及人工智能，与AI生物打印方向无关
边界: This paper does not incorporate AI/ML into any bioprinting workflow stage; it focuses solely on crosslinking methods and cell viability.
动机· 提高含藻细胞水凝胶绿色生物打印的结构保真度
方法· 逐层光交联与一次后打印光交联对比实验
结果· 逐层光交联获得最高结构保真度，细胞活力无显著差异
验证· 与离子交联藻酸盐水凝胶比较形状保真度及细胞活力测定
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the shape fidelity of hydrogel constructs containing algae cells in green bioprinting, which was previously poor or uninvestigated.
Method· A layer-by-layer photo-crosslinking approach was applied during extrusion-based printing of GelMA bioink with Chlamydomonas reinhardtii algae, and compared to once-post-printing photo-crosslinking and ionic-crosslinking with alginate-methylcellulose.
Result· Layer-by-layer photo-crosslinked GelMA constructs achieved the highest shape fidelity among all groups, while maintaining approximately 75% algae cell viability four days post-printing with no significant difference from post-printing crosslinking.
Validation· Validation was performed through shape fidelity comparisons against ionic-crosslinked constructs and cell viability assays using live/dead staining.

通讯: Hiroaki Matsumoto @ Department of Advanced Materials Science, Faculty of Engineering and Design, Kagawa University
