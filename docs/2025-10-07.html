相关性: 与FEA代理建模方向无关，不涉及结构力学模拟或设计优化。

动机· 利用物理定律克服科学领域深度学习数据不足的问题。
方法· 提出RamPINN，双解码器PINN结构，通过希尔伯特变换损失分离共振和非共振信号。

English summary & terms

Result· Trained solely on synthetic data, the model achieves strong zero-shot generalization to real experimental CARS spectra, outperforming existing baselines.
Validation· Validation is performed via numerical experiments on synthetic data and zero-shot testing on real-world experimental data.
