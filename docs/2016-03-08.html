The effect of stress ratio on the fatigue behavior of additively manufactured porous biomaterials

Joep de Krijger Research Repository (Delft University of Technology) 2016-03-08 link
相关性: 该论文直接研究多孔生物材料的疲劳行为与应力比的关系，与用户关注的多孔结构应力屏蔽和疲劳性能高度相关。
边界: This paper does not report surface roughness, as-built morphology, or osseointegration data.
动机· 研究不同应力比下多孔生物材料的疲劳行为，以匹配骨力学性能并避免应力遮挡。
方法· 通过SLM制造金刚石晶胞多孔钛合金试样，在不同应力比下进行疲劳实验并采用DIC观测变形。
结果· 应力比增大（平均应力增加）会显著降低疲劳寿命。
验证· 疲劳实验结合数字图像相关（DIC）方法验证变形行为。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates the fatigue behavior of additively manufactured porous biomaterials under various stress ratios to better understand failure under mean stress conditions for bone implant applications.
Method· Cylindrical diamond unit cell structures were fabricated from Ti-6Al-4V ELI by selective laser melting and tested at stress ratios R=0.1 to 0.8, with digital image correlation (DIC) used to visualize deformation.
Result· Increasing stress ratio (i.e., higher mean stress) leads to reduced fatigue life, demonstrating the sensitivity of porous biomaterials to mean stress.
Validation· Experimental fatigue testing with DIC provided in situ deformation data, validating the mechanical response under different loading conditions.

The effect of stress ratio on the fatigue behavior of additively manufactured porous biomaterials

Joep de Krijger Research Repository (Delft University of Technology) 2016-03-08 link
相关性: 该论文直接研究了增材制造多孔生物材料的疲劳行为，包括应力比对疲劳寿命的影响，与用户关注的多孔结构应力屏蔽、疲劳性能及力学测试高度相关。
边界: This paper does not explicitly link porous structure to stress shielding or stiffness matching, nor does it report surface roughness, as-built morphology, or osseointegration.
动机· 研究不同应力比对增材制造多孔生物材料疲劳行为的影响
方法· 对Ti-6Al-4V ELI粉末制成的金刚石单元多孔结构进行不同应力比（R=0.1-0.8）的疲劳测试，并开发DIC方法观察变形
结果· 不同应力比显著影响疲劳寿命，高应力比下疲劳寿命降低
验证· 通过实验疲劳测试和DIC变形观测验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of stress ratio on the fatigue behavior of additively manufactured porous biomaterials for bone implants.
Method· Cylindrical porous structures with diamond unit cells were fabricated from Ti-6Al-4V ELI powder via selective laser melting and tested under fatigue at stress ratios R=0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7, and 0.8; an experimental DIC method was developed to visualize deformation.
Result· Increased mean stress (higher stress ratio) significantly reduced fatigue life, with distinct S-N curves for each stress ratio.
Validation· Experimental fatigue testing combined with digital image correlation (DIC) for deformation visualization.
