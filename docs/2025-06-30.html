相关性: 该论文主要关注神经网络分类校准，与有限元代理建模或生物力学设计无直接关联。
边界: No surrogate modelling, no FEA, no biomechanics or implant design context; focuses on calibration of classifiers rather than regression or simulation-based UQ.
动机· 解决神经网络过度自信的校准问题。
方法· 基于logit间隙的自适应温度缩放方法SMART。

English summary & terms
Motivation· Modern neural networks are systematically overconfident, necessitating robust post-hoc calibration methods. (paraphrased)
Method· Proposes SMART, a recalibration method that scales logits adaptively using the margin between top two logits (logit gap) and a soft-binned Expected Calibration Error objective.
Result· Achieves state-of-the-art calibration with fewer parameters and robust performance even with limited calibration data.
Validation· Evaluated extensively on diverse classification datasets and architectures, showing consistent improvement over existing methods.

单位: Maxillofacial Surgery and Dental Unit, Fondazione IRCCS Cà Granda Policlinico, 20112 Milan, Italy.; Department of Biomedical, Surgical and Dental Sciences, University of Milan, Via della Commenda 10,
通讯: Pier Paolo Poli @ Maxillofacial Surgery and Dental Unit, Fondazione IRCCS Cà Granda Policlinico, 20112 Milan, Italy.; Department of Biomed [推断]
相关性: 该文仅关注牙科植入体扭矩，与髋关节植入物和股骨柄的生物力学分析无关。
边界: The paper focuses on dental implant insertion torque and does not address femoral stem stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics for hip implants.
动机· 探究高植入扭矩对牙种植体骨整合和长期稳定性的影响。
方法· 系统综述随机对照试验和队列研究，遵循PRISMA指南，纳入11项研究。
结果· 高扭矩保障初期稳定性但导致边缘骨丢失，与骨密度相关。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate whether high insertion torque (>50 Ncm) affects dental implant osseointegration, peri-implant health, and long-term stability.
Method· A scoping review was conducted following PRISMA-ScR guidelines, including randomized clinical trials, observational, and cohort studies (n=11).
Result· High insertion torque ensures adequate primary stability but is associated with greater marginal bone loss depending on bone density, potentially due to compressive forces and ischemia.
Validation· The study is a review of clinical literature; no direct experimental or biomechanical validation is provided.

Validation· Retrospective clinical data from 58 cases supported the findings.
