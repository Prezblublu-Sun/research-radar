CLINN: Conservation Law Informed Neural Network for Approximating Discontinuous Solutions

Weiheng Zeng, Kun Wang, Ruoxi Lu, Tiegang Liu arXiv preprint 2025-09-02 link
相关性: 该论文提出的守恒定律神经网络框架对解决不连续PDE问题具有参考价值，可迁移至植入物有限元代理模型中的激波捕捉任务。
边界: No direct application to biomechanics or implant design; validation only on fluid dynamics benchmarks, no uncertainty quantification.
动机· 解决PINN在守恒律中不守恒和间断分辨率差的问题。
方法· 在损失函数中引入有界性约束、隐式解形式和Rankine-Hugoniot条件，并加入残差自适应细化策略。
结果· 在Burgers方程、交通流和Buckley-Leverett问题上均方误差最高降低99.2%。
验证· 通过三个经典数值算例与标准PINN对比验证精度和间断捕捉能力。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Physics-informed neural networks fail to enforce conservation laws and resolve discontinuities in hyperbolic PDEs such as conservation laws.
Method· CLINN incorporates boundedness constraints, implicit solution forms, and the Rankine-Hugoniot condition into the loss, together with residual-based adaptive refinement near shocks.
Result· CLINN achieves up to 99.2% reduction in mean squared error on inviscid Burgers, LWR traffic flow, and Buckley-Leverett problems compared to vanilla PINN.
Validation· Numerical experiments on three benchmark scalar conservation law problems with exact solutions demonstrate improved accuracy and reduced oscillations.

Effect of Heat Treatment on Microstructures and Mechanical Properties of TC4 Alloys Prepared by Selective Laser Melting

Jian Zhang, Yuhuan Shi, Shen Su, Shengdong Zhang, Honghui Ding et al. Materials 2025-09-02 10.3390/ma18174126
单位: College of Mechanical & Electrical Engineering, Jining University, Jining 273155, China
通讯: Jian Zhang @ College of Mechanical & Electrical Engineering, Jining University, Jining 273155, China
相关性: 该论文研究SLM制备TC4合金热处理后的显微组织与力学性能，但未涉及医用植入物所需的孔隙结构、应力屏蔽或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures, stress shielding, osseointegration, or fatigue testing, which are critical for biomedical implant applications.
动机· 改善SLM制备TC4合金因针状马氏体导致的低塑性。
方法· 对SLM制备的TC4合金进行600°C和800°C退火处理，分析相变与组织演化。
结果· 800°C退火后α'相完全转变，延伸率从5.22%提升至11.43%。
验证· 通过拉伸测试、SEM断口分析和EBSD表征验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To address the reduced ductility of selective laser melted TC4 alloys caused by brittle needle-like α' martensite.
Method· Stress relief annealing at 600°C and high-temperature annealing at 800°C were applied to SLM-fabricated TC4 alloys, with phase composition and microstructure evolution characterized.
Result· Annealing at 800°C completely transformed α' into lath α and rod β phases, increasing elongation from 5.22% to 11.43% while slightly reducing tensile strength.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated via tensile testing, fracture surface SEM, and EBSD grain boundary analysis.
