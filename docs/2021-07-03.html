英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the relationship between teacher communication skills and student learning activeness.
Method· A quantitative descriptive correlation study using questionnaires with a Likert scale.
Result· Teacher communication skills contribute 44.5% to student learning activeness.
Validation· Validity was assessed by two lecturers, and normality tests were performed.
