边界: No biomedical or implant context, no bone-implant interface mechanics, and no uncertainty quantification; the surrogate is trained and validated only on concrete penetration simulations.

英文摘要与术语

Validation· Validation relied on configuration-similarity comparison with published penetration experiments, field-level self-consistency, and cross-velocity leave-one-out extrapolation.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· The study examines whether accurate contact prediction from a world model can be translated into effective force-constrained manipulation decisions.
Method· A compact randomly initialized visuotactile world model is combined with trajectory-level uncertainty calibration and behavior-initialized actor-critic learning in imagination.
Result· Touch reduced endpoint-force prediction error from 1.058 to 0.228 N, yet success within an 8 N per-finger budget reached only 33.3% versus 70.0% for force feedback.
Validation· Evaluation used 160 MuJoCo Lift episodes, 680 control executions across 40 test conditions, and public GelSight force-regression records.
