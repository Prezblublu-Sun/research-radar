Knut Harboe, Christian Lycke Ellingsen, Einar Sudmann, Nils Roar Gjerdet, Kjetil Søreide et al. BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders 2015-04-27 10.1186/s12891-015-0560-z
单位: Department of Clinical Medicine, University of Bergen, Bergen, Norway. knut@harboe.org
通讯: Knut Harboe @ Department of Clinical Medicine, University of Bergen, Bergen, Norway. knut@harboe.org
相关性: 该论文使用山羊模型研究股骨柄的骨附着与拔出力关系，但未涉及应力遮挡、微动或有限元分析。
边界: No stress shielding quantification, no micromotion analysis, no FEA, no contact mechanics, and uses an animal model not directly transferable to human implant design.
动机· 研究骨附着对股骨柄拔出力预测能力。
方法· 山羊模型植入带沟槽股骨柄，6个月后组织学评估骨附着并关联拔出力。
结果· 沟槽钻孔显著影响拔出力，骨附着评分与拔出力相关性弱。
验证· 山羊体内实验结合组织学与力学测试。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate whether bone apposition can predict the retention force of a femoral stem in a weight-bearing goat model.
Method· Femoral stems with longitudinal grooves were implanted in 22 goats for 6 months; after removal, histological bone apposition was scored semi-quantitatively and correlated with pullout force.
Result· Groove drilling significantly influenced retention force, while bone apposition score showed only fair correlation with pullout force.
Validation· In vivo animal experiment with histological evaluation and mechanical pullout testing.

通讯: Mateusz Maciejewski @ Novartis Institutes for Biomedical Research, 250 Massachusetts Avenue, Cambridge, Massachusetts 02139, United States
相关性: 该论文聚焦于药物发现中的主动学习筛选策略，与有限元代理建模或生物力学设计无关。
边界: This paper addresses active learning for chemical screening in drug discovery, with no FEA, surrogate modeling, uncertainty quantification, or biomechanics context.
动机· 提高迭代筛选中的化合物多样性和命中率
方法· 提出基于朴素贝叶斯的弱强化策略，选择低分但正值的化合物

Result· The weak reinforcement strategy consistently achieved higher compound and scaffold hit rates across 81 Novartis assays compared to standard expansion or active learning.
Validation· Retrospective validation on 81 historical high-throughput screening datasets from Novartis.
