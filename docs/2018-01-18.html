相关性: 该论文提出Student's-T过程替代高斯过程，可提高代理模型对异常值的鲁棒性和不确定性量化能力，对生物力学设计中的昂贵仿真优化具有潜在迁移价值。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling for implant/biomechanics design, lacks biomedical context, and does not handle constraints or multi-objective optimization.
动机· 高斯过程对异常值敏感且后验方差仅依赖样本位置，需改进鲁棒性。
方法· 提出Student's-T过程，基于Student's-T分布替代高斯分布，推导期望改进等优化量。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Gaussian processes are sensitive to outliers and their posterior variance depends only on sample locations, motivating a more robust surrogate model.
Method· This paper proposes Student's-T processes, which generalize Gaussian processes using the Student's-T distribution, and derives key quantities for Bayesian optimization such as expected improvement.
Result· Student's-T processes outperform Gaussian processes on canonical test problems and an aerostructural design optimization problem.
Validation· Validation is performed via numerical benchmark functions and an aerostructural design optimization case.
