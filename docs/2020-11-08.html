An integrated sensitivity‐uncertainty quantification framework for stochastic phase‐field modeling of material damage

单位: Department of Computational Mathematics, Science, and Engineering Michigan State University East Lansing Michigan USA
通讯: Mohsen Zayernouri @ Department of Mechanical Engineering Michigan State University East Lansing Michigan USA
相关性: 该论文将不确定性量化与灵敏度分析集成到随机相场损伤模型中，其方法可迁移至生物力学植入物设计的代理建模与不确定性评估。
边界: This paper does not address surrogate modeling for expensive finite element simulations, nor does it apply to implant or biomechanics design; it focuses on stochastic phase-field modeling of material damage with UQ.
动机· 量化随机相场损伤模型中的不确定性与灵敏度，揭示关键参数来源。
方法· 集成蒙特卡洛与概率配置法进行前向不确定性传播及局部-全局灵敏度分析。
结果· 自由能势相关参数对模型输出不确定性贡献最大，需改进模型形式。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify and mitigate sources of unpredictability in stochastic phase-field models of material damage by integrating uncertainty quantification and sensitivity analysis.
Method· A computational framework combining Monte Carlo and probabilistic collocation methods for forward uncertainty propagation and local-to-global sensitivity analysis is applied to a stochastic phase-field damage model.
Result· Model parameters associated with free-energy potentials are identified as the dominant contributors to output uncertainty, motivating improved model forms for damage diffusion.
Validation· The framework is demonstrated on a simulation case of an isotropic linear elastic material with viscous dissipation under small deformations.
