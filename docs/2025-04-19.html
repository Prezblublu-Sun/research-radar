相关性: 该论文提出的复合U-Net代理模型可同时预测应力与损伤场，其自回归架构和空间特征捕捉能力对生物力学植入物设计中的代理建模具有高度可迁移性。
边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, contact mechanics, or patient-specific geometry; it focuses on composite material microstructures under unidirectional strain.

Validation· Validation is performed by comparing predictions against IGFEM numerical benchmarks.
