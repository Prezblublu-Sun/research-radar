Machine learning for graph-based representations of three-dimensional discrete fracture networks

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Background only numerical_benchmark
Manuel Valera, Zhengyang Guo, Priscilla Kelly, Sean Matz, Vito Adrian Cantu et al. arXiv preprint 2017-05-27 10.1007/s10596-018-9720-1
相关性: 该论文使用机器学习加速离散裂缝网络模拟，但与生物力学有限元代理建模无直接关联。
边界: No FEA surrogate, no implant/biomechanics context, no uncertainty quantification, no constraint handling.
动机· 加速离散裂缝网络模拟，避免耗时的粒子追踪。
方法· 结合图论和机器学习（随机森林、SVM）识别主要流动子网络。
结果· 将网络缩减至20%大小，仍能生成一致的穿透曲线。
验证· 与dfnWorks粒子追踪结果对比验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To accelerate discrete fracture network simulations by replacing computationally expensive particle tracking with machine learning predictions.
Method· Graph-based features (node centrality) are used with random forest and support vector machines to classify fractures belonging to the flowing backbone.
Result· The reduced network (about 20% of original size) produces breakthrough curves consistent with full DFN simulations.
Validation· Predictions are validated against particle-tracking backbone paths from the dfnWorks suite.

单位: Department of Orthopaedics, Leiden University Medical Centre, Leiden - The Netherlands
通讯: Christiaan Smeekes @ Department of Orthopaedics, Leiden University Medical Centre, Leiden - The Netherlands
相关性: 该论文报告了CLS非骨水泥髋关节假体的长期临床结果，但未涉及应力遮挡或微动的生物力学量化。
边界: No biomechanical modeling (FEA), no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no contact mechanics, no intact vs implanted comparison.
动机· 评估非骨水泥CLS髋关节系统的长期临床和影像学结果。
方法· 对120例年轻患者进行平均14.6年随访，记录Merle d'Aubigné-Postel评分、聚乙烯磨损和影像学状态。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the long-term clinical and radiographic outcomes of the cementless CLS total hip arthroplasty system in a young patient cohort.
Method· A series of 120 consecutive CLS arthroplasties in patients with mean age 55.9 years were followed for a mean of 14.6 years, recording Merle d'Aubigné-Postel scores, polyethylene wear, and radiographic findings.
Result· The 24-year survival rate was 72.8% with revision for any reason as endpoint, and aseptic cup loosening was the predominant cause of revision.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with Kaplan-Meier survival analysis and nested case-control study.
