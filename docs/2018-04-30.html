边界: This paper focuses on molecular dynamics for thermal transport, not on finite element analysis, surrogate modeling for biomechanics, or implant design.
动机· 量化非平衡分子动力学模拟中热导率预测的不确定性。

Result· Only two out of seven Stillinger-Weber potential parameters significantly contribute to the uncertainty in bulk thermal conductivity estimates for silicon.
Validation· Statistical response surfaces are compared with experimental measurements of bulk thermal conductivity at 300 K.

Validation· Numerical benchmarks using maximum mean discrepancy and comparison with finite Bayesian deep networks.
