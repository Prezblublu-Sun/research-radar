Improved Mechanical Properties and Energy Absorption of BCC Lattice Structures with Triply Periodic Minimal Surfaces Fabricated by SLM

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Transferable Skim only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Miao Zhao, Fei Liu, Guang Fu, Zhengwen Zhang, Tao Zhang et al. Materials 2018-11-29 10.3390/ma11122411
单位: State Key Laboratory of Mechanical Transmission, Chongqing University, Chongqing 400044, China
通讯: Zhengwen Zhang @ College of Engineering, Mathematics and Physical Sciences, University of Exeter, North Park Road, Exeter EX4 4QF, UK
相关性: 该论文通过SLM制造Ti-6Al-4V的BCC点阵结构并测试其压缩性能，但未涉及应力遮挡或骨整合等生物力学评估。
边界: This paper does not link porous structures to stress shielding or stiffness matching, does not include fatigue testing, and does not report surface roughness or osseointegration relevant to biomedical implants.
动机· 探索TPMS方法设计BCC点阵结构以改善力学性能与能量吸收。
方法· 通过SLM制造TPMS与CAD两种BCC点阵并比较其压缩行为。
结果· TPMS样品在弹性模量、屈服强度、压缩强度上均有显著提升。
验证· 压缩实验验证力学性能，SEM观察失效机制，Gibson-Ashby模型预测。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the use of triply periodic minimal surfaces (TPMS) for designing BCC lattice structures with improved mechanical properties and energy absorption.
Method· BCC lattice structures with varying volume fractions were fabricated by selective laser melting (SLM) using Ti-6Al-4V, comparing TPMS-based and CAD-based designs under compression.
Result· TPMS-based lattices exhibited up to 42.2% increase in elastic modulus, 29.5% in yield strength, and 36.6% in compression strength, with superior energy absorption at 30% volume fraction.
Validation· Theoretical Gibson-Ashby model was established and validated through experimental compression tests and SEM fractography.
