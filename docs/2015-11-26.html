Motivation· To evaluate whether an acetabular fracture prognostic nomogram can reliably predict the need for total hip arthroplasty within 2 years after ORIF.
Method· A retrospective case series of 103 posterior wall fracture patients was analyzed, comparing nomogram-predicted risk with actual clinical outcomes.
Result· The nomogram showed low positive predictive values (0.25 for THA, 0.44 for unsatisfactory outcome), indicating insufficient prognostic accuracy.
Validation· Clinical follow-up data from a single trauma center were used for validation.
