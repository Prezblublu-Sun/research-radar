单位: Clinique Porte Océane, Les Sables-d'Olonne, France
通讯: Mathieu Severyns @ Clinique Porte Océane, Les Sables-d'Olonne, France
相关性: 与用户研究方向无关，仅涉及临床手术入路对股骨柄填充率的影响，不包含任何生物力学建模或应力分析。
边界: This paper does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics; it is a purely clinical radiographic study without FEA or biomechanical modeling.
动机· 评估手术入路是否影响非骨水泥股骨柄的髓腔填充率。
方法· 回顾性分析183例患者的X线片，按四种手术入路分组，测量填充率。
结果· 手术入路对填充率无显著影响，股骨形态是主要决定因素。
验证· 临床影像学回顾研究，统计分析组间差异。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine whether the choice of surgical approach influences the femoral canal fill ratio of cementless stems.
Method· A retrospective radiographic analysis of 183 primary THA patients categorized by four surgical approaches, measuring canal fill ratios at multiple levels.
Result· No significant difference in canal fill ratio was found among approaches; femoral morphology (CFI and CCR) was the dominant factor.
Validation· Clinical radiographic study with statistical comparison across groups.
