单位: Department of Orthopedics, 184th Hospital of PLA, Yingtan 335000, China
通讯: Qianhuan Gui @ Department of Orthopedics, 184th Hospital of PLA, Yingtan 335000, China
相关性: 该论文研究的是股骨干骨折内固定板的生物力学性能，而非髋关节植入物或股骨柄的应力遮挡、微动或骨-植入物界面问题。
边界: This paper focuses on fracture fixation plates for femoral shaft fractures, not on hip implants or femoral stems; it does not quantify stress shielding in Gruen zones, micromotion at the bone-implant interface, or compare intact vs. implanted femur for hip arthroplasty.
动机· 比较新型组装锁定加压板与传统锁定加压板在股骨干粉碎性骨折中的生物力学性能。
方法· 使用6对股骨标本建立楔形骨折模型，进行轴向和扭转加载测试，记录位移和应变。
结果· 新型板在轴向和扭转加载下骨折块位移更小，疲劳测试100万次无损坏。
验证· 通过尸体股骨标本的力学测试验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the biomechanical properties of a new assembly locking compression plate (NALCP) with conventional LCP for internal fixation of femoral shaft comminuted fractures.
Method· Six pairs of cadaveric femora were used to create wedge fracture models (AO 32-C2.1) and tested under axial and torsional loading while recording fracture fragment displacement and plate strain.
Result· NALCP showed significantly smaller fracture displacement in axial and torsional tests, and survived 1 million cycles of axial fatigue without failure.
Validation· Mechanical testing on cadaveric femoral specimens with strain gauges and fatigue loading.
