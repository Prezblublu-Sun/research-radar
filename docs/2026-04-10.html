相关性: 该论文关注语言模型的量化不确定性，与有限元替代模型或生物力学设计完全无关。
边界: This paper does not address any aspect of FEA surrogate modeling, expensive simulation sampling, constraint handling, or biomechanics/implant design; it is purely about uncertainty quantification for NLP models.
动机· 在关键应用中需要可信赖的语言模型预测和不确定性估计。
方法· 提出CONFIDE框架，在微调语言模型内部嵌入上应用共形预测。
结果· BERT-tiny测试准确率提升达4.09%，预测集效率优于现有方法。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To enable trustworthy deployment of transformer-based language models in high-stakes applications by providing statistically valid uncertainty estimates.
Method· CONFIDE applies conformal prediction to internal embeddings of encoder-only transformers (e.g., BERT, RoBERTa) using [CLS] token or flattened hidden states to construct class-conditional nonconformity scores.
Result· CONFIDE improves test accuracy by up to 4.09% on BERT-tiny and achieves better correct efficiency than prior conformal baselines.
Validation· Empirical evaluation on multiple NLP benchmark datasets with comparisons to NM2 and VanillaNN.
