英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare revision rates between cemented and cementless acetabular fixation in primary total hip arthroplasty using a large national registry.
Method· Kaplan-Meier survivorship and Cox proportional hazards models were applied to 96,574 cases from the AOANJRR, controlling for stem type, bearing surface, and diagnosis.
Result· No significant difference in revision rate was found between cemented and cementless acetabular fixation across all age and surgeon volume strata.
Validation· Clinical registry data with 23-year follow-up and stratified analysis by age and surgeon volume.
