Use of Adaptive Kriging Metamodeling in Reliability Analysis of Radiated Susceptibility in Coaxial Shielded Cables

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Tarek Bdour, Christophe Guiffaut, Alain Reineix IEEE Transactions on Electromagnetic Compatibility 2015-12-03 10.1109/temc.2015.2501899
单位: OSA, XLIM Research institute, Limoges, France
通讯: Tarek Bdour @ OSA, XLIM Research institute, Limoges, France
相关性: 该论文提出的自适应克里金元建模方法（AK-MCS）可迁移至生物力学植入物设计的可靠性分析，但当前应用于电磁兼容领域。
边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, FEA surrogate for structural mechanics, or uncertainty quantification in biomedical design.
动机· 解决电磁兼容问题中蒙特卡洛模拟计算成本过高的问题。
方法· 提出自适应克里金元模型结合蒙特卡洛模拟（AK-MCS）进行可靠性分析。
结果· 少量真实模型运行即可获得精确的失效概率，显著降低计算成本。
验证· 通过三个同轴屏蔽电缆辐射敏感度数值算例验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce the prohibitive computational cost of Monte Carlo simulation in electromagnetic reliability analysis.
Method· An adaptive Kriging metamodeling method (AK-MCS) is proposed, which actively enriches the design of experiments near the failure region to build an accurate surrogate for failure probability estimation.
Result· The AK-MCS method achieves accurate failure probabilities with only a small number of true model evaluations, outperforming crude Monte Carlo in efficiency.
Validation· Three numerical examples of radiated susceptibility in coaxial shielded cables demonstrate the accuracy and efficiency of the approach.

单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Juntendo University, 2-1-1 Hongo, Bunkyo-ku, Tokyo, 113-0033, Japan
通讯: Hideo Kobayashi @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Juntendo University, 2-1-1 Hongo, Bunkyo-ku, Tokyo, 113-0033, Japan
相关性: 该论文关注手术入路对假体位置的影响，未涉及应力遮挡或微动等生物力学分析。

Motivation· To evaluate changes in component positioning when surgeons transition from posterior to direct anterior approach for total hip arthroplasty during their early experience.
Method· Retrospective radiographic analysis comparing cup anteversion and stem flexion angles between posterior and direct anterior approach groups.
Result· The direct anterior group showed significantly increased cup anteversion and stem flexion, indicating potential issues during the learning curve.
Validation· Clinical radiographic measurements with statistical comparison.

Ex vivo estimation of cementless acetabular cup stability using an impact hammer

Adrien Michel, Romain Bosc, Frédéric Sailhan, Romain Vayron, Guillaume Haïat Medical Engineering & Physics 2015-12-03 10.1016/j.medengphy.2015.10.006
单位: CNRS, Laboratoire de Modélisation et de Simulation Multi-Echelle, UMR CNRS 8208,61 Avenue du Général de Gaulle, Créteil 94010, France
通讯: Guillaume Haïat @ CNRS, Laboratoire de Modélisation et de Simulation Multi-Echelle, UMR CNRS 8208,61 Avenue du Général de Gaulle, Créteil
相关性: 该论文研究髋臼杯稳定性评估，而非股骨柄应力遮挡或微动，与用户研究方向间接相关。
边界: This paper focuses on acetabular cup stability, not femoral stem stress shielding or micromotion, and does not involve FEA or implant-bone interface mechanics for the proximal femur.
动机· 评估髋臼杯初始稳定性对手术成功至关重要。
方法· 使用冲击锤敲击植入物，基于力信号计算指标I。
结果· 指标I与拔出力的相关性R²=0.83，1mm过盈配合稳定性最佳。
验证· 20个牛骨样本的离体实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate whether primary stability of cementless acetabular cups can be assessed via impact hammer force signals.
Method· An impact hammer with a force sensor was used to strike the implant, and an indicator I was derived from partial impulse estimation.
Result· The indicator I correlated significantly with pull-out force (R²=0.83), with optimal stability at 1 mm interference fit.
Validation· Ex vivo experiments on 20 bovine bone samples with varying cavity diameters.

Development and validation of an automated and marker-free CT-based spatial analysis method (CTSA) for assessment of femoral hip implant migration In vitro accuracy and precision comparable to that of radiostereometric analysis (RSA)

Thierry Scheerlinck, Mathias Polfliet, Rudi Deklerck, Gert Van Gompel, Nico Buls et al. Acta Orthopaedica 2015-12-03 10.3109/17453674.2015.1123569
单位: a Department or Orthopedic Surgery and Traumatology , Universitair Ziekenhuis Brussel , Brussels
通讯: Thierry Scheerlinck @ a Department or Orthopedic Surgery and Traumatology , Universitair Ziekenhuis Brussel , Brussels
相关性: 该论文关注髋关节假体迁移的CT测量方法，未涉及应力遮挡或微动等生物力学分析。
边界: This paper focuses on CT-based migration measurement, not on stress shielding, micromotion, or contact mechanics in hip implants.
动机· 开发无标记CT空间分析方法检测假体-骨迁移，并与RSA比较。
方法· 基于CT分割和配准，计算假体与骨的相对位移。
结果· CTSA的精度和准确度与RSA相当，可检测微小迁移。
验证· 体外模型施加已知迁移，评估准确度和精密度。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a marker-free CT-based spatial analysis method for detecting stem-bone migration and validate its accuracy and precision against radiostereometric analysis.
Method· Segmentation and pairwise registration of stem and bone in consecutive CT datasets, followed by transformation to an anatomical coordinate system.
Result· The CTSA method achieved accuracy and precision comparable to RSA, with 95% tolerance intervals below 0.28 mm and 0.20°.
Validation· In vitro validation using imposed known migrations on a stem-bone model and zero-migration models.
