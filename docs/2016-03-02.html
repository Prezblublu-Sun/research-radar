单位: National University of Singapore
通讯: Yehong Zhang @ National University of Singapore
相关性: 该论文提出的多输出高斯过程主动学习方法可迁移至生物力学代理模型的采样效率优化，但未涉及约束处理或不确定性量化在植入物设计中的应用。
边界: This paper does not address constrained optimization, uncertainty quantification for implant design, or biomechanics-specific surrogate modeling.
动机· 多输出高斯过程主动学习中采样位置与测量类型联合选择的计算效率低
方法· 利用稀疏MOGP结构推导新准则，结合松弛子模性设计多项式时间近似算法
结果· 新方法在真实数据集上优于现有MOGP和单输出GP主动学习算法
验证· 在环境监测真实数据集上对比熵准则与基线方法
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Active learning for multi-output Gaussian processes suffers from poor scalability when jointly selecting sampling locations and measurement types.
Method· A novel active learning criterion is derived from sparse MOGP structure, and a polynomial-time approximation algorithm with constant-factor guarantee is developed via relaxed submodularity.
Result· The proposed approach outperforms existing MOGP and single-output GP active learning methods on real-world environmental datasets.
Validation· Empirical evaluation on real-world environmental monitoring datasets comparing entropy-based criteria and baseline methods.

通讯: Phaedon‐Stelios Koutsourelakis @ Professur für Kontinuumsmechanik, Technische Universität München, Boltzmannstrasse 15, 85747 Garching (b. München), Germ
相关性: 该论文提出的变分贝叶斯优化框架可处理高维随机和设计变量，适用于昂贵仿真下的设计优化，与用户关注的代理模型辅助植入物设计有方法上的可迁移性。
边界: This paper does not apply the method to biomedical implant design or biomechanics, lacks experimental validation, and does not address contact mechanics or stress shielding.
动机· 解决高维随机和设计变量下昂贵仿真的优化难题
方法· 将优化问题转化为概率推断，提出变分贝叶斯EM方案

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To address the challenge of optimization under uncertainty with high-dimensional random and design variables, where each forward simulation is computationally expensive.
Method· A variational Bayesian expectation-maximization framework is proposed that recasts the optimization problem as probabilistic inference, enabling efficient local optimization and sensitivity analysis.
Result· The method identifies local optima and low-dimensional sensitivity directions with fewer than 100 forward model calls in numerical examples involving thousands of variables.
Validation· Accuracy is assessed using information-theoretic metrics on synthetic benchmark problems.

单位: Rosenstiel School of Marine and Atmospheric Science University of Miami Miami Florida USA
通讯: Rafael C. Gonçalves @ Rosenstiel School of Marine and Atmospheric Science University of Miami Miami Florida USA

边界: This paper applies UQ to ocean oil transport, not to biomedical implant FEA or surrogate modeling for biomechanics; no contact mechanics, no implant design, no structural optimization.
动机· 量化海洋油输运模拟中输入不确定性对预测的影响

结果· 成功计算了油浓度的均值和标准差，并生成了概率风险图

Motivation· To quantify the impact of input uncertainties on predictions of oil transport in the ocean using a surrogate-based uncertainty quantification framework.
Method· A nonintrusive polynomial chaos method is employed to construct a surrogate from a 1331-member ensemble, enabling probabilistic analysis of oil concentration.
Result· Mean and standard deviation of oil concentration are computed, and probabilistic hazard maps are generated to assess oil impact.
Validation· The surrogate's performance is continuously monitored to identify spatiotemporal regions where its estimates are reliable.
