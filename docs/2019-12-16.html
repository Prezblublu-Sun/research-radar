通讯: Guian Qian @ State Key Laboratory of Nonlinear Mechanics (LNM), Institute of Mechanics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing 100190,

动机· 确定全髋置换前路拉钩安全放置区域以避免股神经损伤
方法· 解剖10具髋关节标本，在髋臼前壁不同钟点位放置标记并测量股神经距离
结果· 股神经在右髋9点/左髋3点位置距髋臼前缘最近（约10.76mm），该处应避免放置拉钩
验证· 尸体解剖测量
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To define a safe zone for anterior retractor placement in total hip arthroplasty to prevent femoral nerve entrapment.
Method· Ten cadaveric hips were dissected, pins placed at clock-face positions along the anterior acetabular wall, and distances from the femoral nerve measured in sequential axial planes.
Result· The femoral nerve was closest to the acetabular rim at plane C (10.76±2.58 mm), corresponding to the 9 o'clock position in the right hip and 3 o'clock in the left, indicating this zone should be avoided for retractor placement.
Validation· Cadaveric dissection and direct measurement.
