通讯: Naoyuki Nomura @ Department of Materials Processing, Graduate School of Engineering, Tohoku University, Sendai 980-8579, Japan
相关性: 该论文研究了低磁性Zr-1Mo合金的粉末床熔融工艺，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能，与生物医用植入物的力学匹配和骨整合方向关联较弱。
边界: This paper does not address porous structure design, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or osseointegration; it focuses on magnetic susceptibility and hardness of a non-porous alloy.
