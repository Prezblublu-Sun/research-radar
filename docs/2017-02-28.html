Selective laser melting porous metallic implants with immobilized silver nanoparticles kill and prevent biofilm formation by methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus

边界: This paper focuses on antibacterial biofilm prevention via silver nanoparticles, and does not address stress shielding, stiffness matching, fatigue behavior, surface roughness, or osseointegration of the porous implant.

Motivation· To address implant-associated infections by developing porous metallic implants with antibacterial properties.

Result· The silver nanoparticle-coated implants effectively killed methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus and prevented biofilm formation.
Validation· Antibacterial efficacy was validated through in vitro bacterial killing and biofilm inhibition assays.
