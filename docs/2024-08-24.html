相关性: 该论文提出了基于应变测量反向推断气动载荷的实时方法，具有不确定性量化，可迁移至生物力学中的载荷估算，但领域差别大。
边界: This paper does not address biomechanical implant design, contact mechanics, or bone-implant interface; it focuses on hypersonic aerodynamics.
动机· 解决高超声速飞行器表面压力难以直接测量的实时估算问题。

结果· 数值实验表明可高效重构表面压力及力/力矩系数，且具噪声鲁棒性。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To enable real-time estimation of aerodynamic surface pressures on hypersonic vehicles where direct measurement is challenging due to harsh aerothermal conditions.
Method· A least-squares formulation with linear constraints from finite element discretization of the linear elasticity PDE is used to derive an explicit inverse map from sparse strain measurements to surface pressures.
Result· Numerical studies demonstrate accurate reconstruction of surface pressure fields and integrated force/moment coefficients, with robustness to sensor noise.
Validation· The method is validated on the IC3X conceptual hypersonic vehicle using numerical simulations.
