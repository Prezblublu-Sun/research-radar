Motivation· To develop a more accurate mortality prediction model for hip arthroplasty patients, as existing comorbidity indices perform poorly.
Method· A new statistical model was developed using LASSO regression on Swedish nationwide registry data, incorporating age, sex, ASA class, and specific comorbidities.
Result· The model achieved good discrimination (AUC > 0.7) and calibration for 90-day mortality after THA due to OA.
Validation· Internal validation and external validation using data from England and Wales.
