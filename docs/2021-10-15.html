Use of a Plasma-Sprayed Titanium-Hydroxyapatite Femoral Stem in Hip Arthroplasty in Patients Older than 70 Years. Is Cementless Fixation a Reliable Option in the Elderly?

Nicola Piolanti, Elisabetta Neri, Enrico Bonicoli, Paolo Domenico Parchi, Stefano Marchetti et al. Journal of Clinical Medicine 2021-10-15 10.3390/jcm10204735
单位: Ortopedia e Traumatologia I, Azienda Ospedaliera Universitaria Pisana, 56126 Pisa, Italy
通讯: Nicola Piolanti @ Ortopedia e Traumatologia I, Azienda Ospedaliera Universitaria Pisana, 56126 Pisa, Italy
相关性: 该论文为临床回顾性研究，未涉及有限元建模或应力/微动定量分析，无法为髋关节假体有限元模拟提供边界条件或验证数据。
边界: No FE modeling, no stress/strain quantification in Gruen zones, no micromotion analysis, no contact mechanics; purely clinical outcome study.

验证· 临床随访和影像学评估
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the reliability of cementless femoral stems in elderly patients undergoing total hip arthroplasty or hemiarthroplasty.
Method· A multicenter retrospective study analyzed clinical and radiographic outcomes of 293 patients (301 hips) aged ≥70 years who received a single type of plasma-sprayed porous titanium-hydroxyapatite stem.
Result· The 10-year cumulative stem survival was 98.5% with no aseptic loosening, and radiographic stress shielding signs were observed in less than 10% of hips.
Validation· Clinical follow-up and radiographic assessment.
