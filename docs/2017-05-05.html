Uncertainty quantification of 2 models of cardiac electromechanics

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project simulation_case UQ
Daniel E. Hurtado, Sebastián Castro, Pedro F. Navarro Madrid International Journal for Numerical Methods in Biomedical Engineering 2017-05-05 10.1002/cnm.2894
单位: Department of Structural and Geotechnical Engineering, School of Engineering Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile Santiago Chile
通讯: Daniel E. Hurtado @ Department of Structural and Geotechnical Engineering, School of Engineering Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile S
相关性: 该论文展示了在心脏电机械耦合模型中应用不确定性量化（UQ）的方法，其UQ框架可迁移至植入物生物力学设计中的代理模型不确定性评估。
边界: This paper does not address surrogate modeling for expensive FEA sampling, nor does it focus on implant or biomechanics design; it is specific to cardiac electromechanics.
动机· 评估参数不确定性对心脏电机械耦合模型预测的影响
方法· 对两种电机械模型进行前向不确定性传播和灵敏度分析，构建线性响应面
结果· 参数变异性在25%内时，关键电导与输出量呈强线性关系；被动力学参数不影响动作电位时程
验证· 通过数值模拟案例验证，未使用实验数据
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To assess the impact of parameter uncertainty on predictions from coupled cardiac electromechanical models.
Method· Forward uncertainty propagation and sensitivity analysis were performed on two electromechanical models, constructing linear response surfaces.
Result· A strong linear relationship between key maximal conductances and quantities of interest was found for up to 25% parameter variability; passive mechanical parameters did not affect action potential duration.
Validation· Validation was conducted through numerical simulation cases without experimental data.

单位: Department of Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering University of Illinois at Urbana‐Champaign Urbana Illinois 61801
通讯: Andrew L. Ferguson @ Department of Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering University of Illinois at Urbana‐Champaign Urbana Illinois 61801
相关性: 该论文专注于分子自由能估计的贝叶斯方法，与生物力学有限元代理建模无直接关联。
边界: This paper addresses molecular free energy estimation, not surrogate modeling for expensive FEA simulations, and lacks any biomechanical or implant design context.
动机· 为加权直方图分析法提供贝叶斯框架以估计自由能及不确定性。
方法· 采用全贝叶斯处理，通过Metropolis-Hastings采样从后验分布生成不确定性估计。
结果· 在丙氨酸二肽和套索肽模拟中验证了方法的统计最优性和不确定性量化能力。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a fully Bayesian treatment of the weighted histogram analysis method for statistically optimal free energy estimation with rigorous uncertainty quantification.
Method· A Bayesian reformulation of WHAM is implemented, using Metropolis-Hastings sampling from the posterior to generate uncertainty estimates, and projecting free energy surfaces into auxiliary order parameters.
Result· The method yields statistically optimal free energy surfaces and reliable uncertainty estimates, demonstrated on alanine dipeptide and a synthetic lasso peptide mimic.
Validation· Validation is performed through simulation case studies on alanine dipeptide and a synthetic lasso peptide, comparing with standard WHAM results.
