&lt;strong&gt;Effective Light Directed Assembly of Building Blocks with Microscale Control&lt;/strong&gt;

Low AI for Bioprinting openalex Peripheral Background only in_vitro Exp. validation
Ngoc‐Duy Dinh 2017-07-21 10.3390/optofluidics2017-04157
单位: Department of Biomedical Engineering, National University of Singapore
通讯: Ngoc‐Duy Dinh @ Department of Biomedical Engineering, National University of Singapore
相关性: 该论文涉及光驱动微组装技术，但未使用AI或机器学习方法，与AI生物打印方向不直接相关。
边界: This paper does not incorporate AI/ML into any bioprinting workflow stage; it is a purely experimental optofluidic assembly method without autonomous decision-making or model training.
动机· 开发低功率、高精度光驱动微组装方法，避免激光损伤生物对象。
方法· 利用金纳米棒光热效应诱导热对流，驱动微凝胶颗粒组装成图案。
结果· 10分钟内形成厘米级功能单元，细胞存活率高，可构建含ECM的微组织。
验证· 通过MSC负载微凝胶培养验证细胞活性和增殖。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a low-power, high-throughput light-directed assembly method for microscale biofabrication that avoids laser-induced damage to biological objects.
Method· Gold nanorods suspended in fluid act as photothermal transducers to generate thermoplasmonic convection flows that assemble microgel building blocks into programmed patterns under low-power infrared laser control.
Result· Centimeter-scale functional units were formed in ~10 minutes with high cell viability and proliferation, enabling scaffold-free microtissues with extracellular matrix.
Validation· In vitro culture of mesenchymal stem cell-laden microgels demonstrated excellent cell viability and proliferation over long-term culture.
