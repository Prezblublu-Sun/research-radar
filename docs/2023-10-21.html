Motivation· To assess in-hospital complications and readmission patterns in patients with developmental dysplasia of the hip undergoing total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective analysis of 13,937 DDH patients from a Chinese national database.
Result· Complication and readmission rates and patterns were reported.
Validation· Large-scale database statistical analysis.
