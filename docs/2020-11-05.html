单位: Department of Mechanical, Manufacturing and Biomedical Engineering, Trinity College Dublin, The University of Dublin, Parsons Building, Dublin 2, Ireland
通讯: Shuo Yin @ Department of Mechanical, Manufacturing and Biomedical Engineering, Trinity College Dublin, The University of Dublin, Pa
相关性: 该论文研究了选区激光熔化Ti6Al4V点阵结构的微观结构和压缩性能，虽未直接涉及应力屏蔽或疲劳，但为生物医用植入物的工艺-结构-性能关系提供了参考。
边界: This paper does not link porous structures to stress shielding or stiffness matching for biomedical implants, nor does it report fatigue, surface roughness, or osseointegration relevant to in vivo performance.

英文摘要与术语

Method· Combined experimental characterization (X-ray CT, SEM, XRD) and finite element modeling to analyze as-fabricated and vacuum-annealed lattice specimens.
Result· Vacuum annealing transformed acicular martensite into lamellar α+β phases and induced nano-precipitates, increasing compressive strength by 26% and improving ductility.
Validation· Validation through compression tests, microstructural analysis, and computed tomography of internal porosity.
