Tomoharu Iwata, Takuma Otsuka arXiv preprint 2019-09-17 link
相关性: 该方法通过迁移贝叶斯优化加速昂贵黑箱函数优化，可迁移至植入物设计中的有限元仿真代理优化问题。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification beyond GP posterior, or biomedical implant design context.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To accelerate expensive black-box optimization by leveraging data from related tasks to reduce target function evaluations.
Method· A Gaussian process with neural network mean and covariance functions that take auxiliary information and feature vectors as input for transfer learning.

Validation· Validated on a synthetic dataset and two real-world optimization tasks (pedestrian traffic regulations and machine learning algorithms).
