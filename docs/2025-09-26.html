High FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark Patient-specific

边界: The paper focuses on brain biomechanics and traumatic brain injury, not on implant design, stress shielding, or hip biomechanics.

英文摘要与术语

Validation· Models are validated on 249 in vivo MRE datasets across frequencies from 20 to 90 Hz, comparing predicted displacement fields against ground truth.
