Physics informed neural networks for elliptic equations with oscillatory differential operators

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Skim only numerical_benchmark
Arnav Gangal, Luis Kim, Sean P. Carney arXiv preprint 2022-12-27 link
相关性: 该论文研究了PINN在多尺度椭圆方程中的训练困难，其NTK分析方法和多尺度处理思路可迁移至生物力学FEA代理模型中的高频振荡问题。
边界: This paper does not address implant/biomechanics design, constraint handling, or uncertainty quantification; it focuses on a pure PDE analysis without any biomedical or engineering application context.
动机· 标准PINN求解多尺度椭圆方程时训练困难，需解释原因。
方法· 通过分析神经正切核矩阵的Frobenius范数随尺度参数ε的变化，揭示训练刚度。
结果· NTK范数以1/ε²增长，导致梯度下降训练困难。
验证· 数值算例验证了优化问题的刚度。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Standard PINNs struggle to solve elliptic PDEs with oscillatory coefficients, and the underlying cause of this difficulty is not well understood.
Method· The Frobenius norm of the neural tangent kernel matrix is analyzed as a function of the scale parameter ε to quantify training stiffness.
Result· The NTK norm grows as 1/ε², making gradient-based optimization increasingly difficult as scale separation increases.
Validation· Numerical examples demonstrate the stiffness of the optimization problem for multiscale coefficients.
