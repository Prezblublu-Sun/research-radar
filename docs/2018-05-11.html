Microstructure and Mechanical Properties of Ti-6Al-4V Alloy Samples Fabricated by Selective Laser Melting

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Jing Gao, Xiaoli Zhao, Yue Ju, Meng Qi, Deliang Zhang Key engineering materials 2018-05-11 10.4028/www.scientific.net/kem.770.179
单位: Northeastern University
通讯: Jing Gao @ Northeastern University
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM制备的Ti-6Al-4V合金的微观结构和力学性能，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能，与用户研究方向中的髋关节植入物力学相关性较低。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or surface characteristics relevant to biomedical implants for stress shielding mitigation.
动机· 研究SLM制备的Ti-6Al-4V合金的微观结构和力学性能。

结果· 退火后试样屈服强度1015 MPa，伸长率7.9%；拉伸方向影响力学性能。

Result· Annealed samples achieved yield strength of 1015 MPa, UTS of 1083 MPa, and elongation of 7.9%; tensile properties varied with build direction.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated through uniaxial tensile tests and microstructural observation via SEM.
