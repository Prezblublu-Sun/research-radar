Pretrain Finite Element Method: A Pretraining and Warm-start Framework for PDEs via Physics-Informed Neural Operators

High FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Direct Read now numerical_benchmark
Yizheng Wang, Zhongkai Hao, Mohammad Sadegh Eshaghi, Cosmin Anitescu, Xiaoying Zhuang et al. arXiv preprint 2026-01-06 link
相关性: 该论文提出的预训练有限元方法直接解决了FEA代理建模中的采样效率问题，且方法可迁移至植入物生物力学设计。
边界: The paper does not include uncertainty quantification (UQ) or constraint handling, and lacks validation on implant-specific biomechanics problems.
动机· 加速有限元求解，同时保持精度和收敛保证。
方法· 提出预训练有限元框架，用物理信息神经算子预训练，再微调作为FEM初始猜测。
结果· 预训练相对误差约1%，微调阶段相比零初始猜测加速一个数量级。
验证· 在线弹性、非线性超弹性等基准问题上验证，涵盖复杂几何、异质材料和任意边界条件。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To accelerate finite element simulations while preserving accuracy and convergence guarantees by leveraging pretrained neural operators.
Method· A Pretrained Finite Element Method (PFEM) is proposed, consisting of a physics-informed pretraining stage using a Transolver-based neural operator on unstructured point clouds, followed by an optional fine-tuning stage where the pretrained prediction serves as an initial guess for conventional FEM solvers.
Result· The pretraining stage achieves relative errors on the order of 1%, and the fine-tuning stage yields speedups of up to one order of magnitude compared to FEM with zero initial guesses.
Validation· Validated on a broad range of benchmark problems including linear elasticity and nonlinear hyperelasticity with complex geometries, heterogeneous materials, and arbitrary boundary conditions.

Motivation· To compare mobility and health-related quality of life between hemiarthroplasty and total hip arthroplasty in elderly patients with femoral neck fractures.
Method· Post hoc analysis of a prospective cohort study using Fracture Mobility Score and EQ-5D, with multivariate logistic and linear regression.
Result· THA was associated with better mobility at 120 and 365 days and better HRQoL at 30 and 120 days postoperatively.
Validation· Clinical follow-up of 333 patients at 30, 120, and 365 days post-surgery.
