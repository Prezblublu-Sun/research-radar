英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To assess the effect of global sagittal spinal deformity on functional disability two years after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A cohort of 208 THA patients was divided into disability and control groups based on HOOS-JR score (cutoff 70), and multivariate analysis identified spinopelvic parameters associated with disability.
Result· A T1PA/PI ratio greater than 0.2 was significantly associated with persistent disability at two-year follow-up.
Validation· Clinical cohort study with ROC curve analysis to determine cutoff values.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To identify risk factors for surgical site infection and mortality following total and hemi hip arthroplasty.
Method· An observational study using multiple logistic regression to analyze risk factors from a 10-year cohort.
Result· Hospital stay was associated with increased infection risk; obesity and inadequate antibiotic prophylaxis were also significant.
Validation· Infection incidences were compared with national NNIS rates.
