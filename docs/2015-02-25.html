Effects of anodizing parameters and heat treatment on nanotopographical features, bioactivity, and cell culture response of additively manufactured porous titanium

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Save for project in_vitro Exp. validation
Saber Amin Yavari, Yoke Chin Chai, A. Böttger, R. Wauthle, Jan Schrooten et al. Materials Science and Engineering C 2015-02-25 10.1016/j.msec.2015.02.050
单位: Faculty of Mechanical, Maritime, and Materials Engineering, Delft University of Technology (TU Delft), Mekelweg 2, 2628 CD Delft, The Netherlands. Electronic address: s.aminyavari@tudelft.nl
通讯: Saber Amin Yavari @ Faculty of Mechanical, Maritime, and Materials Engineering, Delft University of Technology (TU Delft), Mekelweg 2, 2628
相关性: 该论文研究了增材制造多孔钛的阳极氧化和热处理对纳米形貌、生物活性和细胞响应的影响，与用户关注的AM生物医学植入物表面改性相关。
边界: This paper does not include LPBF fabrication details, mechanical testing (static or fatigue), or stress shielding analysis.
动机· 研究阳极氧化参数和热处理对增材制造多孔钛表面纳米形貌及生物活性的影响。
方法· 对选择性激光熔融制备的多孔钛进行不同参数的阳极氧化和热处理，表征表面形貌和化学性质。
结果· 阳极氧化形成纳米管结构，热处理改变晶相，显著增强磷灰石形成和细胞增殖。
验证· 通过SEM、XRD、接触角测量和体外细胞实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate how anodizing parameters and post-heat treatment affect the nanotopography and bioactivity of additively manufactured porous titanium for orthopedic implants.
Method· Porous titanium scaffolds fabricated by selective laser melting were anodized under various voltages and durations, followed by heat treatment, and characterized for surface morphology, chemistry, and wettability.
Result· Anodizing produced nanotubular structures whose dimensions depended on voltage; heat treatment converted amorphous TiO2 to anatase/rutile, improving apatite formation and MC3T3-E1 cell proliferation.
Validation· Validation included SEM, XRD, contact angle measurements, simulated body fluid immersion for apatite formation, and in vitro cell culture assays.

单位: Department of Physiotherapy; Federal University of São Carlos (UFSCar); SP Brazil
通讯: Ana Cláudia Muniz Rennó @ Department of Biosciences; Federal University of São Paulo (UNIFESP); Santos SP Brazil
相关性: 该论文研究生物活性玻璃纤维支架，未涉及LPBF制造、应力遮挡或疲劳测试，与用户研究方向不直接相关。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), no stress shielding or stiffness matching analysis, and no surface roughness or as-built morphology characterization.
动机· 开发可填充不规则骨缺损的柔性纤维玻璃支架
方法· 制备高孔隙率纤维玻璃支架，表征其孔隙率、矿化及形态，并评估细胞增殖和基因毒性
结果· 支架具有互连孔隙，12小时内在SBF中矿化羟基磷灰石层，促进细胞增殖且无DNA损伤，体内60天几乎完全降解
验证· 体外细胞实验和皮下植入大鼠模型验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a flexible fibrous glass scaffold capable of filling irregular bone defects while maintaining bioactivity.
Method· A highly porous fibrous glass scaffold was fabricated and characterized for porosity, mineralization, and morphology; in vitro cell proliferation and genotoxicity were assessed, and in vivo subcutaneous implantation in rats was performed.
Result· The scaffold exhibited interconnected pores, formed a hydroxyapatite layer in SBF within 12 hours, promoted fibroblast and osteoblast proliferation without DNA damage, and degraded almost completely after 60 days in vivo with tissue ingrowth.
Validation· In vitro cell assays (fibroblast and osteoblast) and in vivo subcutaneous rat model.
