边界: This paper addresses active learning for classification, not surrogate modeling, expensive simulation sampling, or biomechanics design.
动机· 减少人工标注成本，批量选择最有价值的未标注样本。

Result· The proposed algorithms achieve competitive performance on 15 datasets and are robust to label noise and class imbalance.
Validation· Extensive numerical benchmarks on binary, multi-class, and multi-label datasets.

通讯: Aldemar Torres Valderrama @ CWI, Amsterdam, Science Park 123, 1098 XG, Amsterdam, The Netherlands. atorresv@cwi.nl  |  Jeroen Witteveen @ CWI, Amsterdam, Science Park 123, 1098 XG, Amsterdam, The Netherlands. atorresv@cwi.nl  |  Maria Navarro @ CWI, Amsterdam, Science Park 123, 1098 XG, Amsterdam, The Netherlands. atorresv@cwi.nl  |  Joke Blom @ CWI, Amsterdam, Science Park 123, 1098 XG, Amsterdam, The Netherlands. atorresv@cwi.nl

边界: This paper focuses on neuronal action potential modeling, not on structural mechanics, implant design, or surrogate modeling for FEA.
动机· 研究神经脉冲动作电位机制中的不确定性传播。
方法· 采用稀疏网格随机配点法对Hodgkin-Huxley模型进行不确定性量化。
