Bioprinted MicrochannelScaffolds Modulate NeuronalDifferentiation of Encapsulated Human Spinal Cord Progenitor Cells

Christy Kwokdinata (17047908), Kyra Chai (21232221), Kieran Lau (10583064), Jerome Tan (21232224), Sing Yian Chew (2883332) Figshare 2025-05-02 10.1021/acsabm.5c00441.s001
相关性: 该论文涉及生物打印，但完全没有人工智能/机器学习方法或工作流的整合。
边界: This paper does not incorporate AI/ML into any bioprinting stage (pre/in/post-printing) or digital twin, nor does it advance from observation to autonomous decision.
动机· 解决脊髓损伤的细胞移植治疗中支架材料与结构优化问题。
方法· 利用DLP生物打印制备含微通道的GelMA支架，包裹人脊髓祖细胞。
结果· 高浓度GelMA促进运动神经元分化，低浓度促进中间神经元分化。
验证· 通过压缩测试、降解实验及免疫荧光染色验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a microchannel scaffold for encapsulating spinal cord progenitor cells to treat spinal cord injury.
Method· Digital light processing bioprinting of gelatin methacrylate-based bioinks with microchannels encapsulating human spinal cord progenitor cells.
Result· Higher GelMA concentration increased motor neuronal differentiation while lower concentration enhanced interneuron differentiation.
Validation· Mechanical testing and in vitro cell differentiation assays were performed.
