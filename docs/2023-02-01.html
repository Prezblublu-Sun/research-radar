Method· A single-plug press-fit technique is used, combined with high tibial osteotomy to correct malalignment.
Result· Pearls and pitfalls of the technique are presented to maximize osseointegration and chondrocyte viability.
Validation· No biomechanical validation; purely a surgical technique report.
