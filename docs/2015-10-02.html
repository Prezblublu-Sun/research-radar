T. Jagadesh, G.L. Samuel Machining Science and Technology 2015-10-02 10.1080/10910344.2015.1085318
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Madras, Chennai, India
通讯: G.L. Samuel @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Madras, Chennai, India
相关性: 该论文研究钛合金微车削的切削力预测，与AM生物医学材料方向无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on machining (subtractive) rather than additive manufacturing, and does not address porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration.
动机· 预测钛合金微车削过程中的切削力以优化加工。
方法· 结合修正Johnson-Cook本构的机理模型和有限元模型。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To predict cutting forces in micro-turning of Ti-6Al-4V for biomedical applications.
Method· A mechanistic model with modified Johnson-Cook material model and a finite element model with shear friction factors were developed.

Validation· Experimental micro-turning tests were conducted to validate the predicted cutting forces.
