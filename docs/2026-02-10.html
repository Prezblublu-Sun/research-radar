边界: This paper does not address biomechanics, implant design, or constraint handling; it focuses on drug release in pharmaceutical systems.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To overcome the limited accuracy of classical drug release models in complex geometries by integrating physical laws with experimental data.

Result· The PINN achieves RMSE below 0.05 using only 6% of the release time for planar films and reduces mean error by up to 40% compared to classical models.
Validation· Validated on published experimental datasets for planar, wrinkled, and crumpled films under noisy and limited-data conditions.
