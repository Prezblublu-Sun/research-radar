通讯: Reza Razaghi @ Mechanical Engineering Department, University of Tabriz, Tabriz, Iran;  |  Alireza Karimi @ Research Department, Basir Eye Health Research Center, Tehran, Iran
相关性: 该论文研究牙科种植体，而非髋关节植入物，与用户研究方向无关。
边界: This paper focuses on dental implants and maxillary bone, not hip implants or femoral stems; it does not quantify stress shielding in Gruen zones, micromotion, or compare intact vs implanted femur.
动机· 研究咀嚼过程中牙科种植体的生物力学性能。
方法· 建立三维动态有限元模型，采用肌肉等效力和轨迹两种方法模拟肌肉力。
结果· 轨迹法得到的应力和变形显著高于肌肉等效力法。
验证· 通过对比两种建模方法的结果进行验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the biomechanical performance of dental prostheses during chewing using dynamic finite element simulation.
Method· A three-dimensional dynamic FE model was developed incorporating two muscle force modeling approaches: muscle equivalent force and trajectory.
Result· The trajectory approach yielded significantly higher contact and von Mises stresses in the implant and bone compared to the muscle equivalent force approach.
Validation· Validation was performed by comparing the results of the two muscle force modeling approaches.
