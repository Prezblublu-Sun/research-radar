相关性: 该论文直接针对颅缝早闭手术规划，使用机器学习替代有限元模拟，实现了实时预测，方法可迁移至植入物设计中的代理建模。
边界: This paper does not address implant stress shielding or contact mechanics, and its surrogate model is specific to cranioplasty rather than general FEA surrogate for implant design.
