On the impact of different system strategies on the material performance of SLM manufactured Ti6Al4V components

单位: StellenBosch University
通讯: Thorsten Hermann Becker @ StellenBosch University
相关性: 本文研究SLM工艺参数对Ti6Al4V力学性能的影响，可为生物医用植入体的增材制造提供工艺参考，但未涉及多孔结构或应力屏蔽。
边界: This paper does not address porous implant structures, stress shielding mitigation, fatigue testing, surface roughness for osseointegration, or any biomedical implant-specific performance metrics.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand how different SLM scanning strategies and process parameters affect the mechanical and metallurgical properties of Ti6Al4V components for potential biomedical applications.
Method· Comparative analysis of building strategies across different SLM systems, correlating process parameters with resulting material performance.
Result· The study highlights the achievable material properties of SLM-produced Ti6Al4V and their deviations from conventionally manufactured counterparts.
Validation· Mechanical testing (e.g., tensile, hardness) and metallurgical characterization were performed on as-built samples.

Probabilistic Linear Multistep Methods

Onur Teymur, Konstantinos Zygalakis, Ben Calderhead arXiv preprint 2016-10-26 link
相关性: 该论文与用户研究方向的相关性较低，因为它仅涉及概率ODE求解器，未涉及FEA代理模型或生物力学设计优化。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modelling, expensive simulation sampling, constraint handling, or biomechanical implant design; it focuses solely on probabilistic ODE solvers without any application to engineering design or biomedical context.
动机· 为线性多步ODE求解器提供概率解释。
方法· 利用高斯过程先验推导Adams-Bashforth和Adams-Moulton方法的概率版本。
结果· 后验均值与确定性解一致，标准差量化局部截断误差。
验证· 理论收敛性证明及数值实验验证收敛阶。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To derive probabilistic versions of classical linear multistep ODE solvers within a Gaussian process framework.
Method· The Adams-Bashforth and Adams-Moulton methods are reformulated using GP priors, with careful covariance design to recover deterministic methods and uncertainty estimates.
Result· The posterior mean matches the deterministic solution, and the posterior standard deviation provides a local truncation error estimate.
Validation· Theoretical convergence proofs and empirical tests up to fifth order demonstrate expected convergence rates.

Suraj Rao, Ross Cunningham, Tuğçe Öztürk, Anthony D. Rollett Materials Performance and Characterization 2016-10-26 10.1520/mpc20160037
单位: Materials Science and Engineering Department, Carnegie Mellon University 1 , 5000 Forbes Avenue, Wean Hall 3325, Pittsburgh, PA15213-3815US
通讯: Suraj Rao @ Materials Science and Engineering Department, Carnegie Mellon University 1 , 5000 Forbes Avenue, Wean Hall 3325, Pittsbu
相关性: 该论文专注于铝合金SLM过程中的孔隙率表征，与生物医学植入物的应力遮挡、机械性能或骨整合无关。
边界: This paper does not involve biomedical materials, stress shielding, LPBF for implants, mechanical testing, fatigue, or osseointegration.
动机· 表征选区激光熔化Al-10Si-1Mg中孔隙的分布
方法· 使用同步辐射X射线计算机断层扫描以1.5μm分辨率测量孔隙
结果· 孔隙尺寸分布随工艺条件显著变化，即使完全熔化区域也存在大量大于5μm的孔隙
验证· 实验测量，未涉及模型验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· Characterize the porosity distribution in Al-10Si-1Mg parts fabricated by selective laser melting.
Method· Synchrotron high-energy X-ray computed tomography was used to measure pore volume fraction, size, and distribution at ~1.5 μm resolution.
Result· Pore size distributions varied substantially with process parameters, and a significant number of pores above 5 μm were found even under fully melted conditions.
Validation· The study is purely experimental, with no computational model validation.

相关性: 该论文为临床回顾性研究，未涉及股骨假体应力遮挡或微动等生物力学分析，与研究方向的有限元建模和力学评估无关。

动机· 比较金属对金属与金属对聚乙烯全髋翻修术后的并发症
方法· 回顾性单中心匹配队列分析
结果· 金属对金属组翻修更早，但术后并发症无显著差异

English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare early complication rates and clinical characteristics between revisions for failed metal-on-metal and metal-on-polyethylene total hip arthroplasties.
Method· Retrospective matched cohort study from a single-center database of 140 revision hips (39 MOM, 101 MOP) performed from 2010 to 2014.
Result· The MOM group underwent revision earlier but showed no significant difference in overall complication rate compared to the MOP group.
Validation· Retrospective clinical data analysis with statistical comparison between matched cohorts.

Prevention of Dislocation Risk During Hip Revision Surgery with the Dual Mobility Concept; Study of a New Generation of Dual Mobility Cups.

Antoine Dangin, Sandrine Boulat, F. Farizon, Rémi Philippot PubMed 2016-10-26 link
单位: Orthopedic and Trauma Surgery Department, University Hospital of Saint-Etienne Saint-Priest-en-Jarez, France
通讯: Antoine Dangin @ Orthopedic and Trauma Surgery Department, University Hospital of Saint-Etienne Saint-Priest-en-Jarez, France
相关性: 论文为临床研究，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡或微动等生物力学量化，与研究方向无关。
边界: No biomechanical quantification of stress shielding, micromotion, or primary stability; no FEA or implant-bone interface analysis.
动机· 评估新型双活动度杯在髋关节翻修术中降低脱位风险的效果。
方法· 回顾性分析91例翻修THA患者使用Novae E TH杯的生存率和并发症。
结果· 术后脱位率3.5%，无菌性松动率为0，再翻修率8.1%。
验证· 通过临床和影像学随访验证，平均33个月。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To assess the efficacy of a new dual mobility cup in reducing dislocation risk after revision total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective clinical study analyzed survival and complication rates in 91 patients receiving the Novae E TH dual mobility cup during revision THA.
Result· The postoperative dislocation rate was 3.5% with no aseptic loosening, and the overall re-revision rate was 8.1% at a mean follow-up of 33 months.
Validation· Clinical and radiographic evaluations were performed to validate outcomes.
