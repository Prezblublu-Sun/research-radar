通讯: David A. Crawford @ Joint Implant Surgeons, New Albany, Ohio
相关性: 该论文仅关注髋臼杯放置的临床准确性，未涉及股骨柄的应力遮挡、微动或生物力学建模。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain, micromotion, or implant-bone interface mechanics; it is purely a clinical radiographic comparison of acetabular component placement.
动机· 比较直接前入路与直接外侧入路对髋臼杯放置准确性的影响
方法· 回顾性分析单医生289例THA术后X光片，测量外展角、前倾角和内侧坐入
结果· 直接前入路在外展角、目标离群值及坐入方面优于外侧入路，且手术侧别影响放置准确性

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine whether the direct anterior approach improves acetabular component alignment compared to the direct lateral approach in primary THA.
Method· Postoperative radiographs of 289 primary THAs by a single surgeon were retrospectively reviewed for abduction, anteversion, and medial cup seating, comparing direct anterior (n=152) vs direct lateral (n=137) approaches.
Result· The direct anterior approach yielded significantly lower abduction angles, fewer outliers for abduction and anteversion targets, and closer seating to the teardrop; laterality effects were also observed.
Validation· Clinical radiographic measurements with statistical comparisons.
