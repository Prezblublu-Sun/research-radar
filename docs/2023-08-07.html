Densification behaviour of laser powder bed fusion processed Ti6Al4V: Effects of customized heat treatment and build direction

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Transferable Save for project mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Akshay Pathania, S. Anand Kumar, Nagesha Bommanahalli Kenchappa Proceedings of the Institution of Mechanical Engineers Part E Journal of Process Mechanical Engineering 2023-08-07 10.1177/09544089231190483
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Jammu, Jammu, J&K, India
通讯: S. Anand Kumar @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Jammu, Jammu, J&K, India
相关性: 该论文研究了LPBF Ti6Al4V的致密化和力学性能，对理解植入物制造工艺有参考价值，但未涉及应力屏蔽或生物医学应用。
边界: This paper does not link porous structure to stress shielding or stiffness matching, nor does it report fatigue, surface roughness, or osseointegration relevant to biomedical implants.
动机· 研究定制热处理和构建方向对LPBF Ti6Al4V致密化行为的影响。
方法· 采用定制热处理、光学显微镜和XRD分析孔隙和微观结构。
结果· 定制热处理使总孔隙率降低两倍，水平方向超级微孔减少44%，垂直方向减少46%，延展性各向异性大幅改善。
验证· 通过力学测试（延展性）和微观结构表征验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· This work investigates the effects of customized heat treatment and build direction on the densification behavior and microstructural evolution of laser powder bed fusion (LPBF) Ti6Al4V alloy.
Method· Customized heat treatment was applied, and optical microscopy and X-ray diffraction were used to evaluate porosity, microstructure, and phase composition.
Result· Customized heat treatment reduced overall porosity by a factor of two compared to as-printed samples, decreased super-micropores by approximately 44% in the horizontal direction and 46% in the vertical direction, and significantly improved ductility isotropy (isotropic index from 0.61 to 0.97).
Validation· Mechanical testing (ductility measurements) and microstructural characterization (optical microscopy, XRD) were performed to validate the effects.
