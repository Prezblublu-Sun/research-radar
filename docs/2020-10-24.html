单位: Institute for Robotics and Cognitive Systems, University of Luebeck, Luebeck, Germany
通讯: Nils Rottmann @ Institute for Robotics and Cognitive Systems, University of Luebeck, Luebeck, Germany
相关性: 该论文提出了一种分层贝叶斯优化方法，可加速机器人控制策略学习，其方法可迁移到植入物设计的昂贵仿真优化中。
边界: The paper focuses on robotic motor control tasks, not on FEA surrogate modeling, implant biomechanics, or expensive simulation sampling efficiency in engineering design.
动机· 机器人控制策略学习需要大量交互，人类却能快速从失败中学习。
方法· 提出分层贝叶斯优化算法，通过高斯过程建模采集函数并加入失败抑制机制。
结果· 在模拟和仿人平衡任务中，该方法在交互次数、计算需求和失败频率上优于标准贝叶斯优化。
验证· 在模拟平衡任务中验证，并与人类实验数据对比。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Robotic control policy learning requires many interactions, whereas humans can infer safe solutions after a single failure.
Method· A hierarchical Bayesian optimization algorithm is developed, using a Gaussian process to model the acquisition function and a mental replay phase to inhibit failure regions.
Result· The method outperforms standard Bayesian optimization in interaction count, computational cost, and failure frequency on simulated and humanoid postural balancing tasks.
Validation· Validation is performed on a simulated postural balancing task and compared with real human data.

边界: This paper addresses variational Bayesian unlearning in machine learning, with no application to FEA surrogate modeling, biomechanics, or implant design; it lacks any connection to expensive simulation sampling, constraint handling, or uncertainty quantification for physical models.
动机· 研究如何从贝叶斯模型中高效遗忘一小部分训练数据。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· This work aims to efficiently unlearn a subset of training data from a Bayesian model without full retraining.
Method· The problem is formulated as minimizing the KL divergence between the approximate posterior after direct unlearning and the exact posterior from retraining, leading to an evidence upper bound optimization.
