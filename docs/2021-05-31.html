Effect of energy input on the density and shape memory effect of NiTi alloy by selective laser melting in different hatch spacing

Sijie Qin, Jianbin Zhan, Yanjin Lu, Jinxin Lin Materials Technology 2021-05-31 10.1080/10667857.2021.1934360
单位: Key Laboratory of Optoelectronic Materials Chemistry and Physics, Fujian Institute of Research on the Structure of Matter, Chinese Academy of Sciences
通讯: Jinxin Lin @ Key Laboratory of Optoelectronic Materials Chemistry and Physics, Fujian Institute of Research on the Structure of Matte
相关性: 该论文研究NiTi合金的SLM工艺参数，未涉及多孔结构或应力遮挡，与研究方向相关性较低。

方法· 改变激光功率和扫描速度，不同扫描间距，分析能量密度。
结果· 高密度(~99.5%)的最优能量密度随激光功率或扫描间距增大而降低。

English summary & terms

Result· The optimal energy density for high relative density (~99.5%) decreased with increasing laser power or hatch spacing.
Validation· Density measurements, XRD, and DSC analysis.
