通讯: Hoang Nam Phan @ Faculty of Road and Bridge Engineering, The University of Danang–University of Science and Technology, Danang 550000, Vi
相关性: 该论文展示了Kriging元模型在地震易损性分析中替代昂贵有限元模拟的方法，其框架可迁移至生物力学植入物设计中的代理建模。
边界: This paper focuses on seismic fragility of bridges, not on implant biomechanics, stress shielding, or any biomedical context.

Motivation· To reduce the computational cost of seismic fragility analysis by replacing expensive finite element simulations with a data-driven metamodel.

Result· The Kriging metamodel efficiently interpolates structural responses and produces fragility curves for different damage states with few simulations.
Validation· The framework is validated on a single-bent reinforced concrete highway bridge case study using OpenSees simulations.
