Evaluating the Prospects of Ti-Base Lattice Infiltrated with Biodegradable Zn-2%Fe Alloy as a Structural Material for Osseointegrated Implants-In Vitro Study.

Noa Gabay, Tomer Ron, Razi Vago, Amnon Shirizly, Eli Aghion Materials (Basel, Switzerland) 2021-08-19 10.3390/ma14164682
单位: Department of Materials Engineering, Ben-Gurion University of the Negev, Beer Sheva 8410501, Israel.
通讯: Eli Aghion @ Department of Materials Engineering, Ben-Gurion University of the Negev, Beer Sheva 8410501, Israel. [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM制备的Ti-6Al-4V点阵结构浸渍可降解Zn-2%Fe合金，但未涉及应力屏蔽或疲劳性能，与用户方向部分相关。
边界: No stress shielding or stiffness matching analysis, no fatigue testing, and no quantitative surface roughness or osseointegration characterization.

结果· 该体系在压缩下保持结构完整性，腐蚀速率可接受，无细胞毒性

English summary & terms
Motivation· To assess the potential of a Ti-6Al-4V lattice infiltrated with biodegradable Zn-2%Fe alloy as a structural material for osseointegrated implants.
Method· Selective laser melting was used to fabricate Ti-6Al-4V lattices, which were then infiltrated with Zn-2%Fe alloy; microstructure, compressive strength, corrosion behavior, and cytotoxicity were evaluated.
Result· The composite lattice maintained mechanical integrity under compression, exhibited acceptable corrosion degradation, and was non-cytotoxic.
Validation· Validation was performed via compression testing, electrochemical corrosion analysis (potentiodynamic polarization and EIS), and indirect cell viability assays.
