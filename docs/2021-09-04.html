通讯: Alexander Schein @ Mechanics & High Performance Computing Group, Technical University of Munich, Parkring 35, 85748, Garching b. München, G
相关性: 该论文针对腹主动脉瘤的有限元模型降阶，其贪婪采样和超缩减方法可迁移至植入物或生物力学设计的代理建模。
边界: This paper does not address implant or bone mechanics, nor does it include uncertainty quantification for surrogate model predictions beyond Monte Carlo sampling.
动机· 降低腹主动脉瘤力学分析中参数化有限元模型的计算成本

英文摘要与术语

Result· The reduced-order model accurately predicts von Mises stress and strain fields across the parameter domain, with Monte Carlo statistics matching the full-order model for three patient-specific geometries.
Validation· Numerical validation on three patient-specific AAA models comparing ROM and FOM statistics.
