Compressive properties of Ti6Al4V Functionally Graded Lattice Structures via topology optimization design and selective laser melting fabrication

Yangli Xu, Guoqin Huang, Tingting Li, Yuanqiang Tan, Tao Bao Materials Science and Engineering A 2022-11-03 10.1016/j.msea.2022.144265
单位: Institute of Manufacturing Engineering, Huaqiao University, Fujian, China
通讯: Yangli Xu @ Institute of Manufacturing Engineering, Huaqiao University, Fujian, China
相关性: 该论文通过拓扑优化和SLM制造Ti6Al4V功能梯度点阵结构，并测试其压缩性能，直接关联植入体刚度匹配以减少应力遮挡，但缺乏疲劳和表面形貌分析。
边界: This paper does not cover fatigue behavior or surface roughness characterization, limiting its utility for osseointegration and long-term performance assessment.

结果· 功能梯度点阵展现出更优的能量吸收和渐进变形

English summary & terms
Motivation· To design and fabricate functionally graded lattice structures (FGLS) for biomedical implants that mimic bone stiffness and reduce stress shielding.
Method· Topology optimization was used to design FGLS with varying relative density, fabricated by selective laser melting, and tested under static compression.
Result· FGLS showed improved energy absorption and more gradual deformation compared to uniform lattice structures.
Validation· Mechanical testing (static compression) combined with simulation.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of different thromboprophylaxis agents on the osseointegration of cementless titanium implants.
Method· Rats underwent ovariectomy and intramedullary titanium implant insertion, followed by 35-day administration of enoxaparin, nadroparin, or fondaparinux, with micro-CT, pull-out testing, and histology.
Result· All anticoagulants reduced osseointegration, with nadroparin showing the most negative impact on bone volume and pull-out force.
Validation· In vivo animal study with micro-CT, mechanical pull-out tests, and histological analysis.
