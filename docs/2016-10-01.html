English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate whether short-stem prostheses achieve superior clinical and radiological outcomes compared to conventional implants in primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials was performed by searching PubMed, Web of Science, and Cochrane Library up to June 2016, and data were analyzed using RevMan 5.3 with fixed- or random-effects models.
Result· Short-stem prostheses significantly reduced thigh pain but showed no significant differences in Harris Hip Scores, WOMAC scores, femoral offset, or leg-length discrepancy compared to conventional implants.
Validation· The analysis included six RCTs involving 552 patients with 572 hips, with quality assessment by two independent reviewers.
