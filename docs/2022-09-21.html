Effect of stress state on the mechanical behavior of 3D printed porous Ti6Al4V scaffolds produced by laser powder bed fusion

单位: Duke University Department of Mechanical Engineering and Materials Science, Durham, NC 27710, United States
通讯: Kaitlin Nelson @ Duke University Department of Mechanical Engineering and Materials Science, Durham, NC 27710, United States
相关性: 该论文直接研究了LPBF制造的Ti6Al4V多孔支架在不同应力状态下的力学行为，与用户关注的应力遮挡、疲劳和骨整合方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing, surface roughness characterization, or osseointegration assessment.
动机· 多孔金属支架在体内承受复杂应力状态，需理解其力学行为以匹配骨骼。
方法· 通过LPBF制备三种晶格结构的Ti6Al4V多孔支架，进行拉伸、压缩、剪切和扭转测试。
结果· Gyroid结构在所有应力状态下均表现出更优的力学性能，应力状态对强度有显著影响。

英文摘要与术语

Method· Three lattice structures (gyroid, strut-based) with varying porosities were fabricated via LPBF from Ti6Al4V and tested under tensile, compressive, shear, and torsional loads.
Result· The gyroid lattice exhibited superior stiffness and strength across all stress states, and stress state significantly influenced scaffold fracture behavior.
Validation· Mechanical testing under multiple loading modes provided the first dataset on stress-state-dependent fracture of 3D printed biomimetic scaffolds.
