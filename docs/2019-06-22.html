Reynolds Stress Perturbation for Epistemic Uncertainty Quantification of RANS Models Implemented in OpenFOAM

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only numerical_benchmark UQ
Luis Rey, Denis F. Hinz, Mahdi Abkar Fluids 2019-06-22 10.3390/fluids4020113
单位: Department of Engineering, Aarhus University, 8000 Aarhus C, Denmark
通讯: Denis F. Hinz @ Kamstrup A/S, Industrivej 28, Stilling, 8660 Skanderborg, Denmark  |  Mahdi Abkar @ Department of Engineering, Aarhus University, 8000 Aarhus C, Denmark
相关性: 该论文涉及RANS模型的不确定性量化，但应用于流体力学，与生物力学植入物设计的代理建模或优化无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on epistemic UQ for RANS turbulence models in fluid dynamics, with no application to solid mechanics, implant design, or surrogate modeling for expensive simulations.
动机· 提高RANS模拟可靠性，量化湍流模型的结构不确定性。
方法· 通过扰动雷诺应力张量中的湍动能和特征值来估计认知不确定性。
结果· 扰动方法成功估计了最准确结果所在的区域。
验证· 在OpenFOAM中实现，并与DNS和实验数据对比验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To improve the reliability of RANS simulations by quantifying the structural (epistemic) uncertainty introduced by the turbulent-viscosity hypothesis.
Method· The epistemic uncertainty is estimated by perturbing the Reynolds stress tensor, specifically the turbulent kinetic energy and eigenvalues, implemented in OpenFOAM.
Result· The perturbation approach successfully bounds the region containing the most accurate results for the backward-facing step benchmark.
Validation· Validation is performed by comparing perturbed RANS results with direct numerical simulation (DNS) and experimental data for the backward-facing step case.

Motivation· To compare femoral tunnel anteversion and length between flexible and rigid reamers in ACL reconstruction.
Method· Retrospective analysis of intra-operative tunnel length and post-operative radiographic anteversion in 37 rigid and 43 flexible reamer patients.
Result· Flexible reamers produced significantly greater anteversion (40° vs 18.6°) and longer tunnels (41mm vs 33.6mm).
Validation· Statistical analysis of clinical patient data.

单位: fax: +541148052749
通讯: Fernando Diaz Dilernia @ fax: +541148052749
相关性: 该论文仅比较不同外科医生进行全髋关节置换术的临床结果，未涉及任何生物力学建模或植入物设计。
边界: This paper is a clinical outcomes study comparing surgeon experience, with no biomechanical modeling, no stress shielding analysis, no micromotion quantification, and no FEA or implant design content.
动机· 比较髋关节外科医生与住院医师进行全髋置换术的长期结果差异
方法· 回顾性分析205例股骨颈骨折患者，比较两组手术并发症和假体存活率
结果· 住院医师组死亡率显著高于髋关节外科医生组，但假体存活率无显著差异

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare long-term clinical outcomes and survivorship of total hip arthroplasty performed by hip surgeons versus orthopaedic residents for displaced femoral neck fractures.
Method· A retrospective study of 205 patients (95 by hip surgeons, 110 by residents) with median 10-year follow-up, using logistic regression to analyze associations between surgeon type and complications.
Result· Patients treated by residents had significantly higher mortality (odds ratio >2), but implant survivorship and functional scores were similar between groups.
Validation· Clinical validation through retrospective cohort analysis with 10-year follow-up data.

Total hip templating using a marker ball (vs. 21% magnification): no difference in accuracy but increase in potential harm

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore clinical
Jorge Manrique, Andrew K. Battenberg, Carol Foltz, Karan Goswami, William J. Hozack Current Orthopaedic Practice 2019-06-22 10.1097/bco.0000000000000777
单位: Department of Orthopaedics & Sports Medicine, University of Washington, Seattle, WA
通讯: Jorge Manrique @ Department of Orthopaedics & Sports Medicine, University of Washington, Seattle, WA
相关性: 该论文仅涉及髋关节置换术的术前模板测量，未涉及任何生物力学分析或植入物设计。
边界: This paper focuses on clinical templating accuracy for implant sizing, with no biomechanical modeling, stress shielding, micromotion, or FEA.
动机· 比较数字模板测量中21%放大与标记球技术的准确性。
方法· 回顾性分析50例THA患者，双盲评估模板尺寸与实际植入物尺寸。
结果· 两种技术准确性相当，但标记球易高估股骨组件尺寸。
验证· 临床数据验证，统计相关性及误差分析。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the accuracy of digital templating using a standard 21% magnification versus a marker ball technique for total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Two blinded evaluators retrospectively templated 50 consecutive THA patients and compared estimates to final implant sizes using correlations and error analysis.
Result· Both techniques showed equal accuracy within one size, but marker ball led to more overestimation of femoral components.
Validation· Clinical validation with 50 patient radiographs and statistical tests.
