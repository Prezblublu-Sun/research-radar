From microstructural design to surface engineering: A tailored approach for improving fatigue life of additively manufactured meta-biomaterials.

S M Ahmadi, R Kumar, E V Borisov, R Petrov, S Leeflang et al. Acta biomaterialia 2019-01-01 10.1016/j.actbio.2018.10.043
单位: Faculty of Mechanical, Maritime, and Materials Engineering, Delft University of Technology (TU Delft), Mekelweg 2, 2628 CD Delft, The Netherlands.
通讯: V A Popovich @ Faculty of Mechanical, Maritime, and Materials Engineering, Delft University of Technology (TU Delft), Mekelweg 2, 2628
相关性: 该论文直接针对增材制造钛合金多孔支架的疲劳性能，通过微观结构和表面工程协同提升疲劳寿命，与用户关于AM生物医学材料方向高度契合。
边界: This paper does not explicitly discuss stress shielding or stiffness matching of the lattice relative to bone, nor does it include osseointegration or in vivo validation.

Method· Combined microstructural design via heat treatments and hot isostatic pressing with surface engineering through sand blasting and chemical etching.
Result· The endurance limit of the lattice meta-biomaterials was doubled due to reduced porosity and introduction of compressive residual stresses.
Validation· Fatigue testing was performed on selective laser melted specimens with various post-treatments to quantify improvement.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate whether dental implants lacking primary stability can still achieve osseointegration and support functional prostheses.
Method· Patients received Trabecular Metal implants in posterior sockets after extraction, with stability groups defined by ISQ at placement.
Result· All implants osseointegrated successfully, with mean ISQ increasing above 70 after five months regardless of initial stability.
Validation· Clinical resonance frequency analysis (ISQ) and radiographic marginal bone level assessment.
