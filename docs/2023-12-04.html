Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
M. R. Mahani, Igor A. Nechepurenko, Yasmin Rahimof, Andreas Wicht arXiv preprint 2023-12-04 10.1088/2632-2153/ad605f
相关性: 该论文提出的贝叶斯优化数据生成方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理模型的训练数据高效采样。
边界: No implant/biomechanics context, no constraint handling, no uncertainty quantification beyond GPR standard deviation.
动机· 科学数据采集成本高，需最小化训练数据量。

Result· ML models trained on the Bayesian optimization-generated database achieve higher accuracy with significantly fewer data points compared to traditional sampling methods.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks comparing model performance across databases from different sampling strategies.
