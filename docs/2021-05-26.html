英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the efficacy of minimally invasive osteoplasty and screw fixation for metastatic periacetabular tumors.
Method· A retrospective review of 38 patients treated with osteoplasty and screw fixation with or without ablation.
Result· Functional outcomes improved, narcotic usage decreased, and complication rates were low.
Validation· Clinical retrospective study without biomechanical validation.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate whether gluteus medius tendon degeneration is more severe in patients with hip osteoarthritis or after total hip arthroplasty compared to controls.
Method· Tendon biopsies from 100 patients in four groups (revision THA via direct lateral or posterior approach, primary OA, and femoral neck fracture) were examined histologically and ultrastructurally.
Result· Revision groups showed significantly smaller collagen fibril diameters and higher total degeneration scores, especially after direct lateral approach.
Validation· Clinical study with four patient groups and histological/ultrastructural analysis.
