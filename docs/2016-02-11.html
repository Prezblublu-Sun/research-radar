Permeability and mechanical properties of triply periodic minimal surface scaffolds for bone regeneration

High AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Read now mechanical_testing Exp. validation
F.S.L. Bobbert Research Repository (Delft University of Technology) 2016-02-11 link
相关性: 该论文直接研究了用于骨再生的TPMS支架的渗透性和力学性能，包括疲劳测试，与用户关于增材制造生物医学植入物的方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not explicitly link porous structure to stress shielding or stiffness matching, nor does it report surface roughness or as-built morphology.
动机· 研究不同TPMS支架的渗透性和力学性能，以评估其作为骨再生支架的适用性。
方法· 通过SLM制造四种TPMS支架，进行micro-CT、静态压缩、疲劳测试和渗透实验及CFD模拟。
结果· 支架的渗透性和力学性能依赖于单元类型和孔隙率，部分支架在10^6次循环后仍保持完整。
验证· 实验与计算结合，包括静态压缩、疲劳测试和渗透实验。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the permeability and mechanical properties of TPMS scaffolds for bone regeneration, considering the influence of architecture and porosity.
Method· Ti6Al4V scaffolds with four TPMS unit cells (primitive, I-WP, gyroid, diamond) were fabricated via SLM and characterized by micro-CT, static compression, fatigue tests, and permeability experiments with CFD simulations.
Result· Permeability and mechanical properties depend on unit cell type and porosity; I-WP and diamond scaffolds survived 10^6 cycles, suggesting suitability for bone regeneration.
Validation· Experimental validation through static compression, fatigue testing, and permeability measurements, complemented by CFD simulations.
