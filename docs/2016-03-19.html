Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ

单位: Institute of Aircraft Reliability Engineering, Department of Engineering Mechanics Northwestern Polytechnical University Xi'an 710072 China
通讯: Yongshou Liu @ Institute of Aircraft Reliability Engineering, Department of Engineering Mechanics Northwestern Polytechnical University
相关性: 该论文提出的主动学习Kriging与随机集蒙特卡洛结合的方法可用于处理含混合不确定性的可靠性分析，可迁移至植入物设计中的不确定性量化。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling for implant biomechanics, nor does it involve multi-objective optimization or constraint handling.

Motivation· To efficiently perform reliability analysis under both aleatory and epistemic uncertainties while reducing computational cost.
Method· An active learning Kriging model is integrated with random-set based Monte Carlo simulation to predict failure probability bounds.
Result· The proposed method accurately estimates failure probability intervals with significantly fewer function evaluations.
Validation· Validation is conducted on four numerical examples demonstrating efficiency and accuracy.

Zheng Zhang, Tsui-Wei Weng, Luca Daniel arXiv preprint 2016-03-19 link
相关性: 该论文针对集成电路和MEMS的高维不确定性量化问题，与生物力学植入物设计的FEA代理建模方向相关性较弱。
边界: This paper focuses on IC/MEMS uncertainty quantification via tensor recovery, with no FEA surrogate modeling, no biomedical context, and no implant design application.
动机· 高维不确定性量化问题计算成本高，传统方法仅适用于少量随机参数。
方法· 通过低秩张量恢复从少量样本估计大量解样本。

Method· A low-rank tensor recovery method estimates a huge number of solution samples from a small set of quadrature samples.
Result· The proposed approach extends stochastic collocation to problems with over 50 random parameters, far beyond the capability of traditional methods.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on integrated circuits and MEMS demonstrate the method's effectiveness.
