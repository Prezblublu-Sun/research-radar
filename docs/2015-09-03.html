单位: Department of General Orthopaedics, Musculoskeletal Oncology, Trauma Surgery, Poznan University of Medical Sciences, Poznan, Poland
通讯: Łukasz Łapaj @ Department of General Orthopaedics, Musculoskeletal Oncology, Trauma Surgery, Poznan University of Medical Sciences, Poz
相关性: 该论文研究银纳米颗粒掺杂羟基磷灰石涂层的骨整合，但未涉及多孔结构、LPBF制造或疲劳测试。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no porous structure design for stress shielding, no mechanical testing (only pull-out and SEM), no fatigue or stiffness matching.
动机· 评估银纳米颗粒掺杂羟基磷灰石涂层对骨整合的影响

Motivation· To evaluate whether silver nanoparticle doping of hydroxyapatite coatings compromises osseointegration while retaining antimicrobial properties.

Validation· In vivo rabbit model with SEM imaging and bone contact area quantification.

相关性: 该论文研究了多孔钛的化学改性以提高生物活性，但未涉及应力遮挡、疲劳性能或LPBF制造。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), no stress shielding or stiffness matching analysis.
动机· 通过化学处理增强多孔钛的体外生物活性和蛋白吸附。
方法· 采用碱、酸、氟化物等处理多孔钛，并浸泡在模拟体液中评估磷灰石沉积。
结果· 氟化物处理样品磷灰石沉积最致密，碱处理样品蛋白吸附最高。
验证· 通过XRD、SEM和Bradford法验证表面形貌、磷灰石生长和蛋白吸附。

Motivation· To enhance the in-vitro bioactivity and protein adsorption of porous titanium through various chemical surface treatments.
Method· Porous titanium samples were treated with alkali, dual acid, citric acid, and fluoride, then immersed in simulated body fluid to evaluate apatite formation.
Result· Fluoride-treated samples exhibited the densest apatite deposition, while alkali-treated samples showed the highest protein adsorption.
Validation· Surface characterization and bioactivity were validated using XRD, SEM, and Bradford protein assay.
