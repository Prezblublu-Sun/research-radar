Result· The method reduces tail residuals while maintaining or improving mean errors across several PDEs, including Burgers, Heat, KdV, and Poisson equations.
Validation· Validation is performed on numerical benchmarks of smooth and discontinuous PDEs, comparing against vanilla PINNs and attention-based variants.
