Chelsea Finn, Kelvin Xu, Sergey Levine arXiv preprint 2018-06-07 link
相关性: 该论文提出了一种概率元学习方法，但未涉及有限元分析或生物力学设计，仅提供不确定性量化的通用思路。
边界: No FEA surrogate modeling, no implant/biomechanics context, no constraint handling or multi-objective optimization.
动机· 小样本学习中任务歧义性导致单一模型不准确。

Method· Extends model-agnostic meta-learning by training a parameter distribution via a variational lower bound and injecting noise into gradient descent at test time.
Result· The method produces plausible classifiers and regressors in ambiguous few-shot problems and enables downstream active learning.
Validation· Evaluated on standard few-shot classification and regression benchmarks.
