Learning Robust Satellite Attitude Dynamics with Physics-Informed Normalising Flow

Carlo Cena, Mauro Martini, Marcello Chiaberge arXiv preprint 2025-08-11 10.1016/j.actaastro.2026.03.047
相关性: 该论文使用物理信息神经网络学习航天器动力学，但缺乏生物医学或有限元分析相关背景。
边界: This paper addresses spacecraft attitude dynamics with PINNs, not related to FEA surrogate modelling for implant or biomechanics design; no structural mechanics, constraints, or UQ.
动机· 提高卫星姿态动力学模型精度和鲁棒性
方法· 基于Real NVP和自注意力机制的物理信息神经网络
结果· 物理信息模型在MPC中控制精度提升62%
验证· Basilisk模拟数据验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the accuracy and robustness of learned spacecraft attitude dynamics for model predictive control.
Method· A physics-informed normalizing flow (Real NVP with self-attention) is trained to incorporate physical constraints into the learned dynamics.
Result· The physics-informed model achieves up to 62% improvement in settling time and 27% lower mean relative error over purely data-driven approaches.
Validation· Validation is performed on simulated data generated with the Basilisk simulator, with comparisons to purely data-driven and traditional MPC baselines.

Learning an Implicit Physics Model for Image-based Fluid Simulation

Emily Yue-Ting Jia, Jiageng Mao, Zhiyuan Gao, Yajie Zhao, Yue Wang arXiv preprint 2025-08-11 link
相关性: 该论文专注于基于单张图像的流体物理动画生成，与有限元代理建模、植入物设计或生物力学优化无关。
边界: No FEA surrogate modelling, no implant/biomechanics context, no design optimization or constraint handling.
动机· 从单张图像生成符合物理规律的4D动画场景
方法· 利用物理信息神经网络预测表面点运动，结合Navier-Stokes损失
结果· 生成物理合理的流体动画，性能优于现有方法
验证· 在图像数据集上与现有方法对比评估
English summary & terms
Motivation· To generate physically plausible 4D animations from a single static image.
Method· A physics-informed neural network predicts surface point motion guided by Navier-Stokes loss, with feature-based 3D Gaussians for appearance.
Result· The method produces physically plausible fluid animations, outperforming existing approaches.
Validation· Qualitative and quantitative comparison on real image datasets against baseline methods.
