R-PINN: Recovery-type a-posteriori estimator enhanced adaptive PINN

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark
Rongxin Lu, Jiwei Jia, Young Ju Lee, Zheng Lu, Chen-Song Zhang arXiv preprint 2025-06-11 link
相关性: 该论文提出的自适应PINN方法可提高局部大梯度区域的求解精度，对生物力学FEA替代建模中的高梯度区域（如应力集中）有潜在应用价值。
边界: This paper does not apply the method to biomedical or implant mechanics problems, lacks uncertainty quantification, and does not address multi-objective or constrained optimization.
动机· 标准PINN在局部大梯度区域误差大，需要自适应改进。
方法· 基于恢复型后验误差估计器自适应调整配置点分布，提出R-PINN算法。
结果· R-PINN比FI-PINN收敛更快，自适应点更少，多区域大误差情况下表现更优。
验证· 在多个PDE基准问题上与FI-PINN对比验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Standard PINNs suffer from large localized errors in regions with steep gradients, motivating an adaptive approach.
Method· A recovery-type a-posteriori error estimator from adaptive FEM is integrated into PINN to adaptively redistribute collocation points, yielding R-PINN.
Result· R-PINN achieves faster convergence with fewer adaptive points and outperforms FI-PINN, especially in problems with multiple high-error regions.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on several PDEs with large local gradients are used to compare R-PINN against FI-PINN.
