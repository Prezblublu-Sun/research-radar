单位: National Joint Engineering Research Center of NC Forming Technology and Equipment School of Mechanical Engineering Nanjing University of Science and Technology Nanjing 210094 P. R. China
通讯: Wenhe Liao @ National Joint Engineering Research Center of NC Forming Technology and Equipment School of Mechanical Engineering Nanji
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM制造的TPMS点阵结构的压缩性能和能量吸收，与生物医学植入物的力学性能匹配相关。
边界: No link to stress shielding or stiffness matching for biomedical implants; no fatigue testing; no surface roughness or osseointegration data.
动机· 研究SLM制造的TPMS点阵结构的压缩性能和能量吸收。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the compression performance and energy absorption of TPMS lattice structures fabricated by SLM.
Method· Mechanical experiments and finite-element simulation were used to analyze deformation and failure modes of four TPMS structures (Gyroid, Diamond, IW, Primitive).
Result· Gyroid and IW structures exhibited higher compression performance and energy absorption with bending-torsional coupling deformation, while Primitive structures had highest strength but lowest energy absorption due to stretch-dominated deformation.
Validation· Experimental compression tests and finite-element simulations were compared to validate deformation modes and mechanical properties.
