On the effect of internal channels and surface roughness on the high-cycle fatigue performance of Ti-6Al-4V processed by SLM

Johannes Günther, Stefan Leuders, Peter Koppa, Thomas Tröster, Sebastian Henkel et al. Materials & Design 2018-01-31 10.1016/j.matdes.2018.01.042
单位: Universität Kassel, Institute of Materials Engineering, Mönchebergstraße 3, 34125 Kassel, Germany
通讯: Johannes Günther @ Universität Kassel, Institute of Materials Engineering, Mönchebergstraße 3, 34125 Kassel, Germany
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM制备Ti-6Al-4V合金内通道和表面粗糙度对高周疲劳性能的影响，直接关联生物医用植入物的疲劳耐久性。
边界: This paper does not explicitly address stress shielding or osseointegration, nor does it evaluate porous structures for stiffness matching.
动机· 探究内部通道和表面粗糙度对SLM Ti-6Al-4V疲劳寿命的影响。
方法· 制备不同内通道几何和表面状态的SLM试样进行高周疲劳测试。
结果· 内通道和表面粗糙度显著降低疲劳性能，粗糙表面疲劳寿命下降明显。
验证· 通过高周疲劳实验数据验证内通道和粗糙度的影响规律。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the influence of internal channels and surface roughness on the high-cycle fatigue performance of selective laser melted Ti-6Al-4V, relevant to biomedical implant applications.
Method· Specimens with varying internal channel geometries and surface conditions were fabricated by SLM and subjected to high-cycle fatigue testing.
Result· Both internal channels and as-built surface roughness significantly reduced fatigue life, with roughness causing more severe degradation.
Validation· Experimental fatigue testing validated the detrimental effects of internal channels and surface roughness on fatigue performance.

Motivation· To identify patient and surgical factors influencing postoperative infection rates after total hip arthroplasty.

Validation· Statistical regression analysis without experimental validation.

通讯: Douglas A. Dennis @ Colorado Joint Replacement, Denver, CO; Department of Biomedical Engineering, University of Tennessee, Knoxville, TN; Bi
相关性: 该论文为临床比较研究，未涉及任何生物力学建模或应力遮挡分析。
边界: No biomechanical modeling, no FEA, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no implant design comparison relevant to femoral stem mechanics.
动机· 比较两种硬对硬摩擦界面全髋关节置换术的中期临床结果。
方法· 前瞻性队列研究，收集患者功能评分和影像学数据。
结果· 两种设计在中期随访中临床结果相似，无显著差异。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare midterm clinical outcomes of two hard-on-hard bearing total hip arthroplasty designs.
Method· Prospective cohort study collecting functional scores and radiographic data from patients.
Result· Both designs showed similar clinical outcomes at midterm follow-up with no significant differences.
Validation· Statistical analysis of clinical follow-up data.
