单位: School of Materials Science and Engineering, Shaanxi University of Technology, Hanzhong 723001, China
通讯: Peiyou Li @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, Shaanxi University of Technology, Hanzhong 723001, China
相关性: 该论文研究了低弹性模量β型Ti-Nb-Sn生物医学合金的微观结构和力学性能，但未涉及多孔结构、LPBF制造或疲劳测试，与用户方向关联较弱。

Motivation· To investigate the differential expression of MFAP4 in synovial membranes of rheumatoid arthritis versus osteoarthritis patients.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the diagnostic value of serum procalcitonin for late periprosthetic joint infection after arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective analysis of 77 revision cases (total hip and knee arthroplasty) comparing serum procalcitonin levels between infected and non-infected groups.
Result· No statistically significant difference in procalcitonin positivity rate was found between the two groups.
Validation· Clinical diagnosis based on Musculoskeletal Infection Society criteria.
