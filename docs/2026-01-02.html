边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, stress shielding, or any biomedical context; it focuses on geothermal reservoir temperature forecasting.
动机· 地热系统温度预测中传统方法计算昂贵且缺乏物理一致性。
方法· 提出扩展Arps递减曲线和方程嵌入神经网络及高斯过程代理模型。
结果· 方程嵌入网络和高斯过程代理均实现高精度温度预测，后者提供校准不确定性。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Reliable temperature forecasting in Enhanced Geothermal Systems is hindered by the high computational cost of THM simulations and the lack of physics consistency in traditional decline curves and ML surrogates.
Method· A physics-consistent framework is proposed, generalizing Arps decline curves with an equilibrium temperature term and embedding them into an equation-informed neural network, alongside a probabilistic Gaussian Process Regression surrogate.
Result· The equation-informed network achieves hold-out RMSE of 4.49 °C, while the Gaussian Process surrogate yields the best accuracy (RMSE 3.39 °C) with well-calibrated uncertainty over 3-60 month horizons.
Validation· Validation is performed using downhole temperature measurements from Utah FORGE and a controlled THM simulation dataset covering multiple design parameters.
