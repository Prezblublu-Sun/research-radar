相关性: 该论文研究了WE43镁合金多孔支架的LPBF制造工艺，但未涉及应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper focuses on densification and dimensional accuracy of LPBF-fabricated Mg alloy scaffolds, but does not address stress shielding, fatigue testing, or osseointegration.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To achieve both high densification and dimensional accuracy in LPBF-fabricated WE43 magnesium alloy porous scaffolds.
Method· LPBF with varied laser power and scanning speed, combined with preset size compensation for strut diameters.
Result· After compensation, relative density reached 99.64-99.79% and dimensional error reduced to 7.16-9.27%, with compressive strength ranging from 4.37 to 21.21 MPa.
Validation· Validation through relative density measurement, dimensional error analysis, and compressive testing.
