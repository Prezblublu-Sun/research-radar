Tuning Hydrogels by Mixing Dynamic Cross‐Linkers: Enabling Cell‐Instructive Hydrogels and Advanced Bioinks

Francis L. C. Morgan, Julia Fernández‐Pérez, Lorenzo Moroni, Matthew B. Baker Advanced Healthcare Materials 2021-10-08 10.1002/adhm.202101576
单位: Department of Complex Tissue Regeneration MERLN Institute Maastricht University Maastricht 6229 ER The Netherlands
通讯: Lorenzo Moroni @ Department of Complex Tissue Regeneration MERLN Institute Maastricht University Maastricht 6229 ER The Netherlands  |  Matthew B. Baker @ Department of Complex Tissue Regeneration MERLN Institute Maastricht University Maastricht 6229 ER The Netherlands
相关性: 该论文研究生物墨水动态交联剂的混合调控，但未涉及任何人工智能方法，与用户AI驱动生物打印的方向不直接相关。
边界: This paper does not use AI/ML in any bioprinting workflow stage; it focuses purely on hydrogel chemistry and rheology tuning.
动机· 开发兼具加工性与ECM仿生性的可调水凝胶生物墨水
方法· 通过混合不同时间尺度的动态交联剂（腙和肟）调控水凝胶性能
结果· 刚度与应力松弛可调两个数量级，且打印后结构可回收
验证· 流变测试、自愈合实验、挤出打印及成纤维细胞培养
English summary & terms
Motivation· To rationally design hydrogels that balance processability and ECM biomimicry for biofabrication.
Method· A modular mixing of dynamic covalent cross-linkers (hydrazone and oxime) to tune viscoelastic properties.
Result· Stiffness and stress relaxation were tunable over two orders of magnitude, with recyclability of printed structures.
Validation· Rheology, self-healing tests, extrusion printing, and primary human dermal fibroblast culture.

Wear of 17-4 PH Stainless Steel Patterned Surfaces Fabricated Using Selective Laser Melting

Michela Sanguedolce, Jurgita Zekonyte, Marco Alfano Applied Sciences 2021-10-08 10.3390/app11199317
单位: Department of Mechanical, Energy and Management Engineering, University of Calabria, Via P. Bucci, 87036 Rende, Italy
通讯: Marco Alfano @ Department of Mechanical and Mechatronics Engineering, University of Waterloo, 200 University Avenue West, Waterloo, ON
相关性: 该论文研究了选区激光熔化17-4PH不锈钢的摩擦磨损性能，未涉及生物医用植入物的应力屏蔽、多孔结构或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper does not address implant stress shielding, stiffness matching, osseointegration, or fatigue; it focuses on tribological wear of patterned steel surfaces without biomedical context.
动机· 探索选区激光熔化制备的17-4PH不锈钢表面纹理的摩擦学性能。
方法· 采用SLM制备蜂窝状纹理表面，通过球盘磨损实验测试摩擦系数。
结果· 基底样品摩擦系数低于纹理样品；润滑条件下减小棱柱间隙可降低摩擦系数。
验证· 通过非接触式测量和不同条件下的磨损实验验证表面形貌和摩擦系数。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the tribological performance of 17-4 PH stainless steel surfaces with hexagonal prism patterns fabricated by selective laser melting.
Method· Honeycomb-patterned surfaces with varying gap spacing and dimples were produced via SLM and characterized using non-contact profilometry; coefficient of friction was measured via ball-on-disk tests under dry and lubricated conditions.
Result· Baseline (as-produced) surfaces exhibited lower COF than textured ones; in lubricated conditions, reducing prism gap decreased COF, and dimples led to up to 30% COF reduction with larger gap spacing.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed through quantitative surface measurements and ball-on-disk wear tests under multiple loads, radii, and lubrication conditions.

Microstructure and properties evolution of selective laser melting Cu-10Sn alloy with various heat treatment conditions

Peng Yang, Dingyong He, Zhen Tan, Wei Shao, Hanguang Fu et al. 2021-10-08 10.1117/12.2601218
单位: Beijing Univ. of Technology (China)
通讯: Peng Yang @ Beijing Univ. of Technology (China)
相关性: 该论文研究SLM Cu-10Sn合金的热处理工艺，未涉及生物医学植入物或骨整合相关。
边界: No biomedical application, no porous structure, no stress shielding or stiffness matching, no fatigue testing, no surface roughness or osseointegration.
动机· 研究热处理参数对SLM Cu-10Sn合金显微组织和力学性能的影响。
方法· 通过SLM制备Cu-10Sn合金，并进行固溶和时效处理，进行拉伸测试和显微表征。
结果· 热处理使柱状晶转变为等轴晶，屈服强度下降但抗拉强度和延伸率提高。
验证· 通过拉伸试验和显微组织观察验证性能变化。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the influence of heat treatment parameters on the microstructure and mechanical properties of selective laser melted Cu-10Sn alloy.
Method· Cu-10Sn alloy was fabricated by SLM and subjected to solution and aging treatments, followed by tensile testing and microstructural characterization.
Result· Heat treatment transformed columnar grains to equiaxed grains, reducing yield strength while increasing ultimate tensile strength and elongation.
Validation· Validation was performed through experimental tensile tests and detailed microstructural analysis.

Motivation· To investigate the effect of process parameters on interfacial cracking in Ti/Al multi-material structures fabricated by SLM.

Validation· Experimental characterization via SEM, EDS, XRD, and microhardness testing.

相关性: 该论文研究钨的选区激光熔化工艺，不涉及生物医学植入物、应力遮挡或多孔结构。
边界: This paper is about SLM of tungsten and its alloys for non-biomedical applications, with no porous structure design, mechanical testing (fatigue/stiffness), or any biomedical context.
动机· 探索钨及其合金选区激光熔化工艺中几何形态、支撑结构和第二相对微观结构的影响。
方法· 通过不同几何形态、支撑结构和添加第二相（Cu/Cu10Sn）制备钨试样，并表征微观结构。
结果· 几何形态和支撑结构影响晶粒尺寸和位错密度，第二相添加显著细化晶粒并抑制裂纹。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how geometrical morphologies, support structures, and second phases affect the microstructure of tungsten fabricated by selective laser melting.
Method· W and W alloy specimens with varying geometries, support designs, and second phase additions (Cu or Cu10Sn) were prepared by SLM and characterized.
Result· Grain size and crack density are strongly influenced by heat dissipation; adding a second phase refines grains and suppresses cracking.
Validation· Microstructural validation via SEM and EBSD analysis.

边界: This paper does not address biomedical implants, porous structures, stress shielding, or osseointegration; it focuses on welding of an aluminum alloy for non-biomedical applications.

Method· Autogenous and overlapped lap welding were performed, followed by T5, T6, and annealing heat treatments.
Result· T6 solution treatment led to spheroidization of eutectic Si, while T5 aging increased microhardness.
Validation· Microstructural characterization and microhardness measurements were conducted.

Primary stability and PES/WES evaluation for immediate implants in the aesthetic zone: a pilot clinical double-blind randomized study.

Arturo Sanchez-Perez, Ana I Nicolas-Silvente, Carmen Sanchez-Matas, Silvia Molina-García, Carlos Navarro-Cuellar et al. Scientific reports 2021-10-08 10.1038/s41598-021-99218-8
单位: Department of Periodontology, Medicine and Dentistry Faculty, CEIR Campus Mare Nostrum, University of Murcia, Murcia, Spain.
通讯: Ana I Nicolas-Silvente @ School of Dentistry, CEIR Campus Mare Nostrum, University of Murcia, Murcia, Spain. ainicolas@um.es.
相关性: 该论文研究的是美学区即刻种植体的稳定性和美学评分，与髋关节植入物及股骨柄无关。
边界: This paper does not quantify hip implant stress shielding, micromotion, or contact mechanics in the femur; it concerns dental implants only.
动机· 评估种植体设计对即刻种植稳定性和美学效果的影响
方法· 20颗两种设计的种植体随机植入上颌前牙，测量ISQ、骨水平和PES/WES评分
结果· 两种设计在稳定性和美学评分上无显著差异，均获得良好骨结合
验证· 临床随机双盲试验，使用Wilcoxon-Mann-Whitney检验分析
English summary & terms
Motivation· To assess whether implant design affects primary stability, secondary stability, bone level, and aesthetic outcomes in immediate dental implants.
Method· Twenty implants with two different designs were randomly placed in the anterior maxilla without grafting, and ISQ, bone level, and PES/WES were evaluated.
Result· No significant differences were found between designs; both achieved adequate osseointegration and stability.
Validation· A pilot double-blind randomized clinical trial with statistical testing using Wilcoxon-Mann-Whitney.
