Motivation· To reduce the human annotation cost for learning image-to-semantics mapping in sim-to-real policy transfer for vision-based robotics.
Method· Proposes pair augmentation using the transition function in the simulator and active learning to minimize required labeled data.
Result· The approach reduces annotation cost without sacrificing transfer performance and outperforms the baseline without annotation.
Validation· Validated through simulation experiments comparing annotation cost and task performance.
