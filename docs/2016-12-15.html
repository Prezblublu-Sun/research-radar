Method· Clinical and laboratory examinations were performed on 56 patients, and statistical analysis identified significant risk factors.
Result· Lower limb venous pathology, inflammatory markers, thrombocytosis, prolonged surgery, and cement use were associated with DVT.
Validation· Clinical patient data with statistical analysis.
