Selective laser melting-wire arc additive manufacturing hybrid fabrication of Ti-6Al-4V alloy: Microstructure and mechanical properties

Xuezhi Shi, Shuyuan Ma, Changmeng Liu, Qianru Wu, Jiping Lu et al. Materials Science and Engineering A 2016-12-15 10.1016/j.msea.2016.12.065
单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, Beijing Institute of Technology, Beijing 100081, China
通讯: Changmeng Liu @ School of Mechanical Engineering, Beijing Institute of Technology, Beijing 100081, China
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V合金的增材制造，但未涉及多孔结构、应力屏蔽或生物医学植入物设计。
边界: This paper does not address porous implant design, stress shielding, fatigue performance, or osseointegration; it focuses on hybrid SLM-WAAM manufacturing rather than LPBF-only biomedical implants.
动机· 研究混合增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的微观结构与力学性能
方法· 结合选区激光熔化与电弧增材制造
结果· 混合制造件具有良好力学性能，各向异性较弱
验证· 通过拉伸测试和显微组织分析验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the microstructure and mechanical properties of Ti-6Al-4V alloy fabricated by a hybrid selective laser melting and wire arc additive manufacturing process.
Method· The hybrid process combines SLM for fine features and WAAM for bulk deposition, followed by heat treatment.
Result· The hybrid fabricated material exhibits comparable tensile strength and ductility to wrought Ti-6Al-4V, with slight anisotropy.
Validation· Validation was performed through tensile testing, microhardness measurement, and microstructural characterization via SEM and EBSD.

相关性: 该论文研究了航空用镍基超合金的增材制造工艺，与生物医学植入物方向无关。
边界: This paper does not involve biomedical implants, porous structures for osseointegration, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or surface roughness characterization relevant to biomedical AM.

English summary & terms
Motivation· This work compares two additive manufacturing processes, selective laser melting and laser metal deposition, for processing Inconel 625 superalloy.
Method· Powder characterization and process parameter optimization were performed to achieve maximum density and hardness.
Result· Selective laser melting produced higher hardness and dimensional accuracy, while laser metal deposition offered higher build-up rate.
Validation· Validation was conducted through density and hardness measurements on fabricated specimens.

Uncertainty quantification of thermal conductivities from equilibrium molecular dynamics simulations

Zuyuan Wang, Salar Safarkhani, Guang Lin, Xiulin Ruan arXiv preprint 2016-12-15 link
相关性: 该论文专注于分子动力学模拟中热导率的不确定性量化，与有限元代理建模或植入物生物力学设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, expensive simulation sampling efficiency, or any constraints/optimization relevant to implant biomechanics design.
动机· 理解分子动力学预测热导率的不确定性来源
方法· 通过固体氩、硅和锗的平衡分子动力学模拟结合格林-久保公式量化热导率不确定性
结果· 发现相对不确定度遵循通用平方根关系，并提出基于期望误差和置信水平的模拟参数选择指南
验证· 不同材料体系和模拟条件下的统计分析验证关系普适性
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand and quantify the uncertainty in thermal conductivities predicted by equilibrium molecular dynamics simulations.
Method· Performed EMD simulations with Green-Kubo formula for solid argon, silicon, and germanium, and analyzed the effects of simulation parameters on uncertainty.
Result· Identified a universal square-root scaling of relative uncertainty with total simulation time and correlation time upper limit, and derived a formula linking error bound, confidence level, and simulation parameters.
Validation· Statistical analysis across multiple materials and conditions confirmed the universal trend and the proposed formula.

Can Low Level Laser Therapy Benefit Bone Regeneration in Localized Maxillary Cystic Defects? - A Prospective Randomized Control Trial.

Ahmed Abbas Zaky, Hanaa Mohamed Mohamed El Shenawy, Tarek Abdel Hamed Harhsh, Mahmoud Shalash, Noha Mohamed Ismael Awad Open access Macedonian journal of medical sciences 2016-12-15 10.3889/oamjms.2016.140
单位: National Institute of Laser Enhanced Sciences, Cairo University, Cairo, Egypt.
通讯: Noha Mohamed Ismael Awad @ Oral Surgery and Medicine Department, National Research Center, Cairo, Egypt. [推断]
相关性: 这篇论文是关于低水平激光治疗对骨再生的临床研究，与增材制造、多孔结构或植入物力学无关。
边界: No additive manufacturing, no porous structure design, no mechanical testing, no implant fabrication or fatigue analysis.
动机· 评估低水平激光治疗对颌骨囊性缺损骨形成的效果。
方法· 随机对照试验，16名患者分为激光组和对照组，术后激光照射。
结果· 90天后激光组骨密度显著高于对照组。
验证· 临床随机对照试验。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the effect of low-level laser therapy on bone formation in maxillary cystic defects after enucleation.
Method· A prospective randomized controlled trial with 16 patients, laser group received diode laser irradiation postoperatively for two weeks.
Result· At day 90, the laser group showed significantly higher bone density changes compared to the control group.
Validation· Clinical randomized controlled trial.
