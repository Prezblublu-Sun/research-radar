Manufacturing of graded titanium scaffolds using a novel space holder technique

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Yunhui Chen, Damon Kent, Michael Bermingham, Ali Dehghan‐Manshadi, Gui Wang et al. Bioactive Materials 2017-07-18 10.1016/j.bioactmat.2017.07.001
单位: Queensland Centre for Advanced Materials Processing and Manufacturing (AMPAM), The University of Queensland, St. Lucia, 4072, Australia
通讯: Yunhui Chen @ Queensland Centre for Advanced Materials Processing and Manufacturing (AMPAM), The University of Queensland, St. Lucia,
相关性: 该论文涉及多孔钛支架的制造，但未涉及LPBF、疲劳测试或应力屏蔽分析。
边界: This paper uses powder metallurgy and space holder technique, not LPBF; no mechanical testing beyond basic compression, no fatigue, no stress shielding or osseointegration evaluation.
动机· 优化钛植入体的力学和生物学性能，通过梯度孔隙结构实现。
方法· 采用糖粒作为空间保持剂，与钛粉压制后水溶去除，再烧结制备梯度多孔钛。
结果· 所得多孔钛具有设计一致的孔隙率和孔径梯度，界面过渡平滑，孔隙连通性好。
验证· 光学显微镜和SEM观察孔隙形态、结构和连通性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To optimize both mechanical and biological properties of titanium implants by introducing graded porosity.
Method· A powder metallurgy approach using sugar pellets as space holders, compacted with titanium powder, removed by water dissolution, and sintered to create porous scaffolds.
Result· The porous titanium exhibited well-controlled graded porosities and pore sizes with smooth transitions and high interconnectivity.
Validation· Morphology and pore structure were characterized by optical microscopy and SEM.
