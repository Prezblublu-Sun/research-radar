Motivation· Conventional 3D bioprinting fails to provide sufficient shear stress for mechanotransduction when using low-viscosity bioinks.

Dislocation incidence and risk factors following direct anterior primary total hip arthroplasty: a consecutive, single-surgeon cohort

Peyman Mirghaderi, Mohammad‐Taha Pahlevan‐Fallahy, Hesan Rezaee, Alireza Moharrami, Hadi Ravanbod et al. BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders 2025-05-05 10.1186/s12891-025-08683-z
单位: Sports Medicine Research Center, Neuroscience Institute, Tehran University of Medical Sciences, Tehran, Iran
通讯: Peyman Mirghaderi @ Sports Medicine Research Center, Neuroscience Institute, Tehran University of Medical Sciences, Tehran, Iran
相关性: 该论文仅关注临床脱位率和风险因素，未涉及股骨假体的生物力学分析或应力遮挡。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain, micromotion, or implant-bone interface mechanics; it is a purely clinical retrospective study without any biomechanical modeling or FEA.
动机· 确定直接前入路全髋置换术后脱位的发生率及风险因素。
方法· 回顾性分析1204例THA患者的影像和临床数据，统计脱位率并识别风险因素。
结果· 脱位率2.57%，DDH诊断、特定假体类型、小杯/小头、Lewinnek安全区外角度为风险因素。
验证· 基于大样本临床队列的回顾性统计分析。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine the incidence and risk factors for dislocation after primary total hip arthroplasty via the direct anterior approach.
Method· A retrospective analysis of 1204 consecutive THA cases was performed, extracting demographic, implant, and radiographic data to identify dislocation risk factors using SPSS.
Result· The dislocation rate was 2.57%; DDH diagnosis, Wagner Cone/SL stems, cup size <52 mm, head size <34 mm, and anteversion/inclination outside Lewinnek safe zone were significant risk factors.
Validation· Clinical validation through a large consecutive cohort with minimum five-year follow-up.

相关性: 该论文仅关注对侧全髋关节置换术的临床发生率，未涉及任何生物力学建模或植入物设计。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, no FEA, no implant design, no stress shielding or micromotion analysis.
动机· 明确初次单侧全髋置换术后对侧置换的长期发生率。
方法· 系统综述与荟萃分析，对21项研究进行Kaplan-Meier分析。

Motivation· To determine the long-term likelihood of contralateral total hip arthroplasty after primary unilateral index THA.
Method· Systematic review and meta-analysis of 21 studies with Kaplan-Meier analysis weighted by sample size.
Result· The likelihood of contralateral THA was 17.8% at 5 years and 22.7% at 10 years.
Validation· Meta-analysis of clinical studies with competing risk analysis.
