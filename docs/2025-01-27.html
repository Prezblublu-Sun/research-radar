Evidential Physics-Informed Neural Networks

High FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Hai Siong Tan, Kuancheng Wang, Rafe McBeth arXiv preprint 2025-01-27 link
相关性: 该论文提出的证据物理信息神经网络（EPINN）将不确定性量化（UQ）集成到PINN中，可迁移至生物力学植入物设计的代理建模，解决昂贵仿真的采样效率问题。
边界: This paper does not apply the method to biomedical implant design or biomechanics FEA, nor does it handle multi-objective constraints or patient-specific geometries.
动机· 现有PINN缺乏可靠的不确定性量化，影响科学发现的可信度。
方法· 基于证据深度学习框架，将PDE残差和数据损失项重参数化为先验超参数函数，并加入KL散度正则化。
结果· 相比贝叶斯PINN，对噪声更敏感，边界条件保持更好，经验覆盖概率更接近名义值。
验证· 在1D和2D非线性微分方程逆问题上进行数值基准测试。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Existing Physics-Informed Neural Networks lack reliable uncertainty quantification, limiting their trustworthiness in scientific discovery.
Method· The proposed Evidential PINN recasts PDE residual and data-fitting loss terms as functions of hyperparameters of an evidential prior, and incorporates a KL divergence regularizer between inverse-gamma distributions.
Result· Compared to Bayesian PINNs, the model shows higher sensitivity to data noise, better preservation of boundary conditions, and empirical coverage probabilities closer to nominal levels.
Validation· Validation is performed on inverse problems of 1D and 2D nonlinear differential equations using numerical benchmarks.

动机· 现有数据驱动方法难以处理Wiener系统输出非线性和Hammerstein系统有限维基函数依赖。

Validation· Numerical benchmarks demonstrate the superiority of the proposed prediction and control algorithms.

Jiaru Zhang, Zesong Wang, Hao Wang, Tao Song, Huai-an Su et al. arXiv preprint 2025-01-27 link
相关性: 该论文关注移动设备上DNN训练的能量估计，与生物力学FEA替代模型或植入物设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on energy estimation for on-device DNN training, not on surrogate modeling for FEA, biomechanics, or implant design.
动机· 移动设备上DNN训练能耗高，需准确估计以优化调度。
方法· 利用层能量可加性，用高斯过程模型逐层学习能耗并累加。
结果· MAPE降低30%，并成功指导能量感知剪枝减少50%能耗。

Method· Exploiting layer-wise energy additivity, Gaussian Process models are fitted to per-layer measurements to estimate total energy.
Result· The method reduces MAPE by up to 30% and guides energy-aware pruning achieving 50% energy reduction.
Validation· Extensive experiments on multiple real-world platforms with various DNN models.
