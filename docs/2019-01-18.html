Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only numerical_benchmark UQ

单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering  and  Wilton E. Scott Institute for Energy Innovation, Carnegie Mellon University, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania 15213, United States
通讯: Venkatasubramanian Viswanathan @ Department of Mechanical Engineering  and  Wilton E. Scott Institute for Energy Innovation, Carnegie Mellon University,
相关性: 该论文关注分子振动频率的不确定性量化，与生物力学有限元代理模型无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on quantum chemistry uncertainty quantification for molecular vibrations, not on surrogate modeling for expensive finite element simulations in biomechanics or implant design.
动机· 准确预测分子振动频率对光谱识别和反应热力学至关重要。
方法· 利用贝叶斯误差估计泛函量化DFT预测谐振动频率的不确定性。
结果· 不确定性估计能覆盖多种泛函的计算结果，弯曲/扭转模式不确定性更高。
验证· 在10个小分子、10个稀有气体二聚体和S22数据集上验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Accurate prediction of molecular vibrational frequencies is critical for identifying spectroscopic signatures and reaction thermodynamics.
Method· A method is developed to quantify uncertainty in DFT-predicted harmonic vibrational frequencies using the Bayesian error estimation functional with van der Waals correction.
Result· Uncertainty estimates bound self-consistent calculations from six exchange-correlation functionals, with bending/torsional modes showing higher uncertainty.
Validation· Validation is performed on 10 small molecules, 10 rare-gas dimers, and the S22 dataset of molecular complexes.
