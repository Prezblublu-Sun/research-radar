A Novel Optimization Layout Method for Clamps in a Pipeline System

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Peixin Gao, Jiwu Li, Jingyu Zhai, Tao Yang, Qingkai Han Applied Sciences 2020-01-04 10.3390/app10010390
单位: School of Electromechanical and Automotive Engineering, Yantai University, Yantai 264005, China
通讯: Jingyu Zhai @ School of Mechanical Engineering, Dalian University of Technology, Dalian 116024, China
相关性: 该论文将代理模型与遗传算法结合用于管道夹具布局优化，其方法可迁移至生物力学植入物设计中的约束优化问题。
边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, stress shielding, or any biomedical context; it focuses on pipeline vibration reduction.
动机· 减少管道系统中夹具布局的振动问题
方法· 结合Sobol全局敏感性分析、神经网络代理模型和遗传算法优化夹具位置与方向
结果· 优化方法显著降低了管道系统的振动响应
验证· 通过典型管道夹具实例的仿真与实验对比验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce vibration in pipeline systems by optimizing the layout of supporting clamps.
Method· A surrogate model based on neural networks is built from finite element data, with global sensitivity analysis (Sobol) to identify influential clamp positions, and a genetic algorithm optimizes clamp position and orientation.
Result· The proposed optimization method significantly reduces vibration displacement and stress in the pipeline system.
Validation· The method is validated by comparing simulation results with experimental measurements on a typical pipeline with clamps.
