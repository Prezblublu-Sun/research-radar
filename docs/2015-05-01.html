Gait training while carrying loads in the early post-operative period improves muscle strength after total hip arthroplasty

Manabu Nankaku, Kuroda Yutaka, Nishimura Jun, Shuichi Matsuda Japanese Physical Therapy Association 2015-05-01 10.14900/cjpt.2014.0027
单位: Rehabilitation Unit, Kyoto University Hospital
通讯: Manabu Nankaku @ Rehabilitation Unit, Kyoto University Hospital
相关性: 该论文关注全髋关节置换术后的康复训练，未涉及植入物力学或应力屏蔽分析。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, no implant design, no FEA, no stress/strain analysis, no micromotion quantification.
动机· 探讨术后早期负重行走训练对髋关节置换患者肌力的影响。
方法· 将患者分为负载训练组和常规训练组，比较肌力恢复。
结果· 负载训练组肌力改善更显著。
验证· 临床随机对照试验。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate whether early gait training with loads improves muscle strength after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Patients were assigned to a load-carrying gait training group or a conventional group, and muscle strength was compared.
Result· The load-carrying group showed significantly greater muscle strength improvement.
Validation· Randomized controlled clinical trial with patient outcome measures.
