Lloyd Roffe, Anthony J. FitzPatrick, Geoffrey W. Rodgers, Tim B. F. Woodfield, Gary J. Hooper Journal of Orthopaedic Research® 2016-10-14 10.1002/jor.23458
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery and Musculoskeletal Medicine; University of Otago Christchurch; 4345 Christchurch New Zealand
通讯: Gary J. Hooper @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery and Musculoskeletal Medicine; University of Otago Christchurch; 4345 Christchurch New
相关性: 该论文研究陶瓷对陶瓷髋关节假体的异响来源，不涉及股骨柄应力遮挡或微动分析。
边界: No quantification of proximal femur stress/strain, no micromotion modeling, no intact vs implanted femur comparison, and no FE boundary conditions.
动机· 探究陶瓷对陶瓷髋关节异响是否来自锥部连接。
方法· 对82例患者进行体内声发射监测，并对5例翻修假体进行体外测试。
结果· 体内异响频率与体外锥部连接频率不匹配，异响主要来自关节面。
验证· 体内外声发射频率对比及假体磨损分析。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine whether squeaking in ceramic-on-ceramic total hip arthroplasty originates from the trunnion morse taper connection or the articular surface.
Method· Acoustic emissions were recorded in vivo from 82 patients with implant problems and in vitro from five retrieved ceramic-on-ceramic components during controlled motions.
Result· In vivo squeak frequencies (0.8 kHz) matched the bearing surface range (0.2 kHz) rather than the trunnion taper range (22.5 kHz), indicating the taper does not directly cause audible squeaking.
Validation· Comparison of in vivo and in vitro acoustic frequency spectra combined with wear analysis of retrieved implants.
