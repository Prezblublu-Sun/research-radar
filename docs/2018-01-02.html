3D Bioprinting of Self‐Standing Silk‐Based Bioink

Low AI for Bioprinting openalex Peripheral Background only animal Exp. validation
Zhaozhu Zheng, Jianbing Wu, Meng Liu, Heng Wang, Chunmei Li et al. Advanced Healthcare Materials 2018-01-02 10.1002/adhm.201701026
单位: National Engineering Laboratory for Modern Silk College of Textile and Clothing Engineering Soochow University Suzhou 215123 People's Republic of China
通讯: Xiaoqin Wang @ National Engineering Laboratory for Modern Silk College of Textile and Clothing Engineering Soochow University Suzhou 2  |  David L. Kaplan @ Department of Biomedical Engineering Tufts University 4 Colby Street Medford MA 02155 USA
相关性: 这篇论文专注于丝素/PEG生物墨水的材料学与生物学验证，完全没有涉及AI或机器学习在生物打印中的应用。
边界: No AI/ML integration at any bioprinting stage; purely material and biological characterization without data-driven modeling or autonomous decision.
动机· 开发一种自支撑的丝素/PEG生物墨水用于3D打印组织工程支架。
方法· 混合丝素与PEG诱导β-折叠形成物理交联水凝胶。

Validation· In vitro cell culture and in vivo subcutaneous implantation in mice confirmed biocompatibility and cell survival for at least 6 weeks.
