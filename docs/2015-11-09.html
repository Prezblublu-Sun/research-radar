Reliability Analysis With Model Uncertainty Coupling With Parameter and Experiment Uncertainties: A Case Studyof 2014 Verification and Validation Challenge Problem

Zhimin Xi, Ren‐Jye Yang Journal of Verification Validation and Uncertainty Quantification 2015-11-09 10.1115/1.4031984
单位: Mem. ASME Industrial and Manufacturing Systems Engineering, University of Michigan–Dearborn, 4901 Evergreen Road, Dearborn, MI 48128 e-mail:
通讯: Zhimin Xi @ Mem. ASME Industrial and Manufacturing Systems Engineering, University of Michigan–Dearborn, 4901 Evergreen Road, Dearbo
相关性: 该论文提出的结合模型不确定性的可靠性分析方法可迁移至植入物设计中的不确定性量化与优化。
边界: This paper does not apply the method to biomedical implant design or biomechanics FEA, nor does it address surrogate modeling for expensive simulations.
动机· 解决2014年V&V挑战问题中模型不确定性与参数、实验不确定性的耦合。
方法· 提出基于copula的偏差近似方法，结合贝叶斯参数估计、EDR方法及U-pooling度量。

英文摘要与术语

Method· A copula-based bias approximation approach is proposed, integrating Bayesian parameter estimation, eigenvector dimension reduction (EDR) for UQ, and U-pooling metric for model bias calibration.
Result· Model uncertainty is successfully incorporated into reliability analysis, demonstrated on the challenge problem.
Validation· Validated on the numerical benchmark problem from Sandia National Laboratory.

边界: This paper focuses on coarse-grained molecular simulations of protein aggregation, not on finite element analysis, surrogate modeling for structural mechanics, or implant design.
动机· 开发适用于块共聚蛋白质序列聚集模拟的粗粒化方法

Motivation· To develop a coarse-graining method suitable for simulating aggregation and phase separation of block-copolymeric protein sequences.
Method· The CAMELOT algorithm combines Boltzmann inversion, nonlinear regression, and Gaussian process Bayesian optimization to parameterize system-specific coarse-grained models from all-atom simulations.
Result· The coarse-grained model accurately reproduces all-atom simulation results and shows that adsorption of N17 on polyQ tracts creates a patchy colloid architecture promoting linear aggregates.
Validation· Validation is performed by direct comparison of coarse-grained simulation results with all-atom simulation data.
