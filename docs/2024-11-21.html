High-throughput bioprinting of spheroids for scalable tissue fabrication.

单位: Department of Biomedical Engineering, Penn State University, University Park, PA, USA.; The Huck Institutes of the Life Sciences, Penn State University, University Park, PA, USA.
通讯: Ibrahim T Ozbolat @ Department of Biomedical Engineering, Penn State University, University Park, PA, USA. ito1@psu.edu.; The Huck Institute
相关性: 该论文将AI控制的数字喷嘴阵列用于高通量球体生物打印，直接相关于AI在生物打印流程中的应用。
边界: This paper does not embed AI into a specific bioprinting workflow stage beyond digital control, lacks autonomous decision-making, and does not advance from observation to autonomous optimization.
