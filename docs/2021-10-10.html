通讯: Xudong Zhi @ Key Lab of Smart Prevention and Mitigation of Civil Engineering Disasters of the Ministry of Industry and Information Te
相关性: 该论文研究的是3D打印折纸启发的蜂窝结构，而非面向生物医学植入物的多孔结构，且未涉及应力遮挡或骨整合。
边界: No link to stress shielding or stiffness matching for biomedical implants; no fatigue testing, surface roughness, or osseointegration assessment.
动机· 提出一种新型折纸启发的周期性多孔结构，并研究其准静态压缩性能。
方法· 通过SLM制造多层金属试样，结合实验、数值模拟和理论分析研究压缩变形与能量吸收。

Validation· Experimental results were validated against numerical simulations and theoretical predictions, showing good agreement.
