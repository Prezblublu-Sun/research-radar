相关性: 该论文提出的主动学习Kriging模型用于混合可靠性分析，其处理随机-区间变量的方法可迁移至植入物设计中的不确定性量化。

Motivation· To improve the efficiency of approximating the bounds of the limit-state surface in hybrid reliability analysis with random and interval variables.

Validation· The method is validated through five numerical case studies comparing with existing methods.
