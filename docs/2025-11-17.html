Real-time distortion prediction in metallic additive manufacturing via a physics-informed neural operator approach

High FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Read now numerical_benchmark
Mingxuan Tian, Haochen Mu, Donghong Ding, Mengjiao Li, Yuhan Ding et al. arXiv preprint 2025-11-17 link
相关性: 该论文提出的物理信息神经算子方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模，解决实时预测问题。
边界: This paper focuses on metal additive manufacturing distortion prediction, not on implant biomechanics or stress shielding.
动机· 金属增材制造中实时变形预测需求迫切，传统数值模拟与机器学习方法存在局限。
方法· 提出物理信息深度算子网络-循环神经网络(PIDeepONet-RNN)，融合热传导方程作为软约束。
结果· 模型在z和y方向最大绝对误差分别为0.9733 mm和0.2049 mm，误差集中在熔池区域。
验证· 使用实验验证的有限元数据集进行训练和测试。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Real-time distortion prediction is critical for defect control in metal additive manufacturing, but numerical simulations are computationally expensive and conventional ML models fail to capture spatiotemporal features.
Method· A physics-informed neural operator (PIDeepONet-RNN) is proposed, incorporating the heat conduction equation as a soft constraint to decouple thermo-mechanical fields and map thermal history to distortion.
Result· The model achieves high accuracy with max absolute errors of 0.9733 mm (z-direction) and 0.2049 mm (y-direction), with errors concentrated in the molten pool region.
Validation· The model is trained and tested on datasets generated from experimentally validated finite element simulations.
