单位: Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Northeastern University, 360 Huntington Ave, Boston, MA 02115, USA
通讯: Jamshid Sourati @ Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Northeastern University, 360 Huntington Ave, Boston, MA 02115, USA
相关性: 该论文关注主动学习中的批量查询策略，与FEA代理建模或植入物设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address surrogate modeling, finite element analysis, or biomechanical design; it focuses on active learning for classification tasks.
动机· 减少标注成本，高效选择批量样本进行标注。
方法· 提出基于互信息的批量主动学习框架，降低计算复杂度。

Motivation· To minimize labeling cost by efficiently selecting a batch of samples for annotation in active learning.

Result· The proposed method achieves efficient active learning across multiple datasets.
Validation· Evaluated on several benchmark classification datasets.

Low Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Background only animal Exp. validation
Daeho Hong D-Scholarship@Pitt (University of Pittsburgh) 2016-02-05 link
相关性: 该论文研究可降解镁锌合金的腐蚀性能与生物相容性，未涉及髋关节假体应力遮挡或微动分析。
边界: This paper focuses on biodegradable Mg-Zn alloy corrosion and biocompatibility for orthopedic fixation, not on hip implant stress shielding, micromotion, or FEA of femoral stems.
动机· 解决永久金属植入物应力遮挡和可降解聚合物强度不足的问题
方法· 开发Mg-Zn-Zr合金并添加Sr、Ce微合金化，表征微观结构与腐蚀性能
结果· Mg-Zn-Zr合金的晶界析出相引发腐蚀，添加Sr、Ce可改善耐蚀性
验证· 大鼠股骨骨折模型植入验证生物安全性
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To address stress shielding from permanent metal implants and insufficient strength of biodegradable polymers for orthopedic fixation.
Method· Mg-Zn-Zr alloys were developed with Sr and Ce microalloying, and characterized for phase, microstructure, in-vitro corrosion, and cytocompatibility.
Result· Mg-Zn intermetallic precipitates at grain boundaries initiated corrosion, while Sr and Ce addition improved corrosion resistance.
Validation· In-vivo biosafety was demonstrated using a rat femoral fracture model with Mg-Zn-Sr-Zr pins.

动机· 探究翻转课堂对学生深度学习策略的影响

单位: Elective Surgery Centre, Silkeborg Regional Hospital, Silkeborg, Denmark
通讯: Lone Ramer Mikkelsen @ Elective Surgery Centre, Silkeborg Regional Hospital, Silkeborg, Denmark
相关性: 该论文研究髋关节置换术后的康复训练，与髋关节假体的生物力学分析无关。
边界: No biomechanical modeling, no stress shielding quantification, no FEA, no implant design or validation.
动机· 描述全髋关节置换后的渐进抗阻训练负荷进展和疼痛反应。
方法· 对随机对照试验干预组数据进行二次分析，记录训练负荷和疼痛评分。
结果· 患者能承受负荷显著增加，疼痛未见恶化，部分有短期疼痛反应。

Motivation· To characterize the progression of training load and pain response during early progressive resistance training after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Secondary analysis of intervention group data from a randomized controlled trial, tracking load and Visual Analog Scale pain scores over 10 weeks.
Result· Patients achieved substantial load increases (67%–166%) without overall pain exacerbation, though some experienced transient pain increases.
Validation· Data from a randomized controlled trial with 37 participants.
