Viktor Zaverkin, David Holzmüller, Henrik Christiansen, Federico Errica, Francesco Alesiani et al. arXiv preprint 2023-12-03 10.1038/s41524-024-01254-1
相关性: 该论文关注分子动力学中的主动学习与不确定性，与生物力学有限元代理建模无直接关联。
边界: No FEA surrogate modeling, no implant/biomechanics context, no constraint handling or multi-objective optimization.
动机· 高效构建训练机器学习原子间势的紧凑数据集。
方法· 用能量不确定性偏置分子动力学，同时捕获外推区域和稀有事件。
结果· 相比传统分子动力学，生成更准确表示构型空间的势函数。
验证· 在丙氨酸二肽和MIL-53(Al)体系上进行模拟验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Efficiently constructing a concise yet comprehensive dataset for training machine-learned interatomic potentials remains an under-explored problem.
Method· Uncertainty-biased molecular dynamics, driven by the MLIP's energy uncertainty and bias stress via automatic differentiation, simultaneously captures extrapolative regions and rare events.
Result· Uncertainty-biased MD yields MLIPs with similar or better accuracy than ensemble-based methods at lower computational cost.
Validation· Validated on alanine dipeptide and MIL-53(Al) systems, showing improved configurational space representation over conventional MD.

通讯: Yuting Lv @ College of Mechanical and Electronic Engineering, Shandong University of Science and Technology, Qingdao, China

边界: This paper does not address any biomedical implant application, stress shielding, LPBF mechanical testing, fatigue, or osseointegration.

Result· The HIP-SLMed composite exhibited low weight loss due to formation of multiple protective oxide films rich in Al, Ti, and Cr.
Validation· Hot corrosion test with weight loss measurements and microstructural analysis.
