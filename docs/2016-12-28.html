英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the accuracy of acetabular cup anteversion measurement between digital radiography and CT in THA patients.
Method· Anteversion angles were measured from CT scans using Amira software and from digital radiographs using TraumaCAD software in 62 post-THA patients.
Result· Radiographic measurements significantly underestimated anteversion compared to CT, with greater underestimation at larger angles.
Validation· Clinical patient data comparing two imaging modalities.
