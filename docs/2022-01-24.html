相关性: 该论文关注CoCr颗粒的局部毒性，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡或微动等生物力学问题。
边界: This paper is a systematic review of local toxicity of CoCr debris from MoM implants, with no quantification of stress shielding, micromotion, or any biomechanical FE modeling of the femur-implant interface.
动机· 系统评价CoCr颗粒对局部软组织的毒性效应及其临床意义。

结果· CoCr离子降低细胞活力、诱导DNA损伤、触发炎症反应，导致骨溶解和软组织损伤。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To systematically review the latest evidence on the local toxic effects of cobalt-chrome debris from metal-on-metal hip implants on soft tissues.
Method· A systematic review following PRISMA guidelines, screening PubMed, Embase, and Cochrane Library for 27 included studies.
Result· CoCr ions reduce cell viability, induce DNA damage, and trigger cytokine secretion leading to inflammation, osteolysis, and soft tissue damage.
Validation· Validation based on 3 human ex vivo studies and 24 preclinical studies (21 in vitro, 3 animal models).
