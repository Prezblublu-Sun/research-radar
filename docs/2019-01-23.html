通讯: Roberto Benavente @ Department of Civil Engineering, Universidad Católica de la Santísima Concepción, Concepción, Chile
相关性: 该论文将贝叶斯不确定性量化应用于地震断层反演，与生物力学植入物设计的代理建模无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on geophysical inverse problems with positivity constraints, not on surrogate modeling for expensive finite element simulations in biomechanics or implant design.
动机· 地震有限断层反演中需要量化不确定性，但现有方法效率低。
方法· 采用对数正态先验和坐标变换实现正约束，通过高斯近似快速估计后验不确定性。
结果· 高斯近似与MCMC采样结果一致，不确定性随深度和方位角间隙增大而增加。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To enable rapid uncertainty quantification in finite fault inversion, which is typically ill-posed and only yields a single regularized solution.
Method· A Bayesian inversion framework with positivity constraints via log-space transformation, using a Gaussian approximation to the posterior derived from the Hessian of the objective function.
Result· The Gaussian approximation yields reasonable uncertainty estimates compared to MCMC, with slip uncertainties increasing with depth and large azimuthal gaps.
Validation· Validation through synthetic experiments and application to the 2015 Illapel earthquake using surface wave data.
