通讯: Rami Al‐Dirini @ Medical Device Research Institute, College of Science and Engineering, Flinders University, Adelaide 5043, Australia e-m  |  Mark Taylor @ Medical Device Research Institute, College of Science and Engineering, Flinders University, Adelaide 5043, Australia e-m
相关性: 该论文直接评估了骨水泥型股骨柄的尺寸和位置变化对初始稳定性的影响，与用户的研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not compare intact vs implanted femur for stress shielding analysis, and does not report proximal femur stress/strain in Gruen zones.
动机· 评估骨水泥型股骨柄对手术尺寸和位置变化的生物力学稳健性。

Motivation· To evaluate the biomechanical robustness of a contemporary cementless femoral stem to surgical variations in sizing and positioning.

Result· The Corail stem exhibited stable primary stability with micromotion and interfacial strains mostly below thresholds, though retroverted and medial positions increased peak micromotion by up to 50%.
Validation· Validation was performed by comparing simulated micromotion and strains against established thresholds (150 μm and 7000 με) from literature.

Motivation· To investigate the incidence and risk factors of complications following spinal anesthesia in total hip arthroplasty.
