Local approximate Gaussian process regression for data-driven constitutive laws: Development and comparison with neural networks

Jan Niklas Fuhg, Michele Marino, Nikolaos Bouklas arXiv preprint 2021-05-07 10.1016/j.cma.2021.114217
相关性: 该论文直接针对FEA代理建模中的计算效率问题，提出的laGPR方法可迁移至植入物/生物力学设计中材料本构的高效替代。
边界: The paper does not apply the surrogate method to implant or biomechanical design, nor does it address constraints or multi-objective optimization explicitly.
动机· 多尺度力学模拟计算成本高，需高效数据驱动代理模型。
方法· 提出局部近似高斯过程回归(laGPR)替代神经网络，基于子集数据训练局部模型预测应力。
结果· laGPR在三维超弹性问题中比ANN更准确可靠。
验证· 通过三维超弹性有限元数值模拟验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Multiscale mechanics simulations often incur prohibitive computational costs, necessitating efficient data-driven surrogate models.
Method· A local approximate Gaussian process regression (laGPR) framework is developed, training local GP models on data subsets to predict stress outputs from strain inputs.
Result· laGPR demonstrates superior accuracy and reliability compared to artificial neural networks for three-dimensional hyperelastic finite-strain problems.
Validation· The method is validated through numerical simulation of a finite-strain 3D hyperelastic problem within a finite element scheme.

Elastically-isotropic open-cell minimal surface shell lattices with superior stiffness via variable thickness design

Qingping Ma, Lei Zhang, Junhao Ding, Shuo Qu, Jin Fu et al. arXiv preprint 2021-05-07 10.1016/j.addma.2021.102293
相关性: 该论文提出利用变厚度设计实现弹性各向同性的TPMS点阵，可通过刚度匹配潜在应用于减少植入物应力遮挡。
边界: No biomedical context, no fatigue testing, no osseointegration or surface roughness characterization.
动机· 均匀厚度的TPMS点阵通常刚度各向异性，需设计各向同性点阵以提升性能。
方法· 提出基于应变能优化的变厚度TPMS点阵设计方法，实现弹性各向同性。
结果· 优化后的N14点阵刚度接近Hashin-Shtrikman上限，且各向异性显著降低。
验证· 采用微激光粉末床熔融制备316L点阵，并通过准静态压缩实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Uniform-thickness TPMS shell lattices are often elastically anisotropic; isotropic lattices are needed for superior mechanical performance.
Method· A strain energy-based optimization algorithm is proposed to design variable-thickness TPMS shell lattices achieving isotropic stiffness.
Result· Optimized N14 lattices maintain over 90% of the Hashin-Shtrikman upper bound for bulk modulus and significantly reduce elastic anisotropy.
Validation· Samples were fabricated by micro laser powder bed fusion from 316L stainless steel and tested under quasi-static compression.

A semigroup method for high dimensional elliptic PDEs and eigenvalue problems based on neural networks

Haoya Li, Lexing Ying arXiv preprint 2021-05-07 10.1016/j.jcp.2022.110939
相关性: 该方法可转移至生物力学有限元代理模型，但未涉及植入物设计或不确定性量化。
边界: It does not address expensive simulation sampling, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification, or biomechanics applications.
动机· 高效求解高维椭圆型偏微分方程及特征值问题。
方法· 利用半群算子将原方程转化为变分问题，并用神经网络参数化求解。
结果· 无需混合二阶导数计算，且边界条件被自动满足。
验证· 通过数值算例验证方法的有效性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To efficiently solve high-dimensional elliptic PDEs and eigenvalue problems that are challenging for traditional numerical methods.
Method· Reformulate the PDEs as variational problems using semigroup operators and parameterize the solution with neural networks, avoiding mixed second-order derivatives and penalty methods for boundary conditions.
Result· The method correctly enforces boundary conditions without penalty terms and yields accurate solutions, with a primal-dual approach for eigenvalue problems that outperforms existing solvers in certain cases.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on standard PDE and eigenvalue problems demonstrate the method's accuracy and efficiency.

Validation· Numerical simulation based on model Hamiltonian; no experimental validation.
