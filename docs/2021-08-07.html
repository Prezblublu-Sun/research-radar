Effect of the Process Atmosphere Composition on Alloy 718 Produced by Laser Powder Bed Fusion

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Camille Pauzon, Andreas Markström, Sophie Dubiez-Le Goff, Eduard Hryha Metals 2021-08-07 10.3390/met11081254
单位: Department of Industrial and Materials Science, Chalmers University of Technology, 41296 Göteborg, Sweden
通讯: Camille Pauzon @ Department of Industrial and Materials Science, Chalmers University of Technology, 41296 Göteborg, Sweden
相关性: 该论文研究LPBF工艺气氛对合金718成分的影响，未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡、疲劳或生物医学应用。
边界: No porous structure, no stress shielding or stiffness matching, no mechanical testing (fatigue or static), no surface roughness or osseointegration, and no biomedical context.
动机· 研究LPBF工艺气氛对合金718成分的影响
方法· 对比氩气和氮气气氛下LPBF成型合金718的成分变化
结果· 氮气气氛导致氮含量略高，氧含量显著降低
验证· 通过粉末原料分析和热力学计算验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of argon vs. nitrogen processing atmosphere on the chemical composition of Alloy 718 fabricated by laser powder bed fusion.
Method· Alloy 718 samples were produced by LPBF under argon and nitrogen atmospheres, and the oxygen and nitrogen contents were measured and compared with the feedstock powder.
Result· Nitrogen atmosphere led to a slight increase in nitrogen content (≈50 ppm) while oxygen content decreased by about 30% (≈150 ppm) regardless of gas type.
Validation· Thermodynamic and kinetic calculations supported the experimental observations, and powder feedstock analysis confirmed the origin of inclusions.

单位: Feevale University, Brazil
通讯: Sandra Raquel Kunst @ Feevale University, Brazil
相关性: 该论文研究阳极氧化钛在模拟体液中的腐蚀性能，不涉及增材制造、多孔结构或力学测试，与用户研究方向无关。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), no porous structure for stress shielding or stiffness matching, and no osseointegration assessment beyond surface oxide characterization.
动机· 评估阳极氧化钛在健康和炎症模拟体液中的耐腐蚀性
方法· 在含HF的H3PO4电解液中阳极氧化钛，进行电化学腐蚀测试
结果· 阻挡型氧化物比多孔氧化物具有更好的耐腐蚀性和生物材料特性

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the corrosion resistance of anodized titanium in simulated body fluid under healthy and inflammatory conditions.
Method· Titanium samples were anodized in H3PO4 electrolyte with or without HF, then subjected to electrochemical corrosion tests.
Result· Barrier-type oxides exhibited superior corrosion resistance and biomaterial characteristics compared to porous oxides.
Validation· SEM, AFM, and electrochemical corrosion tests in SBF with healthy and inflammatory conditions.

通讯: Loreto Monsalve-Guil @ Comprehensive Dentistry for Adults and Gerodontology, Master in Implant Dentistry, Faculty of Dentistry, University of S [推断]

Motivation· To evaluate the effect of sandblasted and acid-etched surface treatments on osseointegration of dental implants.
Method· Two types of sandblasted-etched titanium implants with different roughness were inserted into femoral condyles of rabbits.
Result· Both surfaces achieved favorable osseointegration with similar bone-implant contact percentages.
Validation· Histological and histomorphometric analyses at 12 weeks post-implantation.

Sensitivity and uncertainty quantification of neutronic integral data in the TRIGA Mark II research reactor

Exclude FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Ignore none UQ
M. Makhloul, H. Boukhal, El Mahjoub Chakir, T. El Bardouni, M. Lahdour et al. Nuclear Engineering and Technology 2021-08-07 10.1016/j.net.2021.08.003
单位: Radiations and Nuclear Systems Laboratory, University Abdelmalek Essaadi, Faculty of Sciences of Tetouan, Morocco
通讯: M. Makhloul @ Radiations and Nuclear Systems Laboratory, University Abdelmalek Essaadi, Faculty of Sciences of Tetouan, Morocco
相关性: 该论文研究核反应堆中子数据的不确定度量化，与生物力学有限元代理建模无关。
边界: This paper focuses on nuclear reactor neutronics, not on surrogate modeling for biomechanical FEA or implant design.
动机· 量化TRIGA Mark II反应堆中子核数据的不确定度
方法· 使用MCNP和NJOY99进行敏感性和协方差分析
结果· U235(n,f)反应不确定度偏差最大达2275 pcm
验证· 基于ENDF/B-VII.0等核数据库的数值比较
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify the sensitivity and uncertainty of neutronic integral data for the TRIGA Mark II research reactor.
Method· Sensitivity analysis using MCNP6 with ENDF/B-VII.0, ENDF/B-VII.1, and JENDL-4.0 libraries, and uncertainty quantification via NJOY99 covariance processing.
Result· The highest uncertainty deviations were found for U235(n,f) reaction (up to 2275 pcm), while O-16 and U-238 reactions showed small differences.
Validation· Comparison of sensitivity and uncertainty results across three nuclear data libraries.
