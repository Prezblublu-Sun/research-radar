Physically consistent and uncertainty-aware learning of spatiotemporal dynamics

High FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project simulation_case UQ
Qingsong Xu, Jonathan L Bamber, Nils Thuerey, Niklas Boers, Paul Bates et al. arXiv preprint 2025-10-23 link
相关性: 该论文提出的物理一致神经算子（PCNO）和不确定性量化方法（DiffPCNO）可直接迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模，解决昂贵仿真采样效率与约束处理问题。
边界: This paper does not apply the method to biomedical implant or biomechanics FEA, nor does it address patient-specific design or contact mechanics constraints.
动机· 现有机器学习方法忽略物理定律且无法量化时空预测的不确定性。
方法· 提出物理一致神经算子（PCNO）和扩散模型增强版（DiffPCNO），在傅里叶空间投影满足守恒律。
结果· 在湍流、洪水、大气预报中实现高保真、物理一致且带不确定性的预测。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Existing machine learning methods for spatiotemporal dynamics neglect physical laws and fail to quantify prediction uncertainty.
Method· A physics-consistent neural operator (PCNO) enforces conservation laws via a projection layer in Fourier space, and a diffusion model-enhanced variant (DiffPCNO) quantifies uncertainty using a consistency model.
Result· PCNO and DiffPCNO achieve high-fidelity, physically consistent, and uncertainty-aware forecasts across turbulent flow, flood, and atmospheric systems.
Validation· Validation is performed on numerical simulation cases including turbulent flow, flood modeling, and atmospheric forecasting.

边界: This paper does not involve bioprinting, bioink, or any biomedical context; it focuses on general robot-arm trajectory generation.
动机· 机器人臂轨迹规划需考虑运动结构，现有序列模型常产生无效轨迹。

Motivation· Robotic arm trajectory planning requires motion structure awareness, which sequence models often ignore, leading to invalid or inefficient executions.
Method· A Path-based Transformer is proposed, encoding robot motion with a 3D grid representation and constraint-masked decoding to enforce lattice-adjacent moves and workspace bounds.
Result· The method achieves up to 97.5% reach and 92.5% pick success on an xArm Lite 6 digital twin, with 86.7% end-to-end success across 60 language-specified tasks.
Validation· Physical robot experiments with a depth-camera digital twin validate the approach in controlled and cluttered scenes.
