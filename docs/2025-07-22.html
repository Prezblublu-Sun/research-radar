Significance of Primary Stability in Nonimmediately Loaded Dental Implants: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis.

Nahal Razaghi, Thomas Gill, Anuya Patankar, Aviva Petrie, Shakeel Shahdad The International journal of prosthodontics 2025-07-22 10.11607/ijp.9115
相关性: 这篇论文研究的是牙种植体的初期稳定性，与髋关节植入物和股骨柄的应力遮挡、微动或有限元分析无关。
边界: This paper is a systematic review of dental implant primary stability, not related to proximal femur stress shielding, micromotion, or patient-specific finite element modeling for hip implants.
动机· 探讨牙种植体初期稳定性对骨整合的影响。
方法· 系统综述和Meta分析，检索数据库并纳入测量植入扭矩和稳定系数的研究。
结果· 未发现高植入扭矩或高稳定系数能提高非即刻负载种植体存活率的证据。
验证· 基于临床试验数据的Meta分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To determine whether primary stability measured by insertion torque or implant stability quotient influences osseointegration of nonimmediately loaded dental implants.
Method· Systematic review and meta-analysis of studies reporting insertion torque and/or implant stability quotient at placement with survival data up to one year.
Result· No evidence that insertion torque ≥35 Ncm or implant stability quotient ≥60 improves implant survival compared to lower values.
Validation· Meta-analysis of 7 studies with individual implant data, extracting odds ratios from clinical survival outcomes.
