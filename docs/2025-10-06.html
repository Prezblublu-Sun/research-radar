Motivation· To improve multirotor performance for specific tasks through automated design optimization.
Method· A framework combining reinforcement learning, Bayesian optimization, and CMA-ES to explore motor pose configurations.
Result· Optimized designs outperform conventional configurations in agile waypoint navigation, including fully actuated designs.
Validation· Real-world flight tests confirm sim-to-real transferability for one optimized design.
