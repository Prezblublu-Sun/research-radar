单位: Institute of Functional Interfaces, Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, Eggenstein-Leopoldshafen, Germany.; Institute of Process Engineering in Life Sciences, Section IV: Biomolecular Separation Engine
通讯: Jürgen Hubbuch @ Institute of Functional Interfaces, Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, Eggenstein-Leopoldshafen, Germany.; Institute of [推断]
相关性: 该论文通过多实验室重复性测试和自动图像分析，直接评估了挤出式生物打印的可重复性，为生物打印标准化提供数据支持。
边界: This paper does not embed AI into a bioprinting workflow for autonomous decision-making; it focuses on reproducibility and standardization using automated image analysis.
动机· 生物打印可重复性差，缺乏多实验室标准化数据。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Bioprinting lacks empirical data on reproducibility across laboratories, hindering standardization and clinical translation.
Method· A round robin test with 12 labs printed standardized objects, and three independent groups applied automated image analysis for quality assessment.
Result· Automated image analysis proved robust for quality assurance, but operator intervention due to missing equipment automation significantly affected reproducibility.
Validation· Consistency across three independent image analysis workflows validated the methodology.
