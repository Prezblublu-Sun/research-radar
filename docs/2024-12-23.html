相关性: 该论文研究肩关节置换术，与髋关节植入物方向无关。
边界: This paper focuses on shoulder arthroplasty, not hip implant or femoral stem, and does not address proximal femur stress shielding or hip implant micromotion.
动机· 探究肩关节置换术中髓腔填充率对假体稳定性及骨变形的影响。
方法· 对40例尸体肩标本进行CT扫描，植入短柄和标准柄假体，进行循环加载测试并记录位移与骨变形。

Result· Lower fill ratios preserved native-like bone loading in the medial calcar, but short stems with low fill ratios showed higher risk of tilt and subsidence.
Validation· Cadaveric biomechanical testing with optical tracking of implant micromotion and bone deformation.
