Comparative analysis of residual stress distributions in a braking pedal from a historical motorbike manufactured via selective laser melting and wire arc additive manufacturing

Lukáš Kudrna, František Fojtík, Jindřich Kozák, Petra Váňová, Quoc-Phu Ma et al. The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology 2025-04-28 10.1007/s00170-025-15593-w
单位: Department of Machine and Industrial Design, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, VSB-Technical University of Ostrava, 708 00, Ostrava, Czech Republic
通讯: Lukáš Kudrna @ Department of Machine and Industrial Design, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, VSB-Technical University of Ostrava, 708
相关性: 该论文涉及SLM增材制造和残余应力分析，但应用于摩托车踏板修复而非生物医学植入物。
边界: This paper does not address porous structure, stress shielding, fatigue, surface roughness, or osseointegration in a biomedical context.
动机· 比较SLM与WAAM制造历史摩托车踏板残余应力分布。
方法· 采用钻孔法和切割法实测，结合FEA模拟。
结果· WAAM残余应力低于SLM，归因于层间回火效应。
验证· 半破坏性钻孔实验与有限元仿真对比。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare residual stress distributions in a historical motorbike pedal fabricated by SLM and WAAM for restoration purposes.
Method· Experimental measurement using semi-destructive hole drilling and destructive sectioning, complemented by FEA simulations in Ansys and Simufact Additive.
Result· WAAM-produced pedals exhibit significantly lower residual stresses than SLM ones due to inter-layer tempering during deposition.
Validation· Physical tests (hole drilling and sectioning) validated the simulated residual stress distributions.

Preoperative radiographical degenerative lumbar spondylolisthesis does not affect clinical outcomes of total hip arthroplasty

Takuya Sunagawa, Yaichiro Okuzu, Yutaka Kuroda, Toshiyuki Kawai, Y. Morita et al. Archives of Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery 2025-04-28 10.1007/s00402-025-05876-y
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Kyoto University Graduate School of Medicine, 54 Shogoin-Kawaharacho, Sakyo-Ku, Kyoto, 606-8507, Japan
通讯: Takuya Sunagawa @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Kyoto University Graduate School of Medicine, 54 Shogoin-Kawaharacho, Sakyo-Ku, Kyoto
相关性: 该论文为临床回顾性研究，未涉及髋关节假体的生物力学建模或应力遮挡分析，与用户研究方向无关。
边界: This paper does not quantify femoral stress shielding, micromotion, or involve any FEA or implant biomechanics; it is a retrospective clinical study on THA outcomes with no mechanical analysis.
动机· 探讨术前退变性腰椎滑脱对全髋关节置换术临床结果的影响。
方法· 回顾性分析术前有和无腰椎滑脱的THA患者术后临床评分。
结果· 腰椎滑脱对THA术后临床结果无显著影响。
验证· 基于临床随访数据的统计分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To determine whether preoperative degenerative lumbar spondylolisthesis affects clinical outcomes after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective comparison of clinical scores between THA patients with and without preoperative spondylolisthesis.
Result· Preoperative lumbar spondylolisthesis did not significantly affect postoperative clinical outcomes.
Validation· Statistical analysis of patient-reported outcome measures from clinical follow-up.
total hip arthroplasty · 全髋关节置换术lumbar spondylolisthesis · 腰椎滑脱clinical outcomes · 临床结果
