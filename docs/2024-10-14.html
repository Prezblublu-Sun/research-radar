Bayesian Optimisation with Unknown Hyperparameters: Regret Bounds Logarithmically Closer to Optimal

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark
Juliusz Ziomek, Masaki Adachi, Michael A. Osborne arXiv preprint 2024-10-14 link
相关性: 该论文提出了一种处理超参数未知的贝叶斯优化方法，可用于加速基于有限元分析的植入物设计优化。
边界: This paper does not apply to FEA or biomechanics problems; only validates on synthetic and general real-world benchmarks.
动机· 贝叶斯优化中长度尺度超参数选择不当会导致模型误判和过探索问题。
方法· 提出长度尺度平衡(LB)算法，聚合多个不同长度尺度的代理模型，动态添加小尺度候选值。
结果· LB的累积遗憾界相较于最优长度尺度仅多对数因子，优于现有方法。
验证· 在合成和真实基准测试上进行了实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Bayesian optimization (BO) suffers from misspecified length scale hyperparameters, causing over-exploration and slow convergence.
Method· This work introduces Length scale Balancing (LB), which aggregates multiple base surrogate models with varying length scales and adaptively adds smaller scales.
Result· The cumulative regret bound of LB is only a logarithmic factor away from the oracle BO with optimal length scale, outperforming prior methods.
Validation· The method is validated on synthetic functions and real-world benchmarks, demonstrating improved performance over A-GP-UCB and MLE.
