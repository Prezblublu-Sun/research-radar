Changing treatment of hip fractures in Finland

Oskari K. Leino, Nora Forsbacka, Inari Laaksonen, Keijo Mäkelä, Markus Matilainen et al. Archives of Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery 2024-08-28 10.1007/s00402-024-05462-8
单位: Department of Orthopaedics and Traumatology, Turku University Hospital, University of Turku, Turku, Finland. oskari.leino@utu.fi
通讯: Oskari K. Leino @ Department of Orthopaedics and Traumatology, Turku University Hospital, University of Turku, Turku, Finland. oskari.lein
相关性: 与用户研究方向无关，仅涉及芬兰髋部骨折的治疗趋势流行病学。
边界: No biomechanical modeling, FEA, implant design, stress shielding, or micromotion analysis; purely clinical registry study.
动机· 调查芬兰髋部骨折治疗趋势是否随证据改变。
方法· 分析1997-2018年全国登记数据，计算骨折发生率和手术方式。
结果· 老年组骨折发生率下降，但手术方式显著变化，内固定减少，关节置换增加。
验证· 使用国家行政登记数据，统计分析差异。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine whether clinical practice in hip fracture treatment in Finland has evolved in line with accumulating evidence.
Method· A nationwide register study analyzing operatively treated hip fractures from 1997 to 2018 in Finland, calculating incidence rates per operation type.
Result· Incidence of hip fractures remained relatively constant except in the elderly, but substantial shifts occurred: internal fixation decreased while cemented hemiarthroplasty and hybrid total hip arthroplasty increased.
Validation· Comparison of treatment proportions between 1997 and 2018 using national administrative data with statistical significance testing.
