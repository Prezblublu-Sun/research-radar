相关性: 该论文为临床回顾性研究，未涉及任何生物力学建模或有限元分析。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, no FEA, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no implant design or contact mechanics.
动机· 评估双动全髋假体治疗偏瘫股骨颈骨折的临床效果。
方法· 回顾性分析18例偏瘫股骨颈骨折患者行双动全髋置换术的临床资料。
结果· 术后无脱位、松动等并发症，Harris评分显著改善。
验证· 临床随访及X线评估。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the clinical outcomes of dual mobility total hip arthroplasty in hemiplegic patients with femoral neck fracture.
Method· A retrospective analysis of 18 hemiplegic patients who underwent dual mobility total hip replacement from 2014 to 2016.
Result· No dislocations or loosening occurred, and Harris hip scores improved significantly postoperatively.
Validation· Clinical follow-up and radiographic evaluation.
