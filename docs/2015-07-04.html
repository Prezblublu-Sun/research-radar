Multilevel Quasi-Monte Carlo Methods for Lognormal Diffusion Problems

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Frances Y. Kuo, Robert Scheichl, Christoph Schwab, Ian H. Sloan, Elisabeth Ullmann arXiv preprint 2015-07-04 link
相关性: 该方法可迁移至生物力学有限元不确定性量化，但论文本身不涉及植入物设计或生物医学应用。
边界: This paper is purely methodological on a subsurface flow PDE and does not involve implant mechanics, structural FEA, or biomedical context.
动机· 提高对数正态扩散问题不确定性量化的计算效率。
方法· 提出多层级准蒙特卡洛（MLQMC）结合有限元离散，并给出构造参数。
结果· MLQMC方法优于多层级MC和单层QMC，成本达到O(ε^{-θ})，θ<2。
验证· 通过数值实验验证收敛性和计算增益。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce computational cost of uncertainty quantification for lognormal diffusion problems arising in subsurface flow.
Method· A multilevel estimator based on randomly shifted Quasi-Monte Carlo lattice rules is combined with finite element discretization, with constructive parameters for variance reduction.
Result· The multilevel QMC method achieves an ε-error with cost O(ε^{-θ}) where θ<2, outperforming multilevel MC and single-level QMC even for non-smooth problems.
Validation· Numerical experiments confirm the theoretical convergence rates and demonstrate practical computational gains.
