Biocompatibility and Cellular Behavior of TiNbTa Alloy with Adapted Rigidity for the Replacement of Bone Tissue

单位: Departamento de Citología e Histología Normal y Patológica, Universidad de Sevilla, 41009 Sevilla, Spain
通讯: Mercè Giner @ Departamento de Citología e Histología Normal y Patológica, Universidad de Sevilla, 41009 Sevilla, Spain  |  E. Chicardi @ Departamento de Ingeniería y Ciencia de los Materiales y del Transporte, Escuela Politécnica Superior, Calle Virgen de Á

边界: No LPBF fabrication, no fatigue testing, no as-built surface morphology analysis.
动机· 评估TiNbTa合金作为骨替代材料的力学与生物功能。

结果· TiNbTa合金在20-30%孔隙率时适合替代皮质骨，80%时适合替代松质骨，且生物相容性良好。
验证· 通过压缩力学测试和体外成骨细胞粘附增殖实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate TiNbTa alloy as a prosthetic biomaterial for bone replacement focusing on rigidity and biocompatibility.
Method· TiNbTa foams with varying porosity were fabricated and tested for mechanical properties and in vitro osteoblast response.
Result· TiNbTa foams with 20-30% porosity match cortical bone rigidity and promote cell adhesion, while 80% porosity suits trabecular bone replacement.
Validation· Validation included mechanical compression testing and in vitro cell adhesion/proliferation assays.

单位: School of Materials Engineering, Shanghai University of Engineering Science, Shanghai 201620, China
通讯: Pan Ma @ School of Materials Engineering, Shanghai University of Engineering Science, Shanghai 201620, China  |  Yandong Jia @ Laboratory for Microstructures, Institute of Materials, Shanghai University, Shanghai 200444, China
相关性: 该论文研究铝合金SLM工艺，与用户关注的生物医学植入物、多孔结构、应力屏蔽等方向无关。
边界: This paper focuses on an aluminum alloy for non-biomedical applications and does not address porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue, osseointegration, or any biomedical context.
动机· 研究基板加热对SLM成型Al-20Si-5Fe-3Cu-1Mg合金微观组织和性能的影响
方法· 通过SLM制备不同热历史样品，进行微观表征和室温压缩测试
结果· 基板加热使组织粗化，强度降低但塑性提升至20%
验证· 压缩力学测试验证微观结构变化对力学性能的影响
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates the effect of substrate plate heating on the microstructure and mechanical properties of a high-silicon aluminum alloy processed by selective laser melting.
Method· Samples were fabricated via SLM under different thermal conditions (as-prepared, heat-treated, and with substrate plate heating) and characterized by microscopy and compression testing.
Result· Substrate plate heating coarsened the microstructure, reducing compressive strength from 862 MPa to 474 MPa but improving plastic deformation to 20%.
Validation· Room temperature compression tests were used to correlate microstructural changes with mechanical performance.
