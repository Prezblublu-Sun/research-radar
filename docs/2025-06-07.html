Finite element biomechanical evaluation of internal fixation methods for metacarpal shaft fractures under multiple factors.

Chun-Lin Zhang Computer methods in biomechanics and biomedical engineering 2025-06-07 10.1080/10255842.2025.2516766
单位: Department of Hand Surgery, Beijing Jishuitan Hospital, Beijing, China.
相关性: 该论文研究掌骨骨折内固定方法，与髋关节植入物及股骨柄力学方向无关。
边界: This paper focuses on metacarpal shaft fracture fixation, not on hip implant stress shielding, micromotion, or femoral stem biomechanics.
动机· 比较掌骨骨折不同内固定方法的生物力学性能。
方法· 使用有限元分析对比克氏针、钢板和髓内螺钉。
结果· 髓内螺钉在正常骨中低载荷下稳定，骨质疏松骨中更优。
验证· 仅有仿真计算，无实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate and compare the biomechanical stability of K-wires, plates, and intramedullary screws for metacarpal shaft fracture fixation.
Method· Finite element analysis was performed on models of metacarpal fractures with normal and osteoporotic bone to simulate three fixation methods.
Result· Intramedullary screws provided comparable stability to plates under low loads in normal bone and superior performance in osteoporotic bone.
Validation· The study relied solely on computational simulation without experimental validation.
