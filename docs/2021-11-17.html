English summary & terms
Motivation· The study investigates pulsed electrical stimulation as a method to enhance osseointegration by improving cell survival on titanium implants.
Method· MC3T3-E1 preosteoblasts were cultured on Ti6Al4V implants and subjected to asymmetric charge-balanced pulsed electrical stimulation at varying amplitudes and frequencies for 72 hours.
Result· Pulsed electrical stimulation improved cell survival and collagen production in a dose-dependent manner, suggesting potential for peri-implant bone healing.
Validation· In vitro cell culture experiments with MC3T3-E1 cells on titanium alloy substrates.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To assess the risk of rapidly destructive hip disease associated with intra-articular corticosteroid injections, based on recent findings.
Method· A commentary reviewing existing literature and analyzing the dose-response relationship between corticosteroid injections and adverse hip outcomes.
Result· High-dose (≥80 mg) and multiple injections significantly increase the risk of rapidly destructive hip disease, while low-dose injections show lower risk.
Validation· No experimental validation; the commentary interprets clinical study data.
