Failure analysis of an in-vivo fractured patient-specific Ti6Al4V mandible reconstruction plate fabricated by selective laser melting

Qimin Shi, Yi Sun, Shoufeng Yang, Jeroen Van Dessel, Heinz­-Theo Lübbers et al. Engineering Failure Analysis 2021-03-12 10.1016/j.engfailanal.2021.105353
单位: OMFS-IMPATH Research Group, Department of Biomedical Sciences, KU Leuven (University of Leuven) & Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, UZ Leuven (University Hospitals Leuven), Kapucijnenvoer
通讯: Yi Sun @ OMFS-IMPATH Research Group, Department of Biomedical Sciences, KU Leuven (University of Leuven) & Department of Oral and  |  Shoufeng Yang @ Faculty of Engineering and Physical Sciences, University of Southampton, Southampton SO17 1BJ, UK
相关性: 该论文直接涉及LPBF制造的Ti6Al4V生物医学植入物的体内断裂分析，与用户研究方向高度相关。
边界: Does not systematically link porous structure to stress shielding or stiffness matching, and focuses on a single clinical failure case rather than a parametric mechanical study.

方法· 断口形貌观察、金相分析和有限元模拟

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the failure mechanism of a patient-specific Ti6Al4V mandible reconstruction plate fabricated by selective laser melting that fractured in vivo.
Method· Fractographic examination, metallurgical analysis, and finite element modeling were employed to identify the causes of fracture.
Result· Fatigue fracture was identified as the primary failure mode, with manufacturing defects and unfavorable loading conditions contributing to the premature failure.
Validation· The analysis was validated against the actual clinical fracture case, including medical imaging and retrieved implant examination.
