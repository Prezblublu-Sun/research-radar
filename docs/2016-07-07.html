Computation of Higher-Order Moments of Generalized Polynomial Chaos Expansions

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Éric Savin, Béatrice Faverjon arXiv preprint 2016-07-07 10.1002/nme.5505
相关性: 该论文提出了一种计算多项式混沌展开高阶矩的分析方法，可用于不确定量化，但未涉及有限元或生物力学应用。
边界: Does not involve finite element analysis, biomechanical modeling, or implant design; focuses on polynomial chaos moment computation without application to engineering simulation.
动机· 避免使用高斯求积计算多项式混沌高阶矩，提高效率。

Result· The analytical approach yields moments consistent with Gauss quadrature, offering an efficient alternative for uncertainty quantification.
Validation· Validation is performed by comparing the computed moments against Gauss quadrature results for test cases.
