Additive Manufacturing for Fabrication of Point-of-Care Therapies in Austere Environments.

Low AI for Bioprinting pubmed Peripheral Background only none
Jason Barnhill, Joel D Gaston, Paul I Deffenbaugh, Linzie Wagner, Peter C Liacouras et al. Military medicine 2023-07-22 10.1093/milmed/usad007
单位: Department of Chemistry and Life Science, United States Military Academy, West Point, NY 10996, USA.; Department of Radiology and Radiological Sciences, Uniformed Services University of the Health Sci
通讯: Vincent B Ho @ Department of Radiology and Radiological Sciences, Uniformed Services University of the Health Sciences, Bethesda, MD 20 [推断]
相关性: 该论文涉及生物打印在偏远地区的应用，但未包含AI方法，与你的研究方向（AI+生物打印）无关。
边界: No AI/ML integration at any bioprinting workflow stage, no experimental validation of AI models, no autonomous decision-making.
动机· 探索在偏远环境下利用3D打印制造医疗用品和组织的可行性。
方法· 使用坚固型3D打印机在非洲沙漠环境中打印手术工具、生物绷带和半月板组织。
结果· 成功打印了多种医疗用品，但无菌环境和运输时效仍是挑战。
验证· 通过实际打印和操作验证了打印机功能。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the feasibility of deploying additive manufacturing for on-demand fabrication of medical supplies and tissues in austere field environments.
Method· A ruggedized multi-tool 3D printer was used to fabricate surgical instruments, bioactive bandages, and human meniscal tissue from stem cell-laden bioinks in a sub-Saharan desert setting.
Result· The printer successfully produced a range of biomedical items, though sterile conditions were not achieved and delivery logistics posed challenges.
Validation· Functional validation was performed by printing and handling the fabricated objects on-site.

Exclude FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Ignore none
Han Liu, Hao Li, Xing Yao, Yubo Fan, Dewei Hu et al. arXiv preprint 2023-07-22 link
相关性: 该论文专注于3D医学图像分割中的冷启动主动学习，与FEA及代理模型方向无关。
边界: This paper focuses on active learning for medical image segmentation and does not address FEA surrogate modelling, simulation efficiency, uncertainty quantification, or implant/biomechanics design.
动机· 为了解决3D医学图像分割中初始标注样本选择的冷启动问题。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Addressing the cold-start problem in active learning for efficient annotation of 3D medical images.
Method· Evaluated six cold-start active learning strategies on five 3D medical image segmentation tasks.
Result· Cold-start active learning remains unsolved for 3D segmentation, though important trends were observed.
Validation· Benchmarked on public Medical Segmentation Decathlon datasets.
