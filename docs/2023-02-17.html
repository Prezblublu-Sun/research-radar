Properties Evaluations of Topology Optimized Functionally Graded Lattice Structures Fabricated by Selective Laser Melting

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Save for project mechanical_testing Exp. validation

单位: Institute of Manufacturing Engineering, Huaqiao University, Xiamen 361021, China
通讯: Yangli Xu @ Institute of Manufacturing Engineering, Huaqiao University, Xiamen 361021, China
相关性: 本文研究了面向骨科应用的功能梯度点阵结构的SLM制备与力学性能，涉及刚度匹配和能量吸收，但缺少疲劳与骨整合评估。
边界: This paper does not evaluate fatigue performance, surface roughness for osseointegration, or explicitly address stress shielding in a biomedical context.
动机· 设计并制造具有可调力学性能的梯度点阵结构，以满足不同应用场景的需求。
方法· 采用拓扑优化设计均匀与梯度点阵结构，并通过SLM技术制备，结合有限元与压缩实验评估性能。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To design and fabricate functionally graded lattice structures (FGLSs) with tailored mechanical properties for applications including orthopedics.
Method· Two uniform and two graded lattice designs were created via topology optimization, manufactured by selective laser melting (SLM), and evaluated through finite element simulations and compression tests.
Result· FGLSs exhibited layer-wise mixed deformation; energy absorption capacity increased with relative density; mathematical models predicted mechanical properties with deviations below 10%.
Validation· Experimental compression tests validated the finite element models and mathematical predictions, confirming accuracy within 10% error.
