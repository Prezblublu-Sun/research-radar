On Polynomial Chaos Expansion via Gradient-enhanced $\ell_1$-minimization

Ji Peng, Jerrad Hampton, Alireza Doostan arXiv preprint 2015-06-01 10.1016/j.jcp.2015.12.049
相关性: 该论文直接针对代理建模中的采样效率和不确定性量化，可迁移至植入物/生物力学的FEA代理建模。
边界: Does not handle constraints or multi-objective optimization, and examples are not in biomechanics/implant domain.
动机· 利用梯度信息提高多项式混沌展开的稀疏重建效率。
方法· 提出梯度增强的ℓ1最小化方法，结合导数测量矩阵的条件分析。
结果· 理论上证明导数信息几乎必然改善解恢复条件，数值实验显示计算成本降低。
验证· 通过三个数值算例（人工PCE、椭圆PDE、随机边界泊肃叶流）验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To accelerate sparse polynomial chaos expansion identification by incorporating derivative information in ℓ1-minimization.
Method· Proposes a gradient-enhanced ℓ1-minimization with appropriate normalization and theoretical analysis of measurement matrix coherence and null-space conditions.
Result· Demonstrates both theoretically and empirically that including derivative information almost-surely improves recovery conditions and reduces computational cost.
Validation· Validates on three numerical examples: a manufactured PCE, an elliptic PDE with random inputs, and plane Poiseuille flow with random boundaries.
