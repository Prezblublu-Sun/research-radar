Laser-Treated Titanium Implants

单位: *Scientific Director, Biomaterial Clinical and Histological Research Association, Pescara, Italy. †Biomaterial Clinical and Histological Research Association, Pescara, Italy. ‡Private Practice, Campob

相关性: 该论文研究了激光处理钛种植体表面的骨整合效果，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能，与用户研究方向的相关性有限。
边界: This paper does not cover porous structure design, stress shielding, LPBF fabrication, or fatigue testing; it focuses on laser surface treatment of machined implants.
动机· 比较激光处理与机械加工表面的骨整合强度和质量
方法· 在羊髂骨植入纵向分割的钛种植体，进行组织形态学和生物力学分析
结果· 激光处理表面的骨接触率和反向扭矩值显著高于机械加工表面

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the strength and quality of osseointegration between laser-treated and machined implant surfaces in an in vivo model.
Method· Customized titanium implants with split laser-treated and machined surfaces were inserted into sheep iliac crests, and histomorphometric and biomechanical analyses were performed after 8 weeks.
Result· Laser-treated surfaces exhibited approximately 3-fold higher reverse torque values and 30% greater bone-implant contact compared to machined surfaces.
Validation· In vivo animal study with histological and biomechanical measurements.

Uncertainty Quantification in Metamodel-Based Reliability Prediction

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project simulation_case UQ
Saideep Nannapaneni, Zhen Hu, Sankaran Mahadevan 2016-08-21 10.1115/detc2016-59225
单位: Vanderbilt University, Nashville, TN
通讯: Saideep Nannapaneni @ Vanderbilt University, Nashville, TN
相关性: 该论文提出了基于高斯过程元模型的可靠性分析不确定性量化框架，可迁移至植入物/生物力学设计中的代理模型不确定性评估。
边界: The paper does not apply the method to implant or biomechanics design, nor does it address multi-objective constrained optimization or patient-specific geometry.
动机· 解决元模型在可靠性分析中引入的统计与模型不确定性。
方法· 采用高斯过程元模型，结合辅助变量法和概率积分变换，系统纳入模型偏差项。
结果· 在悬臂梁和两杆支撑板案例中验证了所提框架的有效性。
验证· 通过两个机械系统数值案例进行验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify epistemic uncertainties (statistical and model) in metamodel-based reliability analysis for computationally expensive physics models.
Method· A framework using Gaussian process metamodels for limit-state modeling, incorporating model discrepancy via auxiliary variables and statistical uncertainty via probability integral transform sampling.
Result· The proposed approach effectively propagates metamodel and input distribution uncertainties in component and system reliability analyses, demonstrated on two mechanical systems.
Validation· Validated on a cantilever beam and a two-bar supported panel using numerical simulations.

相关性: 该论文研究各向异性对点阵结构优化的影响，但未涉及生物医学植入物、应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing of biomedical implants, no stress shielding or osseointegration analysis.
动机· 研究材料各向异性和构建方向对点阵结构优化设计的影响。
方法· 通过实验表征材料各向异性，扩展最优准则法进行点阵结构尺寸优化。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the influence of material anisotropy and build orientation on the optimized design of lattice structures fabricated by additive manufacturing.
Method· Material property characterization for compression and tension was performed on inkjet 3D printed specimens, and a generalized optimality criteria method was extended for optimal sizing of lattice structures with displacement, stress, and Euler buckling constraints.
Result· Anisotropy significantly affects the optimized designs, demonstrating the necessity of including anisotropic models in optimization to produce fabricable solutions.
Validation· Experimental compression and tension tests were conducted to characterize the anisotropic material properties of the inkjet 3D printed material.

边界: This paper focuses on injection mold design, not biomedical implants; no LPBF fabrication, mechanical testing, fatigue, or osseointegration.
动机· 轻量化且热效率高的注塑模具设计方法。
方法· 多相热机械拓扑优化，结合宏微观有限元分析。

Result· Significant mass reduction (e.g., 30.3%) is achieved with only minor sacrifices in thermal (3.5%) and mechanical (8.7%) performance.
Validation· The methodology is demonstrated through 2D and 3D numerical examples including an injection mold core.

Sebastian Oberst, Z Zhang, Graeme M. Campbell, Michael M. Morlock, Joseph C. S. Lai et al. UTS ePRESS (University of Technology Sydney) 2016-08-21 link
