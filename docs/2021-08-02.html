Motivation· To investigate the prevalence, risk factors, and complications of hyponatraemia following elective primary hip and knee arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective audit of 1000 consecutive elective arthroplasty patients analyzed postoperative serum sodium levels and associated factors using multivariate logistic regression.
Result· Postoperative hyponatraemia occurred in 21.7% of patients, with age, preoperative sodium, and fasting glucose as independent predictors.
Validation· Single-center retrospective audit without external validation.
