Porous titanium fiber mesh with tailored elasticity and its effect on stromal cells

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Save for project mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Evy Aerts, Jinmeng Li, Mies J. van Steenbergen, Tanika Degrande, John A. Jansen et al. Journal of Biomedical Materials Research Part B Applied Biomaterials 2020-01-14 10.1002/jbm.b.34556
单位: Biomaterials, Department of Dentistry, Radboud Institute for Molecular Life Sciences Radboud University Medical Center Nijmegen The Netherlands
通讯: X. Frank Walboomers @ Biomaterials, Department of Dentistry, Radboud Institute for Molecular Life Sciences Radboud University Medical Center
相关性: 该论文研究了多孔钛纤维网的刚度调控及其对骨髓基质细胞的影响，与用户关注的LPBF制造、应力屏蔽和骨整合方向直接相关。
边界: This paper does not involve LPBF fabrication, fatigue testing, or surface roughness characterization; it uses fiber sintering instead of additive manufacturing.
动机· 调控多孔钛纤维网的刚度以匹配骨组织，促进骨形成细胞行为。
方法· 制备九种不同孔隙率和纤维尺寸的钛纤维网，进行力学测试和细胞培养。
结果· 刚度可通过纤维参数调控，高孔隙率样品刚度接近皮质骨，且支持细胞增殖分化。
验证· 动态力学分析测试刚度，细胞实验验证生物相容性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To tailor the stiffness of porous titanium fiber mesh to match bone tissue and evaluate its effect on bone marrow stromal cells.
Method· Nine types of titanium fiber meshes with varying porosity (60-87%) and fiber size (22-50 μm) were fabricated and characterized mechanically and biologically.
Result· Stiffness decreased with larger fiber sizes and could be tuned; high-porosity meshes with 22 and 35 μm fibers achieved stiffness close to cortical bone, and all meshes supported cell growth and differentiation.
Validation· Dynamic mechanical analysis for stiffness measurement and in vitro cell culture assays for cytocompatibility.

边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, constraint handling, or implant/biomechanics design; it focuses on robust GP regression for general regression tasks with outliers.
