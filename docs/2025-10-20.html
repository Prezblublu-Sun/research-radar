相关性: 该论文采用条件扩散模型进行电池容量预测，与FEA代理模型或生物力学设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on battery capacity prediction and does not address finite element analysis, surrogate modeling for mechanical systems, constraint handling, or optimization in implant/biomechanics design.

Motivation· Accurate capacity prediction with uncertainty quantification is essential for reliable lithium-ion battery management under real-world conditions.
Method· A conditional diffusion U-Net with attention (CDUA) is proposed, integrating feature selection via Pearson correlation and XGBoost, and a noise predictor network for reconstruction.
Result· CDUA achieves a relative mean absolute error of 0.94% and a narrow 95% confidence interval width of 3.74% on real-world vehicle data.
Validation· The model is validated on real-world battery operation data from vehicles, demonstrating superior accuracy and robustness compared to existing methods.

边界: No quantification of proximal femur stress/strain in Gruen zones, no micromotion with contact/friction modeling, no patient-specific FE modeling, no comparison of intact vs implanted femur.

Validation· Clinical radiographic assessment with follow-up imaging.
