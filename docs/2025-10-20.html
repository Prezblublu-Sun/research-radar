Motivation· This study evaluates the mechanical stability of a modular antibiotic hip spacer during two-stage revision for prosthetic joint infection.
Method· A retrospective analysis of 18 patients measured changes in head-stem distance and thread engagement on postoperative radiographs.

Validation· Clinical radiographic assessment with follow-up imaging.
