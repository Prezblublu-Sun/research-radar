通讯: Ziyu Wang @ Dept. of Computer Science, University of Oxford#TAB#
相关性: 该论文提出的随机嵌入贝叶斯优化方法可扩展到高维问题，对用户的高维植入物设计优化具有潜在迁移价值。

结果· HOOS, JR具有高内部一致性、外部效度和反应性，且地板和天花板效应可接受。
验证· 在两个大型关节置换登记数据库中进行内部和外部验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To derive a concise, patient-relevant short-form survey for assessing outcomes after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Item reduction of the HOOS using patient interviews and Rasch modeling to produce a six-item instrument (HOOS, JR).
Result· The HOOS, JR demonstrates high internal consistency, moderate to excellent external validity, and very high responsiveness to THA.
Validation· Validated internally and externally using two large joint replacement registries (HSS and FORCE-TJR).
