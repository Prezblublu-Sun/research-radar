Motivation· To compare generic and specific health-related quality of life instruments in elderly patients after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Cross-sectional study administering SF-36 and WOMAC questionnaires to 88 elderly outpatients and evaluating ceiling/floor effects and internal consistency.
Result· Physical dimensions were most affected; SF-36 showed floor effects in physical and emotional aspects, while WOMAC showed ceiling effects in pain and stiffness.
Validation· Internal consistency assessed via Cronbach's alpha (>0.70 for both instruments).
