Response Surface Models for the Uncertainty Quantification of Eccentric Permanent Magnet Synchronous Machines

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only simulation_case UQ
Zeger Bontinck, Herbert De Gersem, Sebastian Schöps IEEE Transactions on Magnetics 2015-10-15 10.1109/tmag.2015.2491607
单位: Institut Theorie Elektromagnetischer Felder, Technische Universität Darmstadt, Darmstadt, Germany
通讯: Zeger Bontinck @ Institut Theorie Elektromagnetischer Felder, Technische Universität Darmstadt, Darmstadt, Germany
相关性: 该论文使用响应面模型加速电机有限元仿真中的不确定性量化，方法可迁移但领域完全不同。
边界: This paper applies surrogate modeling to electrical machine design, not to biomedical implant or biomechanics FEA; no contact mechanics, no structural optimization, no patient-specific geometry.
动机· 量化永磁同步电机转子偏心对反电动势的影响。
方法· 构建响应面模型替代有限元仿真，结合广义多项式混沌和蒙特卡洛方法。
结果· 响应面模型有效加速了二维和三维偏心情况下的不确定性量化。
验证· 通过有限元仿真结果对比验证响应面模型的精度。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify the effect of rotor eccentricity on the electromotive force in permanent magnet synchronous machines under uncertainty.
Method· A response surface model is constructed from finite-element simulations and combined with generalized polynomial chaos and Monte Carlo methods for uncertainty propagation.
Result· The response surface model significantly accelerates uncertainty quantification for both 2-D static and 3-D inclined eccentricity cases.
Validation· Accuracy of the response surface model is validated against full finite-element simulations.

Using Active Learning for Speeding up Calibration in Simulation Models

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only simulation_case
Mücahit Çevik, Mehmet Ali Ergün, Natasha K. Stout, Amy Trentham‐Dietz, Mark Craven et al. Medical Decision Making 2015-10-15 10.1177/0272989x15611359
单位: Department of Biostatistics and Medical Informatics, University of Wisconsin, Madison, WI, USA (MC)
通讯: Mücahit Çevik @ Department of Biostatistics and Medical Informatics, University of Wisconsin, Madison, WI, USA (MC)
相关性: 该论文展示了主动学习加速仿真校准，但应用于癌症模型而非生物力学或植入物设计。
边界: No FEA surrogate modeling, no implant biomechanics, no constraint handling, no uncertainty quantification.
动机· 加速癌症仿真模型校准过程，减少计算成本。
方法· 使用主动学习算法选择最有希望的参数组合进行仿真评估。
结果· 仅评估1.49%的参数组合即可找到所有有效组合。
验证· 在乳腺癌仿真模型上验证，与标准校准方法对比。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce the computational burden of calibrating cancer simulation models by minimizing the number of parameter combinations evaluated.
Method· An active learning algorithm iteratively selects parameter combinations likely to match observed data, using an artificial neural network as the learner.
Result· The method identified all 69 valid parameter combinations by evaluating only 5,620 out of 378,000 combinations (1.49%).
Validation· Demonstrated on the University of Wisconsin breast cancer simulation model, comparing against standard calibration results.
active learning · 主动学习calibration · 校准simulation model · 仿真模型parameter combination · 参数组合machine learning · 机器学习cancer simulation · 癌症仿真
active learning calibration simulation cancer model

Solenoidal filtering of volumetric velocity measurements using Gaussian process regression

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only numerical_benchmark Exp. validation UQ
I. Azijli, Richard P. Dwight Experiments in Fluids 2015-10-15 10.1007/s00348-015-2067-7
单位: TU Delft, Kluyverweg 1, 2629 HS, Delft, The Netherlands
通讯: I. Azijli @ TU Delft, Kluyverweg 1, 2629 HS, Delft, The Netherlands
相关性: 该论文涉及高斯过程回归在流体速度场滤波中的应用，与FEA代理建模或植入物设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on solenoidal filtering of velocity measurements in fluid dynamics, with no FEA surrogate modeling, no implant biomechanics, and no structural optimization or uncertainty quantification for solid mechanics.
动机· 消除体积速度测量中的非物理散度噪声
方法· 利用高斯过程回归强制速度场满足无散度约束
结果· SGPR方法在合成和实验数据上均优于其他滤波方法
验证· 合成数据与实验数据对比验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To remove spurious divergence from volumetric velocity measurements of incompressible flows by enforcing the solenoidal constraint.
Method· Gaussian process regression is formulated with a divergence-free kernel to produce analytically solenoidal velocity fields, incorporating measurement uncertainty and exploiting Toeplitz structure for efficiency.
Result· The proposed SGPR filter consistently yields more accurate velocity, vorticity, and pressure fields than existing solenoidal filters, and is robust to correlation length choice.
Validation· Validation is performed on two synthetic test cases and two experimental test cases, with pressure field accuracy verified against microphone measurements.
Gaussian process regression · 高斯过程回归solenoidal filtering · 无散度滤波volumetric velocity measurement · 体积速度测量divergence-free · 无散度uncertainty quantification · 不确定性量化Toeplitz matrix · 托普利兹矩阵pressure field reconstruction · 压力场重建
