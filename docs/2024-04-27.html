相关性: 该论文提出了一种新的贝叶斯神经网络不确定性量化方法，虽然未直接应用于FEA代理模型，但其方法可迁移到植入物设计中的不确定性评估。
边界: This paper does not address surrogate modeling for expensive simulations, lacks constraints handling, and does not demonstrate application to implant or biomechanics design.
动机· 解决贝叶斯神经网络中先验定义困难与计算效率低的问题

Motivation· Defining meaningful priors and ensuring computational efficiency in Bayesian neural networks remain challenging for complex real-world applications.
Method· The proposed Neural Adaptive Empirical Bayes (NA-EB) framework integrates implicit generative priors with variational inference and a gradient ascent algorithm for simultaneous hyperparameter selection and posterior approximation.

Validation· Validation is performed on two-spiral problem, regression tasks, 10 UCI datasets, MNIST, and CIFAR-10 classification.

Result· The interlayer remelting process yielded an average microhardness of 376 HV, tensile strength of 974 MPa, and elongation of 10.51%.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated through microhardness testing and room-temperature tensile testing.
