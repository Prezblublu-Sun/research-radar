单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Shanghai, China
通讯: Yu Xia @ School of Mechanical Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Shanghai, China
相关性: 该论文直接研究了多孔钛合金支架的力学性能，包括压缩强度和疲劳性能，与用户关注的LPBF制造、应力屏蔽和疲劳测试高度相关。
边界: This paper does not report surface roughness, as-built morphology, or osseointegration data.

Motivation· Porous titanium scaffolds for load-bearing orthopedic implants require both high porosity for bone ingrowth and adequate mechanical strength and fatigue resistance.
Method· A novel porous scaffold with a framework structure was designed and fabricated via selective laser melting, and its mechanical properties were evaluated.
