Pareto-efficient Acquisition Functions for Cost-Aware Bayesian Optimization

Gauthier Guinet, Valerio Perrone, Cédric Archambeau arXiv preprint 2020-11-23 link
相关性: 该论文提出了一种成本感知贝叶斯优化的Pareto高效方法，可迁移至昂贵的生物力学仿真优化问题。
边界: This paper does not handle constraints, lacks explicit uncertainty quantification focus, and is not validated on biomechanics or implant design problems.
动机· 现有成本感知贝叶斯优化启发式方法鲁棒性不足。

验证· 在多个真实世界黑箱函数优化问题上进行数值验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Common heuristics for cost-aware Bayesian optimization are not robust across diverse problems.
Method· Reformulate cost-aware BO via Pareto efficiency and propose a Pareto-efficient variant of expected improvement.
Result· The proposed acquisition functions achieve up to 50% speed-up and outperform prior methods on 144 real-world black-box optimization problems.
Validation· Validated on a large set of real-world black-box function optimization benchmarks.
