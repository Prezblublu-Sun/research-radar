Validation· Evaluated on two datasets with a novel physical plausibility metric and human intuition alignment.

Efficient Process Development for Laser Powder Bed Fusion of a Soft‐Magnetic Metallic Glass Composite

Julia Löfstrand, Priyanka Saha, Vidhiaza Leviandhika, Urban Wiklund, Alexander Elsen et al. Advanced Engineering Materials 2026-02-08 10.1002/adem.202502061
单位: Department of Physics and Astronomy Uppsala University Uppsala Sweden
通讯: Petra E. Jönsson @ Department of Physics and Astronomy Uppsala University Uppsala Sweden
相关性: 该论文涉及激光粉末床熔融制造软磁金属玻璃复合材料，与生物医学植入物的力学性能、应力遮挡或骨整合无关。
边界: This paper focuses on soft-magnetic properties of metallic glass composites and does not address porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration relevant to biomedical implants.
动机· 开发高效工艺制造软磁金属玻璃复合材料
方法· 采用棋盘格与双曝光打印结合参数优化
结果· 生产出直径5cm的环形磁芯，相对磁导率40
验证· X射线衍射、磁力显微镜等实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop an efficient process for laser powder bed fusion of iron-based metallic glass alloys with optimized soft-magnetic properties.
Method· A workflow combining checkerboard and double-exposure printing with single-track melt-pool imaging and Kerr microscopy was used to select parameters.
Result· A 5 cm ring-shaped core was produced, achieving a relative permeability of 40 at 100 Hz, limited by micron-sized crystalline grains.
Validation· Samples were characterized by X-ray diffraction, magnetometry, magneto-optical imaging, and electron backscatter diffraction.

相关性: 该论文研究上下文赌博机的贝叶斯采样方法，与有限元代理建模及生物力学优化无关。
边界: This paper focuses on contextual bandit algorithms for mobile health interventions, with no FEA, surrogate modeling, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification, or biomechanics context.
动机· 在个性化移动健康干预中，需要自适应复杂非线性行为的决策算法。
方法· 提出贝叶斯森林汤普森采样，将贝叶斯加性回归树融入探索循环。
结果· 理论上有贝叶斯遗憾界，在表格基准上达到最先进性能，并提升实际干预效果。

English summary & terms
Motivation· Personalized mobile health interventions require decision-making algorithms that adapt to complex nonlinear user behaviors.
Method· Introduces Bayesian Forest Thompson Sampling, integrating Bayesian Additive Regression Trees into the exploration loop of contextual bandits.
Result· Theoretically derived a Bayesian regret bound of O(√T) and achieved state-of-the-art regret with near-nominal calibration on tabular benchmarks.
Validation· Validated through synthetic benchmarks and an offline policy evaluation on a micro-randomized trial for alcohol reduction.

Sequential versus Manifold Bayesian Optimization under Realistic Experimental Time Constraints

Boris Slautin, Sergei Kalinin arXiv preprint 2026-02-08 link
相关性: 论文关于材料发现的贝叶斯优化，与FEA代理模型及植入物生物力学设计无直接或可迁移关联。
边界: This paper does not involve FEA simulation, surrogate modeling for mechanical problems, uncertainty quantification beyond standard GP, or any biomedical/implant context.
动机· 比较序列与流形贝叶斯优化在实验时间约束下的表现。
方法· 引入时间感知框架，建模合成与表征时间进行公平基准测试。
结果· 序列BO在短期或批处理无优势时最优，流形BO在多通道合成时更优。
验证· 基于三元和四元组分空间数值实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare sequential and manifold Bayesian optimization under realistic experimental time constraints for autonomous materials discovery.
Method· A time-aware framework modeling synthesis and characterization times enables fair benchmarking of optimization strategies.
Result· Sequential BO is optimal for short experiments or when batching offers no time advantage, while manifold BO becomes favorable with faster accumulation of measurements via multiplexed synthesis.
Validation· Numerical experiments in ternary and quaternary compositional spaces demonstrate the regime transition.
