相关性: 该论文提出的异质近场动力学神经算子方法可用于从实验数据中学习生物组织的本构模型和微观结构，其框架可迁移至植入物力学建模中的材料参数识别和应力场预测。
边界: This paper focuses on constitutive modeling of biological tissues from DIC data, not on surrogate modeling for expensive FEA simulations or implant design optimization.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To discover heterogeneous constitutive laws and fiber orientation fields of biological tissues from experimental mechanical measurements such as DIC data.

Validation· Numerical simulation cases demonstrate the framework's ability to capture anisotropic heterogeneous responses under large deformations.
