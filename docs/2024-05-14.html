英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the risk of early wound complications between direct anterior and direct lateral approaches in primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective review of 579 DA and 167 DL primary THA patients from a single surgeon over 5 years was conducted with univariate and multivariate analyses.
Result· No significant difference in early wound complication rates was found between the DA (6.9%) and DL (8.4%) approaches after controlling for confounders.
Validation· Clinical retrospective study with multivariate logistic regression to adjust for patient demographics and comorbidities.
