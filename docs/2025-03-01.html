Motivation· To improve preoperative evaluation of primary stability for dental implants, which is crucial for osseointegration and immediate loading success.
Method· Combined biomechanical pull-out tests with homogenized finite element simulations using μCT-derived bone volume fraction to estimate implant ultimate force.
Result· The finite element-estimated ultimate force showed higher correlation with experimental measurements than traditional metrics like insertion torque and resonance frequency analysis.
Validation· Validated against mechanical tests on 35 human jawbone samples from two donors, achieving R²=0.85 between simulated and experimental ultimate forces.
