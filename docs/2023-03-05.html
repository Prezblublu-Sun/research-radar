Expectation consistency for calibration of neural networks

相关性: 该论文仅涉及神经网络分类器的置信度校准，与FEA或替代建模方法无关。
边界: This paper focuses on calibrating neural network classifiers for image data, with no application to surrogate modeling, finite element analysis, or biomechanical design optimization.
动机· 改进神经网络预测置信度的校准效果。
方法· 提出后训练阶段基于期望一致的权重缩放方法。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Deep neural networks tend to be overconfident, requiring effective calibration methods for better uncertainty quantification.
Method· We propose expectation consistency (EC), a post-training rescaling of the last layer weights that matches average confidence to average accuracy, grounded in the Nishimori identity.
Result· EC achieves calibration performance comparable to temperature scaling across various architectures and datasets, and can outperform it in certain synthetic settings.
Validation· The method is validated on standard image classification benchmarks (CIFAR-10/100, ImageNet) and synthetic data with asymptotic analysis.
