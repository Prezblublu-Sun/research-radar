相关性: 该论文研究了PEEK和钛的表面形貌与化学性质对骨整合的影响，但未涉及LPBF制造、应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper does not involve LPBF fabrication, stress shielding analysis, fatigue testing, or porous structure design for stiffness matching.
动机· 探究表面形貌和化学性质对植入物骨整合的影响。

Result· Surface topography, particularly porosity, accounted for 65.3% of fixation strength variance, while chemistry accounted for only 5.9%.
Validation· Validation was performed via micro-CT, histology, and biomechanical pullout testing.
