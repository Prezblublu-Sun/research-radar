英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate short-term radiographic outcomes of an EBM-fabricated 3D-printed acetabular component in primary THA.
Method· Multicenter retrospective cohort study analyzing postoperative radiographs of 236 THAs at approximately 2 years.
Result· Radiolucent lines occurred in 20.3% of hips, and no acetabular component revisions were observed.
Validation· Clinical radiographic follow-up without biomechanical or finite element validation.
