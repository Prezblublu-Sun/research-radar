单位: *Employee, Private Dental Praxis Dr. Rossa, Ludwigshafen, Germany. †Scientific Researcher, Laboratory of Tissue Engineering, State University of Medicine and Pharmacy "N. Testemitanu," Chisinau, Moldo
通讯: Peer W. Kämmerer @ Peer W. Kämmerer, MD, DDS, PhD, Department of Oral, Maxillofacial and Plastic Surgery, University Medical Center Rostock
相关性: 该论文研究牙种植体的初级稳定性，与髋关节植入物和股骨柄的应力遮挡、微动及有限元分析无关。
边界: This paper focuses on dental implants and does not address hip implant stress shielding, micromotion, or femoral stem mechanics.
动机· 探究不同植入扭矩对圆柱形和锥形牙种植体初级稳定性的影响。
方法· 将两种种植体以不同扭矩植入猪骨，测量ISQ和推出力。
结果· 圆柱形种植体在30 N·cm扭矩下稳定性最高，锥形种植体在40 N·cm扭矩下稳定性最高。
验证· 离体猪骨实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate how different insertion torques affect the primary stability of cylindrical versus conical dental implants.
Method· Cylindrical and conical dental implants were inserted into fresh porcine bone at torques of 20, 30, 40, and 45 N·cm, and primary stability was assessed via resonance frequency analysis (ISQ) and push-out tests.
Result· The cylindrical implant achieved highest ISQ and push-out force at 30 N·cm, while the conical implant peaked at 40 N·cm; excessive torque reduced stability for both designs.
Validation· Ex vivo experiments on porcine bone with mixed trabecular-cortical quality, using cone beam CT for bone quality assessment.
