Method· Systematic review and meta-analysis following PRISMA guidelines, pooling data from 10 studies with 59,607 patients.
Result· No significant differences in dislocation, periprosthetic joint infection, or aseptic loosening rates were found between surgical sequences.
Validation· Statistical meta-analysis of published clinical studies.
