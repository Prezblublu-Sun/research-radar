相关性: 该论文与用户的研究方向完全无关，不涉及有限元分析、代理模型或生物力学设计。
边界: This paper does not address any aspect of finite element analysis, surrogate modeling, design optimization, or biomechanics. It is a purely theoretical study of statistical properties of a signal processing transform.

English summary & terms
Motivation· Motivated by analyzing complex and non-stationary time series, this work generalizes the scattering transform to include neural activation functions.

Result· Central and non-central limit theorems are established for second-order NAST coefficients under various dependence and stationarity conditions.
Validation· Numerical simulations are provided to illustrate the theoretical results.
