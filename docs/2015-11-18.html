动机· 探索全髋关节置换术后免疫反应与恢复速度的关联。
方法· 使用质谱流式细胞术分析术后血液样本中的免疫细胞。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To identify immune correlates of surgical recovery in patients undergoing primary hip arthroplasty.
Method· Single-cell mass cytometry was used to characterize immune cell subsets in postsurgical blood samples.
Result· Phosphorylated MAPKAPK2 response in CD14+ monocytes and classical dendritic cells accounted for nearly 50% of variability in recovery speed.
Validation· Validated in a cohort of 25 patients with presurgical blood samples predicting recovery.
