相关性: 与用户研究方向无关，该论文为全髋关节置换术后坐骨神经麻痹的临床结果研究，不涉及植入物力学分析。
边界: This paper does not quantify implant stress shielding, micromotion, or use finite element modeling; it is a clinical study on nerve palsy treatment.
动机· 探讨手术神经松解是否影响全髋关节置换术后坐骨神经麻痹的肌力恢复。
方法· 回顾性分析1575例后外侧入路全髋关节置换术，比较保守治疗与手术治疗组临床结果。
结果· 手术组肌力恢复率80%，显著高于保守组12.5%，且早期手术者完全恢复。
验证· 单中心回顾性临床观察，基于手册肌力测试评估恢复。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine whether surgical neurolysis improves muscle power recovery in sciatic nerve palsy following total hip arthroplasty via posterolateral approach.
Method· Retrospective review of 1575 THAs, comparing conservative and surgical treatment groups for motor recovery.
Result· Surgical group achieved 80% recovery rate (MMT>3) versus 12.5% in conservative group; immediate surgery led to full recovery in all cases.
Validation· Single-center retrospective clinical study with manual muscle testing assessment.
