英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare postoperative pain and opioid consumption between regional and general anesthesia in lower extremity joint replacement.
Method· Retrospective analysis of the international PAIN OUT registry using logistic regression and propensity score matching.
Result· Regional anesthesia was associated with less pain and lower opioid consumption in knee arthroplasty, but only lower opioid consumption in hip arthroplasty.
Validation· Clinical registry data with adjusted odds ratios and sensitivity analysis.
