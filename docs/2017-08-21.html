相关性: 该论文专注于水文模型的敏感性分析，与FEA替代建模或生物力学设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, implant biomechanics, or any biomedical engineering context; it focuses on hydrological model sensitivity analysis.
动机· 从水文模型运行中获取更多洞察，打开模型黑箱。

Validation· The methodology is demonstrated on five configurations of the FUSE hydrological model for a river in Belgium.
