Sahil Bhola, Karthik Duraisamy arXiv preprint 2025-12-14 link
相关性: 该论文提出了一种基于流匹配的算子学习方法，能够从低保真度近似映射到高保真度PDE解，并生成不确定性估计，可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模。
边界: The paper does not apply the method to biomedical implant or biomechanics problems, nor does it include any experimental or clinical validation.
动机· 解决数据稀缺下PDE概率代理学习困难的问题

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Learning probabilistic surrogates for PDEs remains challenging in data-scarce regimes, as neural operators require large datasets and generative models lack resolution invariance.
Method· We formulate flow matching in infinite-dimensional function space with a conditional neural operator architecture using feature-wise linear modulation, parameterizing the flow vector field as a sum of linear and nonlinear operators to learn residual corrections from low-fidelity to high-fidelity solutions.
Result· The proposed method accurately learns solution operators across different resolutions and fidelities, producing uncertainty estimates that reflect model confidence even with limited high-fidelity data.
Validation· Numerical experiments on 1D advection, Burgers' equation, and 2D Darcy flow demonstrate the effectiveness of the approach.
