PIS: A Generalized Physical Inversion Solver for Arbitrary Sparse Observations via Set Conditioned Flow Matching

Weijie Yang, Xun Zhang, Simin Jiang, Yubao Zhou arXiv preprint 2025-12-14 link
相关性: 该方法通过流匹配实现稀疏观测下的PDE参数反演，可迁移至植入物生物力学设计的高效代理建模与不确定性量化。
边界: The method is validated on geophysical and structural health monitoring benchmarks, not on implant biomechanics or structural FEA surrogate models.
动机· 解决稀疏观测下PDE参数反演的高病态性与低效问题。
方法· 提出集合条件流匹配结合余弦退火稀疏课程学习的统一反演框架。
结果· 在极端稀疏条件下误差降低88.7%，实现瞬时推理。
验证· 在地下水、波动及结构健康监测等数值基准上验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To overcome accuracy and efficiency bottlenecks in PDE-constrained inverse problems with sparse and irregular observations.
Method· A unified Physical Inversion Solver (PIS) coupling Set-Conditioned Flow Matching with a Cosine-Annealed Sparsity Curriculum for stable inversion from arbitrary off-grid sensors.
Result· PIS achieves up to 88.7% error reduction under extreme sparsity (<1%) and instantaneous inference (50 NFEs) across multiple benchmarks.
Validation· Validated on numerical benchmarks including subsurface characterization, wave-based characterization, and structural health monitoring.
