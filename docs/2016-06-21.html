Uncertainty quantification of inflow boundary condition and proximal arterial stiffness coupled effect on pulse wave propagation in a vascular network

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Background only simulation_case UQ
A Brault, L Dumas, D Lucor arXiv preprint 2016-06-21 10.1002/cnm
相关性: 该论文涉及血流动力学的不确定性量化，与FEA代理建模或生物力学植入物设计无直接关联。
边界: No surrogate ML model for expensive FEA, no implant biomechanics or contact mechanics, and no experimental validation.
动机· 量化心输出与主动脉硬度的不确定性对脉搏波传播的影响
方法· 采用稀疏伪谱方法对降阶脉搏波模型进行随机模拟
结果· 流入条件不确定性显著改变血流速度和压力的均值与标准差
验证· 数值模拟验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify the effect of uncertainties from cardiac output and aortic compliance heterogeneity on pulse wave propagation in a compliant arterial network.
Method· A stochastic sparse pseudospectral method is applied to a reduced-order pulse wave propagation model with random inlet flow and random aortic stiffness field.
Result· Uncertainties in inflow shape and magnitude lead to significant variations in both mean and standard deviation of blood flow velocity and pressure dynamics.
Validation· Validation is performed through numerical simulation using the stochastic reduced-order model.

Jinhui Ma, Wei Sun, Fuqiang Gao, Wanshou Guo, Yunting Wang et al. Scientific Reports 2016-06-21 10.1038/srep28227
单位: Peking University China-Japan Friendship School of Clinical Medicine, 2 Yinghuadong Road, Chaoyang District, Beijing, 100029 China
通讯: Jinhui Ma @ Peking University China-Japan Friendship School of Clinical Medicine, 2 Yinghuadong Road, Chaoyang District, Beijing, 10
相关性: 该论文评估多孔钽植入物治疗股骨头坏死的临床存活率，未涉及应力遮挡、LPBF制造或疲劳测试。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing, no stress shielding analysis, no fatigue evaluation, no surface roughness or osseointegration characterization.
动机· 评估多孔钽植入物治疗股骨头坏死的存活率及风险因素。
方法· 回顾性分析90例患者104髋，使用多孔钽植入物联合骨移植。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the survivorship and risk factors for radiographic progression and conversion to total hip arthroplasty after porous tantalum implant surgery for osteonecrosis of the femoral head.
Method· A retrospective study of 90 patients (104 hips) treated with porous tantalum implant combined with bone grafting from 2008 to 2013.
Result· Only 52.9% of hips survived; age >35 years and ARCO stage III were independent risk factors for conversion to THA.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with Harris hip score, radiographic outcomes, and Cox proportional hazards model.

On the Growth Rate of Tribomaterial in Bovine Serum Lubricated Sliding Contacts

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Alfons Fischer, Daniel Stickel, Christian Schoss, R. Bosman, Markus A. Wimmer Lubricants 2016-06-21 10.3390/lubricants4020021
单位: Department of Orthopedics, Rush University Medical Center, Chicago, IL 60612, USA
通讯: Alfons Fischer @ Department of Orthopedics, Rush University Medical Center, Chicago, IL 60612, USA
相关性: 该论文研究髋关节假体中的摩擦材料生长，但未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡或微动分析。
边界: This paper focuses on tribomaterial growth in CoCrMo contacts, not on femoral stem stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics.
动机· 探究髋关节假体中摩擦材料的生长序列和速率。
方法· 使用销-球磨损试验机在牛血清润滑下磨损CoCrMo合金，并中断测试进行称重和形貌分析。
结果· 磨合期试样因生成摩擦材料而增重，之后材料损失超过生成，进入稳态失重。
验证· 通过称重和形貌测量验证摩擦材料生长，并用于接触力学计算。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the growth sequence and rate of tribomaterial in total hip arthroplasty under bovine serum lubrication.
Method· Pin-on-ball wear tests on low- and high-carbon CoCrMo alloys were interrupted at specific cycles for weighing, topography characterization, and wear analysis.
Result· Specimens gained weight during run-in due to tribomaterial formation, followed by a steady weight loss as material loss exceeded generation.
Validation· Experimental weight measurements and topography data were used as input for contact mechanics calculations.
