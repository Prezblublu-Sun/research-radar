英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Uncertainty quantification in cardiovascular networks is hindered by high computational cost of 3D models and inaccuracy of 1D models for pathologies.

Result· The method enhances solver efficiency and parallelism while maintaining computational cost comparable to 1D models.
Validation· No numerical validation or case study is presented in this paper.
