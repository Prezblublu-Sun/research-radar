Full-field <i>in vitro</i> measurements and <i>in silico</i> predictions of strain shielding in the implanted femur after total hip arthroplasty

High Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Direct Read now mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Souptick Chanda, Alex Dickinson, Sanjay Gupta, Martin Browne Proceedings of the Institution of Mechanical Engineers Part H Journal of Engineering in Medicine 2015-06-25 10.1177/0954411915591617
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, Kharagpur, India
通讯: Sanjay Gupta @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, Kharagpur, India
相关性: 该论文直接测量和预测了全髋关节置换术后股骨应变屏蔽，并关联了Gruen分区的骨密度变化，与用户的研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not include patient-specific geometry or micromotion analysis at the bone-implant interface.
动机· 验证有限元预测的股骨表面应变与实验测量的一致性，并关联术后骨密度变化。
方法· 使用数字图像相关测量完整和植入股骨的表面应变，并与有限元预测对比。
结果· 预测与测量高度线性相关，近端应变下降50%-64%对应骨密度损失。
验证· 通过数字图像相关实验验证有限元预测的应变分布。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To verify finite element predictions of surface strains in intact and implanted femurs against experimental measurements and relate strain changes to clinical bone density alterations.
Method· Digital image correlation was used to measure full-field surface strains in composite femurs, and finite element predictions were compared across Gruen zones.
Result· Strong linear correlation (R>0.92) was found between predicted and measured strains, with proximal strain reductions of 50-64% corresponding to bone density loss.
Validation· Experimental validation using digital image correlation on composite femurs under simulated loading.
