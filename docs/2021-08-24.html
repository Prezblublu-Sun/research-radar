Manufacturability analysis of extremely fine porous structures for selective laser melting process of Ti6Al4V alloy

Liping Ding, Shujie Tan, Wenliang Chen, Yaming Jin, Yicha Zhang Rapid Prototyping Journal 2021-08-24 10.1108/rpj-11-2020-0280
单位: College of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Nanjing, China
通讯: Liping Ding @ College of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Nanjing, China
相关性: 该论文仅涉及Ti6Al4V超细多孔结构的SLM可制造性，未涉及应力遮挡、疲劳或骨整合等生物力学评估。
边界: No mechanical testing, stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration analysis; focus solely on manufacturability.
动机· 研究超细多孔结构在SLM中的可制造性极限。
方法· 通过大量打印实验和统计分析，建立可制造性评估方法。
结果· 可稳定打印90微米特征，工艺参数影响显著。
验证· 打印并测量样品几何精度。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the manufacturability limits of extremely fine porous structures in selective laser melting of Ti6Al4V.
Method· A comprehensive experimental study with numerous printed samples and statistical analysis to evaluate manufacturing results.
Result· The SLM process can achieve features as small as 90 μm under stable control, with process parameters significantly affecting outcomes.
Validation· Experimental validation through manufacturing and dimensional measurement of porous structure samples.

Effect of rescanning with reduced interlayer times on microstructure and properties of titanium alloy via selective laser melting

Yiqing Ma, Meiping Wu, Weipeng Duan, Xiaojin Miao, Haohao Li et al. Proceedings of the Institution of Mechanical Engineers Part B Journal of Engineering Manufacture 2021-08-24 10.1177/09544054211041281
单位: Jiangsu Key Laboratory of Advanced Food Manufacturing Equipment & Technology, Jiangnan University, Wuxi, Jiangsu, China
通讯: Meiping Wu @ Jiangsu Key Laboratory of Advanced Food Manufacturing Equipment & Technology, Jiangnan University, Wuxi, Jiangsu, China  |  Xiaojin Miao @ Jiangsu Key Laboratory of Advanced Food Manufacturing Equipment & Technology, Jiangnan University, Wuxi, Jiangsu, China
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM中重扫描策略对钛合金微观结构和性能的影响，但未涉及多孔结构、应力屏蔽或疲劳等生物医学植入物关键问题。
边界: Does not connect to biomedical implant performance: no porous structure design, no stiffness matching, no fatigue testing, no osseointegration or surface morphology analysis.
动机· 优化SLM中重扫描策略以同时提升零件性能并缩短制造时间
方法· 结合有限元模拟与实验，改变重扫描参数研究熔池凝固行为
结果· 无层间隔的重扫描获得了最佳相对密度和残余应力优化效果
验证· 通过Ti6Al4V样品制备及微观结构、密度、缺陷和残余应力测量验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve part performance and reduce manufacturing time by optimizing rescanning strategies in selective laser melting.
Method· Finite element analysis of molten pool solidification combined with experimental fabrication of Ti6Al4V samples under varying rescanning parameters.
Result· Rescanning without layer interval achieved the highest relative density and best residual stress reduction, with residual stress decreasing from 353 to 294 MPa.
Validation· Experimental characterization of microstructure, relative density, pore defects, and residual stress in fabricated Ti6Al4V samples.

单位: Northrop Grumman Mission Systems, 7323 Aviation Blvd, Baltimore, MD, 21240, USA
通讯: Kevin R. Chasse @ Northrop Grumman Mission Systems, 7323 Aviation Blvd, Baltimore, MD, 21240, USA
相关性: 该论文研究航空铝合金的应力腐蚀开裂，与生物医学植入物多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能完全无关。
边界: This paper focuses on stress corrosion cracking of aerospace aluminum alloy, with no porous structure, no biomedical implant context, no fatigue testing, and no osseointegration or surface roughness characterization.
动机· 研究激光熔融Al7050在氯化物环境中的应力腐蚀开裂倾向性
方法· 对SLM制备的Al7050合金进行慢应变速率拉伸及电化学极化测试
结果· SLM Al7050在氯离子环境中对应力腐蚀开裂敏感，其机理与微观组织相关

English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the stress corrosion cracking susceptibility of additively manufactured Al7050 alloy produced by selective laser melting in chloride environments.
Method· Slow strain rate tensile tests and electrochemical polarization measurements were conducted on SLM-fabricated Al7050 alloy in NaCl solutions.
Result· The SLM Al7050 alloy exhibits susceptibility to stress corrosion cracking in chloride environments, with mechanisms linked to its unique microstructure.
Validation· Fractography and secondary crack analysis confirmed SCC susceptibility.

通讯: Steven O Nielsen @ Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry, The University of Texas at Dallas, 800 West Campbell Road, Richardson, Texas 7 [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究钛表面蛋白质吸附的分子动力学模拟，与增材制造生物医学材料方向无关。
边界: No AM fabrication, no porous structure design, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), no stress shielding analysis, and no surface roughness characterization.
动机· 理解钛植入体表面羟基化对HMGB1蛋白吸附的影响。

结果· 羟基化表面吸附更强，离子强度增加会减少蛋白直接吸附。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand the molecular mechanisms of HMGB1 protein adsorption on titanium implant surfaces with different hydroxylation states.
Method· Molecular dynamics simulations of HMGB1 on fully hydroxylated and nonhydroxylated rutile (110) TiO2 surfaces.
Result· Hydroxylated surface shows higher affinity for HMGB1, and increased ionic strength reduces direct protein adsorption.
Validation· Computational study with no experimental validation.
