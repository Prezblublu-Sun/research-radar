边界: This paper focuses on laser peening surface treatment, not on implant biomechanics or FEA surrogate for biomedical design.
动机· 提高激光喷丸工艺设计的疲劳寿命可靠性，同时降低仿真成本。

Motivation· To efficiently incorporate fatigue life reliability into the design of laser peening treatment while reducing computational cost.
Method· A method combining nonparametric probability density estimation, surrogate modeling, and a novel single explicit analysis using time-dependent damping (SEATD) for efficient FE simulation.
Result· Reliability level significantly affects optimal design: 52 laser spots required for 99% reliability versus 44 for 95%.
Validation· Validated through a finite element simulation case study of a three-point bend coupon.
