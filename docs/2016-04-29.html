Early Migration Predicts Aseptic Loosening of Cementless Femoral Stems: A Long-term Study

Marcus R. Streit, Daniel Haeussler, Thomas Brückner, Tanja Proctor, Moritz M. Innmann et al. Clinical Orthopaedics and Related Research 2016-04-29 10.1007/s11999-016-4857-5
单位: Clinic of Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery, Center for Orthopaedics, Trauma Surgery and Spinal Cord Injury, University of Heidelberg, Schlierbacher Landstrasse 200a, 69118, Heidelberg, Germany. marcus.s
通讯: Marcus R. Streit @ Clinic of Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery, Center for Orthopaedics, Trauma Surgery and Spinal Cord Injury, University of
相关性: 该论文提供了早期迁移与无菌性松动之间的临床关联，但未涉及应力遮挡、接触力学或有限元建模。
边界: This paper is a clinical outcome study with no biomechanical modeling, no FEA, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, and no implant design analysis.
动机· 探究早期迁移能否预测无骨水泥股骨柄的无菌性松动。
方法· 使用EBRA-FCA测量158例THA术后早期轴向迁移，并进行长期随访。
结果· 24个月时松动组迁移显著大于固定组，迁移≥2.7mm预示高松动风险。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine whether early axial migration of cementless femoral stems predicts late aseptic loosening.
Method· Axial stem migration was measured using EBRA-FCA in 158 cementless THAs at regular intervals up to 24 months, with long-term follow-up averaging 21 years.
Result· Stems that later loosened showed significantly greater distal migration at 24 months (4.2 mm vs 0.8 mm), and migration ≥2.7 mm was a strong risk factor for loosening.
Validation· Clinical validation with ROC analysis and competing-events survivorship analysis.
