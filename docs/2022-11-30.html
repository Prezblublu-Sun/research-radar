单位: J.B. Speed School of Engineering, University of Louisville, Louisville, KY 40292, USA
通讯: Subin Shrestha @ J.B. Speed School of Engineering, University of Louisville, Louisville, KY 40292, USA

边界: This paper focuses solely on LPBF process physics (melt pool geometry) without any link to porous structure design, mechanical testing, fatigue, or biomedical implant applications.
动机· 研究LPBF过程中残余热对熔池几何形状的影响

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how residual heat buildup during LPBF scanning affects melt pool dimensions and morphology.
Method· Multi-track specimens with varying scan lengths and hatch spacings were fabricated and analyzed via white light interferometry and metallography, supported by a powder-scale numerical model.
Result· The laser-turn region exhibited the most pronounced residual heat effect, with melt pool depth increasing and track height decreasing as scanning progressed.
Validation· Experimental surface morphology and cross-sectional melt pool boundaries were compared with numerical temperature field results.
