Motivation· To investigate the influence of pore architecture of SLM-fabricated porous 316L stainless steel on cell growth for biomedical implant applications.

Validation· Microstructural evolution was correlated with Vickers microhardness measurements.

Motivation· To assess functional outcomes of total hip arthroplasty in patients with ankylosing spondylitis.
Method· A prospective follow-up study of 75 patients (92 hips) with clinical scoring and range of motion measurements.
Result· Significant improvement in hip function with 87% good-to-excellent outcomes; one case of prosthesis loosening.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with standardized scoring systems (Harris, VAS, BASFI, BASDAI).
