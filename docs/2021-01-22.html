英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the combined heating effect of gradient and RF fields on patients with metallic hip prostheses during MRI.
Method· In silico analysis using an anatomically realistic human model with a CoCrMo hip implant at 12 positions, evaluating separate and superposed exposures at 1.5T and 3T for four clinical sequences.
Result· All sequences except gradient-echo produced temperature increases >1 K after 360 s; gradient-induced heating dominated in EPI and was significant in some cases.
Validation· Numerical simulation with an ad hoc computational algorithm, validated against established safety metrics.
