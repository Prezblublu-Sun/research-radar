英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To design a segmented linear Paul trap for optical clock applications with improved short-term stability and fractional frequency uncertainty of 10^-18.
Method· High-precision finite element method calculations of the electric radiofrequency field were used to minimize excess micromotion of trapped ions.
Result· A scalable design was achieved where the second-order Doppler shift contributes less than 10^-18 to the relative frequency uncertainty.
Validation· Finite element simulations validated the electric field distribution and micromotion levels.
