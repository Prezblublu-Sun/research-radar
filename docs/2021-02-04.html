通讯: Yoshimitsu Okazaki @ Department of Life Science and Biotechnology, National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology, 1–1 Higa
相关性: 该论文直接涉及LPBF制造髋关节柄并进行力学测试，但未探讨多孔结构对应力遮挡或骨整合的影响。
边界: This paper does not link porous structure to stress shielding or stiffness matching, nor does it report surface roughness or osseointegration.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the mechanical performance of artificial hip stems manufactured by hot forging and selective laser melting using biocompatible Ti alloys.
Method· Tensile and fatigue tests were conducted on specimens cut from hot-forged Ti-15-4 stems and SLM-fabricated Ti-15-4-4 and Ti-6-4 rods.
