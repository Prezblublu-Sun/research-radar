Method· A Vancouver B3 fracture was treated by revising the femoral component and reconstructing the proximal femur with cortical strut allograft and cerclage cables.
Result· At one-year follow-up, the patient had satisfactory functional and radiographic outcomes with fracture consolidation.
Validation· Single case report with clinical and radiographic follow-up.
