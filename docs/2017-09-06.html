单位: Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Cambridge, Massachusetts 02139
通讯: Anirban Chaudhuri @ Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Cambridge, Massachusetts 02139
相关性: 该论文提出的多保真自适应代理方法可显著降低耦合系统中不确定性传播的计算成本，适用于植入物生物力学设计中的多学科优化与不确定性量化。
边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, contact mechanics, or patient-specific geometry; it focuses on generic coupled multidisciplinary systems with fixed-point iteration.

Motivation· To reduce the prohibitive computational cost of high-fidelity simulations in uncertainty analysis of feedback-coupled multidisciplinary systems.
Method· A multifidelity uncertainty propagation method that uses adaptive surrogates for coupling variables with adaptive sampling strategies (residual error, information gain, weighted information gain).
Result· The method significantly reduces the number of high-fidelity fixed-point iterations while maintaining accuracy, supported by a rigorous convergence analysis.
Validation· Numerical benchmark examples and convergence analysis demonstrate the effectiveness and accuracy of the proposed approach.
