Paolo Secchi, Daniel S. Balint, Marco Maurizi arXiv preprint 2026-05-12 link

English summary & terms
Motivation· Global neural PDE solvers suffer from poor generalization to unseen geometries and high data generation costs, limiting their scalability.
Method· NEST learns a neural operator on minimal 3×3×3 voxel patches and assembles global solutions via Schwarz iteration with partition-of-unity.
Result· The method generalizes to large, geometrically complex 3D domains far beyond training patch scale for nonlinear hyperelastic problems.
Validation· Numerical experiments on compressible neo-Hookean solids demonstrate generalization across domain size, shape, and boundary conditions.
