Motivation· To evaluate long-term survivorship and risk factors for conversion to total hip arthroplasty after hip preservation surgery.
Method· Multicenter retrospective study analyzing 10-year follow-up data from patients undergoing open or arthroscopic hip preservation.
Result· 90.6% survivorship free of THA at mean 10-year follow-up, with age, obesity, male sex, and chondromalacia identified as key risk factors.
Validation· Multicenter clinical cohort validation with 10-year follow-up.
