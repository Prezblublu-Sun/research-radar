Synthesis and characterization of 3D-printed functionally graded porous titanium alloy

单位: Dental Research Center, Research Institute of Dental Sciences, School of Dentistry, Shahid Beheshti University of Medical Sciences, Tehran, Iran
通讯: Ahmed Hindy @ Dental Research Center, Research Institute of Dental Sciences, School of Dentistry, Shahid Beheshti University of Medica
相关性: 该论文直接研究了3D打印功能梯度多孔钛合金的力学性能与结构设计，与用户关于多孔结构应力遮挡匹配、LPBF制造及力学测试的研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing, surface roughness characterization, or osseointegration assessment.
动机· 为匹配骨组织刚度、减少应力遮挡，设计功能梯度多孔钛合金。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce stress shielding in orthopedic implants by designing functionally graded porous titanium alloy with stiffness matching bone.
Method· Functionally graded porous titanium alloy samples with varying porosity gradients were fabricated via LPBF and characterized by compression testing.
Result· The graded porous structures achieved elastic moduli close to cortical bone while maintaining sufficient compressive strength.
Validation· Mechanical validation was performed through quasi-static compression tests on as-built specimens.

相关性: 该论文直接研究了增材制造多孔植入物中的骨重塑对应力分布的影响，与用户关注的应力遮挡和疲劳性能高度相关。
边界: This paper does not report surface roughness, as-built morphology, or osseointegration metrics, and fatigue is only discussed as a long-term outcome without direct fatigue testing.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· The safety of additive manufactured porous implants is a growing concern due to recent recalls, necessitating evaluation of how bone ingrowth affects stress and strain.
Method· Two novel algorithms simulating bone ingrowth were integrated into finite element analysis and validated against histology from an ovine condylar critical-sized defect model with Ti6Al4V implants fabricated by selective laser sintering.
Result· Partial bone formation reduced stress concentrations to safe levels, improving long-term fatigue resistance, and higher bone ingrowth was predicted for lower-modulus titanium-tantalum alloy implants.
Validation· The finite element predictions were validated against histological results from an in vivo ovine model.
