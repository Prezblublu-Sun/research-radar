A Proof of Concept Study of the Mechanical Behavior of Lattice Structures Used to Design a Shoulder Hemi-Prosthesis

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Skim only simulation_case Patient-specific
Marinela Peto, Oscar A. Aguilar-Rosas, Erick Ramírez-Cedillo, Moisés Jiménez-Martínez, Adriana Hernández et al. Journal of Engineering and Science in Medical Diagnostics and Therapy 2021-06-11 10.1115/1.4051419
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of North Texas, 3940 North Elm Street, Denton, TX 76207
通讯: Marinela Peto @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of North Texas, 3940 North Elm Street, Denton, TX 76207
相关性: 该论文研究了肩关节假体中的晶格结构，涉及应力遮挡和力学性能，但未进行LPBF制造和疲劳测试。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no fatigue testing, and no surface roughness or osseointegration characterization.
动机· 通过晶格结构减轻肩关节假体重量并减少应力遮挡。
方法· 测试三种树脂晶格结构，选择最佳者集成到数字模型中并用钛合金模拟。
结果· HPVC晶格结构应力低于钛合金弹性极限，与骨材料模型应力水平相当。
验证· 通过压缩实验和有限元模拟验证晶格结构力学性能。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce weight and minimize stress shielding in a shoulder hemi-prosthesis by integrating lattice structures.
Method· Three lattice configurations (TVC, HPVC, CD) were fabricated in resin and tested mechanically; the best (HPVC) was incorporated into a digital twin and simulated with Ti6Al4V material properties.
Result· The HPVC lattice yielded maximum von Mises stress of 325 MPa, below the yield limit of titanium alloys, and 52 MPa in an equivalent elastic model, comparable to bone.
Validation· Mechanical compression testing of resin lattices and finite element simulations of the titanium prosthesis.
