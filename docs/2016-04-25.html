Motivation· To evaluate the clinical efficacy of sartorius muscle pedicle iliac bone grafting for avascular necrosis of the femoral head in young adults.
Method· A retrospective study of 40 patients (53 hips) treated with the grafting procedure, with clinical and radiological follow-up averaging 4.2 years.
Result· Excellent or good Harris hip scores were achieved in 80% of cases, with minimal complications.
Validation· Clinical follow-up and radiological assessment.
