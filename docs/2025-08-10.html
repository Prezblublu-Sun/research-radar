Enhancing Corrosion Resistance of Aluminum Alloys Through AI and ML Modeling

Farnaz Kaboudvand, Maham Khalid, Nydia Assaf, Vardaan Sahgal, Jon P. Ruffley et al. arXiv preprint 2025-08-10 link
相关性: 该论文使用机器学习预测铝合金腐蚀，与生物力学有限元代理建模方向无直接关联。
边界: This paper addresses corrosion prediction for aluminum alloys, not biomechanical implant FEA, surrogate modeling, or design optimization under constraints.
动机· 用机器学习预测并优化铝合金的耐腐蚀性能。
方法· 比较随机森林、神经网络和高斯过程回归三种模型。
结果· 高斯过程回归表现最佳，尤其是混合核函数。
验证· 基于公开腐蚀数据库进行训练和测试。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To predict and optimize corrosion resistance of aluminum alloys using machine learning techniques.
Method· Compared Random Forest, neural network, and Gaussian Process Regression (with various kernels) for forward and inverse prediction.
Result· Gaussian Process Regression with hybrid kernels outperformed other models, especially after log-transformation of corrosion rates.
Validation· Validated on a compiled open-source corrosion dataset with standardized preprocessing.
