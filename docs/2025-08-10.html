边界: No validation on biomedical or implant mechanics problems; no uncertainty quantification; no constraint handling.

Result· DOOL outperforms supervised DeepONet and MIONet on typical dissipative equations.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on dissipative PDEs with comparison to supervised baselines.
