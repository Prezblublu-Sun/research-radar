Piezoelectric 3D bioprinting for ophthalmological applications: process development and viability analysis of the technology.

Juliana Lopes Hoehne, Regina Carlstron, Janaína Dernorwsek, Priscila Cardoso Cristovam, Helder Luiz Bachiega et al. Biomedical physics & engineering express 2020-04-15 10.1088/2057-1976/ab7bf9
单位: Department of Ophthalmology and Visual Science, Escola Paulista de Medicina (Sao Paulo School of Medicine), Federal University of Sao Paulo, Sao Paulo, SP, Brazil.
通讯: Paulo Schor @ Department of Ophthalmology and Visual Science, Escola Paulista de Medicina (Sao Paulo School of Medicine), Federal Univ [推断]
相关性: 该论文涉及眼科用压电3D生物打印工艺，但未集成AI/ML，缺乏自主决策能力，与本方向核心（AI驱动生物打印）关联较弱。
边界: No AI/ML methods are used; it is a process development study without autonomous decision or AI integration in any bioprinting stage.
动机· 开发适用于眼科应用的压电3D生物打印工艺。
方法· 利用诊断图像输入，设计并验证了从图像到打印的完整生物制造流程。
结果· 该流程在猪眼离体模型中成功打印含角膜上皮细胞的生物墨水。
验证· 通过猪眼离体打印实验验证工艺可行性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a piezoelectric 3D bioprinting process tailored for ophthalmological applications.
Method· A biofabrication process flow was designed using diagnostic images to generate print scripts, and alignment devices were built for precise printing.
Result· The process successfully printed corneal epithelial cell-laden bioink onto pig eyes ex vivo, demonstrating potential for in situ bioprinting.
Validation· Ex vivo printing on pig eyes was performed to validate the process flow and cell viability.

单位: George W. Woodruff School of Mechanical Engineering, Georgia Institute of Technology, Atlanta, GA.; Parker H. Petit Institute for Bioengineering and Bioscience, Georgia Institute of Technology, Atlant
通讯: Robert E Guldberg @ Knight Campus, University of Oregon, Eugene, OR. [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究了PEEK和钛植入物的表面形貌和化学性质对骨整合的影响，但未涉及AM/LPBF制造、疲劳测试或应力屏蔽，因此与AM生物医学材料方向相关性低。
边界: This paper does not involve additive manufacturing (LPBF), fatigue testing, stress shielding analysis, or as-built surface morphology characterization.
动机· 探究表面形貌和化学性质对PEEK和钛植入物骨整合的影响
方法· 在大鼠胫骨植入光滑、粗糙和多孔的PEEK或钛植入物，8周后进行micro-CT、组织学和生物力学测试
结果· 表面形貌（尤其是多孔性）对固定强度的影响远大于表面化学性质
验证· 体内大鼠模型验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the relative effects of surface topography and chemistry on implant osseointegration for PEEK and titanium.
Method· Smooth, rough, and porous PEEK and titanium implants were placed in rat tibiae and analyzed after 8 weeks via micro-CT, histology, and pullout testing.
Result· Surface topography, particularly porosity, accounted for 65.3% of the variance in fixation strength, while surface chemistry accounted for only 5.9%.
Validation· In vivo rat model with multimodal assessment including biomechanical testing.

单位: Additive Manufacturing Laboratory, Department of Biomechanical Engineering, Faculty of Mechanical, Maritime, and Materials Engineering, Delft University of Technology, the Netherlands. Electronic addr
通讯: I A J van Hengel @ Additive Manufacturing Laboratory, Department of Biomechanical Engineering, Faculty of Mechanical, Maritime, and Materia

Validation· Antibacterial activity was validated through in vitro assays and an ex vivo murine femur model.

Antibiotic-Loaded Hydrogel Coating to Reduce Early Postsurgical Infections in Aseptic Hip Revision Surgery: A Retrospective, Matched Case-Control Study.

Daniele De Meo, Valeria Calogero, Lorenzo Are, Armando U Cavallo, Pietro Persiani et al. Microorganisms 2020-04-15 10.3390/microorganisms8040571
单位: Department of Orthopaedic and Traumatology, Policlinico Umberto I Hospital - Sapienza University of Rome, Piazzale A. Moro, 3, 00185 Rome, Italy.
通讯: Ciro Villani @ Department of Orthopaedic and Traumatology, Policlinico Umberto I Hospital - Sapienza University of Rome, Piazzale A. Mo [推断]
相关性: 该论文涉及髋关节翻修手术中的抗菌涂层，未涉及LPBF制造、力学测试或疲劳性能，与AM生物医学材料方向无关。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing, no fatigue or stress shielding analysis, and no porous implant structure evaluation.
动机· 评估抗生素水凝胶涂层在髋关节翻修术中降低早期感染的效果。
方法· 回顾性匹配病例对照研究，比较ALH组与对照组的感染率。
结果· ALH组无感染，对照组6例感染，差异显著。
验证· 临床随访六个月以上，匹配病例对照验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study evaluates whether an antibiotic-loaded hydrogel coating on cementless hip prostheses reduces early periprosthetic joint infections in revision surgery.
Method· A retrospective 1:1 matched case-control study compared 17 patients receiving the hydrogel coating with 17 controls, assessing infection incidence and bone ingrowth.
Result· Zero infections occurred in the hydrogel group versus six in the control group (p < 0.0001), with no adverse effects on osseointegration.
Validation· Validation was through clinical outcomes with a minimum six-month follow-up and statistical matching.
