通讯: Mathieu Brochu @ Department of Mining and Materials Engineering, McGill University, Wong Building, 3610 University St., Montreal, QC H3A
相关性: 该论文研究LPBF制造316L不锈钢垂直支柱的微观结构和力学性能，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或生物医学植入物背景。

Validation· Experimental testing validated the relationship between microstructure and mechanical properties.
