英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the feasibility and patient satisfaction of remote monitoring for same-day discharge joint replacement patients.
Method· 23 patients undergoing unicompartmental/total knee or total hip arthroplasty used a wireless BP/HR monitor with a hydration protocol.
Result· High patient satisfaction (ease of use 8.9/10) and 27.8% of hypotensive events were managed remotely.
Validation· Pilot clinical study with descriptive statistics from patient surveys.
