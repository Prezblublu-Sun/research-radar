相关性: 这篇综述关注涂层对骨整合和抗菌的作用，但未涉及增材制造、多孔结构或应力遮挡等核心方向。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To assess whether in vivo models are necessary for evaluating dual-biofunctional coatings that promote osseointegration and antibacterial activity.
Method· A systematic review of in vivo studies (clinical and animal) from major databases up to December 2024.
Result· Various coating components (inorganic, peptide, metal-based) were identified as effective for dual biofunctionality.
Validation· Synthesis of 16 in vivo studies including one clinical trial with 4-month outcomes.
