Default Machine Learning Hyperparameters Do Not Provide Informative Initialization for Bayesian Optimization

Nicolás Villagrán Prieto, Eduardo C. Garrido-Merchán arXiv preprint 2026-02-09 link
相关性: 该论文关注机器学习超参数优化，与使用代理模型加速生物力学有限元仿真的研究方向无关。
边界: No FEA validation, no surrogate modeling for physical simulations, no implant or biomechanics context.
动机· 检验默认超参数是否能为贝叶斯优化提供有效初始化。
方法· 用高斯分布初始化默认值进行贝叶斯优化，对比均匀随机基线。
结果· 默认信息没有带来统计显著的收敛优势。
验证· 在三个后端、三个模型和五个数据集上进行了广泛实验。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates whether default hyperparameter values from popular ML libraries can serve as informed initial points for Bayesian optimization.
Method· The authors initialize Bayesian optimization with points drawn from truncated Gaussian distributions centered at library defaults and compare against uniform random initialization across multiple back-ends, model families, and datasets.
Result· Default-informed initialization yields no statistically significant advantage over random sampling in terms of convergence speed or final predictive quality.
Validation· Extensive empirical evaluation with three BO back-ends, three model families, and five benchmark datasets, with statistical significance assessed via one-sided binomial tests.
