Gaussian process modeling in approximate Bayesian computation to estimate horizontal gene transfer in bacteria

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Marko Järvenpää, Michael Gutmann, Aki Vehtari, Pekka Marttinen arXiv preprint 2016-10-20 link
相关性: 该论文使用高斯过程代理模型加速近似贝叶斯计算，方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模与不确定性量化。
边界: It does not address FEA or biomechanics; it is applied to bacterial population genetics with no structural mechanics, constraints, or implant design.
动机· 减少复杂似然模型在近似贝叶斯计算中所需的大量仿真次数。
方法· 使用高斯过程建模仿真与观测数据间的差异，并引入多种变换与新型公式进行模型选择。
结果· 不同高斯过程公式对后验分布精度影响显著，基于期望效用的模型选择可自动化优化。
验证· 通过玩具例子和细菌群体遗传实际案例验证方法有效性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce the number of expensive model evaluations required in approximate Bayesian computation when the likelihood is intractable.
Method· A Gaussian process surrogate models the discrepancy between simulated and observed data, with multiple transformations and novel formulations, and expected utility automates GP model selection.
Result· The choice of GP formulation significantly affects posterior accuracy, and expected utility-based selection effectively identifies the best model.
Validation· Validation is performed on toy examples and a real-world bacterial population genetics model estimating horizontal gene transfer.
