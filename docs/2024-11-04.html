动机· 建立非磨损颗粒诱导的无菌性松动小鼠模型，模拟临床松动。
方法· 在小鼠中植入不同表面和配合的钛合金或PMMA植入物，通过拔出测试和组织学分析评估。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a murine model of aseptic loosening that mimics clinical failure without wear particles, addressing the gap in current models.
Method· Mice received press-fitted roughened titanium implants for normal osseointegration or smooth PMMA, loosely fitted smooth/roughened titanium implants for loosening; pullout testing and immunofluorescence were performed.
Result· The PMMA group exhibited 87% lower pullout load and fibrous tissue with abundant fibroblasts, similar to human revision tissues.
Validation· Model validity was confirmed by comparing murine interfacial cell composition with human samples from revision arthroplasty.
