Motivation· To evaluate the safety and reliability of ceramic-on-metal bearing in short-stem total hip arthroplasty at mid-term follow-up.
Method· Clinical, radiographic, and serum metal ion evaluation of 19 patients with CoM bearing and short stem.
Result· All stems were well-positioned and osseointegrated at mean 8-year follow-up, with minimal rise in blood metal ions.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with radiographic and laboratory assessment.
