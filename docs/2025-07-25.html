单位: Escola de Ciência da Vida Programa de Pós-graduação em Tecnologia em Saúde (PPGTS)

边界: No AI/ML method integrated into the bioprinting workflow; purely a materials and biology study without computational intelligence.
动机· 解决深度皮肤损伤后屏障功能恢复的挑战。
方法· 用3D生物打印制备海藻酸钠水凝胶结合富血小板血浆的敷料。

Validation· In vivo experiments using full-thickness skin wounds in mice with histological analyses.

单位: Hubei Engineering Research Center for BDS-Cloud High-Precision Deformation Monitoring, Artificial Intelligence School, Wuchang University of Technology, Wuhan 430223, China

结果· 室温抗拉强度829 MPa，200°C时达884 MPa，较已有结果提升30%

English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the mechanical properties of laser-melted Cu-based shape memory alloys via nano-precipitation strengthening.
Method· Cu-Al-Mn-Ti shape memory alloys were fabricated by selective laser melting, with TiAl nano-precipitates forming a dual strengthening network.
Result· The alloy achieved a tensile strength of 829.07 MPa at room temperature and 883.68 MPa at 200°C, a ~30% improvement over existing quaternary Cu-based SMAs.
Validation· Mechanical performance was validated through tensile tests at room temperature and 200°C.

Early Mortality Prediction in ICU Patients with Hypertensive Kidney Disease Using Interpretable Machine Learning

Yong Si, Junyi Fan, Li Sun, Shuheng Chen, Minoo Ahmadi et al. arXiv preprint 2025-07-25 link
相关性: 该论文专注于ICU高血压肾病患者的死亡率预测，与FEA替代模型或生物力学设计无关。
边界: This paper is purely clinical ML with no connection to finite element analysis, surrogate modeling, or implant biomechanics.
动机· 为ICU高血压肾病患者建立早期死亡风险预测工具
方法· 基于MIMIC-IV数据库，用CatBoost和SHAP构建可解释风险模型
结果· 模型AUROC达0.88，且通过DREAM算法量化预测不确定性
验证· 使用独立测试集和五折交叉验证评估模型性能
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop an early risk prediction tool for 30-day mortality in ICU patients with hypertensive kidney disease.
Method· A machine learning framework using CatBoost with feature selection and SHAP interpretability, integrating DREAM for uncertainty estimation.
Result· CatBoost achieved an AUROC of 0.88 on the test set with meaningful predictors validated via SHAP and ALE plots.
Validation· Stratified five-fold cross-validation and an independent test set from MIMIC-IV v2.2 were used for performance evaluation.
