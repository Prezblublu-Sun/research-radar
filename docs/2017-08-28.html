Three‐Dimensional Stiff Cellular Structures With Negative Poisson's Ratio

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Dong Li, Jie Ma, Liang Dong, Roderic S. Lakes physica status solidi (b) 2017-08-28 10.1002/pssb.201600785
单位: College of Sciences, Northeastern University Shenyang 110819 P.R. China
通讯: Dong Li @ College of Sciences, Northeastern University Shenyang 110819 P.R. China  |  Roderic S. Lakes @ Department of Engineering Physics, University of Wisconsin Madison WI 53706‐1687 USA
相关性: 该论文研究了具有负泊松比的3D多孔结构，但未涉及应力遮挡、疲劳性能或骨整合，与AM生物医学植入物方向关联较弱。
边界: This paper does not address stress shielding, stiffness matching, fatigue testing, or osseointegration, and the material is not biomedical-grade.
动机· 设计具有负泊松比的高刚度3D多孔结构
方法· 通过交替凹槽设计单元胞，结合FEM模拟与3D打印实验
结果· 泊松比可调至-0.958，结构刚度优于传统负泊松比泡沫
验证· 3D打印样品力学测试与FEM结果对比验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To design a stiff three-dimensional cellular structure with tunable negative Poisson's ratio for potential lightweight applications.
Method· A novel unit cell with alternating cuboid surface indents was designed, and its Poisson's ratio and Young's modulus were analyzed via finite element simulations and validated by 3D-printed samples.
Result· The Poisson's ratio could be tuned from positive to negative, reaching as low as -0.958, and the structure exhibited higher stiffness than re-entrant foams.
Validation· Experimental measurements on 3D-printed samples showed good agreement with finite element predictions.

N. Corrado, Nicolas Durrande, Marco Gherlone, James Hensman, Manuela Mattone et al. Journal of Vibration and Control 2017-08-28 10.1177/1077546317721418
单位: AECOM, Croydon, UK
通讯: Cecilia Surace @ Department of Structural, Geotechnical and Building Engineering, Politecnico di Torino, Torino, Italy
相关性: 该方法使用高斯过程回归定位梁结构中的裂纹，与用户关注的植入物设计优化和代理模型方向关联较弱。
边界: This paper focuses on structural damage detection, not on surrogate modeling for expensive simulation, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification in design optimization, or biomechanical implant applications.
动机· 利用振型导数不连续性定位梁结构中的裂纹位置。
方法· 采用高斯过程回归拟合振型数据，通过协方差函数允许非光滑点并用最大似然估计确定裂纹位置。
结果· 在数值和实验梁上成功定位单裂纹和多裂纹，对噪声和参数变化具有鲁棒性。
验证· 通过含切口的钢梁振动实验验证方法有效性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To locate cracks in beam-like structures by detecting discontinuities in mode-shape derivatives without direct differentiation.
Method· A Gaussian process regression with a non-smooth covariance function is applied to mode-shape data, and crack positions are identified via maximum likelihood estimation.
Result· The method accurately localizes single and multiple cracks in both simulated and experimental beams, showing robustness to noise and parameter variations.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed on a vibrating steel beam with a cut.
