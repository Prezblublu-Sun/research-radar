边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain, micromotion, or contact mechanics; it focuses on clinical treatment decision models for infection, not implant biomechanics.
动机· 为假体周围感染治疗策略选择提供决策支持。
方法· 构建非马尔可夫决策树和马尔可夫模型。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To facilitate the selection of optimal treatment strategies for periprosthetic joint infection after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A framework combining a non-Markovian decision tree and a general Markov model to capture patient states and treatment pathways.
Result· Demonstrated cost-effectiveness analysis and individualized outcome prediction using clinical data from a Russian orthopedic institute.
Validation· Validated on a dataset of patients with PJI from the R.R. Vreden Institute.
