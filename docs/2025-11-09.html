Motivation· To investigate the effects of laser process parameters on melt pool thermodynamics, surface morphology, and residual stress in LPBF of TiAl-based composites.
Method· A coupled discrete element method and computational fluid dynamics (DEM-CFD) approach was used to simulate single-track melt pool dynamics.
Result· Lower laser power and higher scanning speed reduce melt pool size and increase residual stress, while higher power and lower speed induce cracking due to recoil pressure instabilities.
Validation· The simulation model was validated by close agreement with experimental measurements of melt pool geometry and crack morphology.
