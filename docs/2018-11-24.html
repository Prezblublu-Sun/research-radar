Physics-Informed CoKriging: A Gaussian-Process-Regression-Based Multifidelity Method for Data-Model Convergence

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark
Xiu Yang, David Barajas-Solano, Guzel Tartakovsky, Alexandre Tartakovsky arXiv preprint 2018-11-24 10.1016/j.jcp.2019.06.041
相关性: 该论文提出的物理信息协同克里金方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模，但未涉及植入物设计或不确定性量化。
边界: No implant biomechanics, no contact mechanics, no patient-specific geometry, and no uncertainty quantification for design optimization.
动机· 降低多保真度建模中高斯过程超参数优化成本
方法· 结合物理信息克里金与协同克里金，构建多保真度代理模型
结果· 在函数重构和热传导问题中验证了精度与效率
验证· 数值基准测试（Branin函数、热传导、示踪剂扩散）
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce the optimization cost of hyperparameter inference in multifidelity Gaussian process regression by incorporating partial physical knowledge.
Method· Proposes Physics-Informed CoKriging (CoPhIK), which models low-fidelity data with physics-informed Kriging and the discrepancy with a parameterized GP, and integrates a greedy active learning algorithm.
Result· Demonstrates improved efficiency and accuracy in reconstructing the Branin function, a steady-state heat transport problem, and a conservative tracer distribution.
Validation· Validated on numerical benchmarks including the modified Branin function, heat transport, and tracer concentration problems.

单位: Department of Applied Mathematics and Statistics, Johns Hopkins University, Baltimore, MD,

相关性: 该论文提出了一种新的高斯过程先验，可提高代理模型的鲁棒性和变量选择能力，对生物力学设计中的昂贵仿真替代建模有参考价值。
边界: This paper focuses on statistical methodology for Gaussian process emulation and calibration without application to FEA or implant biomechanics, and lacks experimental validation or constraint handling.
动机· 提高高斯过程代理模型在仿真和校准中的鲁棒性与变量选择能力
方法· 提出联合鲁棒先验，结合参考先验优势并简化计算

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To improve the robustness and variable selection capability of Gaussian process emulators and calibrators for expensive computer models.
Method· Proposes a jointly robust prior that retains the tail decay properties of the reference prior while simplifying computation and enabling automatic input relevance determination.
Result· The jointly robust prior yields proper posteriors in calibration and outperforms the reference prior in both emulation accuracy and variable selection.
Validation· Theoretical proofs of posterior propriety and implementation in open-source R packages (RobustGaSP, RobustCalibration).

边界: This paper focuses on uncertainty and sensitivity analysis for biological modeling in carbon ion therapy, not on surrogate modeling for expensive FEA simulations or implant biomechanics.

Motivation· To quantify the impact of input uncertainties in biological models on RBE and RWD in carbon ion therapy.
Method· A variance-based Monte Carlo uncertainty and sensitivity analysis is applied to a carbon ion treatment plan.
Result· Biological uncertainties have a higher impact on RBE and RWD than physical dose uncertainties.
Validation· The method is validated on two carbon ion treatment plans with different biological models and tissue parameters.
