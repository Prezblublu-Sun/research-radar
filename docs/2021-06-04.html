相关性: 提出了一种改进的PINN方法求解非线性高阶PDE，可迁移至生物力学代理模型，但未涉及植入物设计或不确定性量化。
边界: Does not address implant design, biomechanics, or structural FEA surrogate; focuses on phase separation PDEs without uncertainty quantification.

结果· 在Cahn-Hilliard和Allen-Cahn方程上精度显著提升

English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the accuracy of physics-informed neural networks for strongly nonlinear and higher-order partial differential equations.
Method· Propose a backward-compatible PINN (bc-PINN) that sequentially trains a single network over successive time segments while satisfying previous solutions.
Result· bc-PINN achieves significant accuracy improvement on Cahn-Hilliard and Allen-Cahn equations with fewer collocation points.
Validation· Numerical benchmarking on phase separation and reaction-diffusion equations.

相关性: 该论文研究粒子群聚行为的模型学习，与有限元代理建模无直接关联。
边界: Does not address FEA surrogate modeling, expensive simulation sampling, or biomechanics/implant design constraints.
动机· 从数据中发现粒子群聚的个体交互规则。
方法· 用高斯过程非参数化建模径向交互函数，同时推断摩擦参数。

Validation· Numerical experiments on various swarming systems demonstrate effective learning from scarce noisy trajectory data.

Validation· The method is validated on several benchmark image datasets against offline and baseline methods.
