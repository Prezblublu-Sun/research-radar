Metrological characterization of porosity graded β-Ti21S triply periodic minimal surface cellular structure manufactured by laser powder bed fusion

单位: INSTM (Operative center: University of Trento), Trento, 38123, Italy
通讯: Lorena Emanuelli @ INSTM (Operative center: University of Trento), Trento, 38123, Italy
相关性: 该论文直接研究了可用于生物医用植入物的β钛合金多孔结构，通过LPBF制造并测试了弹性模量，与应力屏蔽问题相关。
边界: Does not include fatigue testing, surface roughness characterization, or osseointegration assessment.
动机· 评估新型β钛合金制造梯度多孔结构的可行性，以满足假体力学和生物学要求。
方法· 设计并LPBF制造了两种单元尺寸的TPMS梯度多孔结构，通过SEM和μ-CT表征，进行压缩测试和有限元模拟。
结果· 制造误差小于5%，弹性模量在4.1-10.7 GPa之间，与设计值吻合。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the feasibility of using a novel β-Ti21S alloy for fabricating functionally graded porous structures with smooth transitions for prosthetic applications.
Method· Two TPMS-based graded cellular structures with unit cell sizes of 2.5 mm and 4 mm were designed and fabricated via LPBF, characterized by SEM and μ-CT, and tested under compression with finite element simulation.
Result· The as-built structures showed less than 5% deviation in pore size and ligament thickness, and the stabilized elastic modulus ranged from 4.1 GPa to 10.7 GPa.
Validation· Compression tests and finite element analysis were used to validate the elastic properties of the fabricated structures.
