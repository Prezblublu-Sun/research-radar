单位: Sandia National Lab. (SNL-CA), Livermore, CA (United States)
通讯: Kevin Carlberg @ Sandia National Lab. (SNL-CA), Livermore, CA (United States)
相关性: 该论文提出的保拉格朗日结构模型降阶方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模，但未涉及植入物设计或不确定性量化。
边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, stress shielding, or uncertainty quantification; it focuses on structural dynamics with Rayleigh damping and lacks biomedical context.
动机· 现有非线性模型降阶方法破坏拉格朗日结构，导致能量不守恒和数值不稳定。
方法· 通过矩阵gappy POD和RBS近似拉格朗日量，再应用欧拉-拉格朗日方程导出降阶运动方程。
结果· 在参数化桁架问题上，该方法保持高精度和稳定性，同时显著降低计算时间。
验证· 参数化桁架结构动力学仿真验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Existing nonlinear model reduction techniques often break Lagrangian structure, leading to energy non-conservation and numerical instability.
Method· The method approximates the Lagrangian ingredients (metric, potential, dissipation, external force) using matrix gappy POD and reduced-basis sparsification, then derives reduced equations via the Euler-Lagrange equation.
Result· On a parameterized truss problem, the approach maintains high accuracy and stability while significantly reducing computation time compared to structure-breaking methods.
Validation· Numerical simulation of a parameterized truss structure with Rayleigh damping.
