Motivation· To investigate the feasibility of PNF-based gait rehabilitation in a patient with congenital pelvic malformation after THA.
Method· An 18-week PNF-based motor-control training program was administered to a 44-year-old male patient.
Result· The patient showed improvements beyond minimal clinically important differences in gait, strength, and range of motion.
Validation· Single case report with no control group or quantitative biomechanical validation.
