Compressed particle methods for expensive models with application in Astronomy and Remote Sensing

Luca Martino, Víctor Elvira, Javier López-Santiago, Gustau Camps-Valls arXiv preprint 2021-07-18 10.1109/TAES.2021.3061791
相关性: 该论文提出的压缩蒙特卡洛方法可降低昂贵模型的计算成本，适用于替代建模，但未涉及FEA或生物力学中的约束处理。
边界: The paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, constraint handling, or biomechanics applications; it focuses on Bayesian inference with compressed MC, not optimization with constraints.
动机· 降低复杂昂贵模型在贝叶斯推理中的计算成本
方法· 提出压缩蒙特卡洛方法，仅对精心选取的样本评估昂贵模型
结果· 理论支持与数值实验证明该方法在多个领域有效
验证· 天文和遥感应用的数值实验
English summary & terms
Motivation· To reduce the computational cost of Bayesian inference when evaluating complex and costly models.
Method· A novel compressed Monte Carlo approach is introduced that evaluates the expensive model only at well-chosen nodes selected via a compressed MC scheme.
Result· Theoretical results and numerical experiments in astronomy and remote sensing demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.
Validation· Validation is performed through numerical experiments on real-world applications in astronomy and satellite remote sensing.

Biofabrication of Cell-Laden Gelatin Methacryloyl Hydrogels with Incorporation of Silanized Hydroxyapatite by Visible Light Projection

Jimmy J.–M. Su, Chih‐Hsin Lin, Hsuan Chen, Shyh‐Yuan Lee, Yuan‐Min Lin Polymers 2021-07-18 10.3390/polym13142354
单位: Institute of Oral Biology, School of Dentistry, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, Taipei 112, Taiwan
通讯: Yuan‐Min Lin @ Department of Dentistry, School of Dentistry, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, Taipei 112, Taiwan
相关性: 该论文主要关注水凝胶材料改进和可见光生物打印，未涉及任何人工智能方法。
边界: This paper does not incorporate any AI/ML into the bioprinting workflow; it is purely material and process development without data-driven modeling or automation.
动机· 改善GelMA水凝胶力学性能以支持骨组织再生。
方法· 将硅烷化羟基磷灰石掺入GelMA，并用可见光投影进行光聚合。
结果· 复合水凝胶力学增强、低细胞毒性、促进成骨基因表达。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To overcome the poor mechanical properties of GelMA hydrogels for bone tissue engineering applications.
Method· Silanized hydroxyapatite was incorporated into GelMA and crosslinked using visible light projection lithography.
Result· The composite hydrogel showed enhanced mechanical strength, low cytotoxicity, and improved osteogenic gene expression.
Validation· In vitro cell culture with MG63 cells and hBMSCs was used to evaluate biocompatibility and osteogenesis.

Microstructure effects on the machinability behaviour of Ti6Al4V produced by Selective Laser Melting and Electron Beam Melting process

Samuel Milton, Olivier Rigo, Sebastien LeCorre, Antoine Morandeau, Raveendra Siriki et al. Materials Science and Engineering A 2021-07-18 10.1016/j.msea.2021.141773
单位: Laboratoire de Mécanique Gabriel Lamé, Polytech Tours, Université François-Rabelais, Tours, France
通讯: Samuel Milton @ Laboratoire de Mécanique Gabriel Lamé, Polytech Tours, Université François-Rabelais, Tours, France
相关性: 该论文研究SLM/EBM制备Ti6Al4V的切削加工性，而非植入物力学性能或骨整合，与用户研究方向不相关。
边界: This paper investigates chip morphology and machinability of additive-manufactured Ti6Al4V, not implant-related porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration.
动机· 研究SLM/EBM制备Ti6Al4V的微观结构对切削加工性的影响
方法· 对比SLM和EBM试样的微观结构并分析切削力、切屑形态和表面完整性
结果· 发现SLM试样因α′马氏体组织导致切削力更高、切屑更不连续
验证· 切削实验结合显微组织表征验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand how the as-built microstructures from SLM and EBM affect the machinability of Ti6Al4V.
Method· Cutting tests were performed on SLM and EBM fabricated Ti6Al4V, measuring cutting forces, chip morphology, and surface roughness, correlated with microstructural analysis.
Result· SLM samples with acicular α′ martensite exhibited higher cutting forces and more segmented chips compared to EBM samples with lamellar α+β.
Validation· Experimental machining tests with microstructural characterization.

相关性: 该论文涉及差分隐私和贝叶斯神经网络的不确定性量化，但与FEA替代模型或植入物设计无关。
边界: No FEA validation, no surrogate modeling, no implant mechanics, no simulation sampling efficiency, and no constraint handling.
动机· 探索贝叶斯神经网络在差分隐私框架下的隐私-准确性权衡。
方法· 提出三种DP-BNN方法：DP-SGLD、DP-BBP和DP-MC Dropout。

English summary & terms
Motivation· This paper investigates the underexplored trade-off between privacy and accuracy in Bayesian neural networks under differential privacy.
Method· Three differentially private Bayesian neural networks (DP-SGLD, DP-BBP, DP-MC Dropout) are proposed, each capturing weight uncertainty differently.
Result· DP-SGLD achieves remarkable accuracy under strong privacy guarantees, revealing a new trade-off between privacy and reliability.
Validation· Extensive experiments compare privacy guarantee, prediction accuracy, uncertainty quantification, calibration, computation speed, and generalizability.

Fully Automated Machine Learning Pipeline for Echocardiogram Segmentation

Hang Duong Thi Thuy, Tuan Nguyen Minh, Phi Nguyen Van, Long Tran Quoc arXiv preprint 2021-07-18 link
相关性: 该论文专注于超声心动图分割的自动化机器学习流水线，与FEA代理模型无关。
边界: This paper focuses on medical image segmentation for echocardiograms and does not address surrogate modeling, finite element analysis, or any aspect of computational mechanics relevant to implant or biomechanics design.
动机· 减少超声心动图分割深度模型的标注成本和设计时间。
方法· 提出结合主动学习和神经架构搜索的自动化流水线。
结果· 仅用原始数据集五分之二达到同等精度，自动搜索模型性能与手工设计相当。
验证· 在超声心动图数据集上验证分割精度。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To reduce the annotation cost and model design time for deep learning segmentation of echocardiograms.
Method· An automated pipeline combining active learning for efficient labeling and neural architecture search for model design.
Result· Achieved equivalent segmentation accuracy with only two-fifths of the original training data and automatically searched models matched hand-designed ones.
Validation· Validated on an echocardiogram dataset using Intersection over Union (IoU) accuracy.
