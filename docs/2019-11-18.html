Bone-inspired microarchitectures achieve enhanced fatigue life

单位: Meinig School of Biomedical Engineering, Cornell University, Ithaca, NY 14853;
通讯: Ashley M. Torres @ Meinig School of Biomedical Engineering, Cornell University, Ithaca, NY 14853;
相关性: 该论文直接研究骨启发微结构在循环载荷下的疲劳寿命，与用户关注的LPBF制造多孔植入物的疲劳性能高度相关。
边界: The paper does not involve LPBF fabrication, surface roughness, or osseointegration; it focuses on cancellous bone-inspired microarchitectures and their fatigue behavior using additive manufacturing (likely selective laser melting) but not specifically biomedical implant context.
动机· 微结构材料在循环载荷下易疲劳失效，需提升耐久性。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Microarchitectured materials need to resist cyclic loading for durable applications, but the relationship between microstructure and fatigue life is poorly understood.

Result· A common design strategy to enhance stiffness and strength significantly reduces fatigue life due to increased stress concentrations.
Validation· Fatigue experiments on 3D-printed Ti-6Al-4V lattice specimens with different architectures were conducted and compared to simulations.
