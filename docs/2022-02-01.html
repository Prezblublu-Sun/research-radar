Method· A split-mouth study on 10 patients compared osseodensification using Densah burs versus conventional osteotomy, with CBCT imaging preoperatively and at 3 and 6 months post-implant placement.
Result· Osseodensification significantly increased radiographic bone density and maintained bone expansion for 6 months, but crestal bone levels showed no statistical difference between techniques.
Validation· Clinical CBCT imaging at multiple time points with statistical analysis (paired t-test, Mann-Whitney U test).
