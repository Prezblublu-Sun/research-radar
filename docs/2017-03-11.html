Order of experimentation and advisability of corner measurements

Julian Belz, Oliver Nelles at - Automatisierungstechnik 2017-03-11 10.1515/auto-2016-0112
单位: Universität Siegen, Department Maschinenbau, Institut für Mechanik und Regelungstechnik – Mechatronik, Paul-Bonatz-Str. 9–11, 57068 Siegen Germany
通讯: Julian Belz @ Universität Siegen, Department Maschinenbau, Institut für Mechanik und Regelungstechnik – Mechatronik, Paul-Bonatz-Str.
相关性: 该论文讨论实验设计中的角点测量和实验顺序，与FEA代理模型或生物力学设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on design of experiments for general regression, not on surrogate modeling for expensive simulations, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification, or implant/biomechanics applications.
动机· 研究实验设计中角点测量和实验顺序对模型质量的影响。
方法· 比较添加角点测量到空间填充设计的效果，并提出两种实验顺序确定方法。
结果· 角点测量通常损害模型质量；新顺序方法优于随机序列，略逊于主动学习。
验证· 通过数值基准测试验证模型泛化性能。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the advisability of corner measurements and the optimal order of experimentation for improving model quality in design of experiments.
Method· Two new methods for determining the order of experimentation are proposed and compared with an active learning strategy and random sequences, while the effect of adding corner points to space-filling designs is evaluated.
Result· Corner measurements generally harm model quality, and the proposed order methods significantly outperform random sequences while being slightly inferior to active learning.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks are used to assess generalization performance of the resulting models.
