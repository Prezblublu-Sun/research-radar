Uncertainty Quantification of Composite Structures with Defects using Multilevel Monte Carlo Simulations

Richard Butler, Timothy Dodwell, Raphael T. Haftka, Nam Ho Kim, Tatiana Kim et al. 2015-01-02 10.2514/6.2015-1598
单位: University of Bath
通讯: Richard Butler @ University of Bath
相关性: 该论文的多层级蒙特卡洛方法可用于生物力学植入物设计中的不确定性量化，但缺乏对约束处理和植入物特定问题的验证。
边界: This paper does not address constrained optimization, implant-specific biomechanics, or surrogate modeling for FEA; it focuses on uncertainty quantification for composite structures using multilevel Monte Carlo.
动机· 量化复合材料制造缺陷对结构性能的影响，降低计算成本。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify the impact of manufacturing defects on structural performance of composite materials while reducing computational cost.
Method· A multilevel Monte Carlo method is introduced with theoretical complexity comparison and a practical algorithm.
Result· Up to 152-fold computational speed-ups are achieved, making previously intractable problems feasible.
Validation· Numerical experiments on a composite wing skin panel with three defect types confirm the predicted gains.

边界: This paper does not address implant/biomechanics design, constraint handling, or uncertainty quantification in a multi-objective optimization context.
动机· 标准克里金模型在自适应采样数据下预测精度下降，需改进非平稳协方差建模。
方法· 提出局部优化协方差克里金，通过聚合多个局部平稳协方差结构近似全局非平稳性。
结果· LOC-Kriging在自适应采样下比标准克里金提供更可靠的预测和误差估计。
验证· 通过数值基准测试验证方法有效性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Standard Kriging with stationary covariance yields poor predictions when data are collected adaptively, motivating a locally adaptive covariance approach.
Method· A Locally Optimized Covariance Kriging (LOC-Kriging) method is proposed, which approximates global non-stationary covariance by constructing and aggregating multiple local stationary covariance structures via an optimization formulation.
Result· LOC-Kriging improves prediction accuracy and error bound reliability compared to stationary Kriging, especially under adaptive sampling.
Validation· The method is validated on numerical benchmark problems demonstrating improved efficiency and accuracy.
