边界: The paper focuses on constitutive model discovery for hyperelastic materials, not on implant stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics.

Motivation· Traditional calibration of constitutive models is computationally expensive, motivating the development of a fast surrogate for inverse material characterization.
Method· Two neural operator architectures (PANO and CANO) are proposed to directly map full-field displacement measurements and reaction forces to hyperelastic strain-energy density functions, with physical constraints embedded in the output space.
Result· The trained neural operators enable near-instantaneous material characterization from experimental data, showing robustness to noise, missing data, and varying discretizations.
Validation· Validation is performed on simulated datasets, including unseen data, noisy data, data with missing information, and data from different spatial discretizations and geometries.

相关性: 该论文提出的梯度增强样条维度分解方法可显著提升小样本下的代理模型精度与不确定性量化能力，对生物力学有限元代理建模具有直接迁移价值。
边界: This paper does not apply the method to biomedical or implant mechanics problems, nor does it address multi-objective optimization or constraint handling.
动机· 有限训练数据下高维代理模型系数估计病态，需提升小样本不确定性量化精度。
方法· 提出梯度增强样条维度分解，融合函数值与偏导数，通过岭回归与分组交叉验证平衡正则化。
结果· 在非光滑基准上中位NRMSE仅1.022%，优于克里金法8.731%，且标准差估计更准。
验证· 在二维指数函数、三参数动力系统及30维桁架结构上验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To address ill-conditioned coefficient estimation in high-dimensional surrogate models under limited training data for uncertainty quantification.
Method· Proposes gradient-enhanced spline dimensional decomposition (GE-SDD) that integrates function values and partial derivatives via ridge regression in probability-weighted Sobolev coordinates with grouped K-fold cross-validation.
Result· GE-SDD achieves a median NRMSE of 1.022% on a nonsmooth benchmark, outperforming Kriging (8.731%), and yields more accurate standard-deviation estimates on a 30-dimensional truss problem.
Validation· Validated on a 2D exponential function, a 3-parameter linear dynamical system, and a 30-dimensional 25-bar truss.
