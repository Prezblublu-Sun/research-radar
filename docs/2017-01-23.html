Patchwork Kriging for Large-scale Gaussian Process Regression

Chiwoo Park, Daniel Apley arXiv preprint 2017-01-23 link
相关性: 该方法提出了一种适用于大规模高斯过程回归的补丁克里金方法，可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模中，提高计算效率并提供不确定性量化。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, constraint handling, or biomechanical implant design contexts.

Validation· Validation is performed on three spatial and three higher-dimensional datasets.
