Trade-off between stress shielding and initial stability on an anatomical cementless stem shortening: in-vitro biomechanical study.

Go Yamako, Etsuo Chosa, Koji Totoribe, Shinji Watanabe, Takero Sakamoto Medical engineering & physics 2015-08-01 10.1016/j.medengphy.2015.05.017
单位: Organization for Promotion of Tenure Track, University of Miyazaki, 1-1 Gakuen-kibanadai-nishi, Miyazaki 889-2192, Japan. Electronic address: g.yamako@cc.miyazaki-u.ac.jp.
通讯: Go Yamako @ Organization for Promotion of Tenure Track, University of Miyazaki, 1-1 Gakuen-kibanadai-nishi, Miyazaki 889-2192, Japan
相关性: 该论文直接实验研究了短柄对股骨近端应力遮挡和初始稳定性的影响，与您的髋关节植入物研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not include finite element modeling or patient-specific geometry analysis, and only considers a simplified stem shortening without comprehensive Gruen zone analysis.
动机· 探究股骨柄缩短对应力遮挡与初始稳定性的权衡关系。
方法· 通过尸体股骨实验，比较三种长度解剖型非骨水泥柄的皮质应变和轴向位移/旋转。
结果· 50mm短柄提高近端载荷传递但微动增加，仍低于骨长入阈值。
验证· 实验力学测试：应变片和位移传感器测量。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To experimentally investigate the trade-off between stress shielding and initial stability when shortening an anatomical cementless femoral stem.
Method· In-vitro mechanical testing on cadaveric femurs implanted with stems of three lengths (120, 80, 50 mm), measuring cortical strains via tri-axial strain gauges and axial displacement/rotation via transducers under single-leg stance loading.
Result· The 50 mm stem increased proximal cortical strains relative to intact (31.0% in calcar region) but also increased axial displacement and rotation, though the displacement remained below the 150 µm threshold for bone ingrowth.
Validation· Experimental cadaveric mechanical testing with strain gauges and displacement transducers.
