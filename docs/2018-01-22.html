相关性: 该论文研究了骨质量、种植体设计和手术技术对初始稳定性的影响，但针对的是牙科种植体而非髋关节假体，不过其方法学（微动测量、参数化研究）对股骨柄稳定性研究有参考价值。
边界: This paper focuses on dental implants in polyurethane foam, not femoral stems in bone, and does not quantify stress shielding or micromotion in Gruen zones.

Uncertainty Quantification for Junction Temperature of Automotive LED With Die-Attach Layer Microstructure

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only simulation_case UQ
Jing Zhou, Xingming Long, Jugang He, F. Ren, Liang Fang IEEE Transactions on Device and Materials Reliability 2018-01-22 10.1109/tdmr.2018.2796072
单位: State Key Laboratory of Power Transmission Equipment and System Security and New Technology, Chongqing University, Chongqing, China
通讯: Jing Zhou @ State Key Laboratory of Power Transmission Equipment and System Security and New Technology, Chongqing University, Chong
相关性: 该论文涉及有限元建模与不确定性量化，但应用于LED结温而非生物力学或植入物设计，方法可迁移性有限。
边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, stress shielding, or surrogate modeling for structural optimization; it focuses on LED thermal reliability with no biomedical context.
动机· 量化汽车LED结温的不确定性，考虑焊料层微结构影响。
方法· 结合有限元建模、演化分离谱和蒙特卡洛模拟构建随机有限元模型。
结果· 焊料层温度-位移耦合主导结温，热功率载荷加剧含空洞焊料层的结温恶化。
验证· 通过三种LED模块的统计概率分布和失效风险预测进行验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify the uncertainty in junction temperature of automotive LEDs caused by die-attach layer microstructure and boundary conditions.
Method· A stochastic finite element framework combining evolution separation spectrum and Monte Carlo simulation is developed to model 3D LED structures with random die-attach layer microstructures.
Result· Temperature-mechanical displacement coupling of the die-attach layer significantly affects junction temperature, and thermal power loading increases failure risk for LEDs with voids.
Validation· Statistical probability distributions of junction temperature and failure risk predictions are compared across three LED module types.

边界: This paper does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or any biomechanical metrics; it is a clinical trial methodology study on pain management after hip/knee arthroplasty, with no implant design, FEA, or contact mechanics.
动机· 评估全髋/膝关节置换术后疼痛管理试验的偏倚和样本量随时间的变化。
方法· 基于两项系统综述的数据，计算累积偏倚评分并分析样本量趋势。

Motivation· To investigate the evolution of risk of bias and trial sample size in postoperative pain management RCTs after hip and knee arthroplasty over time.
Method· Data from two systematic reviews were analyzed using cumulative bias scores based on Cochrane domains and run/control charts to assess temporal trends.
Result· Risk of bias decreased from 2007 onward, mainly due to improved randomization and allocation concealment, while sample sizes remained unchanged.
Validation· Temporal trends were evaluated using run and control charts on 171 trials published between 1989 and 2016.
