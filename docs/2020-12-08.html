Research on porous titanium implants and its animal experiments

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Skim only animal Exp. validation
Chunyan Yao, Dongdong Chen, Zhongli Zheng, Qiangsheng Wang, Kaijie Fu Rapid Prototyping Journal 2020-12-08 10.1108/rpj-06-2019-0170
单位: Key Laboratory of E&M, Ministry of Education, Zhejiang University of Technology, Hangzhou, China
通讯: Chunyan Yao @ Key Laboratory of E&M, Ministry of Education, Zhejiang University of Technology, Hangzhou, China
相关性: 该论文直接涉及多孔钛植入物的LPBF制造、力学分析及动物实验，与AM生物医学方向高度相关。
边界: Does not report fatigue testing, surface roughness, or as-built morphology; stress shielding is not explicitly quantified.
动机· 获得多孔表面植入物以促进骨结合与稳定性。
方法· 设计三种孔隙率多孔钛植入物，SLM打印，ABAQUS分析强度，动物实验评估骨生长。
结果· 16%孔隙率植入物骨体积比最大，羟基磷灰石涂层促进骨形成。
验证· 通过动物实验（比格犬）和显微CT定量骨体积验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop porous-surfaced titanium implants that enhance osseointegration and mechanical stability.
Method· Designed implants with three porosities (16%, 21%, 32%), fabricated by selective laser melting, simulated stress via ABAQUS, and conducted in vivo animal experiments with hydroxyapatite coating.
Result· Implants with 16% porosity achieved the highest bone volume fraction (55.38%-79.86%), and hydroxyapatite coating further improved bone formation.
Validation· Validated through beagle dog animal experiments and micro-CT quantification of bone volume and total volume.

相关性: 该论文提出的基于机器学习的主动学习优化算法可加速仿真驱动设计，其方法可迁移至生物力学植入物设计中的昂贵仿真优化问题。
边界: This paper focuses on internal combustion engine design optimization, not on biomedical implant or biomechanics FEA surrogate modeling, and lacks uncertainty quantification and constraint handling beyond simple bounds.

Method· Proposes ActivO, a surrogate-based optimization algorithm that uses an ensemble of weak and strong learners in an active learning loop to explore promising regions and locate optima.
Result· ActivO reduces function evaluations by 80% compared to genetic algorithm in engine design optimization, achieving 1.9% energy savings.
Validation· Validated on a cosine mixture benchmark and a compression-ignition engine simulation case with constraints on pressure and emissions.
