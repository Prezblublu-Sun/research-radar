Bayesian neural networks for weak solution of PDEs with uncertainty quantification

Xiaoxuan Zhang, Krishna Garikipati arXiv preprint 2021-01-13 link
相关性: 该论文提出了一种基于贝叶斯神经网络的PDE弱解求解方法，具有不确定性量化能力，且可迁移至生物力学植入物设计与仿真替代建模。
边界: The paper does not specifically apply the method to implant or biomechanics design, and does not handle constrained multi-objective optimization.
动机· 提高PDE求解效率，实现基于代理模型的高通量设计与决策。
方法· 利用离散残差和贝叶斯神经网络构建无标签的物理约束损失函数。
结果· 所提方法能高效求解稳态扩散、线性和非线性弹性问题，并量化不确定性。
验证· 在数值基准问题上验证了模型精度和不确定性量化能力。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a computationally efficient surrogate PDE solver with uncertainty quantification for high-throughput design and decision-making.
Method· A physics-constrained Bayesian neural network is trained using the discretized residual of PDEs as the loss function, employing an encoder-decoder architecture that treats boundary conditions as inputs.
Result· The method accurately and efficiently solves steady-state diffusion, linear elasticity, and nonlinear elasticity problems, and provides both epistemic and aleatoric uncertainty estimates.
Validation· The framework is validated on numerical benchmarks, demonstrating interpolation and extrapolation capabilities for unseen boundary conditions.
