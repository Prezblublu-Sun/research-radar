英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate clinical and radiological outcomes of direct anterior approach for Crowe grade III DDH in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Five DDH patients underwent DAA-THA; clinical scores and radiographic parameters were recorded.
Result· HHS and JHEQ scores significantly improved; no dislocations or revisions; leg length discrepancy improved.
Validation· Clinical follow-up and radiographic assessment.
