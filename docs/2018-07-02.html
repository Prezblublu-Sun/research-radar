Motivation· To quantify blood loss and identify risk factors in patients undergoing 2-stage exchange arthroplasty for infected total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective review of clinical data comparing blood loss between first and second stage procedures.
Result· Second-stage revision resulted in significantly greater blood loss than the first stage, necessitating enhanced transfusion protocols.
Validation· Statistical analysis of clinical patient data.
