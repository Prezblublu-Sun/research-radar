English summary & terms
Motivation· To overcome stress-shielding effects of homogeneous bone implants by fabricating gradient porous scaffolds that mimic the mechanical transition from cortical to cancellous bone.

Result· The gradient Diamond scaffold achieved a compressive strength of 215.7 MPa and elastic modulus of 4.2 GPa, matching natural bone, with 25% higher permeability and >95% cell viability.
Validation· Mechanical compression testing and in vitro cell proliferation/viability assays (CCK-8, live-dead staining).

Improving scan-power coordination in selective laser melting through master-slave cross-coupled iterative learning control

单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, No. 1001, Ta Hsueh Rd., East Dist, Hsinchu City, 300093, Taiwan, ROC
通讯: Ruei-Yu Huang @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, No. 1001, Ta Hsueh Rd., East Dist, Hsinc
相关性: 该论文关注SLM工艺中的扫描-功率协调控制，未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡、疲劳或生物医学植入物相关研究。
边界: This paper focuses on control system coordination in SLM, not on porous structure design, stress shielding, mechanical testing, or biomedical implant applications.

English summary & terms
Motivation· Mismatched dynamic responses between scanning and power systems in SLM degrade synchronization and geometric accuracy.
Method· A master-slave cross-coupled iterative learning control framework with disturbance observers and robust feedback controllers is proposed.
Result· The method achieves 31.65% reduction in scanning tracking errors, 22.12% in contour errors, and 90.07% in power tracking errors.
Validation· Experimental validation on an EtherCAT-based SLM platform.

Result· Higher scanning speed led to grain refinement and improved corrosion resistance in simulated body fluid, with optimal performance at 1200 mm/s in XOY orientation.
Validation· Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy and potentiodynamic polarization in 3.5% NaCl and simulated body fluid.
