Validation· Evaluated on multiple standard PDE benchmark datasets, including steady-state and time-dependent problems on complex geometries.

Motivation· To compare patient-reported outcome measures after hip and knee arthroplasty between private and public hospitals in the Netherlands.
Method· Cross-sectional analysis of 170,150 procedures from the Dutch Arthroplasty Register using mixed-effect models adjusted for confounders.
Result· No clinically relevant differences in PROMs were found between private and public hospitals.
Validation· Large-scale registry data with statistical adjustment.
