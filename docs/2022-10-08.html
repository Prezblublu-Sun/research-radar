Aspirin/PLGA coated 3D-printed Ti-6Al-4V alloy modulate macrophage polarization to enhance osteoblast differentiation and osseointegration.

Yapeng You, Wanmeng Wang, Ying Li, Yunjia Song, Jian Jiao et al. Journal of materials science. Materials in medicine 2022-10-08 10.1007/s10856-022-06697-w
单位: School of Stomatology, Hospital of Stomatology, Tianjin Medical University, Tianjin, 300070, China.; The Affiliated Stomatology Hospital of Kunming Medical University, Kunming, Yunnan, 650100, China.
通讯: Ying Li @ School of Stomatology, Hospital of Stomatology, Tianjin Medical University, Tianjin, 300070, China. yingli@tmu.edu.cn.
相关性: 该论文虽涉及3D打印钛合金植入物表面改性，但聚焦于药物涂层免疫调控而非多孔结构或应力遮挡，与用户研究方向关联度较低。
边界: This paper does not address porous structure design, stiffness matching, stress shielding, or LPBF-specific mechanical characterization (fatigue, as-built morphology).
动机· 改善钛合金植入物骨整合性能
方法· 在3D打印Ti-6Al-4V表面涂覆阿司匹林/PLGA，免疫调控促进成骨
结果· 涂层促进M2极化、成骨分化，大鼠体内骨整合增强
验证· SEM/XPS/接触角表征、体外细胞实验、大鼠植入实验
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance osseointegration of titanium alloy implants by combining 3D-printed micro-topography with immunomodulatory drug coating.
Method· Aspirin/PLGA coating was applied to 3D-printed Ti-6Al-4V substrates, and their effects on macrophage polarization and osteoblast differentiation were evaluated in vitro and in vivo.
Result· The coating promoted M2 macrophage polarization, enhanced osteoblast proliferation and mineralization in vitro, and improved osseointegration in a rat femoral implantation model.
Validation· Surface characterization (SEM, XPS, contact angle), in vitro cell assays, and in vivo rat implantation with push-out test, micro-CT, and histology.

Effectiveness of photofunctionalized titanium alloy on osseointegration in rats with type 2 diabetes.

Shengdao Jin, Yuji Yamamoto, Yoshifumi Harada, Sho Kaneko, Kazuki Oishi et al. Journal of orthopaedic surgery and research 2022-10-08 10.1186/s13018-022-03346-4
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Hirosaki University Graduate School of Medicine, 5 Zaifu-cho, Hirosaki, Aomori, 036-8562, Japan.
通讯: Yuji Yamamoto @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Hirosaki University Graduate School of Medicine, 5 Zaifu-cho, Hirosaki, Aomori, 036-8
相关性: 该论文研究与3D打印生物医学材料方向无关，未涉及LPBF制造、力学性能测试或多孔结构。
边界: This paper does not involve additive manufacturing, LPBF fabrication, porous structure design, mechanical testing (static or fatigue), or stress shielding analysis.
动机· 研究紫外线光功能化钛合金对2型糖尿病大鼠骨整合的作用
方法· 使用Ti6Al4V圆柱植入物，对正常和糖尿病大鼠进行UV处理，通过microCT和组织学分析骨密度和骨-植入物接触
结果· UV处理显著增加早期骨整合，但对骨体积无显著影响
验证· 大鼠动物模型实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of UV photofunctionalization on Ti6Al4V implant osseointegration in a rat model of type 2 diabetes.
Method· Cylindrical Ti6Al4V implants were UV-treated and implanted in normal and diabetic rats, followed by microCT and histological analysis.
Result· UV treatment significantly increased bone-implant contact at early time points in both normal and diabetic rats, but did not affect bone volume.
Validation· In vivo animal study using Sprague Dawley and diabetic Torii rats.
