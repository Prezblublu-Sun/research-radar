Modeling of silver transport in cubic SiC: Integrating molecular dynamics, bounds averaging, and uncertainty quantification

边界: This paper focuses on nuclear fuel material transport, with no connection to surrogate modeling for FEA, optimization, or biomechanical implant design.
动机· 理解银在SiC中的传输机制以解决核燃料安全问题
方法· 结合分子动力学和边界平均方法建立有效扩散率模型，并通过贝叶斯推断量化不确定性

English summary & terms
Motivation· To resolve unresolved silver transport pathways through SiC in TRISO fuel particles for nuclear safety.
Method· A physics-informed model integrates molecular dynamics for grain boundaries, bounds averaging, and Bayesian inference for uncertainty quantification.
Result· The homogenized model overpredicts diffusivity; a first-principles reversible trapping correction at nano-pores reproduces observed transport.
Validation· Model predictions are compared with experimental diffusivity data, and sensitivity analysis identifies dominant parameters.

单位: Nuffield Department of Orthopaedics, Rheumatology and Musculoskeletal Sciences, University of Oxford, Oxford, UK
通讯: Sophie Cole @ Nuffield Department of Orthopaedics, Rheumatology and Musculoskeletal Sciences, University of Oxford, Oxford, UK
相关性: 该论文聚焦于全髋关节置换术后的活动结果预测工具，完全不涉及生物力学建模或植入物力学分析。
边界: The paper does not quantify femoral stress/strain, micromotion, or contact mechanics; it is a clinical registry study without any biomechanical FE modeling or implant design evaluation.
动机· 开发基于注册数据的工具，告知患者术后活动结果。
方法· 使用条件推理树根据术前预测因子创建活动结果聚类。
结果· 术后活动显著改善，不同时间点预测因子重要性不同。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a data-driven tool from a hip arthroplasty registry to inform patients about expected activity outcomes after surgery.
Method· Conditional inference trees were applied to baseline predictors to generate homogeneous patient clusters for five activity outcomes at 1, 5, and 10 years post-THA.
Result· Activity outcomes improved substantially after surgery, with the most significant gains at 1 year, and predictor importance varied over time for the same outcome.
Validation· The tool was built and validated on a large registry cohort of 6,836 primary elective THA procedures with up to 10-year follow-up.

单位: Faculty of Dentistry Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, Istanbul University, Prof. Dr. Cavit Orhan Tutengil Street No:4 Vezneciler Fatih , Istanbul, Turkey. betulgedik@istanbul.edu.tr.
通讯: Betul Gedik @ Faculty of Dentistry Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, Istanbul University, Prof. Dr. Cavit Orhan Tutengil S
相关性: 该论文研究下颌骨钛网厚度对力学性能的影响，与髋关节植入物及股骨假体完全无关。
边界: This paper focuses on mandibular bone regeneration with titanium mesh, with no hip implant, femoral stem, stress shielding, or micromotion analysis.
动机· 探究钛网厚度对引导骨再生中力学性能的影响。
方法· 基于CBCT重建下颌骨模型，对不同厚度钛网进行有限元分析。
结果· 0.3mm钛网应力最小，力学性能最佳；0.1mm钛网应力集中明显。
验证· 有限元模拟验证不同厚度钛网的应力分布。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of titanium mesh thickness on mechanical strength and stress shielding in guided bone regeneration.
Method· Three-dimensional mandibular models were reconstructed from CBCT and FEA simulations were performed for meshes of 0.1, 0.2, and 0.3 mm thickness under 30 N vertical load.
Result· The 0.3 mm mesh exhibited the lowest stress on mesh and graft, while the 0.1 mm mesh showed high stress concentrations and risk of plastic deformation.
Validation· The study used finite element analysis as the sole validation method, without experimental or clinical confirmation.
