英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the clinical outcomes of primary total hip arthroplasty via the direct anterior approach in a single surgeon series.
Method· Retrospective analysis of 146 consecutive patients over 10 years, collecting demographics, functional scores, and radiographic parameters.
Result· The direct anterior approach demonstrated safety with low complication rates and consistent restoration of hip biomechanical parameters.
Validation· No biomechanical validation; only clinical follow-up with Oxford Hip Score and radiographic measurements.
