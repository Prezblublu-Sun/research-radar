Vacuum heat treatments of titanium porous structures

Shaaz Ghouse, Reece N. Oosterbeek, Aisha Tayub Mehmood, Filippo Vecchiato, David Dye et al. Additive manufacturing 2021-08-20 10.1016/j.addma.2021.102262
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Imperial College London, London SW7 2AZ, United Kingdom
通讯: Jonathan R.T. Jeffers @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Imperial College London, London SW7 2AZ, United Kingdom
相关性: 该论文研究了真空热处理对Ti-6Al-4V多孔结构疲劳性能的影响，与生物医用植入物的疲劳耐久性直接相关。
边界: This paper does not include osseointegration testing, surface roughness characterization, or explicit stress shielding analysis for hip implants.
动机· 改善增材制造Ti-6Al-4V多孔结构的疲劳性能。
方法· 采用真空热处理（亚转变和超转变温度）调控显微组织。
结果· 超转变热处理使疲劳强度提高75%，弹性模量增大。
验证· 通过微结构表征和力学测试验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the fatigue resistance of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V porous structures for biomedical applications.
Method· Vacuum heat treatments at sub-transus (920°C) and super-transus (1050°C, 1200°C) temperatures were applied to modify the microstructure.
Result· Super-transus heat treatment at 1200°C increased the fatigue strength by 75% compared to as-built samples, while also raising the elastic modulus due to strut sintering.
Validation· Validation was performed through microstructural analysis (SEM, EBSD) and mechanical testing (elastic modulus and fatigue tests).

单位: Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile, Departamento de Ingeniería Mecánica y Metalúrgica, Av. Vicuña Mackenna, Santiago, Chile and Study Centre on Advanced and Sustainable Manufacturing, University

边界: This paper is a broad review of SLM technology for medical devices and does not present any specific experimental fabrication, mechanical testing (fatigue or static), stress shielding analysis, or osseointegration data.
动机· 综述SLM在医疗领域的关键方面及研究现状
方法· 系统回顾金属SLM工艺的材料、建模、监测与优化文献

English summary & terms
Motivation· This review aims to summarize key aspects of selective laser melting (SLM) for metallic medical devices, including materials, process modeling, monitoring, and optimization.
Method· The paper systematically reviews the literature on SLM of metallic systems within the healthcare domain, covering established and novel materials, phenomenological and statistical models, online monitoring techniques, and optimization approaches.
Result· Identified research gaps include the need for further investigation of copper alloys for antibacterial applications, limited adoption of artificial intelligence for process optimization, and insufficient development of real-time monitoring methods.
Validation· No experimental validation is performed; this is a literature review.

3D bioprinting of an electroactive and self‐healing polysaccharide hydrogels

单位: The Engineering Research Center of 3D Printing and Bio‐fabrication Beijing Institute of Graphic Communication Beijing China
通讯: Yulong Wang @ The Engineering Research Center of 3D Printing and Bio‐fabrication Beijing Institute of Graphic Communication Beijing C
相关性: 该论文未涉及人工智能在生物打印中的应用，与用户研究方向无关。

动机· 制备具有自愈合和电活性的多糖水凝胶用于3D生物打印。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop electroactive and self-healing polysaccharide hydrogels suitable for 3D bioprinting.
Method· Composite hydrogels were synthesized via Schiff-base reaction between oxidized konjac glucomannan and polyethyleneimine, with carbon nanotubes added for electroactivity.
Result· The hydrogels exhibited shear-thinning, self-healing, and conductivity, enabling 3D bioprinting.
Validation· Rheological, electrical conductivity, and printability tests were performed.
