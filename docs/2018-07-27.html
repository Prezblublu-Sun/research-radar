单位: Department of Technologies and Structures, Technical University of Liberec, Studentská 1402/2, Liberec 461 17, Czechia. Electronic address: petr.henys@tul.cz
通讯: Petr Henyš @ Department of Technologies and Structures, Technical University of Liberec, Studentská 1402/2, Liberec 461 17, Czechia.
相关性: 该论文研究髋臼杯固定而非股骨柄，但模态分析的方法可能间接用于评估植入物稳定性。
边界: This paper focuses on acetabular cup fixation, not femoral stem stress shielding or micromotion, and does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain in Gruen zones.

Validation· Numerical simulation with noise sensitivity analysis.
