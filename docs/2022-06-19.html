英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To provide quantitative biomechanical guidance for stem selection in revision total knee arthroplasty.
Method· A previously validated finite element model was used to analyze the effects of stem design parameters (length, shape, fixation technique, slots/flutes) on bone stress and fracture risk.
Result· Long stems caused stress shielding in distal bone, anatomical shapes and slots reduced bone stress, and cemented tibial stems decreased micromotions.
Validation· The study relied on a previously validated finite element model without new experimental validation.

Validation· Validation was performed through large-scale database matching and statistical comparison of THA incidence between cohorts.
