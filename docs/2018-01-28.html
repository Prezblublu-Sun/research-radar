Algorithmic Linearly Constrained Gaussian Processes

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Markus Lange-Hegermann arXiv preprint 2018-01-28 link
相关性: 该论文提出了一种将线性微分方程约束嵌入高斯过程的方法，可迁移到生物力学FEA代理模型中处理物理约束。
边界: No application to FEA surrogate modeling, no biomedical or implant context, no uncertainty quantification for design optimization.
动机· 将线性微分方程约束嵌入高斯过程先验，解决物理信息融合问题。
方法· 利用Gröbner基参数化方程解空间，通过推前高斯过程构造满足约束的先验。
结果· 成功应用于Maxwell方程组等物理系统，融合噪声观测与代数计算。
验证· 在多个物理和几何示例上验证了方法的有效性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To incorporate linear differential equation constraints into Gaussian process priors for physically consistent modeling.
Method· Parametrizes the solution space of linear PDEs using Gröbner bases and constructs a push-forward Gaussian process prior.
Result· Demonstrated on several examples including Maxwell's equations, enabling combination of noisy observations with exact algebraic constraints.
Validation· Validated on benchmark problems from physics, geomathematics, and control.

Less is more: sampling chemical space with active learning

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Background only numerical_benchmark UQ
Justin S. Smith, Ben Nebgen, Nicholas Lubbers, Olexandr Isayev, Adrian E. Roitberg arXiv preprint 2018-01-28 10.1063/1.5023802
相关性: 该论文专注于化学空间中的主动学习，与生物力学或植入物设计的有限元代理建模无直接关联。
边界: No FEA surrogate modeling, no implant biomechanics, no constraint handling or uncertainty quantification for design optimization.
动机· 自动生成训练通用机器学习势能的数据集。
方法· 基于查询委员会主动学习，自动采样预测不可靠的化学空间区域。
结果· 主动学习仅用10%数据即达到与全数据集相当的精度。
验证· 在COMP6基准数据集上验证能量和力预测精度。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To automate the generation of training data for universal machine learning potentials in molecular energetics.
Method· An active learning algorithm using Query by Committee to sample regions of chemical space where the ensemble of potentials disagrees.
Result· Active learning achieves accuracy comparable to the full dataset with only 10% of the data, and outperforms it with 25%.
Validation· Validation on the COMP6 benchmark dataset of diverse organic molecules, comparing energy and force predictions.

相关性: 该论文研究遥感图像分类中的主动学习采样策略，与有限元代理建模或生物力学设计无关。
边界: This paper focuses on remote sensing image classification, not on surrogate modeling, finite element analysis, or biomechanical design optimization.
动机· 解决遥感图像分割中混合对象导致分类不稳定问题
方法· 提出基于信息熵和主动学习的采样策略，结合零熵与非零熵对象

Result· The optimal ratio of zero- to non-zero-entropy objects lies between 0.2 and 0.6, improving classification stability and accuracy.
Validation· Tested on three groups of high-resolution remote sensing images.

单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Brigham and Women's Hospital, Boston, MA;
通讯: Michael J. Weaver @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Brigham and Women's Hospital, Boston, MA;
相关性: 该论文比较了全髋关节置换术与内固定治疗髋臼骨折的再手术率，未涉及股骨柄假体的应力遮挡或微动分析。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the reoperation rate and functional outcomes of acute total hip arthroplasty versus open reduction internal fixation for displaced acetabular fractures in elderly patients.
Method· A retrospective review of 70 patients (33 ORIF, 37 THA) with mean 22-month follow-up, assessing reoperation rates, Harris Hip Scores, and SF-36.
Result· THA showed a lower reoperation rate (14% vs 30%) and better pain scores, though not statistically significant, with a high conversion rate from ORIF to THA within 2 years.
Validation· Clinical retrospective study with patient interviews and radiographic examination.

通讯: Christopher Lee @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, R Adams Cowley Shock Trauma Center, Baltimore, MD  |  Eric E. Johnson @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, University of California Los Angeles, Los Angeles, CA; and
相关性: 该论文关注髋臼后壁骨折的弹簧板固定技术，未涉及股骨柄假体或应力遮挡分析。
边界: This paper does not quantify femoral stem stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics; it is a clinical retrospective study on acetabular fracture fixation.
动机· 评估弹簧板辅助固定粉碎性髋臼后壁骨折的疗效。

Motivation· To evaluate the clinical outcomes of spring plates as adjunctive fixation for comminuted posterior wall acetabular fractures with marginal fragments.

Result· Excellent radiographic grades in 42 patients, no hardware complications, and 4 patients required total hip arthroplasty due to advanced arthritis.
Validation· Clinical follow-up and radiographic evaluation using Matta criteria.
