Heat Source Modeling in Selective Laser Melting

Elham Mirkoohi, Daniel E. Seivers, Hamid Garmestani, Steven Y. Liang Materials 2019-06-26 10.3390/ma12132052
单位: Woodruff School of Mechanical Engineering, Georgia Institute of Technology, Atlanta, GA 30332, USA
通讯: Elham Mirkoohi @ Woodruff School of Mechanical Engineering, Georgia Institute of Technology, Atlanta, GA 30332, USA
相关性: 该论文侧重于SLM热源建模，未涉及多孔结构、力学测试或生物医学植入物评估，与用户研究方向关联度低。
边界: This paper only covers heat source modeling for SLM temperature prediction, with no LPBF fabrication of porous implants, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), no stress shielding or stiffness matching analysis, and no surface morphology or osseointegration characterization.
动机· 建立SLM温度场解析模型，预测熔池尺寸和热应力
方法· 采用五种热源模型（稳态/瞬态点热源、半椭圆/双椭圆/均匀热源）求解三维热传导方程
结果· 模型预测的熔池几何与实验测量一致，讨论了工艺参数对球化效应的影响
验证· 利用独立实验的熔池几何数据验证模型准确性
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop analytical heat source models for predicting three-dimensional temperature fields in selective laser melting, enabling estimation of molten pool dimensions and thermal stress.
Method· Five heat source models—steady-state moving point, transient moving point, semi-elliptical moving, double elliptical moving, and uniform moving—were solved using separation of variables or Green's functions, incorporating temperature-dependent thermal properties and latent heat.
Result· The predicted temperature fields and melt pool geometries from all models matched experimental measurements, and the influence of process parameters on the balling effect was discussed.
Validation· The models were validated by comparing predicted melt pool dimensions with experimental data from independent laser melting experiments.
