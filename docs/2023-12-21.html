单位: Department of Industrial Design Engineering, Gazi University, Ankara, 06500, Turkey
通讯: Oğulcan Eren @ Department of Industrial Design Engineering, Gazi University, Ankara, 06500, Turkey
相关性: 这篇论文利用深度学习和SLM制造技术优化晶格结构的力学性能，虽然不直接涉及生物医学应用，但其方法可用于设计具有特定力学性能的骨科植入物多孔结构。
边界: This paper does not address stress shielding, osseointegration, or fatigue testing for biomedical implants; it uses aluminum alloy (not medical-grade titanium or biocompatible materials) and focuses on compressive/impact properties rather than fatigue or bone-implant interface.
动机· 利用深度学习优化SLM制造金属晶格结构的力学性能。

Validation· Mechanical testing (compression and impact) of SLM-fabricated AlSi10Mg unit cell samples.
