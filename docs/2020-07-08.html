Continuous and pulsed selective laser melting of Ti6Al4V lattice structures: Effect of post-processing on microstructural anisotropy and fatigue behaviour

High AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Read now mechanical_testing Exp. validation
K. Karami, Anne-Nynke Blok, L. Weber, S.M. Ahmadi, Roumen Petrov et al. Additive manufacturing 2020-07-08 10.1016/j.addma.2020.101433
单位: Faculty of Mechanical, Maritime, and Materials Engineering, Delft University of Technology (TU Delft), Mekelweg 2, 2628 CD, Delft, the Netherlands
通讯: Vera Popovich @ Faculty of Mechanical, Maritime, and Materials Engineering, Delft University of Technology (TU Delft), Mekelweg 2, 2628
相关性: 该论文直接研究了Ti6Al4V点阵结构的LPBF制造、后处理、微观结构各向异性及疲劳行为，与用户对AM生物医学植入物的研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not explicitly link porous structure to stress shielding or stiffness matching, nor does it report surface roughness, as-built morphology, or osseointegration.
动机· 探索连续与脉冲激光扫描模式对Ti6Al4V点阵结构微观结构、缺陷及疲劳性能的影响。
方法· 采用连续和脉冲激光选区熔化制备点阵结构，并施加HIP、喷砂、化学蚀刻等后处理。
结果· 连续激光模式缺陷更少、疲劳抗力更高；脉冲激光模式微观组织更均匀、各向同性更好。
验证· 通过准静态压缩和疲劳实验验证力学性能，结合微观组织表征。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effects of continuous versus pulsed laser scanning modes on the microstructure, internal imperfections, and surface morphology of Ti6Al4V lattice structures fabricated by laser powder bed fusion.
Method· Lattice structures were fabricated using continuous and pulsed laser modes, followed by post-processing treatments including hot isostatic pressing, sandblasting, and chemical etching.
Result· Continuous laser mode resulted in fewer imperfections and higher fatigue resistance, while pulsed laser mode produced a more homogeneous microstructure with equiaxed grains and isotropic behavior.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated through quasi-static compression and fatigue testing, supported by microstructural characterization.
