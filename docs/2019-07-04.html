英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Traditional surrogate models for fatigue analysis rely on simple inclusion geometries that fail to capture complex microstructural features.

Validation· Feasibility and efficiency are demonstrated through a fatigue analysis case study using FFT-based sampling.

Insights and contradictions from student surveys in a 1st year biology lab course

Exclude FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Ignore none
Beth L. Volpov, Natalie G. Schimpf, Pam Kalas, Kathy Nomme Scholarship@Western (Western University) 2019-07-04 link
相关性: 该论文研究生物学实验课程的教学方法，与有限元代理建模或植入物设计无关。
边界: This paper is about biology education and student surveys, with no mention of FEA, surrogate modeling, uncertainty quantification, or biomedical implant design.
动机· 评估生物实验课程中主动学习策略的效果与学生满意度。
方法· 通过定量和开放式调查问卷分析7个学期的学生反馈。
结果· 学生喜欢动手实验，但认为统计和写作最有用却最不喜欢。
验证· 基于学生调查和焦点小组的统计分析。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the effectiveness and student satisfaction of active learning strategies implemented in a first-year biology laboratory course over four years.
Method· Quantitative and open-ended survey questions were analyzed across seven semesters from 2015 to 2018, along with focus groups.
Result· Hands-on experiments were most liked, while statistics and writing were perceived as most useful but least liked, with a disconnect between perceived and actual workload.
Validation· Statistical analysis of survey responses and focus group data from multiple semesters.

相关性: 该论文仅报告双动杯在翻修髋臼重建中的短期临床结果，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡、微动或接触力学建模。
边界: This paper reports clinical outcomes of dual-mobility cups in revision acetabular reconstruction, with no biomechanical modeling, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no FEA, and no comparison of intact vs implanted femur.
动机· 评估双动杯在翻修髋臼重建中对高脱位风险患者的疗效
方法· 前瞻性随访34例翻修THA患者，记录Harris评分和影像学结果

验证· 临床随访和Kaplan-Meier生存分析
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the short-term clinical performance of dual-mobility cups in revision acetabular reconstruction for patients at high risk of instability.
Method· A prospective follow-up of 34 hips in 30 patients undergoing revision THA with dual-mobility cups, assessing Harris Hip Score and radiographic outcomes.
Result· The cumulative survival rate was 91.2% at 3.5 years, with a dislocation rate of 2.9%.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with Kaplan-Meier survival analysis.
