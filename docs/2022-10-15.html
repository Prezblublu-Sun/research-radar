通讯: Hui Chang @ SHANGI Institute for Advanced Materials (Nanjing) Co., Ltd., Nanjing 210038, China  |  Lian Zhou @ SHANGI Institute for Advanced Materials (Nanjing) Co., Ltd., Nanjing 210038, China

Validation· Mechanical compression testing combined with density measurements.

Selective Laser Melting of Inconel 718/TiC Composite: Effect of TiC Particle Size

单位: Institute of Mechanical Engineering, Materials, and Transport, Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University, 195251 St. Petersburg, Russia
通讯: Vadim Sufiiarov @ Institute of Mechanical Engineering, Materials, and Transport, Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University, 19
相关性: 该论文研究高温合金复合材料，不涉及生物医学植入物、多孔结构或骨整合。
边界: This paper does not address porous structure design, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or surface roughness for osseointegration; it focuses on high-temperature alloy composites for non-biomedical applications.
动机· 研究TiC颗粒尺寸对SLM成形Inconel 718复合材料性能的影响。
方法· 采用SLM制备含微米或纳米TiC的复合材料，并进行热等静压和热处理。
结果· 纳米TiC提高强度但降低延伸率，硬度提升约20%。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of TiC particle size on the microstructure and mechanical properties of Inconel 718 matrix composite fabricated by selective laser melting.
Method· Composite powders with 1 wt% micron- or nano-sized TiC were processed by SLM, followed by hot isostatic pressing and heat treatment.
Result· Nano-TiC addition resulted in higher strength at room and elevated temperatures but reduced elongation compared to pure Inconel 718 and micron-TiC composites.
Validation· Mechanical testing including hardness and tensile tests at room temperature and 700°C was performed.

单位: Department of Endodontics and Periodontics, Graduate School of Dentistry, Ohu University, 31-1 Misumido Tomita-machi, Koriyama 963-8611, Japan.
通讯: Keiso Takahashi @ Division of Periodontics, Department of Conservative Dentistry, School of Dentistry, Ohu University, 31-1 Misumido Tomit [推断]
相关性: 该论文完全未涉及AM生物医学材料方向，仅专注于牙周炎患者种植体周围疾病的临床风险因素分析。
边界: This paper is purely clinical, with no additive manufacturing, porous structures, mechanical testing, fatigue analysis, or surface roughness characterization.
动机· 评估牙周炎的分期和分级对种植体周围疾病的影响
方法· 对84名牙周炎患者的325颗种植体进行回顾性分析，使用主成分分析和聚类分析

English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the impact of periodontitis stages and grades on peri-implant diseases and implant failure.
Method· A retrospective analysis of 325 implants in 84 periodontitis patients was conducted using principal component analysis and cluster analysis.
Result· Stage IV periodontitis and bone augmentation were significant risk factors for peri-implantitis and implant loss.
Validation· Validation was performed using long-term clinical follow-up data.
