Efficient Gradient-Enhanced Bayesian Optimizer with Comparisons to Conjugate-Gradient and Quasi-Newton Optimizers for Unconstrained Local Optimization

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Background only numerical_benchmark
André L. Marchildon, David W. Zingg arXiv preprint 2025-04-12 link
相关性: 该论文专注于无约束局部优化的贝叶斯优化方法，未涉及FEA代理建模或生物力学设计中的约束处理与不确定性量化。
边界: This paper does not address expensive simulation sampling efficiency in the context of FEA, does not handle constraints, lacks uncertainty quantification beyond gradient noise, and is not applied to implant/biomechanics design.
动机· 贝叶斯优化常用于全局优化，但局部优化中也能受益于其概率代理模型。
方法· 提出梯度增强的局部贝叶斯优化框架，使用子集构建代理和概率信任区域。
结果· 在无噪声时与共轭梯度和拟牛顿法竞争力相当，有噪声时显著更优。
验证· 在2至40维无约束测试函数及Lorenz 63模型上对比验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Bayesian optimization is traditionally used for global optimization, but its probabilistic surrogates can also benefit local optimization under noisy or expensive evaluations.
Method· A gradient-enhanced local Bayesian optimization framework is proposed, using a subset of evaluation points to construct the surrogate and a probabilistic trust region for acquisition function minimization.
Result· The Bayesian optimizer matches the convergence depth of conjugate-gradient and quasi-Newton optimizers on unimodal problems while often requiring significantly fewer function evaluations, and outperforms them when gradients are noisy.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on 2-40 dimensional unimodal functions and the chaotic Lorenz 63 model with inaccurate gradients.
