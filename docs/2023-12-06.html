Patient reported outcome measures correlate with step-count in total hip arthroplasty

Motivation· This study investigates the correlation between step-count and patient-reported outcome measures during early recovery after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Smartphone-captured step-count data from 666 THA patients were retrospectively analyzed using Spearman correlation and linear regression against PROMs.
Result· UCLA activity score correlated significantly with step-count at all postoperative time windows, while HOOS-12 function correlated preoperatively and at 6 weeks and 3 months.
Validation· Statistical validation using clinical patient data with subgroup analyses and corrections for multiple comparisons.
