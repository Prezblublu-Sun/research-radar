单位: Department of Physics and Astronomy, Michigan State University, East Lansing, Michigan 48824-1321, USA
通讯: G. B. King @ Department of Physics and Astronomy, Michigan State University, East Lansing, Michigan 48824-1321, USA
相关性: 该论文比较了贝叶斯和频率派不确定性量化方法，但应用于核反应领域，与生物力学代理模型或植入物设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, implant biomechanics, or any biomedical application; it focuses on nuclear reaction uncertainty quantification.
动机· 比较贝叶斯与频率派方法在核反应不确定性量化中的表现
方法· 从相同初始假设出发，分别用两种方法计算弹性散射和转移反应的置信区间
结果· 贝叶斯方法得到的不确定性更宽但更准确，频率派方法不确定性过窄

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To directly and systematically compare Bayesian and frequentist approaches for uncertainty quantification in low-energy nuclear reactions.
Method· Starting from identical initial assumptions, confidence intervals for elastic and transfer processes were determined using both methods and evaluated against experimental data via empirical coverage probabilities.
Result· The Bayesian approach produced wider but more realistic uncertainties, while the frequentist approach yielded narrower uncertainties that underestimated the true variability.
Validation· Empirical coverage probabilities computed from experimental data were used to compare the accuracy of the two uncertainty quantification methods.
