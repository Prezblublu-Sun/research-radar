相关性: 该论文为动力系统的不确定性量化提供了一种多元素谱方法，可迁移至代理模型中的不确定性传播，但未涉及FEA或生物力学设计。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop an efficient uncertainty quantification method for stochastic dynamical systems with discontinuities in the probability space.
Method· The multi-element flow-driven spectral chaos partitions the random domain into elements and solves each separately using the FSC method.
Result· The method demonstrates reliability and low computational cost on nonlinear oscillators such as the Van-der-Pol and Kraichnan-Orszag problems.
Validation· Validation is performed on standard numerical benchmarks including Van-der-Pol and Kraichnan-Orszag three-mode problems.
