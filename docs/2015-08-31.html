Fast MOR-Based Approach to Uncertainty Quantification in Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Lorenzo Codecasa, Luca Di Rienzo, Konstantin Weise, Stefanie Groß, Jens Haueisen IEEE Transactions on Magnetics 2015-08-31 10.1109/tmag.2015.2475120
单位: Dipartimento di Elettronica, Politecnico di Milano, Milan, Italy
通讯: Lorenzo Codecasa @ Dipartimento di Elettronica, Politecnico di Milano, Milan, Italy
相关性: 该论文将模型降阶与多项式混沌展开结合用于电磁场不确定性量化，其加速计算和UQ方法可迁移至生物力学代理建模。
边界: This paper focuses on transcranial magnetic stimulation (electromagnetic field) rather than solid mechanics or implant biomechanics, and does not address structural FEA or surrogate modeling for implant design.
动机· 加速经颅磁刺激中感应电场的不确定性量化计算
方法· 采用参数化模型降阶结合多项式混沌展开方法
结果· 计算速度提升两个数量级，均值和标准差相对误差小于0.1%
验证· 与传统非侵入式回归方法对比验证精度
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To accelerate uncertainty quantification of induced electric fields in transcranial magnetic stimulation by reducing computational overhead.
Method· A parametric model order reduction technique is combined with polynomial chaos expansion to efficiently compute the stochastic response.
Result· The proposed method achieves over two orders of magnitude speedup while maintaining relative errors below 0.1% for mean and standard deviation.
Validation· Accuracy is validated by comparison with the traditional non-intrusive regression-based polynomial chaos method.

单位: Surgical Outcomes and Analysis, Kaiser Permanente, 8954 Rio San Diego Drive, Suite 406, San Diego, CA, 92108, USA. Liz.w.paxton@kp.org

方法· 基于大型注册数据库，使用逻辑回归筛选翻修预测因子。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop revision risk calculators for total knee and hip arthroplasties using registry data to support clinical decision-making.
Method· Logistic regression models were built on a large cohort from a health maintenance organization registry to identify preoperative predictors of revision within 5 years.
Result· Age, sex, BMI, diabetes, and diagnosis were significant predictors for TKA; age, sex, and BMI for THA.
Validation· Model performance was assessed using Hosmer-Lemeshow goodness-of-fit and c-statistic.
