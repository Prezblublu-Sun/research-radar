Effects of laying angles on stainless steel fabricated by selective laser melting

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Exp. validation
Cong Ni, Yan Shi, Weizhou Zhang, Wen Wang, Chaojie Liu Materials Science and Technology 2024-01-10 10.1177/02670836231224131
单位: Suzhou Chien-Shiung Institute of Technology, Suzhou, China
通讯: Yan Shi @ School of Mechanical and Electric Engineering, Changchun University of Science and Technology, Changchun, China
相关性: 该论文为AM生物医学金属材料的工艺参数影响提供了基础数据，但未直接涉及髋关节植入物设计或多孔结构。
动机· 研究选择性激光熔化中铺粉角度对316L不锈钢表面粗糙度、密度、残余应力和力学性能的影响。
方法· 以0°到90°的不同铺粉角度制备样品，测量表面粗糙度、密度、残余应力和拉伸性能。

验证· 通过实际实验（拉伸测试、粗糙度测量等）验证了结果，无仿真。

CreINNs: Credal-Set Interval Neural Networks for Uncertainty Estimation in Classification Tasks

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Background only numerical_benchmark UQ
Kaizheng Wang, Keivan Shariatmadar, Shireen Kudukkil Manchingal, Fabio Cuzzolin, David Moens et al. arXiv preprint 2024-01-10 link
相关性: 该论文提出了一种新的不确定性量化方法，但仅用于分类任务，未涉及有限元代理建模或生物力学设计。
边界: This paper focuses on classification uncertainty estimation, not on surrogate modeling for expensive simulations, constraint handling, or implant/biomechanics design.
动机· 提高神经网络分类任务中的不确定性估计质量。
方法· 提出Credal-Set区间神经网络，通过预测概率区间来量化不确定性。
结果· 在标准分类任务上，不确定性估计质量优于或相当于变分贝叶斯网络和深度集成。
验证· 在标准多类和二分类基准数据集上验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To improve the quality of uncertainty estimation in neural network classification tasks.
Method· Proposes Credal-Set Interval Neural Networks (CreINNs) that predict upper and lower probability bounds for each class, capturing weight uncertainty via deterministic intervals.
Result· CreINNs achieve superior or comparable uncertainty estimation quality to variational Bayesian Neural Networks and Deep Ensembles on standard classification tasks.
Validation· Validated on standard multiclass and binary classification benchmarks.
