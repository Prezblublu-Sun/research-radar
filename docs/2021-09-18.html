High Strain Rate Yielding of Additive Manufacturing Inconel 625 by Selective Laser Melting

Kang Du, Laixia Yang, Chao Xu, Bin Wang, Yang Gao Materials 2021-09-18 10.3390/ma14185408
单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, Xi’an University of Science and Technology, Xi’an 710054, China
通讯: Chao Xu @ School of Mechanical Engineering, Xi’an University of Science and Technology, Xi’an 710054, China  |  Bin Wang @ Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Brunel University London, Uxbridge UB8 3PH, UK
相关性: 该论文研究Inconel 625的高应变率力学性能，与生物医学植入物的应力屏蔽、多孔结构或疲劳无关。
边界: This paper studies high strain rate yielding of Inconel 625 for non-biomedical applications, with no porous structure, stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration relevant to biomedical implants.
动机· 研究SLM制造的Inconel 625在高应变率下的力学行为
方法· 使用霍普金森杆进行压缩和拉伸实验，最高应变率3500 s⁻¹
结果· 动态屈服应力约为准静态的两倍，高温下仍表现应变率效应
验证· 与准静态结果对比，验证应变率敏感性
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the mechanical response of SLM-fabricated Inconel 625 under high strain rate loading.
Method· Split Hopkinson pressure bar tests were conducted for compression (up to 3500 s⁻¹, including at 500°C) and tension (up to 2400 s⁻¹) at room temperature.
Result· The alloy exhibited strong rate sensitivity, with dynamic yield stress nearly doubling the quasistatic value, and dynamic tensile strengths exceeding compressive ones.
Validation· Experimental results were compared with quasistatic data to confirm strain rate and temperature effects.

Human Periodontal Ligament Stem Cells Response to Titanium Implant Surface: Extracellular Matrix Deposition.

Guya Diletta Marconi, Luigia Fonticoli, Ylenia Della Rocca, Thangavelu Soundara Rajan, Adriano Piattelli et al. Biology 2021-09-18 10.3390/biology10090931
单位: Department of Medical, Oral and Biotechnological Sciences, University "G. d'Annunzio" Chieti-Pescara, 66100 Chieti, Italy.
通讯: Francesca Diomede @ Department of Innovative Technologies in Medicine & Dentistry, University "G. d'Annunzio" Chieti-Pescara, 66100 Chieti, [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究钛种植体表面对人牙周膜干细胞的影响，不涉及增材制造、多孔结构、应力遮挡或力学测试。
边界: This paper does not involve additive manufacturing (LPBF), porous structure design, mechanical testing, fatigue analysis, or stress shielding evaluation.
动机· 研究钛种植体表面对人牙周膜干细胞细胞外基质沉积的影响。
方法· 比较两种钛表面（TEST vs CTRL）上的细胞培养，分析蛋白和基因表达。
结果· TEST表面促进细胞黏附和细胞外基质成分表达。
验证· 通过SEM、免疫荧光、Western blot和RT-PCR验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the in vitro effects of different titanium dental implant surfaces on human periodontal ligament stem cell morphology and extracellular matrix deposition.
Method· Human PDLSCs were cultured on TEST and CTRL titanium surfaces, and protein expression (Vimentin, Fibronectin, etc.) and gene expression (RUNX2, ALP, etc.) were analyzed.
Result· The TEST surface enhanced cell adhesion and upregulated extracellular matrix components and osteogenic markers.
Validation· Validation was performed via SEM, immunofluorescence, Western blot, and RT-PCR.
