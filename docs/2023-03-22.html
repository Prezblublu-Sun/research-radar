Interpretation Threshold Values for the Oxford Hip Score in Patients Undergoing Total Hip Arthroplasty

单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Copenhagen University Hospital Hvidovre, Copenhagen, Denmark
通讯: Lasse K. Harris @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Copenhagen University Hospital Hvidovre, Copenhagen, Denmark
相关性: 该论文探讨全髋关节置换术后牛津髋关节评分的临床阈值，未涉及任何生物力学建模或植入物设计。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain, micromotion, or any contact mechanics; it is purely a clinical outcomes study without biomechanical modeling.
动机· 确定全髋关节置换术后牛津髋关节评分的临床重要变化阈值

验证· 使用非参数自助法确定95%置信区间
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To establish clinically meaningful interpretation thresholds for the Oxford Hip Score after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Using anchor-based adjusted predictive modeling and item-split methods on data from 706 patients.
Result· MIC, PASS, and TF thresholds were obtained, with baseline severity influencing MIC values.
Validation· Nonparametric bootstrapping was used to determine 95% confidence intervals.
