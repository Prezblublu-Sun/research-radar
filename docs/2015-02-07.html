Inter-subject variability effects on the primary stability of a short cementless femoral stem

High Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Direct Read now simulation_case UQ Patient-specific
M.T. Bah, Junfen Shi, Markus O. Heller, Yanneck Suchier, Fabien Lefèbvre et al. Journal of Biomechanics 2015-02-07 10.1016/j.jbiomech.2015.01.037
单位: Bioengineering Science Research Group, FEE, 5/3019, University of Southampton, Southampton SO17 1BJ, UK. Electronic address: mtb@soton.ac.uk
通讯: M.T. Bah @ Bioengineering Science Research Group, FEE, 5/3019, University of Southampton, Southampton SO17 1BJ, UK. Electronic addr
相关性: 该论文直接量化了短型非骨水泥股骨柄在不同患者间的初始稳定性差异，与用户研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not include implant design optimization or long-term stress shielding analysis.
动机· 探究患者间解剖差异对短型非骨水泥股骨柄初始稳定性的影响。
方法· 基于多个患者CT数据构建有限元模型，模拟不同骨密度和几何条件下的微动。
结果· 患者间变异显著影响微动分布和大小，部分病例超过骨整合阈值。
验证· 通过与文献中实验数据对比验证模型趋势。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate how inter-subject anatomical variability affects the primary stability of a short cementless femoral stem.
Method· Finite element models were constructed from multiple patient CT scans to simulate micromotion under varying bone density and geometry.
Result· Inter-subject variability significantly influenced micromotion magnitude and distribution, with some cases exceeding osseointegration thresholds.
Validation· Model trends were validated against experimental data from the literature.

Influence of Underpreparation on Primary Stability of Implants Inserted in Poor Quality Bone Sites: An In Vitro Study

Low Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Background only in_vitro Exp. validation
Marco Degidi, Giuseppe Daprile, Adriano Piattelli Journal of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery 2015-02-07 10.1016/j.joms.2015.01.029
单位: Private Practice, Bologna, Italy
通讯: Marco Degidi @ Private Practice, Bologna, Italy
相关性: 该论文研究牙科植入物在低质量骨中的稳定性，与髋关节植入物应力遮挡和微动无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on dental implants and does not address hip implant stress shielding, micromotion, or femoral stem mechanics.
动机· 评估低质量骨中植入物预备不足对初期稳定性的影响。
方法· 在模拟低质量骨块中植入不同预备直径的牙科植入物，测量植入扭矩和微动。
结果· 预备不足显著提高了植入扭矩并降低了微动，增强了初期稳定性。
验证· 通过体外骨块实验验证力学稳定性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate whether underpreparation of implant sites can improve primary stability in low-quality bone.
Method· Dental implants were inserted into simulated low-density bone blocks with varying degrees of underpreparation, and insertion torque and micromotion were measured.
Result· Underpreparation significantly increased insertion torque and reduced micromotion, indicating enhanced primary stability.
Validation· In vitro mechanical testing on synthetic bone blocks.
