单位: State Key Laboratory for Mechanical Behavior of Materials, Xi'an Jiaotong University, Xi'an 710049, China. yonghan@mail.xjtu.edu.cn.

Validation· In vivo evaluation was conducted using rabbit femur implantation, comparing coated and uncoated Mg implants.

单位: Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Asan Medical Center, University of Ulsan College of Medicine, Seoul, Korea
通讯: Pil Whan Yoon @ Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Asan Medical Center, University of Ulsan College of Medicine, Seoul, Korea
相关性: 该论文仅涉及血清金属离子浓度，与髋关节假体的生物力学分析完全无关。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain, does not model micromotion with contact/friction, is purely a clinical follow-up study, and provides no finite element boundary conditions.
动机· 观察金属-金属全髋置换术后血清金属离子长期变化趋势
方法· 对患者进行长达数十年的血清钴铬离子浓度追踪测量
结果· 金属离子浓度在术后早期达峰后缓慢下降，个体差异较大
验证· 临床长期随访数据统计分析
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the long-term trends of serum metal ion levels in patients with cementless metal-on-metal total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Longitudinal follow-up measuring cobalt and chromium concentrations in patients' serum over a period of up to several decades.
Result· Metal ion levels peaked early after surgery and gradually decreased, with substantial inter-patient variability.
Validation· Clinical follow-up data with statistical analysis of time-dependent ion concentration changes.
