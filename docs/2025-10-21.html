Validation· No experimental validation is reported; the work is limited to algorithmic development and simulation.

Ensembling Pruned Attention Heads For Uncertainty-Aware Efficient Transformers

Firas Gabetni, Giuseppe Curci, Andrea Pilzer, Subhankar Roy, Elisa Ricci et al. arXiv preprint 2025-10-21 link
相关性: 该论文专注于Transformer在分类任务中的不确定性量化，与有限元代理模型或生物力学设计无任何关联。
边界: This paper addresses only image/text classification tasks with no FEA, surrogate modeling, or biomechanics/engineering simulation context.
动机· 降低Transformer集成模型的计算开销，同时保持不确定性量化性能
方法· 通过剪枝注意力头创建多样成员，并用分组全连接层合并为紧凑多注意力头
结果· 在图像和文本分类任务中，推理速度接近单网络，不确定性量化性能优于深度集成
验证· 在多个分类数据集和零样本ImageNet-1k上验证，无需额外训练
English summary & terms
Motivation· Efficient uncertainty quantification for transformers is needed to reduce computational and memory costs of deep ensembles.
Method· Hydra Ensembles prunes attention heads to create diverse ensemble members and merges them via grouped fully-connected layers in multi-head attention.
Result· The method achieves inference speed close to a single network while matching or surpassing Deep Ensembles in UQ performance across image and text classification tasks.
Validation· Evaluated on image and text classification benchmarks, including zero-shot classification on ImageNet-1k, showing consistent gains over Deep Ensembles.

边界: This paper addresses uncertainty quantification in classification tasks, with no connection to surrogate modeling for expensive simulations, constraint handling, or biomechanical implant design.
动机· 提高深度学习在高风险应用中的不确定性估计可靠性
方法· 提出灵活狄利克雷分布替代标准狄利克雷分布进行证据深度学习

English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the reliability of uncertainty estimation in deep learning for high-stakes applications by overcoming limitations of standard evidential deep learning.
Method· Proposes flexible evidential deep learning (F-EDL) which predicts a flexible Dirichlet distribution over class probabilities, generalizing the standard Dirichlet.
Result· Achieves state-of-the-art uncertainty quantification performance across classical, long-tailed, and noisy in-distribution classification scenarios.
Validation· Validated through empirical experiments on diverse classification benchmarks including long-tailed and noisy datasets.
