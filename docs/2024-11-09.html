UQ of 2D Slab Burner DNS: Surrogates, Uncertainty Propagation, and Parameter Calibration

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark Exp. validation UQ
Georgios Georgalis, Alejandro Becerra, Kenneth Budzinski, Matthew McGurn, Danial Faghihi et al. arXiv preprint 2024-11-09 link
相关性: 该论文展示了基于代理模型的UQ框架（GP和HMS），方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模，但未涉及植入物设计或约束优化。
边界: The paper focuses on combustion DNS surrogates without FEA (finite element analysis), implant mechanics, or constraint handling, making it non-core for biomechanics surrogate modeling.
动机· 对复杂燃烧DNS进行完整的不确定性量化，验证代理模型与参数校准。
方法· 构建GP和分层多尺度代理模型，基于64个拉丁超立方采样，进行贝叶斯校准。
结果· HMS代理模型预测误差小于15%，校准后化学参数高于默认值。
验证· 通过交叉验证和实验数据校准进行验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To demonstrate a complete uncertainty quantification framework for a complex physics-based combustion simulation, addressing surrogate construction, propagation, and calibration.
Method· Two surrogates (Gaussian Process and Hierarchical Multiscale Surrogate) were built from 64 Latin Hypercube simulations and used for Bayesian calibration of latent heat and reaction exponent.
Result· HMS achieved <15% prediction error on multiscale boundary quantities, and Bayesian calibration indicated that default chemical parameters should be increased.
Validation· Validation used cross-validation of surrogates and calibration against experimental regression rate data.

相关性: 该论文开发了软水凝胶3D打印的实时超声监测系统，但未集成AI，仅提供监测手段，与AI驱动生物打印方向相关性较弱。

Motivation· To enable real-time quality control in soft hydrogel 3D printing through in-situ monitoring.
Method· An ultrasound system analyzes reflected signals in time and frequency domains during printing.
Result· Achieves subwavelength resolution of 0.78λ, capturing transient behaviors like interlayer bonding and elastic constants.
Validation· Validated with simulation and experiments under both infilled and grid pattern printing conditions.
