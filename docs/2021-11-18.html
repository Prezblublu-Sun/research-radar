相关性: 该论文将PINN扩展到PDE约束的最优控制问题，方法可迁移到生物力学植入物设计中的约束优化和代理建模。
边界: No validation on biomedical implant design, no uncertainty quantification, and no handling of multi-objective constraints typical in biomechanics.
动机· 将PINN框架扩展到PDE约束的最优控制问题，避免传统方法的高计算成本。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Extend the PINN framework to PDE-constrained optimal control problems to reduce computational cost compared to traditional adjoint-based methods.
Method· Propose guidelines for PINN architecture and a two-step line search strategy to tune the loss function weight, enabling efficient control optimization.
Result· The PINN approach achieves comparable accuracy to adjoint-based optimal control on multiple PDE benchmarks with improved training efficiency.
Validation· Validation is performed by comparing with adjoint-based nonlinear optimal control on distributed control examples of Laplace, Burgers, Kuramoto-Sivashinsky, and Navier-Stokes equations.
