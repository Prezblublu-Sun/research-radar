Model to Predict Need for Subtrochanteric Shortening Osteotomy During Total Hip Arthroplasty for Crowe Type IV Developmental Dysplasia

Low Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Background only clinical
Haiyang Ma, Jingyang Sun, Yinqiao Du, Zhisen Gao, Junmin Shen et al. Medical Science Monitor 2020-10-25 10.12659/msm.926239
单位: The Medical School of Chinese People’s Liberation Army (PLA); Department of Orthopedics, The First Medical Center, Chinese PLA General Hospital, Beijing, China (mainland)
通讯: Haiyang Ma @ The Medical School of Chinese People’s Liberation Army (PLA); Department of Orthopedics, The First Medical Center, Chine
相关性: 该论文关注Crowe IV型DDH术中转子下短缩截骨术的预测模型，未涉及股骨假体应力遮挡、微动或接触力学分析。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain, micromotion, or contact mechanics; it is a clinical prediction model without any biomechanical FE analysis.
动机· 预测Crowe IV型DDH全髋置换术中是否需要转子下短缩截骨
方法· 基于149例患者回顾性数据，用多变量Cox回归建立列线图预测模型
结果· 脱位百分比增加截骨风险，继发髋臼形成和锥形套筒降低风险，C统计量0.918
验证· 临床回顾性数据内部验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a predictive model for the need of subtrochanteric shortening osteotomy during total hip arthroplasty in Crowe type IV developmental dysplasia.
Method· A retrospective study of 149 patients (186 hips) using multivariable Cox regression to construct a nomogram incorporating age, sex, surgical history, sleeve type, secondary acetabulum, and dislocation percentage.
Result· Dislocation percentage increased the likelihood of osteotomy (OR 1.24), while secondary acetabulum (OR 0.10) and cone-shaped sleeve (OR 0.18) decreased it; the model achieved a C-statistic of 0.918.
Validation· Internal validation on the retrospective cohort with discriminative ability assessed by C-statistic.
