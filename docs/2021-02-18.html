An alternative ex vivo method to evaluate the osseointegration of Ti-6Al-4V alloy also combined with collagen.

Francesca Veronesi, Paola Torricelli, Lucia Martini, Matilde Tschon, Gianluca Giavaresi et al. Biomedical materials (Bristol, England) 2021-02-18 10.1088/1748-605X/abdbda
单位: Complex Structure of Surgical Sciences and Technologies, IRCCS Istituto Ortopedico Rizzoli, Via Di Barbiano 1/10, 40136 Bologna, Italy.
通讯: Milena Fini @ Complex Structure of Surgical Sciences and Technologies, IRCCS Istituto Ortopedico Rizzoli, Via Di Barbiano 1/10, 40136 [推断]
相关性: 该论文聚焦于EBM制造的钛合金的体外骨整合评估，缺乏力学测试和应力遮挡分析，与用户对LPBF制造和力学性能的研究方向关联较弱。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no stress shielding analysis, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), and no focus on as-built surface morphology.
动机· 开发一种体外模型来评估EBM钛合金的骨整合性能
方法· 将兔皮质骨段与EBM制造的钛合金圆柱体共培养，并涂覆I型胶原
结果· 胶原涂层在30天和60天显著提高骨-植入物接触和新骨形成
验证· 通过体外培养模型验证，对比不同时间点的骨整合指标
English summary & terms
Motivation· To establish a reliable ex vivo model for evaluating osseointegration of Ti-6Al-4V implants fabricated by electron beam melting.
Method· Rabbit cortical bone segments were cultured with EBM-fabricated Ti-6Al-4V cylinders, with or without type I collagen coating, and assessed at 30, 60, and 90 days.
Result· Collagen coating significantly improved bone-to-implant contact and new bone formation at 30 and 60 days, but not at 90 days.
Validation· Ex vivo culture model comparing coated vs uncoated implants over time, with histological quantification.

Detection and location of microdefects during selective laser melting by wireless acoustic emission measurement

Kaita Ito, Masahiro Kusano, Masahiko Demura, Makoto Watanabe Additive manufacturing 2021-02-18 10.1016/j.addma.2021.101915
单位: National Institute for Materials Science, 1-1 Namiki, Tsukuba, Ibaraki 305-0044, Japan
通讯: Kaita Ito @ National Institute for Materials Science, 1-1 Namiki, Tsukuba, Ibaraki 305-0044, Japan
相关性: 该论文关注SLM过程中微缺陷的声发射监测，不涉及生物医学植入物、多孔结构、应力遮挡或力学测试。
边界: This paper does not address porous structure design, stress shielding, LPBF fabrication of biomedical implants, mechanical testing (static or fatigue), surface roughness, or osseointegration.
动机· SLM过程中微缺陷难以实时监测，需开发新方法。
方法· 提出无线声发射监测系统，记录单道和多道熔覆中的AE信号。
结果· 成功检测到与孔隙和微裂纹相关的突发型AE事件，并定位缺陷。
验证· 通过X射线CT对比验证AE检测结果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Real-time monitoring of microdefect formation during selective laser melting is challenging, motivating the development of a wireless acoustic emission method.
Method· A battery-powered acoustic emission monitoring system was used to detect and locate defects in single- and multi-track SLM tests.
Result· Burst-type AE signals were correlated with pores and microcracks, enabling detection of defects during processing and after laser irradiation.
Validation· AE event locations were validated against X-ray computed tomography cross-sectional images.

结果· 获得平均250nm晶粒和900MPa屈服强度

Motivation· To produce nano/ultrafine grained immiscible Fe-Cu alloy with ultrahigh strength via selective laser melting.

Result· Achieved an average grain size of 250 nm and a yield strength of ~900 MPa, more than double that of SLM-fabricated Fe.
Validation· Mechanical tensile testing confirmed substantial strengthening.

Validation· The study validated the process through dimensional measurements and surface roughness characterization, with abrasive flow machining further improving surface quality.
