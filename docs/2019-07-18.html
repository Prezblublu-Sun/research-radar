Personalized 3D-printed endoprostheses for limb sparing in dogs: Modeling and <i>in vitro</i> testing

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Save for project cadaveric Exp. validation Patient-specific
Anatolie Timercan, Vladimir Braïlovski, Yvan Petit, Bertrand Lussier, Bernard Séguin Medical Engineering & Physics 2019-07-18 10.1016/j.medengphy.2019.07.005
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, École de technologie supérieure, 1100 Notre-Dame West, Montreal, Quebec H3C1K3, Canada. Electronic address: anatolie.timercan.1@ens.etsmtl.ca
通讯: Vladimir Braïlovski @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, École de technologie supérieure, 1100 Notre-Dame West, Montreal, Quebec H3C1K3, Ca
相关性: 该论文涉及个性化3D打印假体用于犬类肢体保留手术，包含LPBF制造、有限元建模和体外力学测试，与AM生物医学方向相关。
边界: The paper focuses on veterinary endoprostheses for limb sparing, not on porous structures for stress shielding or stiffness matching, and does not report fatigue testing, surface roughness, or osseointegration.
动机· 犬类骨肉瘤肢体保留手术中，商用假体需塑形且效果不佳，需个性化方案。
方法· 基于CT重建骨骼，设计个性化假体和切割导板，采用LPBF制造钛合金假体，并建立有限元模型。
结果· 个性化假体可缩短手术时间25-50%，有限元模型表明弹性模量25-50 GPa可改善应力分布。
验证· 通过植入假体的犬类尸体肢体实验验证有限元模型的运动学和力-位移曲线。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To address the sub-optimal outcomes of commercially available plate-endoprosthesis assemblies in canine limb-sparing surgery by developing personalized additively-manufactured implants.
Method· Patient-specific endoprostheses and cutting guides were designed from CT scans, manufactured via laser powder bed fusion (Ti alloy) and fused deposition modeling (ABS), and a finite element model of the instrumented limb was developed.
Result· Personalized endoprostheses reduced surgery time by 25-50%, and the FE model indicated that an implant elastic modulus of 25-50 GPa improves stress distribution.
Validation· The numerical model was validated against cadaveric limb experiments using kinematics and force-displacement diagrams.
