The Effects of Interlocking a Universal Hip Cementless Stem on Implant Subsidence and Mechanical Properties of Cadaveric Canine Femora

Yonathan Buks, Kirk L. Wendelburg, Susan M. Stover, Tanya C. Garcia Veterinary Surgery 2016-01-15 10.1111/vsu.12437
单位: Animal Specialty Group Los Angeles California
通讯: Kirk L. Wendelburg @ Animal Specialty Group Los Angeles California
相关性: 该论文研究了犬股骨柄的沉降和力学性能，但未涉及应力遮挡或Gruen区应变分析，且为动物模型而非人体。
边界: No quantification of proximal femur stress/strain in Gruen zones, no comparison of intact vs implanted femur, and no patient-specific or population-based FE modeling.
动机· 评估联锁螺栓对犬股骨柄沉降和力学性能的影响
方法· 对配对犬股骨植入传统或联锁柄，进行循环加载和破坏测试
结果· 联锁柄显著减少沉降，提高屈服和破坏载荷
验证· 离体生物力学测试，使用高速视频追踪运动
English summary & terms
Motivation· To determine whether an interlocking bolt reduces subsidence and enhances mechanical properties of a cementless femoral stem under cyclic loading.
Method· Paired cadaveric canine femora were implanted with either a traditional or interlocking stem and subjected to cyclic loading at walk, trot, and gallop loads, followed by load-to-failure testing.
Result· The interlocking stem significantly reduced subsidence and increased yield and failure loads compared to the traditional stem.
Validation· Ex vivo biomechanical study using high-speed video kinematic markers to measure implant and bone motions.

单位: State Key Laboratory of Fluid Power Transmission and Control, Zhejiang University, Zheda Road 38, 310027 Hangzhou, China
通讯: Jun Zou @ State Key Laboratory of Fluid Power Transmission and Control, Zhejiang University, Zheda Road 38, 310027 Hangzhou, China
相关性: 该论文研究SLM制造316L不锈钢的摩擦学性能，未涉及骨植入物的应力遮挡、疲劳或多孔结构，与生物医学应用方向关联较弱。
边界: This paper does not cover porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration, and the lubricated tribology context is not directly applicable to biomedical implant mechanics.
动机· 探究SLM工艺对316L不锈钢润滑条件下摩擦学性能的影响
方法· 采用SLM制备316L试样并进行销盘摩擦磨损试验

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the tribological performance of selective laser melted 316L stainless steel under lubricated conditions.
Method· SLM-fabricated 316L specimens were tested using pin-on-disc tribometry under lubricated conditions.
Result· SLM parts exhibited superior wear resistance compared to wrought counterparts, with abrasive wear as the dominant mechanism.
Validation· Experimental pin-on-disc wear tests.

Inferring climate variability from nonlinear proxies: application to palaeo-ENSO studies

Julien Emile‐Geay, Martin P. Tingley Climate of the past 2016-01-15 10.5194/cp-12-31-2016
单位: Department of Earth Sciences & Center for Applied Mathematical Sciences, University of Southern California, Los Angeles, CA, USA
通讯: Julien Emile‐Geay @ Department of Earth Sciences & Center for Applied Mathematical Sciences, University of Southern California, Los Angeles
相关性: 该论文研究古气候代理中的非线性推断，与生物力学代理建模无直接关联。
边界: No FEA, no surrogate modeling for engineering design, no implant or biomechanics context.
动机· 揭示忽略非线性代理-气候关系会导致气候变率重建偏差。
方法· 模拟阈值非线性代理，对比常用方法、经验变换和贝叶斯模型。
结果· 显式处理非线性可显著改善气候变率估计和不确定性量化。
验证· 通过两个古气候记录（Laguna Pallcacocha和Lake Challa）进行案例验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To demonstrate the pitfalls of ignoring nonlinearities in proxy-climate relationships for inferring past climate variability.
Method· Simulated an idealized nonlinear proxy and compared three inference approaches: standard linear methods, empirical transformation, and a Bayesian mechanistic model.
Result· Explicitly accounting for nonlinearity yields significantly better estimates of past climate variations with more accurate uncertainty quantification.
Validation· Applied to two palaeoclimate records (Laguna Pallcacocha and Lake Challa) to show quantitative departures from original studies.

nonlinear proxy Bayesian inversion climate reconstruction uncertainty quantification

Design of a New Honeycomb PCF for Ultraflatten Dispersion over Wideband Communication System

Ankita Bapna, Shivpratap Pandey International Journal of Computer Applications 2016-01-15 10.5120/ijca2016907936
单位: Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering S.S. College of Engineering, Udaipur, Rajasthan, India
通讯: Ankita Bapna @ Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering S.S. College of Engineering, Udaipur, Rajasthan, India
相关性: 该论文涉及光子晶体光纤设计，与生物医学增材制造方向完全无关。
边界: This paper is about photonic crystal fiber for optical communication, with no LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing, no porous implant structure, and no biomedical context.
动机· 设计超平坦色散光子晶体光纤用于宽带通信
方法· 采用三角晶格蜂窝结构，通过有限差分频域法优化空气孔直径
结果· 在1.4-1.71μm波长范围实现0-0.13 ps/(nm·km)超平坦色散
验证· 数值模拟验证，无实验
English summary & terms
Motivation· To design a photonic crystal fiber with ultra-flattened dispersion for wideband optical communication systems.
Method· A honeycomb structure with triangular lattice and three different air-hole diameters in the cladding is proposed, optimized using 2-D finite difference frequency domain method with transparent boundary conditions.
Result· Ultra-flattened dispersion of 0-0.13 ps/(nm·km) is achieved over the wavelength range 1.4 to 1.71 μm.
Validation· Numerical simulation only, no experimental validation.

Promoting active learning in introductory financial accounting through the flipped classroom design

Camillo Lento Journal of Applied Research in Higher Education 2016-01-15 10.1108/jarhe-01-2015-0005
单位: Lakehead University, Thunder Bay, Canada
通讯: Camillo Lento @ Lakehead University, Thunder Bay, Canada
相关性: 该论文研究会计教育中的翻转课堂，与有限元代理建模或生物力学设计完全无关。
边界: This paper is about accounting education pedagogy, with no FEA, surrogate modeling, uncertainty quantification, or biomedical/mechanical design content.
动机· 探索翻转课堂在会计入门课程中的效果
方法· 准实验设计，对比翻转课堂与传统课堂的学生成绩
结果· 翻转课堂提高了学生成绩和通过率
验证· 通过期末考试和课程总成绩进行统计检验
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate whether a flipped classroom design improves student performance in introductory financial accounting.
Method· A quasi-experimental design with control (lecture-based) and experimental (flipped) groups, using Wilcoxon rank-sum test to compare final exam scores and course grades.
Result· The flipped classroom significantly improved grade point averages, final exam performance, and pass rates for both stronger and weaker students.
Validation· Statistical comparison of student performance metrics between control and experimental groups.

PORTFOLIO AS AN ACTIVE LEARNING METHODOLOGY FOR THE STUDY OF THE ENDOCRINE SYSTEM

Luís Henrique Montrezor, Brancaglião Lfc, Gonçalves Vm, Jes Lemos, NF Rocha et al. International Education and Research Journal 2016-01-15 link
单位: Department of Biological Science and Health - Medicine, Araraquara University Center - UNIARA, Araraquara, SP, Brazil#R##N##R##N#Medical Chemistry and Regenerative Medicine Group – QUIMMERA – UNIARA,
通讯: Luís Henrique Montrezor @ Department of Biological Science and Health - Medicine, Araraquara University Center - UNIARA, Araraquara, SP, Brazil#R#
相关性: 该论文是关于医学教育中内分泌系统教学方法的，与有限元代理建模或生物力学设计无关。
边界: This paper is about medical education pedagogy, not about surrogate modeling, finite element analysis, or any computational method for implant design.
动机· 探索档案袋作为主动学习方法在内分泌系统教学中的效果。
方法· 将125名医学生分组制作包含7个主题的档案袋，并用于认知监测测试。
结果· 92.8%的学生认为档案袋有效，多数测试成绩高于平均分。
验证· 通过学生反馈和测试成绩分析评估教学效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the effectiveness of portfolio as an active learning methodology in teaching endocrine system physiology to medical students.
Method· Second-year medical students (n=125) were divided into groups to create portfolios with 7 cards covering 7 topics, which were then used as reference material for a cognitive monitoring test.
Result· 92.8% of students found the portfolio effective, and the majority of test scores were above the course average.
Validation· Validation was based on student opinion surveys and analysis of cognitive monitoring test scores.

The effect of an interactive delirium e-learning tool on healthcare workers’ delirium recognition, knowledge and strain in caring for delirious patients: a pilot pre-test/post-test study

Elke Detroyer, Fabienne Dobbels, Deborah Debonnaire, Kate Irving, Andrew Teodorczuk et al. BMC Medical Education 2016-01-15 10.1186/s12909-016-0537-0
单位: Department of Public Health and Primary Care, Academic Centre for Nursing and Midwifery, KU Leuven, Kapucijnenvoer 35 - PB 7001/4, 3000, Leuven, Belgium. elke.detroyer@med.kuleuven.be
通讯: Elke Detroyer @ Department of Public Health and Primary Care, Academic Centre for Nursing and Midwifery, KU Leuven, Kapucijnenvoer 35 -
相关性: 该论文研究的是电子学习工具对医护人员谵妄识别和知识的影响，与有限元代理建模或生物力学设计无关。
边界: This paper is a clinical education study with no FEA, surrogate modeling, uncertainty quantification, or biomechanics content.
动机· 评估谵妄电子学习工具对医护人员识别、知识和护理压力的效果。
方法· 前后测设计，59名医护人员使用含11个模块的电子学习工具，测量识别、知识和压力。
结果· 工具提高了谵妄识别和知识，但护理压力无改善，效果与完成模块数相关。
验证· 前后测对比及亚组分析，但未控制所有混杂因素。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the effect of an interactive delirium e-learning tool on healthcare workers' recognition, knowledge, and care strain.
Method· A pre-test/post-test pilot study with 59 healthcare workers using an 11-module e-learning tool; outcomes measured via case vignettes, knowledge questionnaire, and strain index.
Result· The e-learning tool significantly improved delirium recognition and knowledge, but care strain did not change; the effect was associated with module completion level.
Validation· Pre-post comparison with subgroup analysis, but adjusted analyses showed attenuated effects.

Functional recovery after muscle sparing total hip arthroplasty in comparison to classic lateral approach – A three years follow-up study

Anton Tudor, Lana Ružić, Mirela Vučković, Tomislav Prpić, Ivan Rakovac et al. Journal of Orthopaedic Science 2016-01-15 10.1016/j.jos.2015.12.010
单位: Orthopaedic Clinic Lovran, University of Rijeka, Croatia
通讯: Lana Ružić @ Faculty of Kinesiology, University of Zagreb, Croatia. Electronic address: lana.ruzic@kif.hr
相关性: 该论文关注临床功能恢复，未涉及股骨应力遮挡或微动等生物力学分析。
边界: This paper is a clinical follow-up study comparing surgical approaches, with no biomechanical modeling, no FEA, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, and no implant design analysis.
动机· 比较微创与经典外侧入路在全髋置换术后三年的功能恢复差异。
方法· 前瞻性队列研究，评估两组患者术后功能评分和并发症。
结果· 微创入路在早期功能恢复上优于经典入路，但三年后差异不显著。
验证· 临床随访数据统计分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare the long-term functional recovery between muscle-sparing and classic lateral approaches in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A prospective cohort study evaluating functional scores and complications over three years post-surgery.
Result· The muscle-sparing approach showed better early recovery, but differences diminished by three years.
Validation· Clinical follow-up data with statistical analysis.
