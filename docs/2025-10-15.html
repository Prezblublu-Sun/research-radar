边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, constraint handling, or uncertainty quantification; it focuses on PDE solving without biomedical application.

Result· TGPS achieves superior accuracy and efficiency on several benchmark PDEs compared to existing methods.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on standard PDE problems demonstrate the method's performance.
