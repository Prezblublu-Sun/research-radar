Method· A retrospective analysis of 189 patients aged 40 years or older who underwent hip arthroscopy, with minimum 3-year follow-up assessing mHHS, HOS-ADL, VAS pain, range of motion, and Tönnis grade.
Result· Significant improvements in clinical scores were observed, though internal rotation and flexion slightly decreased at final follow-up.
Validation· Clinical follow-up data analysis.
