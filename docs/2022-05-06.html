单位: Norman Bethune First Hospital, Jilin University, Changchun 130021, PR China
通讯: Dahui Sun @ Norman Bethune First Hospital, Jilin University, Changchun 130021, PR China
相关性: 该论文直接研究了3D打印多孔钛合金支架的仿生设计，通过调节孔径和孔隙率来匹配骨刚度、减少应力遮挡，并进行了体内外实验验证骨整合效果。
边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing or surface roughness characterization, and the mechanical testing is limited to static compression without dynamic loading.
动机· 钛合金植入物刚度高于骨导致应力遮挡，需设计多孔支架匹配骨力学性能。

Result· ITS scaffolds enhanced cell proliferation and osteogenic differentiation, and the 400 μm pore size ITS scaffold achieved the best osseointegration in a rabbit tibia defect model.
Validation· In vitro cell assays and in vivo rabbit tibia bone defect model were employed to evaluate physicochemical properties and osseointegration.
