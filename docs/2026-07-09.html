Motivation· Current methods for quantifying uncertainty in LLM reasoning ignore the logical validity of intermediate steps.
Method· Proposes GRAPHEVAL, a graph-based reasoning framework with a novel metric GRCS and a medoid-based decoding strategy GSC.
Result· GRCS is consistently negatively correlated with reasoning faithfulness, and GSC trades nominal accuracy for improved reasoning fidelity.
Validation· Experimental validation on multiple LLM models without physical or engineering benchmarks.
