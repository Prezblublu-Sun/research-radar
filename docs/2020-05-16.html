Explicit finite element analysis can predict the mechanical response of conical implant press-fit in homogenized trabecular bone

High Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Direct Read now ex_vivo Exp. validation
Marzieh Ovesy, Marcel Aeschlimann, Philippe K. Zysset Journal of Biomechanics 2020-05-16 10.1016/j.jbiomech.2020.109844
单位: ARTORG Centre for Biomedical Engineering Research, University of Bern, Freiburgstrasse 3, 3010 Bern, Switzerland. Electronic address: marzieh.ovesy@artorg.unibe.ch
通讯: Marzieh Ovesy @ ARTORG Centre for Biomedical Engineering Research, University of Bern, Freiburgstrasse 3, 3010 Bern, Switzerland. Electr
相关性: 该论文直接针对植入体压配过程中的接触力学和骨损伤建模，与髋关节假体应力遮挡和微动分析高度相关。
边界: This paper focuses on dental/orthopedic conical implants in trabecular bone, not specifically on femoral stem stress shielding or micromotion in Gruen zones.
动机· 预测植入体压配后的初始稳定性是临床手术规划的挑战。
方法· 开发显式有限元方法模拟刚性锥形植入体在松质骨中的压配，考虑接触摩擦和骨损伤。
结果· 计算的反作用力和卸载刚度与实验高度相关（R²=0.95和0.94）。
验证· 通过牛松质骨压配实验验证，对比力-位移曲线和刚度。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Predicting primary stability after implant press-fit is a major challenge in surgical planning for dental and orthopedic implants.
Method· An explicit finite element method was developed to simulate the press-fit insertion of a rigid conical implant into homogenized trabecular bone, incorporating frictional contact and element deletion to account for bone damage.
Result· The computed reaction forces and unloading stiffnesses showed high correlations (R²=0.95 and R²=0.94) with experimental measurements.
Validation· The model was validated against ex vivo press-fit experiments on bovine trabecular bone using a cyclic displacement-driven protocol.
