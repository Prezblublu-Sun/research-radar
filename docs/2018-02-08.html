Strong Cosserat Elasticity in a Transversely Isotropic Polymer Lattice

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Z. Rueger, Roderic S. Lakes Physical Review Letters 2018-02-08 10.1103/physrevlett.120.065501
单位: Department of Engineering Physics, Department of Materials Science, University of Wisconsin, Madison, Wisconsin 53706-1687, USA
通讯: Z. Rueger @ Department of Engineering Physics, Department of Materials Science, University of Wisconsin, Madison, Wisconsin 53706-16
相关性: 该论文研究聚合物晶格的Cosserat弹性效应，与AM生物医学方向无直接关联。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no biomedical implant context, no stress shielding or osseointegration, no fatigue testing.
动机· 实验测量聚合物晶格中的强Cosserat弹性效应。
方法· 对三角形晶格进行扭转和弯曲实验，测量尺寸效应。
结果· 扭转和弯曲刚度分别增大36倍和29倍，Cosserat特征长度与晶胞尺寸相当。
验证· 通过实验测量验证理论预测。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To experimentally demonstrate strong Cosserat elasticity effects in polymer lattices, which are much larger than in stretch-dominated lattices.
Method· Torsion and bending experiments were performed on triangular unit cell lattices to measure size effects.
Result· Torsion rigidity increased by a factor of 36 and bending rigidity by 29, with Cosserat characteristic lengths comparable to cell size.
Validation· Experimental measurements confirmed the theoretical predictions of Cosserat elasticity.
