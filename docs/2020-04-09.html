单位: State Key Lab. of Traction Power, Southwest Jiaotong University, Chengdu 610031, China
通讯: Shengchuan Wu @ Henry Royce Institute, Department of Materials, The University of Manchester, Manchester M13 9PL, UK
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM制造缺陷对Ti-6Al-4V疲劳寿命的影响，与用户关注的LPBF制造生物医学植入物的疲劳性能直接相关。
边界: This paper does not link porous structure to stress shielding or stiffness matching, nor does it report surface roughness, as-built morphology, or osseointegration.
动机· 研究SLM制造缺陷对Ti-6Al-4V疲劳寿命的影响机制
方法· 使用μCT表征缺陷，结合NASGRO模型预测疲劳寿命，定义扩展缺陷区(XDZ)评估缺陷威胁
结果· 缺陷中心距表面2r0内时影响显著，紧贴表面时最大

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate how manufacturing defects from selective laser melting affect the fatigue life of Ti-6Al-4V structures.
Method· X-ray micro computed tomography characterized defect population, morphology, dimension, and location; the NASGRO fatigue crack growth model combined with an effective initial crack length defined by cyclic plastic zone and defect radius predicted fatigue life; an extended defect zone (XDZ) indicator ranked defect threat.

Validation· Numerical analysis and experimental fatigue testing validated the model predictions.
