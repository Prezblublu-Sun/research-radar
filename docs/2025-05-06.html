The Effect of Obesity on Prosthesis Positioning in Total Hip Arthroplasty Through the Posterolateral Approach: A Retrospective Study on 614 Total Hip Arthroplasties

Hans Christian S Vistisen, Mads J Rex, Mogens Berg Laursen, Thomas Jakobsen Arthroplasty Today 2025-05-06 10.1016/j.artd.2025.101696
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Aalborg University Hospital, Aalborg, Denmark
通讯: Hans Christian S Vistisen @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Aalborg University Hospital, Aalborg, Denmark
相关性: 与用户方向不相关，该论文纯临床，无生物力学建模、无FEA、无应力遮挡或微动分析。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, no FEA, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no contact mechanics, no implant-bone interface analysis.
动机· 研究肥胖对全髋关节置换假体位置的影响。
方法· 回顾性分析614例THA的术后X线片，比较肥胖与正常体重组的杯体角度和柄对齐。
结果· 肥胖组杯体前倾角显著减小，倾斜角增大。
验证· 基于614例患者数据的统计回归分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the influence of obesity on acetabular component positioning in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective analysis of 614 postoperative radiographs comparing cup anteversion, inclination, and stem alignment between obese and normal-weight groups using adjusted linear regression.
Result· Obese patients had significantly lower anteversion and greater inclination of the acetabular component.
Validation· Statistical analysis of clinical radiographic data from 614 patients.

Complications After Craniofacial Reconstruction With PEEK PSI Insertion: Pathological Comparison of PEEK and Titanium.

Hye Ju Han, Dong Yeon Kim The Journal of craniofacial surgery 2025-05-06 10.1097/SCS.0000000000011388
单位: Department of Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery, Incheon St. Mary's Hospital, College of Medicine, The Catholic University of Korea.
通讯: Dong Yeon Kim @ Department of Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery, St. Vincent's Hospital, College of Medicine, The Catholic University o [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究颅颌面重建中的PEEK植入物并发症，与髋关节假体及股骨柄应力遮挡、微动分析等研究方向无关。
边界: This paper is a clinical case report on craniofacial PEEK implants with histological comparisons; it does not quantify hip implant stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-interface mechanics, nor does it involve finite element analysis.
动机· 探讨PEEK材料在颅颌面个性化植入物中的优势与并发症。
方法· 报告两例PEEK植入物病例并进行组织学比较。
结果· PEEK植入物存在感染和液体积聚等并发症，与钛植入物的组织反应不同。
验证· 临床病例报告和组织学分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates the advantages and complications of polyetheretherketone (PEEK) patient-specific implants in craniofacial surgery.
Method· Two clinical cases of PEEK implants for maxilla and orbit reconstruction are reported, with histological comparison of tissue reactions around PEEK and titanium implants.
Result· PEEK implants are associated with postoperative infection and peri-implant fluid collection, and exhibit distinct pathological tissue responses compared to titanium.
Validation· Clinical case observation and histological examination.
