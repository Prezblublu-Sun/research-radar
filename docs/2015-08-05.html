THA Revisions Using Impaction Allografting With Mesh Is Durable for Medial but Not Lateral Acetabular Defects

Eduardo García‐Rey, Rosário Madero, Eduardo García-Cimbrelo Clinical Orthopaedics and Related Research 2015-08-05 10.1007/s11999-015-4483-7
单位: Orthopaedics Department, Hospital La Paz-Idi Paz, Pº Castellana 261, 28046, Madrid, Spain. edugrey@yahoo.es
通讯: Eduardo García‐Rey @ Orthopaedics Department, Hospital La Paz-Idi Paz, Pº Castellana 261, 28046, Madrid, Spain. edugrey@yahoo.es
相关性: 该论文关注髋臼翻修中的植骨和网笼使用，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡或微动分析。
边界: This paper is a clinical outcome study on acetabular revision with impaction bone grafting, with no biomechanical modeling, no femoral stem stress shielding or micromotion quantification, and no FEA or contact mechanics.
动机· 评估打压植骨联合网笼在髋臼翻修中的长期效果及失败风险因素。
方法· 回顾性分析226例髋臼翻修，比较不同Paprosky缺损类型和网笼使用组的影像学松动率。
结果· 外侧网笼使用是松动的独立危险因素，15年生存率显著低于无网笼组。
验证· 临床随访（平均10年）和回归分析控制混杂变量。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the long-term survivorship and risk factors for failure of impaction bone grafting with mesh in acetabular revision for severe bone defects.
Method· Retrospective cohort study of 226 acetabular revisions with impaction bone grafting, comparing radiographic loosening rates by Paprosky defect type and mesh use (medial, lateral, or both).
Result· Lateral mesh use was the strongest independent risk factor for loosening, with 15-year survivorship significantly lower than no mesh or medial mesh only.
Validation· Clinical follow-up (mean 10 years) with multivariate regression controlling for confounding variables.
