The Static and Fatigue Behavior of AlSiMg Alloy Plain, Notched, and Diamond Lattice Specimens Fabricated by Laser Powder Bed Fusion

Hugo Soul, Patrick Terriault, Vladimir Braïlovski Journal of Manufacturing and Materials Processing 2018-04-20 10.3390/jmmp2020025
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Ecole de Technologie Superieure, 1100, Notre-Dame Street West, Montreal, QC H3C 1K3, Canada
通讯: Vladimir Braïlovski @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Ecole de Technologie Superieure, 1100, Notre-Dame Street West, Montreal, QC H3C 1K
相关性: 该论文研究了AlSiMg合金金刚石点阵结构的静态和疲劳行为，但未涉及生物医学植入物或应力屏蔽匹配。
边界: This paper does not link porous structure to stress shielding or stiffness matching for biomedical implants, nor does it report surface roughness, as-built morphology, or osseointegration.
动机· 研究激光粉末床熔融AlSiMg合金点阵结构的静力和疲劳行为。
方法· 制备三种取向的金刚石点阵及致密试样，进行拉伸和拉-拉疲劳测试。
结果· 点阵取向显著影响疲劳寿命，[001]取向比[011]和[111]取向疲劳强度高约30%。
验证· 通过实验力学测试验证，包括静态拉伸和疲劳循环测试。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the static and fatigue behavior of diamond lattice structures fabricated from AlSiMg alloy via laser powder bed fusion.
Method· Diamond lattice specimens with three cell orientations ([001], [011], [111]) and dense plain/notched specimens were fabricated and subjected to tensile and pull-pull fatigue testing up to 10^7 cycles.
Result· Cell orientation significantly affected fatigue life, with [001]-oriented specimens showing approximately 30% higher fatigue resistance than [011] and [111] orientations.
Validation· Experimental validation through static tensile and force-controlled fatigue testing.
