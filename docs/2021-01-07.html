M. Ulivi, Luca Orlandini, Jacopo Antonino Vitale, Valentina Meroni, Lorenzo Prandoni et al. Acta Orthopaedica 2021-01-07 10.1080/17453674.2020.1865633
单位: IRCCS Istituto Ortopedico Galeazzi, Milan;
通讯: Jacopo Antonino Vitale @ IRCCS Istituto Ortopedico Galeazzi, Milan;
相关性: 该论文为临床随机对照试验，不涉及任何生物力学建模或应力分析。
边界: This paper reports clinical outcomes of surgical approaches; it does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics via FEA or experimental testing.
动机· 比较直接上方入路与后外侧入路在髋关节置换术后的早期功能恢复。
方法· 随机对照试验，包括步态分析、跌倒风险评估和临床测量。
结果· 直接上方入路手术时间更长但出血更少，步态参数无组间差异。
验证· 临床随机对照试验，样本量较小。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare early functional recovery between direct superior and posterolateral approaches in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A randomized controlled trial with gait analysis, fall risk assessment, and clinical measurements before and after surgery.
Result· Direct superior approach showed longer surgical time and lower blood loss, with no inter-group differences in spatiotemporal gait parameters.
Validation· Clinical randomized controlled trial with 45 patients.
