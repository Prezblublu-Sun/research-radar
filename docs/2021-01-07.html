通讯: Anuwat Pongkunakorn @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Lampang Hospital and Medical Educational Center, Lampang, Thailand. dranuwat@hotmail.
相关性: 该论文关注术中股骨柄前倾角测量精度，未涉及应力遮挡、微动或有限元分析。
边界: This paper focuses on surgical measurement accuracy of stem anteversion, with no biomechanical modeling, stress shielding analysis, micromotion quantification, or finite element simulation.
动机· 评估数字量角器和水平仪在术中测量股骨柄前倾角的准确性。
方法· 前瞻性研究107例患者，比较三种术中测量方法与术后CT的真实前倾角。
结果· 数字量角器结合水平仪的方法误差最小，平均偏差0.8°。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the accuracy of a digital protractor combined with a spirit level for intraoperative measurement of femoral stem anteversion in cementless THA.
Method· A prospective study of 107 patients compared three intraoperative estimation methods (visual, digital protractor alone, and digital protractor with spirit level) against postoperative CT measurements.
Result· The combination of digital protractor and spirit level yielded the smallest mean deviation (0.8°) from true anteversion, with 93.9% of estimates within 5° error.
Validation· Validation was performed clinically using postoperative CT scans as the reference standard.
