Optimization of 3D bioprinting of human neuroblastoma cells using sodium alginate hydrogel

Jakub Lewicki, Joost Bergman, Caoimhe Kerins, Ola Hermanson Bioprinting 2019-06-18 10.1016/j.bprint.2019.e00053
单位: Department of Neuroscience, Karolinska Institutet, Biomedicum, SE17177, Stockholm, Sweden
通讯: Jakub Lewicki @ Department of Neuroscience, Karolinska Institutet, Biomedicum, SE17177, Stockholm, Sweden
相关性: 本文未涉及AI或机器学习在生物打印中的应用。
边界: This paper does not integrate AI/ML into any bioprinting workflow stage; it uses a conventional parameter optimization index.
动机· 优化3D生物打印参数以提高细胞活力和打印精度。
方法· 使用参数优化指数（POI）结合FRESH打印方法优化海藻酸钠水凝胶的打印参数。
结果· 确定了最佳参数，在7天培养中保持83%的神经母细胞瘤细胞活力。
验证· 通过细胞培养和活/死染色验证打印条件对细胞活力的影响。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To standardize extrusion-based bioprinting by optimizing printing parameters for sodium alginate hydrogels with human neuroblastoma cells.
Method· The parameter optimization index (POI) was applied to FRESH bioprinting to find optimal pressure and speed for high print fidelity and cell viability.
Result· Optimal parameters yielded 83% viable cells after 7 days, with notable differences between cell-laden and cell-free hydrogels.
Validation· Long-term cell culture assays and viability staining validated the sufficiency of the optimized conditions.

Mycelium Artifacts

Jennifer Weiler, Piyum Fernando, Nipuni Siyambalapitiya, Stacey Kuznetsov 2019-06-18 10.1145/3301019.3325156
单位: Arizona State University, Tempe, AZ, USA
通讯: Jennifer Weiler @ Arizona State University, Tempe, AZ, USA
相关性: 论文使用菌丝体进行生物制造，但未涉及AI集成或生物打印流程，与用户研究方向无关。
边界: This paper does not integrate AI into any bioprinting workflow stage, lacks any experimental validation related to bioprinting, and does not address autonomous decision-making; it focuses on mycelium growth for HCI design.
动机· 探索菌丝体作为可持续生物制造材料在交互设计中的应用
方法· 通过一年跨学科实验，种植菌丝体形成3D形状
结果· 展示了三种菌丝体制造可能性，包括低保真外壳、雕塑和3D模型复制品
验证· 基于实践经验，未涉及定量验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To explore mycelium as a sustainable biofabrication material for interactive system design.
Method· Over one year, an interdisciplinary team experimented with growing 3D forms from mycelium using various substrates and molds.
Result· Three fabrication possibilities were demonstrated: disposable enclosures, sculpted forms, and physical replicas of 3D models.
Validation· Validation is based on hands-on experience and challenges encountered, without quantitative or experimental validation.

Wear Behavior of Aluminum Alloys Made with Selective Laser Melting (SLM) Additive Manufacturing

Nicole Christy D-Scholarship@Pitt (University of Pittsburgh) 2019-06-18 link
相关性: 该论文专注于铝合金的SLM增材制造和磨损行为，与生物医学植入物的应力屏蔽、疲劳或多孔结构无关。
边界: This paper does not address biomedical implant materials, porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue, osseointegration, or any medical context; it focuses on aluminum alloys for engine pistons.
动机· 研究SLM工艺参数对铝合金磨损性能的影响，针对发动机活塞应用。
方法· 通过改变能量密度制备AlSi10Mg样品，进行显微组织和磨损测试。
结果· 高能量密度样品微观结构变化，磨损性能提升。
验证· 与常规制造铝合金进行磨损性能对比验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of SLM process parameters on the wear resistance of aluminum alloys for engine piston applications.
Method· AlSi10Mg coupons were fabricated with varying energy densities via SLM, then characterized for microstructure and wear performance.
Result· Higher energy density led to altered microstructure and melt pools, resulting in improved wear resistance compared to lower energy density samples.
Validation· Wear performance was compared against conventionally manufactured aluminum alloys and across different SLM parameter sets.

The effect of ultraviolet photofunctionalization of titanium instrumentation in lumbar fusion: a non-randomized controlled trial.

Hiroyuki Tominaga, Kanehiro Matsuyama, Yukihiro Morimoto, Takuya Yamamoto, Setsuro Komiya et al. BMC musculoskeletal disorders 2019-06-18 10.1186/s12891-019-2672-3
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Graduate School of Medical and Dental Sciences, Kagoshima University, 8-35-1 Sakuragaoka, Kagoshima, 890-8520, Japan. ygctd828@yahoo.co.jp.
通讯: Hiroyuki Tominaga @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Graduate School of Medical and Dental Sciences, Kagoshima University, 8-35-1 Sakuraga
相关性: 该论文为脊柱融合术中钛器械表面光功能化的临床试验，不涉及增材制造、多孔结构或力学测试，与用户研究方向无关。
边界: This paper is a clinical spine surgery trial with no LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing, no fatigue, no porous structure design, and no stress shielding analysis.
动机· 验证紫外线光功能化对腰椎融合术中钛器械骨结合能力的影响
方法· 非随机对照试验，比较光功能化与未处理钛笼的骨硬化程度
结果· 两组间骨硬化无显著差异，钛笼碳附着率仅20%
验证· 基于术后影像密度测量和X射线光电子能谱分析
English summary & terms
Motivation· To determine whether ultraviolet photofunctionalization enhances osseointegration of titanium instrumentation in lumbar fusion.
Method· A non-randomized controlled trial comparing photofunctionalized and untreated titanium cages in the same patient, with follow-up imaging and XPS analysis.
Result· No significant difference in peri-cage osteosclerosis between treated and untreated cages; carbon contamination was lower than in dental implants.
Validation· Clinical imaging at multiple time points and XPS measurement of carbon attachment.
