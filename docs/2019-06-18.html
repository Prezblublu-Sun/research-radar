A new surrogate modeling method combining polynomial chaos expansion and Gaussian kernel in a sparse Bayesian learning framework

单位: School of Aeronautics Northwestern Polytechnical University Xi'an China
通讯: Zhenzhou Lü @ School of Aeronautics Northwestern Polytechnical University Xi'an China
相关性: 该论文提出了一种结合多项式混沌展开和高斯核的稀疏贝叶斯代理建模方法，可迁移至生物力学有限元代理模型构建，但未涉及植入物设计或生物力学验证。
边界: This paper does not apply the surrogate method to biomedical or implant mechanics problems, lacks uncertainty quantification in the context of biomechanical design, and does not handle constraints or multi-objective optimization.
动机· 针对高维非光滑函数代理建模精度不足的问题，提出混合代理模型方法。

Motivation· To improve surrogate modeling accuracy for high-dimensional and nonsmooth functions, a hybrid approach combining global and local characteristics is proposed.
Method· A hybrid surrogate model is developed by integrating polynomial chaos expansion and Gaussian kernel within a sparse Bayesian learning framework, with parameter optimization via Bayesian evidence maximization.
Result· The proposed method demonstrates superior accuracy and robustness compared to the established PC-Kriging technique on benchmark problems.
Validation· The method is validated through numerical benchmark comparisons against PC-Kriging.
