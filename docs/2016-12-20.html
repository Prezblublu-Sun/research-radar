单位: School of Mathematics and Physics, The University of Queensland, Brisbane QLD 4072, Australia e-mail:
通讯: Andrew D. Cramer @ School of Mathematics and Physics, The University of Queensland, Brisbane QLD 4072, Australia e-mail:  |  Vivien J. Challis @ School of Mathematics and Physics, The University of Queensland, Brisbane QLD 4072, Australia e-mail:  |  Anthony P. 相关性: 该论文直接针对股骨假体的多孔结构设计与SLM制造，与用户研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not include mechanical testing (e.g., compression or fatigue) or osseointegration assessment, focusing solely on design optimization and manufacturability demonstration.
动机· 设计具有渐变微结构的多孔股骨假体以降低剪切应力和骨质吸收。
方法· 通过形状插值生成微结构集，结合应变能约束优化，并用SLM制造原型。
结果· 变微结构设计优于均质设计，微结构集选择显著影响优化结果。
验证· 数值优化验证设计性能，SLM原型展示可制造性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To design porous femoral implants with spatially varying microstructures that reduce shear stress at the bone-prosthesis interface and constrain bone resorption.
Method· A shape interpolation method generates microstructure sets, combined with optimization under strain energy constraint, and a prototype is fabricated via selective laser melting.
Result· Designs with varying microstructures outperform homogeneous ones, and the choice of microstructure set significantly affects the optimized implant design.
Validation· Design performance is verified through numerical optimization, and manufacturability is demonstrated by an SLM-fabricated metal prototype.

Wanlu Chow DR-NTU (Nanyang Technological University) 2016-12-20 link
相关性: 该论文研究胶原生物墨水的快速凝胶化，但未涉及任何AI或机器学习方法。
边界: This paper does not incorporate AI/ML into any bioprinting workflow stage, lacks experimental validation of AI models, and does not advance toward autonomous decision-making.
动机· 解决胶原生物墨水凝胶速度慢、机械强度不足的问题。
方法· 使用快速凝胶胶原前体与缓冲液交替打印，优化打印参数和比例。
结果· 9:1比例胶原凝胶储能模量高，打印30层构建高度约2mm。
验证· 通过线打印、频率扫描和网格打印验证打印效果。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To overcome the slow gelation and poor mechanical strength of collagen bioink for 3D bioprinting.
Method· Alternating layers of fast-gelling collagen precursor and buffer were printed, with optimization of printing parameters and precursor-to-buffer ratio.
Result· A 9:1 ratio yielded fourfold higher storage modulus, and a 30-layer construct of ~2 mm height was achieved.
Validation· Line printing fidelity, frequency sweep rheology, and grid pattern printing were used to validate printability and mechanical properties.

动机· 解决胶原蛋白作为生物墨水时力学强度低和凝胶速度慢的问题。
方法· 使用快速凝胶胶原前体，通过参数优化和缓冲液交替打印构建3D结构。
结果· 优化配比9:1，打印出约2mm高的3D结构，形状良好，但存在液体渗漏。

英文摘要与术语

Result· The optimal precursor-to-buffer ratio was 9:1, yielding a gel with fourfold higher storage modulus, and a 2mm high 3D construct with good shape was achieved.
Validation· Line printing fidelity, rheological frequency sweep tests, and grid pattern printing were performed to validate the printing process and material properties.
