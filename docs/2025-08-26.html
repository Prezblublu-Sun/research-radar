边界: This paper does not address bioprinting-specific materials, cell viability, or any biological context; it focuses on structural optimization for general additive manufacturing.
动机· 实现增材制造中自支撑结构的拓扑优化，减少后处理需求。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a topology optimization framework that produces self-supporting structures suitable for additive manufacturing, reducing the need for post-processing.
Method· A graph neural network is employed as a neural field over the finite element mesh to predict continuous material distributions, with an integrated AM filter to eliminate unsupported overhangs and stress constraints enforced via differentiable p-norm aggregation.
Result· The framework generates stress-constrained, manufacturable topologies under various loading and boundary conditions, demonstrating a practical pathway toward AM-ready designs.
Validation· Numerical experiments validate the framework's ability to produce manufacturable topologies with stress constraints.

边界: This paper does not address multi-objective optimization, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification beyond neural PDE training, or implant/biomechanics-specific applications.
动机· 提高神经PDE替代模型的训练样本效率，减少对大量轨迹数据的依赖。

Result· Accurate neural PDE stencil operators are learned from the computational equivalent of 10 timesteps, outperforming traditional ML emulators trained on thousands of trajectories.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on multiple PDE systems comparing rollout accuracy and stability against naive sampling and traditional emulators.
