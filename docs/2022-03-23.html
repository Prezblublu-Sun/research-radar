Motivation· To investigate the effects of N and Si additions on the microstructural evolution and tensile properties of an SLM-processed high-entropy alloy.
Method· The FeCoCrNiMn-(N,Si) alloy was fabricated by selective laser melting, then cold-rolled and isochronally annealed at various temperatures.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate temperature changes during osteotomy preparations and their potential impact on bone survival.
Method· Two straight and two tapered drill designs were tested on fresh human cadaver tibiae with external irrigation at varying RPMs, performing 240 osteotomies.
Result· Tapered drills generated significantly more heat than straight drills, but none of the measured temperatures exceeded the critical threshold of 47°C.
Validation· Validated using fresh human cadaver tibiae in a controlled experimental setup.
