Validation· Validation was performed using in vitro culture of GelMA hydrogels encapsulating human adipose-derived stem cells over 11 days.

单位: Additive Manufacturing Institute of Science and Technology (AMIST), University of Louisville, Louisville, KY 40292, USA
通讯: Behzad Fotovvati @ Additive Manufacturing Institute of Science and Technology (AMIST), University of Louisville, Louisville, KY 40292, USA
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM工艺参数对Ti6Al4V表面粗糙度的影响，并使用机器学习进行预测，对生物医学植入物的表面质量优化有参考价值。
边界: This paper does not link surface roughness to implant-specific outcomes such as stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration, and lacks any biomedical context or mechanical testing beyond surface profilometry.

方法· 设计单层光栅扫描实验，用白光干涉仪测量粗糙度，训练反向传播神经网络预测模型。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To address the challenge of poor predictability and high variability of surface finish in selective laser melting (SLM) parts.
Method· A fractional factorial design was used to fabricate single-layer raster scanning Ti6Al4V samples, surface roughness was measured by white-light interferometry, and a back-propagation neural network was trained on 216 datasets to predict roughness from process parameters.
Result· Laser power was found to be the most significant parameter; layer thickness contributed 10%–15% to roughness variation, and the ML model achieved a coefficient of determination of 98.8% on a separate test set of 32 samples.
Validation· The model was validated using held-out experimental data from 32 distinct parameter combinations.
