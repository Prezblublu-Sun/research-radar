通讯: Mohammad Khodaei @ Department of Materials Engineering Isfahan University of Technology, Isfahan 84156-83111, Iran
相关性: 该论文使用空间占位法制备多孔钛支架，但未涉及LPBF增材制造或疲劳性能，与用户研究方向关联较弱。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no fatigue testing, no stress shielding or stiffness matching analysis, and no surface roughness or osseointegration evaluation.
动机· 研究占位剂类型和冷压压力对多孔钛支架结构及力学性能的影响。
方法· 采用氯化钠和碳酸氢铵作为占位剂，通过冷压和烧结制备多孔钛，并用SEM和图像分析表征。

Motivation· To investigate the effect of spacer type and cold compaction pressure on the structural and mechanical properties of porous titanium scaffolds for bone tissue engineering.
Method· Porous titanium foams were fabricated using sodium chloride and ammonium bicarbonate as space holders via cold compaction and sintering, with morphology characterized by SEM and image analysis.
Result· Sodium chloride was identified as the superior spacer agent due to its morphological stability and absence of chemical side effects on titanium.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated through compressive testing and porosity measurements.

单位: Earth System Science Interdisciplinary Center University of Maryland College Park Maryland USA
通讯: Qingyun Duan @ Joint Center for Global Change Studies College of Global Change and Earth System Science, Beijing Normal University Beij

通讯: Michael N. Kang @ Insall-Scott-Kelly Institute for Orthopaedics and Sports Medicine
相关性: 该论文研究髋臼周围病变的骨折风险，而非股骨柄或应力遮挡，与用户方向不直接相关。
边界: This paper focuses on acetabular fracture prediction, not femoral stem stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics.
动机· 预测髋臼周围病变的病理性骨折风险
方法· 利用配对半骨盆标本制造缺损，CT测量体积后轴向加载至破坏

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To predict the risk of pathologic fracture in peri-acetabular lesions using a cadaveric biomechanical model.
Method· Eight paired hemi-pelvises with created defects were CT-scanned for volumetric measurement and axially loaded to failure using an MTS system.
Result· Defects larger than 40% of peri-acetabular volume significantly reduced load-bearing capacity, yet failure loads remained about 2.5 times peak physiological loads.
Validation· Cadaveric mechanical testing with paired controls.

单位: Orthopaedic Department Sorlandet Hospital Arendal PO Box 783, Stoa Arendal 4809 Norway
通讯: Knut Erik Mjaaland @ Orthopaedic Department Sorlandet Hospital Arendal PO Box 783, Stoa Arendal 4809 Norway
相关性: 该论文比较两种手术入路的肌肉损伤标志物和疼痛，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡或生物力学分析。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain, micromotion, or implant-bone interface mechanics; it is a clinical trial on surgical approach outcomes without any biomechanical modeling or FEA.
动机· 比较微创前入路与直接外侧入路在全髋置换术中的肌肉损伤和炎症标志物。
方法· 随机对照试验，测量肌酸激酶和C反应蛋白水平及疼痛评分。
结果· 前入路组肌酸激酶更高但疼痛更轻，外侧入路组疼痛更重且用药更多。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To objectively compare muscle damage and inflammation between minimally invasive direct anterior and direct lateral approaches in total hip arthroplasty using biochemical markers.
Method· A prospective randomized controlled trial measuring creatine kinase and C-reactive protein levels pre- and postoperatively, along with pain medication consumption and pain scores.
Result· The anterior approach resulted in higher creatine kinase levels but lower pain and medication use compared to the lateral approach.
Validation· Clinical validation with 163 patients randomized to two surgical approaches.

通讯: Klaus Pfeifer @ Department of Sports Science and Sports, University of Erlangen-Nuremberg, Erlangen and Nuremberg, Germany
