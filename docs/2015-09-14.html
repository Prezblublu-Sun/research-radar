Result· The multilevel approach achieves a favorable trade-off between computational cost and accuracy, outperforming standard single-level methods.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on elliptic PDE inverse problems demonstrate the convergence and efficiency of the proposed framework.

边界: This paper focuses on uncertainty quantification for experimental fluid dynamics (PIV), not on surrogate modeling for expensive simulations, constraint handling, or implant/biomechanics design.
动机· 量化PIV测量中压力场的不确定性。
方法· 基于贝叶斯框架，将速度后验协方差传播至泊松方程。

Method· The posterior covariance of velocity is propagated through the discretized Poisson equation for pressure, incorporating prior knowledge such as divergence-free constraints.

Validation· Validated on a steady Lamb-Oseen vortex (Monte Carlo comparison) and a turbulent boundary layer experiment (comparison with microphone measurements).
