相关性: 该论文研究全髋关节置换术中的促红细胞生成素用药方案，未涉及任何生物力学分析或植入物设计。
边界: This paper is a clinical trial on erythropoietin dosing for blood conservation, with no biomechanical modeling, implant design, FEA, or stress shielding analysis.
动机· 寻找全髋关节置换术中最优的短期促红细胞生成素方案以减少失血。
方法· 随机双盲对照试验，比较三种不同给药时程的rhEPO方案。
结果· 术前5天开始用药组失血最少，术后血红蛋白水平最高。
验证· 180例患者的随机对照临床试验。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine the optimal short-term daily recombinant human erythropoietin regimen for reducing perioperative blood loss in primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A double-blinded randomized placebo-controlled trial comparing three rhEPO dosing schedules (starting 5 days, 3 days, or on the day of surgery).
Result· The regimen starting 5 days preoperatively significantly reduced blood loss and improved postoperative hemoglobin levels compared to later starts.
Validation· Clinical validation with 180 patients in a randomized controlled trial.
