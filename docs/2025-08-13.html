相关性: 该论文涉及扭曲锡硒化物中的量子相，与生物医学增材制造方向完全无关。
边界: This paper is about theoretical condensed matter physics of twisted moiré systems, with no mention of additive manufacturing, biomedical implants, mechanical testing, or any aspect of AM biomedical materials.

Motivation· To investigate the emergence of correlated quantum phases in twisted bilayer SnSe2 in the strong coupling regime.

Validation· The study is purely theoretical, with no experimental validation.

Thermal Tracks: A Gaussian process-based framework for universal melting curve analysis enabling unconstrained hit identification in thermal proteome profiling experiments

Johannes F. Hevler, Shivam Verma, Mirat Soijtra, Carolyn R. Bertozzi arXiv preprint 2025-08-13 link
相关性: 该论文涉及高斯过程在蛋白质热稳定性分析中的应用，与有限元代理建模和植入物设计方向无直接关联。
边界: This paper applies Gaussian processes to thermal proteome profiling, with no connection to finite element analysis, surrogate modeling for mechanics, or implant design; it lacks any structural mechanics, constraints, or optimization context.
动机· 现有方法对非S形熔解曲线分析受限，且统计假设过于严格
方法· 用平方指数核高斯过程灵活建模任意形状熔解曲线
结果· 能无偏识别非常规熔解曲线的蛋白质，提高组学分析敏感性
验证· 框架发布在GitHub，无实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· Standard thermal proteome profiling methods impose sigmoidal assumptions and biased null distributions, missing biologically relevant proteins with non-canonical melting behavior.
Method· Thermal Tracks uses Gaussian processes with squared-exponential kernels to model arbitrary melting curve shapes and generate unbiased null distributions from kernel priors.
Result· The framework enables unbiased hit identification for proteins with unconventional melting profiles, including phase-separating and membrane proteins.
Validation· The method is implemented as a Python package available on GitHub, with no experimental validation reported.
