Beyond jump distance: modular dual mobility versus single mobility cups in robotic total hip arthroplasty - simulation of hip kinematics and impingement

Pascal Kouyoumdjïan, Youssef Jamaleddine, Emeline Chapron, Ahmad Haj Hussein, Mohammad Al Suwaidi et al. International Orthopaedics 2026-01-05 10.1007/s00264-025-06731-w
单位: Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Centre Hospitalier Universitaire de Nîmes, Nîmes, France
通讯: Youssef Jamaleddine @ Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Lebanese American University Medical Center-Rizk Hospital, Beirut, Lebanon. youssefjd@
相关性: 该论文聚焦于髋臼杯设计对运动学和撞击的影响，未涉及股骨柄的应力遮挡或微动分析。
边界: The paper does not quantify femoral stem stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics; it focuses on acetabular cup impingement and kinematics.
动机· 比较模块化双动与单动髋臼杯在机器人全髋置换术中的运动学表现。
方法· 基于机器人手术数据建立髋关节运动学仿真模型，模拟不同杯型的撞击边界。
结果· 双动杯提供了更大的无撞击运动范围，尤其在极端体位时优势明显。
验证· 通过对比已发表文献中的体内运动学数据验证仿真模型的有效性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare the kinematic performance and impingement risk between modular dual mobility and single mobility acetabular cups in robotic total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A computational kinematic simulation was developed using intraoperative robotic data and patient-specific parameters to evaluate impingement-free range of motion.
Result· Dual mobility cups demonstrated a significantly larger impingement-free range of motion, particularly in extreme hip positions.
Validation· Validation was performed by comparing simulated kinematics with in vivo motion data from published literature.
