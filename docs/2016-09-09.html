边界: No biomechanics modeling, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no FEA, no implant design or comparison.
动机· 比较急诊与延迟全髋置换治疗股骨颈骨折的并发症和功能结果。
方法· 前瞻性研究244例患者，分为急诊组和延迟组，评估并发症和功能评分。
结果· 急诊组住院时间更短，功能评分更好，并发症无差异。
验证· 临床前瞻性研究，统计比较组间差异。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine if urgent total hip arthroplasty for femoral neck fractures yields different postoperative complications and functional outcomes compared to delayed surgery.
Method· A prospective study of 244 patients comparing urgent (<12h) vs delayed THA, assessing mortality, complications, and functional scores at discharge, 1 month, and 3 months.
Result· Urgent THA resulted in shorter hospital stay and better functional scores at all time points, with no increase in complications.
Validation· Clinical prospective study with statistical analysis of group differences.
