Efficient batch-sequential Bayesian optimization with moments of truncated Gaussian vectors

Sébastien Marmin, Clément Chevalier, David Ginsbourger arXiv preprint 2016-09-09 link
相关性: 该论文提出了高效的批量贝叶斯优化方法，可用于代理模型辅助的昂贵仿真优化，对用户的FEA替代模型方向具有参考价值。
边界: This paper does not address implant or biomechanics design problems; it is a general methodological study without domain-specific constraints or validation.
动机· 提高贝叶斯优化中批量序列采样的效率
方法· 利用截断高斯向量矩的闭合形式计算多点期望改进准则及其梯度
结果· 实现了梯度驱动的批量采集函数优化，计算速度提升一到两个数量级
验证· 在标准数值基准测试上验证算法效率
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the efficiency of batch-sequential sampling in Bayesian global optimization.
Method· Derive closed-form expressions for moments of truncated Gaussian vectors to compute the multipoint expected improvement criterion and its gradient.
Result· Gradient-based batch acquisition optimization achieves one to two orders of magnitude computational savings.
Validation· Numerical experiments on standard benchmark functions demonstrate efficiency gains.

On the texture, phase and tensile properties of commercially pure Ti produced via selective laser melting assisted by static magnetic field

Nan Kang, Hao Yuan, Pierre Coddet, Zhongming Ren, Charles Bernage et al. Materials Science and Engineering C 2016-09-09 10.1016/j.msec.2016.09.011
单位: University Bourgogne Franche-Comte, IRTES EA7274, F-90100 Belfort, France. Electronic address: nan.kang@utbm.fr
通讯: Nan Kang @ University Bourgogne Franche-Comte, IRTES EA7274, F-90100 Belfort, France. Electronic address: nan.kang@utbm.fr  |  Hao Yuan @ Ecole Nationale d'Ingénieurs de Saint-Etienne (ENISE), 420023 Saint-Etienne, France. Electronic address: hao.yuan@emse.f
相关性: 该论文涉及SLM制备钛合金的织构与拉伸性能，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡、疲劳或骨整合等生物医学植入物核心问题。
边界: This paper focuses on texture and tensile properties of CP-Ti under static magnetic field during SLM, but does not address porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue, surface roughness, or osseointegration relevant to biomedical implants.
动机· 探索静磁场辅助SLM对纯钛织构和力学性能的影响。
方法· 在SLM过程中施加静磁场，制备纯钛样品并分析织构和拉伸性能。
结果· 静磁场促进了特定织构的形成并提高了拉伸强度。
验证· 通过XRD和拉伸测试验证了织构和力学性能的变化。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study aims to investigate the effect of a static magnetic field applied during selective laser melting on the crystallographic texture and mechanical properties of commercially pure titanium.
Method· Commercially pure titanium samples were fabricated via selective laser melting with an external static magnetic field, and their texture, phase composition, and tensile properties were characterized.
Result· The static magnetic field promoted the formation of a specific crystallographic texture and improved the tensile strength of the SLM-produced titanium.
Validation· Validation was performed through X-ray diffraction for texture analysis and tensile testing for mechanical properties.

Uncertainty quantification for generalized Langevin dynamics

Eric Joseph Hall, Markos A. Katsoulakis, Luc Rey-Bellet arXiv preprint 2016-09-09 10.1063/1.4971433
相关性: 该论文主要针对粒子动力学的敏感性分析，不涉及有限元代理建模或生物力学设计。
边界: No FEA context, no surrogate modelling for expensive simulations, no constraint handling, method specific to Langevin dynamics not transferable to implant biomechanics.
动机· 高效计算广义Langevin动力学的目标敏感性指标
方法· 通过公共随机路径耦合并使用有限差分估计器降低方差
结果· 耦合方法在方差最小化意义上达到最优，并通过实例验证效率提升
验证· 在粒子动力学数值示例中验证了估计器的效率
English summary & terms
Motivation· Develop efficient finite difference estimators for goal-oriented sensitivity indices in generalized Langevin dynamics where likelihood ratio methods fail.
Method· Couple nominal and perturbed dynamics via common random path or common noise to form variance-reduced finite difference estimators.
Result· The common random path coupling is proven optimal in minimizing estimator variance, and demonstrated effective on a comprehensive set of particle dynamics examples.
Validation· Theoretical proof of optimality combined with numerical benchmarks on generalized Langevin equation examples.

Image and Video Mining through Online Learning

Andrew Gilbert, Richard Bowden arXiv preprint 2016-09-09 link
相关性: 该论文讨论图像视频的在线聚类，与有限元代理模型无关。
边界: This paper focuses on image/video clustering via online learning and does not address FEA, surrogate modeling, optimization under constraints, uncertainty quantification, or biomechanical implant design.
动机· 减少图像视频标注成本，实现交互式聚类
方法· 基于图像签名的在线学习框架，迭代优化类间相似性
结果· 少量标注即可达到高准确率，UCF11用90样本达86.7%
验证· 在多个图像和视频数据集上评估，对比标准方法
English summary & terms
Motivation· To reduce the labeling effort for image and video datasets, enabling interactive clustering for home users.
Method· An online learning framework using image signatures that capture co-occurrence and frequency, with efficient metric distance computation and discriminative feature selection.
Result· The approach achieves 86.7% accuracy on UCF11 with only 90 labeled examples out of 1200+ videos, demonstrating scalability and efficiency.
Validation· Evaluated on seven datasets including image (15 scene, Caltech101, FG-NET), text-image (ImageTag), active learning (Iris), and video action recognition (UCF11, KTH, Hollywood2) datasets.
