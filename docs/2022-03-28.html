英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine the diagnostic accuracy of open incisional biopsy for periprosthetic joint infection prior to revision total hip arthroplasty in cases with negative or inconclusive joint aspirates.
Method· A retrospective single-center study of 32 consecutive revision THA cases compared microbiology and histopathology results from open incisional biopsy with those from definitive revision surgery.
Result· Open incisional biopsy showed limited diagnostic accuracy, with combined sensitivity of 82% and specificity of 60%, failing to reliably close the diagnostic gap.
Validation· Validation was performed by comparing biopsy results with intraoperative findings during revision arthroplasty as the reference standard.
