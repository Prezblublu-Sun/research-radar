Neural Network-Based Active Learning in Multivariate Calibration

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
A. Ukil, J. Bernasconi arXiv preprint 2015-03-19 10.1109/TSMCC.2012.2220963
相关性: 该论文提出基于神经网络集成不确定性的主动学习方法，虽用于化学计量学，但可迁移至代理建模中的高效采样策略。
边界: No application to finite element analysis, implant biomechanics, or any solid-mechanics surrogate modeling; constrained optimization not addressed.
动机· 减少化学计量学中校准模型所需的训练样本数量以降低成本。
方法· 利用神经网络集成的不一致性选择最有信息的校准样本。
结果· 主动学习比随机采样用更少的样本达到相同精度。
验证· 在真实化学计量校准例上验证了方法的有效性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To minimize the number of training samples required for accurate calibration in multivariate chemometric models.
Method· An active learning approach that selects the next calibration sample where an ensemble of neural networks shows the highest disagreement.
Result· The proposed method achieves target calibration accuracy with fewer training samples than random sampling.
Validation· Tested on a realistic chemometric calibration example demonstrating the efficiency gain.

Exclude FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Ignore none
Douglas Markant, Burr Settles, Todd M. Gureckis Cognitive Science 2015-03-19 10.1111/cogs.12220
单位: Center for Adaptive Rationality Max Planck Institute for Human Development
通讯: Douglas Markant @ Center for Adaptive Rationality Max Planck Institute for Human Development
相关性: 该论文研究人类主动学习中的信息采样策略，与FEA替代模型或生物力学设计无关。
边界: This paper focuses on human cognitive strategies in active learning, with no application to surrogate modeling, finite element analysis, or biomechanical design.
动机· 探索人类在学习过程中如何选择信息采样策略。
方法· 基于主动学习模型分析人类信息收集决策。
结果· 人类偏好局部不确定性采样而非全局不确定性。
验证· 行为实验与模型比较。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To understand how humans decide which information to sample during learning, inspired by active learning in machine learning.
Method· Several alternative models of information sampling were compared, and a behavioral experiment was conducted to distinguish them.
Result· Participants preferred items that resolve local pairwise uncertainty over items with high global uncertainty.
Validation· Model-based analysis of human behavioral data from a controlled experiment.
