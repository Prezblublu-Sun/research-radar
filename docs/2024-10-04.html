Motivation· To determine the prevalence and infection-free implant survival of unexpected positive cultures during presumed aseptic single-stage revision hip and knee arthroplasty.
Method· A single-centre review of 297 patients undergoing aseptic revision, with those having ≥3 positive cultures treated with oral antibiotics for ≥3 months and followed for minimum five years.
Result· 12.5% had unexpected positive cultures, mostly low-virulence organisms, and none developed periprosthetic joint infection or required reoperation at follow-up.
Validation· Clinical cohort study with minimum 5-year follow-up (range 5.1–12.3 years).
