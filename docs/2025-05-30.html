单位: Anesthesiology Department, The Affiliated Changsha Central Hospital, Hengyang Medical School, University of South China, Changsha 41000, Hunan, China

English summary & terms
Motivation· To identify risk factors for arterial line placement complications in elderly patients undergoing hip arthroplasty.
Method· A single-center retrospective study of 134 patients aged ≥65 years undergoing elective total hip arthroplasty with arterial catheterization.
Result· Diabetes and cardiac disease were independent predictors of perioperative complications.
Validation· Clinical data analysis with multivariable logistic regression.
