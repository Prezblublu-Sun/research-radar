Post Processing and Biological Evaluation of the Titanium Scaffolds for Bone Tissue Engineering

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Save for project in_vitro Exp. validation
Bartłomiej Wysocki, Joanna Idaszek, Karol Szlązak, Karolina Strzelczyk, Tomasz Brynk et al. Materials 2016-03-15 10.3390/ma9030197
单位: Faculty of Materials Science and Engineering, Warsaw University of Technology, 141 Woloska Str., Warsaw 02-507, Poland
通讯: Bartłomiej Wysocki @ Faculty of Materials Science and Engineering, Warsaw University of Technology, 141 Woloska Str., Warsaw 02-507, Poland
相关性: 该论文研究钛合金支架的化学抛光、力学性能和体外细胞反应，与用户关注的AM生物医学方向直接相关，但缺少疲劳测试和表面粗糙度量化。
边界: This paper does not cover fatigue testing, as-built surface roughness quantification, or in vivo osseointegration, which are key for evaluating long-term implant performance.
动机· 研究化学抛光对钛合金支架形态、孔隙率和力学性能的影响以匹配骨组织工程需求。
方法· 设计三种孔径的钻石晶格支架，SLM制备后采用不同酸浴化学抛光，并用SEM、μ-CT表征，进行力学测试和干细胞培养。
结果· 化学抛光后支架杨氏模量接近骨组织，小孔径促进细胞滞留和增殖。
验证· 通过力学测试和人骨髓间充质干细胞体外培养实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of chemical polishing on the morphology, porosity, and mechanical properties of additively manufactured titanium scaffolds for bone tissue engineering.
Method· Diamond cell scaffolds with three pore sizes were designed, fabricated via selective laser melting, chemically polished with various acid baths, and characterized by SEM, micro-CT, mechanical testing, and in vitro cell assays.
Result· Chemically polished scaffolds exhibited Young's modulus comparable to cancellous bone, and smaller pores enhanced cell retention and proliferation.
Validation· Validation was performed through mechanical compression tests and in vitro human mesenchymal stem cell culture experiments.
