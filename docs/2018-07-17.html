Study on selective laser melting and heat treatment of Ti-6Al-4V alloy

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Zhechao Fan, Hongwei Feng Results in Physics 2018-07-17 10.1016/j.rinp.2018.07.008
单位: Inner Mongolia Technical College of Mechanics and Electrics, Inner Mongolia 010070, China
通讯: Zhechao Fan @ Inner Mongolia Technical College of Mechanics and Electrics, Inner Mongolia 010070, China
相关性: 该论文研究Ti-6Al-4V合金SLM工艺参数与热处理对力学性能的影响，未涉及医用植入物的多孔结构或应力遮挡等生物力学问题。
边界: This paper focuses on dense Ti-6Al-4V mechanical properties under varying SLM parameters and heat treatment, with no porous structure, stress shielding, fatigue testing, surface roughness, or osseointegration assessment relevant to biomedical implants.
动机· 研究SLM工艺参数和热处理对Ti-6Al-4V合金性能的影响
方法· 同轴送粉SLM制备试样，改变激光功率、扫描速度和方向，并进行固溶时效热处理
结果· 500W激光功率和0.12m/min扫描速度获得最佳强度，回扫提高强度但降低延伸率
验证· 通过拉伸试验和显微组织观察验证力学性能和微观结构
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effects of selective laser melting process parameters and heat treatment on the microstructure and mechanical properties of Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· Ti-6Al-4V samples were fabricated by coaxial powder feeding SLM with varying laser power, scanning speed, and scanning direction, followed by solution and aging heat treatment.
Result· Optimal tensile strength and yield strength were achieved at 500 W laser power and 0.12 m/min scanning speed; round-trip scanning improved strength but reduced elongation.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated via tensile testing and microstructure was characterized by optical microscopy.

单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Sheffield, Sheffield, UK
通讯: Haider Ali @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Sheffield, Sheffield, UK
相关性: 该论文研究SLM工艺参数对Ti6Al4V残余应力和力学性能的影响，但未涉及多孔结构或应力遮挡等生物医学植入物相关设计。
边界: No porous structure, no stress shielding analysis, no biomedical implant context, no fatigue testing.
动机· 研究SLM工艺参数对Ti6Al4V残余应力和力学性能的影响
方法· 实验分析不同功率和曝光时间，结合有限元模拟
结果· 降低功率和增加曝光可降低残余应力并提高延伸率，增加层厚则反之

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· This study investigates the effect of layer thickness and processing parameters on residual stress and mechanical properties of SLM Ti6Al4V.
Method· Experimental analysis varying power and exposure at constant energy density, complemented by finite element simulation in ABAQUS.
Result· Decreasing power and increasing exposure reduced residual stress and improved elongation, while increasing layer thickness lowered residual stress but degraded mechanical properties.
Validation· Validation through mechanical testing and finite element simulation.

单位: 3D Bioprinting Laboratory Sabanci University Nanotechnology Research and Application Center Orhanli‐Tuzla Istanbul 34956 Turkey
通讯: Bahattin Koç @ 3D Bioprinting Laboratory Sabanci University Nanotechnology Research and Application Center Orhanli‐Tuzla Istanbul 3495
相关性: 该论文专注于肌腱脱细胞外基质生物墨水的开发与3D生物打印，未涉及任何人工智能方法，与用户的研究方向不相关。
边界: This paper does not incorporate AI/ML into bioprinting, lacks any computational modeling or autonomous decision-making, and is purely a materials development study.
动机· 开发具有良好形状保真度和生物相容性的肌腱脱细胞基质生物墨水
方法· 通过脱细胞和溶解方法制备dECM预凝胶，并利用微毛细管挤出进行原位凝胶化3D打印
结果· 不同浓度和消化时间的生物墨水表现出可调的凝胶动力学和机械强度，且无细胞毒性

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a decellularized tendon ECM bioink that provides both shape fidelity and biocompatibility for 3D bioprinting without support structures.
Method· Decellularized tendon ECM was processed into pre-gels that undergo rapid gelation at physiological conditions and are extruded through a microcapillary nozzle for in situ gelation.
Result· The bioink's gelation kinetics and mechanical properties were tunable by concentration and digestion time, and encapsulated NIH 3T3 cells remained viable with lineage-specific morphology.
Validation· In vitro cell viability assays confirmed the bioink's non-cytotoxicity and support for cell growth.
