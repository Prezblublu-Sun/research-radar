相关性: 该论文提出的Walsh-Hadamard神经算子能有效处理间断系数PDE，可迁移至生物力学中材料属性突变的代理建模。
边界: No validation on biomedical or implant-related problems, no uncertainty quantification, and no constraint handling.
动机· 标准傅里叶神经算子处理间断系数PDE时存在吉布斯现象，需新方法。
方法· 提出Walsh-Hadamard神经算子，结合矩形波谱与可学习权重。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Standard Fourier neural operators suffer from Gibbs phenomenon when solving PDEs with discontinuous coefficients, motivating a spectral basis better suited for piecewise constant fields.
Method· The Walsh-Hadamard Neural Operator (WHNO) uses Walsh-Hadamard transforms with learnable spectral weights to capture sharp interfaces and global dependencies.
Result· WHNO outperforms FNO on Darcy flow, heat conduction, and Burgers equation, and weighted ensembles of WHNO and FNO reduce MSE by 35-40%.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on three PDE problems with discontinuous coefficients, comparing against FNO under identical conditions.
