通讯: Dadi Jin @ Academy of Orthopaedics of Guangdong Province, Guangzhou, Guangdong 510630, China
相关性: 该论文研究了多孔钽植入物在脊柱融合中的生物相容性，但未涉及应力遮挡、LPBF制造或疲劳测试，与用户研究方向（AM生物医学材料）关联较弱。

单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, CHU Dijon Bourgogne, F-21000, Dijon, France. martzpierre@icloud.com
通讯: Pierre Martz @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, CHU Dijon Bourgogne, F-21000, Dijon, France. martzpierre@icloud.com
相关性: 该论文研究BMI对全髋关节置换术后髋关节活动度和步速恢复的影响，未涉及应力遮挡、微动或有限元分析。
边界: This paper does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics; it focuses on clinical functional outcomes without any biomechanical modeling or FEA.

Motivation· To investigate the influence of body mass index on functional recovery after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A prospective cohort study measuring sagittal hip range of motion and gait speed six months post-surgery.
Result· Higher BMI was associated with reduced hip range of motion and slower gait speed recovery.
Validation· Clinical follow-up data from patients.

Optimal strategies for the prevention of heterotopic ossification after total hip arthroplasty: A network meta-analysis

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore none Review
Liyang Cai, Zhan Wang, Xiangli Luo, Wei She, Hui Zhang International Journal of Surgery 2019-01-05 10.1016/j.ijsu.2018.12.011
单位: Department of Orthopaedics, Gansu Provincial Hospital, 204 Donggangxi Road, 730000, Lanzhou, Gansu Province, China
通讯: Hui Zhang @ Department of Orthopaedics, Gansu Provincial Hospital, 204 Donggangxi Road, 730000, Lanzhou, Gansu Province, China. Elec
相关性: 该论文关注全髋关节置换术后异位骨化的预防策略，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡、微动或生物力学分析。
边界: This paper is a clinical meta-analysis on heterotopic ossification prevention, with no biomechanical modeling, implant design, or FEA of the femur.
动机· 比较不同预防全髋置换术后异位骨化策略的疗效与安全性
方法· 收集相关随机对照试验，进行网络荟萃分析
结果· 放疗预防HO最有效，选择性NSAIDs最安全
验证· 基于已发表临床试验数据的间接比较
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the efficacy and safety of nonselective NSAIDs, selective NSAIDs, and radiation for preventing heterotopic ossification after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A network meta-analysis was conducted on randomized controlled trials using the surface under the cumulative ranking curve and node-splitting methods.
Result· Radiation was the most effective for preventing overall and Brooker grade-specific HO, while selective NSAIDs had the best safety profile.
Validation· The analysis was based on indirect comparisons of published clinical trial data without biomechanical or experimental validation.

Elliot Sappey‐Marinier, Anthony Viste, Yoann Blangero, Romain Desmarchelier, Michel-Henri Fessy International Orthopaedics 2019-01-05 10.1007/s00264-018-4279-y
单位: Hospices Civils de Lyon, Centre Hospitalier Lyon Sud, Service de Chirurgie Orthopédique et Traumatologique, 165 Chemin du Grand Revoyet, 69495, Pierre Benite Cedex, France
通讯: Elliot Sappey‐Marinier @ Hospices Civils de Lyon, Centre Hospitalier Lyon Sud, Service de Chirurgie Orthopédique et Traumatologique, 165 Chemin d
相关性: 该论文仅关注临床脱位和假体周围骨折发生率，未涉及任何生物力学建模或应力遮挡分析。
边界: This paper is a clinical outcome study comparing dislocation and fracture rates between dual mobility and standard cups; it does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or any biomechanical metrics in the proximal femur.
动机· 比较双动杯与标准杯在全髋置换术后脱位和骨折的发生率
方法· 回顾性临床研究，分析两组患者的并发症数据
结果· 双动杯组脱位率显著低于标准杯组，但骨折率无显著差异
验证· 基于临床病例数据的统计分析
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the incidence of dislocation and peri-prosthetic fracture between dual mobility and standard acetabular cups after primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective clinical study analyzing complication rates in two patient cohorts.
Result· The dual mobility cup group had a significantly lower dislocation rate but no significant difference in fracture rate compared to the standard cup group.
Validation· Statistical analysis of clinical case data.
