通讯: Lixian Lian @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, Sichuan University, Chengdu, 610065, PR China
相关性: 该论文直接涉及SLM制造医用锆合金及其力学性能，但未覆盖疲劳、多孔结构或骨整合等核心方向。
边界: This paper covers LPBF fabrication and static mechanical testing of zirconium, but lacks fatigue testing, surface roughness characterization, porous structure design for stiffness matching, and any osseointegration evaluation.

Motivation· To investigate the topological insulating behavior and catalytic activity of gold iodide monolayer for potential nanoelectronics and catalysis applications.
Method· Density functional theory calculations were performed to analyze band structures, edge states, and Gibbs free energy for hydrogen evolution.
