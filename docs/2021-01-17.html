通讯: Sang Soo Kim @ Department of Business Administration, Yong In University, Yongin-si, Korea
相关性: 该论文研究在线教育中的安全与隐私问题，与有限元代理建模或生物力学设计无关。

动机· 探讨在线教育中学生参与度的激励因素与障碍，重点关注安全与隐私问题。
方法· 基于296名学生的问卷调查数据，使用结构方程模型分析。

Motivation· To investigate motivators and barriers to student engagement in real-time online classes, with a focus on security and privacy concerns.
Method· A structural equation model was tested on survey data from 296 university students.
Result· Perceived usefulness and peer behavior directly influence participation intention, while privacy and security concerns negatively affect perceived ease of participation.
Validation· Survey-based empirical validation with structural equation modeling.
