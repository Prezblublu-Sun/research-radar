Deep learning in high dimension: Neural network expression rates for generalized polynomial chaos expansions in UQ

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project theoretical UQ
Christoph Schwab, Jakob Zech Analysis and Applications 2018-07-13 10.1142/s0219530518500203
单位: SAM, ETH Zürich, ETH Zentrum, HG G57.1, CH8092 Zürich, Switzerland
通讯: Christoph Schwab @ SAM, ETH Zürich, ETH Zentrum, HG G57.1, CH8092 Zürich, Switzerland
相关性: 该论文证明了深度神经网络在参数化PDE的UQ中能够克服维度灾难，其理论结果可迁移至生物力学代理模型的构建。
边界: This paper is purely theoretical with no FEA validation, no biomedical context, and no implant mechanics application.
动机· 证明深度神经网络在参数化PDE的UQ中能克服维度灾难
方法· 分析DNN对广义多项式混沌展开的表达速率
结果· 得到与维度无关的逼近速率，DNN可克服维度灾难
验证· 理论证明，无数值实验
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To establish that deep neural networks can overcome the curse of dimensionality when approximating countably-parametric holomorphic maps arising in PDE uncertainty quantification.
Method· The expressive power of DNNs is analyzed by deriving dimension-independent approximation rates for generalized polynomial chaos expansions with sparse coefficient sequences.
Result· Up to logarithmic factors, DNNs achieve a rate of O(N^{-1/2}) in terms of total network parameters, independent of the parameter dimension.
Validation· Theoretical proof only; no numerical experiments or simulations are conducted.

相关性: 该论文研究地球系统模型中的森林生物量不确定性，与生物力学有限元代理模型无关。
边界: This paper focuses on climate modeling and forest biomass uncertainty, with no FEA, surrogate modeling, implant design, or biomechanics context.

Method· Evaluated eight CMIP5 ESMs against remote sensing and ground-based biomass observations across northern extratropical latitudes.
Result· Large biases in forest distribution and carbon pools were found, with pre-industrial spin-up contributing more uncertainty than transient responses.
Validation· Validation against synthesized radar remote sensing and ground-based biomass data.
