动机· 研究新旧髋臼锉在THA中切割深度和热生成的差异。
方法· 使用MTS系统以临床相关力测试新旧锉刀，记录深度和温度。
结果· 新锉刀切割更深且产热更少；旧锉刀需更大力和时间达到相同深度，产热显著增加。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate how wear of acetabular reamers affects cutting depth and heat generation during total hip arthroplasty.
Method· New and used reamers were tested on an MTS system under a clinically relevant force of 87.6 N, measuring depth and thermal profiles.
Result· New reamers achieved 3.4 mm deeper penetration and generated 4.1°C less heat than used reamers; used reamers required 95-318% greater force and produced 16.8°C more heat to match new reamer depth.
Validation· Mechanical testing comparing new and community-used reamers under controlled force and depth conditions.
