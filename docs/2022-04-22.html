Error-in-variables modelling for operator learning

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Ravi G. Patel, Indu Manickam, Myoungkyu Lee, Mamikon Gulian arXiv preprint 2022-04-22 link
相关性: 该论文提出了一种处理输入噪声的算子学习方法，可迁移至生物力学代理模型中的不确定性量化。
边界: No implant or biomechanics application; no FEA surrogate or constraint handling; only PDE operator learning with synthetic noise.
动机· 算子学习中的输入噪声会导致有偏估计，需建模误差变量。
方法· 提出误差变量模型用于MOR-Physics和DeepONet，处理输入输出噪声。
结果· 在高噪声下，误差变量算子学习比普通最小二乘更鲁棒地恢复算子。
验证· 在1D/2D Burgers算子和Kuramoto-Sivashinsky方程上数值验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Noise in independent variables causes biased parameter estimates in operator learning, motivating error-in-variables models.
Method· Error-in-variables models are proposed for MOR-Physics and DeepONet to account for noise in both input and output functions.
Result· The proposed models reduce bias and robustly recover operators under high noise, outperforming ordinary least squares.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on Burgers operator in 1D/2D and Kuramoto-Sivashinsky evolution operator demonstrate effectiveness.

Bayesian operator inference for data-driven reduced-order modeling

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project simulation_case UQ
Mengwu Guo, Shane A. McQuarrie, Karen E. Willcox arXiv preprint 2022-04-22 10.1016/j.cma.2022.115336
相关性: 该论文提出的贝叶斯算子推断方法可用于构建具有不确定量化的降阶模型，有望迁移至生物力学植入物设计的代理建模中。
边界: The paper does not apply the method to biomedical or implant mechanics problems, and lacks validation on structural or contact mechanics benchmarks.
动机· 为降阶模型提供不确定性量化，解决数据驱动建模中的正则化选择问题。
方法· 将学习降阶模型视为贝叶斯逆问题，通过高斯先验和似然得到算子后验分布。
结果· 后验采样高效，预测具有不确定性，正则化项获得统计解释。
验证· 在可压缩欧拉方程和燃烧过程两个仿真案例上验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To endow reduced-order models with uncertainty quantification and provide a statistical interpretation for regularization in data-driven operator inference.
Method· The learning of reduced-order model operators is formulated as a Bayesian inverse problem with Gaussian prior and likelihood, yielding a posterior distribution over operators.
Result· The posterior enables efficient Monte Carlo sampling for predictive uncertainty, and the regularization term is interpreted via empirical Bayes.
Validation· The method is demonstrated on two simulation cases: the compressible Euler equations with noisy data and a single-injector combustion process.
