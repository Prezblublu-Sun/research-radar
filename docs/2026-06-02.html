相关性: 该论文直接研究了梯度多孔Gyroid结构植入物的力学性能与骨整合能力，与用户关于AM生物医学植入物的方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not report fatigue testing, surface roughness/as-built morphology, or explicit stress shielding quantification.
动机· 探究梯度多孔Gyroid结构植入物的力学与骨整合性能。

Validation· Mechanical compression tests and in vivo rabbit femur implantation with histological analysis.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To assess the correlation between dental implant primary stability and implant dimensions (length and diameter) across different bone types.
Method· A cross-sectional observational study measured primary stability of 41 dental implants using a damping capacity device (AnyCheck) at insertion.
Result· Wider and longer implants significantly increased primary stability, while bone quality did not show a statistically significant effect.
Validation· Clinical measurements with statistical hypothesis testing (p-values).
