边界: No porous structure design, no stress shielding or stiffness matching, no fatigue testing, no surface roughness or osseointegration-related characterization.
动机· 研究SLM用Ti-6Al-4V粉末回收对粉末特性的影响

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Background only simulation_case
Kevin Carlberg, Matthew Barone, Harbir Antil arXiv preprint 2015-04-15 link
相关性: 该论文比较了Galerkin与最小二乘Petrov-Galerkin模型降阶方法，但未涉及生物力学或植入物设计中的有限元代理建模。
边界: This paper focuses on model reduction for compressible fluid dynamics, with no application to FEA surrogate modelling, no constraint handling, no uncertainty quantification, and no biomechanics or implant design context.
动机· 比较Galerkin与最小二乘Petrov-Galerkin投影在非线性模型降阶中的性能。
方法· 理论分析结合数值实验，针对线性多步法和Runge-Kutta法两种时间积分方案。
结果· 发现LSPG ROM的误差并非随时间步长单调减小，需匹配基的谱内容。
验证· 在超过一百万自由度的湍流可压缩流动问题中进行数值验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare Galerkin and least-squares Petrov-Galerkin projection methods for nonlinear model reduction, addressing the limited comparative analysis in the literature.
Method· Theoretical analysis and computational comparison are conducted for two common time integrator classes: linear multistep and Runge-Kutta schemes.
Result· It is shown both theoretically and computationally that decreasing the time step does not necessarily reduce the error for LSPG reduced-order models; instead, the time step should be matched to the spectral content of the reduced basis.
Validation· Numerical experiments on a turbulent compressible flow problem with over one million unknowns validate the theoretical findings.

Motivation· To determine whether a count of preoperative pharmaceuticals is associated with postoperative outcomes after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective consecutive case series of 668 elective primary THA patients analyzed medication count and type against complications and length of stay.
Result· Each additional medication increased the risk of extended hospital stay by 1.15 times and 1-year complication risk by 1.19 times; specific drug types (antiplatelet, anticoagulant) showed higher odds ratios.

Motivation· To evaluate the feasibility and outcomes of total hip arthroplasty for postoperative ankylosis after hip fracture internal fixation.
Method· Cementless THA was performed in 23 patients with ankylosis; clinical and radiographic outcomes were assessed.
