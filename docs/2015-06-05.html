相关性: 该论文研究麻醉方式对输血率的影响，与髋关节植入物应力遮挡、微动或有限元分析无关。
边界: This paper focuses on anesthesia and transfusion outcomes in total hip arthroplasty, with no biomechanics modeling, implant design, FEA, or stress shielding analysis.
动机· 探讨神经轴麻醉是否降低全髋关节置换术输血率。
方法· 回顾性分析临床数据，比较不同麻醉方式输血率。
结果· 神经轴麻醉可能降低输血需求。
验证· 临床数据统计分析。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate whether neuraxial anesthesia reduces transfusion rates in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Retrospective analysis of clinical data comparing transfusion rates between neuraxial and general anesthesia.
Result· Neuraxial anesthesia was associated with lower transfusion requirements.
Validation· Statistical analysis of clinical patient data.
