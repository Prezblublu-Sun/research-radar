Preparation of Chitosan/Recombinant Human Collagen-Based Photo-Responsive Bioinks for 3D Bioprinting

Yang Yang, Zixun Wang, Yuanyuan Xu, Jingjing Xia, Zhaoxian Xu et al. Gels 2022-05-19 10.3390/gels8050314
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Tsinghua University, Beijing 100084, China
通讯: Yang Yang @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Tsinghua University, Beijing 100084, China
相关性: 该论文未涉及任何AI方法，仅属于生物打印材料研究，与AI驱动生物打印方向无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not embed AI into any bioprinting workflow stage; it is solely focused on bioink formulation and characterization without machine learning, data-driven modeling, or autonomous decision-making.
动机· 开发可靠的重组人胶原替代动物源胶原，用于生物打印。
方法· 制备壳聚糖/重组人胶原复合光敏生物墨水，通过挤出式打印构建结构。
结果· 生物墨水可调机械性能，打印性良好，支持HUVEC细胞存活。
验证· 体外细胞实验验证生物相容性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop reliable bioinks using recombinant human collagen as an alternative to animal-derived collagen for 3D bioprinting.
Method· Chitosan and methacrylated recombinant human collagen were combined to form photo-responsive composite bioinks for extrusion-based printing.
Result· The bioinks exhibited tunable mechanical properties and improved printability, and sustained HUVEC viability post-printing.
Validation· In vitro cell culture assays confirmed biocompatibility of the printed constructs.

English summary & terms
Motivation· Investigate the effect of annealing on the fracture process and tensile strength of selective laser melted non-combustible magnesium alloys.
Method· Tensile tests and fractography were performed on SLM-fabricated Mg specimens subjected to various annealing conditions.
Result· Annealing enhanced ductility but reduced strength, altering fracture modes from brittle to ductile.
Validation· Validation via mechanical testing and microstructural analysis.

Motivation· To elucidate the material removal behavior of various surface features on selective laser melted 316L stainless steel during electropolishing.
Method· Morphology and roughness were characterized on different surface orientations, and un-sintered powders and sintered areas were categorized and analyzed via experiments and simulations.
Result· Distinct microstructures and feature locations lead to diverse material removal behaviors during electropolishing.
Validation· Experimental surface characterization and finite element simulation were used to validate material removal mechanisms.

单位: Department of Engineering of Materials and of Bioprocesses, School of Chemical Engineering University of Campinas Campinas São Paulo Brazil
通讯: Ângela Maria Moraes @ Department of Engineering of Materials and of Bioprocesses, School of Chemical Engineering University of Campinas Campi
相关性: 该论文研究生物墨水的材料配方，未涉及任何人工智能方法，与AI生物打印方向不相关。
边界: This paper focuses solely on hydrogel formulation and rheological characterization with no AI/ML integration, computational modeling, or autonomous decision-making in the bioprinting workflow.
动机· 开发一种适合3D生物打印的非细胞毒性水凝胶。
方法· 将海藻酸钠与黄原胶混合，用锶离子交联优化配方。
结果· 1%海藻酸钠+2.5%黄原胶配方具有良好打印性和细胞相容性。
验证· 通过流变测试和细胞活力实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To formulate a non-cytotoxic hydrogel suitable for 3D bioprinting of cellularized structures.
Method· Blended alginate and xanthan gum at various ratios and crosslinked with strontium ions to optimize rheological and biological properties.
Result· A composition of 1% alginate and 2.5% xanthan gum yielded shear-thinning, printable hydrogels with high cell viability.
Validation· Rheological characterization and in vitro cell viability assays confirmed the hydrogel's performance.
