Validation· Validation is performed through a simulation case of an aircraft wing under typical flight loading conditions.

单位: Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and Periodontology, School of Dentistry of Ribeirão Preto, University of São Paulo, Ribeirão Preto, SP, Brazil
通讯: Gileade Pereira Freitas @ Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and Periodontology, School of Dentistry of Ribeirão Preto, University of Sã
相关性: 该论文研究了钛植入体表面纳米形貌对骨结合的影响，但未涉及LPBF制造、多孔结构或疲劳性能。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no porous structure, no mechanical testing (only removal torque), no fatigue or stress shielding analysis.
动机· 探究简单低成本的表面纳米形貌处理对钛植入体骨结合的影响
方法· 用H2SO4/H2O2处理钛植入体表面形成纳米形貌，植入兔胫骨后评估骨反应

Motivation· To evaluate whether a simple and low-cost H2SO4/H2O2 treatment creating surface nanotopography on titanium implants can elicit a bone response comparable to commercial microtopographic implants.

相关性: 该论文研究了能量采集系统中的不确定性量化，但未涉及生物力学或植入物设计，方法虽可转移但领域差异大。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, implant biomechanics, or constrained optimization; it focuses on energy harvesting systems with a specific UQ method.
动机· 量化压电能量采集器中参数不确定性对功率输出的影响。
方法· 结合正交法和最大熵原理计算输出响应的概率密度函数。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify the effect of parametric uncertainties on the performance of piezoelectric vibrational energy harvesters.
Method· The method of quadratures propagates weighted points to compute central moments, and the maximum entropy principle reconstructs the probability density function.
Result· Probability density functions for mean power and RMS deflection are obtained and validated against Monte Carlo simulations.
Validation· Numerical benchmark validation using Monte Carlo simulations.
