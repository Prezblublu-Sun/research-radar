单位: Argonne National Laboratory, Lemont, Illinois, United States
通讯: Yang Liu @ Argonne National Laboratory, Lemont, Illinois, United States
相关性: 该论文研究了深度神经网络的不确定性量化方法，可迁移至生物力学代理模型中的不确定性评估。
边界: This paper focuses on turbulence modeling for nuclear reactor safety, not on implant biomechanics, FEA surrogate, or biomedical design optimization.
动机· 深度神经网络作为代理模型时缺乏不确定性量化，现有贝叶斯方法计算昂贵。
方法· 采用蒙特卡洛dropout和深度集成两种高效UQ方法，应用于稠密连接卷积网络。
结果· 深度集成方法在计算成本和不确定性估计性能上优于贝叶斯神经网络。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Deep neural network surrogates for parametric PDEs lack efficient uncertainty quantification, as Bayesian neural networks are computationally prohibitive for complex architectures.
Method· Monte Carlo dropout and deep ensemble methods are applied to a densely connected convolutional network serving as a coarse-mesh turbulence closure model.
Result· The deep ensemble method provides reasonable uncertainty estimates with better scalability and lower computational cost compared to Bayesian neural networks.
Validation· The methods are validated on a nuclear reactor safety analysis benchmark using a turbulence model surrogate.
