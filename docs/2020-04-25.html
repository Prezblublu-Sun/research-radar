Motivation· To investigate the effect of mechanical surface treatments on the corrosion behavior of metallic biomaterials used in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Compressive residual stresses were introduced into Ti6Al4V and CoCrMo samples via deep rolling and microblasting, and corrosion behavior was characterized by repeated anodic polarization.

Validation· Corrosion behavior was validated through repeated anodic polarization measurements comparing treated and polished samples.
