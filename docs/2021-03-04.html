Study on mechanical properties and permeability of elliptical porous scaffold based on the SLM manufactured medical Ti6Al4V

Chenglong Shi, Nana Lu, Yaru Qin, Mingdi Liu, Hongxia Li et al. PLoS ONE 2021-03-04 10.1371/journal.pone.0247764
单位: School of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering, Qinghai Nationalities University, Xining, China
通讯: Chenglong Shi @ School of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering, Qinghai Nationalities University, Xining, China  |  Yaru Qin @ School of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering, Qinghai Nationalities University, Xining, China
相关性: 该论文直接针对SLM制备的多孔钛合金支架，研究其力学与渗透性能以匹配松质骨，与用户关于生物医用增材制造的研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing, surface roughness characterization, or osseointegration evaluation, which are needed for full implant validation.
动机· 研究椭圆孔隙结构骨支架的力学与渗透性能，匹配松质骨。
方法· 设计四种孔径/孔隙率/分布支架，SLM制备Ti6Al4V样本，进行压缩实验与CFD仿真。
结果· 孔隙参数影响力学与渗透性，合理设计可匹配松质骨。
验证· 压缩实验与SEM表征验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the mechanical and permeability properties of elliptical porous scaffolds for matching cancellous bone properties.
Method· Four groups of scaffolds with varying pore size, porosity, and distribution were designed, fabricated by SLM using Ti6Al4V, and evaluated via compression tests and CFD simulations.
Result· The pore parameters significantly influenced mechanical and permeability properties, and optimized designs can match the properties of human cancellous bone.
Validation· Mechanical compression testing and SEM morphology characterization.

单位: Department of Pediatric Orthopedic and Pediatric Orthopedic Oncology, Meyer Children Hospital, Viale Pieraccini 24, 50139 Florence, Italy.
通讯: Rodolfo Capanna @ Orthopedic Department, University of Pisa, Via Paradisa 2, 56124 Pisa, Italy. [推断]

Motivation· To evaluate the clinical outcomes of custom 3D-printed implants for skeletal reconstruction after oncologic resection in pediatric patients.

Validation· Clinical follow-up with functional scoring (MSTS) and complication assessment.

单位: Centre for Translational Bone, Joint and Soft Tissue Research, Faculty of Medicine and University Hospital Carl Gustav Carus, TU Dresden, Dresden, Germany
通讯: Rania Taymour @ Centre for Translational Bone, Joint and Soft Tissue Research, Faculty of Medicine and University Hospital Carl Gustav C
相关性: 该论文不涉及人工智能或机器学习在生物打印中的应用。
边界: This paper does not embed AI/ML into any bioprinting workflow stage; it is purely experimental bioprinting without computational modeling or automation.
动机· 构建肝细胞与成纤维细胞的3D共培养模型以模拟肝微环境。
方法· 采用核壳生物打印技术，用功能化生物墨水独立封装两种细胞。
结果· 成纤维细胞支持肝细胞功能，基质功能化增强细胞活性。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a 3D co-culture model that recapitulates hepatocyte-fibroblast interactions in a physiologically relevant microenvironment.
Method· Core-shell bioprinting with alginate-methylcellulose bioinks functionalized with natural matrix components was used to spatially define hepatocytes and fibroblasts in coaxial compartments.
Result· Fibroblasts stimulated hepatocyte-specific functions such as albumin expression, and matrix functionalization improved cell adhesion, viability, and proliferation.
Validation· In vitro culture and biomarker assays confirmed enhanced hepatocyte functionality and cell viability in the core-shell co-culture system.

On the Use of EBSD and Microhardness to Study the Microstructure Properties of Tungsten Samples Prepared by Selective Laser Melting

Mirza Atif Abbas, Anru Yan, Zhi Yong Wang Materials 2021-03-04 10.3390/ma14051215
单位: Laser College of Engineering, Beijing University of Technology, Beijing 100124, China
通讯: Mirza Atif Abbas @ Laser College of Engineering, Beijing University of Technology, Beijing 100124, China
相关性: 这篇论文研究钨材料的选区激光熔化微观结构，面向核聚变应用，与生物医用植入物或骨整合完全无关。
边界: No biomedical context, no porous structure for bone ingrowth, no stress shielding or stiffness matching, no fatigue testing, no surface roughness or osseointegration assessment.
动机· 研究SLM工艺参数对钨材料微观结构的影响
方法· 采用EBSD和显微硬度法表征不同参数下钨样品的微观结构

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of selective laser melting parameters on the microstructure and hardness of tungsten for plasma-facing components.
Method· Electron backscatter diffraction (EBSD) and Vickers microhardness testing were used to characterize tungsten samples fabricated under different SLM parameters.
Result· SLM parameters significantly influenced the Kikuchi pattern quality and hardness values of the tungsten samples.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed through microhardness measurements and EBSD analysis.
