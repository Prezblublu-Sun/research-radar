Digital volume correlation and micro-CT: An in-vitro technique for measuring full-field interface micromotion around polyethylene implants.

Medium Hip Implant & Femoral Stem pubmed Transferable Save for project mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Chamaiporn Sukjamsri, Diogo M Geraldes, Thomas Gregory, Farah Ahmed, David Hollis et al. Journal of biomechanics 2015-09-18 10.1016/j.jbiomech.2015.05.024
单位: Biomechanics Group, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Imperial College London, London SW7 2AZ, UK.
通讯: Ulrich Hansen @ Biomechanics Group, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Imperial College London, London SW7 2AZ, UK. Electronic addres
相关性: 该论文开发了一种用于测量植入物周围微动的DVC-μCT技术，但研究对象是肩关节盂部件，非髋关节股骨柄，方法可借鉴但非直接相关。
边界: This paper does not address femoral stem mechanics, does not quantify stress shielding in Gruen zones, and does not model contact mechanics of hip implants.
动机· 开发一种无损的全场微动测量方法
方法· 结合数字体积相关与显微CT，在简化实验和真实肩胛骨植入实验中验证
结果· 实现高精度（误差约1.4 μm）的全场微动测量，与有限元预测一致
验证· 通过已知位移实验和手动跟踪点验证，并与有限元比较
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a non-destructive full-field technique for measuring implant interface micromotion.
Method· Digital volume correlation combined with micro-CT (DVC-μCT) was validated on simplified experiments and a realistic glenoid implant in a porcine scapula.
Result· Achieved high accuracy (average error 1.4 μm) and correlated well with manual tracking and finite element predictions.
Validation· Validated using known micromotions and manual point tracking, with comparison to finite element analysis.

Evaluation of a Press Fit, Percutaneous, Skeletally Anchored Endoprosthesis for Prosthetic Limb Attachment: Bone Response and the Effect of Low Intensity Vibration

Medium Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Transferable Save for project animal Exp. validation
Garrett J. Noble OhioLink ETD Center (Ohio Library and Information Network) 2015-09-18 link
单位: the Ohio State University
通讯: Garrett J. Noble @ the Ohio State University
相关性: 该论文研究了压配式经皮骨锚定假体的骨整合和振动治疗，虽非髋关节植入物，但涉及骨-植入物界面力学和有限元建模，对髋关节假体应力遮挡和微动研究有参考价值。
边界: This paper focuses on a percutaneous endoprosthesis in an ovine model, not a femoral stem for hip arthroplasty; it does not quantify stress shielding in Gruen zones or micromotion with contact/friction modeling.
动机· 研究压配式经皮骨锚定假体的骨整合及低强度振动的影响
方法· 羊模型植入假体，分组进行振动治疗，结合有限元预测和实验验证
结果· 振动治疗增加18%骨整合，有限元模型预测的骨损伤与实验一致
验证· 通过组织形态学、推出测试和MicroCT验证骨整合和界面强度
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate initial bone response to a press-fit percutaneous endoprosthesis and the effect of low intensity vibration on osseointegration.
Method· An ovine model was used with press-fit endoprostheses; animals received either sham or low intensity vibration (0.3 g, 45 Hz) for 12 weeks; finite element modeling predicted bone damage from press-fit interference.
Result· Vibration increased osseointegration by 18% in the intracortical region; finite element predictions of bone damage matched experimental observations.
Validation· Validation included histomorphometry, mechanical push-out testing, microCT, and gait analysis.

Anthony R. McAndrew, Paul A. Colegrove, Adrian Addison, Bertrand C.D. Flipo, Michael Russell et al. Materials & Design 2015-09-18 10.1016/j.matdes.2015.09.080
单位: Cranfield University, Cranfield, Bedfordshire MK43 0AL, UK
通讯: Anthony R. McAndrew @ Cranfield University, Cranfield, Bedfordshire MK43 0AL, UK
相关性: 该论文研究线性摩擦焊接工艺，与AM生物医学材料方向无关。
边界: This paper does not involve LPBF fabrication, porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or osseointegration; it focuses on linear friction welding of Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 研究工件几何形状对钛合金线性摩擦焊接的影响。
方法· 使用DEFORM软件建立二维有限元模型并实验验证。
结果· 振荡方向宽度比截面积对焊接输出影响更大。
验证· 通过实验测量温度、微观结构等验证模型。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To understand how workpiece geometry affects thermal fields, material flow, and contaminant removal in linear friction welding of Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Two-dimensional finite element models were developed using DEFORM and validated with experimental data.
Result· The in-plane width in the oscillation direction significantly influences burn-off rate, interface temperature, and contaminant removal compared to cross-sectional area.
Validation· Experimental welds with Widmanstätten microstructure analysis and temperature measurements validated the computational models.
