边界: This paper does not incorporate any AI/ML methods into bioprinting processes, nor does it provide experimental validation or autonomous decision-making.

Validation· No experimental validation; the study is a pure bibliometric survey.

Surface Mechanoengineering of a Zr-Based Bulk Metallic Glass via Ar-Nanobubble Doping To Probe Cell Sensitivity to Rigid Materials.

Lu Huang, Mengkun Tian, Dong Wu, Gerd Duscher, Peter K Liaw et al. ACS applied materials & interfaces 2017-12-20 10.1021/acsami.7b12663
单位: Department of Materials Science and Engineering, University of Tennessee , Knoxville, Tennessee 37996, United States.
通讯: Wei He @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering, University of Tennessee , Knoxville, Tennessee 37996, United States. [推断]
相关性: 该论文涉及表面力学工程和细胞响应，但未涉及增材制造、多孔结构或应力屏蔽，与用户研究方向不相关。
边界: This paper does not involve additive manufacturing (LPBF/AM), does not address porous structures, stress shielding, or mechanical testing such as fatigue. It uses ion implantation to modify surface rigidity of BMG, not relevant to AM biomedical materials direction.
动机· 探究骨细胞对刚性基底机械敏感性的新型材料平台。
方法· 通过氩离子注入在锆基非晶合金中引入纳米气泡以调控表面刚度。
结果· 表面软化影响细胞粘附和生长，低刚度促进细胞增殖。
验证· 体外骨形成细胞实验验证表面力学效应。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a new materials platform for investigating bone cell mechanosensing on rigid substrates with tunable stiffness in the gigapascal range.
Method· Surface rigidity of a Zr-based bulk metallic glass was modulated via low-energy Ar-ion implantation inducing Ar nanobubbles, creating softened transition zones.
Result· Bone-forming cells showed reduced adhesion and actin filament formation on softer surfaces, with lower stiffness associated with higher cell growth rates.
Validation· In vitro cell studies on the ion-implanted BMG surfaces confirmed the influence of mechanical cues on cell behavior.
