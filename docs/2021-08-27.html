Physics-informed machine learning for reduced-order modeling of nonlinear problems

单位: Chair of Computational Mathematics and Simulation Science, École polytechnique fédérale de Lausanne, 1015 Lausanne, Switzerland
通讯: Qian Wang @ Chair of Computational Mathematics and Simulation Science, École polytechnique fédérale de Lausanne, 1015 Lausanne, Swit
相关性: 该论文提出的物理信息神经网络方法可显著加速参数化PDE的求解，有望迁移至植入物生物力学有限元代理建模。
边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, contact mechanics, or uncertainty quantification; it focuses on general nonlinear PDEs without biomedical context.
动机· 加速参数化非线性PDE的求解，降低计算成本。
方法· 提出物理增强神经网络，结合残差损失与数据损失训练降阶模型。
结果· PRNN比PINN和纯数据驱动网络更精确，在线评估速度比Galerkin降阶模型快数个数量级。
验证· 通过多个非线性PDE数值算例验证精度与效率。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To accelerate the solution of parametrized nonlinear PDEs by developing a reduced-order model that is both accurate and computationally efficient.
Method· A physics-reinforced neural network (PRNN) is trained by minimizing a weighted sum of residual loss from reduced-order equations and data loss from projected high-fidelity snapshots.
Result· The PRNN achieves higher accuracy than both PINN and purely data-driven networks, and its online evaluation is orders of magnitude faster than the Galerkin reduced-order model.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on several nonlinear PDE problems demonstrate the accuracy and speedup of the proposed method.
