Biomimetic Hydrogels from Mixed Gellan Gum and Tryptophan Zipper Self-Assembling Peptides.

Riddhesh Doshi, Dhushanthan Mohanathas, Md Shariful Islam, Juanfang Ruan, Richard D Tilley et al. ACS macro letters 2025-05-09 10.1021/acsmacrolett.5c00076
单位: School of Chemistry, Australian Centre for NanoMedicine, University of New South Wales (UNSW Sydney), Sydney, NSW 2052, Australia.
通讯: Kristopher A Kilian @ School of Chemistry, Australian Centre for NanoMedicine, University of New South Wales (UNSW Sydney), Sydney, NSW 2052, [推断]
相关性: 该论文仅涉及水凝胶材料合成，未涉及任何AI或生物打印过程，与用户研究方向无关。
边界: This paper does not incorporate AI into any bioprinting workflow stage, nor does it involve process optimization or autonomous decision-making.
动机· 开发稳定且具有生物活性的合成水凝胶仿生基质。
方法· 将色氨酸拉链自组装肽与结冷胶混合制备复合水凝胶。
结果· 复合水凝胶具有更快凝胶化、更高刚度、自愈合及细胞活性支持。
验证· 通过流变学测试和细胞培养实验验证力学性能和生物相容性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To create synthetic hydrogels that mimic the mechanical and bioactive properties of natural extracellular matrices for tissue engineering.
Method· A hybrid hydrogel system was formed by mixing a tryptophan zipper self-assembling peptide with the polysaccharide gellan gum.
Result· The hybrid hydrogels exhibited enhanced gelation kinetics, increased stiffness, viscoelasticity with shear-thinning and self-healing, and supported cell viability and aggregation.
Validation· Rheological measurements and in vitro cell culture assays confirmed the mechanical tunability and cytocompatibility of the hydrogels.
