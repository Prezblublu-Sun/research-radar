相关性: 该论文与用户的研究方向无关，属于医学教育领域。
边界: This paper is about flipped-classroom teaching in nutrition clinical training, with no FEA, surrogate modeling, uncertainty quantification, or biomechanics content.
动机· 探索翻转课堂在营养实习生临床培训中的应用效果。
方法· 设计三个临床主题，采用课前在线学习与课内互动讨论相结合的方式。
结果· 学员和教师对翻转课堂评价很高，认为能加深知识印象并促进主动学习。
验证· 通过学员自评和教师评价进行效果评估。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effectiveness of flipped-classroom model in nutrition intern clinical training.
Method· Three clinical topics were designed with e-learning outside class followed by interactive problem-based and case-based discussions.
Result· Both trainees and preceptors rated the flipped-classroom highly, noting improved knowledge retention and active learning.
Validation· Evaluation was based on self-assessment scores and preceptor feedback.
