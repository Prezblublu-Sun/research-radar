English summary & terms
Motivation· This study questions the common assumption that Bayesian neural networks are well-suited for out-of-distribution detection due to their epistemic uncertainty.

Result· Empirical evidence shows that proper Bayesian inference with common architectures does not guarantee good OOD detection because the induced kernels fail to reflect the underlying data generating process.
Validation· Validation is performed on synthetic and real datasets comparing GP kernel behavior and HMC-based BNN predictions.
