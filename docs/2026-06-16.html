A Convex Quasilinearization Method for Solving Nonlinear PDEs with Physics-Informed Neural Networks

Gbenga T. Awojinrin, Abdul-Akeem Olawoyin, Rami M. Younis arXiv preprint 2026-06-16 link
相关性: 该论文提出的凸拟线性化方法可高效求解非线性PDE，有望迁移至生物力学有限元代理模型，解决采样效率问题。
边界: The paper does not apply the method to implant/biomechanics design, lacks uncertainty quantification, and does not handle constrained optimization.
动机· 标准PINN非凸训练效率低，需替代方法。
方法· 利用Bellman-Kalaba拟线性化将非线性PDE转化为序列线性子问题，结合线性最小二乘求解。
结果· 在多个基准上单次迭代收敛，参数少两个数量级。
验证· 在Bratu、Burgers、Navier-Stokes等七个数值基准上验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Standard PINNs suffer from nonconvex gradient-based training inefficiency, motivating a convex alternative.
Method· Bellman-Kalaba quasilinearization reduces nonlinear PDEs to a sequence of linear subproblems, each solved via direct linear least-squares QR factorization on a linear-in-parameters trial space.
Result· The method converges in single-digit outer iterations across benchmarks, matching or exceeding published PINN solvers with up to two orders of magnitude fewer parameters.
Validation· Validated on seven numerical benchmarks including scalar nonlinear PDEs, coupled systems, and Darcy flow.
