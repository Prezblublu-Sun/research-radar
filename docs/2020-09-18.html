边界: No biomedical or implant design context; no FEA validation; no constraint handling or multi-objective optimization.
动机· 解决交叉熵方法在昂贵函数评估和局部最优问题上的不足。

Validation· Validation is performed on a parameterized test function with many local minima, comparing the proposed variants against the standard cross-entropy method.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the early dislocation rates of two different posterior capsular repair techniques in primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective cohort study of 841 THAs performed by a single surgeon, comparing capsular repair to the greater trochanter versus side-to-side direct soft-tissue repair.
Result· The side-to-side repair group had zero dislocations, significantly lower than the greater trochanter repair group.
Validation· Multivariable logistic regression was used to adjust for patient and operative factors.
