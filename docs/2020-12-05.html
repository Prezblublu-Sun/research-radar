The Hidden Uncertainty in a Neural Networks Activations

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Background only numerical_benchmark UQ
Janis Postels, Hermann Blum, Yannick Strümpler, Cesar Cadena, Roland Siegwart et al. arXiv preprint 2020-12-05 link
相关性: 该论文研究神经网络激活中的不确定性，但与FEA替代建模或生物力学设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, expensive simulation sampling, constraint handling, or implant/biomechanics design.
动机· 探究神经网络隐藏层分布是否反映认知不确定性。
方法· 利用隐藏表示的负对数似然和熵分别量化认知和偶然不确定性。
结果· 深层表示的不确定性与深度集成方法行为相似但计算成本更低。
验证· 在分类和回归基准数据集上验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· This work investigates whether the distribution of neural network latent representations correlates with epistemic uncertainty for generalization assessment.
Method· Epistemic uncertainty is quantified via the negative log-likelihood of hidden representations, while aleatoric uncertainty is measured by the entropy of the predictive distribution.
Result· Uncertainty from deeper layers behaves similarly to computationally expensive methods like deep ensembles, with improved OOD detection under regularization.
Validation· Empirical validation is performed on classification and regression benchmarks.

uncertainty quantification neural networks epistemic uncertainty aleatoric uncertainty OOD detection
