英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To report functional outcomes of displaced acetabular fractures treated nonoperatively in elderly patients who would otherwise meet operative criteria.
Method· Retrospective case series of 27 patients aged ≥60 years with displaced acetabular fractures treated nonoperatively; outcomes measured by WOMAC and SF-8 scores.
Result· Patients had surprisingly good functional scores (mean WOMAC 12.9), with 24% 1-year mortality and 15% conversion to surgery.
Validation· Clinical outcome scores without biomechanical testing or modeling.

Motivation· To evaluate the accuracy of a new 3D mini-optical navigation tool for total hip arthroplasty in a cadaver model.
Method· Cadaveric total hip arthroplasty was performed using the navigation tool, and accuracy was assessed by comparing planned versus achieved implant positions.
Result· The navigation tool improved the accuracy of implant positioning.
Validation· Validation was performed through cadaveric experiments measuring positional accuracy.
