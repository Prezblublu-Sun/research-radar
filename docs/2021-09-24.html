Result· Heat treatment reduced internal defects and transformed the acicular α'-martensite into a lamellar (α+β) structure, decreasing strength but improving elongation, with an optimal combination at 900 °C.
Validation· Validation was performed through microhardness measurements, compressive testing, and microstructural observation.
