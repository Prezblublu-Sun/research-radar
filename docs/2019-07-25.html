Mechanical properties of lightweight 316L stainless steel lattice structures fabricated by selective laser melting

单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, University of Science and Technology Beijing, 100083, PR China
通讯: Ketai He @ Institute of Artificial Intelligence, University of Science and Technology Beijing, 100083, PR China
相关性: 该论文研究了316L不锈钢点阵结构的力学性能，但未涉及生物医学植入物或应力屏蔽问题。
边界: No link to stress shielding or stiffness matching for biomedical implants; no fatigue testing; no osseointegration or surface roughness analysis.
动机· 探索SLM工艺参数对316L不锈钢点阵结构力学性能的影响。
方法· 设计四面体单元点阵结构，通过SLM制备并进行压缩测试和有限元分析。
结果· 四面体结构具有良好的压缩性能和能量吸收能力，屈服强度与孔隙率相关。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of laser energy density on the mechanical properties of 316L stainless steel lattice structures fabricated by SLM.
Method· Designed a tetrakaidecahedron unit cell lattice, fabricated by SLM at various energy densities, and characterized via compression testing and nonlinear finite element analysis.
Result· The tetrakaidecahedron structure exhibited superior compressive strength and energy absorption, with yield strength correlating with porosity.
Validation· Experimental compression tests and SEM/confocal microscopy validated the mechanical response and strut morphology.

边界: This paper does not cover porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or osseointegration; it focuses on hydrogen embrittlement of a non-biomedical alloy.
动机· 研究SLM制造18Ni300马氏体时效钢的氢脆敏感性。
方法· 电化学预充氢后进行拉伸试验，分析氢浓度和断口形貌。
