Motivation· To identify genetic and pharmacologic risk factors for thrombosis after major joint arthroplasty.
Method· Preoperative and postoperative platelet counts, anti-PF4/heparin antibodies, and coagulation gene mutations were assessed in 212 patients undergoing hip or knee arthroplasty.
Result· Factor V Leiden mutation and anti-PF4/heparin antibodies were significantly associated with post-arthroplasty thrombosis.
Validation· Clinical follow-up over a minimum of 3 years with monitoring for thrombocytopenia, HIT, and thrombosis.
