英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine whether percent body fat is a better predictor than BMI for clinical risks and functional outcomes after total joint arthroplasty.
Method· Prospective data from 215 patients undergoing primary TKA or THA were analyzed using multivariable regression to assess the predictive value of percent body fat versus BMI.
Result· Higher percent body fat predicted complications, UCLA activity score, and pain scores, while BMI did not show significant predictive ability for these outcomes.
Validation· Clinical prospective cohort study with statistical validation.
