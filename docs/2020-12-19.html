边界: No implant or biomechanics application; no contact mechanics or stress analysis; validation on synthetic functions and PDE inversion, not on FEA surrogate tasks.
动机· 利用多保真数据高效训练贝叶斯神经网络，实现函数逼近和PDE反问题求解。

Motivation· To develop a Bayesian neural network framework that leverages multi-fidelity data for efficient function approximation and PDE inverse problem solving.
