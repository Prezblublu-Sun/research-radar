Self-learning Emulators and Eigenvector Continuation

Avik Sarkar, Dean Lee arXiv preprint 2021-07-28 10.1103/PhysRevResearch.4.023214
相关性: 该论文提出自学习仿真器方法，直接针对昂贵仿真的采样效率问题，具有主动学习和误差估计，可迁移至生物力学代理模型。
边界: This paper does not apply the method to biomechanics or implant FEA, and does not handle multi-objective optimization or explicit design constraints.
动机· 提高计算仿真效率，利用自学习仿真器加速求解。
方法· 提出自学习仿真器主动学习协议，结合误差估计和机器学习校正。
结果· 在三个数值例子中展示了准确性改善，包括参数化微分方程和本征值问题。
验证· 数值基准测试（样条插值、简化基方法、本征向量延拓）。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To accelerate computationally expensive scientific calculations by developing self-learning emulators that actively reduce simulation cost.
Method· An active learning protocol that progressively improves emulator accuracy using a fast error estimate corrected by machine learning.
Result· Demonstrated on three examples—transcendental equation, parameterized ODE, and eigenvector continuation—showing improved accuracy and efficiency.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks using cubic spline interpolation, reduced basis method, and eigenvector continuation.

Result· The proposed active learning strategy improves regression performance by focusing on high-error regions.
