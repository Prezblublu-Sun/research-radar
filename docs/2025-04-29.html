Full-field surrogate modeling of cardiac function encoding geometric variability

High FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project simulation_case UQ Patient-specific
Elena Martinez, Beatrice Moscoloni, Matteo Salvador, Fanwei Kong, Mathias Peirlinck et al. arXiv preprint 2025-04-29 link
相关性: 该论文提出了一种结合几何变异的全场代理模型方法，可迁移至植入物生物力学设计中的高效仿真与不确定性量化。
边界: This paper focuses on cardiac electrophysiology surrogate modeling, not on implant biomechanics, stress shielding, or contact mechanics.
动机· 解决心脏功能代理模型对几何形状的依赖，实现跨患者泛化。
方法· 采用分支潜神经映射结合大变形微分同胚度量映射，构建包含几何变异的全场代理模型。
结果· 在13例法洛四联症患者数据上实现平均无量纲均方误差0.0034。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To overcome the geometry-specific limitation of cardiac surrogate models and enable generalization across patients with varying anatomies.
Method· A pipeline combining Branched Latent Neural Maps with large deformation diffeomorphic metric mapping and z-score sampling from statistical shape models to encode geometric variability into full-field surrogates.
Result· The surrogate model achieved an average adimensional mean squared error of 0.0034 on a challenging cohort of 13 pediatric Tetralogy of Fallot patients.
Validation· Validation was performed on numerical simulations of electrophysiology using a synthetic cohort of 52 geometries and the original patient cohort.
