单位: The Christie NHS Foundation Trust
通讯: Azad Dehghan @ The Christie NHS Foundation Trust
相关性: 该论文涉及临床文本去标识化，与有限元代理建模或生物力学设计无关。
边界: This paper focuses on NLP for de-identification of clinical text, with no FEA, surrogate modeling, uncertainty quantification, or implant/biomechanics design.
动机· 自动去除临床文本中的个人身份信息以支持研究。
方法· 结合知识驱动和数据驱动的自然语言处理方法，采用两遍识别策略。
结果· 在临床信函和笔记上分别达到97%和93%的F1值。
验证· 使用60/40数据划分和人工标注基准验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To enable large-scale health research by automatically removing personal identifiable information from clinical free-text while complying with legal and ethical obligations.
Method· A hybrid NLP method combining lexical dictionaries, rules, and linear-chain conditional random fields, with a novel two-pass recognition approach using patient-level dictionaries.
Result· The method achieves token-level F1 scores of 97% on clinical correspondence and 93% on clinical notes, matching human benchmarks.
Validation· Validated on 1700 documents from a UK hospital using a 60/40 train-test split and inter-annotator agreement comparison.

动机· 探究全髋关节置换术后患者满意度是否受性别影响。
方法· 对94名术后至少6个月的患者进行临床状态和满意度问卷调查。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate whether gender influences patient satisfaction after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Clinical status and satisfaction questionnaires were administered to 94 primary THA patients at least 6 months post-surgery.
Result· No significant difference in satisfaction between genders; pain relief was the most satisfactory factor while activity/work level was the least.
Validation· Clinical survey with statistical analysis.
