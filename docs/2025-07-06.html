SimLauncher: Launching Sample-Efficient Real-world Robotic Reinforcement Learning via Simulation Pre-training

Mingdong Wu, Lehong Wu, Yizhuo Wu, Weiyao Huang, Hongwei Fan et al. arXiv preprint 2025-07-06 link
相关性: 该论文专注于机器人强化学习的仿真预训练，与生物打印领域无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not involve bioprinting, biomaterials, or any biomedical context; it is purely about robotic reinforcement learning for dexterous manipulation.
动机· 提高真实世界机器人强化学习的样本效率
方法· 利用仿真预训练策略和价值引导实现高效探索
结果· 在多种灵巧操作任务中实现接近完美的成功率
验证· 在真实机器人上进行多阶段、接触丰富的操作实验
English summary & terms
Motivation· Real-world robotic RL suffers from low sample efficiency and high human intervention, motivating a framework that leverages simulation pre-training.
Method· SimLauncher pre-trains a visuomotor policy in a digital twin and uses it to bootstrap target values and guide exploration during real-world fine-tuning.
Result· SimLauncher achieves near-perfect success rates across multi-stage contact-rich manipulation tasks with significantly improved sample efficiency.
Validation· Real-world robot experiments on dexterous hand manipulation tasks validate the approach, comparing against prior real-world RL methods.

Pranta Saha, Joyce Reimer, Brook Byrns, Connor Burbridge, Neeraj Dhar et al. arXiv preprint 2025-07-06 link
相关性: 该论文专注于生物通路重建和语言模型，与有限元代理建模、植入物设计或生物打印完全无关。
边界: This paper addresses biological pathway reconstruction using small language models, with no connection to computational mechanics, surrogate modelling, uncertainty quantification, or biomedical implant design.
动机· 大型语言模型在生物医学领域容易产生幻觉，需要更精确的小模型。
方法· 使用小型BERT模型结合主动学习，选择性增量训练以预测分子调控关系。

Method· A small BERT-based language model (~110M parameters) is fine-tuned via selective incremental learning using information entropy to choose the most informative training examples.
Result· The model achieves over 80% accuracy in predicting molecular interactions using less than 25% of the training data, with learning driven by high-certainty incorrect examples.
Validation· Validation is performed against known regulatory interactions from manually curated pathway databases.
