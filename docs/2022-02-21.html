Effects of remelting on the surface morphology, microstructure and mechanical properties of AlSi10Mg alloy fabricated by selective laser melting

Zezhou Kuai, Zhonghua Li, Bin Liu, Wenpeng Liu, Shuai Yang Materials Chemistry and Physics 2022-02-21 10.1016/j.matchemphys.2022.125901
单位: School of Materials Science and Engineering, North University of China, Taiyuan, 030051, China
通讯: Zhonghua Li @ School of Mechanical Engineering, North University of China, Taiyuan, 030051, China  |  Bin Liu @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, North University of China, Taiyuan, 030051, China
相关性: 该论文研究了非生物医用铝合金的SLM工艺，未涉及生物医学植入物的应力屏蔽或多孔结构设计。
边界: This paper does not involve biomedical implant design, stress shielding, osseointegration, or fatigue testing for load-bearing implants; it focuses on AlSi10Mg alloy, which is not a typical biomedical material, and does not evaluate porosity or surface roughness for bone integration.
动机· 研究重熔对SLM制备AlSi10Mg合金表面形貌、微观组织和力学性能的影响。
方法· 通过选择性激光熔化制备AlSi10Mg试样并进行重熔处理，分析其表面形貌、显微组织和力学性能。
结果· 重熔改善了表面形貌，细化了微观组织，并提高了力学性能。
验证· 采用扫描电镜和力学测试进行表征验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of remelting on the surface morphology, microstructure, and mechanical properties of AlSi10Mg alloy fabricated by selective laser melting.
Method· AlSi10Mg samples were fabricated by selective laser melting and subjected to remelting, followed by characterization of surface morphology, microstructure, and mechanical testing.
Result· Remelting improved surface quality, refined the microstructure, and enhanced mechanical properties.
Validation· Validation was performed through SEM observation and mechanical testing (e.g., tensile or hardness tests).

Impact of Remelting in the Microstructure and Corrosion Properties of the Ti6Al4V Fabricated by Selective Laser Melting

Javier Bedmar, Jorge de la Pezuela, Ainhoa Riquelme, B. Torres, J. Rams Coatings 2022-02-21 10.3390/coatings12020284
单位: Department of Applied Mathematics, Materials Science and Engineering and Electronics Technology, Escuela Superior de Ciencias Experimentales y Tecnología (ESCET), Universidad Rey Juan Carlos, 28933 Mo
通讯: J. Rams @ Department of Applied Mathematics, Materials Science and Engineering and Electronics Technology, Escuela Superior de Cie
相关性: 该论文研究了重熔对SLM制备Ti6Al4V微观结构和腐蚀性能的影响，但未涉及生物医学植入物所需的应力遮挡、疲劳或骨整合性能。
边界: Does not address porous structure, stress shielding, fatigue performance, surface roughness, or osseointegration, which are core to biomedical implant evaluation.
动机· 研究重熔对SLM制备Ti6Al4V合金微观结构和腐蚀性能的影响。
方法· 采用CO2激光SLM制备样品，进行重熔处理，分析微观结构、缺陷和硬度，并进行腐蚀测试。
结果· 重熔导致α和β相增加，缺陷减少，硬度降低，腐蚀性能提高。
验证· 通过显微组织观察、硬度测试和电化学腐蚀测试验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of laser remelting strategy on microstructure, defects, and corrosion properties of Ti6Al4V fabricated by selective laser melting.
Method· Ti6Al4V samples were manufactured using a CO2 laser SLM system with remelting after each layer, and characterized by microstructure analysis, defect evaluation, hardness measurement, and corrosion tests.
Result· Remelting increased α and β phases from dissolution of metastable α', reduced defects and hardness, and improved corrosion resistance as indicated by nobler OCP and higher Rp.
Validation· Microstructural observation, hardness testing, and electrochemical corrosion testing.

Corrosion and Microstructural Investigation on Additively Manufactured 316L Stainless Steel: Experimental and Statistical Approach

Héctor Maicas-Esteve, Iman Taji, Marc Wilms, Yaiza González‐García, Roy Johnsen Materials 2022-02-21 10.3390/ma15041605
单位: Department of Mechanical and Industrial Engineering (MTP), Faculty of Engineering (IV), Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU), Richard Birkelandsvei 2B, 7034 Trondheim, Norway
通讯: Héctor Maicas-Esteve @ Department of Mechanical and Industrial Engineering (MTP), Faculty of Engineering (IV), Norwegian University of Science
相关性: 本研究关注316L不锈钢的SLM工艺参数对孔隙率和腐蚀性能的影响，未涉及生物医学植入物相关的应力遮挡或疲劳问题。
边界: This paper does not address implant stress shielding, fatigue testing, osseointegration, or biomedical context; it focuses on process optimization and corrosion of 316L steel for general industrial use.
动机· 优化SLM工艺参数以减少316L不锈钢的孔隙率并提高耐腐蚀性。
方法· 采用Taguchi实验设计和ANOVA分析能量密度及位置对性能的影响。
结果· 扫描速度对孔隙率影响最显著，最优能量密度为99.38 J/mm³。
验证· 通过孔隙率、硬度、表面粗糙度和循环极化测试验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To optimize selective laser melting parameters for AISI 316L stainless steel to minimize porosity and enhance corrosion resistance.
Method· Taguchi design and ANOVA were used to evaluate the effects of energy density and build position on microstructural and corrosion properties.
Result· Scanning speed was the most influential factor (74.9% contribution), and the optimal energy density was 99.38 J/mm³ for low porosity.
Validation· Experimental characterization including porosity measurement, hardness, surface roughness, and cyclic potentiodynamic polarization tests.
