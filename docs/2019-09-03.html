单位: UNIVERSITY OF SILESIA, INSTITUTE OF MATERIALS SCIENCE,
通讯: Małgorzata Karolus @ UNIVERSITY OF SILESIA, INSTITUTE OF MATERIALS SCIENCE,
相关性: 该论文研究AlSi10Mg的选区激光熔化残余应力，未涉及生物医用植入物设计或力学性能评估。
边界: This paper does not address biomedical implant materials (e.g., Ti alloys), stress shielding, fatigue testing, or osseointegration; it focuses on residual stress characterization of a non-biomedical alloy (AlSi10Mg).
动机· 分析选区激光熔化AlSi10Mg材料的残余应力分布
方法· 采用sin2ψ法对平行和垂直方向试样及多孔结构进行XRD残余应力测量
结果· 所有试样呈压应力状态，应力范围-5至-15 MPa，晶粒尺寸随结构变化

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To characterize residual stresses in AlSi10Mg fabricated by selective laser melting for different build orientations and lattice structures.
Method· Residual stresses were measured using the classical sin2ψ X-ray diffraction method on solid samples built parallel and perpendicular to the tested surface and on a cellular lattice built perpendicularly.
Result· All samples exhibited compressive residual stresses ranging from -5 to -15 MPa, with crystallite sizes varying by structure (e.g., 800 Å for solid vs. 107 Å for lattice in Mg2Si).
Validation· Experimental validation was performed via non-destructive XRD residual stress measurements on multiple sample types.

Result· Problems in the first two categories achieve linear convergence to the global minimizer set, while non-contractible problems pose fundamental difficulties.
Validation· Theoretical proofs and probabilistic bounds on convergence rate and complexity.
