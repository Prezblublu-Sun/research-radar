Design of a commercial aircraft environment control system using Bayesian optimization techniques

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project simulation_case UQ
Paul Feliot, Yves Le Guennec, Julien Bect, Emmanuel Vazquez arXiv preprint 2016-10-07 link
相关性: 该论文展示了贝叶斯优化在昂贵仿真设计中的应用方法，对生物力学植入物设计中的代理建模具有可迁移性。
边界: No biomedical or implant design context, lacks experimental validation for biomechanics applications.
动机· 减少昂贵仿真的优化迭代次数。
方法· 采用贝叶斯多目标优化结合高斯过程代理模型。
结果· 在重量和熵两个冲突目标中找到帕累托折衷设计。
验证· 通过环境控制系统仿真案例验证优化算法有效性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce the number of expensive simulations required in multi-objective design optimization of an aircraft environment control system.
Method· A Bayesian multi-objective optimization algorithm using Gaussian process surrogates handles nonlinear constraints and non-hypercubic design domains.
Result· A set of Pareto-optimal designs minimizing weight and entropy generation was identified, demonstrating trade-offs between conflicting objectives.
Validation· The algorithm was validated on a simulation case of an environmental control system with hidden constraints due to simulation failures.

通讯: Daniel J. Kelly @ Advanced Materials and Bioengineering Research Centre (AMBER), Royal College of Surgeons in Ireland andTrinity College D

A randomised, controlled, double blind, non‐inferiority trial of ultrasound‐guided fascia iliaca block vs. spinal morphine for analgesia after primary hip arthroplasty

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore clinical Exp. validation
Rachel Kearns, Alan Macfarlane, Andrew Grant, Kathryn Puxty, Patricia Harrison et al. Anaesthesia 2016-10-07 10.1111/anae.13620
单位: Glasgow Royal Infirmary Campus School of Medicine College of Medicine, Veterinary and Life Sciences University of Glasgow Glasgow UK
通讯: Rachel Kearns @ Glasgow Royal Infirmary Campus School of Medicine College of Medicine, Veterinary and Life Sciences University of Glasgo
相关性: 该论文研究全髋关节置换术后镇痛方案，与髋关节植入物应力遮挡、微动及有限元分析的研究方向无关。
边界: This paper is a clinical trial on analgesic methods after hip arthroplasty; it contains no biomechanical modeling, no finite element analysis, no implant stress shielding or micromotion quantification, and no contact mechanics.
动机· 比较超声引导下髂筋膜阻滞与蛛网膜下腔吗啡在全髋关节置换术后镇痛的效果。
方法· 单中心双盲随机非劣效性试验，108例患者分别接受髂筋膜阻滞或蛛网膜下腔吗啡。
结果· 髂筋膜阻滞组术后24小时静脉吗啡消耗量显著高于蛛网膜下腔吗啡组（中位差25 mg）。
验证· 随机对照临床试验，非劣效性设计。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the analgesic efficacy of ultrasound-guided fascia iliaca block versus spinal morphine after primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A single-center, double-blind, randomized, non-inferiority trial allocated 108 patients to receive either fascia iliaca block or spinal morphine plus sham block.
Result· The fascia iliaca block group required a median of 25 mg more intravenous morphine in the first 24 hours than the spinal morphine group (p < 0.001).
Validation· Clinical trial with predefined non-inferiority margin and intention-to-treat analysis.
hip arthroplasty · 全髋关节置换术fascia iliaca block · 髂筋膜阻滞spinal morphine · 蛛网膜下腔吗啡analgesia · 镇痛randomised controlled trial · 随机对照试验non-inferiority · 非劣效性
