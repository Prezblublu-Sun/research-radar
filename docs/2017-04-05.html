英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the relationship between acetabular width and subluxation percentage in developmental dysplasia of the hip (DDH).
Method· A retrospective review of preoperative radiographs and CT scans from 207 female patients with unilateral DDH, measuring acetabular width on the true acetabular plane.
Result· Acetabular width decreased from Crowe groups I to IVb, with subluxation percentage and femoral neck-shaft angle identified as independent factors associated with the acetabular width ratio.
Validation· Statistical analysis of clinical imaging data from a patient cohort.

Motivation· To determine the prevalence of MRI-documented adverse local tissue reactions in asymptomatic patients with ceramic-on-polyethylene total hip replacements.
Method· Forty-four asymptomatic patients (50 hips) with CoP THA and Harris hip score >90 underwent metal artifact reduction sequence MRI.
Result· Fluid collections were found in 18% of asymptomatic hips, with some showing synovial thickening.
Validation· Clinical imaging study without biomechanical or experimental validation.
