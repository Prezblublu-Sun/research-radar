边界: This paper focuses on clinical outcomes and healthcare utilization after hip/knee arthroplasty, with no biomechanical modeling, no FEA, no contact mechanics, and no implant stress/strain analysis.
动机· 探讨当日出院是否增加医疗系统负担。
方法· 对261例髋膝关节置换患者进行前瞻性数据收集。
结果· 当日出院和非当日出院患者术后接触医疗系统无显著差异。
验证· 临床随访数据统计分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate whether discharge on the day of surgery after THA/TKA increases healthcare system burden.
Method· Prospective data collection on 261 consecutive outpatient THA/TKA patients.
Result· No significant differences in postoperative contacts between discharge-on-day-of-surgery and non-discharge groups.
Validation· Statistical analysis of clinical follow-up data.
