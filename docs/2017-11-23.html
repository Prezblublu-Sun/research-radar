单位: Computational Science and Engineering Laboratory, Clausiusstrasse 33, ETH Zürich, CH-8092, Switzerland
通讯: Lina Kulakova @ Computational Science and Engineering Laboratory, Clausiusstrasse 33, ETH Zürich, CH-8092, Switzerland
相关性: 该论文使用贝叶斯不确定性量化推断分子动力学势能参数，与有限元代理建模或生物力学设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on molecular dynamics potential calibration, not on surrogate modeling for expensive finite element simulations, and does not address implant or biomechanics design constraints.
动机· 经典LJ势能参数固定，限制了分子动力学模拟的适用范围。
方法· 采用分层贝叶斯不确定性量化方法推断LJ势能排斥指数。
结果· 发现排斥指数约6.5时对液态氩拟合最佳，而气相需要约12.7。
验证· 使用径向分布函数实验数据和量子力学模拟数据进行验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· The fixed repulsive exponent in the classical Lennard-Jones potential limits the accuracy and applicability of molecular dynamics simulations across different thermodynamic conditions.
Method· A hierarchical Bayesian uncertainty quantification framework is employed to infer the repulsive exponent of the Lennard-Jones potential from experimental and quantum simulation data.
Result· The inferred repulsive exponent is approximately 6.5 for liquid argon and saturated vapor, while a value around 12.7 is preferred for argon gas, demonstrating improved fit over the classical 6-12 potential.
Validation· Validation is performed using experimental radial distribution function data and dimer interaction energies from quantum mechanics simulations.
