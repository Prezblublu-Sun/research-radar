A novel GelMA-OrnMA electrically conductive bioink for developing engineered neural tissues.

Mahmoud A Sakr, Kartikeya Dixit, Kinam Hyun, Sumi Siddiqua, Su Ryon Shin et al. Biomaterials advances 2026-07-01 10.1016/j.bioadv.2026.214787
单位: School of Engineering, University of British Columbia, Kelowna, BC, V1V 1V7, Canada.
通讯: Hitendra Kumar @ Department of Biosciences and Biomedical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Indore, Indore, Madhya Pradesh, 453  |  Keekyoung Kim @ Department of Mechanical and Manufacturing Engineering, University of Calgary, Calgary, Alberta, T2N 1N4, Canada; Depart
相关性: 该论文主要关注导电生物墨水的合成与表征，未涉及任何人工智能方法在生物打印中的应用。
边界: This paper focuses solely on material development and in vitro characterization of a conductive bioink with no integration of AI/ML into any bioprinting workflow stage.
动机· 开发兼具导电性、透明性和生物相容性的神经组织工程生物墨水。
方法· 通过共价引入鸟氨酸甲基丙烯酰胺（OrnMA）到明胶甲基丙烯酰（GelMA）中制备复合水凝胶。
结果· GelMA-OrnMA水凝胶具有与神经组织匹配的刚度、导电性，并促进星形胶质细胞增殖与形态变化。
验证· 通过体外细胞培养和力学/电学测试验证性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop an electrically conductive bioink that combines optical transparency, biocompatibility, and mechanical stiffness suitable for neural tissue engineering.
Method· Zwitterionic ornithine methacryloyl (OrnMA) was covalently incorporated into gelatin methacryloyl (GelMA) to form a composite hydrogel matrix.
Result· The GelMA-OrnMA hydrogel exhibited stiffness matching native neural tissues, electrical conductivity in the physiological range, and supported human astrocyte proliferation and morphological changes.
Validation· Validation included mechanical testing, electrical conductivity measurements, and 3D cell culture assays with human astrocytes.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance osseointegration of titanium implants via a bioactive coating incorporating porcine-derived amelogenin P148.
Method· A layer-by-layer coating of chitosan/hyaluronic acid with embedded P148 was assembled on polydopamine-modified titanium surfaces.
Result· The coating improved cell attachment, proliferation, osteogenic gene expression, and peri-implant bone formation in rats.
Validation· In vitro cell assays and in vivo rat implantation model.
