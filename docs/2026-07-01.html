Redefining microrough titanium surfaces: Thermo-engineered soft-edge microrough surfaces resolve the proliferation-differentiation trade-off kinetics in osteoblasts.

边界: This paper does not involve LPBF fabrication, porous structure design for stress shielding, or fatigue testing; it focuses on surface topography modification via acid etching and thermal contouring.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To resolve the long-standing trade-off between osteoblast proliferation and differentiation on microrough titanium surfaces by targeting microtopographic peak sharpness.
Method· Thermal contouring was applied to acid-etched titanium disks to selectively round sharp micropeaks while preserving roughness amplitude and spatial patterning.
Result· Soft-edge microrough surfaces restored osteoblast proliferation to levels comparable with smooth titanium while maintaining differentiation, and increased in vivo osseointegration energy by 2.5-fold.
Validation· In vivo push-in biomechanical testing in rat femurs combined with comprehensive surface characterization and in vitro cell assays.
