相关性: 该论文仅涉及手术入路角度，未涉及任何生物力学建模或应力分析。
边界: This paper does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or any biomechanical metrics; it is purely a surgical technique study on exposure angles.
动机· 确定直接前入路全髋置换术中股骨近端显露的最佳外旋和外展角度。
方法· 在尸体标本上测量不同外旋和外展角度下的股骨近端显露面积。
结果· 特定角度组合可最大化股骨近端显露，便于手术操作。
验证· 尸体标本实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine optimal hip external rotation and extension angles for proximal femur exposure during direct anterior total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Cadaveric specimens were positioned at varying angles, and the exposed area of the proximal femur was measured.
Result· Specific combinations of external rotation and extension maximized proximal femur exposure.
Validation· Cadaveric experimental validation.
