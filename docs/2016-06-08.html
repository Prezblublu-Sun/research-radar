Hyper-Reduced-Order Models for Subsurface Flow Simulation

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project simulation_case
Seonkyoo Yoon, Zeid M. Alghareeb, John R. Williams SPE Journal 2016-06-08 10.2118/181740-pa
单位: Massachusetts Institute of Technology
通讯: Seonkyoo Yoon @ Massachusetts Institute of Technology
相关性: 该论文提出的超降阶模型方法可显著加速地下流体模拟，其降阶策略（POD、Petrov-Galerkin投影、GNAT）对生物力学有限元代理建模具有参考价值。
边界: This paper focuses on subsurface flow simulation, not on biomedical implant mechanics or FEA surrogate modeling for structural/contact problems.
动机· 降低地下流体模拟的计算成本，加速生产优化与不确定性量化。
方法· 结合POD状态降阶、Petrov-Galerkin约束降阶与GNAT非线性处理，构建超降阶模型。
结果· 实现超过300倍的运行加速，且精度保持良好。
验证· 通过三维油水两相流模拟案例验证加速效果与精度。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce the computational burden of subsurface flow simulation for applications like production optimization and uncertainty quantification.
Method· A hyper-reduced-order modeling procedure combining POD for state reduction, Petrov-Galerkin projection for constraint reduction, and GNAT for nonlinearity treatment.
Result· Achieved a runtime speedup of over 300 compared to the full-order method while maintaining accuracy.
Validation· Validated on a 3D oil/water two-phase subsurface flow simulation case.
