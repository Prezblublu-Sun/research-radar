Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Zhiqiang Liu, Feifei Ji, Mingqiang Wang, Tianyu Zhu Metals 2017-01-20 10.3390/met7010028
单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, Jiangsu University of Science and Technology, Zhenjiang 212003, China
通讯: Zhiqiang Liu @ School of Mechanical Engineering, Jiangsu University of Science and Technology, Zhenjiang 212003, China
相关性: 该论文研究多孔钛合金与硬质合金刀具的摩擦学性能，未涉及生物医学植入物、应力遮挡或骨整合。
边界: This paper focuses on tribological properties in metal cutting, not on biomedical implant applications, stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration.
动机· 研究多孔钛合金加工中刀具与工件的摩擦磨损机制
方法· 微切削条件下测量切削力、速度、温度对摩擦系数和磨损率的影响
结果· 孔隙率是影响摩擦系数和磨损率的主要因素，最佳加工参数为载荷8N、速度400r/min、温度300°C
验证· 通过实验测量摩擦系数和磨损率，分析磨损形貌
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the tribological behavior of porous titanium alloy during micro-cutting with tungsten carbide tools.
Method· Experimental measurement of friction coefficient and wear rate under varying cutting force, speed, and temperature conditions.
Result· Porosity dominates the friction and wear response; optimal cutting parameters are 8 N load, 400 r/min speed, and 300 °C temperature.
Validation· Experimental tribological tests with wear morphology analysis.

Uncertainty Quantification for Fat-Tailed Probability Distributions in Aircraft Engine Simulations

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only simulation_case UQ
Richard Ahlfeld, Francesco Montomoli, Enrico Scalas, Shahrokh Shahpar Journal of Propulsion and Power 2017-01-20 10.2514/1.b36278
单位: Imperial College London, London, England SW7 2AZ, United Kingdom
通讯: Richard Ahlfeld @ Imperial College London, London, England SW7 2AZ, United Kingdom
相关性: 该论文专注于航空发动机仿真中的稀有事件不确定性量化，与生物力学或植入物设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, FEA surrogate modeling for biomedical applications, or constraint handling in design optimization.
动机· 航空发动机仿真中稀有事件概率低但后果严重，需高效不确定性量化方法。
方法· 提出稀疏近似矩基任意多项式混沌方法，利用截断厚尾分布的统计矩构建多项式基。
结果· 在涡轮腔瞬态温度和轮间热气体吸入仿真中，以更低计算成本获得与先前一致的结果。
验证· 通过两个工业航空发动机仿真案例验证方法有效性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Rare events in aircraft engine simulations have low probability but severe consequences, requiring efficient uncertainty quantification methods.
Method· A sparse approximation of moment-based arbitrary polynomial chaos is proposed, constructing polynomial bases from statistical moments of truncated fat-tailed distributions.
Result· The method achieves comparable accuracy to previous approaches in two industrial engine simulations with reduced computational cost.
Validation· Validation is performed on two simulation cases: transient cycle temperature in a turbine cavity and hot-gas ingestion in the interwheel region.

uncertainty quantification polynomial chaos rare event simulation aircraft engine fat-tailed distributions

通讯: Julio C. Facelli @ Department of Biomedical Informatics, University of Utah, Salt Lake City, UT, USA
相关性: 该论文研究乳腺癌风险预测中的不确定性量化，与用户关注的有限元代理模型和植入物设计方向无关。
边界: This paper focuses on clinical risk prediction for breast cancer using family health history, with no connection to finite element analysis, surrogate modeling, or biomechanical implant design.
动机· 临床中家族史信息不准确，影响乳腺癌风险预测。
方法· 用蒙特卡洛模拟量化自报家族史的不确定性对风险分类的影响。
结果· 模型正确分类率52%-64%，但误分类不可忽略。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To assess how uncertainty in self-reported family health history affects breast cancer risk classification in clinical settings.
Method· Monte Carlo simulations were used to propagate uncertainty from self-reported family health history into multiple risk prediction models.
Result· Correct tier-level classification rates ranged from 52% to 64% under uncertain conditions, with non-negligible misclassification.
Validation· Validation was performed via simulation using published uncertainty measurements of self-reported family health history.
