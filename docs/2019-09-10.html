英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the clinical outcomes of total hip arthroplasty as a salvage procedure for failed fixation of intertrochanteric fractures.
Method· A prospective study of 18 patients undergoing total hip arthroplasty, assessed clinically by Harris hip score and radiologically by X-rays.
Result· Mean Harris hip score improved from 35.71 to 88.37 at one year, with no dislocations and full weight-bearing in all patients.
Validation· Clinical follow-up over a mean of three years with no patients lost to follow-up.
