单位: Institute of Armament Technology, Military University of Technology, Kaliskiego 2, 00-908 Warsaw, Poland
通讯: Judyta Sienkiewicz @ Institute of Armament Technology, Military University of Technology, Kaliskiego 2, 00-908 Warsaw, Poland
相关性: 该论文研究了梯度点阵结构的力学性能，与用户关注的植入物应力屏蔽和刚度匹配间接相关。
边界: No biomedical context, no fatigue testing, no surface roughness or osseointegration analysis.
动机· 评估梯度点阵结构在准静态和动态压缩下的力学行为。
方法· 设计并SLM制造了四种梯度拓扑的316L点阵，进行几何、微观结构和压缩测试。
结果· 梯度拓扑影响变形模式和能量吸收，递减梯度结构能量吸收最高。
验证· 通过准静态和动态压缩实验验证力学响应。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the mechanical behavior of gradient lattice structures under quasi-static and dynamic compression.
Method· Four gradient topologies (regular, discrete, increase, decrease) of SS 316L lattices were designed and fabricated via SLM, followed by geometrical quality control, microstructure analysis, and compression tests.
Result· Gradient topology significantly affects deformation mode and energy absorption; the decreasing gradient topology exhibits the highest energy absorption capacity.
Validation· Experimental validation through quasi-static and dynamic compression tests.
