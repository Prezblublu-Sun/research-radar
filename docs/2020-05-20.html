Motivation· To evaluate the long-term clinical outcomes of highly cross-linked polyethylene in cementless total hip arthroplasty following acetabular fractures.
Method· A retrospective review of clinical and radiographic data from patients with a minimum of 5 years of follow-up.
Result· The highly cross-linked polyethylene group showed low wear rates and no osteolysis or revisions.
Validation· Clinical follow-up and radiographic assessment.
