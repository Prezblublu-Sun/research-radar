单位: State Key Laboratory of Powder Metallurgy, Central South University, Changsha 410083, P.R. China
通讯: Lei Zhang @ State Key Laboratory of Powder Metallurgy, Central South University, Changsha 410083, P.R. China. Electronic address: zh
相关性: 该论文涉及多孔钛支架的孔隙结构与力学性能，但使用冷冻铸造而非LPBF，且未涉及应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper uses freeze casting, not LPBF; no fatigue testing, no stress shielding analysis, no surface roughness or osseointegration data.
