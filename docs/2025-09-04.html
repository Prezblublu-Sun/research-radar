A generalized and adaptable tensor-contraction-based cluster expansion formalism for multicomponent solids

Jacob Jeffries, Bochuan Sun, Enrique Martinez arXiv preprint 2025-09-04 10.1016/j.commatsci.2025.114338
相关性: 该论文专注于合金材料的计算方法，与生物打印、髋关节植入物或生物力学完全无关。
边界: No biomedical context, no AM/LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing, no implant design or stress shielding analysis.
动机· 提高多元合金材料模拟的计算效率
方法· 提出张量簇展开方法，将相关函数映射为混合张量收缩
结果· 在TaW和CoNiCrFeMn体系中实现了与基准数据一致的焓和短程有序参数
验证· 与第一性原理基准数据对比验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve computational efficiency of simulating multicomponent alloys by extending cluster expansion to low-symmetry lattices using tensor operations.
Method· Introduced the tensor cluster expansion (TCE) formalism mapping correlation functions to mixed tensor contractions, implemented in the tce-lib code.
Result· Demonstrated accurate enthalpy of mixing and Cowley short-range order parameters for TaW and CoNiCrFeMn systems, matching ground truth DFT data.
Validation· Validated against DFT reference data for two alloy systems.
