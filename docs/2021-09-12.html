Comparison of microstructure and mechanical behavior of Ti-35Nb manufactured by laser powder bed fusion from elemental powder mixture and prealloyed powder

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Transferable Skim only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Jincheng Wang, Yujing Liu, Shun‐Xing Liang, Yusheng Zhang, Lei Wang et al. Journal of Material Science and Technology 2021-09-12 10.1016/j.jmst.2021.07.021
单位: School of Engineering, Edith Cowan University, 270 Joondalup Drive, Joondalup, Perth, WA 6027, Australia
通讯: Lai‐Chang Zhang @ School of Engineering, Edith Cowan University, 270 Joondalup Drive, Joondalup, Perth, WA 6027, Australia
相关性: 该论文研究了Ti-35Nb合金的LPBF制造工艺与力学性能，但未涉及多孔结构或应力遮挡匹配。
边界: No porous structure design, no stress shielding or stiffness matching analysis, no fatigue testing, and no surface roughness or osseointegration evaluation.
动机· 比较元素混合粉与预合金粉LPBF制备Ti-35Nb的微观结构与力学性能差异。
方法· 采用LPBF分别从元素混合粉和预合金粉制备Ti-35Nb，并进行微观表征与力学测试。
结果· 元素混合粉制备的合金具有更细的微观组织和更高的强度，但塑性略低。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the microstructure and mechanical properties of Ti-35Nb alloy fabricated by laser powder bed fusion using elemental powder mixture versus prealloyed powder.
Method· Ti-35Nb samples were manufactured via LPBF from both elemental powder mixture and prealloyed powder, followed by microstructural characterization and mechanical testing.
Result· The alloy from elemental powder mixture exhibited finer microstructure and higher strength but slightly lower ductility compared to that from prealloyed powder.
Validation· Microstructure was validated by SEM and XRD, and mechanical properties were validated by tensile and compression tests.
