单位: Department of Biomaterials Faculty of Dental Science, Kyushu University, Fukuoka, Japan
通讯: Koichiro Hayashi @ Department of Biomaterials Faculty of Dental Science, Kyushu University, Fukuoka, Japan
相关性: 该论文研究了蜂窝支架中通道和微孔对骨缺损重建的影响，但未涉及应力遮挡、LPBF制造或疲劳测试。
边界: This paper does not cover LPBF fabrication, mechanical testing (static or fatigue), stress shielding, or surface roughness characterization.
动机· 解决节段性骨缺损重建中促进骨长入和支架吸收的难题
方法· 制备三种不同通道和微孔体积的碳酸磷灰石蜂窝支架，植入兔尺骨缺损

英文摘要与术语

Result· Larger channel volume enhanced early bone ingrowth, while larger micropore volume promoted scaffold resorption and bone formation at the middle stage; combining both yielded the most new bone.
Validation· In vivo rabbit ulnar defect model with histological evaluation at 4 and 12 weeks postoperatively.
