英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate whether neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio correlates with postoperative pain after total joint arthroplasty.
Method· Retrospective analysis of 101 patients undergoing total hip or knee arthroplasty, recording NLR, VAS scores, and morphine consumption.
Result· A significant positive correlation was found between NLR and VAS at 48 hours postoperatively in the hip arthroplasty group.
Validation· Clinical data with statistical correlation analysis.
