相关性: 该论文仅分析股骨头坏死的临床手术趋势，不涉及任何生物力学建模或植入物设计。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, no FEA, no implant stress/strain/micromotion analysis, no contact mechanics.
动机· 分析日本股骨头坏死手术策略的时间变化趋势
方法· 使用日本DPC数据库进行全国回顾性队列研究
结果· 全髋关节置换术比例从72.6%升至90.6%，保髋手术下降
验证· 基于大规模行政数据，未进行生物力学验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate temporal changes in surgical treatment patterns for osteonecrosis of the femoral head using nationwide data.
Method· A retrospective cohort study was conducted using the Japanese Diagnosis Procedure Combination database from 2012 to 2023.
Result· Total hip arthroplasty increased from 72.6% to 90.6%, while joint-preserving procedures declined, even in younger patients.
Validation· Statistical analysis of administrative data without biomechanical validation.
