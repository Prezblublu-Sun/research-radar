Optimizing permeability of microporous scaffolds through CFD simulation to enhance osseointegration

High AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Read now animal Exp. validation
Zhenpeng Wang, Guiwei Li, Dedong Liu, Ran Wei, Bin Zhao et al. Materials & Design 2025-10-08 10.1016/j.matdes.2025.114885
单位: Department of Orthopedics, The Second Hospital of Jilin University Changchun, 130041, PR China
通讯: Zhenpeng Wang @ Department of Orthopedics, The Second Hospital of Jilin University Changchun, 130041, PR China
相关性: 该论文直接针对多孔支架的渗透率优化，并通过体内实验验证了骨整合效果，与用户研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not explicitly link permeability to stress shielding or stiffness matching, nor does it report fatigue testing or surface roughness/as-built morphology.
动机· 传统支架设计缺乏预测骨整合的流体传输能力。
方法· 结合CFD模拟、SLM制造和兔股骨植入实验，优化支架渗透率。
结果· 发现最佳渗透率窗口6.99–8.82×10⁻⁸ m²，最大化骨长入和细胞粘附。
验证· 通过兔股骨植入模型进行体内验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Conventional scaffold design lacks predictive power for fluid transport and bone ingrowth, necessitating a permeability-guided approach.
Method· A library of Ti6Al4V scaffolds with varied topologies was fabricated via SLM, and permeability was quantified using CFD simulations, followed by in vivo implantation in rabbit femurs.
Result· An optimal permeability window of 6.99–8.82 × 10⁻⁸ m² was identified that maximized early-stage bone ingrowth and cell adhesion.
Validation· In vivo rabbit femur implantation models validated the optimal permeability window for osseointegration.

边界: This paper focuses on crystal plasticity for structural materials, not on biomedical implant mechanics or bone-implant interface simulation.
动机· 全场晶体塑性模型计算成本高，限制其在不确定性量化和多尺度模拟中的应用。
方法· 提出微观结构敏感的GRU代理模型，从三维晶粒结构预测均质化应力响应。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Full-field crystal plasticity models are computationally expensive, limiting their use in uncertainty quantification and multiscale simulations.
Method· A microstructure-sensitive GRU surrogate model is developed to predict homogenized stress responses directly from 3D grain structures and arbitrary deformation histories.
Result· The surrogate reproduces CP predictions with errors of 2-3 MPa for in-distribution and unseen deformation modes, but struggles with out-of-distribution microstructures.
Validation· Validation is performed on over 300,000 CP simulations, comparing surrogate predictions to full-field model outputs for various microstructures and loading paths.
