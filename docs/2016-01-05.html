Very Short-Term Nonparametric Probabilistic Forecasting of Renewable Energy Generation— With Application to Solar Energy

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only numerical_benchmark UQ
Faranak Golestaneh, Pierre Pinson, Hoay Beng Gooi IEEE Transactions on Power Systems 2016-01-05 10.1109/tpwrs.2015.2502423
单位: Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, Singapore
通讯: Faranak Golestaneh @ Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, Singapore
相关性: 该论文专注于可再生能源预测的不确定性量化，与生物力学或植入物设计中的代理建模无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, implant biomechanics, or constraint handling; it focuses on probabilistic forecasting of solar power generation.
动机· 可再生能源预测需要不确定性量化以保障电网可靠性和经济性。
方法· 采用极限学习机结合非参数方法生成短期概率密度预测。
结果· 所提方法在计算效率高的情况下生成可靠的概率预测。
验证· 基于两个不同气候区的太阳能发电站数据进行基准测试。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Uncertainty quantification is essential for reliable and profitable power system operation under renewable energy forecasting.
Method· An extreme learning machine is trained to produce both point and quantile forecasts, combined with a nonparametric approach for predictive densities.
Result· The proposed method yields skillful and reliable probabilistic forecasts with high computational efficiency.
Validation· Evaluated on multiple test cases from two solar power generation sites in different climatic regions.

单位: Associate Professor, Department of Orthopedics, Gujarat Adani Institute of Medical Science, Bhuj, Gujarat, India
通讯: Umang Shihora @ Associate Professor, Department of Orthopedics, Gujarat Adani Institute of Medical Science, Bhuj, Gujarat, India
相关性: 该论文为临床回顾性研究，未涉及任何生物力学建模或应力分析，与用户关注的股骨柄应力遮挡和微动分析无关。
边界: No biomechanical modeling, no finite element analysis, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, and no implant design evaluation.
动机· 评估骨水泥型全髋关节置换术治疗老年股骨颈骨折的疗效。
方法· 对45例60岁以上股骨颈骨折患者行骨水泥型THA，随访1年记录临床和影像学结果。
结果· 91%患者疼痛消失并可独立行走，无假体松动或迁移。
验证· 通过术后临床评分和X线随访验证疗效。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the clinical outcomes of primary cemented total hip arthroplasty for displaced femoral neck fractures in elderly patients.
Method· A prospective study of 45 patients over 60 years old who underwent cemented THA, with clinical and radiological follow-up for one year.
Result· 91% of patients were pain-free and independently mobile, with no acetabular or femoral component loosening or migration observed.
Validation· Clinical outcomes were validated through Harris hip score and radiographic assessment at follow-up.

Experimental evaluation of neural probe's insertion induced injury based on digital image correlation method

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Wenguang Zhang, Yakun Ma, Zhengwei Li Medical Physics 2016-01-05 10.1118/1.4938064
单位: State Key Laboratory of Mechanical System and Vibration, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Shanghai 200240, China
通讯: Wenguang Zhang @ State Key Laboratory of Mechanical System and Vibration, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Shanghai 200240, China
相关性: 该论文研究神经探针插入脑组织造成的损伤，与髋关节植入物和股骨柄的应力遮挡、微动及接触力学无关。
边界: This paper focuses on neural probe insertion into brain tissue phantoms, with no hip implant, femoral stem, stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics.
动机· 开发评估神经探针插入脑组织损伤的系统
方法· 使用硅胶脑组织假体与显微镜DIC方法测量插入引起的应变
结果· 探针楔角60°时最大主应变比40°和20°分别增加40.3%和87.5%
验证· 通过硅胶假体实验验证DIC测量系统的重复性
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop an evaluation system for quantifying brain tissue injury during neural probe insertion using digital image correlation.
Method· A silicone rubber brain phantom with optimized speckle pattern was fabricated, and a microscope-based DIC system measured tissue deformation under varying insertion parameters.
Result· Probe wedge angle significantly affected tissue strain; a 60° wedge increased maximum principal strain by 40.3% and 87.5% compared to 40° and 20°, respectively.
Validation· The evaluation system was validated through repeatable phantom experiments, eliminating biological variability.
