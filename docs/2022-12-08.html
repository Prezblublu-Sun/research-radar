通讯: Bo Li @ School of Mechanical and Power Engineering, East China University of Science and Technology, Shanghai 200237, China
相关性: 该论文涉及激光粉末床熔融工艺参数对高熵合金杆状点阵的成型质量和力学行为的影响，虽然未直接针对生物医学植入物，但其关于点阵结构工艺-性能关系的研究方法可为生物医用多孔植入物的制造提供参考。
边界: This paper does not address biomedical implant applications, stress shielding, stiffness matching, fatigue behavior, or osseointegration; it focuses on process parameters and static mechanical properties of high-entropy alloy rod-lattices.

Result· Defects primarily arise from laser energy diffusion and incomplete powder melting, and mechanical properties show a positive correlation with relative density.
Validation· Mechanical properties were evaluated via tensile testing of micro-rods and compression testing of lattice structures.
