Improved fatigue strength of additively manufactured Ti6Al4V by surface post processing

High AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Read now mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Magnus Kahlin, Hans Ansell, D. N. Basu, A. Kerwin, Lewis Newton et al. International Journal of Fatigue 2020-01-18 10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2020.105497
单位: Division of Engineering Materials, Linköping University, SE-581 83 Linköping, Sweden
通讯: Magnus Kahlin @ Division of Engineering Materials, Linköping University, SE-581 83 Linköping, Sweden
相关性: 该论文直接研究了增材制造Ti6Al4V的疲劳强度，并通过表面后处理改善性能，与用户关注的LPBF制造、机械测试和疲劳性能高度相关。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures, stress shielding, or osseointegration, which are core to the biomedical implant context.
动机· 增材制造零件表面粗糙导致疲劳强度低，需后处理改善。
方法· 对LPBF和E-PBF制造的Ti6Al4V进行五种表面后处理并测试疲劳性能。
结果· 喷丸和离心精加工使LPBF材料疲劳强度达到机加工水平，表面粗糙度不能完全表征疲劳强度。
验证· 通过疲劳实验对比不同后处理方法的疲劳强度。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Additively manufactured Ti6Al4V parts suffer from low fatigue strength due to rough as-built surfaces, necessitating post-processing to enhance performance.
Method· Five surface post-processing methods (shot peening, laser shock peening, centrifugal finishing, laser polishing, linishing) were applied to L-PBF and E-PBF Ti6Al4V, followed by fatigue testing.
Result· Shot peening and centrifugal finishing achieved fatigue strength comparable to machined material, while surface roughness alone was insufficient to predict fatigue strength due to subsurface defects.
Validation· Fatigue experiments were conducted to compare the effectiveness of different surface treatments.

Revealing relationships between porosity, microstructure and mechanical properties of laser powder bed fusion 316L stainless steel through heat treatment

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Tobias Rønneberg, Catrin M. Davies, Paul A. Hooper Materials & Design 2020-01-18 10.1016/j.matdes.2020.108481
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Imperial College London, SW7 2AZ, UK
通讯: Paul A. Hooper @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Imperial College London, SW7 2AZ, UK
相关性: 该论文研究了LPBF 316L不锈钢的热处理对微观结构和力学性能的影响，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或生物医学植入物。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures for stress shielding, does not test fatigue, and lacks biomedical context or osseointegration evaluation.
动机· 揭示LPBF 316L不锈钢中孔隙、微观结构与力学性能之间的关系。
方法· 通过等温热处理选择性去除微观结构特征，评估其对屈服和失效行为的影响。
结果· 各向异性屈服由微观结构引起，而延展性和失效由未熔合孔隙主导。
验证· 通过拉伸测试和微观结构表征验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To understand the incomplete relationships between processing, microstructure, and mechanical properties in laser powder bed fusion of 316L stainless steel.
Method· Isothermal heat treatments were applied to selectively remove microstructural features such as melt pool boundaries and grain structure, and their influence on yield and failure behavior was evaluated.
Result· Anisotropic yield behavior was caused solely by microstructural features, while ductility and failure were dominated by lack-of-fusion porosity, with pore alignment leading to anisotropic ductility.
Validation· Mechanical testing (tensile tests) and microstructural characterization (e.g., EBSD, SEM) were used to validate the findings.

相关性: 该论文提出了一种成本感知的多目标贝叶斯优化方法，但应用于深度神经网络设计，而非生物力学或植入物设计中的有限元代理建模。
边界: This paper focuses on multi-objective optimization for DNN design, not on surrogate modeling for expensive finite element simulations, and does not address constraints, uncertainty quantification, or implant/biomechanics applications.
动机· 多目标优化中不同目标评估成本差异大，现有方法未考虑此问题。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Existing multi-objective optimization methods ignore the varying evaluation costs of different objectives, leading to inefficient use of budget.

Validation· Validated on seven deep neural networks for image recognition, NLP, and speech-to-text tasks.
