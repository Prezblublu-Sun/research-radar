边界: 该论文未涉及代理模型的构建或优化，也未将方法应用于植入物设计或骨-植入物界面的力学分析。

Validation· Statistical convergence analysis demonstrated that contact-sensitive condylar regions require sample sizes exceeding 2,000 for stable sensitivity estimates, whereas global metrics converge with smaller samples.
