PIANO: Physics-informed Dual Neural Operator for Precipitation Nowcasting

Seokhyun Chin, Junghwan Park, Woojin Cho arXiv preprint 2025-11-30 link
相关性: 该论文研究天气预报，与用户关注的有限元代理模型和生物力学设计无关。
边界: This paper addresses precipitation nowcasting using neural operators, with no application to FEA, implant design, biomechanics, or any structural simulation; it lacks validation on mechanical or biomedical problems.
动机· 提升降水短临预报精度与物理一致性
方法· 用物理信息双神经算子(PIANO)结构结合对抗扩散方程约束和生成模型
结果· 在中度和短期强降水事件预测上优于基线模型，季节性变异小
验证· 在卫星和雷达图像数据上验证性能
English summary & terms
Motivation· Precipitation nowcasting is computationally expensive and restrictive, motivating a more efficient and accurate method.
Method· A physics-informed dual neural operator (PIANO) enforces advection-diffusion during training, and a generative model converts satellite to radar images for prediction.
Result· The model improves prediction of moderate and short-term heavy precipitation events with low seasonal variability.
Validation· Validation is performed on benchmark satellite and radar image datasets against baseline models.

Towards Active Synthetic Data Generation for Finetuning Language Models

Samuel Kessler, Menglin Xia, Daniel Madrigal Diaz, Dongge Han, Helia Heshemi et al. arXiv preprint 2025-11-30 link
相关性: 这篇论文讨论的是语言模型微调中的主动合成数据生成，与有限元代理模型或生物力学设计完全无关。
边界: This paper focuses on active learning for language model finetuning, with no connection to FEA, surrogate modeling, uncertainty quantification, or biomechanics.
动机· 研究在微调语言模型时动态生成训练数据是否比静态数据更好。
方法· 利用学生模型的当前状态迭代生成新数据，采用主动学习选择策略。
结果· 在数学和逻辑推理任务上，动态合成数据优于静态生成。
验证· 在四个数据集上使用四种小语言模型进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Investigate whether iterative, closed-loop synthetic data generation guided by student model state improves finetuning over static generation.
Method· Apply simple active learning selection criteria to iteratively query a teacher model for new training samples based on student model uncertainty.
Result· Dynamic synthetic data curation consistently outperforms static generation for a fixed budget across reasoning tasks.
Validation· Empirical validation on four mathematical and logical reasoning datasets using four small language models.
