Motivation· To determine whether excessive acetabular inclination during THA increases wear of highly cross-linked polyethylene liners.
Method· Retrospective analysis of 62 primary THA patients divided into excessive inclination (≥50°) and control groups, measuring polyethylene wear from radiographs.
Result· The excessive inclination group showed significantly higher annual liner wear (0.0254 mm/y vs 0.00446 mm/y), with a weak correlation between inclination angle and wear rate.
Validation· Clinical radiographic follow-up over a mean of 6.3 years.
