Probabilistic Failure Analysis of Composite Beams for Optimum Ply Arrangements under Ballistic Impact

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only simulation_case UQ
Shivdayal Patel, Suhail Ahmad, Puneet Mahajan Jour of Aerospace Science and Technology 2015-02-28 10.17265/2332-8258/2015.01.005
相关性: 该论文涉及复合材料梁的随机有限元分析和概率失效预测，但应用领域为弹道冲击，与生物力学植入物设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, stress shielding, or surrogate modeling for biomedical FEA; it focuses on composite beam failure under ballistic impact.
动机· 考虑材料性能随机性，预测复合材料梁在弹道冲击下的失效概率
方法· 结合随机有限元与高斯过程响应面法，计算不同铺层下的失效概率
结果· 对称正交铺层失效概率比反对称铺层高58%至89%
验证· 通过数值算例与已有文献结果对比验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To account for scatter in material properties and predict failure probability of composite beams under ballistic impact.
Method· Stochastic finite element analysis combined with Gaussian process response surface method to compute probability of failure for various ply arrangements.
Result· Symmetric cross-ply lay-ups exhibit 58% to 89% higher failure probability than anti-symmetric lay-ups.
Validation· Numerical simulation results are compared with existing literature for validation.

Uncertainty Quantification Index of SWMM Model Parameters

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only simulation_case UQ
Gunhui Chung, Kyu Bum Sim, Eung Seok Kim Journal of Korea Water Resources Association 2015-02-28 10.3741/jkwra.2015.48.2.105
相关性: 该论文研究城市水文模型参数的不确定性量化，与生物力学有限元代理建模方向无关。
边界: This paper applies UQ to a hydrological model (SWMM), not to any FEA surrogate, implant mechanics, or biomedical design problem.
动机· 城市不透水面积增加导致洪水风险，SWMM模型参数不确定性影响径流计算精度。
方法· 采用不确定性量化指标（UQI）分析SWMM模型参数对总径流的影响。
结果· 管道粗糙系数总不确定性最大，对径流影响最显著。
验证· 通过案例应用验证UQI方法在参数识别中的有效性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Urban imperviousness increases flood risk, but SWMM model parameter uncertainty limits runoff prediction accuracy.
Method· An Uncertainty Quantification Index (UQI) is used to analyze the contribution of each SWMM parameter to total runoff uncertainty.
Result· Pipe roughness coefficient exhibits the largest total uncertainty and the greatest impact on runoff.
Validation· The UQI method is demonstrated on a case study, identifying key parameters for calibration.
uncertainty quantification · 不确定性量化SWMM · 暴雨管理模型parameter sensitivity · 参数敏感性runoff · 径流pipe roughness · 管道粗糙度
uncertainty quantification SWMM urban hydrology parameter sensitivity
