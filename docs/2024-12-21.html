An explainable operator approximation framework under the guideline of Green's function

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark
Jianghang Gu, Ling Wen, Yuntian Chen, Shiyi Chen arXiv preprint 2024-12-21 link
相关性: 该论文提出的GreenONet框架通过学习格林函数求解PDE，可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模，但未涉及约束优化、不确定性量化或植入物设计。
边界: No constraint handling, no uncertainty quantification, no implant/biomechanics application, and no experimental validation.
动机· 传统数值方法求解PDE计算昂贵，格林函数解析推导困难。
方法· 基于DeepONet结构构建GreenONet，用Trunk Net逼近格林函数，Branch Net逼近辅助梯度，通过积分求解PDE。
结果· 在3D热传导、反应扩散和Stokes方程上，GreenONet精度和泛化能力优于PINN、DeepONet等方法。
验证· 在三个标准PDE基准算例上进行数值验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Traditional numerical solvers for PDEs are computationally expensive, and analytical derivation of Green's functions is challenging for complex systems.
Method· GreenONet, built on DeepONet architecture, uses a Trunk Net to approximate Green's functions and a Branch Net to approximate auxiliary gradients, then performs surface and volume integrals with user-defined boundary conditions and source terms.
Result· On 3D heat conduction, reaction-diffusion, and Stokes equations, GreenONet achieves higher accuracy and better generalization than PINN, DeepONet, PI-DeepONet, and FNO.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on three types of PDEs in bounded domains.
