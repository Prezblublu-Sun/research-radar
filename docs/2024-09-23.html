Automated Microfluidics‐Assisted Hydrogel‐Based Wet‐Spinning for the Biofabrication of Biomimetic Engineered Myotendinous Junction

Marina Volpi, Alessia Paradiso, Ewa Walejewska, Cesare Gargioli, Marco Costantini et al. Advanced Healthcare Materials 2024-09-23 10.1002/adhm.202402075
单位: Faculty of Materials Science and Engineering Warsaw University of Technology Warsaw 02‐507 Poland
通讯: Marco Costantini @ Institute of Physical Chemistry Polish Academy of Sciences Warsaw 01‐224 Poland  |  Wojciech Święszkowski @ Faculty of Materials Science and Engineering Warsaw University of Technology Warsaw 02‐507 Poland
相关性: 该论文涉及生物打印，但未使用任何AI或机器学习方法。
边界: No AI/ML integration, no autonomous process control, no computational modeling for bioprinting.
动机· 模拟肌肉-肌腱连接处的复杂生物力学结构。
方法· 利用微流控辅助3D旋转湿纺技术，优化流速和转速等参数。
结果· 成功制备出具有梯度细胞排列和组织特异性标志物的MTJ仿生支架。
验证· 通过细胞培养和免疫荧光染色验证细胞取向和成熟度。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To biofabricate the complex muscle-tendon junction (MTJ) tissue interface using a hydrogel-based approach.
Method· A microfluidics-assisted 3D rotary wet-spinning strategy was developed, optimizing flow rates, rotational speed, and delivery time.
Result· Compartmentalized scaffolds with gradient cellular arrangement and localized expression of muscle and tenogenic markers were achieved.
Validation· Validation was performed using C2C12 and NIH 3T3 cells, with immunofluorescence confirming cell alignment and tissue-specific maturation.

单位: Tissue Engineering & Additive Manufacturing (TEAM) Lab, Centre for Nanotechnology & Advanced Biomaterials, ABCDE Innovation Centre, School of Chemical & Biotechnology, SASTRA Deemed University, Thanja
通讯: Swaminathan Sethuraman @ Tissue Engineering & Additive Manufacturing (TEAM) Lab, Centre for Nanotechnology & Advanced Biomaterials, ABCDE Innovat [推断]
相关性: 该综述主要讨论生物打印组织的冷冻保存，未涉及人工智能在生物打印中的应用。

动机· 生物打印组织缺乏即时可用保存方法，阻碍按需应用。
方法· 综述了生物打印组织的冷冻保存策略及影响因素。

Method· This review surveys cryopreservation techniques and factors affecting bioprinted tissue viability, including cryoprotectants, polymer types, and crosslinking approaches.
Result· The paper highlights challenges and future directions for creating ready-to-use tissue engineered products via cryopreservation.
Validation· No experimental validation; this is a review article.

Allograft Screws as Fixation of the Scarf Osteotomy.

Kevin Döring, Sebastian Apprich, Markus Hanna, Reinhard Windhager, Stephan Puchner Journal of clinical medicine 2024-09-23 10.3390/jcm13185628
单位: Department of Orthopedics and Trauma Surgery, Medical University of Vienna, 1090 Vienna, Austria.
通讯: Stephan Puchner @ Department of Orthopedics and Trauma Surgery, Medical University of Vienna, 1090 Vienna, Austria. [推断]
相关性: 论文研究同种异体骨螺钉在截骨术中的应用，未涉及增材制造或多孔结构，与用户研究方向无关。
边界: No additive manufacturing, no porous structure design, no LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing.
动机· 探索同种异体骨螺钉替代钛螺钉在截骨术中的可行性和优势
方法· 21例患者前瞻性研究，对比75例回顾性对照组
结果· 同种异体骨螺钉取得良好临床效果，但早期骨折风险较高
验证· 临床随访与影像学评估
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the feasibility and advantages of allograft bone screws compared to titanium screws in Scarf osteotomy.
Method· A prospective study of 21 patients (30 feet) with allograft screws compared to a retrospective control group of 75 patients (82 feet) with headless compression screws.
Result· Allograft screws showed good clinical outcomes but an increased risk of early postoperative fractures.
Validation· Clinical and radiological assessment up to one year postoperatively.

单位: SC Ortopedia-Traumatologia e Chirurgia protesica e dei reimpianti d'anca e di ginocchio, IRCCS Istituto Ortopedico Rizzoli, Via Pupilli 1, Bologna, 40136, Italy. francescocastagnini@hotmail.it
通讯: Francesco Castagnini @ SC Ortopedia-Traumatologia e Chirurgia protesica e dei reimpianti d'anca e di ginocchio, IRCCS Istituto Ortopedico Rizzo

动机· 比较不同髋臼杯在全髋关节置换术中的效果。

Result· Certain acetabular cup designs, especially with highly crosslinked polyethylene, showed better long-term survival.
Validation· Clinical data from a large national joint registry with minimum 5-year follow-up.

“Heterotopic ossification after total hip arthroplasty through direct anterior approach without a dedicated orthopaedic table or direct lateral approach: a quasi-randomized single-center study”

Raffaele Iorio, Matteo Romano Cantagalli, Edoardo Viglietta, Federico Corsetti, Yuri Gugliotta et al. Archives of Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery 2024-09-23 10.1007/s00402-024-05510-3
单位: Orthopaedic Unit, S. Andrea Hospital, University of Rome "La Sapienza", Via di Grottarossa 1035, Rome, Italy
通讯: Raffaele Iorio @ Orthopaedic Unit, S. Andrea Hospital, University of Rome "La Sapienza", Via di Grottarossa 1035, Rome, Italy
相关性: 该论文仅比较不同手术入路对全髋关节置换术后异位骨化的影响，未涉及任何生物力学建模或植入物力学分析。
边界: This paper is a purely clinical comparison of surgical approaches for heterotopic ossification, with no biomechanical modeling, FEA, stress shielding analysis, or implant interface mechanics.
动机· 评估直接前入路与直接外侧入路对全髋置换术后异位骨化的影响
方法· 准随机前瞻性队列研究，50例DAA与50例DLA，6个月后评估HO和HHS
结果· DAA组HO发生率14%显著低于DLA组32%，严重HO也更少
验证· 单中心影像学与临床评分验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To assess the incidence of heterotopic ossification after total hip arthroplasty comparing direct anterior and direct lateral approaches.
Method· A quasi-randomized prospective cohort study with 50 patients per group, evaluating HO via Brooker classification and clinical outcomes via Harris Hip Score at 6 months.
Result· The DAA group showed significantly lower HO incidence (14% vs 32%) and fewer severe cases compared to the DLA group.
Validation· Single-center imaging and clinical score validation.
