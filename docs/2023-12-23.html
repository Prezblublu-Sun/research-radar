Motivation· To investigate the effect of laser power on the microstructural evolution and tensile properties of pure zinc fabricated via laser powder bed fusion.
Method· Pure Zn samples were fabricated by LPBF with varying laser power (40-60 W) and characterized by microstructural analysis and tensile testing.
Result· Increasing laser power enhanced recrystallization, reducing KAM and Taylor factor, leading to significant improvement in ductility; optimal mechanical properties were achieved at 60 W.
Validation· The findings were validated through experimental tensile tests and microstructural observations.
