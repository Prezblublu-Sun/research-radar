English summary & terms
Motivation· To report a case of recurrent hip dislocation after primary total hip arthroplasty treated with a dual mobility acetabular cup.
Method· Revision surgery was performed using a dual mobility acetabular cup in a patient with repeated posterior dislocations.
Result· Intraoperative stability was adequate and short-term outcomes were satisfactory with partial weight-bearing initiated.
Validation· This is a single case report with no quantitative biomechanical validation.
