When Can We Reuse a Calibration Set for Multiple Conformal Predictions?

A. A. Balinsky, A. D. Balinsky arXiv preprint 2025-06-24 link
相关性: 该论文讨论的是共形预测中校准集的复用，未涉及FEA代理模型或生物力学设计。
边界: This paper focuses on statistical calibration reuse for conformal prediction, not on surrogate modeling, expensive simulation sampling, constraint handling, or biomechanics design.
动机· 减少共形预测中频繁校准的开销
方法· 结合Hoeffding不等式与e-共形预测实现单次校准集复用
结果· 在CIFAR-10上验证了复用校准集仍能维持覆盖率保证
验证· 基于CIFAR-10数据集的数值实验
English summary & terms
Motivation· To reduce the overhead of repeated calibration in conformal prediction by enabling reuse of a single calibration set.
Method· Proposes combining Hoeffding's inequality with e-conformal prediction to derive a modified Markov's inequality for constructing prediction sets with quantifiable confidence.
Result· Demonstrates on CIFAR-10 that a single calibration set can be reused while maintaining provable coverage guarantees.
Validation· Numerical benchmark on the CIFAR-10 dataset with a deep neural network.
