通讯: Matthew E. Lynch @ Department of Physical Sciences, United Technologies Research Center, 411 Silver Lane, East Hartford, CT 06108
相关性: 该论文提出用机器学习辅助拓扑优化参数调优，可迁移至FEA替代模型中的超参数优化或约束处理。
边界: This paper focuses on topology optimization parameter tuning, not on surrogate modeling for expensive FEA, lacks uncertainty quantification, and does not address implant/biomechanics design constraints.
动机· 减少拓扑优化中手动调参的试错成本

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce costly trial-and-error in manual tuning of numerical parameters for topology optimization.

Result· The approach reduces the number of wasted tuning runs and achieves good solution quality with optional trade-off between quality and computational time.
Validation· Demonstrated on a simple topology optimization problem with solution quality and computational time objectives.
