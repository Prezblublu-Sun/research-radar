边界: No finite element modeling, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no contact mechanics, and no patient-specific biomechanical analysis.
动机· 比较直接前入路与传统入路对股骨假体生存率的影响

结果· 12年生存率两组无显著差异，DAA不增加无菌性松动风险
验证· 基于大型骨科注册数据库的临床验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare long-term femoral stem survival between direct anterior and conventional approaches using registry data.
Method· Retrospective cohort study of 30,753 primary uncemented THAs from a regional registry, with Kaplan-Meier survivorship and multivariable Cox regression.
Result· No significant difference in 12-year all-cause or aseptic loosening revision rates between approaches.
Validation· Large-scale clinical registry validation with median 4.6-year follow-up.
