通讯: Li Xiaodong @ State Key Laboratory for High Performance Tools College of Mechanical Engineering and Automation Huaqiao University Xia  |  Guangyao Han @ State Key Laboratory for High Performance Tools Institute of Manufacturing Engineering Huaqiao University Xiamen 361021

Validation· Experimental compression tests, digital image correlation, and finite element analysis were used for validation.
