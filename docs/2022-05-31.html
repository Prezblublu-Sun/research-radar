Motivation· To report a rare case of severe acetabular prosthetic protrusion and the surgical reconstruction strategy.
Method· Surgical treatment with an acetabular metal augment and a wide femoral modular reconstruction prosthesis, followed by early revision with a constrained cup.
Result· The patient achieved complete recovery with effective hip function and adequate prosthesis stability despite abductor deficiency.
Validation· Single-case clinical follow-up without biomechanical testing or computational modeling.
