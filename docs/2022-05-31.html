Preparation and mechanical properties analysis of porous structure for bone tissue engineering

单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, Yanshan University, , , China
通讯: Herong Jin @ Key Laboratory of Advanced Forging and Stamping Technology and Science of Ministry of National Education, Yanshan Univer
相关性: 该论文通过SLM制备多孔结构以匹配骨组织弹性模量，直接相关于应力遮挡和植入物力学性能优化。
边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing, surface roughness characterization, or osseointegration assessment.
动机· 解决实体金属植入物弹性模量过高导致的应力遮挡问题

结果· 弹性模量低至0.74 GPa，压缩屈服强度201.91 MPa，退火后力学性能更匹配骨组织

English summary & terms
Motivation· To address the mismatch in elastic modulus between solid metal implants and bone tissue, which causes stress shielding and implant loosening.
Method· Porous structures were designed using a spatial lattice method, fabricated by selective laser melting, and post-processed with vacuum annealing to relieve residual stress.
Result· Elastic modulus as low as 0.74 GPa and compressive yield strength of 201.91 MPa were achieved; annealing increased modulus and slightly decreased strength, improving match to bone.
Validation· Static compression tests were conducted to evaluate the effects of pore shape and porosity on compressive yield strength and elastic modulus.
