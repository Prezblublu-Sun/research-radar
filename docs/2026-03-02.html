Optimization of Processing, Microstructure, and Hardness of an Al–Ce–Ni–Mn–Zr Alloy With Laser Additive Manufacturing

David Fieser, Michael Lawton, Sumit Bahl, Amirkoushyar Ziabari, Tomas Grejtak et al. Advanced Engineering Materials 2026-03-02 10.1002/adem.202502615
单位: Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering Knoxville Tennessee USA
通讯: Anming Hu @ Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering Knoxville Tennessee USA
相关性: 该论文研究的是铝基合金的增材制造工艺与力学性能，与生物医用植入体方向无关。
边界: This paper does not address biomedical implants, stress shielding, osseointegration, or fatigue testing; it focuses on an aluminum alloy for structural applications.
动机· 优化Al-Ce-Ni-Mn-Zr合金的激光增材制造工艺以获得高致密度和高硬度。
方法· 改变激光功率、扫描速度和间距，制备60个样件，表征孔隙率、硬度和微观组织。
结果· 确定了窄工艺窗口（功率350-370W，速度1400-1800mm/s，间距0.14-0.18mm），孔隙率低于0.01%，硬度达160 HV。
验证· 通过X射线CT、扫描电镜和激光共聚焦显微镜验证孔隙率、微观组织和表面粗糙度。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to optimize the laser powder bed fusion processing of an Al-Ce-Ni-Mn-Zr alloy to achieve high density and hardness.
Method· Sixty samples were fabricated with varying laser power, scan speed, and hatch spacing, and characterized for porosity, microstructure, and hardness.
Result· A narrow processing window was identified yielding porosity below 0.01% and hardness up to 160 HV.
Validation· Porosity was quantified via X-ray computed tomography, while microstructure and surface roughness were assessed by SEM and laser confocal microscopy.
