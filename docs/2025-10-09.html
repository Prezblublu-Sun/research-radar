Biomechanical evaluation of asymmetric metaphyseal cone applications for lateral tibial plateau fractures : a finite element study.

Yi Ren, Chloe E H Scott, Shuqiao Xie, Pankaj Pankaj Bone & joint research 2025-10-09 10.1302/2046-3758.1410.BJR-2024-0579.R1
单位: Institute for Bioengineering, School of Engineering, The University of Edinburgh, Edinburgh, UK.
通讯: Pankaj Pankaj @ Institute for Bioengineering, School of Engineering, The University of Edinburgh, Edinburgh, UK. [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究的是胫骨平台骨折的锥形假体生物力学，与髋关节假体方向无关。
边界: This paper focuses on tibial plateau fractures and knee arthroplasty, not on hip implant stress shielding, micromotion, or femoral stem mechanics.
动机· 评估非对称锥体在急性全膝关节置换术中治疗外侧胫骨平台骨折的生物力学表现。
方法· 建立有限元模型，模拟12种骨缺损配置，分析四种载荷下的界面微动和骨应变。
结果· 锥体在Ru≤44%时稳定，微动低于82μm；后外侧区域是关键不稳定区域。
验证· 有限元仿真与临床阈值对比验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the biomechanical performance of asymmetric metaphyseal cones for managing lateral tibial plateau fractures during acute total knee arthroplasty.
Method· A finite element model with a generic tibia geometry and cone system was used to simulate 12 uncontained defect configurations under four loading conditions.
Result· Stable performance (micromotion <50 μm) was observed when the unsupported area ratio Ru ≤44%, with maximum micromotion 82 μm at Ru=56% during stair descending.
Validation· Finite element simulation results were compared against clinical osseointegration and bone strain thresholds.
