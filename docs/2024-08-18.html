Fredholm Neural Networks

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project simulation_case
Kyriakos Georgiou, Constantinos Siettos, Athanasios N. Yannacopoulos arXiv preprint 2024-08-18 10.1137/24M1686991
相关性: 提出了一种基于弗雷德霍姆积分方程的神经网络架构，可求解PDE，有望用于代理建模，但未直接解决采样效率和约束处理问题。
边界: This paper does not address the efficiency of expensive simulation sampling, constraint handling in multi-objective optimization, or provide explicit uncertainty quantification for predictions, and it does not demonstrate transferability to implant or biomechanics design.
动机· 提出一种可解释的深度神经网络架构，用于高效求解积分方程和偏微分方程。
方法· 基于弗雷德霍姆积分方程的不动点迭代格式设计神经网络结构，连接理论与网络参数。
结果· 在线性和非线性椭圆型PDE及边值问题上取得了高精度近似解。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To introduce an explainable deep neural network architecture derived from the fixed-point iteration of Fredholm integral equations for solving differential equations and inverse problems.
Method· Fredholm NNs are constructed by unrolling the fixed-point iteration scheme of second-kind Fredholm integral equations, with interpretable weights and biases linked to the mathematical theory.
Result· The proposed networks achieve high numerical accuracy on linear and nonlinear elliptic PDEs and boundary value problems across the entire domain.
Validation· The method is validated on several numerical examples including elliptic PDEs and boundary value problems.

Result· The trained GPR model effectively separates thermal and mechanical strain contributions and provides distribution information even from complex diffraction patterns.
Validation· Approach is validated on simulated laser melting of an Inconel 625 wall specimen with full thermomechanical and diffraction modeling.

相关性: 该论文未涉及AI或机器学习，仅专注于生物3D打印的材料和生物学性能。
边界: This paper does not incorporate any AI/ML method into the bioprinting workflow; it is purely experimental materials science and biology.
动机· 开发具有免疫调节功能的骨组织工程支架，加速大尺寸骨缺损修复。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a tissue-engineered bone with temporally regulated immune response to address delayed healing and nonunion in large bone defects.
Method· A photocurable hydrogel (bdECM-MA) was synthesized via multiphysics-assisted decellularization, side-chain biochemical modification, and freeze-drying, then combined with silicon-substituted calcium phosphate and stem cells for DLP 3D bioprinting.
Result· The bioprinted construct achieved MPa-level strength and exhibited sequential immunomodulatory properties, activating pro- and anti-inflammatory responses to accelerate bone repair in vivo.
Validation· In vitro cell assays and in vivo rat calvarial defect model demonstrated high cell viability, osteogenesis, angiogenesis, and immune modulation.
