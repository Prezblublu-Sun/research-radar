相关性: 该论文仅涉及全髋关节置换术后的临床评分和影像学参数，未涉及股骨近端应力遮挡或微动分析。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain, micromotion, or implant-bone interface mechanics; it is purely about clinical outcomes and spinal alignment.

Motivation· To evaluate early clinical and radiological changes in hip-spine syndrome patients undergoing staged bilateral THA.

Validation· Clinical prospective cohort without biomechanical modeling or in vitro testing.
