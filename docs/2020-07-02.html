边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, constraint handling, or biomechanical implant design; it focuses on general BO for noisy functions.

Result· BOSH achieves more efficient and higher-precision optimization than standard BO across synthetic benchmarks, simulation optimization, RL, and hyperparameter tuning.
