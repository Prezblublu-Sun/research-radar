英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate national trends in post-acute care costs following total hip arthroplasty over the last decade.
Method· A retrospective analysis of 189,847 primary THA patients from the PearlDiver database (2010-2018) was performed, examining annual trends in discharge disposition and post-acute care resource utilization.
Result· Both total episode-of-care costs and post-acute care costs declined significantly, driven by reduced utilization of skilled nursing facilities, home health, and inpatient rehabilitation.
Validation· Statistical analysis of a large national database with cost standardization to 2020 dollars.
