Method· Commercial calcium phosphate scaffolds were covalently coated with vanillin essential oil derivative.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To analyze implant survivorship and risk factors for revision in patients aged 30 years or younger after total hip arthroplasty using registry data.
Method· Kaplan-Meier survival analysis and Cox proportional hazard models were applied to the German Arthroplasty Registry dataset from 2013 to 2023.
Result· The cumulative revision rate at 8 years was 4.6%, with infection being the most common cause of revision.
Validation· Clinical validation using a large national registry cohort with 1,622 primary THAs.
