英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the medium to long-term clinical outcomes of the direct anterior approach for total hip arthroplasty in patients with severe hip dysplasia (Crowe III and IV).
Method· A retrospective analysis of clinical data from patients with Crowe III/IV dysplasia who underwent total hip arthroplasty via the direct anterior approach.
Result· The direct anterior approach yields satisfactory medium to long-term clinical outcomes in patients with severe dysplasia.
Validation· Clinical outcome assessment based on patient follow-up data.

Motivation· To report a rare case of rapidly destructive coxarthrosis potentially induced by crizotinib in a lung adenocarcinoma patient.
Method· Clinical observation and MRI imaging were used to diagnose and document the hip pathology.
Result· The patient underwent total hip arthroplasty, suggesting a possible association between crizotinib and RDC.
Validation· Single case report without experimental or comparative validation.
