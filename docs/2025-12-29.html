English summary & terms
Motivation· Systemic inflammation after THA suppresses iron indices, causing widespread misclassification of iron deficiency, motivating adjustment using inflammatory markers.
Method· CRP and albumin levels were used to adjust iron biomarkers to improve diagnostic accuracy for iron deficiency.
Result· Adjustment reduced misclassification and provided more accurate perioperative iron status assessment.
Validation· Clinical data from THA patients were analyzed to validate the adjustment method.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the true cumulative incidence of dislocation after THA by surgical approach and assess the impact of bearing size and fixation.
Method· A longitudinal cohort study of 136,810 patients with Kaplan-Meier estimation and multiple Cox regression.
Result· Posterior approach had higher dislocation risk than lateral; head sizes >32mm and dual mobility cups mitigated risk.
Validation· Validated on a large population-based registry with clinical follow-up.

Core decompression can still be an effective treatment in femoral head avascular necrosis

相关性: 该论文为临床回顾性研究，未涉及任何髋关节植入物或股骨柄的力学分析。
边界: No biomechanical modeling, FEA, stress shielding quantification, micromotion analysis, or implant design considerations.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To clarify the inconsistent efficacy of core decompression for femoral head avascular necrosis.
Method· Retrospective analysis of functional and radiological data from 44 hips (33 patients) treated with core decompression.
Result· Significant improvement in Harris hip score postoperatively, but 36.3% of hips required total hip arthroplasty.
Validation· Retrospective clinical outcomes with mean follow-up of 89 months.
