英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To describe the surgical technique and early clinical outcomes of the SuperPath minimally invasive approach for total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective review of 150 consecutive patients undergoing SuperPath THA with detailed surgical steps and postoperative management.
Result· At a mean follow-up of 16 months, no radiological loosening was observed, with low complication rates including two subluxations and one femoral fracture.
Validation· Clinical follow-up of 150 patients without biomechanical testing or finite element analysis.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the clinical and economic impacts of COPD on mortality, cost, and length of stay in patients undergoing elective total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective cohort study using the Nationwide Inpatient Sample (NIS) from 2012 to 2014, identifying patients via ICD-9 codes.
Result· COPD was associated with higher mortality, longer length of stay, and higher total charges in THA patients.
Validation· No experimental validation; statistical analysis of a large administrative database.
