通讯: Wei Sun @ Tissue Mechanics Laboratory, The Wallace H. Coulter Department of Biomedical Engineering Georgia Institute of Technology
相关性: 该论文展示了机器学习作为有限元逆问题替代模型的方法，可迁移至植入物生物力学设计中的代理建模。
边界: This paper focuses on zero-pressure geometry estimation for blood vessels, not on implant stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics.

Validation· The model was validated on a held-out test set of virtual patient data by comparing predicted geometries to FEA-derived ground truth.
