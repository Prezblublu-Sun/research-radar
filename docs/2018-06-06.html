通讯: Trygve Holm‐Glad @ Division of Orthopaedic Surgery Oslo University Hospital Oslo Norway
相关性: 该论文研究腕关节假体的微动测量方法，与髋关节假体应力遮挡和股骨柄稳定性无直接关系。
边界: This paper focuses on wrist arthroplasty micromotion measurement using RSA, not on hip implant stress shielding, femoral stem micromotion, or proximal femur biomechanics.
动机· 评估模型RSA在腕关节假体微动测量中的精度和准确性。
方法· 使用激光扫描获取假体模型，在体模和患者中分析平移和旋转的重复性。

Motivation· To evaluate the precision and accuracy of model-based radiostereometric analysis (RSA) for measuring micromotion in total wrist arthroplasties.

Result· Phantom precision ranged from 0.03 to 0.14 mm for translations and 0.18 to 1.52° for rotations; clinical precision ranged from 0.06 to 0.18 mm and 0.32 to 2.18°, respectively.
Validation· Validation was performed using phantom experiments with known migrations and clinical double examinations to assess precision and accuracy.
