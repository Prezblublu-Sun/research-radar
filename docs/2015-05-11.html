Finite element analysis of sliding distance and contact mechanics of hip implant under dynamic walking conditions

Medium Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Skim only numerical_benchmark

单位: State Key Laboratory for Manufacturing System Engineering, School of Mechanical Engineering, Xi’an Jiaotong University, Xi’an, China
通讯: Yongchang Gao @ State Key Laboratory for Manufacturing System Engineering, School of Mechanical Engineering, Xi’an Jiaotong University,
相关性: 该论文主要关注髋关节假体的接触力学和滑动距离，未涉及股骨柄的应力遮挡或微动分析。
边界: This paper focuses on contact mechanics of the hip implant bearing surface, not on femoral stem stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics.
动机· 开发显式有限元方法预测髋关节假体在动态步行下的接触力学。
方法· 建立显式有限元模型，分析网格敏感性和时间步长对精度和计算成本的影响。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop an explicit finite element method for predicting dynamic contact mechanics of hip implants under normal walking conditions.
Method· An explicit FE model was built, and mesh sensitivity and time step effects on accuracy and computational cost were examined.
Result· Maximum contact pressure and accumulated sliding distance agreed well with previous implicit FE and analytical results.
Validation· Validated by comparison with implicit finite element analysis and analytical methods from prior studies.

方法· 采用树脂铸模蚀刻和电子显微镜观察纳米钛牙种植体表面骨细胞网络。

边界: No biomechanics modeling, no stress shielding or micromotion analysis, no FEA, no implant design or comparison.
动机· 探究高BMI是否影响全髋关节置换术的临床结果。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine whether high body mass index independently affects patient-reported outcomes after primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Oxford Hip Scores from 353 operations were analyzed, comparing preoperative and postoperative changes between BMI ≥30 and <30 groups using Kendall's rank correlation and group comparison.
Result· Patients with BMI ≥30 had lower absolute scores but similar magnitude of improvement as those with BMI <30, with no significant correlation between BMI and score change.
Validation· Statistical analysis of clinical outcome data from a patient cohort.
