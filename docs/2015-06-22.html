通讯: Filippo Zanini @ University of Padova#TAB#
相关性: 该论文研究了增材制造金属零件的CT孔隙率分析精度，但未涉及应力屏蔽、疲劳或骨整合等生物力学方向。
边界: This paper focuses on CT porosity measurement accuracy, not on stress shielding, fatigue testing, surface roughness, or osseointegration relevant to biomedical implants.
动机· 验证CT孔隙分析在AM金属零件中的准确性
