单位: Engineering and Physical Sciences, University of Southampton, Southampton, SO17 1BJ, United Kingdom
通讯: Andrew Hamilton @ Engineering and Physical Sciences, University of Southampton, Southampton, SO17 1BJ, United Kingdom
相关性: 该论文研究了316L不锈钢LPBF工艺中的重扫描参数对致密化和微观结构的影响，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或生物医学植入物力学性能。
边界: This paper does not address porous structure design, stress shielding, stiffness matching, fatigue testing, or osseointegration, which are core to the AM Biomedical Materials direction.
动机· 通过重扫描参数控制LPBF 316L不锈钢的致密度和微观组织。
方法· 采用初始扫描参数致密化，不同重扫描参数细化亚晶粒尺寸。
结果· 重扫描实现99.96%致密度和0.35μm局部细化晶粒，强度硬度可调。
验证· 实验测量致密度和晶粒尺寸，Wilson-Rosenthal模型预测冷却速率。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To achieve both high densification and microstructural refinement in LPBF 316L stainless steel by optimizing rescanning parameters.
Method· A two-step scanning strategy was employed: initial scan for densification and rescan with different parameters for grain refinement.
Result· Rescanning yielded 99.96% density and locally refined cell size of 0.35 μm, enabling tailored strength and hardness.
Validation· Experimental densitometry and microscopy were combined with Wilson-Rosenthal thermal modeling to correlate cooling rate with cell size.
