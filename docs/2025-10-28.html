结果· 模型解释力强（条件R²=0.777），咬合力与咬肌体积正相关，与牙齿活动度负相关。
验证· 基于109次咬合力观测的临床数据验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a personalized predictive bite force model for optimizing functional rehabilitation in head and neck oncology patients undergoing mandibular reconstruction.
Method· A linear mixed-effects model was applied to analyze bite force data from 41 patients, incorporating predictors such as body weight, masseter muscle volume, and tooth mobility.
Result· The model achieved strong explanatory performance (conditional R² = 0.777), with masseter volume positively associated and tooth mobility negatively associated with bite force.
Validation· Clinical validation using 109 bite force measurements from head and neck cancer patients.
