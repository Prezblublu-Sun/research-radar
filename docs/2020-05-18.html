The effects of clinically‐derived parametric data uncertainty in patient‐specific coronary simulations with deformable walls

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project simulation_case UQ Patient-specific
Jongmin Seo, Daniele E. Schiavazzi, Andrew M. Kahn, Alison L. Marsden International Journal for Numerical Methods in Biomedical Engineering 2020-05-18 10.1002/cnm.3351
单位: Department of Pediatrics (Cardiology), Bioengineering and ICME Stanford University Stanford California USA
通讯: Jongmin Seo @ Department of Pediatrics (Cardiology), Bioengineering and ICME Stanford University Stanford California USA
相关性: 该论文展示了在心血管模拟中处理参数不确定性的方法，可迁移至植入物力学模拟中的不确定性量化。
边界: This paper focuses on coronary hemodynamics with deformable walls, not on implant stress shielding or bone-implant interface mechanics.
动机· 量化心血管模拟中输入参数不确定性对输出变异性的影响
方法· 采用蒙特卡洛、准蒙特卡洛、随机配置和多小波随机展开等方法传播不确定性
结果· 入口压力和心肌内压力显著影响所有关注量，弹性模量仅影响血管壁力学响应
验证· 通过三个基准问题和左冠状动脉模拟比较不同不确定性传播方法的收敛性
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify the impact of input parameter uncertainty on simulation output variability in patient-specific coronary models with deformable walls.
Method· Uncertainty propagation was performed using Monte Carlo, Quasi Monte Carlo, stochastic collocation, and multi-wavelet stochastic expansion methods.
Result· Inlet pressure and intramyocardial pressure significantly affect all quantities of interest, while elastic modulus only influences wall mechanics.
Validation· Convergence behaviors of statistics were compared across uncertainty propagation methods on benchmark problems and coronary simulations.
