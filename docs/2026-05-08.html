Bioelectric ink bridge: An electroactive casein bioink for cartilage regeneration by actively restoring the electrophysiological niche.

Shuai Zhu, Zheng Zhou, Xin Chen, Xiaoli He, Lingxiu Yang et al. Biomaterials 2026-05-08 10.1016/j.biomaterials.2026.124289
相关性: 该论文开发了一种电活性生物墨水用于软骨再生，但未涉及AI在生物打印流程中的集成。
边界: This paper does not incorporate AI into any bioprinting workflow stage; it focuses on material design and biological evaluation without computational modeling or autonomous decision-making.
动机· 开发能模拟电生理微环境的生物墨水以促进软骨再生。
方法· 设计季铵化-甲基丙烯酸化酪蛋白电活性生物墨水，结合DLP打印。
结果· QCMA墨水在电场下促进软骨细胞迁移、增殖和基质沉积，大鼠模型显示修复效果。
验证· 大鼠骨软骨缺损模型体内验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a bioink that recapitulates endogenous bioelectric cues for enhanced cartilage regeneration.
Method· A filler-free electroactive bioink (QCMA) was synthesized by quaternizing and methacrylating casein, enabling DLP bioprinting with high fidelity.
Result· QCMA constructs under pulsed electric field activated MAPK signaling, upregulated chondrogenic markers, and improved cartilage repair in a rat model.
Validation· In vivo validation using a rat osteochondral defect model with impedance and voltage recovery measurements.

The Influence of SLM Process Parameters on Young’s Modulus, Poisson’s Ratio, and Impact Toughness of Ti6Al4V

Yan Zeng, Hantao Chen, Li Yu, Shutong Dai, Jianggui Han et al. Applied Sciences 2026-05-08 10.3390/app16104652
单位: Naval University of Engineering, Wuhan 430030, China
通讯: Man Xu @ Naval University of Engineering, Wuhan 430030, China  |  Xiaokai Wang @ State Key Laboratory of Light Superalloys, Wuhan University of Technology, Wuhan 430070, China
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM工艺参数对Ti6Al4V力学性能的影响，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能，与用户方向部分相关。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or osseointegration, which are core to the user's direction on AM biomedical implants.
动机· 揭示SLM工艺参数对Ti6Al4V弹性模量、泊松比和冲击韧性的耦合影响。
方法· 结合激光超声检测、夏比冲击试验、断口观察和缺陷统计分析。
结果· 三个力学性能随工艺参数呈抛物线趋势变化，冲击韧性最敏感，泊松比最不敏感。
验证· 通过缺陷、断口和微观组织分析验证了工艺-缺陷/组织-性能关系。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To systematically investigate the coupled influence of SLM process parameters on Young's modulus, Poisson's ratio, and impact toughness of Ti6Al4V.
Method· Laser ultrasonic testing, Charpy impact testing, fractographic observation, and defect statistical analysis were employed to characterize the mechanical behavior.
Result· All three properties exhibited a non-monotonic parabolic dependence on process parameters, with impact toughness being most sensitive and Poisson's ratio least sensitive.
Validation· The process-defect/microstructure-property relationship was validated through combined defect, fracture, and microstructural analysis.

Designing an Injectable Hydrogel Scaffold and Delivery System for Post-Lumpectomy Breast Tissue Regeneration; Evolution of Animal Model Usage and Effectiveness in Women's Health

Siena Rodeschin Libra 2026-05-08 10.18130/3y4f-sx16
单位: University of Virginia
通讯: Siena Rodeschin @ University of Virginia
相关性: 该论文涉及可注射水凝胶支架用于乳腺组织再生，但未涉及AI或生物打印技术。
边界: This paper does not incorporate AI into any bioprinting workflow stage, lacks experimental validation of AI models, and does not advance toward autonomous decision-making in bioprinting.
动机· 开发可注射水凝胶填充物以促进乳腺切除术后组织再生。
方法· 制备纤维增强微颗粒水凝胶支架，通过迈克尔加成反应实现原位交联。
结果· 支架具有可注射性、原位交联能力及良好的细胞相容性。
验证· 通过流变学、荧光成像和成纤维细胞封装实验验证材料性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop an injectable hydrogel scaffold that fills post-lumpectomy cavities and promotes breast tissue regeneration without UV crosslinking.
Method· A fiber-reinforced microparticle (FEMP) scaffold was fabricated using electrospun nanofibers, hydrogel microparticles, and sacrificial gelatin, crosslinked via Michael-type addition with PEG-SH.
Result· The FEMP scaffold demonstrated injectability, in situ crosslinking, tunable porosity, and cytocompatibility with encapsulated fibroblasts.
Validation· Material properties, porosity, and cytocompatibility were evaluated using rheology, fluorescence imaging, and fibroblast encapsulation assays.
