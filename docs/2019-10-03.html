Motivation· To characterize the pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of dexmedetomidine administered via extravascular routes.
Method· Subcutaneous and intranasal administration experiments were conducted in healthy volunteers and pediatric patients.
Result· Intranasal dexmedetomidine achieved clinically acceptable sedation in pediatric patients and reduced postoperative opioid consumption in adults undergoing total hip arthroplasty.
Validation· Clinical trials validated pharmacokinetic parameters and clinical outcomes.
