单位: Advanced Digital & Additive Manufacturing Center, Khalifa University, Abu Dhabi P.O. Box 127788, United Arab Emirates
通讯: Rashid K. Abu Al‐Rub @ Advanced Digital & Additive Manufacturing Center, Khalifa University, Abu Dhabi P.O. Box 127788, United Arab Emirates
相关性: 该论文研究聚合物点阵的拓扑与力学性能关系，但未涉及金属生物植入物、应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: No metal LPBF, no fatigue testing, no biomedical context such as stress shielding or osseointegration.
动机· 探究不同点阵拓扑对聚合物力学性能的影响。
方法· 设计并利用SLS制造55种聚合物点阵，进行微观表征和压缩测试。
结果· 面基TPMS点阵具有最高刚度，韧带型TPMS适合吸能。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To systematically investigate the influence of lattice topology on the mechanical properties of polymer metamaterials fabricated by SLS.
Method· Designed and fabricated 55 polymer lattices of sheet-based TPMS, ligament-based TPMS, and strut-based topologies via SLS, followed by microstructural characterization and uniaxial compression tests.
Result· Sheet-based TPMS lattices exhibited superior stiffness due to stretching-dominated elastic behavior, while ligament-based TPMS lattices showed bending-dominated behavior suitable for energy absorption.
Validation· Printing quality assessed by SEM and micro-CT; mechanical properties extracted from compression tests.
