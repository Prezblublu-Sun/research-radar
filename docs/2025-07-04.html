Association of preoperative health status with risk of complications after primary total hip arthroplasty: how useful are the measures self-rated health, ASA classification and comorbidity count?

Alice Bonnefoy‐Mazure, Donia Dellagiovanna, Christophe Baréa, Didier Hannouche, Anne Lübbeke BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders 2025-07-04 10.1186/s12891-025-08745-2
单位: Division of Orthopaedics and Trauma Surgery, Faculty of Medicine, Geneva University Hospitals, University of Geneva, Geneva, Switzerland. alice.bonnefoymazure@hcuge.ch
通讯: Alice Bonnefoy‐Mazure @ Division of Orthopaedics and Trauma Surgery, Faculty of Medicine, Geneva University Hospitals, University of Geneva, Gen
相关性: 该论文关注术前健康状况与全髋关节置换术后并发症的风险关联，未涉及股骨柄生物力学、应力遮挡或微动分析。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain or micromotion, does not use FEA, and does not compare intact vs implanted femur; it is a clinical outcomes study.
动机· 评估自评健康、ASA分级和合并症数量与全髋关节置换术后并发症的关联
方法· 前瞻性队列研究，纳入3906例初次全髋关节置换患者，使用Cox回归分析
结果· 健康状况差的患者术后并发症和5年死亡风险显著升高，ASA分级对死亡预测最强
验证· 临床队列验证，采用Kaplan-Meier和Cox回归分析
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate whether self-rated health (SRH) predicts medical complications, revision, and mortality after primary total hip arthroplasty, compared to ASA classification and comorbidity count.
Method· A prospective cohort study of 3906 primary elective THA patients (2002–2019) analyzed associations between preoperative SRH, ASA grade, comorbidity count, and outcomes using Cox regression.
Result· Poorer preoperative health status was associated with higher risks of in-hospital complications, 5-year mortality (HR up to 5.0 for ASA 3–4), and early revision, with ASA most strongly linked to death.
Validation· Clinical cohort validation with reverse Kaplan-Meier and Cox regression adjusted for confounders.
