单位: Jiangsu Key Lab of Design and Manufacture of Extreme Pressure Equipment Nanjing 211816 China
通讯: Jiaxi Zhao @ Jiangsu Key Lab of Design and Manufacture of Extreme Pressure Equipment Nanjing 211816 China
相关性: 该论文研究了Ti6Al4V金刚石点阵结构的压缩行为，与用户关注的LPBF制造多孔植入物的力学性能相关，但未涉及应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper does not link porous structure to stress shielding or stiffness matching, does not include fatigue testing, and does not report surface roughness or osseointegration.
