English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare the external surface area of progressive knife-edge and V-shaped ISO thread designs for dental implants.
Method· Six virtual implant models with two diameters (3.8 and 4.6 mm) and identical length were created and their external surface areas measured in SolidWorks.
Result· The knife-edge design showed +9.9% larger surface area at 4.6 mm diameter but -1.5% at 3.8 mm compared to ISO self-tapping; apical half area was always larger for knife-edge (9.3%-23.5%).
Validation· No experimental validation; purely theoretical geometric analysis.
