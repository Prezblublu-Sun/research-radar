通讯: Henning Haschke @ Institute of Biomechanics, TUHH Hamburg University of Technology, Denickestraße 15, 21073 Hamburg Germany. Electronic ad
相关性: 该论文研究模块化髋关节假体头-柄锥部微动，但未涉及股骨近端应力遮挡或骨-植入物界面，与用户方向间接相关。
边界: This paper focuses on head-stem taper micromotion and fretting corrosion, not on proximal femur stress shielding, bone-implant interface, or Gruen zone analysis.
动机· 研究锥部弯曲刚度对模块化髋假体头-柄界面微动的影响
方法· 制造不同刚度的锥部和柄设计，实验测量六自由度相对运动，结合有限元分析
结果· 低刚度锥部增加微动，增大腐蚀风险；柄设计影响次要

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate how flexural rigidity of the taper affects micromotion at the head-stem junction in modular hip prostheses, which is linked to fretting corrosion failure.
Method· Three stem and two taper designs were manufactured; relative motion was measured in six degrees of freedom under three activity levels, with elastic deformation subtracted using monoblock and finite element analysis.
Result· Less rigid tapers increased micromotion, elevating fretting corrosion risk; stem design had a secondary effect compared to taper design.
Validation· Experimental testing with peak loads representing walking, stair climbing, and stumbling, combined with finite element analysis for elastic deformation correction.
