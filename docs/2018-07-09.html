英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the impact of total hip arthroplasty on quality of life and satisfaction in Japanese and Korean patients.
Method· Patient-reported outcome measures were collected via questionnaires to assess postoperative quality of life and satisfaction.
Result· Significant improvements in quality of life and satisfaction were observed, with cultural differences noted.
Validation· No biomechanical validation; solely based on patient-reported outcomes.
