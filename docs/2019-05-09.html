Kavan Hazeli, Behzad Bahrami Babamiri, Joseph Indeck, Andrew M. Minor, Hesam Askari Materials & Design 2019-05-09 10.1016/j.matdes.2019.107826
单位: Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering Department, University of Alabama in Huntsville, USA
通讯: Kavan Hazeli @ Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering Department, University of Alabama in Huntsville, USA
相关性: 该论文研究了增材制造晶格结构的微观结构与拓扑关系对力学行为的影响，但未涉及生物医学植入物或应力遮挡问题。
边界: This paper does not address stress shielding, stiffness matching, or biomedical implant context; it focuses on Inconel 718 lattice structures for non-biomedical applications.
动机· 探究制造变量（拓扑和热处理）对增材制造晶格结构孔隙分布及力学行为的影响。
方法· 采用SLM制备四种拓扑的Inconel 718晶格，结合X射线CT和热处理，进行准静态和动态力学测试。
结果· 应力释放热处理显著降低孔隙率并提高屈服后流变应力，但后续HIP+SA效果有限；变形趋势与应变率无关。
验证· 通过X射线CT验证孔隙分布，准静态和动态力学测试验证力学性能。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effects of manufacturing variables, including design topology and post-processing heat treatment, on the porosity distribution and mechanical behavior of additively manufactured lattice structures.
Method· Lattice structures with four topologies were fabricated from Inconel 718 using selective laser melting, followed by X-ray computed tomography and heat treatments (as-built, stress relieved, HIP plus solution aged), and tested under quasi-static and dynamic loading.
Result· Stress relief significantly reduced porosity and enhanced flow stress after yielding, while subsequent HIP and solution aging had limited additional effect; deformation trends were independent of strain rate.
Validation· Porosity was validated via X-ray CT, and mechanical behavior was validated through quasi-static compression and Kolsky bar dynamic tests with digital image correlation.
