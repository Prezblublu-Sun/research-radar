Motivation· To examine racial differences in post-THA rehabilitation discharge destination and its association with hospital readmission.
Method· Multinomial logistic regression was applied to a large regional dataset of 68,016 THA patients.
Result· African American patients had higher risk of discharge to IRF or SNF, which was associated with increased 90-day readmission odds.
Validation· Retrospective analysis of a large administrative database with adjusted regression models.
