SELECTIVE TOTAL HIP ARTHROPLASTY APPROACH USE AMONG JUNIOR SURGEONS IMPROVES SAFETY OF INTRODUCING THE ANTERIOR APPROACH: A PROSPECTIVE MULTI-SURGEON COMPARATIVE STUDY

English summary & terms
Motivation· To assess whether selective use of the anterior approach allows junior surgeons to safely introduce it into practice.
Method· A prospective comparative study of 1200 primary THAs performed by junior dual-approach and senior single-approach surgeons.
Result· No significant differences in complication, reoperation, or revision rates between junior and senior surgeons for either approach.
Validation· Clinical validation with 2.5-year follow-up and statistical comparison of outcomes.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To analyze the role of physical therapy in functional recovery after total hip arthroplasty through a literature review.
Method· A literature review of 12 articles from PubMed, SciELO, and other databases (2009–2024).
Result· Early physical therapy interventions improve functional outcomes, reduce hospitalization, and prevent complications.
Validation· No experimental or computational validation; solely a literature review.
