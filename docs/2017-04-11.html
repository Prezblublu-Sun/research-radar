边界: This paper focuses on active learning with comparison queries for classification, with no application to surrogate modeling, finite element analysis, or biomechanical design.
动机· 减少主动学习中标签查询次数，利用比较查询提高效率。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce the number of label queries in active learning by leveraging comparison queries that ask annotators to compare distances to the decision boundary.
Method· Proposes a general framework based on the inference dimension to characterize query complexity when additional queries are determined by a constant number of examples.
Result· Under large-margin or bounded bit-description assumptions, all labels of n samples can be revealed using O(log n) queries, an exponential improvement over classical active learning.
Validation· Theoretical proofs and worst-case lower bounds, without experimental validation.

Motivation· To determine whether preoperative radiographic measurement of acetabular abduction correlates with intraoperative inclinometer readings for cup positioning.
Method· Preoperative and intraoperative abduction angles from 100 consecutive THA patients were compared using intraclass correlation coefficient.
Result· Preoperative and intraoperative angles were nearly identical (ICC=0.96), with 28% of patients having preoperative angles >50°.
Validation· Clinical validation with 100 patients using ICC and Bland-Altman analysis.
