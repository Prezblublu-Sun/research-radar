Decomposition of Uncertainty in Bayesian Deep Learning for Efficient and Risk-sensitive Learning

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Stefan Depeweg, José Miguel Hernández-Lobato, Finale Doshi‐Velez, Steffen Udluft arXiv (Cornell University) 2017-10-19 10.48550/arxiv.1710.07283
单位: Technical University of Munich, Munich, Germany
通讯: Stefan Depeweg @ Technical University of Munich, Munich, Germany
相关性: 该论文提出了一种将贝叶斯深度学习中的不确定性分解为认知和偶然成分的方法，可用于主动学习和风险敏感决策，对代理模型中的不确定性量化具有参考价值。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, implant biomechanics, or constrained optimization; it focuses on general Bayesian deep learning and reinforcement learning.
动机· 贝叶斯神经网络中不确定性分解对决策至关重要。
方法· 提出将不确定性分解为认知和偶然成分的方法。
结果· 成功识别异方差和双峰噪声中的信息点，并定义风险敏感强化学习准则。
验证· 在函数主动学习和强化学习基准上验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Decomposing uncertainty into epistemic and aleatoric components is crucial for informed decision-making in Bayesian neural networks.
Method· A method is proposed to extract and decompose uncertainty from Bayesian neural networks with latent variables.
Result· The decomposition enables effective active learning on functions with heteroscedastic and bimodal noise and defines a risk-sensitive criterion for reinforcement learning.
Validation· Validation is performed on synthetic function regression and reinforcement learning benchmarks.

通讯: Georg A. Mensah @ Institut für Strömungsmechanik und Technische Akustik, Technische Universität Berlin, Berlin 10623, Germany e-mail:
相关性: 该论文研究热声不稳定性中的不确定性量化，与生物力学植入物设计中的有限元代理建模无直接关联。
边界: No implant biomechanics, no FEA surrogate for structural mechanics, no contact mechanics or stress analysis.
动机· 热声不稳定性的参数不确定性影响系统稳定性，需高效量化。

Result· The method accurately predicts stability boundaries on a ducted flame and a laboratory combustor with reduced computational cost.
Validation· Validated against experimental data using network models and the open-source Helmholtz solver PyHoltz.
