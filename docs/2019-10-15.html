Multiphoton 3D Printing of Biopolymer-Based Hydrogels

Kostas Parkatzidis, Μaria Chatzinikolaidou, M. Kaliva, Athina Bakopoulou, Maria Farsari et al. ACS Biomaterials Science & Engineering 2019-10-15 10.1021/acsbiomaterials.9b01300
单位: Institute of Electronic Structure and Laser, Foundation for Research and Technology—Hellas, 70013 Heraklion, Crete, Greece
通讯: Μaria Chatzinikolaidou @ Institute of Electronic Structure and Laser, Foundation for Research and Technology—Hellas, 70013 Heraklion, Crete, Gree  |  Maria Vamvakaki @ Institute of Electronic Structure and Laser, Foundation for Research and Technology—Hellas, 70013 Heraklion, Crete, Gree
相关性: 该论文关于多光子3D打印生物聚合物水凝胶，但完全没有涉及AI或机器学习。
边界: This paper does not use AI/ML in any stage of the bioprinting workflow, nor does it discuss autonomous decision-making or experimental validation of AI models.
动机· 开发适用于3D打印的生物相容性水凝胶材料。
方法· 利用多光子聚合技术，以曙红Y为光引发剂。
结果· 杂化材料表现出优良生物相容性，促进干细胞增殖和矿化。
验证· 通过牙髓干细胞培养和成骨分化实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop biocompatible hydrogels suitable for multiphoton 3D printing without toxic co-initiators.
Method· Multiphoton lithography using eosin Y as the sole photoinitiator to fabricate hybrid hydrogels from gelatin methacrylamide and chitosan derivative.
Result· The hybrid material exhibits enhanced biocompatibility and promotes matrix mineralization after BMP-2 functionalization.
Validation· In vitro cell culture with dental pulp stem cells assessed proliferation and mineralization over 7 days.

Hybrid 3D Printing of Synthetic and Cell‐Laden Bioinks for Shape Retaining Soft Tissue Grafts

Sarah Van Belleghem, Leopoldo Torres, Marco Santoro, Bhushan Mahadik, Arley Wolfand et al. Advanced Functional Materials 2019-10-15 10.1002/adfm.201907145
单位: Fischell Department of Bioengineering Center for Engineering Complex Tissues University of Maryland College Park MD 20742 USA
通讯: John P. Fisher @ Fischell Department of Bioengineering Center for Engineering Complex Tissues University of Maryland College Park MD 207
相关性: 该论文虽涉及生物打印，但未集成人工智能方法。
边界: This paper focuses on dual bioink printing for soft tissue grafts without any AI/ML integration in the printing workflow.
动机· 软组织修复中形状保持困难，需要更好的移植方法。
方法· 采用合成的PEG/海藻酸双网络墨水和细胞负载明胶甲基丙烯酸酯生物墨水进行混合3D打印。
结果· 打印的复合支架具有良好的力学性能和形状保持能力。
验证· 通过力学测试、细胞毒性和CloudCompare形状分析验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Soft tissue repair suffers from poor shape retention and aesthetic outcomes, motivating the development of stable graft constructs.
Method· A dual bioink approach combining synthetic PEG/alginate and cell-laden gelatin methacrylate is used in hybrid 3D printing.
Result· The composite scaffolds show enhanced mechanical properties and long-term shape retention.
Validation· Validation includes mechanical testing, cytotoxicity assays, and shape analysis using CloudCompare software.

3D bioprinting skin

Nasheen Naidoo, W G Kleintjes, E P Kotzee, A Mukendwa, E Blecha et al. South African Journal of Plastic & Reconstructive Aesthetic Surgery & Burns 2019-10-15 10.7196/sajprasb.2019.v2i2.20
单位: Department of Surgery, Faculty of Medicine and Health Sciences, Stellenbosch University, Cape Town, South Africa
通讯: Nasheen Naidoo @ Department of Surgery, Faculty of Medicine and Health Sciences, Stellenbosch University, Cape Town, South Africa
相关性: 此论文涉及3D生物打印皮肤用于烧伤治疗，但未包含任何人工智能相关方法或工作流。
边界: No AI/ML integration into bioprinting workflow; no model training or process control; purely clinical case report.
动机· 利用干细胞和血小板血浆3D生物打印皮肤，解决大面积烧伤供皮不足问题。
方法· 将脂肪干细胞、富血小板血浆与细胞播种结合，在疏水基质敷料上3D打印皮肤。
结果· 2周后移植物存活率20-60%，结果可接受但需改善。
验证· 单个临床病例验证，无对照组。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To address the shortage of donor skin for a pediatric burn patient with 65% TBSA burns using 3D bioprinted skin.
Method· Adipose-derived stem cells combined with platelet-rich plasma and cell seeding were printed onto a hydrophobic matrix dressing.
Result· Graft take at two weeks ranged from 20% to 60%, showing feasibility but requiring further improvement.
Validation· Clinical validation via a single patient case with no control group.

In vitro and in vivo studies of Mg-30Sc alloys with different phase structure for potential usage within bone.

Jianing Liu, Yulin Lin, Dong Bian, Ming Wang, Zefeng Lin et al. Acta biomaterialia 2019-10-15 10.1016/j.actbio.2019.03.009
单位: Academy for Advanced Interdisciplinary Studies, Peking University, Beijing 100871, China.
通讯: Yinong Liu @ School of Mechanical Engineering, The University of Western Australia, Crawley, WA 6009, Australia. Electronic address:  |  Zhigang Xu @ ERC for Revolutionizing Metallic Biomaterials, North Carolina A&T State University, Greensboro, NC 27411, USA. Electroni  |  Yu Zhang @ Department of Orthopedics, Guangdong General Hospital, Guangdong Academy of Medical Sciences, Guangzhou 510080, China. E  |  Yufeng Zheng @ Academy for Advanced Interdisciplinary Studies, Peking University, Beijing 100871, China; Department of Materials Scienc
相关性: 该论文研究了镁钪合金的相结构对骨植入性能的影响，但未涉及增材制造（LPBF）或多孔结构设计，与用户的研究方向无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not involve additive manufacturing, porous structure design for stress shielding, or fatigue testing; it uses conventional casting and focuses on alloy composition and degradation behavior.
动机· 开发可降解镁合金用于骨科植入物，改善力学性能和耐腐蚀性。
方法· 通过热处理调控Mg-30Sc合金的相结构（α、β、α+β），并进行体外降解、细胞毒性和体内植入实验。
结果· β相Mg-30Sc合金具有最佳力学性能（抗压强度603 MPa）和体内降解速率（0.06 mm/y），骨整合良好。
验证· 通过体外降解测试、细胞毒性实验和24周大鼠股骨植入实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop biodegradable Mg-30Sc alloys with tailored phase structures for orthopedic implant applications.
Method· Heat treatment was used to produce single α, single β, and dual-phase microstructures, followed by mechanical, degradation, and biological evaluations.
Result· The β-phase alloy exhibited the best compressive strength (603 MPa) and acceptable in vivo degradation rate (0.06 mm/y) with good osseointegration.
Validation· In vitro degradation, cytotoxicity assays, and 24-week rat femur implantation.

Total Hip Arthroplasty No Better Than Partial Joint Replacement

Katie Robinson 2019-10-15 link
相关性: 该论文仅比较全髋与半髋关节置换的临床结果，未涉及任何应力遮挡、微动或有限元分析，与研究方向无关。
边界: This paper is a clinical outcome comparison study without biomechanical modeling, implant stress analysis, or any FEA/contact mechanics relevant to hip implant design validation.
动机· 比较全髋与半髋关节置换的临床效果。
方法· 临床试验随访24个月。
结果· 全髋置换未显著改善功能或生活质量。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare long-term functional outcomes and quality of life between total hip arthroplasty and hemiarthroplasty.
Method· A clinical study with 24-month follow-up comparing the two surgical procedures.
Result· Total hip arthroplasty did not provide clinically significant improvement over hemiarthroplasty in function or quality of life.
Validation· Prospective clinical trial with patient-reported outcomes.
