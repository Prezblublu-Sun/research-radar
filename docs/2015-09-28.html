通讯: Andre Riemer @ University of Paderborn
相关性: 该论文涉及SLM制造的钛合金和不锈钢的疲劳裂纹扩展，但未涉及多孔结构或应力屏蔽，与用户方向部分相关但不核心。

动机· 分析SLM制造钛合金和不锈钢的疲劳裂纹扩展行为

Result· Residual stresses strongly influence crack growth in Ti-6Al-4V but not in 316L, and heat treatments significantly affect residual lifetime.
Validation· Experimental fatigue crack growth tests and numerical simulations.

单位: Lille Laboratory of Electrical Engineering and Power Electronics, Arts et Métiers ParisTech, Lille, France
通讯: S. Clénet @ Lille Laboratory of Electrical Engineering and Power Electronics, Arts et Métiers ParisTech, Lille, France
相关性: 该论文提出的自适应POD降阶方法可显著减少参数化有限元模型的计算时间，并具备不确定性量化能力，对生物力学植入物设计中的高效仿真有借鉴意义。
边界: The paper focuses on magnetostatic problems rather than biomedical implant mechanics or structural FEA, and does not address contact mechanics or stress shielding.
动机· 减少参数化有限元模型的计算时间，避免维度灾难。
方法· 提出自适应POD方法，在离线/在线框架下动态构建降阶模型。
结果· 降阶模型复杂度与输入参数数量无关，有效缓解维度灾难。

Motivation· To reduce the computational cost of parametric finite element models while avoiding the curse of dimensionality.
Method· An adaptive proper orthogonal decomposition (POD) method is proposed to construct the reduced model on the fly, limiting full model evaluations.
Result· The complexity of the reduced model becomes independent of the number of input parameters, effectively mitigating the curse of dimensionality.
Validation· The method is validated on a stochastic magnetostatic example with 14 uncertain parameters using Monte Carlo simulation.
