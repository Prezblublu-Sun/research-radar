BioLite U-Net: Edge-Deployable Semantic Segmentation for In Situ Bioprinting Monitoring

High AI for Bioprinting arxiv Direct Read now in_vitro Exp. validation
Usman Haider, Lukasz Szemet, Daniel Kelly, Vasileios Sergis, Andrew C. Daly et al. arXiv preprint 2025-09-08 link
相关性: 该论文直接将AI嵌入生物打印的实时监控阶段，并进行了实验验证，与您的研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper focuses on in situ monitoring via semantic segmentation but does not address closed-loop control or autonomous decision-making based on the segmentation output.
动机· 实现生物打印过程的实时监控，确保打印质量和细胞活性。
方法· 提出轻量级BioLite U-Net架构，使用深度可分离卷积在边缘设备上实现语义分割。
结果· 模型在Raspberry Pi上达到92.85% mIoU，每帧推理335毫秒，接近实时。
验证· 使用自建787张RGB图像数据集进行训练和评估，并在Raspberry Pi上测试部署可行性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To enable real-time monitoring of bioprinting processes for ensuring print fidelity and cell viability under resource-constrained hardware.
Method· A lightweight BioLite U-Net architecture employing depthwise separable convolutions is proposed for semantic segmentation of nozzle, bioink, and background in bioprinting images.
Result· BioLite U-Net achieves 92.85% mIoU and 96.17% Dice score, with 335 ms inference per frame on a Raspberry Pi 4B, demonstrating near real-time performance.
Validation· The model is validated on a novel manually annotated dataset of 787 RGB bioprinting images and benchmarked against MobileNet-based baselines on edge hardware.
