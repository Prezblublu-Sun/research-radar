英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the safety of overlapping surgery in total knee and hip arthroplasty by comparing complication rates.
Method· Retrospective review of 2,833 primary total knee and hip arthroplasties comparing 90-day outcomes between overlapping and non-overlapping procedures.
Result· No significant differences in complications, readmissions, or reoperations were found, with only a 5.8-minute increase in operating room time.
Validation· Single-institution retrospective cohort study with 2,833 procedures.
