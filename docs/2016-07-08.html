Genymphas B. Higgs, Daniel W. MacDonald, Jeremy L. Gilbert, Clare M. Rimnac, Steven M. Kurtz et al. The Journal of Arthroplasty 2016-07-08 10.1016/j.arth.2016.06.053
单位: School of Biomedical Engineering, Science and Health Systems, Drexel University, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania
通讯: Genymphas B. Higgs @ School of Biomedical Engineering, Science and Health Systems, Drexel University, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania
相关性: 该论文研究髋关节假体锥部磨损腐蚀，未涉及股骨近端应力遮挡或微动分析。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics; it focuses on taper fretting corrosion in retrieved components.
动机· 探究锥部尺寸对全髋关节置换假体锥部磨损腐蚀的影响。
方法· 对252例钴铬股骨头进行半定量损伤评分，并用有序逻辑回归分析影响因素。
结果· 锥部尺寸对磨损腐蚀无显著影响，而偏移、植入时间、体重和锥部柔度是主要因素。
验证· 基于临床回收假体的多变量统计分析和匹配队列比较。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine whether taper size influences fretting corrosion damage at modular head-neck junctions in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Semiquantitative damage scoring and ordinal logistic regression were applied to 252 retrieved cobalt-chromium femoral heads with two taper sizes.
Result· Taper size did not significantly affect fretting corrosion damage; head offset, implantation time, patient weight, and taper flexibility were significant predictors.
Validation· Multivariable analysis and balanced cohort comparison on clinical retrievals.
