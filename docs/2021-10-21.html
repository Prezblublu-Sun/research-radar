Statistical Finite Elements via Langevin Dynamics

High FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Ömer Deniz Akyildiz, Connor Duffin, Sotirios Sabanis, Mark Girolami arXiv preprint 2021-10-21 link
相关性: 该论文提出了一种基于Langevin动力学的统计有限元方法，能够高效量化有限元模型中的不确定性，可迁移至植入物/生物力学设计中的不确定性量化问题。
边界: This paper does not address implant or biomechanics design, nor does it handle constraints or multi-objective optimization; it focuses on uncertainty quantification in general statFEM forward problems.
动机· 解决统计有限元方法中前向问题的高维积分计算困难

验证· 通过数值基准实验验证采样器性能
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To efficiently solve the intractable forward problem in statistical finite element method (statFEM) that requires integrating over model parameter uncertainty.
Method· The unadjusted Langevin algorithm (ULA) is employed to sample from the posterior distribution, requiring only sparse matrix-vector products instead of full PDE solves.
Result· Theoretical convergence guarantees in Kullback-Leibler divergence and Wasserstein-2 distance are provided, and numerical experiments demonstrate the sampler's efficacy.
Validation· Numerical benchmark experiments on both prior and posterior distributions validate the performance of the proposed sampler.
