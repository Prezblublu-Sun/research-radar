Improved accuracy and precision of bioprinting through progressive cavity pump-controlled extrusion.

Philipp Fisch, Martin Holub, Marcy Zenobi-Wong Biofabrication 2020-12-17 10.1088/1758-5090/abc39b
单位: Department of Health Science and Technology, ETH Zurich, Zurich, Switzerland.
通讯: Marcy Zenobi-Wong @ Department of Health Science and Technology, ETH Zurich, Zurich, Switzerland. [推断]
相关性: 该论文讨论的是生物打印中的挤出过程控制，完全不涉及人工智能。
边界: This paper does not embed AI/ML into any bioprinting workflow stage, and focuses solely on mechanical extrusion control without machine learning or autonomous decision-making.
动机· 微挤出生物打印依赖气动挤压，生物墨水不均匀会导致打印缺陷。
方法· 采用微型渐进腔泵（PCP）的精确挤出技术，与气动系统比较。
结果· PCP在精度和准确性上显著优于气动系统，且细胞存活率良好。
验证· 通过打印精度测试和细胞活性实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Micro-extrusion bioprinting suffers from flow inconsistency due to bioink inhomogeneity when using pneumatic extrusion.
Method· A miniaturized progressive cavity pump (PCP) was evaluated as an alternative extrusion method providing positive displacement for precise volumetric flow control.
Result· The PCP system achieved significantly higher accuracy and precision than pneumatic extrusion while maintaining high cell viability.
Validation· Validation included quantitative comparison of printing accuracy and precision, as well as cell viability assays post-extrusion.

通讯: Ying Mei @ Department of Bioengineering, Clemson University, Clemson, South Carolina 29634-0002, United States

Result· Compartmentalized vascularized tissue constructs were fabricated, showing pericyte-endothelial cell colocalization and angiogenic sprouting with extracellular matrix deposition.
Validation· Validation was performed through in vitro cell culture, imaging, and histological analysis to confirm vascular morphogenesis and network formation.
