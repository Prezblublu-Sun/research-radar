通讯: Dan Leordean @ Department of Manufacturing Engineering, Technical University of Cluj-Napoca, Cluj-Napoca, Romania

Motivation· To fabricate customized implants with region-specific properties by varying SLM process parameters.

Result· Open porosity ranged from 24.8% to 10.83%, Young's modulus from 13.5 to 104.5 GPa, and ultimate tensile stress from 20.2 to 497.5 MPa.
Validation· Mechanical testing (tensile tests) and porosity measurements were performed on fabricated samples.

单位: Texas A&amp;M University, Department of Nuclear Engineering 3133 TAMU, College Station, Texas 77843
通讯: Ryan Kelly @ Texas A&amp;M University, Department of Nuclear Engineering 3133 TAMU, College Station, Texas 77843
相关性: 该论文涉及核废料干式储存罐的混凝土不确定性量化，与生物力学植入物设计或FEA代理建模无直接关联。
边界: No implant biomechanics, no FEA surrogate modeling, no contact mechanics or stress analysis; purely nuclear shielding uncertainty quantification.
动机· 量化核废料干式储存罐中混凝土成分和密度变化对辐射剂量率的不确定性。

结果· 混凝土成分和密度变化是剂量率不确定性的主要来源，中子剂量率变化可达70%以上。
验证· 通过对比不同混凝土成分和密度组合的模拟结果进行验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify the uncertainty in radiation dose rate estimates outside a used nuclear fuel dry cask storage unit due to parametric variability of concrete compositions and densities.
Method· A reference dry cask model was developed for MCNP calculations, using selected concrete compositions from a standardized database and geometry details from the Holtec HI-STORM 100S system.
Result· Concrete composition and density variations are major sources of uncertainty, with neutron dose rate changes up to 70% due to composition alone.
Validation· Validation was performed by comparing multiple simulation cases with different concrete compositions, densities, and decay sources.
