Customized a Ti6Al4V Bone Plate for Complex Pelvic Fracture by Selective Laser Melting

Di Wang, Yimeng Wang, Shibiao Wu, Hui Lin, Yongqiang Yang et al. Materials 2017-01-04 10.3390/ma10010035
单位: School of Mechanical and Automotive Engineering, South China University of Technology, Guangzhou 510640, China
通讯: Yongqiang Yang @ School of Mechanical and Automotive Engineering, South China University of Technology, Guangzhou 510640, China
相关性: 本文涉及通过SLM制造定制化骨盆接骨板，但未研究多孔结构、应力遮挡、疲劳性能或骨整合特性，与用户研究方向不直接相关。
边界: No porous structure for stress shielding mitigation, no fatigue testing, no surface roughness or osseointegration characterization.
动机· 解决骨盆骨折术中接骨板塑形困难、手术时间长的问题。
方法· 利用SLM技术设计并制造定制化Ti6Al4V接骨板，优化支撑结构及热处理工艺。
结果· 接骨板力学性能满足要求，临床应用中匹配良好，手术时间缩短至约2小时。
验证· 通过力学测试和临床手术验证效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To address the challenge of bone plate shaping and long operation time in pelvic fracture surgery.
Method· A customized Ti6Al4V bone plate was designed and fabricated using selective laser melting with optimized support structures and vacuum heat treatment.
Result· The bone plate achieved adequate mechanical properties, good fit in clinical application, and reduced operation time to about two hours.
Validation· Validation included mechanical testing and clinical application with postoperative imaging.

单位: Singapore Centre for 3D Printing, School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore 639798, Singapore
通讯: Ratima Suntornnond @ Singapore Centre for 3D Printing, School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Nanyang Technological University, Sing
相关性: 该论文仅综述支撑材料在生物打印中的作用，不含任何人工智能相关内容。
边界: This paper is a review of support materials in bioprinting and does not involve AI/ML integration into any bioprinting workflow stage.
动机· 综述支撑材料在3D生物打印中的角色和未来展望

English summary & terms
Motivation· This review provides a perspective on the roles and future outlook of support materials in 3D bioprinting to enhance shape fidelity and complexity.
Method· A literature survey approach is used to discuss the combination of multiple hydrogels and sacrificial materials in multi-material bioprinting.
Result· Support materials are identified as key enablers for transitioning from 2.5D lattices to micro-channeled 3D structures.
Validation· No experimental validation is presented; the paper is a review.

单位: Erich Schmid Institute of Materials Science, Austrian Academy of Sciences, Jahnstraße 12, A-8700, Leoben, Austria

相关性: 该论文研究Al-50Si合金的选区激光熔化微观结构与热膨胀行为，与生物医学植入物方向无关。
边界: No biomedical implant context, no stress shielding or stiffness matching analysis, no fatigue or osseointegration evaluation.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the microstructure and thermal expansion behavior of Al-50Si alloy fabricated by selective laser melting.
Method· Al-50Si samples were prepared via SLM and characterized using XRD, SEM, and dilatometry.
Result· The SLM process refined primary silicon and reduced the coefficient of thermal expansion.
Validation· Experimental validation through thermal expansion testing and microstructural analysis.

Preoperative education prior to elective knee arthroplasty surgery does not change patient outcomes

Lawrence Stephen Moulton, Peter A. Evans, Ian Starks, Tony Smith Musculoskeletal Care 2017-01-04 10.1002/msc.1177
单位: Department of Orthopaedics Wrexham Maelor Hospital Wrexham UK
通讯: Lawrence Stephen Moulton @ Department of Orthopaedics Wrexham Maelor Hospital Wrexham UK
相关性: 该论文研究膝关节置换术前教育对临床结果的影响，与髋关节假体力学分析无关。
边界: This paper does not quantify femoral stem stress shielding, micromotion, or any biomechanical modeling; it is a clinical study on preoperative education for knee arthroplasty.
动机· 评估术前教育对膝关节置换术后结果的影响
方法· 回顾性分析563例患者，比较参加与未参加术前教育组的结局
结果· 参加组住院时间略短但无统计学差异，结局评分无显著改善
验证· 使用非参数检验进行统计分析
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate whether preoperative education improves outcomes after total knee arthroplasty within an enhanced recovery programme.
Method· A retrospective analysis of 563 patients compared length of stay and outcome scores between those who attended a preoperative class and those who did not.
Result· No statistically significant difference in length of stay or outcome scores was found between groups.
Validation· Non-parametric statistical tests were used to compare groups.
