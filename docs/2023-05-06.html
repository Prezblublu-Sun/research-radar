Mechanical and corrosion behavior of sheet-based 316L TPMS structures

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Transferable Save for project mechanical_testing Exp. validation

单位: Mechanical Engineering, University of Texas at Dallas, Richardson, TX, USA
通讯: Golden Kumar @ Mechanical Engineering, University of Texas at Dallas, Richardson, TX, USA
相关性: 该论文研究了316L不锈钢TPMS结构的力学与腐蚀行为，可为生物医用多孔植入物的刚度匹配和耐腐蚀设计提供参考，但非直接应用于髋关节应力屏蔽。
边界: This paper uses 316L instead of biomedical Ti alloys, does not explicitly link to stress shielding or hip implant context, and likely includes only static mechanical testing without fatigue or osseointegration assessment.
动机· 研究片状316L不锈钢TPMS结构的力学与腐蚀性能，探索其在承重应用中的潜力。

验证· 通过实验测试验证了不同TPMS构型的力学和腐蚀性能。

Method· LPBF was used to fabricate sheet-based TPMS structures, which were then subjected to quasi-static compression tests and electrochemical corrosion measurements.
Result· The mechanical performance of TPMS structures correlates with relative density, while corrosion behavior is influenced by design type and manufacturing defects.
Validation· Experimental testing validated the mechanical and corrosion performance of various TPMS configurations.
