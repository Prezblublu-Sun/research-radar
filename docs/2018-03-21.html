Mechanical Properties and In Vitro Behavior of Additively Manufactured and Functionally Graded Ti6Al4V Porous Scaffolds

单位: Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Monash University, Clayton, VIC 3800, Australia
通讯: Ezgi Onal @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Monash University, Clayton, VIC 3800, Australia  |  Andrey Molotnikov @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Monash University, Clayton, VIC 3800, Australia
相关性: 该论文直接研究了LPBF制造的功能梯度多孔Ti6Al4V支架的力学性能与体外生物学行为，与您的AM生物医学方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not report fatigue properties, surface roughness characterization, or quantitative osseointegration analysis, focusing instead on static mechanical testing and in vitro cell studies.
动机· 探究功能梯度多孔支架力学性能与生物学响应之间的关系以优化骨科植入物设计
方法· 设计连续梯度BCC点阵结构，通过SLM制造并进行力学测试和体外细胞实验
结果· 最优梯度结构核心小孔（~900 µm）提高强度，外层大孔（~1100 µm）促进细胞渗透和增殖

Method· Continuous gradient structures were designed by varying strut diameter of BCC unit cells, fabricated via selective laser melting (SLM), and subjected to mechanical compression testing and in vitro cell assays.
Result· Optimal gradient scaffolds with small pores (~900 µm) in the core enhanced mechanical strength, while larger pores (~1100 µm) on the outer surface improved cell penetration and proliferation.
Validation· Experimental validation combined mechanical testing (static compression) and in vitro biological assays using cell seeding and proliferation measurements.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the association between provider and hospital volumes and postoperative mortality following total hip arthroplasty in a Canadian province.
Method· A population-based cohort study using hospital discharge abstracts linked to vital statistics, with logistic regression models adjusting for patient and contextual characteristics.
Result· No significant association was found between provider volume or hospital size and 30- or 90-day postoperative mortality.
Validation· Statistical analysis of provincial administrative data.
