单位: Department of Traumatology and Orthopedics, Yijishan Hospital, Wannan Medical College, Wuhu, 241001, Anhui, People's Republic of China.
通讯: Min Yang @ Department of Traumatology and Orthopedics, Yijishan Hospital, Wannan Medical College, Wuhu, 241001, Anhui, People's Rep

English summary & terms
