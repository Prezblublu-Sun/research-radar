边界: This paper focuses on satellite system design, not biomedical implant mechanics or FEA surrogate modeling for biomechanics.
动机· 高维不确定性下小卫星多学科优化效率低，需新方法。
方法· 基于主动子空间降维与多目标联盟算法进行不确定性传播与优化。
结果· 将23维不确定性降至1维，获得分布均匀的Pareto前沿。

Method· A novel multidisciplinary optimization methodology integrates active subspace identification for dimension reduction and a multiobjective alliance algorithm for constrained optimization.
Result· The approach reduces 23-dimensional uncertainty to one dimension and yields well-distributed Pareto-optimal solutions.
Validation· The methodology is validated on a standard speed reducer test problem and an Earth observation satellite case.
