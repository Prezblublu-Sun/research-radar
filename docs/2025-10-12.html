Digital Twin-enabled Multi-generation Control Co-Design with Deep Reinforcement Learning

Ying-Kuan Tsai, Vispi Karkaria, Yi-Ping Chen, Wei Chen arXiv preprint 2025-10-12 link
相关性: 该论文的DT和DRL方法可为生物打印的数字孪生框架提供参考，但未涉及生物打印或生物医学应用。
边界: The paper does not address bioprinting, lacks AI integration into any bioprinting workflow stage, and has no biomedical context or experimental validation in bioprinting.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To address unpredictable uncertainties in real-world systems, the authors propose a multi-generation design paradigm that considers the full lifecycle of a product.
Method· A digital twin-enabled control co-design framework integrates deep reinforcement learning to jointly optimize physical design and controller through continuous model updating and adaptive decision-making.
Result· Demonstrated on an active suspension system, the method yields smoother and more stable control trajectories with enhanced robustness and efficiency.
Validation· Validation is performed through a simulation case study of an active suspension system using road condition and driving behavior data.

Topology and Martensitic Phase Transformations

M. Yin, D. D. Vvedensky arXiv preprint 2025-10-12 link
相关性: 该论文仅涉及晶体拓扑和马氏体相变，未涉及生物医学植入物或增材制造。
边界: This paper does not address LPBF fabrication, mechanical testing, stress shielding, or osseointegration; it is a theoretical study on crystal topology and phase transformations.
动机· 探索晶体中三周期极小曲面（TPMS）与马氏体相变拓扑连续性的关系
方法· 基于电荷密度分布与TPMS的对应关系，分析马氏体相变前后TPMS的拓扑等价性
结果· 发现非磁性晶体中马氏体相变可通过TPMS拓扑连续性进行判断
验证· 基于前期理论模型的推演，无实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the topological continuity of martensitic phase transformations using triply periodic minimal surfaces (TPMS) derived from charge density distributions in crystals.
Method· The study builds on prior work linking TPMS to constant charge density surfaces and analyzes topological equivalence of TPMS before and after martensitic transformations.
Result· It explains that topological continuity of TPMS indicates whether a non-magnetic crystal can undergo a martensitic phase transformation.
Validation· The work is purely theoretical with no experimental or numerical validation.
