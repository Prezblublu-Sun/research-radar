Characterization and Machine Learning-Driven Property Prediction of a Novel Hybrid Hydrogel Bioink Considering Extrusion-Based 3D Bioprinting.

High AI for Bioprinting pubmed Direct Read now mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Rokeya Sarah, Kory Schimmelpfennig, Riley Rohauer, Christopher L Lewis, Shah M Limon et al. Gels (Basel, Switzerland) 2025-01-07 10.3390/gels11010045
单位: Sustainable Product Design and Architecture, Keene State College, Keene, NH 03431, USA.
通讯: Ahasan Habib @ Manufacturing and Mechanical Engineering Technology, Rochester Institute of Technology, Rochester, NY 14623, USA. [推断]
相关性: 该论文将机器学习应用于生物墨水配方优化，直接支持AI驱动的生物打印工作流中的预处理阶段。
边界: No experimental bioprinting validation (only rheological measurements), no autonomous decision-making, no post-printing analysis.
动机· 现有生物墨水粘度预测忽略剪切速率影响，需高效优化配方。
方法· 采用多项式拟合、决策树和随机森林算法，基于组分重量和剪切速率预测粘度。
结果· 随机森林模型表现最佳（R²=0.99，MAE=0.09），可加速生物墨水开发。
验证· 使用169组流变测量数据，80%训练20%验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Existing bioink viscosity predictions ignore shear rate, necessitating efficient formulation optimization.
Method· Machine learning models (polynomial fit, decision tree, random forest) predict viscosity from component weights and shear rate.
Result· Random forest achieved the best performance (R²=0.99, MAE=0.09), enabling rapid bioink formulation.
Validation· Validated on 169 rheological measurements with 80/20 train-validation split.

Result· The method yields improved EEG source localization accuracy and provides feasible estimates of the unknown skull conductivity.
Validation· Validation is performed on numerical simulation benchmarks.
