Umar Hossain, Shaaz Ghouse, Kenneth Nai, Jonathan R.T. Jeffers Additive manufacturing 2021-05-23 10.1016/j.addma.2021.102050
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Imperial College London, South Kensington, London SW7 2AZ, UK
通讯: Jonathan R.T. Jeffers @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Imperial College London, South Kensington, London SW7 2AZ, UK
相关性: 该论文研究了LPBF制造微支柱的力学和形态特性，与用户关注的AM生物医学植入物相关，但未直接涉及应力遮挡或多孔结构匹配。
边界: This paper does not link porous structure to stress shielding or stiffness matching, does not test fatigue, and does not report osseointegration or surface roughness in a biomedical context.
动机· 表征LPBF微支柱力学和形态随构建角度的变化

结果· 弹性模量低于块体材料，Ti6Al4V屈服强度随角度变化显著，低角度支柱形态更差

英文摘要与术语

Method· Uniaxial tensile tests and micro-CT analysis were performed on SS316L and Ti6Al4V struts fabricated at four build angles and three diameters.
Result· Elastic moduli were lower than bulk values; Ti6Al4V yield strength varied from 40% to 98% of bulk depending on angle; lower-angle struts exhibited higher roughness and less circular cross-sections.
Validation· Experimental validation via tensile testing and micro-CT imaging.
