Sebastian Marian Zaharia, Camil Lancea, Lucia Antoneta Chicoș, Mihai Alin Pop, Giampaolo Caputo et al. Transactions of FAMENA 2018-02-12 10.21278/tof.41402
单位: Department of Manufacturing Engineering, Transilvania University of Brasov, Brasov, Romania
通讯: Sebastian Marian Zaharia @ Department of Manufacturing Engineering, Transilvania University of Brasov, Brasov, Romania
相关性: 涉及SLM制造的蜂窝结构，但未直接关联骨科植入物的应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: No explicit link to stress shielding or stiffness matching for orthopedic implants, no fatigue testing, and no osseointegration or surface roughness characterization.
动机· 为蜂窝夹芯结构提供力学和腐蚀性能数据，拓展SLM在航空航天和医疗的应用。
方法· 使用SLM制造316L不锈钢蜂窝芯，进行平压、侧压和盐雾腐蚀测试。
结果· 蜂窝芯的显微硬度225±15 HV0.3，平压和侧压强度及弹性模量被测定，腐蚀寿命可评估。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To characterize mechanical and corrosion properties of SLM-fabricated honeycomb cellular cores for potential applications in aerospace, automotive, and medical implants.
Method· Honeycomb cellular cores made of 316L stainless steel were manufactured using selective laser melting and subjected to microhardness, flatwise and edgewise compression, and accelerated salt spray corrosion tests.
Result· The honeycomb cores exhibited a microhardness of 225±15 HV0.3, determined compressive strengths and elastic moduli, and displayed corrosion resistance under simulated marine conditions.
Validation· Experimental testing including compression and salt spray corrosion was performed to validate mechanical and corrosion performance.
