Motivation· To develop a cost-effective and biomimetic extracellular matrix scaffold for cardiac tissue regeneration.
Method· Composite hydrogels were fabricated from quaternized β-chitin and fibrin-matrigel, characterized mechanically, and used in 3D bioprinting of human engineered heart tissue.

Motivation· To compare failure rates of cemented versus press-fit femoral stems in oncologic reconstruction after tumor resection.
Method· Retrospective review of 252 patients who underwent femoral tumor resection and subsequent arthroplasty with either cemented (n=173) or press-fit (n=79) stems from 1999 to 2020.
Result· Cemented stems had an 11% implant failure rate versus 18% for press-fit stems; infection rates were 14% and 10%, respectively.
