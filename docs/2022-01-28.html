A dimensional assessment of small features and lattice structures manufactured by laser powder bed fusion

Carlos López‐García, Erika García‐López, Héctor R. Siller, Jesús A. Sandoval-Robles, Ciro A. Rodrı́guez Progress in Additive Manufacturing 2022-01-28 10.1007/s40964-022-00263-0
单位: Laboratorio Nacional de Manufactura Aditiva y Digital MADiT, Apodaca, Nuevo León, Mexico, 66628
通讯: Carlos López‐García @ Laboratorio Nacional de Manufactura Aditiva y Digital MADiT, Apodaca, Nuevo León, Mexico, 66628
相关性: 该论文研究了LPBF制造小特征和点阵结构的尺寸精度，但未涉及应力遮挡、疲劳性能或生物医学植入物背景。
边界: This paper focuses on dimensional accuracy of LPBF lattice structures but does not include mechanical testing (static or fatigue), stress shielding analysis, or biomedical implant context.
动机· 评估LPBF制造小特征和点阵结构的尺寸精度。
方法· 制造直壁和点阵结构，使用多种计量技术测量尺寸误差。
结果· 细支柱（250μm）误差较大（5-25%），粗支柱（500μm）误差较小（2-4%）。
验证· 通过焦点变化显微镜、光学显微镜和微CT进行尺寸测量验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify dimensional variations in small features and lattice structures produced by laser powder bed fusion.
Method· Fabricated straight walls and lattice structures (cubic and hexagonal) with varying strut sizes and building orientations, then measured dimensional errors using focus variation microscopy, optical microscopy, and micro-CT.
Result· Fine struts (250 μm) exhibited high relative errors (5–25%), while coarse struts (500 μm) achieved lower errors (2–4%); building orientation had no significant effect on walls below 750 μm.
Validation· Dimensional measurements were validated using three complementary metrology techniques (focus variation, optical microscopy, micro-CT).
