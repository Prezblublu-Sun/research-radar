英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To identify which procedures in hip arthroscopy are predictors of revision surgery or conversion to total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Retrospective review of 1118 patients with multivariate Cox regression analysis.
Result· Age, BMI, labral debridement, and notchplasty increased arthroplasty risk; workers' compensation, capsular repair, and femoral head microfracture increased revision risk.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with minimum 2-year data (mean 50.2 months).

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To assess clinical and functional outcomes of total hip replacement in a prospective cohort.
Method· A three-year prospective study of 58 patients using modified Harris hip score pre- and postoperatively.
Result· Significant improvement in pain, gait, functional activity, and range of motion; anterior thigh pain was the most common complaint.
Validation· Clinical follow-up without biomechanical or computational validation.
