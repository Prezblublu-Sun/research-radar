通讯: Fei‐Yi Hung @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering, National Cheng Kung University, Tainan 701, Taiwan
相关性: 该论文研究了粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V合金的颗粒侵蚀诱导相变，未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper does not address porous structure design, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or osseointegration; it focuses on erosion-induced phase transformation in solid Ti-6Al-4V flakes.

方法· 对PBF态和热处理态试样进行Al2O3颗粒侵蚀实验，分析相变和硬度变化。
结果· 侵蚀诱导α相形成，导致表面软化区、硬化区和稳定区，PBF态强度降低，热处理态强度降低但延性增加。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the particle erosion-induced phase transformation behavior and its effect on mechanical properties of powder bed fusion Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· Erosion experiments with Al2O3 particles at 90° impact angle were conducted on as-PBF and heat-treated Ti-6Al-4V specimens, followed by microstructural and mechanical characterization.
Result· Erosion induced α phase formation, creating a softened surface zone, a hardened zone, and a stable zone; as-PBF specimens showed reduced strength and ductility, while heat-treated specimens exhibited decreased strength but increased ductility.
Validation· Mechanical testing and hardness measurements validated the phase transformation and property changes.
