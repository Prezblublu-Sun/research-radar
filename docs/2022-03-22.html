Study on Interfacial Bonding Properties of NiTi/CuSn10 Dissimilar Materials by Selective Laser Melting

Changhui Song, Zehua Hu, Yunmian Xiao, Yang Li, Yongqiang Yang Micromachines 2022-03-22 10.3390/mi13040494
单位: School of Mechanical and Automotive Engineering, South China University of Technology, Guangzhou 510640, China
通讯: Changhui Song @ School of Mechanical and Automotive Engineering, South China University of Technology, Guangzhou 510640, China
相关性: 该论文研究NiTi与CuSn10异种材料的SLM界面结合，未涉及生物医学植入物、多孔结构、应力屏蔽或疲劳测试。
边界: This paper does not involve porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue testing, surface roughness, or osseointegration, and is not applied to biomedical implants.
动机· 研究NiTi与CuSn10异种材料SLM界面结合性能。
方法· 通过改变SLM工艺参数形成中间层，分析界面缺陷、微观组织和元素扩散。
结果· 界面处生成柱状晶和Ni4Ti3相，细化了晶粒，增强了结合强度。
验证· 微观组织和相分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the interfacial bonding properties of NiTi and CuSn10 dissimilar materials fabricated by selective laser melting.
Method· The study explored different SLM process parameters for forming interlayers and characterized defects, microstructure, and element diffusion at the interface.
Result· Columnar grains and Ni4Ti3 phase were formed at the interface, refining grains and strengthening the bonding.
Validation· Microstructural and phase analysis.

Correlation of microstructural with corrosion behaviour of Ti-6Al-4V specimens developed through selective laser melting technique

N. Jeyaprakash, Che‐Hua Yang, Sundara Subramanian Karuppasamy, Dinesh Kumar Rajendran Proceedings of the Institution of Mechanical Engineers Part E Journal of Process Mechanical Engineering 2022-03-22 10.1177/09544089221087823
单位: Graduate Institute of Manufacturing Technology, National Taipei University of Technology, Taipei, Taiwan
通讯: N. Jeyaprakash @ Graduate Institute of Manufacturing Technology, National Taipei University of Technology, Taipei, Taiwan
相关性: 该论文评估了SLM Ti-6Al-4V的显微组织和耐腐蚀性，但未涉及应力遮挡、疲劳或骨整合等关键植入物性能。
边界: No porous structure, fatigue testing, stress shielding analysis, or osseointegration evaluation; purely material characterization.
动机· 比较SLM和铸造Ti64合金用于生物医学应用的性能差异。
方法· 进行显微组织、硬度和电化学腐蚀测试。
结果· SLM样品具有马氏体组织，硬度更高且耐腐蚀性更好。
验证· 通过FESEM、EDS、显微硬度和阻抗谱验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare the microstructural and corrosion properties of as-cast and selective laser melted Ti-6Al-4V for potential biomedical applications.
Method· Microstructural analysis (FESEM/EDS), Vickers hardness testing, and electrochemical corrosion tests including EIS and surface roughness measurements.
Result· SLM samples exhibited a martensitic microstructure, higher hardness, and superior corrosion resistance due to a stable passive film.
Validation· Validation via combined microstructural characterization, hardness measurements, and electrochemical impedance spectroscopy.

Spidroin striped micropattern promotes chondrogenic differentiation of human Wharton’s jelly mesenchymal stem cells

Anggraini Barlian, Dinda Hani’ah Arum Saputri, Adriel Hernando, Candrani Khoirinaya, Ekavianty Prajatelistia et al. Scientific Reports 2022-03-22 10.1038/s41598-022-08982-8
单位: Research Center for Nanosciences and Nanotechnology, Bandung Institute of Technology, Bandung, West Java, 40132, Indonesia. aang@sith.itb.ac.id
通讯: Anggraini Barlian @ Research Center for Nanosciences and Nanotechnology, Bandung Institute of Technology, Bandung, West Java, 40132, Indones
相关性: 该论文研究丝素蛋白微图案诱导软骨分化，未涉及人工智能或生物打印中的智能控制。
边界: No integration of AI into any bioprinting workflow stage; purely biological study without computational modeling or automation.
动机· 探索蜘蛛丝蛋白微图案诱导干细胞软骨分化的潜力。
方法· 在蜘蛛丝蛋白条纹微图案上培养人沃顿胶间充质干细胞21天。
结果· 1000微米宽度微图案最佳促进糖胺聚糖和SOX9表达。
验证· 免疫细胞化学和基因表达分析验证分化标志物。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate whether spidroin micropatterns can induce chondrogenesis of stem cells without chondrogenic medium.
Method· Human Wharton's jelly mesenchymal stem cells were cultured on spidroin striped micropatterns of 500 µm and 1000 µm widths for 21 days.
Result· The 1000 µm pattern optimally enhanced glycosaminoglycan content and SOX9 expression, while collagen type II was detected from day 14.
Validation· Validation was performed via immunocytochemistry and gene expression analysis of chondrogenic markers.
