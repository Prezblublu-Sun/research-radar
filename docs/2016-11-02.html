Biomechanical optimization of the angle and position for surgical implantation of a straight short stem hip implant

High Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Direct Read now mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Gillian E. Cook, Saeid Samiezadeh, Zachary Morison, Mina S.R. Aziz, Habiba Bougherara et al. Medical Engineering & Physics 2016-11-02 10.1016/j.medengphy.2016.10.002
单位: Institute of Biomaterials and Biomedical Engineering, University of Toronto, Toronto, ON, Canada
通讯: Radovan Zdero @ Department of Mechanical and Industrial Engineering, Ryerson University, Toronto, ON, Canada; Orthopaedic Biomechanics L
相关性: 该论文直接优化短柄髋关节假体的植入角度和位置，以恢复股骨近端应力分布，与用户的研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not report micromotion values or contact/friction modeling at the bone-implant interface.
动机· 确定直型短柄髋假体最佳三维植入角度和位置，使应力分布最接近完整股骨。
方法· 使用合成股骨植入不同角度和位置的假体，通过实验应变验证有限元模型，并采用实验设计分析步态载荷下的应力分布。
结果· 最优方向为9.14°外翻、2.49°前倾、后移0.48mm、下移0.23mm，外翻方向在整个步态周期中表现最佳。
验证· 通过合成股骨实验应变数据验证有限元模型。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine the optimal 3D orientation of a straight short stem hip implant that yields a stress distribution most similar to the intact femur.
Method· Synthetic femurs were implanted with stems at seven maximum angles or positions, validated by experimental strain data, and design of experiments was used to analyze implant orientations under three gait cycle loading conditions.
Result· A global optimal orientation of 9.14° valgus, 2.49° anteversion, 0.48mm posterior, and 0.23mm inferior position produced stress distributions most similar to the intact femur across the gait cycle.
Validation· Finite element model validated against experimental strain measurements from synthetic femurs.
