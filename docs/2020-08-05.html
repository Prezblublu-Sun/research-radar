Manufacturability, Mechanical Properties, Mass-Transport Properties and Biocompatibility of Triply Periodic Minimal Surface (TPMS) Porous Scaffolds Fabricated by Selective Laser Melting

Shuai Ma, Qian Tang, Xiaoxiao Han, Qixiang Feng, Jun Song et al. Materials & Design 2020-08-05 10.1016/j.matdes.2020.109034
单位: State Key Laboratory of Mechanical Transmissions, Chongqing University, Chongqing 400044, China
通讯: Qian Tang @ State Key Laboratory of Mechanical Transmissions, Chongqing University, Chongqing 400044, China  |  Xiaoxiao Han @ State Key Laboratory of Advanced Design and Manufacturing for Vehicle Body, Hunan University, 410082, China
相关性: 该论文通过SLM制造了TPMS多孔支架并进行了力学和生物学验证，与用户对骨科植入物增材制造的研究方向直接相关。
边界: This paper does not address fatigue behavior, surface roughness characterization, or explicit stress shielding quantification, which are key aspects of the AM biomedical implant direction.
动机· 评估不同孔隙率TPMS多孔支架的可制造性和综合性能。
方法· 通过选择性激光熔化制造不同孔隙率的陀螺状支架，并进行压缩、渗透和体外实验。
结果· 支架弹性模量和渗透率与人体骨匹配，制造精度随孔隙率增大而提高。
验证· 实验压缩与渗透测试结合体外细胞增殖实验。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This work aims to comprehensively evaluate the manufacturability, mechanical properties, mass-transport properties, and biocompatibility of TPMS porous scaffolds for bone tissue engineering.
Method· Gyroid scaffolds with varying porosities were fabricated using selective laser melting, and their performance was assessed through compression tests, permeability measurements, and in vitro cell culture.
Result· The scaffolds exhibited elastic modulus and permeability within the range of human bone, and manufacturing accuracy improved with increasing designed porosity.
Validation· Experimental compression and permeability tests validated simulations within 56-73% accuracy, while in vitro assays confirmed biocompatibility and the role of permeability in cell proliferation.

单位: Graduate Students, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Chung-Ang University, 84 Heukseok-ro, Dongjak-gu, Seoul, 06974, South Korea
通讯: Kee Bong Yoon @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Chung-Ang University, 84 Heukseok-ro, Dongjak-gu, Seoul, 06974, South Korea
相关性: 该论文研究不锈钢SLM的各向异性蠕变行为，未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳，与生物医学植入物方向相关性低。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures, stress shielding, stiffness matching, fatigue, or surface roughness/osseointegration relevant to biomedical implants.
动机· 研究SLM不锈钢各向异性蠕变行为以补充高温性能数据
方法· 通过不同取向的蠕变试验结合微观组织表征分析各向异性机制
结果· 发现垂直方向蠕变抗力低于水平方向，归因于晶界和位错结构差异

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the anisotropic creep behavior of stainless steel fabricated by selective laser melting for high-temperature applications.
Method· Creep tests were conducted on specimens oriented differently relative to the build direction, complemented by microstructural characterization.
Result· Vertical specimens exhibited lower creep resistance than horizontal ones, attributed to differences in grain boundary distribution and dislocation substructure.
Validation· Experimental creep curves and microstructure analysis validate the anisotropy mechanism.

单位: Thermomechanical Metallurgy Laboratory (LMTM) – PX Group Chair, Ecole Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL), CH-2002, Neuchâtel, Switzerland
通讯: Hossein Ghasemi‐Tabasi @ Thermomechanical Metallurgy Laboratory (LMTM) – PX Group Chair, Ecole Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL), CH-2002
相关性: 该论文专注于SLM工艺参数在材料间的迁移，未涉及生物医学植入物或相关力学性能。
边界: No biomedical implant context; no porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration; focuses on dense parts for non-biomedical materials.
动机· 减少SLM工艺参数优化的试错成本，提出材料间参数迁移方法
方法· 利用归一化焓和粉末吸收率测量，结合FEM模拟推导参数迁移规则
结果· 成功将青铜参数迁移至红金和316L钢，获得18K金合金最高致密度(99.81%)
验证· 通过FEM仿真和密度测量实验验证迁移规则的有效性
English summary & terms
Motivation· To reduce the trial-and-error in identifying SLM process windows by developing a method to transfer optimal parameters between different materials.
Method· The normalized enthalpy concept and powder absorptivity measurements were used, and FEM simulations justified the translation rule for laser powder interactions.
Result· The proposed translation rule achieved the highest reported relative density (99.81%) for an 18-carat gold alloy and successfully applied to red gold and 316L steel.
Validation· Finite element simulations and experimental density measurements validated the translation rule and the role of powder absorptivity.
