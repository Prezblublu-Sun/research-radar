Selective screw fixation is associated with early failure of primary acetabular components for aseptic loosening

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine whether selective screw supplementation of acetabular components is associated with earlier aseptic loosening compared to nonselective screw use.
Method· A retrospective review of aseptic acetabular component failures after primary THA, comparing revision times between selective and nonselective screw fixation groups.
Result· Selective screw use was independently associated with earlier revision for aseptic loosening (median 1.9 vs 5.6 years).
Validation· Clinical retrospective cohort analysis with statistical adjustment for obesity.
