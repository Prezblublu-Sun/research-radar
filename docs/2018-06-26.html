相关性: 该论文研究术前甲基强的松龙对全髋/膝关节置换术后血糖控制的影响，未涉及任何生物力学或植入物设计内容。

动机· 评估术前单次甲基强的松龙对全髋/膝关节置换术后血糖稳态的影响。
方法· 随机双盲对照试验，134例患者分为甲基强的松龙组和安慰剂组，检测术后血糖、C肽等指标。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the effect of a single preoperative dose of methylprednisolone on glycemic homeostasis early after fast-track total hip and knee arthroplasty.
Method· A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial with 134 patients receiving either 125 mg methylprednisolone or saline, measuring plasma glucose, C-peptide, HOMA indices at multiple time points.
Result· Methylprednisolone transiently increased postoperative plasma glucose and insulin resistance while impairing insulin secretion, with normalization by 48 hours.
Validation· Clinical trial with 122 complete samples, using standardized perioperative protocols and statistical adjustment.
