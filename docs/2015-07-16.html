Christopher L. Peters, Jesse Chrastil, Gregory J. Stoddard, Jill A. Erickson, Mike B. Anderson et al. Clinical Orthopaedics and Related Research 2015-07-16 10.1007/s11999-015-4458-8
单位: Department of Orthopaedics, University of Utah, 590 Wakara Way, Salt Lake City, UT, 84108, USA. chris.peters@hsc.utah.edu
通讯: Christopher L. Peters @ Department of Orthopaedics, University of Utah, 590 Wakara Way, Salt Lake City, UT, 84108, USA. chris.peters@hsc.utah.ed
相关性: 该论文仅关注DDH患者术前X线片预测模块化股骨柄的使用，未涉及任何生物力学分析或应力遮挡评估。
边界: This paper does not quantify stress/strain/micromotion, lacks FEA or contact mechanics, and does not compare intact vs implanted femur.
动机· 术前X线片能否预测DDH患者是否需要模块化股骨柄
方法· 回顾性分析50例DDH患者术前X线参数与术中颈干角的关系
结果· AP颈干角≥142°、侧位颈干角≤153°、股骨前倾角≥32°可预测模块化柄使用
验证· ROC曲线评估各参数预测准确性
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine whether standard preoperative radiographs can predict the need for modular femoral stems in DDH patients undergoing THA.
Method· Retrospective review of 50 DDH patients (79 hips) comparing preoperative radiographic parameters (AP neck-shaft angle, lateral neck-shaft angle, calculated anteversion) between modular and nonmodular stem groups.
Result· AP neck-shaft angle ≥142°, lateral neck-shaft angle ≤153°, and femoral anteversion ≥32° were significant predictors of modular stem use.
Validation· ROC analysis with area under curve values of 73%, 65%, and 80% respectively.
