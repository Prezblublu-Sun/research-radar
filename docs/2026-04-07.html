相关性: 该论文关于半监督统计推断，与生物力学仿真或植入物设计无关。
边界: This paper focuses on semi-supervised mean estimation and confidence intervals, with no application to FEA surrogate modelling, implant mechanics, or any biomedical engineering context.
动机· 半监督均值估计中标签稀少且模型可能误设，需高效置信区间。
方法· 提出基于Bregman投影的交叉拟合熵校准方法MEC，对标记样本重加权。
结果· MEC在更弱假设下达到半参数效率界，置信区间更紧且覆盖接近名义水平。

English summary & terms
Motivation· Semi-supervised mean estimation suffers from label scarcity and model misspecification, requiring efficient confidence intervals with reliable coverage.
Method· MEC uses cross-fitted calibration weights based on Bregman projections to reweight labeled samples, improving robustness and efficiency over existing prediction-powered inference methods.
Result· MEC attains the semiparametric efficiency bound under weaker assumptions, producing tighter confidence intervals with near-nominal coverage in simulations and a real application.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on synthetic data and a real-world dataset compare MEC against CF-PPI and vanilla PPI in terms of coverage and interval length.
