英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine whether the knee-derived microfracture criteria are appropriate for treating chondral lesions in the hip.
Method· A retrospective case series of 70 patients undergoing hip arthroscopy with microfracture, with 2-year follow-up using modified Harris Hip Score.
Result· Microfracture outcomes in the hip were not significantly affected by lesion size or patient age, but conversion to total hip arthroplasty within 2 years was high.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with patient-reported outcome measures (mHHS) and revision surgery rates.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate whether intraoperative low-dose epinephrine infusion reduces blood loss and transfusion requirements in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A randomized controlled trial comparing epinephrine plus tranexamic acid versus tranexamic acid alone during THA.
Result· Epinephrine group showed significantly lower blood loss and reduced transfusion rate.
Validation· Clinical randomized controlled trial with 80 patients.
