Beyond Pinball Loss: Quantile Methods for Calibrated Uncertainty Quantification

Youngseog Chung, Willie Neiswanger, Ian Char, Jeff Schneider arXiv preprint 2020-11-18 link
相关性: 该论文提出的分位数不确定性量化方法可迁移至代理模型中的校准不确定性评估，但未涉及计算成本或生物力学约束。
边界: This paper does not address expensive simulation sampling efficiency, constraint handling in optimization, or transferability to implant/biomechanics design; it focuses on general regression UQ methods without FEA surrogate application.
动机· 现有分位数损失函数限制模型适用性与校准能力，需开发更通用的分位数方法。
方法· 提出超越分位数损失的新方法，支持任意回归模型并在校准与锐度间权衡。
结果· 新方法产生更准确的校准条件分位数，并在高维聚变任务中验证。
验证· 通过标准回归数据集及核聚变不确定性任务进行数值实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Current quantile-based methods using pinball loss restrict model class and fail to target calibration and sharpness simultaneously, motivating the development of more flexible quantile methods.
Method· We propose novel quantile methods that apply to any regression model, allow trade-off between calibration and sharpness, and optimize for centered interval calibration.
Result· The proposed methods produce more accurate conditional quantiles and demonstrate improved calibration on a high-dimensional nuclear fusion uncertainty quantification task.
Validation· Experimental evaluation on standard regression benchmarks and a high-dimensional fusion UQ task validates the effectiveness of the methods.
