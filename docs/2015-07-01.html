Machine oil inhibits the osseointegration of orthopaedic implants by impairing osteoblast attachment and spreading.

Lindsay A Bonsignore, Victor M Goldberg, Edward M Greenfield Journal of orthopaedic research : official publication of the Orthopaedic Research Society 2015-07-01 10.1002/jor.22850
单位: Department of Orthopaedics, Case Western Reserve University, Cleveland, Ohio.; Department of Pathology, Case Western Reserve University, Cleveland, Ohio.
通讯: Edward M Greenfield @ Department of Orthopaedics, Case Western Reserve University, Cleveland, Ohio.; Department of Pathology, Case Western Res [推断]
相关性: 该论文探讨机器油污染对骨整合的抑制作用，不涉及增材制造、多孔结构或机械性能测试。
边界: No additive manufacturing, porous structure design, LPBF fabrication, or mechanical testing of AM implants; focuses on contamination effects on osseointegration using traditional implants.
动机· 探索机器油是否抑制骨科植入物的骨整合。
方法· 采用小鼠模型和MC3T3-E1细胞实验，分析机器油对植入物-骨接触和成骨细胞行为的影响。
结果· 机器油减少骨-植入物接触和拉出力，抑制成骨细胞附着、铺展和分化。
验证· 动物体内实验和体外细胞实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To determine whether machine oil contamination, previously found on failed hip implants, impairs osseointegration of orthopaedic implants.
Method· In vivo mouse femoral implant model and in vitro MC3T3-E1 osteoblast assays were used to assess bone-implant contact, pullout strength, and cell attachment/spreading on titanium alloy surfaces.
Result· Machine oil significantly reduced bone-implant contact and biomechanical pullout force, and inhibited osteoblast attachment, spreading, proliferation, and differentiation.
Validation· Validated through in vivo murine osseointegration model and in vitro cell culture experiments with MC3T3-E1 cells.
