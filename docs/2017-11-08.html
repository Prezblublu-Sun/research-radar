A Probabilistic Design Method for Fatigue Life of Metallic Component

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project mechanical_testing Exp. validation UQ
Danial Faghihi, Subhasis Sarkar, M. Naderi, Jon Rankin, Lloyd A. Hackel et al. ASCE-ASME Journal of Risk and Uncertainty in Engineering Systems Part B Mechanical Engineering 2017-11-08 10.1115/1.4038372
单位: Institute for Computational Engineeringand Sciences,The University of Texas at Austin,Austin, TX 78712e-mail: danial@ices.utexas.edu
通讯: Danial Faghihi @ Institute for Computational Engineeringand Sciences,The University of Texas at Austin,Austin, TX 78712e-mail: danial@ice
相关性: 该论文提出的概率设计框架和贝叶斯不确定性量化方法可迁移至植入物疲劳寿命预测，但未涉及生物力学或植入物设计。
边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, bone-implant interface, or patient-specific geometry; it focuses on metallic coupon fatigue without biomedical context.
动机· 解决金属构件疲劳寿命预测中实验数据和计算模型的不确定性问题。
方法· 结合疲劳试验、连续损伤力学模型、全局灵敏度分析和贝叶斯框架进行概率设计。
结果· 概率设计方法能在数据不完整且有噪声时有效校准模型并评估疲劳寿命可靠性。
验证· 通过Ti6Al4V材料疲劳试验数据验证方法可行性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a general probabilistic design framework that accounts for uncertainties in experimental data and computational models for cyclic fatigue life prediction of metallic components.
Method· The framework integrates fatigue coupon testing, continuum damage mechanics constitutive models, variance-based global sensitivity analysis, Bayesian model calibration, and probabilistic life prediction.
Result· The probabilistic approach enables model calibration under incomplete and noisy data and provides reliability assessment of predicted fatigue life.
Validation· Validation is performed using fatigue test data on Ti6Al4V coupons, demonstrating the feasibility of the probabilistic design methods.
