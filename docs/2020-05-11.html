Insights into unit cell size effect on mechanical responses and energy absorption capability of titanium graded porous structures manufactured by laser powder bed fusion

Lei Yang, Changjun Han, Hongzhi Wu, Liang Hao, Qingsong Wei et al. Journal of the mechanical behavior of biomedical materials/Journal of mechanical behavior of biomedical materials 2020-05-11 10.1016/j.jmbbm.2020.103843
单位: State Key Lab of Materials Processing and Die & Mould Technology, School of Materials Science and Engineering, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan, 430074, China
通讯: Changjun Han @ Singapore Centre for 3D Printing, School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Nanyang Technological University, 6397  |  Chunze Yan @ State Key Lab of Materials Processing and Die & Mould Technology, School of Materials Science and Engineering, Huazhong
相关性: 该论文直接研究了激光粉床熔融钛合金多孔结构的力学性能与能量吸收，对骨植入物应力遮挡匹配有参考价值。
边界: This paper does not cover fatigue testing, surface roughness characterization, or osseointegration assessment; it focuses on quasi-static compressive properties and energy absorption.
动机· 研究单元孔尺寸对多孔结构力学性能与能量吸收的影响。
方法· 采用激光粉床熔融制造分级多孔钛结构并进行准静态压缩测试。
结果· 单元孔尺寸显著影响力学响应和能量吸收能力。
验证· 通过准静态压缩实验验证机械性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of unit cell size on the mechanical response and energy absorption capability of graded porous titanium structures.
Method· Graded porous structures were fabricated by laser powder bed fusion and tested under quasi-static compression.
Result· Unit cell size significantly influences the mechanical response and energy absorption capacity.
Validation· Mechanical behavior was validated through quasi-static compression experiments.

单位: Arts et Metiers Institute of Technology, University of Bordeaux, CNRS, Bordeaux INP, INRAE, I2M Bordeaux, F-33400 Talence, France

边界: It does not specifically address osseointegration or surface roughness, but covers fatigue and LPBF fabrication and mechanical testing.
动机· 研究SLM钛合金点阵结构的多轴高周疲劳性能。
方法· 进行实验疲劳测试和数值模拟分析。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the multiaxial high-cycle fatigue behavior of titanium lattice structures fabricated by SLM for biomedical implant applications.

Validation· The numerical fatigue model was validated against experimental fatigue tests on SLM titanium lattice specimens.

Investigations on Mechanical Properties of Lattice Structures with Different Values of Relative Density Made from 316L by Selective Laser Melting (SLM)

Paweł Płatek, Judyta Sienkiewicz, Jacek Janiszewski, Fengchun Jiang Materials 2020-05-11 10.3390/ma13092204
单位: Faculty of Mechatronics and Aerospace, Military University of Technology, 2 Gen. S. Kaliskiego Street, 00-908 Warsaw, Poland
通讯: Paweł Płatek @ Faculty of Mechatronics and Aerospace, Military University of Technology, 2 Gen. S. Kaliskiego Street, 00-908 Warsaw, Po
相关性: 该论文研究了通过选区激光熔化制造的316L不锈钢点阵结构的力学性能，与生物医学植入物的增材制造相关，但未涉及应力遮挡或疲劳等生物医学关键问题。
边界: This paper does not address stress shielding, stiffness matching, fatigue testing, surface roughness, or osseointegration relevant to biomedical implants.
动机· 探索不同相对密度的316L点阵结构在SLM工艺下的力学行为。
方法· 设计并制造九种不同相对密度的点阵结构，进行几何质量控制、微观结构表征和力学测试。
结果· 高相对密度点阵结构为拉伸主导，低相对密度为弯曲主导；能量吸收与相对密度呈线性关系。
验证· 通过准静态和动态力学测试验证力学性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the mechanical behavior of 316L lattice structures with varying relative densities fabricated by selective laser melting.
Method· Nine lattice structure variants were designed, fabricated via SLM, and subjected to geometrical quality control, microstructure analysis, and quasi-static/dynamic mechanical testing.
Result· High relative density lattices exhibited stretch-dominated behavior while low density ones were bending-dominated; energy absorption showed a linear dependence on relative density under dynamic loading.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated through quasi-static compression and dynamic impact tests.

单位: Faculty of Engineering Technology, University of Twente, P.O. Box 217, 7500 AE, Enschede, the Netherlands
通讯: Davoud Jafari @ Faculty of Engineering Technology, University of Twente, P.O. Box 217, 7500 AE, Enschede, the Netherlands
相关性: 该论文研究不锈钢多孔结构的SLM制备及热物理性能，未涉及生物医用植入物的应力遮挡、刚度匹配或力学测试。
边界: This paper does not address stress shielding, stiffness matching, fatigue, or osseointegration for biomedical implants; it focuses on thermophysical properties for non-biomedical applications.

Method· Porous stainless steel samples were manufactured by controlling laser scan parameters in pulsed mode, and their structural and thermophysical properties were characterized.

Validation· Experimental measurements of thermal conductivity and contact angle were compared with existing models and correlations.
