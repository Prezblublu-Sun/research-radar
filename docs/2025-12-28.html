A Cross-Platform Analysis of Total Hip Arthroplasty Videos on Douyin and TikTok – Content Quality, Engagement, and Ecosystem Disparities (Preprint)

Shunyi Lei, Yanlong Qu, Fei Nan, Siyao Liu, Yan Jin et al. 2025-12-28 10.2196/preprints.90452
相关性: 该论文分析的是髋关节置换术短视频平台内容质量，与髋关节植入物力学建模无关。
边界: This paper does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics; it is a social media content analysis with no biomechanical modeling or implant design.
动机· 比较抖音和TikTok上髋关节置换术视频的质量与用户参与
方法· 系统搜索265个视频和600条评论，使用JAMA、GQS、DISCERN工具评分
结果· 抖音以医生为中心，视频质量更高；TikTok以患者为中心，经验分享更丰富
验证· 统计学检验（卡方检验、Mann–Whitney U检验）
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare the quality, content, and user engagement of total hip arthroplasty videos on Douyin and TikTok.
Method· Systematically searched 265 videos and 600 comments, evaluated quality with JAMA, GQS, and DISCERN tools, and analyzed content themes.
Result· Douyin exhibited a doctor-centered, authority-driven model with higher content quality, while TikTok fostered a patient-centered, community-driven model with abundant experience sharing.
Validation· Statistical analysis using chi-square tests, Mann-Whitney U tests, and Kruskal-Wallis H tests.
