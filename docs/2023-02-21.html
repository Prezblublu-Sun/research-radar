Data-driven reduced-order modelling for blood flow simulations with geometry-informed snapshots

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project simulation_case Patient-specific
Dongwei Ye, Valeria Krzhizhanovskaya, Alfons G. Hoekstra arXiv preprint 2023-02-21 link
相关性: 该论文提出了基于几何信息的降阶代理模型，可迁移至生物力学或植入物设计的代理建模。
边界: This paper does not address structural mechanics, implant-specific constraints, or uncertainty quantification; it focuses on hemodynamics. FEA surrogate for implant design requires extension to solid mechanics.
动机· 解决血流模拟中几何参数化及计算效率低下的问题
方法· 基于曲面配准构建几何感知快照，结合POD和RBF建立非侵入式降阶模型
结果· 在狭窄和分叉血流案例中实现高效准确的代理预测
验证· 通过两个血流模拟案例进行了数值验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Parametric reduced-order models for hemodynamics struggle with unparameterized patient-specific geometries, motivating a data-driven surrogate that can handle shape variations.
Method· A non-intrusive reduced-order model is constructed by using group surface registration to parameterize shapes and formulating geometry-informed snapshots via diffeomorphisms, followed by POD for dimension reduction and RBF interpolation for coefficient prediction.
Result· The surrogate accurately predicts blood flow in stenosis and bifurcation cases with significant computational speedup, enabling potential real-time simulation.
Validation· Numerical validation on two distinct blood flow scenarios (stenosis and bifurcation) demonstrates accuracy and efficiency.
