英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine whether intercalary autograft is mandatory for shortening during total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Retrospective chart review comparing clinical outcomes between patients with and without intercalary autograft.
Result· Intercalary autograft is not necessary for shortening in total hip arthroplasty.
Validation· Clinical retrospective analysis of patient outcomes.
