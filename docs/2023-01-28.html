Influences of surface topography of porous titanium scaffolds manufactured by powder bed fusion on osteogenesis

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Skim only in_vitro Exp. validation
Yongkyung Lee, Ara Jung, Su‐Jin Heo, Bomi Gweon, Dohyung Lim Journal of Materials Research and Technology 2023-01-28 10.1016/j.jmrt.2023.01.153
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Sejong University, Seoul 05006, South Korea
通讯: Bomi Gweon @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Sejong University, Seoul 05006, South Korea  |  Dohyung Lim @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Sejong University, Seoul 05006, South Korea
相关性: 该论文研究了LPBF制造的钛合金多孔支架的表面形貌对成骨分化的影响，但与应力遮挡和力学性能无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not include mechanical testing, fatigue analysis, or stress shielding evaluation, which are central to the user's direction.
动机· 探究PBF打印多孔钛支架的孔形状对表面形貌及骨长入的影响。
方法· 使用PBF制备不同孔形状的Ti-6Al-4V支架，分析曲率和粗糙度，并进行体外细胞实验。
结果· 孔形状改变表面曲率和粗糙度，进而影响成骨细胞的增殖与分化。
验证· 通过体外细胞实验验证表面形貌对成骨活性的影响。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To understand how pore shape influences surface topography and subsequently bone ingrowth in PBF-manufactured titanium scaffolds.
Method· Ti-6Al-4V scaffolds with circular, triangular, and rectangular pores were fabricated via PBF; surface curvature and roughness were analyzed, and Saos-2 cell behavior was assessed in vitro.
Result· Pore shape significantly alters local curvature and roughness, which correlates with differences in osteoblast proliferation and differentiation.
Validation· In vitro cell culture assays using Saos-2 cells provided experimental validation of the biological response to surface topography.

相关性: 论文研究物理信息神经网络中的重参数化方法，可迁移至生物力学设计的替代建模。
边界: This paper lacks uncertainty quantification, constraint handling, and application to implant/biomechanics design.
动机· 比较物理信息神经网络中重参数化与惩罚函数法的求解精度。
方法· 在一维杆和二维弯曲梁问题上应用重参数化PINN。

Validation· Numerical benchmarks on two mechanical engineering problems serve as validation.
