边界: This paper does not address expensive simulation sampling, constraints, uncertainty quantification for FEA, or transferability to implant/biomechanics design; it is purely a regression method for complex-valued signals.
动机· 扩展高斯过程到复数域信号处理

Result· The proposed method achieves remarkable improvement in estimation error on benchmark problems compared to existing approaches.
Validation· Validation is performed on synthetic and benchmark datasets, demonstrating superior performance over linear and neural network baselines.

相关性: 该论文关注主动学习中的样本选择策略，与有限元代理建模或生物力学设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on active learning sample selection for classification tasks, with no connection to surrogate modeling, finite element analysis, or biomedical implant design.
动机· 主动学习中样本分布偏移导致经验风险最小化失效

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Active learning suffers from distribution mismatch between queried samples and the source distribution, undermining the empirical risk minimization principle.

Result· The proposed method selects both discriminative and representative samples, outperforming state-of-the-art batch mode active learning methods.
Validation· Experiments on benchmark datasets and real-world applications demonstrate superior classification performance.

Hiroaki Suzuki, Daiki Takahashi, Kenji Matsuo, Akira Kimoto, Kousuke Matsumoto et al. PubMed 2015-02-17 10.24546/81009221
单位: Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, Kobe University Graduate School of Medicine
通讯: Hiroaki Suzuki @ Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, Kobe University Graduate School of Medicine
相关性: 该论文讨论牙科植入物移除的临床回顾，与髋关节植入物应力遮挡和有限元分析无关。
边界: This paper is a clinical retrospective study on dental implant removal, with no biomechanical modeling, no stress shielding analysis, no micromotion quantification, and no finite element simulation.
动机· 分析牙科植入物移除的原因以改善治疗结果
方法· 回顾2003-2013年19例牙科植入物移除病例的临床数据
结果· 移除多发生于老年患者，早期因初期稳定性不足，后期因种植体周围炎

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To analyze the causes of dental implant removal and improve future treatment outcomes.
Method· A retrospective clinical study of 19 cases (21 jaws) of dental implant removal from 2003 to 2013.
Result· Removal was more frequent in elderly patients; early failures were due to poor primary stability, while late failures were due to peri-implantitis.
Validation· Clinical case review without biomechanical or computational validation.
