Structure and properties of a personalized bio-fixed implant prepared with selective laser melting

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Skim only mechanical_testing Exp. validation Patient-specific
Guoqing Zhang, Junxin Li, Jin Li, Xiaoyu Zhou, Anmin Wang Computer Methods in Biomechanics & Biomedical Engineering 2019-07-14 10.1080/10255842.2019.1616085
单位: School of mechanical and electrical engineering, Zhoukou Normal University, Zhoukou, PR China;
通讯: Guoqing Zhang @ School of mechanical and electrical engineering, Zhoukou Normal University, Zhoukou, PR China;
相关性: 该论文涉及个性化生物固定植入物的SLM制备和力学性能测试，与AM生物医学方向相关。
边界: No fatigue testing, no osseointegration or surface roughness characterization, and only static mechanical properties reported.
动机· 个性化生物固定植入物需要良好的建模效率和应力分布。
方法· 采用逆向和正向方法重建胫骨植入物，结合有限元分析和SLM制造。
结果· SLM制备的多孔结构成型良好，应力分布均匀，力学性能与传统方法相当。
验证· 通过SLM制造和力学性能测试验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Personalized bio-fixed implants require efficient modeling and uniform stress distribution for optimal function.
Method· A tibial implant was designed using reverse and positive reconstruction, finite element analysis, and fabricated by selective laser melting.
Result· The SLM-fabricated porous structure showed good forming quality, uniform stress distribution, and mechanical properties comparable to traditional methods.
Validation· Mechanical testing of the fabricated implant unit structure was performed.

Parametric Study of SLM Processing Parameters on In-Situ Residual Stress

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only simulation_case
Emmanuel Duodu Amoako, Patrick Mensah, Stephen Akwaboa, Samuel Ibekwe, Guoqiang Li 2019-07-14 10.1115/ht2019-3623
单位: Southern University and A&M College, Baton Rouge, LA
通讯: Emmanuel Duodu Amoako @ Southern University and A&M College, Baton Rouge, LA
相关性: 该论文研究SLM工艺参数对残余应力的影响，但未涉及多孔结构、应力屏蔽、疲劳测试或生物医学植入物相关性能。
边界: This paper does not cover porous structure design, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or osseointegration; it focuses solely on residual stress in 316L stainless steel without biomedical context.
动机· 研究SLM工艺参数对残余应力的影响以优化制造质量。
方法· 采用ANSYS有限元热-力学模型模拟316L不锈钢的残余应力。
结果· 扫描速度、扫描间距和预热温度增加可降低残余应力，而熔化温度升高则增加残余应力。
验证· 仅通过数值模拟，无实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of SLM process parameters on residual stress for quality improvement.
Method· A thermo-mechanical finite element model in ANSYS was used to simulate residual stress in 316L stainless steel.
Result· Increasing scan speed, hatch distance, and preheat temperature reduces residual stress, while higher melting temperature increases it.
Validation· Numerical simulation only, no experimental validation.

单位: University of Alberta, Edmonton, AB, Canada
通讯: Yueying Li @ University of Alberta, Edmonton, AB, Canada
相关性: 该论文使用高斯过程回归量化管道凹痕分析模型的误差，与生物力学或植入物设计无关。
边界: No implant biomechanics, no contact mechanics, no patient-specific modeling, no uncertainty quantification for surrogate in biomedical context.
动机· 管道凹痕评估中FEA计算昂贵，需快速准确模型。
方法· 用高斯过程回归量化解析模型与FEA之间的误差。

Motivation· To reduce computational cost of finite element analysis for dent severity assessment in pipelines by developing a fast surrogate model.
Method· Gaussian Process Regression is applied to quantify the error between an analytical strain model and nonlinear FEA results.
Result· The GP model provides probabilistic predictions of maximum equivalent plastic strain with quantified uncertainty.
Validation· Validation is performed by comparing GP predictions with nonlinear FEA simulations for varying dent depths and indenter radii.

Motivation· Electrochemical drilling involves complex multiphysics interactions that are difficult to model analytically, motivating data-driven prediction.
Method· A deep convolutional network with Bayesian hyperparameter optimization is developed to predict hole diameter from feed rate, pulse-on time, and voltage.
Result· The proposed method achieves improved training speed and prediction accuracy compared to a traditional neural network with partial physical knowledge.
Validation· Validation is performed by comparing against a previously published neural network on the same dataset.
