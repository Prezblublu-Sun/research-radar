单位: State Key Laboratory of Solidification Processing, Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi׳an 710072, PR China
通讯: Hongchao Kou @ State Key Laboratory of Solidification Processing, Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi׳an 710072, PR China. Electr
相关性: 该论文研究了各向异性多孔钛的制备、孔结构与压缩行为，直接关联到用户对多孔结构应力屏蔽和刚度匹配的研究方向。
边界: This paper does not include LPBF fabrication, fatigue testing, surface roughness, or osseointegration analysis.
动机· 模拟人松质骨各向异性结构，制备力学匹配的多孔钛植入体。

Motivation· To fabricate anisotropic porous titanium that mimics the structure and mechanical properties of human trabecular bone for implant applications.
Method· Anisotropic porous titanium samples with varying porosity and pore size were fabricated using powder metallurgy, and their pore structure and compressive behavior were characterized.

Validation· Mechanical properties were validated through compression testing and compared with reported trabecular bone data.
