Physical-Mechanical Characteristics and Microstructure of Ti6Al7Nb Lattice Structures Manufactured by Selective Laser Melting.

High AM Biomedical Materials pubmed Direct Read now mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Cosmin Cosma, Igor Drstvensek, Petru Berce, Simon Prunean, Stanisław Legutko et al. Materials (Basel, Switzerland) 2020-09-16 10.3390/ma13184123
单位: Department of Manufacturing Engineering, Technical University of Cluj-Napoca, 400641 Cluj-Napoca, Romania.
通讯: Nicolae Balc @ Department of Manufacturing Engineering, Technical University of Cluj-Napoca, 400641 Cluj-Napoca, Romania. [推断]
相关性: 该论文直接研究Ti6Al7Nb多孔晶格结构的力学性能与微观结构，通过SLM制造并测试了弹性模量降低效果，与研究方向中多孔结构应力屏蔽和骨整合高度相关。
边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing, in vivo osseointegration validation, or patient-specific design considerations.
动机· 研究Ti6Al7Nb合金多孔晶格结构的物理力学性能，以用于骨植入物。
方法· 采用SLM制造三种晶格结构，通过压缩测试、微观结构分析研究其性能。
结果· 多孔结构弹性模量降至6-28 GPa，表面有部分熔化颗粒，断裂为脆性断裂。
验证· 准静态压缩实验及Gibson-Ashby模型预测验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the physical-mechanical properties of Ti6Al7Nb lattice structures fabricated by selective laser melting for potential medical implant applications.
Method· Three lattice topologies were manufactured via SLM using Ti6Al7Nb powder, and characterized through compression testing, metallography, X-ray diffraction, and fractography.
Result· The porous structures exhibited a reduction in elastic modulus from 104 GPa to 6–28 GPa, with strut surfaces covered by partially melted grains and a brittle fracture mode.
Validation· Experimental quasi-static compression tests were performed, and the Gibson-Ashby model was adapted to predict theoretical compressive strength and Young's modulus.
