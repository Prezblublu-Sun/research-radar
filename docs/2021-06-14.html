Validation· The method is validated against standard BVP benchmarks, demonstrating efficiency and accuracy.

通讯: Danilo A. Renzo @ Department of Mechanical, Energy and Management Engineering University of Calabria Rende Italy
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM Ti6Al4V合金在多轴载荷下的疲劳行为，但未涉及多孔结构、应力屏蔽或生物医学植入物相关性能。

结果· 改进模型预测效果优于原Sines模型，发现不同加载下的失效模式。

English summary & terms
Motivation· The study investigates the multiaxial fatigue behavior of Ti6Al4V alloy fabricated by selective laser melting under various loading conditions.

Result· The proposed model provided better predictions than the original Sines model, and different failure modes were observed depending on crack initiation mechanisms.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed using infrared thermography and digital image correlation to monitor temperature and strain, complemented by SEM fractography.

Effects of pore size and porosity on cytocompatibility and osteogenic differentiation of porous titanium.

通讯: Xin-Ping Zhang @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, South China University of Technology, Guangzhou, China. mexzhang@scut.edu.c  |  Ke Zhao @ Hospital of Stomatology, Guanghua School of Stomatology, Sun Yat-sen University, Guangdong Provincial Key Laboratory of  |  Yutao Jian @ Institute of Stomatological Research, Guangdong Provincial Key Laboratory of Stomatology, Sun Yat-sen University, Guangz
相关性: 该论文用粉末冶金法制造多孔钛，缺乏增材制造（LPBF）工艺，但与多孔植入物的力学和生物相容性相关。

结果· 高孔隙率（53.3%）与小孔径（191.6 μm）促进细胞分化与钙化，但早期细胞黏附较少。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To identify optimal porosity and pore size of porous titanium for cytocompatibility and osteogenic differentiation in orthopedic implants.
Method· Six groups of porous titanium samples with varying porosity and pore size were fabricated via powder metallurgy, and their microstructure, compressive mechanical properties, and biological responses were characterized.
Result· Porous titanium with high porosity (53.3%) and small pore size (191.6 μm) showed enhanced protein adsorption, cell differentiation, and calcification, though initial cell adhesion was lower on porous than dense surfaces.
Validation· Validation included compressive testing, protein adsorption assays, confocal microscopy for cell attachment, CCK-8 proliferation, qPCR for differentiation, and Alizarin Red staining for calcification.
