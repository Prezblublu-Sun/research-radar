英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To mitigate stress shielding and aseptic loosening in total hip arthroplasty caused by the stiffness mismatch between metallic femoral stems and bone.

Validation· Finite element models were validated against fatigue testing per ISO 7206 standards using potted implants.
