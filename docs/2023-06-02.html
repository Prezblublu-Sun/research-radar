Motivation· To develop a locoregional strategy for preventing periprosthetic joint infection by generating bactericidal macrophages at the implant surface.
Method· An implant nanoparticle coating delivers plasmid DNA encoding S. aureus-targeted CAR genes and CASP11 shRNA to macrophages, generating super CAR-MΦs that recruit mitochondria to phagosomes for ROS-mediated bacterial killing.
Result· The super CAR-MΦs effectively targeted and eradicated S. aureus, reduced infection, and promoted osteogenesis in a mouse model.
Validation· The coating was validated in a murine periprosthetic infection model with histological, microbiological, and imaging analyses.
