相关性: 该论文提出的区间和模糊物理增强神经网络方法可直接迁移到生物力学有限元代理建模中的不确定性量化问题。
边界: The paper focuses on hyperelastic constitutive modeling with synthetic data and does not address implant-specific biomechanics, patient-specific geometry, or contact mechanics.
动机· 解决稀疏、噪声数据下本构建模的不确定性量化问题。
方法· 提出区间和模糊物理增强神经网络，通过自动微分和alpha-cut插值实现不确定性传播。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Constitutive modeling under uncertainty remains a central challenge for reliable mechanics simulations, especially with sparse and noisy data.

Result· The learned bounds enclose noisy stress observations and generalize to test sets, with uncertainty propagation demonstrated in finite element simulations.
Validation· The framework is validated on synthetic isotropic hyperelastic data with heteroscedastic noise, varying random realizations, and noise magnitudes.
