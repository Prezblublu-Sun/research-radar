英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Clinical language models can produce plausible yet physiologically unsafe recommendations, necessitating improved safety alignment.
Method· The proposed Neurosymbolic Alignment framework couples a 7B clinical LLM with an HGNN-based physiological world model over a biomedical knowledge graph, using homeostatic constraints and drug-interaction penalties to score responses and drive iterative ORPO updates.
Result· The method improves Clinical Safety Score from 69.5% to 90.8% and reduces physician-evaluated harm rate from 14.1% to 5.1%, outperforming GPT-4 on all safety metrics.
Validation· Validation is performed on the Clinical Safety Benchmark with 2,500 scenarios, supplemented by a rule-engine safety score and clinician-labeled calibration.
