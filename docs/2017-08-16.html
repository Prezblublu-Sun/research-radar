单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Ming Chi University of Technology, 84 Gung-juan Rd., Taishan Dist., New Taipei City 24301, Taiwan
通讯: Kuo-Yung Hung @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Ming Chi University of Technology, 84 Gung-juan Rd., Taishan Dist., New Taipei Cit
相关性: 该论文研究钛种植体上磷酸钙薄膜的RF溅射工艺，未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或LPBF制造。
边界: No porous structure, no LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), and no stress shielding or stiffness matching analysis.
动机· 改善HA涂层在体内降解和骨整合问题
方法· RF溅射自研APS HA靶制备CaP薄膜，改变功率、时间和基底粗糙度
结果· Ca/P比随功率增加，低粗糙度提高结晶度，薄膜在SBF中诱导钙磷沉淀

Validation· Film characteristics were validated by synchrotron XRD, FTIR, white light interferometry, SEM/EDS, and in vitro SBF immersion.
