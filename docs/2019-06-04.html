单位: 111 “Biomanufacturing and Engineering Living Systems” Innovation International Talents Base, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Tsinghua University, Beijing 100084, China
通讯: Ting Zhang @ 111 “Biomanufacturing and Engineering Living Systems” Innovation International Talents Base, Department of Mechanical En
相关性: 该论文将图像处理技术用于支架内细胞精准组装，是AI辅助生物打印的直接应用。
边界: Uses classical image processing rather than machine learning, lacks autonomous decision-making, and focuses on post-fabrication cell seeding rather than AI-driven bioprinting workflow optimization.

结果· 细胞分布准确，活性>93%，七天内明显增殖

English summary & terms
Motivation· To achieve accurate multicellular distribution within prefabricated scaffolds for heterogeneous tissue engineering.

Validation· Validation included CellTracker fluorescent monitoring, live/dead staining, phalloidin-F-actin/DAPI staining, and PrestoBlue proliferation assay.

单位: State Key Laboratory of Advanced Special Steel and School of Materials Science and Engineering, Shanghai University, 99 Shangda Road, Shanghai 200444, China
通讯: Guanghui Cao @ State Key Laboratory of Advanced Special Steel and School of Materials Science and Engineering, Shanghai University, 99
相关性: 该论文研究Inconel 718高温合金的激光选区熔化成形与热处理工艺，与生物医用植入物、多孔结构、应力遮挡等方向无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on Inconel 718 superalloy for non-biomedical applications, with no porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration aspects relevant to AM biomedical implants.
动机· 解决SLM成形Inconel 718合金强度高但塑性差的问题。
方法· 开发新型热处理工艺（1150°C均质+700°C单次时效），调控γ″相形貌与再结晶组织。
结果· 塑性提升41%，强度保持不变。
验证· 通过拉伸测试与微观组织表征验证性能与机制。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To overcome the strength-ductility trade-off in selective laser melted Inconel 718 alloy treated by conventional heat treatment.
Method· A novel heat treatment (NHT) comprising homogenization at 1150°C for 2h and single aging at 700°C for 12h was developed to modify precipitate morphology and promote recrystallization.
Result· The NHT increased plasticity by 41% while maintaining ultimate tensile strength comparable to traditional heat treatment.
Validation· Tensile testing and microstructural characterization (SEM, EBSD) were conducted to validate the mechanical properties and underlying mechanisms.
