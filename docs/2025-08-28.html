Hip Hemiarthroplasty vs Total Hip Arthroplasty for Femoral Neck Fractures: Reverse Fragility of Reoperation Rates in Randomized Controlled Trials

Joseph McCafferty, Jack T. Bragg, Kevin Quindlen, Alexis Driscoll, Nicholas R. Pagani et al. Arthroplasty Today 2025-08-28 10.1016/j.artd.2025.101805
单位: Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Tufts Medical Center, Boston, MA, USA
通讯: Joseph McCafferty @ Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Tufts Medical Center, Boston, MA, USA
相关性: 该论文仅比较临床再手术率，不涉及生物力学或植入物设计。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, no implant design, no FEA, no stress shielding or micromotion analysis.
动机· 评估髋关节置换术再手术率差异的统计稳健性。
方法· 使用反向脆弱性指数分析随机对照试验。
结果· 多数试验的阴性结果脆弱，反转少量患者结果即变显著。
验证· 文献荟萃分析，无实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To assess the statistical robustness of randomized controlled trials reporting non-significant differences in reoperation rates between hip hemiarthroplasty and total hip arthroplasty.
Method· The reverse fragility index (RFI) was calculated for each included trial, comparing the number of patients needed to reverse the non-significant result to the number lost to follow-up.
Result· Six of nine studies had a loss to follow-up greater than or equal to their RFI, indicating that the reported non-significance is fragile.
Validation· No experimental validation; the study is a re-analysis of published clinical trial data.
