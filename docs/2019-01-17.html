边界: This paper focuses on acetabular cup anteversion measurement, not on femoral stem stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics.
动机· 提出一种新的髋臼杯前倾角放射学测量方法。
方法· 基于标准前后位X光片，利用几何关系计算前倾角。
结果· 新方法测量准确，与CT测量结果高度一致。
验证· 与CT测量结果进行临床对比验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a new radiographic method for measuring acetabular cup anteversion in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A geometric approach using standard anteroposterior radiographs to calculate anteversion angle.
Result· The proposed method shows high accuracy and strong agreement with CT-based measurements.
Validation· Clinical validation against CT measurements in a patient cohort.
