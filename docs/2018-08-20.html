Estimation of Residual Stress in Selective Laser Melting of a Zr-Based Amorphous Alloy

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only simulation_case Exp. validation
Xing Wei, Di Ouyang, Ning Li, Lin Liu Materials 2018-08-20 10.3390/ma11081480
单位: School of Materials Science and Engineering, and State key Lab for Materials Processing and Die &amp; mold Technology, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan 430074, China
通讯: Di Ouyang @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, and State key Lab for Materials Processing and Die &amp; mold Technology, H  |  Ning Li @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, and State key Lab for Materials Processing and Die &amp; mold Technology, H
相关性: 该论文研究非晶合金的残余应力，与生物医学植入物的多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能无直接关联。
边界: No porous structure, no stress shielding or stiffness matching, no biomedical context, no fatigue testing, no surface roughness or osseointegration.
动机· 准确估计选区激光熔化非晶合金的残余应力以防止疲劳失效。
方法· 结合有限元法、实验和理论分析，研究扫描策略和工艺参数对残余应力的影响。
结果· XY交叉扫描策略残余应力较低，残余应力随棒厚增加而增大，可通过退火或预热基板调控。
验证· 通过实验测量与模拟结果对比验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To accurately estimate residual stresses in selective laser melted amorphous alloys to prevent premature fatigue failure.
Method· A finite element method combined with experiments and theoretical analyses was used to study the effects of scanning strategy and process parameters on residual stress.
Result· The XY cross scanning strategy yields lower residual stress, which increases with bar thickness and can be tuned by annealing or substrate preheating.
Validation· Experimental measurements were compared with simulation results for validation.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To systematically review clinical outcomes and complication rates of total hip arthroplasty in haemophilic patients.
Method· A systematic review of 11 case reports/series and 9 original articles encompassing 206 patients and 226 THAs.
Result· 95% of implants were uncemented; mean follow-up was 73 months with acceptable complication rates.
Validation· No experimental or computational validation; purely a literature review.

Immunoglobulin G4-Related Disease of the Hip

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore none
Wataru Ando, Futoshi Yukioka, Kengo Yamamoto, Tsuyoshi Koyama, Yoshichika Hashimoto et al. Orthopedics 2018-08-20 10.3928/01477447-20180815-06
相关性: 该论文仅报告一例髋关节IgG4相关疾病病例，未涉及任何生物力学分析或植入物设计。
边界: This paper is a clinical case report on IgG4-related disease of the hip, with no biomechanical modeling, implant design, FEA, or stress shielding analysis.
动机· 报告首例髋关节IgG4相关疾病病例
方法· 通过实验室检查、MRI及术后免疫组化确诊
结果· 术后炎症指标下降，IgG4水平降低
验证· 临床病例报告，无实验验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To report the first case of immunoglobulin G4-related disease (IgG4-RD) of the hip joint.
Method· Diagnosis was confirmed through laboratory tests (elevated CRP, IgG, IgG4), MRI, and immunohistochemical analysis of resected tissue during total hip arthroplasty.
Result· Postoperative inflammatory markers normalized and IgG4 levels decreased from 318 to 152 mg/dL at 5 months.
Validation· Single case report with no experimental or numerical validation.
