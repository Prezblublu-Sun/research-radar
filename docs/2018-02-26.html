单位: Orthopaedic Bioengineering Research Laboratory, Department of Mechanical Engineering and School of Biomedical Engineering, Colorado State University, 1374 Campus Delivery, 200 W Lake St, Fort Collins,
通讯: Kirk C. McGilvray @ Orthopaedic Bioengineering Research Laboratory, Department of Mechanical Engineering and School of Biomedical Engineerin
相关性: 该论文直接评估了3D打印多孔钛合金椎间融合器的骨长入能力，与用户关注的AM生物医学植入物方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not quantify stress shielding or stiffness matching, nor does it report fatigue testing or surface roughness characterization.
动机· 比较不同椎间融合器材料的骨长入能力，为临床选材提供依据。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To provide evidence-based comparison of bone ingrowth potential among different interbody cage materials for spinal fusion.
Method· An ovine lumbar fusion model was used to compare PEEK, plasma-sprayed porous titanium-coated PEEK, and 3D-printed porous titanium alloy cages via biomechanical testing, micro-CT, and histomorphometry.
Result· The 3D-printed porous titanium cage significantly reduced range of motion, increased bone ingrowth volume, and enhanced construct stiffness at 16 weeks.
Validation· In vivo animal study with biomechanical, micro-CT, and histological validation.

通讯: Zhen Li @ Division of Applied Mathematics, Brown University, Providence, RI 02912, USA  |  Jie Ouyang @ Department of Applied Mathematics, Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi'an 710129, China
相关性: 该论文通过主动学习从介观动力学构建宏观本构模型，其方法可迁移至生物力学中替代昂贵有限元模拟。
边界: The paper focuses on non-Newtonian fluid flows, not on solid mechanics or implant biomechanics, and lacks uncertainty quantification.

Motivation· To efficiently construct macroscopic constitutive models from mesoscopic simulations, reducing computational cost for non-Newtonian flows.
Method· An active learning framework iteratively selects informative mesoscopic simulation points to train a surrogate constitutive model.
Result· The active learning surrogate accurately predicts macroscopic flow behavior with significantly reduced computational expense.
Validation· Validated on 2D Poiseuille flow and lid-driven cavity flow cases, demonstrating accuracy and efficiency.
