Fatigue life prediction of additively manufactured material: Effects of surface roughness, defect size, and shape

Aref Yadollahi, Mohammad J. Mahtabi, Ali Khalili, Haley Doude, James C. Newman Fatigue & Fracture of Engineering Materials & Structures 2018-03-07 10.1111/ffe.12799
单位: Center for Advanced Vehicular Systems (CAVS) Mississippi State University Starkville MS 39762 USA
通讯: Aref Yadollahi @ Center for Advanced Vehicular Systems (CAVS) Mississippi State University Starkville MS 39762 USA
相关性: 该论文直接研究增材制造材料的疲劳寿命预测，涉及表面粗糙度和缺陷尺寸形状的影响，与用户关注的LPBF制造、疲劳测试及表面形貌高度相关。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures, stress shielding, or osseointegration; it focuses on fatigue life prediction for dense AM materials (Inconel 718) rather than biomedical implants.
动机· 预测增材制造材料疲劳寿命，考虑工艺缺陷和表面粗糙度的影响。
方法· 基于裂纹闭合的疲劳裂纹扩展模型，结合半圆/椭圆表面缺陷假设。
结果· 利用缺陷尺寸和表面轮廓参数可有效预测疲劳寿命的分散性。
验证· 使用文献中LPBF Inconel 718的疲劳测试数据验证模型。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To predict the fatigue life of additively manufactured materials by accounting for process-induced voids and surface roughness, which are the most life-limiting failure mechanisms.
Method· A crack closure-based fatigue crack growth model was employed, assuming semicircular/elliptical surface flaws to represent defects and surface profile features.
Result· The statistical distribution of defect size and shape, along with the maximum valley depth of the surface profile, enables prediction of fatigue life scatter in AM materials.
Validation· The model was validated against experimental fatigue data from literature on laser powder bed fused Inconel 718 specimens with machined and as-built surfaces.
