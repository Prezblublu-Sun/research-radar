Total hip arthroplasty using a three-dimensional porous titanium acetabular cup: an examination of micromotion using subject-specific finite element analysis

单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Gifu University School of Medicine, Yanagido 1-1, Gifu, 501-1194, Japan

相关性: 该论文聚焦于髋臼杯的微动，而非股骨柄，因此与用户关于股骨柄应力遮挡和微动的研究方向关系不大。
边界: This paper focuses on acetabular cup micromotion, not femoral stem stress shielding or micromotion at the stem-bone interface, and does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain in Gruen zones.
动机· 分析3D多孔钛髋臼杯的微动与放射透亮线的关系。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the relationship between micromotion at the cup-bone interface and radiolucent lines in THA using a 3D porous titanium cup.
Method· Subject-specific finite element models were built from CT data to compute micromotion at the cup-bone interface.
Result· Micromotion was significantly larger at DeLee-Charnley Zone 3 and correlated with the occurrence of radiolucent lines at that zone.
Validation· Clinical outcomes (JOA scores, radiological assessment) were compared with FE predictions to validate the correlation.
