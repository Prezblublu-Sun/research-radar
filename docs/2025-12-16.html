Causal Structure Learning for Dynamical Systems with Theoretical Score Analysis

Nicholas Tagliapietra, Katharina Ensinger, Christoph Zimmer, Osman Mian arXiv preprint 2025-12-16 link
相关性: 该论文研究动态系统的因果结构发现，与代理建模和生物力学设计无关。
边界: This paper focuses on causal discovery in dynamical systems, with no surrogate modeling, FEA, uncertainty quantification, or application to implant/biomechanics design.
动机· 现有因果发现方法忽略连续时间动力学或处理不规则采样效果差。
方法· 提出CaDyT，基于差分因果模型和高斯过程推断连续时间动态因果结构。
结果· 在规则和不规则采样数据上均优于现有方法，发现更接近真实动态的因果网络。
验证· 通过合成和现实世界动态系统数据集进行基准测试。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Existing causal discovery methods either discretize time, degrading performance on irregularly sampled data, or ignore the underlying continuous dynamics.
Method· CaDyT employs a difference-based causal model with exact Gaussian Process inference to learn causal structures in continuous-time dynamical systems.
Result· The method outperforms state-of-the-art causal discovery techniques on both regularly and irregularly sampled data, recovering networks closer to the true dynamics.
Validation· Validation is performed on synthetic and real-world dynamical system benchmarks with known ground truth causal graphs.
