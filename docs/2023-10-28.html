Validation of parameter for bioink Hydroxyapatite/Gelatin Scaffold by 3D extrusion bio-printing for orthopedic surgery

Low AI for Bioprinting openalex Peripheral Background only in_vitro Exp. validation
Kamolchat Tappaviboon, Ekapon Ketbunlu, Alongkorn Pimpin, Sarita Morakul, Ratchatin Chancharoen et al. 2023-10-28 10.1109/bmeicon60347.2023.10322099
单位: Chulalongkorn University,Undergraduate School of Mechanical Engineering,Bangkok,Thailand
通讯: Kamolchat Tappaviboon @ Chulalongkorn University,Undergraduate School of Mechanical Engineering,Bangkok,Thailand
相关性: 该论文研究了挤出生物打印参数和生物相容性，但未涉及任何AI或机器学习技术，因此与用户关注的人工智能生物打印方向关系不大。
边界: This paper does not embed AI or machine learning into any stage of the bioprinting workflow; it is a purely experimental parameter optimization and biocompatibility study.
动机· 验证挤出3D生物打印羟基磷灰石/明胶复合生物墨水的打印参数和生物相容性。
方法· 改变打印速度、压力和温度，评估孔隙率和可打印性，并用MTT法检测细胞活力。
结果· 打印速度与可打印性正相关，压力与可打印性负相关，1%羟基磷灰石/20%明胶生物墨水具有良好的生物相容性但细胞增殖较低。
验证· 通过细胞活力实验和MTT实验进行体外生物相容性验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To validate the printing parameters and biocompatibility of hydroxyapatite/gelatin composite bioink for 3D extrusion bioprinting in orthopedic surgery.
Method· Printing parameters (velocity, pressure, temperature) were varied to evaluate pore ratio and printability, and in vitro cell viability was assessed via MTT assay.
Result· Print speed positively correlated with printability while pressure negatively correlated, and a 1% hydroxyapatite/20% gelatin bioink showed good biocompatibility but lower cell proliferation compared to control.
Validation· In vitro cytotoxicity and cell viability assays using osteoblast and fibroblast cells.

通讯: Eric Gärtner @ Faculty of Production Engineering, University of Bremen, Badgasteiner Strasse 1, 28359, Bremen, Germany
相关性: 该论文研究FeMnSi形状记忆合金的激光粉末床熔融工艺，未涉及骨科植入物的应力遮挡、多孔结构或疲劳性能，与用户研究方向无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue testing, surface roughness, or osseointegration typical of biomedical implants; it focuses on the influence of Mn content on shape memory characteristics during LPBF.
动机· 研究铁基形状记忆合金在激光粉末床熔融中的可打印性及其记忆效应。
方法· 通过气雾化制备不同锰含量的FeMnSi合金粉末，并使用LPBF进行3D打印，利用膨胀法表征形状记忆特性。
结果· 锰含量降低提高了形状记忆效应，并影响锁孔熔融模式的起始点。
验证· 采用膨胀法实验测量形状记忆特性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Investigate the suitability of iron-based shape memory alloy powder for laser powder bed fusion and the effect of manganese content on printability and shape memory effect.
Method· FeMnSi alloy powders with varying manganese contents (23.6% and 28.5%) were atomized and processed via LPBF; shape memory characteristics were analyzed using dilatometry.
Result· A reduction in manganese content enhanced the shape memory effect and influenced the onset of keyhole fusion mode during LPBF.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed through dilatometry measurements of shape memory recovery.
