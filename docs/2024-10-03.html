Achieving Generalization in Orchestrating GNSS Interference Monitoring Stations Through Pseudo-Labeling

Exclude FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Ignore none
Lucas Heublein, Tobias Feigl, Alexander Rügamer, Felix Ott arXiv preprint 2024-10-03 link
相关性: 该论文研究GNSS干扰分类，与FEA及代理建模方向无关。
边界: This paper does not involve finite element analysis, surrogate modeling, or any biomechanical or implant design context.
动机· 减少GNSS干扰分类中标注数据需求。
方法· 采用伪标签和不确定性投票的半监督方法。
结果· 仅需5%标注数据即实现高泛化性。
验证· 从室内迁移到实际场景验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· The need to reduce reliance on labeled data for robust GNSS interference classification in diverse environments.
Method· A semi-supervised approach combining pseudo-labeling with Monte Carlo Dropout and Deep Ensemble uncertainty voting.
Result· Achieves high generalization across environments while requiring less than 5% labeled training data.
Validation· Validation includes transfer from indoor lab to real-world highway deployments.
