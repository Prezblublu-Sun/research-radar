A reduced-order modeling for efficient design study of artificial valve in enlarged ventricular outflow tracts

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Alfonso Caiazzo, Romain Guibert, Irène Vignon-Clémentel Computer Methods in Biomechanics & Biomedical Engineering 2016-01-07 10.1080/10255842.2015.1133811
单位: a Weierstrass Institute for Applied Analysis and Stochastics (WIAS) , Berlin , Germany
通讯: Alfonso Caiazzo @ a Weierstrass Institute for Applied Analysis and Stochastics (WIAS) , Berlin , Germany
相关性: 该论文使用降阶模型（POD）加速人工瓣膜设计中的血流动力学模拟，其方法可迁移至植入物力学仿真中的代理建模。
边界: This paper focuses on hemodynamics of a cardiovascular device, not on solid mechanics or stress shielding of orthopedic implants, and lacks contact mechanics or bone-implant interface modeling.
动机· 为扩大右心室流出道设计人工瓣膜支架，需高效模拟血流动力学。
方法· 基于本征正交分解构建降阶模型，以几何参数为变量快速预测流场。
结果· 降阶模型与全阶CFD结果吻合，可高效用于设计参数研究。
验证· 通过对比降阶模型与全阶CFD的力与流场结果进行验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To enable efficient hemodynamic simulation for designing a reducer stent in enlarged right ventricular outflow tracts.
Method· A reduced-order model based on proper orthogonal decomposition is constructed from full CFD solutions on a reference geometry, then used to predict flow fields for varying diameters.
Result· The reduced-order model accurately reproduces forces on the valve and reducer compared to full CFD, enabling rapid design space exploration.
Validation· Validation is performed by comparing reduced-order model predictions of forces and flow fields against full CFD simulations for multiple geometric configurations.

Probing off-Hugoniot states in Ta, Cu, and Al to 1000 GPa compression with magnetically driven liner implosions

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only simulation_case Exp. validation UQ
R. W. Lemke, D. H. Dolan, Devon Gardner Dalton, Justin Brown, K. Tomlinson et al. Journal of Applied Physics 2016-01-07 10.1063/1.4939675
单位: Sandia National Laboratories 1 , PO Box 5800, Albuquerque, New Mexico 87185-1189, USA
通讯: R. W. Lemke @ Sandia National Laboratories 1 , PO Box 5800, Albuquerque, New Mexico 87185-1189, USA
相关性: 该论文涉及极端高压下的材料状态方程，与生物力学植入物设计或FEA代理模型无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on high-pressure physics and equation-of-state calibration for metals, with no FEA surrogate modeling, no biomedical context, no implant mechanics, and no constraint handling or UQ for design optimization.
动机· 获取固体金属在极端压力下的非Hugoniot状态数据。
方法· 利用磁驱动衬套内爆结合光子多普勒测速和数学优化推断压力-密度关系。
结果· 在Al/Ta、Al/Cu和Al衬套实验中实现了约1000 GPa的准等熵压缩，观察到200 GPa以上材料软化。
验证· 通过蒙特卡洛不确定性量化合成数据建立收敛准则，并用实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To obtain off-Hugoniot pressure-density data for solid metals under extreme compression using a novel magnetically driven liner implosion technique.
Method· The method combines photonic Doppler velocimetry measurements of implosion and explosion velocities with magnetohydrodynamic simulation and mathematical optimization to infer the current and the sample's pressure-density state.
Result· Symmetric liner implosions achieved quasi-isentropic compression to ~1000 GPa in Al/Ta, Al/Cu, and Al liners, revealing unexpectedly softer behavior above 200 GPa.
Validation· Validation was performed via Monte Carlo uncertainty quantification on synthetic data and experimental results from the Z-machine.
