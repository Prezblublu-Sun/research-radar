相关性: 该论文关注股骨头坏死的血液循环障碍，而非髋关节植入物或股骨柄的力学性能。
边界: This paper focuses on circulatory obstruction in osteonecrosis of the femoral head, with no quantification of stress shielding, micromotion, or any biomechanical analysis of hip implants.
动机· 评估股骨头坏死不同阶段的血液循环状态以指导治疗。
方法· 结合DSA和MRI分析不同ARCO分期的循环障碍特征。
结果· 早期以静脉淤滞为主，晚期以动脉缺血为主。
验证· 临床影像与手术结果对照。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate circulatory obstruction in different stages of nontraumatic osteonecrosis of the femoral head for guiding treatment.
Method· Combined digital subtraction angiography and magnetic resonance imaging to analyze circulatory status across ARCO stages.
Result· Early-stage NONFH shows venous stasis, while advanced stages exhibit arterial ischemia.
Validation· Clinical imaging and surgical outcomes were correlated.
