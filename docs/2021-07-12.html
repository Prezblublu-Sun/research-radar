单位: Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, Korea University Guro Hospital, Seoul 08308, Korea
通讯: Ui‐Lyong Lee @ Department of Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery, Chungang University Hospital, Seoul 03080, Korea
相关性: 论文研究了多孔钛支架的骨形成能力，但未涉及应力遮挡或刚度匹配，与用户关注点部分相关。
边界: This paper does not quantify stress shielding, stiffness matching, or fatigue performance, and lacks detailed mechanical testing results linking porous structure to implant stability.
