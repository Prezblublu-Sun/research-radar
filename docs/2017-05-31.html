动机· 探讨清创时机对髋关节假体周围感染治疗效果的影响。
方法· 回顾性分析38例感染患者，按手术时间分组比较成功率。

验证· 临床回顾性数据分析。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate whether earlier irrigation and debridement improves success rates for periprosthetic hip infections.
Method· A retrospective review of 38 patients with PJI was conducted, stratifying by time from index procedure (≤4, 4–8, ≥8 weeks) and analyzing treatment success.
Result· Successful I&Ds occurred on average 41.9 days post-THA versus 51.6 days for failures, but the difference was not statistically significant; obesity was associated with higher failure risk.
Validation· Clinical retrospective cohort analysis with statistical comparison.
