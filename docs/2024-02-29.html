单位: Department of Orthopaedics and Traumatology, Başkent University Faculty of Medicine, Konya, Turkey.
相关性: 该论文研究了动力髋部板螺钉系统，而非股骨柄植入物，与用户方向的直接相关性较低。
边界: This paper does not model a femoral stem implant, does not quantify micromotion or contact mechanics at the bone-implant interface, and does not compare intact versus implanted femur.
动机· 研究不同长度动力髋部板的应力分布以减少应力遮挡
方法· 基于CT的人体股骨近端模型进行有限元分析
结果· 短板导致钢板和皮质螺钉应力集中
验证· 仅有仿真模拟，无实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to analyze stress distributions in dynamic hip plate screw systems of varying lengths to mitigate stress shielding during fracture healing.
Method· A 3D proximal femur model reconstructed from CT scans was used in finite element analysis to evaluate plates with 2, 4, and 6 holes in an intertrochanteric fracture configuration.
Result· Shorter plates (2-hole) produced higher stress concentrations on the plate and cortical screws, particularly at the lower screw-cortex interface.
Validation· The results are based solely on finite element simulation without experimental or clinical validation.

Biomechanical analysis of titanium-alloy and biodegradable implants in dual plate osteosynthesis for AO/ASIF type 33-C2 fractures.

Mengmeng Hu, Meng Li, Rui Ma, Xiaoya Li, Xiaomeng Ren et al. Heliyon 2024-02-29 10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e26213
单位: Senior Department of Orthopedics, the Fourth Medical Center of PLA General Hospital, No. 51 Fucheng Road, Beijing, 100048, China.
通讯: Wei Zhang @ Senior Department of Orthopedics, the Fourth Medical Center of PLA General Hospital, No. 51 Fucheng Road, Beijing, 10004 [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究远端股骨骨折双钢板固定，与髋关节假体及股骨柄方向无关。
边界: This paper deals with distal femur fracture fixation using plates, not hip implant stress shielding or micromotion for femoral stems.
动机· 评估不同材料在骨质疏松性远端股骨骨折中的力学效果。
方法· 构建33-C2型骨折有限元模型，比较钛合金、可降解材料及组合使用。
结果· 双可降解板在低模量下有利于早期活动，但承载能力有限；钛合金板满足强度要求但存在应力遮挡。
验证· 通过有限元仿真对比多种加载条件下的刚度、应力与骨折微动。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To assess the biomechanical performance of titanium alloy and biodegradable materials in dual plate fixation for osteoporotic distal femur fractures.
Method· Finite element models of AO/ASIF type 33-C2 fractures were constructed to compare LISS+TAP, LISS+BP, and DBP constructs under five loading conditions.
Result· Double biodegradable plates showed lower stress in plates and femur but higher flexibility, while titanium-alloy constructs provided higher stiffness but increased stress shielding.
Validation· Simulated mechanical testing of structural stiffness, fracture micromotion, and implant stress.

Parametric Design of Porous Structure and Optimal Porosity Gradient Distribution Based on Root-Shaped Implants.

Lijian Liu, Shaobo Ma, Yongkang Zhang, Shouxiao Zhu, Shuxuan Wu et al. Materials (Basel, Switzerland) 2024-02-29 10.3390/ma17051137
单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, Hebei University of Science and Technology, Shijiazhuang 050018, China.
通讯: Guang Yang @ School of Mechanical Engineering, Hebei University of Science and Technology, Shijiazhuang 050018, China. [推断]
相关性: 论文研究牙种植体多孔结构设计，与髋关节植入物方向无关。
边界: This paper focuses on dental root-shaped implants, not hip/femoral implants; does not address femoral stem stress shielding, Gruen zones, or hip biomechanics.
动机· 减少牙种植体应力遮挡，改善生物相容性与长期稳定性。
方法· 参数化设计多孔根形种植体，利用孔隙率与偏置拟合关系控制梯度分布。
结果· 边缘孔隙率20-35%时弹性模量最大降46.6%，中心孔隙率50-90%时渗透率最大增61.2%。
验证· 通过模拟种植体-骨界面微动验证力学性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To reduce stress shielding and improve biocompatibility and long-term stability of porous root-shaped dental implants.
Method· A parametric design method was used to control porosity gradient distribution via fitting relationships between porosity and bias, combined with position functions.
Result· Marginal porosity (20-35%) reduced elastic modulus by up to 46.6%, while central porosity (50-90%) enhanced permeability by up to 61.2%.
Validation· Biomechanical properties were validated through simulated implant-bone interface micromotion values.
