Physics-Informed Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks for multi-material elasticity problems in electronic packaging

Yanpeng Gong, Yida He, Yue Mei, Xiaoying Zhuang, Fei Qin et al. arXiv preprint 2025-08-23 10.1016/j.apm.2026.116793
相关性: 该方法使用物理信息KAN处理多材料弹性问题，其处理材料不连续性的能力可转移到生物力学植入物分析中。
边界: This paper does not address implant-specific biomechanics, uncertainty quantification, or constrained optimization; it focuses on electronic packaging, not biomedical design.
动机· 使用单一网络处理多材料弹性问题中的材料不连续性

结果· 在数值实验中实现了高精度和鲁棒性

English summary & terms
Motivation· To solve multi-material elasticity problems with a single neural network while handling material property discontinuities.
Method· Proposes a Physics-Informed Kolmogorov-Arnold Network (PIKAN) with energy-based formulation and trainable B-spline activation functions.
Result· The proposed method achieves excellent accuracy and robustness in numerical multi-material elasticity experiments.
Validation· Validated through numerical benchmark problems for multi-material elasticity in electronic packaging.

单位: Shanxi Key Laboratory of Controlled Metal Solidification and Precision Manufacturing, School of Materials Science and Engineering, North University of China, Taiyuan Shanxi 030051, P R China
通讯: Shenghua Zhang @ Shanxi Key Laboratory of Controlled Metal Solidification and Precision Manufacturing, School of Materials Science and En  |  Senming Zhang @ Shanxi Key Laboratory of Controlled Metal Solidification and Precision Manufacturing, School of Materials Science and En

Method· Different heat treatments were applied to SLM-fabricated Al-Cu-Mn-Mg-Zr alloy, followed by high-temperature tensile testing and microstructural characterization.
Result· Heat treatment significantly influenced microstructure and high-temperature mechanical properties, with optimized parameters enhancing strength.
Validation· Validation was performed through scanning electron microscopy and high-temperature tensile tests.

3D Printing of Cellular Scaffold as In Vitro Cancer Model

Zeynab Tahir, Hui Leng Choo, Yin How Wong, Asseel Hisham Alregib 2025-08-23 10.1109/icraie65839.2025.11239130
单位: Taylor&#x2019;s University,School of Engineering, Faculty of Innovation and Technology,Subang Jaya,Malaysia,47500
通讯: Zeynab Tahir @ Taylor&#x2019;s University,School of Engineering, Faculty of Innovation and Technology,Subang Jaya,Malaysia,47500
相关性: 该论文使用水凝胶制造癌症模型，与生物医学金属植入物的LPBF制造和力学性能无关。
边界: This paper focuses on hydrogel bioprinting for cancer models, not on LPBF fabrication of metallic implants, mechanical testing, fatigue, or stress shielding.
动机· 开发3D打印细胞支架以改进体外癌症模型
方法· 使用GelMa水凝胶和LAP光引发剂通过桶光聚合打印支架
结果· 确定了最佳LAP浓度和曝光时间，TPMS支架促进细胞附着
验证· 通过乳腺癌细胞培养验证细胞附着和生长
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to design and 3D print a cellular scaffold as an in vitro breast cancer model to overcome the limitations of 2D cultures.
Method· GelMa hydrogel scaffolds with varying LAP photoinitiator concentrations and light exposure times were fabricated via vat photopolymerization, using cubic and TPMS designs.
Result· Optimal fabrication parameters were identified, and TPMS scaffolds showed improved cancer cell attachment and growth compared to cubic scaffolds.
Validation· Cell attachment and proliferation were validated with breast cancer cell cultures over 1-2 weeks.
