通讯: Jibanananda Satpathy @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, VCU Health, Richmond, VA, USA
相关性: 该论文仅讨论髋关节骨关节炎的影像学诊断，未涉及任何植入物力学或有限元分析。
边界: This paper does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or any biomechanical analysis; it is purely a clinical diagnosis study with no implant mechanics or finite element modeling.
动机· 后内侧髋关节骨关节炎难以通过常规X光片诊断，需要新的影像学标志。

English summary & terms
Motivation· Posteromedial hip osteoarthritis is difficult to detect on standard radiographs, motivating the search for new radiographic signs.
Method· A case series of 19 patients was analyzed using plain films, MRI, and intraoperative findings to describe the divergent sign.
Result· The divergent sign correlated with posteromedial OA confirmed by MRI and surgery; 17 patients underwent successful THA.
Validation· Validation was performed against MRI and intraoperative findings as gold standards.
