边界: This paper focuses on biochemical surface modification and in vivo bone regeneration, but does not include porous structure design, LPBF fabrication, mechanical testing, or fatigue analysis.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the effects of immobilizing rhBMP-2 and/or rhPDGF-BB on heparinized titanium implants on in vivo osseointegration and vertical bone regeneration.

Validation· In vivo animal study using beagle dogs with quantitative micro CT and histological analysis.
