Motivation· To evaluate the validity of the ProPublica Surgeon Scorecard by examining its case exclusion and complication capture rates.
Method· Using ACS-NSQIP data from 2012-2014, the proportion of cases meeting ProPublica criteria and the proportion of inpatient complications were calculated, and risk-adjusted performance was compared to established outcome measures.
Result· ProPublica's specifications excluded 82% of cases, missed 84% of complications, and correlated poorly with established complication measures but strongly with readmission.
Validation· Retrospective analysis of a large clinical database (ACS-NSQIP) with statistical correlation.
