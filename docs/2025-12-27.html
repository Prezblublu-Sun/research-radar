PowderJet: Spherical metal powder production via multi-orifice droplet-on-demand metal jetting

Viktor Sukhotskiy, Jesse Ahlquist, Shahryar Mooraj, Ziheng Wu, Eric S. Elton et al. Additive manufacturing 2025-12-27 10.1016/j.addma.2025.105069
单位: Materials Engineering Division, Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, Livermore, CA 94550
通讯: Viktor Sukhotskiy @ Materials Engineering Division, Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, Livermore, CA 94550
相关性: 该论文专注于金属粉末生产，与生物医学植入物的增材制造、应力屏蔽、疲劳测试或骨整合无直接关系。
边界: This paper describes a metal powder production method and does not address porous implant structures, stress shielding, LPBF fabrication with mechanical testing, fatigue behavior, or surface roughness for osseointegration.
动机· 传统气体雾化制粉存在收得率低、球形度差等问题，需新型制粉技术。
方法· 采用多孔喷嘴电磁脉冲液滴按需喷射（Droplet-on-demand）平台制备球形金属粉末。
结果· 成功制备Al4008和Cu110合金粉末，平均直径200µm，球形度>0.95，收得率高。
验证· 结合实验和CFD模拟验证粉末制备过程及可扩展性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Conventional gas atomization for metal powder production suffers from low yield, asphericity, and porosity, motivating a more controlled droplet-on-demand approach.
Method· PowderJet uses electromagnetic pulses to eject molten metal droplets from a multi-orifice nozzle, enabling precise control over powder size and distribution.
Result· Aluminum and copper powders with mean diameter 200 µm, narrow size distribution, and roundness >0.95 were produced, with CFD indicating scalability to finer particles at higher rates.
Validation· Experimental production combined with computational fluid dynamics simulations validated the system's performance and scalability.

Microstructure dependent electrochemical corrosion behavior of SLM inconel 625 alloys in the presence of a molten KCl–Na2SO4 film at 600 °C in air

Xiaobao Zhou, Qilin Wang, Yanjie Ren, Lili Cao, Xiaoxiao Li et al. Journal of Materials Research and Technology 2025-12-27 10.1016/j.jmrt.2025.12.226
单位: School of Intelligent Manufacturing and Energy Engineering, Zhejiang University of Science & Technology, Hangzhou, 310023, China
通讯: Yanjie Ren @ School of Energy & Power Engineering, Changsha University of Science & Technology, Changsha, 410114, China  |  Taijun Pan @ School of Intelligent Manufacturing and Energy Engineering, Zhejiang University of Science & Technology, Hangzhou, 31002
相关性: 该论文研究SLM Inconel 625在高温熔盐中的腐蚀行为，与生物医学植入物、应力遮挡或力学性能无关。
边界: This paper focuses on hot corrosion of SLM IN625 in molten salt, with no biomedical context, no implant design, no mechanical testing, and no osseointegration or stress shielding analysis.
动机· 研究SLM IN625合金在熔盐环境中的腐蚀行为与微观结构的关系。
方法· 通过电化学阻抗谱分析不同热处理状态的SLM IN625在600°C熔盐中的腐蚀。
结果· 发现高亚晶界和低角度晶界比例有助于形成致密保护性氧化层。
验证· 电化学阻抗谱实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Investigate the influence of microstructure on the hot corrosion behavior of SLM IN625 alloys in molten salt environments.
Method· Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy was used to characterize corrosion of as-built and heat-treated SLM IN625 beneath a KCl-Na2SO4 film at 600°C.
Result· High fractions of sub-boundaries and low-angle grain boundaries promoted formation of compact protective oxide scales.
Validation· Experimental validation through electrochemical impedance spectroscopy.

Comparative Fractal Analysis on Orthopantomography Images of Bone Remodeling Changes at 3 Months Between Natural Socket Healing and Immediate Implant Placement.

Andrei Radu, Mihaela Ionescu, Antonia Samia Khaddour, Cristina Maria Munteanu, Monica Mihaela Iacov Crăițoiu et al. Diagnostics (Basel, Switzerland) 2025-12-27 10.3390/diagnostics16010096
单位: Department of Oral Rehabilitation, University of Medicine and Pharmacy of Craiova, 200349 Craiova, Romania.
通讯: Sanda Mihaela Popescu @ Department of Oral Rehabilitation, University of Medicine and Pharmacy of Craiova, 200349 Craiova, Romania. [推断]
相关性: 该论文为牙科种植术后骨愈合的影像学分析，未涉及增材制造、多孔结构或力学测试。
边界: This paper does not cover LPBF fabrication, porous structure design, mechanical testing (static or fatigue), or surface morphology relevant to biomedical implant manufacturing.
动机· 比较自然愈合和即刻种植后牙槽骨重塑的差异
方法· 回顾性分析91例拔牙患者OPG图像，用分形分析评估骨愈合
结果· 即刻种植组骨愈合更快，下颌优于上颌
验证· 临床影像数据统计对比
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare alveolar bone remodeling after tooth extraction between natural healing and immediate implant placement using fractal analysis.
Method· Fractal dimension analysis was retrospectively performed on OPG images from 91 patients at pre-extraction and 3 months post-surgery.
Result· Immediate implant placement showed faster and better bone healing, especially in the mandible.
Validation· Clinical retrospective study with statistical analysis of fractal measurements.
