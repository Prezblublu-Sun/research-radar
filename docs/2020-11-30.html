Motivation· To describe functional outcomes and predictors of conversion to total hip arthroplasty in elderly patients with operatively managed acetabular fractures.
Method· A retrospective registry-based study of 80 patients aged ≥60 years, using survival analysis and multivariate regression to identify factors associated with THA conversion.
