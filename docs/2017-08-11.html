Fear of Falling Restricts Activities of Daily Living after Total Hip Arthroplasty: A One-Year Longitudinal Study

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore none
Koutatsu Nagai, Hisashi Ikutomo, Keiichi Tagomori, Namika Miura, Tadao Tsuboyama et al. Clinical Gerontologist 2017-08-11 10.1080/07317115.2017.1364682
单位: Department of Physical Therapy, School of Rehabilitation, Hyogo University of Health Sciences, Kobe, Japan
通讯: Koutatsu Nagai @ Department of Physical Therapy, School of Rehabilitation, Hyogo University of Health Sciences, Kobe, Japan
相关性: 该论文研究全髋关节置换术后患者对跌倒的恐惧及其对日常生活活动的限制，未涉及任何生物力学建模或植入物设计。
边界: This paper is a clinical observational study on fear of falling after THA, with no biomechanical modeling, no implant stress/strain analysis, no micromotion, and no FEA.
动机· 探究全髋关节置换术后患者跌倒恐惧的普遍性及其对日常活动的影响。
方法· 对98名患者进行术前及术后3、6、12个月的纵向观察，评估12项日常活动中的跌倒恐惧。
结果· 术后1年仍有超过20%的患者在上下楼梯等活动中感到恐惧，约10%因此限制活动。
验证· 基于患者自我报告的问卷调查，无客观测量或验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine the prevalence and time course of fear of falling and its restriction on activities of daily living after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A one-year longitudinal observational study assessing fear of falling in 12 ADLs preoperatively and at 3, 6, and 12 months post-THA in 98 patients.
Result· Fear of falling decreased over time, but over 20% of patients still feared falling during stair use, sitting/standing from floor, and neighborhood walking at 12 months, with about 10% restricting these activities.
Validation· Patient-reported outcomes without objective biomechanical or clinical measurements.
