Motivation· To determine whether rheumatoid arthritis disease activity and postoperative flares predict worse pain and function one year after total hip or knee arthroplasty.
Method· Prospective cohort study enrolling RA patients before THA/TKA, collecting patient-reported outcomes (HOOS/KOOS) and physician-assessed disease activity (DAS28) preoperatively and at one year, with weekly flare questionnaires for 6 weeks postoperatively.
