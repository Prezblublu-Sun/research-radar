通讯: Alireza Doostan @ University of Colorado Boulder, Boulder, CO
相关性: 该论文提出的双保真度不确定性量化方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模，但未涉及植入物设计或生物力学约束。
边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, structural constraints, or multi-objective optimization; it focuses on heat transfer in a ribbed channel.
动机· 降低热边界条件不确定性传播的计算成本
方法· 使用双保真度RANS模型（低保真Spalart-Allmaras和高保真SST k-ω）结合降基和插值

Motivation· To reduce the computational cost of propagating uncertainty in thermal boundary conditions for heat transfer simulations.
Method· A bi-fidelity approach combining a low-fidelity Spalart-Allmaras model (coarse grid, early termination) and a high-fidelity SST k-ω model, using reduced basis and interpolation.
Result· The bi-fidelity strategy accurately approximates the high-fidelity temperature distribution at random heat flux samples with significantly fewer high-fidelity evaluations.
Validation· Validation is performed by comparing bi-fidelity predictions to high-fidelity solutions at randomly selected uncertain heat flux samples.
