英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the safety and complication rates of 'mix and match' use of universal head-neck adapters in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective study of 306 patients (94.1% revisions) treated with UHNA from 2006 to 2022, analyzing outcomes and risk factors.
Result· The overall complication rate was 19.9%, re-revision rate 14.1%, and dislocation was the most common complication (8.8%).
Validation· Clinical follow-up with survival analysis and statistical risk factor identification.
