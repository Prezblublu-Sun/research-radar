Developing and Validating a High-Throughput Robotic System for the Accelerated Development of Porous Membranes

Hongchen Wang, Sima Zeinali Danalou, Jiahao Zhu, Kenneth Sulimro, Chaewon Lim et al. arXiv preprint 2025-08-14 10.1016/j.memsci.2025.124804
相关性: 该论文涉及多孔膜自动化制造，与生物打印方向无关。
边界: This paper does not involve bioprinting, AI/ML, or any biomedical tissue engineering context.
动机· 多孔膜制备过程繁琐，需加速优化。
方法· 开发全自动机器人平台实现NIPS膜制备与压缩测试。
结果· 自动系统重现了聚合物浓度和湿度对膜性能的影响。

English summary & terms
Motivation· The labor-intensive development of porous polymeric membranes requires automated high-throughput platforms to accelerate fabrication parameter optimization.
Method· A fully automated robotic system integrating solution preparation, blade casting, controlled immersion, and compression testing was developed for nonsolvent-induced phase separation membrane fabrication.
Result· The automated system successfully replicated known effects of polymer concentration and humidity on membrane stiffness and uniformity, demonstrating reliability and reproducibility.
Validation· Compression testing was used as a mechanical characterization method to infer membrane porosity and uniformity, validated against expected trends from literature.

Uncertainty-Aware Prediction of Parkinson's Disease Medication Needs: A Two-Stage Conformal Prediction Approach

Ricardo Diaz-Rincon, Muxuan Liang, Adolfo Ramirez-Zamora, Benjamin Shickel arXiv preprint 2025-08-14 link
相关性: 该论文关注帕金森病用药预测，与用户的有限元代理模型方向无关联。
边界: This paper does not involve finite element analysis, surrogate modeling, or any biomechanical/implant design context; it is a clinical prediction task with no connection to simulation efficiency or structural optimization.
动机· 帕金森病用药管理缺乏不确定性量化，影响临床决策。
方法· 提出两阶段共形预测框架，先判断是否调整剂量再预测变化值。
结果· 提供了有统计保证的预测区间，平衡短期精确与长期范围。
验证· 使用631例住院患者电子健康记录验证覆盖率与区间长度。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Clinical management of Parkinson's disease medication lacks systematic methods that account for prediction uncertainty, hindering adoption of machine learning in practice.
Method· A two-stage conformal prediction framework is developed to first identify patients likely to need medication changes and then predict levodopa equivalent daily dose adjustments.
Result· The framework achieves marginal coverage while producing shorter prediction intervals for short-term forecasts and wider intervals for long-term forecasts.
Validation· Validation is conducted on electronic health records from 631 inpatient admissions at University of Florida Health (2011-2021).
