英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare surgical outcomes between direct anterior and lateral approaches in primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A prospective study of 40 matched patients divided into two groups, evaluating functional scores and complications.
Result· The direct anterior approach showed better early functional recovery and fewer complications than the lateral approach.
Validation· Clinical follow-up at 1, 3, and 9 months with statistical comparison.
