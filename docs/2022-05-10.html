通讯: Ruikun Wang @ School of Mechanical and Electric Engineering, Guangzhou University, Guangzhou, People’s Republic of China  |  Jie Liu @ School of Mechanical and Electric Engineering, Guangzhou University, Guangzhou, People’s Republic of China

Motivation· To demonstrate the utility of imageless computer-assisted navigation in complex revision total hip arthroplasty with distorted anatomy.
Method· A case report of a male patient with post-traumatic arthritis who underwent revision THA using imageless CAN to correct malpositioned acetabular components.
Result· At 3-year follow-up, the patient had no dislocation, indicating that CAN reduces the risk of component malposition and instability.
Validation· Single clinical case with 3-year follow-up.
