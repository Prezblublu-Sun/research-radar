Comparing postoperative outcomes of two fully hydroxyapatite-coated collarless stems in total hip arthroplasty through propensity score matching analysis with 2 years follow-up.

Takashi Imagama, Yuta Matsuki, Takehiro Kaneoka, Takehiro Kawakami, Kazushige Seki et al. Scientific reports 2022-11-21 10.1038/s41598-022-24569-9
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Yamaguchi University Graduate School of Medicine, 1-1-1, Minamikogushi, Ube, 7558505, Japan. taka-ima@interlink.or.jp.
通讯: Takashi Imagama @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Yamaguchi University Graduate School of Medicine, 1-1-1, Minamikogushi, Ube, 7558505,
相关性: 该论文比较了两种全羟基磷灰石涂层无领柄的临床和影像学结果，但未涉及生物力学建模或有限元分析。
边界: Does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain or micromotion via FEA, and provides no patient-specific biomechanical modeling.
动机· 比较两种全HA涂层无领柄在THA中的2年临床和影像学差异。
方法· 采用倾向评分匹配法回顾性比较Corail和Hydra柄的术后结果。
结果· Corail组的下沉和3级以上应力遮挡更少，但临床评分无显著差异。
验证· 通过2年随访的临床和影像学评估进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To determine whether there are significant differences in postoperative outcomes between two fully HA-coated collarless stems used in THA.
Method· A propensity score matching analysis was performed to compare 2-year postoperative data from patients receiving either the Corail or Hydra stem.
Result· The Corail group showed significantly less stem subsidence and lower incidence of severe stress shielding, while clinical outcomes were similarly good.
Validation· Clinical validation was conducted through retrospective analysis of modified Harris hip scores and radiographic evaluations with 2-year follow-up.

Analysis of Texture and Anisotropic Elastic Properties of Additively Manufactured Ni-Base Alloys

Thomas Obermayer, Christian Krempaszky, Ewald Werner Metals 2022-11-21 10.3390/met12111991
单位: School of Engineering and Design, Technische Universität München, Boltzmannstraße 15, 85748 Garching, Germany
通讯: Thomas Obermayer @ School of Engineering and Design, Technische Universität München, Boltzmannstraße 15, 85748 Garching, Germany
相关性: 该论文研究增材制造Ni基合金的织构和弹性各向异性，但与生物医学植入物设计中的应力遮挡、多孔结构或疲劳性能无关。
边界: No porous structures, no stress shielding or stiffness matching analysis, no fatigue testing, no surface roughness or osseointegration data, and no biomedical context.
动机· 探讨增材制造镍基合金的织构导致的弹性各向异性。
方法· 结合力学测试和微观结构表征评估宏观弹性行为。
结果· 多种方法获得的弹性性质一致，并揭示了批次内均匀性。
验证· 力学实验与微观结构数据对比验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of crystallographic texture on macroscopic elastic anisotropy in additively manufactured Ni-base alloys.
Method· Combined mechanical testing (tensile loading and impulse excitation) with microstructure characterization (EBSD and high-energy X-ray diffraction) to determine elastic properties.
Result· The elastic properties obtained from different methods showed consistency, indicating batch homogeneity and the effectiveness of texture-based predictions.
Validation· Mechanical experiments were cross-validated with microstructure-based estimates using single-crystal elastic constants and texture data.

Microstructure, Mechanical, In Vitro Biodegradation, and Antimicrobial Behavior of a Mg-Zn-Ca-Sr/ZrO

Girish Chandra, Ajay Pandey, Sarit Prabha, Khushhali M Pandey ACS applied bio materials 2022-11-21 10.1021/acsabm.2c00525
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology, Bhopal462003, India.
通讯: Khushhali M Pandey @ Department of Biological Science and Engineering, Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology, Bhopal462003, India. [推断]
相关性: 该论文涉及可降解镁合金复合材料，与髋关节植入物应力遮挡和微动分析不直接相关。
边界: does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain in Gruen zones, does not model micromotion with contact/friction, and does not compare intact vs implanted femur for FE analysis.
动机· 开发兼具力学性能和抗菌活性的可降解镁合金复合材料。
方法· 采用粉末冶金法制备不同ZrO2含量的Mg-Zn-Ca-Sr/ZrO2复合材料。
结果· 含2%ZrO2的复合材料具有最佳抗压强度、硬度和最低降解速率。
验证· 通过压缩、硬度和浸泡实验及抗菌敏感性测试进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a biodegradable Mg alloy composite with improved mechanical strength, controlled degradation, and antimicrobial properties for orthopedic applications.
Method· A Mg-Zn-Ca-Sr/ZrO2 composite was fabricated via powder metallurgy with 0, 1, 2, and 3 wt% ZrO2 additions.
Result· The composite with 2% ZrO2 exhibited the highest compressive strength (185.6 MPa), hardness (65.2 HRB), and lowest biodegradation rate (2.76 mm/year).
Validation· Validation was conducted through compression tests, hardness measurements, immersion tests for biodegradation, and disk diffusion antimicrobial assays against Staphylococcus aureus.

A Bionic Testbed for Cardiac Ablation Tools

单位: Department of Biomedical Engineering, University of Minnesota—Twin Cities, Minneapolis, MN 55455, USA
通讯: Michael C. McAlpine @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Minnesota—Twin Cities, Minneapolis, MN 55455, USA  |  Brenda M. Ogle @ Department of Biomedical Engineering, University of Minnesota—Twin Cities, Minneapolis, MN 55455, USA
相关性: 该论文涉及生物打印心脏组织用于消融测试，但未集成人工智能技术于生物打印流程中。
边界: This paper does not integrate AI or ML into any bioprinting workflow stage; it focuses on a bionic testbed for ablation tools without autonomous decision-making or model training.
动机· 开发用于评估心脏消融工具的大型仿生组织测试平台
方法· 3D生物打印人类心脏组织盘并耦合水凝胶压力传感器，应用冷冻消融导管
结果· 发现心肌细胞对消融的反应与施加压力密切相关，存活细胞形态改变且连接中断
验证· 体外实验验证细胞响应与压力关联
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a centimeter-scale bionic tissue testbed for evaluating cardiac ablation tools using bioprinted human cardiac tissue and soft pressure sensors.
Method· 3D bioprinting of human pluripotent stem cells in extracellular matrix bioink to form a cardiac disk, coupled with a hydrogel-based pressure sensor using electrical impedance tomography for real-time pressure mapping, then applying a cryoablation catheter.
Result· A close correlation between cell response to ablation and applied pressure was observed, with surviving cardiomyocytes showing rounded morphology and disrupted connectivity.
Validation· In vitro experiments on bioprinted cardiac tissue with pressure sensor validation and cell morphology analysis.

单位: Photonics Division, Institute for Advanced Studies, Trevo Amarante 1, Sao Jose dos Campos 12228-001, SP, Brazil
通讯: Milton Sérgio Fernandes de Lima @ Photonics Division, Institute for Advanced Studies, Trevo Amarante 1, Sao Jose dos Campos 12228-001, SP, Brazil
相关性: 该论文研究立方星结构的激光焊接，与生物医学植入物方向完全无关。
边界: This paper focuses on CubeSat space frame manufacturing with no biomedical context, no porous structures, no stress shielding, no fatigue testing, and no osseointegration assessment.
动机· 探索利用增材制造与激光焊接制造立方星结构的方法。
方法· 采用选区激光熔化和电子束熔化制造钛连接件，并进行激光点焊。
结果· 焊缝穿透良好，硬度接近连接件，可承受超2000 N力。

English summary & terms
Motivation· This study develops a methodology for manufacturing CubeSat structures by combining additive manufacturing and laser beam welding.
Method· Titanium connectors were fabricated via selective laser melting and electron beam melting, then joined to titanium tubes using laser spot welding.
Result· The welds exhibited full penetration, acicular martensite microstructure, and hardness close to that of the connectors, withstanding forces above 2000 N.
Validation· Validation was performed through metallographic analysis, hardness testing, and mechanical force testing.

Motivation· This study aims to optimize the heat treatment procedure for additively manufactured IN718 superalloy components to enhance their mechanical properties.
Method· The relationship between microstructure and mechanical properties is investigated by varying solution and aging heat treatment parameters.

Validation· No experimental validation is reported; the work is based on literature review and expected relationships.
