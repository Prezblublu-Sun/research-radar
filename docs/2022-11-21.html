Motivation· To report a case of aseptic loosening of a high-placed acetabular component managed with revision arthroplasty and morselized bone autograft.
Method· Revision total hip arthroplasty was performed with true acetabular placement and defect closure using cancellous morselized bone autograft.
Result· The wound healed well one week postoperatively and the patient was referred for rehabilitation.
Validation· Single case report without biomechanical testing or comparative analysis.
