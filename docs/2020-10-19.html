Motivation· To investigate whether acetabular morphology parameters are associated with dislocation risk after hip hemiarthroplasty.
Method· A nested case-control study comparing radiological parameters between dislocation and non-dislocation groups.
Result· Smaller lateral centre-edge angle, smaller femoral offset, and larger Tönnis angle were significantly associated with higher dislocation risk.
Validation· Clinical case-control statistical analysis.
