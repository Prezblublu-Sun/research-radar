通讯: Mohammad Khodaei @ Center for Advanced Engineering Research, Majlesi Branch, Islamic Azad University, Isfahan, Iran
相关性: 该论文涉及多孔钛植入体的表面改性及其对力学性能的影响，与用户关注的增材制造生物医用植入体方向相关。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no fatigue testing, no osseointegration or surface roughness data.
