M/NEM devices and uncertainty quantification

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project simulation_case UQ
Namjung Kim IDEALS (University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign) 2018-01-23 link
相关性: 该论文提出了针对制造过程不确定性的系统量化方法，可迁移至植入物力学仿真中的不确定性传播分析。
边界: This paper focuses on MEMS and microfluidic devices, not on implant biomechanics or surrogate modeling for expensive FEA.
动机· 制造过程中输入不确定性导致仿真与实验偏差，需系统量化不确定性。
方法· 通过实验数据建模随机输入变量，并集成到有限元模型中传播不确定性。
结果· 所提方法能估计3D打印和压铸工艺中产品力学性能的空间不确定性。
验证· 基于MEMS谐振器和微流控器件的仿真案例验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Deterministic simulations fail to capture output distributions due to uncertainties in manufacturing processes, necessitating systematic uncertainty quantification.
Method· A systematic approach is developed to model stochastic input variables from experimental data and propagate them through finite element models.
Result· The approach estimates spatial uncertainties in mechanical properties of products from 3D printing and die-casting processes.
Validation· Validation is performed through simulation case studies on MEMS resonators and microfluidic devices.
uncertainty quantification · 不确定性量化finite element model · 有限元模型stochastic input · 随机输入manufacturing process · 制造过程3D printing · 3D打印die-casting · 压铸MEMS · 微机电系统microfluidics · 微流控
uncertainty quantification finite element model manufacturing MEMS microfluidics 3D printing die-casting
