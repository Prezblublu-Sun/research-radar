Integrating LLMs and Digital Twins for Adaptive Multi-Robot Task Allocation in Construction

Min Deng, Bo Fu, Lingyao Li, Xi Wang arXiv preprint 2025-06-22 link
相关性: 该论文讨论建筑场景中基于大语言模型和数字孪生的多机器人任务分配，与生物打印方向无关。
边界: This paper does not involve bioprinting, AI-driven bioprinting workflow stages, or any biomedical application.
动机· 解决建筑工地动态环境中多机器人协调的挑战
方法· 提出结合数字孪生、整数规划和大语言模型的自适应任务分配框架
结果· 大语言模型在约束提取中准确率超97%
验证· 通过案例研究验证优化算法效率和语言模型推理性能
English summary & terms
Motivation· To address the challenge of coordinating multiple robots in dynamic construction environments with unpredictable disruptions.
Method· An adaptive task allocation framework integrating Digital Twins, Integer Programming, and Large Language Models for narrative-driven schedule adaptation.
Result· Top-performing LLMs achieved over 97% accuracy in constraint and parameter extraction.
Validation· A case study demonstrated computational efficiency of optimization and reasoning performance of several LLMs.
