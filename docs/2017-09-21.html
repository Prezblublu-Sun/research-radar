Influence of collars on the primary stability of cementless femoral stems: A finite element study using a diverse patient cohort

Rami Al‐Dirini, D. Huff, Zhang Ju, Thor F. Besier, John G. Clement et al. Journal of Orthopaedic Research® 2017-09-21 10.1002/jor.23744
单位: College of Science and Engineering Medical Device Research Institute (MDRI) Flinders University 1284 South Road, Clovelly Park Adelaide 5043 Australia
通讯: Rami Al‐Dirini @ College of Science and Engineering Medical Device Research Institute (MDRI) Flinders University 1284 South Road, Clovell
相关性: 该论文直接评估了股骨柄的应力遮挡、微动和初级稳定性，与用户的研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not include a detailed comparison of stress shielding in Gruen zones or provide explicit boundary conditions for FE work.
动机· 探讨股骨柄有无领对初级稳定性和载荷传递的影响。
方法· 基于41例患者特异性有限元模型，比较有领和无领Corail柄的力学性能。
结果· 有领与无领柄在微动、界面应变和骨重塑刺激上差异很小，且远小于患者间变异。
验证· 通过患者队列的有限元模拟，未进行实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate whether a collar on cementless femoral stems enhances primary stability and load transfer compared to collarless designs.
Method· Subject-specific finite element models of intact and implanted femora were created for a diverse cohort of 41 subjects, comparing collared and collarless Corail stems with automated sizing and positioning.
Result· Only subtle differences in micromotion, interface strains, and remodeling stimuli were found between collared and collarless stems, with inter-patient variability dominating.
Validation· The study used simulation-based comparison within a patient cohort without experimental validation.
