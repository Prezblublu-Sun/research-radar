A data-driven dynamics simulation framework for railway vehicles

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project simulation_case
Yinyu Nie, Zhao Tang, Fengjia Liu, Jian Chang, Jianjun Zhang Vehicle System Dynamics 2017-10-04 10.1080/00423114.2017.1381981
单位: National Centre for Computer Animation, Bournemouth University, Poole, UK
通讯: Zhao Tang @ State Key Laboratory of Traction Power, Southwest Jiaotong University, Chengdu, People’s Republic of China
相关性: 该论文提出了一种数据驱动的代理模型框架，将有限元模拟的精度与多体动力学仿真的效率结合，其方法可迁移至生物力学植入物设计的代理建模。
边界: This paper focuses on railway vehicle dynamics, not biomedical implant or biomechanics applications, and lacks uncertainty quantification and constraint handling.
动机· 结合有限元精度与多体动力学效率，解决铁路车辆动力学仿真中的计算瓶颈。
方法· 利用机器学习从有限元数据中提取非线性特征，构建代理单元嵌入多体模型进行协同仿真。
结果· 勒让德多项式回归代理单元大幅缩短仿真时间且保持精度。
验证· 通过垂向和纵向动力学案例，与Kriging模型对比验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To combine the accuracy of finite element simulations with the efficiency of multi-body dynamics for railway vehicle dynamics.
Method· Machine learning extracts nonlinear features from FE training data to build surrogate elements that replace mechanical elements in a co-simulation with MB models.
Result· Legendre polynomial regression surrogate elements significantly reduce simulation time without sacrificing accuracy.
Validation· Two case studies (vertical and longitudinal dynamics) compared with Kriging model demonstrate feasibility.
