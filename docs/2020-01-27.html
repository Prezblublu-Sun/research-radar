Dental Implants in Patients with Oral Lichen Planus: A Systematic Review.

Bruno Ramos Chrcanovic, Aline Fernanda Cruz, Ricardo Trindade, Ricardo Santiago Gomez Medicina (Kaunas, Lithuania) 2020-01-27 10.3390/medicina56020053
单位: Department of Prosthodontics, Faculty of Odontology, Malmö University, 214 21 Malmö, Sweden.
通讯: Ricardo Santiago Gomez @ Department of Oral Surgery and Pathology, School of Dentistry, Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais, Belo Horizonte 3127 [推断]
相关性: 该论文是关于口腔扁平苔藓患者种植牙的系统综述，与AM生物医学材料方向无关。
边界: This paper is a clinical systematic review on dental implant survival in oral lichen planus patients, with no LPBF fabrication, mechanical testing, porous structure design, or stress shielding analysis.
动机· 整合口腔扁平苔藓患者种植牙的临床数据并提出建议
方法· 系统综述五个数据库中的22篇文献
结果· 排除异常研究后种植失败率仅2.7%
验证· 文献汇总分析
English summary & terms
Motivation· To synthesize available clinical data on dental implant outcomes in patients with oral lichen planus and provide treatment recommendations.
Method· A systematic review of 22 publications from five electronic databases was conducted.
Result· Excluding one outlier study and oral squamous cell carcinoma cases, the implant failure rate was 2.7% over a mean follow-up of approximately five years.
Validation· The study is a literature review without experimental or mechanical validation.
