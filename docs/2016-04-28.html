I. Azijli, Andrea Sciacchitano, Daniele Ragni, Artur Palha, Richard P. Dwight Experiments in Fluids 2016-04-28 10.1007/s00348-016-2159-z
单位: Aerodynamics, Wind-Energy, Flight Performance and Propulsion Department, Faculty of Aerospace Engineering, Delft University of Technology, Delft, The Netherlands
通讯: I. Azijli @ Aerodynamics, Wind-Energy, Flight Performance and Propulsion Department, Faculty of Aerospace Engineering, Delft Univers
相关性: 该论文关注PIV压力数据的后验不确定性量化，与FEA替代建模或植入物设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, implant biomechanics, or design optimization; it focuses on experimental fluid dynamics uncertainty quantification.
动机· 量化PIV压力数据的不确定性，提高压力场重建精度。
方法· 基于贝叶斯框架，结合先验知识和测量不确定性，通过泊松方程传播协方差。
结果· 方法准确估计压力不确定性，优于线性传播；散度自由约束显著提高精度。
验证· 数值涡旋与湍流边界层实验验证，与麦克风测量对比。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify uncertainty in pressure fields derived from particle image velocimetry (PIV) measurements.
Method· A Bayesian framework that propagates the posterior covariance of velocity through the discretized Poisson equation for pressure, incorporating prior knowledge and measurement uncertainty.
Result· The proposed method accurately estimates pressure uncertainty, outperforming linear propagation by up to 30%, and enforcing divergence-free constraint improves accuracy.
Validation· Validated on a synthetic Lamb-Oseen vortex and experimental turbulent boundary layer data, with comparison to microphone measurements.
