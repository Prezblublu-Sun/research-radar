Effect of geometry on the mechanical properties of Ti-6Al-4V Gyroid structures fabricated via SLM: A numerical study

High AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Read now mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Eric H. Yang, Martin Leary, Bill Lozanovski, David Downing, Maciej Mazur et al. Materials & Design 2019-08-29 10.1016/j.matdes.2019.108165
单位: ARC Training Centre in Additive Biomanufacturing, Australia
通讯: Martin Leary @ ARC Training Centre for Lightweight Automotive Structures, RMIT University, Bundoora, Victoria, Australia
相关性: 该论文直接研究了SLM制造的Gyroid多孔结构的力学性能与几何参数的关系，并匹配人皮质骨刚度，与用户关于多孔结构应力遮挡和LPBF制造的研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper is a numerical study without experimental validation of fatigue properties, surface roughness, or osseointegration.
动机· 量化Gyroid多孔结构几何参数对力学性能的影响，以匹配人皮质骨。

结果· 发现胞元数和表面厚度显著影响弹性模量和压缩强度，并设计了匹配皮质骨的结构。
验证· 数值模型经SLM试样实验数据验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify the effect of geometric parameters on the mechanical response of Gyroid structures for bone implant applications.
Method· Numerical simulations of quasi-static compression on cubic Gyroid structures with OFAT and Taguchi methods, validated by experimental data from SLM-fabricated Ti-6Al-4V specimens.
Result· Cell number and surface thickness strongly influence modulus and compressive strength; a Gyroid structure matching human cortical bone properties was theoretically developed.
Validation· Simulation model verified against experimental compression tests on SLM Ti-6Al-4V Gyroid samples.
