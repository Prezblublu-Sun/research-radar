Bayesian Optimization with Expected Improvement: No Regret and the Choice of Incumbent

Jingyi Wang, Haowei Wang, Szu Hui Ng, Cosmin G. Petra arXiv preprint 2025-08-21 link
相关性: 该论文专注于贝叶斯优化中期望提升策略的理论收敛性，未涉及有限元代理建模或生物力学设计约束。
边界: This paper does not address expensive simulation sampling efficiency for FEA, does not handle constraints, lacks uncertainty quantification beyond GP predictive variance, and provides no transferable application to implant or biomechanics design.
动机· 期望提升策略在噪声环境下累积遗憾上界未知，需理论证明。

验证· 通过数值实验验证理论结果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The cumulative regret upper bound of expected improvement in noisy Bayesian optimization remains open, motivating a theoretical analysis.
Method· We analyze GP-EI with three incumbent choices (BPMI, BSPMI, BOI) and derive cumulative regret upper bounds for SE and Matérn kernels.
Result· GP-EI is proven to be no-regret for BPMI and BSPMI, while BOI achieves sublinear regret or fast converging simple regret.
Validation· Numerical experiments on synthetic functions validate the theoretical regret bounds.

通讯: Yangli Xu @ Institute of Manufacturing Engineering, Huaqiao University, Xiamen 361021, Fujian, China

边界: This paper focuses on high-temperature hardness of a nickel-based superalloy for aerospace applications, with no mention of biomedical implants, stress shielding, stiffness matching, fatigue, or osseointegration.
动机· 研究热处理对SLM成形GH4099高温合金微观组织和硬度的影响。

Multiply Robust Conformal Risk Control with Coarsened Data

Manit Paul, Arun Kumar Kuchibhotla, Eric J. Tchetgen Tchetgen arXiv preprint 2025-08-21 link
相关性: 该论文关注统计推断中的缺失数据问题，不涉及有限元分析、生物力学或植入物设计。
边界: This paper focuses on conformal prediction with missing data and has no connection to FEA surrogate modeling, implant design, or biomechanics problems.
动机· 处理训练数据中缺失值和删失数据带来的预测区间校准问题。
方法· 结合半参数理论的高效影响函数与共形风险控制框架。
结果· 在协变量偏移及单调缺失场景下得到具有更强覆盖性质的预测区间。
验证· 通过多种模拟实验验证方法性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To obtain valid prediction intervals for outcomes when training data have missing or censored observations.
Method· Leverages efficient influence functions from semiparametric theory combined with a novel conformal risk control procedure.
Result· Produces predictively valid intervals with stronger coverage under covariate shift and monotone missingness.
Validation· Validated through multiple simulation studies demonstrating the robustness and efficiency of the proposed method.
