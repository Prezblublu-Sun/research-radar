Validation· Clinical radiographic follow-up with a validated measurement method.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate whether extended oral antibiotic prophylaxis reduces periprosthetic joint infection after aseptic revision total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Retrospective review of 169 consecutive aseptic revision THAs with 80% receiving 7-day oral antibiotic prophylaxis.
Result· Zero infections at 90 days and 1.8% infection rate at mean 45-month follow-up.
Validation· Comparison with published infection rates in revision and primary THA.
