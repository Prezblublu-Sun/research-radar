English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare marginal bone loss at immediate molar implants using customized versus conventional healing abutments.
Method· A prospective randomized controlled trial with 36 patients (18 per group), radiographic evaluation of bone level changes, and regression analysis.
Result· Customized abutments significantly reduced mean marginal bone loss (0.51 mm vs 1.34 mm) with no significant covariate effects.
Validation· 12-month clinical follow-up with intraoral radiographs.

Validation· Experimental evaluation on database benchmarks comparing against existing tuning methods.
