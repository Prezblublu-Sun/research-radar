An investigation on the effect of high speed machining on the osseointegration performance of grade 4 titanium alloy

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only in_vitro Exp. validation
Andrish Reddy 2015-02-12 link
相关性: 该论文研究了高速加工对钛合金表面粗糙度和细胞粘附的影响，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper does not cover porous structure design, stress shielding, LPBF fabrication, or fatigue testing; it focuses on machining surface finish and cell adhesion.
动机· 研究高速加工对钛合金植入物表面质量和骨整合的影响。
方法· 通过车削加工不同切削速度的Grade 4钛合金，测量表面粗糙度、接触角并进行细胞粘附实验。
结果· 切削速度在150-200 m/min时可能获得最优骨整合表面。
验证· 通过细胞粘附实验验证表面特性对骨整合的影响。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of high speed machining on surface integrity and osseointegration potential of grade 4 titanium alloy for implant applications.
Method· Grade 4 titanium was turned at various cutting speeds from conventional to high speed, and surface finish was characterized by profilometry, AFM, and contact angle, followed by cell adhesion assays.
Result· Cutting speeds between 150 m/min and 200 m/min may provide an optimal surface for osseointegration based on the correlation between roughness, contact angle, and cell adhesion.
Validation· In vitro cell adhesion studies were used to predict the bone-bonding ability of the machined surfaces.

Probability Distribution of the Coherence Bandwidth of a Reverberation Chamber

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only theoretical Exp. validation UQ
Luk R. Arnaut, Gabriele Gradoni IEEE Transactions on Antennas and Propagation 2015-02-12 10.1109/tap.2015.2403398
单位: Antennas and Electromagnetics Group, School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science, Queen Mary University of London, London, U.K
通讯: Luk R. Arnaut @ Antennas and Electromagnetics Group, School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science, Queen Mary University of Lon
相关性: 该论文研究混响室的相干带宽概率分布，与FEA代理建模或生物力学设计无关。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification for biomechanics, or implant design; it focuses on electromagnetic wave propagation in reverberation chambers.
动机· 推导混响室相干带宽的概率分布以改善无线传输质量。
方法· 利用波混沌腔的遍历性，将相干带宽表示为随机有效激励带宽的函数。
结果· 得到了相干带宽的理论分布，并通过实测和仿真验证。
验证· 与实测S参数和经验CDF对比，以及虚拟搅拌室仿真。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To derive the probability distribution of coherence bandwidth in a reverberation chamber for improving wireless transmission quality and uncertainty quantification.
Method· Exploiting ergodicity of a dynamic wave chaotic cavity, the coherence bandwidth is expressed in terms of random effective excitation bandwidth, replacing spatial averaging with stir averaging.
Result· A theoretical distribution and statistics for coherence bandwidth are obtained and validated.
Validation· Validated against empirical CDF from measured S-parameter data and analytical simulations of a fictitious wall-stirred chamber.

通讯: Amanda Cunha Regal de Castro @ Universidade Federal do Rio de Janeiro -UFRJ, School of Dentistry, Department of Orthodontics, Rio de Janeiro, RJ, Brazi
相关性: 该论文研究正畸微种植体的螺纹间距对稳定性的影响，与髋关节假体应力遮挡和股骨柄生物力学无关。
边界: This paper focuses on orthodontic mini-implants in artificial bone, not on hip implant stress shielding, femoral stem micromotion, or proximal femur biomechanics.
动机· 评估螺纹间距对正畸微种植体初期稳定性的影响
方法· 在人工骨中测试两种螺纹设计的植入扭矩和移除扭矩

Motivation· To evaluate the effect of thread pitch on the primary stability of orthodontic mini-implants in artificial bone.
Method· Two mini-implant designs with different thread pitches were inserted into artificial bone of two densities, and insertion/removal torque and Periotest values were measured.
Result· Higher insertion torque was observed for the larger pitch design in denser bone, while Periotest values indicated better stability for the smaller pitch design in denser bone.
Validation· Experimental mechanical testing with torque measurements and Periotest assessment.
