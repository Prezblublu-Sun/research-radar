单位: University of Cincinnati , Cincinnati, Ohio, United States
通讯: Varad Maitra @ University of Cincinnati , Cincinnati, Ohio, United States
相关性: 该论文专注于增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的表面粗糙度预测，虽未直接涉及骨科植入物的应力屏蔽或疲劳性能，但其方法和关注点与生物医用植入物的表面质量优化相关。

English summary & terms

Validation· Experimental validation was performed by measuring the arithmetic average roughness (Ra) of ten as-built SLM cubes.

Method· A computational framework integrating a finite element thermal model and a phase-field microstructural model was developed to simulate melt pool solidification.
Result· Model predictions of sub-grain cellular structure size showed good agreement with experimental measurements.
Validation· Experimental characterization of the as-printed microstructure was used to validate the model predictions.
