Transient behavior of electrostatically-actuated micro systems considering squeeze film damping and mechanical shock

Javid Abderezae, Mahdi Moghimi Zand Scientia Iranica 2017-08-20 10.24200/sci.2017.4245
单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, College of Engineering, University of Tehran, Tehran, P.O. Box 14399 - 55961, Iran
通讯: Javid Abderezae @ School of Mechanical Engineering, College of Engineering, University of Tehran, Tehran, P.O. Box 14399 - 559
相关性: 该论文研究微梁在静电力、机械冲击和挤压膜阻尼下的瞬态行为，与用户关注的植入物生物力学或代理模型方向无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on MEMS microbeams with electrostatic actuation and squeeze film damping, not on biomedical implant design, stress shielding, or surrogate modeling for FEA.
动机· 研究微梁在静电力、机械冲击和挤压膜阻尼共同作用下的瞬态行为
方法· 采用Galerkin降阶模型和有限元代码求解偏微分方程
结果· 发现挤压膜阻尼可显著主导机械冲击效应，并延迟静电驱动响应
验证· 与文献中两种简化模型结果对比验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To model the transient dynamics of microbeams under combined electrostatic force, mechanical shock, squeeze film damping, and fringing field effects.
Method· A Galerkin-based reduced-order model and a nonlinear finite element code were developed to solve the governing PDE.
Result· Squeeze film damping was found to dominate mechanical shock effects and delay the electrostatic actuation response.
Validation· Two simplified models were validated against literature results, showing good agreement.

边界: No biomedical context, no implant mechanics, no FEA surrogate for biomechanics; application is purely civil/earthquake engineering.
动机· 评估地震中非结构构件失效概率时，传统蒙特卡洛方法计算成本高。
方法· 采用代理模型（如响应面法、Kriging）替代昂贵有限元模拟进行不确定性量化。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce computational cost of Monte Carlo simulation for seismic failure probability assessment of nonstructural components.
Method· Surrogate models (e.g., response surface, Kriging) are used to replace expensive finite element simulations for uncertainty propagation.
Result· Surrogate models provide efficient and sufficiently accurate estimates of failure probability.
Validation· Validation is performed through numerical simulation case studies comparing surrogate predictions with direct Monte Carlo results.

Method· Structured observations of 26 science lessons and a survey of 62 students were conducted.

Validation· Empirical validation through classroom observations and student questionnaires.

Validation· Validated on a public human activity recognition dataset using accuracy and labeling cost metrics.
