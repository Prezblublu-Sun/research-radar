英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To identify patient-related risk factors for periprosthetic joint infection after total hip arthroplasty through a systematic review and meta-analysis.
Method· A meta-analysis of 34 studies involving 2,141,960 hips was performed, calculating risk ratios with 95% confidence intervals for modifiable and non-modifiable factors.
Result· High BMI, diabetes mellitus, avascular necrosis, femoral neck fracture, rheumatoid arthritis, cardiovascular disease, chronic pulmonary disease, and neurological disease were significant risk factors, while female gender, dysplasia/dislocation, and osteoarthritis were protective.
Validation· Sensitivity analysis and publication bias assessment were conducted to ensure the credibility of the pooled estimates.
