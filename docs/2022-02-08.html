English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the safety of vitamin E-doped polyethylene liners in total hip arthroplasty compared to conventional cross-linked polyethylene.
Method· A nationwide population-based retrospective cohort study using data from the Danish Hip Arthroplasty Register.
Result· VEPE liners had a lower risk of revision for polyethylene-related endpoints but a higher risk of all-cause revision within the first three months.
Validation· Large-scale clinical registry data analysis.
