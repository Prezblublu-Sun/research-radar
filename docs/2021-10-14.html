Seunghyun Moon, Ruimin Ma, Ross Attardo, Charles Tomonto, Mark Nordin et al. Scientific Reports 2021-10-14 10.1038/s41598-021-99959-6
单位: Department of Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering, University of Notre Dame, Notre Dame, IN, 46556, USA
通讯: Tengfei Luo @ Department of Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering, University of Notre Dame, Notre Dame, IN, 46556, USA. tluo@nd.edu
相关性: 该论文直接研究了LPBF Ti-6Al-4V的疲劳寿命与表面粗糙度及孔隙特征的关系，并采用神经网络建模，与用户方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not address porous implant structures for stress shielding or osseointegration, nor does it link stiffness matching to biomedical implant design.
动机· 研究LPBF Ti-6Al-4V疲劳寿命受表面粗糙度和孔隙特征的影响。
方法· 通过改变扫描速度制备试样，结合微CT表征孔隙，采用drop-out神经网络建模。
结果· 神经网络能准确预测疲劳寿命并估计不确定性，表面与内部孔隙特征对疲劳影响不同。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the combined effects of surface roughness and pore characteristics on the fatigue life of laser powder bed fusion Ti-6Al-4V parts.
Method· Fatigue bars were fabricated with varied scanning speeds, characterized by micro-CT for pore geometry, and half were grit-blasted to alter surface roughness; a dropout neural network was used to model fatigue life.
Result· The neural network accurately predicted fatigue life and provided uncertainty estimates, revealing that pore-depletion zones in grit-blasted samples isolate surface and internal pores with distinct features.
Validation· Experimental fatigue testing of 197 specimens validated the model predictions.
