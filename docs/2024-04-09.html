英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of composite bone plates on tibia fracture fixation, aiming to reduce stress shielding and improve healing.
Method· Finite element models of a tibia with a fracture gap were created and fixed with titanium and various composite plates (carbon/epoxy, carbon/PEEK, carbon/UHMWPE).
Result· Most composite plates performed comparably or better than metal plates, with matrix material causing some variations.
Validation· The study used anatomical 3D finite element simulations to evaluate stresses and axial movements at the fracture site.
