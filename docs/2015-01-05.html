单位: College of Materials Science and Technology, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Yudao Street 29, Nanjing 210016, People’s Republic of China
通讯: Dongdong Gu @ College of Materials Science and Technology, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Yudao Street 29, Nanjin
相关性: 该论文研究TiC/AlSi10Mg纳米复合材料的SLM熔池行为，未涉及生物医学植入物的多孔结构、应力屏蔽或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper does not address biomedical implant design, porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or osseointegration; it focuses on molten pool physics of a non-biomedical composite material.
动机· 研究SLM制备TiC/AlSi10Mg纳米复合材料的熔池热行为及物理机制。
方法· 采用有限体积法模拟温度场，考虑马兰戈尼对流及材料非线性，并进行实验验证。
结果· 马兰戈尼对流显著增强传热并改变熔池几何形状，激光功率和扫描速度是主要影响因素。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To understand the thermal behavior and physical mechanisms of the molten pool during selective laser melting of TiC/AlSi10Mg nanocomposites.
Method· A finite volume model incorporating Marangoni convection and temperature-dependent properties simulated temperature evolution and molten pool dynamics.
Result· Marangoni convection intensified convective heat transfer and altered molten pool geometry; higher laser power increased pool size and temperature gradient.
Validation· Experimental characterization of densification, interlayer bonding, and surface morphology validated the thermal simulation predictions.

单位: Surgical Outcomes and Analysis, Kaiser Permanente, 8954 Rio San Diego Drive, Suite 406, San Diego, CA, 92108, USA, liz.w.paxton@kp.org
通讯: Elizabeth W. Paxton @ Surgical Outcomes and Analysis, Kaiser Permanente, 8954 Rio San Diego Drive, Suite 406, San Diego, CA, 92108, USA, liz.w
相关性: 该论文关注髋关节置换术轴承表面的翻修风险，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡、微动或有限元分析。
边界: This paper is a clinical registry study on bearing surface revision rates, with no biomechanical modeling, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, and no FEA or implant design analysis.
动机· 比较金属对传统聚乙烯与金属对高交联聚乙烯轴承表面的翻修风险。
方法· 基于Kaiser Permanente关节置换登记处的26,823例初次THA数据进行倾向评分调整的Cox回归分析。
结果· 金属对传统聚乙烯轴承表面在7年时翻修率更高（5.4% vs 2.8%），调整后风险比为1.75。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the risk of revision between metal-on-conventional polyethylene and metal-on-highly crosslinked polyethylene bearing surfaces in primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Marginal Cox regression models with propensity score adjustments were applied to 26,823 primary THAs from the Kaiser Permanente Total Joint Replacement Registry.
Result· Metal-on-conventional polyethylene bearings had a higher adjusted risk of all-cause revision (HR 1.75) and aseptic revision (HR 1.91) compared to metal-on-HXLPE at 7 years.
Validation· Validation was performed using a large registry with propensity score adjustment and manufacturer-specific analyses to control for confounding.

边界: This paper is a clinical outcome study of periacetabular osteotomy, with no biomechanical modeling, no implant mechanics, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, and no finite element analysis.
动机· 评估既往骨盆截骨术对髋臼周围截骨术结果的影响
方法· 比较既往手术组与初次手术组的临床和影像学结果

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine whether prior pelvic osteotomy compromises the clinical and radiographic outcomes of subsequent periacetabular osteotomy.
Method· A matched cohort study comparing pain, function, activity, radiographic correction, and complications between patients with and without prior pelvic osteotomy.

Validation· Prospective multicenter database with 87% follow-up at mean 2.5 years.
