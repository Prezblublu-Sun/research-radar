边界: This paper focuses on aluminum alloys for aerospace, with no biomedical context, no stress shielding analysis, no fatigue testing, and no osseointegration considerations.
动机· 铝合焊接性差限制了其增材制造应用，需改善工艺性。

结果· 2wt.%和5wt.%TiC完全消除凝固裂纹，SiC显著减少裂纹。

Motivation· Poor weldability of aluminum alloys restricts their use in additive manufacturing, necessitating a method to enhance processability.
Method· Aluminum matrix composites are fabricated by mechanically alloying non-processable Al6082 with TiC and SiC reinforcements for LPBF.
Result· Addition of 2 wt.% and 5 wt.% TiC completely eliminates solidification cracking, while SiC substantially reduces cracking.
Validation· Microstructural characterization via EDS and EBSD, along with mechanical testing, confirms improved processability.
