Method· A descriptive prospective case series of patients undergoing uncemented THA through the Hueter interval between September 2019 and March 2021.
Result· Most patients were female aged 45-60, with low BMI and low anesthetic risk; the approach enabled early ambulation and low complication rates.
Validation· One-year clinical follow-up with Harris hip score and pain scale.
