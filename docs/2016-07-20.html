边界: This paper focuses on nuclear engineering systems and does not address FEA surrogate modeling, implant biomechanics, or biomedical design constraints.

Motivation· To efficiently compute second-order response sensitivities for nonlinear systems, enabling advances in uncertainty quantification and predictive modeling.
Method· The second-order adjoint sensitivity analysis methodology (2nd-ASAM) is developed, which computes exact second-order functional derivatives using adjoint systems.
Result· The 2nd-ASAM reduces computational cost from O(Nα²/2+3Nα/2) to O(Nα) large-scale computations per response, with inherent solution verification.
Validation· Theoretical derivation and symmetry properties ensure correctness; no numerical or experimental validation is provided.
