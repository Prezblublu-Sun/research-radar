单位: Centre for Health and Social Economics CHESS, National Institute for Health and Welfare; and
通讯: Jutta Järvelin @ Centre for Health and Social Economics CHESS, National Institute for Health and Welfare; and  |  Unto Häkkinen @ Centre for Health and Social Economics CHESS, National Institute for Health and Welfare; and  |  Gunnar Rosenqvist @ Centre for Health and Social Economics CHESS, National Institute for Health and Welfare
相关性: 该论文研究芬兰无过错赔偿制度下髋关节置换术患者的医疗成本差异，未涉及任何生物力学或植入物设计内容。
边界: This paper is a health economics study on compensation claims and costs after total hip arthroplasty, with no biomechanical modeling, implant design, stress shielding, micromotion, or FEA analysis.
动机· 比较芬兰无过错赔偿系统中索赔与非索赔患者的医疗成本差异
方法· 使用伽马分布广义线性模型分析CABG、THA、TKA患者注册数据
结果· 未获赔偿的索赔者住院费用高于非索赔者，但获赔与未获赔者差异不显著
验证· 基于芬兰全国注册数据的风险调整成本模型
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To estimate the cost differential between claimants and nonclaimants in the no-fault compensation system in Finland for patients undergoing CABG, THA, or TKA.
Method· A gamma-distributed, log-linked generalized linear model was used to analyze register data on 20,500 CABG, 17,506 THA, and 18,512 TKA patients.
Result· Uncompensated claimants had higher admission costs than nonclaimants, while the cost difference between compensated and uncompensated claimants was statistically insignificant.
Validation· Risk-adjusted cost modeling using nationwide Finnish register data.
