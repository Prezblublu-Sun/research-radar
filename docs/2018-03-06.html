单位: Univ. of Pennsylvania (United States)
通讯: Chamith S. Rajapakse @ Univ. of Pennsylvania (United States)
相关性: 该论文涉及医学图像到3D打印的转换，但未集成AI或机器学习方法。
边界: No AI/ML integration; only a direct conversion algorithm without learning or autonomous decision-making.
动机· 加速医学图像到3D打印的转换过程
方法· 开发JAVA程序跳过三角化步骤直接生成G-Code
结果· 处理时间减少92.23%，文件大小显著降低
验证· 使用HR-pQCT扫描的人股骨图像进行测试
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce processing time and preserve microstructural surface information when converting medical images to 3D printable G-Code.

Result· The direct method achieved a 92.23% reduction in processing time and significantly smaller G-Code file sizes.
Validation· Tested on HR-pQCT images of human cadaveric proximal femurs for synthetic bone graft scaffold generation.

Bone remodeling around short-stemmed hip prostheses

Carl Hellman Gothenburg University Publications Electronic Archive (Gothenburg University) 2018-03-06 link
相关性: 该论文直接评估短柄髋关节假体周围的骨密度变化和应力遮挡，与用户研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper uses DXA clinical measurements only, without finite element modeling or contact mechanics to quantify stress shielding or micromotion.
动机· 评估短柄假体是否减少应力遮挡和骨密度丢失
方法· 对两种短柄假体（CFP和Fitmore）进行术后2年DXA扫描，按Gruen分区测量BMD变化
结果· CFP在所有区域BMD下降更多，Fitmore在侧方区域下降但内侧区域较好
验证· 临床DXA测量与对照组比较
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate whether short-stemmed hip prostheses reduce stress shielding and preserve proximal femoral bone mineral density compared to conventional stems.
Method· DXA scans were performed postoperatively and at 3, 6, 12, and 24 months for CFP and Fitmore stems, measuring BMD changes in Gruen zones 1-7.
Result· The CFP stem showed inferior BMD in all zones, while the Fitmore stem had greater lateral but lesser medial BMD loss, indicating moderate stress shielding.
Validation· Clinical DXA measurements with control group comparison.
