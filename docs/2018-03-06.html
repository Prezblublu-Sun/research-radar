Microstructure evolution, mechanical response and underlying thermodynamic mechanism of multi-phase strengthening WC/Inconel 718 composites using selective laser melting

Mujian Xia, Dongdong Gu, Chenglong Ma, Hongyu Chen, Hongmei Zhang Journal of Alloys and Compounds 2018-03-06 10.1016/j.jallcom.2018.03.049
单位: Jiangsu Provincial Engineering Laboratory for Laser Additive Manufacturing of High-Performance Metallic Components, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Yudao Street 29, Nanjing, 210016
通讯: Dongdong Gu @ Jiangsu Provincial Engineering Laboratory for Laser Additive Manufacturing of High-Performance Metallic Components, Nanj
相关性: 该论文研究WC/Inconel 718复合材料的选择性激光熔化，未涉及生物医学植入物或应力屏蔽问题。
边界: No biomedical implant context, no stress shielding or stiffness matching analysis, no fatigue testing.
动机· 探究WC/Inconel 718复合材料在SLM过程中的微观组织演变与强化机制。
方法· 采用选择性激光熔化制备不同WC含量的复合材料，结合热力学计算分析相变。
结果· WC颗粒促进共晶相形成，复合材料硬度和强度显著提高。
验证· 通过SEM、XRD和力学测试验证微观结构与性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the microstructure evolution and strengthening mechanisms of WC/Inconel 718 composites fabricated by selective laser melting.
Method· Selective laser melting was used to prepare composites with varying WC content, and thermodynamic calculations were performed to analyze phase transformations.
Result· WC particles promoted eutectic phase formation, leading to significant enhancement in hardness and strength.
Validation· Validation via SEM, XRD, and mechanical testing confirms the microstructure and property relationships.

Selective laser melting of maraging steel: mechanical properties development and its application in mold

Yuchao Bai, Yongqiang Yang, Zefeng Xiao, Di Wang Rapid Prototyping Journal 2018-03-06 10.1108/rpj-05-2017-0104
单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, South China University of Technology, Guangzhou, China
通讯: Yuchao Bai @ School of Mechanical Engineering, South China University of Technology, Guangzhou, China
相关性: 该论文研究模具制造，不涉及生物医学植入物或骨整合。
边界: No biomedical implant context, no porous structure for osseointegration, no fatigue testing, no surface roughness evaluation for bone ingrowth.
动机· 验证选区激光熔化能否制造具有随形冷却通道的模具。
方法· 制备SLM马氏体时效钢样品，进行固溶和时效热处理，测试显微组织与力学性能。
结果· 固溶处理降低硬度便于加工，时效处理通过析出相提高强度，成功制造出高质量模具。
验证· 通过显微硬度、拉伸测试和模具制造进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To verify if selective laser melting can fabricate molds with conformal cooling channels and improve mold quality.
Method· Block and tensile specimens of maraging steel were manufactured by SLM, then subjected to solution treatment and aging treatment; microstructure, microhardness, and tensile strength were characterized.
Result· Solution treatment reduced hardness for easier machining, while aging treatment increased strength via precipitation of Ni3Mo, Fe2Mo, and Ni3Ti, enabling successful fabrication of high-quality molds.
Validation· Validation through mechanical testing (microhardness, tensile tests) and demonstration of a mold with conformal cooling channels.

Structural optimization design for antenna bracket manufactured by selective laser melting

Zefeng Xiao, Yongqiang Yang, Di Wang, Changhui Song, Yuchao Bai Rapid Prototyping Journal 2018-03-06 10.1108/rpj-05-2017-0084
单位: School of Mechanical and Automotive Engineering, South China University of Technology, Guangzhou, China
通讯: Zefeng Xiao @ School of Mechanical and Automotive Engineering, South China University of Technology, Guangzhou, China
相关性: 该论文涉及天线支架的轻量化设计，与生物医用植入物的应力屏蔽、多孔结构、疲劳等方向无关。
边界: This paper focuses on aerospace antenna bracket lightweighting, with no relation to biomedical implants, porous structures, stress shielding, or osseointegration.
动机· 利用SLM工艺特点和结构优化实现天线支架轻量化。
方法· 采用拓扑优化与形状优化结合，并基于SLM设计规则重构模型。
结果· 质量减少30.43%，基频提升50.18%，成型精度满足要求。
验证· 对比模态分析数据和扫描数据验证优化效果与成型精度。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To achieve lightweight design of an antenna bracket by combining structural optimization with selective laser melting process characteristics.
Method· Topology optimization for material distribution, redesign for SLM manufacturability, and shape optimization for performance improvement.
Result· A mass reduction of 30.43% and a fundamental frequency increase of 50.18% were achieved, with forming accuracy validated by scanning data.
Validation· Modal analysis compared with stochastic lightweight model; scanning data compared with original model to verify forming accuracy.

Additive manufacturing of laser cutting nozzles by SLM: processing, finishing and functional characterization

Marco Anilli, Ali Gökhan Demir, Barbara Previtali Rapid Prototyping Journal 2018-03-06 10.1108/rpj-05-2017-0106
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Politecnico di Milano, Milan, Italy
通讯: Marco Anilli @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Politecnico di Milano, Milan, Italy
相关性: 该论文与生物医学植入物方向无关，主要研究激光切割喷嘴的增材制造。
边界: This paper focuses on laser cutting nozzles and their finishing processes, with no biomedical implants, porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or osseointegration.
动机· 评估激光切割喷嘴的增材制造及后处理全链条。
方法· 采用SLM制造喷嘴，结合ECM和AFM后处理，进行流动和切割性能表征。
结果· 后处理可降低表面粗糙度80%，单室喷嘴性能与传统相当。
验证· 通过激光切割实验和纹影成像验证喷嘴功能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to assess the complete production chain from selective laser melting to finishing for laser cutting nozzles.
Method· Single and double chamber nozzles were fabricated by SLM, followed by ECM and AFM finishing, and characterized by pressure drop measurement, Schlieren imaging, and laser cutting tests.
Result· Post-processing reduced surface roughness by approximately 80%; single chamber nozzles performed comparably to conventional ones in cutting 1 mm mild steel.
Validation· Functional validation was performed through laser cutting experiments and Schlieren imaging of flow dynamics.
