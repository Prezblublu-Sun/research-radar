Validation· No experimental validation; findings are based on literature analysis.

Adaptions and perceptions on histology and embryology teaching practice in China during the Covid-19 pandemic

Exclude FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Ignore none
Xin Cheng, Lap Ki Chan, Hongmei Cai, Deshan Zhou, Xuesong Yang Translational Research in Anatomy 2021-02-21 10.1016/j.tria.2021.100115
单位: Department of Histology and Embryology, Medical College, Jinan University, Guangzhou, China
通讯: Xuesong Yang @ Department of Histology and Embryology, Medical College, Jinan University, Guangzhou, China
相关性: 该论文讨论的是疫情期间中国组织学与胚胎学教学方式的转变，与有限元代理建模或生物力学优化完全无关。
边界: This paper is about medical education during COVID-19, with no mention of FEA, surrogate modeling, uncertainty quantification, or biomechanical design.
动机· 调查疫情期间中国医学院组织学与胚胎学在线教学的实施情况
方法· 通过微信对全国83所医学院校进行问卷调查
结果· 77%的学校此前已有理论在线教学，疫情期间73%采用同步直播授课
验证· 基于78所医学院校的问卷反馈
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the adaptations and perceptions of histology and embryology teaching in Chinese medical schools during the COVID-19 pandemic.
Method· A nationwide survey was conducted via WeChat among 83 major medical colleges in China.
Result· 77% of schools had prior theoretical online teaching, and 73% adopted synchronous live broadcasting during the pandemic.
Validation· Survey responses from 78 medical schools across mainland China, Hong Kong, and Macao.
