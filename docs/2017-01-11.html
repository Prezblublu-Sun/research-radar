Design optimization of stent and its dilatation balloon using kriging surrogate model

单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, Dalian University of Technology, Dalian, 116023, Liaoning, China
通讯: Bao Zhu @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, Dalian University of Technology, No. 2, Linggong Road, Dalian, 116024, Liao
相关性: 该论文使用Kriging代理模型结合有限元仿真优化支架几何和球囊长度，方法可迁移至植入物设计中的代理建模与多目标优化。
边界: This paper focuses on stent and balloon optimization, not on hip implant stress shielding or bone-implant interface mechanics.
动机· 传统支架优化方法无法找到全局最优解，需提高疲劳寿命和扩张性能。
方法· 提出基于Kriging代理模型的自适应优化方法，结合有限元仿真逐步优化支架几何和球囊长度。

Result· Optimized stents showed increased fatigue life, eliminated dogboning effect, and reduced radial recoil and foreshortening.
Validation· Validation was performed via finite element simulations of diamond-shaped and sv-shaped stent expansion cases.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate whether preoperative patient characteristics and symptom levels differ between Swiss and US cohorts undergoing total hip and knee arthroplasty.
Method· Prospective collection of demographic, clinical, and patient-reported outcome measures (WOMAC, SF-12) from a large Swiss hospital and a US national sample.
Result· Significant differences were found in age, BMI, education, comorbidities, and pain scores between Swiss and US patients for both THA and TKA.
Validation· Statistical comparison of two large prospective cohorts with no biomechanical or implant-specific validation.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine if hybrid combinations of locked and non-locked peripheral screws affect acute glenoid baseplate fixation in reverse shoulder arthroplasty.
Method· Twenty-eight polyurethane bone surrogates were instrumented with a glenoid baseplate and fixated with varying numbers of locked screws, then tested through a 55° abduction arc for 10,000 cycles while recording micromotion.
Result· All constructs survived cyclic loading with micromotion below 150 μm, and no significant differences were found among groups with one to four locked screws.
Validation· Mechanical testing on polyurethane bone surrogates with cyclic loading and micromotion measurement.
