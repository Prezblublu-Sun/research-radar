单位: Additive Manufacturing Center for Mass Customized Production, National Taipei University of Technology, Taipei 10608, Taiwan
通讯: Cho‐Pei Jiang @ Additive Manufacturing Center for Mass Customized Production, National Taipei University of Technology, Taipei 10608, Ta
相关性: 该论文直接研究SLM制造的点阵结构肋骨植入物，并通过力学测试验证了其与人体肋骨力学性能的匹配，对于AM生物医学材料的方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not cover fatigue testing, surface roughness, as-built morphology, or osseointegration, and only partially addresses stress shielding via stiffness matching.

Motivation· To fabricate customized rib implants using selective laser melting with lattice structures to match mechanical properties of human ribs.
Method· Five face-centered cubic (FCC) based lattice structures were designed and manufactured with two laser energy densities, and mechanical tests were conducted.
Result· The FCC-XYZ lattice with 125 J/mm³ met tensile and flexural requirements but exceeded the stiffness of human ribs.
Validation· Mechanical testing including density, tensile, compressive, and flexural tests.
