相关性: 该论文提出的SafeOpt算法在安全约束下优化未知函数，可迁移至植入物设计中的约束优化问题。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, biomechanics, or implant design; it focuses on general safe Bayesian optimization with Gaussian processes.

边界: This paper focuses on data subset selection and active learning for classification, with no surrogate modeling, finite element analysis, uncertainty quantification, or biomedical/mechanical design context.
动机· 从大数据中选择子集训练分类器，最小化性能损失。
方法· 利用子模性连接朴素贝叶斯和最近邻的似然函数，提出过滤主动子模选择。

Validation· Empirical evaluation on benchmark datasets using Naive Bayes, Nearest Neighbor, SVM, and deep neural network classifiers.
