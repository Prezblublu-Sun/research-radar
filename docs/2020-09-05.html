Densification Behavior and Influence of Building Direction on High Anisotropy in Selective Laser Melting of High-Strength 18Ni-Co-Mo-Ti Maraging Steel

单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, National University of Singapore, 9 Engineering Drive 1, Singapore, 117575, Singapore
通讯: Yuchao Bai @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, National University of Singapore, 9 Engineering Drive 1, S
相关性: 该论文研究18Ni马氏体时效钢的SLM致密化行为和各向异性，但未涉及生物医学植入物、多孔结构或应力遮挡。
边界: No porous structure, no biomedical context, no stress shielding or stiffness matching, no fatigue testing, no surface roughness or osseointegration.
动机· 研究SLM工艺参数和构建方向对18Ni马氏体时效钢致密化和各向异性的影响。
方法· 通过改变激光功率、扫描速度和构建方向进行SLM实验，表征密度、显微组织和力学性能。
结果· 发现构建方向导致显著的各向异性，垂直方向强度更高但延性更低。
验证· 通过密度测量、金相观察和拉伸/压缩实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the densification behavior and anisotropy of 18Ni maraging steel fabricated by selective laser melting under different process parameters and building directions.
Method· SLM experiments with varying laser power, scan speed, and building orientation, followed by density, microstructure, and mechanical property characterization.
Result· Significant anisotropy was observed, with vertical builds exhibiting higher strength but lower ductility compared to horizontal builds.
Validation· Validation through density measurements, metallographic analysis, and tensile/compression mechanical testing.

Microstructure–Property Correlation in a Laser Powder Bed Fusion Processed High‐Strength AF‐9628 Steel

Priyanshi Agrawal, Shivakant Shukla, Saket Thapliyal, Priyanka Agrawal, S.S. Nene et al. Advanced Engineering Materials 2020-09-05 10.1002/adem.202000845
单位: Center for Friction Stir Processing Department of Materials Science and Engineering University of North Texas Denton TX 76203 USA
通讯: Rajiv S. Mishra @ Center for Friction Stir Processing Department of Materials Science and Engineering University of North Texas Denton TX
相关性: 该论文研究高强度钢的LPBF工艺与微观结构，未涉及生物医学植入物或骨整合。
边界: No biomedical context, no porous structure for osseointegration, no fatigue testing, and no surface roughness or as-built morphology relevant to implants.
动机· 探究LPBF制备高强度钢AF-9628获得优异力学性能的微观机制。
方法· 通过多尺度显微表征和强化模型计算，分析打印态材料的微观结构与强化贡献。
结果· 打印态材料为单相马氏体，无碳化物，高密度位错，屈服强度与实验值吻合。
验证· 显微表征数据输入强化模型，计算屈服强度与实验值对比验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To understand the microstructural origins of the exceptional strength and ductility in LPBF-processed high-strength AF-9628 steel.
Method· Multi-scale microscopy (melt pool, porosity, grain morphology, phases, dislocations) combined with analytical strengthening models to quantify contributions.
Result· As-printed material exhibits single-phase martensitic laths, no carbides, high dislocation density, and calculated yield strength matches experimental value.
Validation· Experimental microscopy data fed into strengthening models; calculated yield strength validated against measured mechanical test results.

Manufacturing Aluminum/Multiwalled Carbon Nanotube Composites via Laser Powder Bed Fusion

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Eo Ryeong Lee, S. Shin, Naoki Takata, Makoto Kobashi, Masaki Kato Materials 2020-09-05 10.3390/ma13183927
单位: Department of Materials Science and Metallurgical Engineering, Sunchon National University, Suncheon, Jeollanam-do 57922, Korea
通讯: S. Shin @ Department of Materials Science and Metallurgical Engineering, Sunchon National University, Suncheon, Jeollanam-do 57922
相关性: 该论文研究铝基复合材料而非生物医用植入物，与用户方向无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures for stress shielding, fatigue testing, surface roughness, or osseointegration; it focuses on Al/MWCNT composites for structural applications.
动机· 探索激光粉末床熔融制备铝基碳纳米管复合材料的可行性。
方法· 在25种不同激光功率和扫描速度条件下制备Al/MWCNT复合材料。
结果· 低能量密度下烧结良好，高能量密度导致孔隙增多，弹性模量受激光功率影响更大。
验证· 通过微观结构分析和弹性模量、纳米硬度测试验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a novel approach for fabricating aluminum/multiwalled carbon nanotube composites via laser powder bed fusion for structural applications.
Method· Al/MWCNT composites were fabricated under 25 different LPBF conditions by varying laser power and scan speed, and their microstructures and mechanical properties were analyzed.
Result· Favorable sintering occurred at low energy density, while high energy density increased porosity; elastic modulus was more influenced by laser power, whereas nanohardness was more affected by scan speed.
Validation· Mechanical testing (elastic modulus and nanohardness) and microstructural analysis were performed to validate the fabrication process.

单位: Science Education Study Program, Universitas Ivet, Semarang, Indonesia

Motivation· To develop a STEM-project based bioacoustics digital worksheet to enhance students' critical thinking skills.

Method· Prospective study of 51 patients with aseptic loosening using clinical evaluation and X-ray assessment.
Result· Aseptic loosening is more common in cementless prostheses, especially in the femoral stem, and revision surgery most frequently involves the femoral component.
Validation· No validation; purely clinical observation.
