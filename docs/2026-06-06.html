英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate clinical outcomes and complications of direct anterior approach total hip arthroplasty in obese patients.
Method· A systematic review and meta-analysis of studies on direct anterior approach THA in obese patients.
Result· Obese patients have higher complication rates but similar functional outcomes compared to non-obese patients.
Validation· Meta-analysis of published literature, no original experimental validation.
