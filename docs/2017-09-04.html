Non-probabilistic analysis of laminated composites based on fuzzy uncertainty quantification

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only simulation_case UQ
Susmita Naskar, T. Mukhopadhyay, Srinivas Sriramula ePrints Soton (University of Southampton) 2017-09-04 link
单位: Engineering
通讯: Susmita Naskar @ Engineering
相关性: 该论文研究层合复合材料的模糊不确定性量化，与生物力学植入物设计中的代理模型方法关联较弱。
边界: No FEA surrogate model, no implant or biomechanics context, no constraint handling or multi-objective optimization.
动机· 针对层合复合材料中参数不确定性难以用概率模型描述的问题，提出非概率模糊方法。
方法· 采用模糊集理论量化材料参数不确定性，结合有限元分析进行传播。
结果· 模糊方法有效捕捉了复合材料响应的不确定性范围。
验证· 通过数值算例验证模糊方法的有效性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To address parameter uncertainty in laminated composites where probabilistic models are inadequate, a non-probabilistic fuzzy approach is proposed.
Method· Fuzzy set theory is used to quantify material parameter uncertainty and propagate it through finite element analysis.
Result· The fuzzy method effectively captures the uncertainty bounds of composite responses.
Validation· Numerical examples demonstrate the effectiveness of the fuzzy approach.

Effective Behavior of EFL Teachers as Perceived by Undergraduate Students in Indonesia

Exclude FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Ignore none
Mashadi Said English Language Teaching 2017-09-04 10.5539/elt.v10n10p50
单位: English Language Center, Azzahra University, Jakarta, Indonesia
通讯: Mashadi Said @ English Language Center, Azzahra University, Jakarta, Indonesia
相关性: 该论文研究的是印度尼西亚本科生对英语教师有效行为的看法，与有限元代理建模或生物力学设计无关。
边界: This paper is about EFL teaching behaviors, not about surrogate modeling, FEA, optimization, uncertainty quantification, or implant/biomechanics design.
动机· 探索印度尼西亚本科生认为有效的英语教师行为。
方法· 对270名学生进行问卷调查，涵盖七个教学行为维度。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To identify effective EFL teacher behaviors as perceived by Indonesian undergraduate students.
Method· A questionnaire survey was administered to 270 students covering seven dimensions of teaching behavior.
Result· Students perceived that creating an optimally engaging learning situation is the most significant behavior for effective learning.
Validation· Statistical analysis of questionnaire responses.

通讯: Satoshi Hamai @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Graduate School of Medical Sciences, Kyushu University, Fukuoka, Japan
相关性: 该论文研究全髋关节置换术后运动参与，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡或微动分析。

In‐Jin Shon, Jin‐Kook Yoon, Kyung-Tae Hong Korean Journal of Metals and Materials 2017-09-04 10.3365/kjmm.2017.55.9.632
单位: Division of Advanced Materials Engineering, the Research Center of Hydrogen Fuel Cell, Chonbuk National University, Jeonju 54896, Republic of Korea
通讯: In‐Jin Shon @ Division of Advanced Materials Engineering, the Research Center of Hydrogen Fuel Cell, Chonbuk National University, Jeon
相关性: 该论文研究CoZr-ZrO2复合材料的烧结与力学性能，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡、微动或有限元分析。
边界: This paper focuses on material synthesis and mechanical characterization of a ceramic composite for hip arthroplasty, but does not quantify proximal femur stress shielding, micromotion, or use finite element modeling.
动机· 提高ZrO2陶瓷的断裂韧性以用于髋关节置换
方法· 机械化学合成CoZr和ZrO2纳米粉末，脉冲电流活化烧结
结果· 复合材料硬度和断裂韧性优于单一材料，且生物相容性好
验证· 力学测试和生物相容性评估
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To improve the fracture toughness of ZrO2 ceramics for total hip arthroplasty by forming a nanostructured composite.
Method· Nanopowders of CoZr and ZrO2 were mechanochemically synthesized and consolidated via pulsed current activated sintering within two minutes.
Result· The 2CoZr-ZrO2 composite exhibited superior hardness and fracture toughness compared to monolithic CoZr and ZrO2, along with good biocompatibility.
Validation· Mechanical testing (hardness, fracture toughness) and biocompatibility assays.
