相关性: 该论文使用逆建模与不确定性量化方法解决地热模型问题，与生物力学植入物设计无直接关联。

Motivation· To improve computational efficiency of inverse modeling and uncertainty quantification for geothermal reservoir models.
Method· Surrogate models are employed to accelerate the solution of inverse problems and uncertainty propagation.
Result· The surrogate models significantly reduce computational cost while maintaining accuracy.
Validation· Validation is performed through simulation case studies of a geothermal reservoir model.

边界: No biomechanical modeling, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no FEA or contact mechanics.
动机· 评估单块柄在LCPD患者全髋置换中的10年疗效
方法· 回顾71例LCPD患者（88髋）接受非骨水泥单块柄THA的临床与影像学数据
