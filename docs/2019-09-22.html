相关性: 该论文使用物理信息神经网络进行腐蚀疲劳预测，但与生物力学或植入物设计中的有限元代理建模无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on corrosion-fatigue prognosis in aircraft structures, not on surrogate modeling for biomedical FEA or implant design; it lacks contact mechanics, stress shielding, or biomechanical context.
动机· 解决腐蚀疲劳损伤预测中物理模型与数据驱动结合的难题

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To combine physics-based fatigue models with data-driven methods for accurate corrosion-fatigue damage prognosis.
Method· A physics-informed recurrent neural network is proposed, embedding Paris law in physics-informed layers and using data-driven layers to model corrosion-induced bias.
Result· The hybrid model accurately predicts crack growth under corrosion-fatigue and enables damage ranking across an aircraft fleet.
Validation· Validation is performed through a numerical experiment simulating corrosion-fatigue of Al 2024-T3 alloy aircraft wing panels.

Jueming Hu, Heinz Erzberger, Kai Goebel, Yongming Liu Annual Conference of the PHM Society 2019-09-22 10.36001/phmconf.2019.v11i1.812
单位: Arizona State University
通讯: Jueming Hu @ Arizona State University
相关性: 该论文研究无人机交通控制中的概率安全边界，与FEA替代模型和植入物设计无关。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, implant biomechanics, or structural optimization; it focuses on UAV traffic safety with Monte Carlo simulation.
动机· 为无人机交通管理提供概率安全边界，考虑航向方向的不确定性。
方法· 基于实时数据和概率模拟，设计各向异性的动态安全边界。
结果· 不确定性显著影响安全边界大小，速度是最大影响因素。
验证· 通过蒙特卡洛模拟验证安全边界尺寸与失效概率的关系。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a probabilistic operational safety bound for UAV traffic control that accounts for heading direction and real-time uncertainties.
Method· An anisotropic safety bound is computed individually for each UAV using Monte Carlo simulation with inputs including GPS error, weather, and vehicle state.
Result· Uncertainty critically affects bound size; UAV speed has the largest effect, and bound size increases nonlinearly with decreasing allowable failure probability.
Validation· Validation is performed via Monte Carlo simulation sensitivity studies and failure probability impact analysis.
