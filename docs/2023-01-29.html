单位: Fujian Key Laboratory of Intelligent Machining Technology and Equipment, Fuzhou 350118, China
通讯: Hui Wang @ Fujian Key Laboratory of Intelligent Machining Technology and Equipment, Fuzhou 350118, China
相关性: 该论文研究了LaB6增强Ti-6Al-4V复合材料的SLM制备与力学性能，未涉及生物医用植入物的多孔结构、应力屏蔽或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration relevant to biomedical implants; it focuses on material strengthening via reinforcement particles without evaluation of implant-specific performance.
动机· 通过添加LaB6提高SLM成型Ti-6Al-4V的力学性能与耐磨性。
方法· 在Ti-6Al-4V中掺杂不同含量LaB6，进行SLM成型并分析微观结构与力学性能。
结果· 3 wt% LaB6使硬度提升35.71%，抗拉强度提升14.5%，磨损体积减少47.5%。
验证· 通过密度测量、拉伸测试、硬度测试和磨损实验验证性能。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To enhance the mechanical properties and wear resistance of Ti-6Al-4V alloy fabricated by selective laser melting through LaB6 addition.
Method· LaB6 powder was mixed with Ti-6Al-4V at varying weight fractions (0-3 wt%) and processed by SLM; microstructure and mechanical properties were characterized.
Result· Adding 3 wt% LaB6 increased hardness by 35.71%, tensile strength by 14.5%, and reduced wear volume by 47.5% compared to pure Ti-6Al-4V.
Validation· Mechanical testing (tensile, hardness, wear) and density measurements confirmed improved performance with LaB6 addition.
