Failure location prediction by finite element analysis for an additive manufactured mandible implant

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Skim only clinical Patient-specific
Jinxing Huo, Per Dérand, Lars‐Erik Rännar, Jan‐Michaél Hirsch, E. Kristofer Gamstedt Medical Engineering & Physics 2015-07-27 10.1016/j.medengphy.2015.06.001
单位: Division of Applied Mechanics, Department of Engineering Sciences, Uppsala University, Box 534, SE-751 21 Uppsala, Sweden
通讯: E. Kristofer Gamstedt @ Division of Applied Mechanics, Department of Engineering Sciences, Uppsala University, Box 534, SE-751 21 Uppsala, Swede
相关性: 该论文涉及增材制造下颌骨植入物的有限元分析，但未涉及应力遮挡、疲劳测试或表面形貌等核心方向。
边界: No fatigue testing, no stress shielding quantification, no surface roughness or osseointegration analysis.
动机· 预测增材制造下颌骨植入物的失效位置
方法· 基于CT数据重建下颌骨和植入物几何，建立有限元模型分析应力分布
结果· 应力集中位置与体内断裂位置一致，FEA可用于术前设计优化
验证· 与临床断裂案例对比验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To predict the failure location of an additively manufactured mandible implant using finite element analysis.
Method· A patient-specific finite element model was built from CT data to simulate stress distribution during mastication.
Result· The predicted stress concentration matched the in vivo fracture location, demonstrating FEA as a design optimization tool.
Validation· Validated by comparing simulation results with the actual clinical fracture case.

Identifying clinically meaningful benchmarks for gait improvement after total hip arthroplasty

Low Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Background only clinical Exp. validation
Kharma C. Foucher Journal of Orthopaedic Research® 2015-07-27 10.1002/jor.22996
单位: Department of Kinesiology and Nutrition University of Illinois at Chicago Chicago Illinois
通讯: Kharma C. Foucher @ Department of Kinesiology and Nutrition University of Illinois at Chicago Chicago Illinois
相关性: 该论文关注全髋关节置换术后步态改善的临床基准，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡或微动等生物力学建模。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, no implant stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no FEA or contact mechanics.
动机· 缺乏全髋关节置换术后步态改善的临床基准。
方法· 基于145名患者术前和术后步态数据，定义最小临床重要改善值。
结果· 确定了步行速度、髋关节活动度和内收力矩的临床基准。
验证· 通过Harris髋关节评分和逻辑回归验证基准的临床意义。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To establish clinically meaningful benchmarks for gait improvement after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Preoperative and 1-year postoperative gait data from 145 subjects were analyzed to define minimum clinically important improvement values using the 75th percentile of cumulative percent with Harris Hip Score ≥ 80.
Result· Benchmarks for walking speed, hip range of motion, and adduction moment were identified, with significant associations to sex, BMI, and preoperative HHS.
Validation· Clinical validation via Harris Hip Scores and logistic regression analysis.

通讯: Yihua Loo @ Institute of Bioengineering and Nanotechnology, 31 Biopolis Way, The Nanos, #04-01, Singapore 138669

边界: No AI integration; purely material science development of self-assembling peptide hydrogels.
动机· 开发具有足够机械强度的可打印支架以支持三维细胞培养
方法· 设计含赖氨酸的六肽，自组装形成纳米纤维水凝胶

Validation· In vitro culture of human stem cells and primary cells confirmed biocompatibility and formation of gastrointestinal and skin organotypic structures.
