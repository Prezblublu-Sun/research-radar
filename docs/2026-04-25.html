单位: Department of Oral Implantology, The Affiliated Yantai Stomatological Hospital, Binzhou Medical University, Yantai, China; Department of Second Dental Center, Shanghai Ninth People's Hospital, School
通讯: Lingfei Wei @ Department of Oral Implantology, The Affiliated Yantai Stomatological Hospital, Binzhou Medical University, Yantai, Chin

边界: This paper is about calcium phosphate cement bone graft with BMP-2, not about additive manufacturing (LPBF) for biomedical implants; it contains no porous metal structures, stress shielding analysis, fatigue testing, or surface roughness characterization.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate bone regeneration and osseointegration of low-dosage BMP-2 incorporated calcium phosphate cement in lateral ridge augmentation with simultaneous implant placement.
Method· Chronic horizontal alveolar bone defects were created in dog mandibles, and implants were placed with different grafting materials; micro-CT and histology were performed after 3 months.
Result· Low-dosage BMP-2 incorporated CPC significantly enhanced peri-implant bone regeneration and maintained ridge contours compared to CPC alone.
Validation· In vivo canine study with micro-CT and histological analysis.

Comparison of Cemented and Cementless Hemiarthroplasty Outcomes: A Retrospective Analysis of Mortality, Complications, and Subsidence

Murat Taşçı, Furkan Uçar, Elif Reyyan Çadırcıbaşı Düzce Tıp Fakültesi Dergisi 2026-04-25 10.18678/dtfd.1810569
单位: Ümraniye Training and Research Hospital, Department of Orthopaedics and Traumatology
通讯: Murat Taşçı @ Ümraniye Training and Research Hospital, Department of Orthopaedics and Traumatology
相关性: 该论文为临床回顾性分析，比较骨水泥与非骨水泥半髋置换术的死亡率和并发症，未涉及生物力学建模或应力分析。
边界: This paper does not include any finite element analysis, stress shielding quantification, micromotion modeling, or implant design evaluation; it is a purely clinical outcomes study without biomechanical context.
动机· 比较骨水泥与非骨水泥半髋置换术的临床结果和并发症。
方法· 回顾性分析364例股骨颈骨折半髋置换患者。
结果· 两组死亡率与下沉率无显著差异，术前等待时间与一年死亡率相关。
验证· 基于医院病历和影像学数据的回顾性分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare clinical and radiological outcomes between cemented and cementless hemiarthroplasty for femoral neck fractures.
Method· A retrospective cohort study of 364 patients with clinical follow-up and radiographic evaluation of stem subsidence.
Result· No significant difference in mortality or subsidence between groups; preoperative waiting time was associated with early mortality.
Validation· Statistical analysis of patient records and radiographs without biomechanical validation.
