Influence of Laser Powder Bed Fusion Parameters on Surface Characteristics and Mechanical Properties of 316L Porous Bone Scaffolds

High AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Read now mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Shubo Xu, Yanrui Wang, Tianhua Li, Xinyi Zhang, Ying Zhao et al. Advanced Engineering Materials 2026-04-25 10.1002/adem.202502065
单位: School of Materials Science and Engineering Shandong Jianzhu University Jinan China
通讯: Shubo Xu @ School of Materials Science and Engineering Shandong Jianzhu University Jinan China  |  Aijun Tang @ School of Materials Science and Engineering Shandong Jianzhu University Jinan China
相关性: 该论文直接研究了LPBF工艺参数对316L多孔骨支架的表面特性、力学性能和腐蚀行为的影响，与用户关于增材制造生物医用植入物的方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not report fatigue testing, osseointegration, or in vivo validation.
动机· 探究LPBF工艺参数对多孔骨支架性能的影响机制。
方法· 采用正交实验设计结合有限元模拟与实验验证，分析九组参数下的残余应力、表面粗糙度等。
结果· 残余应力和表面粗糙度与能量密度正相关，界面剪切强度呈非线性变化，最佳耐腐蚀性能在78.70 J/mm³能量密度下获得。
验证· 通过表面粗糙度、显微硬度、微观结构和电化学腐蚀测试进行实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To systematically investigate the influence of laser powder bed fusion parameters on residual stress, surface roughness, and mechanical properties of porous bone scaffolds.
Method· An orthogonal experimental design with nine parameter sets was used, combining finite element simulation and experimental characterization of surface roughness, microhardness, microstructure, and electrochemical corrosion.
Result· Residual stress and surface roughness positively correlate with energy density, while interfacial shear strength shows a nonlinear trend; optimal corrosion resistance occurs at 78.70 J/mm³ energy density.
Validation· Experimental validation via surface roughness, microhardness, microstructure, and electrochemical corrosion measurements.

Inference of Online Newton Methods with Nesterov's Accelerated Sketching

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Background only numerical_benchmark UQ
Haoxuan Wang, Xinchen Du, Sen Na arXiv preprint 2026-04-25 link
相关性: 该论文研究在线牛顿方法的推理不确定性量化，但未涉及有限元分析或生物力学设计，方法难以直接迁移。
边界: This paper focuses on online optimization and uncertainty quantification for streaming data, with no application to finite element analysis, surrogate modeling, or biomechanical implant design.
动机· 在线推理中二阶方法计算成本高，需高效不确定性量化方法
方法· 提出带Nesterov加速的草图投影在线牛顿方法，近似计算牛顿方向
结果· 方法达到一阶方法的复杂度，且渐近正态性和协方差估计有理论保证
验证· 在回归模型上通过数值实验验证方法优越性
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Online inference requires efficient uncertainty quantification, but second-order methods are computationally expensive due to solving Newton systems.
Method· An online Newton method with Hessian averaging is proposed, where the Newton direction is approximated via a sketch-and-project solver with Nesterov's acceleration, achieving O(d^2) complexity.
Result· The method achieves asymptotic normality of the last iterate and provides a fully online covariance estimator with non-asymptotic convergence guarantees.
Validation· Extensive experiments on regression models demonstrate the superiority of the proposed method for online inference.

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Background only numerical_benchmark UQ
Qishi Zhan, Minxuan Hu, Liang He, Guansu Wang, Jiaxin Liu arXiv preprint 2026-04-25 link
相关性: 该论文讨论贝叶斯深度学习中的方差问题，与FEA代理模型或植入物设计无直接关联。
边界: No FEA surrogate modeling, no constraint handling, no uncertainty quantification for design optimization, and no biomechanics or implant context.
动机· 揭示单次随机种子评估在贝叶斯深度学习中的不可靠性。
方法· 通过50次重复实验分析CRPS方差轨迹，并比较不同方法。
结果· 发现异方差方法在中等训练量时出现方差峰值，导致单次评估误差极大。
验证· 在六个回归数据集上通过重复实验验证方差轨迹的规律性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To demonstrate that single-seed evaluation of Bayesian deep learning methods can be highly unreliable due to variance in the evaluation metric.
Method· Conducted 50 independent repetitions on six regression datasets and analyzed CRPS variance trajectories across training sizes for different methods.
Result· Methods with heteroscedastic variance heads exhibit pronounced variance peaks at intermediate training sizes, leading to high single-seed estimation error.
Validation· Validated by showing Spearman correlations above 0.96 between local CRPS variance and single-seed error on all real datasets.

通讯: Murat Taşçı @ Ümraniye Training and Research Hospital, Department of Orthopaedics and Traumatology
相关性: 该论文为临床回顾性分析，比较骨水泥与非骨水泥半髋置换术的死亡率和并发症，未涉及生物力学建模或应力分析。
边界: This paper does not include any finite element analysis, stress shielding quantification, micromotion modeling, or implant design evaluation; it is a purely clinical outcomes study without biomechanical context.
动机· 比较骨水泥与非骨水泥半髋置换术的临床结果和并发症。

Motivation· To compare clinical and radiological outcomes between cemented and cementless hemiarthroplasty for femoral neck fractures.
Method· A retrospective cohort study of 364 patients with clinical follow-up and radiographic evaluation of stem subsidence.
Result· No significant difference in mortality or subsidence between groups; preoperative waiting time was associated with early mortality.
Validation· Statistical analysis of patient records and radiographs without biomechanical validation.
