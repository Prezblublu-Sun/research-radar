Enhanced Osteogenesis and Antibacterial Properties of Ketoprofen-Loaded MgCu-MOF74-Coated Titanium Alloy for Bone Implant.

Ziqing Duan, Yifeng Yao, Jiamin Liu, Yanni Tan, Qingge Wang et al. Journal of functional biomaterials 2025-06-14 10.3390/jfb16060222
单位: State Key Laboratory of Powder Metallurgy, Central South University, Changsha 410083, China.
通讯: Hong Wu @ State Key Laboratory of Powder Metallurgy, Central South University, Changsha 410083, China. [推断]
相关性: 该论文涉及钛合金表面MOF涂层用于骨植入，但未涉及增材制造、多孔结构或力学测试，与您的AM生物医学方向不相关。
边界: This paper does not involve additive manufacturing (LPBF), porous structure design, mechanical testing (static or fatigue), or as-built surface morphology; it focuses solely on a drug-releasing coating with in vitro biological evaluation.
动机· 解决钛植入体骨整合差和术后感染导致的镇痛不足问题。

结果· 涂层促进成骨分化并释放镁铜离子提供抗菌性。
验证· 体外细胞实验验证碱性磷酸酶活性和矿化。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To address poor osseointegration and inadequate analgesia due to postoperative infections in titanium implants.
Method· Ketoprofen was loaded into MgCu-MOF74 and anchored onto titanium alloy via dopamine-mediated adhesion.
Result· The coating enhanced osteogenic differentiation and provided antibacterial properties through ion release.
Validation· In vitro cell assays confirmed increased alkaline phosphatase activity and extracellular matrix mineralization.
