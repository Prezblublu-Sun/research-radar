Effects of Total Hip Arthroplasty on Stress Adaptation and Bone Remodeling in Lower Limbs

Abdul Halim Abdullah, Mitsugu Todo Evergreen 2015-03-01 10.5109/1500422
单位: Interdisciplinary Graduate School of Engineering Sciences, Kyushu University | Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, Universiti Teknologi MARA
通讯: Abdul Halim Abdullah @ Interdisciplinary Graduate School of Engineering Sciences, Kyushu University | Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, Univer
相关性: 该论文针对全髋关节置换术后的应力遮挡和骨重塑进行了患者特定有限元分析，与用户的研究方向直接相关。
边界: Does not report micromotion values, lacks explicit contact/friction modeling at the bone-implant interface, and does not quantify stress in Gruen zones by name.
动机· 预测全髋关节置换术后下肢的应力适应和骨重塑。
方法· 基于CT的79岁女性患者特定下肢有限元模型，模拟THA术后站立工况。
结果· 手术侧股骨近端应力改变最大导致骨吸收，远端可能骨增厚。
验证· 无实验验证，仅为计算预测。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To predict stress adaptation and bone remodeling in lower limbs following total hip arthroplasty using finite element analysis.
Method· A CT-based patient-specific finite element model of a 79-year-old female with hip osteoarthritis was constructed, and the hip joint was replaced with a THA prosthesis; a standing load of 60 kg was applied.
Result· The proximal region of the operated limb exhibited the largest stress change leading to bone resorption, while the distal region showed potential bone thickening over 5 years.
Validation· The study is purely computational with no experimental validation; results are based on simulated predictions.
