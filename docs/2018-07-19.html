相关性: 该论文提出的物理信息压缩感知方法可用于减少传感器数量并高效重建温度场，其不确定性量化方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理模型中的稀疏采样与误差补偿。
边界: This paper focuses on temperature monitoring in additive manufacturing, not on surrogate modeling for expensive FEA simulations in implant biomechanics, and does not address multi-objective optimization or constraint handling.
动机· 减少制造过程监测中传感器数量和数据量，实现高效实时温度监测。

Result· The method enables efficient reconstruction of temperature distributions and compensates systematic errors using Gaussian process uncertainty quantification.
Validation· Validation is performed through simulation cases of fused filament fabrication, demonstrating reconstruction accuracy and uncertainty quantification.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the clinical outcomes of total hip arthroplasty via the direct anterior approach versus the transgluteal lateral approach.
Method· A prospective randomized controlled trial evaluating Harris Hip Score, XSFMA, SF-36, Stepwatch Activity Monitor, and timed 25-foot walk.
Result· The direct anterior approach resulted in higher patient activity at 1 year postoperatively, with no significant difference in hip function.
Validation· Clinical randomized controlled trial with 123 arthroplasties and 12-month follow-up.

Motivation· To determine whether intraprosthetic dislocation remains a clinical concern with modern dual mobility acetabular components.
Method· Retrospective review of dislocation rates in dual mobility total hip arthroplasties performed between 2010 and 2016.
