相关性: 该论文将贝叶斯优化用于晶体塑性材料参数标定，其方法可迁移至植入物设计中的昂贵仿真优化问题。
边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, stress shielding, or multi-constraint design optimization; it focuses on crystal plasticity parameter identification.
