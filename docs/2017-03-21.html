边界: This paper is a meta-analysis of tranexamic acid administration for blood loss reduction, with no biomechanical modeling, implant design, or FEA of femoral stem stress shielding or micromotion.
动机· 比较全髋关节置换术中联合静脉与局部应用氨甲环酸与单用静脉的疗效。
方法· 对6项随机对照试验进行荟萃分析，汇总失血量、输血率等指标。
结果· 联合用药可减少总失血量250毫升，降低输血率9.1%。
验证· 基于已发表随机对照试验的荟萃分析。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the efficacy and safety of combined intravenous and topical tranexamic acid versus intravenous alone in reducing blood loss after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A meta-analysis of six randomized controlled trials was conducted, pooling data on total blood loss, hidden blood loss, transfusion rate, hemoglobin drop, length of stay, and deep venous thrombosis.
Result· Combined tranexamic acid reduced total blood loss by 250.37 ml and transfusion rate by 9.1% without increasing complications.
Validation· Meta-analysis of existing randomized controlled trials.
