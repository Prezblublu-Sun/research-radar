Learning Constitutive Relations from Indirect Observations Using Deep Neural Networks

High FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Direct Read now simulation_case UQ
Daniel Z. Huang, Kailai Xu, Charbel Farhat, Eric Darve arXiv preprint 2019-05-29 10.1016/j.jcp.2020.109491
相关性: 该论文直接针对固体力学中的本构关系学习，使用神经网络和有限元方法结合，并包含不确定性量化，方法可迁移至植入物力学建模。
边界: The paper does not apply the method to implant or biomechanics design, nor does it address multi-objective optimization or constraints.
动机· 从间接观测数据中学习力学系统的本构关系并量化不确定性。
方法· 将神经网络表示的本构模型嵌入有限元框架，结合自动微分进行训练。
结果· 神经网络在纤维增强板和橡胶膜问题上优于传统函数逼近器。
验证· 通过两个固体力学数值算例验证框架有效性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To learn constitutive relations directly from indirect observation data with uncertainty quantification for mechanical systems.
Method· A neural network representing the constitutive relation is embedded into a finite element solver, trained via automatic differentiation.
Result· The neural network outperforms piecewise linear, radial basis function, and RBF network approximators on fiber-reinforced plate and rubbery membrane problems.
Validation· Validation is performed on two numerical solid mechanics examples: a multiscale fiber-reinforced plate and a nonlinear rubbery membrane.
