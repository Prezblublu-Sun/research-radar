边界: No biomedical context, no porous structure design, no stress shielding or stiffness matching, no osseointegration or biocompatibility testing.
动机· 研究粉末氧化对高温合金LPBF成形质量的影响。
方法· 在不同氧化条件下处理粉末并分析其性质及打印件质量。

Validation· Validation via powder characterization (e.g., flowability, morphology) and mechanical testing of printed specimens.
