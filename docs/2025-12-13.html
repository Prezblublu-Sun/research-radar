Uncertainty Quantification for Machine Learning: One Size Does Not Fit All

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Paul Hofman, Yusuf Sale, Eyke Hüllermeier arXiv preprint 2025-12-13 link
相关性: 该论文讨论了不确定性量化（UQ）中不同度量方法的适用性，对FEA代理模型中的UQ设计有参考价值。
边界: This paper does not apply UQ to FEA surrogate modeling or biomechanical implant design; it is a general theoretical study on UQ measures for classification tasks.
动机· 机器学习在安全关键应用中需要合适的不确定性量化方法。
方法· 使用灵活的不确定性度量族，区分总不确定性、偶然不确定性和认知不确定性，并通过适当评分规则实例化。
结果· 不同任务需要不同特性的不确定性度量，选择性预测应匹配任务损失，OOD检测中互信息最优，主动学习中基于零一损失的认知不确定性最佳。
验证· 在分类基准数据集上通过数值实验验证各度量在不同任务中的表现。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To address the need for task-specific uncertainty quantification in safety-critical machine learning applications.
Method· A flexible family of uncertainty measures distinguishing total, aleatoric, and epistemic uncertainty is instantiated via proper scoring rules to control characteristics.
Result· Different tasks require different uncertainty measures: selective prediction benefits from matching the task loss, out-of-distribution detection favors mutual information, and active learning is best with zero-one loss-based epistemic uncertainty.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on classification datasets demonstrate the performance of various uncertainty measures across selective prediction, OOD detection, and active learning tasks.
