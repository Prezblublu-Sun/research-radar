Classification and Bayesian Optimization for Likelihood-Free Inference

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project none UQ
Michael U. Gutmann, Jukka Corander, Ritabrata Dutta, Samuel Kaski arXiv preprint 2015-02-19 link
相关性: 该论文提出的基于贝叶斯优化的无似然推断方法可迁移至生物力学设计中的昂贵仿真优化问题，但未涉及具体FEA或植入物应用。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling or implant design; it focuses on general likelihood-free inference without validation on structural mechanics or biomedical problems.
动机· 当似然函数无法解析计算时，如何高效推断模型参数？
方法· 通过分类器构建近似似然函数，结合贝叶斯优化寻找低差异区域。
结果· 实现了无需似然函数的参数推断，并提高了计算效率。
验证· 在合成数据上进行数值验证，未涉及实验或实际应用。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To enable parameter inference for models with intractable likelihoods by efficiently identifying regions of low discrepancy between simulated and observed data.
Method· Use a classifier to approximate the likelihood and Bayesian optimization to guide the search for parameter regions that produce simulated data closely matching observations.
Result· The approach allows likelihood-free inference with improved efficiency compared to standard rejection-based methods.
Validation· Validation is performed on synthetic examples; no experimental or real-world data is used.

相关性: 该论文涉及主动偏好排序学习，与有限元代理建模或植入物设计无关联。
边界: This paper focuses on active learning for ranking via pairwise comparisons, with no connection to finite element surrogate modeling, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification, or biomechanical implant design.
动机· 用少量成对比较高效学习排序。

Method· The authors propose using sorting algorithms, particularly Quicksort, as an active learning strategy for selecting pairwise comparisons.
Result· Empirical results show that repeated sorting performs comparably to state-of-the-art active learning methods at a fraction of the computational cost.
Validation· Validation is performed on synthetic data under the Bradley-Terry model and compared with random sampling and existing active learning approaches.
