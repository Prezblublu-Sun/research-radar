单位: NASA Langley Research Center
通讯: Thomas K. West @ NASA Langley Research Center
相关性: 该论文研究地球再入大气层后体辐射加热的不确定性与敏感性分析，与生物力学植入物设计或有限元代理建模无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on hypersonic entry radiative heating, not on surrogate modeling for biomedical FEA or implant design; no contact mechanics, no structural optimization, no biomechanical context.
动机· 量化地球再入后体辐射加热预测中的不确定性并识别关键参数。
方法· 采用稀疏配点非侵入式多项式混沌与全局非线性敏感性分析。
结果· 从388个参数中识别出4个关键参数，贡献了约95%的不确定性。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify uncertainty and identify dominant parameters in afterbody radiative heating predictions for Earth entry vehicles.
Method· A sparse collocation non-intrusive polynomial chaos method combined with global nonlinear sensitivity analysis was employed.
Result· Only four out of 388 uncertain parameters accounted for nearly 95% of the total uncertainty in afterbody radiation.
Validation· The approach was validated on a simulation case of the Stardust capsule at peak afterbody radiation conditions.
