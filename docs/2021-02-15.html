边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, implant biomechanics, or constraint handling in design optimization.
动机· 解决仅有少量实验数据时系统响应的逆不确定性量化问题。

Result· The hyper-ellipsoid method shows superior computational efficiency for high-dimensional problems compared to interval-based inverse UQ.
Validation· Validated on an analytical test case and a jet engine performance model.
