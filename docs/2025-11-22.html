Validation· Theoretical predictions are compared between diblock and triblock architectures and across segregation strengths.

相关性: 该论文研究成分数据的共形预测，与FEA代理模型或生物力学设计完全无关。
边界: This paper does not address expensive simulation sampling, constraint handling, or any FEA/biomechanics context; it focuses on statistical prediction sets for compositional data.
动机· 成分数据的预测集构建缺乏几何适配方法
方法· 提出三种共形预测策略：残差分位法、近似最高密度区域法和网格离散法
结果· 近似HDR方法鲁棒性较好，网格法降低了预测区域过度覆盖

English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop valid prediction sets for compositional data that respect the geometry of the simplex space.
Method· Three conformal prediction strategies are investigated: quantile residual method, approximate highest density region (HDR), and grid-based discretization over the simplex.
Result· The approximate HDR method shows robust coverage, while the grid discretization reduces overcoverage and prediction region area.
Validation· Validation is performed through simulation studies under various scenarios and two real datasets (sleep stages and biomass allocation).

相关性: 该论文研究遥感图像分类，与有限元代理建模或生物力学方向无关。
边界: This paper focuses on remote sensing image classification with semi-supervised active learning, not on surrogate modeling for expensive simulation, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification, or biomechanics applications.
动机· 减少遥感图像分类中人工标注成本。
方法· 提出分层半监督主动学习框架，融合自训练和层次化样本查询。
结果· 仅用极少标注数据即达到全监督准确率95%以上。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To reduce the costly manual annotation burden in remote sensing image classification by leveraging abundant unlabeled data.
Method· A hierarchical semi-supervised active learning framework that iteratively combines weak-to-strong self-training with a progressive clustering-based query strategy for scalable, diverse, and uncertain sample selection.
Result· The proposed method achieves over 95% of fully-supervised accuracy with only 2–8% labeled training data on three benchmark datasets.
Validation· Extensive experiments on UCM, AID, and NWPU-RESISC45 datasets demonstrate consistent superiority over SSL-only or AL-only baselines.
