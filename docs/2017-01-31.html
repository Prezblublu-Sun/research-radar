Promising characteristics of gradient porosity Ti-6Al-4V alloy prepared by SLM process

Michaela Fousová, Dalibor Vojtěch, Jiří Kubásek, Eva Jablonská, Jaroslav Fojt Journal of the mechanical behavior of biomedical materials/Journal of mechanical behavior of biomedical materials 2017-01-31 10.1016/j.jmbbm.2017.01.043
单位: Department of Metals and Corrosion Engineering, University of Chemistry and Technology Prague, Technická 5, 166 28 Prague 6, Czech Republic. Electronic address: fousovam@vscht.cz
通讯: Michaela Fousová @ Department of Metals and Corrosion Engineering, University of Chemistry and Technology Prague, Technická 5, 166 28 Pragu

方法· 采用SLM工艺制备梯度多孔Ti-6Al-4V合金试样。
结果· 获得了可调控的力学性能，弹性模量接近皮质骨。
验证· 通过压缩实验和微观形貌表征验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To mitigate stress shielding in biomedical implants by designing gradient porosity structures that mimic bone stiffness.

Validation· Mechanical compression tests and microstructural analysis (SEM) were conducted to confirm porosity and stiffness matching.

通讯: Takayoshi Nakano @ Division of Materials and Manufacturing Science, Graduate School of Engineering, Osaka University, 2-1, Yamadaoka, Suita
相关性: 该论文专注于通过选区激光熔化调控钛合金的微观结构以降低弹性模量，但未涉及股骨柄植入物的应力遮挡或界面力学分析。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop low Young's modulus beta-type titanium alloy implants that can mitigate stress shielding in orthopedic applications.
Method· Crystallographic texture was controlled in Ti–15Mo–5Zr–3Al alloy produced by selective laser melting by varying scanning strategies between layers.

单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Fukuoka University Faculty of Medicine, Nanakuma, Fukuoka - Japan
通讯: Hajime Seo @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Fukuoka University Faculty of Medicine, Nanakuma, Fukuoka - Japan
相关性: 该论文研究髋臼杯前倾角测量方法，不涉及股骨柄应力遮挡或微动分析。
边界: This paper focuses on radiographic measurement of acetabular component anteversion, not on femoral stem stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics.

方法· 采用对侧髋关节屈曲45°的侧位X线片，与CT测量结果对比。
结果· 新方法测量的前倾角值更接近CT参考值。
验证· 与CT测量对比，评估观察者间和观察者内信度。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To propose a new radiographic method for measuring acetabular component anteversion that improves accuracy over standard cross-table lateral radiography.

Result· The new method showed mean anteversion values closer to CT reference (22.5° vs 24.9° for standard CL, p=0.112) with excellent reliability.
Validation· Intraobserver/interobserver reliability was assessed, and plain radiography measurements were validated against CT measurements as reference standard.
