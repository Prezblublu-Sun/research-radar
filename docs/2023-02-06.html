相关性: 该论文提出的高斯过程回归替代模型方法可用于生物力学中植入物设计的温度或应力场模拟，具有不确定性量化能力。
边界: Paper focuses on subsurface temperature quantification in additive manufacturing, not on implant biomechanics or stress/strain surrogate modeling for design optimization.
动机· 解决激光熔化过程中难以直接测量亚表面温度的问题

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To overcome the challenge of directly measuring subsurface temperature during laser melting in additive manufacturing.
Method· A Gaussian process regression surrogate model is trained on coupled mechanistic simulations (heat transfer and fluid flow) and X-ray diffraction simulations to predict subsurface temperature distribution metrics with uncertainty.
Result· Temperature metrics are extracted with 5% to 15% uncertainty; maximum solid-phase temperature reaches solidus, while mean and minimum temperatures are hundreds of degrees lower.
