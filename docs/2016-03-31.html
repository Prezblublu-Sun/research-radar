相关性: 该论文涉及植入物表面类型与骨-金属结合强度的关系，但未涉及LPBF制造、疲劳测试或应力屏蔽分析。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no fatigue testing, no stress shielding or stiffness matching analysis, and no surface roughness or as-built morphology characterization.

Motivation· To compare the bone-metal block strength of different implant surfaces under normal and osteoporotic conditions in a rat model.
Method· Implants made of porous titanium, tantalum, and coated surfaces were inserted into rat femoral defects, and axial compression to failure was performed after 14 days.
Result· Porous tantalum implants exhibited the highest failure load in both normal and osteoporotic bone.
Validation· In vivo rat study with biomechanical push-out testing.

Total Hip Arthroplasty in Patient with Aplastic Anemia

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore clinical
Young Wook Lim, Seung Chan Kim, Soon Yong Kwon, Do Joon Park, Yong Sik Kim Hip & Pelvis 2016-03-31 10.5371/hp.2016.28.1.24
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Seoul St. Mary’s Hospital, College of Medicine, The Catholic University of Korea, Seoul, Korea
通讯: Young Wook Lim @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Seoul St. Mary’s Hospital, College of Medicine, The Catholic University of Korea, Seo
相关性: 该论文仅报道再生障碍性贫血患者全髋关节置换术的临床结果，未涉及生物力学分析或植入物设计。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no FEA, no implant design comparison.
动机· 评估再生障碍性贫血患者全髋关节置换术的临床结果
方法· 回顾性分析29例患者45髋的临床与影像学数据
结果· 植入物固定稳定，Harris髋关节评分显著改善
验证· 临床随访3年以上
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the clinical outcomes of total hip arthroplasty in patients with aplastic anemia.
Method· A retrospective review of 29 patients (45 hips) with aplastic anemia who underwent THA, with perioperative transfusion protocol and minimum 3-year follow-up.
Result· Implant fixation was stable and Harris hip scores improved significantly from 54.2 to 90.8 points.
Validation· Clinical and radiographic evaluation with minimum 3-year follow-up.
