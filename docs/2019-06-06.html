Method· Retrospective radiographic analysis of 29 patients measuring pelvic incidence, pelvic tilt, sacral slope, lumbar lordosis, and other parameters before and after THA, compared with 45 normal controls.

Validation· Clinical radiographic measurements with statistical comparison to a normal control group.
