Uncertainty Quantification and Validation of Lattice Structures Fabricated by Selective Laser Melting

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Transferable Skim only mechanical_testing Exp. validation UQ
Recep M. Gorguluarslan, Seung-Kyum Choi, Hae‐Jin Choi 2017-08-06 10.1115/detc2017-67438
单位: Georgia Institute of Technology, Atlanta, GA
通讯: Recep M. Gorguluarslan @ Georgia Institute of Technology, Atlanta, GA
相关性: 该论文提出了一种用于增材制造晶格结构力学性能预测的不确定性量化方法，虽未直接涉及生物医学植入物，但其方法可迁移至医用多孔植入物的设计验证。
边界: No biomedical context, no stress shielding or stiffness matching analysis, no fatigue testing, no surface roughness or osseointegration characterization.
动机· 量化增材制造晶格结构力学性能的不确定性以提高预测精度。
方法· 基于微CT重建单胞有限元模型，采用贝叶斯信息准则进行多尺度随机均质化。
结果· 所提方法能准确预测晶格结构的弹性行为。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To improve predictive accuracy of mechanical response in additively manufactured lattice structures by quantifying uncertainties inherent in the process.
Method· A multi-level stochastic upscaling process using micro-CT reconstructed high-resolution finite element models and Bayesian Information Criterion for parametric uncertainty quantification.
Result· The proposed methodology enabled accurate prediction of elastic behavior of lattice structures, validated against compression tests.
Validation· Experimental compression tests on SLM-fabricated lattice structures were used to validate the stochastic upscaling approach at both strut and lattice levels.
