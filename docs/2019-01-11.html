相关性: 该论文研究SLM制造Inconel 718的疲劳性能，但材料非生物医用、无多孔结构或应力遮挡分析。
边界: No biomedical implant context, no porous structure for osseointegration, no stress shielding or stiffness matching, and material is Inconel 718 not Ti alloy or CoCr.
动机· 检测SLM制造Inconel 718疲劳性能的缺陷不确定性
方法· 使用微型试样统计表面/近表面气孔对疲劳寿命的影响
结果· 表面气孔降低疲劳强度并增大数据分散性，热处理可缓解

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To detect the uncertainty in fatigue properties of SLM-fabricated Inconel 718 parts induced by voids.
Method· Miniature specimens were used to statistically analyze the effect of surface and near-surface voids on fatigue life scatter.
Result· Surface voids reduced fatigue strength and increased data scatter, which could be mitigated by proper heat treatment.
Validation· Fatigue testing of miniature specimens with different void distributions validated the defect-dominated uncertainty.

通讯: Sung Mu Heo @ South Western Sydney Clinical School, UNSW Sydney, Level 2, Clinical Building, Liverpool Hospital, Cnr Elizabeth and Gou
相关性: 该论文研究髋关节置换术后患者报告并发症的可靠性，未涉及任何生物力学建模或植入物设计。
边界: This paper does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or any biomechanical metrics; it is purely a clinical outcomes study with no FEA or implant mechanics.
动机· 验证患者报告并发症的准确性，以评估其在临床实践中的可靠性。
方法· 比较364例髋膝关节置换术后患者与外科医生报告的并发症，计算敏感性和特异性。

Motivation· To assess the accuracy of patient-reported complications after hip or knee arthroplasty for clinical utility.
Method· A retrospective study comparing patient-reported complications from the ACORN registry with surgeon-reported data from electronic medical records in 364 patients.
Result· Patients accurately reported absence of complications but had low sensitivity for reporting actual complications.
Validation· Validation against surgeon-reported complications in electronic medical records.
