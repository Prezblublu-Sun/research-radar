Conductivity Measurement of E-textiles using a Microstrip Ring Resonator

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Tien M. Nguyen, Jae‐Young Chung International Journal of Engineering and Technology 2017-04-30 10.21817/ijet/2017/v9i2/170902067
单位: Dept. of Electrical & Information Engineering, Seoul National Univ. of Sci. & Tech., 232 Gongneung-ro, Nowon-gu, Seoul, 139-743, Korea
通讯: Tien M. Nguyen @ Dept. of Electrical & Information Engineering, Seoul National Univ. of Sci. & Tech., 232 Gongneung-ro, Nowon-gu, Seoul,
相关性: 该论文使用基于代理模型的优化方法测量纺织品电导率，与FEA替代建模和生物力学设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on conductivity measurement of e-textiles using a microstrip ring resonator, with no FEA surrogate modeling, no biomechanics or implant design context, and no uncertainty quantification.
动机· 提出一种高导电纺织材料的电导率测量方法。
方法· 采用微带环形谐振器作为测试夹具，结合基于代理模型的主动学习迭代求解器提取电导率。
结果· 测得Zelt和银涂层电子纺织品的电导率分别为2.29×10^5和3.47×10^5 S/m，与制造商直流电导率接近。
验证· 通过比较测量与仿真的传输系数进行验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a conductivity measurement technique for highly conductive textile materials with improved sensitivity and ease of sample preparation.
Method· A microstrip ring resonator is used as a test fixture, and conductivity is extracted by comparing measured and simulated transmission coefficients using an active learning iterative solver based on surrogate-based optimization.
Result· Conductivities of Zelt and silver-coated e-textile were measured as 2.29×10^5 and 3.47×10^5 S/m, respectively, close to the manufacturers' DC values.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed by comparing measured transmission coefficients with simulated ones.

conductivity measurement e-textiles microstrip ring resonator surrogate-based optimization active learning
