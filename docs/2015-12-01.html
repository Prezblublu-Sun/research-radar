English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare torque-time curves of different dental implant designs in a low bone density model.
Method· Insertion and removal torque-time curves of nine implant designs were recorded using a torque analyzer in type IV artificial bone.
Result· Torque rise rates differed significantly across regions, and increasing taper angle increased torque rate.
Validation· Mechanical testing in artificial bone models.
