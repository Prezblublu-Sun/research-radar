Motivation· To identify specific gene expression patterns that can serve as reliable biomarkers for predicting successful dental implant osseointegration.
Method· A systematic review and meta-analysis following PRISMA guidelines, synthesizing studies from PubMed, Scopus, and Web of Science published between January 2020 and May 2025.
