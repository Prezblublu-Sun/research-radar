单位: Implant Research Center, School of Biomedical Engineering, Science, and Health Systems, Drexel University, 3401 Market Street, Suite 345, Philadelphia, PA, 19104, USA. sk997@drexel.edu
通讯: Sevi B. Kocagöz @ Implant Research Center, School of Biomedical Engineering, Science, and Health Systems, Drexel University, 3401 Market S
相关性: 该论文关注陶瓷头与金属头在头-柄锥部磨损腐蚀中的金属释放，未涉及股骨近端应力遮挡或微动分析。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics; it focuses on taper fretting corrosion and volumetric material loss.
动机· 量化陶瓷头与CoCr头在头-柄锥部磨损腐蚀中的体积材料损失差异。
方法· 使用精密圆度仪测量50对陶瓷头和50对CoCr头-柄锥部的体积损失。
结果· 陶瓷头每年体积损失中位数为0.0 mm³，CoCr头为0.1 mm³，陶瓷头减少一个数量级。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify the volumetric material loss at the head-stem taper junction for ceramic versus CoCr femoral heads in retrieved hip implants.
Method· Volumetric material loss was estimated using a precision roundness machine with 24 equally spaced axial traces and linear least squares interpolation on 50 matched pairs of ceramic and CoCr heads.
Result· Ceramic heads showed a median annual volumetric loss of 0.0 mm³, an order of magnitude lower than CoCr heads (0.1 mm³ per year).
Validation· Validation was performed on 100 retrieved hip prostheses from revision surgeries, with cohorts matched for implantation time, stem flexural rigidity, and lateral offset.
