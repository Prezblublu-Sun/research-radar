Enhanced Osseointegration of Hierarchical Micro/Nanotopographic Titanium Fabricated by Microarc Oxidation and Electrochemical Treatment.

Low AM Biomedical Materials pubmed Peripheral Background only in_vivo Exp. validation
Guanglong Li, Huiliang Cao, Wenjie Zhang, Xun Ding, Guangzheng Yang et al. ACS applied materials & interfaces 2016-02-17 10.1021/acsami.5b10633
单位: Department of Prosthodontics, Oral Bioengineering, and Regenerative Medicine Lab, Ninth People's Hospital Affiliated to Shanghai Jiao Tong University, School of Medicine , 639 Zhizaoju Road, Shanghai
通讯: Xinquan Jiang @ Department of Prosthodontics, Oral Bioengineering, and Regenerative Medicine Lab, Ninth People's Hospital Affiliated to [推断]
相关性: 该论文涉及钛表面改性以促进骨整合，但未涉及增材制造（LPBF）或力学测试，与研究方向关联度低。
边界: This paper focuses on surface coating via microarc oxidation and electrochemical treatment, not on LPBF additive manufacturing, porous structure design, or mechanical testing such as fatigue or stress shielding.
动机· 提高骨科植入物的骨整合速度和成功率。
方法· 通过微弧氧化和电化学还原制备具有微纳多级形貌的钛表面涂层。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To accelerate osseointegration of titanium implants through hierarchical micro/nanotopographic surface modifications.
Method· A duplex coating process combining microarc oxidation (MAO) and electrochemical reduction in alkaline solution (MAO-AK) was developed to create hierarchical micro/nanorough surfaces.
Result· The MAO-AK coating significantly enhanced canine BMSC adhesion, proliferation, osteogenic gene expression, and mineralization in vitro, as well as bone-implant contact ratio in vivo.
Validation· In vitro stem cell assays and in vivo canine femoral implantation with histological analysis confirmed improved osteogenic capacity.

单位: Earth Sciences Division, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, Berkeley, CA, USA
通讯: Soyoung Jeon @ Earth Sciences Division, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, Berkeley, CA, USA
相关性: 该论文专注于气候变化极端事件归因，与FEA代理建模和植入物设计方向无关。
边界: This paper addresses climate extreme event attribution, not surrogate modeling for expensive simulations, constraint handling, UQ for point predictions, or biomechanics design.
动机· 气候模型对极端事件有偏差，需校正归因风险比。
方法· 基于分位数重缩放模型输出，估计调整后的风险比。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Climate model biases in extreme events necessitate correction for reliable attribution statements.
Method· A quantile-based rescaling of model output is applied to estimate an adjusted risk ratio with uncertainty.
Result· The lower bound of the risk ratio is relatively insensitive to event magnitude and probability.
Validation· Demonstrated on the 2011 central US heatwave using Community Earth System Model output.
