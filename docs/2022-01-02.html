单位: Construction Division, Korea Conformity Laboratories, Construction Technology Research Centre, Seoul, Korea
通讯: Kwang‐Min Park @ Construction Division, Korea Conformity Laboratories, Construction Technology Research Centre, Seoul, Korea
相关性: 该论文提出了Kelvin晶格单元的形状控制方法，并通过有限元分析研究了相对密度对屈服强度的影响，与用户关注的LPBF多孔结构力学性能相关。
边界: No LPBF fabrication or experimental mechanical testing; only FEA simulation; no fatigue, surface roughness, or osseointegration data.

Validation· Validation was performed solely through finite element simulation without experimental fabrication or testing.
