单位: Computer Engineering Department, San Jose’ State University, San Jose, CA 95192, USA
通讯: Maria Chierichetti @ Department of Aerospace Engineering, San Jose’ State University, San Jose, CA 95192, USA
相关性: 该论文将机器学习作为有限元分析的替代模型，方法可迁移至生物力学植入物设计，但仅针对一维梁结构，缺乏约束处理、不确定性量化和生物医学验证。
边界: This paper does not address multi-objective constraints, uncertainty quantification, or implant/biomechanics applications; it only demonstrates surrogate modeling on a simple 1D beam with point predictions.
动机· 用机器学习替代有限元分析，实现实时应力预测以优化维护计划。
方法· 训练多种回归模型（决策树、神经网络）作为一维梁的有限元替代模型。
结果· 神经网络能准确估计梁的应力分布，优于决策树。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To enable real-time stress prediction for condition-based maintenance by replacing expensive finite element analysis with machine learning surrogates.
Method· Several regression models including decision trees and artificial neural networks are trained as surrogate models to directly estimate the stress distribution of a one-dimensional beam from reduced sensor data.
Result· Artificial neural networks provide the most accurate stress estimation among the tested models, demonstrating feasibility of ML-based FEA surrogates.
Validation· Model accuracy is validated against high-fidelity finite element simulations of a 1D beam under dynamic loading.
