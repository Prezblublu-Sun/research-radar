Method· An active deep learning method is proposed that combines epistemic uncertainty and sample diversity to select informative batches for labeling to estimate tree density from Sentinel-2 images.
Result· First country-scale oil palm density maps at 10m GSD are produced for Indonesia and Malaysia, achieving a mean absolute error of ±7.3 trees/ha.
Validation· Validation is performed against an independent set of labeled samples and comparison with official state-level estimates.
