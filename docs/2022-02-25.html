Heat treatment of titanium manufactured by selective laser melting: Microstructure and tensile properties

Yabao Hu, Hanning Chen, Xiaohui Jia, Xiaodan Liang, Jianbo Lei Journal of Materials Research and Technology 2022-02-25 10.1016/j.jmrt.2022.02.106
单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, Tiangong University, Tianjin 300387, China
通讯: Hanning Chen @ School of Computer Science and Technology, Tiangong University, Tianjin 300387, China  |  Jianbo Lei @ Laser Technology Institute, Tiangong University, Tianjin 300387, China
相关性: 该论文涉及SLM制造钛合金的热处理与拉伸性能，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡、疲劳或表面形貌等方向核心问题。
边界: This paper does not address porous structure design, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or surface roughness/osseeintegration relevant to AM biomedical implants.
动机· 优化SLM钛合金力学性能以满足骨植入物要求
方法· 采用SLM制备钛样品并进行550-750°C热处理
结果· 热处理使α相粗化、α′相增多，拉伸强度和延性均提高
验证· 通过拉伸测试和断口分析验证力学性能变化
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance mechanical properties of selective laser melted titanium for bone implant applications.
Method· Titanium samples were fabricated by SLM and subjected to heat treatments at 550, 650, and 750 °C for 2 hours.
Result· Increasing heat treatment temperature led to thicker α laths and denser α′ needles, improving tensile strength, elongation, and shrinkage.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated via tensile testing and fracture surface analysis.

FRESH 3D bioprinting a contractile heart tube using human stem cell-derived cardiomyocytes

Jacqueline M. Bliley, Joshua W. Tashman, Maria Stang, Brian Coffin, Daniel J. Shiwarski et al. Biofabrication 2022-02-25 10.1088/1758-5090/ac58be
单位: Department of Biomedical Engineering, Carnegie Mellon University, Pittsburgh, PA, United States of America
通讯: Adam W. Feinberg @ Department of Biomedical Engineering, Carnegie Mellon University, Pittsburgh, PA, United States of America
相关性: 该论文专注于3D生物打印心脏管模型，未涉及AI技术，与AI生物打印方向不相关。
边界: This paper does not integrate any AI/ML method into the bioprinting workflow; it is purely experimental tissue engineering without computational modeling or autonomous decision-making.
动机· 利用3D生物打印构建胚胎时期线性心脏管模型
方法· 采用FRESH 3D生物打印技术将人类干细胞来源的心肌细胞和成纤维细胞打印成管状结构
结果· 打印的心脏管在3-4天后出现同步收缩并维持长达一个月，钙成像显示各向异性传导
验证· 通过免疫荧光、钙成像和荧光珠示踪评估细胞结构和功能
English summary & terms
Motivation· To engineer a simplified human heart tube model reminiscent of embryonic development using 3D bioprinting.
Method· Freeform Reversible Embedding of Suspended Hydrogels (FRESH) bioprinting was used to fabricate tubular constructs with human stem cell-derived cardiomyocytes and cardiac fibroblasts.
Result· The bioprinted heart tubes exhibited synchronous contractions from day 3-4 post-fabrication, sustained for up to one month, with anisotropic calcium wave propagation.
Validation· Structural and functional validation via immunofluorescence, calcium imaging, and bead displacement tracking within the lumen.

A Biocompatible Ultrananocrystalline Diamond (UNCD) Coating for a New Generation of Dental Implants.

Orlando Auciello, Sandra Renou, Karam Kang, Deborah Tasat, Daniel Olmedo Nanomaterials (Basel, Switzerland) 2022-02-25 10.3390/nano12050782
单位: Department of Materials Science and Engineering and Bioengineering, Original Biomedical Implants (OBI-USA), University of Texas at Dallas, Dallas, TX 75080, USA.; Original Biomedical Implants-México (
通讯: Daniel Olmedo @ Cátedra de Anatomía Patológica, Facultad de Odontología, Universidad de Buenos Aires, Buenos Aires C1122AAH, Argentina.; [推断]
相关性: 该论文涉及生物相容性涂层，但未涉及AM制造、多孔结构或力学性能，与用户研究方向无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not involve LPBF fabrication, porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or surface roughness characterization relevant to AM biomedical materials.
动机· 评估UNCD涂层对钛微种植体生物相容性的影响。
方法· 在大鼠胫骨中植入UNCD涂层和未涂层的钛微种植体，30天后组织学分析。
结果· UNCD涂层组与对照组骨结合率无显著差异，均表现出良好生物相容性。
验证· 通过大鼠动物模型和组织形态学评估生物相容性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the biocompatibility of ultrananocrystalline diamond (UNCD) coated titanium micro-implants for dental applications.
Method· UNCD-coated and uncoated titanium micro-implants were implanted into the tibial bone marrow of Wistar rats and histologically analyzed after 30 days.
Result· Both groups showed similar bone-to-implant contact percentages, indicating that UNCD coating does not impair osseointegration.
Validation· In vivo animal model (Wistar rats) with histomorphometric analysis.
