英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate midterm outcomes and return-to-sport rates in high-level athletes with borderline hip dysplasia after primary hip arthroscopy.
Method· Retrospective cohort study with propensity-score matching to a control group of athletes with normal acetabular coverage.
Result· Athletes with borderline hip dysplasia achieved comparable patient-reported outcomes and return-to-sport rates to the control group, with no conversions to total hip arthroplasty.
Validation· Minimum 5-year clinical follow-up with validated psychometric thresholds (MCID, PASS, MOIST).
