The select of internal architecture for porous Ti alloy scaffold: A compromise between mechanical properties and permeability

High AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Read now mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Guisheng Yu, Zhibin Li, Shuangjian Li, Qiang Zhang, Youlu Hua et al. Materials & Design 2020-04-24 10.1016/j.matdes.2020.108754
单位: Institute of Advanced Wear & Corrosion Resistant and Functional Materials, Jinan University, Guangzhou 510632, PR China
通讯: Shuangjian Li @ Institute of Advanced Wear & Corrosion Resistant and Functional Materials, Jinan University, Guangzhou 510632, PR China  |  Xiaojian Wang @ Institute of Advanced Wear & Corrosion Resistant and Functional Materials, Jinan University, Guangzhou 510632, PR China
相关性: 该论文直接研究了多孔钛合金支架的内部结构对力学性能和渗透性的影响，与用户关注的AM生物医学植入物方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing, surface roughness characterization, or osseointegration evaluation.
动机· 为多孔骨科植入物设计提供指导，平衡力学性能与渗透性。
方法· 设计并SLM制造三种拓扑结构（primitive, gyroid, bcc）的Ti-6Al-4V多孔支架，进行压缩/拉伸测试和有限元模拟。
结果· Gyroid支架的压缩和拉伸强度最高，约为BCC的两倍，但渗透性仅为BCC的20%。
验证· 通过准静态力学测试和落水头法渗透实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To provide guidance for designing porous orthopedic implants by balancing mechanical properties and permeability.
Method· Three topological porous structures (primitive, gyroid, bcc) with 65% porosity were designed and fabricated via selective laser melting using Ti-6Al-4V, followed by quasi-static compression/tensile tests and finite element simulations.
Result· Gyroid scaffolds exhibited the highest compressive and tensile strengths (392.1 MPa and 321.3 MPa), nearly double those of BCC scaffolds, but their permeability was only 20% of BCC scaffolds.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated by quasi-static tests, and permeability was measured using the falling head method and compared with CFD results.

Validation· Bone regeneration and osseointegration were assessed using micro-CT, histology, and ex vivo torsional mechanical testing.
