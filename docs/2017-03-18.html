相关性: 该论文关注术前规划准确性，未涉及应力遮挡或微动等生物力学分析。
边界: No biomechanical modeling, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no FEA.
动机· 评估双平面低剂量X线片术前三维规划的准确性和可重复性。

Method· Preoperative planning was performed on 20 patients and compared with postoperative implant parameters.

Validation· Validation was performed by comparing preoperative plans with postoperative CT measurements.

单位: Tufts University School of Medicine, Boston, Massachusetts; Maine Medical Center Research Institute, Scarborough, Maine
通讯: Brian J. McGrory @ Tufts University School of Medicine, Boston, Massachusetts; Maine Joint Replacement Institute, Portland, Maine; Division
相关性: 该论文研究金属对聚乙烯全髋关节置换术中的机械辅助缝隙腐蚀，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡或微动等生物力学问题。
边界: This paper is a clinical study on corrosion prevalence, with no biomechanical modeling, no FEA, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, and no implant design analysis.
动机· 评估金属对聚乙烯全髋关节置换中机械辅助缝隙腐蚀的患病率和病因。
方法· 对1352例连续THA患者进行横断面研究，检测血清钴铬水平并分析临床影像数据。

Result· Symptomatic MACC prevalence was 3.2%, with higher risk for M/L Taper stems and implants from 2009.
Validation· Clinical follow-up and laboratory tests in a large patient cohort.
