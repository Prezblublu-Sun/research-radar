Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Skim only theoretical UQ
H. C. Lie, T. J. Sullivan, A. L. Teckentrup arXiv preprint 2017-12-15 10.1137/18M1166523
相关性: 本文为贝叶斯反问题中随机代理模型提供了理论基础，可迁移至有限元代理建模的不确定性量化。
边界: This paper is purely theoretical with no application to biomechanics, implant design, or constraints handling; it does not address expensive simulation sampling efficiency in a practical sense.
动机· 为随机代理模型在贝叶斯反问题中的使用提供稳定性保证。

英文摘要与术语

Method· Bound the Hellinger distance between exact and approximate posteriors in terms of moments of the log-likelihood difference.

Validation· Example applications in large-data misfit models and probabilistic solution of ODEs are provided.
