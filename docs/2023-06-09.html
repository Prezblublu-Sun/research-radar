Motivation· To model the uncertainty caused by finite element discretization error within a Bayesian framework, improving the reliability of simulation predictions.
Method· A Gaussian process prior is assumed over the solution space and updated using observations from a coarse finite element discretization, with a fine discretization representing the true field; two specific priors (white noise and Green's function-based) are investigated.
