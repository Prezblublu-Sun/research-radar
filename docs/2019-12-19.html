Validation· Validation included mechanical testing (microhardness, static tensile) and in vitro cytotoxicity assessment.

Motivation· To identify risk factors for early wound complications after direct anterior total hip arthroplasty with mesh tape closure.
Method· A retrospective review of 75 consecutive patients comparing wound complication rates between smokers and non-smokers.
Result· Smokers had a significantly higher wound complication rate (17.2%) compared to non-smokers (2.2%).
Validation· Clinical retrospective data analysis.
