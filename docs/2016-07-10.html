Uncertainty Quantification of Low Void Fraction Measurements Using Wire-Mesh Sensors in Horizontal Air-Water Flows

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only experimental Exp. validation UQ
Étienne Lessard, Robert C. Bowden, Sun-Kyu Yang 2016-07-10 10.1115/fedsm2016-7843
单位: Canadian Nuclear Laboratories, Chalk River, ON, Canada
通讯: Étienne Lessard @ Canadian Nuclear Laboratories, Chalk River, ON, Canada
相关性: 该论文涉及两相流测量中的不确定性量化，与FEA代理建模或植入物设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on experimental void fraction measurement uncertainty in two-phase flow, with no FEA, surrogate modeling, or biomedical implant context.
动机· 量化水平低空隙率两相流中丝网传感器测量的不确定性。
方法· 通过实验测量与物理模型对比，结合速度测量计算气体表观速度。
结果· 修正后测量与模型差异在高流量下降至6%，多数参数不确定性在20%内。
验证· 与两相流模型及流量计测量值对比验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a revised methodology and quantify uncertainty for wire-mesh sensor void fraction measurements in horizontal low void fraction flows.
Method· Experimental measurements in a horizontal flow loop over a range of superficial velocities, combined with interfacial velocity measurements to compute gas superficial velocity based on physical theory.
Result· Corrected void fraction trends showed average percent differences of 36%, 21%, and 6% for low, medium, and high gas flow rates, with most parameters agreeing within 20% uncertainty.
Validation· Validation by comparing computed gas superficial velocity with flowmeter measurements and two-phase pipe flow models.
uncertainty quantification · 不确定性量化wire-mesh sensor · 丝网传感器void fraction · 空隙率two-phase flow · 两相流superficial velocity · 表观速度
uncertainty quantification two-phase flow wire-mesh sensor void fraction

PIV Uncertainty: Computational and Experimental Evaluation of the Peak Ratio Method

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only simulation_case Exp. validation UQ
Stamatios Pothos, Aaron Boomsma, Dan Troolin, Sayantan Bhattacharya, Pavlos P. Vlachos 2016-07-10 10.1115/fedsm2016-7926
单位: TSI Inc., Shoreview, MN
通讯: Stamatios Pothos @ TSI Inc., Shoreview, MN
相关性: 该论文研究PIV的不确定性量化方法，与FEA代理模型或植入物设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on uncertainty quantification for particle image velocimetry (PIV) in fluid dynamics, not on surrogate modeling for expensive simulations, constraint handling, or biomechanical implant design.
动机· 评估PIV测量中的不确定性量化方法。
方法· 比较主峰比、互信息和图像匹配三种方法在合成湍流边界层和实验射流中的表现。
结果· 主峰比方法在实验和合成数据中表现最佳。
验证· 通过高动态范围PIV系统与LDV测量对比验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate uncertainty quantification methods for planar particle image velocimetry (PIV).
Method· Three methods (primary peak ratio, mutual information, image matching) were compared using synthetic turbulent boundary layer flow and experimental jet flow with dual-PIV systems.
Result· The primary peak ratio method outperformed the others in both synthetic and experimental cases.
Validation· Validation was performed by comparing high-resolution PIV with LDV measurements at a point.

PIV uncertainty quantification peak ratio method mutual information image matching

Bactericidal Application of Biosynthesized Silver Nanoparticles

Exclude AI for Bioprinting openalex Peripheral Ignore in_vitro Exp. validation
Hala Ezzat Abou El-Hassayeb International Journal of Current Microbiology and Applied Sciences 2016-07-10 10.20546/ijcmas.2016.507.090
单位: Department of Marine Microbiology, National Institute of Oceanography and Fisheries, Alexandria, Egypt
通讯: Hala Ezzat Abou El-Hassayeb @ Department of Marine Microbiology, National Institute of Oceanography and Fisheries, Alexandria, Egypt
相关性: 该论文涉及银纳米颗粒的生物合成及其抗菌应用，与AI生物打印方向无关。
边界: This paper does not involve AI, bioprinting, or any computational modeling; it is purely a biosynthesis and antibacterial study.
动机· 利用绿脓杆菌生物合成银纳米颗粒并评估其抗菌活性。
方法· 通过绿脓杆菌还原银离子合成纳米颗粒，并用UV-Vis、XRD、TEM表征。
结果· 合成的银纳米颗粒对多种细菌有抗菌作用，尤其对合成菌株本身效果最强。
验证· 通过UV-Vis光谱、XRD和TEM确认纳米颗粒形成，并通过抗菌实验验证活性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To biosynthesize silver nanoparticles using Pseudomonas aeruginosa and evaluate their antibacterial efficacy.
Method· Silver nanoparticles were synthesized by reducing silver ions with Pseudomonas aeruginosa culture filtrate, characterized by UV-Vis, XRD, and TEM.
Result· The biosynthesized nanoparticles exhibited antibacterial activity against Pseudomonas aeruginosa, Staphylococcus aureus, and Escherichia coli.
Validation· Nanoparticle formation was confirmed by spectroscopic and microscopic techniques, and antibacterial activity was tested via standard assays.

Aplicación y evaluación de los métodos de aprendizaje activo colaborativo en la docencia de Salud Pública en Fisioterapia

Exclude FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Ignore none
María Dueñas, Alejandro Salazar, Begoña Siles Ojeda, Helena De Sola, Inmaculada Failde Educación Médica 2016-07-10 10.1016/j.edumed.2016.05.001
单位: Centro Universitario de Enfermería Salus Infirmorum, Universidad de Cádiz, Cádiz, España
通讯: María Dueñas @ Centro Universitario de Enfermería Salus Infirmorum, Universidad de Cádiz, Cádiz, España
相关性: 该论文是关于物理治疗公共卫生课程中协作式主动学习教学法的教育研究，与有限元代理建模或生物力学设计完全无关。
边界: This paper is an educational study on collaborative active learning in physiotherapy public health teaching, with no mention of surrogate modeling, finite element analysis, optimization, uncertainty quantification, or any computational mechanics method.
动机· 评估协作式主动学习在物理治疗公共卫生教学中的效果
方法· 对50名物理治疗学生实施协作式主动学习，通过活动完成率和满意度调查评估
结果· 100%完成活动，68%通过课程，学生对自主准备内容评价高但对同伴讲解评价低
验证· 描述性横断面研究，使用问卷调查和活动完成数据
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the effectiveness and student satisfaction of collaborative active learning in a Public Health course for Physiotherapy students.
Method· A cross-sectional study applying collaborative active learning to 50 third-year Physiotherapy students, with data collected on activity submission and a satisfaction survey.
Result· All students submitted all activities, 68% passed the course, and students rated self-prepared content highly but found peer explanations insufficient.
Validation· Descriptive analysis of survey responses and activity completion rates without statistical modeling or comparison groups.

Day of Surgery and Surgical Start Time Affect Hospital Length of Stay After Total Hip Arthroplasty

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore none
Aakash Keswani, Christina M. Beck, Kristen Meier, Adam C. Fields, Michael J. Bronson et al. The Journal of Arthroplasty 2016-07-10 10.1016/j.arth.2016.04.013
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Mount Sinai Hospital, New York, New York
通讯: Aakash Keswani @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Mount Sinai Hospital, New York, New York
相关性: 该论文研究手术时间对住院时长的影响，与髋关节植入物生物力学无关。
边界: This paper does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or any biomechanical analysis of hip implants.
动机· 探讨手术日与手术开始时间对全髋关节置换术后住院时长的影响。
方法· 回顾性分析患者数据，统计不同手术时间组的住院天数差异。
结果· 下午手术和周五手术的患者住院时间更长。
验证· 基于临床数据的统计分析。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate whether day of surgery and surgical start time influence hospital length of stay after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Retrospective analysis of patient records comparing length of stay across different surgery days and start times.
Result· Afternoon surgeries and surgeries on Fridays were associated with longer hospital stays.
Validation· Statistical analysis of clinical data.

total hip arthroplasty length of stay surgical timing
