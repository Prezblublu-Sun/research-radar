Topology Optimisation for Compliant Hip Implant Design and Reduced Strain Shielding.

High Hip Implant & Femoral Stem pubmed Direct Read now mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Nathanael Tan, Richard J van Arkel Materials (Basel, Switzerland) 2021-11-25 10.3390/ma14237184
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Imperial College London, London SW7 2AZ, UK.
通讯: Richard J van Arkel @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Imperial College London, London SW7 2AZ, UK. [推断]
相关性: 该论文通过拓扑优化设计顺应性髋关节植入物，以减少应力遮挡，直接相关于用户的研究方向。
边界: This paper does not report micromotion values, contact/friction modeling, or patient-specific femur geometry; it neither provides strain distribution in Gruen zones nor compares intact vs implanted femur.
动机· 减少髋关节置换术后由植入物刚度引起的应力遮挡和骨丢失。
方法· 采用SIMP方法进行拓扑优化，设计多孔和选择性空心两种股骨柄，并进行有限元分析和实验测试。
结果· 选择性空心植入物使股骨表面应变比实心植入物增加25个百分点，刚度降低约40%。
验证· 通过实验测量植入物刚度并与有限元预测对比验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Stiff total hip arthroplasty implants cause strain shielding and bone loss, motivating the development of compliant implant designs via topology optimisation.
Method· The Solid Isotropic Material with Penalisation (SIMP) method was adapted to design two hip stems (porous and selectively hollowed) for additive manufacturing, followed by finite element analysis and experimental stiffness measurement.
Result· The selectively hollowed implant increased peri-implanted femur surface strains by up to 25 percentage points relative to a solid implant, while both optimised stems achieved ~40% reduction in equivalent stiffness.
Validation· Stiffness results from three-point bending experiments were compared with finite element predictions, showing good agreement across solid and optimised designs.
