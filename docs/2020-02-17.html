边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain, micromotion, or contact mechanics; it focuses on leg length measurement accuracy without any implant biomechanics or FEA.

方法· 改进光学测量系统并在模型和尸体上测试
结果· 改进后精度显著提高，但临床适用仍需进一步优化

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To improve the accuracy of leg length measurement after total hip arthroplasty to reduce patient dissatisfaction and injury.
Method· An improved optic measurement system was developed and tested on joint models and cadaveric specimens.
Result· The improved system showed significantly higher accuracy than the previous version, but further refinement is needed for clinical application.
Validation· Validation was performed using model setups and cadaveric tissues with inter-observer reliability assessment.
