Anchoring and resulting primary stability of a kinked compared to a straight uncemented femoral stem

High Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Direct Read now mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Markus Heinecke, Fabian Rathje, Frank Layher, Georg Matziolis Archives of Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery 2017-11-07 10.1007/s00402-017-2833-5
单位: Orthopaedic Department, University Hospital Jena, Campus Eisenberg, Klosterlausnitzer Street 81, 07607, Eisenberg, Germany. markus_heinecke@web.de
通讯: Markus Heinecke @ Orthopaedic Department, University Hospital Jena, Campus Eisenberg, Klosterlausnitzer Street 81, 07607, Eisenberg, Germa
相关性: 该论文直接评估了非骨水泥股骨柄的锚定和初始稳定性，与用户研究方向高度相关。
边界: Does not quantify stress shielding or bone remodeling; focuses only on primary stability and micromotion.
动机· 比较弯曲与直型非骨水泥股骨柄的锚定和初始稳定性。
方法· 在尸体股骨上植入两种柄，测量微动和拔出强度。
结果· 弯曲柄在特定区域微动更小，初始稳定性更好。
验证· 通过尸体股骨力学测试验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the anchoring and primary stability of a kinked versus a straight uncemented femoral stem.
Method· Cadaveric femurs were implanted with both stem designs, and micromotion and pull-out strength were measured.
Result· The kinked stem exhibited lower micromotion in certain regions, indicating better primary stability.
Validation· Mechanical testing on cadaveric femurs.

单位: Department of Industrial Engineering and Systems Management, Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology, New Borg Elarab, Egypt;
通讯: Ibrahim Eldesouky @ Department of Industrial Engineering and Systems Management, Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology, New Borg
相关性: 该论文直接评估了低刚度髋关节假体柄的力学性能，包括应力/应变分布，与用户研究方向高度相关。
边界: The paper does not quantify micromotion or contact mechanics at the bone-implant interface, nor does it compare intact vs implanted femur in a patient-specific manner.
动机· 开发低刚度髋关节假体柄以减少应力遮挡并提高初始稳定性。
方法· 设计多孔低刚度柄，通过电子束熔融制造，并进行压缩/弯曲测试和有限元分析。
结果· 多孔柄相比实心柄增加了骨内von Mises应力和最大主应变。
验证· 通过力学测试和有限元模拟验证假体柄的机械性能。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a low-stiffness hip stem that reduces stress shielding and enhances initial stability in cementless THA.
Method· A porous low-stiffness stem was designed, fabricated via electron beam melting, and evaluated through compression/bending tests and finite element analysis under stair descending loads.
Result· The porous stem generated higher von Mises stresses and maximum principal strains in the bone compared to a solid stem.
Validation· Mechanical testing and finite element simulation were used to validate the stem's mechanical performance.
