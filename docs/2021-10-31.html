On the importance of self-consistency in recurrent neural network models representing elasto-plastic solids

High FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project simulation_case
Colin Bonatti, Dirk Mohr Journal of the Mechanics and Physics of Solids 2021-10-31 10.1016/j.jmps.2021.104697
单位: Chair of Computational Modeling of Materials in Manufacturing, Department of Mechanical and Process Engineering, Swiss Federal Institute of Technology (ETH), Zurich, Switzerland
通讯: Dirk Mohr @ Chair of Computational Modeling of Materials in Manufacturing, Department of Mechanical and Process Engineering, Swiss F
相关性: 该论文提出了一种自洽的循环神经网络架构作为弹塑性材料的代理模型，可集成到有限元框架中，对解决生物力学植入物设计中的高成本仿真问题具有迁移价值。
边界: This paper focuses on elasto-plastic solids without biomedical context, implant mechanics, or uncertainty quantification.
动机· 现有循环神经网络作为材料代理模型缺乏自洽性，响应依赖增量步长。
方法· 提出线性化最小状态单元（LMSC）架构，在定义中集成自洽性。
结果· 小模型（<5000参数）精确复制偏量弹塑性行为，并集成到显式有限元中。
验证· 在弹塑性示例和含数万单元、数百万增量步的部件级仿真中验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Existing recurrent neural network surrogate material models lack self-consistency, making their response dependent on increment size.
Method· A Linearized Minimal State Cell (LMSC) architecture is proposed that integrates self-consistency into its definition.
Result· Small models with fewer than 5000 parameters precisely replicate deviatoric elasto-plastic behavior and are integrated into explicit finite element simulations.
Validation· Validation is performed on an elastoplastic example and component-level simulations with tens of thousands of elements and millions of increments.

边界: This paper focuses on HPDC manufacturing process, not on biomedical implant design, FEA surrogate for biomechanics, or uncertainty quantification.
动机· 解决高压压铸仿真计算耗时问题，需要快速替代模型。

Result· The metamodels achieved high accuracy for secondary dendrite arm spacing prediction and good accuracy for misrun risk and shrinkage level, while predictions for porosity and grain density were less accurate.
