单位: Structural Integrity, Faculty of Aerospace Engineering, Delft University of Technology, Delft, the Netherlands
通讯: Laura Angélica Ardila Rodríguez @ Structural Integrity, Faculty of Aerospace Engineering, Delft University of Technology, Delft, the Netherlands
相关性: 该论文研究了选择性激光熔化Ti6Al4V表面的粘接性能，未涉及多孔结构或植入物力学特性。

结果· 45°打印方向表面粗糙度最大，粘接性能最佳

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of surface morphology of SLM Ti6Al4V adherends on adhesive bond performance.
Method· Adherends were fabricated at 0°, 45°, and 90° build orientations to create different surface roughnesses, then UV/Ozone treated and tested with tensile tests after primer application.
Result· The 45° build orientation exhibited the highest surface roughness and best adhesive strength, with improved aging resistance after salt spray exposure.
Validation· Surface morphology was characterized by SEM and laser confocal microscopy; adhesive strength was validated via tensile testing and salt spray aging.

Validation· Validated against density functional theory (DFT) calculations, demonstrating energy errors on the order of tens of meV/atom.
