Influences of Horizontal and Vertical Build Orientations and Post-Fabrication Processes on the Fatigue Behavior of Stainless Steel 316L Produced by Selective Laser Melting

Paul Wood, Tomasz Libura, Zbigniew L. Kowalewski, Gavin Williams, Ahmad Serjouei Materials 2019-12-14 10.3390/ma12244203
单位: Institute for Innovation in Sustainable Engineering, College of Engineering and Technology, University of Derby, DE1 3HD, UK
通讯: Paul Wood @ Institute for Innovation in Sustainable Engineering, College of Engineering and Technology, University of Derby, DE1 3HD  |  Ahmad Serjouei @ Department of Engineering, School of Science and Technology, Nottingham Trent University, Nottingham, NG11 8NS, UK
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM工艺参数和后处理对316L不锈钢疲劳行为的影响，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或生物医学植入物应用。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures, stress shielding, stiffness matching, or biomedical implant context; it focuses on build orientation and post-processing effects on fatigue of solid SS316L.
动机· 研究SLM制造不锈钢316L的疲劳行为受构建方向和后处理的影响。
方法· 通过SLM制备不同构建方向和后处理（去应力、机加工、喷丸）的试样，进行疲劳测试。
结果· 水平构建且机加工的试样疲劳性能最优，去应力处理影响不大；喷丸改善垂直构建试样的疲劳强度。
验证· 通过疲劳测试和断口分析验证工艺参数与力学性能的关系。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effects of build orientation and post-fabrication processes on the fatigue behavior of SLM-produced stainless steel 316L.
Method· Specimens were fabricated with horizontal and vertical build orientations and subjected to stress-relief, machining, and shot-peening, followed by fatigue testing.
Result· Horizontally built and machined specimens exhibited superior fatigue performance, while stress relief had negligible effect; shot-peening improved fatigue strength of vertical specimens.
Validation· Fatigue tests and fractography were used to correlate process parameters with mechanical properties.

Validation· Clinical data analysis with multivariate logistic regression.
