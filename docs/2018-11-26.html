Calibration and Uncertainty Quantification of VISTA Ablator Material Database Using Bayesian Inference

Przemyslaw Rostkowski, Simone Venturi, Marco Panesi, Ali D. Omidy, Haoyue Weng et al. Journal of Thermophysics and Heat Transfer 2018-11-26 10.2514/1.t5396
单位: University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign, Urbana, Illinois 61801
通讯: Przemyslaw Rostkowski @ University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign, Urbana, Illinois 61801
相关性: 该论文展示了贝叶斯推断在材料模型校准与不确定性量化中的应用，其方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理模型的不确定性量化。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling for implant or biomechanics design; it focuses on thermal protection material calibration with no biomedical context.
动机· 校准烧蚀材料模型并量化不确定性，以改进热防护系统设计。
方法· 采用贝叶斯推断结合敏感性分析，校准VISTA材料模型参数并量化多源不确定性。
结果· 不确定性前向传播显示模型输出总不确定性大幅降低。
验证· 使用阿波罗4号飞行数据及合成数据验证校准方法。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To calibrate an ablative material model and quantify uncertainties from parametric, modeling, and data sources for improved thermal protection system design.
Method· Bayesian inference with sensitivity analysis (Pearson and Sobol) is used to calibrate the VISTA material model parameters and quantify multiple uncertainty sources.
Result· Forward propagation of quantified uncertainties shows a significant reduction in total output uncertainty.
Validation· The calibration methodology is validated using synthetic data and Apollo 4 flight temperature data.
