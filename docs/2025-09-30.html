Motivation· To examine changes in physical function and locomotive syndrome status at different postoperative stages after total hip and knee arthroplasty.
Method· A cross-sectional study categorized 118 THA/TKA patients into four postoperative duration groups and assessed physical function and LS status using standardized tests and questionnaires.
Result· Muscle power-dependent functions and LS severity showed time-dependent deterioration beyond 10 years post-surgery, while basic mobility remained stable.
Validation· Clinical functional tests and GLFS-25 questionnaire with subgroup analysis.
