Motivation· To overcome unbounded dead-reckoning drift in GNSS-denied underwater environments under severe acoustic communication delays.
Method· An asynchronous two-speed Kalman filter is proposed, decoupling estimation into a fast GP-compensated dead-reckoning thread and a slow thread for delayed cooperative updates, with a Variational History Distillation projection.
