Min-Ho Hong Journal of Korean Acedemy of Dental Technology 2015-03-30 10.14347/kadt.2015.37.1.23
相关性: 该论文仅涉及牙科用Ni-Cr金属粉末的制备与表征，未涉及LPBF打印、多孔结构、力学测试或生物力学评估，与用户研究方向无关。
边界: This paper focuses on powder production and characterization, lacking LPBF fabrication, mechanical testing, stress shielding analysis, and any biomedical implant context required for am_biomedical direction.
动机· 制备用于牙科选择性激光熔融的Ni-Cr-Mo金属粉末。

Result· Mean particle sizes ranged from 37.65 to 66.93 μm depending on process, with distinct powder morphologies.
Validation· Only powder characteristics were measured; no further validation of LPBF fabrication or mechanical properties.

Diagnóstico de infección periprotésica utilizando proteína C - reactiva sinovial

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore clinical Exp. validation Patient-specific
Gabriel Martorell, Martín Buttaro, Mauricio Quinteros, Fernando Comba, Gerardo Zanotti et al. Revista del Hospital Italiano de Buenos Aires 2015-03-30 10.51987/rev.hosp.ital.b.aires.v35i1.737
单位: Centro de Cadera, Instituto de Ortopedia y Traumatología Carlos E. Ottolenghi, Hospital Italiano de Buenos Aires
通讯: Gabriel Martorell @ Centro de Cadera, Instituto de Ortopedia y Traumatología Carlos E. Ottolenghi, Hospital Italiano de Buenos Aires
相关性: 该论文仅涉及临床诊断，完全没有涉及生物力学、应力遮挡或有限元分析等与研究方向相关的内容。
边界: This paper is a clinical diagnostic study on periprosthetic infection, with no biomechanical modeling, no FEA, no implant design evaluation, and no contact mechanics or stress analysis.
动机· 评估滑液C反应蛋白在髋关节翻修术中诊断假体周围感染的价值
方法· 前瞻性比较研究，对76例髋关节翻修患者同时进行滑液CRP检测和术中冰冻切片分析
结果· 滑液CRP阈值9.5 mg/L时，敏感性和特异性分别达90%和94%，与冰冻切片结果一致
验证· 以肌肉骨骼感染学会标准为金标准，与冰冻切片结果进行对比验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the diagnostic value of synovial C-reactive protein (SCRP) for periprosthetic joint infection in revision total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A prospective comparative study with 76 patients undergoing hip revision, measuring SCRP and analyzing frozen sections intraoperatively.
Result· At a threshold of 9.5 mg/L, SCRP showed 90% sensitivity and 94% specificity, matching the performance of frozen section analysis.
Validation· Validation against the Musculoskeletal Infection Society criteria, with comparison to intraoperative frozen section as reference.
