单位: School of Materials Science and Engineering, Tianjin University, Tianjin, 300350, China
通讯: Lianyong Xu @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, Tianjin University, Tianjin, 300350, China
相关性: 该论文研究了低含量石墨烯纳米片增强316L不锈钢的SLM工艺，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或生物医学植入物应用。
边界: This paper does not address porous structure design, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or osseointegration, and lacks biomedical context.

Validation· Mechanical properties were validated through tensile testing, microhardness, EBSD, and TEM analysis.
