边界: The paper focuses on robot kinematic calibration, not on FEA surrogate modeling, implant biomechanics, or design optimization under constraints.

Validation· Validation is performed via simulation on three robot arm models, including a candidate for a future NASA mission and a Barrett WAM arm.

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Background only none UQ
Qingyi Wang, Shenhao Wang, Dingyi Zhuang, Haris Koutsopoulos, Jinhua Zhao arXiv preprint 2023-03-07 link
相关性: 该论文处理时空不确定性量化，但应用于交通领域，与FEA替代模型和植入物设计无关。
边界: Does not address FEA surrogate modeling, cost-effective simulation sampling, or biomechanical constraints; its domain is transportation demand prediction.
动机· 现有出行需求预测忽略不确定性，提出概率图神经网络量化时变数据不确定性。
方法· 构建概率图神经网络框架，结合确定性与概率假设，预测芝加哥公交与共享出行需求。
结果· 概率假设对不确定性预测影响更大，截断高斯与拉普拉斯分布表现最优，且对领域偏移稳健。
验证· 基于芝加哥真实出行数据及疫情前后时段测试。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Motivated by the lack of uncertainty quantification in travel demand prediction, this work proposes probabilistic graph neural networks (Prob-GNN) to model spatiotemporal uncertainty.
Method· The method integrates deterministic and probabilistic assumptions and is applied to transit and ridesharing demand forecasting in Chicago.
Result· It finds that probabilistic assumptions dominate uncertainty prediction, with truncated Gaussian and Laplace distributions yielding best performance, and that the model remains stable under domain shifts.
Validation· The approach is validated on real-world Chicago transit data across pre- and post-COVID periods.
