Causes and predictors of failed same-day home discharge following primary hip and knee total joint arthroplasty: a Canadian perspective

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore none
Aaron Gazendam, Daniel Tushinski, Mansi Patel, Kamal Bali, Danielle Petruccelli et al. Hip International 2022-07-17 10.1177/11207000221111101
单位: Department of Surgery, McMaster University, Hamilton, ON, Canada
通讯: Aaron Gazendam @ Department of Surgery, McMaster University, Hamilton, ON, Canada
相关性: 该论文关注全髋关节置换术后当日出院失败的原因，不涉及任何生物力学分析或植入物设计。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, no implant design, no FEA, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification.
动机· 评估全关节置换术后当日出院失败的原因和预测因素。
方法· 回顾性分析527例计划当日出院患者的临床数据，采用多变量回归分析。
结果· 术后低血压和患者选择不当是主要失败原因，THA、ASA IV级和手术开始时间晚是独立预测因素。
验证· 基于临床队列的回顾性统计分析。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To identify causes and predictors of failed same-day home discharge after total joint arthroplasty.
Method· Retrospective review of 527 consecutive patients scheduled for same-day discharge, with multivariate regression analysis.
Result· Postoperative hypotension and inappropriate patient selection were leading causes; THA, ASA class IV, and later operative start time were independent predictors.
Validation· Clinical cohort retrospective analysis.
