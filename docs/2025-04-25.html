Biofabrication of nano-silver composites from Indian catmint-Anisomeles ovata flower buds extract and evaluation of their potential in-vitro biological applications

Shashiraj Kariyellappa Nagaraja, Bidhayak Chakraborty, Hakan Anıl, Meghashyama Prabhakara Bhat, Sreenivasa Nayaka Pharmacological Research - Natural Products 2025-04-25 10.1016/j.prenap.2025.100246
单位: Department of Studies in Botany, Karnatak University, Dharwad, Karnataka 580003, India
通讯: Sreenivasa Nayaka @ Department of Studies in Botany, Karnatak University, Dharwad, Karnataka 580003, India
相关性: 该论文涉及纳米银复合材料的绿色合成和生物应用，与AI辅助生物打印方向无关。
边界: No AI, no bioprinting, no computational modeling; purely biological synthesis and in-vitro testing.
动机· 利用植物提取物绿色合成纳米银复合材料并评估生物活性。
方法· 以印度猫薄荷花蕾提取物为还原剂，生物合成纳米银复合材料。
结果· 合成的纳米银复合材料表现出良好的抗菌和抗氧化活性。
验证· 通过体外抗菌、抗氧化和细胞毒性实验验证性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a green synthesis method for nano-silver composites using plant extracts and evaluate their biological potential.
Method· Silver nanoparticles were biosynthesized using Anisomeles ovata flower bud extract as a reducing agent.
Result· The biosynthesized nano-silver composites exhibited significant antibacterial and antioxidant activities.
Validation· Validation was performed through in vitro antibacterial, antioxidant, and cytotoxicity assays.

Biofabrication of Spatially Organized Temporo‐mandibular Fibrocartilage Assembloids

Alexandre Dufour, Lucie Essayan, Beomjoon Kim, Vincent Salles, Christophe A. Marquette Advanced Healthcare Materials 2025-04-25 10.1002/adhm.202405000
单位: 3d.FAB Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1 CNRS INSA CPE‐Lyon Villeurbanne 69622 France
通讯: Alexandre Dufour @ 3d.FAB Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1 CNRS INSA CPE‐Lyon Villeurbanne 69622 France  |  Vincent Salles @ LIMMS, CNRS‐IIS IRL 2820 The University of Tokyo Tokyo 153‐8505 Japan  |  Christophe A. Marquette @ 3d.FAB Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1 CNRS INSA CPE‐Lyon Villeurbanne 69622 France
相关性: 该论文完全不涉及AI或机器学习在生物打印中的应用。
边界: This paper describes a tissue engineering approach with no AI/ML integration, no bioprinting, and no autonomous decision-making.
动机· 构建模拟天然颞下颌关节纤维软骨的组织工程方法。
方法· 将MSC球体接种到具有微米纤维双结构支架中培养时控组装体。
结果· 工程化组织在细胞分布、基质成分和力学性能上部分模拟了天然组织。
验证· 通过组织学、免疫染色和纳米压痕验证组织结构和力学性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a tissue engineering strategy for recapitulating the stratified structure of temporomandibular joint fibrocartilage.
Method· Assembloids were formed by seeding human adipose-derived MSC spheroids into scaffolds with dual micron-scale fiber architecture.
Result· The engineered tissue exhibited a native-like fibrous zone, collagen organization, and zonal micromechanical properties, though with lower modulus in the fibrous zone.
Validation· Histological staining, immunohistochemistry, and nanoindentation were used to compare engineered and native tissues.

Preoperative frailty in patients undergoing total knee or hip arthroplasty affects FIM scores at one week postoperatively

Ikki Yoshida, Yohei Sawaya, Yukinobu Hiiragi, Shunsuke Kikuchi, Tomohiko Urano Nippon Ronen Igakkai Zasshi Japanese Journal of Geriatrics 2025-04-25 10.3143/geriatrics.62.178
单位: Department of Rehabilitation, International University of Health and Welfare SHIOYA Hospital
通讯: Ikki Yoshida @ Department of Rehabilitation, International University of Health and Welfare SHIOYA Hospital
相关性: 与用户方向无关，不涉及髋关节植入物的生物力学分析。
边界: No biomechanical analysis, no implant or bone stress/strain, no FE modeling, no contact mechanics.
动机· 评估术前虚弱对TKA/THA术后短期功能恢复的影响。
方法· 前瞻性队列研究，使用虚弱问卷和FIM评分评估。
结果· 虚弱患者术后FIM下降更显著。
验证· 统计分析比较虚弱与非虚弱组FIM评分变化。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the impact of preoperative frailty on short-term functional outcomes after total knee or hip arthroplasty.
Method· Prospective cohort study assessing frailty via QMCOO and J-CHS criteria and measuring Functional Independence Measure (FIM) pre- and postoperatively.
Result· Frail patients exhibited significantly greater decline in FIM scores at one week postoperatively compared to non-frail patients.
Validation· Comparison of FIM score changes between frailty groups using statistical tests.
