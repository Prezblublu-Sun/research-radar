Motivation· To assess the quality of welded joints in SLM-fabricated stainless steel using non-destructive testing methods.
Method· X-ray computed tomography, digital radiography, and ultrasonic testing were employed to detect and characterize defects in welded plates and pipes.

Validation· Experimental validation was performed on SLM-manufactured 12Kh18N10T steel samples with known defects.
