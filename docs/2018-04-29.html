Trong Nghia Hoang, Quang Minh Hoang, Ruofei Ouyang, Kian Hsiang Low Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence 2018-04-29 10.1609/aaai.v32i1.11788
单位: Massachusetts Institute of Technology
通讯: Trong Nghia Hoang @ Massachusetts Institute of Technology
相关性: 该方法通过因子图分解高维贝叶斯优化，可迁移至生物力学设计中的多约束优化问题。
边界: No FEA validation, no biomedical context, no implant mechanics, and no uncertainty quantification beyond standard BO.
动机· 高维贝叶斯优化面临维度灾难和变量依赖问题。
方法· 提出基于因子图分解的稀疏表示与分布式消息传递获取函数优化。
结果· 在合成和真实实验（如1811超参数稀疏GP）中优于现有方法。
验证· 通过数值基准和真实超参数优化实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· High-dimensional Bayesian optimization suffers from the curse of dimensionality and inability to exploit variable dependencies.
Method· A sparse factor graph representation of the objective is used to design an acquisition function optimized via distributed message passing.
Result· The method outperforms state-of-the-art HBO algorithms on synthetic and real-world tasks, including a sparse GP with 1811 hyperparameters.
Validation· Validation is performed on numerical benchmarks and a real-world hyperparameter tuning problem.
