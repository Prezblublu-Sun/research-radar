Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv UQ Patient-specific
Caleb C. Berggren, David Jiang, Y. F. Jack Wang, Jake A. Bergquist, Lindsay C. Rupp et al. arXiv preprint 2024-01-26 link
相关性: 展示了将不确定性量化和代理模型应用于生物力学模拟的完整流程，可启示髋关节FEA及替代建模中的不确定性处理方法。
动机· 需要量化材料参数不确定性对冠状动脉生物力学模拟结果的影响，以提高临床可靠性。

通讯: Yun Jung Yang @ Department of Biological Sciences and Bioengineering, Inha University, Incheon, 22212, Republic of Korea

动机· 开发兼具可打印性和生物功能性的新型生物墨水。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a bioink that simultaneously meets the opposing requirements of printability and biochemical functionality for extrusion-based bioprinting.
Method· A blend of silk fibroin and iota-carrageenan was used, with photo-crosslinking of silk fibroin to rapidly improve printability and structural integrity.
Result· The printed constructs achieved a Young's modulus of approximately 250 kPa and supported high cell viability (>98%) for keratinocyte and myoblast culture.
Validation· Rheological characterization, printability assessment, and in vitro cell culture experiments were performed.

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Libo Zhou, Zeai Peng, Zhiming Hu, Jiaqing Liu, Jian Chen et al. Advanced Engineering Materials 2024-01-26 10.1002/adem.202301785
单位: Institute of Energy and Power Engineering Changsha University of Science and Technology Changsha 410114 P. R. China
通讯: Libo Zhou @ Institute of Energy and Power Engineering Changsha University of Science and Technology Changsha 410114 P. R. China
相关性: 该论文研究Inconel 625合金的LPBF工艺，但未涉及生物医学植入物、多孔结构或应力遮挡。
边界: No porous structure, no stress shielding analysis, no biomedical context, no fatigue testing, no osseointegration or surface morphology characterization.
动机· 通过扫描策略调控Inconel 625合金的晶粒形态与力学性能。
方法· 改变激光粉末床熔融的扫描旋转角度，制备不同晶粒形态的样品。
结果· 增大扫描旋转角促进等轴晶，提高抗拉强度与屈服强度。
验证· 拉伸测试验证力学性能，EBSD表征晶粒取向。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To tailor grain morphology and mechanical properties of Inconel 625 alloy by varying scanning strategies in laser powder bed fusion.
Method· Samples were fabricated with different scanning rotation angles (0°, 45°, 67°, 90°) to control heat flow direction and grain structure.
Result· Increasing rotation angle promoted equiaxed grains and enhanced ultimate tensile strength from 1267 to 1304 MPa and yield strength from 760 to 801 MPa.
Validation· Mechanical testing (tensile) and EBSD analysis were used to correlate grain morphology with mechanical properties.
