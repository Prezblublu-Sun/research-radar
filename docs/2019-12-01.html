English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate mid- to long-term clinical and radiological outcomes of a cementless rectangular tapered stem in elderly Asian patients classified by Dorr femoral geometry.
Method· A retrospective review of 107 patients (112 hips) who underwent total hip arthroplasty with the C2 stem, assessing implant fixation, radiolucent lines, stress shielding, and thigh pain.
Result· All stems achieved stable fixation with a 97.6% survival rate; Dorr type A femurs showed significantly higher incidences of stress shielding, radiolucent lines, and thigh pain.
Validation· Clinical and radiological follow-up with Kaplan-Meier survival analysis.
