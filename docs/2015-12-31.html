通讯: Jiří Šafka @ Technical University of Liberec
相关性: 该论文研究AlSi12合金SLM工艺的尺寸精度，未涉及生物医学植入物、多孔结构或力学性能。
边界: No porous structure, no stress shielding or stiffness matching, no LPBF fabrication of biomedical implants, no mechanical testing (fatigue or static), no surface roughness or osseointegration.
