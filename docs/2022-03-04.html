通讯: Jonathan R.T. Jeffers @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Imperial College London, SW7 2AZ London, United Kingdom
相关性: 该论文直接研究了随机点阵结构的力学性能调控，包括刚度匹配和疲劳性能，与用户关注的AM生物医学植入物方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not explicitly link porous structure to stress shielding or implant-level biomechanics, nor does it report surface roughness or osseointegration.
动机· 探索随机点阵结构设计参数与力学性能的统一关系，以替代多种周期点阵的单独表征。
方法· 制造不同连接度、支柱密度和厚度的纯钛随机点阵，进行准静态和疲劳压缩测试。
结果· 连接度从4增至14使疲劳强度提高60%，刚度范围0.1-8 GPa，建立了多变量线性回归模型预测相对密度。
验证· 通过实验压缩测试验证力学性能预测模型。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To establish a unified relationship between stochastic lattice design parameters and mechanical properties, avoiding separate characterization of multiple periodic lattices for orthopedic implants.
Method· Stochastic titanium lattices with varying connectivity (4-14), strut density (3-7 struts/mm³), and strut thickness (230-300 µm) were fabricated via LPBF and tested under quasi-static and fatigue compression.
Result· Increasing connectivity from 4 to 14 improved fatigue strength by 60% at fixed relative density, with stiffness ranging 0.1-8 GPa, and a multivariable linear regression model predicted relative density from design parameters.
Validation· Experimental compression tests validated the mechanical property predictions and the unified regression model.
