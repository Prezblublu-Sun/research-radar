Motivation· Reconstructing the shape of continuum manipulators from sparse, noisy sensor data remains challenging due to their infinite-dimensional nature.
Method· We combine a low-dimensional Geometric Variable Strain parameterization with factor graph inference, deriving a novel kinematic factor via Magnus expansion to encode rod geometry.
Result· The proposed method achieves mean position errors below 2 mm in simulation and a sixfold reduction in orientation error compared to a Gaussian process regression baseline.
Validation· Validation is performed through simulation experiments on a 0.4 m long tendon-driven continuum robot under three different measurement configurations.
