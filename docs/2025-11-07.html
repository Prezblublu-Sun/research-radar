Method· Retrospective analysis of prospectively collected clinical data from 79 patients at a tertiary center.
Result· One-year mortality was 16.4%, with significant declines in mobility and increases in frailty.
Validation· Statistical analysis of clinical outcomes without biomechanical or FE validation.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify mortality and complication rates after hip/knee arthroplasty in solid organ transplant patients compared to matched controls.
Method· A cohort study using the Turkish Ministry of Health database with propensity score matching to create comparable groups.
Result· Transplant patients had higher medical complications and prosthetic joint infection rates, with fracture history being an independent predictor of 90-day mortality.
Validation· Propensity score matching was used to balance covariates between transplant and control groups.
