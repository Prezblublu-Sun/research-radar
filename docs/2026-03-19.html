Tunable rod-shaped microgels with surface grooves for hierarchical anisotropy in 3D bioprinted tissues

Esfandyar Askari, Yasaman Maaref, Mahdieh Shokrollahi Barough, Neda Mohaghegh, Alireza Hassani Najafabadi et al. Biomaterials 2026-03-19 10.1016/j.biomaterials.2026.124133
单位: Laboratory for Innovations in Microengineering (LiME), Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Victoria, Victoria, BC, V8P 5C2, Canada
通讯: Esfandyar Askari @ Laboratory for Innovations in Microengineering (LiME), Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Victoria, Vic
相关性: 该论文研究各向异性微凝胶用于生物打印，但完全不涉及AI/ML技术，与AI生物打印方向无关。
边界: This paper does not integrate AI/ML into any stage of the bioprinting workflow, lacks experimental validation of an AI model, and does not advance towards autonomous decision-making.
动机· 构建可打印的各向异性微凝胶以模拟组织结构。
方法· 微流控制备表面沟槽棒状微凝胶，压缩成生物墨水进行挤出打印。
结果· 多尺度排列增强细胞分化和成熟表型。
验证· 通过hiPSC-CM和C2C12细胞培养验证排列与分化效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To create anisotropic building blocks for 3D bioprinting that enable hierarchical alignment recapitulating native tissue architecture.
Method· Flow-focusing microfluidics fabricates rod-shaped microgels with tunable surface grooves, which are compacted into a shear-thinning bioink for extrusion bioprinting.
Result· The printed constructs exhibit multi-scale alignment from subcellular to construct level, enhancing myogenic differentiation markers in C2C12 cells.
Validation· In vitro culture of hiPSC-CMs and C2C12 myoblasts confirms enhanced alignment and maturation compared to isotropic controls.

Model Reference Adaptive Control For Gust Load Allevation of Nonlinear Aeroelastic

Nikolaos D. Tantaroudas, Guanqun Gai, Ilias Karachalios arXiv preprint 2026-03-19 link
相关性: 该论文是关于非线性气动弹性系统的阵风载荷缓解控制，与有限元代理建模或生物力学植入物设计无关。
边界: This paper focuses on adaptive control for aeroelastic gust alleviation and does not address FEA surrogate modeling, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification, or biomedical implant design.
动机· 开发适用于非线性气动弹性系统的阵风载荷缓解控制方法。
方法· 基于Lyapunov稳定性理论设计模型参考自适应控制器。
结果· MRAC在离散阵风和随机湍流中均能有效减小翼尖挠度。
验证· 通过3自由度翼型和Global Hawk无人机两个算例进行仿真验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop an effective gust load alleviation control method for nonlinear aeroelastic systems.
Method· A Model Reference Adaptive Controller is designed using Lyapunov stability theory, operating on a nonlinear reduced-order model derived from Taylor series and eigenvector projection.
Result· MRAC achieves significant wing-tip deflection reductions under both discrete gusts and stochastic turbulence, outperforming H-infinity robust control with comparable control effort.
Validation· The method is validated through simulation case studies on a 3DOF aerofoil with cubic stiffness nonlinearities and a Global Hawk UAV model.
