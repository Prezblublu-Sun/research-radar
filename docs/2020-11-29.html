Medium AM Biomedical Materials pubmed Peripheral Background only none Patient-specific Review

单位: Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Southern University of Science and Technology, Shenzhen 518055, China.
通讯: Sie Chin Tjong @ Department of Physics, City University of Hong Kong, Tat Chee Avenue, Kowloon, Hong Kong, China. [推断]
相关性: 该综述讨论了PEEK及其复合材料在骨替代中的应用，涉及刚度和骨整合，但未包含LPBF制造、疲劳测试或多孔结构对应力遮挡的分析。

Result· Carbon fiber-reinforced PEEK can match cortical bone tensile properties, but hydrophobic PEEK lacks bioactivity and degradability for porous scaffolds.
Validation· No experimental validation is provided; the work is a literature review.

Active Output Selection Strategies for Multiple Learning Regression Models

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Background only none
Adrian Prochaska, Julien Pillas, Bernard Bäker arXiv preprint 2020-11-29 10.5220/0010181501500157
相关性: 该论文涉及主动学习提高采样效率，但运用于汽车标定任务，不涉及约束优化、不确定性量化或生物力学设计。
边界: This paper does not address constraints, uncertainty quantification, or implant/biomechanics design, and lacks validation on FEA or biomechanics problems.
动机· 减少模型标定所需的测试时间
方法· 主动选择交叉验证误差最高的输出模型进行学习
结果· 在最佳情况下减少30%的采样点数
验证· 在含噪玩具示例和基准数据集上验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce the number of test bench experiments required for model-based drivability calibration.
Method· An active output selection strategy that selects the output model with the highest cross-validation error as the leading model to query next.
Result· The proposed strategy reduces the number of required points by up to 30% compared to sequential space-filling designs and outperforms existing active learning strategies.
Validation· The method is validated on three noisy toy examples and a benchmark dataset, with no application to real-world or biomechanical systems.

Effect of the Instruction Time of the Day on Student Learning

Exclude FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Ignore none
Nasim Muhammad, Gaganpreet Sidhu, Seshasai Srinivasan IJPTE International Journal of Pedagogy and Teacher Education 2020-11-29 10.20961/ijpte.v4i2.43070
单位: McMaster University/Mohawk COllege
通讯: Nasim Muhammad @ McMaster University/Mohawk COllege
相关性: 该论文研究教学时间对学习效果的影响，与有限元代理建模或生物力学设计无关。
边界: This paper does not address surrogate modeling, finite element analysis, uncertainty quantification, or any engineering design optimization.
动机· 探究一天中不同授课时间对学生学习效果的影响。
方法· 将174名学生分为三个不同上课时间的班级，由同一教师教授相同内容。
结果· 上午和下午早期的班级表现优于下午晚期的班级。
验证· 通过三个班级的成绩对比进行验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate whether the time of day when instruction occurs affects student learning outcomes in a programming course.
Method· A total of 174 first-year engineering students were divided into three sections with different class times, taught identical material by the same instructor.
Result· Students in morning and early afternoon sections outperformed those in the late afternoon section, with evidence of long-term retention across all sections.
Validation· Comparison of exam performance across three sections with controlled teaching conditions.
