Effect of Space Holders on Fabrication of Porous Titanium Alloy-Hydroxyapatite Composite through Powder Injection Molding

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Farrahshaida Mohd Salleh, Abu Bakar Sulong, Muhammad Rafi Raza, Norhamidi Muhamad, T. Lim Sains Malaysiana 2017-09-30 10.17576/jsm-2017-4609-38
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering COMSATS Institute of Information Technology, Sahiwal Pakistan
通讯: Farrahshaida Mohd Salleh @ Department of Mechanical Engineering COMSATS Institute of Information Technology, Sahiwal Pakistan
相关性: 该论文涉及多孔钛合金/羟基磷灰石复合材料的制备，但未涉及应力遮挡、疲劳性能或增材制造，与用户研究方向关联度低。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no fatigue testing, no stress shielding analysis, no surface roughness or osseointegration evaluation.
动机· 研究不同空间保持剂对粉末注射成型多孔钛合金/羟基磷灰石复合材料性能的影响。
方法· 使用NaCl和PMMA作为空间保持剂，通过溶剂脱脂和热脱脂两步法去除粘结剂，并在不同温度下烧结。
结果· NaCl空间保持剂成功形成孔隙，烧结后样品未断裂，1300°C时密度3.05 g/cm³，抗压强度91.7 MPa，孔隙率11.9 vol%。
验证· 通过密度、抗压强度和孔隙率测量进行验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of different space holders on the fabrication of porous titanium alloy/hydroxyapatite composites via powder injection molding.
Method· Feedstock containing 80 wt.% titanium alloy/hydroxyapatite and 20 wt.% space holder (NaCl or PMMA) was debound via solvent and thermal debinding, then sintered at 1100-1300°C.
Result· Samples with NaCl space holder retained integrity and achieved a density of 3.05 g/cm³, compressive strength of 91.7 MPa, and porosity of 11.9 vol% at 1300°C.
Validation· Physical and mechanical properties were characterized by density, compressive strength, and porosity measurements.
