英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the efficacy of intraoperative intramuscular analgesic infiltration for postoperative pain control in hip surgery patients.
Method· 30 patients undergoing hip arthroplasty or hemiarthroplasty were divided into two groups; the treatment group received an intramuscular cocktail of bupivacaine, morphine, and methylprednisolone during muscle suture.
Result· The infiltration group showed lower VAS pain scores, reduced opioid consumption, and shorter hospitalization.
Validation· Clinical randomized controlled trial with daily VAS monitoring for 7 days.
