单位: Sandia National Laboratories Albuquerque New Mexico
通讯: John M Emery @ Sandia National Laboratories Albuquerque New Mexico
相关性: 该论文使用随机降阶模型构建替代模型，处理激光焊接中的几何和材料不确定性，其方法可迁移到生物力学植入物设计中的不确定性量化。
边界: This paper focuses on laser weld reliability, not on implant biomechanics or FEA surrogate for hip implant design.
动机· 激光焊接结构可靠性预测需要大量精细有限元分析，传统方法效率低。
方法· 提出基于随机降阶模型的替代模型，校准塑性参数以捕捉不确定性。
结果· 替代模型在简化验证问题中与传统蒙特卡洛模拟结果吻合良好。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Accurate finite element predictions of laser weld reliability require numerous high-resolution meshes with varying geometry and material properties, making traditional approaches inefficient.
Method· A surrogate model based on stochastic reduced-order models is constructed, calibrating plasticity parameters to experimental necking observations to capture uncertainty in weld microstructure and geometry.
Result· The proposed method shows remarkable agreement with traditional Monte Carlo simulation on a simplified verification problem.
Validation· The method is validated on a simplified verification problem by comparison with Monte Carlo simulation.
