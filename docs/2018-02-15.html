Regulating Degradation Behavior by Incorporating Mesoporous Silica for Mg Bone Implants

Youwen Yang, Xiaoning Guo, Chongxian He, Chengde Gao, Cijun Shuai ACS Biomaterials Science & Engineering 2018-02-15 10.1021/acsbiomaterials.8b00020
单位: Jiangxi University of Science and Technology, Ganzhou 341000, China
通讯: Cijun Shuai @ Jiangxi University of Science and Technology, Ganzhou 341000, China
相关性: 该论文使用选择性激光熔化技术制备镁基复合材料以调控降解行为，但缺乏对疲劳性能和应力遮挡的评估，与用户关注的植入体力学匹配方向部分相关。
边界: Does not quantify stress shielding, fatigue performance, or surface roughness for osseointegration; focuses on degradation control rather than mechanical stiffness matching or implant primary stability.

方法· 通过选择性激光熔化将介孔二氧化硅掺入ZK60镁合金
结果· 含8wt% MS的复合体降解速率降低57%，并促进磷灰石沉积
验证· 体外降解实验和生物相容性测试验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To address the rapid degradation of magnesium alloy bone implants under physiological conditions.
Method· Mesoporous silica was incorporated into ZK60 magnesium alloy via selective laser melting to enhance corrosion resistance.
Result· The composite with 8 wt% mesoporous silica reduced degradation rate by 57% and promoted apatite deposition.
Validation· In vitro degradation tests and biocompatibility assays were conducted to validate the improved performance.
