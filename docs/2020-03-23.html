边界: This paper focuses on computational complexity reduction in GP bandits, not on surrogate modeling for expensive simulations, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification, or biomechanical implant design.
动机· 降低高斯过程多臂老虎机中后验计算复杂度以支持长迭代周期。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To enable Gaussian process bandit algorithms to handle long iteration horizons by reducing the cubic computational complexity of posterior updates.
Method· A conditional entropy thresholding test that selectively incorporates actions into the GP posterior only when their information gain exceeds a threshold ε.
Result· Sublinear regret bounds are achieved while maintaining sublinear growth of posterior complexity, with a finite complexity bound leading to ε-regret.
Validation· Numerical experiments on global optimization benchmarks demonstrate state-of-the-art accuracy-complexity trade-offs.
