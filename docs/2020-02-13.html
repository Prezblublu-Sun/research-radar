通讯: Srinidhi Nagaraja @ G. RAU, Inc., Scotts Valley, CA 95066
相关性: 该论文直接展示了FEA在医疗器械中的验证、验证和不确定性量化（VVUQ）方法，与用户的研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper focuses on spinal rods under three-point bending, not on hip implants or stress shielding, and does not involve surrogate modeling or optimization.

Result· Differences between FEA and experimental results were less than 10% for all quantities of interest, and rod diameter and material model were identified as key uncertainty drivers.
Validation· Validation was achieved by comparing mechanical properties from FEA with experimental data from two laboratories, showing good agreement.

方法· 提出一种基于手术步骤的新计算方法。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Published operative times fail to capture the true effort exerted by surgeons during total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A novel approach is introduced that calculates operative times based on individual surgical steps to better quantify surgeon work.
Result· The proposed method provides a more accurate representation of surgeon effort compared to traditional operative time metrics.
Validation· No experimental validation is provided; the study is a methodological proposal.
