通讯: Weidong Song @ State Key Laboratory of Explosion Science and Technology, Beijing Institute of Technology, Beijing 100081, China
相关性: 该论文直接研究了Ti-6Al-4V多孔结构的力学行为，与用户关注的LPBF制造、刚度匹配和应力屏蔽高度相关。
边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing, surface roughness characterization, or osseointegration assessment.
动机· 研究菱形十二面体多孔钛合金的力学性能以匹配骨组织刚度。
