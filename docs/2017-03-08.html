Micromotion-induced peri-prosthetic fluid flow around a cementless femoral stem

High Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Direct Read now simulation_case Patient-specific
Valérie Malfroy Camine, Alexandre Terrier, Dominique P. Pioletti Computer Methods in Biomechanics & Biomedical Engineering 2017-03-08 10.1080/10255842.2017.1296954
单位: Laboratory of Biomechanical Orthopedics, Ecole Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne, Lausanne, Switzerland
通讯: Dominique P. Pioletti @ Laboratory of Biomechanical Orthopedics, Ecole Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne, Lausanne, Switzerland
相关性: 该论文直接量化了骨-植入物界面的微动和流体流动，与髋关节植入物应力遮挡和微动研究高度相关。
边界: This paper does not quantify stress shielding or bone remodeling in Gruen zones, nor does it compare intact vs implanted femur.
动机· 探究微动诱导的骨-植入物界面流体流动对无菌性松动的影响。
方法· 使用2D和3D有限元模型，结合全因子设计和CT图像，分析微动和间隙对流体速度的影响。
结果· 局部微动对界面峰值流速影响最大，3D模型预测颗粒组织中流速达2.2 mm/s，小梁骨中达3.9 mm/s。
验证· 基于同一股骨的实验测量边界条件进行数值模拟验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To characterize micromotion-induced interstitial fluid flow at the bone-implant interface and its potential role in aseptic loosening of cementless femoral stems.
Method· Finite element models of 2D transverse sections and a 3D femur were developed using CT images and experimental boundary conditions, with a full-factorial design to assess the influence of material properties, micromotion, and gap on fluid velocity.
Result· Local micromotion was the dominant factor affecting peak fluid velocity; the 3D model predicted peak velocities up to 2.2 mm/s in granulation tissue and 3.9 mm/s in trabecular bone, with shear stresses ranging from 0.1 to 12.5 Pa.
Validation· Boundary conditions were derived from experimental measurements on the same femur, and the model was validated through comparison with literature and sensitivity analysis.
