英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate whether bearing surface type (ceramic-on-ceramic vs. polyethylene) influences dislocation risk and muscle degeneration after total hip arthroplasty revision.
Method· A retrospective study of 240 revision THA patients compared dislocation rates and CT-assessed muscle atrophy between ceramic-on-ceramic and polyethylene bearings.
Result· Ceramic-on-ceramic bearings had significantly fewer dislocations (1% vs. 18%) and no osteolysis or fatty muscle degeneration compared to polyethylene.
Validation· Clinical follow-up over 10-20 years with CT imaging and histological analysis.
