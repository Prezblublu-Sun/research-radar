动机· 遥感数据加载速度慢，GPU利用率低。

Motivation· Training deep learning models on petabyte-scale Earth observation data faces severe GPU underutilization due to inefficient data loading from cloud storage.

Result· Optimized configurations achieve a 20x increase in remote data loading throughput and 4x in local throughput, maintaining 85-95% GPU utilization and matching model accuracy within fixed time budgets.
Validation· Validation is performed on three public Earth observation benchmarks, comparing accuracy and throughput against default PyTorch loaders.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the mortality risk associated with periprosthetic joint infections following total hip arthroplasty compared to aseptic revisions.
Method· A nationwide cohort study using data from the Danish Hip Arthroplasty Register and the Danish Microbiology Database, with inverse probability of treatment weighting to adjust for confounders.
