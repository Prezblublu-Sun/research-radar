边界: This paper does not address expensive simulation sampling efficiency in FEA, does not handle constraints, lacks uncertainty quantification for point predictions, and is not validated on implant/biomechanics design problems.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Parallelizing multi-task Bayesian optimization is challenging due to the combinatorial explosion of sample locations and the need to estimate the effect of multiple new samples on the posterior.

Result· The proposed parallel multi-task Bayesian optimization achieves 69% to 100% parallel efficiency compared to the sequential version.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on standard optimization problems are used to validate the method.
