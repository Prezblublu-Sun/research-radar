Evaluation of Highly Porous Dental Implants in Postablative Oral and Maxillofacial Cancer Patients

单位: *Faculty Member, Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Sciences, "Sapienza" University of Rome, Rome, Italy. †Director, Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Sciences, Maxillofacial Specialization Schoo
通讯: Edoardo Brauner @ *Faculty Member, Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Sciences, "Sapienza" University of Rome, Rome, Italy. †Director, D  |  G Guarino @ Faculty Member, Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Sciences, “Sapienza” University of Rome, Rome, Italy  |  Sara Jamshir @ Faculty Member, Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Sciences, “Sapienza” University of Rome, Rome, Italy  |  Piero Papi @ Faculty Member, Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Sciences, “Sapienza” University of Rome, Rome, Italy
相关性: 该论文评估了多孔钽金属增强牙种植体在口腔癌患者中的临床效果，但未涉及LPBF制造、疲劳测试或应力遮挡分析。
边界: This paper is a clinical case series on porous tantalum dental implants, with no LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), no stress shielding analysis, and no as-built morphology characterization.
动机· 评估多孔钽金属增强牙种植体在口腔癌术后患者中的临床疗效。
方法· 对6名患者植入25枚种植体，通过共振频率分析和X线片测量种植体稳定性和骨水平。
结果· 一年内种植体存活率100%，骨吸收平均0.57 mm，ISQ值稳定。
验证· 临床前瞻性病例系列，随访12个月。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the clinical efficacy of porous tantalum trabecular metal-enhanced titanium dental implants in postablative oral cancer patients.
Method· A prospective clinical case series with 25 implants placed in 6 patients, using resonance frequency analysis and radiographic bone level measurements over 12 months.
Result· 100% implant survival with mean crestal bone loss of 0.57 mm and stable ISQ values after functional loading.
Validation· Clinical case series with 12-month follow-up.
