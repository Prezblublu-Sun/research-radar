Learning in latent spaces improves the predictive accuracy of deep neural operators

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark
Katiana Kontolati, Somdatta Goswami, George Em Karniadakis, Michael D. Shields arXiv preprint 2023-04-15 link
相关性: 该论文提出的潜空间深度算子网络方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模，提高采样效率。
边界: No validation on biomedical or implant-related FEA problems, no uncertainty quantification, and no constraint handling.
动机· 高维PDE数据冗余噪声大，影响算子学习效率。
方法· 用自编码器将输入输出映射到潜空间，再训练DeepONet。
结果· 潜空间方法在多个时变PDE上精度和效率均优于标准DeepONet。
验证· 在脆性断裂、对流、大气流动等数值基准上验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· High-dimensional PDE data contain redundant and noisy features that hinder gradient-based optimization of neural operators.
Method· The latent deep operator network (L-DeepONet) uses autoencoders to map input and output functions to a low-dimensional latent space before training the operator network.
Result· L-DeepONet achieves higher accuracy and computational efficiency than standard DeepONet on several time-dependent PDEs.
Validation· Validated on numerical benchmarks including brittle fracture, convective fluid flows, and multiscale atmospheric flows.

通讯: Yang Liu @ Department of Prosthodontics, Dalian Stomatological Hospital, Dalian, 116021, China. liuyang913015@163.com.
相关性: 该论文使用有限元分析研究短种植体的应力分布，但未涉及增材制造、多孔结构或疲劳测试，与用户研究方向不匹配。

动机· 研究骨条件和骨整合率对短种植体应力分布的影响

Motivation· To investigate how bone quality and osseointegration rate affect stress distribution in short dental implants using finite element analysis.
Method· Anisotropic jaw bone models with three bone conditions and four osseointegration rates were created, and von Mises stress was computed under vertical and oblique loading.
Result· Maximum cortical bone stress increased with deteriorating bone quality and higher osseointegration rate; oblique loading produced stresses 1.3–1.8 times higher than vertical loading.
Validation· No experimental validation; solely computational finite element modeling.
