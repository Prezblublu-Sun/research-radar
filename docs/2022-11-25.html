英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the survival rate of transtrochanteric rotational osteotomy for osteonecrosis of the femoral head.
Method· A meta-analysis of 19 studies was performed using conversion to total hip arthroplasty as the endpoint.
Result· The overall hip survival rate was 58%, with Asian patients showing 68% versus 41% in non-Asian patients.
Validation· The study is a meta-analysis of published literature without experimental validation.
