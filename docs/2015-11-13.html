相关性: 该论文研究离心铸造制造晶格结构，而非LPBF或生物医学植入物，与用户方向关联度低。
边界: This paper focuses on centrifugal casting of lattice structures, not LPBF fabrication, and does not address stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration for biomedical implants.
动机· 研究离心铸造中薄壁晶格结构的金属流动填充特性。
方法· 建立考虑表面张力的Navier-Stokes模型，并用MATLAB和ANSYS模拟。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the flow-filling behavior of molten metals in thin-walled lattice structures during centrifugal casting.
Method· An analytical model based on the Navier-Stokes equation with surface tension was developed and implemented in MATLAB, with numerical simulations performed in ANSYS using a user-defined function.
Result· Aluminum alloy exhibited good flow filling at thin wall thicknesses (∼0.5 mm) due to low surface tension, while copper alloy showed poor filling due to high surface tension and early solidification.
Validation· Experimental centrifugal casting of cubic truss lattice structures with varying wall thickness was conducted and compared with simulation results.
