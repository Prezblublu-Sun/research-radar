Influence of SLM on compressive response of NiTi scaffolds

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Save for project mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Narges Shayesteh Moghaddam, Soheil Saedi, Amirhesam Amerinatanzi, Ahmadreza Jahadakbar, Ehsan Saghaian et al. 2018-03-22 10.1117/12.2305251
单位: The Univ. of Toledo (United States)
通讯: Narges Shayesteh Moghaddam @ The Univ. of Toledo (United States)
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM制备的多孔NiTi支架的超弹性和压缩性能，实现了骨匹配刚度，但未涉及疲劳寿命和表面形态，与用户对AM生物材料的方向部分相关。
边界: This paper does not cover fatigue testing beyond 10 cycles, surface roughness, as-built morphology, or osseointegration, limiting its completeness for implant validation.
动机· 研究SLM制备的多孔NiTi支架超弹性和压缩性能，以实现骨匹配刚度。
方法· 通过SLM制备不同孔隙率(32-58%)的NiTi试样，热处理后测试超弹性响应和循环行为。
结果· 孔隙率增加导致不可恢复应变增大，但循环后超弹性稳定，弹性模量从47 GPa降至9 GPa。
验证· 通过压缩试验和10次循环测试验证力学性能。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the superelasticity and compressive properties of SLM-fabricated porous NiTi scaffolds for bone-matching stiffness.
Method· NiTi scaffolds with varying porosities (32-58%) were fabricated via SLM, solution annealed and aged, then tested for superelastic response and cyclic behavior.
Result· Higher porosity increased irrecoverable strain, but after 10 cycles superelasticity stabilized; modulus decreased from 47 GPa to 9 GPa with 58% porosity.
Validation· Mechanical compression and cyclic testing on fabricated scaffolds.
