Physics-Informed Polynomial Chaos Expansions

Lukáš Novák, Himanshu Sharma, Michael D. Shields arXiv preprint 2023-09-04 link
相关性: 该方法直接针对昂贵仿真模型的代理建模问题，通过物理约束提高精度并支持不确定性量化，对生物力学植入物设计中的代理建模具有高度可迁移性。
边界: Does not include a specific implant or biomechanics FEA case study; the numerical examples are abstract and not directly tied to biomedical simulation.
动机· 有限实验数据下构建准确代理模型困难，需融入物理约束
方法· 提出物理信息多项式混沌展开，结合数据与微分方程及边界条件约束
结果· 方法在精度上优于标准稀疏PCE，且计算开销小
验证· 通过多个确定性数值算例验证，并应用于不确定性量化
English summary & terms
Motivation· Building accurate surrogate models for costly physical simulations with limited data necessitates incorporating physics constraints.
Method· A physics-informed polynomial chaos expansion (PCE) method is proposed that integrates experimental data with constraints from differential equations and boundary conditions in a computationally efficient manner.
Result· The proposed algorithm yields superior approximation accuracy compared to standard sparse PCE without significant additional computational burden.
Validation· The method is validated on several deterministic numerical examples of increasing complexity and applied to uncertainty quantification.

通讯: Seunghun S. Lee @ Institute for Biomechanics, Department of Health Sciences and Technology, ETH Zurich, Zurich, Switzerland. Electronic ad

English summary & terms
Motivation· To create affordable, assemblable patient-specific titanium scaffolds for bone defect repair.
Method· Three porosity levels of LEGO-inspired titanium scaffolds were fabricated via SLM and acid-etched for surface modification.
Result· Scaffolds showed adequate mechanical properties, enhanced cell proliferation, and osteogenic differentiation in porous groups.
Validation· Mechanical testing, FEA, and in vitro cell assays (viability, proliferation, osteogenesis) were conducted.

A Self‐Sustaining Hydrogels with Autonomous Supply of Nutrients and Bioactive Domains for 3D Cell Culture

Mehrzad Zargarzadeh, Maria C. Gomes, Sónia G. Patrício, Catarina A. Custódio, João F. Mano Advanced Functional Materials 2023-09-04 10.1002/adfm.202214372
单位: Department of Chemistry CICECO – Aveiro Institute of Materials University of Aveiro 3810‐193 Aveiro Portugal
通讯: Catarina A. Custódio @ Department of Chemistry CICECO – Aveiro Institute of Materials University of Aveiro 3810‐193 Aveiro Portugal  |  João F. Mano @ Department of Chemistry CICECO – Aveiro Institute of Materials University of Aveiro 3810‐193 Aveiro Portugal
相关性: 该论文讨论了用于3D细胞培养的自维持水凝胶，但完全没有涉及人工智能或生物打印中的AI应用。
边界: This paper focuses on hydrogel material development for 3D cell culture and bioprinting, but does not incorporate any AI/ML method nor address autonomous decision-making in bioprinting workflow.
动机· 开发自维持水凝胶以持续供应营养和生物活性因子，用于3D细胞培养。
方法· 将光交联血小板裂解物与酶降解海带多糖结合，制备自供养水凝胶。
结果· 该水凝胶可作为生物墨水打印细胞负载结构，并用于构建3D体外肿瘤模型。
验证· 通过体外细胞培养和打印实验验证水凝胶的生物相容性和功能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a self-sustaining hydrogel that can autonomously supply nutrients and bioactive molecules for long-term 3D cell culture.
Method· UV-responsive laminaran-methacrylate and platelet lysate-methacrylate were combined with glucoamylase to create a hybrid hydrogel that degrades laminaran to release glucose and growth factors.
Result· The hydrogel served as a bioink for freestanding microgels and cell-laden constructs, and demonstrated potential as a 3D in vitro cancer model.
Validation· In vitro cell culture and 3D bioprinting experiments confirmed the material's biocompatibility and functionality.
