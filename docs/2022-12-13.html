Dual Accuracy-Quality-Driven Neural Network for Prediction Interval Generation

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Giorgio Morales, John W. Sheppard arXiv preprint 2022-12-13 10.1109/TNNLS.2023.3339470
相关性: 该论文提出了一种生成预测区间的新方法，可迁移至生物力学代理模型中的不确定性量化。
边界: This paper does not apply the method to FEA surrogate modeling, implant design, or biomechanics; it focuses on general regression tasks with benchmark datasets.
动机· 提高深度学习回归模型的不确定性量化可靠性。

结果· 在多个基准数据集上生成更窄且保持名义覆盖率的预测区间。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To enhance the reliability of deep learning regression models by generating high-quality prediction intervals that are both narrow and probabilistically accurate.

Result· The proposed method produces significantly narrower prediction intervals while maintaining nominal coverage compared to three state-of-the-art methods across multiple datasets.
Validation· Validation on synthetic data, eight benchmark datasets, and a real-world crop yield prediction dataset.

动机· 评估微创入路全髋关节置换术的远期结果。
方法· 比较55例患者传统与微创入路的临床指标。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the long-term clinical outcomes of total hip arthroplasty performed via a minimally invasive surgical approach.
Method· A comparative analysis was conducted on 55 patients with dysplastic coxarthrosis, divided into traditional (n=25) and minimally invasive (n=30) groups.
Result· The minimally invasive approach resulted in significantly lower pain scores (VAS), reduced C-reactive protein levels, and shorter rehabilitation periods compared to the standard approach.
Validation· Validation was performed through clinical laboratory tests (CRP, ESR) and VAS pain assessment pre- and postoperatively.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare long-term functional outcomes between metal-on-metal and metal-on-polyethylene total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Muscle mass was measured by DXA, muscle power by leg extensor rig, and step asymmetry by inertial measurement unit.
Result· No significant differences in most functional outcomes, but MoM group had higher thigh muscle mass.
Validation· Cross-sectional clinical study with mean 6.5-year follow-up.
