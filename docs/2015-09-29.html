通讯: Aysel Aydın @ Division of Nuclear Medicine, Department of Radiology, Hospital of the University of Pennsylvania, 3400 Spruce Street, P
相关性: 该论文仅研究THA术后FDG-PET影像模式，未涉及任何生物力学建模或应力遮挡分析。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, no stress/strain quantification, no micromotion analysis, no FEA, and no implant design comparison.
动机· 确定无并发症THA患者术后FDG-PET的典型摄取模式。
方法· 回顾性分析62例无症状THA患者的76次PET扫描，按术后时间分组评估摄取部位。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To characterize typical 18F-FDG uptake patterns in asymptomatic patients after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Retrospective analysis of 76 PET scans from 62 patients, with uptake assessed qualitatively in periprosthetic regions and stratified by postoperative time intervals.
Result· Increased FDG uptake was predominantly confined to the proximal prosthesis (femoral neck and proximal shaft), with minimal activity in the distal femoral segment.
Validation· Clinical imaging study without biomechanical or experimental validation.
