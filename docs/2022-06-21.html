相关性: 该论文关注手术室护理干预与陶瓷假体对全髋关节置换术的效果，完全不涉及生物力学分析或应力遮挡相关研究。

动机· 评估手术室护理干预与纳米陶瓷假体对全髋关节置换术的效果
方法· 将120例患者随机分为常规组和干预组，加强干预组手术室护理并使用纳米陶瓷假体
结果· 干预组Harris评分和满意度显著提高，并发症显著降低
验证· 临床随机对照试验，比较两组患者的髋关节评分和并发症发生率
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the effect of operating room nursing intervention combined with nanoceramic prosthesis on clinical outcomes of total hip arthroplasty.
Method· 120 patients were randomly assigned to routine or intervention groups; the intervention group received enhanced nursing and nanoceramic prostheses.
Result· The intervention group showed significantly higher Harris hip scores and nursing satisfaction, and significantly lower complication rates.
Validation· Randomized controlled clinical trial comparing hip scores, complications, and satisfaction between two groups.
