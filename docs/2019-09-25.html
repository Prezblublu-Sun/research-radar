Evaluation of Low Cycle Fatigue Performance of Selective Laser Melted Titanium Alloy Ti–6Al–4V

Peng Zhang, Allen He, Fei Liu, Kaifei Zhang, Junjie Jiang et al. Metals 2019-09-25 10.3390/met9101041
单位: College of Engineering, Mathematics and Physical Sciences, University of Exeter, North Park Road, Exeter EX4 4QF, UK
通讯: David Z. Zhang @ College of Engineering, Mathematics and Physical Sciences, University of Exeter, North Park Road, Exeter EX4 4QF, UK
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM制备的Ti-6Al-4V的低周疲劳性能，对生物医学植入物的疲劳评估有参考价值，但未涉及多孔结构或骨整合。
边界: No porous structure, stress shielding, or osseointegration; lacks biomedical implant context.
动机· 评估选区激光熔化Ti-6Al-4V的低周疲劳性能
方法· 对成型态和退火态SLM Ti-6Al-4V进行低周疲劳测试并与锻造材料对比
结果· 成型态SLM材料低周疲劳性能与锻造相当，但退火后因晶粒粗化而劣化
验证· 通过低周疲劳实验和SEM断口分析验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the low cycle fatigue performance of selective laser melted Ti-6Al-4V, which is critical for safety-critical structures under dynamic loading.
Method· Low cycle fatigue tests were conducted on as-built and annealed SLM Ti-6Al-4V specimens, and compared with wrought material; cyclic stress-strain and strain-life curves were modeled using Ramberg-Osgood and Basquin-Coffin-Manson equations.
Result· As-built SLM Ti-6Al-4V exhibited comparable LCF life to wrought at lower strain amplitudes but degraded at higher amplitudes due to porosity; annealing worsened performance due to grain coarsening.
Validation· The study is validated through low cycle fatigue experiments and scanning electron microscopy of fracture surfaces.

SLM Ti-6Al-4V low cycle fatigue mechanical testing as-built annealed wrought fracture surfaces

单位: Comparative Medicine Institute, North Carolina State University, Raleigh, NC, 27695, United States of America
通讯: Parth Chansoria @ Comparative Medicine Institute, North Carolina State University, Raleigh, NC, 27695, United States of America
相关性: 该论文虽涉及生物打印，但未使用人工智能或机器学习，不符合AI驱动生物打印的研究方向。
边界: This paper does not incorporate AI/ML into the bioprinting workflow; it focuses on empirical process physics and computational models without autonomous decision-making or learning.
动机· 研究超声辅助生物打印的工艺物理机制以实现细胞有序排列
方法· 建立计算模型并与实验结合分析超声参数对打印质量的影响
结果· 发现频率和电压对细胞束宽、间距和活性有显著影响
验证· 通过MG63细胞在藻酸盐中的实验验证模型和参数效应
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand the underlying physics of ultrasound-assisted bioprinting for controlled cellular organization within 3D constructs.
Method· Computational models were developed and validated against experiments to analyze effects of ultrasound parameters on temperature, pressure, and alignment time.
Result· Key process parameters (frequency, voltage amplitude) were identified that significantly affect strand width, inter-strand spacing, and cell viability.
Validation· Experimental validation using MG63 cells in alginate bioink as a model system.

单位: University of Toronto, Department of Chemical Engineering and Applied Chemistry, 200 College St. Toronto, ON M5S 3E5, Canada
通讯: Nancy T. Li @ University of Toronto, Department of Chemical Engineering and Applied Chemistry, 200 College St. Toronto, ON M5S 3E5, Ca  |  Alison P. McGuigan @ University of Toronto, Department of Chemical Engineering and Applied Chemistry, 200 College St. Toronto, ON M5S 3E5, Ca

Motivation· To enhance the manufacturing robustness of the TRACER platform for multi-cell type studies in 3D tumor models.
Method· A contact capillary-wicking bioprinting process was developed using a PDMS nozzle to deposit cell-laden collagen hydrogel onto cellulose scaffolding sheets.
Result· Co-culture strips of tumor cells and fibroblasts were successfully printed with maintained pattern quality and cell viability.
Validation· Print pattern quality and reproducibility were assessed, and co-culture printing was demonstrated.

Alginate dependent changes of physical properties in 3D bioprinted cell-laden porous scaffolds affect cell viability and cell morphology.

单位: Institute for Biomechanics, ETH Zurich, Leopold-Ruzicka-Weg 4, 8093 Zurich, Switzerland.
通讯: Ralph Müller @ Institute for Biomechanics, ETH Zurich, Leopold-Ruzicka-Weg 4, 8093 Zurich, Switzerland. [推断]
相关性: 该论文没有涉及任何AI/ML方法，纯粹是生物材料与细胞实验研究。
边界: No AI/ML integration into bioprinting workflow; purely experimental study on alginate concentration effects without computational modeling.
动机· 探究海藻酸钠浓度对3D生物打印支架物理性质及细胞行为的影响
方法· 制备不同浓度海藻酸钠的细胞负载支架，测试力学性能并培养细胞
结果· 低浓度支架细胞活性高且形成网络，高浓度支架结构稳定但细胞活性低
验证· 通过体外细胞实验和力学测试验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how alginate concentration in 3D bioprinted scaffolds affects physical properties and cell behavior.
Method· Four alginate concentrations (0.8%-2.3%) were used to fabricate cell-laden porous scaffolds via 3D bioprinting, followed by mechanical testing and cell culture.
Result· Lower alginate concentration (0.8%) yielded higher cell viability (84%) and interconnected cellular networks, while higher concentration (2.3%) provided better structural integrity but lower viability (68%).
Validation· Experimental validation through compressive modulus measurements and live cell imaging over 14 days.

Effect of Process Parameters on Residual Stresses, Distortions, and Porosity in Selective Laser Melting of Maraging Steel 300

Method· Systematic variation of laser power, scanning speed, and layer thickness with measurement of residual stresses, distortions, and porosity.
Result· High residual stress correlates with large distortions, while porous parts exhibit lower residual stresses.
Validation· Experimental measurement and analysis of interdependencies.
