Experimental investigations of the nickel alloy laser melting parameters influence on porosity and surface roughness of the complex geometry products during the process of their three-dimensional formation

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
JSC “Science and Innovations”, Moscow, Russia, A. V. Dub, V. V. Beregovskiy, E.V. Tretyakov, S. A. Shchurenkova Non-ferrous Metals 2016-12-26 10.17580/nfm.2016.12.06
相关性: 该论文研究了镍基合金SLM工艺参数对孔隙率和表面粗糙度的影响，但未涉及生物医学植入物、应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: No link to stress shielding or stiffness matching, no biomedical context, no fatigue testing, no osseointegration or as-built morphology for implants.
动机· 研究镍合金SLM工艺参数对复杂几何产品孔隙率和表面粗糙度的影响
方法· 实验改变激光功率和扫描速度，分析孔隙率、表面粗糙度与能量密度的关系
结果· 确定了最优工艺参数：激光功率275W，扫描速度605mm/s，孔隙率0.58-3.18%，粗糙度Ra 8.6-19.3μm
验证· 通过截面金相和粗糙度测量验证工艺参数影响
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the influence of selective laser melting parameters on porosity and surface roughness of complex geometry nickel alloy parts.
Method· Experimental variation of laser power (245-305 W) and scanning speed (605-1005 mm/s) to analyze porosity and surface roughness as functions of linear energy density.
Result· Optimal parameters (275 W, 605 mm/s) yielded porosity 0.58-3.18% and surface roughness Ra 8.6-19.3 μm, with high speeds causing coarse pores and low speeds increasing fine pores.
Validation· Metallographic cross-sections and surface profilometry validated the porosity and roughness measurements.
