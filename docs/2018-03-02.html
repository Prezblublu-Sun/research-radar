A multilevel Markov Chain Monte Carlo approach for uncertainty quantification in deformable registration

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project simulation_case UQ Patient-specific
Sandra Schultz, Heinz Handels, Jan Ehrhardt 2018-03-02 10.1117/12.2293588
单位: Univ. of Lübeck (Germany)
通讯: Sandra Schultz @ Univ. of Lübeck (Germany)
相关性: 该论文提出的多层级MCMC不确定性量化方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理模型的不确定性评估。
边界: This paper focuses on deformable image registration uncertainty, not on surrogate modeling for expensive finite element simulations or implant design optimization.
动机· 图像引导诊疗中需要评估配准结果的可靠性。
方法· 将MCMC嵌入多层级框架以降低贝叶斯后验采样的计算成本。
结果· 多层级MCMC在保持准确性的同时显著降低了计算负担。
验证· 通过脑MRI图像与后验分布真值对比验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Reliable uncertainty quantification in deformable image registration is critical for clinical decision-making but computationally prohibitive with standard MCMC.
Method· A multilevel Markov Chain Monte Carlo framework is integrated into a B-spline transformation model to reduce sampling cost by exploiting multi-resolution image representations.
Result· The multilevel approach achieves comparable accuracy to ground-truth posterior distributions while substantially reducing computational cost.
Validation· Validation on brain MRI images by comparing posterior estimates to a reference ground-truth distribution and assessing cost reduction.

边界: This paper focuses on taper junction failure of modular revision hip stems, not on proximal femur stress shielding, micromotion at bone-implant interface, or intact vs implanted femur comparison.
动机· 评估设计、患者和手术因素对翻修髋关节假体锥形连接失效风险的影响。

Motivation· To assess the influence of implant design, patient, and surgical factors on the risk of taper failure in modular revision hip stems.

Validation· The finite element model was validated against the analytical bending model for pristine, fatigued, and corroded conditions.
