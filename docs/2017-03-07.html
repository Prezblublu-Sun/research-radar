A comparison of efficient uncertainty quantification techniques for stochastic multiscale systems

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project simulation_case UQ
Grigoriy Kimaev, Luis Ricardez‐Sandoval AIChE Journal 2017-03-07 10.1002/aic.15702
单位: Dept. of Chemical Engineering University of Waterloo Waterloo ON Canada N2L 3G1
通讯: Luis Ricardez‐Sandoval @ Dept. of Chemical Engineering University of Waterloo Waterloo ON Canada N2L 3G1
相关性: 该论文比较了多项式混沌展开和幂级数展开在多尺度系统中的不确定性量化效率，其方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理模型的不确定性量化。
边界: This paper focuses on chemical vapor deposition multiscale systems, not on biomedical implant FEA or surrogate modeling for biomechanics.
动机· 比较高效不确定性传播方法在多尺度系统中的性能
方法· 使用PCE和PSE对薄膜沉积多尺度模型进行不确定性量化
结果· 单参数时PSE最优，多参数时PCE结合NISP方法精度高且计算成本低
验证· 通过化学气相沉积薄膜形成模型进行仿真验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the performance of efficient uncertainty propagation techniques for stochastic multiscale systems without closed-form expressions.
Method· Polynomial Chaos Expansion and Power Series Expansion were applied to a multiscale model of thin film formation by chemical vapor deposition for single and multivariate uncertainty quantification.
Result· Second-order PSE was most accurate for single parameter uncertainty, while second-order PCE with NISP provided high accuracy at half the computational cost for multivariate uncertainty.
Validation· The methods were validated on a simulation case of thin film deposition, and robust optimization was performed to satisfy constraints.

通讯: Wenhao Song @ Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Shandong Provincial Hospital Affiliated to Shandong University, 324 Jingwu Road, Ji'na
相关性: 该论文研究耻骨联合浮动损伤的固定方法，与髋关节植入物和股骨柄的应力遮挡、微动分析无关。
边界: This paper focuses on pelvic ring fixation for floating pubic symphysis, not on hip implant or femoral stem stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics.
动机· 比较六种固定方法治疗浮动耻骨联合的生物力学性能。
方法· 建立三维有限元模型，施加压缩和旋转载荷，分析刚度、微动和应力。
结果· 内固定和皮下固定恢复压缩刚度，但旋转刚度不足；内固定减少骨折间隙微动。
验证· 有限元模拟，无实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the biomechanical performance of six fixation methods for floating pubic symphysis using finite element analysis.
Method· A 3D finite element model of floating pubic symphysis was created, and compressive and rotational loads were applied to evaluate construct stiffness, fracture gap micromotion, and von Mises stress.
Result· Internal fixation and subcutaneous rod fixation restored compressive stiffness, but rotational stiffness remained insufficient; internal fixation significantly reduced fracture gap micromotion.
Validation· Finite element simulation without experimental validation.
