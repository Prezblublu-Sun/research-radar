Spatio-temporal Gaussian processes modeling of dynamical systems in systems biology

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Skim only simulation_case UQ
Mu Niu, Zhenwen Dai, Neil Lawrence, Kolja Becker arXiv preprint 2016-10-17 link
相关性: 该论文将高斯过程与偏微分方程结合进行代理建模并实现不确定性量化，方法可迁移至生物力学有限元替代模型，但未涉及约束处理或植入物设计。
边界: No solid mechanics or implant design application; no handling of constraints typical in biomechanical optimization.
动机· 系统生物学中难以对转录后调控过程进行定量建模并恢复连续时空场。
方法· 使用高斯过程作为潜在函数先验，将偏微分方程信息编码入核函数，混合蒙特卡洛进行推断。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Quantitative modeling of post-transcriptional regulation and recovery of continuous spatio-temporal fields remains challenging in systems biology.
Method· Gaussian processes are employed as a prior over latent functions, encoding PDE information into the kernel, and inference is performed via Hybrid Monte Carlo.
Result· Spatio-temporal fields of protein concentration and mRNA expression are reconstructed without directly solving the PDE.
Validation· Reconstruction is demonstrated on synthetic or real biological data, but no explicit comparison to ground truth is reported.
