相关性: 该论文直接评估了髋关节假体的应力遮挡和骨重塑行为，与用户研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not report micromotion values or contact/friction modeling at the bone-implant interface.
动机· 探索基于DHS的髓外固定系统用于髋关节假体的可行性。
方法· 基于QCT图像建立股骨体素有限元模型，施加肌肉和关节力，比较DHS系统与传统长柄设计。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the feasibility of an extramedullary fixation system based on the Dynamic Hip Screw for hip joint prostheses in patients with narrow medullary canals.

Result· The DHS-based fixation exhibited very high screw stresses exceeding endurance limits and induced substantial stress shielding in periprosthetic bone, suggesting it may not be suitable for hip arthroplasty.
Validation· The finite element model was validated by simulating an experimental in-vitro compression test on a cadaveric proximal femur.
