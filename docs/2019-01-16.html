相关性: 该论文仅涉及生物打印肝芯片的制造，未包含任何人工智能或机器学习方法。

动机· 现有肝芯片缺乏细胞外基质和胆道系统，需要改进。

Motivation· Current liver-on-a-chip models lack extracellular matrix and biliary systems, limiting their physiological relevance.

Result· The chip with a biliary channel enhanced biliary system formation and liver-specific gene expression and functions, and showed effective drug response.
Validation· Validation was performed through in vitro assays of liver function and drug toxicity testing with acetaminophen.

相关性: 该论文关注多材料点阵结构的鲁棒拓扑优化，未涉及生物医学植入物、应力遮挡或疲劳测试。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing, no biomedical context, no stress shielding or osseointegration.
动机· 提出多材料点阵结构在材料与载荷不确定性下的鲁棒拓扑优化方法。

Method· A generalized material interpolation scheme and univariate dimension reduction with Gauss quadrature are used to propagate uncertainties, optimizing a weighted sum of mean and standard deviation of compliance.
Result· Cantilever beam examples demonstrate efficiency and flexibility; accuracy is validated against Monte Carlo simulations.
Validation· Numerical benchmark validation via Monte Carlo comparison.
