边界: This paper focuses on spinal fusion cages, not hip implants; it does not quantify femoral stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics.
动机· 开发不同填充模式的椎间融合器以减少PLIF术后并发症。
方法· 基于CT图像建立L1-L2节段有限元模型，分析不同填充模式融合器的应力。
结果· 蜂窝填充模式融合器表现出最小的Von Mises应力和最大主应力。
验证· 仅通过有限元仿真对比不同设计，无实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop interbody cages with various infill patterns to reduce complications such as stress shielding in PLIF surgery.
Method· Finite element models of L1-L2 vertebrae were constructed from CT scans, and cages with different infill patterns were analyzed under physiological loading conditions.
Result· The honeycomb infill pattern cage exhibited the lowest Von Mises and maximum principal stresses, indicating superior biomechanical performance.
Validation· The study relied solely on finite element simulations without experimental validation.
