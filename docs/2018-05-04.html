Motivation· To evaluate midterm outcomes and return to sport in athletes undergoing hip arthroscopy.
Method· Prospective case series of 77 hips in athletes with minimum 5-year follow-up, collecting patient-reported outcomes and sports participation data.
Result· 80.3% returned to sport, 71.2% at same or higher ability; no conversions to total hip arthroplasty.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with comparison of 2- and 5-year outcomes.
