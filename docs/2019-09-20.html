Method· A cognitive task analysis was developed via modified Delphi method and implemented as a web-based tool; 36 surgical residents were randomized to cognitive training or standard manual/video training before performing simulated THA.
Result· The cognitive training group completed the simulated procedure 35% faster, made 69% fewer instrument selection errors, and achieved more accurate acetabular cup orientation.
