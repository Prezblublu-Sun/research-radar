相关性: 该论文提出的算子学习方法能够达到机器精度，且适用于多种非线性PDE问题，对生物力学FEA替代建模具有高度可迁移性。
边界: This paper does not apply the method to biomedical or implant mechanics problems, nor does it address constraints or uncertainty quantification in design optimization.

Result· The method achieves machine precision on multiple nonlinear forward and inverse PDE problems, and the foundation model FONKNORIS generalizes to unseen PDEs.
Validation· Validated on benchmark problems including nonlinear elliptic, Burgers, Darcy flow, Calderón, inverse wave scattering, and seismic imaging.
