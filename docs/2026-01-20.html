High FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project simulation_case
Fabian Greifeneder, Wolfgang Fenz, Benedikt Alkin, Johannes Brandstetter, Michael Giretzlehner et al. arXiv preprint 2026-01-20 link
相关性: 该论文提出的自回归深度学习代理模型用于软组织实时模拟，其方法可迁移至植入物生物力学设计中的高效仿真。
边界: This paper does not address implant design, stress shielding, or bone-implant interface mechanics; it focuses on brain tissue deformation for surgical simulation.
动机· 实现虚拟神经外科手术中脑组织变形的实时高保真模拟。
方法· 基于Universal Physics Transformers构建自回归代理模型，采用随机教师强制训练策略。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To achieve real-time, accurate simulation of brain deformation for interactive neurosurgical simulators, overcoming the computational limitations of traditional numerical solvers.
Method· An autoregressive deep learning surrogate model based on Universal Physics Transformers, trained with a stochastic teacher forcing strategy to reduce error accumulation during long rollouts.
Result· The surrogate model achieves sub-10 ms inference per step on meshes up to 150,000 nodes, reducing maximum prediction error from 6.7 mm to 3.5 mm.
Validation· Validated on a large dataset generated from nonlinear finite element simulations covering diverse instrument-tissue interaction scenarios.
