相关性: 该论文仅涉及髋关节置换术后股骨偏距变化与功能结果的关系，未涉及应力遮挡、微动或有限元建模。
边界: This paper does not quantify stress/strain, micromotion, or contact mechanics; it lacks finite element modeling or implant stability analysis.
动机· 分析全髋置换术中股骨偏距变化是否影响术后功能。
方法· 前瞻性收集18个月内的非骨水泥全髋置换病例，测量术前术后股骨偏距。

验证· 临床随访数据统计分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Investigate whether changes in femoral offset after cementless total hip arthroplasty affect functional outcomes.
Method· Prospective study over 18 months measuring preoperative and postoperative femoral offset in patients undergoing total hip replacement.
Result· No significant correlation was found between femoral offset change and postoperative functional scores.
Validation· Clinical data collection and statistical analysis of patient outcomes.
