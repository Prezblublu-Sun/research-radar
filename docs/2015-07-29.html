Structural Reliability Analysis of Complex Systems: Applications to Offshore and Composite Structures

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only simulation_case UQ
Marco N. Coccon Padua@research (University of Padova) 2015-07-29 link
相关性: 该论文专注于海洋和航空结构的系统可靠性分析，与生物力学植入物设计或FEA代理建模无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address surrogate modeling for FEA, does not involve implant or biomechanics design, and lacks uncertainty quantification methods transferable to biomedical applications.
动机· 开发复杂结构系统可靠性分析的新方法，提高效率并减少计算成本。
方法· 采用遗传算法识别主导失效模式，结合多尺度系统可靠性分析。
结果· 成功验证了所提方法在海洋平台和复合材料强度预测中的准确性和效率。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop efficient methodologies for reliability analysis of complex structural systems, minimizing computational costs while capturing dominant failure modes.
Method· A genetic algorithm-based multi-point parallel search identifies critical failure sequences, followed by a multi-scale system reliability analysis incorporating statistical dependence.
Result· The proposed approach demonstrates accuracy and efficiency validated against Monte Carlo simulation for offshore jacket platforms and composite strength prediction.
Validation· Validation is performed using crude Monte Carlo simulation for the offshore case and comparison with an analytical model for composites.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the clinical efficacy of extensor mechanism allograft for chronic abductor deficiency after revision THA.
Method· Retrospective analysis of 11 patients who received fresh-frozen knee extensor mechanism allograft reconstruction.
Result· Most patients showed improved limp and abductor strength, but only five achieved good Harris hip scores.
Validation· Clinical follow-up at mean 33 months with pre- and post-operative functional scores.
