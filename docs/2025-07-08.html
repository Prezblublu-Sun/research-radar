英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the impact of hypothyroidism on short-term and long-term complications after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective cohort study using the TriNetX database with propensity score matching to compare hypothyroid and non-hypothyroid patients.
Result· Hypothyroid patients had significantly higher rates of anemia within 90 days and increased risks of implant failure, periprosthetic fractures, and aseptic loosening at 2 years.
Validation· Propensity score matching and Kaplan-Meier survival analysis were used to control confounders and assess implant survival.
