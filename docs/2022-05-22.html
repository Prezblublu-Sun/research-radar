相关性: 该论文提出了一种快速高斯过程后验均值预测方法，可提高代理模型的采样效率，但未涉及约束处理或不确定性量化，且缺乏生物力学设计验证。

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project simulation_case
Wenhao Ding, Qing He, Hanghang Tong, Ping Wang arXiv preprint 2022-05-22 link
相关性: 该论文提出的物理信息神经算子方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模，但未涉及植入物设计或不确定性量化。
边界: No FEA validation, no biomedical context, no implant mechanics, no uncertainty quantification.
动机· 多体动力学中参数化耦合ODE求解效率低，需高效代理模型。
方法· 提出PINO-MBD，将物理方程嵌入神经算子损失函数，并设计多解嵌入方法。
结果· 在车辆-轨道耦合动力学问题上，解及其一阶二阶导数均达最优。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Efficiently solving parameterized coupled ODEs in multi-body dynamics is crucial for design iteration.
Method· Proposes PINO-MBD, a physics-informed neural operator that embeds governing equations into the loss function and introduces two physics embedding methods to handle multiple solution components.
Result· Achieves state-of-the-art accuracy in predicting solutions and their first and second derivatives on a classic vehicle-track coupled dynamics problem.
Validation· Validated on a numerical simulation case of vehicle-track coupled dynamics.

James M. Borgman, Paul Conway, Carmen Torres-Sánchez Powder Technology 2022-05-22 10.1016/j.powtec.2022.117546
单位: Multifunctional Materials Manufacturing Laboratory, Wolfson School of Mechanical, Electrical and Manufacturing Engineering, Loughborough University, Ashby Road, Loughborough, Leicestershire LE11 3TU,
通讯: James M. Borgman @ Multifunctional Materials Manufacturing Laboratory, Wolfson School of Mechanical, Electrical and Manufacturing Engineeri
相关性: 本文关注Ti-Nb合金粉末的制备工艺，未涉及植入体力学性能或生物相容性。
边界: No porous structure, no stress shielding analysis, no mechanical testing beyond hardness, no fatigue or osseointegration data.
动机· 开发低成本SLM用Ti-Nb合金粉末。
方法· 使用无机过程控制剂研磨粉末，避免有机PCA引入的间隙相。
结果· 成功制备了具有合适物理性能和硬度的Ti-Nb合金。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a cost-effective method for producing Ti-Nb alloy powders suitable for selective laser melting, enabling small-batch bespoke alloys without the high volume requirements of atomization.
Method· Inorganic process control agents (tin and calcium) were used during ball milling of elemental Ti and Nb powders to prevent interstitial phase contamination during SLM.
Result· Calcium was effective as a PCA, yielding powders with appropriate flowability and particle size, and SLM-fabricated parts showed homogeneous microstructure and adequate hardness.
Validation· Powder properties (morphology, size, flow) and SLM part hardness were characterized using SEM, XRD, and microhardness testing.
