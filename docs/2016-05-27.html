单位: NASA Langley Research Center

相关性: 该论文涉及航空噪声预测的不确定性量化，与生物力学有限元代理建模方向无关。
边界: This paper focuses on aircraft noise prediction with UQ, not on surrogate modeling for FEA, biomechanics, or implant design.
动机· 量化先进飞机概念系统噪声预测的不确定性。
方法· 使用直接蒙特卡洛方法进行10,000次仿真，评估95%置信区间的不确定性。

Result· The total uncertainty band for cumulative system noise prediction was reduced from 12.2 to 9.6 EPNL dB over the study period.
Validation· Validation relied on NASA's modeled hybrid wing body aircraft concepts and integrated system-level experimental data.
