The Mini Wheelbot: A Testbed for Learning-based Balancing, Flips, and Articulated Driving

Motivation· To provide a testbed for learning-based control algorithms on an unstable balancing robot.
Method· Bayesian optimization for controller tuning and imitation learning from nonlinear MPC for flips and driving.
Result· The robot can stand up from any orientation and follow user commands for the first time in its class.
Validation· Experimental demonstration with video.
