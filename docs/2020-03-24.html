Tension-compression asymmetric mechanical behaviour of lattice cellular structures produced by selective laser melting

High AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Read now mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Sunil Raghavendra, A. Molinari, V. Fontanari, Michele Dallago, Valerio Luchin et al. Proceedings of the Institution of Mechanical Engineers Part C Journal of Mechanical Engineering Science 2020-03-24 10.1177/0954406220912786
单位: Department of Industrial Engineering, University of Trento, Italy
通讯: Sunil Raghavendra @ Department of Industrial Engineering, University of Trento, Italy
相关性: 该论文直接研究了LPBF制造的多孔Ti6Al4V结构的力学性能，包括应力屏蔽、疲劳测试和形态表征，与用户的研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not explicitly link porous structure to osseointegration or surface roughness, and fatigue testing is limited to cyclic tension/compression without high-cycle fatigue data.
动机· 研究LPBF制造的多孔钛合金结构的力学性能，以避免应力屏蔽并改善骨整合。
方法· 制备三种不同拓扑和孔隙率的Ti6Al4V多孔结构，进行形态表征及单轴拉伸/压缩和循环测试。
结果· 发现制造偏差导致支柱厚度偏离设计值，且压缩与拉伸弹性模量存在不对称性。
验证· 通过实验力学测试和Gibson-Ashby模型验证有限元模拟结果。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the mechanical behavior of additively manufactured porous Ti6Al4V structures for biomedical implants to avoid stress shielding and improve osseointegration.
Method· Three lattice configurations (regular, irregular, random) at three porosity levels were fabricated by selective laser melting, followed by morphological characterization and monotonic/cyclic tensile and compression tests.
Result· Manufacturing deviations caused strut thickness variations, and off-axis loading in compression led to asymmetry in Young's modulus between tension and compression.
Validation· Experimental mechanical data were compared with finite element simulations and validated using the Gibson-Ashby model for cellular structures.
