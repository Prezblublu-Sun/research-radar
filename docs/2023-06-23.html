Motivation· To compare hospital length of stay and discharge disposition between robotic-arm assisted and conventional total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Retrospective analysis of 1,732 primary THA patients with univariate and multivariate logistic regression for predictors of delayed discharge.
Result· Robotic-arm assistance was associated with a shorter median LOS (54 vs 60 hours, p<0.001), but discharge disposition was comparable between groups.
Validation· Single-institution retrospective study; results need confirmation from randomized controlled trials and long-term cost-effectiveness analyses.
