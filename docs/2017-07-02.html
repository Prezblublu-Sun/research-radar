相关性: 该论文专注于核反应堆物理中的不确定性量化，与生物力学或植入物设计无关。

相关性: 该论文涉及核反应堆中的不确定性分析，与生物力学或植入物设计无关。
边界: This paper focuses on nuclear reactor thermal-hydraulics, with no FEA surrogate modeling, no biomedical context, and no implant mechanics.
动机· 分析熔盐堆自然循环中峰值温度的不确定性。
方法· 耦合Dakota与GenFlow，采用LHS采样进行不确定性和敏感性分析。

Method· Coupling Dakota (UQ framework) with GenFlow (reactor code) using Latin hypercube sampling for sensitivity and uncertainty analysis.
Result· Power load factor is the most influential parameter; 95% confidence interval for peak temperature is [809.5, 816.3]°C.
Validation· Simulation-based validation using GenFlow code for pump coast-down accident scenario.

Naor Vardi, Elihu Anouchi, Tony Yamin, Srimanta Middey, Michael Kareev et al. arXiv preprint 2017-07-02 10.1002/adma.201605029
相关性: 该论文完全不涉及增材制造、生物医学植入物或力学性能，属于凝聚态物理领域。
边界: This paper studies memory effects in metal-insulator phase transitions of transition metal oxides, with no mention of additive manufacturing, biomedical implants, porous structures, mechanical testing, or fatigue.
动机· 研究过渡金属氧化物中金属-绝缘体相变的新记忆效应。
方法· 通过温度斜坡反转实验，结合理论建模分析。
结果· 发现斜坡反转记忆效应源于相边界在晶格中的“疤痕”。
验证· 实验观察与理论模型支持。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate a novel memory effect observed during metal-insulator phase transitions in transition metal oxides such as VO2 and NdNiO3.
Method· Temperature ramp reversal experiments were performed during the MIT, supported by theoretical modeling of lattice strain and phase separation.
Result· The ramp-reversal memory effect is attributed to 'scars' left by phase boundaries at the reversal temperature, locally increasing the transition temperature.
Validation· Experimental resistance measurements and theoretical modeling confirm the universality of the effect in multiple TMOs.
