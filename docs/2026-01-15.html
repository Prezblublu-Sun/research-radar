Five-Year Outcomes of Immediate Implant Placement in a Focal Cemento-Osseous Dysplasia Site: A Contralateral Comparison With the Socket Shield Technique.

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem pubmed Peripheral Ignore clinical Exp. validation Patient-specific
Wanqian Zheng, Yiyang Chen, Zhiyuan Li, Sheng Zheng The Journal of craniofacial surgery 2026-01-15 10.1097/SCS.0000000000012376
单位: School/Hospital of Stomatology, Zhejiang Chinese Medical University, Hangzhou, Zhejiang.
通讯: Sheng Zheng @ School/Hospital of Stomatology, Zhejiang Chinese Medical University, Hangzhou, Zhejiang.; Division for International Col [推断]
相关性: 与髋关节植入物和股骨柄研究方向无关，涉及牙科即刻种植。
边界: Does not involve hip implant, femoral stem, stress shielding, micromotion, or any femur biomechanics; purely dental clinical case.
动机· 比较局灶性牙骨质-骨异常增生位点与对侧牙槽盾技术即刻种植的长期效果。
方法· 对一名患者双侧前牙分别采用刮除后即刻种植和牙槽盾技术，随访5年。
结果· 两种方法均获得稳定骨整合，牙槽盾技术位点唇侧改建更少。
验证· 5年临床及CBCT影像学评估。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Evaluate the long-term outcomes of immediate implant placement in a focal cemento-osseous dysplasia site compared to the socket shield technique in the same patient.
Method· A 40-year-old female received immediate implant after curettage at #11 and socket shield technique at #21, both immediately provisionalized.
Result· Both implants showed stable osseointegration over 5 years, with less labial ridge remodeling at the socket shield site.
Validation· Clinical and CBCT evaluations at 5-year follow-up.
