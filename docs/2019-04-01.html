Is a modular dual mobility acetabulum a viable option for the young, active total hip arthroplasty patient?

D Nam, R Salih, C R Nahhas, R L Barrack, R M Nunley The bone & joint journal 2019-04-01 10.1302/0301-620X.101B4.BJJ-2018-0834.R1
单位: Rush University Medical Center, Chicago Illinois, USA.
通讯: R M Nunley @ Washington University Orthopedics, Barnes Jewish Hospital, St. Louis, Missouri, USA. [推断]
相关性: 该论文为临床结果研究，未涉及生物力学建模或有限元分析，与用户研究方向不直接相关。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, finite element analysis, stress shielding quantification, or micromotion analysis; purely clinical outcomes.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate clinical outcomes, metal ion levels, and periprosthetic BMD of a modular dual mobility acetabulum in young active total hip arthroplasty patients.
Method· A prospective study of 43 patients measured Harris Hip Scores, whole blood cobalt levels, and periprosthetic femoral BMD at one and two years postoperatively.
Result· Harris Hip Scores significantly improved, but mean cobalt levels increased and four patients had elevated levels at two years; BMD was maintained in most Gruen zones.
Validation· Clinical study with power analysis and mixed model repeated measures for statistical validation.
