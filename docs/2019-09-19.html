单位: State Key Laboratory of Advanced Design and Manufacturing for Vehicle Body, College of Mechanical and Vehicle Engineering, Hunan University, Changsha 410082, China
通讯: Lixiong Cao @ State Key Laboratory of Advanced Design and Manufacturing for Vehicle Body, College of Mechanical and Vehicle Engineerin

Validation· Validated on three numerical examples and two engineering applications.

边界: This paper applies GP to seabed logging for hydrocarbon depth estimation, not to FEA surrogate modeling, implant biomechanics, or any biomedical context.
