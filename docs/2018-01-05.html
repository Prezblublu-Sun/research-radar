Low-Cycle Fatigue Behaviour of AISI 18Ni300 Maraging Steel Produced by Selective Laser Melting

Ricardo Branco, J.D. Costa, F. Berto, Seyed Razavi, J.A.M. Ferreira et al. Metals 2018-01-05 10.3390/met8010032
单位: CEMMPRE, Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Coimbra, Rua Luís Reis Santos, 3030-788 Coimbra, Portugal
通讯: Ricardo Branco @ CEMMPRE, Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Coimbra, Rua Luís Reis Santos, 3030-788 Coimbra, Portugal
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM制造的马氏体钢的低周疲劳行为，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或生物医学植入物相关性能。
边界: No porous structure, no stress shielding or stiffness matching analysis, no biomedical context, and no surface roughness or osseointegration evaluation.
动机· 研究SLM制造马氏体钢的低周疲劳行为，填补该领域空白
方法· 进行应变控制低周疲劳试验，结合SEM断口分析
结果· 材料呈现非Masing行为，低周疲劳寿命短，裂纹源于表面和内部缺陷
验证· 通过疲劳试验和SEM断口观察验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To systematically investigate the cyclic plastic behavior of selective laser melted AISI 18Ni300 maraging steel under low-cycle fatigue conditions.
Method· Fully-reversed strain-controlled low-cycle fatigue tests were conducted with strain amplitudes from 0.3% to 1.0%, followed by SEM fractography.
Result· The material exhibited non-Masing behavior, slight strain-softening, and a very low transition life of about 35 reversals due to high strength and low ductility.
Validation· Experimental fatigue tests and SEM analysis of fracture surfaces.
