单位: Peter the Great Saint-Petersburg Polytechnic University
通讯: Anatoly Popovich @ Peter the Great Saint-Petersburg Polytechnic University
相关性: 该论文研究了选区激光熔化Ti-6Al-4V合金的微观结构和力学性能，但未涉及多孔结构设计、应力遮挡匹配或生物医学应用。
边界: This paper studies SLM-fabricated Ti-6Al-4V but does not address porous structures for stiffness matching, stress shielding, fatigue performance, surface roughness, or osseointegration relevant to biomedical implants.

通讯: Chunyan Wang @ Dalian Jiaotong University
相关性: 该论文研究了多孔钛的孔隙形态与应力分布，但未涉及LPBF制造、疲劳测试或骨整合，与用户方向关联度低。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing (only simulation), no fatigue, no surface roughness or osseointegration data.
动机· 研究多孔钛孔隙形态对应力分布的影响。
方法· 使用PMMA造孔剂制备多孔钛，并用Ansys模拟应力分布。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of pore morphology on stress distribution in porous titanium for biomedical implants.
Method· Porous titanium was fabricated using PMMA spacer particles, and stress distribution was simulated via finite element analysis in Ansys.
Result· Stress concentrates around interconnected pores and weak cell walls; stress concentration factor increases with porosity and pore size.
Validation· The study relies solely on finite element simulation without experimental validation.
