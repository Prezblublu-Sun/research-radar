Four-Year Results of Summit Stems for Total Hip Arthroplasty in Japanese Patients

Low Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Background only clinical Exp. validation
Tomonori Tetsunaga, Tomonori Tetsunaga, Tôru Satô, Naofumi Shiota, Tomoko Tetsunaga et al. Hip International 2015-09-09 10.5301/hipint.5000287
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Okayama Medical Centre, Kitaku, Okayama - Japan
通讯: Tomonori Tetsunaga @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Okayama Medical Centre, Kitaku, Okayama - Japan  |  Tomoko Tetsunaga @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Okayama Medical Centre, Kitaku, Okayama - Japan
相关性: 该论文报告了Summit股骨柄在日本人患者中的临床与影像学结果，涉及应力遮挡和骨整合，但未进行有限元分析或应力/微动的定量测量。
边界: This paper is a clinical outcome study without any biomechanical modeling, FEA, or quantitative stress/strain/micromotion data in Gruen zones.
动机· 评估Summit股骨柄在日本人患者中至少4年的临床与影像学结果。
方法· 对84例患者87髋行Summit柄THA，随访平均52个月，分析X线片和临床评分。
结果· 80髋中60髋（75%）显示骨整合，应力遮挡发生率62.5%，15%为三度遮挡。
验证· 回顾性临床研究，通过X线片评估骨整合与应力遮挡分级。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the clinical and radiographic outcome of Summit femoral stems in Japanese patients with a minimum follow-up of 4 years.
Method· A retrospective study of 87 hips in 84 patients undergoing Summit stem THA, with radiographic analysis for osseointegration and stress shielding.
Result· Osseointegration was observed in 75% of hips, and stress shielding occurred in 62.5%, with 15% classified as third-degree.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with radiographs at a mean of 52 months, using Gruen zone analysis for osseointegration and Engh's classification for stress shielding.

通讯: Michael A. Mont @ Center for Joint Preservation and Replacement, Rubin Institute for Advanced Orthopedics, Sinai Hospital of Baltimore, Ba

边界: No biomechanical modeling, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no FEA, no implant design or contact mechanics.
动机· 评估双活动杯在翻修THA中降低脱位的效果

通讯: Brent A. Lanting @ Division of Orthopaedic Surgery, Department of Surgery, Schulich School of Medicine and Dentistry, London, Ontario - Can
相关性: 该论文仅涉及全髋关节置换术的成本分析，未涉及任何生物力学建模或植入物设计。
边界: This paper is a clinical cost analysis with no biomechanics modeling, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no FEA, and no implant design or validation.
动机· 比较单次双侧与分次双侧全髋关节置换术的成本差异。
方法· 回顾性分析24例患者，从医院和卫生部角度进行成本分析。
结果· 单次双侧组平均成本显著低于分次双侧组，节省主要来自手术室人员及准备费用。
验证· 无生物力学验证，仅统计成本数据。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the cost savings and perioperative complications of single-stage bilateral versus two-stage direct anterior total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective cost analysis from hospital and Ministry of Health perspectives was performed on 24 patients undergoing either single-stage or two-stage bilateral DAA THA.
Result· Single-stage bilateral THA resulted in significant cost savings, primarily from reduced operating room staff and setup costs, with no difference in complication rates.
Validation· No biomechanical or experimental validation; only statistical comparison of clinical cost data.
