Templating digital radiographs using acetate templates in Total Hip Arthroplasty

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore clinical Exp. validation
Rajamani Perumal, Vignesh P. Krishnamoorthy, Pradeep Mathew Poonnoose Journal of Arthroscopy and Joint Surgery 2017-08-19 10.1016/j.jajs.2017.08.004
单位: Department of Orthopaedics-Unit II, Christian Medical College, Vellore, Tamil Nadu 632004, India
通讯: Pradeep Mathew Poonnoose @ Department of Orthopaedics-Unit II, Christian Medical College, Vellore, Tamil Nadu 632004, India
相关性: 该论文仅涉及术前模板测量技术，未涉及任何生物力学分析或应力遮挡评估。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, no FEA, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no implant-bone interface analysis.
动机· 传统模板在数字化X光片上难以使用，需低成本替代方法。
方法· 将金属球标记放大至30mm，用醋酸模板在LCD屏幕上测量假体尺寸。
结果· 髋臼杯准确预测率70%，股骨柄44%，可靠性良好。
验证· 54例髋关节回顾性研究，与手术实际尺寸比较。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a low-cost hybrid technique for preoperative templating in THA using acetate templates on digital radiographs without specialized software.
Method· A 30mm metal ball taped to the greater trochanter was used to calibrate magnification, and standard acetate templates were overlaid on digital radiographs displayed on an LCD monitor to estimate implant sizes.
Result· Acetabular cup size was accurately predicted in 70% of hips and within one size in 96%; femoral stem size was accurately predicted in 44% and within one size in 82%, with good intra- and interobserver reliability.
Validation· Retrospective analysis of 54 hips comparing templated sizes to actual implanted sizes, with ICC for reliability.
