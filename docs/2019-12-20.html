Result· Clinical improvement with average Harris score of 77; no infections; one asymptomatic intraprosthetic dislocation observed.
Validation· Clinical evaluation and radiographic assessment including Gruen zone analysis for osseointegration.

Motivation· To report a rare case of neglected traumatic hip dislocation with posterior wall acetabular fracture managed with total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Uncemented total hip arthroplasty with posterior acetabular wall reconstruction was performed.
Result· Harris hip score improved from 46 to 82 and limb length discrepancy reduced from 7 cm to 1 cm postoperatively.
Validation· Clinical follow-up assessed functional outcomes and pain relief.
