英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare patient characteristics between those who underwent THA and those continuing conservative treatment after femoral head collapse.
Method· Retrospective analysis of 70 patients with post-collapse ONFH, comparing demographic, clinical, and radiographic data.
Result· Patients continuing conservative treatment had higher rates of prior core decompression and less superomedial collapse.
Validation· No biomechanical validation; purely clinical statistical comparison.
