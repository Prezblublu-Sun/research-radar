单位: Central University of Technology, Free State
通讯: Ina Yadroitsava @ Central University of Technology, Free State
相关性: 该论文研究SLM Ti6Al4V合金的残余应力，未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能，对生物医用植入物方向的参考价值有限。
边界: No porous structure, no stress shielding analysis, no fatigue testing, no osseointegration or surface morphology characterization.
动机· 研究SLM Ti6Al4V合金的残余应力分布
方法· 采用X射线衍射和悬臂梁弯曲实验测量残余应力
结果· 近顶面存在拉伸残余应力，扫描方向应力约为垂直方向的两倍
验证· 通过实验测量与数值模拟对比验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To characterize residual stress in SLM Ti6Al4V alloy specimens using X-ray diffraction and cantilever bending tests.
Method· X-ray diffraction measured residual stresses in rectangular specimens and cantilevers; numerical simulation was performed for comparison.
Result· Tensile residual stresses were found near the top surface, with stress along the laser scanning direction twice that perpendicular to it.
Validation· Experimental measurements were validated against numerical simulation and cantilever bending before and after heat treatment.

边界: No LPBF fabrication, no porous structure, no mechanical testing for biomedical implants, no stress shielding or osseointegration.

方法· 等温单轴压缩实验获取应力应变数据并拟合模型

验证· 与实验数据对比验证模型准确性
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a flow stress model specific to hot extrusion of Ti-6Al-4V for reducing twisting defects and improving yield.

Validation· The model was validated by comparing predicted flow stress with experimental data from compression tests.
