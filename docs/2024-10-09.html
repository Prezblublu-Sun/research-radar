Motivation· Fine-tuned large language models tend to become overconfident under limited adaptation data, necessitating calibrated uncertainty quantification.
Method· Functional-level uncertainty quantification is achieved via a mixture-of-experts fine-tuning framework that aligns confidence with correctness through a calibration loss.

Validation· Validation is performed on four multiple-choice benchmarks and two open-ended generative QA tasks, assessing calibration and accuracy both in-distribution and under distribution shift.
