边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, FEA surrogate modeling for biomedical applications, or contact mechanics; it focuses on aerodynamic analysis of compressor blades.
动机· 高效评估制造变异性对压气机叶片气动性能的影响。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To efficiently and accurately evaluate the impact of manufacturing variability on the aerodynamics of compressor blades for robust design.
Method· A quadratic curve approximation combined with Karhunen-Loève expansion models manufacturing variability as a truncated Normal process, and SAMBA PC with CFD is used for stochastic aerodynamic analysis.
Result· The SAMBA PC method outperforms Monte Carlo simulation in efficiency; uncertainty quantification shows that aerodynamic loss fluctuations are mainly due to separation loss after the suction peak and boundary-layer loss on the suction surface.
Validation· Numerical simulation of a high subsonic compressor cascade under design incidence validates the framework.
