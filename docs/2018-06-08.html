Motivation· To investigate whether low birth weight and preterm birth are risk factors for hip osteoarthritis requiring total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Systematic review of three databases with qualitative synthesis and economic modeling using Australian national data.
Result· Low birth weight and preterm birth are causally associated with hip osteoarthritis, contributing to a substantial number of total hip arthroplasties and associated costs.
Validation· Quality assessment using NHLBI tool and causal inference using Bradford Hill criteria.
