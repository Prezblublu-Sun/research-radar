Finite Element Modeling of Selective Laser Melting 316L Stainless Steel Parts for Evaluating the Mechanical Properties

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Transferable Skim only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Arman Ahmadi, Narges Shayesteh Moghaddam, Mohammad Elahinia, H.E. Karaca, Reza Mirzaeifar Volume 2: Materials; Biomanufacturing; Properties, Applications and Systems; Sustainable Manufacturing 2016-06-27 10.1115/msec2016-8594
单位: The University of Toledo, Toledo, OH
通讯: Arman Ahmadi @ The University of Toledo, Toledo, OH
相关性: 该论文研究激光选区熔融316L不锈钢的力学性能建模与验证，虽不直接针对生物医学植入物，但其工艺-结构-性能关系方法对生物医学AM材料研究有参考价值
边界: Does not address porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration relevant to biomedical implants
动机· 建立考虑晶界和熔池边界的SLM材料力学响应计算框架
方法· 采用内聚区方法将熔池近似为重叠圆柱，结合各向异性弹性和晶体塑性本构
结果· 模型能预测晶粒尺寸、熔池尺寸和连通性对宏观力学性能的影响
验证· 通过SLM制备316L狗骨试样进行拉伸实验验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a computational framework that accounts for grain boundaries and melt pool boundaries in predicting the mechanical response of selective laser melted materials.
Method· A 3D finite element model is constructed with melt pools approximated as overlapping cylinders, using cohesive zone interfaces for both grain and melt pool boundaries, and anisotropic elasticity with crystal plasticity for intragranular behavior.
Result· The model effectively captures the influence of grain size, melt pool size, and pool connectivity on the macroscopic mechanical properties of SLM-produced 316L stainless steel.
Validation· Tensile tests on SLM-fabricated dog bone specimens with varying scan speeds are used to validate the finite element predictions.

Validation· Material properties were calibrated using experimental data from NiTi bulk samples, but the FEA results were not experimentally validated.
