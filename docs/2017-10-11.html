相关性: 该论文研究了增材制造的多孔结构用于初始固定，与应力遮挡和骨整合间接相关。
边界: Does not quantify stress shielding or stiffness matching, and lacks fatigue testing and surface roughness characterization.
动机· 探索增材制造如何改善植入物的初始固定。

英文摘要与术语

Result· The barbed design achieved over double the pull-out force with less than one-third the insertion force compared to conventional press-fit pegs.
Validation· Validation included synthetic bone optimization and subsequent cadaveric testing.
