Validation· Ex vivo animal bone experiments confirmed the generation of a clean, smooth osteotomy surface with minimal thermal damage.

Is There Still a Place for Threaded Spherical Acetabular Components in Modern Arthroplasty? Observations Based on an Average 14-Year Follow-Up.

Marek Drobniewski, Bartosz Gonera, Łukasz Olewnik, Adam Borowski, Kacper Ruzik et al. Journal of clinical medicine 2025-05-24 10.3390/jcm14113683
单位: Clinic of Orthopaedic and Paediatric Orthopaedics, Medical University of Lodz, 90-419 Łódź, Poland.
通讯: Andrzej Borowski @ Clinic of Orthopaedic and Paediatric Orthopaedics, Medical University of Lodz, 90-419 Łódź, Poland. [推断]
相关性: 该论文是临床随访研究，未涉及股骨近端应力遮挡或微动的生物力学建模。
边界: No biomechanical modeling, no stress/strain quantification in Gruen zones, no micromotion analysis, no FE simulation, and no comparison of intact vs implanted femur.
动机· 评估球形螺旋髋臼杯在THA中的长期临床效果。
方法· 回顾性分析277例使用该假体的患者，平均随访13.8年。
结果· 假体5年和10年存活率分别为98.3%和94.0%。
验证· 临床评分与影像学随访。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the long-term clinical and radiographic outcomes of cementless THA using a threaded spherical acetabular cup.
Method· Retrospective analysis of 277 patients (293 hips) with a mean 13.8-year follow-up, assessing MAP and VAS scores and implant survival.
Result· The five- and ten-year survival rates were 98.3% and 94.0%, with aseptic loosening being the main revision cause.
Validation· Clinical evaluation and radiographic classification at final follow-up.
