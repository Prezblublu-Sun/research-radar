通讯: Christian Schulze @ Biomechanics and Implant Technology Research Laboratory, Department of Orthopaedics, University Medicine Rostock, Rostoc

边界: This paper focuses on acetabular cup deformation and primary stability, not on femoral stem stress shielding, micromotion, or proximal femur mechanics.
