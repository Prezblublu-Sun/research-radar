Optimising β-Ti21S Alloy Lattice Structures for Enhanced Femoral Implants: A Study on Mechanical and Biological Performance.

High AM Biomedical Materials pubmed Direct Read now mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Lorena Emanuelli, Melika Babaei, Raffaele De Biasi, Anton du Plessis, Andrea Trivisonno et al. Materials (Basel, Switzerland) 2025-01-03 10.3390/ma18010170
单位: INSTM Operative Center, University of Trento, 38122 Trento, Italy.; Department of Industrial Engineering, University of Trento, 38123 Trento, Italy.
通讯: Massimo Pellizzari @ Department of Industrial Engineering, University of Trento, 38123 Trento, Italy. [推断]
相关性: 该论文直接研究了β-Ti21S合金多孔结构在股骨植入物中的应用，涉及应力遮挡、LPBF制造、力学测试和生物学性能，与用户方向高度相关。
边界: The paper does not include fatigue testing for all structures (only TPMS 2.5 and AUX 3.5 have fatigue data) and lacks surface roughness or as-built morphology characterization.
动机· 为减少股骨植入物应力遮挡，探索β-Ti21S合金多孔结构。
方法· 通过LPBF制造四种多孔结构，进行准静态力学和生物学测试。
结果· TPMS 2.5结构在力学和生物学性能上表现最佳，但TPMS 1.5可打印性差。
验证· 微CT、准静态压缩、疲劳测试及细胞毒性实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To mitigate stress shielding in femoral implants, this study investigates the integration of lattice structures made from β-Ti21S alloy.
Method· Four lattice designs (auxetic bow-tie and TPMS) were fabricated via laser powder bed fusion and subjected to quasi-static mechanical and biological tests.
Result· The TPMS 2.5 structure exhibited the best combination of mechanical and biological properties, while TPMS 1.5 showed poor printability.
Validation· Validation included micro-CT imaging, quasi-static compression, fatigue testing, and in vitro cytotoxicity assays.

Fusion-DeepONet: A Data-Efficient Neural Operator for Geometry-Dependent Hypersonic and Supersonic Flows

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark
Ahmad Peyvan, Varun Kumar, George Em Karniadakis arXiv preprint 2025-01-03 link
相关性: 该论文提出了一种数据高效的神经算子Fusion-DeepONet，用于几何依赖的流场预测，其方法可迁移至生物力学中植入物几何相关的有限元代理建模。
边界: This paper focuses on hypersonic/supersonic flows in aerospace, not on biomedical implant mechanics, FEA surrogate for stress/strain, or uncertainty quantification.
动机· 加速航空航天外形优化中几何依赖流场的预测
方法· 提出Fusion-DeepONet，融合几何与工况参数映射至流场，并引入导数增强损失
结果· 在均匀网格上精度与U-Net相当，在不规则网格上优于MeshGraphNet和Vanilla-DeepONet
验证· 基于高保真DGSEM模拟数据，在三个超声速/高超声速问题上验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To accelerate shape optimization in aerospace by developing a data-efficient surrogate for geometry-dependent hypersonic and supersonic flows.
Method· Fusion-DeepONet is proposed, which takes geometry and flow parameters as inputs to predict flow fields, with a derivative-enhanced loss for accurate spatial derivatives.
Result· Fusion-DeepONet achieves comparable accuracy to U-Net on uniform grids and outperforms MeshGraphNet and Vanilla-DeepONet on irregular grids, with fewer trainable parameters.
Validation· Validated on three high-fidelity simulation datasets (inviscid hypersonic flow, reentry capsule, converging-diverging nozzle) generated by a high-order entropy-stable DGSEM solver.

Active Learning Enables Extrapolation in Molecular Generative Models

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Background only numerical_benchmark
Evan R. Antoniuk, Peggy Li, Nathan Keilbart, Stephen Weitzner, Bhavya Kailkhura et al. arXiv preprint 2025-01-03 link
相关性: 该论文关注分子生成模型中的主动学习外推，与生物力学有限元代理建模无直接关联。
边界: No FEA surrogate modeling, no implant/biomechanics context, no constraint handling or uncertainty quantification for structural optimization.
动机· 分子生成模型难以提出超越训练数据的改进分子。
方法· 构建主动学习闭环，迭代优化分子生成模型与量子化学模拟。
结果· 主动学习生成分子性质外推至训练数据范围外0.44标准差，稳定分子比例提升3.5倍。
验证· 在分子性质预测和生成任务上对比多种基线模型。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Generative models for molecular discovery often fail to propose molecules that improve upon known training data due to poor generalization of property predictors.
Method· An active-learning closed-loop pipeline iteratively refines molecular generative models using feedback from quantum chemical simulations to improve generalization.
Result· The active learning approach generates molecules with properties extrapolating 0.44 standard deviations beyond training data and improves out-of-distribution classification accuracy by 79%.
Validation· Benchmarked against multiple generative models on molecular property prediction and generation tasks.

Result· NLP shows potential in identifying safety issues, but challenges remain in data annotation and model interpretability.
