Generalizing Transfer Bayesian Optimization to Source-Target Heterogeneity

单位: School of Computer Science and Engineering, Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore
通讯: Alan Tan Wei Min @ School of Computer Science and Engineering, Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore
相关性: 该论文提出了一种可处理源任务与目标任务特征维度不同的迁移贝叶斯优化方法，可应用于昂贵仿真优化问题，但未涉及生物力学或植入物设计。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification beyond BO, or biomechanics/implant design context.

结果· 在合成函数和工程设计中验证了算法有效性，加速了优化过程。
验证· 通过数值基准测试和实际工程案例验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Existing transfer Bayesian optimization methods assume identical feature spaces between source and target tasks, limiting their applicability to real-world problems where feature dimensions may differ.
Method· A generalized transfer BO algorithm is proposed that jointly transforms source features while training probabilistic regression models for the target task, accommodating changes in feature dimensionality.
Result· The method demonstrates significant performance improvements on synthetic benchmarks and real-world engineering design problems, enabling efficient optimization with heterogeneous source-target tasks.
Validation· Validation is performed using numerical benchmark functions and realistic examples from engineering design and machine learning configuration.
