单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Nelson Mandela University, Port Elizabeth 6001, South Africa
通讯: Anton du Plessis @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Nelson Mandela University, Port Elizabeth 6001, South Africa
相关性: 该论文直接研究了LPBF制造的gyroid点阵结构的微孔率对力学性能（包括疲劳）的影响，与用户关注的AM生物医学植入物方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not explicitly link porous structure to stress shielding or stiffness matching for biomedical implants, nor does it report surface roughness, as-built morphology, or osseointegration.
动机· 研究LPBF制造的点阵结构中微孔率对力学性能的影响
方法· 制备不同微孔分布的gyroid点阵样品，进行静态压缩和疲劳测试
结果· 少量锁孔型微孔（~0.2%）影响不大，而熔合不足型微孔是关键，尤其当热等静压无法有效闭合时
验证· X射线断层扫描验证微孔分布，对比应力消除态和热等静压态
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of microporosity induced by laser powder bed fusion on the mechanical performance of gyroid lattice structures.
Method· Gyroid lattice samples with different microporosity distributions were fabricated via LPBF and tested under static compression and compression-compression fatigue.
Result· Keyhole porosity of ~0.2% had negligible effect, while lack-of-fusion porosity was critical, especially when hot isostatic pressing failed to close pores.
Validation· Microporosity distributions were validated by X-ray tomography, and samples were tested in both stress-relieved and hot isostatic pressed conditions.
