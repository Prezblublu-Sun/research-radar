边界: This paper focuses on telemedicine reliability for hip physical exams, with no biomechanical modeling, no stress shielding quantification, no micromotion analysis, and no FEA or implant mechanics.
动机· 评估远程医疗进行髋关节体格检查的可靠性
方法· 对50名患者100个髋关节进行远程与面对面检查的观察者间和观察者内可靠性分析
结果· 髋关节活动度和TUG测试可靠性良好，但肌肉营养和Trendelenburg测试可靠性低

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the reliability of hip physical examinations conducted via telemedicine compared to in-person assessments.
Method· Intra- and inter-observer reliability analysis was performed on 100 hips from 50 patients across osteoarthritis, THA, and normal groups using standardized video guidance.
Result· Hip range of motion and TUG test showed good to excellent reliability, while muscle trophism and Trendelenburg test had low reliability.
Validation· Reliability was assessed using kappa statistics and ICC, with a standardized instructional video to improve consistency.
