英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the clinical and radiographic outcomes of a modular uncemented stem with proximal fixation and distal anchorage in revision total hip arthroplasty for Paprosky II and IIIA femoral bone defects.
Method· A retrospective analysis of 22 revision cases using the S-ROM stem, with mean follow-up of 62 months, assessing Harris Hip Score, radiographic integration, offset restoration, and limb length discrepancy.
Result· Offset was restored in 72.3% of cases, limb length in 68.2%, bone union in 77.27%, and the complication rate was 31.8% including dislocations, fractures, and nerve palsy.
Validation· Clinical and radiographic evaluation without biomechanical testing or finite element analysis.
