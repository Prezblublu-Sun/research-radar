Lattice Microarchitecture for Bone Tissue Engineering from Calcium Phosphate Compared to Titanium

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Transferable Save for project animal Exp. validation
Tse-Hsiang Chen, Chafik Ghayor, Barbara Siegenthaler, Felix Schuler, Jasmine Rüegg et al. Tissue Engineering Part A 2018-07-12 10.1089/ten.tea.2018.0014
单位: University of Zurich
通讯: Franz E. Weber @ University of Zurich
相关性: 该论文比较了钙磷陶瓷与钛合金支架的骨再生效果，与AM生物医学材料方向相关，但未涉及应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper does not directly address stress shielding, stiffness matching, fatigue performance, or surface roughness characterization of AM biomedical implants.
动机· 比较钙磷陶瓷与钛合金在骨组织工程中的性能差异。
方法· 使用相同CAD设计的TCP（陶瓷光刻）和钛（激光烧结）支架，植入兔颅骨缺损。
结果· 非临界缺损中两者骨再生相似，TCP加BMP-2后更优；临界缺损中钛由于机械稳定性更优。
验证· 兔颅骨体内植入4周后的组织学评估。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the in vivo bone regeneration performance of calcium phosphate and titanium lattice scaffolds with identical microarchitecture for bone tissue engineering.
Method· TCP scaffolds were fabricated via lithography-based ceramic manufacturing and titanium scaffolds via laser sintering, both with identical CAD-designed lattice geometry, and implanted in noncritical- and critical-size rabbit calvarial defects.
Result· In noncritical defects, both scaffolds performed equally, and TCP with BMP-2 showed improved bone regeneration; in critical defects, titanium outperformed TCP due to superior mechanical stability.
Validation· In vivo rabbit calvarial defect model with histomorphometric analysis after 4 weeks.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce the labor-intensive acquisition of training samples for supervised land cover classification.
Method· A novel method combining segmentation (adapted SLIC), hierarchical clustering (bisecting k-means), and active learning (probabilistic active learning with local density).
Result· Achieves the same overall accuracy while reducing required training samples by 95%.
Validation· Evaluated on three aerial remote sensing datasets (Abenberg, Potsdam, Vaihingen) and compared with three state-of-the-art methods.
