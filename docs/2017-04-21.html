单位: Sandia National Laboratories PO Box 5800, Albuquerque 81785 NM USA
通讯: Brad Boyce @ Sandia National Laboratories PO Box 5800, Albuquerque 81785 NM USA
相关性: 该论文研究了LPBF制造中的缺陷统计，但未涉及生物医用植入物的具体问题如应力屏蔽或疲劳。
边界: Does not address porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration relevant to biomedical implants.
动机· 揭示增材制造中罕见缺陷对力学性能的影响

Result· Rare defects, associated with interconnected porosity, caused a substantial ductility loss in approximately 2% of specimens.
Validation· The approach was validated experimentally through systematic tensile testing of a large population of nominally identical specimens.

Time Series Prediction for Graphs in Kernel and Dissimilarity Spaces

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Background only numerical_benchmark
Benjamin Paaßen, Christina Göpfert, Barbara Hammer arXiv preprint 2017-04-21 10.1007/s11063-017-9684-5
相关性: 该论文研究图结构的时间序列预测，与FEA代理模型或生物力学设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address expensive simulation sampling, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification, or implant/biomechanics design.
动机· 预测图结构整体未来状态，而非单边或节点。
方法· 将时间序列预测转化为回归问题，使用图核和伪欧几里得嵌入。
结果· 简单回归方法适用于理论模型，高斯过程回归对真实数据预测误差更优。
验证· 在理论模型和智能辅导系统真实数据集上验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To predict the future state of an entire graph as a whole, rather than focusing on individual edges or nodes.
Method· Formulates time series prediction as a regression problem using dissimilarity- or kernel-based methods (e.g., 1-NN, kernel regression, Gaussian process) with graph kernels and pseudo-Euclidean embedding.
Result· Simple regression methods suffice for theoretical graph evolution models, while Gaussian process regression significantly improves prediction error on real-world data.
Validation· Evaluated on two theoretical graph evolution models and two real datasets from intelligent tutoring systems.
