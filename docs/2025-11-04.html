英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine the success rate of two-stage total hip arthroplasty exchange for periprosthetic joint infection and identify risk factors for reinfection.
Method· A prospective cohort study of 187 patients with Kaplan-Meier survival analysis and Cox proportional hazards regression.
Result· Overall success rate was 85.6%; difficult-to-treat pathogens and prior failed two-stage exchange were independent predictors of reinfection.
Validation· Clinical validation with mean follow-up of 54.2 months.
