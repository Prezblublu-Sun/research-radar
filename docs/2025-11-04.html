EVALUATION OF FEMORAL, ACETABULAR, AND GLOBAL OFFSET RESTORATION FOLLOWING HIP SPACER IMPLANTATION DURING STAGED TOTAL HIP ARTHROPLASTY REVISION

Luca Cavagnaro, Valentina Providenti, Emilio Ferrari Orthopaedic Proceedings 2025-11-04 10.1302/1358-992x.2025.12.088

English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the restoration of femoral, acetabular, and global offset following hip spacer implantation in two-stage revision THA for chronic PJI.
Method· A retrospective analysis of radiographic measurements from 40 patients with chronic PJI undergoing two-stage revision with articulating hip spacers.
Result· Optimal offset restoration was associated with improved functional scores (HHS and OHS), though complete restoration was rarely achieved, especially in cases with severe bone loss or varus morphology.
Validation· Clinical retrospective study with standardized anteroposterior pelvic radiographs and correlation with functional outcomes.
