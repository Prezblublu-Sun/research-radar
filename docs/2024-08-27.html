方法· 将时间域从神经网络中解耦，构造闭式梯度解析解。

Method· A domain-decoupled PINN (DD-PINN) is proposed that separates the time domain from the feedforward neural network, enabling closed-form gradient computation instead of automatic differentiation.

Validation· The method is validated on three numerical benchmarks: a nonlinear mass-spring-damper, a five-mass chain, and a two-link robot system.
