Assessing the compressive and tensile properties of TPMS-Gyroid and stochastic Ti64 lattice structures: A study on laser powder bed fusion manufacturing for biomedical implants

Miguel Araya, Matias Jaskari, Timo Rautio, Teodolíto Guillén-Girón, Antti Järvenpää Journal of Science Advanced Materials and Devices 2023-12-12 10.1016/j.jsamd.2023.100663
单位: Bio-inspired Processes and Materials Research Group, Instituto Tecnológico de Costa Rica, Cartago, 30101, Costa Rica
通讯: Miguel Araya @ Bio-inspired Processes and Materials Research Group, Instituto Tecnológico de Costa Rica, Cartago, 30101, Costa Rica
相关性: 该论文直接针对LPBF制造的Ti64点阵结构用于骨科植入物，评估了其力学性能与人体骨的匹配性，以减轻应力遮挡。
边界: This paper does not report fatigue testing or osseointegration data, focusing only on static compressive and tensile properties.
动机· 评估Ti64点阵结构的力学性能，以减轻骨科植入物的应力遮挡效应。
方法· 采用LPBF制备TPMS Gyroid和随机Voronoi点阵，通过两种应力计算方法及SEM/DIC进行表征。
结果· 两种点阵在孔隙率0.1–0.5范围内表现出与人体骨相似的力学性能，有助于延长植入物寿命。
验证· 通过压缩/拉伸力学测试、SEM形貌和DIC应变场分析验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To assess the mechanical properties of Ti64 lattice structures for orthopedic implants to mitigate stress shielding.
Method· TPMS Gyroid and stochastic Voronoi lattices were fabricated by laser powder bed fusion and characterized using two stress calculation methods, SEM, and DIC.
Result· Both lattice types exhibited elastic modulus and yield stress comparable to human bone within a relative density range of 0.1–0.5, suggesting potential to reduce stress shielding.
Validation· Mechanical testing (compression and tension) combined with SEM morphology and digital image correlation strain mapping validated the structural performance.
