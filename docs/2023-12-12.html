Assessing the compressive and tensile properties of TPMS-Gyroid and stochastic Ti64 lattice structures: A study on laser powder bed fusion manufacturing for biomedical implants

Miguel Araya, Matias Jaskari, Timo Rautio, Teodolíto Guillén-Girón, Antti Järvenpää Journal of Science Advanced Materials and Devices 2023-12-12 10.1016/j.jsamd.2023.100663
单位: Bio-inspired Processes and Materials Research Group, Instituto Tecnológico de Costa Rica, Cartago, 30101, Costa Rica
通讯: Miguel Araya @ Bio-inspired Processes and Materials Research Group, Instituto Tecnológico de Costa Rica, Cartago, 30101, Costa Rica
相关性: 该论文直接针对LPBF制造的Ti64点阵结构用于骨科植入物，评估了其力学性能与人体骨的匹配性，以减轻应力遮挡。
边界: This paper does not report fatigue testing or osseointegration data, focusing only on static compressive and tensile properties.
动机· 评估Ti64点阵结构的力学性能，以减轻骨科植入物的应力遮挡效应。
方法· 采用LPBF制备TPMS Gyroid和随机Voronoi点阵，通过两种应力计算方法及SEM/DIC进行表征。
结果· 两种点阵在孔隙率0.1–0.5范围内表现出与人体骨相似的力学性能，有助于延长植入物寿命。
验证· 通过压缩/拉伸力学测试、SEM形貌和DIC应变场分析验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To assess the mechanical properties of Ti64 lattice structures for orthopedic implants to mitigate stress shielding.
Method· TPMS Gyroid and stochastic Voronoi lattices were fabricated by laser powder bed fusion and characterized using two stress calculation methods, SEM, and DIC.
Result· Both lattice types exhibited elastic modulus and yield stress comparable to human bone within a relative density range of 0.1–0.5, suggesting potential to reduce stress shielding.
Validation· Mechanical testing (compression and tension) combined with SEM morphology and digital image correlation strain mapping validated the structural performance.

Design and Compression Behavior Exploration of Skeletal and Sheet Triply Periodic Minimal Surface Structures

Yadong Li, Bin Liu, Zhonghua Li, Zezhou Kuai, Wenhua Du et al. Advanced Engineering Materials 2023-12-12 10.1002/adem.202301589
单位: College of Materials Science and Engineering North University of China 3 Xueyuan Road Taiyuan 030051 China
通讯: Bin Liu @ College of Materials Science and Engineering North University of China 3 Xueyuan Road Taiyuan 030051 China
相关性: 本文研究了TPMS多孔结构的压缩性能及LPBF制造，与生物医学植入物的力学性能相关，但未涉及疲劳或应力屏蔽。
边界: No fatigue testing, no surface roughness or osseointegration analysis, and no direct link to stress shielding or implant stiffness matching.
动机· 探索TPMS多孔结构的压缩性能，为工程制造提供高性能超材料。
方法· 参数化设计六种TPMS结构，用Ti6Al4V粉末激光熔融制备不同相对密度样品并进行静态压缩测试。
结果· 相同密度下片状结构力学性能优于骨架结构，片状金刚石结构最优。
验证· 通过压缩实验验证力学性能差异，分析变形模式。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the compressive behavior of various TPMS structures for potential use as high-performance porous materials in engineering manufacturing.
Method· Six TPMS designs (skeletal and sheet types) were parametrically designed, fabricated via selective laser melting in Ti6Al4V at four relative densities, and subjected to static compression tests.
Result· Sheet structures exhibited superior mechanical properties over skeletal ones at the same relative density, with sheet diamond showing the best performance.
Validation· Experimental compression tests and deformation analysis validated the mechanical performance differences and failure modes.

单位: Department of Orthopaedic Medical Engineering Osaka University Graduate School of Medicine Suita Osaka Japan
通讯: Nobuhiko Sugano @ Department of Orthopaedic Medical Engineering Osaka University Graduate School of Medicine Suita Osaka Japan

边界: Does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics; no FEA of Gruen zones or intact vs implanted femur comparison.

Result· Increasing the required internal rotation angle reduced the safe zone, and optimal stem anteversion depended on head size and required internal rotation.
Validation· The study used computer simulation without experimental or clinical validation.

Biochemical Evaluation of the Effects of Concentrated Growth Factor Liquid on Osseointegration: A Split-Mouth Design Study.

Muhammet Atilgan, Osman Fatih Arpag, Oğuzhan Özcan, Filiz Kaçmaz The International journal of oral & maxillofacial implants 2023-12-12 10.11607/jomi.10066
相关性: 该论文涉及临床牙种植体骨整合的生化评估，未涉及增材制造、多孔结构或力学测试。

动机· 评估浓缩生长因子液对牙种植体骨整合的生化影响。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the biochemical effects of concentrated growth factor liquid on osseointegration of dental implants.
Method· A split-mouth design was used with 40 mandibular implants in 20 patients; one side received CGF liquid while the other served as control.
Result· CGF liquid improved implant stability at 12 weeks but did not significantly affect marginal bone loss.
Validation· Clinical validation was performed via cytokine analysis of peri-implant crevicular fluid and resonance frequency analysis.
