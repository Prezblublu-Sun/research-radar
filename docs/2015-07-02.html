Motivation· To improve detection sensitivity of methicillin-resistant strains in orthopaedic infections.
Method· A modified real-time PCR method with centrifugation enrichment was used to detect the mecA gene.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a noncontact method for measuring ciliary beat frequency that can be translated to in vivo clinical use.
Method· Doppler optical coherence tomography (D-OCT) was applied to ex vivo rabbit tracheal mucosa to detect ciliary motion via Doppler signal.
Result· Ciliary beat frequency measured by D-OCT (9.36 ± 1.22 Hz) showed no significant difference from phase-contrast microscopy (9.08 ± 0.48 Hz).
Validation· Validation was performed by comparing D-OCT measurements with high-speed digital video phase-contrast microscopy.
