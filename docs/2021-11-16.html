Enhanced osseointegration through direct energy deposition porous coating for cementless orthopedic implant fixation.

Dong Jin Ryu, Ara Jung, Hun Yeong Ban, Tae Yang Kwak, Eun Joo Shin et al. Scientific reports 2021-11-16 10.1038/s41598-021-01739-9
单位: Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Inha University Hospital, 27 Inhang-Ro, Jung-Gu, Incheon, 22332, South Korea.; Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Samsung Medical Center, Sungkyunkwan University Schoo
通讯: Bomi Gweon @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Sejong University, 209 Neungdong-ro, Gwangjin-Gu, Seoul, 05006, South Korea. bgweo  |  Dohyung Lim @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Sejong University, 209 Neungdong-ro, Gwangjin-Gu, Seoul, 05006, South Korea. dli34  |  Joon Ho Wang @ Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Samsung Medical Center, Sungkyunkwan University School of Medicine, 81 Irwon-Ro, Gangn
相关性: 该论文研究了直接能量沉积（DED）多孔涂层对骨整合的增强作用，与用户关注的增材制造生物医学植入物方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not explicitly link porous structure parameters to stress shielding or stiffness matching, focusing instead on coating technique comparison and osseointegration.
动机· 评估DED多孔涂层相对于传统方法的骨整合优势。
方法· 比较DED、TPS和PBF涂层的孔隙率、疲劳性能及体外体内骨整合。
结果· DED涂层具有更高孔隙率、良好细胞活性和骨整合表现。
验证· 包括机械疲劳测试、体外细胞实验和动物体内植入。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To systematically evaluate the advantages of direct energy deposition porous coating for cementless orthopedic implant fixation compared to conventional techniques.
Method· Comparative analysis of DED, TPS, and PBF coatings including porosity measurement, fatigue testing, in vitro osteoblast culture, and in vivo rat model.
Result· DED coating showed higher porosity (48.54%), comparable fatigue cycles, enhanced cell proliferation and osteoblast activity, and superior bone-implant contact in vivo.
Validation· Mechanical fatigue tests, in vitro cell assays (2-week culture), and in vivo animal implantation (rat model).

通讯: Qingsheng Yang @ Department of Engineering Mechanics, Beijing University of Technology, Beijing, China  |  Xia Liu @ Department of Engineering Mechanics, Beijing University of Technology, Beijing, China

Validation· Validation was performed through nanoindentation experiments and corresponding CPFE simulations.
