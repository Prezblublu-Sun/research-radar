Effects of Two Dental Implant Micromotor Systems for Dental Implant Placement on Implant Stability and Removal Torque: An Animal Experiment.

Keunbada Son, Young-Tak Son, Sung-Min Hwang, Jae Mok Lee, Jin-Wook Kim et al. Materials (Basel, Switzerland) 2025-08-29 10.3390/ma18174048
单位: Advanced Dental Device Development Institute (A3DI), Kyungpook National University, Daegu 41940, Republic of Korea.
通讯: Kyu-Bok Lee @ Department of Dental Science, Graduate School, Kyungpook National University, Daegu 41940, Republic of Korea.; Departmen [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究牙科植入物而非髋关节植入物，与用户研究方向无关。
边界: This paper studies dental implants in rabbit tibiae, not hip implants or femoral stems; it contains no contact mechanics, stress shielding analysis, or FE modeling of proximal femur.
动机· 比较两种牙科植入用微电机系统对植入稳定性和移除扭矩的影响。
方法· 在兔胫骨中植入钛种植体，使用共振频率分析和显微CT评价。
结果· 集成激光的微电机系统初期稳定性更高，但四周后与常规系统无显著差异。
验证· 动物实验验证，包括ISQ、移除扭矩和骨-种植体间隙测量。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate whether a diode laser-integrated micromotor system affects implant stability and removal torque compared to a conventional system in an animal model.
Method· Twenty titanium implants were placed in rabbit tibiae using two micromotor systems; implant stability quotient (ISQ), bone-implant gap (BIG), and removal torque were measured post-sacrifice.
Result· The laser-integrated system yielded higher immediate ISQ, but after four weeks ISQ, removal torque, and BIG were comparable between groups.
Validation· In vivo rabbit study with longitudinal ISQ, micro-CT, and mechanical removal torque testing.
