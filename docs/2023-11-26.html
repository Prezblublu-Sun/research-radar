Investigation of Biomaterial Ink Viscosity Properties and Optimization of the Printing Process Based on Pattern Path Planning

Medium AI for Bioprinting openalex Transferable Skim only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Jiahao Wu, Chunya Wu, Siyang Zou, Xiguang Li, Bo Ho et al. Bioengineering 2023-11-26 10.3390/bioengineering10121358
单位: State Key Laboratory of Robotics and System, Harbin Institute of Technology, Harbin 150080, China
通讯: Chunya Wu @ Key Laboratory of Micro-Systems and Micro-Structures Manufacturing, Harbin Institute of Technology, Harbin 150080, China
相关性: 该论文研究了生物墨水流变特性与打印工艺优化，虽未直接利用AI/ML，但其统计建模和路径规划方法可迁移至AI辅助打印流程。
边界: This paper does not use AI/ML methods; it relies on response surface methodology and CFD, lacking data-driven predictive models or autonomous decision-making. for AI for Bioprinting.
动机· 研究不同粘度生物墨水的可打印性，优化挤出打印过程。
方法· 利用响应面法预测流变特性，结合CFD模拟和路径规划优化打印。
结果· 流变预测精度达96%，打印线条长度精度达99%。
验证· 通过低/高粘度墨水打印实验验证模型和路径优化。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the printability of biomaterial inks with varying viscosities and optimize the extrusion printing process.
Method· Response surface methodology was used to predict rheological properties from material components, combined with Latin hypercube sampling and CFD to simulate flow, and pattern path planning to improve print accuracy.
Result· Prediction accuracy for power-law index reached 96%, and filament length accuracy reached 99% after path optimization.
Validation· Experimental printing trials with low- and high-viscosity inks validated the predictions and path planning improvements.

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ

相关性: 该方法利用贝叶斯优化和Pareto集学习解决昂贵多目标优化问题，可迁移至生物力学中的代理模型优化。
边界: This paper does not involve FEA simulations, biomechanics context, or implant design; it is validated on abstract optimization problems.
动机· 现有的Pareto集学习方法在处理昂贵黑箱多目标优化时不稳定且效率低。
方法· 提出Co-PSL，先通过贝叶斯优化预热获得高斯过程先验，再进行可控Pareto集学习。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Existing Pareto set learning methods suffer from instability and inefficiency when applied to expensive black-box multi-objective optimization problems.
Method· Co-PSL first uses warm-starting Bayesian optimization to obtain quality Gaussian process priors, then performs controllable Pareto set learning to map preferences to Pareto solutions.
Result· Co-PSL demonstrates improved stability and efficiency across synthetic and real-world multi-objective optimization benchmarks, reducing expensive function evaluations.
Validation· Validation was conducted on numerical benchmarks and a real-world engineering problem, comparing against state-of-the-art PSL methods.

Efficient interpolation of molecular properties across chemical compound space with low-dimensional descriptors

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Background only numerical_benchmark
Yun-Wen Mao, Roman V. Krems arXiv preprint 2023-11-26 10.1088/2632-2153/ad360e
相关性: 本文使用高斯过程回归进行分子性质插值，与FEA代理建模方向无关，但GP方法可能有方法论参考价值。
边界: No FEA simulation, no surrogate modeling for solid mechanics, no constraint handling, no uncertainty quantification for design optimization, and no biomedical implant context.
动机· 在分子化学空间中用少量数据高效插值分子性质。
方法· 结合Coulomb矩阵特征值和Gershgorin圆定理构建9维描述符，用于高斯过程回归。
结果· 训练100个分子即可高精度预测S×T和ZPVE，测试集达2万分子。
验证· 与图神经网络对比，展示描述符优越性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Achieve accurate interpolation of molecular properties in chemical compound space with very limited training data.
Method· Construct 9-dimensional descriptors from Coulomb matrix eigenvalue distributions and Gershgorin circle theorem, then apply Gaussian process regression with variable-form kernels.
Result· Models trained on 100 molecules predict entropy×temperature and zero-point vibrational energy with errors below 1 kcal/mol for over 78% of 20,000 test molecules.
Validation· Demonstrate improved accuracy over graph neural network baselines on a large diverse molecular dataset.

Exclude FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Ignore numerical_benchmark
Hengtong Hu, Lingxi Xie, Xinyue Hue, Richang Hong, Qi Tian arXiv preprint 2023-11-26 10.1145/3633779
相关性: 该论文研究图像分类中的一位监督学习，与FEA和替代建模方向无关。
边界: This paper addresses image classification with one-bit supervision; it does not involve FEA, surrogate modeling, uncertainty quantification, or any application to implant/biomechanics design.
动机· 减少图像分类任务中标注成本，提出新的一位监督设置。
方法· 通过多阶段训练和负标签抑制结合半监督学习实现从“是/否”反馈中学习。
结果· 一位监督在多个基准上超越全标注和半监督方法。
验证· 在标准图像分类基准数据集进行实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce annotation cost in image classification, this work proposes a novel one-bit supervision setting where only binary correctness feedback is provided.
Method· A multi-stage training paradigm with negative label suppression integrated into semi-supervised learning is developed to leverage one-bit annotations.
Result· The proposed one-bit supervision approach outperforms full-bit and semi-supervised methods on several image classification benchmarks.
Validation· Experimental validation is conducted on standard image classification datasets, demonstrating efficiency and effectiveness.
