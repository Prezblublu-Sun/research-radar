Motivation· To investigate the association between preoperative plasma fibrinogen concentration and intraoperative bleeding in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective observational study of 227 patients undergoing primary total hip arthroplasty, analyzing preoperative fibrinogen levels and bleeding outcomes.
Result· Preoperative fibrinogen ≤388 mg/dl was associated with an increased risk of intraoperative bleeding ≥300 ml.
Validation· Clinical retrospective data with statistical analysis.
