单位: Department of Mechanical, Industrial and Manufacturing Engineering, The University of Toledo, Toledo, OH, USA
通讯: Mohammad Elahinia @ Department of Mechanical, Industrial and Manufacturing Engineering, The University of Toledo, Toledo, OH, USA
相关性: 该论文直接通过增材制造多孔NiTi降低刚度以匹配骨组织，缓解应力遮挡，与用户研究方向高度相关。

Validation· Simulation results were validated by mechanical testing of additively manufactured porous NiTi samples.

边界: No link to additive manufacturing, biomedical implants, porous structures, stress shielding, mechanical testing, or any aspect of AM biomedical materials.
动机· 探索太赫兹驱动非晶铁磁体中超快自旋-晶格散射。
方法· 使用单周期太赫兹场和飞秒磁光克尔效应激发和探测磁化动力学。

Result· Ultrafast demagnetization due to spin-lattice scattering is observed only in the amorphous sample, with a timescale comparable to spin-conserving electronic scattering (~30 fs).
Validation· Validation is performed via THz conductivity measurements and quantitative modeling of spin-lattice scattering rates.
