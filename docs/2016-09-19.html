通讯: Michael Worlicek @ Departement of Orthopedic Surgery, University of Regensburg, Asklepios Medical Centre, Kaiser-Karl-V-Allee 3, D-93077, B
相关性: 该论文关注髋关节置换术中股骨前倾角测量，未涉及应力遮挡或微动等生物力学分析。

Motivation· To determine whether native femoral anteversion can serve as a reliable reference for implant anteversion in cementless THA with a straight, tapered stem.

Result· Native femoral version correlated poorly with implant version (r=0.39), while final broach version strongly predicted implant version (r=0.89).
Validation· Clinical validation with intraoperative navigation and postoperative CT scans.
