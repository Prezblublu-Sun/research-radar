Motivation· To systematically identify and evaluate Chinese cross-culturally adapted hip-specific Patient Reported Outcome Measures (PROMs) for clinical use and registry adoption.
Method· A systematic review of five databases was conducted, and six identified PROMs were assessed using the EMPRO tool by two independent reviewers with inter-rater reliability calculated.
Result· Only OAKHQOL and HAGOS met the acceptability threshold; none of the Chinese adapted versions commonly used in arthroplasty registries were rated acceptable.
Validation· Inter-rater reliability was assessed using intra-class correlation coefficients (ICC), ranging from 0.73 to 0.93.
