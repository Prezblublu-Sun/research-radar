Compression fatigue behavior and failure mechanism of porous titanium for biomedical applications

单位: State Key Laboratory of Solidification Processing, Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi'an 710072, PR China
通讯: Hongchao Kou @ State Key Laboratory of Solidification Processing, Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi'an 710072, PR China. Electr
相关性: 该论文直接研究多孔钛的压缩疲劳行为，与用户关注的LPBF制造多孔植入物疲劳性能高度相关。
边界: Does not include LPBF fabrication details, surface roughness, or osseointegration assessment.
动机· 研究多孔钛的压缩疲劳行为及失效机制，为骨科植入物设计提供依据。
方法· 制备不同孔隙率的多孔钛试样，进行压缩疲劳测试并分析断口形貌。
结果· 疲劳强度随孔隙率增加而降低，失效机制为局部塑性塌陷和裂纹扩展。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the compression fatigue behavior and failure mechanism of porous titanium for biomedical implant applications.
Method· Porous titanium specimens with varying porosities were fabricated and subjected to compression fatigue testing, followed by fractographic analysis.
Result· Fatigue strength decreased with increasing porosity, and failure was governed by localized plastic collapse and crack propagation.
Validation· Mechanical testing and SEM fractography were used to validate fatigue performance and failure modes.
