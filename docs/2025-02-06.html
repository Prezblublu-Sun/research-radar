AL-PINN: Active Learning-Driven Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Efficient Sample Selection in Solving Partial Differential Equations

High FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Direct Read now numerical_benchmark UQ
Keon Vin Park arXiv preprint 2025-02-06 link
相关性: 该论文直接针对PINN训练样本效率问题，结合主动学习与不确定性量化，可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模。
边界: The paper does not apply the method to implant or biomechanics design, nor does it handle constrained optimization or multi-objective scenarios.
动机· 标准PINN需要大量训练样本，计算成本高。
方法· 提出AL-PINN，利用蒙特卡洛dropout估计不确定性，主动选择高不确定性区域样本。
结果· 在基准PDE和气候数据上，以更少样本达到相当或更优精度。
验证· 在已知解析解的基准PDE和WeatherBench气候数据上验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Standard PINNs require many training samples for high accuracy, leading to high computational cost.
Method· AL-PINN integrates Monte Carlo Dropout for epistemic uncertainty estimation and active learning to adaptively select high-uncertainty regions for additional training.
Result· On benchmark PDEs and WeatherBench data, AL-PINN achieves comparable or superior accuracy with significantly fewer training samples.
Validation· Validated on benchmark PDE problems with known analytical solutions and real-world WeatherBench climate data.

边界: The paper does not apply the method to biomedical implant design or biomechanics FEA, nor does it address multi-objective constrained optimization.
动机· 神经PDE模型缺乏可靠的不确定性量化，限制了其在关键应用中的部署。
方法· 提出一种模型无关的物理信息共形预测框架，利用物理残差作为非一致性分数实现无数据UQ。
结果· 在多个复杂PDE上提供了边际和联合覆盖保证，并在等离子体建模中验证了有效性。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Neural PDE models lack robust uncertainty quantification, hindering their deployment in critical applications.
Method· A model-agnostic, physics-informed conformal prediction framework is introduced, using physics residual errors as nonconformity scores for data-free UQ.
Result· The method provides marginal and joint coverage guarantees across prediction domains for complex PDEs, validated on plasma modeling.
Validation· Validated on neural PDE models for plasma modeling and shot design in fusion reactors.

Mixture of neural operator experts for learning boundary conditions and model selection

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project simulation_case UQ
Dwyer Deighan, Jonas A. Actor, Ravi G. Patel arXiv preprint 2025-02-06 link
相关性: 该论文提出了一种混合神经算子专家模型，可处理边界条件和模型选择，其方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模中的约束处理和不确定性量化。
边界: This paper does not apply the method to biomedical or implant mechanics problems, lacks validation on solid mechanics or FEA benchmarks, and does not address design optimization or stress shielding.
动机· 现有神经算子在处理非平凡边界条件时存在局限性，且缺乏模型选择能力。
方法· 结合体积惩罚法和混合专家模型，提出一种空间条件混合专家神经算子。
结果· 在圆盘和四分之一圆盘上成功恢复非线性算子，并从DNS中提取LES模型，获得远超过DNS时间范围的预测样本。
验证· 通过圆盘上的非线性算子恢复和通道流LES模型提取进行验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Existing neural operators struggle with non-trivial boundary conditions and lack model selection capabilities, limiting their applicability to complex physical systems.
Method· A mixture of neural operator experts is introduced, combining volume penalization with a spatially conditioned Mixture of Experts to handle boundary conditions and enable model selection.
Result· The method successfully recovers a nonlinear operator on a disk and quarter disk, and extracts a large eddy simulation model from direct numerical simulation of channel flow, producing posterior predictive samples far beyond the DNS time horizon.
Validation· Validation is performed through nonlinear operator recovery on disk geometries and LES model extraction from DNS of channel flow.
