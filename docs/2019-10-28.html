单位: University of Wyoming 1 , Laramie, Wyoming, . E-mail: dgrana@uwyo.edu (corresponding author); mliu4@uwyo.edu
通讯: Darío Graña @ University of Wyoming 1 , Laramie, Wyoming, . E-mail: dgrana@uwyo.edu (corresponding author); mliu4@uwyo.edu
相关性: 该论文研究地震数据中的岩相分类，与生物力学有限元代理建模方向无关。
边界: No FEA surrogate modeling, no implant/biomechanics context, no constraint handling or multi-objective optimization.
动机· 比较深度学习与蒙特卡洛方法在地震岩相分类中的表现。
方法· 使用循环神经网络、接受/拒绝采样和马尔可夫链蒙特卡洛进行岩相分类。
结果· 大数据集下机器学习更准确但低估不确定性；有先验时蒙特卡洛方法精度相当且不确定性量化更稳健。
验证· 在井位处将分类结果与参考岩相剖面比较。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To benchmark deep learning against Monte Carlo methods for facies classification from seismic data, focusing on accuracy and uncertainty quantification.
Method· Three algorithms (recurrent neural network, Monte Carlo acceptance/rejection, and Markov chain Monte Carlo) were applied to classify facies from seismic data.
Result· With large training sets, machine learning yields higher accuracy but underestimates uncertainty; with informative priors, Monte Carlo methods achieve similar accuracy with more robust uncertainty quantification.
Validation· Predictions were compared to reference facies logs at well locations to compute classification accuracy.
