ArGEnT: Arbitrary Geometry-encoded Transformer for Operator Learning

Wenqian Chen, Yucheng Fu, Michael Penwarden, Pratanu Roy, Panos Stinis arXiv preprint 2026-02-12 link
相关性: 该方法将几何编码与算子学习结合，可直接迁移至基于有限元的植入物力学代理模型构建。
边界: Does not specifically validate on implant biomechanics or contact mechanics, nor include experimental validation.
动机· 在变几何物理系统中高效求解算子学习问题。
方法· 提出ArGEnT模型，将Transformer注意力机制与点云几何编码结合作为DeepONet主干。
结果· 在流体、固体和电化学基准上精度和泛化性显著优于标准DeepONet。
验证· 在多个物理领域数值基准上进行精度和泛化性对比验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enable efficient surrogate modeling for physical systems with complex and varying geometries in many-query regimes.
Method· Proposes ArGEnT, a geometry-aware attention-based architecture using point-cloud encoding integrated as the trunk network into DeepONet.
Result· Achieves significantly improved prediction accuracy and generalization on benchmarks from fluid dynamics, solid mechanics, and electrochemistry compared to standard DeepONet.
Validation· Validated through numerical benchmark problems across multiple physical domains including fluid, solid, and electrochemical systems.
