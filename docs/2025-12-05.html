边界: This paper focuses on tibial plateau fracture fixation using plates, not on femoral stem implants or hip joint biomechanics; it does not quantify proximal femur stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics.
动机· 建立胫骨平台骨折内固定力学稳定性的多指标综合评价体系。
方法· 通过有限元分析模拟15种内固定方案，用变异系数法确定指标权重。
结果· 内侧钢板固定稳定性最优，为临床选择提供参考。
验证· 仅有限元仿真，无实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To establish a multi-indicator integrated evaluation system for the mechanical stability of tibial plateau fracture fixation.
Method· Finite element analysis was performed on 15 internal fixation schemes, and the coefficient of variation method was used to determine objective weights of four key indicators.
Result· Medial plate fixation exhibited superior stability, offering a reference for clinical fixation selection.
Validation· The study relied solely on finite element simulations without experimental validation.
