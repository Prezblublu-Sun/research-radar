A Comparative Study and Introduction of a New Heat Source Model for the Macro-Scale Numerical Simulation of Selective Laser Melting Technology

Hao Zhang, Shuai Wang, Junjie Wang, Zhiqiang Yan Materials 2026-01-25 10.3390/ma19030480
单位: School of Physics and Electronic Information, Yan’an University, Yan’an 716000, China
通讯: Hao Zhang @ School of Physics and Electronic Information, Yan’an University, Yan’an 716000, China
相关性: 与生物医用植入物方向无关，主要关注SLM热源模型数值模拟。
边界: Does not address porous structure design, stress shielding, fatigue, surface roughness, or implant-specific mechanical testing.
动机· 提高SLM熔池形态预测精度，对比多种热源模型。
方法· 基于ANSYS APDL建立三维瞬态导热有限元模型。
结果· 动态热源模型预测熔池宽度和深度误差分别为1.0%和5.5%。
验证· 与实验测量熔池尺寸对比验证准确性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve melt pool prediction accuracy in SLM by comparing heat source models.
Method· A 3D transient heat conduction finite element model was built in ANSYS APDL with four heat source models.
Result· The dynamic heat source model predicted melt pool width and depth with relative errors of 1.0% and 5.5%.
Validation· Predicted melt pool dimensions were validated against experimental measurements.

Anesthetic Strategies for Closed Reduction of Dislocated Total Hip Arthroplasty: A Scoping Review

Roland Knudsen, Rikke Helene Frølund Bjulf, Aurelien‐Xuan Rosendal Bahuet, Mathias T. Steensbæk, Anne‐Sofie Linde Jellestad et al. Acta Anaesthesiologica Scandinavica 2026-01-25 10.1111/aas.70189
单位: Department of Anesthesiology Copenhagen University Hospital—North Zealand Hillerød Denmark
通讯: Roland Knudsen @ Department of Anesthesiology Copenhagen University Hospital—North Zealand Hillerød Denmark
相关性: 该论文关注全髋关节置换术后脱位复位中的麻醉策略，未涉及任何骨植入物力学或应力遮挡分析。
边界: This paper does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant mechanics; it is a clinical review of anesthetic techniques with no biomechanical modeling or implant evaluation.
动机· 综述全髋关节置换术后脱位复位麻醉策略的现状与知识缺口。
方法· 系统检索文献，筛选57项研究并分析麻醉方式与成功率。
结果· 镇静与全麻复位成功率差异大，证据质量有限。
验证· 未进行实验验证，仅为文献综述。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To synthesize existing literature on anesthetic strategies for closed reduction of dislocated total hip arthroplasty and identify best practice and knowledge gaps.
Method· A scoping review following systematic guidelines, with independent screening and data extraction from 22 included studies.
Result· Success rates varied widely; sedation in the emergency department with propofol showed higher success than other regimens, but evidence is limited.
Validation· No experimental validation; the study is a literature review without primary data.
