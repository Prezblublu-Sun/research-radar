英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the clinical outcomes of vascularised iliac bone grafting for femoral neck nonunion in young adults.
Method· A retrospective analysis of 22 patients treated with vascularised iliac grafting and internal fixation.
Result· Mean union time was 5.4 months, and Harris Hip Score improved from 55.3 to 85.2.
Validation· Clinical follow-up and radiographic evaluation.
