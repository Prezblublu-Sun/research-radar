High-Throughput Unsupervised Profiling of the Morphology of 316L Powder Particles for Use in Additive Manufacturing

Emmanuel Akeweje, Conall Kirk, Chi-Wai Chan, Denis Dowling, Mimi Zhang Computational Materials Science 2025-12-06 10.1016/j.commatsci.2025.114433
单位: School of Computer Science and Statistics, Trinity College Dublin, Ireland
通讯: Emmanuel Akeweje @ School of Computer Science and Statistics, Trinity College Dublin, Ireland
相关性: 该论文研究SLM金属粉末形态的机器学习分类，不涉及生物医学植入物设计或力学性能。
边界: No porous structure design, no stress shielding or stiffness matching, no LPBF implant fabrication, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), and no osseointegration or surface roughness analysis.
动机· 传统粉末表征方法低通量且定性，无法捕捉工业批次异质性。
方法· 结合高通量成像、形状提取与无监督聚类（VAE、形状描述子、功能聚类）对316L粉末形态分类。
结果· 傅里叶描述子加k-means管道在准确性、速度和效率上最优。
验证· 基于内部有效性指标（Davies-Bouldin指数、Calinski-Harabasz分数）和计算时间评估。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Conventional powder characterization methods are low-throughput and qualitative, failing to capture the heterogeneity of industrial-scale batches.
Method· An automated machine learning framework couples high-throughput imaging with shape extraction and clustering (VAE, shape descriptors, functional clustering) to profile metallic powder morphology at scale.
Result· The Fourier-descriptor plus k-means pipeline achieved the best accuracy, speed, and efficiency among three approaches.
Validation· Internal validity metrics (Davies-Bouldin index, Calinski-Harabasz score) and runtime evaluation were used to assess clustering performance.

Emergy-based assessment and improvement of the selective laser melting process for multiple parts using shared support structures

Mengdi Gao, Qingyang Wang, Lei Li, Qiang Li, Conghu Liu et al. Results in Engineering 2025-12-06 10.1016/j.rineng.2025.108621
相关性: 该论文讨论选择性激光熔化工艺的环境影响评估和共享支撑结构设计，与用户所需的AM生物医用材料方向完全无关。
边界: This paper focuses on sustainability and emergy analysis of SLM process with shared support structures, with no mention of biomedical implants, porous structures, mechanical testing, or osseointegration.
动机· 评估SLM工艺的环境影响并提高可持续性
方法· 建立emergy流模型，引入共享支撑结构同时成型多零件
结果· 共享支撑结构降低能耗5.5%，材料使用17.2%，可持续性指数提升1.04%
验证· 案例研究验证了共享支撑结构方法的有效性
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the environmental impact of selective laser melting (SLM) and improve its sustainability using a unified sustainability metric.
Method· An emergy flow model integrating resource consumption, waste, labor, time, quality, and costs was developed, and a shared support structure design for simultaneous multi-part fabrication was introduced.
Result· The shared support structure method reduced energy consumption by 5.5%, support material usage by 17.2%, and improved the sustainability index (SI) by 1.04%.
Validation· A case study demonstrated the effectiveness of the shared support structure in improving resource efficiency and sustainability.

Use of Short Femoral Stems in Primary Total Hip Arthroplasty: A Mid-Term Safety Analysis: Prospective series of 5,876 cases at 4.4-year average follow-up.

Christian Delaunay, Christian Brand, Pierre-alban Bouché, Sophie Putman, A. Poichotte et al. Open Access CRIS of the University of Bern 2025-12-06 10.48620/93484
相关性: 该论文为临床登记研究，不涉及任何生物力学建模或有限元分析。
边界: This paper only reports clinical revision rates and survival of short femoral stems, with no biomechanical quantification, stress/strain analysis, micromotion modeling, or FE validation.
动机· 评估短股骨柄在全髋置换术中的中期安全性。
方法· 基于法国多中心登记数据，分析5876例短柄置换的翻修率和生存率。
结果· 短柄翻修率与传统柄相当，脱位为主要翻修原因。
验证· 临床随访数据验证，平均随访4.4年。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To assess the mid-term safety of short femoral stems in primary total hip arthroplasty using a multicenter registry.
Method· A prospective analysis of 5,876 short-stem THAs from the French RENACOT registry, with revision rates and Kaplan-Meier survival estimates.
Result· Short stems showed comparable 10-year survival to conventional stems, with dislocation as the leading cause of revision.
Validation· Clinical registry data with average 4.4-year follow-up and 733 revisions analyzed.
