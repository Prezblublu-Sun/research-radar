英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare intraoperative and postoperative outcomes between direct anterior and posterolateral approaches in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A systematic review and meta-analysis of 18 randomized and non-randomized controlled trials involving 34,873 patients.
Result· Direct anterior approach showed shorter hospital stay, lower dislocation rate, but longer operative time, more blood loss, and higher fracture rate compared to posterolateral approach.
Validation· Meta-analysis of published studies; no original experimental validation.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine the safe time to resume driving after total hip arthroplasty by evaluating emergency braking performance.
Method· A prospective study measured brake reaction time and braking force in 25 THA patients using a car simulator and measuring sole preoperatively and at 6 days, 2, 4, and 6 weeks postoperatively.
Result· Brake reaction time was significantly prolonged and braking force reduced at 6 days and 2 weeks post-surgery, but no significant differences were found after 4 weeks.
Validation· Clinical patient data with preoperative and postoperative comparisons.
