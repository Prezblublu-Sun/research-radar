Human stem cell based corneal tissue mimicking structures using laser-assisted 3D bioprinting and functional bioinks

Anni Sorkio, Lothar Koch, Laura Koivusalo, Andrea Deiwick, Susanna Miettinen et al. Biomaterials 2018-04-16 10.1016/j.biomaterials.2018.04.034
单位: BioMediTech Institute and Faculty of Medicine and Life Sciences, University of Tampere, Arvo Ylpön katu 34, FI-33520 Tampere, Finland; Laser Zentrum Hannover e.V., Hollerithallee 8, 30419 Hannover, Ge
通讯: Heli Skottman @ BioMediTech Institute and Faculty of Medicine and Life Sciences, University of Tampere, Arvo Ylpön katu 34, FI-33520 Tam
相关性: 该论文不涉及任何AI或机器学习方法，因此与AI生物打印方向无直接关联。
边界: No AI or machine learning integrated into any bioprinting workflow stage; purely experimental bioprinting with biological characterization.
动机· 开发更接近天然角膜的3D结构，满足临床需求。
方法· 利用激光辅助生物打印和功能性生物墨水，打印含人干细胞的角膜仿生组织。
结果· 成功构建分层角膜上皮和基质结构，细胞存活良好，并表达关键蛋白。
验证· 通过细胞活力、蛋白表达及猪角膜器官培养验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To address the need for native-like 3D corneal constructs using human stem cells and laser-assisted bioprinting.
Method· Laser-assisted bioprinting with functional bioinks based on recombinant laminin and collagen I was used to fabricate stratified corneal epithelium and lamellar stroma.
Result· Printed constructs showed high cell viability, proper morphology, and key protein expression, with stromal implants integrating into porcine corneal organ cultures.
Validation· Validation included cell viability assays, immunohistochemistry for protein markers, and ex vivo implantation in porcine corneas.

Patient‐Specific Bioinks for 3D Bioprinting of Tissue Engineering Scaffolds

Negar Faramarzi, Iman K. Yazdi, Mahboubeh Nabavinia, Andrea Gemma, Adele Fanelli et al. Advanced Healthcare Materials 2018-04-16 10.1002/adhm.201701347
单位: Biomaterials Innovation Research Center Department of Medicine Brigham and Women's Hospital Harvard Medical School Boston MA 02139 USA
通讯: Jeremy N. Ruskin @ Department of Medicine Massachusetts General Hospital Harvard Medical School Boston MA 02114 USA  |  Ali Tamayol @ Biomaterials Innovation Research Center Department of Medicine Brigham and Women's Hospital Harvard Medical School Bost
相关性: 该论文聚焦于生物墨水的材料开发，未涉及AI或机器学习在生物打印中的应用。
边界: No AI/ML integration in bioprinting workflow; the paper focuses solely on material development without any computational modeling or autonomous decision.
动机· 开发含自体生长因子的患者特异性生物墨水。
方法· 将富血小板血浆掺入藻酸盐基水凝胶中，用于3D打印。
结果· 生物墨水能释放生长因子，增强血管化和干细胞迁移。
验证· 体外实验验证打印和交联性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop patient-specific bioinks incorporating autologous growth factors for personalized tissue engineering.
Method· Platelet-rich plasma was integrated into alginate-based hydrogels to create printable bioinks that crosslink upon exposure to native calcium ions.
Result· The bioink platform enabled controlled release of growth factors, potentially enhancing vascularization and stem cell recruitment.
Validation· In vitro experiments demonstrated printability and crosslinking of the bioink.

边界: Does not incorporate AI or machine learning into any bioprinting workflow stage; it uses flow cytometry for post-printing analysis.

方法· 采用流式细胞术对3D生物打印结构进行细胞活力和分布分析

English summary & terms
Motivation· This paper aims to establish flow cytometry as an analytical strategy for characterizing 3D bioprinted cell structures.
Method· Flow cytometry was applied to analyze cell viability, distribution, and phenotype in 3D bioprinted constructs.
Result· Flow cytometry provided quantitative assessment of cell health and uniformity within the bioprinted structures.
Validation· The method was validated using in vitro experiments with bioprinted cell-laden hydrogels.

Investigating the Residual Stress Distribution in Selective Laser Melting Produced Ti-6Al-4V using Neutron Diffraction

L Anderson, А.M. Venter, Bey Vrancken, Deon Marais, J. Van Humbeeck et al. Materials research proceedings 2018-04-16 10.21741/9781945291678-11
单位: Department of Mechanical and Mechatronic Engineering, Stellenbosch University, South Africa
通讯: L Anderson @ Department of Mechanical and Mechatronic Engineering, Stellenbosch University, South Africa
相关性: 该论文研究SLM钛合金残余应力分布，未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡、疲劳或骨整合等生物医学方向核心问题。
边界: This paper does not address porous structure design for stiffness matching, fatigue testing, surface roughness, or osseointegration, and lacks any biomedical context.
动机· 理解SLM制备Ti-6Al-4V部件内部残余应力分布
方法· 使用中子衍射测量不同层厚和曝光策略下矩形样品残余应力
结果· 增加层厚降低应力梯度，改变曝光策略可形成更均匀应力场
验证· 中子衍射实验测量验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to characterize the volumetric residual stress distribution in selective laser melted Ti-6Al-4V parts, which is critical for predicting distortion and mechanical performance.
Method· Neutron diffraction was employed to measure lattice strains through the thickness of rectangular SLM-produced Ti-6Al-4V samples built with varying layer thicknesses and exposure strategies.
Result· Increasing layer thickness reduces stress gradients, while altering the exposure strategy can homogenize the stress field and prevent preferential orientation of stresses.
Validation· Experimental measurements using neutron diffraction were performed to validate the residual stress distributions.
