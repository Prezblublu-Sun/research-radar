Motivation· To address the challenge of head-to-head overtaking in autonomous racing by learning opponent behavior.
Method· A data-driven planner called Predictive Spliner uses Gaussian Process regression to model opponent trajectories and compute feasible overtaking maneuvers.
Result· The method achieves an average success rate of 84.5% and is 8.4% faster than the previous state-of-the-art on a 1:10 scale platform.
Validation· Experimental validation on a physical 1:10 scale autonomous racing car using LiDAR perception.
