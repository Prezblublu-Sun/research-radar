Motivation· To replicate findings that total joint arthroplasty reduces cardiovascular event risk in osteoarthritis patients.

Motivation· To understand demographic differences between adolescent and adult patients undergoing primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Analyzed data from the Kids' Inpatient Database and Nationwide Inpatient Sample from 1997-2009.
