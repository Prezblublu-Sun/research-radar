Predictive prosthetic socket design: part 1—population-based evaluation of transtibial prosthetic sockets by FEA-driven surrogate modelling

High FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Direct Read now numerical_benchmark Patient-specific
Joshua Steer, Peter Worsley, Martin Browne, Alex Dickinson Biomechanics and Modeling in Mechanobiology 2019-06-29 10.1007/s10237-019-01195-5
单位: Bioengineering Science Research Group, Faculty of Engineering and Physical Sciences, University of Southampton, Southampton, UK
通讯: Joshua Steer @ Bioengineering Science Research Group, Faculty of Engineering and Physical Sciences, University of Southampton, Southamp
相关性: 该论文直接使用代理模型加速有限元分析，并考虑了人群形态变异和约束条件，方法可直接迁移至植入物设计中的应力屏蔽和微动分析。
边界: This paper focuses on prosthetic socket design rather than hip implants, and does not address stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics.
动机· 解决假肢接受腔有限元分析计算耗时、难以临床转化的问题
方法· 构建参数化模型，用Kriging代理模型拟合设计空间响应，实现实时预测
结果· 预测误差小于4 kPa（压力）和3%（应变），计算速度提升六个数量级
验证· 与有限元解析解对比验证代理模型精度
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To overcome the computational burden of finite element analysis for prosthetic socket design and enable clinical translation.
Method· A parametric model incorporating population-based morphological variation and socket design parameters was coupled with a Kriging surrogate model to predict interfacial pressures and soft tissue strains.
Result· The surrogate model achieved real-time predictions (1.6 ms) with errors below 4 kPa in pressure and 3% in strain, reducing computational cost by six orders of magnitude.
Validation· Validation was performed by comparing surrogate predictions against finite element analysis results across the design space.

相关性: 该论文研究麻疹预测，与FEA替代建模或生物力学设计无关。
边界: This paper focuses on epidemiological forecasting of measles counts, with no FEA, surrogate modeling, constraint handling, or biomechanics context.
动机· 麻疹疫情风险上升，需要改进预测方法。
方法· 使用负二项分布和潜在高斯过程建模整数函数型时间序列。

验证· 基于历史麻疹数据验证预测性能。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Rising measles risk due to declining vaccination rates demands improved forecasting with uncertainty quantification.
Method· Models measles counts as an integer-valued functional time series using a negative-binomial distribution with a latent Gaussian process decomposed via data-driven basis expansion.
Result· Achieves more accurate multi-month-ahead point forecasts and tighter, well-calibrated prediction intervals compared to existing models.
Validation· Validated on historical pre- and post-vaccine measles incidence data.
