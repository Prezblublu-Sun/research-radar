Study on Fatigue Behavior and Life Prediction of Laser Powder Bed Fused Ti6Al4V Alloy at 400 °C

Liangliang Wu, Ruida Xu, Jiaming Zhang, Huichen Yu, Zehui Jiao Materials 2025-12-18 10.3390/ma18245678
单位: Beijing Key Laboratory of Aeronautical Materials Testing and Evaluation, Aero Engine Corporation of China Key Laboratory of Materials Testing and Evaluation, AECC Beijing Institute of Aeronautical Mat
通讯: Liangliang Wu @ Beijing Key Laboratory of Aeronautical Materials Testing and Evaluation, Aero Engine Corporation of China Key Laboratory  |  Zehui Jiao @ Beijing Key Laboratory of Aeronautical Materials Testing and Evaluation, Aero Engine Corporation of China Key Laboratory
相关性: 与用户研究方向（生物医学植入物、应力遮挡、骨整合）无关，属于航空发动机高温疲劳研究。
边界: This paper focuses on high-temperature fatigue of Ti6Al4V for aerospace, with no biomedical implant context, no stress shielding analysis, and no osseointegration or surface morphology characterization.
动机· 研究激光粉末床熔融Ti6Al4V在400℃下的高周疲劳行为。
方法· 在不同应力比下进行疲劳试验，结合蠕变损伤建立复合寿命预测模型。
结果· 表面/亚表面缺陷是疲劳裂纹源，低应力比下寿命分散大，高应力比下疲劳断口呈混合特征。
验证· 通过与实际疲劳寿命比较，评估了单模型与复合模型的预测精度。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the high-cycle fatigue behavior of selective laser melted Ti6Al4V alloy at 400°C under various stress ratios for aerospace applications.
Method· Fatigue tests were conducted at stress ratios of -1, 0.1, 0.3, 0.5, and 0.8, and composite models combining fatigue and creep damage were developed using the linear damage rule.
Result· Surface and subsurface defects initiated cracks, fatigue life showed large scatter at low stress ratios, and fracture surfaces transitioned to mixed tensile-endurance and fatigue failure with increasing stress ratio.
Validation· Model predictions were compared with experimental fatigue life, identifying the best composite model for high-cycle fatigue life prediction.
