A clustering adaptive Gaussian process regression method: response patterns based real-time prediction for nonlinear solid mechanics problems

High FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Direct Read now simulation_case
Ming-Jian Li, Yanping Lian, Zhanshan Cheng, Lehui Li, Zhidong Wang et al. arXiv preprint 2024-09-15 link
相关性: 该论文提出的聚类自适应高斯过程回归方法直接解决了固体力学中非线性问题的实时预测需求，与用户关注的有限元代理建模方向高度契合。
边界: The paper does not address implant or biomechanics design, nor does it include uncertainty quantification beyond point prediction.
动机· 解决非线性固体力学问题中数值模拟耗时、难以实时预测的难题。
方法· 提出聚类自适应高斯过程回归方法，通过自适应采样和分治策略实现高效预测。
结果· 仅需约20个样本即可在1秒内获得高精度预测，误差比传统方法降低1-3个数量级。
验证· 通过材料、几何和边界条件非线性的多个算例验证方法有效性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To overcome the time-consuming nature of numerical simulations for nonlinear solid mechanics problems, enabling real-time prediction.
Method· A clustering adaptive Gaussian process regression (CAG) method is proposed, which uses adaptive sample generation and a divide-and-conquer strategy with pre-prediction classification.
Result· The CAG method achieves predictions within one second with high accuracy using only about 20 samples, reducing errors by 1-3 orders of magnitude compared to traditional GPR.
Validation· The method is validated on several nonlinear solid mechanics problems involving material, geometric, and boundary condition nonlinearities.

Robots that Suggest Safe Alternatives

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Background only simulation_case UQ
Hyun Joe Jeong, Rosy Chen, Andrea Bajcsy arXiv preprint 2024-09-15 link
相关性: 该论文涉及机器人安全控制与目标空间中的可达性分析，与FEA替代模型和植入物设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on robot safety filtering and goal-space reachability, with no FEA surrogate modeling, no uncertainty quantification for simulation, and no biomedical or implant design context.
动机· 使机器人能在无法安全执行用户目标时自动建议安全替代方案。
方法· 在目标空间中利用可达性分析计算安全-存活价值网络，作为安全过滤器。
结果· SALT框架能有效预测执行成败，提出与用户期望一致的替代目标。
验证· 在室内导航和Franka Panda桌面操作仿真中验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Enable robots to automatically suggest safe alternative goals when they cannot confidently execute a user's desired goal.
Method· Pose alternative suggestion as a safe control problem in goal space, using offline reachability analysis to compute a goal-parameterized reach-avoid value network.
Result· The SALT framework learns to predict successful and failed executions, is less pessimistic than open-loop UQ, and proposes alternatives aligned with human preferences.
Validation· Validated in simulation experiments with indoor navigation and Franka Panda tabletop manipulation.
