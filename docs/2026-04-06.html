单位: Department of Prosthodontics, Geriatric Dentistry and Craniomandibular Disorders, Charité University of Medicine, Charité Center 03, Assmannshauser Str. 4-6, 14197, Berlin, Germany. ssm@bluewin.ch.; B
通讯: Serge Szmukler-Moncler @ Department of Prosthodontics, Geriatric Dentistry and Craniomandibular Disorders, Charité University of Medicine, Charit
相关性: 该研究聚焦于牙种植体与残留牙根的界面组织学反应，未涉及增材制造、多孔结构或力学性能测试，与用户研究方向无关。
边界: This paper does not involve additive manufacturing, porous structure design, mechanical testing (static or fatigue), stress shielding, or stiffness matching; it is a purely histological animal study.
动机· 探索种植体意外接触残留牙根时的组织学反应。
方法· 在猪下颌模型中植入三枚种植体，10和12周后通过组织学评估界面反应。
结果· 种植体与牙根紧密接触时无软组织介入，部分接触时骨结合仍发生并伴有骨牙本质重塑。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To characterize the biological response of dental tissues when implants inadvertently contact inflammation-free retained root fragments.
Method· Three implants were placed in a pig mandible with retained root remnants; after 10-12 weeks, histological analysis using light and electron microscopy was performed.
Result· Implants fully embedded in root dentin showed direct contact without soft tissue, while partial root contact allowed osseointegration and active remodeling of osteodentin.
Validation· Validation relied on descriptive histology in an animal model; no quantitative or mechanical validation was performed.
