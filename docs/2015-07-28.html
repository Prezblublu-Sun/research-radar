Uncertainty quantification in virtual surgery hemodynamics predictions for single ventricle palliation

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project simulation_case UQ Patient-specific
Daniele E. Schiavazzi, Giuseppe Arbia, C. Baker, Anthony M. Hlavacek, Tain‐Yen Hsia et al. International Journal for Numerical Methods in Biomedical Engineering 2015-07-28 10.1002/cnm.2737
单位: Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering Department University of California at San Diego San Diego CA U.S.A
通讯: Alison L. Marsden @ Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering Department University of California at San Diego San Diego CA U.S.A
相关性: 该论文展示了在虚拟手术血流动力学预测中结合贝叶斯推断、Kriging代理模型和稀疏网格随机配点进行不确定性量化的完整流程，其方法可迁移至植入物力学仿真中的不确定性传播与代理建模。
边界: This paper focuses on hemodynamics in congenital heart surgery, not on solid mechanics, implant design, or bone-implant interface, and does not address contact mechanics or stress shielding.
动机· 临床数据不确定性导致虚拟手术预测结果不可靠，需量化预测置信区间。
方法· 结合贝叶斯参数估计、Kriging代理模型和稀疏网格随机配点，实现从临床数据到血流动力学预测的不确定性传播。
结果· 量化了术后血流动力学预测的统计变异性，给出了置信区间。
验证· 基于单心室姑息手术的虚拟病例，对比有无肺动脉狭窄的模型结果。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Clinical input uncertainties undermine the reliability of virtual surgery predictions, necessitating a framework to quantify confidence in simulation outputs.
Method· A methodology combining Bayesian parameter estimation with a Kriging surrogate for inverse problem and sparse grid stochastic collocation for forward uncertainty propagation is proposed.
Result· Statistical variability in post-operative hemodynamic predictions is quantified, enabling placement of confidence intervals on simulation outputs.
Validation· Demonstrated on a virtual stage II single ventricle palliation surgery with and without pulmonary artery stenosis.

[A comparison of two approaches to ultrasound-guided fascia iliaca compartment block for analgesia after total hip arthroplasty].

Ning Wang, Min Li, Wei Yue, Xiangyang Guo PubMed 2015-07-28 link
单位: Department of Anesthesiology, Peking University Third Hospital, Beijing 100191, China
通讯: Ning Wang @ Department of Anesthesiology, Peking University Third Hospital, Beijing 100191, China
相关性: 该论文研究全髋关节置换术后镇痛方法，与股骨柄应力遮挡、微动或生物力学建模无关。
边界: This paper focuses on postoperative analgesia techniques and does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or any biomechanical analysis of the femoral stem.
动机· 比较两种超声引导下髂筋膜间隙阻滞方法对全髋关节置换术后镇痛效果。
方法· 将60例患者随机分为探头平行或垂直于腹股沟韧带两组，记录阻滞时间、成功率及术后镇痛药用量。
结果· 探头垂直于腹股沟韧带组外侧皮神经阻滞成功率更高，术后舒芬太尼用量更少。
验证· 临床随机对照试验，比较两组患者术后疼痛评分和并发症。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the efficacy of two ultrasound-guided fascia iliaca compartment block approaches for postoperative analgesia after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Sixty patients were randomized to receive FICB with the ultrasound probe parallel or perpendicular to the inguinal ligament; block times, success rates, and postoperative opioid consumption were recorded.
Result· The perpendicular approach achieved higher lateral femoral cutaneous nerve block success and reduced sufentanil consumption compared to the parallel approach.
Validation· Clinical randomized controlled trial with postoperative pain scores and adverse event monitoring.
