[Research progress in biomechanics of different fixation methods for medial opening-wedge high tibial osteotomy].

Hongyun Shao, Qida Duan, Ning Luo, Fuyang Wang, Liangliang Cheng et al. Zhongguo xiu fu chong jian wai ke za zhi = Zhongguo xiufu chongjian waike zazhi = Chinese journal of reparative and reconstructive surgery 2025-06-15 10.7507/1002-1892.202504010
单位: Department of Orthopedics, Affiliated Zhongshan Hospital of Dalian University, Dalian Liaoning, 116001, P. R. China.
通讯: Dewei Zhao @ Department of Orthopedics, Affiliated Zhongshan Hospital of Dalian University, Dalian Liaoning, 116001, P. R. China. [推断]
相关性: 该论文关注膝关节截骨术的生物力学，而非髋关节植入物，与用户方向不直接相关。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress, micromotion, or compare intact vs implanted femur; it is a review of knee osteotomy fixation biomechanics.
动机· 总结不同固定方式在内侧开放楔形胫骨高位截骨术中的生物力学研究进展
方法· 综述国内外文献，分析不同固定技术的生物力学特点
结果· 内侧专用钢板系统因刚度和稳定性成为主流，但存在软组织刺激和应力遮挡；填充块可减少轴向位移73%-76%，降低钢板应力90%
验证· 文献综述，无具体实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To summarize biomechanical research on fixation methods for medial opening-wedge high tibial osteotomy to guide clinical selection.
Method· A literature review analyzing biomechanical performance of various fixation techniques.
Result· Medial-specific plates offer high stiffness but cause stress shielding; filler blocks reduce axial displacement by 73%-76% and plate stress by 90%.
Validation· The study is a review without original experimental validation.

α-Asarone Promotes Tendon-Bone Healing Through Regulating Dmp1 Transcription via Targeting Transcription Factor PPARG in BMSCs.

Tong Li, Guanzhi Li, Xiao Deng, Tingrun Zhu, Jiao Jiao Li et al. FASEB journal : official publication of the Federation of American Societies for Experimental Biology 2025-06-15 10.1096/fj.202403274RR
单位: Division of Orthopaedics and Traumatology, Department of Orthopaedics, Nanfang Hospital, Southern Medical University, Guangzhou, China.
通讯: Kairui Zhang @ Division of Orthopaedics and Traumatology, Department of Orthopaedics, Nanfang Hospital, Southern Medical University, Gu [推断]
相关性: 该论文完全未涉及增材制造、多孔结构或生物力学测试，与用户研究方向无关。
边界: This paper is purely biological and pharmacological, with no additive manufacturing, porous structures, or mechanical testing of biomedical implants.
动机· 探索α-细辛脑在肌腱-骨愈合中的作用机制。
方法· 小鼠模型与BMSCs体外实验结合网络药理学和RNA-seq。
结果· αASA通过调控PPARG增强Dmp1转录，促进成骨分化和愈合。
验证· 小鼠模型体内实验和体外细胞实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the therapeutic potential of α-Asarone in tendon-bone healing and its underlying molecular mechanisms.
Method· In vivo mouse model and in vitro BMSC cultures were combined with network pharmacology and RNA-seq to identify targets. Surface plasmon resonance and luciferase assays confirmed PPARG-Dmp1 interaction.
Result· α-Asarone promotes osteogenic differentiation and improves tendon-bone healing by downregulating PPARG, thereby enhancing Dmp1 transcription.
Validation· Animal model (mouse) and in vitro cell culture experiments.
