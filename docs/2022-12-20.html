边界: No implant or biomechanics context, no FEA surrogate for structural mechanics, no uncertainty quantification, no constraint handling.
动机· 用PINN模拟潮汐驱动的港口通道流动，克服传统方法对周期性的限制。
方法· 设计周期性时间假设的PINN，并在训练中重采样评估点以提升小批量性能。
结果· 重采样策略以近零成本改善了模型精度，但湍流近似存在局限。

Motivation· To simulate tidal-driven flow in a port channel using a PINN that enforces periodic temporal behavior, overcoming limitations of conventional numerical methods.

Result· Resampling improves model accuracy at near-zero computational cost, but approximations in the Navier-Stokes equations limit turbulence modeling.
Validation· Validation is performed on a synthetic port channel based on the Santos estuarine system, using simulation as ground truth.
