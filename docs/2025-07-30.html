Two-piece Zirconia Implants: An Office based Retrospective Study with up to 7 and mean 3 Year Follow-Up.

J Tartsch, B Chrcanovic, A Wennerberg, R Kohal, T Albrektsson The International journal of oral & maxillofacial implants 2025-07-30 10.11607/jomi.11469
相关性: 该论文研究了氧化锆种植体的临床结果，与增材制造生物医学材料的研究方向无关。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing (fatigue or static), no porous structure, no stress shielding analysis.
动机· 评估两件式氧化锆种植体的长期临床效果。
方法· 回顾性分析167颗种植体在106名患者中长达7年的数据。
结果· 有效存活率98.2%，边缘骨吸收小，表面粗糙度低。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the long-term clinical outcomes of two-piece zirconia implants as a metal-free alternative to titanium.
Method· A retrospective study analyzed 167 implants placed in 106 patients with up to 88 months follow-up, including survival, marginal bone loss, and surface roughness.
Result· The implants showed a high survival rate of 98.2% with minimal marginal bone loss and low surface roughness.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with life-table survival analysis and descriptive statistics.

Generative Active Learning for Long-tail Trajectory Prediction via Controllable Diffusion Model

相关性: 该论文专注于自动驾驶中的轨迹预测，与FEA替代模型或生物力学植入物设计无关。
边界: This paper addresses trajectory prediction for autonomous vehicles, with no finite element analysis, surrogate modeling, structural mechanics, uncertainty quantification, or biomedical implant context.
动机· 解决自动驾驶轨迹预测中的长尾场景学习困难问题
方法· 提出生成式主动学习框架，利用可控扩散模型增强罕见样本
结果· 在多个数据集上提升长尾样本预测精度，同时保持头部样本性能

English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve trajectory prediction performance on rare long-tail scenarios in autonomous driving without altering model architecture.
Method· Introduces a generative active learning framework that identifies failing tail samples and augments them using a controllable diffusion model with tail-aware guidance.
Result· The method significantly boosts prediction accuracy on tail samples while also enhancing head sample performance on multiple benchmark datasets.
Validation· Validated on WOMD and Argoverse2 datasets using popular backbones QCNet and MTR, demonstrating effectiveness across both tail and head distributions.
