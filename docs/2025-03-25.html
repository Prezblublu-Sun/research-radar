Invertible Koopman neural operator for data-driven modeling of partial differential equations

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark
Yuhong Jin, Andong Cong, Lei Hou, Qiang Gao, Xiangdong Ge et al. arXiv preprint 2025-03-25 link
相关性: 该论文提出的可逆Koopman神经算子方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模，但未涉及植入物设计或约束优化。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling for implant biomechanics, does not handle constraints or uncertainty quantification, and lacks biomedical context.
动机· 现有Koopman算子方法在构建可观测函数及其逆时存在不足，且处理偏微分方程效率低。
方法· 提出可逆Koopman神经算子，利用可逆神经网络同时参数化可观测函数及其逆，并在频率空间学习低频模态演化。
结果· IKNO在数值和真实案例上优于其他神经算子，且具有分辨率不变性。
验证· 通过多个数值基准和真实世界PDE案例验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Existing Koopman operator methods suffer from poor construction of observable functions and their inverses, and are inefficient for PDE modeling.
Method· The Invertible Koopman Neural Operator (IKNO) uses an invertible neural network to jointly parameterize the observable function and its inverse, and learns the evolution of low-frequency modes in frequency space.
Result· IKNO outperforms existing neural operators on numerical and real-world PDE examples while maintaining resolution invariance.
Validation· Validation is performed on multiple numerical benchmarks and real-world PDE datasets.

相关性: 该论文提出了一种数据高效的傅里叶神经算子（FNO）框架，用于预测3D风场和温度场，其方法（如局部特征编码、深度集成不确定性量化）可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模，但未涉及植入物或生物力学问题。
边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, stress shielding, or bone-implant interface; it focuses on urban microclimate CFD surrogate modeling.
动机· 城市微气候CFD模拟计算昂贵，需要快速准确的替代模型。
方法· 提出FLUME-FNO，利用多方向距离特征和局部补丁增强数据，从少量CFD数据学习。

Motivation· Conventional CFD for urban microclimate is computationally prohibitive, motivating a data-efficient surrogate model for rapid prediction.
Method· FLUME-FNO encodes local geometry via Multi-Directional Distance Features and uses patching to augment limited CFD data for training a Fourier Neural Operator.
Result· The model achieves mean absolute errors of 0.2 m/s for wind speed and 0.19 °C for temperature on unseen urban configurations using only 23 CFD simulations.
Validation· A deep ensemble provides uncertainty estimates, demonstrating trustworthy predictions within acceptable accuracy thresholds.

边界: This paper does not address expensive simulation sampling efficiency, constraint handling, or uncertainty quantification in the context of implant/biomechanics design.
动机· 传统因果贝叶斯优化需要已知因果图，限制了实际应用。

结果· 在线性情况下推导出闭式后验，非线性情况使用高斯过程近似，性能与基准相当且可扩展。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Traditional causal Bayesian optimization requires a fully specified causal graph, limiting its applicability in real-world scenarios where such graphs are unknown.
Method· A new method is proposed that learns a Bayesian posterior over the direct causal parents of the target variable, enabling optimization without prior knowledge of the causal graph, with a closed-form posterior for linear cases and a Gaussian process approximation for nonlinear cases.
Result· The proposed method performs competitively with existing benchmarks and scales well to larger graphs, demonstrating practical utility for optimization under causal uncertainty.
Validation· Validation is performed on numerical benchmarks, comparing against standard causal Bayesian optimization methods.
