Motivation· To investigate the association between body mass index and reoperation/failure rates after two-stage exchange for infected total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Retrospective cohort study comparing clinical outcomes across BMI groups.
Result· Higher BMI is correlated with increased reoperations and failures.
Validation· Clinical cohort with statistical analysis.
