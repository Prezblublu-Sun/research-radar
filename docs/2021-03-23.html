英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the accuracy of a new portable accelerometer-based navigation system for cup placement and leg length measurement in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Cup position and intraoperative leg length change were measured using New HipAlign in 60 THAs, and compared with postoperative radiographic and CT measurements.
Result· Mean cup abduction was 40.3° and anteversion 15.8°, with 93% of cups within the Lewinnek safe zone; leg length measurement error was 0.8±3.4 mm.
Validation· Validation was performed by comparing intraoperative navigation data with postoperative pelvic radiographs and CT scans.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To identify changes in reasons for 90-day emergency room visits and readmissions after total joint arthroplasty during a 5-year period with evolving rapid recovery protocols.
Method· A retrospective cohort study comparing 1980 patients (2017-2018) with a previously published cohort of 7466 patients (2013-2017) using univariate analysis.
Result· The rapid recovery protocol reduced surgical readmissions but increased medical readmissions, with falls and gastrointestinal symptoms becoming more common.
Validation· Single-institution retrospective data analysis.
