Study of titanium lattice structures fabricated via selective laser melting

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Save for project mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Nellian Alagu Subramaniam DR-NTU (Nanyang Technological University) 2016-05-30 link
相关性: 该论文研究了通过选择性激光熔化制造的钛合金点阵结构，直接探讨了点阵结构对应力遮挡的影响，并进行了压缩力学测试，但缺乏疲劳性能和表面形貌分析。
边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing, surface roughness characterization, or osseointegration assessment, which are key for long-term implant performance.
动机· 为减少生物植入体的应力遮挡效应，研究点阵结构钛合金的性能。
方法· 用SLM制造cpTi、Ti64和Ti-Ta点阵结构，分析微观结构、硬度及压缩性能。
结果· Ti-Ta点阵结构在强度和弹性模量方面综合表现优于cpTi和Ti64。
验证· 通过压缩测试、显微硬度测试和孔隙率计算进行验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To mitigate stress shielding in biomedical implants, lattice structures of titanium alloys were investigated for their mechanical compatibility with bone.
Method· Commercially pure titanium, Ti-6Al-4V, and titanium-tantalum lattice structures were fabricated via selective laser melting, followed by microstructure, microhardness, and compression property analysis.
Result· Ti-Ta lattice structures exhibited a favorable balance of strength and elastic modulus compared to cpTi and Ti64 lattice structures, making them suitable for implant applications.
Validation· Experimental validation included compression tests, microhardness measurements, and porosity calculations from manufactured samples.
