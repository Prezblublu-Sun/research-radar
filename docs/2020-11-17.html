Data Driven Modeling of Interfacial Traction Separation Relations using a Thermodynamically Consistent Neural Network

Congjie Wei, Jiaxin Zhang, Kenneth M. Liechti, Chenglin Wu arXiv preprint 2020-11-17 link
相关性: 该方法为界面本构建模提供了带物理约束的神经网络框架，可直接迁移至植入物-骨界面模拟中的代理模型构建。
边界: This paper does not specifically address implant biomechanics or stress shielding, and lacks experimental validation in a biomedical context.
动机· 现有模型难以捕捉混合模式下的界面牵引-分离关系
方法· 提出热力学一致神经网络，加入守恒和耗散约束并优化权重
结果· 生成的混合模式牵引-分离面与实验数据吻合良好
验证· 通过物理约束比较验证方法可行性
English summary & terms
Motivation· To model interfacial traction-separation relations under mixed-mode conditions where existing theoretical models fail, especially with sparse data.
Method· A thermodynamically consistent neural network (TCNN) is developed, incorporating constraints of thermodynamic consistency, maximum energy dissipation path control, and J-integral conservation into the loss function, with Bayesian optimization to tune constraint weights.
Result· The TCNN produces well-behaved mixed-mode traction-separation surfaces that maintain fidelity to experimental data while satisfying physical constraints.
Validation· The approach is validated by comparing model predictions against a range of physical constraints and demonstrating convergence with multiple constraints.

Validation· The proposed method is validated on numerical benchmark cases of subsurface flow with comparison to standard surrogates.

Effect of process parameters on the microstructure and mechanical properties of AA2024 fabricated using selective laser melting

Mulla Ahmet Pekok, Rossitza Setchi, Michael J. Ryan, Quanquan Han, Dongdong Gu The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology 2020-11-17 10.1007/s00170-020-06346-y
单位: Cardiff School of Engineering, Cardiff University, Cardiff, CF24 3AA, UK
通讯: Mulla Ahmet Pekok @ Cardiff School of Engineering, Cardiff University, Cardiff, CF24 3AA, UK
相关性: 该论文研究AA2024铝合金的选择性激光熔化工艺参数，不涉及生物医学植入物或多孔结构，与用户方向无关。
边界: This paper does not involve biomedical materials, porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration; it focuses solely on SLM processing of an aluminum alloy for non-biomedical applications.
动机· 探索SLM工艺参数对AA2024铝合金微观结构和力学性能的影响
方法· 改变激光功率、扫描间距和扫描速度，分析能量密度对致密度和显微硬度的影响
结果· 低能量密度导致裂纹和孔隙，高能量密度产生气孔；低能量密度获得细晶组织，显微硬度最高
验证· 通过相对密度、阿基米德密度和显微硬度测试进行验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates the effect of selective laser melting process parameters on the microstructure and mechanical properties of AA2024 aluminum alloy to overcome manufacturing challenges.
Method· Laser power, hatch spacing, and scanning speed were varied to analyze the influence of energy density on relative density, Archimedes density, and microhardness.
Result· Low energy density caused cracks and porosity due to incomplete fusion, while high energy density led to gas pores; lower energy density produced finer grains and higher microhardness.
Validation· Validation was performed through relative density measurements, Archimedes density, and microhardness testing.
