单位: Department of Plastic Surgery, Union Hospital, Tongji Medical College, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan 430022, People's Republic of China
通讯: Jiaming Sun @ Department of Plastic Surgery, Union Hospital, Tongji Medical College, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wu
相关性: 该论文关注乳腺组织工程，使用聚氨酯支架匹配软组织刚度，未涉及髋关节植入物的应力遮挡或疲劳问题。
边界: This paper does not address stress shielding, does not use LPBF metals, does not include fatigue testing, and does not report surface roughness or osseointegration.
动机· 设计力学性能匹配乳腺组织的3D打印支架

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To design 3D-printed scaffolds with mechanical properties matching native breast tissue for breast tissue engineering.
Method· Four polyurethane scaffolds with crystal lattice-like architectures (N5S4, N9S8, N7S6, N4S6) were designed and fabricated by additive manufacturing.
Result· The softest scaffold (N5S4) had compressive modulus similar to native breast tissue, and showed higher adipose survival and vascularization in a rat model compared to a stiffer counterpart.
Validation· The scaffolds were validated through compressive mechanical testing, in vivo implantation in nude rats with delayed fat injection, and finite element analysis of deformation and stress distribution.

Method· A registry-based cohort study using data from the Norwegian Arthroplasty Register (2005–2017) including 66,995 primary THAs.

Validation· Validation through large-scale clinical registry data with multivariable regression.
