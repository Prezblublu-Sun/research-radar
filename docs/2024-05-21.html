单位: School of Materials Science and Engineering, North University of China, Taiyuan 030051, China
通讯: Peikang Bai @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, North University of China, Taiyuan 030051, China  |  Zhanyong Zhao @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, North University of China, Taiyuan 030051, China
相关性: 该论文研究了TiC/Ti6Al4V复合材料经SLM成型及热处理后的微观结构和硬度变化，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡、疲劳或骨整合等生物医学植入物关键指标。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures for stiffness matching, fatigue testing, surface roughness, or osseointegration, which are critical for biomedical implant applications.
动机· 研究热处理对SLM成型TiC/Ti6Al4V复合材料微观结构和硬度的影响。
方法· 采用SLM制备Ti6Al4V和TiC/Ti6Al4V复合材，并进行固溶和时效热处理。
结果· 固溶温度升高使马氏体转变为板条α相，时效时间延长导致强化相粗化、硬度降低。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of solution and aging treatments on the microstructural evolution and mechanical properties of TiC/Ti6Al4V composites fabricated by selective laser melting.
Method· Ti6Al4V and TiC/Ti6Al4V composites were manufactured by selective laser melting, followed by solution treatment at varying temperatures and aging treatment for different durations.
Result· Increasing solution temperature transformed acicular martensite into plate-bar alpha phase, while prolonged aging time coarsened the strengthening phase and significantly reduced microhardness.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated through microhardness measurements post heat treatment.
