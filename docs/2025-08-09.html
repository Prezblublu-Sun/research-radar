Anthony Bellotti, Xindi Zhao arXiv preprint 2025-08-09 link
相关性: 该论文讨论共形预测在可信AI中的应用，但与替代建模或生物力学设计的直接关联性较低。
边界: This paper is a review of conformal prediction for trustworthy AI with no application to surrogate modeling, FEA, or biomechanics design; it lacks any method for handling constraints or simulation efficiency.
动机· 综述共形预测在可信AI中的潜力

English summary & terms
Motivation· This review explores how conformal prediction can contribute to trustworthy AI beyond its marginal validity.
Method· The authors survey conformal prediction theory and its extensions for generalization risk and AI governance.
Result· They demonstrate that conformal prediction provides well-calibrated uncertainty quantification and can be used for bias identification.
Validation· Numerical experiments are provided to illustrate calibration performance and bias mitigation.

Validation· Simulation experiments validate the algorithm's performance in complex environments.

INNOVATIVE APPROACHES TO TELEREHABILITATION AFTER TOTAL HIP ARTHROPLASTY: EVALUATION OF THE EFFECTIVENESS OF A MOBILE APPLICATION WITH EXERCISE MONITORING

Zhandos Kurban, Sholpan Bulekbayeva, Nataliya Slivkina, A. A. Aldakuatova, D. Z. Seitenova Medicine and ecology 2025-08-09 10.59598/me-2305-6053-2025-115-2-93-97
单位: Np JSC «Astana Medical University»
通讯: Zhandos Kurban @ Np JSC «Astana Medical University»
相关性: 该论文仅探讨全髋关节置换后的远程康复效果，未涉及假体力学或骨-植入物界面分析。
边界: This paper focuses on telerehabilitation outcomes after THA and contains no biomechanical modeling, stress shielding analysis, micromotion quantification, or implant design considerations.
动机· 评估远程康复在全髋置换后的效果是否优于传统康复
方法· 前瞻性随机对照试验，50例患者分为远程康复组和对照组
结果· 远程康复组在生活质量、疼痛和肌力方面显著改善
验证· 临床问卷和量表的前瞻性对比研究
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare the effectiveness of telerehabilitation versus standard rehabilitation following total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A prospective randomized controlled trial enrolled 50 patients undergoing primary THA, allocated to telerehabilitation (n=25) or standard care (n=25).
Result· The telerehabilitation group showed statistically significant improvements in quality of life, pain scores, and muscle strength compared to controls.
Validation· Clinical validation using SF-12, VAS, MRC scales, and goniometry in a prospective comparative design.
