Conformal Prediction for Stochastic Decision-Making of PV Power in Electricity Markets

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Background only none UQ
Yvet Renkema, Nico Brinkel, Tarek Alskaif arXiv preprint 2024-03-29 link
相关性: 该论文将共形预测应用于光伏电力市场，与FEA替代建模或生物力学设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, implant biomechanics, or any biomedical/mechanical design optimization; it focuses on energy forecasting and market bidding.
动机· 提高光伏发电日前预测的准确性以优化电力市场投标。
方法· 使用共形预测量化机器学习点预测的不确定性，生成预测区间和累积分布函数。
结果· 结合k近邻和Mondrian分箱的共形预测优于线性分位数回归，在多种投标策略下实现高利润和低能量不平衡。
验证· 在光伏功率数据集上比较不同共形预测变体与基准方法。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To enhance day-ahead photovoltaic power predictions for improved electricity market participation under uncertainty.
Method· Conformal prediction variants are applied to quantify uncertainty of machine learning point forecasts, producing prediction intervals and cumulative distribution functions for optimal bidding.
Result· Conformal prediction with k-nearest neighbors and Mondrian binning outperforms linear quantile regressors, achieving up to 93% of potential profit with minimal energy imbalance when combined with expected utility maximization and conditional value at risk.
Validation· Empirical evaluation on photovoltaic power data comparing multiple conformal prediction methods and bidding strategies.
