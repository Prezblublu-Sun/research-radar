Differential proteomics profile of microcapillary networks in response to sound pattern-driven local cell density enhancement

Nicola Di Marzio, R. Tognato, Elena Della Bella, Valentina De Giorgis, Marcello Manfredi et al. Biomaterials and Biosystems 2024-03-29 10.1016/j.bbiosy.2024.100094
单位: AO Research Institute Davos, 7270 Davos, Switzerland
通讯: Tiziano Serra @ AO Research Institute Davos, 7270 Davos, Switzerland
相关性: 该论文使用声波图案化构建微毛细管网络，未涉及AI或生物打印工作流，仅作为生物制造技术背景参考。
边界: This paper does not embed AI into any bioprinting workflow stage, lacks experimental validation of an AI model, and does not advance towards autonomous decision-making in bioprinting.
动机· 利用声波图案化调控微毛细管网形成，探索局部细胞密度的影响。
方法· 声波诱导细胞凝聚并结合蛋白质组学分析差异表达蛋白。
结果· 细胞密度梯度引导毛细管样结构形成，VEGF-A是关键上游调控因子。
验证· 体外细胞实验与蛋白质组学分析验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of sound-induced local cell density enhancement on microcapillary network formation and its proteomic profile.
Method· Sound-based hydrodynamic forces patterned HUVEC and pericytes into defined geometries, followed by proteomic analysis and pathway prediction.
Result· Local density gradients guided capillary-like structure formation, and VEGF-A was identified as a major upstream regulator.
Validation· In vitro experiments with HUVEC and pericytes combined with numerical simulation and proteomic profiling.

单位: Center of Dental Medicine, Oral Biotechnology & Bioengineering, University of Zurich, Plattenstrasse 11, 8032 Zurich, Switzerland
通讯: Franz E. Weber @ Center for Applied Biotechnology and Molecular Medicine (CABMM), University of Zurich, Winterthurerstrasse 190, 8057 Zur
相关性: 该论文与用户的生物制造方向不直接相关，因为它关注的是陶瓷支架的外泌体功能化，而非机械性能或植入稳定性。
边界: does not address stress shielding, stiffness matching, LPBF fabrication, mechanical testing, or fatigue; focuses on exosome functionalization for bone tissue engineering.

结果· 体内实验未观察到外泌体显著促进骨长入

English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance bone healing by incorporating exosomes derived from bone marrow mesenchymal stromal cells into ceramic scaffolds.
Method· BMSC-derived exosomes were loaded onto 3D-printed ceramic Gyroid scaffolds, and their release kinetics and osteogenic potential were evaluated.
Result· In vivo implantation showed no significant improvement in bone ingrowth with exosome-loaded scaffolds compared to controls.
Validation· The study combined in vitro cell assays and in vivo animal implantation to assess the osteoconductive properties.

Coaxial 3D Bioprinting Process Research and Performance Tests on Vascular Scaffolds

单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, Hangzhou Dianzi University, Hangzhou 310018, China
通讯: Manli Xu @ The 2nd Affiliated Hospital of Zhejiang Chinese Medical University, Hangzhou 310005, China  |  Rougang Zhou @ Mstar Technologies, Inc., Room 406, Building 19, Hangzhou Future Science and Technology City (Haichuang Park), No. 998,
相关性: 该论文未涉及任何人工智能方法，与AI生物打印方向无关。
边界: This paper does not use AI/ML at any stage of the bioprinting workflow; it relies on theoretical modeling and experimental optimization instead.
动机· 开发满足血管支架要求的3D打印血管组织。
方法· 同轴3D生物打印结合模具法，通过理论模型和仿真优化参数。
结果· 制备的血管支架降解性、吸水性及力学性能满足要求，细胞相容性良好。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To fabricate vascularized tissue with suitable biocompatibility, mechanical performance, and complex vascular network structure for treating cardiovascular diseases.
Method· A coaxial 3D bioprinting method combined with a mold method is proposed; theoretical models of coaxial jet and vascular network are developed and simulated to obtain optimal process parameters.
Result· The fabricated vascular scaffolds show satisfactory degradability, water absorption, and mechanical properties, and support HUVEC proliferation over seven days.
Validation· Cellular experiments with HUVECs and fluorescence microscopy confirm biocompatibility.

Structure and mechanical properties of PR-03N18K9M5TYu steel grade fabricated by selective laser melting and post-processing

A. O. Kayasova, F. A. Baskov, T. A. Lobova, Е. А. Левашов Izvestiya Non-Ferrous Metallurgy 2024-03-29 10.17073/0021-3438-2024-1-70-80
单位: National University of Science and Technology «MISIS»
通讯: A. O. Kayasova @ National University of Science and Technology «MISIS»
相关性: 该论文研究的是PR-03N18K9M5TYu钢的SLM制备和后处理，不涉及任何生物医学应用或骨科植入物的应力屏蔽问题。
边界: No porous structure for stress shielding matching, no biomedical context, no fatigue testing, no surface roughness or osseointegration evaluation, and the material is a steel alloy not typically used for biomedical implants.
动机· 研究SLM制备的PR-03N18K9M5TYu钢后处理对结构性能的影响
方法· 采用SLM制备样品，进行热等静压和热处理，并进行拉伸和硬度测试
结果· 后处理显著提高了强度和弹性模量，最优结构为细晶均匀组织
验证· 通过拉伸测试、硬度测量和断口扫描验证性能
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of hot isostatic pressing and heat treatment on the structure and mechanical properties of PR-03N18K9M5TYu steel fabricated by selective laser melting.
Method· Samples were fabricated by SLM and subjected to HIP followed by hardening and aging, then characterized by tensile testing, indentation, and microstructural analysis.
Result· The combined post-processing led to a fine-grained homogeneous structure with improved strength and elastic modulus, attributed to dispersed hardening from Ni3Ti precipitates.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated through tensile tests (strength and ductility) and indentation measurements (hardness and modulus), with fractographic analysis showing ductile dimple fracture.

The Effects of Heat Treatment on the Microstructure and Mechanical Properties of a Selective Laser Melted AlCoFeNi Medium-Entropy Alloy

Xinyang Han, Xiangwei Li, Bokai Liao, Youzhao Zhang, Lei Xu et al. Materials 2024-03-29 10.3390/ma17071582
单位: Centre of Excellence for Advanced Materials, Dongguan 523808, China
通讯: Xiangwei Li @ Centre of Excellence for Advanced Materials, Dongguan 523808, China  |  Xingpeng Guo @ School of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering, Guangzhou University, Guangzhou 510006, China
相关性: 该论文研究SLM制备的AlCoFeNi中熵合金的热处理效应，与生物医学植入物的应力屏蔽、孔隙结构、疲劳等方向完全无关。
边界: This paper does not address biomedical implants, porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration; it is a pure materials science study on a non-biomedical alloy.
动机· 研究热处理对SLM制备的AlCoFeNi中熵合金微观结构和力学性能的影响。
方法· 采用SLM制备合金，并进行不同温度和时间的老化处理。
结果· 450°C老化4h后，获得BCC+B2+FCC多相结构，硬度、强度显著提升，韧性良好。
验证· 通过硬度测试和拉伸试验验证力学性能变化。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of heat treatment on the microstructure and mechanical properties of an SLM-fabricated AlCoFeNi medium-entropy alloy.
Method· The alloy was fabricated by selective laser melting and subjected to aging at various temperatures and durations.
Result· Aging at 450°C for 4 h produced a complex BCC + B2 + FCC structure with enhanced hardness (45.7 HRC), ultimate tensile strength (1535 MPa), and yield strength (1489 MPa) while maintaining good toughness.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated through hardness testing and uniaxial tensile tests.
