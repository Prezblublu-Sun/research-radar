边界: This paper does not apply the method to biomedical implant design or biomechanics FEA, nor does it handle multi-objective constraints typical in implant optimization.

Motivation· To investigate changes in sexual activity and its impact on patients with stiff hips and their partners before and after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· An anonymous survey was distributed to 65 heterosexual patients and their partners, assessing sexual satisfaction, quality of life, and influencing factors pre- and post-operatively.
Result· Total hip arthroplasty significantly improved sexual satisfaction, quality of life, and dyadic relationships for both patients and their partners.
Validation· Validation is based on survey responses and statistical analysis, with no experimental or computational biomechanical validation.
