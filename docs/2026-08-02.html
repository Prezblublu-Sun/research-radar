相关性: 该论文展示了PINN在岩土材料多物理场重建中的应用，其方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模，但未涉及植入物或生物医学场景。
边界: This paper does not address implant mechanics, bone-implant interface, or any biomedical context; it focuses on geotechnical freezing problems, and lacks uncertainty quantification and constraint handling beyond simple displacement constraints.

结果· 同时预测温度、冻结分数和位移场，位移预测精度高（R2=0.993），温度预测较差（R2=0.600）。

Validation· The model was validated against laboratory freezing experiments on saturated subbase materials from the Dashixia Project.
