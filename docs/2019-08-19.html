单位: ARC Training Centre for Lightweight Automotive Structures (ATLAS), Australian Research Council Grant IC160100032, Australia
通讯: Martin Leary @ ARC Training Centre in Additive Biomanufacturing, Australia
相关性: 该综述总结了SLM点阵结构的力学性能，但未专门针对生物医学植入物的应力屏蔽或骨整合进行讨论。
边界: This paper is a general review of SLM lattice structures without specific focus on biomedical implants, stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration.

Motivation· There is a lack of overarching analysis on the mechanical response of SLM lattice structures despite increasing research.

Result· Correlations between relative density and mechanical properties follow the Gibson-Ashby model across many unit cell topologies.
Validation· The study is a meta-analysis of existing literature without new experimental validation.

边界: No biomedical context, no porous implant design, no fatigue testing, no osseointegration or surface morphology analysis.

Method· Experimental peel and normal strength tests combined with numerical thermal simulation of AlSi10Mg block supports.

Validation· Experimental mechanical testing and numerical thermal analysis.
