通讯: Karen Synnøve Ohm @ University of Stavanger
相关性: 该论文比较了地震反演中的不确定性量化方法，与生物力学代理建模和植入物设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on seismic inversion uncertainty quantification, not on surrogate modeling for expensive simulations, constraint handling, or biomechanics/implant design.
动机· 比较三种不确定性量化方法在非线性地震反演中的表现。
方法· 使用Hessian矩阵、迭代集成平滑器和集成数据同化三种方法进行不确定性估计。
结果· Hessian和EDA方法趋势一致但幅度略有不同，iES方法不确定性随深度增加。
验证· 基于卷积正演模型的数值基准测试。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare three uncertainty quantification methods for non-linear seismic inversion with a convolutional forward model.
Method· Uncertainty is estimated via the Hessian matrix of the least squares function, iterative ensemble smoother (iES), and ensemble data assimilation (EDA).
Result· Hessian and EDA show similar trends with slight magnitude differences, while iES uncertainty increases with depth and is narrower for shallow layers.
Validation· Numerical benchmark using a convolutional forward model.
