A study of selective laser melting process for pure zinc and Zn10mg alloy on process parameters and mechanical properties

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Muhammad Waqas, Dingyong He, Zhen Tan, Peng Yang, Mu Gao et al. Rapid Prototyping Journal 2023-08-11 10.1108/rpj-04-2022-0138
单位: Faculty of Materials and Manufacturing, Beijing University of Technology, Beijing, China
通讯: Muhammad Waqas @ Faculty of Materials and Manufacturing, Beijing University of Technology, Beijing, China
相关性: 论文研究了用于生物医学植入物的锌合金的SLM工艺，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or osseointegration; it focuses on dense zinc alloys.
动机· 为生物医用可降解锌合金植入物获得高力学性能。
方法· 采用SLM技术结合响应面法优化工艺参数，制备高致密度锌和Zn10Mg试样。
结果· 最优参数下相对密度达99.63%，压缩强度214±13 MPa。
验证· 通过压缩测试和腐蚀行为分析验证性能。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To fabricate zinc and Zn-10Mg alloys with high mechanical properties via selective laser melting for biomedical implant applications.
Method· Response surface methodology was used to optimize SLM parameters to achieve high relative density in pure Zn and Zn-10Mg samples.
Result· Under optimal processing conditions, a relative density of 99.63% and compressive strength of 214±13 MPa were obtained.
Validation· The mechanical properties were validated through compression testing and corrosion behavior analysis.

边界: This paper focuses on PDE discovery for soft sensors in industrial processes, with no application to implant biomechanics, FEA surrogate modeling, or uncertainty quantification.

边界: This paper does not apply Gaussian process regression to FEA surrogate modeling, does not handle constraints or uncertainty quantification for design, and lacks any biomechanics or implant-related context.
动机· 最大熵分布中拉格朗日乘子的计算是瓶颈，寻求更高效的代理模型。

Method· Gaussian process regression with various kernels is used to map given moments to Lagrange multipliers, with hyperparameters optimized via log-likelihood.
Result· The trained surrogate achieves accurate closure for non-equilibrium distributions in BGK and Boltzmann relaxation tests.
Validation· Validation is performed on numerical benchmarks of kinetic equation relaxation problems.
