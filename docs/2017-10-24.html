Motivation· To investigate the influence of porosity and pore size on the mechanical properties and bone ingrowth of porous titanium alloy for orthopedic implants.
Method· Porous Ti6Al4V scaffolds with three porosity levels (68.3%, 75.4%, 83.1%) and corresponding pore sizes were fabricated via coagulant-assisted foaming using egg-white foam, followed by mechanical testing and in vivo implantation in rabbit femoral defects.
Result· Samples with 75.4% porosity and 178 μm average pore size exhibited the best osteogenic effects and significantly higher bone formation (52% more bone volume and >90% deeper penetration) compared to empty defects after 12 weeks.
Validation· Validation included compressive mechanical testing, in vitro osteogenesis assays (hMSCs), and in vivo micro-CT quantification with histological staining (H&E, Masson trichrome).
