Bayesian Calibration, Validation and Uncertainty Quantification for Predictive Modelling of Tumour Growth: A Tutorial

Joe Collis, Anthony J. Connor, Marcin Paczkowski, Pavitra Kannan, Joe Pitt‐Francis et al. Bulletin of Mathematical Biology 2017-03-13 10.1007/s11538-017-0258-5
单位: School of Mathematical Sciences, University of Nottingham, Nottingham, UK. Joe.Collis@nottingham.ac.uk
通讯: Joe Collis @ School of Mathematical Sciences, University of Nottingham, Nottingham, UK. Joe.Collis@nottingham.ac.uk
相关性: 该论文展示了贝叶斯校准与不确定性量化在生物医学预测建模中的完整流程，方法可迁移至植入物力学代理模型的UQ。
边界: This paper focuses on tumor growth modeling, not on FEA surrogate modeling or implant biomechanics, and lacks constraint handling or optimization.

方法· 采用贝叶斯方法结合MCMC进行模型参数校准与验证。
结果· 展示了校准与UQ如何提升预测可靠性并量化不确定性。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To provide a comprehensive tutorial on Bayesian calibration, validation, and uncertainty quantification for predictive models of tumor growth.
Method· A Bayesian framework with Markov Chain Monte Carlo sampling is employed to calibrate model parameters and quantify predictive uncertainty.
Result· The tutorial demonstrates how calibration and UQ improve prediction reliability and provide uncertainty bounds.
Validation· Validation is performed using synthetic data and a real tumor growth case study.
