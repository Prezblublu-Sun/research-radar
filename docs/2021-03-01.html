Porous structure design and mechanical behavior analysis based on TPMS for customized root analogue implant.

Kaile Song, Zhaohui Wang, Jing Lan, Songhua Ma Journal of the mechanical behavior of biomedical materials 2021-03-01 10.1016/j.jmbbm.2020.104222
单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, Shandong University, PR China.
通讯: Songhua Ma @ School of Mechanical Engineering, Shandong University, PR China; Shenzhen Research Institute of Shandong University, PR
相关性: 该论文直接针对增材制造生物医学植入物的应力遮挡问题，通过TPMS多孔结构设计降低弹性模量，并进行了力学测试和有限元验证。
边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing, surface roughness characterization, or experimental osseointegration evaluation.
动机· 传统实心牙种植体弹性模量过高导致应力遮挡，需设计多孔结构以匹配骨组织。
方法· 基于TPMS设计不同孔隙率（30%-60%）的P和G结构立方样品，通过压缩实验和有限元分析评估力学性能与应力分布。
结果· 30%和40%孔隙率的P和G结构消除了应力遮挡效应，屈服强度高于皮质骨。
验证· 通过压缩实验测量杨氏模量、泊松比和屈服强度，结合FEA验证应力分布。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Solid dental implants cause stress shielding due to high Young's modulus, necessitating porous structures to match bone stiffness.
Method· P and G TPMS structures with four porosities (30%-60%) were designed, fabricated as cubic samples, and characterized via compression tests and finite element analysis for stress distribution.
Result· P and G structures with 30% and 40% porosity eliminated stress shielding and exhibited yield strengths exceeding cortical bone.
Validation· Experimental compression tests provided Young's modulus, Poisson's ratio, and yield strength; FEA validated the stress distribution at the bone-implant interface.
