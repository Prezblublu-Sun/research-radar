High strain rate mechanical behavior of Ti-6Al-4V octet lattice structures additively manufactured by selective laser melting (SLM)

Sindhura Gangireddy, Mageshwari Komarasamy, Eric J. Faierson, Rajiv S. Mishra Materials Science and Engineering A 2018-12-24 10.1016/j.msea.2018.12.101
单位: Advanced Materials and Manufacturing Processes Institute, University of North Texas, Denton, TX 76207, United States
通讯: Sindhura Gangireddy @ Advanced Materials and Manufacturing Processes Institute, University of North Texas, Denton, TX 76207, United States
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM制造的Ti-6Al-4V八面体点阵结构在高应变率下的力学行为，与用户关注的LPBF制造和力学测试相关，但未涉及应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper does not address stress shielding, stiffness matching, fatigue behavior, surface roughness, or osseointegration, which are core to the user's direction.
动机· 研究SLM制造的Ti-6Al-4V八面体点阵在高应变率下的力学行为。
方法· 通过SLM制备不同相对密度的八面体点阵，进行准静态和动态压缩测试。
结果· 点阵的屈服强度和能量吸收具有应变率敏感性，且随相对密度增加而增强。
验证· 实验力学测试验证了本构模型。

Motivation· To investigate the high strain rate mechanical behavior of Ti-6Al-4V octet lattice structures fabricated by selective laser melting.
Method· Octet lattice structures with varying relative densities were fabricated via SLM and subjected to quasi-static and dynamic compression tests.
Result· The yield strength and energy absorption of the lattices exhibited strain rate sensitivity and increased with relative density.
Validation· Experimental mechanical testing validated the constitutive model.

Effect of Hatch Spacing on Melt Pool and As-built Quality During Selective Laser Melting of Stainless Steel: Modeling and Experimental Approaches

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Zhichao Dong, Yabo Liu, Weibin Wen, Jingran Ge, Jun Liang Materials 2018-12-24 10.3390/ma12010050
单位: Beijing Key Laboratory of Lightweight Multifunctional Composite Materials and Structures, Beijing Institute of Technology, Beijing 100081, China
通讯: Jingran Ge @ Beijing Key Laboratory of Lightweight Multifunctional Composite Materials and Structures, Beijing Institute of Technolog  |  Jun Liang @ Beijing Key Laboratory of Lightweight Multifunctional Composite Materials and Structures, Beijing Institute of Technolog
相关性: 该论文研究SLM工艺参数对熔池和成形质量的影响，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或生物医学植入物。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or osseointegration; it focuses on process parameters for dense 316L SS parts.
动机· 研究扫描间距对SLM成形316L不锈钢熔池及质量的影响。
方法· 结合三维有限元模型与实验，分析不同扫描间距下的温度场、熔池尺寸和微观结构。
结果· 扫描间距增大导致熔池深度增加、宽度减小，优化间距100μm可获得全致密光滑表面。
验证· 通过实验验证模拟预测的最优扫描间距下的致密度和表面质量。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of hatch spacing on melt pool characteristics and as-built quality in selective laser melting of 316L stainless steel.
Method· A combined 3D finite element model and experimental approach was used to study temperature field, melt pool geometry, and microstructure under varying hatch spacing.
Result· Increasing hatch spacing increased melt pool depth and decreased width; an optimized hatch spacing of 100 μm yielded fully dense parts with smooth surfaces.
Validation· Experimental validation confirmed the simulated optimal hatch spacing produced high relative density and surface quality.
