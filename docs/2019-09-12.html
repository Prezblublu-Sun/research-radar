Biocompatible Glycine‐Assisted Catalysis of the Sol‐Gel Process: Development of Cell‐Embedded Hydrogels

Laurine Valot, Marie Maumus, Titouan Montheil, Jean Martínez, Danièle Noël et al. ChemPlusChem 2019-09-12 10.1002/cplu.201900509
单位: IBMM Univ Montpellier CNRS ENSCM Montpellier France
通讯: Ahmad Mehdi @ ICGM Univ Montpellier CNRS ENSCM Montpellier France  |  Gilles Subra @ IBMM Univ Montpellier CNRS ENSCM Montpellier France
相关性: 该论文涉及生物墨水的制备，但未融入任何AI技术。
边界: No AI integration in any bioprinting workflow stage; purely chemical catalysis and material characterization.
动机· 提出一种通用的溶胶-凝胶3D生物打印方法
方法· 筛选亲核催化剂，测量粘度变化
结果· 甘氨酸和NaF协同作用，实现细胞封装
验证· 小鼠间充质干细胞包埋实验验证生物相容性
English summary & terms
Motivation· To propose a generic method for sol-gel 3D bioprinting under biocompatible conditions.
Method· Screened nucleophilic catalysts and monitored viscosity over time to study gelation kinetics.
Result· Glycine and NaF showed synergistic catalytic effect, enabling cell embedding without toxicity.
Validation· Biocompatibility validated by embedding primary mouse mesenchymal stem cells in the hydrogel.

Laser Welding of SLM-Manufactured Tubes Made of IN625 and IN718

Torsten Jokisch, Angelina Marko, Sergej Gook, Ömer Üstündağ, Andrey Gumenyuk et al. Materials 2019-09-12 10.3390/ma12182967
单位: Siemens AG, Huttenstr. 12, 10553 Berlin, Germany
通讯: Angelina Marko @ Fraunhofer Institut für Produktionsanlagen und Konstruktionstechnik, Pascalstr. 8-9, 10587 Berlin, Germany
相关性: 该论文涉及SLM制造和力学测试，但未涉及生物医学植入物、应力遮挡、疲劳或骨整合等核心方向。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration in a biomedical context.
动机· 研究激光焊接SLM制造的镍基合金管的可行性。
方法· 用Yb:YAG盘式激光焊接SLM制造的Inconel 625和718管，并检测残余应力和缺陷。
结果· 焊接质量良好，但最终搭接区出现气孔。
验证· 通过残余应力测量和缺陷检查验证焊接质量。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the feasibility of laser beam welding for joining SLM-manufactured nickel-based alloy tubes.
Method· SLM-manufactured Inconel 625 and 718 tubes were welded using a Yb:YAG disk laser and examined for residual stresses and defects.
Result· Good weld quality was achieved except for pores in the final overlap area due to capillary instabilities.
Validation· Experimental residual stress and defect analysis validated the welding quality.

相关性: 该论文研究了TiO2纳米管侧向间距对成骨细胞分化的影响，属于生物材料表面改性领域，与用户的增材制造生物医学植入物方向关联较弱。
边界: This paper does not involve LPBF fabrication, mechanical testing, fatigue analysis, or porous implant design; it focuses solely on nanotopography effects on cell behavior.
动机· 研究TiO2纳米管侧向间距对骨再生的影响。
方法· 制备78 nm直径、18和80 nm间距的TiO2纳米管，培养MC3T3-E1细胞。
结果· 较大间距增强成骨分化标志物表达（碱性磷酸酶、骨桥蛋白、骨钙素）。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of TiO2 nanotube lateral spacing on pre-osteoblast cell behavior and osseointegration potential.
Method· TiO2 nanotubes with ~78 nm diameter and lateral spacings of 18 nm and 80 nm were fabricated, and MC3T3-E1 murine pre-osteoblast cells were cultured on them.
Result· Larger lateral spacing (80 nm) enhanced osteogenic differentiation, with increased expression of alkaline phosphatase, osteopontin, and osteocalcin.
Validation· In vitro cell assays including viability, morphology, actin cytoskeleton, focal adhesions, and differentiation markers.

Mechanical and rheological characterization of hydrogels and hydrogel precursors for cartilage tissue engineering and biofabrication

Alina Weizel, Thomas Distler, Christian Polley, Rainer Detsch, Aldo R. Boccaccini et al. Transactions on Additive Manufacturing Meets Medicine 2019-09-12 link
单位: Chair of Microfluidics University of Rostock Rostock Germany
通讯: Alina Weizel @ Chair of Microfluidics University of Rostock Rostock Germany
相关性: 该论文仅涉及水凝胶的力学和流变学表征，未涉及任何人工智能或机器学习方法，与AI生物打印方向无关。
边界: This paper contains no AI/ML model, no integration into bioprinting workflow, and no autonomous decision-making; it is purely experimental characterization.
动机· 表征用于软骨组织工程的水凝胶力学和流变性质。
方法· 对ADA-GEL等三种水凝胶进行单轴压缩和流变测试。
结果· 测得杨氏模量9–21 kPa，松弛时间4.5–143.5秒。
验证· 通过实验力学测试验证材料性能差异。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To characterize the mechanical and rheological properties of hydrogels for cartilage tissue engineering and biofabrication.
Method· Uniaxial compression and rheological tests were performed on three hydrogel materials (ADA-GEL, alginate-gelatine, Chondrofillerliquid).
Result· Young's moduli ranged from 9 to 21 kPa and relaxation times (τ1/2) from 4.5 to 143.5 seconds, with ADA-GEL showing shear-thinning and temperature-dependent printability.
Validation· Experimental mechanical testing under simulated physiological conditions provided the validation.
