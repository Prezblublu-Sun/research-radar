Xiaojie Cui, Xiaoxin Li, Changqing Hu, Dingguo Zhao, Yan Liu et al. Metals 2025-05-14 10.3390/met15050541
单位: School of Metallurgy and Energy, Metallurgical Engineering, North China University of Science and Technology, Tangshan 063210, China
通讯: Changqing Hu @ School of Metallurgy and Energy, Metallurgical Engineering, North China University of Science and Technology, Tangshan 0
相关性: 该论文研究系泊链钢的SLM工艺，与生物医学植入物无关，无任何生物医学背景。
边界: No porous structure for bone ingrowth, no stress shielding analysis, no fatigue testing, no biomedical or implant-related context.
动机· 研究SLM制备系泊链钢的工艺参数对性能的影响
方法· 改变激光功率、扫描速度等参数进行SLM实验
结果· 优化参数下获得最高综合力学性能
验证· 通过显微硬度、拉伸、冲击等测试验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of selective laser melting parameters on the microstructure and mechanical properties of 22MnCrNiMo mooring chain steel.
Method· SLM experiments with varied laser power, scanning speed, layer thickness, and scanning spacing.
Result· Optimal parameters yield microhardness of 513.2 HV0.5, tensile strength of 1223 MPa, yield strength of 1114 MPa, elongation of 8.5%, and impact energy of 127 J.
Validation· Validation through microhardness testing, tensile testing, and impact testing.

Total Hip Arthroplasty: The Influence of Different Management Structures in Germany and the Netherlands on Preoperative Expectations, Self-Efficacy, and Postoperative Satisfaction

Yvet Mooiweer, Roy E. Stewart, B.W. Dijkstra, Robert Oosterveld, Martin Stevens et al. Cureus 2025-05-14 10.7759/cureus.84088
单位: Department of Health Services Research, School of Medicine and Health Sciences, Carl von Ossietzky Universität Oldenburg, Oldenburg, DEU
通讯: Yvet Mooiweer @ Department of Health Services Research, School of Medicine and Health Sciences, Carl von Ossietzky Universität Oldenburg
相关性: 该论文研究THA患者管理对期望和满意度的影响，与髋关节植入物生物力学无关。
边界: This paper does not contain any biomechanical analysis of the femoral stem, stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics.
动机· 比较德国和荷兰THA管理对患者期望和满意度的影响
方法· 跨国前瞻性多中心观察研究，使用问卷评估
结果· 术前自我效能与满意度相关，但国家因素无影响
验证· 临床观察数据统计回归
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how different THA management structures in Germany and the Netherlands affect preoperative expectations, self-efficacy, and postoperative satisfaction.
Method· A transnational prospective multicenter observational study with validated questionnaires (HSS-HRES, SER, HOOS) and regression analysis.
Result· Preoperative self-efficacy was significantly related to satisfaction in simple regression, but country had no influence; higher HOOS Sport and QOL scores predicted higher satisfaction.
Validation· Statistical validation using student t-tests, Chi-square tests, and multiple regression on clinical cohort data.
