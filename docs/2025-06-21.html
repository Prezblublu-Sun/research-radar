LFR-PINO: A Layered Fourier Reduced Physics-Informed Neural Operator for Parametric PDEs

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark
Jing Wang, Biao Chen, Hairun Xie, Rui Wang, Yifan Xia et al. arXiv preprint 2025-06-21 link
相关性: 该论文提出了一种高效的物理信息神经算子，可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模，但未涉及植入物设计或不确定性量化。
边界: No validation on biomedical or implant mechanics problems, no uncertainty quantification, and no constraint handling.
动机· 现有物理信息神经算子表达有限或计算成本高，需高效通用求解器。
方法· 提出分层超网络与频域降维结合的LFR-PINO架构。
结果· 在四个PDE基准上误差降低22.8%-68.7%，内存减少28.6%-69.3%。
验证· 在标准PDE数值基准上验证，未涉及生物力学或植入物案例。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Existing physics-informed neural operators suffer from limited expressiveness or high computational cost, motivating a more efficient and generalizable solver.
Method· LFR-PINO introduces a layered hypernetwork for layer-specific parameter generation and a frequency-domain reduction strategy to reduce parameter count while preserving spectral features.
Result· The method achieves 22.8%-68.7% error reduction and 28.6%-69.3% memory savings over state-of-the-art baselines on four parametric PDE benchmarks.
Validation· Validation is performed on standard numerical PDE benchmarks without application to biomedical or implant mechanics.
