Measurement of the Melt Pool Length During Single Scan Tracks in a Commercial Laser Powder Bed Fusion Process

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Jarred C. Heigel, Brandon Lane Journal of Manufacturing Science and Engineering 2017-08-08 10.1115/1.4037571
单位: Intelligent Systems Division, Engineering Laboratory, National Institute of Standards and Technology, Gaithersburg, MD 20899 e-mail:
通讯: Jarred C. Heigel @ Intelligent Systems Division, Engineering Laboratory, National Institute of Standards and Technology, Gaithersburg, MD 2
相关性: 该论文研究LPBF熔池长度测量，但未涉及生物医学植入物、多孔结构或力学性能。
边界: No porous structure, no stress shielding or stiffness matching, no mechanical testing (fatigue or static), no surface roughness or osseointegration.
动机· 测量商业LPBF过程中熔池长度，理解工艺参数影响。
方法· 使用高速红外相机测量单道扫描熔池长度，检测固液转变。
结果· 熔池长度在2mm内达到稳态，随激光功率增加而增加，与扫描速度关系不明显。
验证· 通过多次重复实验验证测量结果。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To measure melt pool length in a commercial LPBF machine and understand its dependence on process parameters.
Method· High-speed infrared thermography (1800 fps) was used to capture temperature profiles during single-track laser scans on nickel alloy 625, detecting the liquidus-solidus transition to determine melt pool length.
Result· Melt pool length reached steady state within 2 mm of scan start, increased with laser power, but showed no clear trend with scan speed at high power.
Validation· Multiple replications across different scan lengths and parameter combinations confirmed the measurements.
