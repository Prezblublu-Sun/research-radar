An In Vivo Evaluation of Biocompatibility and Implant Accuracy of the Electron Beam Melting and Commercial Reconstruction Plates

单位: Advanced Manufacturing Institute, King Saud University, Riyadh 11421, Saudi Arabia
通讯: Khaja Moiduddin @ Advanced Manufacturing Institute, King Saud University, Riyadh 11421, Saudi Arabia
相关性: 该论文涉及电子束熔融（EBM）制造的多孔网状重建板，但未量化应力遮挡或刚度匹配，且缺乏疲劳测试。
边界: No stress shielding quantification, no stiffness matching analysis, no fatigue testing, and no surface roughness or as-built morphology characterization.
动机· 评估电子束熔融定制重建板与商业板的生物相容性和植入精度。
方法· 山羊下颌骨节段缺损模型，植入三种重建板（EBM带网孔、EBM无网孔、商业板），术后3个月进行影像学和组织学分析。

验证· 山羊体内实验，包括影像学、组织学和植入精度测量。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the biocompatibility and implant fitting accuracy of custom electron beam melting (EBM) reconstruction plates with mesh compared to commercial plates in a goat mandibular defect model.
Method· Fifteen goats were divided into three groups receiving EBM plates with mesh, EBM plates without mesh, or commercial reconstruction plates; after 3 months, radiographic, histological, and fitting accuracy analyses were performed.
Result· EBM plates with mesh showed superior implant fitting accuracy and enhanced bone ingrowth and osseointegration compared to the other groups.
Validation· In vivo animal study with clinical, histological, and radiographic assessments.
