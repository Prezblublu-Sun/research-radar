英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the effects of epidural anesthesia and spinal-epidural anesthesia on hemodynamics in elderly patients undergoing total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Eighty-four elderly patients were divided into two groups receiving either spinal-epidural or epidural anesthesia; heart rate, systolic and diastolic blood pressure were monitored at multiple time points.
Result· Spinal-epidural anesthesia showed faster onset, less hemodynamic fluctuation, and better anesthetic effect than epidural anesthesia alone.
Validation· Clinical randomized controlled trial with statistical comparison between groups.
