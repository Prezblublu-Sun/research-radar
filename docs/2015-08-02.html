Efficient Uncertainty Quantification in Structural Dynamic Analysis Using Two-Level Gaussian Processes

High FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project simulation_case UQ
Kai Zhou, Pei Cao, Jiong Tang 2015-08-02 10.1115/detc2015-47724
单位: University of Connecticut, Storrs, CT
通讯: Kai Zhou @ University of Connecticut, Storrs, CT
相关性: 该论文提出的两级高斯过程方法可有效解决结构动力学中的昂贵仿真采样问题，并包含不确定性量化，对生物力学植入物设计中的代理建模具有直接借鉴意义。
边界: This paper focuses on structural dynamics and CMS-based model reduction, not on implant biomechanics, contact mechanics, or bone-implant interface modeling.
动机· 结构动力学不确定性量化中，大规模有限元模型计算成本过高。
方法· 利用两级高斯过程融合低阶CMS模型与全阶FEM数据，并通过贝叶斯推断校准降阶模型。
结果· 实现了快速采样与响应预测，有效校正了低保真数据的偏差。
验证· 通过结构动力学案例验证了方法的有效性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce the prohibitive computational cost of uncertainty quantification in structural dynamic analysis with large-scale finite element models.
Method· A two-level Gaussian process emulation is developed that integrates low-fidelity data from component mode synthesis and high-fidelity data from full-scale FEM, with Bayesian inference for model calibration.
Result· The method enables rapid sampling and response prediction under uncertainty, correcting bias in low-fidelity data.
Validation· Effectiveness is validated through case studies in structural dynamics.

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Skim only simulation_case UQ
Zhen Hu, Sankaran Mahadevan, Xiaoping Du 2015-08-02 10.1115/detc2015-47925
单位: Vanderbilt University, Nashville, TN
通讯: Zhen Hu @ Vanderbilt University, Nashville, TN
相关性: 该论文提出了时间相关可靠性分析中的不确定性量化框架，其代理模型方法可迁移至生物力学植入物设计的可靠性分析。
边界: This paper focuses on time-dependent reliability analysis with epistemic uncertainty, but does not address implant biomechanics, FEA surrogate for stress shielding, or multi-objective constrained optimization.
动机· 解决时间相关可靠性分析中数据与模型不确定性对结果的影响。
方法· 采用贝叶斯方法建模认知不确定性，构建条件可靠度指标的代理模型。
结果· 代理模型避免了双重循环计算，高效量化了不确定性。
验证· 通过数学算例和工程应用验证方法有效性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify the effects of data and model uncertainty on time-dependent reliability analysis results.
Method· A Bayesian approach models epistemic uncertainty in random variables and stochastic processes, and a surrogate model for the conditional reliability index is built to avoid double-loop computation.
Result· The surrogate model enables efficient uncertainty quantification without evaluating the original limit-state function.
Validation· The method is demonstrated on a mathematical example and an engineering application.

单位: University at Buffalo - SUNY, Buffalo, NY
通讯: Arpan Mukherjee @ University at Buffalo - SUNY, Buffalo, NY
相关性: 该论文提出了一种基于非负矩阵分解的不确定性量化方法，但应用于复杂网络系统而非生物力学或植入物设计，方法转移性有限。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, implant biomechanics, or constrained optimization; it focuses on uncertainty quantification for general networked dynamical systems.
动机· 大型网络系统状态预测困难，传统不确定性量化方法计算成本高且精度低。
方法· 利用非负矩阵分解将系统域划分为簇，最小化簇间交互、最大化簇内交互。
结果· 在两个动力系统上验证，不确定性传播误差与现有方法相当或更优。
验证· 在两个经典动力系统上进行数值基准测试。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Predicting the behavior of large networked systems with complex nonlinear dynamics is computationally expensive and inaccurate with conventional uncertainty quantification methods.
Method· A non-negative matrix factorization based decomposition partitions the system domain into clusters minimizing inter-cluster and maximizing intra-cluster interactions, considering node dynamics.
Result· Validation on two well-known dynamical systems shows competitive or better RMS errors in uncertainty propagation compared to existing methods.
Validation· Numerical benchmark tests on two standard dynamical systems.
