Motivation· To investigate the low-cycle fatigue properties and dimensional limitations of as-built thin-walled components by laser powder bed fusion.

Result· The strain-life of PBF-LB samples was comparable to wrought samples, but fatigue strength ranked as CW wrought > PBF-LB > SA wrought, with only cyclic softening observed in PBF-LB due to suppressed martensitic transformation.
Validation· Experimental fatigue testing and microstructural analysis validated the thin-wall effect and surface roughness influence.
