Motivation· To compare clinical outcomes of total hip arthroplasty versus bipolar hemiarthroplasty for displaced femoral neck fractures in elderly patients.
Method· Narrative review analyzing advantages, complications, and selection criteria of THA and BHA.
Result· THA offers better pain relief and mobility for active patients, while BHA is suitable for less mobile patients; surgical technique and post-operative care are critical for outcomes.
Validation· The paper is a literature review and does not present original clinical data or experimental validation.
