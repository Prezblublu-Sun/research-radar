Bionic mechanical design and 3D printing of novel porous Ti6Al4V implants for biomedical applications

High AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Read now mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Wenming Peng, Yunfeng Liu, Xianfeng Jiang, Xingtao Dong, Janice Jun et al. Journal of Zhejiang University SCIENCE B 2019-07-10 10.1631/jzus.b1800622
单位: Key Laboratory of E&M (Zhejiang University of Technology), Ministry of Education & Zhejiang Province, Hangzhou 310023, China
通讯: Wenming Peng @ Key Laboratory of E&M (Zhejiang University of Technology), Ministry of Education & Zhejiang Province, Hangzhou 310023, C
相关性: 该论文直接研究了多孔Ti6Al4V植入物的仿生设计与3D打印，涉及刚度匹配、力学测试和有限元分析，与用户研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing, surface roughness characterization, or osseointegration evaluation.
动机· 为颌面外科设计具有仿生结构和匹配骨模量的多孔钛合金植入物。
方法· 采用“降维分层设计”构建杆连接网状结构，通过SLM制备并表征。
结果· 多孔植入物的弹性模量(2.23-6.36 GPa)与人体骨匹配，屈服强度达21-123 MPa。
验证· 通过压缩实验和有限元分析验证力学性能，并用Gibson-Ashby模型预测。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To design and fabricate porous Ti6Al4V implants with bionic structures and mechanical properties matching human bone for maxillofacial surgery.
Method· A layer-by-layer design concept was used to construct layered slice and rod-connected mesh structure (LSRCMS) implants, fabricated by selective laser melting (SLM).
Result· The LSRCMS implants achieved Young's modulus of 2.23-6.36 GPa and yield strength of 21-123 MPa, with porosity ranging from 61% to 81%.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated by compression tests and finite element analysis, and the Gibson-Ashby model accurately predicted stiffness and strength.
