Nicholas B. Frisch, Jonathan R. Lynch, Robin Pourzal, Richard F. Banglmaier, Craig D. Silverton Arthroplasty Today 2018-07-21 10.1016/j.artd.2018.06.001
单位: Ascension Crittenton Hospital, Rochester, MI, USA
通讯: Nicholas B. Frisch @ Ascension Crittenton Hospital, Rochester, MI, USA
相关性: 该论文研究模块化髋关节假体颈部-柄连接处的几何形状和接触，但未涉及股骨近端应力遮挡或微动，与用户研究方向不直接相关。
边界: Does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain or micromotion at bone-implant interface; focuses on modular taper junction geometry and contact, not femoral stem-bone interaction.
动机· 探究模块化髋关节假体颈部-柄锥形连接面的几何特征对稳定性的影响
方法· 使用三维光学扫描测量18个组件锥形表面的接触距离和角度
结果· 颈部与柄锥形表面形状偏离理想设计，可能导致相对运动
验证· 通过回归分析验证角度和深度测量结果
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate geometric features of the male and female taper surfaces that drive stability within the neck-stem junction of modular hip implants.
Method· Three-dimensional surface scans of 18 modular hip components were analyzed to measure normal distances between neck and stem taper surfaces, producing contact proximity color maps and contour plots.
Result· Deviations from ideal design dimensions in neck and stem taper shapes were found, potentially contributing to relative motions and increased risk of failure.
Validation· Angle measurements and neck seated depth were analyzed via regression to validate geometric deviations.
