Mechanical Properties Directionality and Permeability of Fused Triply Periodic Minimal Surface Porous Scaffolds Fabricated by Selective Laser Melting

Jianhua Ye, Weihui He, Tieping Wei, Changning Sun, Shoujin Zeng ACS Biomaterials Science & Engineering 2023-07-25 10.1021/acsbiomaterials.3c00214
单位: Fujian Key Laboratory of Intelligent Machining Technology and Equipment (Fujian University of Technology), Fuzhou 350118, China
通讯: Shoujin Zeng @ Fujian Key Laboratory of Intelligent Machining Technology and Equipment (Fujian University of Technology), Fuzhou 350118
相关性: 该论文直接研究了SLM制造的TPMS多孔支架的力学性能和渗透性，并涉及减少应力遮挡效应，与用户关于增材制造生物医学材料方向高度相关。
边界: Does not cover fatigue testing, surface roughness characterization, or osseointegration evaluation.
动机· 利用TPMS多孔支架模拟骨结构，减少应力遮挡并促进营养运输。
方法· 设计融合Gyroid和Diamond单元的TPMS多孔支架，通过SLM制造并进行准静态压缩实验和CFD模拟。
结果· 支架屈服强度367.7-419.4 MPa，弹性模量10.6-11.3 GPa，渗透率5.70-6.34e-8 m²。
验证· 通过准静态压缩实验和基于Navier-Stokes方程的CFD模拟验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To design titanium alloy porous scaffolds that mimic trabecular and cortical bone structure, reducing stress-shielding and enabling nutrient transport.
Method· Fused TPMS porous scaffolds based on Gyroid and Diamond units were fabricated by selective laser melting, and their mechanical properties were tested via quasi-static compression while permeability was simulated using CFD.
Result· The scaffolds achieved yield strengths of 367.7–419.4 MPa, elastic moduli of 10.6–11.3 GPa, and permeabilities of 5.70–6.34 × 10⁻⁸ m².
Validation· Validation was performed through quasi-static compression experiments and CFD simulations based on the Navier–Stokes model.

通讯: Tairui Zhang @ School of Mechanical Engineering Southeast University Nanjing China  |  Weiqiang Wang @ College of Electromechanical Engineering Qingdao University of Science and Technology Qingdao China

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the accuracy of different spherical indentation test models in predicting uniaxial tensile and fracture properties of Ti-6Al-4V alloys from various manufacturing processes.

Result· All models except the empirical one predicted strength within 10% error, but the incremental model inaccurately computed yield strength; the critical stress model effectively estimated fracture toughness.
Validation· Experiments on three manufacturing routes, combined with theoretical analysis and finite element simulations, validated the models' predictions and error sources.
