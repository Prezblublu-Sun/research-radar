Critical Influences of Particle Size and Adhesion on the Powder Layer Uniformity in Metal Additive Manufacturing

Low AM Biomedical Materials arxiv Peripheral Background only simulation_case
Christoph Meier, Reimbar Weissbach, Johannes Weinberg, Wolfgang A. Wall, A. John Hart arXiv preprint 2018-04-18 10.1016/j.jmatprotec.2018.10.037
相关性: 该论文研究金属增材制造中粉末铺展过程，未涉及植入物设计、应力遮挡或疲劳测试。
边界: This paper focuses on powder recoating process and layer uniformity, not on porous implant design, stress shielding, mechanical testing, or osseointegration.
动机· 研究粉末粒径和粘附性对铺粉质量的影响
方法· 采用离散元法模拟粉末铺展过程
结果· 细粉粘附力主导重力，导致铺粉质量差
验证· 数值模拟，无实验验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate how particle size and adhesion affect powder layer uniformity in metal additive manufacturing.
Method· A discrete element method (DEM) model incorporating frictional contact, rolling resistance, and cohesive forces was used to simulate the powder recoating process.
Result· Decreased particle size and increased cohesiveness significantly reduce powder layer quality, with cohesive forces dominating gravity for fine powders.
Validation· The study is based on numerical simulations without experimental validation.

Modeling and Characterization of Cohesion in Fine Metal Powders with a Focus on Additive Manufacturing Process Simulations

Low AM Biomedical Materials arxiv Peripheral Background only simulation_case Exp. validation
Christoph Meier, Reimar Weissbach, Johannes Weinberg, Wolfgang A. Wall, A. John Hart arXiv preprint 2018-04-18 10.1016/j.powtec.2018.11.072
相关性: 该论文研究金属粉末的粘聚力建模，与AM生物医学植入物的应力屏蔽、疲劳或骨整合无直接关联。
边界: No porous structure, no stress shielding or stiffness matching, no LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing (fatigue or static), no surface roughness or osseointegration.
动机· 建立细金属粉末粘聚力的模型以改进增材制造过程模拟。
方法· 基于离散元法，结合范德华力与表面能提出粘附力模型。
结果· 校准得到Ti-6Al-4V粉末的有效表面能为0.1 mJ/m²，远低于平板接触值。
验证· 通过漏斗实验测量休止角进行数值与实验对比验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To model cohesive interactions in fine metal powders for improved powder-bed additive manufacturing simulations.
Method· A discrete element method (DEM) model incorporating frictional contact, rolling resistance, and a van der Waals-based adhesion force law calibrated by surface energy.
Result· An effective surface energy of 0.1 mJ/m² was found for Ti-6Al-4V powder, significantly lower than flat-surface values, and neglecting cohesion drastically underestimates the angle of repose.
Validation· Numerical angle of repose predictions were fitted to experimental funnel test data to calibrate the surface energy parameter.
