A Bayesian learning framework for fast prediction and uncertainty quantification of additively manufactured multi-material components

单位: Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Virginia Tech, Blacksburg, VA 24061, USA
通讯: David Higdon @ Department of Statistics, Virginia Tech, Blacksburg, VA 24061, USA  |  Hang Z. Yu @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Virginia Tech, Blacksburg, VA 24061, USA
相关性: 该论文直接针对增材制造多材料组件的快速预测与不确定性量化，方法可迁移至植入物力学性能预测。
边界: Does not apply surrogate to implant biomechanics or stress shielding; focuses on AM process parameters and material properties.
动机· 解决多材料增材制造组件性能预测中计算成本高和不确定性量化不足的问题。
方法· 提出贝叶斯学习框架，结合有限元仿真数据构建代理模型，实现快速预测与不确定性量化。
结果· 框架在预测多材料组件力学响应时精度高，且有效量化了输入参数不确定性带来的预测波动。
验证· 通过多材料悬臂梁和热-力耦合仿真案例验证预测精度与不确定性量化效果。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To address the high computational cost and lack of uncertainty quantification in predicting the performance of additively manufactured multi-material components.
Method· A Bayesian learning framework is proposed, which builds a surrogate model from finite element simulation data to enable fast prediction and uncertainty quantification.
Result· The framework achieves high accuracy in predicting mechanical responses of multi-material components and effectively quantifies prediction variability due to input uncertainties.
Validation· Validation is performed through simulation cases of multi-material cantilever beams and thermo-mechanical coupling scenarios.
