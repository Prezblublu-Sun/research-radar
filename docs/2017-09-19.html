英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To assess whether the conventional bioequivalence limits are adequate to ensure the safety and efficacy of rivaroxaban in patients undergoing hip or knee replacement.
Method· Exposure-response modeling using NONMEM on clinical data from 7145 patients across multiple phase 2 and 3 trials, followed by simulations.
Result· A 20% change in AUC does not statistically alter the relative risk of major bleeding and is unlikely to compromise efficacy.
Validation· Model adequacy was confirmed via predictive checks and nonparametric bootstrap.
