相关性: 该论文直接研究SLM制备的多孔Ti6Al4V椎间融合器，涉及力学性能匹配、表面特性、生物相容性及体内骨长入，与用户方向高度相关。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of additive angle during SLM on surface properties and biocompatibility of porous Ti6Al4V interbody fusion cages.
Method· Ti6Al4V discs with six different build angles were fabricated by SLM; surface roughness, wettability, cell morphology, and proliferation were characterized, and a porous cage with 70% porosity and 600 μm pores was mechanically tested and implanted in Beagle tibiae.
Result· Higher additive angle increased surface roughness and hydrophobicity but did not significantly affect cell adhesion or proliferation; the cage exhibited an elastic modulus of 0.51 GPa and compressive strength of 94.7 MPa, and in vivo bone ingrowth was comparable to commercial porous tantalum.
Validation· In vitro cell assays with MC3T3E1 cells and in vivo histological analysis in a Beagle tibia model.
