Conservative model reduction for finite-volume models

Kevin Carlberg, Youngsoo Choi, Syuzanna Sargsyan arXiv preprint 2017-11-30 10.1016/j.jcp.2018.05.019
相关性: 该方法针对有限体积模型提出保守降阶技术，可应用于生物力学有限元代理模型的约束保持。
边界: It does not address finite-element discretizations, lacks full uncertainty quantification, and is not validated on implant/biomechanics problems.
动机· 开发保持有限体积模型保守性的降阶方法。
方法· 通过最小残差目标和非线性等式约束的优化问题实现保守Galerkin和LSPG投影。
结果· 保守降阶模型相比非保守方法具有更低的状态误差并保证全局守恒。
验证· 参数化准一维欧拉方程数值实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop model reduction techniques that inherently preserve conservation laws in finite-volume discretizations.
Method· Conservative reduced-order models are derived by formulating an optimization problem with a minimum-residual objective and nonlinear equality constraints enforcing subdomain conservation, with hyper-reduction for nonlinear terms.
Result· The proposed conservative ROMs yield lower state-space errors and guarantee global conservation compared to standard non-conservative projections.
Validation· Numerical experiments on a parameterized quasi-1D Euler equation benchmark demonstrate the method's effectiveness.

单位: College of Engineering Mathematics and Physical Sciences, University of Exeter, Exeter, UK and Technical College of Civil Aviation and Meteorology, Esbeah, Libya
通讯: Hamza Hassn Alsalla @ College of Engineering Mathematics and Physical Sciences, University of Exeter, Exeter, UK and Technical College of Civi
相关性: 该论文研究了316L不锈钢的SLM工艺参数对力学性能的影响，但未涉及多孔结构或生物医学植入物应用。

动机· 探究SLM工艺中构建方向对316L不锈钢部件性能的影响。

Result· Build orientation significantly affected fracture toughness (ranging from 145 to 176 MPa m^(1/2)), with an average density of 96% and tensile properties superior to conventionally manufactured material.
Validation· Validation was performed through mechanical testing (hardness, tensile, fracture toughness) and microstructural characterization.
