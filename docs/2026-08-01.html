Motivation· To address implant-associated infections and loosening via immune-driven strategy.
Method· A composite coating of phase-transited lysozyme on sodium titanate nanowires was constructed on Ti surface for high-throughput screening of optimal dosage.
Result· Moderate PTL dosage achieved optimal balance between bacterial clearance and bone regeneration through macrophage modulation.
Validation· In vitro macrophage assays and in vivo infectious bone regeneration models validated the coating efficacy.
