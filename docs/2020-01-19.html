Finding Optimal Points for Expensive Functions Using Adaptive RBF-Based Surrogate Model Via Uncertainty Quantification

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Ray-Bing Chen, Yuan Wang, C. F. Jeff Wu arXiv preprint 2020-01-19 link
相关性: 该论文提出的基于RBF的自适应代理模型结合不确定性量化进行全局优化，方法可迁移至生物力学植入物设计中的昂贵仿真优化问题。
边界: This paper does not address implant/biomechanics design, does not handle constraints, and lacks validation on engineering or biomedical problems.
动机· 昂贵函数全局优化中采样效率低，需高效代理模型方法。
方法· 提出自适应RBF贝叶斯代理模型，结合期望改进准则选择新采样点。
结果· 在标准测试函数上表现优于对比方法，尤其非光滑函数。
验证· 标准数值测试函数仿真验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Global optimization of expensive functions suffers from low sampling efficiency, motivating the development of adaptive surrogate models.
Method· An adaptive RBF-based Bayesian surrogate model is proposed, with parameters updated sequentially and a sample version of expected improvement criterion for point selection.
Result· The method shows advantages on standard test functions, especially when the true surface is not smooth.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on standard test functions are used for validation.

Nonintrusive Uncertainty Quantification of Dynamic Power Systems Subject to Stochastic Excitations

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Background only simulation_case UQ
Yiwei Qiu, Jin Lin, Xiaoshuang Chen, Feng Liu, Yonghua Song arXiv preprint 2020-01-19 link
相关性: 该方法针对电力系统随机激励的不确定性量化，与生物力学或植入物设计无关。
边界: This paper focuses on power system dynamics with stochastic excitations, not on FEA surrogate modeling for biomedical implant design; it lacks any biomechanics context, contact mechanics, or implant-related constraints.
动机· 现有电力系统随机激励不确定性量化方法多为侵入式，难以实际应用。
方法· 提出基于Itô过程谱表示和自适应稀疏概率配点法的非侵入式多项式混沌展开方法。
结果· 在IEEE 39节点和118节点系统上准确高效地量化了动态响应概率分布和高阶矩。
验证· 通过IEEE标准测试系统的仿真案例验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Existing methods for quantifying uncertainty in power systems under stochastic excitations are mostly intrusive and not compatible with commercial simulation software, limiting practical adoption.
Method· A nonintrusive polynomial chaos expansion method is proposed, using spectral representation of Itô processes and an adaptive sparse probabilistic collocation method to compute the expansion coefficients.
Result· The method accurately and efficiently quantifies the probability distribution and high-order moments of dynamic responses in IEEE 39-bus and 118-bus test systems.
Validation· Validation is performed through simulation case studies on standard IEEE test systems.
