Motivation· To provide an updated review of musculoskeletal infection literature with emphasis on periprosthetic joint infection (PJI).
Method· A literature search was conducted using the NCBI database for English-language articles published from January to December 2020, focusing on PJI, spine, and trauma infections.
Result· The incidence of PJI has not improved over 15 years, and treatment outcomes remain unchanged between 2000-2010 and 2011-2016.
Validation· This is a narrative review without experimental or clinical validation.
