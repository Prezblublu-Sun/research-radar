相关性: 该论文关注全髋关节置换术中术前骨盆倾斜分析对透视时间的影响，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡、微动或生物力学建模。
边界: This paper focuses on surgical workflow and fluoroscopy reduction, with no quantification of stress shielding, micromotion, or any biomechanical modeling of the femoral stem.
动机· 减少直接前入路全髋置换术中透视时间及辐射暴露。
方法· 术前计算骨盆倾斜角并告知透视技师，对比盲法组与告知组的透视时间。
结果· 告知组平均透视时间显著减少（28.65秒 vs 23.61秒），但均在安全范围内。
验证· 100例患者的前瞻性对照研究，比较两组透视时间差异。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine whether communicating preoperative pelvic tilt angle to the fluoroscopy technician reduces intraoperative fluoroscopy time during direct anterior total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Pelvic tilt was measured preoperatively in 100 consecutive patients; the technician was blinded for the first 50 cases and informed for the next 50, and fluoroscopy duration was recorded.
Result· Mean fluoroscopy time decreased significantly from 28.65 seconds in the control group to 23.61 seconds in the test group (p=0.033), though both remained within safe radiation limits.
Validation· Prospective clinical study with 100 patients, comparing blinded versus informed fluoroscopy technician groups.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate whether continuous lumbar plexus block can reduce surgical stress response after hip arthroplasty compared to other analgesic methods.
Method· A prospective randomized study of 60 patients undergoing total hip arthroplasty, measuring serum cortisol, insulin, glucose, and CRP at multiple time points.
Result· Continuous lumbar plexus block and epidural analgesia significantly lowered cortisol levels postoperatively, while spinal anesthesia provided the best overall stress reduction.
Validation· Clinical trial with 60 patients, randomized into four groups, with statistical analysis of hormonal and inflammatory markers.
