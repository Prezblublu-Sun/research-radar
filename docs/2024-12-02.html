相关性: 该论文提出了一种基于形变的框架来学习变域PDE的解映射，可迁移至植入物设计中的几何变化问题，但未涉及生物力学或不确定性量化。
边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, stress shielding, or uncertainty quantification; it focuses on theoretical PDE solution mapping on varying domains without biomedical or design optimization context.
动机· 解决变域PDE解映射的学习问题，无需区域微分同胚。
方法· 建立基于形变的度量空间框架，将解映射表示为度量到Banach映射，并用神经网络学习。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To learn solution mappings of PDEs defined on varying domains without requiring diffeomorphic transformations.
Method· A deformation-based metric space framework identifies the union of functions on varying domains, representing the solution mapping as a continuous metric-to-Banach mapping learned by neural networks.
Result· The framework preserves linearity for linear PDEs when using linearity-preserving neural operators like MIONet, and supports local geometric changes.
Validation· Numerical experiments on star-shaped domains validate the theoretical convergence and effectiveness.
