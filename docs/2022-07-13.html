English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the analgesic efficacy of a cocktail containing diprospan for pain control after primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A prospective randomized observer-blinded trial with 120 patients assigned to three groups receiving different local infiltration analgesia strategies.
Result· The cocktail with diprospan provided superior and longer-lasting pain relief, particularly for resting pain, compared to ropivacaine alone or control.
Validation· Clinical validation through a randomized controlled trial with blinded observers.
