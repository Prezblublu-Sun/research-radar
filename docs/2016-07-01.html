英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the long-term benefit of multiple joint procedures on self-reported activities in persons with haemophilia.
Method· Three cases were followed for a mean of 6 years, with measurements of active range of motion, Hemophilia Activities List, and McMaster Toronto Arthritis patient disability questionnaire.
Result· Post-operative pain was minimal, and improvements in knee range of motion and lower extremity activity scores were observed.
Validation· Case series without control group or statistical inference.
