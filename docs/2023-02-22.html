Varad Maitra, Jing Shi Advanced Engineering Materials 2023-02-22 10.1002/adem.202300075
单位: Department of Mechanical and Materials Engineering College of Engineering and Applied Science University of Cincinnati Cincinnati OH 45221 USA
通讯: Jing Shi @ Department of Mechanical and Materials Engineering College of Engineering and Applied Science University of Cincinnati
相关性: 该论文关注SLM工艺参数与表面粗糙度的预测模型，但与用户关注的FEA代理模型及植入物生物力学设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, constraint-handling optimization, uncertainty quantification, or transferability to implant biomechanics design.
动机· 利用已有文献数据预测选区激光熔化Ti-6Al-4V合金的表面粗糙度。
方法· 建立五种机器学习模型和一种传统回归模型，基于文献数据和实验验证进行十折交叉验证。
结果· 高斯过程回归和神经网络模型表现最佳，均方根误差分别为0.51和0.58 μm。
验证· 通过自制样品实测表面粗糙度验证模型预测能力。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To assess whether reliable predictive models for surface roughness of laser additively manufactured alloys can be developed using aggregated literature data with minimal new experimentation.
Method· Five supervised machine learning models and one traditional regression model were trained and compared via tenfold cross-validation on a comprehensive mined dataset, then validated by fabricating and measuring cubic Ti-6Al-4V samples.
Result· Gaussian process regression and neural network models achieved the highest accuracy with root-mean-squared errors of 0.51 and 0.58 μm respectively, deemed industry-ready.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed by measuring surface roughness of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V samples and comparing model predictions.

通讯: Shangting You @ Department of NanoEngineering, University of California, San Diego, La Jolla, CA 92093, USA  |  Yi Xiang @ Department of NanoEngineering, University of California, San Diego, La Jolla, CA 92093, USA  |  Shaochen Chen @ Department of Bioengineering, University of California, San Diego, La Jolla, CA 92093, USA
相关性: 该论文不涉及任何人工智能方法，仅通过添加碘海醇来减少光散射，属于生物打印工艺优化，与人工智能驱动的生物打印方向无关。

Motivation· The resolution of DLP-based bioprinting degrades at high cell densities due to light scattering, limiting the fabrication of vascularized thick tissues.
Method· Iodixanol was added to the bioink to reduce light scattering by tenfold, thereby restoring fabrication resolution.
Result· A resolution of 50 μm was achieved for bioinks with 0.1 billion cells/mL, and thick tissues with fine vascular networks remained viable and showed endothelialization and angiogenesis after 14 days of perfusion culture.
Validation· Tissue viability and vascularization were validated through perfusion culture, fluorescent imaging, and histological analysis.
