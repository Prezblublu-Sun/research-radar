通讯: Didier Talamona @ School of Engineering and Digital Sciences, Nazarbayev University, 53 Kabanbay Batyr Ave., Astana, Kazakhstan
相关性: 该论文主要研究AlSi10Mg点阵结构的热处理对其力学性能和能量吸收的影响，未涉及生物医学植入物中的应力屏蔽或骨整合。
边界: No biomedical context, no fatigue testing, no stress shielding analysis, no surface roughness or osseointegration evaluation.
动机· 通过热处理调控AlSi10Mg点阵结构的力学性能和能量吸收。
方法· 对SLM制造的AlSi10Mg点阵和块状样品进行固溶和时效热处理，并分析微观结构变化。

Method· Solution and aging heat treatments applied to SLM-fabricated AlSi10Mg lattice and bulk specimens, with microstructural and mechanical characterization.
Result· Solution treatment reduced strength but improved ductility and energy absorption; subsequent aging restored strength for certain temperatures.
Validation· Mechanical testing (compression and tensile) with statistical t-test.
