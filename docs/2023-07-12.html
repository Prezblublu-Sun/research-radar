Influence of stress-relieving heat treatments on the efficacy of Co-Cr-Mo-W alloy copings fabricated using selective laser melting

单位: Department of Advanced Prosthodontics, Oral Health Sciences, Graduate School of Medical and Dental Sciences, Tokyo Medical and Dental University, Tokyo, Japan
通讯: Hein Linn Htat @ Department of Advanced Prosthodontics, Oral Health Sciences, Graduate School of Medical and Dental Sciences, Tokyo Medic
相关性: 该论文涉及SLM制造Co-Cr合金牙冠，但与用户关注的髋关节植入物应力屏蔽和疲劳性能无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on dental copings rather than load-bearing hip implants, does not address porous structures or stress shielding, and lacks fatigue testing.
动机· 评估应力消除热处理对SLM钴铬合金牙冠性能的影响。
方法· 通过SLM制造Co-Cr样品，进行750°C和1150°C应力消除处理，比较其微观结构、表面粗糙度、金属-陶瓷结合强度、边缘和内部适合作。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the influence of stress-relieving heat treatments on the mechanical and fit properties of SLM-fabricated Co-Cr alloy dental copings.
Method· SLM-manufactured Co-Cr samples were stress-relieved at 750°C and 1150°C, and their microstructure, surface roughness, metal-ceramic bond strength, marginal/internal fit, Vickers hardness, and residual stress were compared to as-built samples.
Result· Post-heat treatments degraded bond strength and marginal fit, while as-built samples exhibited superior performance, suggesting direct application of as-built SLM crowns is viable.
Validation· Mechanical testing including bond strength, hardness, and residual stress measurements, along with marginal gap assessment using microscopy, validated the findings.
