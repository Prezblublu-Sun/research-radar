EquiNO: A Physics-Informed Neural Operator for Multiscale Simulations

High FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Direct Read now simulation_case
Hamidreza Eivazi, Jendrik-Alexander Tröger, Stefan Wittek, Stefan Hartmann, Andreas Rausch arXiv preprint 2025-03-27 link
相关性: 该论文提出的EquiNO方法通过硬约束平衡条件，显著加速多尺度模拟，可直接应用于生物力学植入物设计的代理建模。
边界: The paper does not apply the method to implant or biomechanics design, nor does it include uncertainty quantification or handle constraints beyond equilibrium.
动机· 多尺度模拟计算成本高，需要高效且物理一致的代理模型。
方法· 提出EquiNO，通过POD投影硬约束平衡条件，结合有限元与算子学习。
结果· 相比传统方法加速超8000倍，在有限数据集上仍保持准确。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Multiscale simulations are computationally prohibitive for many-query scenarios, motivating the development of efficient and physically consistent surrogate models.
Method· EquiNO hard-enforces equilibrium by projecting solutions onto divergence-free POD basis functions, integrating finite element method with operator learning.
Result· The method achieves speedup factors exceeding 8000-fold over traditional methods while maintaining accuracy even with limited training data.
Validation· Validation is performed on quasi-static solid mechanics problems using numerical simulation cases.
