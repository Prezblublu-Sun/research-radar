A correlative imaging based methodology for accurate quantitative assessment of bone formation in additive manufactured implants

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only ex_vivo Exp. validation
Hua Geng, Naomi M. Todd, Aine Devlin‐Mullin, Gowsihan Poologasundarampillai, Taek Bo Kim et al. Journal of Materials Science Materials in Medicine 2016-05-06 10.1007/s10856-016-5721-6
单位: School of Materials, The University of Manchester, Oxford Road, Manchester, M13 9PL, UK
通讯: Hua Geng @ School of Materials, The University of Manchester, Oxford Road, Manchester, M13 9PL, UK
相关性: 该论文聚焦于骨形成定量评估方法，而非应力遮挡或疲劳性能，与用户研究方向间接相关。
边界: This paper does not address stress shielding, stiffness matching, LPBF fabrication, mechanical testing, fatigue, or surface roughness.
动机· 开发准确量化增材制造植入物中骨形成的方法。
方法· 结合显微CT与组织形态计量学的相关成像方法。
结果· 首次量化了组织切片的体积收缩（约15%），并证明了切片位置的重要性。
验证· 通过3D打印钛晶格植入物验证方法有效性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop an accurate methodology for quantifying bone formation within complex lattice structures of additive manufactured implants.
Method· A correlative imaging approach combining micro computed tomography and histomorphometry with coarse graining registration.
Result· The technique quantified for the first time the volumetric shrinkage of histology sections (~15%) and demonstrated up to 30% offset due to section location.
Validation· Validated using 3D printed titanium lattice implants in an ex vivo model.

Motivation· To evaluate the effect of intraprosthetic drilling on the structural integrity and strength of a femoral hip stem in the context of periprosthetic fracture fixation.
Method· Three finite element models of increasing complexity were created in ANSYS to simulate a Vancouver C fracture fixed with a plate and screws drilled through the stem, varying hole positions and numbers.
Result· Stresses at the drill hole decreased while increasing in surrounding stem material; maximum stress remained at the prosthesis neck and stayed below the yield limit of titanium alloy.
Validation· The study used a sawbone model and FE simulation without experimental validation.
