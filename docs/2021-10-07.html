Multi-material digital light processing bioprinting of hydrogel-based microfluidic chips

Anant Bhusal, Elvan Dogan, Hai-Anh Nguyen, Olga Labutina, Daniel Nieto et al. Biofabrication 2021-10-07 10.1088/1758-5090/ac2d78
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Rowan University, Glassboro, NJ, 08028, United States of America
通讯: Anant Bhusal @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Rowan University, Glassboro, NJ, 08028, United States of America
相关性: 该论文开发了多材料DLP生物打印平台，但未涉及任何AI/ML方法，与将AI集成到生物打印工作流的研究方向无关。
边界: This paper does not incorporate AI/ML into the bioprinting workflow; optimization is manual parametric, not data-driven or autonomous.
动机· 开发多材料DLP生物打印机以快速制备水凝胶微流控芯片
方法· 优化PEGDA/GelMA复合生物墨水的打印参数与组分比例
结果· 展示了芯片的力学性能可调、流动测试成功及细胞包裹活性
验证· 通过力学测试、动态流动实验和细胞封装培养验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a multi-material DLP-based bioprinter for rapid one-step fabrication of hydrogel microfluidic chips for organ-on-chip applications.
Method· A composite PEGDA/GelMA bioink was optimized by varying light exposure time, composition, and layer thickness through parametric experiments.
Result· Microfluidic chips with tunable mechanical properties were fabricated, and dynamic flow tests along with encapsulated tumor cells demonstrated bioactivity and perfusion capability.
Validation· The approach was validated through mechanical characterization, fluidic experiments, and in vitro cell encapsulation and seeding assays.

Validation· Tensile testing and SEM fracture surface analysis were used for validation.

Mechanical Property Comparison of Ni–Cr–Mo Alloys Fabricated via One Conventional and Two New Digital Manufacturing Techniques

Kyung-Ran Yang, Takao Hanawa, Tae‐Yub Kwon, Bong‐Ki Min, Min-Ho Hong Applied Sciences 2021-10-07 10.3390/app11199308
单位: Department of Dentistry, Graduate School, Kyungpook National University, 2177 Dalgubeol-daero, Jung-gu, Daegu 41940, Korea
通讯: Tae‐Yub Kwon @ Department of Dental Biomaterials, School of Dentistry and Institute for Biomaterials Research & Development, Kyungpook  |  Min-Ho Hong @ Department of Dental Laboratory Science, College of Health Sciences, Catholic University of Pusan, 57 Oryundae-ro, Geumj
相关性: 该论文比较了不同制造工艺对Ni-Cr-Mo合金力学性能的影响，未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能，与用户研究方向（AM生物医学材料中的应力遮挡和骨整合）关联度低。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration; it focuses on bulk mechanical properties of dental alloys without implant-specific geometry or surface characterization.
动机· 比较三种制造工艺对Ni-Cr-Mo合金微观结构和力学性能的影响
方法· 采用SLM、SMM和LWC制备试样，进行拉伸测试和微观分析
结果· LWC合金屈服强度最高，但三种合金均满足ISO标准要求
验证· 通过6组重复拉伸实验和微观表征验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to compare the microstructures and mechanical properties of Ni-Cr-Mo alloys fabricated via selective laser melting, soft metal milling, and conventional lost-wax casting.
Method· Disc and dumbbell specimens were prepared and subjected to microstructural analysis and tensile tests to determine mechanical properties.
Result· The lost-wax cast alloy exhibited statistically higher yield strength, while all three alloys met the ISO 22674 standard for type 5 materials.
Validation· Mechanical testing with six replicates per group and microstructural characterization were performed.

单位: 3B's Research Group—Biomaterials Biodegradables and Biomimetics Headquarters of the European Institute of Excellence on Tissue Engineering and Regenerative Medicine University of Minho AvePark, Parqu
通讯: Rui L. Reis @ 3B's Research Group—Biomaterials Biodegradables and Biomimetics Headquarters of the European Institute of Excellence on  |  Utkan Demirci @ Canary Center at Stanford for Cancer Early Detection Department of Radiology Stanford School of Medicine Palo Alto CA 9
相关性: 该论文主要聚焦在水凝胶光子构建体的生物制造，未涉及人工智能在生物打印中的应用。
边界: This paper does not incorporate any AI or machine learning methods into the bioprinting workflow.
动机· 利用水凝胶光子构建体实现多尺度检测和生物制造。
方法· 通过湿纺和同轴3D打印制备多糖基水凝胶光纤。
结果· 实现了柔性光子结构、分子检测和活体光纤生物制造。
验证· 通过物理刺激响应、表面等离子体共振检测和细胞培养验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To integrate soft hydrogel materials with micro/nanooptics for bioengineering applications, enabling multifunctional photonic platforms.
Method· Multilayer hydrogel fibers were fabricated using wet-spinning of ionic-crosslinked polysaccharides, and a tricoaxial 3D printing nozzle was employed for continuous living fiber fabrication.
Result· The hydrogel fibers exhibited tunable optical properties, enabled molecular detection of SARS-CoV-2, and allowed quantification of 3D cancer progression and drug susceptibility through light interaction with living cells.
Validation· The study validated the optical modulation via physical stimuli, molecular detection with plasmonic nanoparticles, and the functionality of living optical fibers with cancer cell spheroids.
