Motivation· To assess the influence of formal direct anterior approach training on outcomes in primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Retrospective comparison of 726 primary THA cases between a surgeon with formal DAA training and four surgeons without.
Result· No significant differences in complications except longer operative times for the trained surgeon, possibly due to experience differences.
Validation· Retrospective clinical data analysis with multivariable adjustment.
