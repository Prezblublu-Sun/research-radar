单位: Department of Materials Engineering

相关性: 该论文研究了GelMA/GG生物墨水的力学性能与细胞活性平衡，未涉及任何AI方法，仅作为生物墨水背景参考。
边界: This paper does not integrate AI/ML into any bioprinting workflow stage; it is a purely experimental material characterization study without computational modeling or autonomous decision-making.
动机· 平衡生物墨水力学强度与细胞活性，以应用于中枢神经软组织工程。

验证· 流变测试、力学测试、酶解实验、细胞培养及活死染色验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To balance mechanical strength and cell viability in GelMA/GG bioinks for soft-tissue engineering of the central nervous system.
Method· Blended gelatin methacrylate with gellan gum at various concentrations and characterized rheological, mechanical, degradation, and cell viability properties.
Result· Higher gellan gum content increased stiffness but reduced cell viability; formulations with 4.0% GelMA/0.25% GG and 2.5% GelMA/0.5% GG maintained over 85% viability after 14 days.
Validation· Rheometry, uniaxial compression, enzymatic degradation, and live/dead cell assays.

Validation· Clinical validation through retrospective chart review and statistical analysis of 120 patients.
