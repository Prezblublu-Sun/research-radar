English summary & terms
Motivation· To review and compare measurement tools for assessing patient satisfaction after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A descriptive literature review was conducted by searching PubMed, Science Direct, and Google Scholar.
Result· Six different satisfaction scales were identified, each offering a unique perspective on patient satisfaction.
Validation· No experimental validation; the study is a review of existing surveys.
