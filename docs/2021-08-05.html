英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To capture lessons from supporting the rapid transition to remote learning during the Covid-19 pandemic in higher education.
Method· Two models were developed: a route for shifting course components to remote delivery and an active learning cycle adapted from media practice.
Result· Key lessons include the importance of active learning, communication, community building, and retaining curriculum jewels.
Validation· Qualitative feedback from academics involved in the transition process.
