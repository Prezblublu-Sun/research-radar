Fast computation of uncertainty quantification measures in the geostatistical approach to solve inverse problems

All-polyethylene tibial components generate higher stress and micromotions than metal-backed tibial components in total knee arthroplasty

Low Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Background only simulation_case
Jean Brihault, Alessandro Navacchia, Silvia Pianigiani, Luc Labey, Ronny De Corte et al. Knee Surgery Sports Traumatology Arthroscopy 2015-05-09 10.1007/s00167-015-3630-8
单位: Faculté de Médicine, Université F. Rabelais, Tours, France
通讯: Jean Brihault @ Faculté de Médicine, Université F. Rabelais, Tours, France
相关性: 该论文研究膝关节胫骨假体而非髋关节股骨柄，不涉及股骨近端应力遮挡或Gruen分区分析。
边界: This paper focuses on tibial components in total knee arthroplasty, not on femoral stems in hip implants; it does not quantify stress shielding in Gruen zones, does not model the proximal femur, and does not compare intact vs implanted femur.
动机· 比较全聚乙烯与金属背衬胫骨假体的生物力学性能。
方法· 使用有限元模型模拟深蹲动作，评估不同假体设计下的应力和微动。
结果· 全聚乙烯假体产生更高且不均匀的应力及五倍微动。
验证· 基于已验证的肌肉骨骼模型施加载荷，无实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To biomechanically compare all-polyethylene versus metal-backed tibial components in total knee arthroplasty.
Method· A finite element model was developed using loads from a validated musculoskeletal model during a squat activity at 120° of flexion.
Result· All-polyethylene components resulted in significantly higher and more uneven cancellous bone stresses and fivefold increased micromotions.
Validation· The study used a validated musculoskeletal model for loading but no experimental validation of the FE results.

Factors Associated with Restricted Hip Extension during Gait in Women after Total Hip Arthroplasty

Low Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Background only clinical Exp. validation Patient-specific
Rui Tsukagoshi, Hiroshige Tateuchi, Yoshihiro Fukumoto, Haruhiko Akiyama, Kazutaka So et al. Hip International 2015-05-09 10.5301/hipint.5000286
单位: Department of Rehabilitation Science, Hyogo University of Health Sciences, Kobe - Japan
通讯: Rui Tsukagoshi @ Department of Rehabilitation Science, Hyogo University of Health Sciences, Kobe - Japan
相关性: 该论文研究全髋关节置换术后步态中髋关节伸展受限的因素，未涉及应力遮挡、微动或有限元分析。
边界: This paper does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics; it focuses on clinical gait analysis and muscle strength, not implant biomechanics or FEA.
动机· 探究全髋置换术后步态中髋伸展受限的临床因素。
方法· 对67名女性患者进行步态分析、肌力与关节活动度测量及回归分析。
结果· 髋外展肌力矩、疼痛和被动伸展活动度是主要影响因素。
验证· 临床步态数据与统计回归验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To identify clinical factors associated with restricted hip extension during gait after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Biomechanical gait analysis and isometric strength measurements were performed on 67 female THA patients, followed by stepwise multiple regression.
Result· Hip abductor torque, hip pain, and passive hip extension range of motion significantly predicted peak hip extension angle.
Validation· Clinical gait data and statistical regression analysis.

单位: Department of Orthopaedics, Medical University of Vienna, Waehringer Guertel 18 - 20, 1090, Vienna, Austria. reinhard.schuh@meduniwien.ac.at
通讯: Reinhard Schuh @ Department of Orthopaedics, Medical University of Vienna, Waehringer Guertel 18 - 20, 1090, Vienna, Austria. reinhard.sc
相关性: 该论文研究踝关节韧带修复的生物力学，与髋关节植入物应力遮挡和微动分析无关。
边界: This paper focuses on ankle ligament reconstruction, not hip implant stress shielding, micromotion, or femoral stem mechanics.
动机· 比较不同踝关节韧带修复方法的生物力学稳定性
方法· 使用新鲜冷冻人体标本进行准静态扭转测试

Motivation· To compare the biomechanical stability of different anterior talofibular ligament reconstruction techniques.
Method· Quasi-static torsion tests were performed on fresh-frozen human cadaveric specimens assigned to three repair groups.

Validation· Cadaveric biomechanical testing with statistical analysis.

Treatment of periprosthetic femoral fractures after femoral revision using a long stem

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore clinical
Young‐Woo Kim, Chiaki Tanaka, Hiroshi Tada, Hiroshi Kanoe, Takaaki Shirai BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders 2015-05-09 10.1186/s12891-015-0565-7
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Kyoto City Hospital, 1-2 Mibu,higashitakada-cho, Nakagyo, Kyoto, 604-8845, Japan. woochan76@hotmail.co.jp
通讯: Young‐Woo Kim @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Kyoto City Hospital, 1-2 Mibu,higashitakada-cho, Nakagyo, Kyoto, 604-8845, Japan. woo
相关性: 该论文仅关注临床骨折治疗结果，未涉及任何生物力学建模或应力遮挡分析。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, no FEA, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no implant design comparison.
动机· 探讨长柄翻修后股骨假体周围骨折的治疗特点。
方法· 回顾性分析11例Vancouver B1型骨折患者的治疗与随访数据。
结果· 多数B1型骨折通过切开复位内固定成功愈合，但横断骨折伴骨质量差需再次翻修。
验证· 临床随访结果，无生物力学验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To characterize clinical and radiographic features of periprosthetic femoral fractures after revision with a long stem.
Method· Retrospective review of 11 female patients with Vancouver type B1 fractures treated between 1998 and 2013.
Result· Most B1 fractures healed with open reduction and internal fixation; transverse fractures with poor bone quality may require revision.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with radiographic assessment, no biomechanical testing.
