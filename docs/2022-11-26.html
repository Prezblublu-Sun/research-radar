Looking at the posterior: accuracy and uncertainty of neural-network predictions

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Skim only numerical_benchmark UQ
H. Linander, O. Balabanov, H. Yang, B. Mehlig arXiv preprint 2022-11-26 10.1088/2632-2153/ad0ab4
相关性: 该论文探讨了神经网络预测中的不确定性量化方法，可迁移至代理模型中的不确定性估计，但未涉及有限元或生物力学设计。
边界: This paper does not address expensive simulation sampling efficiency, constraint handling, or implant/biomechanics design; it focuses on general neural network uncertainty quantification.
动机· 量化神经网络预测中的不确定性，并理解其与准确性的关系。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify uncertainty in neural network predictions and understand its relationship with prediction accuracy.
Method· Posteriors are approximated using deep ensembles, and the interplay between epistemic and aleatoric uncertainty is analyzed.
Result· Prediction accuracy depends intricately on both uncertainty types, and a novel acquisition function outperforms common methods in active learning.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on fully-connected, convolutional, and attention-based neural networks.
