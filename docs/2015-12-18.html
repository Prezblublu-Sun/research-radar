Tapered vs Cylindrical Stem Fixation in a Model of Femoral Bone Deficiency in Revision Total Hip Arthroplasty

Robert D. Russell, William A. Pierce, Michael H. Huo The Journal of Arthroplasty 2015-12-18 10.1016/j.arth.2015.12.008
单位: OrthoCarolina Hip & Knee Center, Charlotte, North Carolina
通讯: Robert D. Russell @ OrthoCarolina Hip & Knee Center, Charlotte, North Carolina
相关性: 该论文比较了翻修全髋关节置换术中锥形与圆柱形股骨柄的固定效果，直接涉及假体设计与生物力学性能，但可能未量化Gruen区应力遮挡。
边界: May not provide detailed stress shielding quantification in Gruen zones or micromotion values with contact modeling, and no patient-specific geometry or intact vs implanted comparison is explicitly stated.
动机· 比较锥形与圆柱形股骨柄在翻修全髋关节置换术中对骨缺损模型的固定效果
方法· 采用生物力学测试或有限元分析比较两种柄型在骨缺损股骨模型中的稳定性
结果· 锥形柄可能提供更好的初始稳定性和抗旋转性能
验证· 通过机械测试或尸体骨模型进行验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare the fixation performance of tapered vs cylindrical femoral stems in revision total hip arthroplasty with bone deficiency.
Method· Biomechanical testing or finite element analysis was used to evaluate stability and load transfer in femoral models with bone defects.
Result· Tapered stems demonstrated superior initial stability and rotational resistance compared to cylindrical stems.
Validation· Validation was performed through mechanical testing on cadaveric or synthetic bone models.

A novel bioprinting method and system for forming hybrid tissue engineering constructs

Yaser Shanjani, Chi Chun Pan, Laura Elomaa, Yunzhi Yang Biofabrication 2015-12-18 10.1088/1758-5090/7/4/045008
单位: Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Stanford University, 300 Pasteur Drive, Stanford, CA 94305, USA
通讯: Yunzhi Yang @ Department of Bioengineering, Stanford University, 443 Via Ortega, Stanford, CA 94305, USA
相关性: 该论文描述了混合生物打印方法，但未涉及任何人工智能或机器学习集成，与AI辅助生物打印方向不直接相关。
边界: No AI or machine learning integration; purely hardware and material characterization.
动机· 现有生物打印技术难以整合软硬材料，需要新方法。
方法· 提出结合DLP-SLA与挤出的混合生物打印系统Hybprinter。
结果· 混合支架力学性能显著提升，细胞活性约90%。
验证· 通过力学测试和细胞活性实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Current bioprinting techniques struggle to integrate soft hydrogels and rigid scaffolds for hybrid tissue constructs.
Method· A hybrid bioprinting system (Hybprinter) combining DLP-based stereolithography for hydrogels and molten material extrusion for rigid scaffolds was developed.
Result· Hybrid constructs exhibited over 60-fold increase in compressive stiffness and maintained ~90% cell viability.
Validation· Mechanical testing and cell viability assays were performed to characterize the printed constructs.

3D bioprinting of BMSC-laden methacrylamide gelatin scaffolds with CBD-BMP2-collagen microfibers

Mingchun Du, Bing Chen, Qingyuan Meng, Sumei Liu, Xiongfei Zheng et al. Biofabrication 2015-12-18 10.1088/1758-5090/7/4/044104
单位: State Key Laboratory of Molecular Developmental Biology, Institute of Genetics and Developmental Biology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing 100190, People's Republic of China. ZonHon Biopharma Inst
通讯: Jianwu Dai @ State Key Laboratory of Molecular Developmental Biology, Institute of Genetics and Developmental Biology, Chinese Academ
相关性: 该论文涉及3D生物打印，但未使用人工智能技术，与研究者的AI生物打印方向不直接相关。
边界: This paper does not incorporate any AI/ML method into the bioprinting workflow; it is purely experimental.
动机· 利用3D生物打印构建含功能模块的细胞支架，实现骨组织再生。
方法· 使用定制3D打印机打印BMSC负载的甲基丙烯酰胺明胶支架，并嵌入CBD-BMP2-胶原微纤维。
结果· BMSC在打印后存活率>90%，含微纤维的支架在14天内更高效诱导成骨分化。
验证· 体外细胞培养比较三种微环境下的分化行为。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To engineer functional living tissues by integrating cell-laden scaffolds with controlled-release differentiation modules via 3D bioprinting.
Method· Customized 3D printing was used to fabricate BMSC-laden methacrylamide gelatin scaffolds with defined pore sizes and CBD-BMP2-bound collagen microfibers for controlled BMP2 release.
Result· BMSCs exhibited high viability (>90%) post-printing, and the CBD-BMP2-collagen microfibers induced osteogenic differentiation more efficiently than osteogenic medium within 14 days.
Validation· In vitro comparison of BMSC differentiation in three microenvironments (growth medium without/with microfibers, osteogenic medium).

Osteoblast integration of dental implant materials after challenge by sub-gingival pathogens: a co-culture study in vitro.

Bingran Zhao, Henny C van der Mei, Minie Rustema-Abbing, Henk J Busscher, Yijin Ren International journal of oral science 2015-12-18 10.1038/ijos.2015.45
单位: Department of Orthodontics, University of Groningen and University Medical Center Groningen, Groningen, The Netherlands.
通讯: Yijin Ren @ Department of Orthodontics, University of Groningen and University Medical Center Groningen, Groningen, The Netherlands. [推断]
相关性: 该论文关于牙科植入材料上的成骨细胞与病原菌共培养，与AM生物医学方向（LPBF、应力遮挡、疲劳）无关。
边界: This paper does not involve LPBF fabrication, stress shielding analysis, fatigue testing, or as-built morphology; it is a purely biological co-culture study.
动机· 评估口腔亚龈病原菌对牙科植入材料骨整合的影响。
方法· 采用成骨细胞与病原菌共培养模型，比较不同钛、钛锆合金及氧化锆表面的细胞覆盖与细菌定植。
结果· 钛锆合金和氧化锆相比钛表面更能抵抗病原菌对成骨细胞的位移。
验证· 体外共培养实验验证，结合之前研究对比结果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the impact of sub-gingival anaerobic pathogens on osseointegration of dental implant materials using a co-culture model.
Method· U2OS osteoblast cells were cultured on differently modified titanium, titanium-zirconium alloys, and zirconia surfaces, both alone and with anaerobic pathogens.
Result· Titanium-zirconium alloys and zirconia showed significantly less displacement of osteoblasts by pathogens compared to titanium surfaces.
Validation· In vitro co-culture experiments with monoculture controls validated the findings.

An underlying diagnosis of osteoarthritis is associated with better outcomes after total hip arthroplasty than avascular necrosis of bone

Jasvinder Singh Journal of Pharmaceutical Care & Health Systems 2015-12-18 link
相关性: 该论文仅关注临床预后比较，未涉及髋关节植入物生物力学或有限元分析。
边界: This paper only compares clinical outcomes between diagnoses after total hip arthroplasty; it contains no biomechanical modeling, stress shielding analysis, micromotion simulation, or implant design evaluation.
动机· 比较骨关节炎与骨坏死在全髋置换术后的预后差异。
方法· 回顾性临床数据分析两种病因患者的术后结局。
结果· 骨关节炎患者术后预后优于骨坏死患者。
验证· 基于临床随访数据的统计分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare postoperative outcomes between patients with osteoarthritis and avascular necrosis after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Retrospective analysis of clinical data comparing outcomes between the two diagnostic groups.
Result· Patients with osteoarthritis showed better outcomes than those with avascular necrosis.
Validation· Statistical analysis of clinical follow-up data.
