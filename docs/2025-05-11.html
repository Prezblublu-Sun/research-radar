Method· Titanium samples were printed at 0°, 40°, and 90° build angles, hydrothermally nanostructured, and characterized for roughness and antimicrobial activity against MRSA and C. albicans.

边界: This paper does not involve bioprinting, bioink, or any biomedical application; it focuses on civil structural dynamics.
动机· 结构动态响应有限元模拟计算成本高，需高效替代模型。
方法· 提出多输入算子网络MIONet，结合物理信息损失和Schur补法加速训练。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Finite element modeling for dynamic structural analysis is computationally expensive, motivating the need for efficient surrogate models.
Method· A Multiple Input Operator Network (MIONet) with a second trunk network for temporal encoding is proposed, incorporating a physics-informed loss based on precomputed dynamic matrices and a Schur complement to reduce training cost.
Result· The model achieves FEM-level accuracy on a beam and a bridge case, with over 100x faster inference than GRU-based DeepONet.
Validation· Validated on numerical simulations of a simple beam and the KW-51 bridge.
