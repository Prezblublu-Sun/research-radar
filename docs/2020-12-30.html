英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To assess whether the sterility of iodophor-impregnated adhesive drape is maintained at incision closure in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Swabs were collected from skin before drape application, from drape surface at closure, and from skin after drape removal in 225 primary THA patients, followed by microbiological culture.
Result· The contamination rate of skin after drape removal was significantly higher than that of skin after initial sterilization and that of the drape surface at closure.
Validation· Clinical microbiological analysis with statistical comparison of contamination rates.
