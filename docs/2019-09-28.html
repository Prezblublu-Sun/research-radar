英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of morbid obesity on patient-reported outcomes after primary total joint arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective review of 755 primary total joint arthroplasty cases comparing patients with BMI ≥40 to those with BMI <40.
Result· Morbid obesity negatively affected patient-reported outcomes only in total knee arthroplasty during the first 6 months postoperatively.
Validation· Multivariate analyses were performed to control for confounding factors.
