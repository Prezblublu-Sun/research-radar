单位: Department of Aerospace Structures and Materials, Faculty of Aerospace Engineering, Delft University of Technology (TU Delft), Kluyverweg 1, 2629 HS Delft, The Netherlands
通讯: Reza Hedayati @ Department of Aerospace Structures and Materials, Faculty of Aerospace Engineering, Delft University of Technology (TU D
相关性: 该论文主要关注多孔超材料的力学性能解析公式精度提升，未涉及LPBF制造、疲劳测试或骨整合评估。
边界: This paper does not include LPBF fabrication, mechanical testing (fatigue or static), surface roughness, or osseointegration; it focuses solely on analytical beam theory conversion for lattice structures.
动机· 提高多孔超材料力学性能解析公式的精度，以快速预测植入物性能。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To improve the accuracy of analytical relationships for predicting mechanical properties of permeable metamaterials used in orthopedic implants.
Method· A conversion approach from Euler-Bernoulli to Timoshenko beam theory is presented and applied to six unit cell types (BCC, hexagonal packing, rhombicuboctahedron, diamond, truncated cube, truncated octahedron).
Result· The converted Timoshenko-based relationships reduce the discrepancy between analytical and numerical results by an order of magnitude.
Validation· Validation is performed via finite element numerical simulations comparing analytical predictions to numerical results.
