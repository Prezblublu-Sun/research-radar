Dexamethasone Is Associated With Lower Rates of Pulmonary Complications Following Cemented Femoral Fixation During Primary Total Hip Arthroplasty

Andrew Vega, Kevin C Liu, Sagar Telang, Mary K. Richardson, Jay R. Lieberman et al. The Journal of Arthroplasty 2025-04-02 10.1016/j.arth.2025.03.069
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Keck School of Medicine of the University of Southern California, Los Angeles, California
通讯: Andrew Vega @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Keck School of Medicine of the University of Southern California, Los Angeles, Califo
相关性: 该论文为临床医学研究，不涉及任何生物力学建模或有限元分析。
边界: This paper is a clinical study on pharmacological intervention during THA, with no biomechanical modeling, stress shielding analysis, micromotion quantification, or finite element simulation.
动机· 研究地塞米松对全髋置换中骨水泥股骨固定后肺部并发症的影响。
方法· 回顾性临床数据分析，比较使用与未使用地塞米松的患者。
结果· 地塞米松组肺部并发症发生率较低。
验证· 基于大规模临床数据库的统计分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate whether dexamethasone reduces pulmonary complications after cemented femoral fixation in primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective cohort study analyzing clinical outcomes between patients who received dexamethasone and those who did not.
Result· Dexamethasone was associated with significantly lower rates of pulmonary complications.
Validation· Statistical analysis of a large clinical database with propensity score matching.

Increasing Limb Length During Stage 1 Revision Leads to Higher Rates of Instability Following Stage 2 Revision Total Hip Arthroplasty

Ian M. Clapp, Dalton L Braathen, Brenna E. Blackburn, Christopher E. Pelt, Lucas A. Anderson et al. The Journal of Arthroplasty 2025-04-02 10.1016/j.arth.2025.03.071
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, University of Utah, Salt Lake City, Utah
通讯: Ian M. Clapp @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, University of Utah, Salt Lake City, Utah
相关性: 该论文仅关注临床翻修手术后的稳定性，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡或生物力学建模。
边界: This paper only reports clinical outcomes of hip arthroplasty revision and does not include any biomechanical modeling, stress shielding quantification, or finite element analysis.
动机· 探究一期翻修中肢体延长对二期翻修后稳定性的影响
方法· 回顾性分析临床数据，比较不同肢体延长量下的脱位率
结果· 较大肢体延长与更高的二期翻修后不稳定率相关
验证· 基于临床病例统计，无生物力学验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To determine whether increasing limb length during stage 1 revision total hip arthroplasty increases the risk of instability after stage 2 revision.
Method· A retrospective clinical cohort study comparing dislocation rates among patients with varying degrees of limb lengthening during the first stage of revision.
Result· Greater limb lengthening was associated with significantly higher rates of instability after the second stage revision.
Validation· Clinical outcome analysis without any biomechanical or computational modeling validation.

Ex vivo comparison of drilling techniques for optimizing primary stability of zirconia dental implants in different bone densities.

Kawe Sagheb, Senem Yildirimturk, Sebahat Kaya, Shengchi Fan, Marius Morlock et al. International journal of implant dentistry 2025-04-02 10.1186/s40729-025-00603-z
单位: Department of Prosthetic Dentistry, University Medical Centre, Augustusplatz 2, 55131, Mainz, Germany.
通讯: Sebahat Kaya @ Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, Plastic Surgery, University Medical Centre, Johannes Gutenberg - Universit
相关性: 该论文研究牙科种植体而非髋关节植入物，与用户研究方向无关。
边界: This paper focuses on dental implant primary stability in porcine bone, not on hip implant stress shielding, micromotion, or FEA of the femur.
动机· 研究不同钻孔方案对氧化锆牙种植体初期稳定性的影响。
方法· 在猪骨中模拟不同骨密度，使用四种螺纹切割方案并测量植入扭矩。
结果· 减少螺纹切割在软骨中提高稳定性，但硬骨需全螺纹切割。
验证· 离体猪骨实验结合曼-惠特尼U检验统计分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how drilling protocol modifications affect the primary stability of zirconia dental implants in varying bone densities.
Method· Four thread-cutting protocols (none, one-third, two-thirds, full) were tested in porcine hip and tibia bones simulating D2/D3 and D4 bone, with insertion torque measured.
Result· Reduced thread cutting significantly improved insertion torque in soft bone, while full thread cutting was required for hard bone to meet manufacturer guidelines.
Validation· Ex vivo mechanical testing on fresh porcine bone with statistical comparison via Mann-Whitney U test.
