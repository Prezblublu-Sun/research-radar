Discovering Physics-Informed Neural Networks Model for Solving Partial Differential Equations through Evolutionary Computation

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Skim only numerical_benchmark
Bo Zhang, Chao Yang arXiv preprint 2024-05-18 10.1016/j.swevo.2024.101589
相关性: 该论文提出进化计算自动搜索PINNs架构，可迁移至生物力学代理模型设计，但未涉及约束优化或不确定性量化。
边界: No constraint handling, no uncertainty quantification, and no application to implant or biomechanics design.
动机· 现有PINNs模型依赖人工设计，搜索空间有限，难以发现高性能架构。
方法· 提出进化计算方法，同时搜索层数、神经元数、跳跃连接和参数化激活函数。
结果· 进化搜索的模型在Klein-Gordon、Burgers和Lamé方程上精度和收敛速度优于贝叶斯优化和随机搜索。
验证· 在三个经典PDE基准上比较不同搜索方法的模型性能。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Existing PINN models are manually designed with limited search spaces, hindering the discovery of superior architectures.
Method· An evolutionary computation method is proposed to simultaneously search for layer numbers, neuron counts, shortcut connections, and parametric activation functions expressed as binary trees.
Result· Models discovered by the evolutionary method achieve higher approximation accuracy and faster convergence on Klein-Gordon, Burgers, and Lamé equations compared to Bayesian optimization and random search.
Validation· Performance is validated on three benchmark PDEs with comparisons to Bayesian optimization and random search.

Motivation· To accelerate the discovery of novel optical materials by leveraging federated databases and machine learning.

Result· Identified promising high-refractive-index material candidates for further theoretical and experimental investigation.
Validation· No experimental validation; the approach relies on database screening and automated calculations.

A Unified Approach Towards Active Learning and Out-of-Distribution Detection

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Background only numerical_benchmark
Sebastian Schmidt, Leonard Schenk, Leo Schwinn, Stephan Günnemann arXiv preprint 2024-05-18 link
相关性: 该论文关注主动学习和分布外检测的统一框架，未涉及有限元分析或代理模型，与用户研究方向无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address expensive simulation sampling, surrogate modeling, uncertainty quantification, or any biomechanics/implant design context.
动机· 现有主动学习和分布外检测方法相互独立，缺乏统一框架。
方法· 提出SISOM方法，利用特征空间距离度量统一解决两个任务。
结果· 在OpenOOD基准测试中取得第一或第二名，主动学习任务中达到顶尖性能。
验证· 在多个公开基准数据集上评估性能。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Current active learning and out-of-distribution detection methods are studied separately, lacking a unified solution.
Method· SISOM leverages feature space distance metrics to jointly address active learning and OOD detection.
Result· SISOM achieves top-1 or top-2 performance on OpenOOD benchmarks and top-1 on three active learning benchmarks.
Validation· Extensive experiments on standard benchmarks demonstrate effectiveness.

单位: Department of Surgery, Faculty of Medicine, University of Salamanca, 37007 Salamanca, Spain.
通讯: Aritza Brizuela-Velasco @ Faculty of Health Sciences, Miguel de Cervantes European University, 47012 Valladolid, Spain. [推断]
相关性: 该研究与增材制造生物医学植入物无关，未涉及孔隙结构、应力遮挡或LPBF制造。
边界: No LPBF or AM fabrication, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), no porous structure design for stress shielding, no surface roughness or osseointegration metrics relevant to implant design.
动机· 探索1%二甲双胍对牛源性异种移植物引导骨再生的增强效果。

Motivation· To evaluate whether locally applied 1% metformin enhances bone neoformation and osseointegration in a guided bone regeneration animal model using bovine-derived xenografts.
Method· Critical-sized calvarial defects in rabbits were filled with particulate bovine hydroxyapatite/hyaluronate grafts, with or without 1% metformin, and histomorphometric analysis was performed after 6 weeks.
Result· Metformin-treated defects showed higher percentages of new bone area and bone-biomaterial contact in both proximal and distal regions, though differences were not statistically significant.
Validation· In vivo rabbit calvarial defect model with histomorphometric quantification.
