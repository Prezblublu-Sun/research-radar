Ti6Al4V orthopedic implant with biomimetic heterogeneous structure via 3D printing for improving osteogenesis

Xuan Pei, Linnan Wang, Changchun Zhou, Lina Wu, Haoyuan Lei et al. Materials & Design 2022-07-20 10.1016/j.matdes.2022.110964
单位: College of Biomedical Engineering, Sichuan University, Chengdu 610064, China
通讯: Changchun Zhou @ College of Biomedical Engineering, Sichuan University, Chengdu 610064, China  |  Yujiang Fan @ College of Biomedical Engineering, Sichuan University, Chengdu 610064, China
相关性: 该论文直接涉及通过SLM制造多孔钛合金植入物以促进骨再生，与您的研究方向高度相关。
边界: The paper does not explicitly address stress shielding, fatigue testing, or surface roughness characterization.
动机· 受到自然界多孔组织异质结构的启发，设计仿生骨植入物以促进骨再生。
方法· 基于Voronoi-Thiessen图设计异质多孔结构，并通过SLM制造，结合有限元分析力学与流体特性。
结果· 准均匀结构具有类似天然骨的不规则拓扑，半异质结构表现出更好的骨生成效果。
验证· 通过有限元仿真以及体外细胞实验和体内动物实验进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Inspired by natural heterogeneous porous structures, this study aims to design biomimetic bone implants that enhance osteogenesis through controlled topology.
Method· Heterogeneous porous structures were designed using Voronoi-Thiessen diagrams, fabricated by selective laser melting, and characterized via finite element analysis for mechanical and hydrodynamic properties.
Result· Quasi-homogeneous structures exhibited irregular topology mimicking natural bone, while semi-heterogeneous structures demonstrated superior osteogenesis in vitro and in vivo.
Validation· Validation was performed through finite element simulations, in vitro cell assays, and in vivo animal experiments.
