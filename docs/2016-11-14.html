Stem geometry changes initial femoral fixation stability of a revised press-fit hip prosthesis: A finite element study.

High Hip Implant & Femoral Stem pubmed Direct Read now simulation_case
Robert D Russell, Michael H Huo, Danieli C Rodrigues, Victor Kosmopoulos Technology and health care : official journal of the European Society for Engineering and Medicine 2016-11-14 10.3233/THC-161235
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, University of Texas Southwestern Medical Center, Dallas, TX, USA.
通讯: Victor Kosmopoulos @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, University of North Texas Health Science Center, Fort Worth, TX, USA.; Department of [推断]
相关性: 该论文直接研究了股骨柄几何形状对初始固定稳定性的影响，包括微动和接触压力，与用户的髋关节植入物方向高度相关。
边界: Does not quantify stress shielding or bone remodeling, does not report stress/strain in Gruen zones, and does not compare intact vs implanted femur.
动机· 比较圆柱形和锥形股骨柄在翻修全髋关节置换术中的初始固定稳定性。
方法· 建立有限元模型，模拟不同压配条件，分析接触压力和微动。
结果· 圆柱形柄比锥形柄接触压力更低、微动更小；0.05 mm压配产生更高接触压力。
验证· 有限元模拟，无实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the initial fixation stability of cylindrical and tapered femoral stems under different press-fit conditions in revision total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A finite element model of a femur with trabecular bone defect was created, and two generic stem geometries were simulated with 0.05 mm and 0.01 mm press-fit, applying physiological loading.
Result· Cylindrical stems consistently showed lower contact pressures and less micromotion than tapered stems; the 0.05 mm press-fit generated higher contact pressures regardless of stem geometry.
Validation· Validation was solely based on finite element simulation without experimental confirmation.
