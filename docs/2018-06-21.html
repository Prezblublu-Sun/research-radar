边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, contact mechanics, or bone remodeling; it focuses on Stokes flow in porous media without any biomedical context or experimental validation.
动机· 直接数值模拟随机多孔介质中的Stokes流计算成本高，需要降阶模型加速。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Direct numerical simulation of Stokes flow through random porous media is computationally expensive, motivating the need for reduced-order models to accelerate simulations.
Method· A probabilistic Darcy-type reduced-order model is proposed that learns a map from fine-scale pore topology to effective diffusivity using only a few tens of full-order Stokes simulations.
Result· The learned reduced-order model significantly accelerates uncertainty quantification tasks and provides quantitative confidence metrics for predictive estimates.
Validation· The model is validated on numerical benchmarks, demonstrating its accuracy and efficiency compared to full-order simulations.
