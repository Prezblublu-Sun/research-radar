单位: 3D Systems, Grauwmeer 14, 3001, Leuven, Belgium
通讯: Manisha Bisht @ 3D Systems, Grauwmeer 14, 3001, Leuven, Belgium
相关性: 该论文研究了激光粉末床熔融制造Ti-6Al-4V过程中熔池监测与拉伸性能的关系，对生物医学植入物的增材制造质量控制有参考价值。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures, fatigue testing, surface roughness, or osseointegration relevant to biomedical implants.
动机· 建立熔池监测事件与拉伸性能的关联，实现增材制造在线质量控制
方法· 使用DMP-meltpool监测Ti-6Al-4V ELI的SLM过程，分析事件密度与拉伸性能的关系
结果· 发现塑性伸长率与事件密度呈反比，表明该方法可预测缺陷

Method· Ti-6Al-4V ELI bars were built using LPBF and monitored with DMP-meltpool; event density was compared to tensile test results.
Result· An inverse relationship between plastic elongation and event density was observed, indicating defect detection capability.
Validation· Validation was performed by mechanical tensile testing of LPBF-fabricated specimens.

相关性: 该论文研究铝合金粉末特性对SLM可加工性的影响，完全不涉及生物医用植入物、多孔结构、应力遮挡或力学性能测试。

动机· 研究粉末特性对选区激光熔化AlSi12合金可加工性的影响。
