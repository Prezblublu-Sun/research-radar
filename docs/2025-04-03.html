边界: No FEA surrogate modeling, no implant/biomechanics context, no constraint handling or multi-objective optimization.
动机· 学习任意分布的高密度区域，用于置信集和不确定性量化。
方法· 提出一种非适当算法，输出椭球体，体积竞争因子为exp(Õ(d^{1/2}))。
结果· 与最优球体相比，算法在体积上达到指数级竞争比，并证明了适当学习的难解性。
验证· 理论证明，无实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compute high-density confidence sets for arbitrary distributions with minimal volume, a fundamental problem in high-dimensional statistics and uncertainty quantification.
Method· An improper algorithm that outputs an ellipsoid achieving a volume competitive ratio of exp(Õ(d^{1/2})) against the optimal ball, using coresets and convex optimization.
Result· The algorithm provides an exponential improvement over previous coreset-based methods, and a computational hardness result shows proper learning of balls is intractable within exp(Õ(d^{1-o(1)})) approximation.
Validation· Theoretical analysis with no experimental validation; results are proven under standard statistical learning assumptions.
