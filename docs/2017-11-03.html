边界: No FEA surrogate modeling, no biomedical or implant context, and the method is demonstrated on a generic engineering example rather than biomechanics.

Motivation· To address the neglect of metamodel and data uncertainties in traditional robustness design optimization.

Validation· Validated through a single engineering example with comparative studies against traditional methods.
