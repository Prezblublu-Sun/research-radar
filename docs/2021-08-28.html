Development of novel hybrid TPMS cellular lattices and their mechanical characterisation

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Transferable Save for project mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Nejc Novak, Oraib Al‐Ketan, Matej Borovinšek, Lovre Krstulović‐Opara, Reza Rowshan et al. Journal of Materials Research and Technology 2021-08-28 10.1016/j.jmrt.2021.08.092
单位: Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, University of Maribor, Maribor, Slovenia
通讯: Nejc Novak @ Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, University of Maribor, Maribor, Slovenia  |  Oraib Al‐Ketan @ Core Technology Platforms Operations, New York University Abu Dhabi, Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates
相关性: 该论文研究了混合TPMS点阵结构的力学性能，与用户关注的通过多孔结构匹配刚度以减少应力遮挡有一定关联，但未涉及生物医学植入物或骨整合。
边界: This paper does not address stress shielding, osseointegration, or any biomedical context; it focuses on crashworthiness for protective equipment.
动机· 探索混合TPMS点阵结构能否比单一结构具有更优的力学性能。
方法· 通过隐式函数设计Gyroid和Diamond的线性和径向混合点阵，采用LS-DYNA有限元模拟和实验验证。
结果· 混合点阵的力学性能可通过成分比例调控，实验与模拟结果吻合良好。
验证· 对三种相对密度的单一结构进行动态和准静态实验验证，再预测混合结构并实验确认。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate whether hybrid TPMS cellular lattices can achieve superior mechanical properties compared to uniform lattices.
Method· Hybrid lattices (Gyroid and Diamond) were designed using implicit functions in linear and radial configurations, and their mechanical behavior was predicted via validated LS-DYNA finite element models and confirmed by LPBF fabrication and mechanical testing.
Result· The hybrid lattices exhibited tunable mechanical properties that agreed well between simulation and experiment, demonstrating potential for crashworthiness applications.
Validation· Finite element models were validated against experimental data for three relative densities of constituent lattices under dynamic and quasi-static loading, then applied to predict hybrid lattice behavior, which was subsequently verified experimentally.

Motivation· To evaluate the effectiveness of a diabetes educational mobile app in enhancing medical students' learning during the COVID-19 pandemic.
Method· A mobile app with educational modules and videos was used for 32 medical students, and satisfaction was measured via a 10-item Likert-scale questionnaire.

Validation· Descriptive statistical analysis of questionnaire responses from a single cohort of 32 students.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the infection risk of single-stage conversion to total hip arthroplasty after failed internal fixation of proximal femoral fractures.
Method· Retrospective analysis of 58 patients undergoing one-stage conversion THA, including preoperative laboratory results, hip aspirations, intraoperative microbiology, and sonication.
Result· Positive microbiological results were found in 10% of patients, and the early postoperative periprosthetic joint infection rate was 5.8%.
Validation· Clinical retrospective study with intraoperative microbiological diagnostics.
