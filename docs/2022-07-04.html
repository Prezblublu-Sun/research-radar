边界: This paper is a clinical cohort study comparing surgical approaches for THA, with no biomechanical modeling, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no FEA, and no implant design analysis.
动机· 比较后外侧入路与直接前入路对全髋关节置换术后患者活动参与的影响。
方法· 观察性队列研究，使用问卷评估术后1年内活动参与，以医院为工具变量校正混杂因素。
结果· 总体功能恢复相似，但就业患者中直接前入路恢复更好，更快重返工作。
验证· 临床观察性研究，无生物力学验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare postoperative activity and participation between posterolateral and direct anterior approaches for primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· An observational cohort study using questionnaires to assess the ICF domain 'activity and participation' preoperatively and at 3, 6, and 12 months, with hospital as an instrumental variable.
Result· Overall functional recovery was comparable, but among employed patients, the direct anterior approach yielded better functional recovery and faster return to work.
Validation· Clinical observational study without biomechanical validation.
