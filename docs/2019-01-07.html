Augmentation of DMLS Biomimetic Dental Implants with Weight-Bearing Strut to Balance of Biologic and Mechanical Demands: From Bench to Animal

High AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Read now animal Exp. validation
Jenny Zwei‐Chieng Chang, Pei‐I Tsai, Mark Yen‐Ping Kuo, Jui‐Sheng Sun, San‐Yuan Chen et al. Materials 2019-01-07 10.3390/ma12010164
单位: School of Dentistry, College of Medicine, National Taiwan University, Taipei 10051, Taiwan
通讯: Mark Yen‐Ping Kuo @ School of Dentistry, College of Medicine, National Taiwan University, Taipei 10051, Taiwan  |  Jui‐Sheng Sun @ Department of Orthopedic Surgery, College of Medicine, National Taiwan University, Taipei 10002, Taiwan
相关性: 该论文直接研究了DMLS制造的多孔钛合金牙种植体，通过添加承重支柱平衡力学与生物学需求，并进行了力学测试和动物实验验证，与用户研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper focuses on dental implants rather than femoral stems or hip implants, and does not explicitly quantify stress shielding or micromotion at the bone-implant interface.
动机· 弹性模量不匹配导致牙种植体周围骨吸收，需优化设计平衡力学与生物学需求。
方法· 设计不同孔隙率DMLS钛合金种植体，添加纵向承重支柱，进行三点弯曲、扭转和稳定性测试及体内实验。
结果· 60%孔隙率促进成骨基因表达，承重支柱增强力学强度，体内实验显示良好骨整合和拔出强度。
验证· 通过力学测试和动物植入实验验证设计有效性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Elastic modulus mismatch between dental implants and bone can cause peri-implant bone resorption, necessitating design optimization to balance mechanical strength and biological integration.
Method· Ti6Al4V dental implants with varying porosities were fabricated by DMLS and augmented with a longitudinal weight-bearing strut; mechanical tests (three-point bending, torsion, stability) and in vivo animal experiments were conducted.
Result· Implants with 60% porosity showed maximal ALP gene expression, and the strut augmentation improved mechanical strength; in vivo results demonstrated good bone ingrowth and higher pull-out strength.
Validation· Validation included mechanical testing and in vivo animal studies with histological and pull-out strength evaluation.
