单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Amirkabir University of Technology, Tehran, Iran
通讯: Mohamad Sadeghi @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Amirkabir University of Technology, Tehran, Iran
相关性: 该论文研究SLM工艺参数对不锈钢表面粗糙度的影响，未涉及生物医学植入物的多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能，仅作为增材制造背景参考。
边界: This paper focuses on surface roughness optimization of stainless steel 316L without any biomedical context, porous structure design, stress shielding analysis, fatigue testing, or osseointegration evaluation.
动机· 寻找SLM工艺参数优化表面粗糙度的最优值及扫描策略。
方法· 实验测量不同激光功率、扫描速度、 hatch间距和扫描模式下的表面粗糙度。

Motivation· To identify optimal SLM process parameters and scanning strategies for minimizing surface roughness in stainless steel 316L parts.
Method· Systematic experimental study varying laser power, scanning speed, hatch spacing, and scan pattern angles, measuring surface roughness with laser profilometry.
Result· Scanning speed is the most influential factor; optimal parameters (270 W, 900 mm/s, 0.06 mm hatch, 75° angle) with chessboard strategy yield 2.5× better surface quality.
Validation· Experimental measurement of surface roughness via laser profilometry across different parameter combinations.
