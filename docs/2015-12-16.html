Hamiltonian Monte Carlo and Borrowing Strength in Hierarchical Inverse Problems

Joseph B. Nagel, Bruno Sudret ASCE-ASME Journal of Risk and Uncertainty in Engineering Systems Part A Civil Engineering 2015-12-16 10.1061/ajrua6.0000847
单位: Ph.D. Candidate, ETH Zürich, Institute of Structural Engineering, Chair of Risk, Safety and Uncertainty Quantification, Stefano-Franscini-Platz 5, CH-8093 Zürich, Switzerland (corresponding author)
通讯: Joseph B. Nagel @ Ph.D. Candidate, ETH Zürich, Institute of Structural Engineering, Chair of Risk, Safety and Uncertainty Quantification,
相关性: 该论文提出的分层逆问题贝叶斯框架和哈密顿蒙特卡洛方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理模型的不确定性量化，但未涉及具体植入物设计或接触力学。
边界: No FEA surrogate modeling, no implant mechanics, no constraint handling or multi-objective optimization.
动机· 解决分层逆问题中样本稀缺时材料参数推断的统计强度借用问题
方法· 采用哈密顿蒙特卡洛结合分层贝叶斯框架进行多层级模型校准
结果· 相比经典MCMC，后验计算速度显著提升
验证· 通过数值算例对比算法性能
English summary & terms
Motivation· To efficiently infer material properties of individual specimens in hierarchical inverse problems by borrowing statistical strength from ensemble tests under scarce evidence.
Method· A Bayesian framework integrating Hamiltonian Monte Carlo with staged estimation and multilevel calibration for hierarchical inverse problems.
Result· Hamiltonian Monte Carlo drastically accelerates Bayesian posterior computations compared to classical MCMC.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on identically manufactured structural elements demonstrate algorithm performance.
