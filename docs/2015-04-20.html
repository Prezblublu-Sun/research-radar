An efficient Kriging method for global sensitivity of structural reliability analysis with non-probabilistic convex model

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Yishang Zhang, Yongshou Liu, Xufeng Yang, Bin Zhao Proceedings of the Institution of Mechanical Engineers Part O Journal of Risk and Reliability 2015-04-20 10.1177/1748006x15580631
单位: School of Mechanics, Civil Engineering and Architecture, Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi’an, P.R. China
通讯: Yishang Zhang @ School of Mechanics, Civil Engineering and Architecture, Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi’an, P.R. China
相关性: 该论文提出的基于主动学习Kriging的全局灵敏度分析方法可迁移至生物力学植入物设计中的不确定性量化与可靠性分析。
边界: This paper focuses on non-probabilistic convex model reliability sensitivity, not on FEA surrogate modeling for implant biomechanics or stress shielding.
动机· 解决非概率凸模型下结构可靠性灵敏度分析计算成本高的问题。
方法· 提出基于主动学习Kriging的全局灵敏度指标求解方法。
结果· 通过算例验证了所提方法在描述变量对可靠度影响方面的正确性和可行性。
验证· 采用数值算例和工程案例进行验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To efficiently compute global sensitivity indices for non-probabilistic reliability analysis under convex models, where Monte Carlo simulation is computationally prohibitive.
Method· An active learning Kriging surrogate is constructed to approximate the limit state function and compute moment-independent global sensitivity indices.
Result· The proposed method accurately quantifies the influence of uncertain variables on structural reliability with reduced computational cost.
Validation· Numerical examples and an engineering case study demonstrate the correctness and applicability of the approach.

单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Kobe University Graduate School of Medicine, 7-5-1 Kusunoki-cho, Chuo-ku, Kobe, 650-0017, Japan. shayashi@med.kobe-u.ac.jp
通讯: Shinya Hayashi @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Kobe University Graduate School of Medicine, 7-5-1 Kusunoki-cho, Chuo-ku, Kobe, 650-0
相关性: 该论文关注短锥形楔形柄在微创全髋关节置换术中的对线准确性，未涉及应力遮挡、微动或接触力学建模。
边界: This paper does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics; it focuses on surgical alignment accuracy without any FE or biomechanical modeling.
动机· 评估短锥形楔形柄在微创THA中的对线准确性及影响因素。
方法· 回顾性分析110例THA，比较直柄与锥形楔形柄的术后对线误差。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the accuracy of short tapered wedge stem alignment and identify factors contributing to malalignment during mini-invasive total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective study of 110 hips comparing postoperative stem alignment errors between straight and tapered wedge stems using CT-based 3D templating.
Result· Short tapered wedge stems achieved alignment accuracy comparable to straight stems, but high BMI significantly increased alignment error.
Validation· Clinical validation via CT scans and 3D templating software comparing planned versus actual stem orientation.
