通讯: Lianzhi Huo @ Institute of Remote Sensing and Digital Earth, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing 100101, P. R. China
相关性: 该论文专注于遥感图像分类的主动学习，与FEA替代建模或生物力学设计无关。
边界: No FEA, surrogate modeling, uncertainty quantification, or biomechanics context; method is specific to remote sensing image classification.
动机· 提高遥感图像分类中训练样本的选取效率。
方法· 基于图论的主动学习方法，将多样性选择转化为最密k子图问题。

Validation· Experimental validation on two remote sensing datasets (VHR multispectral and hyperspectral).

单位: Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Rush University Medical Center, Chicago, IL, USA
通讯: Mathew T. Mathew @ Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Rush University Medical Center, Chicago, IL, USA; Department of Biomedical Science, Un
相关性: 该论文研究髋关节植入物模块连接处的微动腐蚀，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡或骨-植入物界面力学。
边界: This paper focuses on fretting-corrosion at the modular head-stem junction, not on proximal femur stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics.
动机· 探究pH变化对髋关节植入物模块连接处微动腐蚀行为的影响。
方法· 使用定制装置模拟髋关节模块连接，在不同pH水平下进行微动腐蚀实验。
结果· pH影响腐蚀机制，pH 4.5组形成摩擦层降低损伤，pH 7.6组以粘着磨损为主。
验证· 体外实验，使用新生牛血清模拟滑液环境。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of pH variation on fretting-corrosion behavior at the modular head-stem junction of hip implants.
Method· A custom setup applied sinusoidal fretting motion to Ti alloy rods against CoCrMo pins in calf serum at four pH levels (3.0, 4.5, 6.0, 7.6).
Result· pH influenced corrosion mechanisms; pH 4.5 promoted tribolayer formation reducing metal loss, while pH 7.6 led to adhesive wear and higher potential drop.
Validation· In vitro fretting-corrosion tests with electrochemical and mechanical monitoring.
