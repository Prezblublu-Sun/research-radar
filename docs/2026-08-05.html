边界: This paper does not address biomedical or implant-specific mechanics, lacks uncertainty quantification, and does not validate on any biomechanical FEA case.

方法· 提出CL-PINN，结合贝叶斯主动参数选择、动态损失加权、稀疏物理约束回放和参数子网络，顺序学习不同参数任务。

英文摘要与术语

Result· Multi-seed experiments on five benchmarks show that CL-PINN achieves higher and more balanced accuracy than fixed-sampling and grid-greedy baselines while reducing objective-loss queries.
Validation· Validation is performed via numerical benchmarks on a continuous function and four parameterized PDEs, with multi-seed evaluations under prescribed resource protocols.
