Effects of the unit cell topology on the compression properties of porous Co-Cr scaffolds fabricated via selective laser melting

High AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Read now mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Changjun Han, Chunze Yan, Shifeng Wen, Tian Xu, Shuai Li et al. Rapid Prototyping Journal 2017-01-16 10.1108/rpj-08-2015-0114
单位: State Key Lab of Materials Processing and Die and Mould Technology, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan, China
通讯: Changjun Han @ State Key Lab of Materials Processing and Die and Mould Technology, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan
相关性: 该论文直接研究了SLM制造的多孔Co-Cr支架的压缩性能与单元拓扑的关系，对骨植入物设计具有直接参考价值。
边界: This paper does not cover fatigue testing, surface roughness characterization, or osseointegration, which are key for long-term implant performance.
动机· 评估不同单元拓扑结构对SLM钴铬合金多孔支架压缩性能的影响。
方法· 设计四种拓扑结构，通过SLM制造，结合有限元模拟和压缩实验。
结果· FCC单元应力集中最小，不同拓扑的压缩模量和强度排序为FCC>BCC>SHC>CCP。
验证· 压缩实验验证有限元结果，并通过体外细胞培养测试细胞初始响应。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Evaluate the influence of unit cell topology on compression properties of SLM-fabricated Co-Cr porous scaffolds for load-bearing bone implants.
Method· Designed four topologies (CCP, FCC, BCC, SHC), fabricated via SLM, and characterized via FE simulation and compression testing.
Result· FCC topology exhibited minimal stress concentration; compression modulus and strength followed descending order FCC > BCC > SHC > CCP.
Validation· Compression experiments validated FE predictions, and in vitro cell culture assessed initial cell response.
