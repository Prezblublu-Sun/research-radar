Quasi-Static compressive responses and fatigue behaviour of Ti-6Al-4 V graded lattice structures fabricated by laser powder bed fusion

Long Bai, Cheng Gong, Xiaohong Chen, Jia Zheng, Liming Xin et al. Materials & Design 2021-09-14 10.1016/j.matdes.2021.110110
单位: State Key Laboratory of Mechanical Transmission, Chongqing University, Chongqing 400030, China
通讯: Yuanxi Sun @ State Key Laboratory of Mechanical Transmission, Chongqing University, Chongqing 400030, China
相关性: 该论文直接研究了LPBF制造的Ti-6Al-4V梯度点阵结构的准静态压缩和疲劳行为，并将其与骨植入应用关联，对应应力遮挡和刚度匹配。
边界: Does not report surface roughness, as-built morphology, or osseointegration testing.
动机· 探索梯度点阵结构的力学性能以匹配骨植入物需求
方法· 采用激光粉末床熔融制造Ti-6Al-4V梯度点阵样品，进行准静态压缩和疲劳实验
结果· 垂直梯度结构弹性模量和能量吸收提升，径向梯度结构疲劳性能更优
验证· 通过准静态压缩和动态疲劳实验验证力学性能
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the mechanical properties of graded lattice structures for potential bone implant applications by matching stiffness and enhancing energy absorption.
Method· Laser powder bed fusion was used to fabricate Ti-6Al-4V graded lattice samples, followed by quasi-static compression and fatigue testing.
Result· Vertical graded structures exhibited increased elastic modulus (17.53%) and energy absorption (59.43%) compared to uniform structures, while radial graded structures showed better fatigue performance.
Validation· Mechanical testing under quasi-static compression and dynamic fatigue conditions.

Validation· Validation was performed by imaging differentiated myotubes and observing fibroblast migration and endothelial cell organization.

Hardening of Selective Laser Melted M2 Steel

Mei Yang, Yishu Zhang, Haoxing You, Richard Smith, Richard D. Sisson Proceedings of the ... ASM Heat Treating Society Conference 2021-09-14 10.31399/asm.cp.ht2021p0007
单位: Worcester Polytechnic Institute, Worcester, Massachusetts, USA
通讯: Mei Yang @ Worcester Polytechnic Institute, Worcester, Massachusetts, USA
相关性: 该论文研究工具钢的增材制造和热处理，与生物医学植入物、应力遮挡或骨整合无关。
边界: This paper focuses on M2 tool steel hardening and has no biomedical context, no porous implant design, no stress shielding evaluation, and no fatigue testing.
动机· 研究SLM制造M2高速钢是否需要热处理以达到高硬度。
方法· 通过SLM制备M2钢样件，进行淬火和回火处理，利用XRD、SEM、EDS表征。
结果· 热处理后SLM M2钢硬度显著提高，与锻造件相当，但碳化物尺寸更小。
验证· 与锻造件同步热处理对比，进行硬度测试和微观组织观察。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To determine whether heat treatment is necessary for achieving high hardness in selective laser melted M2 high-speed steel.
Method· M2 steel samples were fabricated by SLM, followed by quenching and tempering, and characterized using XRD, optical microscopy, SEM, and EDS.
Result· Heat treatment significantly increased the hardness of SLM M2 steel to levels comparable to wrought counterparts, with finer carbide size.
Validation· Validation was performed by comparing hardness and microstructure with simultaneously heat-treated wrought M2 steel.
