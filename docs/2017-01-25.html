单位: Bioengineering Research Group, School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Queen’s University Belfast, BT9 5AH, UK
通讯: Chi-Wai Chan @ Bioengineering Research Group, School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Queen’s University Belfast, BT9 5AH, UK
相关性: 该论文研究激光表面处理对骨科植入物抗菌性能的影响，未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡、LPBF制造或疲劳测试。
边界: This paper does not address porous structure design, stress shielding, LPBF fabrication, or mechanical testing (static or fatigue).
动机· 骨科植入物感染难以治疗，需研究激光表面处理增强抗菌性能。
方法· 用连续波光纤激光在氮气环境中处理CP Ti、Ti6Al4V和CoCrMo表面，分析粗糙度、形貌、化学和润湿性。
结果· 激光处理的CP Ti和Ti6Al4V表面细菌粘附减少并具有杀菌效果，CoCrMo无类似效果。
验证· 通过细菌培养、SEM、活/死染色和XPS验证抗菌性能。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Bacterial infection of orthopaedic implants is difficult to treat, motivating the study of laser surface engineering to enhance antibacterial properties.
Method· Continuous wave fibre laser treatment was applied to CP Ti, Ti6Al4V, and CoCrMo in a nitrogen atmosphere, and surface roughness, topography, chemistry, and wettability were characterized.
Result· Laser-treated CP Ti and Ti6Al4V surfaces showed reduced bacterial adhesion and bactericidal effects, while CoCrMo did not exhibit similar antibacterial properties.
Validation· Antibacterial performance was validated via Staphylococcus aureus culture, SEM imaging, live/dead staining, and XPS analysis.
