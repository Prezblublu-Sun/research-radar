边界: This paper focuses on multiphase flow in underground hydrogen storage, not on implant biomechanics, FEA surrogate for solid mechanics, or biomedical design constraints.
动机· 快速模拟地下储氢中多相流以支持实时管理。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Fast modeling of hydrogen plume migration and pressure evolution is essential for real-time management of underground hydrogen storage.
Method· A factorized Fourier improved neural operator (FFINO) is proposed, incorporating experimental relative permeability curves as uncertain inputs to accelerate multiphase flow simulation.
Result· FFINO reduces trainable parameters by 38.1%, training time by 17.6%, and improves accuracy by 9.8% for hydrogen plume and 16.3% for pressure buildup, with inference 7,850 times faster than numerical simulators.
Validation· The model is validated against numerical simulations using comprehensive metrics, with sensitivity analysis identifying injection rate as the most influential parameter.
