通讯: Yong Zhang @ College of Mechanical Engineering and Automation, Huaqiao University, Xiamen, China, 361021
相关性: 该论文使用Kriging代理模型和遗传算法优化蜂窝填充管的耐撞性，方法可迁移至植入物设计中的多目标优化和代理建模。

Validation· The finite element model was validated against experimental crash test results.

Validation· Statistical analysis of clinical CT data from 112 patients.
