Deformation mechanisms and post-yielding behavior of additively manufactured lattice structures

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Skim only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Behzad Bahrami Babamiri, Hesam Askari, Kavan Hazeli Materials & Design 2019-12-30 10.1016/j.matdes.2019.108443
单位: Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering Department, University of Alabama in Huntsville, USA
通讯: Kavan Hazeli @ Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering Department, University of Alabama in Huntsville, USA
相关性: 该论文研究了增材制造晶格结构的变形机制，但未涉及生物医学植入物或应力屏蔽问题。
边界: This paper does not address stress shielding, stiffness matching, or biomedical implant context; it focuses on nickel-based superalloy lattice structures for non-biomedical applications.
动机· 揭示增材制造晶格结构的屈服后变形机制。
方法· 提出修正体积硬化模型并耦合Johnson-Cook损伤准则进行有限元模拟。
结果· 模型能准确预测晶格结构的应力-应变响应和损伤演化。
验证· 结合实验测量与有限元模拟验证模型。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the governing deformation mechanisms at the onset of yielding and post-yielding behavior of additively manufactured lattice structures.
Method· An anisotropic generalization of the volumetric hardening model (MVHM) coupled with Johnson-Cook damage criterion and Hillerborg's fracture energy method is developed and implemented in finite element simulations.
Result· The MVHM accurately captures tension-compression asymmetry and hydrostatic pressure sensitivity, enabling prediction of damage initiation and evolution in lattice structures.
Validation· Experimental measurements and finite element simulations are combined to validate the proposed yield criterion and damage model.
