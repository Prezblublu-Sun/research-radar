单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, G.L. Bajaj Institute of Technology and Management, Greater Noida, 201308, India
通讯: Nagendra Kumar Maurya @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, G.L. Bajaj Institute of Technology and Management, Greater Noida, 201308, India
相关性: 该论文研究AlSi10Mg-TiB2复合材料的SLM工艺与力学性能，未涉及生物医学植入物设计或骨整合评估。

验证· 采用拉伸和硬度测试验证力学性能提升。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the effect of TiB2 particle reinforcement on the mechanical properties of AlSi10Mg composites fabricated by selective laser melting.
Method· AlSi10Mg composites with varying weight fractions of TiB2 were fabricated via SLM and subjected to mechanical testing.
Result· The addition of TiB2 particles significantly improved hardness and strength but reduced ductility.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated through tensile and hardness tests.
