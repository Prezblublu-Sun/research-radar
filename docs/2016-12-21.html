单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Kermanshah University of Technology, 63766-67178 Kermanshah, Iran
通讯: Zahra Gorgin Karaji @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Kermanshah University of Technology, 63766-67178 Kermanshah, Iran
相关性: 该研究涉及增材制造多孔镍钛合金及表面生物功能化，但未涉及力学性能测试或应力遮挡评估。
边界: This paper does not include mechanical testing (compression, fatigue) or stress shielding analysis, which are essential for the user's focus on implant stiffness matching and fatigue behavior.

Motivation· To develop multifunctional porous nitinol that combines superelasticity with biofunctionalized surfaces for improved bone regeneration.
Method· Porous nitinol with triply periodic minimal surface architecture was fabricated via selective laser melting and surface-biofunctionalized with polydopamine-immobilized rhBMP2.
