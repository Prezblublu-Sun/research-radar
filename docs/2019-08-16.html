Uncertainty quantification of discontinuous outputs via a non-intrusive bifidelity strategy

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Claudio Canuto, Sandra Pieraccini, Dongbin Xiu Journal of Computational Physics 2019-08-16 10.1016/j.jcp.2019.108885
单位: Dipartimento di Scienze Matematiche, Politecnico di Torino, Corso Duca degli Abruzzi 24, 10129 Torino, Italy
通讯: Sandra Pieraccini @ Dipartimento di Ingegneria Meccanica e Aerospaziale, Politecnico di Torino, Corso Duca degli Abruzzi 24, 10129 Torino, I
相关性: 该论文提出的非侵入式双保真度不确定性量化方法可迁移至植入物力学仿真中的不连续输出（如接触分离）的UQ问题。
边界: No implant or biomechanics application; method demonstrated on generic discontinuous functions and fluid dynamics, not on FEA surrogate or implant design.
动机· 高效量化不连续输出的不确定性，避免高保真模型的高计算成本。
方法· 提出非侵入式双保真度策略，结合低保真模型与少量高保真样本进行多保真融合。
结果· 在多个不连续函数和激波问题中，双保真度方法比单保真度方法显著降低方差。
验证· 通过数值基准测试（不连续函数和流体激波问题）验证精度与效率。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To efficiently quantify uncertainty in discontinuous outputs while avoiding the high computational cost of high-fidelity models.
Method· A non-intrusive bifidelity strategy is proposed that combines a low-fidelity model with a small number of high-fidelity samples via multifidelity fusion.
Result· The bifidelity approach significantly reduces variance compared to single-fidelity methods on several discontinuous functions and shock problems.
Validation· Validation is performed on numerical benchmarks including discontinuous functions and fluid shock problems.
