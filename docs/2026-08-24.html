边界: This paper focuses on generic dissipative PDEs and does not address biomedical FEA, implant mechanics, or uncertainty quantification.

Result· Extensive numerical benchmarks demonstrate that IMNO outperforms standard FNO in terms of accuracy and stability for long-horizon predictions on nonlinear dissipative PDEs.
