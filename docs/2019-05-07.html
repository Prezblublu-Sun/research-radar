Dual modulation of crystallinity and macro-/microstructures of 3D printed porous titanium implants to enhance stability and osseointegration.

Medium AM Biomedical Materials pubmed Direct Save for project mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Ping Song, Cheng Hu, Xuan Pei, Jianxun Sun, Huan Sun et al. Journal of materials chemistry. B 2019-05-07 10.1039/c9tb00093c
单位: School of Manufacturing Science and Engineering, Sichuan University, Chengdu 610065, China.
相关性: 该论文通过3D打印多孔钛植入体的宏观结构和表面微观形貌双重调控，直接增强了骨整合和稳定性，与生物医用增材制造方向高度相关。
边界: The paper does not include fatigue testing or explicit stress shielding analysis, which are key focuses for the AM Biomedical Materials direction.
动机· 通过双重调控3D打印多孔钛植入体的宏观结构和表面微观形貌，增强稳定性和骨整合。
方法· 设计不同宏观多孔结构并3D打印，随后进行碱处理、热处理和电化学沉积羟基磷灰石涂层。
结果· 双重调控支架的压缩强度范围宽（36.76-139.97 MPa），6个月后新骨形成率高于传统3D打印支架（58.1% vs 36.1%）。
验证· 通过体外细胞实验和体内植入6个月的动物实验验证骨整合效果。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To enhance implant stability and osseointegration through dual modulation of macro-architecture and micro-surface topography of 3D printed porous titanium scaffolds.
Method· Macro-porous architectures were designed and fabricated by 3D printing, followed by alkali treatment, heat treatment, and electrochemical deposition of hydroxyapatite coating for surface modulation.
Result· The dual-modulated scaffolds exhibited a wide range of compressive strengths (36.76-139.97 MPa) and achieved a higher new bone formation rate (58.1%) compared to conventional 3D printed scaffolds (36.1%) after 6 months of implantation.
Validation· In vitro cell assays and in vivo osseointegration experiments in animals were conducted to validate cytocompatibility and bone-implant integration.
