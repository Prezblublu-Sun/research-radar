Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project simulation_case UQ

相关性: 该论文提出的生成模型结合了条件归一化流和高斯过程，能够处理高维输出并提供不确定性量化，可迁移至生物力学代理模型中的约束优化与不确定性传播。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, implant biomechanics, or multi-objective constrained optimization; it focuses on spectroscopic data in physical sciences.
动机· 物理科学中有限训练数据导致预测不确定性难以量化，尤其在推断新数据时。

英文摘要与术语

Validation· Validated on laser-induced breakdown spectroscopy data from the ChemCam instrument onboard the Mars rover Curiosity.

Motivation· To reduce the high cost and inefficiency of manual annotation in HD map production.
Method· An end-to-end AI-based active learning system (THMA) integrating supervised, self-supervised, and weakly supervised learning.
Result· The system labels over 30,000 km of HD map data daily with >90% automation, accelerating the process by more than tenfold.
Validation· Deployed in Tencent Map, serving over 1,000 labeling workers.
