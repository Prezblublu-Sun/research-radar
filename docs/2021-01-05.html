边界: This paper is a clinical trial on VTE prophylaxis drugs; it does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or any biomechanical metrics of the femoral stem.
动机· 比较阿司匹林与利伐沙班在全髋关节置换术后预防静脉血栓的疗效与安全性。
方法· 前瞻性随机对照试验，70例患者分别接受阿司匹林或利伐沙班预防。
结果· 两组在VTE发生率、失血量及功能评分上无显著差异。
验证· 通过超声DVT检出率、临床出血事件等指标验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the efficacy and safety of 100 mg aspirin twice daily versus rivaroxaban for VTE prophylaxis after primary THA.
Method· A prospective randomized controlled trial enrolled 70 patients receiving either aspirin or rivaroxaban for 5 weeks post-THA.
Result· No significant differences were found in symptomatic VTE, DVT rate, total blood loss, or Harris hip scores between groups.
Validation· Clinical outcomes including Doppler ultrasound DVT detection and bleeding events were used for validation.
