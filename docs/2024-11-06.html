英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare functional outcomes of total hip arthroplasty performed via anterior, lateral, and posterior approaches.
Method· A cross-sectional study of 101 patients assessed with modified Harris Hip Score at one year postoperatively.
Result· The anterior approach was associated with quicker ambulation, shorter hospital stay, and better functional progression.
Validation· Statistical analysis of clinical patient data with one-way ANOVA.
