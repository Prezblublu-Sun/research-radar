Structure-Property-Function Evaluation of a β-Type Ti-Nb-Zr Alloy for Dental Implant Applications with Short-Term Clinical Validation.

单位: Section of Restorative Dentistry, Division of Preventive and Restorative Sciences, Los Angeles School of Dentistry, University of California, 714 Tiverton, Los Angeles, CA 90095, USA.
通讯: Yang-Jin Yi @ Department of Prosthodontics, Section of Dentistry, Seoul National University Bundang Hospital, Seongnam 13620, Republic [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究牙科植入物，与髋关节植入物及股骨柄的研究方向无关。
边界: This paper focuses on dental implants, with no hip/femoral stem biomechanics, stress shielding quantification, micromotion analysis, or contact modeling.
动机· 评估新型β型Ti-Nb-Zr合金在牙科植入物中的应用潜力。
方法· 通过实验室测试和随机对照临床试验对比TNZ与MG4T合金性能。
结果· TNZ合金疲劳强度更高，临床成功率100%，骨改建正常。
验证· 12个月随机对照临床试验与力学测试验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the structure-property-function relationship of a novel β-type Ti-Nb-Zr alloy for dental implants to address stress shielding concerns.1
Method· Laboratory tensile and fatigue tests were combined with a randomized, single-blind, controlled clinical trial over 12 months comparing TNZ and modified Grade 4 cpTi implants.2
Result· TNZ implants showed superior fatigue resistance without increased elastic modulus, achieved 100% clinical success and survival, and exhibited time-dependent bone remodeling without pathological adaptation.3
Validation· Validation included mechanical testing (tensile, fatigue) and a clinical trial with 77 participants, fractal dimension analysis of trabecular bone, and assessment of peri-implant parameters.4
