相关性: 该论文聚焦于反应堆物理中的约束量灵敏度与不确定性分析，与生物力学或植入物设计中的代理建模无直接关联。
边界: This paper addresses constrained sensitivity and uncertainty quantification in nuclear reactor physics, not in biomedical FEA or implant design, and lacks transferable methodology for surrogate modeling in biomechanics.
动机· 解决反应堆物理中约束量灵敏度与不确定性分析的歧义问题。
方法· 提出约束一阶灵敏度系数和协方差矩阵归一化的统一理论，结合多项式混沌展开。

验证· 通过三个解析和两个数值例子验证理论。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To resolve ambiguities in sensitivity and uncertainty analysis of reactor physics problems involving constrained quantities.
Method· A consistent theory for constrained first-order sensitivity coefficients and covariance matrix normalization is developed, integrated with polynomial chaos expansion.
Result· Different normalization procedures yield ambiguous sensitivity coefficients and uncertainties, highlighting the need for proper covariance data.
Validation· The theory is demonstrated on three analytical and two numerical examples including fission spectrum and isotopic distribution uncertainties.
