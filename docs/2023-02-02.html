Motivation· To improve uncertainty estimation in image retrieval by introducing a Bayesian encoder for metric learning.
Method· The Laplace Approximation is applied to learn a distribution over network weights, with novel decomposition of the Generalized Gauss-Newton approximation.
Result· The proposed Laplacian Metric Learner achieves state-of-the-art predictive performance and well-calibrated uncertainties.
Validation· Evaluated on standard image retrieval benchmarks for predictive accuracy and out-of-distribution detection.
