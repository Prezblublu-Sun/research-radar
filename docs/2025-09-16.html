English summary & terms
Motivation· To provide clinical and histological evidence for cartilage regeneration after intra-articular injection of autologous microfragmented adipose-derived stem cells in hip osteoarthritis.
Method· A 46-year-old female patient with symptomatic hip OA received a single ultrasound-guided injection of autologous microfragmented AMSCs following unsuccessful hip arthroscopy.
Result· At 12 months the patient was pain-free; symptom recurrence led to total hip arthroplasty at 18 months, and histology revealed hyaline-like cartilage in previously degenerated areas.
Validation· Histological analysis of the resected femoral head obtained during later total hip arthroplasty provided direct tissue-level validation of cartilage repair.
