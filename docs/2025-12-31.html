Method· A systematic review and multilevel meta-analysis of seven studies (948 patients) using random-effects models with Hartung-Knapp adjustment.
Result· No statistically significant differences were found among capsule preserved, repaired, and unrepaired groups in mHHS, HOS-ADL, HOS-SSS, or VAS pain scores.
Validation· Validation is based on pooled analysis of existing clinical studies; no experimental or biomechanical validation was performed.
