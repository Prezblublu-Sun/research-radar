边界: This paper is a meta-analysis methodology review in orthopaedic surgery, with no biomechanical modeling, implant design, FEA, or stress shielding analysis.
动机· 评估髋膝关节置换术荟萃分析的质量和临床实用性。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the quality and clinical utility of meta-analyses in total hip and knee arthroplasty.
Method· A systematic review of meta-analyses published in three major orthopaedic journals from 2000 to 2017 was conducted, with quality assessed using the Oxman-Guyatt Index and PRISMA checklist.
Result· The majority of meta-analyses were of low to moderate quality, and only a minority were deemed clinically important.
Validation· The study is a literature review with expert assessment; no experimental validation was performed.
