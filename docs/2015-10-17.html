A guide to uncertainty quantification and sensitivity analysis for cardiovascular applications

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project simulation_case UQ Patient-specific
Vinzenz Gregor Eck, Wouter P. Donders, Jacob Sturdy, Jonathan Feinberg, Tammo Delhaas et al. International Journal for Numerical Methods in Biomedical Engineering 2015-10-17 10.1002/cnm.2755
单位: Division of Biomechanics, Department of Structural Engineering NTNU Trondheim Norway
通讯: Wouter P. Donders @ Department of Biomedical Engineering, School for Mental Health and Neuroscience Maastricht University Maastricht The Ne
相关性: 该论文提供了心血管模型中不确定性量化和敏感性分析的系统方法，可迁移至植入物力学仿真中的不确定性处理。
边界: This paper focuses on cardiovascular models rather than implant biomechanics or FEA surrogate modeling, and does not address constraint handling or multi-objective optimization.
动机· 心血管模型临床决策需要量化预测不确定性。
方法· 采用蒙特卡洛和多项式混沌方法进行全局不确定性和敏感性分析。
结果· 多项式混沌比蒙特卡洛更高效，且针对关键输入减少不确定性可有效降低预测不确定性。
验证· 通过两个心血管临床模型（FFR和CT）进行案例验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To enable clinical decision-making with cardiovascular models, precise knowledge of prediction reliability is essential, motivating the need for practical uncertainty quantification and sensitivity analysis methods.
Method· This work presents a six-step guide for global uncertainty quantification and variance-based sensitivity analysis using Monte Carlo and polynomial chaos methods, applicable to any model without implementation changes.
Result· Both Monte Carlo and polynomial chaos yield identical results, but polynomial chaos requires an order of magnitude fewer model evaluations, and targeted reduction of uncertainty in significant inputs efficiently reduces prediction uncertainty.
Validation· The guide is demonstrated on two clinically relevant cardiovascular models: fractional flow reserve estimation and total arterial compliance estimation.
uncertainty quantification · 不确定性量化sensitivity analysis · 敏感性分析Monte Carlo · 蒙特卡洛polynomial chaos · 多项式混沌fractional flow reserve · 血流储备分数total arterial compliance · 总动脉顺应性patient-specific · 患者特异性
uncertainty quantification sensitivity analysis cardiovascular modeling Monte Carlo polynomial chaos fractional flow reserve total arterial compliance

Hydroxyapatite-lignin composite as a metallic implant-bone tissue osseointegration improver: experimental study in dogs

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only animal Exp. validation
Fabrício Luciani Valente, L.C. Santos, Rodrigo Viana Sepúlveda, Gustavo Pereira Gonçalves, Renato Barros Eleotério et al. Ciência Rural 2015-10-17 10.1590/0103-8478cr20150110
单位: Universidade Federal de Viçosa, Brazil
通讯: Fabrício Luciani Valente @ Universidade Federal de Viçosa, Brazil
相关性: 该论文研究了羟基磷灰石-木质素复合材料作为金属植入物与骨组织整合的促进剂，但未涉及多孔结构设计、LPBF制造或疲劳性能。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no porous structure design, no mechanical testing (only pull-out force), no fatigue or stiffness matching analysis.
动机· 评估羟基磷灰石-木质素复合材料促进金属植入物与骨整合的效果
方法· 在犬胫骨植入Schanz钉，一侧涂覆复合材料，另一侧对照，进行组织学和生物力学分析
结果· 复合材料具有生物相容性和骨传导性，但未能显著提高钉的锚固力
验证· 犬体内实验，组织学、X射线衍射和拔出力测试
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate whether a hydroxyapatite-lignin composite coating can improve osseointegration of metallic intramedullary pins.
Method· Schanz pins coated with the composite were implanted in one tibia of dogs, with the contralateral uncoated pin as control; histological, XRD, and biomechanical pull-out tests were performed at 8, 60, and 150 days.
Result· The composite was biocompatible and osteoconductive but did not significantly enhance pin anchorage compared to uncoated controls.
Validation· In vivo animal study with histological analysis, X-ray diffraction, and mechanical pull-out testing.

边界: No LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), no stress shielding or stiffness matching analysis, and no surface roughness or osseointegration data.

Validation· Feedstock rheology was characterized by capillary rheometry, and phase composition was validated by XRD.
