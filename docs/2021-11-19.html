Compensating the cell-induced light scattering effect in light-based bioprinting using deep learning

通讯: Jiaao Guan @ Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of California San Diego, 9500 Gilman Dr, La Jolla, CA 9209
相关性: 本文将深度学习嵌入数字光处理生物打印的工艺优化阶段，通过少量实验数据自动补偿细胞引起的光散射效应，提升打印精度。
边界: The paper focuses on compensating light scattering within a single layer, not yet extended to full multilayer 3D bioprinting or autonomous decision-making beyond parameter optimization.
动机· 解决细胞引起的光散射导致DLP生物打印精度下降和参数试错浪费的问题。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To eliminate trial-and-error parameter optimization caused by cell-induced light scattering in DLP bioprinting, which wastes expensive biomaterials and cell lines.
Method· A deep learning model with learning-based data augmentation learns from a small set of trial prints to automatically generate optimal printing parameters that compensate for scattering effects.
Result· The proposed method substantially improves intra-layer printing resolution and demonstrates potential for application in multilayer 3D bioprinting.
Validation· Experimental bioprinting of cell-laden constructs validated the resolution improvement and compared performance against uncompensated prints.
