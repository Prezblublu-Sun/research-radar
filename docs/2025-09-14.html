CF-PEEK vs. Titanium Dental Implants: Stress Distribution and Fatigue Performance in Variable Bone Qualities.

Nurdan Polat Sağsöz, Fahri Murat, Sema Nur Sevinç Gül, Abdullah Tahir Şensoy, Irfan Kaymaz Biomimetics (Basel, Switzerland) 2025-09-14 10.3390/biomimetics10090619
单位: Faculty of Dentistry, Atatürk University, 25240 Erzurum, Turkey.
通讯: Irfan Kaymaz @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Faculty of Engineering and Architecture, Erzurum Technical University, 25050 Erzur [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究牙种植体而非髋关节假体，与研究方向无关。
边界: This paper studies dental implants, not femoral stems; no hip-specific stress shielding, micromotion, or Gruen zone analysis.
动机· 比较CF-PEEK与钛牙种植体在不同骨密度下的生物力学性能。
方法· 建立下颌磨牙种植体FEA模型，施加垂直和斜向载荷分析应力应变。
结果· CF-PEEK降低应力遮挡但疲劳寿命较差，尤其在低密度骨中。
验证· 通过FEA模拟验证，无实验或临床验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare the biomechanical behavior of CF-PEEK versus titanium dental implants under varying bone densities and loading conditions.
Method· Finite element models of a mandibular molar implant system with four bone configurations were subjected to vertical and oblique static loads.
Result· CF-PEEK implants provided more uniform stress distribution but exhibited higher strain and lower fatigue life under oblique loading.
Validation· No experimental validation; purely computational FEA simulation.
