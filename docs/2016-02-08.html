The positive effect of hot isostatic pressing on improving the anisotropies of bending and impact properties in selective laser melted Ti-6Al-4V alloy

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Transferable Skim only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Ming-Wei Wu, Pang-Hsin Lai Materials Science and Engineering A 2016-02-08 10.1016/j.msea.2016.02.023
单位: Department of Materials and Mineral Resources Engineering, National Taipei University of Technology, No. 1, Section 3, Zhong-Xiao E. Rd., Taipei 10608, Taiwan, ROC
通讯: Ming-Wei Wu @ Department of Materials and Mineral Resources Engineering, National Taipei University of Technology, No. 1, Section 3, Z
相关性: 该论文研究了热等静压对选区激光熔融Ti-6Al-4V合金力学各向异性的影响，虽未涉及生物医学植入物，但其对SLM钛合金力学性能的改善具有参考价值。
边界: This paper does not address porous structure for stress shielding, fatigue behavior, surface roughness, or osseointegration; it focuses on static bending and impact properties of dense SLM Ti-6Al-4V with HIP.
动机· 研究热等静压对选区激光熔融Ti-6Al-4V合金弯曲和冲击各向异性的改善效果。
方法· 对SLM制备的Ti-6Al-4V进行HIP处理，测试弯曲和冲击性能。
结果· HIP有效降低了各向异性，提高了弯曲强度和冲击韧性。
验证· 通过实验测试弯曲强度和冲击韧性验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of hot isostatic pressing on reducing anisotropy in bending and impact properties of selective laser melted Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· HIP treatment was applied to SLM Ti-6Al-4V samples, followed by bending and impact tests.
Result· HIP significantly reduced mechanical anisotropy and improved both bending strength and impact toughness.
Validation· Experimental mechanical testing (bending and Charpy impact) was conducted.
