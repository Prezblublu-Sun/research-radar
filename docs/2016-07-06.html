Method· Students modeled the femoral component as a composite beam and varied implant size, material properties, and loading conditions to analyze stress shielding.
Result· Smaller and more compliant femoral components minimize stress shielding effects.
Validation· No validation; the activity is purely educational with no experimental or numerical verification.
