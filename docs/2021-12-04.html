英文摘要与术语
Motivation· This study aims to describe the cultivation of tolerance values in SMK Muhammadiyah 2 Tulungagung through Ismuba learning.
Method· A qualitative case study approach was used, collecting data via observation, interviews, and documentation from the principal, teachers, and students.
Result· The results show that tolerance values are instilled through active learning in both theory and practice, with no cases of intolerance or blasphemy among students and alumni.
Validation· Validation was conducted through direct observation of student and alumni behavior by all teachers.
