Onur Bilge, Muhammed Furkan Kucuksen, Mustafa Cihat Avunduk, Ülkü Kerimoğlu International Journal of Surgery Case Reports 2025-10-25 10.1016/j.ijscr.2025.112095
单位: Necmettin Erbakan University, Meram Faculty of Medicine, Department of Orthopedics and Traumatology, Konya, Turkey
通讯: Onur Bilge @ Necmettin Erbakan University, Meram Faculty of Medicine, Department of Orthopedics and Traumatology, Konya, Turkey
相关性: 该论文为临床病例报告，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡或微动的定量分析，与用户研究方向间接相关。
边界: This paper is a clinical case report on hip resurfacing arthroplasty, lacking any biomechanical modeling, stress shielding quantification, micromotion analysis, or finite element simulation.
动机· 评估HemiCAP髋关节表面置换在股骨头坏死治疗中的长期存活和骨整合。
方法· 对11年后取出植入物的股骨头进行放射学和组织形态学分析。

验证· 通过放射学和组织学评估验证植入物的长期存活。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the long-term survival and osseointegration of the HemiCAP hip resurfacing system in treating femoral head osteonecrosis.
Method· Radiological and histomorphological analyses were performed on an explanted femoral head and implant after 11 years of follow-up.
Result· The HemiCAP implant achieved successful osseointegration and survived 11 years without failure.
Validation· Validation was performed through radiological imaging and histomorphometric analysis of the retrieved implant-cadaveric specimen.

Microstructure and mechanical properties of laser printed Ti–25V–15Cr burn-resistant alloy using elemental powders including ball-milled Ti

Xingrun Shan, J.X. Liu, Dawei Wang, C. Y. Lu, Ming Yan Journal of Materials Research and Technology 2025-10-25 10.1016/j.jmrt.2025.10.204
单位: Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Southern University of Science and Technology, Shenzhen, 518055, China
通讯: Ming Yan @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Southern University of Science and Technology, Shenzhen, 518055, China
相关性: 该论文研究的是阻燃钛合金，而非生物医学植入物材料，与用户研究方向无关。
边界: The paper focuses on a burn-resistant titanium alloy for fire safety, with no biomedical context, no stress shielding analysis, no fatigue testing, no osseointegration or surface morphology relevant to implants.
动机· 研究低成本球磨钛粉在LPBF制备阻燃钛合金中的可行性
方法· 通过粉末表征、打印、微观分析和力学测试评价球磨钛粉效果
结果· 球磨钛粉降低成本但导致氧含量升高、塑性下降，高温蠕变性能差
验证· 力学测试（拉伸、硬度、蠕变）和微观组织表征
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the feasibility of using cost-effective ball-milled titanium powder in LPBF for fabricating burn-resistant Ti-25V-15Cr alloy.
Method· Systematic characterization of powder properties, printing quality, microstructure, and mechanical performance of specimens made with ball-milled Ti powder.
Result· Ball-milled Ti powder reduced cost by 65.8% but increased oxygen content, coarsened β-grains, and significantly deteriorated tensile plasticity and creep resistance due to oxygen embrittlement and void defects.
Validation· Mechanical testing including tensile, hardness, and creep tests, along with microstructural analysis.

Motivation· To compare the clinical efficacy of enhanced versus express workflows in robotic-arm assisted total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Retrospective cohort study recording operation time, complications, radiographic parameters, and Harris hip scores.
Result· Enhanced workflow increased surgical time but showed no significant advantages in joint stability or functional scores.
Validation· Clinical data statistical analysis.
