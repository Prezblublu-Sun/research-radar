Conformalized Polynomial Chaos Expansion for Uncertainty-aware Surrogate Modeling

High FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Dimitrios Loukrezis, Dimitris G. Giovanis arXiv preprint 2025-10-25 link
相关性: 该论文提出的共形多项式混沌展开方法能够为代理模型提供不确定性量化，可迁移至植入物/生物力学设计中的昂贵仿真替代建模。
边界: This paper does not apply the method to biomedical or implant mechanics problems, nor does it address multi-objective or constrained optimization scenarios.
动机· 为数据驱动的多项式混沌展开代理模型提供预测不确定性区间。
方法· 将基于jackknife的共形预测集成到多项式混沌展开中，利用留一法残差计算预测区间。
结果· 该方法在低精度模型和小数据集下仍能提供目标覆盖率的预测区间。
验证· 通过四个基准模型和两个电气工程设计用例验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To equip data-driven polynomial chaos expansion surrogate models with predictive uncertainty intervals.
Method· Integrates jackknife-based conformal prediction into polynomial chaos expansions, leveraging closed-form leave-one-out residuals.
Result· The method produces predictive intervals achieving target coverage even for low-accuracy models trained on small datasets.
Validation· Validated on four benchmark models and two electrical engineering design use-cases.

相关性: 该论文提出的不确定性量化框架可直接迁移至生物力学代理模型中的材料本构发现与参数不确定性评估。
边界: Does not apply the method to implant or biomechanics design, nor does it address expensive simulation sampling efficiency or constrained optimization.
动机· 现有本构模型发现方法缺乏灵活的不确定性量化，且受限于先验选择或线性系数。
方法· 提出部分贝叶斯框架，结合高斯过程、归一化流和Sobol敏感性分析进行模型发现与不确定性量化。
结果· 在各项同性和实验各向异性数据上成功发现稀疏且可解释的本构模型。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Existing methods for constitutive model discovery lack flexible uncertainty quantification and are limited by prior selection or linear coefficient assumptions.
Method· A partially Bayesian framework is proposed that augments stress-deformation data with a Gaussian process, approximates parameter distributions via normalizing flows, and distills them by matching function distributions, followed by Sobol sensitivity analysis for sparsity.
Result· The framework successfully discovers sparse and interpretable constitutive models for both isotropic and experimental anisotropic data.
Validation· Validation is performed on numerical benchmarks and experimental anisotropic data.

Motivation· To compare the clinical efficacy of enhanced versus express workflows in robotic-arm assisted total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Retrospective cohort study recording operation time, complications, radiographic parameters, and Harris hip scores.
Result· Enhanced workflow increased surgical time but showed no significant advantages in joint stability or functional scores.
Validation· Clinical data statistical analysis.
