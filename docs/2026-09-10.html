A variational physics-informed graph neural network for heterogeneous solid mechanics

High FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark

相关性: 该文提出变分物理信息图神经网络求解非均质固体力学，可迁移至植入物/骨-植入界面的FEA代理建模与参数化反演。
边界: No implant or bone-implant interface validation, no patient-specific geometry, and no contact mechanics; it is a general heterogeneous solid mechanics solver.
动机· 解决PINN在异质材料界面应力跳变处精度对正则化宽度和界面权重敏感的问题。
方法· 在自适应共形网格图上按单元赋本构，最小化离散总势能，仅含一阶导数且无惩罚项。
结果· 在刚度比跨越四个量级时von Mises误差低于3.58%，优于强形式PINN的5.58%。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Coordinate-based PINNs struggle with stress jumps at bimaterial interfaces because their accuracy depends on prescribed regularization widths or interface penalties.
Method· A variational, label-free physics-informed graph neural network minimizes the discrete total potential energy on a conforming adaptive mesh graph with per-element constitutive assignment and Dirichlet conditions enforced by construction.
Result· With a single fixed architecture and loss, the von Mises error stays below 3.58% across a stiffness-contrast sweep from 10^-2 to 10^2, outperforming a strong-form PINN at 5.58%.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on small-strain elasticity and finite-strain Neo-Hookean hyperelasticity in two and three dimensions.
