通讯: Didier Lucor @ LIMSI, CNRS Université Paris‐Saclay, Campus universitaire bât 508 Rue John von Neumann Orsay Cedex F‐91405 France

Validation· Validation is performed through numerical simulation case studies without experimental or clinical data.

Blood loss and transfusion rate in short stem hip arthroplasty. A comparative study

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore clinical
Josef Hochreiter, Wilfried Hejkrlik, K. Emmanuel, Wolfgang Hitzl, Reinhold Ortmaier International Orthopaedics 2016-12-10 10.1007/s00264-016-3365-2
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, St. Vincent Hospital Linz, Seilerstätte 4, A-4020, Linz, Austria
通讯: Josef Hochreiter @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, St. Vincent Hospital Linz, Seilerstätte 4, A-4020, Linz, Austria
相关性: 该论文仅关注短柄与直柄髋关节置换术的失血量和输血率，未涉及任何生物力学、应力遮挡或有限元分析。
边界: This paper is a clinical comparison of blood loss and transfusion rates, with no biomechanical modeling, stress shielding analysis, micromotion quantification, or FE validation relevant to hip implant mechanics.
动机· 比较短柄与直柄髋关节假体在失血量和输血率方面的差异。
方法· 回顾性比较124例短柄和141例直柄全髋置换术患者的临床数据。
结果· 短柄组失血量（1139 ml）和输血率（8%）均显著低于直柄组。
验证· 基于单外科医生连续病例的临床回顾分析。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine whether short-stem hip arthroplasty results in lower blood loss and transfusion rates compared to straight-stem designs.
Method· Retrospective comparison of 124 short-stem and 141 straight-stem THA patients treated by a single surgeon, analyzing calculated blood loss and transfusion requirements.
Result· Short-stem THA showed significantly lower calculated blood loss (1139 vs 1358 ml, p<0.001) and transfusion rate (8% vs 15.6%, p<0.001).
Validation· Clinical retrospective cohort study with statistical comparison of surgical outcomes.
