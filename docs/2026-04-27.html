Method· A custom guiding sleeve was designed to create a protected corridor for acetabular reaming and cup insertion.
Result· The sleeve demonstrated technical feasibility in a cadaveric study, preventing soft-tissue impingement.
Validation· Feasibility was assessed in three fresh-frozen cadaveric hips.
