相关性: 该论文提出的可学习分数幂基神经网络架构，有望用于生物力学有限元代理模型中奇异函数（如应力集中）的高效逼近。
边界: No direct application to FEA surrogate modeling for implant biomechanics; no validation on biomedical or structural mechanics problems.
动机· 标准激活函数难以逼近具有奇异或分数幂行为的函数。

Validation· Validation on PINN benchmarks including a singular ODE and stiff boundary-layer problems, where learned exponents match known solution structure.

Motivation· To coordinate multiple pre-trained generative models for generating feasible, diverse, and high-performing system-level designs under coupling constraints.
