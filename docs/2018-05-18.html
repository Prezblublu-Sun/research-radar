通讯: Teresa A Alonso-Rasgado @ Bioengineering Research Group School of Materials The University of Manchester Manchester United Kingdom
相关性: 该论文研究髋臼侧骨水泥界面应力与股骨头尺寸的关系，未涉及股骨柄或股骨近端应力遮挡。
边界: This paper focuses on acetabular cement mantle and pelvic bone stresses, not on femoral stem stress shielding, micromotion, or proximal femur mechanics.
动机· 探究股骨头尺寸对髋臼骨水泥界面应力的影响，解释大直径股骨头高失败率原因。
方法· 基于DIC验证的有限元模型，分析28/32/36mm股骨头在700N和1000N载荷下的骨盆及骨水泥应力。
结果· 骨水泥-皮质骨界面应力随股骨头直径增大而增加（最高9%），松质骨应力变化不大。
验证· 使用数字图像相关法（DIC）在复合半骨盆实验上验证有限元模型。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of femoral head size on acetabular cement mantle stresses, aiming to explain higher failure rates with larger heads.
Method· A finite element model validated by Digital Image Correlation (DIC) on a composite hemipelvis was used to simulate 28, 32, and 36 mm heads under 700 N and 1000 N single-leg stance loads.
Result· Peak stresses at the cortical bone-cement interface increased by up to 9% with larger head diameter, while trabecular bone stresses remained relatively unchanged.
Validation· The FE model was validated against surface strains measured by DIC during experimental testing of a composite hemipelvis with a cemented acetabular cup.
