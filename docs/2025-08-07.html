Physics-Informed Time-Integrated DeepONet: Temporal Tangent Space Operator Learning for High-Accuracy Inference

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark
Luis Mandl, Dibyajyoti Nayak, Tim Ricken, Somdatta Goswami arXiv preprint 2025-08-07 10.1016/j.cma.2026.118917
相关性: 该论文提出了一种结合物理信息的时间积分深度算子网络，用于长时间序列PDE求解，其方法可迁移至生物力学FEA代理建模中的瞬态问题。
边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, constraint handling, or uncertainty quantification; it focuses on pure PDE surrogate modeling without biomedical context.
动机· 现有深度算子网络在长时间PDE预测中精度和稳定性不足。
方法· 提出PITI-DeepONet，学习时间导数算子并用经典时间积分推进。
结果· 在多个基准PDE上误差降低42%-98%，超越全卷积分和自回归方法。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Existing deep operator networks suffer from poor accuracy and stability for long-term time-dependent PDE predictions.
Method· PITI-DeepONet learns the time-derivative operator from the current state and integrates it using classical time-stepping schemes.
Result· The method reduces mean relative L2 errors by 42%-98% on multiple benchmark PDEs compared to full rollout and autoregressive approaches.
Validation· Validated on numerical benchmarks including 1D/2D heat, Burgers, Allen-Cahn, and Kuramoto-Sivashinsky equations.
