Guicheng Wang, Yuelong Zou, C.Y.P. Qiao, Pengwei Shen, Zhisheng Wu et al. Journal of Materials Research and Technology 2025-08-07 10.1016/j.jmrt.2025.08.043
单位: National Key Laboratory of Advanced Stainless Steel, Taiyuan University of Science and Technology
通讯: Yan Li @ National Key Laboratory of Advanced Stainless Steel, Taiyuan University of Science and Technology
相关性: 论文研究了2205双相不锈钢的SLM工艺参数对微观结构和性能的影响，但未涉及生物医学植入物所需的应力遮挡、疲劳或骨整合等关键问题。
边界: No porous structure for stress shielding, no fatigue testing, no surface roughness or osseointegration assessment, and no biomedical context.
动机· 探究SLM工艺参数对2205双相不锈钢微观结构和性能的影响
方法· 使用不同体积能量密度SLM制备样品，并用Ansys-APDL模拟熔池凝固
结果· 原位退火时间与奥氏体含量正相关，腐蚀性能由双钝化膜和电偶效应共同决定
验证· 通过SEM、EBSD、TEM和力学测试验证微观结构和性能
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the influence of selective laser melting process parameters on the microstructure, corrosion resistance, and mechanical properties of 2205 duplex stainless steel.
Method· Samples were fabricated by SLM at various volumetric energy densities, and melt pool solidification was simulated using Ansys-APDL.
Result· In-situ annealing time correlates positively with austenite content; corrosion resistance is governed by a dual-layer passive film and galvanic coupling between phases.
Validation· Microstructural characterization by SEM, EBSD, TEM, and mechanical testing confirmed the findings.

Promoting Surface Energy and Osteoblast Viability on Zirconia Implant Abutments Through Glass-Ceramic Spray Deposition Technology.

Wen-Chieh Hsu, Tao-Yu Cha, Yu-Chin Yao, Chien-Ming Kang, Sheng-Han Wu et al. Journal of functional biomaterials 2025-08-07 10.3390/jfb16080288
单位: School of Dentistry, College of Oral Medicine, Chung Shan Medical University, Taichung 40201, Taiwan.; Center for Tooth Bank and Dental Stem Cell Technology, College of Oral Medicine, Taipei Medical U
通讯: Tzu-Yu Peng @ School of Dentistry, College of Oral Medicine, Taipei Medical University, Taipei 11031, Taiwan. [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究氧化锆基台的玻璃陶瓷喷涂表面改性，与AM生物医学材料方向无直接关联，未涉及LPBF制造、多孔结构或力学性能测试。
边界: This paper does not involve LPBF fabrication, porous structure design, mechanical testing (static or fatigue), or stress shielding analysis.
动机· 研究玻璃陶瓷喷涂沉积对氧化锆基台表面能及成骨细胞活力的影响
方法· 制备氧化锆圆片并进行抛光、喷砂、玻璃陶瓷喷涂及氢氟酸蚀刻处理，表征表面特性并评估MG-63细胞反应
结果· 玻璃陶瓷喷涂增强了亲水性，经氢氟酸蚀刻后表面电位升高，促进了细胞附着和活力
验证· 通过接触角、表面能、表面电位测量及细胞活性、黏附和形态观察进行验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate whether glass-ceramic spray deposition (GCSD) can improve the surface properties and osteoblast response of zirconia abutments.
Method· Zirconia discs were polished, airborne-particle abraded, or GCSD-coated (with/without HF etching); surface wettability, energy, and potential were measured, and MG-63 cell adhesion and viability were assessed.
Result· GCSD significantly increased hydrophilicity and surface energy; HF etching increased surface potential; the GE group showed enhanced cell attachment and highest viability comparable to titanium.
Validation· Validation included contact angle goniometry, surface energy calculation, surface potential measurement, PrestoBlue assay, SEM, and confocal microscopy for cell morphology.

边界: No implant mechanics, no FEA simulation, no biomedical design context, no contact mechanics or stress analysis.
动机· 标准贝叶斯反演计算昂贵，需要低成本替代方法。
方法· 比较PLS、SVM、KNN、决策树、随机森林等8种回归方法，评估预处理影响。
结果· 最优模型在JWST WASP-39b案例上验证了性能。

Method· A systematic benchmark of eight regression methods including PLS, SVM, KNN, DT, RF, voting, stacking, and XGB, with various data preprocessing schemes.
Result· The best performing model was validated on JWST observation of WASP-39b, demonstrating accuracy and uncertainty quantification across the parameter range.
Validation· Validation against synthetic benchmark data and a real JWST case study, comparing accuracy, precision, and speed.
