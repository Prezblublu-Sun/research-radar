3D Bioprinting Technology – One Step Closer Towards Cardiac Tissue Regeneration

Mira Chingale, Ke Cheng, Ke Huang Frontiers in Materials 2022-02-02 10.3389/fmats.2021.804134
单位: Department of Molecular Biomedical Sciences and Comparative Medicine Institute, North Carolina State University, Raleigh, NC, United States
通讯: Ke Cheng @ Department of Molecular Biomedical Sciences and Comparative Medicine Institute, North Carolina State University, Raleigh  |  Ke Huang @ Department of Molecular Biomedical Sciences and Comparative Medicine Institute, North Carolina State University, Raleigh
相关性: 该论文综述了3D生物打印在心脏组织再生中的应用，但未涉及AI/ML集成，与研究方向关联性较弱。
边界: The paper is a review of 3D bioprinting techniques for cardiac tissue and does not discuss any AI or machine learning integration into the bioprinting workflow.
动机· 心血管疾病治疗需求推动3D生物打印心脏组织研究
方法· 综述了挤出、喷墨、激光等3D生物打印技术整合多种细胞和支架构建心脏组织
结果· 总结了心脏补片、体外模型、瓣膜和血管的打印进展及挑战
验证· 本文为综述，无新实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· The need for cardiac tissue regeneration motivates the use of 3D bioprinting for constructing functional cardiac tissues.
Method· This review summarizes extrusion, inkjet, and laser-based 3D bioprinting techniques that integrate multiple cell lines and scaffolds to create complex 3D cardiac structures.
Result· The review highlights progress and challenges in printing cardiac patches, in vitro models, valves, and blood vessels.
Validation· This is a review paper without new experimental validation.

单位: Novoheart Irvine CA 92617 USA
通讯: Erin G. Roberts @ Novoheart Irvine CA 92617 USA  |  Ronald A. Li @ AstraZeneca Irvine CA 92617 USA
相关性: 该论文未涉及AI，而是聚焦于生物打印中的物理支持结构改进。
边界: This paper does not integrate any AI/ML method into bioprinting workflow; it focuses solely on a mechanical balloon support for tissue stability.

方法· 使用超柔顺弹性体气球作为内衬支撑
结果· 减少泄漏，提高均匀性，实现多日纵向测量
验证· 通过组织形成和生物数据验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the stability, performance, and physiological relevance of bioengineered human mini-hearts for drug testing.
Method· An ultra-compliant elastomer balloon is employed as a permanent lining inside the mini-heart to prevent leakage and support tissue contraction.
Result· The balloon lining eliminates fluid leakage, increases fabrication uniformity, and enables multiday pressure–volume measurements.
Validation· Biological data from mini-hearts demonstrate reduced variability in cardiac parameters compared to previous methods.

Motivation· To investigate how phase transformation influences the mechanical properties of Co-Cr-Mo alloy fabricated by selective laser melting.

Validation· Phase identification via XRD and microstructure observation via SEM, correlated with mechanical test results.
