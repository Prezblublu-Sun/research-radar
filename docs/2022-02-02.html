3D-printed porous Ti6Al4V scaffolds for long bone repair in animal models: a systematic review

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only none Review
Yifei Gu, Yi Sun, Sohaib Shujaat, Annabel Braem, Constantinus Politis et al. Journal of Orthopaedic Surgery and Research 2022-02-02 10.1186/s13018-022-02960-6
单位: Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, University Hospitals Leuven, Leuven, Belgium
通讯: Yifei Gu @ Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, University Hospitals Leuven, Leuven, Belgium
相关性: 该综述总结了3D打印多孔Ti6Al4V支架在动物模型中的成骨效果，但未涉及应力遮挡、疲劳性能或表面形貌等核心方向。
边界: This is a systematic review of animal studies on osteogenesis, not a primary study on LPBF fabrication, mechanical testing (fatigue or static), stress shielding, or surface roughness characterization.
动机· 评估3D打印多孔Ti6Al4V支架在动物长骨缺损模型中的成骨潜力。
方法· 系统检索PubMed等数据库，筛选46项动物研究，使用SYRCLE和CAMARADES工具评估偏倚风险。
结果· 孔径500-600μm、孔隙率60-70%的十二面体和金刚石孔支架成骨效果最佳。
验证· 基于组织学和micro-CT的动物实验数据汇总，未进行meta分析。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To systematically evaluate the osteogenic potential of 3D-printed porous Ti6Al4V scaffolds for repairing long bone defects in animal models.
Method· A systematic review of 46 animal studies identified through PubMed, Web of Science, and Embase, with risk of bias assessed using SYRCLE and CAMARADES tools.
Result· Pore sizes of 500-600 μm and porosities of 60-70% with dodecahedron or diamond pore shapes were associated with optimal osteogenesis.
Validation· Validation relied on histological and micro-CT outcomes from animal studies; no meta-analysis was performed due to heterogeneity.
