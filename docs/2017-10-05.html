Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore clinical Exp. validation
Mary Alice Gortemoller, B.R. Allen, Rochelle Forsyth, Kimberly Theiss, Kristal Cunningham et al. Annals of Pharmacotherapy 2017-10-05 10.1177/1060028017735859
单位: St Vincent’s HealthCare, Jacksonville, FL, USA
通讯: B.R. Allen @ St Vincent’s HealthCare, Jacksonville, FL, USA
相关性: 该论文研究全髋和全膝关节置换术中使用氨甲环酸的给药途径，不涉及任何生物力学建模或植入物设计。
边界: This paper is a clinical study on tranexamic acid administration, with no biomechanical modeling, no stress shielding analysis, no micromotion quantification, and no implant design or FEA.
动机· 比较口服与静脉注射氨甲环酸在关节置换术中的止血效果。
方法· 单中心回顾性图表审查，比较口服和静脉给药的血红蛋白下降。
结果· 口服氨甲环酸在全髋关节置换中非劣效于静脉给药，但在全膝关节置换中未达到非劣效标准。
验证· 临床回顾性研究，有样本量计算和统计检验。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the efficacy of oral versus intravenous tranexamic acid in reducing perioperative blood loss in total hip and knee arthroplasty.
Method· A single-center retrospective chart review comparing hemoglobin reduction between oral and IV tranexamic acid groups.
Result· Oral tranexamic acid was noninferior to IV in total hip arthroplasty but failed to meet noninferiority in total knee arthroplasty.
Validation· Clinical retrospective study with power analysis and noninferiority margin testing.
