Uncertainty-Aware Predictive Safety Filters for Probabilistic Neural Network Dynamics

Bernd Frauenknecht, Lukas Kesper, Daniel Mayfrank, Henrik Hose, Sebastian Trimpe arXiv preprint 2026-04-29 link
相关性: 该论文关注安全强化学习中的预测性安全过滤器，与有限元代理建模和植入物设计无关。
边界: This paper addresses safe reinforcement learning and control, not surrogate modeling for finite element analysis, lacks any connection to biomechanics or implant design, and does not involve expensive simulation sampling or constraint handling in that context.
动机· 现有预测性安全过滤器对模型要求高，难以扩展到高维复杂动力学。
方法· 提出UPSi，利用概率集成神经网络构建可达集，并引入明确的安全约束。
结果· 在安全RL基准测试中，UPSi显著提高了探索安全性，同时保持性能。
验证· 在标准安全RL基准上通过仿真实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Existing predictive safety filters rely on first-principles models or Gaussian processes, limiting scalability to complex dynamics in reinforcement learning.
Method· UPSi formulates future outcomes as reachable sets using probabilistic ensemble neural networks and enforces a certainty constraint to prevent model exploitation.
Result· UPSi achieves substantial improvements in exploration safety over prior neural network PSFs while maintaining performance comparable to standard MBRL.
Validation· The method is validated on standard safe reinforcement learning benchmarks in simulation.
