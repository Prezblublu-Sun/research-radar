Predictive Value of Patient-Reported Outcomes for Functional Performance After Total Hip Arthroplasty

Yusuf Polat, Elif Aygün Polat OSMANGAZİ JOURNAL OF MEDICINE 2025-12-15 10.20515/otd.1795514
单位: ORDU ÜNİVERSİTESİ, TIP FAKÜLTESİ, CERRAHİ TIP BİLİMLERİ BÖLÜMÜ, ORTOPEDİ VE TRAVMATOLOJİ ANABİLİM DALI

相关性: 该论文研究全髋关节置换术后患者报告结果与功能表现的关系，不涉及生物力学建模或植入物设计。
边界: Does not quantify stress/strain in Gruen zones, micromotion, or any finite element analysis; purely clinical outcomes.
动机· 明确患者报告结果能否预测全髋置换术后的客观功能表现。
方法· 对50名THA患者进行Oxford髋评分、SF-12、VAS疼痛评估及TUG、FSST、4MGS功能测试。
结果· OHS和活动相关疼痛可独立预测术后活动能力和平衡能力，静息疼痛无显著关联。
验证· 通过多变量线性回归控制混杂变量，验证PROMs与功能测试的关联。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To determine whether patient-reported outcome measures can independently predict objective physical performance after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Fifty unilateral THA patients were assessed using Oxford Hip Score, SF-12, VAS pain scales, and performance-based tests (TUG, FSST, 4MGS).
Result· OHS and activity-related pain were independent predictors of all three performance tests, while rest pain was not associated with any measure.
Validation· Multivariate linear regression controlling for demographics and comorbidities validated the predictive associations.

Five years of experience in hip replacement in patients with sequelae of hip fractures

Mykola Ankin, Ya. S. Zavertilenko, Mykola M. Barylovych, Viktoriia Ladyka The Ukrainian Journal of Clinical Surgery 2025-12-15 10.26779/2786-832x.2025.6.50
单位: P. L. Shupyk National Medical Academy of Postgraduate Education, Kyiv
通讯: Mykola Ankin @ P. L. Shupyk National Medical Academy of Postgraduate Education, Kyiv
相关性: 该论文为临床回顾性研究评估髋关节置换术后功能结果，未涉及任何生物力学建模或应力分析。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no FEA, no patient-specific implant analysis.
动机· 评估髋部骨折后遗症患者全髋关节置换术的五年结果
方法· 回顾性分析46例患者分组比较一期与二期手术效果
结果· 二期手术Harris评分优于一期，5年假体稳定率91.3%
验证· Harris评分量表评估功能结果
English summary & terms
Motivation· To retrospectively evaluate the five-year functional outcomes of total hip arthroplasty in patients with sequelae of hip fractures.
Method· A retrospective analysis of 46 patients was performed, comparing one-stage and two-stage surgical approaches.
Result· Two-stage surgery yielded better Harris scores (92±6 vs 88±8) with 91.3% implant stability at 5 years.
Validation· Functional outcomes were assessed using the Harris hip score without biomechanical validation.
