通讯: Chunze Yan @ State Key Laboratory of Materials Processing and Die & Mould Technology, School of Materials Science and Engineering, Hu  |  Shoufeng Yang @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Member of Flanders Make, KU Leuven (Catholic University of Leuven), 3001 Leuven, B
相关性: 该论文直接研究了通过SLM制造的梯度Gyroid点阵结构的力学性能，与用户关注的AM生物医学植入物（如多孔植入物）的力学匹配和疲劳性能高度相关。
边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing, surface roughness characterization, or osseointegration evaluation, which are core to biomedical implant validation.

Result· Structures with gradient parallel to the loading direction exhibited layer-by-layer collapse with sub-layer phenomena, and a mathematical model was developed to predict mechanical properties based on relative density.
Validation· Experimental compression tests on SLM-fabricated specimens validated the manufacturing repeatability and the predicted deformation behavior.
