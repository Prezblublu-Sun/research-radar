单位: School of Materials Science and Engineering, North University of China, Taiyuan Shanxi, 030051, China
通讯: Senming Zhang @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, North University of China, Taiyuan Shanxi, 030051, China  |  Shenghua Zhang @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, North University of China, Taiyuan Shanxi, 030051, China  |  Bin Liu @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, North University of China, Taiyuan Shanxi, 030051, China

动机· 研究SLM参数对含Zr的Al-Cu-Mn-Mg合金成形性、微观组织和力学性能的影响。
