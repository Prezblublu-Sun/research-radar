Frictional behavior between bone and additively manufactured Ti6Al4V implants is affected by bone density and surface texture under varying loads

High AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Read now mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Ali Abedi, Farzam Farahmand, Mohammadjavad Salmanimehrjardi, H. Khonsari Scientific Reports 2025-11-24 10.1038/s41598-025-25586-0
单位: Mechanical Engineering Department, Sharif University of Technology, Tehran, Iran
通讯: Ali Abedi @ Mechanical Engineering Department, Sharif University of Technology, Tehran, Iran
相关性: 该论文直接研究了增材制造Ti6Al4V植入物与不同密度骨之间的摩擦行为，与用户关注的骨-植入物界面力学和应力遮挡高度相关。
边界: This paper does not cover stress shielding quantification, fatigue testing, or osseointegration outcomes.
动机· 骨-植入物界面的摩擦系数数据有限，尤其缺乏不同骨质量和表面纹理的组合数据。
方法· 使用往复摩擦测试装置，测量不同骨密度和SLM表面纹理的Ti6Al4V植入物在不同法向应力下的摩擦系数。
结果· 摩擦系数范围0.37-0.95，受表面粗糙度、骨密度和法向应力显著影响，但交互效应不显著。
验证· 通过实验测量不同骨密度和表面纹理组合的摩擦系数进行验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Limited data exist on coefficients of friction for combinations of bone quality and implant surface morphology, especially for additively manufactured implants.
Method· Interfacial COFs were measured using a reciprocal friction test apparatus for three levels of cancellous bone density and four Ti6Al4V surface textures produced by SLM under three normal stress levels.
Result· COF ranged from 0.37 to 0.95, significantly influenced by surface roughness, bone density, and normal stress, but not their interactions.
Validation· Experimental friction tests on human cancellous bone and SLM-fabricated Ti6Al4V surfaces.
