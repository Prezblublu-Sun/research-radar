An overlapping domain decomposition method for the solution of parametric elliptic problems via proper generalized decomposition

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark
Marco Discacciati, Ben J. Evans, Matteo Giacomini arXiv preprint 2023-07-30 10.1016/j.cma.2023.116484
相关性: 该方法适用于参数化椭圆问题的代理建模，但尚未应用于生物力学或植入物设计。
边界: This paper does not address uncertainty quantification, constraint handling, or implant/biomechanics applications; it is validated only on numerical benchmarks for diffusion and convection-diffusion problems.
动机· 高效求解参数化椭圆问题的代理模型
方法· 结合重叠域分解和广义特征分解的非侵入式方法
结果· 数值算例显示精度高且鲁棒性优于传统方法
验证· 扩散和对流扩散问题的数值基准测试
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To efficiently construct surrogate models for parametric linear elliptic problems by reducing computational cost.
Method· A non-intrusive proper generalized decomposition (PGD) strategy coupled with an overlapping domain decomposition (DD) method using arbitrary Dirichlet interface conditions and an algebraic formulation.
Result· The DD-PGD approach achieves high accuracy and robustness, outperforming standard high-fidelity DD methods on parametric diffusion and convection-diffusion problems.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on parametric diffusion and convection-diffusion problems demonstrate accuracy and computational efficiency.
