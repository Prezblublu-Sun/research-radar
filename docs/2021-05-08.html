通讯: Erica Liverani @ Department of Industrial Engineering Università di Bologna Bologna Italy
相关性: 该论文直接研究了通过LPBF制造的钴铬合金梯度多孔结构，评估了力学性能、应力分布和体外生物学响应，与用户关于AM生物医学植入物的方向高度相关。

Method· Uniform and graded CoCr lattice structures were fabricated by selective laser melting and evaluated via compression testing, DIC analysis, and in vitro osteoblast culture.

Validation· Mechanical compression tests, digital image correlation, and in vitro cell assays with gene expression analysis.

通讯: Emre Akgun @ Faculty of Engineering, Environment and Computing, Coventry University, Coventry CV1 5FB, UK
相关性: 该论文研究了线弧增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的疲劳性能与孔隙缺陷的关系，对理解增材制造植入物的疲劳行为有参考价值，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或生物医学背景。
边界: This paper does not cover porous structure design for stiffness matching, stress shielding, or any biomedical implant context; it focuses on fatigue of wire+arc AM Ti-6Al-4V with process-induced porosity.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the source of fatigue life scatter in additive manufactured Ti-6Al-4V and the influence of process-induced porosity defects.

Result· The scatter in fatigue life is primarily attributed to pore location (surface vs. embedded) rather than pore size; surface pores above a threshold size reduce fatigue life by two orders of magnitude.
Validation· Experimental fatigue tests supported by local elastic stress analysis.
