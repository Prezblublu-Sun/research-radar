Integration of a Three-Dimensional-Printed Titanium Implant in Human Tissues: Case Study

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Save for project clinical Exp. validation Patient-specific
Jong Woong Park, Chae Ahn Song, Hyun Guy Kang, June Hyuk Kim, K. Lim et al. Applied Sciences 2020-01-11 10.3390/app10020553
单位: Division of Convergence Technology, Research Institute, National Cancer Center, Goyang 10408, Korea
通讯: Hyun Guy Kang @ Division of Convergence Technology, Research Institute, National Cancer Center, Goyang 10408, Korea
相关性: 该论文展示了3D打印钛合金植入物在人体中的骨整合和软组织整合，与AM生物医学方向直接相关。
边界: No LPBF fabrication details, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), no stress shielding or stiffness matching analysis.
动机· 验证3D打印钛合金植入物在人体中的组织整合能力。
方法· 对骨肿瘤患者进行定制化3D打印Ti6Al4V桡骨和尺骨植入物手术，并回收植入物进行组织学评估。
结果· 植入物在骨连接处和轴部均显示出显著的骨整合和软组织整合。
验证· 通过组织学定量评估骨整合和软组织整合率。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the osseointegration and soft tissue integration of a 3D-printed titanium alloy implant in a human clinical setting.
Method· A customized Ti6Al4V radius and ulna implant was fabricated via 3D printing and implanted in a patient undergoing limb salvage surgery; the retrieved implant was histologically analyzed.
Result· The implant exhibited substantial bone integration at junctions (up to 10.81% enhancement) and high soft tissue adhesion (up to 59.50%) along the shaft.
Validation· Histological evaluation of the retrieved implant quantified tissue integration rates.

通讯: Pucun Bai @ College of Materials Science and Engineering, Inner Mongolia University of Technology, Hohhot 010051, China
相关性: 该论文研究了IN718合金的热处理工艺，未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或生物医学植入物。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue testing, surface roughness, or osseointegration relevant to biomedical implants.
动机· 研究SLM制备IN718合金在固溶热处理中析出相和晶界的演变。

Motivation· To investigate the evolution of precipitates and grain boundaries in SLM-fabricated IN718 superalloy during solution heat treatment.
Method· Solution heat treatment at various temperatures was applied, and the dissolution of Laves phases, grain boundary types, and recrystallization behavior were characterized.
Result· Static recrystallization initiated at 1090°C and completed at 1130°C, with annealing twins refining the grain size.
Validation· Microhardness testing was used to correlate microstructural evolution with mechanical properties.

通讯: Yan Yin @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, LanZhou University of Technology, GanSu, China
相关性: 该论文研究的是铝青铜的SLM工艺，而非生物医用植入物材料，与用户方向无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address biomedical implants, porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or osseointegration.
动机· 优化铝青铜SLM工艺参数并表征其性能。
方法· 正交实验设计结合信噪比分析确定最优参数。
结果· 致密度达98.94%，显微硬度384.1HV，抗拉强度575-595MPa。
验证· 通过密度、硬度、拉伸测试及微观结构观察验证。

Motivation· To optimize the selective laser melting process parameters for BrAl10Fe4 aluminium-bronze and characterize the resulting material properties.
Method· An orthogonal experiment with four factors and three levels combined with signal-to-noise ratio analysis was used to determine optimal parameters.
Result· The optimal parameters yielded a density of 98.94%, microhardness of 384.1 HV, and tensile strength of 575-595 MPa, with a network dendritic microstructure.
Validation· Validation was performed through density measurement, microhardness testing, tensile testing, and microstructural observation.
