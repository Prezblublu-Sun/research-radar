相关性: 该论文关注髋臼杯的CT检测方法，而非应力遮挡或疲劳性能，与用户研究方向间接相关。
边界: This paper focuses on CT metrological examination of acetabular cups, not on stress shielding, stiffness matching, LPBF fabrication, mechanical testing, or fatigue.
