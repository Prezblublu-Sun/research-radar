Analytical results for uncertainty propagation through trained machine learning regression models

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Andrew Thompson arXiv preprint 2024-04-17 link
相关性: 该论文提供了解析不确定性传播方法，可迁移到FEA代理模型的不确定性量化中，但未涉及生物力学或植入物设计。
边界: No FEA surrogate application, no biomechanics context, no constraint handling.
动机· 机器学习回归模型缺乏解析的不确定性传播方法。
方法· 推导了线性、核岭回归、高斯过程、支持向量机和相关向量机输出均值和方差的解析表达式。
结果· 解析方法在计算效率上优于蒙特卡洛方法且精度相当。
验证· 通过数值基准测试和锂离子电池健康状态应用进行了验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Analytical uncertainty propagation for trained ML regression models is lacking in metrology.
Method· Closed-form expressions for mean and variance are derived for linear, penalized linear, kernel ridge, Gaussian process, SVM, and RVM models.
Result· Analytical propagation achieves higher computational efficiency than Monte Carlo with comparable accuracy.
Validation· Validated on numerical benchmarks and a lithium-ion battery state-of-health metrology application.

边界: This paper focuses on LPBF process parameters and melting efficiency, but does not address implant-specific properties such as stress shielding, fatigue performance, surface roughness, or osseointegration.

Motivation· To investigate the effects of process parameters on powder melting efficiency in laser powder bed fusion.
Method· Melting efficiency was quantified by experimentally measuring melt pool dimensions under varying laser power and scan speed.
Result· Melting efficiency depends nonlinearly on process parameters and differs between stainless steel and titanium alloy.
Validation· Experimental measurements were compared with analytical models to validate the findings.

Inpatient Hospital Costs, Emergency Department Visits, and Readmissions for Revision Hip and Knee Arthroplasty

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore clinical
Michael P. Murphy, Amir Boubekri, Carlo Eikani, Nicholas M. Brown The Journal of Arthroplasty 2024-04-17 10.1016/j.arth.2024.04.032
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery and Rehabilitation, Loyola University Medical Center, Maywood, Illinois
通讯: Michael P. Murphy @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery and Rehabilitation, Loyola University Medical Center, Maywood, Illinois
相关性: 该论文仅分析翻修手术的经济成本，未涉及生物力学或有限元分析。
边界: This paper does not quantify femoral stress/strain, micromotion, or contact mechanics; it is purely clinical and economic, with no implant mechanics or FEA.
动机· 评估翻修髋膝关节置换术的住院费用和再入院风险因素。
方法· 对772例翻修手术进行回顾性分析，利用混合效应线性回归模型。
结果· 肝病增加髋翻修再入院风险，无菌性松动降低膝翻修再入院风险。
验证· 基于单中心临床数据回顾性分析。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate factors influencing hospital costs, emergency visits, and readmissions after revision hip and knee arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective analysis of 772 revision cases using mixed-effects linear regression to identify predictors of costs and readmission.
Result· Liver disease significantly increases ED readmission risk in revision THA, while aseptic loosening reduces it in revision TKA; age and comorbidities drive higher costs.
Validation· Validated using retrospective clinical data from a single institution over 2007-2019.
