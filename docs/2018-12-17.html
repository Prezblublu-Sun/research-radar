Controllable fabrication of hydroxybutyl chitosan/oxidized chondroitin sulfate hydrogels by 3D bioprinting technique for cartilage tissue engineering

Low AI for Bioprinting openalex Peripheral Background only in_vitro Exp. validation
Cuidi Li, Kan Wang, Xiaojun Zhou, Tao Li, Yan Xu et al. Biomedical Materials 2018-12-17 10.1088/1748-605x/aaf8ed
单位: Georgia Tech Manufacturing Institute, Georgia Institute of Technology, Atlanta, United States of America
通讯: Cuidi Li @ Georgia Tech Manufacturing Institute, Georgia Institute of Technology, Atlanta, United States of America
相关性: 该论文使用3D生物打印技术制造水凝胶支架用于软骨组织工程，但未涉及任何AI方法。
边界: No AI/ML integration in any bioprinting workflow stage; purely material and biological characterization.
动机· 制备具有可设计结构的生物相容性细胞负载水凝胶用于软骨修复。
方法· 通过希夫碱反应合成羟丁基壳聚糖/氧化硫酸软骨素水凝胶，并利用3D打印牺牲模具构建多孔结构。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To fabricate biocompatible cell-laden hydrogels with patient-specific geometries for articular cartilage regeneration.
Method· Covalent hydrogels were synthesized from hydroxybutyl chitosan and oxidized chondroitin sulfate via Schiff-base reaction, and structured using 3D bioprinted sacrificial molds.
Result· Optimized hydrogels exhibited uniform porous structure (100 μm), supported human adipose-derived stem cell viability, and elicited minimal pro-inflammatory response in vitro and in vivo.
Validation· In vitro cell culture and in vivo subcutaneous implantation in mice for 7 days.
