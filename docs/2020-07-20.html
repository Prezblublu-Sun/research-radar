Motivation· To investigate the potential ototoxic effects of cobalt ions released from metal-on-metal hip implants on auditory and vestibular function.
Method· A questionnaire-based pilot study comparing 20 patients with MoM hip implants and 20 matched controls, assessing auditory, vestibular, and neurological symptoms.
Result· Patients reported significantly higher rates of tinnitus and general auditory symptoms, but no dose-response relationship with blood cobalt levels was found.
Validation· Validation through group comparison and subgroup analysis based on blood cobalt concentration.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the long-term wear rate and survivorship of a second-generation highly cross-linked polyethylene liner in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective study of 40 hips with clinical and radiographic follow-up measuring linear wear rate and acetabular abduction angle.
Result· Linear wear rate was 0.037 mm/year with no osteolysis and low revision rate.
Validation· Clinical follow-up over 13 years with radiographic wear measurement.
