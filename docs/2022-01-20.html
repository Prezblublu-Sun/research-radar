Variability in Femoral Preparation and Implantation Between Surgeons Using Manual and Powered Impaction in Total Hip Arthroplasty

High Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Direct Read now cadaveric Exp. validation
Tobias Konow, Johanna Bätz, David Beverland, Tim Board, Frank Lampe et al. Arthroplasty Today 2022-01-20 10.1016/j.artd.2021.10.005
单位: Institute of Biomechanics, TUHH Hamburg University of Technology, Hamburg, Germany
通讯: Tobias Konow @ Institute of Biomechanics, TUHH Hamburg University of Technology, Hamburg, Germany
相关性: 该论文直接量化了全髋关节置换术中股骨柄植入的微动、压配和接触面积，与用户研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not report proximal femur stress/strain/SED in Gruen zones, nor does it compare intact vs implanted femur.
动机· 量化不同经验外科医生在股骨柄植入过程中的变异性及其对初始稳定性的影响。
方法· 三位经验丰富的外科医生在16对尸体股骨上使用手动和动力撞击植入股骨柄，通过CT和循环加载测试评估。
结果· 手动撞击导致压配和接触面积变异更大，较大柄微动更小但可能增加骨折风险。
验证· 尸体股骨实验结合循环加载测试验证初始稳定性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify the variability in femoral stem implantation among experienced surgeons and its effect on primary stability and periprosthetic fracture risk.
Method· Three experienced surgeons implanted stems in 16 cadaveric femur pairs using manual and powered impaction, with CT imaging and cyclic loading tests to assess stem tilt, press-fit, contact area, and micromotion.
Result· Manual impaction led to higher variability in press-fit and contact area; larger stems reduced micromotion but may increase fracture risk.
Validation· Cadaveric experiments with cyclic loading tests validated primary stability measurements.
