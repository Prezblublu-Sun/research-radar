Validation· Validation included in vitro cytotoxicity and proliferation assays as well as in vivo animal experiments.

The effects of boriding on metal-ceramic bond strength of Co–Cr alloy fabricated by selective laser melting

Yakup Uzun, Nuran YANIKOĞLU, H. Kovacı, A.F. Yetim, Ayhan Çelik Journal of Adhesion Science and Technology 2020-12-02 10.1080/01694243.2020.1854003
单位: Faculty of Engineering, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Ataturk University, Erzurum, Turkey
通讯: Yakup Uzun @ Faculty of Engineering, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Ataturk University, Erzurum, Turkey
相关性: 该论文虽涉及SLM制造CoCr合金并进行力学测试，但主要关注牙科用金属-陶瓷结合强度，未涉及应力遮挡、疲劳性能、表面粗糙度或骨整合等关键方向。
边界: Does not address porous structures, stress shielding, stiffness matching, fatigue, or osseointegration for biomedical implants; focus is on dental prosthetic bond strength.
动机· 探究渗硼处理对SLM制造CoCr合金与陶瓷结合强度的影响
方法· 通过SLM制备不同厚度CoCr试件，经渗硼处理后与瓷粉烧结，并进行断裂测试
结果· 渗硼处理使金属-陶瓷结合强度提升14%-74%，以0.1mm厚度增强最显著
验证· 采用体外断裂实验对比渗硼与未渗硼试样的结合强度
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of boriding on the metal-ceramic bond strength of Co-Cr alloy fabricated by selective laser melting.
Method· Co-Cr substructures of varying thickness were fabricated by SLM, borided at 900°C, coated with porcelain, and subjected to fracture tests.
Result· Boriding increased bond strength by 14% to 74% depending on thickness, with the highest improvement at 0.1 mm.
Validation· In vitro fracture tests compared borided and untreated specimens.

Microstructure and Residual Stress Evolution of Laser Powder Bed Fused Inconel 718 under Heat Treatments

Giulio Marchese, Eleonora Atzeni, Alessandro Salmi, Sara Biamino Journal of Materials Engineering and Performance 2020-12-02 10.1007/s11665-020-05338-z
单位: Department of Applied Science and Technology, Politecnico di Torino, Corso Duca degli Abruzzi 24, 10129, Turin, Italy
通讯: Giulio Marchese @ Department of Applied Science and Technology, Politecnico di Torino, Corso Duca degli Abruzzi 24, 10129, Turin, Italy
相关性: 该论文研究镍基高温合金的LPBF工艺与热处理，与生物医用植入体的孔隙结构、应力遮挡、疲劳性能及骨整合等方向无关。
边界: This paper focuses on Inconel 718 superalloy and heat treatment effects on microstructure and residual stress, with no biomedical context, no porous implant design, no fatigue testing, and no osseointegration or surface morphology relevant to orthopedic implants.
动机· 研究热处理对LPBF成形Inconel 718微观组织和残余应力的影响
方法· 采用不同温度（800-1065°C）热处理并结合显微分析和硬度测试
结果· 800°C热处理保持原始组织并降低残余应力，更高温度导致熔池溶解和δ相或碳化物形成
验证· 通过硬度测试和残余应力测量验证热处理效果
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study investigates the influence of various heat treatments on the microstructure, hardness, and residual stresses of laser powder bed fused Inconel 718 to prevent deformation during removal from the build platform.
Method· Specimens were subjected to heat treatments at temperatures ranging from 800°C to 1065°C, followed by microstructural characterization and hardness testing.
Result· Heat treatment at 800°C reduced residual stresses while preserving the as-built microstructure, whereas higher temperatures dissolved melt pools and dendritic structures, forming δ phases or carbides.
Validation· Validation was performed through hardness measurements and residual stress analysis on components with narrow internal channels.

单位: Department of Materials Science and Engineering, National Chiao Tung University, Hsin-chu 30010, Taiwan.
通讯: Nien-Ti Tsou @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering, National Chiao Tung University, Hsin-chu 30010, Taiwan. [推断]
相关性: 这篇论文关于牙科植入物的愈合模式模拟，未涉及AM制造、力学测试或疲劳分析，与用户方向无关。
边界: This paper contains no LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), no surface roughness or as-built morphology analysis, and no osseointegration experiments.
动机· 揭示牙种植体几何设计对生物物理刺激和愈合模式的影响。
方法· 基于机械调节算法的二维轴对称有限元模型预测组织分化历史。
结果· 预测愈合模式与实验观察高度一致，并解释了软组织覆盖和骨吸收等现象。

English summary & terms
Motivation· The study aims to reveal how dental implant geometry influences biophysical stimuli and subsequent healing patterns for improved design guidelines.
Method· A two-dimensional axisymmetric finite element model based on a mechano-regulatory algorithm was developed to predict tissue differentiation history around eight implant designs.
Result· Predicted healing patterns showed excellent agreement with experimental observations, reproducing features like soft tissue coverage and crestal bone loss.
Validation· Validation was performed by comparing predicted healing patterns with experimental observations reported in the literature.

Effects of Different Undersizing Site Preparations on Implant Stability.

Bernardo Ferreira Lemos, Paula Lopez-Jarana, Carlos Falcao, Blanca Ríos-Carrasco, Javier Gil et al. International journal of environmental research and public health 2020-12-02 10.3390/ijerph17238965
单位: Faculty of Health Sciences, Fernando Pessoa University, 4249-004 Porto, Portugal.; Porto Dental Institute, 4150-518 Porto, Portugal.
通讯: Mariano Herrero-Climent @ Porto Dental Institute, 4150-518 Porto, Portugal. [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究牙科植入物的稳定性，与髋关节植入物和股骨柄的力学分析无直接关系。
边界: This paper focuses on dental implant primary stability in bovine bone, with no hip implant context, no proximal femur stress/strain analysis, no micromotion or contact modeling relevant to femoral stems.
动机· 研究不同预备尺寸对牙种植体初期稳定性的影响
方法· 在牛骨上比较四种预备方案，测量植入扭矩和稳定性系数
结果· 减小预备尺寸可提高植入扭矩和稳定性，去除皮质钻效果更好
验证· 离体牛骨机械测试验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how different undersized site preparations affect the primary stability of dental implants.
Method· Four preparation protocols (standard and three undersized variations) were tested in bovine bone, measuring insertion torque and implant stability quotient (ISQ).
Result· Undersized preparations increased both IT and ISQ, with protocols omitting the cortical drill showing the highest stability.
Validation· Ex vivo mechanical tests on fresh bovine bone.
