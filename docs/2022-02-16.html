方法· 通过微观结构分析和双回路电化学动电位测试研究不同激光功率的影响。
结果· 低激光功率下抗晶间腐蚀性能更优，归因于大晶粒和特殊晶界。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the intergranular corrosion behavior of FeCoCrNi high-entropy alloy fabricated by selective laser melting under different laser powers.

Validation· Experimental validation was performed using double-loop electrochemical potentiokinetic reactivation measurements.
