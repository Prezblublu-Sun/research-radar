Osteogenic Potential of Additively Manufactured TiTa Alloys

High AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Read now in_vitro Exp. validation
Erin G. Brodie, Kye J. Robinson, Elizabeth Sigston, Andrey Molotnikov, Jessica E. Frith ACS Applied Bio Materials 2021-01-03 10.1021/acsabm.0c01450
单位: Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Monash University, Clayton, Victoria 3800, Australia
通讯: Erin G. Brodie @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Monash University, Clayton, Victoria 3800, Australia  |  Jessica E. Frith @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Monash University, Clayton, Victoria 3800, Australia
相关性: 该论文直接研究了LPBF制造的TiTa合金的力学性能（低模量）和体外成骨分化，与用户关注的应力遮挡、LPBF制造及生物相容性高度相关。
边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing, surface roughness characterization, or osseointegration in animal models.
动机· Ti-6Al-4V模量高且生物惰性，易导致应力遮挡和骨整合失败，需开发低模量活性合金。
方法· 激光粉末床熔融制备Ti25Ta和Ti65Ta合金，抛光后通过体外hBMSC培养评估表面化学对成骨的影响。
结果· TiTa合金相比Ti-6Al-4V显著提高碱性磷酸酶活性和矿化，Ti25Ta与Ti65Ta生物学响应相当。
验证· 通过XPS、EDS表征表面成分，体外细胞实验验证成骨分化能力。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Ti-6Al-4V's high modulus and bioinert surface cause stress shielding and poor osseointegration, motivating the development of low-modulus bioactive alloys for additive manufactured bone implants.
Method· Laser powder bed fusion was used to fabricate Ti25Ta and Ti65Ta alloys, and polished substrates were tested with human bone marrow mesenchymal stromal cells to isolate the chemical effect of Ta content on osteogenesis.
Result· Both TiTa alloys exhibited significantly higher alkaline phosphatase activity and mineralization than Ti-6Al-4V, with no major difference between the two compositions.
Validation· Surface chemistry was characterized by EDS and XPS, and osteogenic potential was validated through in vitro cell assays.
