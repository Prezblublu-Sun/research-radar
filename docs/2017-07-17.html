Comparison of Various Surrogate Models to Predict Stress Intensity Factor of a Crack Propagating in Offshore Piping

Arvind Keprate, R. M. Chandima Ratnayake, Shankar Sankararaman Journal of Offshore Mechanics and Arctic Engineering 2017-07-17 10.1115/1.4037290
单位: Department of Mechanical and Structural Engineering and Material Science, University of Stavanger, Stavanger 4036, Norway e-mail:
通讯: Arvind Keprate @ Department of Mechanical and Structural Engineering and Material Science, University of Stavanger, Stavanger 4036, Norwa  |  R. M. Chandima Ratnayake @ Department of Mechanical and Structural Engineering and Material Science, University of Stavanger, Stavanger 4036, Norwa  |  Shankar Sankararaman @ NASA Ames Research Center, SGT Inc., Moffett Field, CA 94035 e-mail:
相关性: 该论文比较了多种代理模型预测应力强度因子，其中高斯过程回归提供了不确定性量化，方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模。
边界: This paper focuses on offshore piping crack propagation, not implant biomechanics or bone-implant interface, and lacks experimental validation in a biomedical context.
动机· 用代理模型替代有限元法预测裂纹应力强度因子，降低计算成本。
方法· 比较六种代理模型（MLR、PR、GPR、NN、RVR、SVR），基于70个训练点和30个测试点。
结果· 高斯过程回归因能提供预测不确定性而被选为最佳模型。
验证· 通过有限元数据训练和测试，使用RMSE、AAE、MAE、R²指标比较。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce computational cost of finite element simulations for predicting stress intensity factors in propagating cracks by employing surrogate models.
Method· Six surrogate models (MLR, PR, GPR, NN, RVR, SVR) were trained on 70 FEM data points and tested on 30 points, with performance evaluated using RMSE, AAE, MAE, and R².
Result· Gaussian process regression was selected as the best surrogate due to its ability to provide uncertainty estimates, enabling adaptive training and improved accuracy.
Validation· Numerical benchmark using FEM-generated data for crack propagation in offshore piping, with cross-validation metrics.
