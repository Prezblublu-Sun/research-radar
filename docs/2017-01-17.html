通讯: Bo Su @ School of Oral and Dental Sciences, University of Bristol, Bristol, UK
相关性: 该论文直接通过冷冻铸造制备具有各向异性和梯度孔隙结构的钛合金支架，并评估其压缩强度与应力屏蔽的关系，与用户的研究方向高度相关。

Validation· Micro-CT imaging confirmed anisotropic pore morphology, and mechanical testing validated compressive strength within cortical bone range.

Validation· Validated on three high-dimensional real-world streaming problems with comparison to random sampling.
