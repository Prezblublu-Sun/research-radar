Investigation of the Possibility of Tailoring the Chemical Com-Position of the NiTi Alloy by Selective Laser Melting

E. V. Borisov, Kirill Starikov, Anatoly Popovich, Tatiana Tihonovskaya Metals 2021-09-16 10.3390/met11091470
单位: Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University (SPbPU), Polytechnicheskaya 29, 195251 St. Petersburg, Russia
通讯: E. V. Borisov @ Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University (SPbPU), Polytechnicheskaya 29, 195251 St. Petersburg, Russia
相关性: 该论文研究NiTi合金的SLM工艺参数对成分和相变温度的影响，未涉及多孔结构、力学性能或应力屏蔽等用户关注的核心问题。
边界: This paper does not evaluate porous structures, mechanical properties (e.g., stiffness, fatigue), or surface morphology for osseointegration; it focuses solely on chemical composition and density optimization during SLM of NiTi.
动机· 研究SLM工艺参数对NiTi合金成分和相变温度的影响。
方法· 通过调整激光参数和多次扫描，分析镍蒸发对成分的影响。
结果· 高能量密度导致镍蒸发增加，降低镍含量；多次低能量扫描可实现类似效果。
验证· 通过密度测量和DSC分析验证成分变化。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This work investigates how selective laser melting parameters affect the chemical composition and transformation temperatures of NiTi alloys.
Method· The study systematically varied laser energy density and used single and double scanning to analyze nickel evaporation and its effect on composition.
Result· Higher energy density increases nickel evaporation, reducing Ni content, and double scanning at lower energy achieves similar compositional shifts.
Validation· The compositional changes were validated through density measurements and differential scanning calorimetry (DSC) analysis.

单位: Department of Orthopaedics Surgery, Graduate School of Medicine, Chiba University, 1-8-1 Inohana, Chuo-ku, Chiba 260-8670, Japan
通讯: K Konno @ Department of Orthopaedics Surgery, Graduate School of Medicine, Chiba University, 1-8-1 Inohana, Chuo-ku, Chiba 260-867
相关性: 该论文仅评估术后评估软件的可靠性，未涉及髋关节假体的生物力学分析或应力遮挡。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain, micromotion, or compare intact vs implanted femur; it only assesses measurement reliability of implant size and position from CT.
动机· 评估全髋关节置换术后三维评估软件的可靠性。
方法· 利用术后CT数据，通过组内相关系数分析测量的一致性和可重复性。
结果· 假体大小、对齐和定位的测量具有高一致性和可靠性。
验证· 基于20例髋关节术后CT数据的临床可靠性分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To assess the interobserver and intraobserver reliability of a CT-based postoperative evaluation software for total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Postoperative CT scans of 20 hips were analyzed for component size, alignment, and positioning using intraclass correlation coefficients.
Result· The software demonstrated high concordance rates (>94%) and very good intraobserver and interobserver reliability for both cup and stem measurements.
Validation· Validation was performed using clinical CT data from 20 patients, with reliability statistics computed for all measured parameters.
