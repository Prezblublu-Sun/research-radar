英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare clinical outcomes between posterolateral and lateral approaches in total hip arthroplasty for avascular necrosis.
Method· A retrospective study of 46 cases analyzed Harris hip score, operative time, incision length, and blood loss.
Result· No significant differences were found between the two approaches in the measured clinical outcomes.
Validation· Clinical retrospective data with statistical analysis.
