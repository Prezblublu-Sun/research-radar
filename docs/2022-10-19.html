通讯: Zongchen Li @ Empa-Swiss Federal Laboratories for Materials Science and Technology , Duebendorf, Switzerland
相关性: 该论文研究了增材制造Ti-6Al-4V中熔合缺陷对疲劳性能的影响，但缺乏植入物相关的多孔结构、应力遮挡或骨整合分析。
边界: No biomedical context, no porous structure design for stiffness matching, no experimental fatigue testing, no osseointegration or surface morphology.
动机· 揭示熔合缺陷对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V高周疲劳的损害机制

验证· 数值模拟对比，无实验验证
英文摘要与术语

Result· Lack-of-fusion defects induce higher stress concentration than pores, and their orientation and aspect ratio significantly influence fatigue behavior.
Validation· Parametric numerical study without experimental fatigue testing.
