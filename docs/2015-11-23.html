动机· 需要精确表征大型混合离子库仑晶体的离子数和微动能量。
方法· 引入一种准平衡方法，结合模拟与实验观测来确定离子数和微动能量。
结果· 模拟与实验观测吻合良好，方法适用于超过10^3个离子的晶体。
验证· 与实验观测对比验证模拟结果。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Accurate characterization of ion numbers and micromotion energies in large mixed-ion Coulomb crystals is needed for applications in precision spectroscopy and cold-ion chemistry.
Method· A quasiequilibrium method is introduced to determine ion numbers and micromotion energies by combining simulations with experimental observations.
Result· The simulations show compelling agreement with experimental observations for crystals with over 10^3 ions.
Validation· Validation is performed by comparing simulation results with experimental observations.
