SSPINNpose: A Self-Supervised PINN for Inertial Pose and Dynamics Estimation

Markus Gambietz, Eva Dorschky, Altan Akat, Marcel Schöckel, Jörg Miehling et al. arXiv preprint 2025-06-13 10.1145/3806199
相关性: 该论文涉及物理信息神经网络用于人体运动动力学估计，与您的有限元代理建模方向无直接关系。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling or implant biomechanics; it focuses on human pose and joint moment estimation from IMU data without structural mechanics or contact mechanics.
动机· 避免传统监督学习依赖光学动捕标签的局限性
方法· 自监督PINN结合人体物理模型从IMU数据直接估计关节运动和力矩
结果· 在行走和跑步中关节角度误差8.7度，力矩误差4.9%BWBH%，延迟3.5ms
验证· 与光学运动捕捉数据对比验证准确性
English summary & terms
Motivation· To overcome the need for ground truth labels from optical motion capture in real-time human movement estimation.
Method· A self-supervised physics-informed neural network that uses a physics model of the human body to generate virtual sensor data for training directly on IMU measurements.
Result· The method achieves joint angle RMSD of 8.7 deg and joint moment RMSD of 4.9 BWBH% for walking and running up to 4.9 m/s with 3.5 ms latency.
Validation· Validation against optical motion capture data for joint kinematics and kinetics during gait.

Uncovering Bias Paths with LLM-guided Causal Discovery: An Active Learning and Dynamic Scoring Approach

Khadija Zanna, Akane Sano arXiv preprint 2025-06-13 link
相关性: 该论文涉及LLM引导的因果发现用于公平性审计，与有限元代理建模或植入物生物力学设计完全无关。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, sampling efficiency, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification, or any biomechanics/implant design context.
动机· 利用LLM语义先验改进因果发现中的公平相关路径恢复
方法· 提出混合LLM引导的广度优先搜索，结合主动学习和动态评分策略
结果· 在含噪声和混淆的UCI成人数据集上，该方法优于基线恢复公平关键路径
验证· 基于半合成数据集的结构恢复与路径敏感性评估
English summary & terms
Motivation· Improving recovery of fairness-relevant causal pathways in noisy, confounded data by integrating LLM semantic priors with statistical causal discovery.
Method· A hybrid LLM-guided causal discovery framework using breadth-first search with active learning and a composite scoring function combining mutual information, partial correlation, and LLM confidence.
Result· The proposed active, dynamically-scored LLM method outperforms baselines in recovering fairness-critical graph structures under noise and confounding.
Validation· A semi-synthetic benchmark based on the UCI Adult dataset with embedded bias pathways and noise confounders.

Is it safe to use ceramic on polyethylene bearings in revision hip arthroplasty for ceramic fracture?

Christopher Gunn, Vivek Thakker, Sophie Williams, Timothy N Board, Henry Wynn-Jones et al. Bone & Joint Open 2025-06-13 10.1302/2633-1462.66.bjo-2025-0030.r1
单位: Wrightington, Wigan & Leigh NHS Foundation Trust, Wigan, UK
通讯: Christopher Gunn @ Wrightington, Wigan & Leigh NHS Foundation Trust, Wigan, UK
相关性: 该论文关注陶瓷骨折翻修术的临床结局和磨损率，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡或微动建模。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics; it is a clinical wear study without FEA or biomechanical modeling.
动机· 评估陶瓷骨折翻修术中使用陶瓷对聚乙烯轴承的安全性。
方法· 回顾性收集翻修患者数据，测量线性磨损率，并进行体外测试。
结果· CoP轴承组磨损率低，体外测试无早期灾难性磨损。
验证· 临床随访和体外磨损测试。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the safety of using ceramic-on-polyethylene bearings in revision total hip arthroplasty after ceramic fracture.
Method· Retrospective data collection on 12 patients with linear wear measurement from radiographs and in vitro testing of bearing surfaces with embedded ceramic particles.
Result· Ceramic-on-polyethylene bearings showed wear rates comparable to primary arthroplasty and no early catastrophic wear in vitro.
Validation· Clinical follow-up (mean 3.8 years) with wear measurement and in vitro particle embedding tests.
