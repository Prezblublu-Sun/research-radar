Jian Li, Dongxiao Zhang, Tianhao He, Qiang Zheng arXiv preprint 2022-05-28 10.1016/j.geoen.2022.211368
相关性: 该论文提出的耦合理论引导神经网络（TgNN）代理模型方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模，但未涉及植入物设计或生物力学约束。
边界: This paper focuses on subsurface two-phase flow in porous media, not on implant biomechanics, contact mechanics, or structural FEA surrogate modeling.
动机· 提高地下两相流不确定性量化中正向模拟的计算效率。
方法· 构建耦合理论引导神经网络（TgNN）代理模型，将物理方程和随机场作为损失项。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To accelerate uncertainty quantification of subsurface two-phase flow by reducing the computational cost of forward simulations.
Method· A coupled theory-guided neural network (TgNN) surrogate model is developed, incorporating governing equations, stochastic parameter fields, and boundary conditions into the loss function.
Result· The TgNN surrogate achieves satisfactory accuracy and robustness, outperforming purely data-driven models, and enables efficient Monte Carlo-based UQ.
Validation· The model is validated on numerical benchmarks with varying labeled data sizes, collocation points, and noise levels.

Routine Indwelling Urinary Catheterization Is Not Necessary During Total Hip Arthroplasty Performed Under Spinal Anesthesia

动机· 评估全髋关节置换术中常规导尿的必要性
方法· 回顾性分析991例患者，比较导尿与未导尿组的泌尿系统并发症

验证· 临床回顾性数据分析
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine whether routine indwelling urinary catheterization is necessary for patients undergoing total hip arthroplasty under spinal anesthesia.
Method· A retrospective review of 991 consecutive primary THA patients compared urinary complications between those with and without routine catheterization.
Result· Routine catheterization was associated with higher urinary tract infection rates but lower minor postoperative urinary retention rates, with no difference in major retention.
Validation· Clinical retrospective cohort analysis with multivariate regression.
