Using Bayesian deep learning approaches for uncertainty-aware building energy surrogate models

Paul Westermann, Ralph Evins arXiv preprint 2020-10-05 link
相关性: 该论文将贝叶斯深度学习用于建筑能耗代理模型，其不确定性量化方法可迁移至生物力学植入物设计中的昂贵仿真替代建模。
边界: This paper does not address implant or biomechanics design, does not handle constraints in optimization, and is validated on building energy simulation, not biomedical FEA.
动机· 将贝叶斯深度学习用于代理模型，以量化预测不确定性并加速仿真。

结果· 两种贝叶斯模型精度相当，通过将高不确定性样本转移至高保真模型可将误差降低30%。

English summary & terms

Method· Two Bayesian models, dropout neural networks and stochastic variational Gaussian processes, are trained to emulate a high-dimensional building energy simulation with 35 inputs and 12 outputs.
Result· Both Bayesian approaches achieve competitive accuracy, and transferring the 10% most uncertain samples to the high-fidelity model reduces errors by up to 30%.
Validation· Validated on a complex building energy performance simulation case, benchmarking accuracy and uncertainty estimation.
