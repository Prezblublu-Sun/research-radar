Relationship between mechanical properties and density of Ti obtained by additive technology

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Transferable Skim only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Yu. N. Loginov, S. I. Stepanov, A. V. Yudin, JSC “NPO TSNIITMASH”, Moscow, Russia, E. V. Tretyakov et al. Tsvetnye Metally 2018-05-31 10.17580/tsm.2018.05.07
相关性: 该论文研究了通过SLM制备的纯钛样品的密度与力学性能之间的关系，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper does not cover porous structures for stress shielding reduction, fatigue testing, or surface roughness/osseointegration characterization.
动机· 建立SLM制备纯钛样品的密度与力学性能之间的关系。
方法· 采用SLM技术制备不同密度的纯钛样品，进行拉伸测试和微观结构分析。
结果· 屈服强度和延伸率与相对密度呈线性正相关，密度92%时屈服强度300 MPa，更高密度时超过500 MPa。
验证· 通过静水称重法测量密度，拉伸测试验证力学性能。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To establish the relationship between relative density and mechanical properties of commercially pure titanium samples fabricated by selective laser melting.
Method· Samples with varying densities were produced by SLM, and tensile tests along with microstructural analysis were conducted.
Result· Yield strength and elongation to fracture showed a direct linear proportionality with relative density, with yield strength ranging from 300 MPa at 92% density to over 500 MPa at higher densities.
Validation· Density was measured by hydrostatic weighing, and mechanical properties were validated through tensile testing.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the mechanical differences of chemically cleaned bone allografts when supplemented with blood, clotted blood, platelet concentrate, or platelet gel.
Method· Uniaxial compression tests were performed on four groups of allografts standardized by grain size distribution, before and after compaction.
Result· No statistically significant difference in yield limit after compaction was found among the groups.
Validation· Mechanical testing via uniaxial compression was used to compare yield limits.
