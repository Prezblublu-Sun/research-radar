Motivation· To characterize the temporal radiographic patterns of bone ingrowth, radiolucent line resolution, and bone resorption in cementless total knee arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective review of 301 knees from 221 patients who underwent robotic-assisted cementless TKA with 3D-printed porous implants, analyzing serial radiographs for trabecular line formation, RLLs, and bone resorption.
Result· Trabecular line formation occurred in over 90% of knees, with bone ingrowth typically between 4.5 and 8.9 months, while stress-shielding-related bone resorption continued beyond 14 months.
Validation· Clinical validation using minimum 2-year radiographic follow-up data from a single institution.
