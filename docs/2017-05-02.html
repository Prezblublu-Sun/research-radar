A Novel Approach to Forecasting Financial Volatility with Gaussian Process Envelopes

Syed Ali Asad Rizvi, Stephen J. Roberts, Michael A. Osborne, Favour Nyikosa arXiv preprint 2017-05-02 link
相关性: 该论文研究金融波动预测，与FEA替代建模或生物力学设计无关。
边界: This paper applies Gaussian processes to financial time series forecasting, with no FEA, surrogate modeling, constraints, UQ, or biomechanics context.
动机· 用高斯过程预测金融波动，替代传统GARCH模型。
方法· 对收益率包络线进行GP回归，比较三种包络策略。
结果· GP在均方误差上比随机游走好20%，比GARCH好50%。
验证· 在四种货币对的半小时数据上做两年样本外测试。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To improve financial volatility forecasting by applying Gaussian process regression to time series envelopes.
Method· Three GP regression approaches are compared: on absolute/squared returns, on return envelopes, and on separate positive/negative return envelopes.
Result· GP models outperform random walk by 20% and GARCH by 50% in mean squared error on out-of-sample currency data.
Validation· Validation is performed on half-hourly data of four currency pairs over two years using accuracy measures and loss functions.
