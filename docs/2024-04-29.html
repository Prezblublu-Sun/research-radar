Paul G. Beckman, Christopher J. Geoga arXiv preprint 2024-04-29 link
相关性: 提出了一种高效计算高斯过程协方差函数的自适应积分方法，可提高代理模型的训练和预测效率。
边界: This paper does not address expensive simulation sampling efficiency, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification, or implant/biomechanics design; it is a purely numerical method for fast covariance evaluation.
动机· 高效计算任意谱密度对应的协方差函数及其导数。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To enable flexible stationary covariance function design by efficiently evaluating covariance functions from arbitrary continuous, integrable spectral densities.
Method· An adaptive integration framework using high-order panel quadrature, nonuniform fast Fourier transform, and Nyquist-informed panel selection with novel truncation error bounds.
Result· Demonstrates orders of magnitude speedup over uniform quadrature, allowing evaluation at millions of locations to user-specified tolerance in seconds on a laptop.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks and gradient-based maximum likelihood estimation on a long-memory spectral model for wind velocities.

Motivation· To determine whether a minimally invasive posterolateral approach preserving the piriformis muscle offers advantages over the standard posterolateral approach in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A prospective randomized study of 200 patients undergoing primary THA compared perioperative bleeding, postoperative pain, recovery speed, component orientation, size, fit, complications, and functional outcomes at one year.
Result· The minimally invasive group showed faster recovery, more anteverted acetabular components, smaller component sizes, and lower pain at 48 hours, but no differences in complications or functional outcome at one year.
Validation· Clinical outcomes were validated through patient follow-up data with minimum one-year follow-up and statistical analysis.
