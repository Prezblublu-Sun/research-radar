Laser-Based Hybrid Manufacturing of Endosseous Implants: Optimized Titanium Surfaces for Enhancing Osteogenic Differentiation of Human Mesenchymal Stem Cells.

Guenaelle Bouet, Frédéric Cabanettes, Guillaume Bidron, Alain Guignandon, Sylvie Peyroche et al. ACS biomaterials science & engineering 2019-09-09 10.1021/acsbiomaterials.9b00769
单位: Ecole Nationale d'Ingénieurs de Saint-Etienne, Laboratoire de Tribologie et Dynamique des Systèmes, UMR 5513 CNRS, University of Lyon, 58, rue Jean Parot, 42023 Saint-Etienne, France.
通讯: Virginie Dumas @ Ecole Nationale d'Ingénieurs de Saint-Etienne, Laboratoire de Tribologie et Dynamique des Systèmes, UMR 5513 CNRS, Unive [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究了增材制造钛合金植入体的表面优化，但与用户的应力屏蔽和疲劳方向无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not link porous structure to stress shielding or stiffness matching, and it lacks any mechanical testing (static or fatigue).
动机· 提高增材制造钛合金植入体的表面生物活性以实现骨整合。
方法· 结合增材制造与飞秒激光加工制备功能性表面，并评估人间充质干细胞行为。
结果· 飞秒激光处理后的表面粗糙度降低，细胞铺展和成骨分化标志物显著提高。
验证· 体外细胞实验，包括黏附、增殖和成骨分化检测。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the biofunctionality of additively manufactured titanium implant surfaces for improved osseointegration.
Method· A hybrid approach combining additive manufacturing with femtosecond laser processing was used to create optimized surface topographies, and human mesenchymal stem cell responses were evaluated.
Result· The femtosecond laser treatment reduced surface roughness and significantly improved cell spreading and osteogenic differentiation markers compared to as-printed surfaces.
Validation· In vitro cell assays measuring adhesion, proliferation, collagen synthesis, and mineralization.

通讯: K Jane Grande-Allen @ Department of Bioengineering, Rice University, 6100 Main Street, Houston, Texas 77005, United States. [推断]

方法· 制备荧光卟啉水凝胶微粒，实现深度2 mm内的比率氧传感。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To enable oxygen monitoring at millimeter depths within large engineered tissues for improving vascularization strategies.

Result· The microparticles accurately measured oxygen gradients and cellular consumption rates in 3D constructs at depths up to 2 mm.
Validation· Validation was performed using cellularized hydrogels with primary human cells and various polymerization chemistries.

单位: Department of Biomedical Engineering, TOBB University of Economics and Technology, 43 Sogutozu Street, 06560 Ankara, Turkey.; Brigham and Women's Hospital, Renal Division, 4 Blackfan Circle Street, 02
相关性: 该论文研究纳米柱壳聚糖/明胶薄膜的骨诱导能力，但未涉及LPBF增材制造、应力遮挡或疲劳力学性能。
边界: This paper does not involve LPBF fabrication, mechanical testing (static/fatigue), stress shielding, or any metallic implant design; it focuses on polymer film surface topography for osteogenesis.
动机· 模仿细胞外基质纳米结构以促进骨整合。
方法· 利用纳米多孔阳极氧化铝模板制备纳米柱壳聚糖/明胶薄膜。
结果· 纳米结构显著增强间充质干细胞和成骨细胞的成骨分化与矿化。
验证· 体外细胞实验：基因表达（RUNX2等）和矿化染色（茜素红）。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To mimic the nanoscale extracellular matrix topography for enhancing osteointegration of orthopedic implants.
Method· Nanopillared chitosan/gelatin films were fabricated using nanoporous anodic alumina molds via a nonlithographic technique.
Result· The nanotopography upregulated osteogenic gene markers (RUNX2, osteopontin, osteocalcin) and increased mineralization in both MSCs and Saos-2 cells.
Validation· In vitro assays including RT-qPCR, alizarin red staining, and calcium quantification on two cell lines.
