Hybrid Fourier Neural Operator-Plasma Fluid Model for Fast and Accurate Multiscale Simulations of High Power Microwave Breakdown

相关性: 该论文的混合FNO-物理模型方法可迁移至生物力学FEA代理建模，但未涉及植入物设计或约束优化。
边界: No biomechanics or implant FEA context; no constraint handling or uncertainty quantification; validation only on plasma streamer simulations.
动机· 高功率微波击穿多尺度模拟计算开销大，需快速替代方法。

English summary & terms
Motivation· Multiscale simulations of high-power microwave breakdown are computationally expensive, motivating a fast surrogate for the electromagnetic solver.
Method· A Fourier Neural Operator replaces the FDTD-based EM field updates, coupled with a differential plasma fluid solver in a hybrid framework.
Result· The hybrid model predicts streamer shape, velocity, and evolution accurately for unseen incident fields, achieving 60x speedup.
Validation· Validated against FDTD plasma-fluid simulations for 2D microwave streamer formation with new incident fields not seen in training.
