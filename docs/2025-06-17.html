Validation· Experimental validation via repeated positioning and pipetting tests.

Resolving Phonons in Superconductor Bi2Sr2CaCu2O8+δ at Sub-Unit-Cell Resolution

Xiaowen Zhang, Jiade Li, Xiaoyue Gao, Ruochen Shi, Bo Han et al. arXiv preprint 2025-06-17 link
相关性: 该论文完全不涉及增材制造生物医学材料方向的内容。
边界: The paper studies phonon modes in a cuprate superconductor using EELS, with no connection to additive manufacturing, biomedical implants, mechanical testing, or osseointegration.
动机· 探究超导材料中声子与超导机制的关联。
方法· 使用亚单胞分辨的电子能量损失谱识别层特定声子模式。
结果· 发现CuO2面内及BiO层的声子模式，并观察到超结构引起的频率调制。
验证· 通过扫描透射电镜实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the role of phonons in cuprate superconductors and their layer-specific localization.
Method· Sub-unit-cell resolved electron energy loss spectroscopy in a scanning transmission electron microscope was used to identify phonon modes from different layers.
Result· Several phonon modes with layer-specific localization were revealed, along with a periodic modulation of phonon frequencies correlated with the superstructure.
Validation· Experimental validation via STEM-EELS measurements.
