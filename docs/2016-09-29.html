Python Active-subspaces Utility Library

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project none
Paul G. Constantine, Ryan Howard, Andrew Glaws, Zachary Grey, Paul Diaz et al. The Journal of Open Source Software 2016-09-29 10.21105/joss.00079
单位: Colorado School of Mines, Golden, CO
通讯: Paul G. Constantine @ Colorado School of Mines, Golden, CO
相关性: 该论文介绍了一种用于降维的主动子空间方法，可应用于代理建模中的昂贵仿真采样效率问题。
边界: This paper presents a software library for active subspaces without any application to FEA surrogate modeling, constraint handling, or biomechanical implant design.
动机· 降低高维仿真模型参数研究的计算成本。
方法· 基于函数偏导数的特征值分解识别主动子空间。
结果· 提供Python库实现主动子空间方法，支持降维后的优化与不确定性量化。
验证· 无实验验证，仅介绍库的功能与使用。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce the computational cost of parameter studies in expensive simulation models by discovering low-dimensional structure.
Method· Active subspaces are identified via eigendecomposition of a matrix derived from partial derivatives of the function.
Result· A Python library is provided to implement active subspace methods, enabling dimension reduction for optimization and uncertainty quantification.
Validation· No experimental validation; the paper describes the library's functionality and usage.

单位: Professor Lab. de Mathématiques de Versailles, CNRS, UVSQ, Université Paris-Saclay, Versailles 78035, France e-mail:
通讯: Laurent Dumas @ Professor Lab. de Mathématiques de Versailles, CNRS, UVSQ, Université Paris-Saclay, Versailles 78035, France e-mail:
相关性: 该论文提出了一种基于一维模型的逆问题方法，结合不确定性量化，可迁移至植入物生物力学设计中的代理建模与参数校准。
边界: This paper focuses on hemodynamics and arterial stiffness, not on implant biomechanics, FEA surrogate modeling, or structural optimization.
动机· 动脉僵硬度难以直接测量，需通过数值方法间接估计。
方法· 基于一维血流模型和逆问题校准动脉刚度分布，并开展不确定性量化。
结果· 模型能提供鲁棒且个体化的下肢血流动力学参数估计。

Motivation· Arterial stiffness is difficult to measure directly, motivating a numerical approach to estimate it from noninvasive measurements.
Method· A one-dimensional hemodynamic model is calibrated via an inverse problem using in vivo data, with uncertainty quantification of input parameters.
Result· The calibrated model provides robust subject-specific estimates of arterial stiffness and pulse pressure.
Validation· Validation is performed through simulation of a lower limb model, demonstrating the approach's feasibility.

Intraobserver Reliability and Interobserver Agreement in Radiographic Classification of Heterotopic Ossification

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore none
Georgios I. Vasileiadis, Yodhiaki Itoigawa, Derek F. Amanatullah, Luis Pulido-Sierra, Jeremy R. Crenshaw et al. Orthopedics 2016-09-29 10.3928/01477447-20160926-05
相关性: 该论文仅涉及髋关节置换术后异位骨化的影像学分类可靠性，未涉及任何生物力学建模或植入物设计。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, no implant stress/strain analysis, no micromotion, no FEA, no patient-specific femur modeling.
动机· 评估两种异位骨化分类系统的观察者间和观察者内可靠性。
方法· 三位临床医生独立对236例THA术后X光片进行分级，计算Cohen's kappa系数。
结果· 两种分类系统均显示中等至显著可靠性，但缺乏足够清晰度。
验证· 基于临床X光片分级和kappa统计。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare intraobserver reliability and interobserver agreement of the Brooker and Della Valle classification systems for heterotopic ossification after THA.
Method· Three clinicians independently graded radiographs of 236 patients using both classification systems, and Cohen's kappa was calculated.
Result· Both systems showed moderate to substantial reliability, but lacked sufficient clarity and were subject to interpretation.
Validation· Clinical radiographic grading with kappa statistics.
