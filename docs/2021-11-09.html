Quantification and prediction of lack-of-fusion porosity in the high porosity regime during laser powder bed fusion of Ti-6Al-4V

Patcharapit Promoppatum, Raghavan Srinivasan, S.S. Quek, Sabeur Msolli, Shashwat Shukla et al. Journal of Materials Processing Technology 2021-11-09 10.1016/j.jmatprotec.2021.117426
单位: Institute of High Performance Computing, A⁎STAR, 1 Fusionopolis Way, #16-16 Connexis, Singapore, 138632, Singapore
通讯: Mark Hyunpong Jhon @ Institute of High Performance Computing, A⁎STAR, 1 Fusionopolis Way, #16-16 Connexis, Singapore, 138632, Singapore
相关性: 该论文研究了LPBF Ti-6Al-4V的乏熔孔隙预测，虽未涉及应力屏蔽或骨整合，但对骨骼植入物制造过程控制有参考价值。
边界: This paper does not link porous structure to stress shielding or stiffness matching, does not include mechanical or fatigue testing, and does not report surface roughness or osseointegration. It focuses solely on porosity quantification and prediction in LPBF Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 量化预测激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V中高孔隙率下的乏熔孔隙
方法· 实验表征结合模型预测，分析工艺参数对乏熔孔隙形成的影响
结果· 实现了对乏熔孔隙含量和尺寸的准确预测，验证了模型在宽工艺窗口的适用性
验证· 通过实验数据与预测结果对比验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to quantify and predict lack-of-fusion porosity in laser powder bed fusion of Ti-6Al-4V, particularly in the high porosity regime.
Method· Experimental characterization and predictive modeling were used to correlate process parameters with porosity formation.
Result· The model accurately predicted porosity levels across a range of process conditions, demonstrating robustness for process optimization.
Validation· Validation was performed by comparing model predictions with experimental porosity measurements from microscopy and X-ray CT.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how sample building orientation affects porosity and mechanical properties of Ti6Al4V produced by Selective Laser Melting.
Method· SLM samples were fabricated at different orientations, followed by porosity analysis and mechanical testing.
Result· Building orientation significantly influences porosity and mechanical properties, indicating the need for process optimization.
Validation· Validation was performed through experimental mechanical tests on as-built specimens.

Elastin-like Polypeptide-Based Bioink: A Promising Alternative for 3D Bioprinting

Michèle Dai, Jean-Philippe Belaïdi, Guillaume Fleury, Élisabeth Garanger, Maïté Rielland et al. Biomacromolecules 2021-11-09 10.1021/acs.biomac.1c00861
单位: L’Oréal Recherche Avancée, 1 avenue Eugène Schueller, 93600 Aulnay-sous-Bois, France
通讯: Maïté Rielland @ L’Oréal Recherche Avancée, 1 avenue Eugène Schueller, 93600 Aulnay-sous-Bois, France
相关性: 该论文主要研究弹性蛋白样多肽基生物墨水的开发，不涉及任何人工智能或机器学习方法。
边界: This paper does not integrate AI/ML into any bioprinting workflow stage, lacks experimental validation of AI models, and does not address autonomous decision-making.
动机· 开发适用于3D生物打印的弹性蛋白样多肽生物墨水。
方法· 通过光聚合交联和功能化修饰构建可打印的ELP基水凝胶。
结果· ELP基生物墨水具有良好打印性、力学性能和生物相容性。
验证· 通过人真皮成纤维细胞培养和细胞外基质蛋白表达验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a bioink based on elastin-like polypeptide that meets the stringent requirements for 3D bioprinting including printability, stability, and biocompatibility.
Method· Elastin-like polypeptide was chemoselectively modified with cross-linkable moieties and functionalized with GRGDS peptide or collagen I for cell adhesion, then photopolymerized to form a 3D network.
Result· The ELP-based bioinks exhibited excellent printability, mechanical properties, and biocompatibility, supporting cell viability and ECM protein expression.
Validation· In vitro culture of dermal normal human fibroblasts and assessment of cell viability, adhesion, and ECM markers (pro-collagen I, elastin, etc.) after 18 days.

相关性: 与生物医学植入物的应力屏蔽、疲劳或骨整合无关。
边界: This paper does not address any biomedical context, implant design, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or osseointegration; it focuses solely on surface finishing of additively manufactured 316L stainless steel via electrochemical machining.
动机· 改善SLM不锈钢零件表面缺陷。
方法· 使用射流电化学加工并优化参数。
结果· 确定了最佳工艺参数组合。
验证· 通过实验验证优化结果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to improve the surface quality of additively manufactured 316L stainless steel parts by removing surface defects such as adhered particles and pores.
Method· Jet electrochemical machining (JECM) was employed, and process parameters (electrolyte temperature, processing time, current density) were optimized using Taguchi orthogonal array and ANOVA.
Result· Optimal parameter combinations were identified to maximize defect removal while minimizing material removal thickness and surface roughness.
Validation· Experimental verification was conducted using the optimized parameters, confirming improved surface performance.

单位: Heping Hospital Affiliated to Changzhi Medical College, Changzhi 046000, Shanxi, China.
通讯: Wenhao Zhou @ Shaanxi Key Laboratory of Biomedical Metal Materials, Northwest Institute for Non-ferrous Metal Research, Xi'an 710016, [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究涂层抗菌与成骨性能，未涉及LPBF增材制造、多孔结构或力学性能测试。
边界: This paper focuses on a nanoparticle-loaded silk fibroin coating for antibacterial and osteogenic purposes, but does not involve LPBF fabrication, porous metallic scaffold design, fatigue testing, stress shielding analysis, or as-built surface morphology characterization, which are core to the AM Biomedical Materials direction.
动机· 设计具有抗菌和成骨功能的仿生涂层以提高植入体成功率。
方法· 制备丝素蛋白基涂层并嵌入纳米羟基磷灰石和银纳米颗粒。
结果· 涂层增加表面粗糙度，抑制细菌粘附并促进成骨细胞增殖和粘附。
验证· 通过TEM表征和体外细胞实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To design a biomimetic coating with antibacterial and osteogenic functions to improve the success rate of hard tissue implants.
Method· A silk fibroin-based coating embedded with nanohydroxyapatite and silver nanoparticles was fabricated.
Result· The coating increased surface roughness, inhibited bacterial adhesion, and promoted osteoblast adhesion, spreading, and proliferation.
Validation· The coating was validated by TEM characterization and in vitro cell experiments with MC3T3-E1 osteoblasts.
