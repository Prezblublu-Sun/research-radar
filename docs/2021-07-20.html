Compression properties and optimization design of SLM Ti6Al4V square pore tissue engineering scaffolds

Xiaoquan Shi, Yazhou Sun, Pengju Wang, Ziyang Ma, Haitao Liu et al. Proceedings of the Institution of Mechanical Engineers Part H Journal of Engineering in Medicine 2021-07-20 10.1177/09544119211028061
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering and Automation, Harbin Institute of Technology, Harbin, Heilongjiang, China
通讯: Xiaoquan Shi @ Department of Mechanical Engineering and Automation, Harbin Institute of Technology, Harbin, Heilongjiang, China
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM Ti6Al4V多孔支架的压缩性能，但未涉及疲劳性能、表面形貌或应力遮挡与骨整合的直接关联。
边界: The paper does not link porous structure to stress shielding or stiffness matching, nor does it report fatigue, surface roughness, or osseointegration.
动机· 通过多孔支架解决骨缺损问题，优化孔结构以提升力学性能。
方法· 使用SLM制备方形孔支架，基于相对密度映射优化为拱形结构。
结果· 优化后支架在相同孔隙率下承载能力提高20%-30%。
验证· 通过压缩实验验证，Gibson-Ashby模型预测误差低于8%。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To address bone defects by designing porous scaffolds with improved mechanical performance through structural optimization.
Method· Selective laser melting (SLM) was used to fabricate Ti6Al4V square-pore scaffolds, and a relative density mapping (RDM) method was applied to optimize the pore architecture into arch shapes.
Result· The optimized arch structures increased the load-bearing capacity by 20%-30% at the same porosity compared to the original square pores.
Validation· Compressive strength tests were conducted on SLM-fabricated scaffolds, and the results were compared with the Gibson-Ashby model, showing less than 8% error for porosities between 40% and 60%.

单位: Faculty of Sustainable Design Engineering, University of Prince Edward Island, Charlottetown, PE, Canada
通讯: Ali Ahmadi @ Faculty of Sustainable Design Engineering, University of Prince Edward Island, Charlottetown, PE, Canada

相关性: 该论文研究基于偏好的学徒学习与离线强化学习，与FEA替代模型或生物力学设计完全无关。
边界: This paper does not address expensive simulation sampling, constraints handling, uncertainty quantification, or any aspect of FEA surrogate modelling or biomechanics design.
动机· 从人类偏好学习奖励函数通常需要仿真器或实际交互，本文利用离线数据降低这一需求。

English summary & terms
Motivation· Learning reward functions from human preferences typically requires a simulator or costly physical rollouts; this work leverages offline data from related tasks to reduce that requirement.
Method· The approach uses pool-based active learning to craft preference queries, learns a distribution over reward functions, and optimizes a policy via offline RL without real-world interactions or an accurate simulator.
Result· Empirical results show that combining offline RL with learned human preferences enables agents to perform novel tasks not explicitly demonstrated in the offline data.
Validation· The method is validated on a subset of existing offline RL benchmarks and newly proposed offline apprenticeship learning benchmarks.
