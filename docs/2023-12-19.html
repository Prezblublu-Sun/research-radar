边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, constraint handling, or implant/biomechanics design; it focuses on learning nonlinear dynamics from time series data.

Motivation· To evaluate the long-term survival and complication rates of different cementless femoral stem types in primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A registry-based study analyzed 53,626 implants categorized by the Mont classification system.
Result· Survival and complication rates varied significantly across different stem types.
Validation· Large-scale registry data with multivariable regression.
