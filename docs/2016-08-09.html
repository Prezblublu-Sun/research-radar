单位: College of Science, Swansea University, Singleton Campus, Swansea SA2 8PP, UK
通讯: Cinzia Giannetti @ College of Science, Swansea University, Singleton Campus, Swansea SA2 8PP, UK
相关性: 该论文关注制造业公差合成中的不确定性量化，与生物力学有限元代理建模无直接关联。
边界: No FEA surrogate, no implant/biomechanics context, no constraint handling or multi-objective optimization.
动机· 提高制造业过程鲁棒性，减少意外结果和浪费。
方法· 提出基于分位数回归树和似然比检验的不确定性量化算法。

Motivation· To improve manufacturing process robustness by quantifying uncertainty in tolerance synthesis under Industry 4.0.

Result· Enables visualization of optimal process ranges and probabilistic robustness prediction for quality improvement.
Validation· No experimental validation; purely mathematical formulation and algorithmic proposal.

Wear Analysis of Second-generation Highly Cross-Linked Polyethylene in Primary Total Hip Arthroplasty

Low Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Background only clinical Exp. validation
Christopher Samujh, Samrath J. Bhimani, Langan S. Smith, Arthur L. Malkani Orthopedics 2016-08-09 10.3928/01477447-20160808-02
相关性: 该论文关注髋关节置换中聚乙烯内衬的磨损率，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡或微动分析。
边界: This paper focuses on polyethylene wear rates from clinical radiographs, with no biomechanical modeling, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no FEA, and no implant design or bone-implant interface analysis.
动机· 评估第二代高交联聚乙烯内衬在髋关节置换中的磨损性能。
方法· 回顾性分析60例患者术后5.5年X线片，使用软件测量线性与体积磨损率。
结果· 平均线性磨损率0.025 mm/y，较第一代高交联聚乙烯降低2.7倍。
验证· 临床随访与影像学测量验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the wear performance of second-generation highly cross-linked polyethylene liners in primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A single-center retrospective review of 60 patients with mean 5.5-year follow-up, using digitized radiographs and wear analysis software to measure linear and volumetric wear rates.
Result· The mean linear wear rate was 0.025 mm/y, which is 2.7 times lower than first-generation highly cross-linked polyethylene.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with radiographic wear measurement.

Shear stress and von Mises stress distributions in the periphery of an embedded acetabular cup implant during impingement

Low Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Christoph M. Arndt, Alexandra Görgner, Carsten Klöhn, Roger Scholz, Christian Voigt Biomedizinische Technik/Biomedical Engineering 2016-08-09 10.1515/bmt-2016-0107
单位: Leipzig University, Faculty of Medicine , Department of Orthopaedic, Trauma and Plastic Surgery, Laboratory for Biomechanics , Liebigstraße 20 , 04103 Leipzig , Germany
通讯: Christian Voigt @ Leipzig University, Faculty of Medicine , Department of Orthopaedic, Trauma and Plastic Surgery, Laboratory for Biomecha
相关性: 该论文研究髋臼杯假体撞击时的应力分布，而非股骨柄假体的应力遮挡或微动，与用户研究方向间接相关。
边界: This paper focuses on acetabular cup impingement and periprosthetic bone stress, not on femoral stem stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics in the proximal femur.
动机· 评估全髋置换中撞击事件对髋臼假体周围骨应力的影响
方法· 通过三维应变测量实验，计算撞击载荷下髋臼杯周围1620个应变点的von Mises和剪切应力
结果· 最坏撞击情况下，界面2.5 mm处von Mises应力0.57 MPa，剪切应力-0.3 MPa，不足以导致骨损伤
验证· 实验应变测量与文献剪切应力准则对比验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine whether impingement events during total hip arthroplasty pose a risk of bone failure at the acetabular implant-bone interface.
Method· Three-dimensional strain gage measurements were performed on a hemispherical cup implant under worst-case impingement loading, and von Mises and shear stresses were calculated from 1620 strain values at 2.5 mm interface distance.
Result· Peak von Mises stress of 0.57 MPa and tilting shear stress of -0.3 MPa were observed, indicating that impingement is unlikely to cause periprosthetic bone failure in healthy patients.
Validation· Experimental strain measurements were compared against a shear stress criterion derived from literature.

单位: Department of Teaching and Learning, Florida International University, 11200 S.W. 8th Street, Miami, Florida 33199, USA
通讯: Remy Dou @ Department of Teaching and Learning, Florida International University, 11200 S.W. 8th Street, Miami, Florida 33199, USA
相关性: 该论文研究物理教育中的自我效能感，与有限元代理建模或生物力学设计无关。
边界: This paper does not address surrogate modeling, finite element analysis, uncertainty quantification, or any biomedical/mechanical design optimization.
动机· 研究物理课程中学生自我效能感下降的原因
方法· 通过社交网络中心度指标预测自我效能感变化
结果· PageRank中心度与掌握经验相关，入度与言语说服相关
验证· 使用自陈量表与社交网络数据回归分析
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the decrease in physics self-efficacy observed in active learning classrooms and explore social network factors.
Method· Bootstrapped linear regressions were used to predict post-self-efficacy scores from centrality measures (inDegree, outDegree, PageRank) while controlling for pre-test scores.
Result· PageRank centrality predicted overall self-efficacy and mastery experiences; inDegree and outDegree were associated with verbal persuasion and vicarious learning.
Validation· Empirical validation using survey data (SOSESC-P) and social network data from two semesters of an introductory physics course.
