英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the association between prosthesis component orientation and functional outcomes after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A cross-sectional study of 38 primary THR patients measuring acetabular abduction, anteversion, and femoral anteversion from radiographs and correlating with Harris Hip Score and range of motion.
Result· No significant differences in functional outcomes were found between safe and non-safe orientation zones, except for internal rotation.
Validation· Clinical patient data with statistical analysis.

Motivation· To improve surgical outcomes of total hip replacement in patients with dysplastic coxarthrosis by addressing acetabular bone defects.
