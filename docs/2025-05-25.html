相关性: 该论文提出了一种基于扩展fiducial推断的PINN不确定性量化方法，可应用于生物力学代理模型中的不确定性评估。
边界: The paper does not apply the method to any FEA surrogate or biomechanics design problem, lacking validation on implant-related simulations.
动机· 现有PINN不确定性量化方法依赖先验或dropout率，无法构建诚实置信集。

Result· The approach enables honest confidence sets solely from observed data, overcoming limitations of Bayesian and dropout methods.
Validation· Theoretical derivation without numerical benchmarks or experimental validation.
