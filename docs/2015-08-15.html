Responsive Polypeptide-DNA Hydrogel Crosslinked by G-quadruplex

Low AI for Bioprinting openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Yu Shao, Chuang Li, Zhou Xu, Ping Chen, Zhongqiang Yang et al. 2015-08-15 link
相关性: 该论文研究了一种响应性多肽-DNA水凝胶，但未涉及任何AI或机器学习方法，与AI生物打印方向无关。
边界: This paper does not incorporate AI/ML into any bioprinting workflow stage, lacks experimental validation of bioprinting, and does not advance toward autonomous decision-making.
动机· 开发一种具有响应性的DNA基水凝胶用于生物打印。
方法· 通过点击化学合成多肽-DNA，并引入G-四链体结构形成水凝胶。
结果· 水凝胶具有热和离子响应性，机械强度可达2.4 kPa。
验证· 通过流变学、CD光谱和PAGE验证凝胶形成和响应性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a responsive DNA-based supramolecular hydrogel with potential for tissue engineering and 3D bioprinting.
Method· A hydrogel was prepared by mixing polypeptide-DNA conjugates with G-quadruplex structures via copper-catalyzed click chemistry.
Result· The hydrogel exhibited reversible thermal and ionic responsiveness with a storage modulus up to 2.4 kPa.
Validation· Gel formation and responsiveness were validated using rheology, circular dichroism, and polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis.
