Motivation· To evaluate the efficacy of platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) alone for guided bone regeneration in maxillary sinus augmentation with immediate implant placement.
Method· An interventional pre-post study on 30 dental patients with initial bone height 4-6 mm, using PRF in sinus lift and immediate implant placement, with CBCT and clinical follow-up.
Result· PRF alone achieved significant bone augmentation, with implant stability quotient >70 at 6 months and no complications.
Validation· Clinical pre-post study with CBCT imaging and statistical analysis (Chi-squared and paired t-test).
