Analysis of strain and stress concentrations in micro-lattice structures manufactured by SLM

Laura Boniotti, S. Foletti, S. Beretta, L. Patriarca Rapid Prototyping Journal 2019-10-21 10.1108/rpj-10-2018-0270
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Politecnico di Milano, Milano, Italy
通讯: Laura Boniotti @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Politecnico di Milano, Milano, Italy
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM制造微晶格结构中的应力集中，对于理解增材制造多孔植入物的力学行为具有参考价值，但未直接涉及生物医学应用。
边界: Does not address biomedical implant context, no link to stress shielding or stiffness matching, no fatigue testing, and no osseointegration or surface roughness for bone ingrowth.
动机· 研究SLM制造微晶格结构中的几何缺陷引起的应力集中。
方法· 结合三维断层扫描、实验测试、数字图像相关和有限元建模，比较设计几何和制造几何。
结果· 材料的力学性能与应力集中系数的统计分布直接相关。
验证· 通过实验与有限元对比验证应力集中因子。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates the effects of geometrical irregularities on stress/strain concentrations in micro-lattice structures fabricated by selective laser melting (SLM).
Method· The authors combined 3D tomography, experimental testing, digital image correlation (DIC), and finite element (FE) models based on both as-designed and as-manufactured geometries to evaluate local deformations and stress concentration factors.
Result· The mechanical properties of the printed samples are directly related to the statistical distribution of the stress concentration factor, which can serve as a quality measure.
Validation· Validation was performed by comparing experimental DIC measurements with FE predictions, using a stress concentration factor as the comparative metric.
