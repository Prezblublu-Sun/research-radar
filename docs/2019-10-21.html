相关性: 该论文直接评估了股骨柄假体的初始稳定性，并考虑了手术变异性和患者队列，与用户研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not report stress shielding or bone remodeling, and does not compare intact vs implanted femur for strain energy density distribution.

Motivation· To develop a computationally efficient method for assessing how surgical variability affects the primary stability of a contemporary femoral stem across a patient cohort.

Result· Surgical variability significantly influenced micromotion patterns, though some regions showed robustness to variations.
Validation· The finite element model was validated by comparing predicted micromotion with experimental data from the literature.
