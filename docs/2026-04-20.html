边界: This paper is a bibliometric analysis of stemless shoulder arthroplasty, with no biomechanical modeling, no FEA, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, and no hip implant context.
动机· 分析无柄肩关节置换术的研究趋势和热点。
方法· 使用Scopus数据库进行文献计量分析，提取元数据并生成共著网络。
结果· 共366篇文章，年均增长14.5%，美国贡献最多。
验证· 无实验验证，为文献计量分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To analyze global trends and emerging themes in stemless shoulder arthroplasty research over 17 years.
Method· A bibliometric analysis was conducted using Scopus database, extracting metadata and generating co-authorship networks.
Result· 366 articles were identified with 5767 citations and 14.5% annual growth; the US led in publications.
Validation· No experimental validation; the study is a bibliometric review.
