Motivation· To evaluate the efficacy of intra-operative periarticular injections for pain management in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A quasi-experimental study with randomization into injection and control groups, analyzing outcomes with SPSS.

Motivation· To compare bone fixation of conical versus hemispherical trapezial cup designs in a validated animal model.
