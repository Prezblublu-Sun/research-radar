Quantitating the effect of prosthesis design on femoral remodeling using high‐resolution region‐free densitometric analysis (<scp>DXA‐RFA</scp>)

Medium Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Direct Save for project clinical Exp. validation Patient-specific
Mohsen Farzi, R. M. Morris, Jeannette Østergaard Penny, Lang Yang, José M. Pozo et al. Journal of Orthopaedic Research® 2017-02-07 10.1002/jor.23536
单位: Department of Electronic and Electrical Engineering Centre for Computational Imaging &amp; Simulation Technologies in Biomedicine (CISTIB) University of Sheffield Sheffield United Kingdom
通讯: J. Mark Wilkinson @ University of Sheffield, Academic Unit of Bone Metabolism Northern General Hospital Sheffield United Kingdom
相关性: 该论文使用高分辨率DXA-RFA技术量化不同假体设计对股骨骨重塑的影响，直接关联髋关节假体应力遮挡评估。
边界: This paper uses clinical DXA data rather than finite element analysis, and does not report micromotion or contact mechanics.
动机· 传统DXA难以分辨不同假体设计导致的细微骨重塑差异。
方法· 应用高分辨率DXA-RFA技术分析两种临床试验中的假体周围骨密度变化。
结果· DXA-RFA揭示了不同假体设计在骨丢失区域和幅度上的细微差异。
验证· 基于两项随机临床试验的DXA扫描数据验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Conventional DXA analysis lacks resolution to detect subtle, spatially complex bone remodeling differences due to prosthesis design.
Method· DXA region-free analysis (DXA-RFA) was applied to periprosthetic BMD scans from two previous randomized clinical trials comparing different prosthesis designs.
Result· DXA-RFA resolved previously undetected differences in magnitude and area of bone loss and gain among cemented, cementless, and resurfacing prostheses.
Validation· Validated using clinical DXA data from two randomized trials with statistical significance (p < 0.001).

相关性: 该论文涉及激光烧结和晶格结构设计，但应用于摩托车车架，而非生物医学植入物。
边界: No biomedical context, no stress shielding or osseointegration, no fatigue testing, no surface roughness or as-built morphology for implants.
动机· 开发结合拓扑优化与晶格梁设计的直接激光烧结摩托车车架新方法。

Motivation· To develop a novel design methodology for direct-laser-sintered motorcycle frames by combining topology optimization and lattice-beam implementation.

Validation· Designs were validated through finite element static and modal analyses under nominated loading conditions.

单位: Department of Movement Science, School of Kinesiology University of Michigan Ann Arbor Michigan
通讯: M. Melissa Gross @ Department of Movement Science, School of Kinesiology University of Michigan Ann Arbor Michigan
相关性: 该论文研究解剖学教学中图像与文本主动学习的效果，与有限元代理建模、植入物设计或生物力学优化无关。
边界: This paper is about educational pedagogy in anatomy, not about surrogate modeling, FEA, or any computational method for implant design.
动机· 探究图像与文本主动学习对解剖学考试成绩的影响
方法· 在功能解剖课程中穿插图像和文本练习，分析学生参与度与成绩相关性
结果· 图像练习参与度与考试成绩显著正相关，文本练习则无显著关联
验证· 基于231名学生成绩与问卷调查的统计分析
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate whether image-based active learning exercises improve anatomy examination performance more than text-based exercises.
Method· In a functional anatomy course, 12 image-based and text-based active learning exercises per session were administered; participation data and exam scores from 231 students were analyzed using correlation and regression.
Result· Participation in image-based exercises was significantly correlated with exam performance on both image- and text-based questions, while text-based exercise participation showed no significant correlation.
Validation· Statistical analysis of student exam scores and survey responses, controlling for demographics and prior GPA.

Improving labeling efficiency in automatic quality control of MRSI data

Exclude FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Ignore none
Nuno Pedrosa de Barros, Richard McKinley, Roland Wiest, Johannes Slotboom Magnetic Resonance in Medicine 2017-02-07 10.1002/mrm.26618
单位: Support Center for Advanced Neuroimaging Inselspital Bern Switzerland
通讯: Nuno Pedrosa de Barros @ Support Center for Advanced Neuroimaging Inselspital Bern Switzerland
相关性: 该论文专注于MRSI数据质量控制的主动学习，与FEA替代建模或生物力学设计无关。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, expensive simulation sampling, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification, or implant/biomechanics design.
动机· 提高MRSI数据自动质量控制中标注任务的效率。
方法· 使用不确定性采样的主动学习策略，减少需要标注的数据量。
结果· 短TE和长TE分别减少72.97%和62.09%的标注量，分类精度无显著影响。
验证· 基于28,432个MRSI谱图数据，由专家标注并模拟测试。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To improve the efficiency of manual labeling in automatic quality control of MR spectroscopy imaging data.
Method· An active learning approach using uncertainty sampling was applied to select the most informative spectra for labeling.
Result· The proposed strategy reduced labeling requirements by up to 72.97% for short TE and 62.09% for long TE without significant loss in classification accuracy.
Validation· Validation was performed on 28,432 spectra from 18 brain tumor patients, with expert labels and simulation experiments.

Daniel Harte, Philip Hamill, Caroline Williams-Condell, Stephanie J. Lewis British Journal of Occupational Therapy 2017-02-07 10.1177/0308022616685583
单位: Advanced Occupational Therapist, Southern Health and Social Care Trust, Portadown, UK
通讯: Daniel Harte @ Advanced Occupational Therapist, Southern Health and Social Care Trust, Portadown, UK
相关性: 该论文不涉及髋关节植入物的生物力学分析或有限元建模，与研究方向无关。
边界: Does not quantify implant stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics; no FEA or patient-specific modeling.
动机· 调查术前评估对全髋关节置换术后住院时间的影响。
方法· 回顾性分析101名患者的临床数据，使用统计建模分析变量与住院时间的关系。
结果· 术前评估未显著缩短住院时间，但医疗状况、独居和护理需求显著增加住院时间。
验证· 基于单中心回顾性数据，缺乏多中心验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Investigate whether preoperative assessment by allied health professionals reduces length of stay after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Retrospective data review of 101 primary THA patients with statistical modeling of variables associated with length of stay.
Result· Preoperative assessment did not significantly reduce length of stay, while medical unfitness, living alone, and care package needs significantly increased it.
Validation· Single-center retrospective design without prospective or multi-site validation.
