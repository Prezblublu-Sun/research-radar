通讯: M.A. Leeflang @ Department of Biomechanical Engineering, Delft University of Technology, Mekelweg2, Delft 2628CD, The Netherlands
相关性: 该论文涉及LPBF制造和力学测试，但未关注应力遮挡或疲劳性能，且结构设计为可展开而非多孔支架。

相关性: 该论文关注微分代数方程的不确定性量化，但未涉及生物力学或植入物设计，方法转移性有限。
边界: No biomedical context, no implant mechanics, no FEA surrogate for structural design; focuses on DAE benchmark circuits and pendulum.

Validation· Validation on benchmark RLC circuit and nonlinear pendulum using Monte Carlo simulations.

Result· The approach successfully identifies and corrects key uncertain components, demonstrated on quantum-scale chemical kinetics and fuel cell materials screening.
Validation· The method is validated on two physico-chemical numerical examples, showing improved model reliability.
