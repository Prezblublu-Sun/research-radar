Elastic Modulus of ECM Hydrogels Derived from Decellularized Tissue Affects Capillary Network Formation in Endothelial Cells

Mako Kobayashi, Junpei Kadota, Yoshihide Hashimoto, Toshiya Fujisato, Naoko Nakamura et al. International Journal of Molecular Sciences 2020-08-31 10.3390/ijms21176304
单位: Institute of Biomaterials and Bioengineering, Tokyo Medical and Dental University, Tokyo 101-0062, Japan
通讯: Tsuyoshi Kimura @ Institute of Biomaterials and Bioengineering, Tokyo Medical and Dental University, Tokyo 101-0062, Japan
相关性: 该论文研究了脱细胞ECM水凝胶的弹性模量对内皮细胞毛细血管网络形成的影响，但未涉及AI或机器学习方法。
边界: This paper does not incorporate any AI/ML into bioprinting workflow; it is purely a biological characterization study.
动机· 评估脱细胞ECM水凝胶中毛细血管网络的形成。
方法· 使用脱细胞小肠黏膜下层和膀胱基质制备水凝胶，通过压缩测试测量弹性模量，并接种内皮细胞观察网络形成。
结果· 低刚度水凝胶促进更长的分支毛细血管网络结构。
验证· 通过细胞培养实验观察网络结构形成。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate capillary network formation in decellularized extracellular matrix hydrogels for tissue regeneration applications.
Method· Decellularized ECM hydrogels from small intestine submucosa and urinary bladder matrix were prepared using sodium deoxycholate and high hydrostatic pressure, and their elastic moduli were measured by compression testing.
Result· Capillary network formation was enhanced on hydrogels with lower matrix stiffness, with long branched networks observed on HHP-derived hydrogels.
Validation· In vitro cell seeding experiments with microvascular-derived endothelial cells were used to assess network formation.

3D Printing of a Reactive Hydrogel Bio-Ink Using a Static Mixing Tool.

María Puertas-Bartolomé, Małgorzata K Włodarczyk-Biegun, Aránzazu Del Campo, Blanca Vázquez-Lasa, Julio San Román Polymers 2020-08-31 10.3390/polym12091986
单位: Institute of Polymer Science and Technology, ICTP-CSIC, Juan de la Cierva 3, 28006 Madrid, Spain.; CIBER's Bioengineering, Biomaterials and Nanomedicine, CIBER-BBN, Health Institute Carlos III, Monfor
通讯: Julio San Román @ Institute of Polymer Science and Technology, ICTP-CSIC, Juan de la Cierva 3, 28006 Madrid, Spain.; CIBER's Bioengineerin [推断]
相关性: 该论文不涉及人工智能技术，因此与AI生物打印方向无关。
边界: This paper does not incorporate any AI/ML method, nor does it address autonomous decision-making or digital twins in bioprinting.
动机· 解决水凝胶生物墨水机械性能差和打印形状保真度低的问题
方法· 使用双注射器静态混合工具实现含细胞水凝胶的原位交联打印
结果· 成功打印多层3D支架，具有良好生物相容性和机械稳定性
验证· 通过细胞培养和机械测试验证支架性能
English summary & terms
Motivation· Hydrogel bio-inks suffer from weak mechanical properties leading to poor printability, and current high-viscosity approaches compromise cell viability.
Method· A dual-syringe system with a static mixing tool enables in situ covalent crosslinking of low-viscosity carboxymethyl chitosan and oxidized hyaluronic acid during extrusion.
Result· Multi-layered 3D scaffolds with moderate swelling, good biocompatibility, and mechanical stability over 14 days of culture were fabricated.
Validation· In vitro cell culture and mechanical testing confirmed biocompatibility and structural integrity.

Pores and the formation mechanisms of SLMed AlSi10Mg

Tingting Wang, Shimin Dai, Hailong Liao, Haihong Zhu Rapid Prototyping Journal 2020-08-31 10.1108/rpj-02-2020-0036
单位: Wuhan National Laboratory for Optoelectronics, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan, China
通讯: Tingting Wang @ Wuhan National Laboratory for Optoelectronics, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan, China
相关性: 该论文研究SLM AlSi10Mg合金中的孔隙特征，与生物医学植入物设计、应力遮挡或骨整合无关。
边界: This paper does not cover biomedical implant design, stress shielding, fatigue, surface roughness for osseointegration, or any biological context.
动机· 系统研究SLM AlSi10Mg中孔隙特征及形成机制，以制备高性能零件。
方法· 通过不同工艺参数制造试样，利用OM和SEM观察孔隙形貌。
结果· 发现圆形和 irregular 孔隙，分别由元素蒸发、H2O、高激光能量和空心粉末等引起。
验证· 利用光学显微镜和扫描电镜进行显微观察，但无定量力学验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To fabricate high-performance parts, this paper systematically investigates pore characteristics and formation mechanisms in SLMed AlSi10Mg.
Method· Cubes were manufactured under different laser power, scan speed, and scan space; OM and SEM were used to observe pore morphology.
Result· Round pores with smooth inner walls were found within melt pools due to elemental evaporation and hollow powder; irregular pores with rough inner walls resulted from uneven surfaces and keyhole instability.
Validation· Validation was performed via optical and electron microscopy observation, without quantitative mechanical testing.

Validation· Meta-analysis of four rabbit studies with DerSimonian-Laird estimator.

Motivation· To investigate whether hip and knee arthroplasty improve health-related quality of life compared to the general population.
Method· Prospective collection of 15D scores pre- and post-operatively from patients undergoing total hip or knee arthroplasty.

Validation· Clinical follow-up at 6 and 12 months with comparison to Finnish control population.
