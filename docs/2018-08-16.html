Two-Level Surrogate-Assisted Differential Evolution Multi-Objective Optimization of Electric Machines Using 3-D FEA

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project simulation_case
Narges Taran, Dan M. Ionel, David G. Dorrell IEEE Transactions on Magnetics 2018-08-16 10.1109/tmag.2018.2856858
单位: SPARK Laboratory, University of Kentucky, Lexington, KY, USA
通讯: Narges Taran @ SPARK Laboratory, University of Kentucky, Lexington, KY, USA
相关性: 该论文提出了一种两级代理辅助优化算法，可显著减少昂贵3D有限元分析的调用次数，其方法框架可迁移至生物力学植入物设计中的多目标优化问题。
边界: This paper focuses on electric machine design, not biomedical implant or biomechanics FEA, and lacks uncertainty quantification and constraint handling for implant-specific requirements.
动机· 减少三维有限元分析在多目标优化中的计算成本
方法· 提出内外两级代理辅助优化框架，内层用克里金模型快速探索，外层用3D FEA精确评估
结果· 相比传统方法，大幅减少FEA调用次数，同时找到高质量帕累托前沿
验证· 通过电机设计案例验证算法效率与精度
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce the computational burden of 3-D finite-element analysis in multi-objective optimization of electric machines.
Method· A two-level surrogate-assisted optimization framework is proposed, where an interior level uses cheap kriging models for exploration and an exterior level evaluates promising designs with expensive 3-D FEA.
Result· The algorithm achieves high-quality Pareto fronts with significantly fewer FEA evaluations compared to conventional methods.
Validation· Validation is performed on an electric machine design case study, demonstrating efficiency and accuracy.

通讯: A. Prakash @ Department of Civil Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati 1 , M-Block, R. No. 307, Guwahati, Assam 781039
相关性: 该论文使用copula方法量化土工材料的水分保持曲线的不确定性，与生物力学植入物设计中的代理建模和不确定性量化无直接关联。
边界: This paper applies copula-based uncertainty quantification to geotechnical water retention curves, not to FEA surrogate modeling, implant biomechanics, or any biomedical context.
动机· 量化粉煤灰水分保持曲线中由测量方法和材料变异性引起的不确定性。
方法· 使用四种copula函数构建van Genuchten参数的双变量密度函数，并选择最佳拟合。
结果· 高斯copula结合对数正态边缘分布能充分捕捉WRCC的不确定性范围。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify uncertainties in the water retention characteristic curve of fly ash arising from measurement limitations and material variability.
Method· Four copula functions (Gaussian, Frank, Plackett, No. 16) were used to construct bivariate density functions of van Genuchten parameters, with the best fit selected via statistical analysis.
Result· The Gaussian copula with lognormal marginals adequately captures the significant uncertainty range in the water retention curve.
Validation· Validation was performed by statistical analysis of 54 data combinations and goodness-of-fit comparison among copulas.
