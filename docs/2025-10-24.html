English summary & terms
Motivation· To describe the benefits of a rehabilitation nursing plan for safe transition after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A descriptive case report using Barthel index, Functional Independence Measure, and other assessment instruments.

Surgical approaches in total hip arthroplasty do not influence the bacterial spectrum of acute postoperative periprosthetic joint infections

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate whether surgical approach influences bacterial spectrum in acute postoperative periprosthetic joint infections after THA.
Method· Retrospective cohort study comparing infection cultures between posterolateral and direct anterior approaches.
Result· No significant difference in bacterial spectrum was found between the two surgical approaches.
Validation· Retrospective clinical data analysis.
