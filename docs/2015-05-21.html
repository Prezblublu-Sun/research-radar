Quantification of airfoil geometry-induced aerodynamic uncertainties - comparison of approaches

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Dishi Liu, Alexander Litvinenko, Claudia Schillings, Volker Schulz arXiv preprint 2015-05-21 link
相关性: 虽然面向气动领域，但其不确定性量化和代理模型方法可迁移至生物力学植入物设计。
边界: No biomechanical or implant design context, no FEA surrogate specifically for biomedical applications.
动机· 比较五种方法在气动几何不确定性量化中的效率。
方法· 对比准蒙特卡罗、多项式混沌、梯度增强Kriging、径向基函数和点配置多项式混沌。
结果· 梯度增强代理方法在相同计算成本下精度更优。
验证· 基于9维高斯随机变量的翼型气动性能数值实验。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Efficient uncertainty quantification in aerodynamic simulations is challenging due to high computational cost, especially for geometry-induced uncertainties with many variables.
Method· Five methods including quasi-Monte Carlo, polynomial chaos with sparse quadrature, gradient-enhanced Kriging, radial basis functions, and point collocation polynomial chaos are compared for estimating aerodynamic statistics under random airfoil geometry perturbations.
Result· Gradient-enhanced surrogate methods achieve better accuracy than direct integration methods at the same computational cost.
Validation· Numerical benchmark on an airfoil parameterized by 9 independent Gaussian variables.

uncertainty quantification surrogate modelling polynomial chaos Kriging radial basis functions aerodynamic airfoil

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore clinical
Vipin Asopa, William Cobain, David K. Martin, Greg Keene, Adrian Bauze ANZ Journal of Surgery 2015-05-21 10.1111/ans.13174
单位: Orthopaedic Surgery Sportsmed.SA Adelaide South Australia Australia
通讯: Vipin Asopa @ Orthopaedic Surgery Sportsmed.SA Adelaide South Australia Australia
相关性: 该论文讨论的是关节置换术后静脉血栓栓塞的预防方案，与髋关节植入物的生物力学分析无关。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain, micromotion, or any biomechanical modeling; it is purely a clinical study on thromboprophylaxis.
动机· 评估分阶段抗凝方案在关节置换术后预防静脉血栓的效果
方法· 回顾性分析9035例髋膝关节置换术患者，采用住院期间低分子肝素+出院后阿司匹林方案
结果· 症状性静脉血栓发生率2.55%，致命性肺栓塞率0.03%
验证· 基于11年临床数据回顾性分析
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the safety and efficacy of a staged chemoprophylaxis protocol using low-molecular-weight heparin followed by aspirin for venous thromboembolism prevention after arthroplasty.
Method· Retrospective analysis of 9035 hip and knee arthroplasty patients receiving LMWH in hospital and aspirin for 6 weeks post-discharge.
Result· The staged protocol resulted in a symptomatic VTE rate of 2.55%, a PE rate of 1.28%, and a fatal PE rate of 0.03%.
Validation· Clinical outcomes from an 11-year retrospective database with 6-week follow-up.
