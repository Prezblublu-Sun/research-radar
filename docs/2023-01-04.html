通讯: Hesham Abdelbary @ Department of Surgery, Faculty of Medicine, University of Ottawa, Ottawa, Ontario, Canada.; The Ottawa Hospital, Ottawa, [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究的是假体周围感染模型，而非髋关节假体的应力遮挡或微动分析。

Validation· In vivo imaging, MRI, viable colony counts, and microCT were used to assess infection and osseointegration.
