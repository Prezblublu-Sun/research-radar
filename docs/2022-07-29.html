相关性: 该论文研究全髋关节置换术后疼痛管理，未涉及任何生物力学建模或植入物设计。
边界: This paper is a clinical study on pain management after THA, with no biomechanical modeling, implant design, or FEA analysis.
动机· 评估FICB联合帕瑞昔布对THA术后疼痛控制的效果
方法· 回顾性研究，72例THA患者，治疗组加用FICB，对照组仅用帕瑞昔布
结果· 治疗组在静息和运动VAS评分及炎症因子方面优于对照组
验证· 临床回顾性数据统计分析
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the efficacy of fascia iliaca compartment block as an adjunct to parecoxib for pain management after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective study of 72 THA patients, with the treatment group receiving additional FICB and the control group receiving parecoxib alone.
Result· The treatment group showed significantly lower VAS scores and inflammatory markers at multiple time points postoperatively.
Validation· Clinical retrospective data analysis with statistical comparison.
