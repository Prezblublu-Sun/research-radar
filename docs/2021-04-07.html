Influence of local heat flow variations on geometrical deflections, microstructure, and tensile properties of Ti-6Al-4 V products in powder bed fusion systems

Igor Drstvenšek, Franc Zupanič, Tonica Bončina, Tomaž Brajlih, Snehashis Pal Journal of Manufacturing Processes 2021-04-07 10.1016/j.jmapro.2021.03.054
单位: Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, University of Maribor, Maribor, Slovenia
通讯: Snehashis Pal @ Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, University of Maribor, Maribor, Slovenia
相关性: 该论文研究LPBF工艺参数对Ti-6Al-4V零件几何变形、微观结构和拉伸性能的影响，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能，与生物医学植入物的直接应用关联较弱。
边界: This paper does not investigate porous structure, stress shielding, fatigue behavior, or osseointegration, which are central to the user's direction on biomedical implants.
动机· 研究局部热流变化对SLM和EBM制造Ti-6Al-4V零件的几何、微观结构和拉伸性能的影响。
方法· 通过3D扫描比较颅骨植入物与CAD模型，并分析不同位置样品的微观结构和拉伸性能。
结果· SLM导致强烈变形，EBM影响尺寸但形状不变；冷却速率差异引起α相形态变化。
验证· 实验验证：3D扫描几何、SEM微观结构、拉伸测试。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how local variations in heat flow during SLM and EBM affect the geometric accuracy, microstructure, and tensile properties of Ti-6Al-4V components.
Method· 3D scanning was used to compare cranial implants with their CAD models, and microstructural analysis and tensile testing were performed on samples from different locations.
Result· SLM samples exhibited severe deformation, while EBM samples showed size deviations without shape change; cooling rate variations led to distinct α colony morphologies and orientations.
Validation· Experimental validation included 3D geometric scanning, scanning electron microscopy for microstructure, and uniaxial tensile testing.

Ensemble Inference Methods for Models With Noisy and Expensive Likelihoods

相关性: 该论文的方法（集成卡尔曼、集成高斯过程采样器）可转移至生物力学替代建模中的不确定性量化，但未涉及FEA或植入物设计。
边界: No FEA validation, no biomechanics context, no constraint handling, and no implant design application.
动机· 解决昂贵且噪声模型中的参数校准问题。

English summary & terms

Result· Ensemble Gaussian process samplers outperform both ensemble Kalman and Langevin methods on a climate model example.
Validation· Empirical demonstration on a climate science forward model with rapid parameter-space fluctuations.
