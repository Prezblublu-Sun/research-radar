通讯: Julia Granacher @ Industrial Process and Energy Systems Engineering, École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne, Sion, Switzerland
相关性: 该论文提出的主动学习代理模型方法可用于解决生物力学植入物设计中的昂贵仿真采样效率问题，但缺乏不确定性量化在约束处理中的具体应用。
边界: This paper focuses on chemical process superstructure optimization, not implant biomechanics or FEA-based design, and does not address multi-constraint handling or patient-specific geometry.
动机· 解决复杂过程仿真优化中计算成本高的问题
方法· 提出主动学习策略，基于预测不确定性选择新数据点，持续更新代理模型
结果· 代理模型测试均方误差低于2%，优化中60%以上情况选择简单代理模型
验证· 在三个不同规模和复杂度的仿真模型上测试
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce computational burden in simulation-based optimization of complex process superstructures.
Method· An active learning algorithm that enriches surrogate model training data by selecting points with highest prediction uncertainty, and only uses surrogates when prediction quality meets a threshold.
Result· Surrogate models achieved test mean squared errors below 2%, and simpler surrogates were favored in over 60% of optimization cases, with significant time savings for complex simulations.
Validation· Tested on three process simulation models of varying complexity, comparing active learning against random sampling.
