Improving registration accuracy during total hip arthroplasty: a cadaver study of a new, 3-D mini-optical navigation system

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore cadaveric Exp. validation
Michael B. Cross, Ran Schwarzkopf, Theodore T. Miller, Eric Bogner, Jeffrey M. Muir et al. Hip International 2017-02-09 10.5301/hipint.5000533
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Hospital for Special Surgery, New York - USA
通讯: Jonathan M. Vigdorchik @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, NYU Langone Medical Center-Hospital for Joint Diseases, New York - USA
相关性: 该论文研究髋关节置换术中导航系统的注册精度，不涉及股骨柄应力遮挡或微动分析。
边界: No quantification of proximal femur stress/strain, no micromotion modeling, no contact mechanics, no FEA, and no comparison of intact vs implanted femur.
动机· 评估新型微型导航系统在全髋置换术中的注册误差。
方法· 3名医生在6具尸体上使用导航系统进行THA，对比CT测量。
结果· 导航系统与CT测量的外展角和前倾角平均绝对差异分别为4.2°和4.0°。
验证· 尸体实验与术后CT影像对比验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the registration error of a novel mini-navigation system for total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Three surgeons performed THA on six cadavers using the navigation system; cup position was compared with postoperative CT measurements.
Result· Mean absolute differences between navigation and CT were 4.2° for inclination and 4.0° for anteversion.
Validation· Cadaveric study with postoperative CT imaging as reference.

3D Optical Investigation of 2 Nail Systems Used in Tibiotalocalcaneal Arthrodesis: A Biomechanical Study

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore cadaveric Exp. validation
Julia Evers, Martin Lakemeier, Dirk Wähnert, Martin Schulze, Martinus Richter et al. Foot & Ankle International 2017-02-09 10.1177/1071100717690805
单位: Department of Trauma-, Hand- and Reconstructive Surgery, University Hospital Münster, Münster, Germany
通讯: Julia Evers @ Department of Trauma-, Hand- and Reconstructive Surgery, University Hospital Münster, Münster, Germany
相关性: 该论文研究胫距跟关节融合术中的髓内钉系统，与髋关节植入物和股骨柄的应力遮挡、微动及骨-植入物界面无关。
边界: This paper focuses on tibiotalocalcaneal arthrodesis nails, not hip implants or femoral stems; no quantification of proximal femur stress shielding, micromotion at bone-implant interface, or comparison with intact femur.
动机· 比较两种融合钉系统在胫距跟关节融合术中的稳定性
方法· 在6对新鲜冷冻人体下肢标本中植入两种钉系统，进行准静态和循环加载测试，使用3D光学追踪测量运动
结果· HAN钉在旋转和跖屈/背屈中稳定性优于A3钉，尤其在距下关节
验证· 人体尸体标本实验验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the primary stability of two retrograde intramedullary nail systems for tibiotalocalcaneal arthrodesis using 3D optical motion analysis.
Method· Six pairs of fresh-frozen human cadaveric lower legs with osteoporotic bone were implanted with either HAN or A3 nails, and quasi-static and cyclic loading tests were performed with 3D motion tracking.
Result· The HAN nail exhibited significantly lower rotational and dorsi-/plantarflexion motion than the A3 nail, especially at the subtalar joint.
Validation· Cadaveric biomechanical testing with optical measurement.
