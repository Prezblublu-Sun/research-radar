Motivation· To evaluate the clinical efficacy of arthroscopic iliopsoas fractional lengthening for persistent groin pain after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A prospective multicenter study enrolled 56 patients and compared pre- and postoperative patient-reported outcomes and hip flexion strength.
Result· Significant improvements in pain and function were observed, with nearly 90% reporting pain relief and two-thirds reporting increased strength.
Validation· Clinical follow-up data only; no biomechanical or imaging-based validation was performed.
