相关性: 该论文探讨将LLM作为主动学习中的采集策略，虽面向材料优化，但其方法可迁移至生物力学或植入物设计中的昂贵仿真采样问题。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, implant biomechanics, or uncertainty quantification; it focuses on materials optimization with LLM-based acquisition policies.
动机· 降低昂贵材料评估中的采样成本，探索LLM作为采集策略的可行性。
方法· 在四个有限池材料优化任务中，对比五种LLM与GP方法及随机选择，并测试不同候选呈现策略。
结果· LLM策略通常优于随机选择，但与GP相比性能混合，且对任务和呈现方式敏感。
验证· 基于四个回顾性材料优化基准任务进行数值对比。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce the cost of evaluating materials candidates, this study explores whether open-weight LLMs can serve as standalone acquisition policies in active learning.
Method· Five LLMs were evaluated across four retrospective finite-pool materials optimization tasks under different candidate presentation strategies, and compared with random selection and Gaussian process baselines.
Result· LLM policies generally outperform random selection but show mixed performance relative to GP methods, with high sensitivity to task, model, initialization, and presentation.
Validation· The study uses retrospective numerical benchmarks on four materials optimization tasks, without experimental validation.
