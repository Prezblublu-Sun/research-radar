结果· 提问、示范主动学习策略、使用视频和沉默能促进主动学习。
验证· 基于162次咨询会话的实证数据分析。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To identify supervisory strategies that increase therapists' active learning engagement during training.
Method· Sequential analysis was applied to examine the probability of therapist active learning following specific consultant behaviors.
Result· Consultant questions, modeling of active learning, use of video, and silence were associated with higher likelihood of therapist active learning.
Validation· Empirical analysis of 162 consultation sessions from community therapists implementing a specific intervention.
