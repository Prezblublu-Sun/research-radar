相关性: 该论文研究AlSiMg合金的选区激光熔化工艺参数，完全不涉及生物医学植入物或骨整合。
边界: No biomedical implant context, no stress shielding or stiffness matching, no LPBF for biomedical materials, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), no surface roughness or osseointegration analysis.
动机· 研究扫描策略对选区激光熔化AlSiMg合金微观结构和性能的影响。
方法· 采用四种扫描策略制备AlSi8Mg试样，并用SEM、XRD等方法表征。
结果· 在能量密度200 J/mm³、90°层间旋转策略下获得99.97%致密度。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of scanning strategy on the microstructure, porosity, and density of an AlSiMg alloy fabricated by selective laser melting.
Method· Specimens from non-spherical AlSi8Mg powder were produced using four different scanning strategies and characterized by SEM, XRD, and density measurements.
Result· A relative density of 99.97% was achieved with an energy density of 200 J/mm³ and a 90° rotation scanning strategy per odd layer.
Validation· Validation was performed through SEM imaging, elemental mapping, and density comparison to silumin.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To assess the risk of second revision and mortality following first-time revision due to prosthetic joint infection after primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A registry-based study analyzing 1,669 patients from the Danish Hip Arthroplasty Register, including 416 treated with debridement, antibiotics, and implant retention.
Result· Patients with first-time revision due to PJI had an increased risk of second revision (RR 2.7) and second revision due to PJI (RR 6.3), but no statistically significant increase in mortality.
Validation· Clinical registry data with adjusted relative risks and confidence intervals.
