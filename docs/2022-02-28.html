English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the biocompatibility of silicon hydroxyapatite and its osteogenic potential when combined with stromal vascular fraction in a rabbit bone defect model.
Method· Si-HA granules were synthesized via wet precipitation and implanted with enzymatically/non-enzymatically derived SVF in rabbit tibiae.
Result· Si-HA was non-hemolytic and non-toxic, and enhanced bone formation with mature bone observed histologically, especially with non-enzymatically derived SVF.
Validation· Validation included in vitro hemolysis and Alamar Blue assays, and in vivo histomorphometric analysis after six weeks implantation.
