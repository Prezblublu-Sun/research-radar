方法· 通过等离子体电解氧化在增材制造的多孔Ti-6Al-4V植入物表面掺入银和铜纳米颗粒。
结果· 银铜协同作用实现强抗菌性并降低银的细胞毒性。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop self-defending bone implants with intrinsic antibacterial properties to prevent implant-associated infections.
Method· Additively manufactured porous Ti-6Al-4V implants were biofunctionalized via plasma electrolytic oxidation incorporating varying ratios of silver and copper nanoparticles into the TiO2 surface layer.
Result· Synergistic bactericidal behavior between silver and copper ions enabled strong antibacterial activity while reducing silver cytotoxicity by tenfold.
Validation· In vitro antibacterial assays and ex vivo murine femur model confirmed antibacterial efficacy; MC3T3-E1 cell assays showed no cytotoxicity.
