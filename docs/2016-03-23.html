Gaussian Process Morphable Models

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project simulation_case
Marcel Lüthi, Christoph Jud, Thomas Gerig, Thomas Vetter arXiv preprint 2016-03-23 link
相关性: 该论文提出的高斯过程形态模型（GPMM）可用于形状建模和非刚性配准，其高斯过程框架可迁移至生物力学设计中的代理建模或不确定性量化。
边界: This paper focuses on statistical shape modeling and non-rigid registration, not on solving expensive simulation sampling efficiency, handling constraints in optimization, or providing uncertainty quantification for surrogate modeling in implant/biomechanics design.
动机· 传统统计形状模型依赖示例数据，缺乏灵活性。
方法· 用高斯过程建模形状变化，通过Karhunen-Loeve展开和Nystrom近似实现连续形态模型。
结果· GPMM可结合数据驱动和样条模型，实现多尺度、空间变化配准。
验证· 在3D前臂图像分割任务中验证模型有效性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Statistical shape models are limited by the span of example data, motivating a more flexible continuous shape representation.
Method· We propose Gaussian Process Morphable Models (GPMMs) that model shape variations via a Gaussian process, approximated by the leading Karhunen-Loeve expansion using the Nystrom method.
Result· GPMMs allow combining data-driven and spline-based models, enabling multi-scale and spatially-varying non-rigid registration without changing the fitting algorithm.
Validation· The method is validated on a clinical use case of 3D forearm image segmentation.

Medium Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Direct Save for project simulation_case
Diana Chen, Victor Kosmopoulos 2016-03-23 link
相关性: 该论文直接研究了髋关节翻修术中股骨柄锥度对固定刚度的影响，与用户关注的股骨柄应力遮挡和初始稳定性方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not report stress shielding in Gruen zones, micromotion values, or patient-specific geometry; it focuses on construct stiffness and contact area in simplified diaphyseal models.
动机· 比较不同锥度股骨柄在翻修THA中的初始固定稳定性
方法· 使用有限元方法模拟两种骨缺损模型，改变远端柄锥度（0-2.5°）
结果· 锥度增加导致接触面积、骨应力、植入物应力和刚度下降，2°以上刚度急剧降低
验证· 仅数值模拟，无实验验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare initial fixation stability of tapered femoral stems with varying taper angles in revision THA scenarios.
Method· Finite element models of two diaphyseal bone loss configurations were used to simulate revision THA, with distal stem taper angles ranging from 0 to 2.5 degrees in 0.5-degree increments.
Result· Increasing taper angle reduced contact area, average bone stress, implant stress, and construct stiffness; stiffness dropped sharply beyond 2 degrees in the shorter diaphyseal model.
Validation· The study is purely computational with no experimental validation.
