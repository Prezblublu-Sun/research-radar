Motivation· To investigate the impact of constructivism-based learning with TAI cooperative model on student learning outcomes in triangle geometry.
Method· Developed instructional devices and evaluated effectiveness using descriptive analysis, one-sample t-test, and simple linear regression.
Result· The learning approach significantly improved outcomes, with average score 78.87 exceeding the minimum of 65, and student activity contributing 75.1% to results.
Validation· Validation included expert evaluation of devices, classical mastery tests, and regression analysis of activity influence.

Method· A quasi-experimental design with an experimental group taught using active learning strategies and a control group taught with traditional methods.

Validation· Pre-test and post-test comparisons with statistical significance testing.
