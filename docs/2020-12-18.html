André Edelmann, Monique Dubis, Ralf Hellmann Materials 2020-12-18 10.3390/ma13245786
单位: Applied Laser and Photonics Group, University of Applied Sciences Aschaffenburg, 63743 Aschaffenburg, Germany
通讯: André Edelmann @ Applied Laser and Photonics Group, University of Applied Sciences Aschaffenburg, 63743 Aschaffenburg, Germany
相关性: 该论文展示了定制接骨板的数字到物理工艺链，但与用户研究方向（多孔结构、应力遮挡、疲劳、表面形态）无关。
边界: No porous structure, no stress shielding or stiffness matching, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), and no surface roughness or osseointegration.
动机· 实现患者个性化接骨板的数字到物理工艺链。
方法· 基于CT数据设计，SLM钛合金打印，应力消除退火后处理。
结果· 接骨板与3D打印骨模型高度匹配，无需术中调整。
验证· 在SLS聚酰胺骨模型上进行装配验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Demonstrate a complete digital-to-physical process chain for fabricating patient-specific osteosynthesis plates.
Method· CT data were processed into an STL model, and the plate was fabricated by selective laser melting in titanium with optimized parameters and stress relief annealing.
Result· The plate achieved high fitting accuracy on a 3D-printed bone model without need for intraoperative bending or trimming.
Validation· Fitting was validated by manual assembly onto a polyamide bone model produced by selective laser sintering from the same CT data.

“True” Cumulative Incidence of and Risk Factors for Hip Dislocation within 2 Years After Primary Total Hip Arthroplasty Due to Osteoarthritis

L. Hermansen, Bjarke Viberg, Lars Hansen, Søren Overgaard Journal of Bone and Joint Surgery 2020-12-18 10.2106/jbjs.19.01352
单位: Department of Orthopaedics, Hospital of South West Jutland, Esbjerg, Denmark
通讯: L. Hermansen @ Department of Orthopaedics, Hospital of South West Jutland, Esbjerg, Denmark
相关性: 这篇论文是关于全髋关节置换术后脱位发生率和风险因素的临床流行病学研究，没有涉及生物力学建模或有限元分析。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain, micromotion, or implant-bone interface mechanics; it is purely clinical with no FE modeling or patient-specific computational analysis.
动机· 获取全髋关节置换术后脱位的真实发生率
方法· 基于丹麦髋关节置换登记处的数据，结合全国病历审查
结果· 2年累积脱位发生率为3.5%，较注册数据高50%
验证· 通过全国病历审查验证诊断编码准确性
English summary & terms
Motivation· To determine the true cumulative incidence of hip dislocation after primary THA, accounting for closed reductions missed in registry data.
Method· Retrospective cohort study linking Danish Hip Arthroplasty Register data with nationwide patient file review to identify all dislocations within 2 years.
Result· The true 2-year cumulative incidence of dislocation was 3.5%, 50% higher than register-only estimates, with age, sex, fixation, approach, and head size as independent risk factors.
Validation· Validation through comprehensive manual review of patient files to confirm non-matching codes.
