Motivation· To mimic the natural bone mineralization process for preparing mechanically enhanced collagen hydrogels.
Method· Alkaline phosphatase and vinylphosphonic acid are covalently bonded into a collagen network via photo-crosslinking for in situ mineralization.
Result· The mineralized collagen hydrogel exhibits enhanced toughness and supports cell growth while being compatible with 3D bioprinting.
Validation· In vitro cell culture and mechanical testing validated the hydrogel's performance.

Validation· In vitro cell assays and in vivo animal implantation tests were conducted.
