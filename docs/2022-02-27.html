英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To assess the cost-effectiveness of robotic arm-assisted total hip arthroplasty compared to manual surgery.
Method· Incremental cost-effectiveness ratios were calculated using EQ-5D data from private and public healthcare databases.
Result· Robotic THA was cost-effective despite higher costs due to greater health-related quality of life gains.
Validation· No experimental validation; the study relied on clinical outcome data and economic modeling.

Total Hip Arthroplasty When Utilised in Young Adults Is a Cost-Effective Procedure: A 10-Year and Lifetime Cost-Utility Analysis

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore none
Paul Stirling, Navnit S. Makaram, Nick D Clement, Deborah J. MacDonald, Gavin J. Macpherson Cureus 2022-02-27 10.7759/cureus.22651
单位: Edinburgh Orthopaedics, Royal Infirmary of Edinburgh, Edinburgh, GBR
通讯: Navnit S. Makaram @ Edinburgh Orthopaedics, Royal Infirmary of Edinburgh, Edinburgh, GBR
相关性: 该论文仅关注全髋关节置换术的成本效益分析，未涉及任何生物力学建模或植入物设计。
边界: This paper is a health economics study with no biomechanics modeling, no stress shielding analysis, no micromotion quantification, and no FEA or implant mechanics.
动机· 评估25岁以下患者全髋关节置换术的成本效益
方法· 基于EQ-5D和牛津髋关节评分计算质量调整生命年
结果· THA在年轻患者中具有成本效益，每QALY成本低
验证· 前瞻性队列研究，33例患者10年随访
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine the cost-effectiveness of total hip arthroplasty in patients aged 25 years and under.
Method· Prospective cohort study using EQ-5D and Oxford hip score to calculate QALYs gained.
Result· THA is cost-effective in young adults with low cost per QALY.
Validation· Clinical cohort of 33 patients with 10-year follow-up.
