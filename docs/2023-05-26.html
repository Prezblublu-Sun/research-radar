通讯: Tünde Anna Kovács @ Bánki Donát Faculty of Mechanical and Safety Engineering, Óbuda University, Népszínház u. 8., H-1081 Budapest, Hungary

边界: No fatigue testing, stress shielding, or mechanical properties; only surface roughness characterization.
动机· 评估不同表面处理对3D打印、铸造和锻造Ti6Al4V合金粗糙度的影响。

Result· SLM samples blasted with Al2O3 followed by HF etching exhibited the highest surface roughness (Ra=2.043 µm, Rz=11.742 µm).
Validation· Surface roughness parameters were experimentally measured and compared across manufacturing routes and treatments.
