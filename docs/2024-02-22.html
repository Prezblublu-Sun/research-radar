边界: No FEA validation, no biomechanics context, no implant design application.
动机· 现有安全学习方法局限于局部探索，无法发现与初始数据不连通的全局安全区域。

Motivation· Existing safe learning methods are limited to local exploration around initial seed data and cannot discover safe regions disconnected from the seed set.
Method· This paper proposes safe transfer sequential learning that leverages abundant offline data from a related source task to guide exploration in the target task, with a computationally efficient approximation.
Result· The method achieves global exploration across multiple disjoint safe regions with lower data consumption compared to state-of-the-art methods, while maintaining comparable computational efficiency.
Validation· Validation is performed on numerical benchmarks, comparing exploration capability and runtime against baseline methods.
