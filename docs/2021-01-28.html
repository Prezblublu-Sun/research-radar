Experimental realisation of build orientation effects on the mechanical properties of truly as-built Ti-6Al-4V SLM parts

Alireza Dareh Baghi, Shahrooz Nafisi, Reza Hashemi, Heike Ebendorff‐Heidepriem, Reza Ghomashchi Journal of Manufacturing Processes 2021-01-28 10.1016/j.jmapro.2021.01.027
单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, University of Adelaide, SA, 5005, Australia
通讯: Alireza Dareh Baghi @ School of Mechanical Engineering, University of Adelaide, SA, 5005, Australia
相关性: 该论文研究了建造方向对SLM钛合金力学性能的影响，虽未直接涉及生物医学植入物，但可为骨科植入物的增材制造工艺优化提供参考。
边界: This paper does not address biomedical implant applications such as stress shielding, fatigue behavior, or osseointegration; it focuses on static mechanical characterization of as-built SLM parts.

验证· 通过打印态试样的力学测试进行实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how build orientation influences the mechanical performance of as-built Ti-6Al-4V parts produced by selective laser melting, which is critical for additive manufacturing process optimization.
Method· Tensile testing was conducted on specimens built at different orientations, accompanied by surface roughness measurement and microstructural analysis.
Result· Build orientation significantly affected ultimate tensile strength and elongation, with horizontally built specimens exhibiting superior ductility.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed through mechanical testing of as-built specimens without post-processing.
