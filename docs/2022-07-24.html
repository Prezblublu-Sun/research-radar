Validation· Validation was performed by comparing simulated stiffness tensors and fatigue life with six well-known TPMS structures using the Universal Elastic Anisotropy Index.

Motivation· To compare patient satisfaction between periacetabular osteotomy and total hip arthroplasty for osteoarthritis secondary to developmental dysplasia of the hip in an Asian cohort.
Method· Propensity score matching was applied to compare postoperative satisfaction, Oxford Hip Score, UCLA activity score, and sports participation between PAO and THA patients stratified by Kellgren-Lawrence grade.
Result· PAO yielded greater satisfaction for grade II OA, while THA yielded better satisfaction and pain scores for grade III OA.
Validation· Clinical validation using patient-reported outcome measures from 737 patients.
