通讯: Ashok Athalye @ Institute of Chemical Technology, Mumbai, India
相关性: 该论文为天然生物聚合物用于生物墨水的综述，未涉及任何AI或机器学习方法。

Motivation· To review natural biopolymers as potential bioinks, balancing biocompatibility and mechanical properties.
Method· Literature review covering sources, mechanical and rheological properties, and modifications of natural biopolymers for bioprinting.
Result· Natural biopolymers offer excellent biocompatibility but require blending or crosslinking to improve mechanical strength and printability.
Validation· No experimental validation; this is a review article.

Nurse-Led Preoperative Education Impact on Anxiety and Pain in Total Hip Arthroplasty Patients: A scoping review

Norhaini Majid, Luqman Wafi Che Hasnan, Mohd Ramadan Ab Hamid, Suryanto Suryanto Environment-Behaviour Proceedings Journal 2026-01-17 10.21834/e-bpj.v11i35.7455
单位: 1) Centre for Nursing Studies, Faculty of Health Sciences, Universiti Teknologi MARA, Malaysia. 2) Telehealth and Remote Patient Care Research Interest Group (TeleCare), Faculty of Pharmacy, Universit
通讯: Norhaini Majid @ 1) Centre for Nursing Studies, Faculty of Health Sciences, Universiti Teknologi MARA, Malaysia. 2) Telehealth and Remote
相关性: 该论文是关于全髋关节置换术的护理教育，与髋关节假体生物力学建模完全无关。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, no implant design, no FEA, no stress shielding analysis, no micromotion quantification, and no contact mechanics; purely a clinical review of perioperative nursing education.
动机· 探索护理主导的术前教育对全髋关节置换患者焦虑和疼痛的影响。
方法· 遵循PRISMA-ScR指南的综述，从5个数据库筛选11项研究进行分析。
结果· 多数研究显示术前焦虑降低，但对术后疼痛的影响因干预差异不一致。
验证· 文献综述，无实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To synthesize evidence on whether nurse-led preoperative education reduces preoperative anxiety and postoperative pain in total hip arthroplasty patients.
Method· A scoping review following PRISMA-ScR guidelines, screening 210 records from five databases to include 11 studies.
Result· Nurse-led education consistently reduced preoperative anxiety, but its effect on postoperative pain was inconsistent across studies due to heterogeneity in intervention delivery.
Validation· No experimental validation; the study is a scoping review of existing literature.

单位: Clinic of Orthopaedic and Paediatric Orthopaedics, Medical University of Lodz, 90-419 Lodz, Poland
通讯: Marek Drobniewski @ Clinic of Orthopaedic and Paediatric Orthopaedics, Medical University of Lodz, 90-419 Lodz, Poland

边界: This paper is a clinical outcomes study of a revision hip arthroplasty system; it does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics, nor does it include finite element modeling or patient-specific biomechanical analysis.

方法· 回顾性分析使用Bioball系统的髋关节翻修病例

English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate long-term clinical and radiological outcomes of the Bioball System in revision hip arthroplasty with older 14/16 tapers.
Method· Retrospective analysis of revision cases using the Bioball System to restore stability and offset without femoral stem removal.
Result· The Bioball System effectively restores hip stability and offset with favorable long-term outcomes.
Validation· Clinical and radiological follow-up over extended periods.
