The Mechanical Properties and In Vitro Biocompatibility of PM-Fabricated Ti-28Nb-35.4Zr Alloy for Orthopedic Implant Applications

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Skim only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Wei Xu, Ming Li, Cuié Wen, Shaomin Lv, Chengcheng Liu et al. Materials 2018-03-30 10.3390/ma11040531
单位: Institute for Advanced Materials and Technology, University of Science and Technology Beijing, Beijing 100083, China
通讯: Xin Lu @ Beijing Key Laboratory for Advanced Powder Metallurgy and Particulate Materials, University of Science and Technology Be  |  Xuanhui Qu @ Beijing Key Laboratory for Advanced Powder Metallurgy and Particulate Materials, University of Science and Technology Be
相关性: 该论文涉及钛合金的力学性能与生物相容性，但未采用LPBF工艺，也未研究多孔结构或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper uses powder metallurgy (PM) instead of LPBF, does not fabricate porous structures, and lacks fatigue testing and surface roughness characterization.
动机· 开发低成本、高生物相容性的钛合金骨科植入材料。
方法· 采用粉末冶金工艺制备Ti-28Nb-35.4Zr合金，研究球磨和烧结温度的影响。
结果· 球磨粉末在1550°C烧结可获得全致密合金，压缩屈服强度1058 MPa，弹性模量50.8 GPa，细胞毒性0级。
验证· 通过压缩测试和体外细胞毒性实验验证力学性能和生物相容性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a low-cost, highly biocompatible titanium alloy for orthopedic implants via powder metallurgy.
Method· Ti-28Nb-35.4Zr alloy was fabricated by powder metallurgy, investigating the effects of mechanical milling and sintering temperature on microstructure and properties.
Result· Ball-milled powders sintered at 1550°C achieved full density, with compressive yield strength of 1058 MPa, elastic modulus of 50.8 GPa, and grade 0 cytotoxicity.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated by compression testing, and biocompatibility by in vitro cytotoxicity tests on MC3T3-E1 cells.

Uncertainty Quantification in Life Cycle Assessments: Exploring Distribution Choice and Greater Data Granularity to Characterize Product Use

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only none UQ
Stephen A. Ross, Lynette Cheah Journal of Industrial Ecology 2018-03-30 10.1111/jiec.12742
单位: Engineering Systems and Design Singapore University of Technology and Design Singapore
通讯: Lynette Cheah @ Engineering Systems and Design Singapore University of Technology and Design Singapore
相关性: 该论文关注生命周期评估中的不确定性量化，与FEA替代模型和植入物设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper applies uncertainty quantification to life cycle assessment, not to finite element analysis, surrogate modeling, or biomechanical implant design.
动机· 生命周期评估中产品使用阶段参数不确定性大，需改进表征方法。
方法· 采用蒙特卡洛模拟，对比不同概率分布和数据粒度对结果不确定性的影响。
结果· 数据粒度提高可降低输出不确定性，但收益递减；分布选择影响较小。
验证· 基于3年家庭空调实际用电数据驱动模拟。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Uncertainty in life cycle assessments is dominated by product use stage parameters, requiring better characterization methods.
Method· A stochastic Monte Carlo simulation was used to compare the effects of different probability distributions and data granularity on output uncertainty.
Result· Higher data granularity reduced output uncertainty with diminishing returns, while distribution choice had limited impact.
Validation· The approach was validated using three years of appliance-level electricity data from domestic residences.
