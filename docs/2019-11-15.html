英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the accuracy of preoperative digital-template planning in total hip arthroplasty via direct anterior approach and its impact on short-term outcomes.
Method· A retrospective analysis comparing preoperative digital-template predictions with actual prosthesis sizes and alignment measurements from postoperative radiographs.
Result· Digital-template planning showed high accuracy, reducing operation time and intraoperative fluoroscopy frequency without increasing complication risk.
Validation· Clinical retrospective study with radiographic and functional outcome assessment.
