通讯: Noor Daras @ Department of Prosthodontics, Loma Linda University, Loma Linda, California, USA. [推断]

动机· 解决牙槽嵴提升术中窦膜穿孔的封堵难题

Motivation· To address the challenge of managing Schneiderian membrane perforations during crestal sinus augmentation with limited visual access.
Method· A dehydrated amnion-chorion membrane configured in a waffle cone shape was used to seal perforations, allowing simultaneous bone grafting and implant placement.

Validation· Validation was through clinical and radiographic evaluation at immediate postoperative, 6-month, and 2-year follow-ups.
