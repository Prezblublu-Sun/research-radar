Adrien Weihs, Andrea L. Bertozzi, Matthew Thorpe arXiv preprint 2025-10-30 link
相关性: 该论文研究超图学习理论，与FEA代理模型、不确定性量化和生物力学设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on hypergraph regularization theory and does not address FEA surrogate modeling, expensive simulation sampling, constraints, uncertainty quantification, or implant biomechanics.
动机· 扩展高阶超图学习的理论基础。

English summary & terms
Motivation· This work extends the theoretical foundation of higher-order hypergraph learning by proving consistency and convergence rates for a truncated version.
Method· The authors analyze the consistency of truncated HOHL and derive explicit convergence rates when used as a regularizer in fully supervised learning.
Result· Strong empirical performance is demonstrated in active learning and datasets lacking geometric structure.
Validation· Validation is performed through numerical experiments on benchmark datasets for active learning and semi-supervised learning.

Transparent AI-driven personalized risk prediction system for acute kidney injury after total hip arthroplasty

Jiaojiao Tai, Linbang Wang, Li Yang, M Chen, Ziqiang Yan et al. Scientific Reports 2025-10-30 10.1038/s41598-025-21810-z
单位: Department of Orthopedics, Honghui Hospital, Xi'an Jiaotong University, Xi'an, 710054, Shaanxi, China
通讯: Jiaojiao Tai @ Department of Orthopedics, Honghui Hospital, Xi'an Jiaotong University, Xi'an, 710054, Shaanxi, China
相关性: 该论文使用机器学习预测全髋关节置换术后急性肾损伤，与髋关节植入物力学无关。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain, micromotion, or interface mechanics; it focuses solely on clinical AKI prediction without any implant design or biomechanical modeling.
动机· 全髋关节置换术后急性肾损伤预测困难，需高精度模型。
方法· 基于4601例患者多维数据，使用CatBoost和SMOTE-ENN构建预测模型。
结果· 内部验证AUC 0.95，外部验证AUC 0.65，模型具有临床可解释性。
验证· 内部交叉验证和独立外部医院数据集验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a transparent ML model for predicting acute kidney injury after total hip arthroplasty using multidimensional clinical data.
Method· Random Forest, Lasso, and mutual information for feature selection; CatBoost with SMOTE-ENN for class imbalance; SHAP for interpretability.
Result· CatBoost achieved AUC 0.95 internal and 0.65 external validation, with Cystatin C and surgical duration as key predictors.
Validation· Internal validation on 4601 patients and external validation on an independent hospital dataset (n=240).
