Guoqiang Liu, Zhiyuan Kong, Zhenyu Jia, Jianhua Wang ACS Omega 2025-10-30 10.1021/acsomega.5c06943
单位: Department of Orthopedics, General Hospital of Southern Theater Command of PLA, No.111 Liuhua Road, Guangzhou 510010, China
通讯: Guoqiang Liu @ Department of Orthopedics, General Hospital of Southern Theater Command of PLA, No.111 Liuhua Road, Guangzhou 510010, Ch
相关性: 该论文直接研究了LPBF制备的多孔TiTa支架的力学性能与骨整合效果，与用户关于AM生物医学植入物的方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing or surface roughness characterization, which are important for long-term implant performance.
动机· 钛生物惰性且弹性模量高，钽骨整合好但加工难，需开发兼具两者优势的植入材料。
方法· 通过LPBF制备不同Ta含量的多孔TiTa支架，保持孔隙结构一致，测试力学与生物学性能。

验证· 体外细胞实验和体内动物实验验证了骨整合效果。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Titanium's bioinertness and high elastic modulus limit osseointegration, while tantalum's superior osteogenesis is offset by high cost and processing difficulty, motivating the development of TiTa composites.
Method· Porous TiTa scaffolds with varying Ta content (25, 50, 75%) were designed with consistent porosity and fabricated via laser powder bed fusion, followed by mechanical testing and biological evaluation.
Result· TiTa50 and TiTa75 scaffolds exhibited elastic modulus and yield stress matching human cancellous bone, and achieved comparable osseointegration to pure tantalum in vitro and in vivo.
Validation· In vitro MC3T3-E1 cell assays and in vivo animal implantation studies validated the osseointegration performance.
