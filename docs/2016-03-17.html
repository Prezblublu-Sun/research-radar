The effect of defects on the mechanical response of Ti-6Al-4V cubic lattice structures fabricated by electron beam melting

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Skim only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Everth Hernández-Nava, Chris Smith, F. Derguti, Samuel Tammas‐Williams, Fabien Léonard et al. Acta Materialia 2016-03-17 10.1016/j.actamat.2016.02.029
单位: Department of Material Science & Engineering, The University of Sheffield, Sir Robert Hadfield Building, Mappin St, Sheffield S1 3JD, UK
通讯: Everth Hernández-Nava @ Department of Material Science & Engineering, The University of Sheffield, Sir Robert Hadfield Building, Mappin St, Shef
相关性: 该论文研究了电子束熔化制造的Ti-6Al-4V立方点阵结构的缺陷对力学性能的影响，与用户关注的增材制造生物医学植入物的力学性能相关。
边界: This paper does not link porous structure to stress shielding or stiffness matching, does not cover fatigue, and does not report surface roughness or osseointegration.
动机· 研究电子束熔化制造Ti-6Al-4V立方点阵结构中缺陷对力学性能的影响。
方法· 通过EBM制造不同热处理条件的点阵结构，进行压缩实验和微观结构分析。
结果· β相变热处理导致屈服强度下降10%，压缩屈服应力与支柱直径关系符合简单模型。
验证· 压缩实验与理论模型对比验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the influence of micro- and macro-scale defects on the mechanical response of Ti-6Al-4V cubic lattice structures fabricated by electron beam melting.
Method· Lattice structures were manufactured by EBM with heat treatments above and below the β-transus temperature, and compression tests combined with microstructural analysis were performed.
Result· Heat treatment above β-transus caused a 10% loss in yield strength, and the variation of compressive yield stress with strut diameter agreed well with simple compression-based models.
Validation· Experimental compression results were compared with analytical models and numerical simulations.

Result· No significant increase in deep infection risk was found for same-day bilateral THA.
Validation· No biomechanical validation; only clinical statistical analysis.

Motivation· To investigate factors influencing the choice of anesthetic technique for primary hip and knee arthroplasty.
Method· A literature review comparing neuraxial and general anesthesia outcomes and utilization patterns.
Result· Neuraxial anesthesia shows advantages in mortality and morbidity, but its use varies geographically.
Validation· No experimental validation; based on literature review.
