Efficient Bayesian Inference of Atomistic Structure in Complex Functional Materials

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project simulation_case Exp. validation
Milica Todorović, Michael U. Gutmann, Jukka Corander, Patrick Rinke arXiv preprint 2017-08-30 10.1038/s41524-019-0175-2
相关性: 贝叶斯优化方法可迁移至FEA替代建模，但缺乏约束处理和生物力学背景。
边界: No FEA validation, no biomechanics context, and no constraint handling; the method is demonstrated on materials science rather than implant design.
动机· 加速复杂材料原子结构搜索，避免昂贵量子力学计算。
方法· 采用贝叶斯优化结构搜索（BOSS）结合似然自由贝叶斯方案进行主动学习。
结果· 成功识别C60在TiO2表面的最有利吸附构型，与实验一致。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To enable efficient inference of atomistic structure in complex functional materials where direct quantum mechanical simulation is prohibitive.
Method· A building-block-based Bayesian Optimization Structure Search (BOSS) approach with a likelihood-free Bayesian scheme for active learning sampling of configurations.
Result· BOSS identified the most favorable adsorption configurations of C60 on the TiO2 anatase surface, showing good agreement with experimental images.
Validation· The inferred structures were validated against detailed experimental images of the surface adsorbate.

Validation· Simulation results were compared with theoretical predictions for bulk materials.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the long-term effects of local infiltration analgesia on functional outcome, quality of life, and chronic pain after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A secondary analysis of a double-blind randomized trial comparing intrathecal morphine and local infiltration analgesia in 80 patients.
Result· No significant differences were found between groups in functional outcome, quality of life, or persistent post-surgical pain up to 2 years.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with patient-reported outcome measures (HOOS, EQ-5D, SF-36) and adverse event monitoring.
