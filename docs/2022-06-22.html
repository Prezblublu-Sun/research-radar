英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of cane-assisted walking on frontal plane hip joint loading in patients with late-stage unilateral hip osteoarthritis.
Method· Gait analysis using a 3D motion capture system was performed under four conditions: with and without a cane on both the affected and non-affected sides.
Result· Cane walking reduced peak hip adduction moment by approximately 25% and pain scores significantly, but increased loading on the non-affected side.
Validation· Clinical gait experiment with 27 patients scheduled for total hip arthroplasty.
