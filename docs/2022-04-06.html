Motivation· To evaluate the radiographic progression-free survival of core decompression augmented with bone marrow aspirate concentrate for osteonecrosis of the femoral head.
Method· A retrospective cohort study of 61 femoral heads (40 patients) was conducted, analyzing radiographic progression and conversion to total hip arthroplasty.
Result· No significant differences in progression-free or conversion-free survival were found compared to core decompression alone or with cement augmentation.
Validation· Clinical outcomes were compared to historical control groups from a prior randomized trial.
