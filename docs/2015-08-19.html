单位: the University of Texas at, Austin
通讯: Reetam Biswas @ the University of Texas at, Austin
相关性: 该论文专注于地震反演中的参数化与不确定性量化，与生物力学替代模型或植入物设计无直接关联。
边界: No biomedical context, no FEA surrogate modeling, no implant or biomechanics design application.

Validation· The method is validated on pre-stack angle stack AVA inversion for estimating impedance profiles.

边界: This paper focuses on distal tibial fracture fixation with intramedullary nails, not on hip implant stress shielding, micromotion, or femoral stem mechanics.
动机· 评估新型髓内钉系统在胫骨远端骨折中的剪切稳定性。
方法· 建立胫骨-髓内钉三维有限元模型，分析不同材料和额外螺钉的影响。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the shear stability of a novel intramedullary nail design for distal tibial fractures.
Method· A 3D finite element model of the tibia-nail system with a transverse gap was developed, comparing different materials and an extra screw above the fracture.
Result· The extra screw dramatically reduced shear interfragmentary movements but also restricted axial micromotion, suggesting use with low-modulus materials.
Validation· The finite element model was validated against in vitro cadaveric tests.
