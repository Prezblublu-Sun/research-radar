Conditional Normalizing Flow Surrogate for Monte Carlo Prediction of Radiative Properties in Nanoparticle-Embedded Layers

Fahime Seyedheydari, Kevin Conley, Simo Särkkä arXiv preprint 2025-08-27 link
相关性: 该论文提出的条件归一化流代理模型方法可迁移至植入物生物力学设计中的昂贵仿真采样问题。
边界: This paper focuses on radiative transfer surrogate modeling, not on finite element analysis (FEA) or implant biomechanics context.
动机· 解决纳米颗粒嵌入层辐射特性蒙特卡洛仿真计算效率低的问题。
方法· 使用条件归一化流学习辐射特性输出关于输入参数的条件分布。
结果· 模型预测精度高且能提供可靠的不确定性估计。
验证· 基于Mie理论和蒙特卡洛仿真的数值验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To efficiently predict radiative properties of nanoparticle-embedded scattering media while avoiding expensive Monte Carlo simulations.
Method· A conditional normalizing flow surrogate learns the full conditional distribution of optical outputs given input parameters such as absorption and scattering coefficients.
Result· The model achieves high predictive accuracy and provides principled uncertainty quantification for reflectance, absorbance, and transmittance.
Validation· Validation is performed on synthetic data generated from Monte Carlo radiative transfer simulations with Mie-theory-derived optical properties.

边界: No FEA, surrogate modeling, structural mechanics, or biomedical context; purely focused on active learning for object detection in autonomous driving.
动机· 降低自动驾驶目标检测主动学习的高计算成本和不稳定评估

English summary & terms
Motivation· To reduce the high computational cost and unreliable evaluation of active learning methods in real-world object detection.
Method· Proposes object-based set similarity (OSS), a metric that measures similarity between training sets and target domains using object-level features, eliminating the need for detector training.
Result· OSS effectively identifies ineffective AL methods before training and selects representative validation sets for robust evaluation on three autonomous driving datasets.
Validation· Validated on KITTI, BDD100K, and CODA datasets with two detector architectures (EfficientDet, YOLOv3) using uncertainty-based AL methods.
