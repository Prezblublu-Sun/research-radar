High FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Read now simulation_case UQ
I. B. C. M. Rocha, P. Kerfriden, F. P. van der Meer arXiv preprint 2020-07-11 link
相关性: 该论文提出了一种基于高斯过程的在线代理模型构建方法，可显著降低多尺度有限元分析的计算成本，其不确定性量化与自适应采样策略可直接迁移至植入物生物力学设计中的代理建模。
边界: This paper does not address implant-specific biomechanics, contact mechanics, or patient-specific geometry, and lacks validation on biomedical or implant-related problems.
动机· 多尺度有限元计算成本过高，亟需高效代理模型。
方法· 在线训练高斯过程代理模型，利用梯度信息和贪婪采样自适应构建本构流形。
结果· 无需离线数据，显著提升计算效率，在弹塑性算例中验证有效性。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Concurrent multiscale FE2 is computationally prohibitive due to nested micromodel evaluations, motivating the need for efficient surrogate constitutive models.
Method· Gaussian process surrogate models are trained online using gradient-enhanced data from a small set of anchor micromodels, with greedy selection and uncertainty-triggered refinement.
Result· The method achieves significant computational savings without offline training, demonstrated on elastoplastic problems with tapered bar, plate with cutouts, and crack growth.
Validation· Validation is performed through numerical simulations of nonlinear structural examples, including plasticity and crack propagation.
