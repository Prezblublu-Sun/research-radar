Lattice structures of Cu-Cr-Zr copper alloy by selective laser melting: Microstructures, mechanical properties and energy absorption

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Zhibo Ma, David Z. Zhang, Fei Liu, Junjie Jiang, Miao Zhao et al. Materials & Design 2019-12-06 10.1016/j.matdes.2019.108406
单位: State Key Laboratory of Mechanical Transmissions, Chongqing University, Chongqing 400044, China
通讯: David Z. Zhang @ College of Engineering, Mathematics and Physical Sciences, University of Exeter, North Park Road, Exeter EX4 4QF, UK
相关性: 该论文研究铜合金点阵结构的力学性能，与生物医用植入物的应力屏蔽或骨整合无关。
边界: No biomedical context, no stress shielding or stiffness matching for implants, no fatigue testing, no surface roughness or osseointegration assessment.
动机· 首次通过SLM制造Cu-Cr-Zr铜合金点阵结构并研究其力学性能。
方法· 设计TPMS点阵结构，改变胞元尺寸和体积分数，进行压缩实验。
结果· 点阵结构表现出长应力平台，无应力崩溃，能量吸收性能随参数变化显著。
验证· 通过压缩实验和微观结构表征验证力学性能。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To fabricate and characterize lattice structures of Cu-Cr-Zr copper alloy via selective laser melting for energy absorption applications.
Method· Triply periodic minimal surface lattice structures with varying cell size and volume fraction were designed and manufactured by SLM, followed by compression testing and microstructural analysis.
Result· The lattice structures exhibited a long stress plateau without collapse, and their mechanical and energy absorption properties varied significantly with cell size and volume fraction.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated through compression tests and microstructure observation.

Construction of Cellular Substructure in Laser Powder Bed Fusion

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only theoretical
Yafei Wang, Chenglu Zhang, Chenfan Yu, Leilei Xing, Kailun Li et al. 2019-12-06 link
相关性: 该论文研究激光粉末床熔融中细胞亚结构的构建，但与应力屏蔽、疲劳或生物医学植入物无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on crystallographic orientation and substructure morphology in LPBF of stainless steel, with no biomedical context, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), no porous structure design, and no osseointegration or surface roughness analysis.
动机· 建立激光粉末床熔融中细胞亚结构的一般模型
方法· 通过二维形貌确定Bunge欧拉角和生长方向
结果· 扫描速度增加时亚结构从类细胞枝晶转变为细胞
验证· 基于316L不锈钢单道实验数据验证模型
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a general model for constructing cellular substructure in laser powder bed fusion from its two-dimensional morphology.
Method· All three Bunge Euler angles are determined to specify the unique growth direction, and the crystallographic orientation is obtained to distinguish cell-like dendrite from cell.
Result· With increasing scanning velocity, the substructure transitions from cell-like dendrite to cell, with a critical growth rate of approximately 0.31 ms⁻¹ at 200 W.
Validation· The model is validated using single-track experiments on austenitic stainless steel 316L.
