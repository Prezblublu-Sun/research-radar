Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Shai Feldman, Stephen Bates, Yaniv Romano arXiv preprint 2021-10-02 link
相关性: 该方法通过深度生成模型和分位数回归实现多输出预测区间，可迁移至FEA代理模型的不确定性量化。
边界: No FEA or biomechanics context; method is validated on general regression tasks, not on surrogate modeling for implant design.
动机· 现有多输出分位数回归方法生成的预测区域形状受限且缺乏覆盖保证。
方法· 用深度生成模型学习响应变量的单峰表示，再应用分位数回归，并结合共形预测调整覆盖水平。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Existing multiple-output quantile regression methods produce predictive regions with restricted shapes and lack coverage guarantees.
Method· A deep generative model learns a unimodal representation of the response, then quantile regression is applied on the representation, and conformal prediction extends to multivariate settings to guarantee coverage.
Result· The proposed method yields significantly smaller predictive regions with finite-sample coverage guarantees.
Validation· Experiments on synthetic and real datasets demonstrate superiority over existing techniques.
