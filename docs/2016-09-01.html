Influence of Microarchitecture on Osteoconduction and Mechanics of Porous Titanium Scaffolds Generated by Selective Laser Melting

通讯: Franz E. Weber @ Oral Biotechnology &amp; Bioengineering, Center of Dental Medicine, University of Zurich, Zurich, Switzerland
相关性: 该论文直接研究了LPBF制备的多孔钛支架的微结构对力学性能和骨传导的影响，与用户的研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing, surface roughness characterization, or explicit stress shielding analysis.
动机· 探究多孔钛支架的最优微结构以实现骨传导和力学性能的平衡。
方法· 利用选择性激光熔化制备五种不同微结构的多孔钛支架，并进行体内植入和力学测试。
结果· 所有微结构均表现出优异的骨传导性，但力学强度差异达三倍，最高可达松质骨的三倍。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To identify the optimal microarchitecture of porous titanium scaffolds that balances osteoconduction and mechanical strength for bone substitution.
Method· Five different microarchitectures with pore sizes from 700 to 1300 μm were fabricated by selective laser melting and evaluated in vivo in rabbit calvarial defects and by compression testing.
Result· All architectures showed excellent osteoconduction, but yield strength varied by a factor of three, reaching up to three times that of cancellous bone at porosities of 72.3-88.4%.
Validation· In vivo rabbit calvarial defect model and mechanical compression testing.

Result· Stovepipe femurs with large stems exhibited significantly lower equivalent stress in the proximal-medial region, correlating with greater bone mineral density loss at one year, indicating stress shielding.
Validation· Finite element predictions were validated against patient-specific bone mineral density changes measured one year post-operatively.
