IMPACT OF HEAT TREATMENTS ON THE CORROSION RESISTANCE OF A BIOCOMPATIBLE CO–CR–W ALLOY FABRICATED BY SLM

相关性: 该文仅涉及SLM制造Co-Cr-W合金的腐蚀性能，未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡、力学测试或疲劳行为。
边界: This paper does not report porous structures, stress shielding analysis, mechanical testing (static or fatigue), surface roughness, or osseointegration, which are central to the AM Biomedical Materials direction.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how heat treatments influence the corrosion resistance of SLM-fabricated Co-Cr-W alloy for biomedical applications.
Method· Samples were fabricated by selective laser melting and subjected to various heat treatment protocols.
Result· Heat treatments reduced internal stresses and improved microstructure, leading to enhanced corrosion resistance.
Validation· Corrosion resistance was evaluated through electrochemical testing.

Detection of AI Generated Images Using Combined Uncertainty Measures and Particle Swarm Optimised Rejection Mechanism

Rahul Yumlembam, Biju Issac, Nauman Aslam, Eaby Kollonoor Babu, Josh Collyer et al. arXiv preprint 2025-12-20 10.1038/s41598-025-28572-8
相关性: 该论文专注于AI生成图像检测，与有限元代理建模或生物力学设计无关。
边界: This paper addresses AI-generated image detection using uncertainty measures, with no connection to FEA surrogate modeling, constraint handling, or biomechanical implant design.
动机· 区分AI生成图像与自然图像的挑战
方法· 结合Fisher信息、MC Dropout熵和深度核学习方差，用PSO优化权重和阈值
结果· 组合不确定性方法在分布偏移下实现约70%错误拒绝率
验证· 在多个生成器（GLIDE、VQDM等）和对抗攻击下评估
English summary & terms
Motivation· The growing photorealism of AI-generated images necessitates robust detection methods.
Method· A framework integrating Fisher information, Monte Carlo Dropout entropy, and Deep Kernel Learning variance, optimized via Particle Swarm Optimization for adaptive rejection.
Result· The combined uncertainty measure rejects approximately 70% of misclassified AI-generated images under distribution shifts.
Validation· Evaluated on multiple unseen generators (GLIDE, VQDM, etc.) and under FGSM/PGD adversarial attacks.
