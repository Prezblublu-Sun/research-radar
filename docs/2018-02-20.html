单位: École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne, Lausanne, Switzerland
通讯: Paul Rolland @ École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne, Lausanne, Switzerland
相关性: 该论文提出了一种高维贝叶斯优化的方法，可迁移至生物力学植入物设计中的昂贵仿真优化问题。
边界: This paper does not address constraints, uncertainty quantification, or implant/biomechanics-specific applications.
动机· 高维贝叶斯优化中，现有方法假设变量子集不重叠，限制了适用性。
方法· 提出允许任意重叠的加性模型，通过图表示依赖关系并设计消息传递算法优化采集函数。

Motivation· Existing high-dimensional Bayesian optimization methods assume disjoint variable subsets, limiting their applicability to problems with overlapping dependencies.

Result· The proposed method demonstrates effectiveness on both synthetic and real-world benchmarks.
Validation· Validation is performed on standard numerical benchmarks and real-world datasets.
