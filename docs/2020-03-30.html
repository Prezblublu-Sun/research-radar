Motivation· To compare clinical outcomes of subtrochanteric shortening osteotomy versus Paavilainen proximal osteotomy in total hip arthroplasty for Crowe type III and IV dysplasia.
Method· A retrospective study of 36 hip arthroplasties (22 Paavilainen, 14 subtrochanteric) with mean follow-up of 42.3 months.
Result· Both osteotomies improved Harris and HOOS scores significantly, with no clear advantage of one over the other, though complications were higher in the subtrochanteric group.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with Harris Hip Score and HOOS, no biomechanical testing.
