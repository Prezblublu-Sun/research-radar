单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Tsinghua University, Beijing 100084, China
通讯: Peng Wen @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Tsinghua University, Beijing 100084, China
相关性: 该论文研究了可降解Zn合金多孔支架的LPBF制造与力学性能，与AM生物医学方向直接相关，但未涉及应力遮挡或疲劳性能。

动机· 纯Zn强度不足，需开发可降解Zn合金多孔支架

Domenico Quagliarella, Andrea Serani, Matteo Diez, Michele Pisaroni, Pénélope Leyland et al. AIAA Aviation 2019 Forum 2019-06-15 10.2514/6.2019-3555
单位: Italian Aerospace Research Center (CIRA)
通讯: Domenico Quagliarella @ Italian Aerospace Research Center (CIRA)
相关性: 该论文在空气动力学翼型上系统比较了多种不确定性量化方法，其方法论可直接迁移至植入物力学设计中的不确定性传播问题。
边界: This paper applies UQ benchmarking to an airfoil in aerodynamics, not to biomedical implants or biomechanical FEA, and lacks any implant-specific constraints or validation.
动机· 系统比较不同不确定性量化方法在几何与操作不确定性下的表现。
方法· 在NACA 2412翼型上应用多项式混沌、高斯过程等方法进行UQ基准测试。
结果· 不同UQ方法在精度和效率上存在显著差异，为方法选择提供指导。
验证· 通过数值基准案例（翼型升阻力系数）对比各方法预测的统计矩。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To systematically benchmark various uncertainty quantification methods under combined geometrical and operational uncertainties on a standard aerodynamic shape.
Method· Polynomial chaos expansion, Gaussian process regression, and other UQ techniques are applied to the NACA 2412 airfoil with random perturbations in geometry and flow conditions.
Result· Significant differences in accuracy and computational cost among UQ methods are identified, providing guidance for method selection in similar problems.
Validation· Validation is performed by comparing predicted statistical moments (mean, variance) of lift and drag coefficients against a high-fidelity Monte Carlo reference.
