单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering and Mechanics, Drexel University, Philadelphia, PA, United States of America.
通讯: Li-Hsin Han @ Department of Mechanical Engineering and Mechanics, Drexel University, Philadelphia, PA, United States of America.; Auth [推断]
相关性: 该论文未涉及AI技术，与AI生物打印方向无直接关联。

Validation· In vitro culture of human umbilical vein endothelial cells (HUVECs) inside self-folded microtubes.

Validation· Live/dead assay and WST-1 assay were performed to assess biocompatibility of the printed constructs

Simulation of the Evolution of Thermal Dynamics during Selective Laser Melting and Experimental Verification Using Online Monitoring

Peiying Bian, Xiaodong Shao, Jingli Du, Fangxia Ye, Xiuping Zhang et al. Sensors 2020-08-10 10.3390/s20164451
单位: School of Mechano-Electronic Engineering, Xidian University, Xi’an 710071, China
通讯: Peiying Bian @ School of Mechano-Electronic Engineering, Xidian University, Xi’an 710071, China
相关性: 该论文研究SLM热动力学模拟，未涉及生物医学植入物或骨固定评估。
边界: This paper focuses on SLM thermal simulation and online monitoring, with no biomedical application, no porous structure design, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), and no assessment of stress shielding or osseointegration.
动机· 理解SLM工艺参数对熔池形成的影响机制
方法· 建立动态移动热源的有限元熔池演化模拟方法
结果· 模拟与在线高速摄像实验吻合良好
验证· 通过在线监控高速摄像获取熔池图像进行验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand the mechanism by which selective laser melting process parameters affect molten pool formation.
Method· A dynamic finite element simulation with a moving heat source was developed to model molten pool evolution.
Result· Simulation results showed good agreement with high-speed camera images of the molten pool.
Validation· Experimental validation via online monitoring with laser supplementary light captured molten pool surface and cross-section images.
