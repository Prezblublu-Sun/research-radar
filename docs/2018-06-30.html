Uncertainty Quantification of Electronic and Photonic ICs with Non-Gaussian Correlated Process Variations

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Chunfeng Cui, Zheng Zhang arXiv preprint 2018-06-30 link
相关性: 该论文提出的非高斯相关不确定性量化方法可迁移至植入物生物力学设计中的代理模型构建，但缺乏生物力学或植入物相关验证。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling for implant biomechanics, lacks biomedical context, and validates only on electronic/photonic ICs.

Motivation· To address the long-standing challenge of non-Gaussian correlated process variations in uncertainty quantification for integrated circuits and photonics.

Validation· Validated on electronic and photonic ICs with up to 57 non-Gaussian correlated variation parameters, demonstrating superior efficiency and accuracy.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the stability of sandblasted, large-grit, acid-etched (SLA) implants with tapered straight body design and identify factors affecting implant stability.
Method· Resonance frequency analysis (RFA) was performed on 44 implants in 26 patients to measure implant stability quotient (ISQ) values at baseline and after 12 weeks.
Result· Mean ISQ values increased from 69.4 at baseline to 81.4 at healing abutment connection, with significant correlations to bone quality and jawbone type.
Validation· Clinical study with 26 patients and 44 implants, using RFA measurements.
