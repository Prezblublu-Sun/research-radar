英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce opioid exposure after hip and knee arthroplasty through a pharmacist-led intervention.
Method· Patients were randomized to usual care or intervention involving mailed materials and pharmacist telephone calls, targeting high-risk patients.
Result· The intervention reduced morphine equivalent dose by 62% in THA patients but showed no significant effect in TKA patients.
Validation· Randomized pragmatic clinical trial with 561 patients.
