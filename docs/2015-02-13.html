单位: Laboratory of Biophysics, Faculty of Electrical Engineering, University of Ljubljana, Ljubljana SI-1000, Slovenia. Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Chair of Surface Science and Corrosi
相关性: 本文综述了TiO2纳米管对骨整合和细胞行为的影响，但未涉及LPBF制造或力学性能。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), no stress shielding analysis, no porous structure design.
动机· 综述钛纳米结构在生物医学中的应用，特别是骨整合优化。

相关性: 该论文研究MOS晶体管中的陷阱相互作用，与AM生物医学材料方向无关。

英文摘要与术语

Result· The extracted distance between the two interface states matches the inter-atomic distance in the silicon lattice at the Si/SiO2 interface.
Validation· The analytical equation was validated against measurement data.
