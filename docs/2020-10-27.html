Method· Logistic regression was applied to 104,055 THA cases and 520,275 population controls from Danish national registers.
Result· Lower education and income were associated with higher THA risk in younger groups, but the disparity diminished over time.
Validation· Large-scale national registry data with population controls.

Motivation· To evaluate early outcomes and complications of total hip arthroplasty in patients with avascular necrosis of the femoral head.
Method· Retrospective analysis of demographic data, comorbidities, and Harris hip scores in 44 patients.
Result· Mean patient age was 42.1 years; Harris hip scores improved postoperatively, but no biomechanical metrics were reported.
Validation· Clinical follow-up and score comparison.
