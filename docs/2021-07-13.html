A Transfer Learning-Based Multi-Fidelity Point-Cloud Neural Network Approach for Melt Pool Modeling in Additive Manufacturing

Xufeng Huang, Tingli Xie, Zhuo Wang, Lei Chen, Qi Zhou et al. ASCE-ASME Journal of Risk and Uncertainty in Engineering Systems Part B Mechanical Engineering 2021-07-13 10.1115/1.4051749
单位: Department of Industrial and Manufacturing Systems Engineering, University of Michigan-Dearborn , 2340 Heinz Prechter Engineering Complex (HPEC), Dearborn, MI 48128
通讯: Xufeng Huang @ Department of Industrial and Manufacturing Systems Engineering, University of Michigan-Dearborn , 2340 Heinz Prechter En
相关性: 该论文提出的多保真点云神经网络方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模，解决昂贵仿真采样效率问题。
边界: This paper does not address implant or biomechanics design; it focuses on melt pool modeling in additive manufacturing.
动机· 熔池建模对增材制造不确定性量化至关重要，但有限元仿真耗时。
方法· 提出多保真点云神经网络，利用迁移学习融合低保真解析模型和高保真有限元数据。
结果· 该方法能高效预测三维熔池热历史，在电子束增材制造中验证了有效性。
验证· 基于Ti-6Al-4V电子束增材制造有限元仿真案例验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Efficient surrogate modeling of melt pool thermal history is needed to enable uncertainty quantification in additive manufacturing, as high-fidelity finite element simulations are computationally expensive.
Method· A multi-fidelity point-cloud neural network (MF-PointNN) is developed, which first trains a base PointNN on low-fidelity analytical model data and then fine-tunes it with a small set of high-fidelity finite element simulation data via transfer learning.
Result· The trained MF-PointNN efficiently maps input variables and spatial positions to thermal histories, enabling accurate prediction of three-dimensional melt pool geometry under uncertainty.
Validation· The approach is validated on a simulation case of electron beam additive manufacturing of Ti-6Al-4V, demonstrating improved prediction accuracy over single-fidelity models.
