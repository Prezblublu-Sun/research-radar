Achieving ultrahigh-strength in beta-type titanium alloy by controlling the melt pool mode in selective laser melting

单位: National Engineering Research Center of Near-net-shape Forming for Metallic Materials, South China University of Technology, Guangzhou 510640, China
通讯: Chao Yang @ National Engineering Research Center of Near-net-shape Forming for Metallic Materials, South China University of Technol  |  Youhua Li @ National Engineering Research Center of Near-net-shape Forming for Metallic Materials, South China University of Technol
相关性: 与AM生物医学方向部分相关，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳等关键问题。
边界: This paper focuses on melt pool mode optimization for strength in SLM-produced beta titanium alloy, but does not address porous structure design, stress shielding, fatigue testing, surface morphology, or osseointegration relevant to biomedical implants.
动机· 通过控制熔池模式提高SLM成型β钛合金的强度。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To achieve ultrahigh strength in beta-type titanium alloy by controlling the melt pool mode during selective laser melting.
Method· Systematic variation of laser power and scan speed to achieve different melt pool modes (conduction, transition, keyhole).
Result· Ultrahigh tensile strength (>1400 MPa) was obtained in the transition mode due to refined grain structure and reduced defects.
Validation· Tensile testing and microstructural analysis (SEM, EBSD) were performed to correlate strength with melt pool characteristics.

Validation· Numerical experiments on multiple standard benchmark functions from the literature.
