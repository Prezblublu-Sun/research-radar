单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, Jiangsu University, Zhenjiang, 212013, PR China
通讯: Weiwei Deng @ School of Mechanical Engineering, Jiangsu University, Zhenjiang, 212013, PR China  |  Jinzhong Lu @ School of Mechanical Engineering, Jiangsu University, Zhenjiang, 212013, PR China
相关性: 该论文研究IN718镍基高温合金的电脉冲处理，与生物医学植入物、应力遮挡或骨整合无关。
边界: The paper focuses on IN718 superalloy for non-biomedical applications, with no porous structure, stress shielding analysis, fatigue testing, or osseointegration relevant to AM biomedical implants.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the microstructure and mechanical performance of IN718 alloy produced by laser powder bed fusion via electrical pulse treatment.
Method· Electrical pulse treatment was applied to LPBF-fabricated IN718, and microstructural evolution and mechanical properties were characterized.
Result· The treatment refined grains and simultaneously improved strength and ductility.
Validation· Validation through tensile testing and microstructural analysis.
