Stem geometry changes initial femoral fixation stability of a revised press-fit hip prosthesis: A finite element study

High Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Direct Read now simulation_case
Robert D. Russell, Michael H. Huo, Danieli C. Rodrigues, Victor Kosmopoulos Technology and Health Care 2016-07-12 10.3233/thc-161235
单位: University of Texas Southwestern Medical Center
通讯: Victor Kosmopoulos @ University of North Texas
相关性: 该论文直接比较了圆柱形和锥形股骨柄在翻修THA中的初始固定稳定性，包括接触压力和微动，与用户的研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not report stress/strain in Gruen zones, does not compare intact vs implanted femur, and does not include patient-specific geometry or population-based analysis.
动机· 比较不同柄几何形状和压配条件对翻修THA初始固定稳定性的影响。
方法· 基于CT数据建立股骨有限元模型，模拟圆柱形和锥形柄在两种压配下的接触压力和微动。
结果· 圆柱形柄微动更小，但锥形柄接触压力更低；压配条件影响大于几何形状。
验证· 仅有限元模拟，无实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the initial fixation stability of cylindrical and tapered stem designs under different press-fit conditions for revision total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A finite element femur model with a trabecular bone defect was created from CT data, and two generic stem geometries (cylindrical vs. 2-degree tapered) were simulated with 0.05-mm and 0.01-mm press-fit under physiological loading.
Result· Cylindrical stems consistently showed lower relative micromotion, while tapered stems produced lower average contact pressure; press-fit condition had a greater influence on contact pressure than stem geometry.
Validation· The study is purely computational with no experimental validation.
