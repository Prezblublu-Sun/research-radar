Metallurgical and Machinability Characteristics of Wrought and Selective Laser Melted Ti-6Al-4V

Manikandakumar Shunmugavel, Ashwin Polishetty, Junior Nomani, Moshe Goldberg, Guy Littlefair Journal of Metallurgy 2016-10-10 10.1155/2016/7407918
单位: School of Engineering, Faculty of Science, Engineering and Built Environment, Deakin University, Waurn Ponds, Geelong, VIC, Australia
通讯: Manikandakumar Shunmugavel @ School of Engineering, Faculty of Science, Engineering and Built Environment, Deakin University, Waurn Ponds, Geelong, V
相关性: 该论文研究SLM Ti-6Al-4V的切削加工性，未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能，与生物医学植入物设计方向关联较弱。
边界: This paper focuses on machinability of SLM Ti-6Al-4V, not on porous structure, stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration relevant to biomedical implants.
动机· 比较SLM与锻造Ti-6Al-4V的切削加工性差异
方法· 在不同切削速度下进行端面车削实验，测量刀具磨损、切削力和表面质量

Motivation· To compare the machinability of selective laser melted and wrought Ti-6Al-4V in face turning operations.
Method· Face turning experiments were conducted at cutting speeds from 60 to 180 m/min, measuring tool wear, cutting forces, and surface finish.
Result· SLM Ti-6Al-4V exhibited poorer machinability with higher tool wear and cutting forces compared to the wrought alloy.
Validation· Experimental measurements of tool wear, cutting forces, and surface roughness validated the findings.

The stability of dual-taper modular hip implants: a biomechanical analysis examining the effect of impact location on component stability

Low Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Nicholas B. Frisch, Jonathan R. Lynch, Richard F. Banglmaier, Craig D. Silverton Arthroplasty Today 2016-10-10 10.1016/j.artd.2016.08.005
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Henry Ford Health System, Detroit, MI, USA
通讯: Jonathan R. Lynch @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Henry Ford Health System, Detroit, MI, USA
相关性: 该论文研究双锥模块化髋关节假体的撞击方向对组件稳定性的影响，但未涉及股骨近端应力遮挡或微动分析。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain or micromotion at the bone-implant interface, nor does it include FEA or patient-specific modeling.
动机· 研究撞击方向对双锥模块髋关节假体组件稳定性的影响。
方法· 使用材料试验机测量不同撞击方向下组件的分离力。
结果· 轴向撞击稳定性最佳，离轴撞击使分离力降低达35%。
验证· 通过机械测试验证不同撞击条件下的分离力差异。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate how the direction of impaction affects the stability of dual-taper modular hip implant components.
Method· Distraction forces required to disassemble head-neck and neck-stem tapers were measured after hand pressing and simulated hammer blows at various impact directions using a custom fixture on a servohydraulic materials test machine.
Result· Axial impaction in line with the taper axis produced the highest distraction forces, while off-axis impacts reduced forces by up to 35%.
Validation· Experimental mechanical testing with controlled impaction directions and distraction force measurements.

通讯: Merrill A. Ritter @ The Center for Hip and Knee Surgery, Mooresville, Indiana - USA
相关性: 该论文仅基于临床评分预测THA失败，未涉及任何生物力学建模或应力遮挡分析。

Result· Pain score ≤30, walk score ≤5, stairs score ≤2, and limp score ≤8 were significant predictors of failure.
Validation· Validated on a large clinical cohort with long-term follow-up.
