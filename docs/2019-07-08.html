Long Bai, Changyan Yi, Xiaohong Chen, Yuanxi Sun, Junfang Zhang Materials 2019-07-08 10.3390/ma12132192
单位: State Key Laboratory of Mechanical Transmission, Chongqing University, Chongqing 400044, China
通讯: Long Bai @ State Key Laboratory of Mechanical Transmission, Chongqing University, Chongqing 400044, China
相关性: 该论文研究了BCC点阵结构的梯度支柱设计以改善力学性能，虽未直接涉及生物医学植入物，但其设计方法和力学测试对AM生物医学支架的刚度匹配和疲劳设计有参考价值。
边界: This paper does not address biomedical implant context, stress shielding, osseointegration, or fatigue testing; it focuses on static compression of lattice structures for general lightweight applications.
动机· 改善BCC点阵结构节点应力集中导致的力学性能不足
方法· 提出梯度支柱设计方法，增加节点圆角半径，获得GBCC单元
结果· GBCC相比BCC初始刚度提高至少38.20%，塑性破坏强度提高至少34.12%

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To improve the poor mechanical properties of BCC lattice structures caused by stress concentration at nodes.
Method· A graded-strut design method is proposed to increase the corner radii at BCC nodes, creating a GBCC unit cell.
Result· GBCC samples exhibit at least 38.20% higher initial stiffness and 34.12% higher plastic failure strength than BCC samples at the same relative density.
Validation· Quasi-static uniaxial compression experiments and finite element analysis are performed and compared.
