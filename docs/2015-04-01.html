English summary & terms
Motivation· Develop a temporal abutment screw that fractures in a controlled manner under occlusal overload to protect osseointegration in immediate loaded single implants.
Method· Seven screw prototypes were tested under dynamic loading (150,000 cycles at 50 N) and static fracture strength tests to compare fracture resistance.
Result· Prototypes 2, 5, and 6 failed during dynamic loading and showed statistically significant differences, suggesting a protective mechanism against occlusal overload.
Validation· Mechanical testing was performed on 112 implant-abutment specimens to validate fracture strength and failure modes.
