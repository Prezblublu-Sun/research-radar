NRSeg: Noise-Resilient Learning for BEV Semantic Segmentation via Driving World Models

Siyu Li, Fei Teng, Yihong Cao, Kailun Yang, Zhiyong Li et al. arXiv preprint 2025-07-05 link
相关性: 该论文专注于自动驾驶的BEV语义分割，与生物制造、植入物或代理建模方向无关。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, implant biomechanics, or any aspect of biomedical engineering; it is a computer vision paper for autonomous driving.
动机· 利用驾驶世界模型生成合成数据增强BEV分割模型鲁棒性
方法· 提出噪声鲁棒学习框架NRSeg，包括一致性度量、双分布预测和局部语义排除模块
结果· 在无监督和半监督任务中mIoU分别提升13.8%和11.4%

English summary & terms
Motivation· To leverage synthetic data from driving world models for improving BEV segmentation robustness while addressing generation noise.
Method· Proposes NRSeg, a noise-resilient framework with a perspective-geometry consistency metric, bi-distribution parallel prediction, and hierarchical local semantic exclusion.
Result· Achieves state-of-the-art performance with 13.8% and 11.4% mIoU improvements on unsupervised and semi-supervised tasks.
Validation· Evaluated on standard autonomous driving datasets, comparing against existing methods.
