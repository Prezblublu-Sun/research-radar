相关性: 该论文提出了一种用于PDE求解的高效神经网络方法，虽未涉及生物力学或植入物设计，但其训练效率提升的思想可迁移到代理模型构建中。

Validation· Validation on Burgers, Helmholtz, and Navier-Stokes equations demonstrates efficiency gains.

Kecheng Chen, Jie Liu, Renjie Wan, Victor Ho-Fun Lee, Varut Vardhanabhuti et al. arXiv preprint 2023-02-26 link

边界: This paper addresses CT image reconstruction domain adaptation, not FEA surrogate modeling, implant stress shielding, or biomechanical design.

Method· We propose a Bayesian uncertainty alignment scheme that jointly minimizes distribution discrepancies in both latent and image spaces between source and target domains.
Result· The proposed method outperforms existing methods in quantitative metrics and visual quality on simulated and clinical datasets.
Validation· Validation was performed on two simulated datasets and one clinical low-dose CT imaging dataset.
