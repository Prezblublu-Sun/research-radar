通讯: Philipp Damm @ Julius Wolff Institute, Charité, Universitätsmedizin Berlin, Berlin, Germany

边界: This paper does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics; it focuses on muscle damage and joint contact forces without FEA or implant design analysis.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To identify risk factors for revision-requiring complications within 3 years after transtrochanteric rotational osteotomy for osteonecrosis of the femoral head.
Method· A retrospective review of 120 patients was conducted, and multivariate logistic regression was used to analyze associations between patient factors and complications.
Result· Previous alcohol abuse was independently associated with postoperative complications (odds ratio 13.5).
Validation· Clinical retrospective data analysis with multivariate statistics.
