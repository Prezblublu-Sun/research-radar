相关性: 该论文提出了一种带不确定量化的物理信息场理论方法，可转移至FEA代理建模，但未直接应用于植入物设计或约束处理。
边界: This paper does not address expensive simulation sampling efficiency, does not handle constraints in optimization, and is not validated on implant or biomechanics problems.
动机· 为物理系统建模提供与数值离散无关的不确定性量化方法

Motivation· To develop a discretization-independent Bayesian inference framework for physical fields that incorporates physical laws and quantifies uncertainty.
Method· Physics-informed Information Field Theory (PIFT) encodes physical laws into functional priors and uses stochastic gradient Langevin dynamics to sample from the joint posterior over field and parameters.
Result· The method remains robust under model-form error and automatically transitions to a regression problem when physics is unreliable.
Validation· Validated on analytical and numerical examples including the Klein-Gordon equation and nonlinear inverse problems with varying degrees of model-form error.

Motivation· To address the risk of fatal pulmonary embolism after total hip arthroplasty and review prophylactic strategies.
Method· Review of evidence from Japanese orthopedic guidelines (2017) and the International Consensus Meeting (2020) on VTE prevention.
Result· Underscores the importance of perioperative VTE prophylaxis and outlines treatment protocols.
Validation· No mechanical or biomechanical validation; purely clinical consensus-based recommendations.
