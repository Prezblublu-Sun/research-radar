Method· Gait analysis was performed on two patient groups post-THA, measuring spatiotemporal and kinematic parameters.
Result· The minimally invasive group showed faster recovery in some gait parameters, but long-term differences were not significant.
Validation· Clinical gait data collection and statistical analysis.
