Motivation· Mimicking hierarchical branched networks such as vasculature remains a challenge in biofabrication.

Validation· Perfusability of the branched interconnected structures is demonstrated by casting in a hydrogel matrix and subsequent perfusion.

Reproducibility, criterion-related validity, and minimal clinically important difference of the stair negotiation test after total Hip arthroplasty

Masanobu Murao, Manabu Nankaku, Takumi Kawano, Koji Goto, Yutaka Kuroda et al. Physiotherapy Theory and Practice 2022-05-17 10.1080/09593985.2022.2078255
单位: Rehabilitation Unit, Kyoto University Hospital, Kyoto, Japan
通讯: Masanobu Murao @ Rehabilitation Unit, Kyoto University Hospital, Kyoto, Japan
相关性: 本文仅涉及全髋关节置换术后患者的功能评估，未涉及股骨假体的生物力学分析、应力遮挡或微动等核心内容。
边界: This paper is a clinical outcome study of stair negotiation test after THA, with no biomechanical modeling, FEA, stress shielding, micromotion, or implant design analysis.
动机· 评估全髋关节置换术后患者爬楼梯能力的客观测试方法
方法· 对60名患者进行爬楼梯测试并计算组内相关系数和最小临床重要差异
结果· 爬楼梯测试具有良好重复性和中等效度，MCID为1.98秒
验证· 通过术前术后评分和相关性分析验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To determine the reproducibility, validity, and minimal clinically important difference of the stair negotiation test for patients undergoing total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Sixty THA patients performed the stair negotiation test pre- and post-surgery; ICC and Spearman correlation were used for reliability and validity, and anchor- and distribution-based methods for MCID.
Result· The SNT showed excellent reproducibility (ICC=0.97) and moderate criterion-related validity (r=0.40), with an anchor-based MCID of 1.98 seconds.
Validation· Clinical validation using preoperative OHSQ7 scores and change scores as anchors.
