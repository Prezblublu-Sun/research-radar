Manufacturability study in laser powder bed fusion of biomedical Ti alloys for orthopedic implants: an investigation of mechanical properties, process-induced porosity and surface roughness

Mudassar Rehman, Yanen Wang, Kashif Ishfaq, Haiou Yang, Ray Tahir Mushtaq et al. Rapid Prototyping Journal 2023-04-24 10.1108/rpj-02-2022-0042
单位: Biological Additive Manufacturing University-Enterprise Joint Research Center of Shaanxi Province, Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi'an, China and Industrial and Manufacturing Engineering Depa
通讯: Mudassar Rehman @ Biological Additive Manufacturing University-Enterprise Joint Research Center of Shaanxi Province, Northwestern Polytech
相关性: 该论文直接研究LPBF制造Ti6Al4V骨科植入物的力学性能与表面粗糙度，并关注弹性模量匹配，但缺少疲劳测试和骨整合评估。
边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing, osseointegration assessment, or direct correlation of porous structure to stress shielding reduction.
动机· 通过工艺参数优化实现医用钛合金植入物的力学性能与表面质量匹配。
方法· 结合Ansys Additive孔隙率仿真与全因子实验，进行多响应优化。
结果· 优化后获得接近皮质骨的弹性模量（12.9 GPa）和低表面粗糙度（2.72 μm）。
验证· 仿真与实验孔隙率对比及验证实验。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To fine-tune LPBF process parameters for biomedical Ti alloys to achieve compressive strength, near-bone elastic modulus, controlled porosity, and sufficient surface roughness for load-bearing implants.
Method· Parametric porosity simulations using Ansys Additive followed by full factorial experiments and multi-response optimization of laser power, scanning speed, and scan orientation.
Result· Optimal parametric sets yielded compressive strength of 672.78 MPa, elastic modulus of 12.932 GPa, porosity of 0.751%, and surface roughness of 2.72 μm.
Validation· Experimental validation of porosity simulations and confirmatory experiments showed ~15% overall improvement between predicted and experimental values.

通讯: Xueting Chen @ Key Laboratory for Anisotropy and Texture of Materials, Ministry of Education, Key Laboratory for Laser Application Tech

边界: This paper focuses on structural alloy steel for non-biomedical applications, with no mention of porous structures, stiffness matching, fatigue, surface roughness, or osseointegration relevant to biomedical implants.

结果· 能量密度100.7 J/mm³时缺陷最少，强度和韧性最佳。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To optimize selective laser melting parameters for achieving high strength and toughness in 24CrNiMoY alloy steel.
Method· The study varied laser energy density and characterized the resulting microstructure and mechanical properties of SLM-fabricated samples.

Validation· Mechanical properties were validated through hardness testing, tensile testing, and Charpy impact testing.
