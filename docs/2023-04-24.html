单位: Biological Additive Manufacturing University-Enterprise Joint Research Center of Shaanxi Province, Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi'an, China and Industrial and Manufacturing Engineering Depa
通讯: Mudassar Rehman @ Biological Additive Manufacturing University-Enterprise Joint Research Center of Shaanxi Province, Northwestern Polytech
相关性: 该论文直接研究LPBF制造Ti6Al4V骨科植入物的力学性能与表面粗糙度，并关注弹性模量匹配，但缺少疲劳测试和骨整合评估。
边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing, osseointegration assessment, or direct correlation of porous structure to stress shielding reduction.

Result· Optimal parametric sets yielded compressive strength of 672.78 MPa, elastic modulus of 12.932 GPa, porosity of 0.751%, and surface roughness of 2.72 μm.
Validation· Experimental validation of porosity simulations and confirmatory experiments showed ~15% overall improvement between predicted and experimental values.
