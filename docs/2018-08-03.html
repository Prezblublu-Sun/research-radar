Defects-dictated tensile properties of selective laser melted Ti-6Al-4V

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Transferable Skim only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Thomas Voisin, Nicholas P. Calta, Saad A. Khairallah, Jean‐Baptiste Forien, Levente Balogh et al. Materials & Design 2018-08-03 10.1016/j.matdes.2018.08.004
单位: Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, Livermore, CA 94550, USA
通讯: Thomas Voisin @ Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, Livermore, CA 94550, USA  |  Yinmin Wang @ Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, Livermore, CA 94550, USA
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM Ti-6Al-4V的缺陷与拉伸性能关系，虽未涉及多孔结构或应力遮挡，但工艺-缺陷-性能关系对AM生物医学植入物的疲劳和力学性能评估有参考价值。
边界: This paper does not cover porous structures, stress shielding, stiffness matching, fatigue testing, or osseointegration; it focuses on static tensile properties of dense SLM Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 揭示SLM Ti-6Al-4V中缺陷对拉伸性能的主导作用
方法· 通过实验表征缺陷类型与分布，结合拉伸测试分析缺陷-性能关联
结果· 缺陷（如气孔、未熔合）显著降低延伸率，但对强度影响较小
验证· 实验拉伸测试与断口形貌观察
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To elucidate how process-induced defects govern the tensile properties of selective laser melted Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Characterization of defect types and distributions via microscopy and X-ray computed tomography, correlated with uniaxial tensile testing.
Result· Porosity and lack-of-fusion defects drastically reduce ductility while having a minor effect on ultimate tensile strength.
Validation· Experimental tensile tests and fractographic analysis.
