相关性: 该论文通过增材制造实现局部增韧和疲劳寿命提升，其方法可迁移至生物医用植入物的疲劳性能优化，但未涉及多孔结构或应力遮挡。

Result· Experimental fatigue crack growth tests demonstrated up to 57% local reduction in crack growth rate and up to 13% extension in fatigue life, primarily in rounded-end ligament specimens, with crack closure and bridging identified as dominant toughening mechanisms.
Validation· The findings are validated through experimental fatigue crack growth testing on LPBF-fabricated specimens, including analysis of ligament geometry effects and surface roughness limitations.
