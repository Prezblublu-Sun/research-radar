边界: This paper focuses on active learning for classification, not on surrogate modeling, expensive simulation sampling, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification, or biomechanics applications.
动机· 探究主动学习何时能真正提高数据效率。

Validation· Validated on 21 benchmark datasets from OpenML with accompanying asymptotic theoretical analysis.
