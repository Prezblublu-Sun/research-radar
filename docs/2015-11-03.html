单位: Orthopaedic Research Laboratory, Department of Orthopaedic Surgery and Traumatology, Odense University Hospital, Institute of Clinical Research, University of Southern Denmark, Odense C, Denmark
通讯: Ming Ding @ Orthopaedic Research Laboratory, Department of Orthopaedic Surgery and Traumatology, Odense University Hospital, Institu
相关性: 该论文主要关注骨移植材料和生物反应器激活的颗粒对植入物固定的影响，未涉及LPBF制造、多孔结构设计或疲劳性能。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no porous structure design for stress shielding, no fatigue testing, and no surface roughness or as-built morphology characterization.
动机· 评估生物反应器激活的颗粒对植入物固定和早期骨形成的影响
方法· 在羊股骨远端制造缺损，植入钛钉并填充不同骨移植材料，6周后评估
结果· 生物反应器激活颗粒未显著优于同种异体骨，且未带来额外益处

边界: No porous structure, stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration; the focus is on tooling for automotive injection molding, not biomedical implants.
动机· 比较增材与减材制造在汽车工模具生产中的可行性。
