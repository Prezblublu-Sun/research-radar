英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the dose-dependent association between early postoperative opioid use and complications after primary total hip and knee arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective analysis of 1,525,985 patients from the Premier Healthcare Database, stratified into quintiles by morphine milligram equivalents (MMEs) within the first 3 postoperative days, using multivariate logistic regression.
Result· Higher opioid exposure (>172 MMEs) was associated with significantly increased odds of periprosthetic joint infection, venous thromboembolism, and other complications.
Validation· Large-scale database analysis with multivariate adjustment for confounders.
