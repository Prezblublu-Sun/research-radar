Controlling the porosity of 316L stainless steel parts manufactured via the powder bed fusion process

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Skim only none Exp. validation
Abdullah Yahia AlFaify, James Hughes, Keith Ridgway Rapid Prototyping Journal 2018-11-21 10.1108/rpj-11-2017-0226
单位: Department of Industrial Engineering, King Saud University, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia and The Advanced Manufacturing Research Centre with Boeing, The University of Sheffield, Sheffield, UK
通讯: Abdullah Yahia AlFaify @ Department of Industrial Engineering, King Saud University, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia and The Advanced Manufacturing Research
相关性: 该论文专注于316L不锈钢的LPBF工艺参数优化以降低孔隙率，未涉及生物医学植入物所需的应力屏蔽、多孔结构或疲劳性能。
边界: No explicit link to biomedical implants, no porous structure for stiffness matching, no fatigue testing, no surface roughness or osseointegration assessment.
动机· 减小LPBF工艺制造的316L不锈钢件孔隙率，提高致密度。
方法· 采用响应面法（RSM）设计实验，系统研究点距、曝光时间等参数对密度的影响。
结果· 发现点距、曝光时间和层厚显著影响密度，并建立了精确的密度预测回归模型。
验证· 通过实验测量密度验证回归模型预测准确性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To achieve high-density 316L stainless steel parts by systematically studying the impact of pulsed-laser powder bed fusion process parameters on porosity.
Method· Response surface methodology was employed to design experiments and analyze the effects of point distance, exposure time, hatching distance, and layer thickness on part density.
Result· Point distance, exposure time, and layer thickness were identified as critical factors, and a regression model was developed that accurately predicts part density.
Validation· Experimental density measurements using the Archimedes method validated the regression model's predictions.
