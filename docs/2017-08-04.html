Heat Transfer Modeling of Spent Nuclear Fuel Using Uncertainty Quantification and Polynomial Chaos Expansion

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Imane Khalil, Quinn Pratt, Harrison Schmachtenberger, Roger Ghanem Journal of Heat Transfer 2017-08-04 10.1115/1.4037501
单位: Shiley-Marcos Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of San Diego, 5998 Alcala Park, San Diego, CA 92110
通讯: Imane Khalil @ Shiley-Marcos Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of San Diego, 5998 Alcala Park, San Diego, CA 92110
相关性: 该论文展示了多项式混沌展开(PCE)在核废料热传递建模中的不确定性量化应用，其方法可迁移至生物力学植入物设计的代理建模。
边界: This paper does not address biomedical implant design, contact mechanics, or structural FEA; it focuses on heat transfer in nuclear engineering.
动机· 解决核废料热模拟中参数不确定性的高效计算问题
方法· 采用多项式混沌展开构建代理模型，量化十输入参数对温度分布的影响
结果· 预测燃料组件中心最大平均温度为462K，实现连续参数范围的敏感性分析
验证· 通过二维模型与二阶PCE的数值基准验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To efficiently quantify the impact of input parameter uncertainty on heat transfer simulations of spent nuclear fuel assemblies.
Method· A polynomial chaos expansion (PCE) surrogate model is developed to map ten uncertain input parameters to the temperature distribution in a 2D model of a 9×9 BWR fuel assembly.
Result· The maximum mean center temperature is predicted as 462 K, and sensitivity analysis identifies the most influential parameters.
Validation· The PCE surrogate is validated against a numerical benchmark case, demonstrating accuracy for complex heat transfer problems.
