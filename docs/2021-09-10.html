Tunable metacrylated hyaluronic acid-based hybrid bioinks for stereolithography 3D bioprinting

Rafaeal Hossain Rakin, Hitendra Kumar, Ashna Rajeev, Giovanniantonio Natale, Fréderic Ménard et al. Biofabrication 2021-09-10 10.1088/1758-5090/ac25cb
单位: School of Engineering, The University of British Columbia, Kelowna, BC V1V 1V7, Canada
通讯: Rafaeal Hossain Rakin @ School of Engineering, The University of British Columbia, Kelowna, BC V1V 1V7, Canada
相关性: 该论文仅涉及生物墨水材料的合成与表征，未使用任何人工智能方法，不直接相关于AI生物打印研究方向。
边界: This paper focuses solely on bioink material synthesis and characterization, lacking any AI/ML integration, data-driven modeling, or autonomous decision-making in the bioprinting workflow.
动机· 开发可调性的甲基丙烯酰化透明质酸基杂化生物墨水用于立体光刻3D生物打印。
方法· 通过改变合成时间调控甲基丙烯酰化程度，并与明胶甲基丙烯酰基复合制备杂化墨水。
结果· 杂化墨水相比纯MeHA刚度提升55%，支持细胞粘附且保持高活性。
验证· 通过流变表征和细胞活性实验验证力学性能与生物相容性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop tunable methacrylated hyaluronic acid-based hybrid bioinks with enhanced mechanical and biological properties for stereolithography 3D bioprinting.
Method· Synthesized MeHA with varying degrees of methacrylation by controlling synthesis duration and combined it with gelatin methacryloyl to form a hybrid bioink.
Result· The hybrid bioink exhibited a 55% increase in stiffness compared to MeHA alone, supported cell adhesion, and maintained high cell viability.
Validation· Rheological characterization and in vitro cell viability assays validated mechanical performance and biocompatibility.

单位: Applied Laser and Photonics Group, University of Applied Sciences Aschaffenburg, Wuerzburger Str. 45, 63743 Aschaffenburg, Germany

边界: This paper does not link porous structure to stress shielding or stiffness matching, lacks mechanical testing (static or fatigue), and does not report surface roughness, as-built morphology, or osseointegration.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To achieve tunable anisotropic permeability in selective laser melted structures through process parameter control.
Method· A single-line scan strategy and triangular-based structures are used to vary pore size and directionality, with a mathematical correlation between process parameters, porosity, and permeability.
Result· The proposed method enables fabrication of structures with adjustable anisotropic permeability, where flow ratios can be controlled from 0.25 to equal bidirectional flow.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed by manufacturing structures and measuring permeability to verify the mathematical model.

通讯: Yanyan Chen @ Key Laboratory for Nano-Bio Interface Research, Division of Nanobiomedicine, Suzhou Institute of Nano-Tech and Nano-Bion  |  Jianwu Dai @ Key Laboratory for Nano-Bio Interface Research, Division of Nanobiomedicine, Suzhou Institute of Nano-Tech and Nano-Bion

Prediction of Epitaxial Grain Growth in Single-Track Laser Melting of IN718 Using Integrated Finite Element and Cellular Automaton Approach

Amir Reza Ansari Dezfoli, Yu‐Lung Lo, M. Mohsin Raza Materials 2021-09-10 10.3390/ma14185202
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, National Cheng Kung University, No.1, Daxue Rd., East Dist., Tainan City 701, Taiwan
通讯: Yu‐Lung Lo @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, National Cheng Kung University, No.1, Daxue Rd., East Dist., Tainan City 701, Taiw
相关性: 该论文涉及增材制造工艺模拟，但材料为镍基高温合金，非生物医用材料（如钛合金、镁合金），未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡、疲劳或骨整合。
边界: This paper focuses on grain growth simulation for IN718 superalloy, not biomedical materials; no porous structure, stress shielding, fatigue testing, surface roughness, or osseointegration experiments.
动机· 预测SLM加工IN718合金的外延晶粒生长模式
方法· 集成三维有限元和元胞自动机模型模拟单道SLM过程
结果· 热输入增加使平均晶粒尺寸增大20%，基底晶粒减小使晶粒尺寸减小17%
验证· 通过模拟结果与文献趋势对比验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To predict epitaxial grain growth in single-track selective laser melting of IN718 alloy through integrated simulation.
Method· A combined three-dimensional finite element and cellular automaton model was developed to simulate the SLM process considering laser scattering, melt surface evolution, and heterogeneous nucleation.
Result· Increasing heat input from 250 to 500 J/m increased average grain size by ~20%, while reducing initial substrate grain size by 50% decreased grain size by 17%.
Validation· The model was validated by comparing simulated grain morphology and size trends with published experimental observations.
