Thermohydraulic performance prediction of an additively manufactured minichannel gas-to-gas heat exchanger

Vishwjeet Choudhary, A. M. Aneesh, Chennu Ranganayakulu Scientific Reports 2026-05-07 10.1038/s41598-026-51667-9
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Birla Institute of Technology and Science, Pilani, 333031, Rajasthan, India
通讯: Vishwjeet Choudhary @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Birla Institute of Technology and Science, Pilani, 333031, Rajasthan, India
相关性: 该论文研究增材制造微型通道换热器的热工水力性能，与生物医学植入物的应力屏蔽、疲劳或骨整合无关。
边界: This paper focuses on heat exchanger thermohydraulics, not on porous structures for stress shielding, LPBF fabrication with mechanical testing, fatigue, or osseointegration relevant to biomedical implants.
动机· 研究增材制造微型通道换热器的热工水力性能，考虑表面粗糙度影响。
方法· 实验测量与数值模拟结合，开发经验修正因子。
结果· 过渡SST模型预测最接近，提出独立粗糙度参数。
验证· 实验与数值对比验证修正因子有效性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To characterize the thermohydraulic performance of additively manufactured minichannel heat exchangers and account for surface roughness effects.
Method· Experimental measurements using a nitrogen-nitrogen test facility and numerical simulations with laminar, realizable k-ε, and transition SST models, along with empirical correction factors.
Result· Transition SST model showed best agreement; independent roughness parameters for momentum and heat transfer were proposed.
Validation· Validated against experimental data with average percentage differences of 66.6% and 51.7% for friction factor and Nusselt number.

Pre-operative two-step test predicts reciprocal stair-climbing performance one week after total hip arthroplasty

Naoki Higashijima, Masanori Fujii, Saki Matsumoto, Yasuo Takei, Koichiro Kishikawa et al. Journal of Joint Surgery and Research 2026-05-07 10.1016/j.jjoisr.2026.04.004
相关性: 该论文关注全髋关节置换术后临床功能恢复，未涉及生物力学建模或植入物设计。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, no implant stress/strain analysis, no micromotion or contact mechanics, no FEA.
动机· 预测全髋置换术后一周爬楼梯能力。
方法· 术前两步测试评估患者功能。
结果· 术前测试可预测术后爬楼梯表现。
验证· 临床患者数据验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To predict stair-climbing performance one week after total hip arthroplasty using a simple preoperative test.
Method· A two-step test was administered preoperatively and correlated with postoperative stair-climbing ability.
Result· The preoperative two-step test significantly predicted stair-climbing performance at one week post-surgery.
Validation· Clinical validation with patient cohort data.
