English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the impact of patient education on lifestyle adherence after total hip arthroplasty in a Moroccan context with traditional habits.
Method· A retrospective study of 67 patients (70 hips) using PMA, WOMAC, Parker scores and a custom adherence questionnaire.
Result· Significant functional improvement was observed despite low adherence to Western lifestyle precautions, highlighting the need for culturally adapted education.
Validation· No biomechanical validation; only clinical outcome scores and survey data.
