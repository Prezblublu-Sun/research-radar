Squeaking and other noises in patients with ceramic-on-ceramic total hip arthroplasty

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore clinical Exp. validation Patient-specific
Fabio Baruffaldi, Rosaria Mecca, Susanna Stea, Alina Beraudi, Barbara Bordini et al. Hip International 2019-07-21 10.1177/1120700019864233
单位: Laboratory of Medical Technology, IRCCS Istituto Ortopedico Rizzoli, Bologna, Italy
通讯: Fabio Baruffaldi @ Laboratory of Medical Technology, IRCCS Istituto Ortopedico Rizzoli, Bologna, Italy
相关性: 该论文研究陶瓷对陶瓷全髋关节置换术后噪音现象，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡、微动或接触力学建模。
边界: This paper focuses on clinical noise analysis and bearing wear, with no biomechanical modeling, no stress shielding quantification, no micromotion, and no implant-bone interface mechanics.
动机· 分析陶瓷对陶瓷全髋关节置换术后噪音的物理特性及其与假体失效的关系。
方法· 对46例噪音患者进行影像学和关节液分析，按陶瓷内衬骨折风险分组，标准化记录噪音事件。
结果· 翻修组患者步行时尖啸声峰值频率显著高于对照组，小头易发短噪音，大头易发尖啸声。
验证· 临床患者分组对比，翻修病例与对照组噪音特征差异分析。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To characterize the physical properties of noise in ceramic-on-ceramic total hip arthroplasty and evaluate its prognostic value for bearing failure.
Method· 46 patients with noisy CoC bearings were assessed via X-ray, CT, and joint fluid analysis, classified into high- and low-risk groups for ceramic liner fracture, and noise events were recorded and analyzed using a standardized protocol.
Result· Peak squeaking frequency during walking was significantly higher in revised patients; small heads (28 mm) were associated with short-noise, while large heads (≥32 mm) with squeaking.
Validation· Clinical comparison between revised cases and non-revised controls, with statistical analysis of noise characteristics.

Two-stage total hip arthroplasty following skeletal traction after extensive soft tissue release for severe limb-length discrepancy

Soong Joon Lee, Kang Sup Yoon Hip International 2019-07-21 10.1177/1120700019865742
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery SMG-SNU Boramae Medical Center, Seoul, Republic of Korea
通讯: Kang Sup Yoon @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery SMG-SNU Boramae Medical Center, Seoul, Republic of Korea
相关性: 该论文仅涉及临床手术流程，未包含任何生物力学建模或应力分析。
边界: No biomechanical modeling, no FEA, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no implant design or contact mechanics.
动机· 评估两阶段全髋置换术治疗严重肢体不等长的效果
方法· 对24例患者行广泛软组织松解后骨骼牵引，再行二期全髋置换
结果· 肢体不等长从5.0cm改善至1.4cm，Harris评分提高，无永久神经并发症
验证· 临床随访平均6.3年
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the outcomes of two-stage total hip arthroplasty following skeletal traction after extensive soft tissue release for severe limb-length discrepancy.
Method· 24 hips underwent extensive soft tissue release, 2-week skeletal traction, then second-stage THA; 7 required subtrochanteric shortening osteotomy.
Result· Limb-length discrepancy improved from 5.0 to 1.4 cm, Harris Hip Score improved from 57.1 to 88.3, with no permanent neurological complications.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with mean 6.3 years.
