英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To examine the correlation between PROMIS computer adaptive tests and the modified Harris Hip Score in patients undergoing primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Prospective enrollment of 48 patients who completed PROMIS and mHHS assessments preoperatively and at 3, 6, 12, and 52 weeks postoperatively.
Result· Strong and consistent correlations (r=0.73-0.80) were observed between PROMIS and mHHS at all postoperative time points.
Validation· Clinical validation with prospectively collected patient-reported outcome data.

边界: This paper is a clinical retrospective study on acetabular fracture fixation, with no biomechanical modeling, no femoral stem implant, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, and no FEA.
动机· 评估内侧支撑钢板在髋臼骨折中的临床效果。
方法· 回顾性分析46例接受内侧支撑钢板固定的髋臼骨折患者。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the clinical outcomes of medial buttressing for quadrilateral plate fixation in acetabular fractures.
Method· A retrospective study of 46 patients with acute or periprosthetic acetabular fractures treated with a specific medial buttressing implant.
Result· Medial buttressing was successful in all but one patient, with a low secondary THA rate (13.5%) but significant decline in quality of life.
Validation· Clinical follow-up at median 3 years, no biomechanical testing or FEA validation.
