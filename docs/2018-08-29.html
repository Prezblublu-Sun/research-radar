英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate trends in postoperative DVT prophylaxis and DVT rates within 6 months after THA or TKA using a large claims database.
Method· Retrospective analysis of Truven MarketScan databases (2004-2013) for patients undergoing THA/TKA, collecting medication claims and DVT outcomes.
Result· Warfarin was most prescribed, but aspirin, fondaparinux, and rivaroxaban were associated with significantly lower DVT risk within 6 months.
Validation· Multivariate regression analysis on 369,483 patients from a large claims database.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To characterize the incidence, timing, and risk factors of emergency department visits and inpatient readmissions within 90 days after lower-extremity arthroplasty.
Method· Multivariate logistic regression was applied to New York State Inpatient and Emergency Department Databases for 124,234 TKA and 76,411 THA patients from 2009 to 2013.
Result· 13.79% of patients required unscheduled acute care, with 61.05% of readmissions occurring within the first week post-discharge.
Validation· Retrospective cohort study using large-scale administrative databases.
