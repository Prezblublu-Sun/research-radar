通讯: Wei‐Xin Ren @ School of Civil Engineering; Hefei University of Technology; Hefei Anhui Province 230009 China
相关性: 该论文提出的基于高斯过程模型的不确定性量化方法可迁移至植入物力学仿真中的代理建模，但未涉及生物力学或植入物设计。
边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, contact mechanics, or any biomedical application; it focuses on general uncertainty quantification for complex systems.
动机· 高效计算任意分布参数下复杂系统输出的均值和方差
方法· 基于高斯过程模型，将高维积分分解为一维积分，采用解析或高斯求积计算
结果· 所提方法在精度和效率上均优于蒙特卡洛模拟

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To efficiently compute the mean and variance of complex system outputs under arbitrary parameter probability distributions.
Method· A Gaussian process model is used to derive closed-form expressions for mean and variance by decomposing high-dimensional integrals into one-dimensional integrals, computed analytically for uniform/normal distributions or via Gaussian quadrature otherwise.
Result· The proposed method achieves accuracy comparable to Monte Carlo simulation with significantly reduced computational cost.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks with various distributions are compared against Monte Carlo simulation.
