单位: IDMEC, Instituto Superior Técnico, Universidade de Lisboa, Lisbon, Portugal
通讯: Paulo R. Fernandes @ IDMEC, Instituto Superior Técnico, Universidade de Lisboa, Lisbon, Portugal. Electronic address: paulo.rui.fernandes@tec
相关性: 该论文研究了TPMS支架的渗透性，与用户关注的应力遮挡和疲劳性能无直接关联，但多孔结构设计方法可借鉴。

Validation· Validation was limited to numerical simulation without experimental verification.
