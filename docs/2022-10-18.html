Equispaced Fourier representations for efficient Gaussian process regression from a billion data points

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark
Philip Greengard, Manas Rachh, Alex Barnett arXiv preprint 2022-10-18 link
相关性: 该论文提出了一种高效的傅里叶高斯过程回归方法，可扩展到十亿数据点，其加速思想可迁移到生物力学代理模型的构建中。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification for implant design, or any biomedical context.
动机· 解决大规模高斯过程回归的计算瓶颈，实现十亿数据点的高效推理。
方法· 利用等距傅里叶网格近似平移不变核，通过FFT加速矩阵向量乘法。
结果· 在二维Matérn回归中，标准桌面2分钟处理10^9数据点，精度10^{-3}。
验证· 数值实验对比平方指数和Matérn核，与秩结构求解器比较加速比。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To overcome the computational bottleneck of Gaussian process regression for massive datasets, enabling inference from billions of data points.
Method· Approximates translation-invariant kernels using complex exponentials on an equispaced Fourier grid, leveraging FFT for O(M log M) matrix-vector products.
Result· Achieves 2D Matérn-3/2 regression from N=10^9 points in 2 minutes on a desktop with posterior mean accuracy 10^{-3}.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on squared-exponential and Matérn kernels in 1D-3D, showing 1-2 orders of magnitude speedup over rank-structured solvers.
