Effect of Alkali-Acid-Heat Chemical Surface Treatment on Electron Beam Melted Porous Titanium and Its Apatite Forming Ability

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only in_vitro Exp. validation
Suzan Bsat, Saber Amin Yavari, Maximilian Munsch, E.R. Valstar, Amir A. Zadpoor Materials 2015-04-08 10.3390/ma8041612
单位: Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Carleton University, 1125 Colonel by Drive, Ottawa, ON K1S 5B6, Canada
通讯: Suzan Bsat @ Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Carleton University, 1125 Colonel by Drive, Ottawa, ON K1S 5B6, Cana
相关性: 该论文涉及多孔钛合金的表面生物功能化处理，但未涉及应力屏蔽、疲劳性能或力学测试，与用户研究方向关联度较低。
边界: This paper focuses on surface chemical treatment and apatite formation of EBM porous titanium, but does not include LPBF fabrication, mechanical testing (static or fatigue), stress shielding analysis, or osseointegration evaluation.
动机· 为EBM多孔钛合金表面生物功能化提供优化碱酸热处理参数。
方法· 采用不同浓度和时间的碱酸热处理改性EBM多孔Ti6Al4V表面，并通过SBF浸泡评估磷灰石形成能力。
结果· 碱酸热处理成功在表面形成纳米拓扑结构和二氧化钛层，促进磷灰石沉积。
验证· 通过SEM和EDS表征表面形貌和化学成分，SBF浸泡验证磷灰石形成。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To optimize alkali-acid-heat treatment parameters for bio-functionalizing the surface of electron beam melted porous titanium alloy for orthopedic applications.
Method· EBM porous Ti6Al4V samples were subjected to alkali-acid-heat treatment with varying alkali concentrations and immersion times, followed by simulated body fluid immersion to assess apatite formation.
Result· The treatment created nano-topographical features (200-300 nm) and a titania layer, enabling apatite deposition on all treated samples except those with the mildest treatment.
Validation· Surface morphology and chemistry were characterized by SEM and EDS before and after SBF testing.

Validation· Porosity, microstructure, and impurity uptake were characterized to optimize the sintering cycle.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To report rare cases of neurological deterioration induced by sitting after cervicothoracic posterior decompression with instrumented fusion.
Method· Two case descriptions including surgical details and postoperative neurological assessments.
Result· After one month of strict bed rest, patients showed no paralysis when sitting.
Validation· Case report without experimental or numerical validation.
