English summary & terms
Motivation· To determine whether cerclage wires used for extended trochanteric osteotomy fixation in two-stage revision THA become colonized with bacteria.
Method· Sonication of explanted cerclage wires and spacers was performed to detect bacterial contamination or colonization.
Result· Only one case of contamination and no colonization was found on cerclage wires, suggesting systematic exchange may not be necessary.
Validation· Clinical case series with 26 patients and sonication culture.
