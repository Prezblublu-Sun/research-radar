Three-dimensional plotting of a cell-laden alginate/methylcellulose blend: towards biofabrication of tissue engineering constructs with clinically relevant dimensions

单位: Centre for Translational Bone, Joint and Soft Tissue Research; University Hospital Carl Gustav Carus and Faculty of Medicine, Technische Universität Dresden; Germany
通讯: Anja Lode @ Centre for Translational Bone, Joint and Soft Tissue Research; University Hospital Carl Gustav Carus and Faculty of Medi
相关性: 该论文不涉及任何人工智能或机器学习方法，与AI辅助生物打印方向无关。
边界: No AI or machine learning methods are used; it is purely a materials science study for bioprinting without any computational intelligence.
动机· 开发适合细胞包埋和大尺寸3D打印的海藻酸盐基材料。
方法· 在海藻酸盐溶液中添加甲基纤维素以提高粘度，实现精确3D绘图。
结果· 所得支架具有高弹性、稳定性和微孔性，细胞存活率高。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop an alginate-based material suitable for cell embedding and fabrication of large 3D tissue engineering constructs via 3D plotting.
Method· Methylcellulose was added to a low-concentration alginate solution to enhance viscosity, enabling accurate deposition without high technical efforts.
Result· The resulting scaffolds exhibited high elasticity, stability, and enhanced microporosity, with embedded cells showing high viability and maintained differentiation potential.
Validation· In vitro cell viability and adipogenic differentiation assays were performed to evaluate the material's suitability for cell embedding.

Process optimisation of selective laser melting using energy density model for nickel based superalloys

L. N. Carter, X. Wang, N. Read, Raja H.U. Khan, M. Aristizabal et al. Materials Science and Technology 2015-07-22 10.1179/1743284715y.0000000108
单位: School of Metallurgy and Materials, The University of Birmingham, Edgbaston B15 2TT, UK
通讯: Moataz M. Attallah @ School of Metallurgy and Materials, The University of Birmingham, Edgbaston B15 2TT, UK
相关性: 该论文专注于镍基高温合金的选择性激光熔化工艺优化，与生物医学植入物的多孔结构、应力遮挡、疲劳或骨整合等方向无关。
边界: This paper does not cover biomedical implants, porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or osseointegration; it focuses on SLM process optimization for nickel superalloys.
动机· 探究能量密度参数在镍基高温合金SLM工艺优化中的适用性。
方法· 对比γ'和γ'/γ''强化镍基高温合金，分析能量密度与致密度及裂纹密度的关系。
结果· 致密度随能量密度增加而提高，但裂纹密度与能量密度无直接关联。
验证· 通过实验测量样品致密度（孔隙面积）和裂纹密度进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the applicability of the energy density parameter for process optimisation in selective laser melting of Ni-based superalloys.
Method· A comparative study was conducted on γ' and γ'/γ'' strengthened Ni superalloys, correlating energy density with build density and cracking density.
Result· Build density increased with energy density, but no direct correlation was found between energy density and cracking density.
Validation· Experimental measurements of void area (density) and crack density were used for validation.
