Prediction of Damage Formation in Total Hip Arthroplasty using Finite Element Method

Abdul Halim Abdullah, Mitsugu Todo Kyushu University Institutional Repository (QIR) (Kyushu University) 2015-02-01 10.15017/1485124
相关性: 论文使用CT数据建立患者特异性THA有限元模型，预测冲击载荷下的骨损伤模式，对理解假体周围骨折有参考价值。
边界: This paper does not quantify stress shielding in Gruen zones or micromotion with contact/friction modeling; it focuses on damage formation under impact rather than the biomechanical metrics central to hip implant evaluation.
动机· 预测全髋关节置换术后冲击载荷下的骨损伤模式，避免假体周围骨折。

结果· 不同载荷配置下骨损伤模式不同，侧向弯曲条件导致最多单元失效。
验证· 通过数值模拟结果对比不同工况，未进行实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To predict bone damage patterns under high impact loading in total hip arthroplasty patients to prevent periprosthetic fractures.
Method· A CT-based patient-specific nonlinear finite element model of a femoral shaft with a hip prosthesis was developed and subjected to isometric impact loads normalized to body weight.
Result· Lateral bending condition produced the highest number of element failures, and damage at the bone-implant interface indicated possible loosening.
Validation· The study relies solely on simulation-based comparison of loading configurations without experimental validation.
