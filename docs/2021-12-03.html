英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate whether enhanced recovery after surgery (ERAS) practices can improve postoperative recovery and reduce length of stay in a private hospital setting for primary total hip or knee arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective cohort study comparing 111 patients in an ERAS group (2017-2018) with 117 patients in a conventional group (2012-2014) undergoing unilateral primary THA or TKA.
Result· The ERAS group had significantly shorter mean length of stay (3.28 vs 5.16 days, p<0.001) and a higher proportion discharged by postoperative day 3 (77.5% vs 13.7%).
Validation· Statistical analysis of clinical data from a single private hospital.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the primary stability of two cementless posterior-stabilized femoral knee components for predicting bone ingrowth potential.
Method· Micromotions at the implant-bone interface were measured using digital image correlation in six cadaveric femur pairs under two loading conditions, and push-off forces were recorded.
Result· The Attune PS implant exhibited lower micromotions and higher push-off force, indicating superior initial fixation compared to the Triathlon PS design.
Validation· Cadaveric experimental validation with comparison to clinical outcomes and previous cruciate-retaining implant results.
