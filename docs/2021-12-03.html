Recent advances in Ti-6Al-4V additively manufactured by selective laser melting for biomedical implants: Prospect development

A.N. Aufa, Mohamad Zaki Hassan, Zarini Ismail Journal of Alloys and Compounds 2021-12-03 10.1016/j.jallcom.2021.163072
单位: Razak Faculty of Technology and Informatics, Universiti Teknologi Malaysia, Jalan Sultan Yahya Petra, 54100 Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia
通讯: Mohamad Zaki Hassan @ Razak Faculty of Technology and Informatics, Universiti Teknologi Malaysia, Jalan Sultan Yahya Petra, 54100 Kuala Lumpur
相关性: 这篇综述全面总结了Ti-6Al-4V的SLM制造及其在生物医学植入物中的应用，涵盖了多孔结构、力学性能（包括疲劳）和表面形态，与用户研究方向直接相关。
边界: This is a review paper without original experimental data or quantitative finite element analysis; it does not provide new stress shielding or fatigue testing results.
动机· 综述SLM制备Ti-6Al-4V在生物医用植入物领域的最新进展与发展前景
方法· 系统回顾SLM工艺参数、微观组织、力学性能及生物学性能的相关研究
结果· 总结了SLM制备Ti-6Al-4V植入物在疲劳、腐蚀和骨整合方面的关键发现
验证· 无原始实验，基于文献回顾与归纳
English summary & terms
Motivation· To review recent advances and future prospects of selective laser melting (SLM) for fabricating Ti-6Al-4V biomedical implants, covering process, microstructure, and properties.
Method· A systematic literature review of SLM parameters, post-treatments, mechanical behavior (including fatigue), surface modifications, and biological responses.
Result· Identified key correlations between SLM parameters, microstructure, and implant performance, with emphasis on fatigue strength and osseointegration.
Validation· No original experiments; synthesis of findings from multiple published studies.

3D Bioprinting of Pectin-Cellulose Nanofibers Multicomponent Bioinks

Matteo Pitton, Andrea Fiorati, Silvia Buscemi, Lucio Melone, Silvia Farè et al. Frontiers in Bioengineering and Biotechnology 2021-12-03 10.3389/fbioe.2021.732689
单位: Department of Chemistry, Materials, and Chemical Engineering "G. Natta", Politecnico di Milano, Milan, Italy
通讯: Silvia Farè @ Department of Chemistry, Materials, and Chemical Engineering "G. Natta", Politecnico di Milano, Milan, Italy
相关性: 该论文仅关注生物墨水的配方开发，完全未涉及人工智能或机器学习在生物打印中的应用。
边界: This paper does not integrate AI/ML into any bioprinting workflow stage and lacks algorithmic modeling or autonomous decision-making.
动机· 克服果胶粘度低限制生物打印的难题
方法· 将果胶与TEMPO氧化纤维素纳米纤维复合制备多组分生物墨水
结果· 优化墨水可打印性并保持>80%细胞活性，支架弹性模量1.8 kPa
验证· 流变学测试、打印性评估及体外细胞培养验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To overcome the low viscosity limitation of pectin for 3D bioprinting applications.
Method· Multicomponent bioinks were formulated by combining pectin with TEMPO-oxidized cellulose nanofibers (TOCNFs) and rheologically optimized.
Result· The optimized bioink achieved high printability, stable scaffolds with elastic modulus 1.8 kPa, and >80% cell viability with increasing metabolic activity.
Validation· Rheological characterization, printability tests, and in vitro cell culture with L929 fibroblasts.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a vertical embedded extrusion bioprinting strategy capable of fabricating liner-shaped tissues such as intestinal villi and hair follicles.
Method· A uniaxial or coaxial nozzle system was used for vertical embedded extrusion bioprinting, and process parameters were optimized.
Result· Bioprinted intestinal villi showed proper Caco-2 cell proliferation and ZO-1/villin expression, and hair follicle structures with keratinized keratinocytes and dermal papilla cells were formed.

Selective laser melting with changing input energy periodically for production of partially hardened laminate structure in H13 steel

Hajime Miyauchi, Hiroaki Matsumoto, Kozo Yokota Journal of Manufacturing Processes 2021-12-03 10.1016/j.jmapro.2021.11.028
单位: Graduate School of Engineering, Kagawa University, Takamatsu 761-0396, Japan
通讯: Hajime Miyauchi @ Graduate School of Engineering, Kagawa University, Takamatsu 761-0396, Japan
相关性: 该论文研究H13工具钢的选择性激光熔化工艺，与生物医学植入物或生物打印无关。
边界: This paper does not address biomedical implant design, porous structures for osseointegration, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or surface roughness for biomedical applications; it focuses on SLM parameter optimization for steel laminate structures.
动机· 通过周期性变化激光能量实现H13钢分层硬度梯度。
方法· 在SLM过程中交替高低能量输入以形成部分硬化层状结构。
结果· 成功制备了具有可控硬度梯度的层状钢件。
验证· 通过硬度测试和微观结构表征验证了层状效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To produce partially hardened laminate structures in H13 steel by periodically varying laser energy during selective laser melting.
Method· Alternating high and low energy inputs were applied in a layer-wise fashion during SLM to create a hardness gradient.
Result· Laminate structures with controlled hardness variations were successfully fabricated.
Validation· The laminate structure and hardness gradient were validated through microhardness measurements and microstructural analysis.

Lipid Nanocarriers-Loaded Nanocomposite as a Suitable Platform to Release Antibacterial and Antioxidant Agents for Immediate Dental Implant Placement Restorative Treatment.

Giuseppe Angellotti, Alessandro Presentato, Denise Murgia, Giulia Di Prima, Fabio D'Agostino et al. Pharmaceutics 2021-12-03 10.3390/pharmaceutics13122072
单位: Dipartimento di Discipline Chirurgiche, Oncologiche e Stomatologiche (DICHIRONS), Università degli Studi di Palermo, 90127 Palermo, Italy.; Dipartimento di Scienze e Tecnologie Biologiche Chimiche e F
通讯: Viviana De Caro @ Dipartimento di Scienze e Tecnologie Biologiche Chimiche e Farmaceutiche (STEBICEF), Università degli Studi di Palermo, [推断]
相关性: 与用户研究方向无关，不涉及增材制造或生物力学。
边界: This paper does not include any additive manufacturing, LPBF fabrication, or mechanical testing; it focuses on drug release from a nanocomposite for dental implants.
动机· 开发多功能纳米复合材料支持即刻牙种植。
方法· 制备负载槲皮素纳米脂质载体的壳聚糖基复合材料。
结果· 复合材料具有高孔隙率、抗氧化和抗菌性能。
验证· 体外和离体药物释放及抗菌活性测试。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To design a multifunctional nanocomposite for supporting immediate dental implant placement.
Method· Quercetin-loaded nanostructured lipid carriers were embedded in a chitosan-based solid matrix containing ciprofloxacin.
Result· The nanocomposite exhibited high porosity, antioxidant activity, and antimicrobial properties against Staphylococcus aureus.
Validation· In vitro drug release and ex vivo accumulation studies with mathematical modeling.
