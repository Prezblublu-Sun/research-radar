Motivation· To describe mid-term relative survival after hip fracture arthroplasty and identify factors influencing mortality.
Method· Multivariable Cox proportional hazards modeling on 43,891 patients from the Swedish Hip Arthroplasty Register.
Result· Excess mortality was highest in the first six months post-surgery and remained elevated for up to 8 years.
Validation· Relative survival compared to the general Swedish population.
