S.M. Ahmadi, Saber Amin Yavari, R. Wauthle, Behdad Pouran, Jan Schrooten et al. Materials 2015-04-21 10.3390/ma8041871
单位: Faculty of Mechanical, Maritime and Materials Engineering, Delft University of Technology (TU Delft), Mekelweg 2, 2628 CD Delft, The Netherlands
通讯: S.M. Ahmadi @ Faculty of Mechanical, Maritime and Materials Engineering, Delft University of Technology (TU Delft), Mekelweg 2, 2628 C
相关性: 该论文研究了不同单元胞结构的多孔钛合金的力学性能与相对密度的关系，直接相关于AM生物医学植入物的刚度匹配设计。
边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing, surface roughness characterization, or osseointegration assessment.
动机· 研究多孔钛合金相对密度与压缩性能的关系
方法· 采用选区激光熔化制备六种不同单元胞的多孔Ti6Al4V并进行压缩测试
结果· 压缩性能随相对密度呈幂律增加，单元胞构型影响弹性模量
验证· 通过力学测试验证了构型与密度对性能的影响
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the relationship between relative density and compressive properties of porous Ti6Al4V with different unit cell configurations for bone-mimicking biomaterials.

Validation· Mechanical compression tests were conducted to validate the power-law relationship and the influence of unit cell geometry on stiffness.
