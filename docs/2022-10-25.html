Umberto Paggi, Lore Thijs, Brecht Van Hooreweder Additive Manufacturing Letters 2022-10-25 10.1016/j.addlet.2022.100095
单位: 3DSystems-LayerWise, Grauwmeer 14, 3000 Leuven, Belgium
通讯: Brecht Van Hooreweder @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, KU Leuven, Celestijnenlaan 300, 3000 Leuven, Belgium
相关性: 与用户研究方向无直接关联，属于金属增材制造工艺改进，不涉及生物医学植入物或应力屏蔽。
边界: This paper does not address porous structure design for stress shielding reduction, does not include mechanical testing (static or fatigue), and lacks any biomedical implant context.
动机· 改善金属LPBF打印件的表面质量和生产效率。
方法· 设计并打印带接触式和无接触式支撑的叶轮案例进行对比。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve surface quality and productivity in laser powder bed fusion of metal parts by developing contactless supports.
Method· An impeller case was printed with both conventional and contactless supports, and the processing time and surface roughness were compared.
Result· Contactless supports significantly reduced processing time and improved surface roughness compared to conventional supports.
Validation· Validation was performed by comparing surface roughness and processing time measurements of the printed impeller cases.

English summary & terms

Validation· Clinical observation of a single case with ethics committee approval.
