Motivation· To report the clinical outcomes and safety of simultaneous bilateral total hip arthroplasty via an anterior approach.
Method· A single case of a 56-year-old patient undergoing bilateral THA was reviewed for perioperative and short-term follow-up data.

Validation· Clinical observation of a single case with ethics committee approval.
