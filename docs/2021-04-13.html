English summary & terms
Motivation· High-stakes applications demand interpretable AI models, yet interpretability is subjective and personalized.
Method· A bi-objective evolutionary algorithm synthesizes models with trade-offs between accuracy and user-specific interpretability, learned via a neural network trained on user feedback collected through uncertainty-based active learning.
Result· The approach learns diverse interpretability estimates across users, and users prefer models found with personalization over non-personalized ones.
Validation· User studies with 61 participants on two real-world datasets validate the personalization effectiveness.
