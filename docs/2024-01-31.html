通讯: Giovanna Orsini @ Department of Clinical Sciences and Stomatology, Polytechnic University of Marche, Ancona, Italy. g.orsini@staff.univpm.
相关性: 该论文研究激光处理表面而非增材制造，且未涉及力学测试或应力遮挡，与方向不相关。
边界: No LPBF or AM fabrication, no mechanical testing (fatigue or static), no porous structure or stress shielding evaluation, and the focus is on soft tissue collagen organization rather than implant biomechanics.
动机· 比较激光处理与机械加工愈合基台周围胶原组织的结构差异。

Result· Collagen near laser-treated surfaces was more abundant, oriented perpendicularly, and had a higher triple-helix content, resembling natural tooth collagen.
Validation· Clinical trial with human gingival samples validated via combined FTIRI and histological methods.
