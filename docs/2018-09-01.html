英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the molecular events in implant-adherent cells and peri-implant bone during osseointegration between smokers and nonsmokers.
Method· Twenty-four smokers and 24 nonsmokers received machined and oxidized titanium mini-implants; gene expression in retrieved implants and bone was analyzed by qPCR at 1, 7, and 28 days.
Result· Oxidized implants showed higher expression of bone remodeling genes in smokers, while machined implants induced more proinflammatory cytokines.
Validation· Human in vivo retrieval study with quantitative PCR analysis of implant-adherent cells and peri-implant bone.
