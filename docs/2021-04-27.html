单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery at Diakovere Annastift, Hannover Medical School, 30625 Hannover, Germany
通讯: Stefan Budde @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery at Diakovere Annastift, Hannover Medical School, 30625 Hannover, Germany

动机· 探究双平面数字模板化能否提高短柄髋臼假体的尺寸规划准确率。

Validation· Clinical validation through three independent surgeons templating on actual postoperative X-rays.
