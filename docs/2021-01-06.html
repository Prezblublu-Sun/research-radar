Motivation· To evaluate patients' lived experiences with a recalled metal-on-metal hip resurfacing implant known for high complication rates.
Method· Semi-structured interviews with 14 patients still living with the ASR implant, analyzed using content analysis.
Result· Most patients reported satisfaction and no negative impact from the recall, though they anticipated earlier revision surgery.
Validation· No validation; purely qualitative interview study.
