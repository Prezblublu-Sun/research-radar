英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the long-term outcomes of different prosthetic bearing surfaces in total hip arthroplasty, considering patient age and follow-up duration.
Method· A systematic review and network meta-analysis of 59 randomized controlled trials comparing various bearing interfaces.
Result· Ceramic-on-ceramic and highly cross-linked polyethylene interfaces showed lower revision rates in younger patients, while metal-on-polyethylene performed better in older patients.
Validation· Study quality was assessed using the Cochrane risk of bias tool.
