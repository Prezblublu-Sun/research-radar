Nonlinear analysis of compressive behavior of 17-4PH steel structures with large spherical pores built by selective laser melting

Sabina Luisa Campanelli, Lorenzo Santoro, Luciano Lamberti, Fabrizia Caiazzo, Vittorio Alfieri Journal of Materials Science 2022-01-14 10.1007/s10853-021-06851-9
单位: Department of Mechanics, Mathematics and Management, Polytechnic of Bari, Via Orabona 4, 70125, Bari, Italy
通讯: Sabina Luisa Campanelli @ Department of Mechanics, Mathematics and Management, Polytechnic of Bari, Via Orabona 4, 70125, Bari, Italy
相关性: 该论文研究选择性激光熔化制备的多孔17-4PH不锈钢结构的压缩行为，但未涉及生物医学植入物的应力屏蔽或骨整合评估。
边界: This paper does not link porous structure to stress shielding or stiffness matching for biomedical implants, does not cover fatigue, and does not report surface roughness or osseointegration relevant to AM biomedical materials.
动机· 研究大尺寸球形孔洞多孔钢结构的压缩行为。
方法· 采用选择性激光熔化制备17-4PH不锈钢多孔样品并进行非线性压缩分析。
结果· 揭示了孔洞尺寸对压缩性能和失效模式的影响。
验证· 通过实验压缩测试验证有限元模型。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the compressive behavior of steel structures with large spherical pores fabricated by selective laser melting.
Method· Specimens with varying pore sizes were built from 17-4PH stainless steel using SLM and subjected to nonlinear finite element analysis and mechanical testing.
Result· The compressive response and failure mechanisms were found to be highly dependent on pore size and shape.
Validation· Experimental compression tests were conducted to validate the numerical predictions.

Aspirin Is an Effective Prophylaxis for Venous Thromboembolism in Ambulatory Patients with Femoral Neck Fracture Undergoing Hip Arthroplasty

Emanuele Chisari, Timothy L. Tan, Roshan Shah, Matthew Levitsky, Nicolas Piuzzi et al. Journal of Bone and Joint Surgery 2022-01-14 10.2106/jbjs.21.00168
单位: Rothman Orthopaedic Institute at Thomas Jefferson University, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania
通讯: Emanuele Chisari @ Rothman Orthopaedic Institute at Thomas Jefferson University, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania
相关性: 该论文仅关注髋关节置换术后的药物预防血栓，不涉及生物力学或植入物设计。
边界: This paper is purely clinical, with no biomechanical modeling, no implant stress analysis, no micromotion, and no femoral stem mechanical evaluation.
动机· 评估阿司匹林在髋部骨折术后预防静脉血栓的效果
方法· 回顾性多中心研究，倾向评分匹配比较阿司匹林与其他抗凝药
结果· 阿司匹林组VTE率1.98%，非劣效于其他抗凝药
验证· 基于1141例患者的临床数据验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the efficacy of aspirin for venous thromboembolism prophylaxis in patients undergoing hip arthroplasty for femoral neck fracture.
Method· A multicenter retrospective study with propensity score matching compared aspirin to other anticoagulants in 1141 patients.
Result· Aspirin resulted in a 1.98% VTE rate, demonstrating noninferiority to more potent anticoagulation.
Validation· Clinical validation using medical records and imaging-confirmed outcomes.
