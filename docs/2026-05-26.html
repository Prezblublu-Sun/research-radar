Uncertainty quantification in constitutive model calibration and discovery in computational solid mechanics

David Anton LeoPARD - TU Braunschweig Publications And Research Data 2026-05-26 10.24355/dbbs.084-202605081450-0
单位: Institut für Angewandte Mechanik
通讯: David Anton @ Institut für Angewandte Mechanik
相关性: 该论文直接针对固体力学中本构模型标定与发现的UQ问题，提出了基于PINN的实时统计标定和无需先验的UQ框架，方法可迁移至植入物/生物力学设计中的代理建模与不确定性传播。
边界: This paper focuses on constitutive model calibration/discovery in solid mechanics, but does not specifically address implant design, bone-implant interface mechanics, or multi-objective constrained optimization.

结果· 在线弹性和超弹性材料上验证了方法的有效性，实现了物理一致且可解释的UQ。

English summary & terms
Motivation· Existing UQ methods for constitutive model calibration and discovery are either computationally prohibitive for real-time applications or lack flexibility in handling complex posterior distributions.
Method· A parametric physics-informed neural network enables near real-time statistical calibration, and an automated UQ framework using normalizing flows infers complex probability densities without prior knowledge.
Result· The methods demonstrate accurate UQ for linear elastic and hyperelastic materials, validated through numerical tests and experimental data.
Validation· Validation includes multiple numerical benchmarks and experimental data for linear elastic and hyperelastic materials.
