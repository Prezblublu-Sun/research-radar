单位: Faculty of Mechanical and Manufacturing Engineering UniversitiTun Hussein Onn Malaysia, Parit Raja, BatuPahat, Johor, Malaysia
通讯: Raus A.A @ Faculty of Mechanical and Manufacturing Engineering UniversitiTun Hussein Onn Malaysia, Parit Raja, BatuPahat, Johor, Ma
相关性: 该论文研究了AlSi10Mg的SLM制造及力学性能，但材料非生物医用常用（如钛合金），且未涉及多孔结构、疲劳或骨整合。

Result· Optimal parameters (350 W, 1650 mm/s, 0.13 mm) yield highest relative density.
Validation· Validation limited to density measurement without mechanical or biomedical characterization.
