单位: National Demonstration Center for Experimental Mechanical and Electrical Engineering Education, Tianjin University of Technology , Tianjin 300382, China; , Tianjin 300382, China
通讯: Li Lan Gao @ National Demonstration Center for Experimental Mechanical and Electrical Engineering Education, Tianjin University of Te
相关性: 该论文关注髋关节假体的磨损预测，未涉及股骨柄应力遮挡或微动分析，因此与用户研究方向关联度较低。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain or micromotion, does not compare intact vs implanted femur, and does not provide boundary conditions relevant to implant-bone interface mechanics.
动机· 评估Zr-Nb髋假体在真实步态下的耐磨性并优化径向间隙设计

结果· Zr-Nb假体磨损率较CoCrMo降低82%，峰值接触压力减小39%
验证· 通过销盘磨损试验校准磨损系数，并对比不同材料的磨损性能
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the wear performance of Zr-Nb-on-Zr-Nb hip prostheses under physiological gait and optimize radial clearance design.
Method· A wear prediction scheme integrating pin-on-disk tests and finite element analysis to determine wear coefficients using the Archard model and dynamic contact mechanics.

Validation· Validation via pin-on-disk wear tests to calibrate wear coefficients and comparative finite element analysis of different material combinations.

StrADiff: A Structured Source-Wise Adaptive Diffusion Framework for Linear and Nonlinear Blind Source Separation

Yuan-Hao Wei arXiv preprint 2026-04-04 link
相关性: 该论文研究盲源分离方法，与FEA代理模型或生物力学设计无关。
边界: This paper addresses blind source separation with diffusion models and GP priors, not surrogate modeling, FEA acceleration, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification, or biomedical design.
动机· 无需监督标签实现盲源分离，且适用于线性和非线性混合。
方法·
结果· 在线性混合中稳定恢复源轨迹，非线性混合中性能适度下降。
验证· 在线性和非线性混合数据集上进行数值实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The paper aims to perform unsupervised blind source separation under linear and nonlinear mixing without requiring supervised labels or post-processing.
Method· A structured source-wise adaptive diffusion framework with individual reverse diffusion per latent source, jointly optimizing source generation, regularization, and reconstruction with a GP prior.
Result· The framework recovers meaningful latent source trajectories in an unsupervised manner, achieving stable performance for linear mixtures and moderate degradation under nonlinear mixtures.
Validation· The method is validated on numerical benchmark experiments with both linear and nonlinear synthetic mixtures.
