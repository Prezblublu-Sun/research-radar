Motivation· To evaluate the outcomes of fast-track total hip arthroplasty using the direct superior approach and compare patient-reported outcome measures with the posterolateral approach.
Method· A retrospective single-surgeon series of 50 DSA patients was compared with 858 matched PLA patients from a national registry.
Result· The DSA group showed shorter hospital stay and low pain scores, but PROMs were not clinically superior to the PLA group.
Validation· Clinical registry-based retrospective cohort comparison.
