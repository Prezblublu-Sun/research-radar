Motivation· To report the true 1-year incidence of dislocation after primary THA and validate an algorithm for identifying dislocations in the Danish National Patient Register.
Method· A retrospective study of 5,415 primary THAs using data from the Danish Hip Arthroplasty Register and a comprehensive review of hospital contacts.
Result· The true 1-year dislocation incidence was 2.8%, with the algorithm showing sensitivity close to 95% and positive predictive value above 94%.
Validation· Validation was performed by comparing algorithm-based identification against a manual review of 1,762 hospital contacts.
