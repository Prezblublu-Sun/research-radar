动机· 传统回归模型处理删失数据有偏，需要更可靠的剩余寿命预测方法。

Method· A Rashomon perspective is introduced, considering multiple survival models with similar performance to quantify uncertainty in survival probability predictions.
Result· Relying on a single model increases risk, and longer censoring times lead to greater variability in survival probabilities.
Validation· The approach is validated on the CMAPSS aircraft engine dataset.

Manan Tayal, Aditya Singh, Shishir Kolathaya, Somil Bansal arXiv preprint 2025-02-16 link
相关性: 该论文关注自主系统的安全与性能协同优化，与FEA代理建模和植入物设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, implant biomechanics, or expensive simulation sampling; it focuses on control of autonomous systems with safety constraints.
动机· 自主系统需同时保证安全与性能，但现有方法难以兼顾。
方法· 将安全与性能协同优化建模为状态约束最优控制问题，用物理信息机器学习求解HJB方程。
结果· 提出基于共形预测的验证策略，量化学习误差，恢复高置信度安全值函数。

Motivation· Autonomous systems require simultaneous optimization of safety and performance, but existing methods either lack formal safety guarantees or neglect performance.
Method· The co-optimization is formulated as a state-constrained optimal control problem, and the resulting HJB equation is approximated using a physics-informed machine learning framework.
Result· A conformal prediction-based verification strategy quantifies learning errors and recovers a high-confidence safety value function with probabilistic performance bounds.
Validation· The framework is validated through several case studies of complex, high-dimensional autonomous systems.

相关性: 该论文研究在线共形预测与无遗憾学习的关系，与有限元代理建模或生物力学设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address expensive simulation sampling, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification for surrogate models, or biomechanics/implant design.
动机· 探索无遗憾学习与在线共形预测之间的理论联系。
方法· 通过理论分析建立阈值校准覆盖与交换遗憾之间的紧密联系，并推广到群组条件覆盖。
结果· 证明了跟随扰动领导者算法族可用于在对抗环境中提供群组条件覆盖保证。

Motivation· To investigate the theoretical relationship between no-regret learning and online conformal prediction, particularly in adversarial settings.
Method· Establishes a tight connection between threshold calibrated coverage and swap-regret, and extends to group-conditional coverage using follow-the-perturbed-leader algorithms.
Result· Demonstrates that algorithms in the follow-the-perturbed-leader family can provide group-conditional coverage guarantees in adversarial environments.
Validation· Experiments using a multi-group generalization of the ACI algorithm validate the theoretical findings.
