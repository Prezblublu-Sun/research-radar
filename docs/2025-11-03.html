Outcomes of arthroplasty in persons with hemophilia: A survey of Medicare data

Mohamad Ali Tfaily, Tinatin Muradashvili, Christine L. Kempton, Stacey A. Fedewa Blood 2025-11-03 10.1182/blood-2025-3064
单位: 1Emory University School of Medicine, Department of Hematology and Medical Oncology, Atlanta, United States
通讯: Mohamad Ali Tfaily @ 1Emory University School of Medicine, Department of Hematology and Medical Oncology, Atlanta, United States
相关性: 该论文仅关注血友病患者关节置换术后的临床结果，未涉及任何生物力学建模或植入物设计。
边界: This paper is a clinical outcomes study using Medicare claims data, with no biomechanical modeling, no FEA, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, and no implant design or contact mechanics.
动机· 研究血友病患者关节置换术后再入院率与死亡率
方法· 基于2000-2020年Medicare数据，倾向评分匹配比较血友病与普通人群
结果· 血友病患者90天再入院率显著高于普通人群（19.7% vs 8.7%）
验证· 使用大型行政数据库进行倾向评分匹配分析
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare 90-day readmission and mortality rates after total hip/knee arthroplasty between persons with hemophilia and the general population using Medicare claims data.
Method· A cohort study using Medicare claims from 2000-2020, identifying persons with hemophilia via validated algorithms and propensity score matching with the general population.
Result· Persons with hemophilia had significantly higher 90-day readmission rates (19.7% vs 8.7%) compared to the general population.
Validation· Propensity score matching and generalized estimating equations were used to adjust for confounders.
