Motivation· To assess whether cemented fixation in hip arthroplasty for fractures is associated with increased cardiopulmonary morbidity and mortality.
Method· A secondary analysis of the HIP ATTACK trial data using logistic regression adjusting for covariates to compare composite adverse outcomes between cemented and uncemented fixation.
