边界: This paper does not quantify stress/strain, micromotion, or any biomechanical parameter; it is purely a clinical epidemiology analysis of cemented vs. uncemented fixation outcomes.
动机· 评估骨水泥固定与髋部骨折术后心肺并发症的相关性
方法· 对HIP ATTACK随机试验数据进行二次分析，用logistic回归调整协变量

Method· A secondary analysis of the HIP ATTACK trial data using logistic regression adjusting for covariates to compare composite adverse outcomes between cemented and uncemented fixation.

Validation· Validation is based on retrospective analysis of a large randomized controlled trial dataset without additional data collection.
