通讯: Maureen P. Hall @ Center for Teaching and Learning, Ross University School of Medicine, P. O Box 266, Roseau, 00152, Commonwealth of Domin
相关性: 该论文是关于医学教育中模拟教学的效果评估，与有限元代理建模、植入物设计或生物力学优化无关。
边界: This paper is an educational study on simulation-based learning for cardiovascular physiology, with no FEA, surrogate modeling, uncertainty quantification, or biomedical implant design context.
动机· 评估模拟教学对医学生心血管生理学知识短期保留的影响。
方法· 比较仅讲座与讲座加模拟两种教学方式的学生考试成绩。
结果· 模拟加讲座组在总结性考试中表现更好，但期末考试无显著差异。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate whether integrating simulation with lectures improves short-term knowledge retention of cardiovascular physiology in first-year medical students.
Method· A quasi-experimental design comparing exam scores of students receiving lecture-only versus lecture-plus-simulation curriculum using Wilcoxon rank-sum tests.
Result· Students in the simulation-plus-lecture group performed significantly better on the summative exam but not on the final exam.
Validation· Statistical analysis of exam scores with a priori alpha level of 0.10.

单位: Georgia Tech Research Institute, Atlanta, Georgia 30332, USA
通讯: Thomas E. deLaubenfels @ Georgia Tech Research Institute, Atlanta, Georgia 30332, USA
相关性: 该论文研究离子阱中的量子控制，与髋关节植入物和股骨柄的应力遮挡、微动或生物力学完全无关。
边界: This paper does not address hip implant stress shielding, micromotion, contact mechanics, or any biomechanical or orthopedic context.
动机· 控制离子阱中载波与边带的耦合强度
方法· 利用芯片陷阱表面作为反射镜形成驻波，移动离子位置
结果· 观察到载波和边带跃迁的周期性抑制，相位差180度

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To control the relative coupling strengths of carrier and motional sideband transitions in a trapped ion system.
Method· A resonant optical standing wave is formed using a microfabricated chip trap surface as a mirror, and the ion is displaced along the standing wave.
Result· Periodic suppression of carrier and sideband transitions is observed, with cycles 180 degrees out of phase.
Validation· Ion displacement is measured using standing-wave fringes and compared with micromotion amplitudes to validate trap models.
