通讯: Zhiqing Yang @ Ji Hua Laboratory, Foshan 528200, China  |  Johan Moverare @ Division of Engineering Materials, Department of Management and Engineering, Linköping University, Linköping SE-58183, S
相关性: 该论文研究了LPBF 316L不锈钢的疲劳性能，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或生物医学植入物应用。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures, stress shielding, stiffness matching, or biomedical implant context; it focuses on microstructural fatigue of dense 316L steel.
动机· 比较LPBF与传统316L不锈钢的疲劳性能差异

Validation· Microindentation hardness tests and S-N fatigue curves validated the mechanical performance differences.
