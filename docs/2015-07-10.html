单位: University of Liège
通讯: Carlos Felipe Guzmán @ University of Liège
相关性: 该论文研究Ti-6Al-4V的塑性各向异性和损伤模型，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡、LPBF制造或疲劳测试，与用户方向关联度低。
边界: No porous structure, no stress shielding or stiffness matching, no LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing on biomedical implants, no fatigue or osseointegration.
动机· 评估Ti-6Al-4V合金的塑性各向异性和损伤模型预测能力
方法· 通过单轴拉伸、压缩和平面应变实验标定Hill48和CPB06屈服准则，结合Gurson损伤模型进行有限元模拟
结果· Hill48和CPB06模型能较好预测力-位移曲线，但损伤模型预测能力有限
验证· 与简单剪切和缺口拉伸实验的力-位移及应变场对比验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To assess the predictive capability of anisotropic plasticity and damage models for Ti-6Al-4V alloy under various loading conditions.
Method· Monotonic tension, compression, and plane strain tests were conducted to calibrate Hill'48 and CPB06 yield criteria, and an extended Gurson damage model was implemented in finite element simulations.
Result· Both Hill'48 and CPB06 models accurately reproduced force-displacement curves, while the damage model showed limited predictive ability for fracture.
Validation· Model predictions were validated against simple shear and notched tensile tests, comparing force-displacement curves and local strain fields.
