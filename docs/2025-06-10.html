Motivation· To investigate the cumulative incidence of femoral stem revision and complications in cemented versus uncemented hip arthroplasty for proximal femoral metastatic bone disease.
Method· A multicenter retrospective cohort study of 337 primary hip arthroplasties, with separate reporting of outcomes due to substantial baseline differences between fixation groups.
Result· The study reports stem revision and complication rates separately for cemented and uncemented groups, without direct comparison due to confounding.
Validation· Clinical follow-up data from six centers over 11 years.
