Microstructure and mechanical property of selective laser melted Ti6Al4V dependence on laser energy density

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Transferable Save for project mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Jie Cai Han, Jingjing Yang, Hanchen Yu, Jie Yin, Ming Gao et al. Rapid Prototyping Journal 2017-03-20 10.1108/rpj-12-2015-0193
单位: Wuhan National Laboratory for Optoelectronics, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan, China
通讯: Jie Cai Han @ Wuhan National Laboratory for Optoelectronics, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan, China
相关性: 该论文研究了激光能量密度对SLM Ti6Al4V微观结构和力学性能的影响，为生物医用钛合金植入物的增材制造工艺优化提供了参考。
边界: Does not evaluate porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue performance, or osseointegration, which are critical for biomedical implants.
动机· 研究激光能量密度对SLM成形Ti6Al4V微观结构和力学性能的影响规律。
方法· 采用不同能量密度SLM制备Ti6Al4V试样，表征微观组织和力学性能。
结果· 优化能量密度可获得高致密度和硬度，拉伸强度达1268 MPa，伸长率4%。
验证· 通过实验测试验证了微观结构与力学性能的依赖关系。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· This paper investigates how laser energy density influences the microstructure and mechanical properties of SLM-fabricated Ti6Al4V to establish processing-structure-property relationships.
Method· Ti6Al4V samples were fabricated by selective laser melting under various energy densities, and their microstructures and mechanical properties were systematically characterized.
Result· An optimized energy density window yielded high relative density, microhardness, tensile strength of 1268 MPa, yield strength of 1030 MPa, and elongation of 4%, with residual tensile stress increasing with energy density.
Validation· Experimental characterization (microscopy, microhardness, tensile testing, residual stress measurement) validated the dependence on laser energy density.

单位: School of Engineering, Deakin University, Waurn Ponds, Australia
通讯: AmirMahyar Khorasani @ School of Engineering, Deakin University, Waurn Ponds, Australia
相关性: 该论文研究了选区激光熔化Ti-6Al-4V在不同退火热处理下的力学性能，对生物医用植入体制造工艺有参考价值，但未涉及应力屏蔽、多孔结构或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper does not connect porous structure design to stress shielding, does not report fatigue testing, and lacks surface roughness or osseointegration evaluation relevant to biomedical implants.
动机· 为满足手术植入体标准，优化SLM Ti-6Al-4V的退火热处理工艺。
方法· 对SLM和锻造试样进行不同退火处理，分析微观结构和力学性能。
结果· 再结晶退火使SLM试样获得最佳强度与延展性组合。

Motivation· To optimize annealing heat treatments for selective laser melted Ti-6Al-4V to meet ASTM standards for surgical implants.
Method· Various annealing treatments (stress relief, mill, recrystallization, β annealing) were applied to SLM and wrought Ti-6Al-4V, followed by microstructural and mechanical characterization.

Validation· Mechanical properties were validated via tensile and hardness tests according to ASTM F2924-14, with fracture surface analysis.
