Modeling age-related changes in the mechanical behavior of the fracture-fixated human tibia bone during healing.

Hajer Ketata, Mohamed Kharrat, Maher Dammak Medical engineering & physics 2020-07-01 10.1016/j.medengphy.2020.05.011

动机· 研究年龄对胫骨骨折愈合生物力学响应的影响。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how age affects biomechanical response of fractured tibia during healing.
Method· Developed 3D finite element models incorporating age-dependent bone stiffness and time-varying callus properties.
Result· Young patients showed load transfer via callus at 10 weeks post-surgery, while elderly patients lacked sufficient transfer even after 16 weeks.
Validation· Simulation-based comparison of two age groups without experimental or clinical validation.

The development of a novel autologous blood glue aiming to improve osseointegration in the bone-implant interface.

Anita Sanghani-Kerai, Melanie Coathup, Robyn Brown, George Lodge, Liza Osagie-Clouard et al. Bone & joint research 2020-07-01 10.1302/2046-3758.97.BJR-2019-0073.R3
单位: Institute of Orthopaedics and Musculoskeletal Science, Division of Surgery and Interventional Sciences, University College London, Royal National Orthopaedic Hospital, Stanmore, UK.
通讯: Gordon Blunn @ Institute of Orthopaedics and Musculoskeletal Science, Division of Surgery and Interventional Sciences, University Colle [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究自体血胶对骨整合的影响，但未涉及髋关节植入物的生物力学分析，与用户方向关联度低。
边界: No stress/strain or micromotion quantification, no contact/friction modeling, no patient-specific FE, no intact vs. implanted comparison; purely an in vitro cell biology study.
动机· 开发自体血胶以增强骨-植入物界面的骨整合
方法· 将不同浓度PRP与骨髓细胞混合，制备自体血胶，并评估细胞增殖和活性
结果· 2.5倍浓度PRP的血胶在14天内支持细胞最佳生长，且细胞活性高于纤维蛋白胶
验证· 体外细胞实验比较不同PRP浓度和纤维蛋白胶
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop an autologous blood glue incorporating bone marrow cells and platelet-rich plasma to improve osseointegration at the bone-implant interface.
Method· Gels with 1.25x, 2.5x, and 5x concentrated PRP with or without bone marrow nucleated cells were tested for cell proliferation, morphology, and viability at days 7 and 14.
Result· The 2.5x PRP concentration supported maximal cell growth and maintained cell characteristics; cells in the autologous gel showed significantly higher viability compared to fibrin glue.
Validation· In vitro cell culture assays comparing three PRP concentrations and a commercial fibrin glue control.
