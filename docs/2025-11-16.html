An Active Learning Interatomic Potential For Defect-Engineered CoCrFeMnNi High-Entropy Alloy

Manish Sahoo, Akash Deshmukh, Yash Kokane, Jayaprakash H M, Raghavan Ranganathan arXiv preprint 2025-11-16 link
相关性: 该论文研究高熵合金的原子间势，与FEA代理模型或生物力学设计无关。
边界: This paper focuses on molecular dynamics potential development for high-entropy alloys, not on surrogate modeling for finite element analysis or implant/biomechanics design.
动机· 高熵合金成分空间大，缺乏原子间势阻碍新合金发现。
方法· 使用主动学习训练矩张量势，并采用BFGS优化算法。
结果· MTP势在物理性质预测上优于传统MEAM势。
验证· 通过误差指标、状态方程、拉伸和纳米压痕验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The vast compositional space of high-entropy alloys necessitates accelerated discovery via computational methods, yet the lack of interatomic potentials hinders progress.
Method· A moment tensor potential was trained using machine learning with active learning and BFGS optimization on defect-rich configurations.
Result· The developed MTP outperforms the traditional MEAM potential in predicting physical properties of CoCrFeMnNi.
Validation· Validation included error metrics, equation of state, uniaxial tensile deformation, nano-indentation, and solid-liquid interface stability.

Acoustic Cell Patterning-Assisted Digital Light Processing for Bioprinting Anisotropic Tissues

单位: Virginia Polytechnic Institute and State University , , ,
通讯: Yingshan Du @ Virginia Polytechnic Institute and State University , , ,
相关性: 该论文没有涉及任何人工智能方法或模型，与AI生物打印方向无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not incorporate AI/ML into any bioprinting workflow stage; it focuses on acoustic cell patterning without computational optimization or autonomous decision-making.
动机· 现有生物打印技术难以实现各向异性组织结构的精确控制。
方法· 将声波细胞图案化方法与激光辅助数字光处理生物打印集成。
结果· 成功打印出毫米至厘米尺度的三维各向异性组织构建体，细胞存活率高。
验证· 通过打印分辨率、三维可扩展性和各向异性能力验证，以及细胞活力评估。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Current bioprinting techniques struggle to achieve biomimetic anisotropic tissue structures with controlled cell alignment.
Method· An acoustic patterning device using standing waves is integrated with digital light processing bioprinting to align cells during fabrication.
Result· Millimeter- to centimeter-scale 3D constructs with anisotropic features, including cones, nerve conduits, and vascular branches, were produced with high cell viability.
Validation· Proof-of-concept experiments validated patterning effects, printing resolution, 3D scalability, and fibroblast cell viability and morphology.

边界: The paper does not address biomedical implants, stress shielding, stiffness matching, LPBF fabrication with mechanical testing, fatigue, surface roughness, or osseointegration.

动机· 探索增材制造在电机转子中的应用以降低扭矩脉动和噪声

Result· The AM-enabled rotor designs significantly reduced torque ripple and eddy current losses, improving overall machine efficiency.
Validation· Only numerical FEM simulations were performed; no experimental validation was conducted.

Modern approach to the prevention of venous thromboembolic complications in patients before and after hip arthroplasty

Stanislav Bondarenko, Oleksandr Vysotskyi TRAUMA 2025-11-16 10.22141/1608-1706.5.26.2025.1047
单位: Sytenko Institute of Spine and Joint Pathology of NAMSU, Kharkiv, Ukraine
通讯: Stanislav Bondarenko @ Sytenko Institute of Spine and Joint Pathology of NAMSU, Kharkiv, Ukraine
相关性: 该论文关注全髋关节置换术后静脉血栓栓塞并发症的预防，未涉及任何生物力学或植入物力学分析。
边界: This paper is a clinical study on thromboprophylaxis after hip arthroplasty; it does not quantify stress/strain, micromotion, or implant-bone interface mechanics, nor does it use FEA or patient-specific modeling.
动机· 降低髋关节置换术后静脉血栓栓塞并发症的风险
方法· 基于Caprini评分进行风险分层并实施个体化预防方案
结果· 预防方案使深静脉血栓发生率降低5.35%，下肢水肿降低3%
验证· 与文献数据对比临床疗效
English summary & terms
Motivation· To reduce the incidence of venous thromboembolic complications after total hip arthroplasty through risk-stratified prophylaxis.
Method· Patients were stratified into low, moderate, and high risk groups using a modified Caprini score, and a tailored thromboprophylaxis protocol was applied.
Result· The proposed prophylaxis reduced deep vein thrombosis by 5.35% and lower extremity edema by 3% compared to literature rates.
Validation· Clinical outcomes were compared with published epidemiological data.
