Layer by layer coating for bio-functionalization of additively manufactured meta-biomaterials

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only in_vitro Exp. validation
Saber Amin Yavari, Michiel Croes, Behnam Akhavan, Fatemeh Jahanmard, C.C. Eigenhuis et al. Additive manufacturing 2019-12-09 10.1016/j.addma.2019.100991
单位: Department of Orthopedics, University Medical Center Utrecht, Utrecht 3508GA, the Netherlands
通讯: Saber Amin Yavari @ Department of Orthopedics, University Medical Center Utrecht, Utrecht 3508GA, the Netherlands
相关性: 该论文涉及增材制造多孔钛合金支架的表面涂层功能化，但未直接研究应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper focuses on bio-functional coatings for infection prevention and bone regeneration, but does not address stress shielding, stiffness matching, fatigue testing, or mechanical characterization of the porous structure.
动机· 为增材制造多孔金属支架赋予抗菌和促骨再生功能。
方法· 在SLM制备的钛合金陀螺点阵表面涂覆含BMP-2和万古霉素的明胶-壳聚糖多层涂层。
结果· 涂层持续释放药物2-3周，抗菌效果显著，促进间充质干细胞成骨分化。
验证· 体外释放、抗菌实验、干细胞分化及大鼠皮下植入8周。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To endow additively manufactured porous metallic meta-biomaterials with multifunctional surface coatings that prevent infection and stimulate bone regeneration.
Method· Layer-by-layer coatings of gelatin and chitosan containing BMP-2 and vancomycin were applied to selective laser melted CP Ti sheet gyroid structures.
Result· Sustained release of both drugs for 2-3 weeks, strong antibacterial activity (8-log reduction), enhanced osteogenic differentiation (2-4 fold increase in ALP and mineralization), and no foreign body response in vivo.
Validation· In vitro release profiles, antibacterial assays, stem cell differentiation, and 8-week subcutaneous implantation in rats.
