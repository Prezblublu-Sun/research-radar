Identification and Predictive Analysis of a Multi-Area WECC Power System Model Using Synchrophasors

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only simulation_case
Govind Chavan, Matthew Weiss, Aranya Chakrabortty, Subhashish Bhattacharya, Armando Salazar et al. IEEE Transactions on Smart Grid 2016-03-22 10.1109/tsg.2016.2531637
单位: Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, North Carolina State University, Raleigh, NC, USA
通讯: Govind Chavan @ Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, North Carolina State University, Raleigh, NC, USA
相关性: 该论文研究电力系统降阶建模，与生物力学或植入物设计无关。
边界: No biomedical context, no implant mechanics, no FEA surrogate for solid mechanics, no uncertainty quantification.
动机· 利用同步相量数据构建电力系统降阶模型以进行稳定性分析。
方法· 通过模态分解提取慢振荡，估计五机等效模型参数。
结果· 模型能准确预测振荡频率和阻尼，并评估线路损失事件的临界性。
验证· 使用不同故障场景（发电机损失、线路损失）进行验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To construct a reduced-order dynamic equivalent model of a large power system using synchrophasor measurements for stability assessment.
Method· Slow oscillations are extracted via modal decomposition to estimate parameters of a five-machine equivalent, including transmission impedances, inertia, and damping.
Result· The reduced-order model accurately predicts oscillation mode frequencies and damping factors, and enables criticality analysis of line loss events.
Validation· Validated against multiple contingency scenarios such as generation and line loss in the WECC system.
