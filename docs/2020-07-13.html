相关性: 该论文展示了PINN在热传递问题中处理未知边界条件的能力，其方法可迁移至生物力学中植入物边界条件未知的场景。
边界: This paper does not address implant biomechanics, stress shielding, or any biomedical application; it focuses on convective heat transfer with unknown thermal boundary conditions.
动机· 传统数值方法需要完整边界条件，实际中难以满足。
方法· 利用PINN结合少量温度测量数据，同时求解流场和温度场。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Traditional numerical methods require explicit thermal boundary conditions on all boundaries, which are often unavailable in real applications.

Validation· High-fidelity spectral/hp-element method (SEM) simulations are used to quantitatively evaluate PINN predictions.
