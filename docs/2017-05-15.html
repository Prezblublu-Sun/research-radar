动机· 评估髋臼旋转截骨术治疗成人髋臼发育不良的长期生存率及风险因素。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the long-term survival rate and risk factors for osteoarthritis progression after rotational acetabular osteotomy in adulthood hip dysplasia.
Method· Kaplan-Meier survival analysis and Cox proportional hazards regression were performed on 56 patients (65 hips) with a mean follow-up of 15 years.
Result· The 10-year non-OA progression rate was 76.7% and THA-free survival rate was 92.3%, with older age identified as a significant risk factor.
Validation· Clinical follow-up data with statistical survival analysis.
