Motivation· To evaluate the efficacy and safety of aspirin for venous thromboembolism prophylaxis after orthopedic surgery based on randomized trials and guidelines.
Method· Systematic review of nine randomized controlled trials and four international guidelines comparing aspirin with anticoagulants.
Result· Aspirin is noninferior to low-molecular-weight heparin for mortality after fracture surgery and useful as extended prophylaxis after initial anticoagulation in arthroplasty, though anticoagulants remain superior when initiated immediately.
Validation· Findings derived from existing clinical trial data and guideline recommendations.
