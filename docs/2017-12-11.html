Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore clinical Exp. validation
Binbin Yu, Huaxiu Ning, Xiaolin Chen The Journal of practical nursing 2017-12-11 10.3760/cma.j.issn.1672-7088.2017.35.004
相关性: 该论文研究Norton评分对老年全髋关节置换术后并发症的预测价值，未涉及任何生物力学建模或植入物力学分析。
边界: This paper is a clinical nursing study on postoperative complication prediction using Norton Scale, with no biomechanics modeling, no implant design, no FEA, and no stress shielding or micromotion analysis.
动机· 评估Norton评分对老年全髋关节置换术后并发症的预测价值
方法· 前瞻性研究，根据Norton评分分组，比较术后并发症发生率
结果· 低Norton组并发症发生率显著高于高Norton组，Norton评分可独立预测并发症
验证· 临床前瞻性队列研究，Logistic回归分析验证预测模型
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the prognostic value of Norton Scale for postoperative complications after total hip arthroplasty in elderly patients.
Method· A prospective study divided patients into low Norton (≤14) and high Norton (≥15) groups and compared complication rates.
Result· Low Norton group had significantly higher rates of wound infection, urinary tract infection, urinary retention, bedsore, and deep vein thrombosis; low Norton was independently associated with complications.
Validation· Clinical prospective cohort study with logistic regression analysis showing 86.9% accuracy, 96.5% sensitivity, and 60.0% specificity.
