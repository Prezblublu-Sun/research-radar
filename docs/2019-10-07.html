Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark

单位: Department of Statistics, Virginia Tech, Blacksburg, VA
通讯: Boya Zhang @ Department of Statistics, Virginia Tech, Blacksburg, VA
相关性: 该论文提出了一种基于距离分布的高斯过程代理模型实验设计方法，可提高采样效率，对生物力学代理模型构建有借鉴意义。
边界: This paper focuses on general GP surrogate design for computer experiments, without any application to FEA, implant biomechanics, or uncertainty quantification in biomedical contexts.
动机· 现有空间填充设计在未知超参数时效率低，需改进实验设计方法。
方法· 通过逆向工程随机设计的最佳性质，优化设计点间距离分布。
结果· 所提距离分布设计在静态和序贯设置中均优于传统空间填充设计。
验证· 在数值基准测试上比较预测误差和长度尺度估计。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Standard space-filling designs for Gaussian process surrogates perform poorly when hyperparameters are unknown, motivating a new design approach.
Method· The authors reverse-engineer the distance distribution properties of random designs that yield best length-scale estimates and numerically optimize pairwise distances for given sample size and dimension.
Result· The proposed distance-based designs and their hybrids with space-filling schemes outperform conventional designs in both static and sequential settings.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on synthetic functions compare prediction error and length-scale estimation accuracy.
