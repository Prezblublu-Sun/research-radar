Myriam Cilla, Sara Checa, Georg N. Duda Journal of Orthopaedic Research® 2017-02-08 10.1002/jor.23540
单位: Aragón Institute of Engineering Research (I3A) University of Zaragoza Zaragoza Spain
通讯: Georg N. Duda @ Berlin‐Brandenburg Center for Regenerative Therapies Charité − Universitätsmedizin Berlin Berlin Germany
相关性: 该论文直接研究髋关节假体设计对应力遮挡的影响，与用户研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not report micromotion values or contact/friction modeling, and does not provide explicit boundary conditions for FE work.
动机· 探究髋关节假体设计变化能否避免应力遮挡以减少骨丢失。
方法· 使用有限元方法比较完整和植入股骨模型，施加患者特异性肌肉和关节接触力。
结果· 微小的几何和材料变化对应力遮挡影响不大，需大幅重新设计才能改善。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To understand whether changes in hip implant shape and material properties can avoid strain shielding and minimize bone loss after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Finite element analysis was used to compare intact and implanted femurs under patient-specific muscle and joint contact forces.
Result· Small geometric and material modifications have limited effect on strain shielding; substantial redesign is required for improvement.
Validation· Validation was performed through simulation-based comparison of strain distributions between intact and implanted femurs.
