单位: Department of Rehabilitation Medicine, Dongying Shengli Hospital, Dongying 257000, China
通讯: Wei Yan @ Department of Rehabilitation Medicine, Dongying Shengli Hospital, Dongying 257000, China
相关性: 该论文仅比较两种手术的临床疗效，未涉及任何生物力学建模或应力遮挡分析。
边界: No biomechanical modeling, no FEA, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no implant design or contact mechanics.
动机· 比较人工股骨头置换与全髋置换治疗老年股骨颈骨折的临床效果。
方法· 回顾性分析100例患者，分为两组进行手术效果对比。
结果· 全髋置换组功能评分优良率更高，并发症更少。
验证· 临床统计对比，P值验证差异显著性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare clinical outcomes of artificial femoral head replacement versus total hip arthroplasty in elderly femoral neck fracture patients.
Method· Retrospective analysis of 100 patients divided into two groups receiving either treatment.
Result· Total hip arthroplasty showed higher functional scores and lower complication rates.
Validation· Statistical comparison of clinical outcomes with P-value significance testing.
