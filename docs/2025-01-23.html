Wasserstein-regularized Conformal Prediction under General Distribution Shift

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Background only numerical_benchmark UQ
Rui Xu, Chao Chen, Yue Sun, Parvathinathan Venkitasubramaniam, Sihong Xie arXiv preprint 2025-01-23 link
相关性: 该论文研究分布偏移下的保形预测，与FEA替代建模或生物力学设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on conformal prediction under distribution shift, with no application to FEA surrogate modeling, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification for biomechanics, or implant design.
动机· 现有保形预测方法在分布偏移下覆盖保证不足，且多限于协变量偏移。
方法· 提出基于Wasserstein距离的覆盖间隙上界，并设计重要性加权与正则化表示学习算法WR-CP。
结果· WR-CP在六个数据集上将覆盖间隙降至3.2%，预测集比最坏情况方法平均小37%。
验证· 在六个基准数据集上进行数值实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Existing conformal prediction methods suffer from coverage gaps under distribution shift and are mostly limited to covariate shift, motivating a more general approach.
Method· A Wasserstein distance-based upper bound for the coverage gap is derived, and an algorithm combining importance weighting and regularized representation learning (WR-CP) is proposed to reduce the bound.
Result· WR-CP reduces coverage gaps to 3.2% across confidence levels and produces prediction sets 37% smaller than worst-case approaches on average.
Validation· Numerical experiments on six benchmark datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.
