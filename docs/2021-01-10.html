Motivation· To evaluate clinical outcomes and complications of large-diameter femoral heads in cementless total hip replacement in an Indian population.
Method· Prospective study of 70 patients with mean follow-up of 86.5 months, assessing Harris Hip Scores and radiographic findings.
Result· Harris Hip Score improved from 38.8 preoperatively to 90.4 postoperatively, with low dislocation rate and improved range of motion.
Validation· Clinical follow-up without biomechanical testing or finite element analysis.
