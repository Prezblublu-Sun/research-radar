Inverse design of anisotropic microstructures using physics-augmented neural networks

High FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project simulation_case
Asghar A. Jadoon, Karl A. Kalina, Manuel K. Rausch, Reese Jones, Jan N. Fuhg arXiv preprint 2024-12-17 link
相关性: 该论文提出的物理增强神经网络用于各向异性微结构逆设计，其方法可迁移至生物力学植入物设计中的代理建模与约束优化问题。
边界: This paper does not address implant-specific biomechanics, stress shielding, or patient-specific geometry; it focuses on composite microstructures and hyperelastic materials without biomedical context.
动机· 解决各向异性微结构逆设计中的类型与方向识别及参数优化问题。
方法· 使用部分输入凸神经网络构建应变能代理模型，结合进化策略求解逆问题。
结果· 模型能正确识别各向异性类型与方向，并恢复逆设计参数。
验证· 通过合成宏观数据与均匀化数据验证，并集成到有限元框架中测试。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To address the two-fold inverse design problem of anisotropic microstructures: identifying anisotropy type and orientation, and finding optimal design parameters for desired mechanical response.
Method· A forward surrogate model using partially Input Convex Neural Networks (pICNNs) is trained on macroscopic stress-strain data from computational homogenization, incorporating polyconvexity and physics constraints, then an evolution strategy solves the inverse problem.
Result· The invariant-based model successfully identifies anisotropy type and preferred directions, and recovers correct design parameters for inverse problems even with different preferred directions than training data.
Validation· Validation on synthetic macroscale and homogenized data, plus integration into a finite element framework to invert design parameters for desired macroscopic response.
