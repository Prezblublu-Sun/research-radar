英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To determine whether discarding the skin knife blade after incision reduces bacterial contamination in deeper tissues.
Method· Surface samples from 344 skin blades and 344 control blades were cultured and compared using bivariate two-sample z-tests.
Result· No significant difference in contamination rates was found between skin blades (4.9%) and control blades (5.2%).
Validation· Clinical sampling with Replicate Organism Detection and Counting plates and statistical hypothesis testing.
