Uncertainpy: A Python Toolbox for Uncertainty Quantification and Sensitivity Analysis in Computational Neuroscience

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project simulation_case UQ
Simen Tennøe, Geir Halnes, Gaute T. Einevoll Frontiers in Neuroinformatics 2018-08-14 10.3389/fninf.2018.00049
单位: Centre for Integrative Neuroplasticity, University of Oslo, Oslo, Norway
通讯: Gaute T. Einevoll @ Centre for Integrative Neuroplasticity, University of Oslo, Oslo, Norway
相关性: 该论文提供了适用于神经科学的开源不确定性量化工具，其基于多项式混沌展开的方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理模型的不确定性分析。
边界: This paper focuses on computational neuroscience models, not on FEA surrogate modeling for implant biomechanics, and lacks validation on solid mechanics or structural optimization problems.
动机· 神经科学模型参数不确定性大，缺乏易用的不确定性量化工具。
方法· 开发基于多项式混沌展开的Python工具箱Uncertainpy，支持特征级不确定性分析。
结果· 在三个神经科学案例中验证了工具箱的高效性和易用性。
验证· 通过Hodgkin-Huxley模型、多室模型和网络模型案例验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Computational neuroscience models suffer from poorly constrained parameters, and uncertainty quantification methods are not yet standard practice in the field.
Method· We present Uncertainpy, an open-source Python toolbox that uses polynomial chaos expansions to perform efficient uncertainty quantification and sensitivity analysis on existing neuroscience models without modifying model equations.
Result· The toolbox successfully quantifies uncertainty and sensitivity in three diverse neuroscience case studies, including single-neuron, multi-compartmental, and network models.
Validation· Validation is performed through simulation case studies of the Hodgkin-Huxley model, a thalamic interneuron model, and a recurrent network model.
