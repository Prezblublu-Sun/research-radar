边界: No uncertainty quantification, no constraint handling, and only validated on numerical benchmarks without biomechanics or implant context.

English summary & terms

Result· The method achieves accuracy comparable to the finite element method on challenging problems with fine geometric features and concentrated loads.
Validation· Validation is performed by comparing against finite element solutions on several numerical benchmarks.
