通讯: Anna Nikishova @ Computational Science Lab, Institute for Informatics, Faculty of Science, University of Amsterdam, Amsterdam, The Nether
相关性: 该论文展示了在心血管模型中应用不确定性量化和敏感性分析的方法，可迁移至植入物生物力学设计中的代理模型不确定性评估。
边界: This paper does not address surrogate modeling for expensive FEA, nor does it involve implant biomechanics or structural optimization.
动机· 量化支架内再狭窄多尺度模型中的不确定性，提高预测可靠性。
方法· 采用拟蒙特卡洛方法进行不确定性量化，结合Sobol敏感性分析识别关键参数。
结果· 模型输出平均新内膜面积存在约30%不确定性，内皮再生时间是最敏感参数。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify uncertainties in a multiscale model of in-stent restenosis, improving the reliability of simulation predictions.
Method· A quasi-Monte Carlo method was employed for uncertainty quantification, and Sobol sensitivity analysis was used to identify the most influential input parameters.
Result· The model output exhibited approximately 30% uncertainty in mean neointimal area, with endothelium regeneration time being the most sensitive parameter.
Validation· Validation was performed through simulation of a two-dimensional restenosis model without experimental comparison.

Comparative studies of error metrics in variable fidelity model uncertainty quantification

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Jiexiang Hu, Yang Yang, Qi Zhou, Ping Jiang, Xinyu Shao et al. Journal of Engineering Design 2018-08-22 10.1080/09544828.2018.1513126
单位: The State Key Laboratory of Digital Manufacturing Equipment and Technology, School of Mechanical Science and Engineering, Huazhong University of Science &amp; Technology, Wuhan, People’s Republic of C
通讯: Ping Jiang @ The State Key Laboratory of Digital Manufacturing Equipment and Technology, School of Mechanical Science and Engineering
相关性: 该论文比较了变保真度代理模型的不确定性量化误差度量，方法可迁移至植入物/生物力学设计中的代理模型不确定性评估。
边界: This paper focuses on error metrics for variable-fidelity surrogate models in general engineering design, without application to FEA surrogate modeling or implant biomechanics.
动机· 变保真度代理模型预测不确定性大，需合适误差度量来量化。
方法· 系统比较了四种误差度量（bootstrap、LOO、MSE、PEMF）在变保真度模型上的表现。
结果· 低维问题MSE最优，高维问题LOO最优，提供了误差度量选择指南。
验证· 通过数值算例和长圆柱压力容器设计问题验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Variable-fidelity surrogate models suffer from large prediction uncertainty, necessitating appropriate error metrics for reliable uncertainty quantification.
Method· Four error metrics (bootstrap, leave-one-out, mean square error, and predictive estimation of model fidelity) are systematically compared on variable-fidelity surrogate models.
Result· MSE performs best in low-dimensional problems while LOO error excels in high-dimensional problems, leading to a guideline for metric selection.
Validation· Validated on numerical examples with varying features and a long cylinder pressure vessel design problem.
