Hydrostatic Pressure Enhances Chondrogenic Differentiation of Mesenchymal Stem Cells in Silk Fibroin-Based 3D Bioprinted Hydrogels

Jennifer Fritz, Anna‐Christina Moser, Alexander Otahal, Karina Kramer, Salih Casurovic et al. Biomacromolecules 2025-05-20 10.1021/acs.biomac.5c00048
单位: Austrian Cluster for Tissue Regeneration, Donaueschingenstrasse 13, Vienna 1200, Austria
通讯: Jennifer Fritz @ Austrian Cluster for Tissue Regeneration, Donaueschingenstrasse 13, Vienna 1200, Austria
相关性: 该论文研究生物打印中静水压对软骨分化的影响，不涉及任何AI/ML方法。
边界: This paper does not incorporate AI/ML into any bioprinting workflow stage; it is purely experimental biology with no computational or autonomous decision-making component.
动机· 探究生理性静水压对3D生物打印丝素水凝胶中干细胞软骨分化的影响。
方法· 在含TGFβ-3或不含的培养基中，对细胞负载的丝素水凝胶施加循环静水压。
结果· TGFβ-3联合静水压显著增强了软骨标志物表达和基质沉积。
验证· 通过细胞活力、基因表达和组织学染色验证分化效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate whether cyclic hydrostatic pressure can enhance chondrogenic differentiation of mesenchymal stem cells in 3D bioprinted silk fibroin hydrogels.
Method· Human infrapatellar fat pad-derived MSCs were encapsulated in silk fibroin-based bioinks and subjected to cyclic hydrostatic pressure (up to 10 MPa) with or without TGFβ-3 supplementation.
Result· TGFβ-3 combined with hydrostatic pressure yielded the highest chondrogenic marker expression and localized extracellular matrix deposition.
Validation· Differentiation was validated via metabolic activity assays, gene expression analysis, and histological staining for glycosaminoglycans and collagen.

3D Printed Human Amnion-Based Bioactive Hybrid Dressingsfor Effective Management of Complex Infected Wounds

Satarupa Sarkar (17204017), A Pratap Choudhari (21394592), Anwesha Mukherjee (1855948), Varun Rathi (21394595), Bodhisatwa Das (1412161) et al. Figshare 2025-05-20 10.1021/acsami.5c06672.s001
相关性: 该论文未涉及任何人工智能或机器学习方法，与AI生物打印研究方向无关。
边界: This paper does not integrate AI into any bioprinting workflow stage; it is a purely material and biological study of a 3D printed wound dressing.
动机· 开发能有效管理感染伤口的3D打印敷料。
方法· 基于脱细胞羊膜制备生物墨水，通过挤出打印制备混合敷料。
结果· 敷料促进组织再生、减少细菌负荷并改善血管新生。
验证· 通过大鼠感染伤口模型验证敷料疗效。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a bioactive dressing that effectively manages infected wounds by combining regenerative and antimicrobial properties.
Method· A bioink was formulated from cryo-milled decellularized human amnion particles and extruded onto a polyurethane foam substrate via 3D printing.
Result· The hybrid dressing achieved comparable wound closure to standard care but superior tissue quality, neovascularization, and regulated CD31 expression.
Validation· Preclinical efficacy was evaluated using a rodent Staphylococcus aureus-infected wound model.
