Proliferation and osteogenic differentiation of rat BMSCs on a novel Ti/SiC metal matrix nanocomposite modified by friction stir processing.

Low AM Biomedical Materials pubmed Peripheral Background only in_vitro Exp. validation
Chenyuan Zhu, Yuting Lv, Chao Qian, Haixin Qian, Ting Jiao et al. Scientific reports 2016-12-13 10.1038/srep38875
单位: Department of Prosthodontics, Ninth People's Hospital, affiliated to Shanghai JiaoTong University School of Medicine, Shanghai Key Laboratory of Stomatology, No. 639, Zhizaoju Road, Shanghai, 200011,
通讯: Fuqiang Zhang @ Department of Prosthodontics, Ninth People's Hospital, affiliated to Shanghai JiaoTong University School of Medicine, Sh [推断]
相关性: 该论文涉及钛基纳米复合材料的力学与生物相容性，但制备方法为搅拌摩擦加工而非增材制造，且未针对假体应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper uses friction stir processing, not LPBF or any additive manufacturing, and does not address porous structure, stress shielding, fatigue, or stiffness matching relevant to biomedical implants.
动机· 制备具有优良力学和生物相容性的Ti/SiC纳米复合材料。
方法· 采用搅拌摩擦加工技术制备纳米复合材料，并进行微观结构与力学表征。
结果· 改性后的纳米复合材料促进大鼠骨髓间充质干细胞黏附、增殖及成骨分化。
验证· 通过SEM、TEM、纳米压痕及体外细胞实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To fabricate a titanium/silicon carbide nanocomposite with enhanced mechanical properties and biocompatibility for potential implant applications.
Method· Friction stir processing was employed to disperse SiC nanoparticles in titanium, followed by microstructural characterization and mechanical testing via nanoindentation and hardness measurements.
Result· The nanocomposite surface improved adhesion, proliferation, and osteogenic differentiation of rat bone marrow stromal cells compared to unmodified titanium.
Validation· Validation included microstructural observation (SEM/TEM), mechanical testing, and in vitro cell assays (immunofluorescence, MTT, ALP, PCR).
