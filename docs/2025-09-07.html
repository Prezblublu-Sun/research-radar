相关性: 该论文直接研究了LPBF制备的多孔Ti-6Al-4V合金的力学性能，包括能量吸收、各向异性和应力分布，与用户对AM生物医学植入物的研究方向高度相关。

Motivation· To overcome mechanical anisotropy and stress concentration in porous Ti-6Al-4V alloys for aerospace and biomedical applications.
Method· Gradient-disordered porous architectures with trapezo-rhombic dodecahedron cells were fabricated via laser powder bed fusion and characterized by compression tests and finite element simulations.

边界: This paper does not address constraint handling, uncertainty quantification, or implant/biomechanics design; it focuses on unconstrained PDE surrogate modeling with pure numerical benchmarks.
动机· 神经算子在大数据下有效，但低数据场景泛化差，需更数据高效的代理模型。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Neural operators require large datasets and generalize poorly in low-data regimes, motivating a more data-efficient surrogate paradigm for time-dependent PDEs.
