方法· 对SLM试样进行临界区间热处理，通过XRD、SEM、EDS表征微观结构，并进行动电位极化测试
结果· 720°C回火形成的逆转变奥氏体提高了点蚀电位，降低了腐蚀电流密度

Motivation· This study investigates the effect of intercritical heat treatment on the pitting corrosion resistance of additively manufactured maraging steel.
Method· Selective laser melted maraging steel specimens were tempered at temperatures near austenite formation, followed by microstructural characterization and potentiodynamic polarization tests in NaCl solution.
Result· Tempering at 720°C promoted reversed austenite precipitation along martensite lath boundaries, enhancing pitting potential and reducing corrosion rate.
Validation· Experimental validation through electrochemical measurements and microstructural analysis with XRD/SEM/EDS.

Cheol Ho Pyeon, Kota Morioka Journal of Nuclear Science and Technology 2022-01-09 10.1080/00223131.2021.2017371
单位: Kyoto University
通讯: Cheol Ho Pyeon @ Kyoto University
相关性: 该论文涉及核反应堆的不确定性量化，与生物力学有限元代理模型或植入物设计完全无关。
边界: This paper focuses on nuclear reactor criticality uncertainty quantification, with no FEA surrogate modeling, no biomedical context, no implant mechanics, and no constraint handling or optimization.
动机· 量化轻水慢化轻水反射堆的临界与反应性不确定性
方法· 使用SCALE系统的KENO-VI、TSUNAMI-3D、TSAR和TSURFER模块进行特征值计算与截面调整
结果· 发现keff偏差主要由铀-235裂变谱不确定性引起，截面调整可降低不确定性
验证· 与京都大学临界装置实验数据对比验证

Motivation· To quantify uncertainties in criticality and reactivity for light-water-moderated and light-water-reflected cores with highly-enriched uranium fuel.
Method· Eigenvalue calculations and uncertainty quantification were performed using KENO-VI, TSUNAMI-3D, TSAR, and TSURFER modules of the SCALE code system.
Result· The discrepancy in keff was mainly attributed to the uncertainty of the 235U fission spectrum, and cross-section adjustment effectively reduced uncertainties.
Validation· Numerical results were compared with experimental data from the Kyoto University Critical Assembly.
