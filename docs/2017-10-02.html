单位: University of Calgary

相关性: 该论文将贝叶斯优化应用于油藏模拟，而非生物力学或植入物设计，但方法本身（GP代理+采集函数）可迁移至昂贵仿真优化问题。
边界: This paper applies Bayesian optimization to reservoir engineering, not to FEA surrogate modeling for implant biomechanics; it lacks biomedical context, constraint handling, and uncertainty quantification beyond point prediction.
动机· 油藏多井垫优化中传统方法计算成本过高

Motivation· Conventional optimization methods are computationally prohibitive for multi-well pad design due to expensive reservoir simulations.
Method· A Bayesian optimization framework using Gaussian process surrogate and expected improvement acquisition function is developed to efficiently search the design space.
Result· The optimized scenario achieves 36% higher oil recovery and 55.7% higher net present value compared to a field reference case.
Validation· Validation is performed by comparing the optimized result against a field reference case in a simulation study.

通讯: Daniël Hoornenborg @ MC Slotervaart, Amsterdam - The Netherlands
相关性: 该论文通过RSA测量了HA涂层对Zweymuller型股骨柄迁移的影响，但未涉及应力遮挡、接触力学或有限元分析。

Validation· Clinical RSA imaging at multiple time points up to 24 months.
