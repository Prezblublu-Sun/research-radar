Experimental Investigation of Friction Factor Performance in Additively Manufactured PCHE-Type Semicircular Channels with Corner Filleting

Lam Lam, Yifan Yang, Jiahang Chen, Lap Mou Tam, Afshin J. Ghajar Fluids 2026-06-07 10.3390/fluids11060142
单位: Department of Electromechanical Engineering, Faculty of Science and Technology, University of Macau, Avenida da Universidade, Taipa, Macau SAR 999078, China
相关性: 该论文研究增材制造PCHE通道的摩擦因子，与生物医学植入物的应力屏蔽、疲劳或骨整合无关。
边界: This paper focuses on heat exchanger channel friction factors, with no biomedical implant context, no stress shielding analysis, no mechanical testing of porous structures, and no osseointegration or fatigue evaluation.
动机· 研究增材制造PCHE通道中圆角设计对摩擦因子的影响。
方法· 实验测试四种SLM打印不锈钢通道，测量摩擦因子并表征表面形貌。
结果· 圆角设计使摩擦因子增加约9%，并降低了转捩雷诺数。
验证· 与现有光滑通道和粗糙通道关联式对比，并开发了经验关联式。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of corner filleting on friction factor in additively manufactured PCHE-type semicircular channels with high surface roughness.
Method· Four SLM-printed stainless steel test sections (semicircular and rounded-edge) with hydraulic diameters of 2 mm and 4 mm were experimentally tested using water over Re 500–7000 and heat flux 20–90 kW/m2.
Result· Rounded edges increased friction factor by approximately 9% for both diameters and lowered the transition Reynolds number compared to smooth channels.
Validation· Experimental data were compared with existing smooth PCHE and rough AM mini-channel correlations, and two empirical correlations were developed.
