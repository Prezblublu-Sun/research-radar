TS-RSR: A provably efficient approach for batch Bayesian Optimization

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Zhaolin Ren, Na Li arXiv preprint 2024-03-07 link
相关性: 该论文提出了一种批量贝叶斯优化方法，可迁移至植入物设计中的昂贵仿真优化问题。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modeling, implant biomechanics, or uncertainty quantification in physical simulations.
动机· 提高批量贝叶斯优化中采样效率，减少冗余并平衡探索与利用。
方法· 提出TS-RSR方法，通过最小化Thompson采样近似下的遗憾-不确定性比来协调批量采样。
结果· 在多个合成和真实测试函数上达到最先进性能，并提供了理论收敛保证。
验证· 在标准数值基准函数上与其他批量BO算法对比验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To improve sampling efficiency in batch Bayesian optimization by reducing redundancy and balancing exploration and exploitation.
Method· Proposes TS-RSR, which coordinates batch sampling by minimizing a Thompson sampling approximation of a regret-to-uncertainty ratio.
Result· Achieves state-of-the-art performance on synthetic and realistic test functions, with rigorous theoretical convergence guarantees.
Validation· Validated on standard numerical benchmark functions against competitive batch BO algorithms.
