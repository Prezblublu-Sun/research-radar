英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate whether the type of anesthesia is associated with early periprosthetic joint infection after total hip or knee arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective observational study of 3,909 arthroplasties with propensity score matching and multivariable logistic regression.
Result· General anesthesia was associated with a higher risk of early PJI (OR 2.0, 95% CI 1.0-3.7) compared to spinal anesthesia.
Validation· Propensity score matching and multivariable regression on clinical data.
