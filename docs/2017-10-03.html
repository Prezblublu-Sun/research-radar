Improving stress shielding following total hip arthroplasty by using a femoral stem made of β type Ti-33.6Nb-4Sn with a Young's modulus gradation.

High Hip Implant & Femoral Stem pubmed Direct Read now simulation_case
Go Yamako, Dennis Janssen, Shuji Hanada, Thomas Anijs, Kiyohide Ochiai et al. Journal of biomechanics 2017-10-03 10.1016/j.jbiomech.2017.08.017
单位: Organization for Promotion of Tenure Track, University of Miyazaki, Miyazaki, Japan. Electronic address: g.yamako@cc.miyazaki-u.ac.jp.
通讯: Go Yamako @ Organization for Promotion of Tenure Track, University of Miyazaki, Miyazaki, Japan. Electronic address: g.yamako@cc.miy
相关性: 该论文直接针对髋关节假体应力遮挡问题，使用有限元模拟评估梯度模量股骨柄的骨重塑效果，与用户的方向高度相关。
边界: No experimental validation or micromotion quantification; only simulation-based bone remodeling prediction.
动机· 传统股骨柄刚度大导致应力遮挡，本文提出梯度模量设计以减少骨吸收。
方法· 采用Ti-33.6Nb-4Sn合金并通过局部热处理实现模量梯度，利用有限元模拟骨重塑。
结果· 梯度模量柄在Gruen 7区10年骨密度比Ti-6Al-4V柄高42.6%，且应力强度比更低。
验证· 有限元应变自适应骨重塑模拟，无实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce stress shielding-induced bone resorption after total hip arthroplasty by designing a femoral stem with a gradient in Young's modulus.
Method· A cementless stem made of Ti-33.6Nb-4Sn alloy with a graduated Young's modulus from proximal (82.1 GPa) to distal (51.0 GPa) was developed using local heat treatment and evaluated via finite element bone remodeling simulation.
Result· The predicted bone mineral density loss in Gruen zone 7 was 45.8% at 10 years for the gradient stem, which was 42.6% higher than that for a conventional Ti-6Al-4V stem, with lower stress-strength ratio.
Validation· Strain-adaptive bone remodeling simulation without experimental or clinical validation.
