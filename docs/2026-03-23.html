相关性: 此论文专注于联邦学习和隐私保护医疗AI，未涉及有限元分析、代理建模或生物力学设计。
边界: The paper focuses on federated learning for medical image privacy and uncertainty quantification, with no mention of FEA, surrogate modeling, or biomechanical implant design.
动机· 解决联邦学习中数据异质性和隐私限制导致的预测可靠性问题。
方法· 提出TrustFed框架，结合表示感知客户端分配和软近邻阈值聚合。

English summary & terms
Motivation· Addressing predictive reliability degradation due to data heterogeneity and privacy constraints in federated learning for medical imaging.
Method· TrustFed introduces representation-aware client assignment and soft-nearest neighbor threshold aggregation for distribution-free uncertainty quantification.
Result· Provides finite-sample coverage guarantees validated on over 430,000 medical images across six modalities.
Validation· Extensive numerical experiments on diverse medical imaging datasets.
