Motivation· To design porous metallic biomaterials that simultaneously mimic the topological, mechanical, and mass transport properties of trabecular bone.
Method· Sixteen porous Ti-6Al-4V structures based on four triply periodic minimal surfaces were fabricated by selective laser melting and characterized for topology, quasi-static mechanical properties, fatigue resistance, and permeability.
