Iterative Model-Learning Scheme via Gaussian Processes for Nonlinear Model Predictive Control of (Semi-)Batch Processes

Tai Xuan Tan, Alexander Mitsos, Eike Cramer arXiv preprint 2026-04-24 link
相关性: 该论文的GP模型学习和不确定性量化方法可迁移至FEA替代模型的主动学习和约束优化问题。
边界: This paper applies GP surrogate modeling to chemical process control, not to FEA or structural/ biomedical mechanics.
动机· 批量过程非线性模型预测控制因模型成本高而难以应用。
方法· 提出基于高斯过程的迭代模型学习方案，利用初始轨迹数据逐步更新GP并嵌入NMPC。
结果· 四次迭代后跟踪误差降低83%，经济目标下产物质量提升17倍。
验证· 在半间歇聚合反应器仿真中验证，与全模型NMPC性能相当。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Adopting NMPC for batch processes is hindered by the cost and unavailability of dynamic models.
Method· A Gaussian process model-learning NMPC scheme is proposed that initializes from a single trajectory and iteratively updates the GP with new observations.
Result· After four iterations, tracking error reduced by 83%, and under economic objective final product mass increased 17-fold by iteration eight.
Validation· The approach is validated in silico on a semi-batch polymerization reactor and achieves performance on par with full-model NMPC.
