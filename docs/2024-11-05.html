Result· The software-based method was found to be more convenient, efficient, and accurate than the 3D printing model method.
Validation· No experimental validation was reported; the comparison was based on design accuracy and efficiency metrics.

Motivation· To evaluate trends in cementation fixation type and associated survival rates in total hip arthroplasty using a large Italian registry.
Method· Retrospective analysis of 137,256 primary and 4,857 revision THA procedures from 2000 to 2020, stratified by fixation technique and age.
Result· Cementless fixation dominates in patients under 40 (99.5%), while fully cemented stems show the highest 10-year survival (94.8%).
Validation· Registry-based observational study with comparison to published literature.
