Motivation· To evaluate the accuracy of digital templating in predicting implant size for total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective study of 215 patients analyzed preoperative digital templating on AP and lateral radiographs.
Result· The accuracy of predicting implant size within one size was above 95% for both stem and cup, with exact prediction around 70-77%.
Validation· Validation was performed by comparing templated sizes to the actual implanted sizes used during surgery.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· This narrative review aims to critically evaluate the influence of dental implant macrogeometric features on primary stability.
Method· A comprehensive literature search was conducted in PubMed, Embase, and Cochrane Library, followed by screening, quality assessment, and data extraction.
Result· Larger diameter and conical shape increase bone-implant contact area and improve primary stability, while implant length beyond 12 mm shows no additional benefit.
Validation· No experimental or numerical validation was performed; this is a literature review.
