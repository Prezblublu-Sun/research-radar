Learn Fast, Forget Slow: Safe Predictive Learning Control for Systems with Unknown and Changing Dynamics Performing Repetitive Tasks

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Peripheral Background only mechanical_testing Exp. validation UQ
Christopher D. McKinnon, Angela P. Schoellig arXiv preprint 2018-10-15 link
相关性: 该论文关注地面车辆的路径跟踪控制，与生物力学有限元代理建模无直接关联。
边界: No FEA, no surrogate modeling for biomechanics, no implant design, no uncertainty quantification in structural mechanics.
动机· 提高地面车辆在变化工况下重复路径跟踪的长期性能。
方法· 使用加权贝叶斯线性回归建模未知动力学，结合管状模型预测控制。
结果· 在900kg地面机器人上验证了3公里行驶中的快速适应与长期学习能力。
验证· 真实地面机器人实验，包含物理和人为动力学变化。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To improve long-term repetitive path following for ground vehicles under changing and initially unknown operating conditions.
Method· Weighted Bayesian Linear Regression models unknown dynamics, integrated with Tube Model Predictive Control for long prediction horizons.
Result· Experimental validation on a 900 kg ground robot over 3.0 km demonstrates fast adaptation and long-term learning under both physical and artificial dynamics changes.
Validation· Real-world experiments on a ground robot with stereo camera localization, including deliberate dynamics alterations.

相关性: 该论文关注超参数学习的迁移方法，与FEA代理建模或生物力学设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on hyperparameter optimization for machine learning, not on surrogate modeling for expensive simulations, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification, or biomechanics applications.
动机· 减少超参数学习中重复探索的成本
方法· 利用数据集分布嵌入实现跨任务高斯过程迁移

Method· A joint Gaussian process model on hyperparameters and data representations using distribution embeddings into reproducing kernel Hilbert spaces.
Result· The proposed method achieves faster convergence compared to baselines, requiring only a few evaluations of the target objective.
Validation· Validated on standard hyperparameter optimization benchmarks.

单位: Department of Engineering, Aarhus University, 8000 Aarhus C, Denmark.
通讯: Menglin Chen @ Department of Engineering, Aarhus University, 8000 Aarhus C, Denmark; Interdisciplinary Nanoscience Center, Aarhus Unive
相关性: 该论文涉及生物打印中的纳米纤维制造，但完全没有涉及AI或机器学习。
边界: No AI/ML integration, no automation, no digital workflow, and no autonomous decision-making.
动机· 开发3D大孔水凝胶纳米纤维用于细胞培养和组织再生。
方法· 湿法静电纺丝结合钙离子交联和紫外光交联制备海藻酸钠/明胶纳米纤维。
结果· 纳米纤维支持间充质干细胞高增殖和心肌细胞成熟。
验证· 通过间充质干细胞和iPSC来源的心肌细胞体外培养验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To fabricate three-dimensional macroporous hydrogel nanofibers that enhance cell adhesion, proliferation, and maturation for tissue engineering.
Method· Wet electrospinning of alginate/gelatin with polyethylene oxide and Pluronic F127, combined with in situ Ca2+ crosslinking and subsequent UV crosslinking, to produce stable nanofiber meshes.
Result· The alginate/gelatin hydrogel nanofibers exhibited low cytotoxicity and supported over 8-fold mesenchymal stem cell proliferation over 5 weeks and maturation of human iPSC-derived ventricular cardiomyocytes.
Validation· In vitro cell culture assays using mesenchymal stem cells and human iPSC-derived cardiomyocytes confirmed biocompatibility and improved performance over bulk hydrogels.

单位: School of Biochemistry and Cell Biology, University College Cork, Cork, Ireland.
通讯: Ruslan I Dmitriev @ School of Biochemistry and Cell Biology, University College Cork, Cork, Ireland; Institute for Regenerative Medicine, I.
相关性: 该论文未涉及AI或生物打印流程，与用户方向无关。
边界: No AI or machine learning integration into bioprinting; focuses on biosensor scaffold design and FLIM imaging, not bioprinting process.
动机· 开发可测量pH和Ca2+梯度的纤维素基生物传感器支架，用于组织工程。
方法· 利用纤维素结合域融合荧光蛋白制备支架，并通过FLIM检测pH和Ca2+。
结果· 支架成功检测到细胞外酸化，并实现与氧合监测的多参数成像。

Motivation· To design cellulose-based biosensor scaffolds capable of quantitative pH and Ca2+ gradient measurement for engineered tissue models.
Method· Cellulose-binding domain fused with fluorescent proteins was used to label cellulose matrices, enabling fluorescence lifetime imaging of pH and Ca2+.
Result· The biosensor scaffolds detected extracellular acidification in response to membrane depolarization and allowed multiparametric imaging with oxygenation monitoring.
Validation· Validation was performed using 3D cultures of human colon cancer cells and mouse intestinal organoids.
