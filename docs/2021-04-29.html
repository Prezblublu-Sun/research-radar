单位: Key Lab of Bionic Engineering, Ministry of Education, Jilin University, Changchun, 130022, China
通讯: Yunting Guo @ Key Lab of Bionic Engineering, Ministry of Education, Jilin University, Changchun, 130022, China  |  Zhihui Zhang @ Key Lab of Bionic Engineering, Ministry of Education, Jilin University, Changchun, 130022, China
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM-NiTi形状记忆合金的工艺参数与力学性能，但对多孔结构及应力屏蔽无涉及，与用户方向的直接匹配度有限。
边界: Does not link porous structure to stress shielding, lacks fatigue testing (only 10 cycles), and does not report surface roughness or osseointegration metrics.
动机· 研究相同能量密度下SLM-NiTi工艺参数对成形与性能的影响
方法· 设计五组相同能量密度的SLM-NiTi试样，采用XRD、SEM、DSC和循环拉伸测试等表征

验证· 通过应力控制循环拉伸实验验证形状记忆效应
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of process parameters on the forming quality and performance of SLM-NiTi shape memory alloys under constant energy density.
Method· Five sets of SLM-NiTi samples with the same energy density (40–90 J/mm³) were fabricated and characterized using XRD, SEM, DSC, and stress-controlled cyclic tensile tests.
Result· Surface quality depended on the P/V ratio; an ultrahigh tensile strength of 735 MPa and elongation of 10.88% were achieved, with a shape recovery ratio of 76.1% after ten cycles.
Validation· Shape memory effect was validated via stress-controlled cyclic tensile tests up to 400 MPa.
