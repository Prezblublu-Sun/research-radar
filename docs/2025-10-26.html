Clustering by Denoising: Latent plug-and-play diffusion for single-cell data

Dominik Meier, Shixing Yu, Sagnik Nandy, Promit Ghosal, Kyra Gan arXiv preprint 2025-10-26 link
相关性: 该论文专注于单细胞RNA测序数据的聚类去噪，与用户关注的有限元代理建模和植入物力学设计方向无关。
边界: This paper does not address finite element analysis, surrogate modeling, or any biomechanical or implant design context; it focuses on single-cell genomics clustering using diffusion models.
动机· 解决单细胞RNA测序数据中噪声导致聚类不准确的问题
方法· 提出隐空间即插即用扩散框架，通过吉布斯采样分离观测空间和去噪空间
结果· 在合成和真实数据上提高了聚类准确性和生物学一致性
验证· 在合成和真实单细胞基因组数据上验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve clustering accuracy in single-cell RNA-seq data corrupted by measurement noise and biological variability.
Method· A latent plug-and-play diffusion framework with Gibbs sampling that separates the observation space from the denoising space, steering the denoising trajectory via input-space reintroduction of noise.
Result· The method improves clustering accuracy on synthetic data across varied noise levels and yields biologically coherent clusters on real scRNA-seq data.
Validation· Validated on both synthetic and real single-cell genomics datasets with noise and dataset shifts.

Centrum: Model-based Database Auto-tuning with Minimal Distributional Assumptions

Yuanhao Lai, Pengfei Zheng, Chenpeng Ji, Yan Li, Songhan Zhang et al. arXiv preprint 2025-10-26 link
相关性: 该论文专注于数据库系统自动调优，与有限元分析和替代建模方向完全无关。
边界: This paper addresses DBMS auto-tuning with no application to FEA, surrogate modeling for mechanical systems, or implant/biomechanics design.
动机· 现有高斯过程贝叶斯优化在数据库调优中假设被违反，性能受限。
方法· 提出Centrum，采用两阶段随机梯度提升集成和自适应共形预测实现无分布假设的代理建模。
结果· 在两种数据库和三种工作负载上，Centrum优于21种最先进方法。
验证· 物理和仿真实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The fundamental assumptions of GP-BO are widely violated in DBMS performance modeling, limiting tuning efficiency and effectiveness.
Method· Centrum improves distribution-free point and interval estimation via a two-phase learning procedure of stochastic gradient boosting ensembles and a generalized locally-adaptive conformal prediction for uncertainty estimation.
Result· Extensive experiments on two DBMSs and three workloads show Centrum outperforms 21 state-of-the-art methods.
Validation· Physical and simulation experiments on database systems.

Variational Polya Tree

Lu Xu, Tsai Hor Chan, Kwok Fai Lam, Lequan Yu, Guosheng Yin arXiv preprint 2025-10-26 link
相关性: 该论文涉及密度估计和不确定性量化，但与FEA或替代模型在生物力学设计中的应用无关。
边界: This paper does not address expensive simulation sampling, constraint handling, or surrogate modeling for implant/biomechanics design.
动机· 现有密度估计方法缺乏可解释性和不确定性量化。
方法· 提出变分Polya树模型，使用随机变分推断计算后验分布。
结果· 在真实数据和图像上展示了与深度密度估计方法的竞争力。
验证· 在真实数据集和图像上评估性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Existing density estimation methods lack interpretability and uncertainty quantification, motivating a Bayesian nonparametric approach.
Method· The variational Polya tree model employs stochastic variational inference to compute posterior distributions for flexible density estimation.
Result· The method achieves competitive performance on real data and images compared to state-of-the-art deep density estimation techniques.
Validation· The model is validated on real datasets and image data, with code publicly available.
