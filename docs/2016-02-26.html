单位: Centre for Water Systems, College of Engineering, Mathematics and Physical Sciences, University of Exeter, North Park Road, Harrison Building, Exeter, EX4 4QF, UK
通讯: Wei Qi @ Centre for Water Systems, College of Engineering, Mathematics and Physical Sciences, University of Exeter, North Park R  |  C. Zhang @ School of Hydraulic Engineering, Dalian University of Technology, Dalian 116024, China  |  Guangtao Fu @ Centre for Water Systems, College of Engineering, Mathematics and Physical Sciences, University of Exeter, North Park R  |  Chris Sweetapple @ Centre for Water Systems, College of Engineering, Mathematics and Physical Sciences, University of Exeter, North Park R  |  Hang Zhou @ School of Hydraulic Engineering, Dalian University of Technology, Dalian 116024, China
相关性: 该论文研究水文预报中的降水产品与模型不确定性，与FEA替代模型或植入物设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on hydrology and precipitation products, with no FEA, surrogate modeling, or biomechanics context.
动机· 评估全球高分辨率降水产品在水文模拟中的适用性及不确定性来源。
方法· 采用两种水文模型与六种降水产品组合，量化降水、模型及交互作用对径流不确定性的贡献。
结果· 降水产品与模型交互作用对极端径流不确定性贡献显著，优质降水产品不保证优质径流模拟。
验证· 基于中国东北地区实测径流数据验证模拟结果。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the applicability of six fine-resolution precipitation products and quantify uncertainty contributions in ensemble discharge simulations.
Method· Two hydrological models of different complexity were combined with six precipitation products to decompose uncertainty contributions from precipitation, models, and their interactions.
Result· Interactions between precipitation products and hydrological models contribute significantly to discharge uncertainty, and a better precipitation product does not guarantee better discharge simulation.
Validation· Validation was performed using observed discharge data from a basin in northeastern China.
