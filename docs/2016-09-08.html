通讯: Guohua Gao @ Shell Global Solutions US, Inc
相关性: 该论文提出的分布式高斯-牛顿方法用于历史匹配中的不确定性量化，其多局部高斯近似和高效采样策略可迁移至生物力学代理模型中的不确定性量化问题。

结果· 新方法在效率和精度上优于RML和EnKF，与MCMC相当。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To efficiently quantify uncertainty in model parameters and production forecasts after conditioning to production data.
Method· A distributed Gauss-Newton method is used to find multiple local minima in parallel, and the posterior is approximated as a weighted sum of local Gaussian distributions around each minimum.
Result· The proposed approach achieves comparable accuracy to MCMC while significantly reducing computational cost compared to RML and EnKF.
Validation· Validated on a nonlinear toy problem and a real-field reservoir history matching case with strong nonlinearity.
