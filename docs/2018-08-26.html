Integrative Design, Build, Test Approach for Biomedical Devices With Lattice Structures

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Skim only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Paul Egan, Isabella Bauer, Kristina Shea, Stephen J. Ferguson 2018-08-26 10.1115/detc2018-85355
单位: Swiss Federal Institute of Technology, Zurich, Switzerland
通讯: Paul Egan @ Swiss Federal Institute of Technology, Zurich, Switzerland
相关性: 该论文涉及多孔支架的设计与力学测试，但使用Polyjet而非LPBF，且未涉及应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no fatigue testing, no stress shielding analysis, and no surface roughness or osseointegration characterization.
动机· 开发集成设计-制造-测试方法以表征骨组织工程用点阵结构。
方法· 使用Polyjet打印不同孔隙率和梁径的点阵，进行准静态压缩测试。
结果· 点阵弹性模量28-180MPa，椎体融合器刚度1.7-7.2kN/mm，可承受典型脊柱载荷。
验证· 通过显微镜验证打印精度，压缩测试验证力学性能。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop an integrated design-build-test methodology for characterizing beam-based lattice structures for bone tissue engineering.
Method· Lattices with 50% and 70% porosity and beam diameters of 0.4-1.0 mm were fabricated via polyjet printing and tested under quasi-static compression.
Result· Lattice elastic moduli ranged from 28 to 180 MPa, and vertebral fusion cage prototypes achieved stiffnesses of 1.7-7.2 kN/mm, suitable for spinal loads up to 1.65 kN.
Validation· Build accuracy was validated by microscopy, and mechanical properties were validated through compression testing.
