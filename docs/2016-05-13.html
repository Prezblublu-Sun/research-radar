英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the long-term survivorship and factors associated with failure after Bernese periacetabular osteotomy for developmental hip dysplasia.
Method· A retrospective study of 158 dysplastic hips with Kaplan-Meier survival analysis and multinomial logistic regression.
Result· At 18 years, survival rate was 74%, 21% underwent THA, and 53% remained asymptomatic.
Validation· Clinical follow-up over 14-22 years with validated outcome scores (UCLA, Harris hip, WOMAC, HOOS).
