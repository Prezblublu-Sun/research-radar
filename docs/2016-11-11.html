On the Effective Properties of 3D Metamaterials

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only theoretical
Mohamed Abdelhamid, Aleksander Czekanski 2016-11-11 10.1115/imece2016-67407
单位: York University, Toronto, ON, Canada
通讯: Mohamed Abdelhamid @ York University, Toronto, ON, Canada
相关性: 该论文研究晶格结构的力学性能，但未涉及生物医学植入物或增材制造验证。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing, no biomedical context, no fatigue or osseointegration.
动机· 建立八面体桁架单胞的连续模型以描述晶格结构的有效力学性能。
方法· 将几何参数（晶格角、加载方向等）纳入刚度矩阵，并引入表面弹性模型考虑尺寸效应。
结果· 分析了各几何参数对刚度张量的影响，确定了不同晶格角下最高弹性模量的加载方向。
验证· 纯理论推导，无实验或数值验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop a continuum model for the octet-truss unit cell that captures the effective mechanical properties of lattice structures.
Method· Geometric parameters (lattice angle, loading direction, etc.) are incorporated into the stiffness matrix, and the Gurtin-Murdoch surface elasticity model is used to include size effects.
Result· The influence of each geometric parameter on the stiffness tensor is analyzed, and the loading direction yielding the highest elastic modulus is identified for various lattice angles.
Validation· The study is purely theoretical with no experimental or numerical validation.
