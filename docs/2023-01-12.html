Effect of Porosity and Heat Treatment on Mechanical Properties of Additive Manufactured CoCrMo Alloys

High AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Read now mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Tu‐Ngoc Lam, Kuang-Ming Chen, Cheng-Hao Tsai, Pei‐I Tsai, Meng–Huang Wu et al. Materials 2023-01-12 10.3390/ma16020751
单位: Department of Materials Science and Engineering, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, Hsinchu 30010, Taiwan
通讯: E‐Wen Huang @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, Hsinchu 30010, Taiwan
相关性: 该论文直接研究多孔CoCrMo合金的弹性模量与骨匹配，以减少应力遮挡，与AM生物医学材料方向高度相关。
边界: Paper does not evaluate fatigue performance, surface roughness, or osseointegration, which are important for long-term implant success.
动机· 减少金属生物材料的应力遮挡效应，匹配骨刚度。
方法· 选择性激光熔化制备不同孔隙率（20-80%）的bcc结构CoCrMo合金并进行热处理。
结果· 80%孔隙率合金的弹性模量和屈服强度最接近骨组织。
验证· 压缩力学实验与Gibson-Ashby理论模型对比验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To minimize the stress shielding effect of metallic biomaterials by matching the elastic modulus of bone.
Method· Selective laser melting was used to fabricate body-centered cubic porous CoCrMo alloys with designed volume porosities of 20, 40, 60, and 80%, followed by solution annealing and aging heat treatment.
Result· The elastic modulus and yield strength decreased with increasing porosity, and the 80% porosity alloy exhibited the closest match to bone tissue properties.
Validation· Experimental compressive mechanical properties were compared with analytical Gibson-Ashby model predictions.

Validation· Validation via four-point bending tests on additively manufactured specimens.

相关性: 该方法是一种高效求解PDE的谱替代模型，可迁移至生物力学FEA替代建模，但未涉及不确定性量化或约束优化。
边界: No uncertainty quantification, no constraint handling, and no application to implant or biomechanics design.
动机· 提供高效且精确的PDE求解替代模型，避免传统方法对高性能计算的依赖。
方法· 基于切比雪夫多项式构造Sobolev空间的谱近似，结合变分公式求解线性和非线性PDE。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop an efficient surrogate model for solving PDEs that avoids high-performance computing while maintaining high accuracy.
Method· A polynomial surrogate model (PSM) based on Chebyshev polynomials approximates general Sobolev spaces and solves PDEs via a variational formulation that yields convex optimization for linear problems.
Result· The PSM outperforms physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) in both accuracy and runtime, and solves prominent PDEs on a local machine without HPC.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on linear and nonlinear PDEs demonstrate exponential convergence and efficiency advantages over PINNs.
