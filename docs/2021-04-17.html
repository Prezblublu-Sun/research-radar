Motivation· To evaluate the minimum 5-year outcomes and conversion rate to total hip arthroplasty after isolated acetabular osteoplasty for femoroacetabular impingement syndrome.
Method· A retrospective case series of 86 patients undergoing hip arthroscopy with isolated acetabular osteoplasty for FAI with labral tears, excluding those with femoroplasty or prior ipsilateral hip surgery.
Result· At average 7.4-year follow-up, patient-reported outcomes were good to excellent, with 72.1% achieving PASS, a 3.5% revision rate, and a 5.8% conversion rate to THA.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with patient-reported outcome measures and conversion to THA as endpoints.
