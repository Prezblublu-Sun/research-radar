英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the efficacy of laser destruction of the articular branch of the obturator nerve in elderly patients with degenerative coxarthrosis who are not candidates for total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A prospective study of 34 patients who underwent laser ablation (970 nm, 9 Hz, 3 W, 100 J) with 12-month follow-up.
Result· Significant reduction in pain, improvement in quality of life and hip function, with low complication rates.
Validation· Clinical validation using VAS, SF-36, and Harris hip score.
