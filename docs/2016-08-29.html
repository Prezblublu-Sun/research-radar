边界: The paper focuses on reservoir two-phase flow, not on implant biomechanics, FEA surrogate modeling, or any biomedical context.
动机· 高效量化非均质多孔介质两相流中的不确定性。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To efficiently quantify uncertainty in saturation predictions for highly heterogeneous porous media flows.
Method· A streamline-based distribution method identifies hidden smooth random fields and nonlinear mappings to estimate saturation CDF and PDF.
Result· The proposed method matches Monte Carlo statistics in highly heterogeneous cases while being computationally inexpensive.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks on high-variance lognormal permeability fields, anisotropic layered fields, and channelized systems.

Uncertainty quantification using a self-supervised surrogate-assisted parallel Metropolis-Hastings algorithm

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project simulation_case UQ
Mohammad Sayyafzadeh Proceedings 2016-08-29 10.3997/2214-4609.201601822
相关性: 该论文提出了一种自监督代理辅助并行Metropolis-Hastings算法用于不确定性量化，其方法可迁移至生物力学植入物设计中的昂贵仿真采样问题。
边界: This paper does not address implant/biomechanics design, constraint handling, or multi-objective optimization; it focuses on reservoir simulation and uncertainty propagation in a different domain.
动机· 减少并行MCMC算法中大量数值模拟的计算成本
方法· 提出自监督代理辅助策略，利用两个代理模型（高温和低温链）并基于模糊规则动态选择使用仿真或代理
结果· 在相同计算量下，所提算法比传统并行MCMC获得更优的概率密度近似
验证· 通过IC故障模型仿真验证，并与标准并行MCMC对比
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce the computational cost of parallel tempered Metropolis-Hastings algorithms that require expensive numerical simulations at each step.
Method· A self-supervised surrogate-assisted algorithm is proposed, employing two surrogates (for high- and low-temperature chains) and a heuristic fuzzy rule to stochastically decide between exact simulation and surrogate based on estimated fidelities.
Result· The proposed algorithm achieves a significantly better approximation of the probability density function compared to the standard parallel tempered Metropolis-Hastings algorithm under the same computational budget.
Validation· Validation is performed on an IC-fault model simulation, comparing results with the unassisted parallel tempered Metropolis-Hastings algorithm over various chain lengths.
