单位: Department of Materials Science and Engineering, National Chiao Tung University, 1001 University Road, Hsinchu 30010, Taiwan.; Department of Physics, College of Education, Can Tho University, Can Tho
通讯: E-Wen Huang @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering, National Chiao Tung University, 1001 University Road, Hsinchu 30010, Ta [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究了增材制造椎弓根螺钉的几何与材料优化，通过添加生物活性玻璃降低弹性模量以减少应力遮挡，但未进行机械测试和疲劳分析。

Result· The composite with optimal bioactive glass content exhibited lower elastic modulus, reduced stress shielding, and improved osseointegration and bone volume fraction at 120 days post-implantation.
Validation· In vivo study using a sheep model with histological and radiographic evaluation at 120 days.
