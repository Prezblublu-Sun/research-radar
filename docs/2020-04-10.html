单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Boston University, Boston, MA 02215, USA

Morgan @ Department of Biomedical Engineering, Boston University, Boston, MA 02215, USA  |  Keith A. Brown @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Boston University, Boston, MA 02215, USA
相关性: 该论文提出的贝叶斯优化与自动化实验框架可直接迁移至植入物设计中的昂贵仿真采样效率问题。
边界: This paper focuses on experimental optimization of mechanical toughness in AM structures, not on FEA surrogate modeling or implant biomechanics.
动机· 增材制造设计空间巨大，实验仍是评估非线性力学性能的金标准。

English summary & terms
Motivation· The vast design space of additive manufacturing and the need for experimental evaluation of nonlinear mechanical properties motivate an efficient exploration strategy.

Result· The BEAR achieves an almost 60-fold reduction in the number of experiments needed to identify high-toughness structures compared to grid-based search.
Validation· Validation is performed through automated mechanical testing of a parametric family of additively manufactured structures.
