单位: Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Missouri University of Science and Technology, Rolla, Missouri, United States
通讯: Krishna C. R. Kolan @ Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Missouri University of Science and Technology, Rolla, Missouri, Unit
相关性: 该论文研究了不同孔隙几何形状对生物活性玻璃支架细胞增殖的影响，但未涉及应力遮挡、疲劳性能或LPBF制造，与用户研究方向相关性有限。
边界: This paper does not cover LPBF fabrication, mechanical testing (static or fatigue), stress shielding, or osseointegration; it focuses on SLS of bioactive glass scaffolds with in vitro cell studies.
动机· 探究孔隙几何形状对骨修复支架细胞增殖的影响。

Result· Scaffolds mimicking trabecular bone architecture promoted significantly higher cell proliferation than traditional lattice structures.
Validation· In vitro cell culture experiments with osteoblast-like cells over 2, 4, and 6 days.
