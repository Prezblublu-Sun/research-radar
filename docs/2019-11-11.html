单位: Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Monash University, VIC, 3800, Australia
通讯: Víctor Esteban Reyes-Cruz @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Monash University, VIC, 3800, Australia
相关性: 该论文研究了选区激光熔化316L不锈钢的残余应力对腐蚀行为的影响，未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或机械性能。

Motivation· To investigate how residual stress from selective laser melting affects the corrosion behavior of 316L stainless steel.

Validation· Experimental validation through electrochemical testing.

单位: University of New Orleans, New Orleans, LA
通讯: M. Shafiqur Rahman @ University of New Orleans, New Orleans, LA
相关性: 该论文研究铜铬锆合金选区激光熔化工艺的热行为和熔池动力学，不涉及生物医用植入物的多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper focuses on thermal-fluid simulation of Cu-Cr-Zr alloy SLM process, with no mention of porous structures, stress shielding, mechanical testing, fatigue, surface roughness, or osseointegration relevant to biomedical implants.
动机· 研究铜铬锆合金SLM工艺的热行为与熔池动力学，以降低制造成本
方法· 建立三维CFD模型，包含移动锥形体积热源和温度依赖热物性，模拟熔池流动
结果· 获得了不同激光参数下的温度、冷却速率、密度和流速分布
验证· 与经典Stefan问题的解析结果进行对比验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the thermal behavior and melt-pool dynamics during SLM of Cu-Cr-Zr alloy for process optimization and cost reduction.
Method· A 3D CFD model with a moving conical volumetric heat source and temperature-dependent properties was developed to simulate the SLM process.
Result· Temperature profiles, cooling rates, density, thermal conductivity, and melt-pool velocities were obtained for various laser parameters.
Validation· Model validation was performed by comparing simulation results with analytical solutions from the classical Stefan problem.

单位: State Key Laboratory of Oral Diseases, Department of Head and Neck Oncology, National Clinical Research Center for Oral Diseases, West China Hospital of Stomatology, Sichuan University, Chengdu 610041
通讯: Huixu Xie @ State Key Laboratory of Oral Diseases, Department of Head and Neck Oncology, National Clinical Research Center for Oral [推断]

边界: No LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), and no discussion of stress shielding or stiffness matching; it is a conventional bioceramic scaffold study.
动机· 改善磷酸钙陶瓷支架的骨传导性
方法· 在磷酸钙中掺杂锂离子制备多孔支架
结果· 掺杂锂后表面更粗糙，降解性更好，促进成骨

English summary & terms
Motivation· To overcome the poor osteoconductivity of calcium polyphosphate bioceramic scaffolds for bone regeneration.
Method· Lithium ions were doped into calcium polyphosphate scaffolds via conventional ceramic processing, and their microstructure and properties were characterized.
Result· Lithium doping resulted in rougher surface, improved degradation, and enhanced osteoblast proliferation and differentiation in vitro, as well as better osteoconductivity in a rabbit cranial defect model in vivo.
Validation· In vitro cell assays (proliferation and differentiation) and in vivo animal study (micro-CT and histology) were performed.
