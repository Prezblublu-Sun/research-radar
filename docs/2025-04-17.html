单位: School of Integrated Design Engineering, Graduate School of Science and Technology, Keio University, Yokohama 223-8522, Japan.

动机· 传统无血管肝细胞模型因传质受限导致功能衰退，需构建血管化肝球体。
方法· 将原代肝细胞与成纤维细胞共培养形成多细胞球体，嵌入纤维蛋白-胶原水凝胶，并在微流道中接种内皮细胞诱导血管网络。

Motivation· Traditional avascular hepatocyte culture models suffer from mass transfer limitations, necessitating vascularization to sustain hepatocyte function in vitro.
Method· Multicellular spheroids of primary hepatocytes and fibroblasts were formed in concave wells, embedded in fibrin-collagen hydrogel, and endothelial cells were seeded in adjacent microfluidic channels to induce microvascular networks.
Result· The vascularized hepatic spheroid exhibited sinusoid-like structures and significantly enhanced albumin secretion and urea synthesis.
Validation· Microvascular invasion was confirmed by fluorescence imaging, and hepatic function was quantified via biochemical assays for albumin and urea.

单位: Surgical Area, Hospital Clínic de Barcelona, University of Barcelona, Barcelona, Spain
通讯: Jenaro Á. Fernández-Valencia @ Department of Orthopedic Surgery and Traumatology, Hospital Clínic de Barcelona, University of Barcelona, Barcelona, Spa
相关性: 该论文仅关注医院手术流程效率，未涉及股骨假体生物力学或有限元分析。
边界: This paper focuses on hospital operational efficiency and Lean Six Sigma, with no biomechanical modeling, implant mechanics, stress shielding, micromotion, or finite element analysis of the hip implant.
动机· 评估精益六西格玛方法提高全髋关节置换术效率的效果
方法· 前后对照的准实验研究，分析改进措施对手术效率的影响
结果· 手术模块平均例数从1.76增至2.79，换台时间从29分钟降至18分钟
验证· 通过前后数据对比和统计检验验证效率提升
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the impact of Lean Six Sigma methodology on improving operating room efficiency for total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A quasi-experimental pre-post intervention study was conducted at a public university hospital, analyzing changes in surgical times, changeover times, and number of procedures per module.
Result· The average number of procedures per morning module increased from 1.76 to 2.79, and the average changeover time decreased from 29±12 min to 18±12 min, achieving a 50% improvement in efficiency.
Validation· Statistical comparison of pre- and post-intervention data from 24 planned sessions confirmed the improvements.

Validation· Clinical follow-up of a single case without biomechanical testing.
