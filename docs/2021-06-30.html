Motivation· To describe pre- and postoperative functional recovery in a recreational cyclist undergoing total hip arthroplasty for femoroacetabular impingement.
Method· Patient-reported outcome measures and strength assessments were performed before and at 8 and 24 months after surgery.
Result· Progressive improvement in pain, function, and strength deficits was observed, with abductor deficit reducing from 64.8% to 6.5%.
Validation· Single case report with clinical outcome measures.

Validation· No experimental validation; this is a literature review.
