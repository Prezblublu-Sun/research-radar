Novel DeepONet architecture to predict stresses in elastoplastic structures with variable complex geometries and loads

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark
Junyan He, Seid Koric, Shashank Kushwaha, Jaewan Park, Diab Abueidda et al. arXiv preprint 2023-06-06 10.1016/j.cma.2023.116277
相关性: 该方法可迁移至生物力学中的应力预测代理模型，但尚未在植入物或生物医学场景中验证。
边界: This work does not include biomedical context, implant-specific geometries, or uncertainty quantification, and lacks experimental validation on biomechanical problems.
动机· 加速复杂几何与载荷下弹塑性应力场的预测
方法· 提出以ResUNet为干网络的DeepONet架构，编码几何与载荷信息
结果· 新架构与纯ResUNet精度相当，但内存效率更高且灵活性强
验证· 与有限元模拟及两种基线模型对比验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To accelerate the prediction of full-field elastoplastic stress responses for complex geometries under variable loads, bypassing expensive finite element simulations.
Method· A novel DeepONet architecture is proposed, employing a ResUNet as the trunk network to encode complex input geometries and a fully-connected branch network for parametric loads, with element-wise fusion of latent representations.
Result· The proposed DeepONet achieves comparable accuracy to a standalone ResUNet while being more memory-efficient and flexible; both networks predict stress fields orders of magnitude faster than FEM and accurately identify stress concentration points.
Validation· Validation is performed by comparing against finite element simulations and two baseline models (standalone ResUNet and vanilla DeepONet) on topology-optimized geometries.
