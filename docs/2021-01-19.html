通讯: Maryam Tilton @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Pennsylvania State University, University Park, PA, USA  |  Guha Manogharan @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Pennsylvania State University, University Park, PA, USA
相关性: 该文直接研究了激光粉末床熔融制造的钛合金多孔结构与骨力学匹配，属于AM生物医学材料核心方向。
边界: This paper does not address fatigue performance, surface roughness, or osseointegration, which are key for long-term implant success.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Investigate the structure-property relationships of additively manufactured meta-biomaterials based on minimal surfaces for orthopedic applications.

Validation· Validation via mechanical testing and fracture analysis confirmed topology-dependent mechanical response.
