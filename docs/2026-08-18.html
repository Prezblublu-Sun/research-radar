英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate whether socioeconomic disadvantage is associated with worse postoperative outcomes after primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective cohort study using the TriNetX database, stratifying patients by presence of SDoH Z-codes and applying 1:1 propensity score matching to compare short- and long-term complications.
Result· Patients with socioeconomic risk factors had significantly higher risks of surgical site infection, deep vein thrombosis, sepsis, myocardial infarction, and mortality up to one year postoperatively.
Validation· The study used propensity score matching on a large multi-institutional clinical database to ensure covariate balance and robust outcome comparisons.
