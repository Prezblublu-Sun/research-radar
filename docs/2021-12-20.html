Advanced composite material: effect of composite SiC on compressive strength and hardness of porous titanium

Jialong Kang, Yaoran Cui, Jingjing Song, Hongxia Mao, Guibao Qiu et al. Journal of Materials Research and Technology 2021-12-20 10.1016/j.jmrt.2021.12.037
单位: College of Materials Science and Engineering, Chongqing University, No. 174 Shazheng Street, Shapingba District, Chongqing, 400044, China
通讯: Guibao Qiu @ Chongqing Key Laboratory of Vanadium–Titanium Metallurgy and New Materials, Chongqing University, Chongqing, 400044, Chi
相关性: 论文研究粉末冶金多孔钛，不涉及AM制造，可能与钛合金背景相关但非核心。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no fatigue testing, no stress shielding or stiffness matching analysis, no osseointegration data.
动机· 提高多孔钛作为骨植入材料的力学性能。
方法· 采用粉末冶金法制备不同SiC含量的多孔钛复合材料。
结果· 3wt% SiC时抗压强度最高达191.1 MPa，硬度显著提升。
验证· 通过压缩和硬度测试验证力学性能改善。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the mechanical properties of porous titanium for bone implant applications by adding SiC as a reinforcing agent.
Method· Porous titanium composites with varying SiC content (0–7.5 wt%) were fabricated via conventional powder metallurgy.
Result· The addition of 3 wt% SiC resulted in a maximum compressive strength of 191.1 MPa and significantly improved hardness.
Validation· Mechanical testing including compression and hardness measurements were conducted to validate the improvements.

Freeform 3D Bioprinting Involving Ink Gelation by Cascade Reaction of Oxidase and Peroxidase: A Feasibility Study Using Hyaluronic Acid-Based Ink

Shinji Sakai, Ryohei Harada, Takashi Kotani Biomolecules 2021-12-20 10.3390/biom11121908
单位: Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Graduate School of Engineering Science, Osaka University, 1–3 Machikaneyama, Osaka 560-8531, Japan
通讯: Shinji Sakai @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Graduate School of Engineering Science, Osaka University, 1–3 Machikane
相关性: 该论文不涉及任何人工智能或机器学习方法，与AI在生物打印中的应用方向无关。
边界: No AI/ML methodology, no data-driven modeling, no automation or decision-making component.
动机· 开发一种细胞相容的快速凝胶化方法用于自由形态生物打印。
方法· 利用氧化酶和过氧化物酶的级联反应使挤出墨水凝胶化。
结果· 成功制备了具有良好形状保真度和细胞活力的透明质酸水凝胶结构。
验证· 通过细胞培养10天保持85%以上存活率验证细胞相容性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a cytocompatible and rapid gelation method for freeform bioprinting of soft hydrogel constructs.
Method· Extrusion of ink containing choline, HRP, and a cross-linkable hyaluronic acid derivative into a supporting material with choline oxidase to induce enzymatic cascade gelation.
Result· Three-dimensional hyaluronic acid-based hydrogel constructs were fabricated with good shape fidelity and high cell viability over 10 days.
Validation· In vitro cell culture of mouse fibroblast cells encapsulated in the printed hydrogels confirmed cytocompatibility with over 85% viability.

Scaling the U-net: segmentation of biodegradable bone implants in high-resolution synchrotron radiation microtomograms.

Ivo M Baltruschat, Hanna Ćwieka, Diana Krüger, Berit Zeller-Plumhoff, Frank Schlünzen et al. Scientific reports 2021-12-20 10.1038/s41598-021-03542-y
单位: Deutsches Elektronen-Synchrotron DESY, Notkestr. 85, 22607, Hamburg, Germany. ivo.baltruschat@desy.de.
通讯: Ivo M Baltruschat @ Deutsches Elektronen-Synchrotron DESY, Notkestr. 85, 22607, Hamburg, Germany. ivo.baltruschat@desy.de.  |  Julian Moosmann @ Institute of Materials Physics, Helmholtz-Zentrum Hereon GmbH, 21502, Geesthacht, Germany. julian.moosmann@hereon.de.
相关性: 该论文仅涉及植入物图像分割，未涉及LPBF制造、力学测试或应力遮挡等研究方向。
边界: This paper focuses on image segmentation of implants using U-net, without any LPBF fabrication, mechanical testing, stress shielding analysis, fatigue, or as-built morphology characterization.
动机· 高分辨率SRμCT图像分割难以准确表征植入物降解与骨整合。
方法· 对2D U-net进行复合缩放，并融合多轴预测。
结果· 复合缩放U-net结合软投票多轴融合在降解层分割中取得最高IoU。
验证· 与半自动分割方法对比定量参数偏差更小。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Accurate segmentation of high-resolution synchrotron radiation microtomograms is challenging yet essential for quantifying osseointegration and biodegradation of bone implants.
Method· Systematically scaled the 2D U-net architecture by width, depth, and input size, and extended multi-axes prediction fusion with soft voting.
Result· Compound scaling combined with soft-voted multi-axes fusion achieved the highest intersection over union for the degradation layer class.
Validation· Segmentation parameters from the model deviated less from high-quality ground truth than those from semi-automatic segmentation.

Socioeconomic status and use of analgesic drugs before and after primary hip arthroplasty: a population-based cohort study of 103,209 patients during 1996–2018

André S Klenø, Martin Bækgaard Stisen, Nina M Edwards, Inger Mechlenburg, Alma B Pedersen Acta Orthopaedica 2021-12-20 10.2340/17453674.2021.955
单位: Department of Clinical Epidemiology, Aarhus University Hospital, Aarhus N, DK. ank@clin.au.dk
通讯: André S Klenø @ Department of Clinical Epidemiology, Aarhus University Hospital, Aarhus N, DK. ank@clin.au.dk
相关性: 该论文为全髋关节置换术后镇痛药使用的流行病学研究，不涉及任何生物力学分析、有限元模拟或植入物设计。
边界: This paper does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics; it is a clinical epidemiological study without any FEA, contact modeling, or implant design analysis.
动机· 研究社会经济地位对全髋关节置换术前后镇痛药使用趋势的影响
方法· 基于丹麦髋关节置换登记处103,209名患者的数据，分析处方记录与社会经济标志物关联
结果· 低教育、独居、低财富患者术前术后镇痛药使用率更高
验证· 基于大型注册数据库的统计描述，无实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate trends in analgesic utilization before and after total hip arthroplasty and their association with socioeconomic status.
Method· A population-based cohort study using the Danish Hip Arthroplasty Register, linking prescription data with markers of education, cohabiting status, and wealth.
Result· Lower socioeconomic status was associated with higher prevalence of analgesic use both before and after surgery, with overall peak use at 0–3 months post-THA.
Validation· Large-scale registry-based descriptive statistics, no experimental or mechanical validation.
