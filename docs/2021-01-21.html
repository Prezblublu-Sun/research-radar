3D printed Ti6Al4V bone scaffolds with different pore structure effects on bone ingrowth

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Skim only animal Exp. validation
Fuyuan Deng, Linlin Liu, Zhong Li, Juncai Liu Journal of Biological Engineering 2021-01-21 10.1186/s13036-021-00255-8
单位: Department of Orthopaedics, The Affiliated Hospital of Southwest Medical University, Luzhou, 646000, Sichuan, China
通讯: Fuyuan Deng @ Department of Orthopaedics, The Affiliated Hospital of Southwest Medical University, Luzhou, 646000, Sichuan, China
相关性: 该论文研究了不同孔隙结构的3D打印钛合金支架对骨长入的影响，涉及LPBF制造和体内实验，但未包含力学测试和疲劳分析。
边界: Does not include mechanical testing (static or fatigue) or stress shielding evaluation, which are required for the AM biomedical direction.
动机· 探索不同孔隙结构对骨支架骨长入的影响。
方法· 通过SLM制备四种拓扑结构的钛合金支架，植入兔股骨进行体内实验，并利用CFD分析流体特性。
结果· 菱形晶格单元(DIA)结构骨长入最佳，其内部流速差异最小且流径最长。
验证· 兔股骨体内植入6和12周后Micro-CT和组织切片分析。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the influence of different pore structures on bone ingrowth in porous titanium alloy scaffolds fabricated by selective laser melting.
Method· Four types of Ti6Al4V scaffolds with similar porosity and pore size but different lattice topologies were manufactured by SLM and implanted in rabbit distal femurs, complemented by CFD analysis of fluid flow.
Result· The diamond lattice (DIA) structure exhibited the best bone ingrowth, attributed to its minimal internal fluid velocity variation and longest flow trajectory.
Validation· In vivo evaluation through micro-CT and histological sectioning at 6 and 12 weeks post-implantation.
