单位: The State Key Laboratory of Digital Manufacturing Equipment and Technology, School of Mechanical Science and Engineering, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan, PR China
通讯: Ping Jiang @ The State Key Laboratory of Digital Manufacturing Equipment and Technology, School of Mechanical Science and Engineering
相关性: 该论文提出的自适应采样方法可迁移至生物力学代理模型构建，提高有限元仿真效率。
边界: No biomedical or implant context; validation on aerodynamic coefficient, not on biomechanics or implant design.
动机· 降低变保真度代理模型构建中的计算成本。
方法· 提出基于改进分层克里金的自适应采样方法，通过多项式响应面调整低保真模型。
结果· 在相同仿真成本下，该方法比传统方法获得更精确的代理模型。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To reduce the computational burden of building variable-fidelity surrogate models in complex engineering design.
Method· An adaptive sampling method based on improved hierarchical kriging is proposed, where the low-fidelity model is adjusted via a polynomial response surface.
Result· The proposed method yields more accurate metamodels at the same simulation cost compared to existing metamodeling and sequential sampling methods.
Validation· Validated on two numerical examples and an aerodynamic coefficient modeling of an aircraft.
