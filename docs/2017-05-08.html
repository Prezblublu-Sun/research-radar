Tibial revision knee arthroplasty with metaphyseal sleeves: The effect of stems on implant fixation and bone flexibility

Jan Nadorf, Stefan Kinkel, Simone Gantz, Eike Jakubowitz, Jan Philippe Kretzer PLoS ONE 2017-05-08 10.1371/journal.pone.0177285
单位: Laboratory of Biomechanics and Implant Research, Center of Orthopedics and Traumatology, University Hospital Heidelberg, Heidelberg, Germany
通讯: Jan Nadorf @ Laboratory of Biomechanics and Implant Research, Center of Orthopedics and Traumatology, University Hospital Heidelberg,
相关性: 该论文研究胫骨翻修假体中的应力遮挡和微动，方法可迁移至股骨柄假体分析，但未涉及髋关节。
边界: This paper focuses on tibial knee revision implants, not femoral hip stems, and does not quantify proximal femur stress shielding in Gruen zones or include hip-specific loading conditions.
动机· 评估胫骨翻修假体中不同柄组合对固定和应力遮挡的影响
方法· 在合成胫骨上植入假体，施加扭矩并用数字图像相关测量微动和变形
结果· 长柄减少微动但增加近端应力遮挡，短柄平衡微动和骨变形
验证· 合成骨实验与数字图像相关测量
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the effect of different stem combinations on implant fixation and stress shielding in tibial revision knee arthroplasty with metaphyseal sleeves.
Method· Four stem configurations were implanted in synthetic tibiae with AORI Type I defects; axial and varus-valgus torques were applied, and relative micromotions and bone deformation were measured using digital image correlation.
Result· Longer stems reduced proximal micromotions but increased proximal stress shielding, while short stems offered a balance between micromotion reduction and more natural bone deformation.
Validation· Mechanical testing on synthetic bones with digital image correlation for full-field displacement measurement.

Effect of HAp and β‐TCP incorporation on the tribological response of Ti6Al4V biocomposites for implant parts

单位: Center for Microelectromechanical Systems (CMEMS‐UMinho), University of Minho Guimarães Portugal
通讯: C.S. Abreu @ Center for Microelectromechanical Systems (CMEMS‐UMinho), University of Minho Guimarães Portugal
相关性: 该论文研究了HAp和β-TCP添加对Ti6Al4V生物复合材料摩擦学性能的影响，但未涉及多孔结构、LPBF制造或疲劳测试，与用户研究方向关联度低。
边界: This paper does not cover LPBF fabrication, porous structure design, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or surface roughness characterization relevant to AM biomedical implants.

方法· 采用热压法制备Ti6Al4V+HAp/β-TCP复合材料，进行球-平面往复滑动摩擦试验。
结果· 添加生物活性相增加了摩擦系数和磨损率，HAp颗粒拔出主导磨损机制。
验证· 通过摩擦试验和磨损表面显微观察验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To accelerate bone ingrowth and reduce implant fixation time by incorporating bioactive calcium phosphates into Ti6Al4V.
Method· Ti6Al4V biocomposites with 10 vol% HAp or β-TCP were fabricated via hot pressing and subjected to reciprocating sliding tribological tests against Al2O3 in PBS at 37°C.
Result· Addition of bioactive phases increased both coefficient of friction and wear rate, with pull-out of HAp clusters being a dominant wear mechanism.
Validation· Tribological tests and SEM micrographs of worn surfaces validated the wear mechanisms and friction behavior.
