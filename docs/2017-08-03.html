单位: Department of Prosthodontics, Hubei‐MOST KLOS &amp; KLOBM, School and Hospital of Stomatology Wuhan University Wuhan China
通讯: Jiawei Wang @ Department of Prosthodontics, Hubei‐MOST KLOS &amp; KLOBM, School and Hospital of Stomatology Wuhan University Wuhan Ch
相关性: 该论文仅涉及牙科植入体框架的适配性评估方法，与应力屏蔽、骨整合或疲劳性能无关。
边界: This paper does not address stress shielding, stiffness matching, fatigue testing, or osseointegration; it focuses on dimensional accuracy of Co-Cr frameworks.
动机· 提出一种评估多单元植入体框架适配性的新方法。

验证· 与印模复制法对比验证新方法的有效性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To propose an alternative method for evaluating the overall adaptation of implant-supported multi-unit prosthetic frameworks.

Result· Milled frameworks showed significantly better adaptation (0.046 ± 0.057 mm distance difference) than SLM frameworks (0.073 ± 0.048 mm).
Validation· The new method was validated by comparison with the conventional impression replica technique for marginal gap measurement.
