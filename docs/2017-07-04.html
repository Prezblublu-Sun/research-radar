Uncertainty Quantification Metrics with Varying Statistical Information in Model Calibration and Validation

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Sifeng Bi, Saurabh Prabhu, Scott Cogan, Sez Atamturktur AIAA Journal 2017-07-04 10.2514/1.j055733
单位: University of Burgandy Franche Comté, 25000 Besancon, France
通讯: Sifeng Bi @ University of Burgandy Franche Comté, 25000 Besancon, France
相关性: 该论文讨论了模型校准与验证中不同统计信息水平的度量指标，对不确定性量化有参考价值，可迁移至生物力学代理模型的验证。
边界: This paper focuses on general model calibration and validation metrics without application to FEA surrogate modeling or implant biomechanics.
动机· 研究不同统计信息水平的度量指标对模型校准与验证结果的影响。
方法· 基于欧氏距离、马氏距离和巴氏距离三种度量指标进行校准与验证对比。
结果· 度量指标的选择显著影响校准模型预测，巴氏距离包含最多统计信息。
验证· 通过两个案例研究，包括交叉验证与测量变异性分析。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate how different validation metrics with varying levels of statistical information affect model calibration and validation outcomes.
Method· Three distance-based metrics (Euclidean, Mahalanobis, Bhattacharyya) are compared in a calibration-validation framework.
Result· The choice of metric significantly influences calibrated model predictions, with Bhattacharyya distance incorporating the most statistical information.
Validation· Two case studies demonstrate the metrics' impact, including cross-validation against measurement variability.

Low FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Background only theoretical UQ
Pınar Acar, Veera Sundararaghavan AIAA Journal 2017-07-04 10.2514/1.j055689
单位: University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, Michigan 48109
通讯: Pınar Acar @ University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, Michigan 48109
相关性: 该论文关注微结构属性不确定性量化，与生物力学植入物设计中的代理模型和约束优化无直接关联。
边界: No surrogate modeling for expensive FEA, no implant or biomechanics context, no constraint handling or multi-objective optimization.
动机· 量化电子背散射衍射数据中取向分布函数的不确定性对材料性能的影响。
方法· 在Rodrigues基本区域内开发线性平滑方案，并用高斯假设建模联合概率分布。
结果· 通过随机变量直接变换解析计算非线性性能的不确定性。
验证· 理论推导，无实验或数值验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To account for uncertainty in electron backscatter diffraction data when predicting microstructural properties.
Method· A linear smoothing scheme in the Rodrigues fundamental region is developed to compute the orientation distribution function, and joint multivariate probability distributions are modeled with a Gaussian assumption.
Result· Uncertainty in nonlinear engineering properties is analytically obtained via direct transformation of random variables in homogenization.
Validation· Theoretical derivation without experimental or numerical validation.
