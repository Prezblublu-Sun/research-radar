Transient temperature evolution of Selective Laser Melting process based on multilayer finite element model

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only simulation_case
Dongming Xiao, He Kuanfang, Di Wang 红外与激光工程 2015-09-25 link
相关性: 该论文仅关注SLM过程中的温度场模拟，未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能，与用户研究方向关联度低。
边界: This paper focuses on thermal simulation of SLM without any link to porous structure design, stress shielding, mechanical testing, or osseointegration.
动机· 建立SLM多层瞬态温度场有限元模型以理解工艺参数影响。
方法· 采用温度相关材料属性及粉末-固体转换，建立两层单元有限元模型。
结果· 峰值温度随激光功率增大和扫描速度减小而升高，且略偏向激光束后方。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To establish a multilayer transient temperature finite element model for Selective Laser Melting to understand the effect of process parameters on temperature field.
Method· A two-layer finite element model incorporating temperature-dependent material properties and powder-to-solid transition was developed.
Result· Peak temperature and thermal gradient increase with higher laser power and lower scan speed, with the peak slightly shifting behind the laser beam center.
Validation· Simulation results were validated against single-track experimental data.
