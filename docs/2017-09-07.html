通讯: Dongyun Zhang @ Institute for Laser Engineering, Beijing University of Technology, Pingleyuan No. 100, Chaoyang District, Beijing 100124
相关性: 本文针对拓扑优化设计的Ti-6Al-4V多孔结构，通过SLM制造并测试其力学性能，为匹配不同骨骼部位的弹性模量提供参考，对髋关节植入物应力遮挡研究有借鉴意义。
边界: This paper does not address fatigue behavior, surface roughness, or osseointegration, and is not specifically focused on hip implant stress shielding or micromotion.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To combine topology optimization and selective laser melting for fabricating porous structures that match the elastic modulus of human bone.
Method· Porous structures with porosity from 40% to 80% and unit cell size from 2 to 8 mm were designed via topology optimization, fabricated by SLM, and mechanically tested.

Validation· Experimental compressive tests were conducted, and failure models were proposed based on the Gibson-Ashby model to describe compressive behavior.
