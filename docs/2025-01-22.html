Stochastic Deep Learning Surrogate Models for Uncertainty Propagation in Microstructure-Properties of Ceramic Aerogels

High FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Md Azharul Islam, Dwyer Deighan, Shayan Bhattacharjee, Daniel Tantalo, Pratyush Kumar Singh et al. arXiv preprint 2025-01-22 link
相关性: 该论文提出的贝叶斯CNN代理模型用于不确定性传播的方法，可迁移至植入物力学性能的随机模拟与设计优化。
边界: This paper focuses on ceramic aerogels rather than biomedical implants or biomechanics, and does not address implant-specific constraints or patient-specific geometries.
动机· 克服陶瓷气凝胶微观结构随机性对力学性能影响的计算成本。
方法· 结合格子玻尔兹曼模拟与有限元，用贝叶斯CNN构建微观结构生成和性能预测代理模型。
结果· 代理模型能有效生成微观结构图像并准确预测应变能，实现不确定性传播。
验证· 通过数值模拟数据训练，测试分布内和插值形态的泛化能力。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To overcome the prohibitive computational cost of repeated physics-based simulations for characterizing the impact of microstructure randomness on mechanical properties of ceramic aerogels.
Method· A framework combining Lattice Boltzmann simulations for microstructure formation and finite element analysis for mechanical properties, with Bayesian Convolutional Neural Networks as surrogate models for both microstructure generation and property prediction.
Result· The Bayesian CNN surrogate accurately predicts strain energy for in-distribution microstructures and demonstrates generalization to interpolated morphologies, enabling efficient uncertainty propagation.
Validation· Numerical validation using physics-based simulation data, testing on in-distribution and interpolated microstructures.
