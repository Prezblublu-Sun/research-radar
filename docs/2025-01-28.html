Method· A misspecified GP is conditioned to satisfy the PDE in a weak sense with a modified source, and the loss is replaced by an empirical negative-Sobolev norm, leading to NeS-PINN.
Result· The proposed method preserves convergence guarantees for rough-forced PDEs and extends PINNs to stochastic equations.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks demonstrate convergence and accuracy.
