单位: Department of Mechanical, Chemical and Materials Engineering, University of Cagliari, Cagliari 09123, Italy e-mail:
通讯: Tiziano Ghisu @ Department of Mechanical, Chemical and Materials Engineering, University of Cagliari, Cagliari 09123, Italy e-mail:
相关性: 该论文展示了在工业问题中结合多项式混沌与最小二乘逼近进行高效不确定性量化的方法，可迁移至植入物/生物力学设计中的昂贵仿真采样问题。

Validation· Validation is performed on simulation cases of two large bypass-ratio jet engine fans with shape uncertainty.

通讯: Maged Awadalla @ College of Science and Engineering Flinders University 1284 South Rd., Clovelly ParkAdelaide South Australia Australia

边界: This paper studies tibial trays in knee revision, not femoral stems in hip arthroplasty; no proximal femur stress shielding or Gruen zone analysis.

方法· 基于CT图像建立有限元模型，模拟AORI IIA骨缺损，分析不同假体组合的微动和应变分布。

验证· 单一患者CT模型，无实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of varying stem and metaphyseal sleeve sizes on the primary stability of cementless revision tibial trays in reconstructing AORI IIA defects.
Method· A finite element model was constructed from CT images of a single tibia with a virtual AORI IIA defect, and parametric variations of stem size, sleeve size, BMI, and bone modulus were simulated.
Result· Stemless sleeves provided adequate primary stability (average micromotion <50 μm), and adding a stem did not improve stability; BMI and bone modulus affected strain distribution but not micromotion in the sleeve region.
Validation· Single-patient simulation without experimental validation.
