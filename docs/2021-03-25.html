Effect of Heat Treatment on the Microstructure and Mechanical Properties of Selective Laser-Melted Ti64 and Ti-5Al-5Mo-5V-1Cr-1Fe

单位: Institute of System Engineering, China Academy of Engineering Physics, Mianyang 621900, China
通讯: Tong Liu @ Chengdu Science and Technology Development Center, China Academy of Engineering Physics, Chengdu 610200, China  |  Xudong Cui @ Institute of Chemical Materials, China Academy of Engineering Physics, Mianyang 621900, China
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM钛合金的热处理对微观结构和力学性能的影响，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能，与生物医学植入物的直接关联较弱。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or osseointegration, which are core to the AM biomedical direction.
动机· 通过热处理调控SLM钛合金的非平衡微观结构以改善力学性能。
方法· 对SLM制备的Ti64和Ti-55511合金进行不同工艺的热处理，分析微观结构演变。
结果· Ti64经950°C退火获得层状结构，断裂韧性达90.8 MPa·m^(1/2)；循环热处理获得双态结构，综合性能优异。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To tailor the metastable microstructure of SLM-fabricated titanium alloys via post-process heat treatment for improved mechanical properties.
Method· Selective laser melting of Ti-6Al-4V and Ti-5Al-5Mo-5V-1Cr-1Fe followed by various heat treatments (annealing, cyclic heat treatment) to produce lamellar and bimodal microstructures.
Result· Ti-6Al-4V annealed at 950°C achieved a lamellar structure with the highest fracture toughness of 90.8 ± 2.1 MPa·m^(1/2), while cyclic heat treatment yielded a bimodal structure with balanced strength, ductility, and toughness.
Validation· Mechanical testing including tensile, impact, and fracture toughness measurements confirmed the property improvements.
