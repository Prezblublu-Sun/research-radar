动机· 利用数字孪生优化空中基站的部署位置和参数。

Validation· Numerical evaluation in large network scenarios (50 UEs, 10 ABS) across Sionna and AODT digital twin platforms.

ViT-EnsembleAttack: Augmenting Ensemble Models for Stronger Adversarial Transferability in Vision Transformers

Hanwen Cao, Haobo Lu, Xiaosen Wang, Kun He arXiv preprint 2025-08-17 link
相关性: 该论文关于视觉变换器的对抗攻击，与FEA代理建模和生物力学植入物设计无关。
边界: This paper addresses adversarial robustness in computer vision, with no surrogate modeling, no finite element analysis, no biomechanics, and no implant design application.
动机· 增强集成视觉变换器对抗攻击的迁移性
方法· 通过三种对抗增强策略和贝叶斯优化生成增强模型并集成
结果· 显著提升了对抗攻击在ViT上的迁移性
验证· 在多个ViT架构和数据集上进行攻击成功率测试
English summary & terms
Motivation· Adversarial attacks on Vision Transformers suffer from limited transferability; ensemble methods can help but are underexplored for ViTs.
Method· Three augmentation strategies (Multi-head dropping, Attention score scaling, MLP feature mixing) are applied to surrogate ViTs, optimized via Bayesian optimization, then ensembled with automatic reweighting and step size enlargement.
Result· The proposed ViT-EnsembleAttack achieves state-of-the-art adversarial transferability on ViT architectures across multiple datasets.
Validation· Extensive experiments on standard image classification benchmarks (e.g., ImageNet) with diverse ViT models measure attack success rates.
