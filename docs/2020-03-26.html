Motivation· Bayesian optimization has largely focused on continuous parameters, lacking efficient strategies for categorical variables in autonomous experimentation.

Result· Gryffin outperforms state-of-the-art categorical optimization algorithms when domain knowledge is provided, and refines this knowledge to promote scientific understanding.
Validation· Validated on comprehensive benchmarks and three real-world examples in materials science and chemistry.
