边界: This paper does not address expensive simulation sampling, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification for surrogate models, or transferability to implant/biomechanics design.

Motivation· Deep neural networks often rely on spurious correlations, degrading generalization to diverse scenarios.
Method· Proposes Evidential Alignment, a framework using uncertainty quantification via second-order risk minimization and evidential calibration to debias models without group annotations.
Result· Empirical results show significant improvement in group robustness across architectures and data modalities.
Validation· Validated on standard image classification benchmarks with spurious correlations.

Validation· Evaluated on multiple standard COD datasets and compared with existing encoder-decoder methods.
