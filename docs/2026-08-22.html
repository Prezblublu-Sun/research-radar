Motivation· Mobile robots require tightly regulated DC power under rapidly varying loads, motivating hybrid control strategies that combine fast nonlinear regulation with predictive optimization.
Method· A cascade architecture integrating sliding mode control for inner-loop regulation and an analytically solved model predictive controller for finite-horizon duty-cycle correction is proposed for a DC-DC buck converter.
Result· The hybrid controller reduces maximum voltage deviation by 43.6% and settling time by 66.1% compared to MPC-only, while PID achieves the lowest RMSE and final-phase error.
Validation· The approach is validated through averaged-model simulations and switching-level Simulink models with explicit PWM and parasitics, confirming output within ±2% of reference.
