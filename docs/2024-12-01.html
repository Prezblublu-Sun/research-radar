英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the influence of surface roughness and design features of titanium dental implants on primary stability and osseointegration.
Method· Sixty implants were categorized by roughness (smooth, moderately rough, highly rough) and design (cylindrical, tapered, hybrid); primary stability was assessed via insertion torque and RFA, and osseointegration via histomorphometry in rabbit models.
Result· Highly rough surfaces and tapered designs yielded significantly higher insertion torque, ISQ values, BIC, and BAFO at all time points.
Validation· In vivo rabbit model with histological and histomorphometric analyses at 4, 8, and 12 weeks.
