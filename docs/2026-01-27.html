Defect-based multiaxial fatigue life assessment of 3D-printed bulk metallic glasses

单位: Institute of General Mechanics (IAM), RWTH Aachen University, Aachen, Germany
通讯: Songyun Ma @ Institute of General Mechanics (IAM), RWTH Aachen University, Aachen, Germany
相关性: 该论文研究了3D打印金属玻璃的多轴疲劳行为，与用户关注的生物医学植入物疲劳相关，但材料体系不同。
边界: This paper does not address porous structure for stress shielding, biomedical implant context, or osseointegration; it focuses on bulk metallic glass fatigue with defect modeling.
动机· 3D打印金属玻璃的多轴疲劳行为尚未被充分研究。
方法· 进行多轴疲劳实验，结合含孔隙的RVE模型和修正Dang Van准则预测寿命。
结果· 提出的模型能准确预测疲劳寿命，94%的预测值落在2σ散带内。
验证· 通过17个试样的多轴疲劳实验验证。
英文摘要与术语

Method· Multiaxial fatigue tests were conducted on SLM-fabricated Zr-based BMG, and defect-based RVE simulations with a modified non-local Dang Van criterion were developed.
Result· The proposed model predicts multiaxial fatigue life with 94% of predictions within the 2σ scatter bands.
Validation· Experimental validation using 17 specimens under proportional and non-proportional loading paths.

E-QRGMM: Efficient Generative Metamodeling for Covariate-Dependent Uncertainty Quantification

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Zhiyang Liang, Qingkai Zhang arXiv preprint 2026-01-27 link
相关性: 该论文提出的E-QRGMM方法通过插值加速生成式元建模，并支持协变量相关的不确定性量化，可迁移至生物力学代理模型中的高效采样与UQ。
边界: This paper does not apply the method to FEA or biomechanics problems, lacks validation on implant or structural mechanics benchmarks, and does not address multi-objective or constrained optimization.
动机· 现有协变量相关不确定性量化方法计算成本高，难以高效生成条件分布。
方法· 提出E-QRGMM框架，结合三次Hermite插值与梯度估计加速分位数回归生成式元建模。
结果· 在保持收敛率的同时将网格复杂度从O(n^{1/2})降至O(n^{1/5})，实现精度与速度的更好权衡。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Covariate-dependent uncertainty quantification in simulation-based inference is computationally expensive, and existing methods struggle with efficient conditional distribution generation.
Method· E-QRGMM accelerates quantile-regression-based generative metamodeling by integrating cubic Hermite interpolation with gradient estimation, reducing grid complexity from O(n^{1/2}) to O(n^{1/5}).
Result· The method achieves a superior trade-off between distributional accuracy and training speed compared to QRGMM and other deep generative models on synthetic and real datasets.
Validation· Validation is performed on synthetic and practical datasets, comparing distributional accuracy and computational efficiency against QRGMM and advanced deep generative models.

边界: This paper focuses on econometric forecasting of energy markets, with no FEA, surrogate modeling, uncertainty quantification, or biomedical implant context.
动机· 探究结构计量模型在能源预测中能否兼顾精度与可解释性。
方法· 将TVP-SVAR与DCC-GARCH、t-copula等依赖结构结合，并与多种机器学习模型对比。

Result· Copula-enhanced TVP-SVAR and GPR achieve statistically equivalent predictive accuracy, but only the econometric model provides interpretable impulse responses and tail-risk diagnostics.
