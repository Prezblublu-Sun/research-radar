Validation· Mechanical testing (tensile, microhardness), SEM/EDS, and in vitro biocompatibility assays with osteoblasts and gingival fibroblasts.

边界: This paper does not address expensive simulation sampling, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification for surrogate models, or biomechanics/implant design.
动机· 现有隐私保护共形预测依赖数据分割，降低样本效率。
方法· 提出基于差分隐私的全数据共形预测框架，避免数据分割。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Existing private conformal prediction methods rely on data splitting, which reduces effective sample size and prediction efficiency.
Method· A full-data privacy-preserving conformal prediction framework is proposed, leveraging differential privacy stability to avoid data splitting and a conservative private quantile routine.
Result· The framework provides a universal coverage floor under generic differential privacy and asymptotically recovers the nominal coverage level with refined mechanism-specific analysis.
Validation· Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate sharper prediction sets compared to split-based private baselines.
