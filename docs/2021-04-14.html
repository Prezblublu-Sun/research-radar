English summary & terms

Five Degree-of-Freedom Property Interpolation of Arbitrary Grain Boundaries via Voronoi Fundamental Zone Octonion Framework

边界: No application to biomechanics, implant design, or structural FEA; only demonstrated on grain boundary property interpolation.
动机· 降低高计算成本的晶界属性模拟负担
方法· 利用Voronoi基本区八元数框架进行五自由度属性插值
结果· 高斯过程回归使均方根误差相比常数模型降低83%
验证· 通过非平滑验证函数及Fe/Ni模拟数据集评估
English summary & terms
Motivation· To reduce computational burden of expensive grain boundary property simulations by developing efficient surrogate models.
Method· Proposes a Voronoi fundamental zone octonion (VFZO) framework for five degree-of-freedom property interpolation, enabling fast distance computation and surrogate modeling via barycentric, inverse-distance, nearest-neighbor, and Gaussian process regression methods.
Result· Gaussian process regression achieves an 83.0% reduction in root mean square error relative to a constant mean model, and outperforms prior work on noisy Fe datasets by 34.4%.
Validation· Validated on a non-smooth analytical function and simulated bicrystal datasets for Fe and Ni using 50,000 random input and 10,000 prediction grain boundaries.
