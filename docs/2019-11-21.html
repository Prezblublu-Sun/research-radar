单位: State Key Laboratory of Material Processing and Die & Mould Technology, School of Materials Science and Engineering, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan 430074, China
通讯: Lichao Zhang @ State Key Laboratory of Material Processing and Die & Mould Technology, School of Materials Science and Engineering, Hua
相关性: 该论文研究的是用于注塑模具的TiC/AISI420不锈钢复合材料的SLM工艺，与生物医学植入物无关。
边界: This paper focuses on injection mold applications and does not address biomedical implants, porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration.
动机· 提高注塑模具在高压高强环境下的硬度和抗拉强度。
方法· 利用SLM制备5 wt% TiC/AISI420复合材料，研究能量密度对致密化和性能的影响。
结果· 在最优参数下获得了近全致密组织，硬度592 Hv，抗拉强度1452 MPa，延伸率8.65%。
验证· 通过SEM、XRD、拉伸和硬度测试验证微观结构和力学性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the hardness and tensile strength of injection molds for high-intensity and high-pressure environments.
Method· Selective laser melting was used to fabricate 5 wt% TiC/AISI420 composites, and the effect of volumetric energy density on densification, microstructure, and properties was investigated.
Result· Near-full density was achieved at optimized parameters, yielding microhardness of 592 Hv, tensile strength of 1452 MPa, and elongation of 8.65%.
Validation· Microstructural evolution and mechanical properties were validated via SEM, XRD, tensile tests, and hardness measurements.

单位: Department of Materials and Mineral Resources Engineering, National Taipei University of Technology, Taipei, Taiwan, Republic of China
通讯: Shih‐Hsien Chang @ Department of Materials and Mineral Resources Engineering, National Taipei University of Technology, Taipei, Taiwan, Rep

Motivation· To investigate the effects of porosity elimination and post-treatments on the mechanical properties of precipitation hardening stainless steel fabricated by selective laser melting.
