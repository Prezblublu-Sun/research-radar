On the effects of geometry, defects, and material asymmetry on the mechanical response of shape memory alloy cellular lattice structures

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Skim only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Mohammad Reza Karamooz-Ravari, Sajedeh Nasr Esfahani, Masood Taheri Andani, Mahmoud Kadkhodaei, Abbas Ghaei et al. Smart Materials and Structures 2016-01-04 10.1088/0964-1726/25/2/025008
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Graduate University of Advanced Technology, Kerman 76311-33131, Iran
通讯: Mohammad Reza Karamooz-Ravari @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Graduate University of Advanced Technology, Kerman 76311-33131, Iran
相关性: 该论文涉及NiTi形状记忆合金多孔结构的LPBF制造与压缩测试，但未针对骨科植入物的应力遮挡或疲劳性能进行研究。
边界: This paper does not address stress shielding, stiffness matching for biomedical implants, fatigue testing, or surface roughness/osseointegration.
动机· 评估几何、缺陷和材料不对称性对形状记忆合金多孔结构力学响应的影响
方法· 采用SLM制造NiTi多孔和致密样品，进行压缩实验，并用基于微平面理论的三维本构模型进行有限元模拟
结果· 微结构缺陷和材料不对称性显著影响多孔结构的力学响应

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To assess the effects of geometry, microstructural imperfections, and material asymmetry on the mechanical response of shape memory alloy cellular lattice structures.
Method· NiTi cellular and dense samples were fabricated via selective laser melting and tested in compression; a 3D microplane-based constitutive model was used in finite element simulations.
Result· Microstructural imperfections and asymmetric material response significantly influence the mechanical behavior of cellular lattices.
Validation· Experimental compression tests on SLM-fabricated NiTi samples validated the finite element predictions.

Hui Wang, Sanjairaj Vijayavenkataraman, Yang Wu, Zhen Shu, Jie Sun et al. International Journal of Bioprinting 2016-01-04 10.18063/ijb.2016.01.005
相关性: 该论文研究电喷雾喷射参数对PCL纤维支架成型的影响，未涉及任何人工智能方法。
边界: No AI/ML methods are used or integrated into any bioprinting workflow stage.
动机· 制造具有复杂几何形状的PCL纤维支架以模拟天然组织微结构。
方法· 研究电压、浓度、距离、速度等电喷雾参数对纤维直径的影响，并优化打印半环形和螺旋形支架。
结果· 成功打印出复杂几何形状的PCL支架，螺旋形预期促进细胞迁移。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To fabricate PCL fibrous scaffolds with complex geometries that mimic native tissue microarchitecture for tissue engineering.
Method· Systematically investigated the effects of electrohydrodynamic jetting parameters (voltage, concentration, distance, stage speed, feed rate) on fiber diameter and used optimized parameters to print semi-lunar and spiral scaffolds.
Result· Demonstrated the capability to print complex-geometry PCL scaffolds with controlled fiber diameter, with spiral geometry expected to enhance cell migration.
Validation· Parameter effects were validated by measuring printed fiber diameters and examining scaffold morphology.

Enhanced Nucleate Pool Boiling From Microstructured Surfaces Fabricated by Selective Laser Melting

Exclude AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Ignore mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Jin Yao Ho, Kin Keong Wong, K.C. Leong, Chun Yang 2016-01-04 10.1115/mnhmt2016-6616
单位: Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, Singapore
通讯: Jin Yao Ho @ Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, Singapore
相关性: 该论文研究SLM制造微结构表面以增强池沸腾换热，与生物医学植入物的应力屏蔽、疲劳或骨整合无关。
边界: This paper focuses on heat transfer enhancement via SLM microstructures, with no biomedical context, no implant mechanics, no mechanical testing for osseointegration or fatigue.
动机· 探索SLM制造微结构表面增强核态沸腾换热的可能性。
方法· 使用SLM制备四种微结构表面（微腔、微翅、矩形沟道、方形沟道），在FC-72工质中进行饱和池沸腾实验。
结果· 微腔和微翅表面平均换热系数提升高达51.2%，微翅表面在热流17.9 W/cm²时增强63.5%。
验证· 通过池沸腾实验对比微结构表面与光滑表面的换热系数和临界热流密度。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Investigate the feasibility of using SLM-fabricated microstructured surfaces to enhance nucleate pool boiling heat transfer.
Method· Four microstructured surfaces (micro-cavity, micro-fin, micro-channel, micro-square channel) were fabricated via SLM using AlSi10Mg powder, and saturated pool boiling experiments were conducted with FC-72 coolant under atmospheric conditions.
Result· Micro-cavity and micro-fin surfaces showed up to 51.2% enhancement in average heat transfer coefficient, with the micro-fin surface achieving a 63.5% enhancement at 17.9 W/cm² heat flux.
Validation· Experimental comparison of heat transfer coefficients and critical heat fluxes between microstructured and plain surfaces, supported by visual observations of bubble nucleation.

Validation· Qualitative and quantitative data from case study visits and comparison classes.
