英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the outcomes of bilateral total hip arthroplasty in patients with dysplastic hip arthritis complicated by osteopenia or osteoporosis.
Method· Clinical evaluation and radiographic anthropometric analysis of the proximal femur were performed in 72 female patients, comparing those with and without reduced bone mineral density.
Result· The main group achieved an average Harris hip score of 88.2, while the control group scored 89.1, with cementless implants used predominantly.
Validation· Validation was based on clinical outcome scores and preoperative radiographic indices (cortical, Singh, morpho-cortical, Noble, Spotorno).
