英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate dental implant survival in grafted alveolar cleft patients and compare outcomes between early and modern treatment eras.
Method· A systematic review of 18 studies was conducted, stratifying by era (1997-2008 vs 2010-2026) and calculating weighted pooled survival rates with two-proportion Z-test.
Result· Weighted pooled survival improved significantly from 91.2% in early studies to 94.2% in modern cohorts, a 3.0% absolute increase.
Validation· No experimental validation; the study is a meta-analysis of published clinical data.
