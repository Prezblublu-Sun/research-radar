Is the energy density a reliable parameter for materials synthesis by selective laser melting?

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only none Exp. validation
Konda Gokuldoss Prashanth, S. Scudino, T. Maity, J. Das, J. Eckert Materials Research Letters 2017-03-09 10.1080/21663831.2017.1299808
单位: Erich Schmid Institute of Materials Science, Austrian Academy of Sciences, Leoben, Austria
通讯: Konda Gokuldoss Prashanth @ Erich Schmid Institute of Materials Science, Austrian Academy of Sciences, Leoben, Austria
相关性: 这篇论文讨论了选择性激光熔化中的能量密度参数可靠性，但未涉及生物医学植入物、多孔结构或应力屏蔽等方向。
边界: This paper does not cover porous structures, stress shielding, mechanical testing (fatigue/compression), surface roughness, or osseointegration relevant to biomedical implants.
动机· 评估选择性激光熔化中能量密度参数作为能量转移指标的可靠性。
方法· 在恒定能量密度下改变激光功率和速度，分析熔化和试样质量。
结果· 能量密度公式只能提供近似估计，需考虑扫描间距和材料特性。
验证· 通过实验对比不同参数下的熔化和试样质量。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate whether the energy density parameter reliably represents the energy transferred to the powder bed in selective laser melting.
Method· Varying laser power and scanning speed while keeping energy density constant to analyze melting behavior and bulk specimen quality.
Result· The conventional energy density equation provides only an approximate estimation, and factors like hatch spacing and material properties should be included.
Validation· Experimental comparison of melt characteristics and specimen quality under different processing parameters.
