A Single Formulation for Uncertainty Propagation in Turbomachinery: SAMBA PC

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Richard Ahlfeld, Francesco Montomoli Journal of Turbomachinery 2017-07-28 10.1115/1.4037362
单位: Uncertainty Quantification Lab, Department of Aeronautics, Imperial College of London, London SW7 2AZ, UK
通讯: Francesco Montomoli @ Uncertainty Quantification Lab, Department of Aeronautics, Imperial College of London, London SW7 2AZ, UK e-mail:
相关性: 该论文提出的SAMBA PC方法是一种高效的UQ方法，可处理稀缺数据和多种输入分布，对生物力学植入物设计中的不确定性量化具有潜在迁移价值。
边界: This paper focuses on turbomachinery CFD applications, not on FEA surrogate modeling for implant biomechanics, and lacks validation on structural mechanics or biomedical design problems.
动机· 解决涡轮机械中多种输入分布和稀缺数据下的UQ难题
方法· 提出基于矩的稀疏多项式混沌方法SAMBA PC，直接传播实验数据
结果· 仅需少量模拟即可保持5%误差，线性处理高维输入
验证· 通过解析测试函数和低压涡轮叶片CFD模拟验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To address the challenges of uncertainty quantification in turbomachinery with diverse input types and scarce data.
Method· Proposes SAMBA PC, a moment-based sparse polynomial chaos method that directly propagates experimental data without fitting parametric distributions.
Result· Achieves linear scaling with input dimensions and maintains 5% error with only 21 simulations for ten random variables.
Validation· Validated on nonlinear analytic test functions and a low-pressure turbine blade CFD simulation.

单位: Department of Biomedical Engineering, CARIM School for Cardiovascular Diseases, Maastricht University, Universiteitssingel 50, Room 3.358, 6229 ER, Maastricht, The Netherlands. m.heusinkveld@maastrich
通讯: Maarten H. G. Heusinkveld @ Department of Biomedical Engineering, CARIM School for Cardiovascular Diseases, Maastricht University, Universiteitssing
相关性: 该论文涉及动脉壁力学模型的不确定性量化，但未涉及植入物设计或有限元代理模型。
边界: No implant mechanics, no surrogate modeling for FEA, no optimization or constraint handling.
动机· 量化动脉壁力学模型的不确定性以评估血管药物疗效。
方法· 基于虚拟数据集和全局方差敏感性分析，量化模型参数不确定性。
结果· 胶原承载力的不确定性可能掩盖药物效果，增加测量重复次数可降低不确定性。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify uncertainty in arterial wall constitutive model predictions for non-invasive assessment of vascular drug therapies.
Method· Generated virtual data with measurement error, fitted single-exponential curves to obtain parameter distributions, and performed global variance-based sensitivity analysis.
Result· Collagen load bearing uncertainty could conceal drug effects; increasing measurement repetitions from 3 to 10 reduced uncertainty by 50%.
Validation· Simulated vascular drug treatment to assess the impact of model uncertainty on predicted outcomes.
