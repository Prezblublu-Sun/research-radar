Hot Corrosion of Ti–Re Alloys Fabricated by Selective Laser Melting

Low AM Biomedical Materials openalex Peripheral Background only none
Kamil Majchrowicz, Zbigniew Pakieła, Dorota Moszczyńska, Tomasz Kurzynowski, Edward Chlebus Oxidation of Metals 2017-12-18 10.1007/s11085-017-9825-2
单位: Faculty of Materials Science and Engineering, Warsaw University of Technology, Wołoska 141, 02-507, Warsaw, Poland
通讯: Kamil Majchrowicz @ Faculty of Materials Science and Engineering, Warsaw University of Technology, Wołoska 141, 02-507, Warsaw, Poland
相关性: 该论文涉及SLM工艺制备Ti-Re合金，但研究的是热腐蚀而非生物医学应用，与用户的方向无关。
边界: This paper does not address porous structure for stress shielding, fatigue, or osseointegration; it focuses on hot corrosion without any biomedical context or mechanical testing.
动机· 研究SLM制备的Ti-Re合金在高温盐环境下的热腐蚀行为。
方法· 在600°C的Na2SO4+NaCl混合盐中进行热腐蚀实验，并用SEM/EDS/XRD分析微观结构。
结果· 未溶解的铼颗粒导致氧化膜化学不均匀，影响抗热腐蚀性能。
验证· 通过腐蚀实验和微观表征验证了腐蚀机理。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the hot corrosion behavior of Ti-Re alloys fabricated by selective laser melting due to the potential of SLM for novel alloy production.
Method· Hot corrosion tests were conducted in a mixture of Na2SO4 and NaCl salts at 600°C, and the corrosion products were characterized by SEM with EDS and XRD.
Result· The hot corrosion resistance was influenced by the chemical inhomogeneity of the oxide scale caused by undissolved rhenium particles during SLM.
Validation· Experimental hot corrosion tests with microstructural analysis were performed.

Motivation· To evaluate the sensitivity and specificity of acetabular cup version assessment using only anteroposterior hip and pelvis radiographs.
Method· Retrospective radiographic analysis of two patient groups (with and without dislocation after THA) using the sign of anteversion insufficiency (SAI).
