单位: Cornell University, Ithaca, United States

相关性: 该论文提出的函数网络贝叶斯优化方法可应用于植入物设计中的多阶段仿真优化，提高采样效率。
边界: Does not include uncertainty quantification for constraints, nor is it validated on biomechanical or implant design problems.
动机· 标准贝叶斯优化忽略中间输出，查询效率低。

结果· 在合成和实际问题上显著优于标准贝叶斯优化方法。

英文摘要与术语

Method· Model each node with a Gaussian process and maximize the expected improvement under the implied posterior via sample average approximation.
Result· The proposed method dramatically outperforms standard Bayesian optimization on synthetic and real-world problems.
Validation· Validation on numerical benchmarks and real-world case studies.
