A self consistent theory of Gaussian Processes captures feature learning effects in finite CNNs

Gadi Naveh, Zohar Ringel arXiv preprint 2021-06-08 link
相关性: 该论文研究有限宽度卷积神经网络中的特征学习理论，与用户关注的有限元代理建模、生物力学优化等方向完全无关。
边界: This paper does not address surrogate modeling for expensive simulations, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification for engineering design, or any biomedical/implant context.
动机· 探索有限宽度神经网络中的特征学习效应

English summary & terms
Motivation· To capture feature learning effects in finite-size convolutional neural networks beyond infinite-width limit.
Method· Derives a self-consistent Gaussian Process theory for networks trained with noisy gradient descent.
Result· Identifies a sharp transition between feature learning and lazy learning regimes.
Validation· Validated on a toy two-layer linear CNN model.

No difference in whole-blood metal ions between 32-mm and 36- to 44-mm femoral heads in metal-on-polyethylene total hip arthroplasty: a 2-year report from a randomised control trial

Kristine Ifigenia Bunyoz, Georgios Tsikandylakis, Kristian R. L. Mortensen, Kirill Gromov, Maziar Mohaddes et al. Hip International 2021-06-08 10.1177/11207000211022623
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Copenhagen University Hospital, Hvidovre, Denmark
通讯: Kristine Ifigenia Bunyoz @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Copenhagen University Hospital, Hvidovre, Denmark
相关性: 该论文仅关注全髋关节置换术后金属离子水平，未涉及任何生物力学或有限元分析。
边界: No biomechanical modeling, no stress shielding or micromotion analysis, no FEA or implant mechanics.
动机· 研究股骨头大小对全髋置换术后金属离子水平的影响
方法· 随机对照试验，比较32mm与36-44mm股骨头的血金属离子浓度
结果· 两年随访显示两组金属离子水平无显著差异
验证· 临床随机对照试验，统计检验
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate whether larger femoral head sizes increase taper corrosion-related metal ion levels in MoP THA.
Method· A randomized controlled trial comparing 32-mm heads with the largest possible heads (36–44 mm) and measuring blood metal ions at 1 and 2 years.
Result· No significant difference in cobalt, chromium, or titanium levels between groups at either time point.
Validation· Prospective randomized clinical trial with statistical comparison.
