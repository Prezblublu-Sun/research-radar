相关性: 该论文研究集成学习方法的理论性质，与有限元代理模型或生物力学设计无直接关系。
边界: This paper does not address FEA surrogate modelling, expensive simulation sampling, constraint handling, or uncertainty quantification, and lacks transferability to implant/biomechanics design.
动机· 探究集成模型中增加模型数量是否总能提升性能。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· The paper investigates whether adding more models to an ensemble always improves its average performance under convex and nonconvex loss functions.
Method· A theoretical analysis is conducted using convexity of loss functions and a new result on monotonicity of tail probabilities.
Result· For convex losses, ensemble loss decreases monotonically with more models; for nonconvex losses, ensembles of good models improve while ensembles of bad models worsen.
Validation· The theoretical findings are illustrated on a medical melanoma diagnosis task and a movie rating prediction experiment.
