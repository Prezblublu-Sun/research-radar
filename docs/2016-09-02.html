单位: Master de Implantología, Facultad de Odontología, Universidad de Sevilla, Spain
通讯: Eugenio Velasco‐Ortega @ Master de Implantología, Facultad de Odontología, Universidad de Sevilla, Spain
相关性: 该论文研究了牙科植入物的粗糙度和残余应力对疲劳和骨整合的影响，但未涉及多孔结构或LPBF制造工艺。
边界: This paper does not involve LPBF fabrication, porous structure design, or stiffness matching for stress shielding; it focuses on surface treatments of commercial dental implants.
动机· 研究不同表面处理对牙科植入物疲劳和骨整合的影响。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the influence of surface roughness and residual stresses on the fatigue and osseointegration behavior of dental implants.
Method· Fatigue tests and in vivo rabbit implantation were conducted on CP-titanium implants with four surface treatments: as-received, acid etching, spark-anodization, and grit-blasting.
Result· Grit-blasting improved fatigue life due to compressive residual stresses and enhanced osseointegration with higher mechanical retention.
Validation· Validation was performed through S-N curve fatigue testing and histometric/pull-out tests after 4 and 10 weeks of implantation in rabbits.
