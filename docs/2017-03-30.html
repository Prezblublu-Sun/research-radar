通讯: Wei Song @ Department of Biomedical Engineering, Wayne State University, Detroit, MI, United States of America
相关性: 该论文涉及钛植入体的纳米纤维涂层和骨整合，但未涉及LPBF制造、多孔结构或疲劳测试。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no porous structure design for stiffness matching, no fatigue testing, and no surface roughness or as-built morphology characterization.
动机· 开发载药纳米纤维涂层以同时促进骨整合和抑制感染。
方法· 通过同轴静电纺丝在钛表面沉积PCL/PVA纳米纤维涂层，负载多西环素。

Validation· In vivo rat tibia implantation model with μCT, histomorphometry, and mechanical push-in testing.
