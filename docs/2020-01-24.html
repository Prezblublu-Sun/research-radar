Motivation· To review the clinical application of MRI for evaluating periprosthetic pathology after arthroplasty.
Method· This review summarizes artifact reduction techniques and the use of 1.5 T MRI for assessing complications in total knee, hip, and shoulder arthroplasty.
Result· MRI provides diagnostic value for periprosthetic pathology when appropriate pulse sequences are used.
Validation· No experimental validation; this is a literature review.
