英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the radiographic effectiveness of the SuperPath technique in acetabular cup placement during total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective analysis of 756 THA cases measured acetabular inclination, anteversion, leg length discrepancy, and femoral/acetabular offset.
Result· Acetabular cup orientation was within Lewinnek's safe zone in 80-85% of cases for inclination and 76-79% for anteversion, with a low dislocation rate of 0.3%.
Validation· Clinical retrospective study with statistical comparison across consecutive years.
