Experimental investigation of laser scan strategy on the microstructure and properties of Inconel 718 parts fabricated by laser powder bed fusion

Bharath Bhushan Ravichander, Kiriti Mamidi, Vignesh Ram Kumar Rajendran, Behzad Farhang, Aditya Ganesh-Ram et al. Materials Characterization 2022-01-31 10.1016/j.matchar.2022.111765
单位: Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering, The University of Texas at Arlington, Arlington, TX, USA
通讯: Amirhesam Amerinatanzi @ Material Science Engineering, The University of Texas at Arlington, Arlington, TX, USA
相关性: 该研究聚焦于Inconel 718合金的激光粉末床熔融工艺参数，未涉及医用植入物材料、多孔结构或应力遮挡，与用户研究方向无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address biomedical implant materials, porous structures for stress shielding, fatigue performance, or osseointegration; it focuses solely on Inconel 718 process-microstructure-property relationships.
动机· 研究扫描策略对IN718合金LPBF成形件性能的影响
方法· 改变激光扫描策略（如条带、岛状）进行实验对比
结果· 发现扫描策略影响晶粒取向和显微硬度，但对拉伸性能影响较小
验证· 通过金相观察、硬度测试和拉伸实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how laser scan strategy affects the microstructure and mechanical properties of Inconel 718 parts fabricated by LPBF.
Method· Different scan strategies (e.g., stripe, island) were applied and samples were characterized.
Result· Scan strategy influenced grain orientation and microhardness but had limited effect on tensile properties.
Validation· Validation via optical microscopy, microhardness testing, and tensile testing.

Effect of Process Parameters on Distortions Based on the Quantitative Model in the SLM Process

Sheng Zou, Libao Pang, Chang Xu, Xinyi Xiao Applied Sciences 2022-01-31 10.3390/app12031567
单位: School of Transportation and Logistics Engineering, Wuhan University of Technology, Wuhan 430063, China
通讯: Xinyi Xiao @ Mechanical and Manufacturing Engineering Department, Miami University, Oxford, OH 45056, USA
相关性: 该论文主要研究SLM工艺参数对变形的影响，未涉及生物医学植入物的多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue, osseointegration, or any biomedical context; it focuses solely on distortion modeling in SLM process.
动机· 分析SLM工艺参数对变形的影响机制。
方法· 建立考虑位移、塑性应变和热应变的定量模型，并通过瞬态数值方法计算。
结果· 变形随层数增加而减小，随扫描速度先增后减，提高支撑刚度可降低变形。
验证· 仅数值模拟，无实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand the distortion mechanism in selective laser melting by investigating effects of process parameters and scan strategies.
Method· A quantitative model incorporating displacements, plastic strains, and thermal strains was developed, and transient numerical simulations were performed.
Result· Distortion decreases with build height, increases with scanning speed up to 700 mm/s then decreases, and increasing support stiffness reduces distortion.
Validation· No experimental validation; the study relies solely on numerical simulations.

Mechanical properties and energy absorption of AlSi10Mg Gyroid lattice structures fabricated by selective laser melting

Siqi Wu, Lei Yang, Xiao Yang, Peng Chen, Jin Su et al. Smart Manufacturing 2022-01-31 10.1142/s2737549821500010
单位: State Key Laboratory of Materials Processing and Die & Mould Technology, School of Materials Science and Engineering, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, China
通讯: Lei Yang @ School of Transportation and Logistics Engineering, Wuhan University of Technology, Wuhan 430063, China  |  Chunze Yan @ Shenzhen Huazhong University of Science and Technology Research Institute, Shenzhen 518000, China
相关性: 该论文研究Al合金多孔结构，无任何生物医学背景或植入物相关设计。
边界: This paper uses AlSi10Mg alloy for non-biomedical engineering applications, with no stress shielding analysis, no fatigue testing, no surface roughness or osseointegration assessment, and no biomedical context.
动机· 研究SLM制造AlSi10Mg Gyroid点阵结构的力学性能与能量吸收特性。
方法· 采用Johnson-Cook本构模型的有限元分析与压缩实验验证。
结果· 有限元预测误差小于25%，能量吸收能力随体积分数增加而提高。
验证· 通过SLM制备样品的压缩实验验证模拟准确性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the compressive mechanical properties and energy absorption of AlSi10Mg gyroid lattice structures fabricated by selective laser melting for engineering applications.
Method· Finite element analysis using the Johnson-Cook material model was performed and validated by compression tests on SLM-fabricated gyroid lattice samples with varying volume fractions.
Result· FE simulations agreed with experiments (most errors <25%) and showed that energy absorption capacity increased from 1.33 to 9.61 J/mm³ with rising volume fraction.
Validation· Validation was conducted by comparing FE predictions with experimental compression tests on as-built SLM samples.

The association between socioeconomic status and the 30- and 90-day risk of infection after total hip arthroplasty

Nina M Edwards, Claus Varnum, Rob G. H. H. Nelissen, Søren Overgaard, Alma B Pedersen The Bone & Joint Journal 2022-01-31 10.1302/0301-620x.104b2.bjj-2021-1030.r1
单位: Department of Clinical Epidemiology, Aarhus University Hospital, Aarhus, Denmark
通讯: Nina M Edwards @ Department of Clinical Epidemiology, Aarhus University Hospital, Aarhus, Denmark
相关性: 该论文研究社会经济地位与全髋关节置换术后感染风险的关系，未涉及生物力学、应力遮挡或微动分析。
边界: This paper analyzes clinical infection rates and socioeconomic factors, but does not quantify femoral stress/strain, micromotion, or any biomechanical aspect relevant to implant performance.
动机· 探究社会经济地位对全髋关节置换术后感染风险的影响。
方法· 基于丹麦103,901例患者的登记数据，计算累积发病率并调整风险比。
结果· 低社会经济地位患者术后30天和90天感染风险显著升高。
验证· 大规模临床登记数据统计分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate whether socioeconomic status is associated with infection risk after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Individual-level SES markers from Danish health registries were linked to 103,901 THA patients; cumulative incidence and adjusted risk ratios were calculated using pseudo-observation and generalized linear regression.
Result· Patients living alone, with low education, low income, or low savings had significantly higher risks of any infection within 30 and 90 days after THA.
Validation· Retrospective analysis of national registry data with 103,901 patients, adjusting for multiple confounders.

Kazuki Orita, Koji Goto, Yutaka Kuroda, Toshiyuki Kawai, Yaichiro Okuzu et al. The Bone & Joint Journal 2022-01-31 10.1302/0301-620x.104b2.bjj-2021-1079.r1
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Kyoto University Hospital, Kyoto, Japan
通讯: Kazuki Orita @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Kyoto University Hospital, Kyoto, Japan
相关性: 该论文为临床随访研究，未涉及股骨近端应力分析、微动或有限元建模，与用户的研究方向无关。
边界: This paper does not include any biomechanical modeling, FEA, stress shielding analysis, or micromotion quantification relevant to hip implant mechanics.
动机· 评估第一代高交联聚乙烯在非骨水泥全髋置换术中的远期耐磨性能。
方法· 回顾性分析29例患者35髋，使用Martell分析软件测量二维聚乙烯磨损。
结果· 19年随访显示低磨损率和无骨溶解，总体稳态磨损率为0.013 mm/年。
验证· 通过临床随访和影像学测量验证长期耐磨性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the long-term wear performance of first-generation annealed highly cross-linked polyethylene in cementless total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective study of 29 patients (35 hips) with wear measured using Martell's Hip Analysis Suite on serial radiographs.
Result· At a mean follow-up of 19.1 years, the steady-state wear rate was 0.013 mm/year with no osteolysis or loosening.
Validation· Clinical follow-up and radiographic wear analysis provide validation.

Dual-scale single marker calibration for digital templating of total hip arthroplasty in standing radiographs: a prospective clinical study

Christian Ries, Christoph Baltin, Stefan Haneder, Peer Eysel, Martin Hellmich et al. Archives of Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery 2022-01-31 10.1007/s00402-022-04355-y
单位: Division of Orthopaedics, Department of Trauma and Orthopaedic Surgery, University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf, Martinistr. 52, 20246, Hamburg, Germany
通讯: Christoph Kolja Boese @ Department of Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery, University Hospital of Cologne, Joseph-Stelzmann-Str. 24, 50931, Cologne,
相关性: 该论文仅涉及术前模板校准，未涉及生物力学分析或植入物力学性能。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, no FEA, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no implant-bone interface mechanics.
动机· 提高全髋关节置换术中数字模板校准的准确性。
方法· 比较双尺度单标记法与外部校准标记法的校准误差。
结果· 双尺度单标记法显著降低了校准误差且不受BMI影响。
验证· 前瞻性临床研究，100例单侧THA患者。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the accuracy of digital templating in total hip arthroplasty by reducing calibration errors from external markers.
Method· A prospective clinical study comparing dual-scale single marker (DSSM) and external calibration marker (ECM) methods in standing pelvis radiographs.
Result· DSSM significantly reduced absolute relative deviation from internal calibration factor, with errors consistent across BMI subgroups.
Validation· Clinical validation with 100 unilateral THA patients, using implant components as reference calibration factor.
