单位: Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, Yildiz Technical University, Istanbul, Turkey
通讯: Emre Özeren @ Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, Yildiz Technical University, Istanbul, Turkey
相关性: 该论文直接研究了SLM制造的CoCr支架的混合结构设计对力学和生物学性能的影响，与用户方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing, surface roughness characterization, or osseointegration evaluation.
动机· 通过混合结构设计改善SLM制造骨支架的力学和生物学性能。
方法· 设计不同单元细胞组合的混合支架，采用SLM制造并进行压缩和细胞活性测试。
结果· 全致密表面层显著提高抗压强度，高混合度支架细胞活性最佳。
验证· 压缩测试和细胞活性实验验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To improve the mechanical and biological properties of bone scaffolds fabricated by selective laser melting through a structural hybrid design approach.
Method· Hybrid scaffolds combining different unit cells (dodecahedron, grid, octet-truss) with partially dense or fully dense surface layers were designed, fabricated via SLM, and tested under compression and cell viability assays.
Result· Fully dense surface layers increased compressive strength from 56 to 100 MPa, and the scaffold with the highest hybridity exhibited the best cell viability while maintaining competitive strength.
Validation· Mechanical compression tests and in vitro cell viability experiments.
