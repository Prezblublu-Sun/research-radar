相关性: 该论文专注于微波波导的频率扫描加速，与生物力学有限元代理建模无直接关联。
边界: No biomedical context, no implant mechanics, no surrogate model for solid mechanics or biomechanics design.

Validation· Numerical benchmarks demonstrate accuracy and computational efficiency.
