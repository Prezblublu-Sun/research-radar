单位: Tribology Research Institute, School of Mechanical Engineering, Southwest Jiaotong University, Chengdu 610031, China
通讯: Xiaogang Zhang @ Tribology Research Institute, School of Mechanical Engineering, Southwest Jiaotong University, Chengdu 610031, China  |  Zhongmin Jin @ Leeds Joint School, Southwest Jiaotong University, Chengdu 610031, China
相关性: 该论文直接研究各向异性Gyroid点阵结构的弹性响应，与用户关注的AM生物医学植入物应力屏蔽和刚度匹配高度相关。
边界: This paper does not include LPBF fabrication, mechanical testing, fatigue analysis, or surface roughness characterization.
动机· 骨修复需要各向异性点阵结构，但Gyroid结构各向异性研究不足。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Bone repair requires anisotropic cellular structures, yet the anisotropic behavior of Gyroid structures is underexplored.
Method· A parametric finite element study was conducted to evaluate the elastic response of anisotropic Gyroid cellular structures under compression by varying geometrical parameters.
Result· The degree of anisotropy ranged from 0.17 to 3.6, and the elastic modulus from 0.03 to 5.6 GPa for titanium alloy, matching human trabecular bone properties.
Validation· Validation is limited to finite element simulation without experimental fabrication or mechanical testing.
