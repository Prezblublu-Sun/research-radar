“Why Did You Blend My Learning?” A Comparison of Student Success in Lecture and Blended Learning Introduction to Sociology Courses

Exclude FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Peripheral Ignore none
Yvonne M. Luna, Stephanie A. Winters Teaching Sociology 2017-01-13 10.1177/0092055x16685373
单位: Northern Arizona University, Flagstaff, AZ, USA
通讯: Yvonne M. Luna @ Northern Arizona University, Flagstaff, AZ, USA
相关性: 该论文研究社会学课程的教学方法比较，与FEA及替代建模方向完全无关。
边界: This paper is about educational pedagogy and student performance, with no mention of finite element analysis, surrogate modeling, uncertainty quantification, or biomechanics.
动机· 比较混合学习与传统讲座式教学对学生成绩的影响。
方法· 准实验设计，对比两个班级的考试成绩和前-后测改进。
结果· 混合学习班的前-后测改进显著更大，尤其缩小了种族成绩差距。
验证· 基于实际课程成绩和测试数据的统计分析。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare the effectiveness of blended learning versus traditional lecture in an introductory sociology course.
Method· A quasi-experimental design comparing exam grades and pre-posttest improvements between two sections taught by the same instructor.
Result· The blended learning section showed significantly greater pre-posttest improvement, particularly narrowing the achievement gap for students of color and non-first-year students.
Validation· Statistical analysis of actual course grades and pre-posttest scores from a large public university.

education blended learning sociology
