单位: Department of Civil, Structural and Environmental EngineeringUniversity at Buffalo New York
通讯: Xiao Liang @ Department of Civil, Structural and Environmental EngineeringUniversity at Buffalo New York
相关性: 该论文聚焦于结构健康监测中的损伤诊断，而非生物力学或植入物设计中的代理建模。
边界: This paper does not address surrogate modeling for expensive simulations, constraint handling, uncertainty quantification in implant design, or biomechanics applications.
动机· 实现地震后建筑结构损伤的快速可靠诊断。
方法· 基于累积强度损伤特征和混淆分数矩阵优化的SVM分类框架。
结果· 框架能在近实时中鲁棒识别损伤，并考虑地震不确定性和误分类后果。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To enable near real-time and robust damage diagnosis of building structures after earthquakes.
Method· A data-driven framework using cumulative intensity-based damage features and SVM classification with Bayesian optimization, where an objective function based on confusion score matrix is optimized to balance accuracy and conservativeness.
Result· The framework achieves robust and reliable damage identification in near real-time while accounting for seismic hazard uncertainties and misclassification consequences.
Validation· Validated through 5,400 nonlinear time history analyses and 44 shake table tests on RC frames.
