English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare clinical outcomes between total hip arthroplasty and hemiarthroplasty in patients with neck of femur fractures.
Method· A retrospective study at a single hospital using the Oxford Hip Score questionnaire administered 3 years post-surgery.
Result· Both groups showed mild-to-moderate impairment; THA had shorter hospital stay but longer operative time, with no statistically significant differences in OHS.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with patient-reported outcome measures.
