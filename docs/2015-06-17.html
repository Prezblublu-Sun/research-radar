Botond Szabó, Aad van der Vaart, J. H. van Zanten The Annals of Statistics 2015-06-17 10.1214/14-aos1270
单位: Kansrekening en Statistiek
通讯: Botond Szabó @ Kansrekening en Statistiek
相关性: 该论文讨论非参数贝叶斯可信集的频率覆盖，与有限元代理建模或生物力学设计无直接关联。
边界: No FEA surrogate modeling, no implant/biomechanics context, no constraint handling or multi-objective optimization.
动机· 研究自适应非参数贝叶斯可信集的频率覆盖性质。
方法· 采用经验贝叶斯选择先验正则性，构造后验概率中心集。
结果· 对光滑尾参数有正确的不确定性量化，但对某些参数可能严重失准。
验证· 理论证明与反例分析。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the frequentist coverage properties of Bayesian credible sets in nonparametric settings with adaptive priors.
Method· Empirical Bayes is used to select the regularity of the prior, and central credible sets are constructed from the posterior distribution.
Result· Adaptive credible sets provide correct uncertainty quantification for polished tail parameters but can fail for some parameters within the regularity scale.
Validation· Theoretical proofs and counterexamples demonstrate the coverage behavior.
