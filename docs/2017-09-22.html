边界: This paper focuses on engineering education outreach and does not address surrogate modeling, finite element analysis, uncertainty quantification, or biomechanical design.
动机· 探索工程教育中融入主动服务学习的影响。
方法· 采用前后测纵向混合方法，评估学生工程师和职前教师的自我效能。
结果· 职前教师的信心和自我效能显著提升，双方均描述同伴指导模式的好处。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the impact of embedding active service learning in undergraduate engineering curricula on students' self-efficacy and attitudes.
Method· A pre-post longitudinal mixed-methods design was used to measure changes in education outreach self-efficacy among student engineers and teaching self-efficacy among pre-service teachers.
Result· Significant improvements were found in pre-service teachers' confidence and self-efficacy, with both groups qualitatively reporting benefits from the paired peer mentor model.
Validation· Validation was conducted through pre- and post-surveys and qualitative feedback from participants.
