Nan Chen, Andrew J. Majda Entropy 2018-07-04 10.3390/e20070509
单位: Department of Mathematics and Center for Atmosphere Ocean Science, Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences, New York University, New York, NY 10012, USA
通讯: Nan Chen @ Department of Mathematics and Center for Atmosphere Ocean Science, Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences, New York
相关性: 该论文专注于随机多尺度非线性系统的条件高斯框架，与FEA替代建模或生物力学设计无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address finite element analysis, surrogate modeling for expensive simulations, or implant/biomechanics design; it focuses on stochastic differential equations and data assimilation.
动机· 开发高效处理多尺度非线性随机系统的预测与不确定性量化方法。
方法· 利用条件高斯结构推导闭合解析公式，结合混合策略与块分解求解高维Fokker-Planck方程。
结果· 实现了高效统计精确的算法，适用于湍流、神经科学等领域的复杂随机系统。
验证· 通过多个理论示例（如反应扩散模型、湍流模型）展示方法有效性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop an efficient framework for prediction, state estimation, and uncertainty quantification in complex multiscale nonlinear stochastic systems.
Method· Exploiting the conditional Gaussian structure to derive closed analytical formulas for conditional statistics, combined with a hybrid strategy and block decomposition to solve high-dimensional Fokker-Planck equations.
Result· The framework yields computationally efficient and statistically accurate algorithms, demonstrated on examples from turbulence, neuroscience, and ecology.
Validation· Validation is theoretical, with illustrative examples from various domains including stochastically coupled reaction-diffusion models and large-scale dynamical systems.

方法· 对56名医学生在三个校区完成同一生理学课程，使用调查和标准化测试评估
