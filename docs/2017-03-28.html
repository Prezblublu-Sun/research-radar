Laser beam melting 3D printing of Ti6Al4V based porous structured dental implants: fabrication, biocompatibility analysis and photoelastic study

High AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Read now mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Fei Yang, Chen Chen, Qianrong Zhou, Yiming Gong, RuiXue Li et al. Scientific Reports 2017-03-28 10.1038/srep45360
单位: Department of Stomatology, Zhongshan Hospital, Fudan University, Shanghai, 200032, China
通讯: Duan Ma @ Key Laboratory of Metabolism and Molecular Medicine, Ministry of Education, Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Bio
相关性: 该论文直接涉及激光束熔化3D打印多孔钛合金牙种植体，并进行了应力遮挡分析和生物相容性评估，对增材制造生物医学植入物的研究具有直接参考价值。
边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing, surface roughness characterization, or as-built morphology analysis, and focuses on dental implants rather than hip implants.
动机· 通过多孔结构改善牙种植体的骨诱导性和应力遮挡效应。
方法· 采用激光束熔化3D打印制备三种孔径的多孔Ti6Al4V种植体，结合光弹性法和有限元分析力学性能。
结果· 多孔种植体在颈部应力遮挡降低，350 μm孔径在细胞增殖和成骨基因表达方面最优。
验证· 光弹性实验和有限元模拟验证力学分布，体外细胞实验验证生物相容性。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To improve osteoinduction and reduce stress shielding of dental implants through tailored porous structures.
Method· Laser beam melting (LBM) 3D printing was used to fabricate Ti6Al4V dental implants with three controlled pore sizes (200, 350, 500 μm), and mechanical stress distribution was analyzed using photoelastography and finite element simulation.
Result· Porous implants exhibited lower stress shielding at the implant neck and a denser stress distribution at the bottom compared to non-porous controls; the 350 μm pore size showed the best biocompatibility and osteogenic gene expression.
Validation· Mechanical validation was performed via photoelastography and finite element simulation, while biocompatibility was validated through in vitro cell proliferation, adhesion, and ALP activity assays.

Validation· Experimental observation of ligament dynamics and post-printing cell viability assays.

Tribocorrosion in Total Hip Arthroplasty

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore none Review
John M. Dundon, Prem N. Ramkumar, Claudette M. Lajam JBJS Reviews 2017-03-28 10.2106/jbjs.rvw.16.00055
单位: The Orthopedic Institute of New Jersey, Hackettstown, New Jersey
通讯: John M. Dundon @ The Orthopedic Institute of New Jersey, Hackettstown, New Jersey
相关性: 该论文主要讨论全髋关节置换术中的摩擦腐蚀现象，未涉及股骨柄的应力遮挡、微动或有限元分析。
边界: This paper focuses on tribocorrosion at modular junctions, not on stress shielding, micromotion, or any biomechanical modeling of the femoral stem.
动机· 综述全髋关节置换术中金属模块界面的摩擦腐蚀问题。
方法· 文献综述，总结临床和实验研究。
结果· 摩擦腐蚀与大头、长颈、高偏心距股骨柄相关，召回模块化柄应警惕。
验证· 无，为综述文章。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To review the clinical and mechanical aspects of tribocorrosion at modular junctions in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A literature review synthesizing findings from clinical studies and retrieval analyses.
Result· Tribocorrosion is associated with larger femoral heads, longer neck lengths, and higher-offset stems; recalled modular stems warrant suspicion.
Validation· No validation; this is a review article.
