单位: Christchurch Regenerative Medicine and Tissue Engineering (CReaTE) Group, Department of Orthopaedic Surgery and Musculoskeletal Medicine, University of Otago Christchurch, Christchurch 8011, New Zeala

相关性: 本文涉及生物组装自动化，但未使用AI/ML技术，与AI生物打印方向关联较弱。
边界: This paper does not embed any AI/ML into the bioprinting workflow; it focuses on fluidic automation without intelligent decision-making or data-driven methods.
动机· 实现微组织模块的自动化精确组装，构建复杂组织工程结构。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop an automated platform for precise assembly of micro-tissue modules into hybrid tissue constructs with clinically relevant scale and shape.

Validation· Functional validation via automated assembly of chondrocyte micro-tissues and GelMA microspheres in PEGT/PBT scaffolds, with no significant difference in viability compared to manual assembly.
