A Deep Learning-Accelerated Data Assimilation and Forecasting Workflow for Commercial-Scale Geologic Carbon Storage

High FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project simulation_case UQ
Hewei Tang, Pengcheng Fu, Christopher S. Sherman, Jize Zhang, Xin Ju et al. arXiv preprint 2021-05-09 link
相关性: 该论文提出的深度学习代理模型和数据同化框架可有效解决昂贵模拟的采样效率问题，并量化不确定性，可迁移至植入物/生物力学设计中的有限元替代建模。
边界: This paper applies surrogate modeling and data assimilation to geologic carbon storage, not to FEA-based structural or implant biomechanics problems.
动机· 地质碳储存中监测数据同化与预测的计算成本高，亟需快速方法。
方法· 结合深度学习代理模型与集成平滑器，实现高效历史匹配与预测。
结果· 在工作站上不到一小时内完成具有不确定性量化的数据同化与预测。
验证· 基于三维平顶储层模型的数值模拟验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Fast data assimilation and forecasting for commercial-scale geologic carbon storage is computationally prohibitive due to high-dimensional parameter spaces.
Method· A workflow using deep convolutional neural networks (wide residual network and residual U-Net) as surrogate models within an Ensemble Smoother Multiple Data Assimilation framework.
Result· The workflow completes history matching and reservoir forecasting with uncertainty quantification in less than one hour on a personal workstation.
Validation· Validation against a flat three-dimensional reservoir model representative of a clastic shelf depositional environment.

Validation· Experimental fatigue testing and surface topography characterization.
