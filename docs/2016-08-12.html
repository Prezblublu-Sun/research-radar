Mechanical Behaviour of Gas Nitrided Ti6Al4V Bars Produced by Selective Laser Melting

Peter Franz, Aamir Mukhtar, Warwick Downing, G.C. Smith, Ben Jackson Key engineering materials 2016-08-12 10.4028/www.scientific.net/kem.704.225
单位: TiDA Ltd
通讯: Peter Franz @ TiDA Ltd
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM制备Ti6Al4V的气体氮化处理，虽涉及力学性能，但未关注多孔结构、疲劳或骨整合，与用户方向关联有限。
边界: It does not address porous structure for stiffness matching, does not report fatigue testing, and does not link to implant osseointegration or in-vivo validation.
动机· 研究SLM制备Ti6Al4V零件的气体氮化处理对力学性能的影响。
方法· 对SLM Ti6Al4V进行气体氮化热处理，并进行拉伸和显微硬度测试。
结果· 气体氮化显著提高了表面硬度，但拉伸强度略有下降。
验证· 通过拉伸试验和显微硬度剖面验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the mechanical behavior of gas nitrided Ti6Al4V bars produced by selective laser melting.
Method· SLM-fabricated Ti6Al4V samples were subjected to gas nitriding and heat treatment, followed by tensile and microhardness testing.
Result· Gas nitriding produced a hard compound layer (TiN, Ti2N) and diffusion zone, increasing surface microhardness while slightly reducing tensile strength.
Validation· Mechanical testing (tensile, microhardness) and microstructural analysis (XRD, EDS).

单位: Fraunhofer Institute for Manufacturing Technology and Advanced Materials (IFAM)
通讯: Alexander Kirchner @ Fraunhofer Institute for Manufacturing Technology and Advanced Materials (IFAM)
相关性: 该论文研究了电子束熔融Ti-6Al-4V的力学性能（包括疲劳），为生物医学植入物的增材制造提供了基础力学数据，但未涉及多孔结构或应力屏蔽。
边界: Does not address porous architectures or biomedical implants; no stress shielding or osseointegration analysis.
动机· 评估EBM钛合金的安全可靠性所需的静力和疲劳强度
方法· 对EBM制造的Ti-6Al-4V试样进行不同热处理，进行拉伸和疲劳测试以及裂纹扩展分析
结果· 获得了不同热处理下的力学行为与微观结构演变的关联
验证· 通过拉伸和疲劳测试以及与传统制造和SLM对比验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate static and fatigue strength critical for safety-relevant parts made by electron beam melting of Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Ti-6Al-4V specimens fabricated by EBM were subjected to various heat treatments, then tested for tensile and fatigue properties, crack propagation, and fractography.
Result· The mechanical behavior was correlated with microstructural evolution induced by the heat treatments.
Validation· Results were compared with conventionally manufactured and SLM Ti-6Al-4V, providing experimental validation.

Role of molten pool mode on formability, microstructure and mechanical properties of selective laser melted Ti-6Al-4V alloy

Jingjing Yang, Jie Cai Han, Hanchen Yu, Jie Yin, Ming Gao et al. Materials & Design 2016-08-12 10.1016/j.matdes.2016.08.036
单位: Wuhan National Laboratory for Optoelectronics, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan 430074, PR China
通讯: Zemin Wang @ Wuhan National Laboratory for Optoelectronics, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan 430074, PR China
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM Ti-6Al-4V的熔池模式对成形性和力学性能的影响，但未涉及多孔结构、应力屏蔽、疲劳或表面形貌等生物医学植入物关键关注点。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures for stiffness matching, fatigue testing, surface roughness, or osseointegration, which are central to the AM biomedical implant direction.
动机· 研究熔池模式对SLM Ti-6Al-4V成形性和性能的影响
方法· 通过改变激光功率和扫描速度控制熔池模式，分析微观结构和力学性能
结果· 熔池模式显著影响微观组织和拉伸性能，传导模式优于过渡和锁孔模式
验证· 通过光学显微镜、SEM和拉伸测试验证结果
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how molten pool mode influences the formability, microstructure, and mechanical properties of selective laser melted Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· Laser power and scanning speed were varied to achieve conduction, transition, and keyhole melting modes, followed by microstructural characterization and mechanical testing.
Result· Conduction mode yielded superior densification and mechanical properties compared to transition and keyhole modes due to fewer defects.
Validation· Experimental validation via density measurement, optical and electron microscopy, and tensile testing.

A Phase Field Approach for Modeling Melting and Re-Solidification of Ti-6Al-4V during Selective Laser Melting

单位: Technische Universiat Munchen
通讯: Peter Holfelder-Schwalme @ Technische Universiat Munchen
相关性: 该论文主要研究SLM过程中Ti-6Al-4V的微观结构演变相场模拟，未涉及多孔结构、力学性能或生物医学应用。
边界: This paper does not address porous implant structures, mechanical testing (static or fatigue), stress shielding, surface roughness, or osseointegration relevant to AM biomedical implants.
动机· 模拟SLM加工Ti-6Al-4V时的微观结构演变
方法· 基于自由焓的多相场模型描述相变
结果· 模型成功捕捉β钛到熔体的转变过程
验证· 与传统模型结果进行对比验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To model the microstructure evolution during selective laser melting of Ti-6Al-4V, focusing on phase transformations.
Method· A multi-phase field model based on free enthalpy was employed to simulate nucleation and growth during melting and re-solidification.
Result· The model captured the β-titanium to melt transition and showed qualitative agreement with conventional modeling approaches.
Validation· Validation was performed by comparing simulation results with outputs from conventional modeling methods.

Processing and Characterization of Ti-6Al-4V Samples Manufactured by Selective Laser Melting

Perla Sarria, Yadir Torres, F.J. Gotor, Eliel Gutiérrez, Melchor Rodríguez et al. Key engineering materials 2016-08-12 10.4028/www.scientific.net/kem.704.260
单位: High Polytechnic Institute “José Antonio Echeverría”
通讯: Perla Sarria @ High Polytechnic Institute “José Antonio Echeverría”
相关性: 这篇论文涉及选择性激光熔化Ti-6Al-4V的工艺与表征，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或疲劳评估，与用户研究方向关联较弱。
边界: It does not study porous structures, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or osseointegration for biomedical implants.
动机· 研究选择性激光熔化法制备Ti-6Al-4V的工艺与性能。
方法· 对SLM制备的Ti-6Al-4V样品进行退火处理，并通过SEM、XRD、接触轮廓仪、显微压痕和划痕测试进行表征。
结果· 退火后表面形成氧化层，提高了低载荷下的划痕抗力，但去除氧化层后硬度降低。
验证· 通过显微压痕和划痕测试进行力学性能评估。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the processing and characterization of Ti-6Al-4V samples fabricated by selective laser melting (SLM) and the effect of annealing.
Method· Ti-6Al-4V sheets were manufactured by SLM and subsequently annealed for stress relief, then characterized using SEM, XRD, contact profilometry, micro-indentation, and scratch tests.
Result· The annealed samples exhibited an oxide layer that increased micro-hardness and scratch resistance at low loads, but reduced bulk hardness after oxide removal.
Validation· Mechanical properties were evaluated through micro-indentation and scratch tests.

Structure-Preserving Galerkin POD Reduced-Order Modeling of Hamiltonian Systems

Yuezheng Gong, Qi Wang, Zhu Wang arXiv preprint 2016-08-12 10.1016/j.cma.2016.11.016
相关性: 该论文针对哈密顿系统的结构保持降阶建模，与用户关注的生物力学FEA代理模型、不确定性量化或多目标约束优化无直接关联。
边界: This paper focuses on Hamiltonian structure-preserving ROM, lacking application to general FEA, surrogate modeling for implant biomechanics, uncertainty quantification, or constraint handling.
动机· 传统POD降阶会破坏哈密顿系统的结构，需开发保持结构的降阶模型。
方法· 使用移位快照的POD基和伽辽金投影，在ROM中保持哈密顿结构并改进函数近似。
结果· 推导了先验误差估计，数值算例验证了方法的有效性和精度。
验证· 通过多个数值算例进行基准测试验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Standard POD-ROM destroys the Hamiltonian structure, motivating the development of structure-preserving reduced-order models for Hamiltonian systems.
Method· A Galerkin projection framework is modified by using POD bases from shifted snapshots to preserve the Hamiltonian structure and improve Hamiltonian function approximation.
Result· A rigorous a priori error estimate is derived, and numerical examples demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed structure-preserving ROM.
Validation· Validation is performed through several numerical benchmark examples.
