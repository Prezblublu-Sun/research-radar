Importance of locking plate positioning in proximal humeral fractures as predicted by computer simulations

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Save for project simulation_case Patient-specific
James Fletcher, Markus Windolf, R. Geoff Richards, Boyko Gueorguiev, Jan Buschbaum et al. Journal of Orthopaedic Research® 2019-01-29 10.1002/jor.24235
单位: AO Research Institute Davos Clavadelerstrasse 8, 7270 Davos Platz Switzerland
通讯: П. Варга @ AO Research Institute Davos Clavadelerstrasse 8, 7270 Davos Platz Switzerland
相关性: 该论文使用计算模拟评估锁定板位置对骨折固定稳定性的影响，其仿真框架和应变分析方法可迁移至植入物力学性能优化。
边界: This paper focuses on clinical fracture fixation rather than surrogate modeling for expensive simulations, and lacks uncertainty quantification or constraint handling.
动机· 量化锁定板位置变化对肱骨近端骨折固定失败风险的影响
方法· 基于CT数据建立虚拟肱骨模型，模拟不同板位置和螺钉配置下的应变分布
结果· 近端板位降低螺钉周围应变，远端板位增加应变和固定失败风险
验证· 使用已验证的计算模型模拟三种生理载荷工况
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify the effect of craniocaudal locking plate positioning on fixation failure risk in multifragmented proximal humeral fractures.
Method· Twenty-one virtual humeri models were created from CT data, and a validated computational model simulated three physiological loading conditions with varying plate positions and screw configurations.
Result· Proximal plate positioning decreased peri-screw strains while distal positioning increased strains, suggesting that even small distal malpositioning may reduce fixation stability.
Validation· The computational model was previously validated against experimental data, and simulations were performed under multiple loading scenarios.

单位: Applied and Environmental Geophysics Group, Institute of Earth Sciences, University of Lausanne
通讯: Giulia De Pasquale @ Applied and Environmental Geophysics Group, Institute of Earth Sciences, University of Lausanne
相关性: 该论文使用概率方法推断地下界面，与生物力学植入物设计中的有限元代理建模无直接关联。
边界: No FEA surrogate modeling, no implant biomechanics, no constraint handling or multi-objective optimization.
动机· 地球物理数据推断地下界面时，传统平滑约束难以准确捕捉不连续面且缺乏不确定性量化。
方法· 提出经验贝叶斯-吉布斯采样框架，在MCMC中分离界面与物性更新。
结果· 合成和实测电阻率数据表明，该方法比确定性反演更可靠地估计基岩电阻率及其空间变化。
验证· 合成数据和野外实测电阻率层析成像数据验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Geophysical inversion for subsurface interfaces suffers from smoothness constraints that blur discontinuities and lack uncertainty quantification.
Method· An empirical-Bayes-within-Gibbs formulation separates interface and property updates in a Markov chain Monte Carlo scheme.
Result· On synthetic and field resistivity data, the method yields more reliable estimates of bedrock resistivity and its spatial variations than deterministic inversions.
Validation· Validated on synthetic and surface-based electrical resistivity tomography datasets.
