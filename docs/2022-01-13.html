英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the rates and risk factors for return to work and workability after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Longitudinal data from two cohorts were analyzed using Cox proportional hazard models to identify occupational risk factors for stopping work due to hip problems.
Result· While most patients returned to work, those in physically demanding jobs had higher non-return rates, and prolonged standing, kneeling/squatting, and heavy lifting were significant risk factors for leaving work.
Validation· Clinical questionnaire follow-up with a 58% response rate and median 7.5 years follow-up.
