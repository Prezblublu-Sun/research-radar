相关性: 该论文研究全髋关节置换术中氨甲环酸的止血效果，未涉及股骨假体应力遮挡或生物力学分析。
边界: This paper does not quantify proximal femur stress/strain, micromotion, or any biomechanical modeling; it is a clinical study on blood loss reduction with TXA.
动机· 明确哪些全髋关节置换术患者能从局部氨甲环酸中获益最多
方法· 回顾性队列研究，比较123例用药与111例对照的输血率
结果· 女性且年龄>65岁的患者输血减少最显著
验证· 临床回顾性数据分析
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To identify patient subgroups that benefit most from topical tranexamic acid in primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Retrospective cohort study comparing transfusion rates in 123 TXA-treated patients versus 111 controls.
Result· Female patients over 65 years old showed the most consistent reduction in transfusion requirements.
Validation· Clinical retrospective data analysis.
