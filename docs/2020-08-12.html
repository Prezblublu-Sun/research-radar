通讯: Johan Moverare @ Division of Engineering materials, Department of Management and Engineering, Linköping University, SE 58183, Linköping S
相关性: 该论文研究增材制造IN718的高温保载疲劳行为，未涉及生物医学植入物、多孔结构或应力遮挡。
边界: No porous structure, no stress shielding or stiffness matching, no biomedical context, no osseointegration or surface roughness.
动机· 比较增材制造与传统IN718的保载疲劳行为，揭示位错亚结构对裂纹的影响。

Method· IN718 samples fabricated by SLM with different heat treatments and EBM with different scanning strategies were tested under 2160 s dwell-fatigue at 550°C.
Result· The dislocation cell substructure in SLM materials increases susceptibility to creep damage, reducing dwell-fatigue cracking resistance.
Validation· Microstructure characterization and mechanical testing were used to correlate dislocation substructures with cracking mechanisms.
