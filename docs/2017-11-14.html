Strategies for functionally graded lattice structures derived using topology optimisation for Additive Manufacturing

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Transferable Skim only simulation_case
Ajit Panesar, Meisam Abdi, Duncan L. Hickman, Ian Ashcroft Additive manufacturing 2017-11-14 10.1016/j.addma.2017.11.008
单位: Department of Aeronautics, Imperial College London, SW7 2AZ, UK
通讯: Ajit Panesar @ Department of Aeronautics, Imperial College London, SW7 2AZ, UK
相关性: 该论文研究了基于拓扑优化的功能梯度点阵结构设计策略，虽未直接涉及生物医学植入物，但其刚度匹配和增材制造考虑对生物医学应用有参考价值。
边界: No biomedical context, no stress shielding or osseointegration evaluation, no fatigue testing, and no surface roughness or as-built morphology characterization.
动机· 提出从拓扑优化结果导出功能梯度点阵结构的策略，以提升增材制造设计性能。
方法· 通过有限元分析评估不同点阵策略在特定刚度和制造可行性上的表现。
结果· 利用拓扑优化的梯度点阵策略比非优化结构比刚度提高40-50%。
验证· 有限元模拟验证两种加载工况下的结构性能。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop design strategies for deriving functionally graded lattice structures from topology optimization results that are suitable for additive manufacturing.
Method· Finite element analysis was used to evaluate the mechanical performance and manufacturing considerations of various lattice strategies under intended and variable loading scenarios.
Result· Lattice strategies leveraging topology optimization achieved approximately 40-50% higher specific stiffness compared to non-optimized structures.
Validation· Validation was performed through finite element simulations for two loading scenarios, assessing solution optimality and robustness.
