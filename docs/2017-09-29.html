边界: This paper focuses on remote sensing image classification, not on surrogate modeling, finite element analysis, or biomechanical design.
动机· 解决高分辨率遥感图像分类中训练样本获取成本高的问题。
方法· 基于批量主动学习生成优化的对象训练库，结合多分类器系统。

Result· The proposed method achieves equivalent accuracy to traditional object-oriented image analysis with a training library orders of magnitude smaller, and doubles accuracy on highly heterogeneous data.
Validation· Validated on three very high-resolution airborne datasets with varying complexity, and compared against self-learning semi-supervised SVM.

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the predictive validity of the Pain Disability Questionnaire (PDQ) psychosocial and functional subscales for one-year outcomes after total knee arthroplasty.
Method· Preoperative PDQ subscale scores were collected from 130 TKA patients and associated with one-year WOMAC and SF-36 scores using multivariate regression.
Result· Baseline PDQ psychosocial and functional disability scores significantly predicted one-year WOMAC total scores and SF-36 Mental Composite Scores.
Validation· Validation was performed through multivariate regression models correlating PDQ subscales with established outcome measures (WOMAC, SF-36).
