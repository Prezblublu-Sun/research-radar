通讯: Andrei Florin Danet @ Cardiac Surgery, Carol Davila University of Medicine and Pharmacy, Bucharest, ROU
相关性: 该论文为临床回顾性研究，未涉及任何髋关节植入物的生物力学分析或有限元建模。
边界: This paper is a clinical retrospective study on comorbidities and hospital stay, with no biomechanics modeling, no implant stress/micromotion analysis, and no FEA.
动机· 探讨术前合并症对全髋关节置换术后住院时间的影响
方法· 回顾性分析85例患者，按住院时长分组并进行卡方检验

English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates the association between preoperative comorbidities and length of hospital stay following total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective cohort of 85 patients was stratified into short and prolonged stay groups, and chi-square analysis was used to test associations with binary comorbidity variables.
Result· Cardiovascular and hepatic comorbidities, including congestive heart failure, liver cirrhosis, and arterial hypertension, were significantly associated with prolonged hospitalization.
Validation· Validation is limited to statistical significance testing in a single-center retrospective dataset without external validation.
