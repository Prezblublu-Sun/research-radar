相关性: 该论文直接比较了短柄与标准髋关节假体植入后的股骨应力分布，为应力遮挡研究提供了关键参考。
边界: This paper does not include micromotion analysis, contact mechanics, or experimental validation.

Validation· Simulation-based comparison against the intact femur model, without experimental validation.
