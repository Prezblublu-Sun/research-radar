Performance of laser sintered Ti-6Al-4V implants with bone-inspired porosity and micro/nanoscale surface roughness in the rabbit femur.

High AM Biomedical Materials pubmed Direct Read now animal Exp. validation
David J Cohen, Alice Cheng, Kaan Sahingur, Ryan M Clohessy, Louis B Hopkins et al. Biomedical materials (Bristol, England) 2017-04-28 10.1088/1748-605X/aa6810
单位: Department of Biomedical Engineering, Virginia Commonwealth University, Richmond, VA, United States of America.
相关性: 该论文直接研究了激光烧结制备的仿生多孔Ti-6Al-4V植入物在动物模型中的骨整合效果，与用户关注的AM生物医学植入物的力学及生物学性能高度相关。
边界: This paper does not address fatigue performance or stress shielding quantification, which are core aspects for AM biomedical implants in load-bearing applications.
动机· 为改善骨植入物的长期稳定性，研究激光烧结多孔钛合金植入物的骨整合效果。

验证· 通过动物体内实验、力学测试、microCT和组织形态学评估。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To improve long-term stability of bone-interfacing implants by combining laser-sintered porosity and surface roughness.
Method· Laser-sintered Ti-6Al-4V implants with trabecular bone-inspired porosity and micro/nanoscale surface roughness were tested in a rabbit femoral model.
Result· Porous implants showed significantly higher new bone volume than solid implants, though pull-out strength did not differ.
Validation· In vivo animal model with mechanical testing, microCT, and histomorphometry.

边界: This paper does not include fatigue testing or stress shielding quantification, and the mechanical testing is limited to pull-out strength without stiffness matching analysis.

英文摘要与术语

Method· The patient underwent bilateral simultaneous total hip arthroplasty, total knee arthroplasty, and ankle replacement with subtalar fusion using computer-assisted techniques.
Result· The patient regained walking ability at 4 months postoperatively and returned to work and daily activities.
Validation· No validation; this is a clinical case report without biomechanical testing or finite element analysis.
