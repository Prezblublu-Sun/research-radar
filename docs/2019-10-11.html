通讯: Zhihua Yang @ Institute for Advanced Ceramics, School of Materials Science and Engineering, Harbin Institute of Technology, Harbin, 15  |  Dechang Jia @ Institute for Advanced Ceramics, School of Materials Science and Engineering, Harbin Institute of Technology, Harbin, 15
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM制备Ti-6Al-4V/B4C纳米复合材料的微观结构与力学性能，虽未直接涉及生物医学植入物的应力遮挡或骨整合，但为生物医学用钛合金的增材制造提供了材料力学数据。

Result· The nanocomposites achieved up to 45% increase in hardness and 26% increase in ultimate compressive strength compared to the Ti-64 alloy.
Validation· Mechanical testing (microhardness and compression) and microstructural analysis (SEM, XRD) were performed.

Method· A retrospective multicenter study of 396 revision hip arthroplasty patients grouped by time interval between primary and revision surgery.
Result· Early revisions were associated with surgery-related factors and had higher survival rates than late revisions.
Validation· Clinical follow-up data with Kaplan-Meier survival analysis.

Motivation· To investigate the Stark effect on a trapped ion with extremely high polarizability in a Rydberg state.
Method· Excite a trapped ion to a Rydberg state and measure changes in trapping potential due to the quadratic Stark effect.
Result· Observed tunable changes in trap stiffness, equilibrium position, and minimum potential.
Validation· Demonstrated coherent control via Rabi oscillations between a low-lying state and a Rydberg state.
