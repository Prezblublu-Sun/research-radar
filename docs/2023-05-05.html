No-Regret Constrained Bayesian Optimization of Noisy and Expensive Hybrid Models using Differentiable Quantile Function Approximations

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project numerical_benchmark UQ
Congwen Lu, Joel A. Paulson arXiv preprint 2023-05-05 link
相关性: 该论文提出了一种带约束的贝叶斯优化方法，可迁移到植入物设计中昂贵的仿真优化问题。
边界: This paper does not apply the method to FEA or implant/biomechanics design; it lacks biomedical context and experimental validation on mechanical systems.
动机· 高效求解含噪声观测的混合模型约束全局优化问题

Method· Proposes Constrained Upper Quantile Bound (CUQB), a deterministic acquisition function exploiting composite structure, optimized via differentiable sample average approximation.
Result· CUQB achieves substantially improved sampling efficiency over traditional Bayesian optimization on environmental calibration and reactor optimization problems.
Validation· Numerical experiments on test problems with theoretical regret and constraint violation bounds.

通讯: Ivan Ortiz-Garcia @ Faculty of Dentistry, University of Seville, 41009 Seville, Spain. [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究钛表面形貌对成骨细胞黏附和细菌定植的影响，但未涉及增材制造或多孔结构，与用户方向的应力遮挡和疲劳性能无关。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no porous structure, no mechanical testing (static/fatigue), and no evaluation of stress shielding or stiffness matching.

方法· 比较四种钛表面处理（机加工、酸蚀、喷砂、喷砂+酸蚀）的粗糙度、润湿性和表面能。
结果· 喷砂表面粗糙度更高，成骨细胞黏附增强，但细菌黏附无显著差异。

Motivation· To optimize titanium surface topography for enhanced osteoblastic adhesion and reduced bacterial colonization.
Method· Four surface treatments (machined, acid-etched, grit-blasted, and combined) were characterized by confocal microscopy, contact angle, and surface energy measurements, followed by in vitro assays.
Result· Grit-blasted surfaces exhibited higher roughness (Ra up to 2.13 μm) and increased osteoblast adhesion at 21 days, but no significant difference in bacterial adhesion among groups.
Validation· In vitro cultures of SaOs-2 osteoblast-like cells and four bacterial strains (S. gordonii, S. oralis, A. viscosus, E. faecalis) with colorimetric assays.

单位: National Centre for Biological Sciences, Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, Bangalore 560065, India
通讯: M Sreepadmanabh @ National Centre for Biological Sciences, Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, Bangalore 560065, India
相关性: 该论文提出了用于3D细胞培养和生物打印的微凝胶生长介质，但未涉及任何人工智能方法。
边界: This paper does not incorporate AI or machine learning into any stage of the bioprinting workflow, lacking model training, prediction, or autonomous decision-making.
动机· 现有微凝胶制备方法复杂费时或影响培养基离子，需要简便高效的方案。

结果· 所得微凝胶透明、多孔、可调刚度，支持多种细胞培养和生物打印。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Current protocols for fabricating jammed microgels are complex, time-consuming, or incompatible with ionic-sensitive culture media, necessitating a simpler and accessible method.
Method· A rapid, high-throughput method is introduced to synthesize jammed microgels by flash-solidifying agarose granules directly in the desired culture medium.
Result· The resulting growth medium is optically transparent, porous, yield-stress, self-healing, and tunable, making it suitable for 3D cell culture and bioprinting.
Validation· Biocompatibility was validated through absorbance-based growth assays, antibiotic selection, RNA extraction, and live cell encapsulation across multiple cell types.
