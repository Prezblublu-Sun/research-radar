相关性: 该论文主要研究微生物粘附和抗菌肽，与髋关节假体应力遮挡和微动分析无关。

动机· 研究新型DLC涂层抗细菌粘附和生物膜形成能力，以及抗菌肽在感染性假体松动中的作用。
方法· 使用静态和动态流动条件评估细菌粘附，免疫组化检测hBD-3表达。
结果· DLC-PTFE-h涂层抗生物膜形成最优，DLC次之；hBD-3在感染组织中高表达。
验证· 体外细菌粘附实验和免疫组化染色验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the anti-biofouling properties of novel diamond-like carbon hybrid coatings and investigate the cellular sources of human beta-defensin-3 in septic joint implant loosening.
Method· Bacterial adhesion and biofilm formation were assessed under static and dynamic flow conditions; immunohistochemistry and double immunofluorescence were used to localize hBD-3 in peri-implant tissues.
Result· DLC-PTFE-h coating showed superior resistance to biofilm formation, and hBD-3 was predominantly expressed by macrophages and neutrophils in infected tissues.
Validation· In vitro bacterial assays and immunohistochemical analysis of clinical tissue samples.

Nursing care applied to impaired physical mobility in post operative period of arthroplasty hip in Intensive Care Unit (ICU)- systematized review of literature

相关性: 该论文关注髋关节置换术后护理，未涉及任何生物力学建模或植入物设计。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, no implant design, no FEA, no stress shielding or micromotion analysis.
动机· 了解全髋关节置换术后ICU患者活动受限的护理措施。
方法· 文献综述，检索BVS和PubMed数据库。

验证· 无实验验证，仅为文献综述。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To identify effective nursing interventions for impaired physical mobility in ICU patients after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A systematic literature review was conducted using BVS and PubMed databases with specific descriptors.
Result· Fifteen articles were selected, highlighting the importance of assessment tools for detecting impaired mobility.
Validation· No experimental validation; the study is a literature review.
