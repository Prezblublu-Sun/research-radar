通讯: Franz E. Weber @ CABMM, Center for Applied Biotechnology and Molecular Medicine, University of Zurich, Zurich, Switzerland
相关性: 该论文研究了TPMS支架的力学性能与体内骨整合能力，对骨组织工程中的多孔结构设计有直接参考价值。
边界: The paper does not cover LPBF fabrication of metallic implants, fatigue testing, or stress shielding quantification for hip implants.
