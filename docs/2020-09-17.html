Motivation· To evaluate the effect of a single preoperative dose of methylprednisolone on pain management and recovery in elderly patients undergoing total hip arthroplasty.

Motivation· To compare the stability of a novel locked proximal ulna nail versus an angular stable plate in comminuted proximal ulna fractures.
