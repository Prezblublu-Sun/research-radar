Computer-aided multiple-head 3D printing system for printing of heterogeneous organ/tissue constructs

Jin Woo Jung, Jung‐Seob Lee, Dong‐Woo Cho Scientific Reports 2016-02-22 10.1038/srep21685
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH), 77 Cheongam-Ro. Nam-Gu. Pohang. Gyeongbuk, 37673 Korea
通讯: Jin Woo Jung @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH), 77 Cheongam-Ro. Nam-Gu. Poh
相关性: 该论文涉及多喷头生物打印系统，但未集成AI技术，仅使用CAD/CAM路径规划。
边界: This paper does not incorporate AI or machine learning at any bioprinting stage; it is a CAD/CAM system for path generation without autonomous decision-making or experimental validation beyond demonstration prints.
动机· 实现异质性组织/器官支架的简便设计与打印
方法· 开发自动路径生成算法，结合多喷头系统打印四种材料
结果· 成功打印外耳、肾脏和牙齿等异质性组织模型
验证· 仅展示打印实物，无定量验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enable intuitive design and fabrication of heterogeneous cell-laden hydrogel scaffolds with free-form 3D geometry.
Method· An algorithm automatically generates printing paths from a 3D CAD model for a multiple-head system dispensing frame, two cell-laden hydrogels, and support material.
Result· Heterogeneous tissue models of outer ear, kidney, and tooth were successfully printed.
Validation· Validation is limited to demonstration prints without quantitative mechanical or biological characterization.

Evaluation of a Permanent Synthetic Osteochondral Implant in the Equine Medial Femoral Condyle

Kirsty A. Husby, Shannon K. Reed, David A. Wilson, Keiichi Kuroki, John R. Middleton et al. Veterinary Surgery 2016-02-22 10.1111/vsu.12453
单位: Department of Clinical Sciences Oregon State University Corvallis Oregon
通讯: Shannon K. Reed @ Department of Veterinary Medicine and Surgery College of Veterinary Medicine, University of Missouri Columbia Missouri
相关性: 该论文评估了马膝关节中的合成骨软骨植入物，但未涉及LPBF制造、应力遮挡或疲劳测试。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing (static or fatigue), no porous structure design for stress shielding, and no surface roughness or as-built morphology characterization.
动机· 评估合成骨软骨植入物在马股骨内侧髁中的骨长入和整合。
方法· 对6匹马进行双侧膝关节手术，植入钛基聚碳酸酯聚氨酯植入物，6个月后评估。
结果· 植入物允许骨长入，但出现跛行和并发症。
验证· 通过组织学、放射学和跛行评估验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate bone ingrowth, integration, and tolerance of a synthetic osteochondral implant in the equine medial femoral condyle.
Method· Bilateral femorotibial arthrotomies were performed in six adult horses to place a polycarbonate urethane implant with a titanium base in one medial femoral condyle, with the contralateral side as sham control, followed by 6-month evaluation.
Result· The implant allowed bone ingrowth within the titanium base and provided a synthetic articular surface, but complications including lameness and incisional issues occurred.
Validation· Validation was performed through histologic assessment, radiographic evaluation, lameness scoring, and synovial fluid analysis.

Uncertainty Quantification of Neutron Flux Due to Cross Section Data in Neutron Shielding Calculation

Kimihiro Yokoi Atomic Energy Society of Japan 2016-02-22 link
相关性: 该论文研究核反应堆中子屏蔽计算中的不确定性量化，与生物力学或植入物设计无关。
边界: No biomedical context, no implant mechanics, no FEA surrogate modeling for structural design.
动机· 量化中子屏蔽计算中截面数据不确定性对中子通量的影响。
方法· 采用不确定性传播方法，结合截面数据协方差矩阵进行蒙特卡洛模拟。
结果· 获得了中子通量的不确定度范围，识别了关键截面数据。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To quantify the impact of cross-section data uncertainties on neutron flux in shielding calculations.
Method· Uncertainty propagation using Monte Carlo simulation with covariance matrices of cross-section data.
Result· Uncertainty ranges of neutron flux were obtained and key cross-section data were identified.
Validation· Numerical simulation on a benchmark shielding problem.

Dealing with Dead Ends

单位: TU Dresden, Dresden, Germany
通讯: Stefanie Roos @ TU Dresden, Dresden, Germany
相关性: 该论文研究暗网路由算法，与AM生物医学材料方向完全无关。
边界: This paper is about peer-to-peer network routing algorithms, with no mention of additive manufacturing, biomedical materials, porous structures, mechanical testing, or osseointegration.
动机· 暗网路由延迟高，现有算法性能未充分分析。
方法· 提出NextBestOnce路由算法，利用邻居信息实现多项式对数性能。

English summary & terms
Motivation· Darknets suffer from high message delivery delays due to insufficient routing strategies, and the impact of embedding inaccuracies on greedy routing has not been adequately analyzed.
Method· We propose NextBestOnce, a provable polylog routing algorithm based only on neighbor information, and extend it to incorporate neighbor-of-neighbor information.
Result· We show that Freenet's routing algorithm cannot achieve polylog performance, while NextBestOnce achieves polylog performance and is further improved by using neighbor-of-neighbor information.
Validation· Theoretical analysis and proofs are provided; no experimental validation is performed.
