通讯: Prveen Bidare @ Heriot-Watt University, Institute of Photonics and Quantum Sciences, Edinburgh, EH14 4AS, UK

边界: This paper does not address porous structure design, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or osseointegration; it focuses on process physics in high-pressure atmospheres.

Method· High-speed and schlieren imaging were employed to observe melt pool dynamics, spatter, and denudation under argon and helium at pressures up to 5 bar.
Result· High-pressure helium reduced spatter and plasma formation, yielding surface quality comparable to atmospheric argon but at higher scan speeds.
Validation· Surface smoothness and continuity were compared across conditions using imaging and built layer assessment.

Correspondences Between Music and Involuntary Human Micromotion During Standstill

Víctor González, Agata Żelechowska, Alexander Refsum Jensenius Frontiers in Psychology 2018-08-07 10.3389/fpsyg.2018.01382
单位: RITMO Centre for Interdisciplinary Studies in Rhythm, Time and Motion, Department of Musicology, University of Oslo, Oslo, Norway
通讯: Víctor González @ RITMO Centre for Interdisciplinary Studies in Rhythm, Time and Motion, Department of Musicology, University of Oslo, Osl
相关性: 该论文研究音乐与人体不自主微运动的关系，与髋关节植入物应力遮挡或股骨柄生物力学完全无关。
边界: This paper does not address hip implant stress shielding, femoral stem design, bone-implant interface mechanics, or any finite element analysis of the proximal femur.
动机· 探究音乐与站立时人体不自主微运动之间的对应关系
方法· 71名参与者站立6分钟，播放不同音乐，用红外光学系统捕捉头部运动
结果· 电子舞曲引发最大运动量，脉冲清晰度对垂直运动影响最显著
验证· 通过交叉相关分析评估运动与音乐特征之间的延迟
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify the correspondence between involuntary body micromotion during standstill and various musical features such as pulse clarity, amplitude, and spectral centroid.
Method· 71 participants stood still for 6 minutes while listening to alternating silence and music stimuli (EDM, Classical Indian, Norwegian fiddle); head motion was captured using a marker-based infrared optical system.
Result· EDM induced the highest quantity of motion, and pulse clarity had the most significant effect on vertical motion across conditions.
Validation· Cross-correlation analysis was used to evaluate the delay between motion and musical features.

Mid-to long-term results of total hip arthroplasty after contralateral lower extremity amputation

Exclude Hip Implant & Femoral Stem openalex Peripheral Ignore clinical Exp. validation
Hong-Man Cho, Jae-Woong Seo, Hyun‐Ju Lee, Kyu-Bok Kang, Jung‐Ryul Kim et al. Acta Orthopaedica et Traumatologica Turcica 2018-08-07 10.1016/j.aott.2018.06.014
单位: Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Gwangju Veterans Hospital, Gwangju, South Korea. Electronic address: chm1228@hanmail.net
通讯: Hong-Man Cho @ Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Gwangju Veterans Hospital, Gwangju, South Korea. Electronic address: chm1228@hanmail.n
相关性: 该论文仅报告临床结果，未涉及任何生物力学建模或应力分析。
边界: No biomechanical modeling, no FEA, no stress shielding or micromotion quantification, no implant design analysis.
动机· 评估对侧截肢患者全髋关节置换术的中长期效果。
方法· 回顾性分析54例对侧膝下截肢患者的THA临床与影像学结果。
结果· 截肢组术后3个月功能评分较低，但6个月后与对照组无差异。
验证· 临床随访与影像学评估。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate mid-to long-term outcomes of cementless THA in patients with contralateral below-knee amputation.
Method· Retrospective review of 54 amputee patients undergoing THA, compared with a matched non-amputee control group.
Result· Amputee group had lower Harris Hip Score and activities of daily living at 3 months, but no differences after 6 months.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with Harris Hip Score, activities of daily living, and radiographic assessment.
