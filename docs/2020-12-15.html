Three-dimensional Volumetric Filling Ratio Predicts Stress Shielding in Short-stem Anatomic Total Shoulder Arthroplasty.

Haluk Celik, Aakash Chauhan, Cesar Flores-Hernandez, Darryl D'Lima, Heinz Hoenecke The Journal of the American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons 2020-12-15 10.5435/JAAOS-D-19-00444
单位: From the Shiley Center for Orthopaedic Research and Education (SCORE) at Scripps Clinic (Dr. Celik, Dr. Chauhan, Mr. Flores-Hernandez, Dr. D'Lima, and Dr. Hoenecke), and the Division of Sports Medicin
相关性: 该论文研究肩关节假体，与髋关节植入物方向无关。
边界: This paper is about shoulder arthroplasty and humeral stress shielding, not hip implant or femoral stem; it does not quantify proximal femur stress shielding, micromotion, or include any hip-related FE modeling.
动机· 预测肩关节置换术后肱骨应力遮挡。
方法· 利用CT测量三维体积填充率并进行ROC分析。
结果· 三维体积填充率是应力遮挡的良好预测指标。
验证· 临床病例对照研究验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To predict stress shielding after short-stem anatomic total shoulder arthroplasty using three-dimensional CT volumetric filling ratio.
Method· Measured the 3D volumetric filling ratio of metaphyseal and diaphyseal stem segments using MIMICS and assessed predictive strength via ROC analysis.
Result· The 3D VFR method significantly distinguished patients with stress shielding from those without, with an AUC of 92%.
Validation· A case-control study with preoperative CT and minimum 3-year radiographic follow-up.
