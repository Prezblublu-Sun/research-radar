Ahmad Baroutaji, Arun Arjunan, J. David Beal, John Robinson, Júlio Coroado Materials 2023-01-02 10.3390/ma16010430
单位: Additive Manufacturing of Functional Materials Research Group, Centre for Engineering Innovation and Research, University of Wolverhampton, Telford Innovation Campus, Telford TF2 9NT, UK
通讯: Ahmad Baroutaji @ Additive Manufacturing of Functional Materials Research Group, Centre for Engineering Innovation and Research, Universit
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM气氛氧含量对AlSi10Mg TPMS点阵结构力学性能的影响，但未涉及生物医用背景、应力屏蔽或疲劳性能。
边界: No biomedical implant context, no stress shielding analysis, no fatigue testing, no surface characterization for osseointegration, and uses AlSi10Mg alloy not biocompatible materials.
动机· 探究SLM腔体氧含量对AlSi10Mg TPMS点阵力学性能的影响
方法· 制备三种TPMS点阵（原始、螺旋、金刚石），在100 ppm和1000 ppm氧浓度下进行准静态拉伸和压缩实验
结果· 氧含量对点阵压缩性能影响有限，主要取决于几何构型；低氧含量提高延伸率但略微降低强度
验证· 准静态拉伸和压缩力学测试
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the effect of oxygen content in the SLM build chamber on the macroscopic mechanical properties of AlSi10Mg TPMS lattices.
Method· Fabricated primitive, gyroid, and diamond TPMS lattices via SLM under 100 ppm and 1000 ppm oxygen, followed by quasi-static tensile and compression tests.
Result· Oxygen content had a minor effect on compressive properties, which were primarily geometry-dependent; lower oxygen improved elongation but slightly reduced yield and ultimate strength.
Validation· Quasi-static mechanical testing (tensile and compression).
