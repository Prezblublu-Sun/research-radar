通讯: Luboš Řehounek @ Czech Technical University in Prague, Faculty of Civil Engineering, Department of Mechanics, Thákurova 7, 166 29 Prague
相关性: 该论文研究了多孔牙种植体对骨应力分布的影响，与用户关注的应力遮挡和刚度匹配方向相关。
边界: No LPBF fabrication, no mechanical testing, no fatigue analysis, no surface roughness or osseointegration data.
