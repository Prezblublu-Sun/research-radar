Evaluation of SS 316L TPMS Lattice Structures Manufactured Using SLM Process

单位: Department of Manufacturing Engineering and Industrial Management College of Engineering Pune (COEP) Pune, Maharashtra 411005, India
通讯: Shaikh Faisal @ Department of Manufacturing Engineering and Industrial Management College of Engineering Pune (COEP) Pune, Maharashtra 4
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM制造的SS316L TPMS点阵结构的表面粗糙度和尺寸精度，与生物医学植入物的骨整合相关，但未涉及应力遮挡或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper does not evaluate stress shielding, stiffness matching, or fatigue behavior; it focuses solely on surface roughness and dimensional accuracy of as-built TPMS lattices.
动机· 评估SLM制造的SS316L TPMS点阵结构的表面粗糙度和尺寸精度，以用于生物医学植入物。
方法· 设计并SLM打印Gyroid和Diamond两种TPMS点阵结构，进行密度测试、激光扫描和表面粗糙度测量。
结果· Diamond结构尺寸精度更高，Gyroid结构表面粗糙度更高，两者内表面粗糙度均在10-20 µm范围内。
验证· 通过密度测试和激光扫描验证尺寸精度，表面粗糙度测量验证表面质量。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To evaluate the surface roughness and dimensional accuracy of SLM-fabricated SS316L TPMS lattice structures for potential biomedical implant applications.
Method· Gyroid and Diamond TPMS lattice structures were designed and manufactured via SLM, followed by density tests, laser scanning for dimensional deviations, and surface roughness measurements at multiple locations.
Result· The Diamond lattice exhibited higher dimensional accuracy, while the Gyroid lattice showed higher surface roughness; both had inner surface roughness within the 10-20 µm range favorable for bone-tissue interaction.
Validation· Validation was performed through density measurements, laser scanning for geometric accuracy, and surface roughness profilometry.
