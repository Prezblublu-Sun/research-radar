Pairing an arbitrary regressor with an artificial neural network estimating aleatoric uncertainty

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Skim only none UQ
Pavel Gurevich, Hannes Stuke arXiv preprint 2017-07-23 link
相关性: 该方法提供了一种通用的神经网络不确定性量化框架，可迁移到代理建模中预测不确定性。
边界: This paper does not apply the method to FEA surrogate modeling, does not address expensive simulation sampling, and lacks any validation on engineering or biomechanics problems.
动机· 在回归任务中量化不同形式的偶然不确定性
方法· 通过联合损失函数同时训练两个神经网络，一个预测，一个估计不确定性
结果· 小λ值提高干净区域对损失的贡献，使预测优于传统方法
验证· 在合成数据和基准回归任务上验证
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· The paper aims to quantify aleatoric uncertainty in regression tasks performed by artificial neural networks.
Method· Two neural networks are trained simultaneously with a joint loss function, where one network predicts and the other estimates the locally averaged loss (uncertainty).
Result· Smaller hyperparameter λ leads to better predictions by increasing the relative contribution of clean input regions to the loss.
Validation· The method is validated on synthetic and benchmark regression datasets showing improved prediction accuracy and uncertainty estimation.

Learning uncertainty in regression tasks by artificial neural networks

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling openalex Transferable Skim only numerical_benchmark UQ
Pavel Gurevich, Hannes Stuke arXiv (Cornell University) 2017-07-23 link
相关性: 该论文提出了一种通用的神经网络回归不确定性量化方法，可迁移至代理模型中的不确定性估计。
边界: This paper does not apply the method to FEA surrogate modeling, lacks biomechanical or implant design context, and does not address constraint handling or multi-objective optimization.
动机· 解决神经网络回归任务中不同类型不确定性的量化问题。
方法· 同时训练两个神经网络，一个预测，另一个估计预测的不确定性。
结果· 该方法能有效量化不确定性，甚至提升预测性能。
验证· 通过超参数分析和与标准神经网络对比验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To quantify different forms of uncertainty in neural network regression without assuming a predefined noise distribution.
Method· Train two neural networks simultaneously with a joint loss function: one for prediction and the other for estimating the locally averaged loss as uncertainty.
Result· The approach effectively quantifies uncertainty and can improve prediction accuracy compared to standard neural networks.
Validation· Hyperparameter analysis and comparison with standard neural networks on regression tasks.
