单位: Orthopedic Surgery, University Medical Center Regensburg, Bad Abbach/Regensburg, Germany
通讯: Benjamin Craiovan @ Orthopedic Surgery, University Medical Center Regensburg, Bad Abbach/Regensburg, Germany
相关性: 该论文关注髋臼杯方向测量，而非股骨柄应力遮挡或微动分析，与用户研究方向不直接相关。
边界: This paper focuses on acetabular cup orientation measurement, not on femoral stem stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics.
动机· 验证一种用于全髋置换术后髋臼杯方向测量的向量算术方法。
方法· 对123例患者进行X线片和3D-CT测量比较，评估观察者间和观察者内可靠性。
结果· X线测量与CT测量在倾斜角上相关性极好，前倾角相关性中等，骨盆倾斜是主要误差来源。
验证· 以3D-CT重建为金标准进行临床验证。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To validate a vector arithmetic method for measuring acetabular cup orientation after total hip arthroplasty in clinical practice.
Method· Cup anteversion and inclination were measured on AP radiographs by two observers and compared with 3D-CT reconstructions in 123 patients.
Result· Radiographic measurements showed excellent correlation for inclination (r=0.81) and moderate for anteversion (r=0.65), with pelvic tilt being the main error source.
Validation· Clinical validation against 3D-CT as the gold standard.
