边界: This paper does not address implant design, biomechanics, or FEA surrogate modeling for biomedical applications; it focuses on a generic impact oscillator with Hertz contact.
动机· 高效量化冲击振荡器中的随机不确定性。

Validation· Results are validated against Monte Carlo simulation and time-domain PCE for single and multiple impact cases.
