Generalization of Urban Wind Environment Using Fourier Neural Operator Across Different Wind Directions and Cities

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Save for project simulation_case
Cheng Chen, Geng Tian, Shaoxiang Qin, Senwen Yang, Dingyang Geng et al. arXiv preprint 2025-01-09 link
相关性: 该论文展示了FNO在流体力学代理建模中的通用性，可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模。
边界: No implant or biomechanics context; no structural mechanics or contact mechanics; no uncertainty quantification.
动机· 高保真CFD计算成本高，难以用于实际城市风环境模拟。
方法· 使用傅里叶神经算子（FNO）模型，基于大涡模拟数据训练，预测不同风向和城市布局下的流场。
结果· FNO模型预测准确，计算时间减少99%，且通过分块训练和SDF数据增强泛化能力。
验证· 基于大涡模拟数据验证，对比不同城市布局和风向下的预测精度。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· High-fidelity CFD simulations are computationally prohibitive for real urban wind environment analysis.
Method· A Fourier Neural Operator (FNO) model is trained on large eddy simulation data to predict flow fields under varying wind directions and urban layouts, using spatial block training and signed distance function (SDF) inputs.
Result· The FNO model achieves accurate predictions with 99% reduction in computational time and improved generalization across different wind directions and cities.
Validation· Validation is performed by comparing FNO predictions with large eddy simulation data for multiple urban configurations and wind conditions.

Validation· Empirical EEG data and simulation in a digital twin environment were used for evaluation.
