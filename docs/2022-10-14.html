Motivation· To examine differences in presentation and outcomes of THA patients with and without prior hip arthroscopy.
Method· A retrospective study with propensity score matching was performed on 125 patients.
Result· Patients with prior hip arthroscopy had lower-grade osteoarthritis but similar postoperative outcomes.
Validation· Clinical data analysis from a single center.
