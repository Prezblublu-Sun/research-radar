英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To identify independent risk factors for revision surgery or conversion to total hip arthroplasty after hip arthroscopy using a large heterogeneous population.
Method· A retrospective analysis of 3,957 patients from the New York Statewide Planning and Research Cooperative System database (2011-2012) was conducted, examining surgical volume, age, sex, and other covariates.
Result· Low surgeon volume, female sex, older age, obesity, and pre-existing arthritis were significant risk factors for revision or conversion to THA.
Validation· Statistical analysis of a large statewide database with minimum 2-year follow-up.
