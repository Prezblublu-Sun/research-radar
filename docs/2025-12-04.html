英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To compare functional outcomes of cemented vs uncemented THA in a rural Indian population with limited healthcare access.
Method· Prospective cohort study with 62 patients randomly assigned to cemented or uncemented groups, assessed by Harris Hip Score up to 6 months.
Result· Cemented THA showed significantly better Harris Hip Scores at all postoperative time points compared to uncemented THA.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with statistical comparison of functional scores.
