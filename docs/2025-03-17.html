Motivation· Conventional h-adaptive FEM suffers from high interpolation cost when remeshing at each adaptive iteration.
Method· A neural network is trained as a mesh-free surrogate of the finite element solution, requiring only one training per time step with parameter initialization from the previous step.
Result· The proposed algorithm achieves desired accuracy within at most seven space-adaptive iterations per time step, significantly reducing computational time.
Validation· Numerical experiments on parabolic equations demonstrate the efficiency in capturing dynamic singularities.
