Hydromechanical mechanism behind the effect of pore size of porous titanium scaffolds on osteoblast response and bone ingrowth

High AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Read now animal Exp. validation
Pengrong Ouyang, Hui Dong, Xijing He, Xuan Cai, Yibin Wang et al. Materials & Design 2019-08-23 10.1016/j.matdes.2019.108151
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, the 2nd Hospital of Xi'an Jiaotong University, Xi'an, China
通讯: Xijing He @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, the 2nd Hospital of Xi'an Jiaotong University, Xi'an, China  |  Zhongmin Jin @ State Key Laboratory for Manufacturing Systems Engineering, School of Mechanical Engineering, Xi'an Jiaotong University,
相关性: 该论文直接研究了多孔钛支架孔径对骨再生和细胞响应的影响，与用户关于AM生物医学材料的研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not address stress shielding or stiffness matching, nor does it include fatigue testing or surface roughness characterization.
动机· 探究多孔钛支架孔径影响骨再生的水力学机制及最优孔径。
方法· SLM制备不同孔径多孔钛支架，结合CFD分析和体内植入实验。
结果· 650 μm孔径支架因适宜壁面剪切应力促进细胞响应和骨长入最佳。
验证· 兔股骨髁植入模型验证骨长入效果。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To elucidate the hydromechanical mechanism by which pore size influences bone regeneration in porous titanium scaffolds and identify the optimal pore size.
Method· Porous titanium scaffolds with varying pore sizes (400–1100 μm) were fabricated via selective laser melting, and their hydromechanical properties were analyzed using computational fluid dynamics, followed by in vitro cell culture and in vivo rabbit implantation.
Result· Scaffolds with 650 μm pores exhibited the best bone ingrowth due to favorable wall shear stress, which enhanced cell penetration, adhesion, proliferation, and differentiation.
Validation· In vivo rabbit distal femoral condyle implantation model was used to validate bone ingrowth performance.

单位: The University of Texas at Dallas 1 , Department of Mathematical Sciences, 800 W Campbell Rd., Richardson, Texas 75080, . E-mail: georgia.stuart@utdallas.edu ; sminkoff@utdallas.edu ; luisfelipe.perei
通讯: Georgia Stuart @ The University of Texas at Dallas 1 , Department of Mathematical Sciences, 800 W Campbell Rd., Richardson, Texas 75080,
相关性: 该论文使用两阶段MCMC加速地震反演中的不确定性量化，方法可迁移但领域差异大。
边界: No biomedical or implant design context; no FEA surrogate modeling; no constraint handling.
动机· 减少全波形反演中MCMC的高计算成本。
方法· 提出两阶段MCMC，用粗网格滤波器快速拒绝不可接受的样本。

Motivation· To reduce the computational cost of Markov chain Monte Carlo for full-waveform inversion by rejecting unacceptable proposals early.
Method· A two-stage MCMC algorithm is introduced that uses a coarse-grid filter based on operator upscaling to quickly reject poor velocity samples before fine-grid evaluation.
Result· The two-stage method reduces overall computation time by up to 40% and increases acceptance rate from 9% to 90% without compromising posterior accuracy.
Validation· Four numerical experiments on synthetic seismic inversion problems demonstrate the method's efficiency and accuracy compared to standard Metropolis-Hastings MCMC.
