Nikola B. Kovachki, Samuel Lanthaler, Hrushikesh Mhaskar arXiv preprint 2024-05-25 link
相关性: 该论文从理论上分析算子学习的数据复杂度，未涉及任何生物力学或植入物设计应用。
边界: No FEA surrogate modeling, no biomedical context, no constraint handling or uncertainty quantification.
动机· 探究算子学习所需数据样本量的理论下界。
方法· 利用n-宽度理论推导Lipschitz和Fréchet可微算子类的数据复杂度下界。
结果· 一般算子类存在数据复杂度诅咒，但参数高效的算子（如FNO）可实现代数级数据效率。

英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To establish theoretical lower bounds on the number of input-output samples required for operator learning to achieve a given accuracy.
Method· Derives lower bounds on n-widths for general Lipschitz and Fréchet differentiable operator classes, and shows parametric efficiency implies data efficiency using the Fourier neural operator as a case study.
Result· General operator classes suffer from a curse of data-complexity, while algebraically parameter-efficient operators achieve algebraic data efficiency.
Validation· Purely theoretical analysis without numerical experiments.
