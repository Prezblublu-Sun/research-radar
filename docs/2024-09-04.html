英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Existing adversarial attacks on skeleton-based human action recognition suffer from poor transferability, and the underlying reasons are not well understood.
Method· TASAR improves transferability by smoothing the loss function via a dual Bayesian optimization strategy and incorporating motion dynamics to disrupt spatial-temporal coherence.
Result· Extensive experiments on a large-scale benchmark demonstrate the superiority of TASAR over existing methods.
Validation· A comprehensive benchmark with 7 S-HAR models, 10 attack methods, 3 datasets, and 2 defense models is constructed for evaluation.
