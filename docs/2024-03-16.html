Motivation· To evaluate the effectiveness of nursing intervention based on Orem's self-care model in elderly patients after total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective study comparing postoperative outcomes between intervention and control groups using VAS, Barthel index, and Harris Hip Score.
Result· The intervention group showed significantly better pain, daily living, and hip function scores, and lower pneumonia incidence.
Validation· Statistical analysis of retrospective clinical data.
