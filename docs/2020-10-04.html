Influence of processing parameters of selective laser melting on high‐cycle and very‐high‐cycle fatigue behaviour of Ti‐6Al‐4V

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Transferable Skim only mechanical_testing Exp. validation
Leiming Du, Guian Qian, Liang Zheng, Youshi Hong Fatigue & Fracture of Engineering Materials & Structures 2020-10-04 10.1111/ffe.13361
单位: LNM, Institute of Mechanics Chinese Academy of Sciences Beijing 100190 China
通讯: Liang Zheng @ School of Science Harbin Institute of Technology Shenzhen 518055 China  |  Youshi Hong @ LNM, Institute of Mechanics Chinese Academy of Sciences Beijing 100190 China
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM工艺参数对Ti-6Al-4V疲劳性能的影响，但未涉及多孔结构、应力遮挡或骨整合，与AM生物医学植入物的直接相关性有限。
边界: This paper does not address porous structures, stress shielding, stiffness matching, surface roughness, or osseointegration, which are core to the AM biomedical implant direction.
动机· 探究SLM工艺参数对Ti-6Al-4V高周和超高周疲劳性能的影响。
方法· 采用正交实验设计结合方差分析，研究激光功率、扫描速度等参数对孔隙率的影响，并进行超声疲劳测试。
结果· 发现激光功率对孔隙率影响最大，孔隙率最小的组疲劳强度最高，裂纹起源于未熔合缺陷。
验证· 通过验证组实验和SEM断口观察验证了最优工艺参数组合。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To investigate the influence of selective laser melting processing parameters on the high-cycle and very-high-cycle fatigue behavior of Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Orthogonal experimental design with analysis of variance was used to examine the effects of laser power, scan speed, layer thickness, and hatch spacing on porosity, followed by ultrasonic fatigue testing at 20 kHz.
Result· Laser power had the greatest influence on porosity; the group with the lowest porosity exhibited the highest fatigue strength, and fatigue cracks initiated at lack-of-fusion defects.
Validation· Validation experiments and SEM fractography confirmed the optimal parameter combination.

动机· 目标检测中置信度往往过度自信，需要更可靠的预测不确定性。
