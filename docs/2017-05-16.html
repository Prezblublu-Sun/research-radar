Advanced reduced-order models for moisture diffusion in porous media

Medium FEA & Surrogate Modelling arxiv Transferable Skim only numerical_benchmark
Suelen Gasparin, Julien Berger, Denys Dutykh, Nathan Mendes arXiv preprint 2017-05-16 10.1007/s11242-018-1106-2
相关性: 该论文提出的降阶模型方法可迁移至生物力学有限元代理建模，但未涉及植入物设计或不确定性量化。
边界: This paper focuses on moisture diffusion in porous media, not on implant biomechanics, stress shielding, or any biomedical application, and lacks uncertainty quantification.
动机· 降低多孔介质中水分扩散模拟的计算成本。
方法· 采用谱降阶模型和广义分解法构建降阶模型。
结果· 降阶模型精度高，计算量降低约一个数量级。
验证· 与参考解对比，误差低于10^{-2}。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· To develop computationally efficient numerical methods for moisture diffusion in porous media without sacrificing accuracy.
Method· Two reduced-order modeling techniques, Spectral-ROM and Proper Generalized Decomposition, are applied to linear and nonlinear unsteady diffusion problems.
Result· Both methods achieve accurate solutions with model order reduced by approximately tenfold and errors below 10^{-2}.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks against reference solutions for one-dimensional moisture diffusion cases.

相关性: 该论文专注于航空动力学中的不确定性量化，其方法（如多项式混沌、克里金）可迁移至生物力学植入物设计的代理建模。
边界: This paper does not address biomedical implant design, FEA surrogate for biomechanics, or any contact mechanics/stress shielding problems.
动机· 航空模拟中计算昂贵，需高效不确定性量化方法。
方法· 比较准蒙特卡洛、多项式混沌、克里金等五种方法。
结果· 梯度增强克里金和径向基函数在几何随机扰动下效率最高。
验证· 基于TAU代码的跨声速RANS流场数值基准。
英文摘要与术语
Motivation· Efficient uncertainty quantification is needed for computationally expensive aerodynamic simulations with random geometry perturbations.
Method· Five methods including quasi-Monte Carlo, polynomial chaos with sparse quadrature, gradient-enhanced Kriging, radial basis functions, and point collocation polynomial chaos are compared.
Result· Gradient-enhanced Kriging and radial basis functions show superior efficiency for estimating statistics under random geometry variations.
Validation· Numerical benchmarks using the TAU code for transonic RANS flow around an airfoil.
