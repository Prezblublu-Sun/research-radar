单位: Centre for Additive Manufacturing, Faculty of Engineering, University of Nottingham, Nottingham NG7 2RD, UK
通讯: Ian Maskery @ Centre for Additive Manufacturing, Faculty of Engineering, University of Nottingham, Nottingham NG7 2RD, UK
相关性: 该论文研究了双螺旋晶格的压缩失效和能量吸收，但未涉及应力遮挡、疲劳或生物医学植入物背景。
边界: No link to stress shielding or stiffness matching, no fatigue testing, no biomedical context, and no surface roughness or osseointegration data.
动机· 探索双螺旋晶格在轻量化吸能应用中的失效模式与能量吸收性能。

Result· Heat treatment eliminated brittle fracture, achieving specific energy absorption of 16×10^6 J/m^3 up to 50% strain.
Validation· Quasi-static compression experiments validated failure modes and energy absorption performance.
