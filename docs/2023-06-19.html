3D printing customised stiffness-matched meta-biomaterial with near-zero auxeticity for load-bearing tissue repair

Medium AM Biomedical Materials openalex Direct Read now mechanical_testing Exp. validation

单位: Additive Manufacturing of Functional Materials (AMFM) Group, Centre for Engineering Innovation and Research, University of Wolverhampton, Telford Innovation Campus, Telford, TF2 9NT, United Kingdom
通讯: Arun Arjunan @ Additive Manufacturing of Functional Materials (AMFM) Group, Centre for Engineering Innovation and Research, University
相关性: 该论文直接针对承重组织修复的刚度匹配问题，采用LPBF制造CoCrMo超材料，并建立代理模型预测力学性能，与用户研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not report fatigue behavior, surface roughness, or osseointegration, which are critical for long-term implant performance.

结果· 实现近零泊松比（-0.037）和17.21 GPa的目标刚度，力学性能可调。

Motivation· Develop a customized stiffness-matched meta-biomaterial for load-bearing bone repair to facilitate personalized treatments.

Result· The meta-biomaterial achieved near-zero auxeticity (−0.037) and targeted stiffness of 17.21 GPa, with tunable mechanical properties.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated through prototype testing and numerical modeling.
