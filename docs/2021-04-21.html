English summary & terms
Motivation· This work aims to reduce complications in intervertebral fusion devices by employing additively manufactured lattice structures with tailored mechanical compliance and fluid permeability to enhance osseointegration.

Validation· Validation was performed through experimental compression tests and fluid permeability measurements on as-fabricated lattice specimens.
